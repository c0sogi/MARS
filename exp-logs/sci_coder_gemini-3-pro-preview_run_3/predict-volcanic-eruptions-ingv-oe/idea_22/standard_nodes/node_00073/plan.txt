The optimization strategy focuses on reducing overfitting and improving feature quality based on previous lessons. 

1.  **Model Regularization**: The previous solution (Idea 22) exhibited significant overfitting (Train MAE ~21k vs Val MAE ~2.65M). I will reduce the model complexity by lowering `num_leaves` from 127 to 63 and increasing L1/L2 regularization (`reg_alpha`, `reg_lambda`) to 1.0.
2.  **Texture Features**: I will implement the "Trend vs. Texture" decomposition strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049). Instead of returning an empty dictionary for texture, I will compute the signal residual (Raw - Savitzky-Golay Trend) and extract statistical moments (Std, Skew, Kurtosis) from it to capture high-frequency roughness.
3.  **Spectral Bands**: I will replace the generic equal-width frequency splitting with domain-specific band-pass filters (Low: 0.1-3Hz, Mid: 3-10Hz, High: 10-45Hz) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058). This aligns the features with physically relevant seismic tremor frequencies.
4.  **Temporal Aggregation**: I will replace the flattened window features (which are sensitive to time shifts) with aggregated window statistics (e.g., mean of window means, std of window stds) to create shift-invariant descriptors of signal evolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).
