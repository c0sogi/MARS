Based on the failure analysis of the previous solution (severe overfitting, high error correlation with kurtosis/window features) and the provided lessons, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Noise Reduction in Derivatives**: I will reduce the Savitzky-Golay polynomial order from 3 to 2. Higher-order polynomials fit high-frequency noise, which gets amplified during differentiation (calculating velocity/acceleration). Restricting this to quadratic smoothing helps isolate the physical trend (Cite solution_lesson_node_00094).

2.  **Shift-Invariant Temporal Features**: I will replace the flattened window features (e.g., `w0_mean`, `w1_mean`) with hierarchical aggregated statistics (e.g., `win_mean_std`, `win_rms_avg`). Since the data segments are random temporal snapshots, specific window indices are arbitrary; aggregating them captures the signal's volatility and texture regardless of the start time (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050, solution_lesson_node_00077).

3.  **Robust Feature Boundaries**: I will add explicit quantile features (5th and 95th percentiles) to the kinematic streams (Trend, Velocity, Acceleration). These are often more robust to outliers than moments like kurtosis, especially for impulsive seismic signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).

4.  **Regularization**: I will significantly increase `lambda_l1` and `lambda_l2` and reduce `num_leaves` to 63 to constrain the model's capacity and force it to learn generalizable patterns rather than memorizing noise.
