The issue stems from two related problems in `library/feature_extraction.py` that occur when a sensor is entirely offline (all-NaNs). First, `df.fillna(df.mean())` fails to impute missing values for columns that are completely empty because the mean itself is NaN (Cite debug_lesson_1). Second, even if imputed with zeros, the resulting constant signal has zero variance, causing `skew` and `kurtosis` calculations to result in NaNs due to division by zero (Cite debug_lesson_4).

I will fix this by:
1.  Chaining `.fillna(0)` to the imputation step in `extract_segment_features` to handle all-NaN columns.
2.  Wrapping the `skew` and `kurtosis` outputs in `np.nan_to_num` within `compute_kinematics` to safely handle zero-variance signals.
