To improve the model's generalization and robustness, I will implement **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016). The previous solution showed that the Vision Transformer (ViT) architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015) provides superior performance but tends to oscillate around a high accuracy plateau. SWA helps by averaging the weights of the model across the final epochs, finding a flatter minimum in the loss landscape which typically leads to better generalization on unseen data.

I will modify the training pipeline to:
1.  Extend the training duration from 10 to 12 epochs.
2.  Use a standard Cosine Annealing schedule for the first 8 epochs.
3.  Switch to SWA for the final 4 epochs (starting at epoch 8), using a constant learning rate of `1e-5` and collecting weight averages.
4.  At the end of training, update the Batch Normalization statistics for the SWA model and evaluate it.
5.  Submit the SWA model if it outperforms the best snapshot from the standard training phase.
