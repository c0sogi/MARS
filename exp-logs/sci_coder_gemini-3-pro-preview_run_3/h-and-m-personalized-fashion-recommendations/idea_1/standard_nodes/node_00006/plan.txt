My strategy focuses on optimizing the **Time-Aware Transition Graph** by refining the graph construction and hyperparameters to better balance trend following with repurchase behavior.

1.  **Hyperparameter Tuning**: I will adjust `DECAY_RATE` from 2.5 to 1.0 and increase `WEEKS_TO_KEEP` to 16. This allows the model to capture trends over a longer window while still prioritizing recent data, addressing the "Recency-Weighted" aspect (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`). I will also increase `HISTORY_LENGTH` to 30 to "maximize context utilization" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00005`).
2.  **Transition Matrix Normalization**: I will modify `model.py` to apply L1 normalization to the transition matrix. Previously, the matrix contained raw weighted counts, causing globally popular items to generate disproportionately high scores that drowned out the repurchase signal. Normalizing the rows to sum to 1 converts these counts into transition probabilities ($P(j|i)$), making the transition scores comparable in scale to the user history weights. This ensures the `REPURCHASE_WEIGHT` parameter effectively controls the blend between "Explore" (Next Item) and "Exploit" (Repurchase).
