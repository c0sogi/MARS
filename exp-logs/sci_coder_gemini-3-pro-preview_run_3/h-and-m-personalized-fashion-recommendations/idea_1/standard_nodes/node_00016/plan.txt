Based on the provided lessons, specifically **Lesson 00012 (Importance-Based History Truncation)**, I will optimize the user history construction process. The current implementation selects the "last N" transactions chronologically. However, this can discard frequently purchased items if a user has a recent burst of activity on other items. The improved strategy aggregates transactions by item, summing their time-decayed weights (capturing both Recency and Frequency), and then selects the top N items by this total weight. This ensures that high-signal items are retained in the user's history vector used for graph propagation.

I will also update the validation threshold in `runfile.py` to `0.026059042` as requested.
