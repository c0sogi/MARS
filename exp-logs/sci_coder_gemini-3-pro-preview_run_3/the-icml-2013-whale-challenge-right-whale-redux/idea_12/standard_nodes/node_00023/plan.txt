To optimize the solution, I will revert the complex Teacher-Student semi-supervised pipeline to a robust Supervised Learning baseline, as indicated by **Lesson 00022**. The previous solution failed to beat the baseline because the "Teacher" model was trained with a suboptimal batch size (32), likely degrading Batch Normalization statistics in the EfficientNetV2-M backbone.

My strategy is as follows:
1.  **Optimize Batch Size**: Increase `BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to 64. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**. This aligns with the configuration of the current best solution and improves gradient estimation and BN stability.
2.  **Simplify Training Pipeline**: Remove the pseudo-labeling and student training stages. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**. By focusing resources on a single, well-optimized supervised training stage, we avoid error propagation from a weak teacher and reduce complexity.
3.  **Extended Convergence**: Increase `EPOCHS` to 25 and `PATIENCE` to 7. Since we are running a single stage, we can afford a longer training duration to ensure the model reaches its peak performance (referencing **Lesson 00008** regarding the importance of reaching the true peak).
4.  **Architecture Retention**: Maintain the `tf_efficientnetv2_m` backbone with `GeM` pooling and `WeightedBCELoss`, as these components are proven to be effective (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
