To address the `torch.OutOfMemoryError` caused by the `tf_efficientnetv2_m` backbone exhausting the 16GB GPU memory, I will implement two changes based on **debug_lesson_1**. First, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 64 to 32 to directly lower the memory footprint of activations. Second, I will modify `library/trainer.py` to implement Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) by initializing a `GradScaler` and wrapping forward passes in `autocast`. This reduces memory usage further and speeds up training on Tensor Core GPUs (Cite debug_lesson_1).
