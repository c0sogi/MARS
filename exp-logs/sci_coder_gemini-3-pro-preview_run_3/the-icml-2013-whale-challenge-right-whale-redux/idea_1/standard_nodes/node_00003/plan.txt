**Strategy:**
1.  **Data Augmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001):** I will introduce on-the-fly `FrequencyMasking` and `TimeMasking` (SpecAugment) to the training pipeline. This forces the model to learn robust features rather than relying on specific frequency bands or time segments, addressing the overfitting observed in the previous run's later epochs.
2.  **Regularization:** I will add a `Dropout` layer (p=0.5) to the `ShallowCNN` architecture before the final classification head. This complements the augmentation by preventing co-adaptation of neurons.
3.  **Optimization Schedule:** I will implement a `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler in the training loop. This allows for a higher initial learning rate that decays smoothly, often helping the model settle into a better local minimum compared to a constant learning rate.
4.  **Hyperparameter Tuning:** I will increase the batch size to 256 (leveraging the A100 GPU) and extend training to 20 epochs to allow the scheduler and regularization sufficient time to work. I will also increase the patience for early stopping.
5.  **Resolution Preservation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002):** I will explicitly maintain the current Log-Mel Spectrogram resolution (64 Mels, ~32 time steps), as previous experiments showed that increasing resolution without adjusting the model's receptive field leads to performance degradation.
