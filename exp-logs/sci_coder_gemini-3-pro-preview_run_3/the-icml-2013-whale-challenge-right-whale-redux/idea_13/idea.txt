Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the specific lessons learned, I propose a **Corrected Noisy Student Self-Training Pipeline**.

The previous attempt at semi-supervised learning (`idea_12`) failed not because the methodology was flawed, but because the "Teacher" model was suboptimal (specifically, a reduced batch size compromised the Batch Normalization statistics). As established in **Lesson 22**, a Student model can only amplify the knowledge of a strong Teacher; it cannot fix a weak baseline. Therefore, this strategy structurally evolves the solution by wrapping the **Optimized Supervised Baseline** (`idea_6` configuration) into a rigorous **Self-Training loop**, ensuring that the Teacher utilizes the full regularization and batch size capacity identified as critical in **Lesson 24** and **Lesson 7**.

# Model

**Backbone: EfficientNetV2-Medium**
I will utilize the **EfficientNetV2-Medium** architecture for both the Teacher and the Student.
*   **Rationale:** Previous experiments confirmed that this architecture significantly outperforms Transformer-based models (like ConvNeXt) for spectrogram analysis (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016). It strikes the optimal balance between capacity and generalization for this dataset size.

**Head: Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**
Both models will employ **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** to aggregate feature maps.
*   **Rationale:** Whale calls are transient events. GeM pooling allows the model to dynamically focus on the high-activation time-frequency regions (the call) while suppressing the dominant background noise, a mechanism proven superior to Global Average Pooling for weakly supervised detection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

# Data

**Input Representation: High-Fidelity Log-Mel Spectrograms**
I will generate Log-Mel Spectrograms using the optimal parameters identified in **Lesson 19**:
*   **Resolution:** **384 Mel bands** to match the Compound Scaling capacity of the V2-Medium backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Temporal Dynamics:** A large FFT window (N_FFT=1024) for frequency definition, paired with a **short hop length (approx. 10ms)** to capture rapid temporal transients (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Normalization:** **Instance-level Min-Max Normalization** will be used to preserve spectral contrast, avoiding the signal degradation caused by frequency-wise standardization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).

# Training

**Strategy: Robust Noisy Student Self-Training**
This multi-stage pipeline is designed to leverage the unlabeled test data without compromising the baseline quality.

1.  **Phase 1: Optimized Teacher Training**
    *   Train the EfficientNetV2-Medium on labeled data.
    *   **Critical Correction:** Explicitly maintain a **Batch Size of 64** (using Gradient Accumulation if necessary) to ensure robust Batch Normalization statistics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Regularization Stack:** Apply the full stack of regularization: **Stochastic Depth (DropPath)**, **Classifier Dropout**, and aggressive **SpecAugment** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024). This ensures the Teacher is a robust, well-generalized model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

2.  **Phase 2: Soft Pseudo-Labeling**
    *   Use the converged Teacher (with best validation weights) to predict **soft probabilities** for the entire unlabeled `test2` dataset. Soft labels are preserved to convey uncertainty information.

3.  **Phase 3: Noise-Injected Student Training**
    *   Train a fresh EfficientNetV2-Medium (the Student) on the combined dataset (Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test).
    *   **Noise Injection:** The Student is trained with **Mixup Augmentation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004) and **SpecAugment** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003). This "noise" forces the Student to learn a smoother decision boundary than the Teacher.
    *   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross-Entropy**. For labeled data, apply **Inverse Class Frequency Weighting** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). For pseudo-labeled data, calculate the loss against the soft targets. Mixup losses will be calculated by mixing the **weighted scalar losses** to preserve gradients (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

**Optimization**
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate Scheduler** for stable convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

# Evaluation

**Inference Strategy**
*   **Checkpoint Reloading:** During both Teacher and Student training phases, the pipeline will monitor Validation AUC and save the best model state. Before generating predictions (for pseudo-labeling or final submission), the system will **explicitly reload these best-saved weights**, discarding the potentially overfitted state from the final epoch (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
