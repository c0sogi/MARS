Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the **Rotational-Invariant Heterogeneous Ensemble with Multi-Level GeM**. This strategy structurally evolves the solution by correcting the erroneous "gravity prior" assumption identified in Lesson 61, upgrading the feature aggregation mechanism to **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**, and stabilizing the high-capacity backbone with **Model EMA**.

### Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** that maximizes inductive bias diversity by pairing a CNN (Texture Expert) with a Vision Transformer (Context Expert).

1.  **Backbone 1: EfficientNetV2-M**
    *   **Architecture:** We utilize **EfficientNetV2-M**. Its **Fused-MBConv** blocks are empirically superior at capturing the high-frequency textural details (e.g., rust edges) required for this task compared to standard depthwise convolutions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   **Head:** We implement **Multi-Level Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. We extract feature maps from the last three reduction stages (strides 8, 16, and 32). Instead of Global Average Pooling (which dilutes small lesion signals), we apply a learnable **GeM Pooling** layer ($p=3$) to each stage and concatenate the outputs. This ensures the model retains sensitivity to localized high-activation anomalies **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

2.  **Backbone 2: Swin Transformer Small**
    *   **Architecture:** We utilize the **Swin Transformer Small**. Its hierarchical windowed attention captures global context and long-range dependencies, providing a complementary error profile to the CNN **Cite solution_lesson_node_00061**.
    *   **Head:** We apply the same **Multi-Level GeM Pooling** strategy to the hierarchical outputs of the Swin backbone.

3.  **Constraint:** We strictly avoid complex, uninitialized structures like Feature Pyramid Networks (FPN) or Multi-Task heads, adhering to the "Backbone → Pooling → Linear" pattern to prevent overfitting on this small dataset **Cite solution_lesson_node_00059**.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   Images will be resized to **384x384**. This resolution balances the need to resolve minute disease lesions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004** with the memory constraints of the A100 GPU when using AMP **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.

2.  **Augmentation:**
    *   **Structural Innovation (Rotational Invariance):** We explicitly reject the "gravity prior" assumption. We will employ a **Strong Geometric Augmentation** strategy that includes `ShiftScaleRotate`, `RandomFlip` (Horizontal **and** Vertical), and `Transpose`. Treating the images as rotationally invariant maximizes data efficiency and robustness **Cite solution_lesson_node_00061**.
    *   **Strict Exclusions:**
        *   **No Spatial Occlusion:** We exclude Cutout/CoarseDropout to prevent the removal of sparse disease indicators **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
        *   **No Photometric Augmentation:** We exclude Brightness/Contrast shifts to preserve critical color signals (e.g., rust red vs. scab brown) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.

### Training
1.  **Loss Function:** We use **Cross-Entropy Loss with Inverse Frequency Class Weights**. This aligns the optimization objective with the macro-averaged evaluation metric by penalizing misclassifications of the minority "multiple_diseases" class more heavily **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

2.  **Optimization & Stability:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
    *   **Relaxed Patience:** We set Early Stopping patience to **10 epochs**. This allows the high-capacity Swin backbone to overcome initial volatility and converge fully during the learning rate decay phase **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Model EMA:** We implement **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**. This stabilizes the training of the Swin Transformer by smoothing the optimization trajectory, mitigating the high variance observed in previous experiments **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Precision:** Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) is used to maximize batch size **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference Weights:** We generate predictions using the **EMA weights**.
2.  **Rotational Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):**
    *   We generate predictions for 4 views: **Original**, **Horizontal Flip**, **Vertical Flip**, and **Transpose**.
    *   **Rationale:** Unlike previous attempts that restricted TTA based on false gravity priors, we leverage the full rotational invariance of the macro leaf photography to marginalize out framing noise **Cite solution_lesson_node_00061**.
3.  **Ensemble:** The final submission is the unweighted average of the probability vectors from all trained models (5-Fold Cross-Validation).
