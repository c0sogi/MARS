Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, I will implement a **Heterogeneous Parallel Funnel Ensemble (HPFE)**.

The previous solution (MRPFE) failed because it varied embedding dimensions (8, 16, 32) across streams, which Lesson 00098 identifies as detrimental compared to fixed embedding dimensions. The strategy is to fix the embedding dimension to 16 for all streams and instead enforce diversity through architectural capacity (width) and regularization (dropout), as supported by Lesson 00066 and Lesson 00098.

Specific changes:
1.  **`library/config.py`**: Update `STREAMS_CONFIG` to use a fixed `emb_dim` of 16 for all streams. Configure streams with varying `hidden_layers` (Standard vs. Wide) and `dropout` rates (0.15, 0.20, 0.25) to leverage initialization and regularization diversity (Cite Lesson 00066, Lesson 00077). Update `WORKING_DIR` to `idea_31`.
2.  **`library/model.py`**: Rename `MRPFEModel` to `HPFEModel` to reflect the architectural shift. The underlying logic remains a parallel ensemble but now configured for homogeneous input representations.
3.  **`library/train_eval.py`** & **`runfile.py`**: Update references to `HPFEModel`.
