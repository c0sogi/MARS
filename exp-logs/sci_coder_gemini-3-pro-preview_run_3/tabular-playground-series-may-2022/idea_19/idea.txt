# Proposed Strategy: Heterogeneous Parallel Funnel Ensemble (HPFE)

## Model
We will implement a **Heterogeneous Parallel Funnel Ensemble (HPFE)**. This architecture evolves the "Parallel Independent Funnel Ensemble" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00064) by introducing **architectural and regularization diversity** across the streams. Instead of training five identical clones (which maximizes variance reduction only via initialization noise), we will instantiate five streams with distinct capacity profiles and dropout rates. This ensures the ensemble members learn slightly different representations of the manifold, decorrelating their errors and improving the robustness of the aggregated prediction.

1.  **Parallel Backbone Structure:** The model will consist of **5 independent streams** trained simultaneously within a single computational graph.
    *   **Input Stage (Shared Logic, Independent Weights):** Each stream possesses its own independent **Entity Embedding** layers.
        *   **Categorical Inputs:** The 10 decomposed character positions of `f_27` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) and discrete features `f_29`, `f_30` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
        *   **Capacity:** Embedding dimension set to **16** for all categorical variables (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
        *   **Fusion:** **Early Fusion** (concatenation) of flattened embeddings and normalized continuous features (`f_00`–`f_28`, `unique_character_count`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

2.  **Heterogeneous Streams:**
    *   **Streams 1 & 2 (The Anchors):** Standard **Funnel MLP** (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.20**. This retains the proven optimal backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    *   **Stream 3 (High Capacity):** **Wide Funnel MLP** (1024 $\to$ 512 $\to$ 256) with **Dropout 0.25**. The increased width allows for capturing subtler patterns, while higher dropout prevents overfitting.
    *   **Stream 4 (Aggressive Fit):** Standard Funnel MLP (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.15**. This stream is tuned to fit the training data more tightly.
    *   **Stream 5 (Conservative Fit):** Standard Funnel MLP (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.25**. This stream acts as a regularizer.

3.  **Common Architectural Constraints:**
    *   **Activation:** ReLU (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
    *   **Bottleneck Avoidance:** All streams terminate at a width $\ge$ 128, explicitly avoiding tapering to 64 to prevent information loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Exclusions:** No LayerNorm (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039), no Input Noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053), no Token Dropout (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).

## Data
The preprocessing pipeline is designed to ensure maximum signal fidelity and correct previous vocabulary alignment errors:

1.  **Transductive Vocabulary Alignment:** We will fit the categorical `OrdinalEncoder` on the **concatenation of the Train, Validation, and Test sets**. This ensures every unique token in the test set has a distinct embedding index, preventing signal loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
2.  **String Decomposition:** `f_27` will be split into 10 fixed-position character columns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). We avoid N-gram expansion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
3.  **Feature Engineering:** Compute and append `unique_character_count` of `f_27` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
4.  **Normalization:** StandardScaler for all continuous features.
5.  **Data Volume:** Utilize the **full training dataset**, strictly avoiding subsampling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

## Training
The training configuration is calibrated to balance stability with super-convergence, addressing previous optimizer pitfalls:

1.  **Optimizer:** **Adam** (Standard) with Weight Decay **1e-5**. We revert to standard Adam because previous experiments showed that `1e-5` is optimal for Adam (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023), whereas AdamW requires a completely different magnitude of weight decay that led to overfitting when mismatched (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063).
2.  **Loss Function:** Sum of Binary Cross-Entropy losses from all 5 streams.
3.  **Scheduler:** **One-Cycle Learning Rate Policy** to facilitate super-convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
4.  **Batch Size:** **1024**. We leverage the implicit regularization of moderate batch sizes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
5.  **Strategy:** Direct Supervised Learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

## Evaluation
1.  **Preprocessing:** Apply global vocabulary mappings and training set scalers to the test set.
2.  **Inference:** The HPFE model will output 5 probability predictions per sample.
3.  **Submission:** The final prediction will be the **arithmetic mean** of the 5 probabilities.
