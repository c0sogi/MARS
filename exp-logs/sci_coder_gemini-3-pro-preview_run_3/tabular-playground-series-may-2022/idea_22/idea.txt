# Proposed Strategy: Anchor-Variant Parallel Funnel Ensemble (AV-PFE)

## Model
We will implement an **Anchor-Variant Parallel Funnel Ensemble (AV-PFE)**. This architecture is a structural evolution of the "Heterogeneous Parallel Funnel Ensemble" that formalizes the diversity strategy into "Anchors" (stability) and "Variants" (exploration), while strictly enforcing independent representation learning.

1.  **Parallel Independent Architecture:** The model will consist of **5 independent streams** trained simultaneously within a single computational graph (Cite solution_lesson_node_00064).
    *   **Independent Embeddings:** Each stream will possess its own **independent Entity Embedding layers**. We explicitly reject shared embeddings to ensure that each stream learns a unique, decorrelated representation of the categorical manifold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Input Configuration:**
        *   **Categorical:** 10 decomposed `f_27` characters (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) and discrete features `f_29`, `f_30` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
        *   **Capacity:** Embedding dimension fixed at **16** for all streams (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018, solution_lesson_node_00027).
        *   **Fusion:** Early Fusion (concatenation) with normalized continuous features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

2.  **Anchor-Variant Topology:**
    *   All streams utilize the **Funnel MLP** backbone (decreasing layer widths) to leverage its compressive inductive bias (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004). We avoid ResNets and Transformers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Streams 1 & 2 (The Anchors):** Standard Funnel (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.20**. These provide the robust, proven baseline performance.
    *   **Stream 3 (Capacity Variant):** Wide Funnel (1024 $\to$ 512 $\to$ 256) with **Dropout 0.25**. This stream utilizes increased width to capture subtler, high-frequency patterns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Stream 4 (Aggressive Variant):** Standard Funnel (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.10**. This low-regularization stream aggressively fits the training data.
    *   **Stream 5 (Conservative Variant):** Standard Funnel (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.30**. This high-regularization stream acts as a stabilizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00069).

3.  **Architectural Constraints:**
    *   **Activation:** Standard **ReLU** exclusively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
    *   **Output:** Each stream projects its final layer (width $\ge$ 128) to a single output neuron. We explicitly avoid tapering to 64 units to prevent information bottlenecks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Exclusions:** No LayerNorm (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039), No Input Noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053), No Token Dropout (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).

## Data
The preprocessing pipeline is designed to ensure maximum signal fidelity and correct previous vocabulary alignment errors:

1.  **Transductive Vocabulary Alignment:** We will fit the categorical `OrdinalEncoder` on the **concatenation of the Train, Validation, and Test sets**. This ensures every unique token in the test set has a distinct embedding index, preventing signal loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
2.  **String Decomposition:** `f_27` will be split into 10 fixed-position character columns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). We avoid N-gram expansion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
3.  **Feature Engineering:** Compute and append `unique_character_count` of `f_27` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
4.  **Normalization:** StandardScaler for all continuous features.
5.  **Data Volume:** Utilize the **full training dataset**, strictly avoiding subsampling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

## Training
The training configuration is precisely calibrated to correct the optimization failures observed in previous iterations:

1.  **Scheduler:** **One-Cycle Learning Rate Policy** with `max_lr=1e-2`. We explicitly restore the high learning rate to facilitate Super-Convergence, correcting the failure caused by reducing it to `1e-3` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00072).
2.  **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a calibrated weight decay of **1e-4**. We increase the decay slightly from `1e-5` to account for AdamW's decoupled implementation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063) while avoiding the overly aggressive `1e-2` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
3.  **Loss Function:** The training objective is the **sum of Binary Cross-Entropy losses** from all 5 streams.
4.  **Batch Size:** **1024**. We leverage the implicit regularization of moderate batch sizes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
5.  **Strategy:** Direct Supervised Learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

## Evaluation
1.  **Preprocessing:** Apply global vocabulary mappings and training set scalers to the test set.
2.  **Inference:** The AV-PFE model will output 5 probability predictions per sample.
3.  **Submission:** The final prediction will be the **arithmetic mean** of the 5 probabilities.
