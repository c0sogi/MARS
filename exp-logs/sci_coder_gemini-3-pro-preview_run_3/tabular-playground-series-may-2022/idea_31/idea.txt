# Proposed Strategy: Frequency-Enhanced Parallel Funnel Ensemble (FEPFE)

## Model
We will implement a **Frequency-Enhanced Parallel Funnel Ensemble (FEPFE)**. This architecture builds upon the successful "Heterogeneous Parallel Funnel Ensemble" backbone but introduces a **Feature Engineering Innovation** to resolve the persistent error correlation with categorical features identified in previous iterations.

1.  **Parallel Independent Architecture:**
    *   The model will consist of **5 independent streams** instantiated within a single computational graph (Cite solution_lesson_node_00064).
    *   **Independent Representation:** Each stream will possess its own **independent Entity Embedding layers** (Dim 16) for the categorical features. We explicitly reject shared embeddings to ensure decorrelated representation learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Input Fusion:** Each stream will independently flatten its embeddings and concatenate them with the augmented continuous feature set (**Early Fusion**) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

2.  **Backbone Topology (Heterogeneous Funnel):**
    *   We retain the **Heterogeneous Funnel MLP** structure to enforce ensemble diversity through capacity and regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Streams 1, 2, 3 (Anchors):** Standard Funnel MLP (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128).
    *   **Streams 4, 5 (Capacity Variants):** Wide Funnel MLP (1024 $\to$ 512 $\to$ 256).
    *   **Activation:** Standard **ReLU** exclusively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
    *   **Bottleneck Avoidance:** All streams terminate at width $\ge$ 128 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Exclusions:** No Layer/Batch Normalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039, solution_lesson_node_00091).

3.  **Regularization Configuration:**
    *   We employ **Regularization Heterogeneity** while respecting the **Safety Floor** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077).
    *   **Dropout Rates:** Varied across streams in the safe range of **0.20 to 0.30**. We explicitly avoid rates below 0.15 to prevent overfitting.

## Data
The preprocessing pipeline introduces a distinct feature engineering paradigm to address the categorical signal bottleneck:

1.  **Feature Innovation: Positional Frequency Encoding:**
    *   Previous failure analysis indicated prediction errors were correlated with specific character positions in `f_27` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00096). Embeddings alone may struggle to capture the statistical rarity of specific states.
    *   **Action:** For each of the 10 decomposed character columns of `f_27`, we will compute the **Frequency Encoding** (normalized count of each token's occurrence in the combined Train+Test set).
    *   These 10 new continuous features will be appended to the input vector, providing the model with explicit statistical context ("How rare is this machine state?") to augment the learned embeddings.

2.  **Standard Preprocessing:**
    *   **String Decomposition:** Decompose `f_27` into 10 fixed-position character columns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    *   **Transductive Alignment:** Fit `OrdinalEncoder` on Train+Validation+Test (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Aggregate Signal:** Compute `unique_character_count` of `f_27` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Normalization:** Scale all continuous features (including the new Frequency features) using **StandardScaler**.
    *   **Data Volume:** Utilize the **full training dataset** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

## Training
The training configuration is calibrated to balance super-convergence with the specific sensitivities of AdamW, addressing previous tuning conflicts:

1.  **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a calibrated weight decay of **2e-5**.
    *   *Rationale:* Previous experiments showed `1e-5` (Adam baseline) was slightly prone to categorical errors, while `1e-4` (AdamW) caused underfitting on continuous features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00096). We select **2e-5** as the optimal midpoint to regularize without suppressing the continuous manifold.
2.  **Scheduler:** **One-Cycle Learning Rate Policy** with `max_lr=1e-2`. We explicitly maintain the high learning rate to ensure the model can escape local minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00072).
3.  **Loss Function:** The training objective is the **sum of Binary Cross-Entropy losses** calculated independently for each of the 5 streams.
4.  **Batch Size:** **1024** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
5.  **Duration:** Train for **50 Epochs**. We explicitly avoid premature truncation to allow the high-capacity streams to fully converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080).

## Evaluation
1.  **Preprocessing:** Apply the global (transductive) vocabulary mappings, frequency encoding maps, and training set scalers to the test set.
2.  **Inference:** The FEPFE model will generate 5 independent probability predictions per sample.
3.  **Submission:** The final prediction will be the **arithmetic mean** of these 5 probabilities.
