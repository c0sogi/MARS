### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Structural Innovation (U-Net++ & Deep Supervision):** I will employ a **U-Net++** decoder architecture. Unlike the standard U-Net, U-Net++ utilizes nested, dense skip pathways that reduce the semantic gap between the encoder and decoder feature maps (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015). I will enable **Deep Supervision**, calculating the loss at multiple nested decoder nodes (L1, L2, L3, L4). This forces the model to learn semantically discriminative features at various scales, improving gradient flow and boundary precision.
*   **Input-Architecture Alignment (Correction):** Unlike Idea 5, which attempted to modify the first convolutional layer (leading to performance degradation), I will strictly maintain the encoder's original 3-channel input structure. I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth, constructing the input tensor as `[Seismic_Image, Seismic_Image, Depth_Map]` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014). This preserves the integrity of the pre-trained ImageNet weights, allowing the model to immediately leverage robust texture detectors.
*   **Attention:** I will integrate **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules within the decoder. This mechanism is critical for recalibrating features to suppress background noise, addressing the "empty mask" failure mode (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Padding:** Input images (101x101) will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** to preserve texture statistics at the boundaries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Target Normalization (Critical Fix):** I will explicitly ensure ground truth masks are normalized to the **[0, 1]** range (float) before loss calculation. This prevents the "negative infinity loss" divergence observed when using raw [0, 255] values (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips** and **Random Brightness/Contrast**. I will explicitly **exclude** aggressive geometric distortions (e.g., GridDistortion), which degrade performance on this physics-constrained data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008). However, I will *not* remove augmentation entirely, as it is required for stability during the multi-stage training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

### Training
**Loss Curriculum & Scheduling:**
*   **Two-Stage Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up:** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** (applied to all Deep Supervision outputs) to establish stable convergence.
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (applied to the final output) to directly optimize the Jaccard index (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Synchronization:** The switch to Lovász-Hinge will be scheduled **early** (e.g., epoch 15 of 50) to ensure the fine-tuning phase begins well before early stopping can terminate the run (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Adaptive Scheduling:** I will use the **ReduceLROnPlateau** scheduler. This is preferred over fixed Cosine schedules for fine-tuning with surrogate losses, as it reacts to metric stagnation by forcing the model into sharper minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Metric-Driven Optimization:** The scheduler and model checkpointing will be driven by the **Optimized mAP** (calculated via a threshold sweep at the end of each epoch), not the mAP at a fixed 0.5 threshold. This ensures we select models with the best discriminative power (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** I will generate predictions for both the original image and a horizontally flipped version, averaging the outputs. This reduces variance and boosts the metric without retraining (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Threshold Optimization:** I will not use a default 0.5 probability threshold. Instead, I will perform a post-inference sweep on the validation set to identify the single optimal binarization threshold that maximizes the mAP. This is critical for filtering out low-confidence false positives in empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
