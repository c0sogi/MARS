**Strategy:**
1.  **Enable Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP):** I will integrate `torch.cuda.amp` into the training loop. This allows the model to use the A100's Tensor Cores, significantly reducing memory usage and speeding up computation. This enables larger batch sizes without exceeding memory limits.
2.  **Increase Batch Size:** With AMP enabled, I will double the `BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to 64. Larger batches provide more stable Batch Normalization statistics and smoother gradient estimates, which is beneficial for the optimization of the non-convex Lovasz-Hinge loss.
3.  **Extend Training Duration:** I will increase `EPOCHS` from 50 to 80 and `EARLY_STOPPING_PATIENCE` from 12 to 20. The previous solution stopped early; this change ensures the model has sufficient time to converge in the high-precision regime.
4.  **Adjust Loss Curriculum:** I will shift the `LOVASZ_SWITCH_EPOCH` from 15 to 20. This gives the model a slightly longer warm-up with BCE+Dice (beneficial with the larger batch size) while leaving a substantial 60 epochs for the critical fine-tuning phase (Cite **Lesson 00020**).
5.  **Refine Scheduler:** I will increase `SCHEDULER_PATIENCE` from 5 to 7 to prevent premature learning rate decay, ensuring the optimizer has enough time to navigate the loss landscape before reducing the step size.
