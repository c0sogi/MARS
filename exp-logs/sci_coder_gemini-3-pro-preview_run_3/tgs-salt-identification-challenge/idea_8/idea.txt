Based on the empirical insights from the provided lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **Stratified K-Fold Ensemble Pipeline** as the primary structural innovation. While previous iterations relied on a single train/validation split, this approach fundamentally changes the training paradigm to leverage the entire dataset for training while robustly estimating generalization error, directly addressing the high variance and data scarcity issues identified in the lessons.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will retain the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. It provides the necessary capacity for seismic textures without the redundancy of adding encoder-side attention, which was proven ineffective when the decoder already has attention (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Decoder:** I will use a **U-Net++** decoder with **Deep Supervision**. The nested skip pathways effectively bridge the semantic gap between the encoder and decoder, and deep supervision enforces feature consistency at multiple scales (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into the decoder nodes to explicitly suppress background noise in empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will strictly use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth. The input tensor will be constructed as `[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`. This preserves the pre-trained ImageNet weights of the 3-channel encoder, avoiding the performance degradation caused by modifying the first convolutional layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** to maintain texture continuity at the boundaries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This combination provides necessary regularization for the structured seismic data without introducing destructive interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Target Normalization:** Ground truth masks will be explicitly cast to **float [0, 1]** to prevent the "negative infinity loss" divergence observed with integer targets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
**Pipeline Structure (Structural Innovation):**
*   **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation:** Instead of a single hold-out set, I will partition the training data into 5 folds, stratified by salt coverage. I will train 5 separate models, each using 80% of the data for training and 20% for validation. This addresses the run-to-run variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003) and allows the final solution to utilize 100% of the available training data.

**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Mixed Precision & Scaled Batch Size:** I will use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to enable a batch size of **64**. This stabilizes Batch Normalization statistics and improves generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Two-Stage Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up:** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** (on all Deep Supervision outputs) to establish convergence.
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (on the final output) to optimize the IoU metric directly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Scope Correction:** I will calculate the Lovász-Hinge loss **per-image** and average the results, rather than flattening the batch. This aligns the optimization objective with the Mean Average Precision metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Scheduling:** I will use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP**. The loss switch will be scheduled early (e.g., epoch 15 of 80) to ensure the fine-tuning phase is fully executed before early stopping (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009, solution_lesson_node_00013).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Ensemble Prediction:** For the test set, I will generate predictions using all 5 trained models and average their probability maps. This effectively reduces the variance of the predictions and smooths decision boundaries.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Each of the 5 models will also use TTA (averaging original and horizontally flipped inputs) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Robust Threshold Optimization:** I will concatenate the **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from all 5 validation runs to form a complete dataset prediction. I will perform a threshold sweep on this aggregated OOF set to find the single global probability threshold that maximizes the mAP. This provides a much more statistically significant estimate of the optimal threshold than a single small validation set (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001, solution_lesson_node_00011).
