Based on the extensive failure analysis and empirical findings from the provided lessons, I propose the **Holistic High-Fidelity Stratified Ensemble** strategy.

This solution introduces a structural evolution from the previous single-model paradigms to a fully converged **Stratified K-Fold Ensemble Pipeline**. It specifically corrects the critical data normalization flaw that caused previous ensemble attempts to fail (Lesson 29) and rejects the "short-training" compromise that degraded performance in resource-constrained scenarios (Lesson 24). By leveraging the full 24-hour compute budget to train every fold to deep convergence, this strategy directly addresses the high variance (Lesson 3) and data scarcity (Lesson 25) inherent to the task.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. This architecture provides superior texture extraction capabilities compared to ResNet34 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) and avoids the redundancy of encoder-side attention (SE-ResNeXt) since the decoder handles feature recalibration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Decoder:** The decoder will use the **U-Net++** architecture with **Deep Supervision**. The nested dense skip pathways effectively bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder, while Deep Supervision (calculating loss at nodes L1, L2, L3, L4) enforces discriminative feature learning at multiple scales (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node. This is critical for suppressing background noise in "empty" masks, addressing the primary correlation between salt coverage and error (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth. The input tensor will be constructed as `[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`. This preserves the pre-trained weights of the 3-channel encoder, avoiding the performance degradation caused by modifying the first convolutional layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Dataset Consolidation:** I will merge the provided `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` into a single dataset and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Splitting** based on salt coverage. This ensures the model learns from 100% of the available data, correcting the "recursive splitting" error (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Uniform Input Normalization (Critical Correction):** Unlike previous attempts that applied ImageNet statistics to the multiplexed input (causing failure), I will apply **Uniform Scaling** (dividing by 255.0 to range `[0, 1]`) to all three channels (`Seismic`, `Seismic`, `Depth`). This maintains the signal consistency required for the pre-trained filters to operate correctly on the duplicated channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** to maintain texture statistics at boundaries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This provides necessary regularization without introducing destructive interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019, solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Target Normalization:** Ground truth masks will be explicitly cast to **float [0, 1]** to prevent negative infinity loss divergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP):** I will use AMP to enable a batch size of **64**. This stabilizes Batch Normalization statistics and improves generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Full Convergence Schedule:** Each of the 5 folds will be trained for **80 epochs**. I will explicitly prioritize the "depth" of convergence over speed, ensuring the fine-tuning phase is not truncated (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Two-Stage Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up (Epochs 1-15):** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** (on all Deep Supervision outputs) to establish stable convergence.
    2.  **Fine-tuning (Epochs 16-80):** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (on the final output) to optimize the IoU metric directly. The switch is scheduled early to provide a long runway for the surrogate loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Loss Scope:** The Lovász-Hinge loss will be calculated **per-image** and averaged, rather than flattened over the batch, to align with the Mean Average Precision metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Adaptive Scheduling:** I will use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP** (derived from a dynamic threshold sweep). This ensures learning rate decays are triggered by stagnation in discriminative power rather than calibration drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011, solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Synchronization:** The loss switch will be scheduled well before any early stopping trigger (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** During validation, predictions will be cropped back to the original **101x101** resolution *before* metric calculation to eliminate padding noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Threshold Optimization:** I will aggregate the **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from all 5 folds and perform a single threshold sweep to identify the global probability threshold that maximizes the mAP (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Ensemble Prediction:** For the test set, I will average the probability maps from all 5 trained models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Each model in the ensemble will also apply TTA (averaging original and horizontally flipped inputs) to further reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
