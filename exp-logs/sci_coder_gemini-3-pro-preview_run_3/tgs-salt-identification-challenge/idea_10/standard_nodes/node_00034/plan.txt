To optimize the solution, I will implement a "Depth Over Width" strategy by switching from a 5-fold cross-validation ensemble to a single model trained to full convergence on the fixed train/validation split. This approach leverages the lessons that extended training is critical for surrogate losses like Lovasz-Hinge (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}) and that recursive splitting of pre-partitioned data reduces effective training size (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00025}). Additionally, I will equalize the Deep Supervision weights to maximize regularization (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00033}).

Here are the specific changes:
1.  **`library/config.py`**: Add `VALIDATION_STRATEGY = "fixed"`, set `FOLDS = 1`, and increase `EPOCHS` to 100.
2.  **`library/dataset.py`**: Modify `SaltDataset` to implement the "fixed" strategy, selecting indices based on the IDs present in `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` instead of using K-Fold splitting.
3.  **`runfile.py`**:
    -   Update `DeepSupervisionLoss` weights to `[1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 1.0]`.
    -   Remove the fold loop to train a single model (Fold 0).
    -   Adjust configuration overrides to reflect the 100-epoch schedule.
