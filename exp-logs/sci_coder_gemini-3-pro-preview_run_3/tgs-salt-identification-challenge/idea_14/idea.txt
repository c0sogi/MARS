Based on the comprehensive analysis of the failure modes and empirical successes documented in the lessons, I propose the **Stabilized Semi-Supervised U-Net++ Ensemble** strategy.

This solution introduces a structural evolution from a purely supervised pipeline to a **Soft-Target Self-Training Regime**. It specifically addresses the "Data Scarcity" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025) and "Empty Mask" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) challenges by leveraging the unlabeled test data, while rigorously correcting the numerical instability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043) and optimization shocks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) that caused previous semi-supervised attempts to fail.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. This provides superior texture extraction compared to ResNet34 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) without the redundancy of encoder-side attention (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Decoder Structure:**
    *   **Nested Architecture:** I will use **U-Net++** to bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
    *   **Lightweight Capacity:** The decoder will be configured with **lightweight channel widths** (starting at 16 filters: `[16, 32, 64, 128, 256]`). This acts as a structural regularizer to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Stride 2 Output:** The decoder will terminate at **Stride 2** (1/2 resolution) and use bilinear upsampling for the final output. This avoids artifacts caused by learnable operations at the noisy full resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
    *   **Deep Supervision:** I will enable Deep Supervision on all nested nodes and explicitly assign **Equal Weights (1.0)** to all auxiliary heads to maximize regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node to suppress background noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth (`[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`), preserving the pre-trained ImageNet weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Consolidated Stratification:** I will merge the provided training and validation metadata and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Splitting** based on salt coverage. This ensures the model learns from 100% of the labeled data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Uniform Normalization:** Input channels will be scaled uniformly to `[0, 1]`. I will explicitly reject ImageNet normalization to preserve signal consistency across the multiplexed channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This provides regularization without destructive interpolation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Target Normalization:** Ground truth masks will be cast to **float [0, 1]** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
**Phase 1: Supervised High-Fidelity Training**
I will train a 5-Fold Ensemble on the labeled data with the following stabilized protocol:
*   **Convergence:** Train for **80 epochs** per fold to ensure deep convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Precision:** Use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** with Batch Size **64** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up:** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** (Epochs 1-15).
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (Epochs 16-80), calculated **per-image** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Stability Corrections:**
    *   **Float32 Casting:** I will explicitly cast logits and targets to **Float32** before the Lovász loss calculation to prevent the `NaN` divergence observed in FP16 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
    *   **Conservative LR:** I will enforce a learning rate $\le 1e-4$ during the loss switch to prevent gradient shock (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
*   **Scheduling:** Use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

**Phase 2: Soft-Target Self-Training (Fine-Tuning)**
Instead of retraining from scratch (which causes divergence, Cite solution_lesson_node_00042), I will **fine-tune** the converged Phase 1 models:
1.  **Prediction:** Generate predictions on the test set using the Phase 1 Ensemble with TTA.
2.  **Soft Targets:** Do not binarize. Retain the **soft probability maps** to preserve uncertainty information (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
3.  **Refinement:** Fine-tune the ensemble for an additional 20 epochs on a combined dataset (Labeled + Soft-Pseudo-Labeled).
    *   **Loss:** Use **Soft-BCE** for the pseudo-labeled data (to respect uncertainty) and Lovász-Hinge for the labeled data.

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** Validation predictions will be cropped to **101x101** *before* metric calculation to eliminate padding noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Thresholding:** I will aggregate OOF predictions from the Phase 2 ensemble to find the single global binarization threshold that maximizes mAP (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Final Prediction:** The submission will be generated by the Phase 2 Ensemble (5 models), each using **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002). The averaged probability maps will be binarized using the global threshold.
