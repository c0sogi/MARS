The `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler in newer versions of PyTorch (specifically 2.8.0+ as installed) no longer accepts the `verbose` argument, causing a `TypeError`. To fix this, I will modify `library/train.py` to remove the `verbose=False` parameter from the scheduler initialization in both Stage 1 and Stage 2 of the training loop.
