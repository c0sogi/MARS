Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the available computational budget, I propose the **Semi-Supervised High-Fidelity Stratified Ensemble** strategy.

This solution introduces a structural evolution from a purely supervised learning paradigm to a **Semi-Supervised Self-Training Pipeline**. By leveraging the 24-hour compute budget (which vastly exceeds the ~2 hours required for the previous best solution), this strategy directly addresses the "Data Scarcity" (Lesson 25) and "Empty Mask" (Lesson 1) challenges by incorporating high-confidence pseudo-labels from the test set. It rigorously retains the architectural and optimization refinements proven in previous iterations.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will retain the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. This backbone provides the optimal balance of capacity and stability, avoiding the redundancy of encoder-side attention found in SE-ResNeXt (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017) while outperforming standard ResNets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Decoder Structure:**
    *   **Nested Architecture:** I will use **U-Net++** with **Deep Supervision** to bridge the semantic gap and enforce multi-scale feature consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
    *   **Lightweight Configuration:** The decoder will use a **lightweight channel width** (starting at 16 filters) to act as a structural regularizer against overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Stride 2 Output:** The decoder will terminate at **Stride 2** (1/2 resolution) and use bilinear upsampling for the final output. This prevents artifacts caused by learnable operations at the noisy full resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
    *   **Weighting:** Deep Supervision auxiliary heads will be assigned **equal weights** to maximize regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node to suppress background noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth (`[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`), preserving the pre-trained ImageNet weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Consolidated Stratification:** I will merge `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Splitting** based on salt coverage to utilize 100% of the labeled data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Uniform Normalization:** Input channels will be scaled uniformly to `[0, 1]`. I will explicitly reject ImageNet normalization to preserve signal consistency across the multiplexed channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This provides regularization without destructive interpolation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Pseudo-Labeling (Structural Innovation):** I will generate a new training dataset for the second stage by predicting the unlabelled test set using the Stage 1 ensemble. High-confidence predictions will be added to the training pool, effectively increasing the dataset size by ~33%.

### Training
**Two-Stage Semi-Supervised Pipeline:**
1.  **Stage 1 (Supervised):** Train a 5-Fold Ensemble on the original labeled data.
    *   **Convergence:** Train for **80 epochs** per fold to ensure deep convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
    *   **Optimization:** Use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** with Batch Size **64** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Loss Curriculum:** Warm-up with **BCE + Dice** (Epochs 1-15), then fine-tune with **Lovász-Hinge** (Epochs 16-80) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007). The Lovász loss will be calculated **per-image** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Stability:** Enforce a **Conservative Learning Rate ($\le 1e-4$)** during the loss switch to prevent gradient shock (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Scheduling:** Use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

2.  **Pseudo-Label Generation:**
    *   Predict the test set using the Stage 1 Ensemble with **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   Binarize predictions using the **Global OOF Optimized Threshold** derived from Stage 1 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

3.  **Stage 2 (Semi-Supervised):** Retrain a fresh 5-Fold Ensemble on the combined **Labeled + Pseudo-Labeled** dataset.
    *   The training procedure (epochs, loss, optimizer) remains identical to Stage 1, but the model now benefits from the expanded data diversity.

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** Validation predictions will be cropped to **101x101** before metric calculation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Thresholding:** I will aggregate OOF predictions from the Stage 2 ensemble to find the final global binarization threshold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Final Prediction:** The submission will be generated by the Stage 2 Ensemble (5 models), each using TTA. The averaged probability maps will be binarized using the Stage 2 global threshold.
