Based on the detailed analysis of previous iterations and the specific lessons learned, I propose the following **Holistic Stratified K-Fold Ensemble Strategy**.

This solution introduces a structural evolution in the **training pipeline paradigm**. Instead of the previous single train/validation split (which wastes 20% of the data) or the flawed "recursive" cross-validation (which reduced training data size), this approach consolidates all available labeled data (Training + Validation sets) and trains a fully converged ensemble. This directly addresses the data scarcity and variance issues identified in the lessons while strictly adhering to the optimization protocols that maximize the IoU metric.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. This architecture provides the necessary capacity for seismic textures without the redundancy of encoder-side attention, as the decoder handles feature recalibration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Decoder:** The decoder will be a **U-Net++** structure with **Deep Supervision**. The nested skip pathways bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder more effectively than standard U-Net, and Deep Supervision (calculating loss at nodes L1, L2, L3, L4) enforces feature consistency at multiple scales (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node. This is critical for suppressing background noise in "empty" masks, a primary failure mode (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will strictly use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth. The input tensor will be constructed as `[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`. This preserves the pre-trained ImageNet weights of the 3-channel encoder, avoiding the performance degradation caused by modifying the first convolutional layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Dataset Consolidation (Structural Innovation):** I will concatenate the provided `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` files into a single master dataset of 3000 images. This corrects the "recursive splitting" error of previous attempts and ensures the model learns from 100% of the available diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded from 101x101 to **128x128** using **reflection padding** to maintain texture continuity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This combination provides necessary regularization without introducing destructive interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019, solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Target Normalization:** Ground truth masks will be explicitly cast to **float [0, 1]** to prevent the "negative infinity loss" divergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
**Pipeline Structure:**
*   **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation:** The consolidated dataset (3000 images) will be split into 5 folds, stratified by salt coverage. I will train 5 separate models. This ensures every image is used for training in 4 out of 5 folds, maximizing data utilization.

**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Mixed Precision & Scaled Batch Size:** I will use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to enable a batch size of **64**. This stabilizes Batch Normalization statistics and improves generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Full Convergence Schedule:** Each fold will be trained for **80 epochs**. I will explicitly avoid truncating the training (as seen in previous ensemble attempts), prioritizing the "depth" of convergence for each ensemble member (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Two-Stage Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up (Epochs 1-15):** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** (on all Deep Supervision outputs) to establish convergence.
    2.  **Fine-tuning (Epochs 16-80):** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (on the final output) to optimize the IoU metric directly. The switch is scheduled early to provide a long "runway" for the surrogate loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Loss Scope:** The Lovász-Hinge loss will be calculated **per-image** and averaged, rather than flattened over the batch, to align with the Mean Average Precision metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Scheduling:** I will use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP**. This ensures learning rate decays are triggered by stagnation in discriminative power rather than calibration drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013, solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Safety:** The loss switch will be synchronized to occur well before any early stopping trigger (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

### Evaluation
**Validation & Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** During validation, predictions will be cropped back to the original **101x101** resolution *before* metric calculation. This eliminates noise from padding and aligns validation scores with the competition metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Threshold Optimization:** I will collect the **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from all 5 folds. I will perform a single threshold sweep on this aggregated OOF set to identify the global probability threshold that maximizes the mAP. This provides a statistically robust estimate for the binarization cutoff (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Ensemble Prediction:** For the test set, I will generate predictions using all 5 trained models. Each model will also apply **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** (averaging original and horizontally flipped inputs) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002). The final submission will be the average of these 10 probability maps (5 models × 2 TTA views), binarized using the globally optimized threshold.
