The strategy involves optimizing the training pipeline by shifting from a "width-first" approach (shallow ensemble) to a "depth-first" approach (converged single model), as indicated by the lessons on time-constrained optimization and surrogate loss convergence.

1.  **Single-Fold Deep Training**: Instead of training 5 folds for 12 epochs each (which leads to under-convergence of the Lovasz loss), I will train a single fold (Fold 0) for **80 epochs**. This provides the necessary runway for the Lovasz-Hinge loss to effectively optimize the IoU metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020, solution_lesson_node_00024).
2.  **Increased Batch Size**: I will increase the batch size from 32 to **64**. Utilizing Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) on the A100 GPU allows this scaling, which stabilizes optimization and improves generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
3.  **Configuration Updates**: I will modify `runfile.py` to implement these overrides and restrict the training and inference loops to a single fold. I will also update `library/config.py` to optimize worker count for the available vCPUs.
