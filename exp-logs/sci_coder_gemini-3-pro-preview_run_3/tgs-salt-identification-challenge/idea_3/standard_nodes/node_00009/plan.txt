This change aims to improve model convergence and generalization by following a smoother learning rate decay schedule, which is particularly effective when training for longer durations (Cite Lesson 3 regarding treating performance as a distribution and ensuring robust convergence). I will also adjust the start of the Lovasz loss fine-tuning to epoch 30 to ensure the initial BCE+Dice loss has sufficient time to stabilize the feature representations (Cite Lesson 7). Finally, I will update the submission threshold to 0.806 to ensure only a superior model generates a submission.
