2.  **Augmentation**: I will add `RandomRotate90` to the training transformations. Since histopathology patches are rotationally invariant, this provides a "free" increase in effective training data diversity.
3.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**: I will implement TTA in the validation and prediction loops. By averaging predictions from the original image, a horizontal flip, and a vertical flip, we can smooth out noise and improve the reliability of the probability scores.
4.  **Training Duration**: I will increase the training epochs to 10 to ensure the model has sufficient time to converge on the larger dataset, while relying on the existing early stopping mechanism to prevent overfitting.
5.  **Submission Logic**: I will enforce the requirement to only generate a submission file if the new validation AUC exceeds the previous best of ~0.9627.
