The strategy involves simplifying the pipeline to focus on a single, high-performance architecture (`convnext_tiny`) trained on the full dataset, rather than an under-trained heterogeneous ensemble. This decision is supported by **Lesson 00015**, which showed that mixing disparate performers can degrade accuracy, and **Lesson 00003**, which emphasized that scaling to the full dataset is the most reliable way to improve performance. We will extend training to 20 epochs to ensure full convergence as per **Lesson 00014**, while maintaining the effective 5-fold cross-validation (**Lesson 00013**) and Test Time Augmentation (**Lesson 00005**). The complex meta-learner stacking will be replaced by robust fold averaging.
