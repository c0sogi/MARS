The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` was caused by the combination of a high image resolution (640x640) and batch size (16) exceeding the 16GB VRAM capacity of the GPU. To resolve this, I will reduce the `IMG_SIZE` to 512 in `library/config.py`. This reduction in spatial dimensions significantly decreases the memory footprint of the activation maps required for the U-Net decoder and backpropagation, ensuring the training process fits within the available hardware constraints.
