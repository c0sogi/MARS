# Optimized Strategy: Multi-Task EfficientNetV2 with Auxiliary Segmentation

## Model
I propose a **Multi-Task Learning (MTL)** architecture to explicitly address the challenge of detecting thin, linear structures (catheters) against complex anatomical backgrounds.
*   **Backbone:** Replace the ResNet-34 backbone with **EfficientNetV2-S**. This architecture utilizes Fused-MBConv blocks, which offer a superior balance of parameter efficiency and feature extraction capability compared to standard residual blocks.
*   **Structural Innovation (Auxiliary Segmentation Head):** To force the model to learn the spatial localization of catheters (rather than just global image statistics), I will attach a lightweight **Segmentation Decoder** (e.g., a Feature Pyramid Network or U-Net style decoder) to the encoder's intermediate feature maps. This head will predict a binary mask indicating the pixel-wise presence of any catheter/line.
*   **Classification Head:** The primary output remains a multi-label classification head attached to the global average pooled output of the final encoder stage, producing the 11 target probabilities.

## Data
*   **Preprocessing (Geometry):** To prevent the geometric distortion of thin lines identified in previous attempts, I will employ **Aspect-Ratio Preserving Resizing (Letterboxing)**. Images will be scaled so their longest side matches the target dimension, with the remaining area padded with constant values, ensuring catheters retain their physical morphology **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Preprocessing (Signal):** I will increase the input resolution to **640x640** and apply **CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization)** during augmentation. This combination enhances the local contrast of subtle catheter tips and prevents them from being lost during downsampling **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
*   **Auxiliary Targets:** For the subset of training data with segmentation annotations (`train_annotations.csv`), I will generate binary ground-truth masks to supervise the auxiliary head.

## Training
*   **Loss Function:** The training objective will be a weighted sum of the **Classification Loss (BCEWithLogitsLoss)** and the **Auxiliary Segmentation Loss (Dice Loss)**. The segmentation loss will be masked (set to zero) for samples lacking annotations, allowing the model to leverage the extra supervision where available without discarding unannotated data.
*   **Optimization Dynamics:** I will maintain a **Batch Size of 32** (or equivalent effective batch size via gradient accumulation) to ensure the model receives a sufficient number of weight updates per epoch. This avoids the convergence issues observed when naively increasing batch size without scaling epochs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
*   **Strategy:** Training will proceed with the AdamW optimizer and a Cosine Annealing scheduler. The segmentation head serves as a regularizer during training and will be discarded during inference.

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** During the test phase, the auxiliary segmentation head will be detached. The input images will undergo the same 640x640 letterbox preprocessing and normalization. The model will output the 11 classification probabilities directly from the primary head for the submission file.
