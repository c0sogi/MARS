Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous attempts and the derived lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **Hybrid Pooling Architecture** and **Multi-Channel Feature Engineering** to address the specific challenges of sparse events and polyphony, while strictly adhering to the efficiency constraints identified in the lessons.

### Model
I propose a **Hybrid Attention-Max EfficientNet** architecture. This design structurally evolves the pooling mechanism to capture both transient (sparse) and continuous (polyphonic) sound events simultaneously.

*   **Backbone:** I will utilize **EfficientNet-B0** initialized with ImageNet weights. This backbone provides the optimal balance of feature extraction capability and training iteration velocity, avoiding the convergence issues seen with heavier backbones like EfficientNet-B2 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Input Adaptation (Structural Innovation):** Instead of replicating the single-channel spectrogram three times, I will construct a **3-Channel Input** consisting of the **Log-Mel Spectrogram**, **Delta features** (velocity), and **Delta-Delta features** (acceleration). This leverages the ImageNet-pretrained weights (which expect diverse information across RGB channels) more effectively by providing explicit dynamic spectral information.
*   **Hybrid Pooling Head (Structural Innovation):** I will replace the single pooling layer with a **Parallel Hybrid Pooling** block. The feature map from the backbone will be fed into two parallel branches:
    1.  **Non-Linear Attention Pooling:** A learnable attention mechanism using a `Linear -> Tanh -> Linear -> Softmax` scoring network. This restores the non-linearity crucial for distinguishing relevant signals, correcting the linear simplification failure of Idea 3 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014), and effectively handles polyphonic overlapping sounds (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    2.  **Global Max Pooling:** A parallel branch that computes the maximum activation across time. This ensures that extremely sparse, high-intensity events (like a gunshot) are not "diluted" by the weighted averaging of attention, preserving the robust detection of sparse events identified in Idea 1 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    The outputs of these two branches will be concatenated to form the final embedding vector for classification.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Audio will be processed into Log-Mel Spectrograms with 128 Mel bins.
*   **Sampling Rate:** I will strictly use a **32 kHz sampling rate**. This reduces the input dimensionality compared to 44.1 kHz, enabling faster training epochs and allowing the model to converge fully within the time limit (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Duration Strategy:** All inputs will be fixed to **30 seconds**. Shorter clips will be padded with silence, and longer clips will be truncated. This ensures the model views the full context, avoiding the label noise introduced by random cropping (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Augmentation:** I will apply **Mixup** and **SpecAugment** (Time and Frequency Masking) to improve generalization on the multi-label distribution.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** The model will be trained using **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss (BCEWithLogitsLoss)**.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** I will use the **AdamW** optimizer paired with a **OneCycleLR** scheduler to facilitate super-convergence.
*   **Metric-Driven Early Stopping:** The training loop will monitor **Validation LWLRAP** (Label-Weighted Label-Ranking Average Precision) to trigger early stopping. I will strictly decouple this from Validation Loss to prevent premature stopping when the loss rises due to calibration probability shifts while ranking performance is still improving (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **State Preservation:** When saving the best model checkpoint, I will explicitly use `copy.deepcopy()` to ensure the weights corresponding to the peak metric are preserved, preventing the degradation observed in previous runs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the full 30-second spectrograms (with Delta/Delta-Delta channels) of the test files. No test-time cropping or windowing will be applied, ensuring the inference pipeline matches the training signal integrity.
