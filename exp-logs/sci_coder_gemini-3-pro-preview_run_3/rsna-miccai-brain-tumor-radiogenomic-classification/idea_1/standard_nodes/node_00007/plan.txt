My strategy involves:
1.  **Data Augmentation**: Integrating `albumentations` into the training pipeline to apply spatial transformations (flips, rotations) and intensity perturbations. This forces the model to learn invariant features rather than memorizing specific slice artifacts.
2.  **Optimizer Regularization**: Switching from `Adam` to `AdamW` with a weight decay of `0.01` to penalize overly complex weights.
3.  **Extended Training**: Increasing the number of epochs from 15 to 20 to accommodate the slower convergence rate typically caused by heavy augmentation.
4.  **Conditional Submission**: Modifying the execution script to only generate a submission if the validation AUC exceeds the previous best of `0.6818`.
