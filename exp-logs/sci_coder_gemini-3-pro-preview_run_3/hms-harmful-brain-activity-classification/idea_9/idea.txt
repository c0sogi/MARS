Based on the accumulated lessons and the analysis of the "Current Best" (Idea 8), I propose the **Siamese Equivariant Dual-Stream Network**.

This solution introduces a **Structural Innovation** by replacing the monolithic processing of the 50-second EEG clip with a **Siamese (Weight-Shared) Architecture**. This explicitly enforces spatial equivariance—processing anatomical regions (e.g., Left vs. Right Hemisphere) through identical feature extractors—to optimally distinguish between Lateralized (LPD/LRDA) and Generalized (GPD/GRDA) patterns, addressing the spatial integrity risks identified in **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00006}**.

# Model

The architecture consists of two parallel streams (Detail and Context) that converge via a Gated Fusion mechanism.

1.  **Stream A: Siamese Anatomical Encoder (50s Window)**
    *   **Input:** The 19-channel EEG signal is split into **4 Anatomical Chains** (Left Lateral, Right Lateral, Left Parasagittal, Right Parasagittal) based on the standard 10-20 system. Each chain is converted into a Mel-Spectrogram.
    *   **Architecture:** Instead of stacking all 19 channels into a single tensor (which forces an early 19$\to$3 channel bottleneck), we treat the 4 chains as a batch of 4 distinct views.
    *   **Backbone:** A single **EfficientNet-B0** (Pretrained on ImageNet) with shared weights processes each of the 4 views independently. This ensures that the feature extraction for the left hemisphere is mathematically identical to the right hemisphere (equivariance).
    *   **Pooling:** We apply **Attention Pooling** (rather than Global Average Pooling) across the temporal dimension of the feature maps. This allows the model to focus on the specific time segments (e.g., the central 10 seconds) where the seizure occurs, addressing the label offset ambiguity.
    *   **Output:** The stream produces 4 distinct feature vectors representing the morphological state of the 4 brain regions. These are concatenated to form the `Detail_Feature`.

2.  **Stream B: Global Context Encoder (10m Window)**
    *   **Input:** The provided Kaggle Spectrograms covering the 10-minute window, stacked depth-wise (4 channels).
    *   **Architecture:** **EfficientNet-B0** (Pretrained on ImageNet).
    *   **Rationale:** This preserves the successful orthogonal context modeling from **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00017}** and **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}**.
    *   **Output:** A single `Context_Feature` vector.

3.  **Fusion Head: Gated Context Mechanism**
    *   We retain the Gated Fusion developed in **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}**. The `Context_Feature` is passed through a sigmoid gate to modulate the `Detail_Feature`:
        $$Feature_{final} = [Detail_{LL}, Detail_{RL}, Detail_{LP}, Detail_{RP}] \otimes \sigma(W(Context))$$
    *   This allows the long-term background rhythm to suppress or enhance specific regional findings before the final classification.

# Data

1.  **Stream A Preprocessing (Dynamic Siamese Generation):**
    *   Load the 50-second raw EEG from `train_eegs`.
    *   **Montage Splitting:** Map the 19 electrodes into 4 groups (chains).
    *   **Spectrograms:** Compute Log-Mel Spectrograms for each group.
    *   **Stacking:** For each group, stack its constituent electrodes along the depth axis.
        *   *Example:* The "Left Lateral" input will have shape `(Mel=128, Time=500, Depth=5)`.
    *   **Normalization:** Normalize to ImageNet statistics.

2.  **Stream B Preprocessing:**
    *   Load the 10-minute spectrograms, log-transform, and resize to `(256, 256)` with `Depth=4` (preserving the 4 regions as channels) **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00006}**.

3.  **Augmentation:**
    *   **MixUp:** Applied to the final batches.
    *   **SpecAugment:** Applied independently to the spectrograms of both streams to prevent noise memorization **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}**.

# Training

*   **Loss Function:** **Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Strategy:** Use a consistent learning rate (e.g., `3e-4`) for the entire network. Since both streams use **Pretrained Backbones** (EfficientNet-B0), we avoid the instability of mixing scratch/pretrained models **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}**.
*   **Batch Construction:** For Stream A, the 4 anatomical views are stacked into the batch dimension (effective batch size = $4 \times N$) for the backbone pass, then reshaped back to $(N, 4, Features)$ for fusion. This is computationally efficient and fits within the budget.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference Pipeline:** Iterate through `test.csv`.
2.  **Process:**
    *   Load 50s EEG $\to$ Generate 4 Siamese Spectrogram inputs.
    *   Load 10m Spectrogram $\to$ Generate 1 Context input.
    *   Pass through the Siamese-Equivariant Network.
3.  **Prediction:** The final Softmax output provides the probability distribution for the 6 classes.
