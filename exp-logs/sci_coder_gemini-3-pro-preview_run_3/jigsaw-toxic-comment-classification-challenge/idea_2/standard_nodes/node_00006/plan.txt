The issue is caused by data leakage in the feature engineering step within `library/data_processing.py`. The TF-IDF vectorizers are currently fitted on a concatenation of both the training and validation datasets (`all_text`), which allows information from the validation set (such as term frequencies) to leak into the model training process, resulting in an invalid validation metric. To fix this, the vectorizers must be fitted **only** on the training data (`train_text`), and then used to transform the validation and test sets.
