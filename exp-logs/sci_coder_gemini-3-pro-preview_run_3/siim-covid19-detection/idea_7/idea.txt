# Idea 7: Cascade R-CNN with ConvNeXt V2 and Attention-Gated Classification

## Model
I propose a **Multi-Task Cascade R-CNN** utilizing a **ConvNeXt V2-Base** backbone. This architecture represents a structural evolution from the Swin Transformer (Idea 6) and ResNeXt (Idea 5) approaches, leveraging a modern "ConvNet for the 2020s" design that combines the efficiency of standard convolutions with the hierarchical design principles of Transformers.

-   **Backbone (Structural Innovation)**: The **ConvNeXt V2-Base** backbone is selected for its ability to learn high-quality representations through Global Response Normalization (GRN) and large kernel sizes (7x7). This backbone offers a distinct advantage over the Swin Transformer (Idea 6) by avoiding the window-shifting complexity while maintaining a large global receptive field, which is crucial for capturing the diffuse patterns of "Typical" COVID-19 appearances.
-   **Detection Head**: I will retain the **Cascade R-CNN** architecture. Its multi-stage refinement strategy (training detectors at increasing IoU thresholds of 0.5, 0.6, 0.7) is proven to be superior for high-precision localization tasks compared to single-stage detectors (Idea 2/4).
-   **Study Classification Head (Structural Innovation)**: Instead of the simple Global Average Pooling (GAP) or Global Context blocks used in previous ideas, I will implement a **Multi-Head Self-Attention (MHSA) Pooling** mechanism attached to the deepest FPN layer. This head treats the spatial feature map as a sequence of tokens and learns to dynamically weigh informative regions (e.g., lung opacities) while suppressing irrelevant background (e.g., bones, diaphragm) before aggregating them into a study-level prediction vector. This explicitly models the relationship between local findings and the global diagnosis.

## Data
-   **Preprocessing (Strategic Retention)**: I will strictly implement **Letterbox Resizing** for all training and test images. By resizing the longest dimension to the target size (e.g., 800 pixels) and padding the shorter dimension with zeros, I ensure that the aspect ratio of the chest radiographs is preserved. This prevents the geometric distortion of lung opacities that was identified as a critical failure mode in direct resizing approaches **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**.
-   **Augmentation**: I will employ a robust augmentation pipeline including **CoarseDropout**, **OpticalDistortion**, **CLAHE**, and **ShiftScaleRotate**. I will explicitly exclude "Mosaic" augmentation to preserve the global anatomical structure required for the attention-based study classification.

## Training
-   **Loss Function**: The model will be trained end-to-end using a multi-task objective:
    -   **Cascade Detection Loss**: Sum of Cross-Entropy and Smooth L1 losses for the three detection stages.
    -   **Study Classification Loss**: **Focal Loss** will be applied to the attention-gated classification head to address the class imbalance between "Typical" and "Atypical" cases.
-   **Optimizer**: I will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a cosine annealing learning rate schedule, as ConvNeXt architectures require weight decay handling similar to Transformers for optimal convergence.

## Evaluation
-   **Inference**: For each test image, the model will generate bounding boxes and study-level probabilities.
-   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**: Inference will be performed on both the original and horizontally flipped images.
-   **Fusion Strategy**: The resulting bounding boxes from TTA will be merged using **Weighted Boxes Fusion (WBF)** rather than NMS. WBF averages the coordinates of clustered boxes based on confidence, providing a more accurate localization than simply selecting the best box.
-   **Prediction Logic**:
    -   The study label is determined by the class with the highest probability from the Attention-Gated Head.
    -   If the study label is "Negative for Pneumonia", the image prediction is forced to `none 1 0 0 1 1`.
    -   Otherwise, the WBF-fused bounding boxes are formatted as the "opacity" prediction string.
