# Proposed Solution: Stratified Ensemble with ImageNet-21k Priors and Greedy Model Soups

## Model
We will utilize the **ConvNeXt-Small** architecture with a fundamental change in initialization.
*   **Architecture Choice:** We strictly retain **ConvNeXt-Small** as the backbone. Empirical evidence confirms this is the capacity "sweet spot" for this dataset, significantly outperforming "Tiny" variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) while avoiding the overfitting and optimization instability observed with "Base" and "Large" variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013, Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Initialization (Structural Innovation):** We will initialize the model with weights pre-trained on **ImageNet-21k** (and fine-tuned on 1k), rather than the standard ImageNet-1k weights used in previous baselines. This leverages a prior learned from ~14 million images, providing far richer feature separation for the 120 dog breeds than the standard 1 million image subset.
*   **Head:** A simple linear layer mapping to 120 classes, initialized randomly.

## Data
We will enforce the rigid preprocessing pipeline identified as optimal, strictly avoiding the modifications that caused regressions in previous ideas.
*   **Preprocessing Pipeline:** The training data will undergo **`RandomResizedCrop`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandomHorizontalFlip`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandAugment`**.
    *   We explicitly retain `RandomResizedCrop` to prevent the overfitting caused by static resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   We ensure `RandomHorizontalFlip` is applied *before* `RandAugment` to guarantee the model learns essential spatial symmetries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Resolution:** Inputs will be fixed at **224x224**. We explicitly reject scaling to higher resolutions (e.g., 384x384) as previous experiments proved this necessitates reducing batch size, which destabilizes training and degrades performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Targets:** We will use **Hard Labels** (0 or 1) and strictly avoid Label Smoothing. This ensures the training objective maximizes the Log Loss metric without artificial penalties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Training
We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** loop enhanced by **Greedy Model Soups**.
*   **Convergence Priority:** We will run the full **30-epoch** schedule for *each* of the 5 folds. We explicitly reject shortening the training duration, as previous attempts showed that ensembling cannot compensate for underfitted models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Phases:** We will apply the **Two-Phase Transfer Learning** strategy:
    1.  **Warm-up:** Freeze the backbone and train the head for 1 epoch to align weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Unfreeze the entire network and train for 30 epochs.
*   **Optimization:** We will use a **Conservative Learning Rate** of `1e-5` for fine-tuning. We reject aggressive rates or complex Layer-Wise Decay, which disrupt pre-trained features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022). The scheduler will be **Cosine Annealing** with `T_max=30` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Structural Innovation (Greedy Model Soup):** To achieve the generalization benefits of weight averaging without the implementation risks of SWA wrappers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021), we will implement **Greedy Weight Averaging** per fold.
    1.  Save checkpoints for the final 10 epochs.
    2.  Sort checkpoints by validation Log Loss.
    3.  Iteratively average the weights of the sorted checkpoints, keeping the new averaged model *only if* it improves the validation Log Loss.
    This constructs a single, optimally calibrated model per fold.

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will generate predictions using the **Greedy Soup Models** from all 5 folds.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each model, we will predict probabilities for the original image and a **horizontally flipped** version. These two views will be averaged.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probabilities from all 5 folds. This hierarchical strategy combines **Weight-Space Ensembling** (Intra-fold Soup) and **Prediction-Space Ensembling** (Inter-fold Bagging) to minimize variance and Log Loss.
