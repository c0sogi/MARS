# Proposed Solution: Stratified Hierarchical Ensemble with ImageNet-21k Priors and Greedy Model Soups

## Model
We will utilize the **ConvNeXt-Small** architecture, incorporating a critical initialization upgrade to leverage superior priors.
*   **Architecture Choice:** We strictly retain **ConvNeXt-Small** as the backbone. It has been empirically identified as the capacity "sweet spot" for this dataset, significantly outperforming "Tiny" variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) while avoiding the optimization instability and overfitting observed with "Base" and "Large" variants on this limited data regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013, Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Initialization (Structural Innovation):** We will initialize the backbone with weights pre-trained on **ImageNet-21k** (fine-tuned on 1k), rather than the standard ImageNet-1k weights. This massive increase in pre-training data (14M vs 1M images) provides a far richer feature space for fine-grained discrimination, addressing the data scarcity issue structurally.
*   **Head:** A simple linear layer mapping to the 120 breed classes, initialized randomly.

## Data
We will enforce the rigid preprocessing pipeline identified as optimal, strictly avoiding the modifications that caused regressions in previous experiments.
*   **Preprocessing Pipeline:** The training data will undergo **`RandomResizedCrop`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandomHorizontalFlip`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandAugment`**.
    *   We explicitly retain `RandomResizedCrop` to prevent the overfitting caused by static resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   We ensure `RandomHorizontalFlip` is applied *before* `RandAugment` to guarantee the model learns essential spatial symmetries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Resolution:** Inputs will be fixed at **224x224**. We explicitly reject scaling to higher resolutions (e.g., 384x384) as previous experiments proved this necessitates reducing batch size, which destabilizes training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Targets:** We will use **Hard Labels** (0 or 1) and strictly avoid Label Smoothing. This ensures the training objective maximizes the Log Loss metric without artificial penalties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Training
We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** loop enhanced by a **Greedy Model Soup** construction phase.
*   **Convergence Priority:** We will run the full **30-epoch** schedule for *each* of the 5 folds. We explicitly reject shortening the training duration to fit a time budget, as previous attempts showed that ensembling cannot compensate for underfitted models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024). Given the efficiency of ConvNeXt-Small, this fits comfortably within the 24-hour limit.
*   **Phases:** We will apply the successful **Two-Phase Transfer Learning** strategy:
    1.  **Warm-up:** Freeze the backbone and train the head for 1 epoch to align weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Unfreeze the entire network and train for 30 epochs.
*   **Optimization:** We will use a **Conservative Learning Rate** of `1e-5` for the fine-tuning phase. We explicitly reject aggressive learning rates (e.g., `1e-4`) or complex Layer-Wise Decay schemes, as these have been shown to disrupt the pre-trained weights and degrade performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Scheduler:** We will use a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler with `T_max=30` epochs to allow for deep convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Greedy Model Soup (Structural Innovation):** To achieve the generalization benefits of weight averaging without the implementation risks of SWA wrappers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021), we will implement **Greedy Weight Averaging** manually at the end of each fold.
    1.  Save checkpoints for the final 5-10 epochs.
    2.  Sort checkpoints by validation Log Loss.
    3.  Iteratively average the weights of the sorted checkpoints, keeping the new averaged model *only if* it improves the validation Log Loss.
    This constructs a single, optimally calibrated model per fold (Intra-Fold Ensemble).

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will generate predictions using the **Greedy Soup Models** from all 5 folds.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each of the 5 models, we will predict probabilities for the original image and a **horizontally flipped** version. These two views will be averaged to produce the model's prediction.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probabilities from all 5 models (Inter-Fold Bagging). This hierarchical strategy minimizes variance at both the weight level and the prediction level.
