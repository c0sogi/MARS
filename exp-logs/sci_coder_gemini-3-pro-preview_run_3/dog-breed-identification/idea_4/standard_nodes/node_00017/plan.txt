Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, I will optimize the solution by reverting the model architecture to **ConvNeXt Small** and removing the progressive resizing strategy. The previous attempt with `convnext_base` and high-resolution fine-tuning (384x384) underperformed (`0.1744` vs `0.1400`) due to the limited dataset size and reduced batch sizes required for larger models/resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016). Smaller models like `convnext_small` have proven to generalize better on this task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

I will also extend the fine-tuning phase to **30 epochs** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler. This extended duration allows the learning rate to decay more gradually, facilitating better convergence into a robust minimum (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007). The batch size will be increased to **64** to stabilize gradients, and the input resolution will be fixed at **224x224** throughout training to avoid the instability associated with resolution transitions.
