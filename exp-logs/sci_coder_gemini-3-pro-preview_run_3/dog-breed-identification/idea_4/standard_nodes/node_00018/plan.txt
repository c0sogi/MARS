Based on the lessons learned, specifically `solution_lesson_node_00016` and `solution_lesson_node_00009`, I will revert the model architecture from `convnext_base` to `convnext_small`. The previous experiments demonstrated that the larger Base model and high-resolution fine-tuning strategy led to overfitting and degraded performance compared to the Small variant on this specific dataset.

I will also optimize the training schedule by removing the ineffective high-resolution Phase 3 and extending the standard resolution Phase 2 to 30 epochs. This applies `solution_lesson_node_00005` and `solution_lesson_node_00007`, allowing the model more time to converge with a proper cosine decay schedule without the instability of resolution changes. The batch size will be increased to 64 to improve throughput and gradient estimation stability.
