Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and the specific failure modes identified, I propose the following optimized strategy. The core structural innovation is the implementation of a **Hierarchical Ensemble Strategy** that combines **Weight-Space Ensembling (Model Soup)** within each fold and **Prediction-Space Ensembling (Bagging)** across folds. This approach maximizes generalization and stability while strictly adhering to the constraints identified in the lessons.

# Proposed Solution: Hierarchical Stratified Ensemble (Model Soup + Bagging)

## Model
We will utilize the **ConvNeXt-Small** architecture as the sole backbone.
*   **Architecture Choice:** We explicitly retain `ConvNeXt-Small` because it has been empirically proven to be the capacity "sweet spot" for this dataset. It significantly outperforms smaller variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) but avoids the overfitting and optimization instability observed with "Base" and "Large" variants on this limited data regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013, Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Hierarchical Structure:**
    1.  **Level 1 (Intra-Fold): Manual Weight Averaging.** For each fold, instead of using a single final checkpoint, we will average the weights of the checkpoints from the final few epochs (e.g., epochs 26-30). This creates a "Model Soup" that flattens the loss landscape and improves calibration without the implementation risks of SWA wrappers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    2.  **Level 2 (Inter-Fold): Homogeneous Bagging.** We will train 5 independent instances of this averaged model on stratified folds. We reject heterogeneous ensembling (mixing architectures) as previous attempts showed that including weaker models dilutes the performance of the strong ConvNeXt-Small backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

## Data
We will enforce the rigid preprocessing pipeline identified as optimal, strictly avoiding the modifications that caused regression in previous ideas.
*   **Preprocessing Pipeline:** The training data will undergo **`RandomResizedCrop`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandomHorizontalFlip`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandAugment`**.
    *   We explicitly retain `RandomResizedCrop` to prevent the overfitting caused by static resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   We ensure `RandomHorizontalFlip` is applied *before* `RandAugment` to guarantee the model learns essential spatial symmetries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Resolution:** Inputs will be fixed at **224x224**. We explicitly reject scaling to higher resolutions (e.g., 384x384) as previous experiments proved this necessitates reducing batch size, which destabilizes training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Targets:** We will use **Hard Labels** (0 or 1) and strictly avoid Label Smoothing. This ensures the training objective maximizes the Log Loss metric without artificial penalties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Training
We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** training loop.
*   **Convergence Priority:** We will run the full **30-epoch** schedule for *each* of the 5 folds. We explicitly reject shortening the training duration to fit a time budget, as this previously resulted in underfitted models that degraded ensemble performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024). Given the efficiency of ConvNeXt-Small, this fits comfortably within the compute budget.
*   **Phases:** We will apply the successful **Two-Phase Transfer Learning** strategy for each fold:
    1.  **Warm-up:** Freeze the backbone and train the head for 1 epoch to align weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Unfreeze the entire network and train for 30 epochs.
*   **Optimization:** We will use a **Conservative Learning Rate** of `1e-5` for the fine-tuning phase. We explicitly reject aggressive learning rates (e.g., `1e-4`) or complex Layer-Wise Decay schemes, as these have been shown to disrupt the pre-trained weights and degrade performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Scheduler:** We will use a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler with `T_max=30` epochs to allow for deep convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will generate predictions for the test set using the **Weight-Averaged Models** from all 5 folds.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each of the 5 models, we will predict probabilities for the original image and a **horizontally flipped** version. These two views will be averaged to produce the model's prediction.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probabilities from all 5 models. This combination of Weight Averaging (across epochs), Bagging (across folds), and TTA (across views) minimizes variance at every level of the pipeline.
