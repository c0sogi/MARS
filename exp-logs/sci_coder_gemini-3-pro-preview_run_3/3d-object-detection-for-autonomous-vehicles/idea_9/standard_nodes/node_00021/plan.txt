The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` is caused by the high memory footprint of the 800x800 feature maps generated from the `[0.25, 0.25, 8.0]` voxel size. I will increase the `VOXEL_SIZE` to `[0.5, 0.5, 8.0]` in `library/config.py`, which reduces the grid resolution to 400x400 and fits within the GPU memory. Additionally, I will modify `library/dataset.py` to explicitly clamp the calculated voxel indices to the grid boundaries to prevent potential out-of-bounds errors arising from floating-point discretization at the edges (Cite `debug_lesson_9`).
