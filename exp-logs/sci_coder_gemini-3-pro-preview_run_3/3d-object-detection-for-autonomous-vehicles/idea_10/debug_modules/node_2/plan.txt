The `RuntimeError` during concatenation in the `ResNetFPN` module indicates a spatial dimension mismatch between the upsampled feature maps. Specifically, the third feature level currently has a cumulative downsampling factor of 8 (strides $1 \times 2 \times 4$), resulting in a $240 \times 240$ grid. When upsampled by the corresponding factor of 4, this results in a $960 \times 960$ map, which mismatches the $1920 \times 1920$ resolution of the other levels. To resolve this, I will change the `LAYER_STRIDES` in `library/config.py` from `[1, 2, 4]` to `[1, 2, 2]`. This ensures the third level has a cumulative stride of 4 ($1 \times 2 \times 2$), producing a $480 \times 480$ map that upsamples correctly to $1920 \times 1920$ (Cite debug_lesson_3).
