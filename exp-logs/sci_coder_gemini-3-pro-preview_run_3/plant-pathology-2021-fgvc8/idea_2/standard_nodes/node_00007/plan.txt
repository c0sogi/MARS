Based on the provided lessons, specifically **Lesson 00001** (ResNet18 baseline has high capacity and works well with ColorJitter), **Lesson 00004** (`RandomResizedCrop` is superior to affine transforms for generalization), and **Lesson 00006** (MixUp/CutMix require extended training horizons which we do not have), I will optimize the solution by reverting to the robust ResNet18 baseline configuration while ensuring optimal hyperparameters.

The previous solution (Idea 2) underperformed (0.8644 vs 0.8855 baseline) likely due to the combination of `EfficientNet-B0` and heavy regularization (`MixUp`/`CutMix`) not converging within the short epoch limit.

**Optimization Strategy:**
1.  **Architecture**: Switch from `efficientnet_b0` back to `resnet18`. ResNet18 has proven sufficient capacity (Train F1 ~0.97) and faster convergence (Cite Lesson 00001).
2.  **Loss Function**: Revert from `AsymmetricLoss` to `BCEWithLogitsLoss`. While ASL handles imbalance, the standard BCE loss provided the best baseline results (0.8855).
3.  **Regularization**: Disable `MixUp` and `CutMix`. These techniques require significantly more epochs to pay off (Cite Lesson 00006). We will rely on `RandomResizedCrop` and `ColorJitter` (already in `dataset.py`) which are proven effective for this dataset (Cite Lesson 00001, 00004).
4.  **Hyperparameters**:
    *   **Batch Size**: Increase to 64 (from 32) to stabilize gradients and improve throughput (Cite Lesson 00005).
    *   **Learning Rate**: Set to `1e-4` (standard for ResNet finetuning).
    *   **Epochs**: Keep at 15 to ensure full convergence with Early Stopping.
