The issue is **Data Leakage** caused by mixing the designated hold-out validation set with the training data during Cross-Validation. In `library/data_loader.py`, the `get_loaders` function explicitly concatenates the training subset (`X_train_part`) and the validation subset (`X_val_part`) into `X_full` before generating the K-Fold splits. This causes the models to be trained on samples that are later used in `runfile.py` for the final "unseen" evaluation, invalidating the metric. To fix this, I will modify `get_loaders` to use only the training subset (`X_train_part`, `y_train_part`, `angles_train_part`) for the cross-validation process, ensuring the hold-out set remains strictly excluded. This solution applies the principle from **Cite debug_lesson_7** regarding the prevention of leakage by excluding holdout sets from CV splits.
