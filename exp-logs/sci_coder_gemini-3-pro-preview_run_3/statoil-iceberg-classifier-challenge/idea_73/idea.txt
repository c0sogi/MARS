Based on the comprehensive analysis of the experiment history and the derived lessons, I propose the **Asymmetric Dual-Statistic Isomorphic CNN (ADSI-CNN)**.

This solution represents a structural synthesis designed to resolve the "Feature Richness vs. Capacity" dilemma identified in **Lesson 163** and **Lesson 124**. We need to capture **Texture** (to distinguish ice from water/clutter) and **Shadows** (to distinguish objects from noise), but we cannot simply add more pooling statistics without either exploding the classifier's parameter count (overfitting) or aggressively compressing channels (destroying features).

This solution introduces an **Asymmetric Readout Strategy**. Instead of applying the same pooling operations at every stage, we allocate specific statistics to the network depth where they are most physically relevant: **Shadows (Min-Pooling)** are extracted from **Stage 3** (where spatial resolution is higher, preserving shadow edges), while **Texture (MAD-Pooling)** is extracted from **Stage 4** (where the receptive field is global, capturing material properties). This allows us to capture Peak, Shadow, and Texture information while maintaining the exact **256-dimensional** feature footprint of the robust baseline.

# Model
The architecture is a **Custom 4-Stage Attentive Convolutional Network** optimized for physics-informed, parameter-efficient feature extraction.

*   **Backbone:** The network employs a **Plain CNN** structure (no residual connections) with 4 sequential blocks. We explicitly retain this over ResNets to enforce aggressive sequential downsampling (75x75 $\to$ ~4x4), which filters high-frequency speckle noise via convolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046).
    *   **Block Structure:** `Conv2d` (bias=True) $\to$ `BatchNorm2d` $\to$ `LeakyReLU` $\to$ **Hybrid SE Module** $\to$ `MaxPool2d`.
    *   **Bias Retention:** We explicitly retain bias terms to preserve initialization dynamics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00076).
    *   **Activation:** **`LeakyReLU`** (negative slope 0.1) preserves negative values (shadows) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091).
    *   **Width Strategy:** **64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 128** (Early Expansion) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).
    *   **Attention:** **Hybrid SE** uses Global Average Pooling for the squeeze operation (low-pass filter) to be robust to noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00104), with reduction ratio $r=16$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00146).

*   **Structural Innovation (Asymmetric Dual-Statistic Readout):**
    *   We extract features from **Stage 3** and **Stage 4** using **Decoupled Projections** (separate 1x1 convolutions mapping 128 $\to$ 64 channels) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00144).
    *   **Stage 3 (Shape & Shadow Focus):** On the Stage 3 projection, we apply **Global Max Pooling** (Peaks) and **Global Min Pooling** (Shadows).
        *   *Rationale:* Stage 3 has higher spatial resolution ($9 \times 9$). It is best suited for capturing the sharp contrast boundary between a bright target and its cast shadow (Cite solution_lesson_node_00102).
    *   **Stage 4 (Existence & Texture Focus):** On the Stage 4 projection, we apply **Global Max Pooling** (Peaks) and **Global MAD Pooling** (Texture).
        *   *Rationale:* Stage 4 has global receptive fields ($4 \times 4$). It is best suited for characterizing the overall material property (roughness) of the object using **Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00163), which is robust to outliers unlike Standard Deviation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00145).
    *   **Result:** The final vector is $64_{S3\_Max} + 64_{S3\_Min} + 64_{S4\_Max} + 64_{S4\_MAD}$. Total size is **256**. This captures the full physical signature (Intensity, Shadow, Texture) without exceeding the capacity limit of the baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00124).

*   **Feature Fusion (Raw Scale):** The feature vector is concatenated with the scalar **incidence angle**. We explicitly **do not normalize** the angle (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).

*   **Classification Head:** `Linear(257 -> 256)` $\to$ `LeakyReLU` $\to$ `Dropout(0.5)` $\to$ `Output`.
    *   Includes non-linear interaction layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00155).
    *   Dropout applied after activation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

*   **Initialization:** **PyTorch Default Initialization** (Kaiming Uniform/Fan-In) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078).

# Data
*   **Input Composition:** 3-channel tensor (HH, HV, Synthetic Avg) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Resolution:** Native **75x75** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Augmentation:** `torchvision` RandomHorizontalFlip and RandomVerticalFlip (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Leak-Free Preprocessing:** Imputation of missing incidence angles is performed using the median calculated **only on the training fold indices** and applied to the validation fold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00147).

# Training
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensembling** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a **constant learning rate** (e.g., 1e-3). This decouples weight decay from adaptive updates, stabilizing fusion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Loss Function:** **`BCEWithLogitsLoss`** combined with **L2 Regularization (Weight Decay)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
*   **Duration:** Generous budget (e.g., 75 epochs) with moderate patience (e.g., 12 epochs) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00094).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Explicitly **disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
*   **Ensembling:** Simple average of predictions from the 5 CV models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
