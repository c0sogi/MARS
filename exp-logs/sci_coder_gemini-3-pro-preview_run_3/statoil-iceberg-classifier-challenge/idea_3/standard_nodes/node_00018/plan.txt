To optimize the performance of the iceberg classification model, I will implement a strategy focused on improving generalization and convergence through better optimization techniques and regularization.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Optimizer & Scheduler**: I will switch from `Adam` to `AdamW` (Adam with Weight Decay), which is known to generalize better by properly decoupling weight decay from the gradient update. I will also introduce a `ReduceLROnPlateau` learning rate scheduler. This will allow the model to dynamically lower the learning rate when the validation loss plateaus, enabling it to settle into better local minima.
2.  **Regularization**: The previous logs showed significant overfitting (Train Loss ~0.15 vs Val Loss ~0.25). I will increase the Dropout rate in the fully connected layer from `0.2` to `0.5` to enforce stronger regularization.
3.  **Training Duration**: To accommodate the learning rate scheduler and ensure the model has enough time to converge after LR reductions, I will increase the maximum number of epochs from 40 to 50 and increase the early stopping patience from 8 to 12.
4.  **Configuration**: I will update the working directory to `idea_5` to ensure a clean run with new cache files.

These changes are targeted at the training dynamics without altering the core architecture (SimpleCNN) or data processing, which were identified as effective in the Lessons.
