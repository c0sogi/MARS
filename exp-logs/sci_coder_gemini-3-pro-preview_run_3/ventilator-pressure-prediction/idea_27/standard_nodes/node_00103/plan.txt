To optimize the LGRH-Net solution, I will focus on correcting the training dynamics and ensuring sufficient model capacity and training duration, based on the provided lessons.

1.  **Switch to Monotonic Learning Rate Decay**: The previous solution used `CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts`, which Lesson 102 identifies as detrimental for high-precision regression tasks because aggressive restarts prevent the optimizer from settling into deep, narrow minima. I will replace this with `ReduceLROnPlateau` to ensure monotonic decay and stable fine-tuning.
2.  **Increase Update Budget (Epochs)**: The previous run was limited to 5 epochs, which is insufficient for convergence. Lesson 71 highlights the critical importance of the "Update Budget" for hybrid RNNs. I will increase the epoch count to 50, which should fit within the time limit while allowing convergence.
3.  **Balance Model Capacity**: Lesson 57 emphasizes that the CNN/TCN branch must have capacity commensurate with the LSTM branch. I will increase the `tcn_filters` from 64 to 128 to better balance the 512-unit LSTM branch, preventing underfitting of high-frequency resistive dynamics.
