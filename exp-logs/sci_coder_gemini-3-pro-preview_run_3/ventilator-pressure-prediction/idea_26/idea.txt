Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, specifically addressing the "Pointwise Stem" failure of Idea 25 (Lesson 96), the "Regularization Sensitivity" of Idea 24 (Lesson 93), and the "Kinematic Directionality" requirements of Idea 23 (Lesson 90), I propose the **Direct-Kinematic Residual-Hybrid Network (DKRH-Net)**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation by strictly enforcing a **Direct-Temporal Interface**, removing the latent projection stems that obscured physical signal dynamics in previous iterations. It combines this with the fully corrected kinematic feature set and the specific regularization regime required for unscaled regression.

### 1. Model: Direct-Kinematic Residual-Hybrid (DKRH-Net)

The architecture retains the **Parallel Hybrid** design to structurally decouple resistive and elastic dynamics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**, but fundamentally alters the input stage to maximize physical signal fidelity.

*   **Input Layer: Direct-Temporal Interface**
    *   **Structural Innovation:** We explicitly **reject the 1x1 Pointwise Stem** (projection layer) used in previous iterations. Instead, the raw input features are fed **directly** into the first Temporal Convolutional layer of the resistive branch.
    *   **Rationale:** Pointwise stems force the network to process features in isolation before mixing them over time. By applying a large-kernel temporal convolution immediately, the model's first layer acts as a learnable finite-difference operator, capturing essential rates of change (velocity/acceleration) from the raw physical data instantly. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00096**.

*   **Branch 1: Deep Residual Dense TCN (The Resistive Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models high-frequency, derivative-dependent dynamics ($P \propto R \cdot \dot{V}$).
    *   **Structure:** A deep stack of **Residual Dense Blocks** (Input $\to$ Conv $\to$ BN $\to$ GELU $\to$ Dropout $\to$ Conv $\to$ BN $\to$ GELU $\to$ Dropout $\to$ Add). This enables significant depth without optimization degradation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00075**.
    *   **Geometry:**
        *   **Dilation = 1 (Dense):** Strictly use dense convolutions to preserve high-fidelity local information for derivative modeling. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**.
        *   **Kernel Size = 9:** Use large kernels to smooth physical signals and approximate derivatives better than small kernels. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026, solution_lesson_node_00062**.
        *   **Centered Padding:** Use non-causal padding to exploit the offline nature of the task. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.
    *   **Regularization:** Apply **Dropout** explicitly within the blocks to prevent co-adaptation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00066**.

*   **Branch 2: High-Capacity Bidirectional LSTM (The Elastic Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models low-frequency, integral-dependent dynamics ($P \propto V/C$).
    *   **Capacity:** A **3-layer Bidirectional LSTM** with **512 hidden units**. We prioritize capacity here to serve as the system's numerical integrator. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**.

*   **Fusion Head: Wide-Latent Integration**
    *   **Structure:** The outputs of both branches are **concatenated** and projected to a **Wide Hidden Layer (e.g., 1024 units)** before the final output. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.
    *   **Constraint:** No input-output skip connections; gradients must flow through the integrator. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.

### 2. Data: Complete Kinematic State Engineering

We employ a rigorous feature engineering pipeline that resolves the conflict between "Momentum" and "Intent" by explicitly including both.

*   **Kinematic Features:**
    *   **Backward Velocity (Momentum):** Explicitly compute $u_{in}(t) - u_{in}(t-1)$. This captures the instantaneous physical momentum acting on the system. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00090**.
    *   **Forward Lookahead (Intent):** Explicitly include raw shifted columns for $u_{in}$ at $t+1 \dots t+4$ to provide zero-lag future context. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00051**.
    *   **Proportional:** Raw $u_{in}$.
*   **Physical State:**
    *   **Numerical Integration:** Calculate Volume ($Area$) using physically accurate integration $\sum u_{in} \cdot dt$. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**.
    *   **Time-Delta (`dt`):** Explicitly include $dt$ to allow learning of variable-rate physics. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00067**.
    *   **Input Completeness:** Explicitly **retain `u_out`** to allow the Bi-LSTM to explain expiratory drops during backpropagation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00072**.
    *   **Physics Interactions:** Pre-compute $R \cdot u_{in}$ and $Area / C$ and concatenate via input. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**.
*   **Exclusions:**
    *   Exclude raw `time_step` (destabilizes CNNs). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00069**.
    *   Exclude Positional Encodings (high-frequency noise). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00085**.
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   Use `RobustScaler` (Median/IQR) to handle valve outliers. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.

### 3. Training: Corrected Regularization Regime

We enforce a training regime that specifically corrects the "Regularization Sensitivity" failure of previous attempts.

*   **Regularization:** We strictly use **Low Weight Decay (e.g., 1e-4)**. High weight decay on unscaled regression targets penalizes the large weights necessary to scale the output, causing underfitting. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00093**.
*   **Batch Size:** Strictly use a **Batch Size of 128**. This balances gradient noise for generalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018** with stability for Batch Normalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00076**.
*   **Gradient Clipping:** Apply `clip_grad_norm_(max_norm=1.0)` to prevent exploding gradients. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00065**.
*   **Budget:** Train for **80+ epochs** to synchronize distinct gradient dynamics. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
*   **Dataset:** Train on the **Full Training Set (100%)** to avoid phase transition failures. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**.
*   **Loss Function:** **Masked L1 Loss (MAE)**, calculated strictly on the inspiratory phase (`u_out == 0`). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
