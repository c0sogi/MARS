Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Lookahead-Augmented Non-Causal Hybrid (LAN-Net)**. This strategy corrects the "Modulation" failure of the previous iteration (Idea 11) by reverting to **Input Concatenation** (Lesson 46), while retaining and enhancing the **Non-Causal** and **High-Capacity** structural advancements that proved effective in Ideas 9 and 10.

### 1. Model: Lookahead-Augmented Non-Causal Hybrid (LAN-Net)

The architecture employs a parallel hybrid design to structurally decouple resistive and elastic dynamics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**, optimized specifically for the **offline** nature of the task.

*   **Input Layer: Deep Contextual & Lookahead Injection**
    *   **Structure:** At *every time step*, the input vector consists of:
        1.  **Current PID State:** $u_{in}$, $u_{in\_cumsum}$ (Volume), $\Delta u_{in}$ (Accel).
        2.  **Static Physics:** $R, C$.
        3.  **Interaction Terms:** $R \cdot u_{in}, Volume / C$.
        4.  **Lookahead Features (Innovation):** $u_{in}(t+1), u_{in}(t+2)$ and $\Delta u_{in}(t+1)$.
    *   **Rationale:** We explicitly reject the "FiLM" modulation (Idea 11) which caused overfitting **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**. Instead, we return to **Input Concatenation** to condition the network on physics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**. The **Lookahead Features** provide the network with immediate, zero-lag access to future valve transitions, reinforcing the non-causal advantages without requiring deep backpropagation to "see" the future.

*   **Branch 1: Non-Causal Pyramidal TCN (The Resistive Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models high-frequency, derivative-dependent dynamics ($R \cdot \dot{V}$).
    *   **Padding:** Uses **Centered Padding** (Non-Causal) to see both past and future context at every layer. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.
    *   **Geometry:** Uses a **Wide Kernel (Size 7)** to smooth physical signals and approximate derivatives better than small kernels. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.
    *   **Scaling:** Implements **Pyramidal Channel Scaling** (64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 512) to match capacity with the growing receptive field. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00024**.

*   **Branch 2: High-Capacity Bidirectional LSTM (The Elastic Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models low-frequency, integral-dependent dynamics ($V/C$).
    *   **Capacity:** A **3-layer Bidirectional LSTM** with **512 hidden units**. We explicitly prioritize capacity here to serve as the numerical integrator. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**.

*   **Fusion Head: Coupled Latent Integration**
    *   **Structure:** The outputs of both branches are **concatenated** and passed to a deep Dense MLP.
    *   **Constraint:** We explicitly **reject Additive Decomposition** (Idea 8) to allow learning of non-linear coupling. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**.
    *   **Constraint:** We explicitly **exclude the Input-Output Skip Connection** to force gradients through the LSTM integrator. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.

### 2. Data: Robust Pipeline with Lookahead Engineering

We employ a rigorous pipeline to handle outliers and ensure synchronization.

*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Robust Scaling:** Use `RobustScaler` (Median and IQR) instead of `StandardScaler`. This prevents extreme valve settings from dominating the gradients and accelerates convergence. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.
*   **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **PID State:** Compute Proportional, Integral (Volume), and Derivative (Acceleration) features. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    *   **Lookahead Generation:** Create shifted columns for $t+1$ and $t+2$ for $u_{in}$ and its derivative.
    *   **Physics Interactions:** Pre-compute $R \cdot u_{in}$ and $Volume / C$. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   **Unified Configuration:** Define a single `FEATURE_CONFIG` to synchronize generation and model input. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   **Automated Cache Invalidation:** Automatically delete stale `.npy` files at start-up. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.

### 3. Training: Noise-Injected Optimization & Extended Budget

The training regime prioritizes generalization and convergence.

*   **Loss Function:** **Masked L1 Loss (MAE)**. Calculated only where `u_out == 0` (inspiratory phase). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Batch Size:** Strictly use a **Small Batch Size (128)**. This introduces gradient noise to act as implicit regularization, preventing the hybrid model from trapping in sharp local minima. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with `ReduceLROnPlateau`.
*   **Budget:** Train for **80 epochs**. Hybrid architectures with distinct gradient dynamics require extended training to synchronize. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.

### 4. Evaluation

*   **Inference:** The test set is processed using the identical `FEATURE_CONFIG`, `RobustScaler`, and `Lookahead` pipeline.
*   **Submission:** Predictions are generated in batches (size 128), flattened, and joined with `id` for the final submission.
