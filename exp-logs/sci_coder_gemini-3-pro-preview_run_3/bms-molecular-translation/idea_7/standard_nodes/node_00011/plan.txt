The `CUDA out of memory` error was caused by the computation graph growing too large during training, specifically due to the combination of a batch size of 64 and a long sequence length (450 steps) in the attention-based Seq2Seq model. This exceeded the A100 GPU's 40GB memory limit. To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `runfile.py` from 64 to 32, which will reduce the memory required to store intermediate activations for backpropagation.
