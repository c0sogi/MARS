The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the default behavior of gradient checkpointing in Hugging Face Transformers, which uses `use_reentrant=True`. This legacy mode is known to conflict with PyTorch's Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) and `GradScaler` during backpropagation (Cite debug_lesson_4). To fix this, I will modify `library/model.py` to explicitly disable reentrant checkpointing by passing `gradient_checkpointing_kwargs={"use_reentrant": False}` when enabling gradient checkpointing on the backbone model.
