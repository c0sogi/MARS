The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` typically arises when the computation graph is corrupted or when gradient checkpointing interacts incorrectly with the backward pass. To fix this, I will disable `use_gradient_checkpointing` in `library/config.py`, as the A100 GPU has sufficient memory to train without it. Additionally, I will replace the in-place tensor modification in `library/model.py` (where attention weights are masked) with an out-of-place `masked_fill` operation to preserve the integrity of the autograd graph.
