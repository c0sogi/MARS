The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the default reentrant implementation of gradient checkpointing in Hugging Face Transformers, which creates conflicts in the autograd graph during the backward pass. I will fix this in `library/model.py` by explicitly setting `use_reentrant=False` when enabling gradient checkpointing. Additionally, I will update `library/engine.py` to cast logits to `float32` before applying softmax in both validation and prediction loops. This ensures that the resulting probabilities sum exactly to 1.0, preventing assertion errors related to numerical precision in mixed-precision environments (Cite debug_lesson_6).
