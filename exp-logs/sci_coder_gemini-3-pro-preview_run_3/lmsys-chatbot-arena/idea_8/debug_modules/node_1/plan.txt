The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the default behavior of PyTorch's gradient checkpointing (`use_reentrant=True`), which conflicts with the Siamese architecture where the same backbone is called twice in a single forward pass. To fix this, I will explicitly disable reentrant checkpointing in `library/model.py` (Cite debug_lesson_7). Additionally, to prevent numerical instability and overflow errors in Mixed Precision training, I will cast attention scores to `float32` before masking in `library/model.py` (Cite debug_lesson_8) and cast logits to `float32` before the softmax operation in `library/engine.py` (Cite debug_lesson_6).
