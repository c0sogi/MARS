The issue is that the model's output logits are in `float16` precision due to the Mixed Precision (`autocast`) context. Applying `softmax` directly to `float16` tensors results in probability distributions that do not sum to exactly 1.0, causing the assertion failure in the verification step. To fix this, I will explicitly cast the logits to `float32` before applying `softmax` in `library/engine.py`. This ensures numerical stability and valid probability distributions (Cite debug_lesson_6).
