The issue is that the `GraphOptimizer.solve_trajectory` method requires the raw Weighted Least Squares (WLS) ECEF coordinates (`WlsPositionXEcefMeters`, etc.) to establish anchor positions, but these columns are currently dropped during the feature engineering phase in `FeatureEngine.process_drive`. The `row_dict` constructed in `process_drive` only includes derived features and approximate coordinates, causing a `KeyError` downstream.

To fix this, I will modify `library/feature_eng.py` to explicitly include `WlsPositionXEcefMeters`, `WlsPositionYEcefMeters`, and `WlsPositionZEcefMeters` in the `row_dict` dictionary within the `process_drive` method. This ensures these critical columns are persisted in the feature DataFrame, satisfying the requirements of the optimization module. This aligns with the principle of persisting necessary context identifiers and base data through projection steps (Cite debug_lesson_15).
