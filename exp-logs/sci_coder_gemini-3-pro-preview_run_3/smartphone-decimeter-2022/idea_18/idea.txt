Based on the lessons learned, specifically the superiority of **Gradient Boosting** for sensor residuals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010), the effectiveness of **Geometric Projection** features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043), and the high precision of **Carrier Phase (TDCP)** for relative motion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046), I propose the **Dual-Projection Residual Boosting with Hierarchical Graph Fusion** strategy.

This approach evolves the previous best ideas by 1) enriching the feature space with explicit "Doppler Forces" alongside Pseudorange Forces to better detect multipath, and 2) upgrading the trajectory reconstruction to a hierarchical graph optimization that prioritizes carrier-phase precision while robustly falling back to Doppler velocity when necessary.

### 1. Model: Dual-Projected LightGBM Ensemble
The predictive core remains an ensemble of **LightGBM Regressors**, optimized to map instantaneous signal snapshots to position errors.

*   **Architecture:** Two independent models will be trained:
    *   **East Model:** Predicts the Easting error ($\Delta E$).
    *   **North Model:** Predicts the Northing error ($\Delta N$).
*   **Target:** The model predicts the **ENU (East-North-Up) Residuals** ($P_{GT} - P_{WLS}$) in meters. This leverages the WLS baseline as a robust physical prior (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and avoids the distortion of geodetic coordinates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

### 2. Data: Point-Wise Dual-Physics Features
The preprocessing pipeline strictly adheres to a **Point-Wise** paradigm (no temporal windowing) to prevent trajectory memorization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011), but significantly enhances the physical descriptors fed to the model.

*   **Coordinate Transformation:** Convert Ground Truth and WLS positions to a local ENU frame.
*   **Dual-Projection Feature Engineering (Innovation):**
    For each timestamp, calculate the Line-of-Sight vector ($\vec{u}_i$) for every satellite and compute two distinct "Force" vectors:
    1.  **Pseudorange Force:** $\vec{F}_{PR} = \sum w_i \cdot (r_{PR,i}) \cdot \vec{u}_i$. This represents the net geometric error pulling the position estimate.
    2.  **Doppler Force:** $\vec{F}_{Dop} = \sum w_i \cdot (r_{Dop,i}) \cdot \vec{u}_i$. Here, $r_{Dop,i}$ is the residual between the measured pseudorange rate and the theoretical range rate at the WLS position.
    *   *Rationale:* High Doppler residuals indicate non-line-of-sight signals (multipath). By projecting these residuals, we explicitly tell the model *which direction* the multipath is coming from (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Geometry Context:** Elements of the geometry matrix (DOP) to scale the forces.
*   **Global Context:** Aggregated signal quality (Mean Cn0) and IMU dynamics.

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. This L1 loss minimizes the median error, ensuring the predicted "anchors" are robust to the massive outliers inherent in urban GNSS data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Validation:** **GroupKFold** cross-validation grouped by `drive_id`. This ensures the model is evaluated on completely unseen drives, enforcing generalization.

### 4. Evaluation: Hierarchical Graph Fusion
The inference pipeline replaces simple smoothing with a **Hierarchical Factor Graph Optimization** that fuses the ML predictions with the best available kinematic physics.

1.  **Anchor Generation:** Use the trained LightGBM ensemble to predict residuals and correct the WLS baseline, producing a sequence of absolute positions $\hat{P}_{ML}$.
2.  **Hierarchical Odometry Estimation:**
    For every pair of timestamps $(t-1, t)$, estimate the relative displacement vector $\Delta \vec{x}_t$ using a priority hierarchy:
    *   **Tier 1: RANSAC TDCP:** Solve Time-Differenced Carrier Phase using RANSAC. This provides mm-level precision (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046).
    *   **Tier 2: RANSAC Doppler:** If TDCP fails (cycle slips), solve Doppler velocity using RANSAC. This provides cm/s-level precision.
    *   **Tier 3: Constant Velocity:** Fallback if signal is lost.
3.  **Global Graph Optimization:**
    Solve for the trajectory $X = \{x_0, \dots, x_N\}$ that minimizes:
    $$ J(X) = \sum_{t} \rho(x_t - \hat{P}_{ML, t}) + \sum_{t} \lambda_t ||(x_t - x_{t-1}) - \Delta \vec{x}_{Odom, t}||^2 $$
    *   **Anchor Term:** $\rho$ is a **Huber Loss**. It pulls the trajectory towards the ML predictions but allows it to "break" away from large ML outliers (fixing the smearing issue of Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
    *   **Shape Term:** The L2 penalty enforces the precise shape derived from the Hierarchical Odometry. $\lambda_t$ is high for TDCP and lower for Doppler.
