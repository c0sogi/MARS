The `CUDA Out of Memory` error occurs because the `BATCH_SIZE` of 512 generates intermediate activation tensors (specifically in the attention mechanism) that, when aggregated over the sequence length for backpropagation, exceed the available 16 GB of GPU memory. To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 512 to 128. This reduction decreases the memory required for storing activations and gradients per step, ensuring the training process fits within the hardware constraints.
