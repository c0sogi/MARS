Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00012` ("Data Volume Dominance") explicitly demonstrates that data scale is the most critical factor for minimizing RMSE in this task. The previous solution's regression to 3.54 RMSE was largely due to data starvation.

To make full-scale training feasible within the time limit, I will also optimize the validation process during training. Evaluating early stopping metrics on the full 11M validation set at every step is computationally expensive and unnecessary for convergence checks. I will modify the trainer to use a representative subsample for early stopping while preserving the full validation set for the final rigorous scoring.
