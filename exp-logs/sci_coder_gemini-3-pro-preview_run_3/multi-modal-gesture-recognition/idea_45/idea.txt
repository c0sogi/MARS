Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the progression of "Previous Ideas," I propose the **Split-Horizon Physically-Aligned Moderate-Capacity Network (SH-PAM-CN)**.

This solution represents a structural synthesis that explicitly resolves the **"Input Engineering Trilemma"** (Magnitude vs. Hierarchy vs. Saturation) and the **Receptive Field Constraint** (Lesson 00057). It replaces the risky high-dilation schedules of previous attempts with a **Split-Horizon Dilation Strategy** that ensures stability within the training window. It combines this with a **Physically-Aligned Moderate-Capacity Encoder** and **Robust Polymorphic Ingestion**, strictly adhering to the stability constraints established in the lessons.

# Model
The architecture is a **Three-Stage Cascaded Network** designed for geometric invariance, optimal capacity, and stable temporal refinement.

1.  **Stage 1: Physically-Aligned Moderate-Capacity Encoder**
    *   **Input:** An **Early Fusion** vector consisting of two streams:
        *   **Skeleton Stream:** Kinematically Consistent features (Position, Velocity, Acceleration).
            *   **Preprocessing:** We apply **Root-Relative Centering** (subtracting Hip Center) to ensure translation invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00102).
            *   **Physical Scaling:** We explicitly convert units from millimeters to meters (multiply by 0.001) *after* derivation. This aligns the magnitude of Position (~1.0) with Audio, preventing gradient dominance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00082), while strictly preserving the **Signal-to-Noise Hierarchy** (Position $\gg$ Acceleration) required to filter noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00085).
        *   **Audio Stream:** MFCC features processed via **Standard Scaling** (zero mean, unit variance).
        *   **Fusion:** Concatenation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00097).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Bi-directional GRU (Bi-GRU)** with a hidden dimension of **96 units per direction** (192 total).
        *   *Rationale:* This "Goldilocks" capacity avoids the bottleneck of lightweight encoders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066) while preventing the overfitting observed in high-capacity (256-unit) encoders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00110).
    *   **Regularization:** **Dropout (p=0.4)** is applied to encoder outputs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091).
    *   **Output:** Initial frame-wise class probabilities ($P_1$) over 21 classes.

2.  **Stage 2: Split-Horizon Non-Causal Refinement**
    *   **Input:** Strictly the **class probabilities** ($P_1$) from Stage 1 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Architecture:** A stack of **Gated Dilated Temporal Convolutional Layers** ($\tanh \times \sigma$) using **Standard Convolutions** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Structural Innovation (Split-Horizon Schedule):** We employ a restricted **Monotonic Schedule**: $[1, 2, 4, 8]$.
        *   *Rationale:* This yields a receptive field of **31 frames**. Unlike previous attempts using dilation 16 (RF=63), this ensures the temporal span is comfortably less than half the training window size (64), strictly adhering to Lesson 00057. This prevents the model from learning edge artifacts caused by zero-padding.
    *   **Causality:** **Centered Padding** (Non-Causal) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078).
    *   **Output:** Refined class probabilities ($P_2$).

3.  **Stage 3: Independent Split-Horizon Refinement**
    *   **Structure:** Identical to Stage 2 (Dilation $[1, 2, 4, 8]$) but with **independent weights** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).
    *   *Cumulative Receptive Field:* By stacking two independent stages of RF=31, the total effective receptive field ($\sim 62$) covers the window duration through cascaded refinement, achieving the necessary context without the instability of a single deep stage.
    *   **Output:** Final class probabilities ($P_3$).

# Data
The data pipeline incorporates critical engineering fixes for robustness and strict physical consistency.

1.  **Robust Polymorphic Parsing (Structural Requirement):**
    *   The data loader implements a **Polymorphic Parser** for the `.mat` files. It explicitly checks the data type of the `Skeleton` field (struct array vs. cell array vs. primitive) and handles each case dynamically. This prevents the "silent failure" (zero-filling) that crippled previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).

2.  **Kinematically Consistent Augmentation:**
    *   **Load:** Raw 3D Joint Positions ($P_{raw}$).
    *   **Center:** Apply **Root-Relative Centering**.
    *   **Augment:** Apply random 3D rotations (Y-axis) and scaling.
    *   **Derive:** Calculate **Velocity** and **Acceleration** from the *augmented* positions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   **Scale:** Apply **Physical Scaling** (mm to meters) to all kinematic features.

3.  **Sampling Strategy:**
    *   **Windowing:** Generate training samples using **Sliding Windows of 64 frames** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Stride:** Use a **Moderate Stride** (e.g., 32 frames) to avoid overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).

# Training
The training strategy prioritizes stability and smoothness using Deep Supervision.

1.  **Cascaded Loss Function:**
    *   $L_{total} = L(P_1) + L(P_2) + L(P_3)$ (Deep Supervision) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Weighted Cross-Entropy:** Weight of **0.2** for "background" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Log-Space Smoothing Loss:** Truncated MSE on log-probabilities.
    *   **Calibration:** **Low Weight** ($\lambda=0.15$) and **Low Threshold** (1.0) to prevent under-segmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00111, solution_lesson_node_00055).

2.  **Optimizer:**
    *   **Adam** optimizer with **Weight Decay** (L2) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** **Full Sequence Inference** (Batch Size=1) to preserve global temporal context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00079).
2.  **Model Selection:** Select best checkpoint based on **Levenshtein Distance** on full sequences (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052).
3.  **Decoding:**
    *   Apply `argmax` to $P_3$.
    *   Perform **Run-Length Encoding**.
    *   **Min Duration Filter:** Remove segments shorter than **5 frames** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071).
