Based on the rigorous analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the progression of "Previous Ideas," I propose the **Decoupled-Norm Gated Central-Kinematic Network (DGC-KN)**.

This solution represents a structural synthesis that explicitly resolves the **"Input Engineering Trilemma"** (Magnitude vs. Hierarchy vs. Saturation) identified in Lessons 00082, 00085, and 00096. It introduces a **Decoupled-Norm Gating** mechanism that allows for dynamic noise suppression without saturating on raw inputs or destroying the critical signal-to-noise hierarchy. It combines this with the **Central-Difference Kinematics** and **RF-Aligned Refinement** strategies validated in Lesson 00127, strictly adhering to the stability constraints established in the lessons.

# Model
The architecture is a **Three-Stage Cascaded Network** designed for noise robustness, optimal capacity, and temporal stability.

1.  **Stage 1: Decoupled-Norm Gated Encoder**
    *   **Input:** An **Early Fusion** vector consisting of two streams:
        *   **Skeleton Stream:** Kinematically Consistent features (Position, Velocity, Acceleration) derived via **Central Differences** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00127). We explicitly **retain Raw Millimeters** to ensure the robust skeletal signal dominates the auxiliary audio signal, preventing "Audio Dominance" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00082).
        *   **Audio Stream:** MFCC features.
    *   **Structural Innovation (Decoupled-Norm Input Gating):** To solve the conflict between noise suppression (Gating) and raw feature magnitude (Saturation), we split the input processing into two parallel paths:
        *   *Path A (Gate Generation):* $X_{norm} = LayerNorm(X_{raw})$. This normalized copy is passed through a linear layer and Sigmoid activation to generate a mask $G \in [0, 1]$. Normalization here prevents the vanishing gradients observed in Lesson 00096.
        *   *Path B (Signal Retention):* The original $X_{raw}$ is retained to preserve the natural **Signal-to-Noise Hierarchy** (Position $\gg$ Acceleration) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00085).
        *   *Fusion:* $\tilde{X} = X_{raw} \odot G$. This allows the model to dynamically suppress noisy channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058) without altering the physical scale of the valid signal.
    *   **Backbone:** A **2-Layer Bi-directional GRU (Bi-GRU)** with a hidden dimension of **96 units per direction** (192 total). This "Goldilocks" capacity avoids the bottlenecks of lightweight encoders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066) and the instability of high-capacity models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00110).
    *   **Regularization:** **Dropout (p=0.4)** is applied between GRU layers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091).
    *   **Output:** Initial frame-wise class probabilities ($P_1$) over 21 classes.

2.  **Stage 2: RF-Aligned Monotonic Refinement**
    *   **Input:** Strictly the **class probabilities** ($P_1$) from Stage 1 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Architecture:** A stack of **Gated Dilated Temporal Convolutional Layers** ($\tanh \times \sigma$) using **Standard Convolutions** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Dilation Schedule:** **Monotonically Increasing** $[1, 2, 4, 8, 16]$.
        *   *Rationale:* This yields a receptive field of **63 frames**, matching the 64-frame training window almost perfectly ($63 < 64$). This maximizes context without reading invalid zero-padding, correcting the RF mismatch failures of previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00127, solution_lesson_node_00057).
    *   **Causality:** **Centered Padding** (Non-Causal) to leverage future context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078).
    *   **Output:** Refined class probabilities ($P_2$).

3.  **Stage 3: Independent RF-Aligned Refinement**
    *   **Structure:** Identical to Stage 2 but with **independent weights** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).
    *   **Output:** Final class probabilities ($P_3$).

# Data
The data pipeline incorporates critical engineering fixes for robustness and advanced kinematic derivation.

1.  **Robust Polymorphic Parsing (Structural Requirement):**
    *   The data loader implements a **Polymorphic Parser** for the `.mat` files, dynamically handling struct vs. cell array vs. primitive types to prevent "silent failure" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).

2.  **Central-Difference Kinematics (Structural Innovation):**
    *   **Load:** Raw 3D Joint Positions ($P_{raw}$).
    *   **Center:** Apply **Root-Relative Centering** (subtracting Hip Center) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00102).
    *   **Augment:** Apply random 3D rotations (Y-axis) and scaling.
    *   **Derive:** Use **Central Differences** (`np.gradient`) instead of forward differences. This provides a phase-aligned, smoother estimate of Velocity and Acceleration, reducing high-frequency noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00127).
    *   **Fusion:** Concatenate $P_{aug}$, $V_{central}$, $A_{central}$, and Audio MFCCs.

3.  **Sampling Strategy:**
    *   **Windowing:** Generate training samples using **Sliding Windows of 64 frames** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Stride:** Use a **Moderate Stride** (e.g., 32 frames) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).

# Training
The training strategy prioritizes stability and smoothness using Deep Supervision.

1.  **Cascaded Loss Function:**
    *   $L_{total} = L(P_1) + L(P_2) + L(P_3)$ (Deep Supervision) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Weighted Cross-Entropy:** Weight of **0.2** for "background" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Log-Space Smoothing Loss:** Truncated MSE on log-probabilities.
    *   **Calibration:** **Low Weight** ($\lambda=0.15$) and **Low Threshold** (1.0) to prevent under-segmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00111, solution_lesson_node_00055).

2.  **Optimizer:**
    *   **Adam** optimizer with **Weight Decay** (L2) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** **Full Sequence Inference** (Batch Size=1) to preserve global temporal context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00079).
2.  **Model Selection:** Select best checkpoint based on **Levenshtein Distance** on full sequences (Cite solution_lesson_node_00075).
3.  **Decoding:**
    *   Apply `argmax` to $P_3$.
    *   Perform **Run-Length Encoding**.
    *   **Min Duration Filter:** Remove segments shorter than **5 frames** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071).
