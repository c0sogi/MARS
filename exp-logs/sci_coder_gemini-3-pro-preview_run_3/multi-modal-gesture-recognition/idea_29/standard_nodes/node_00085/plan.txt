Based on **Lesson 69 (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00069})**, I will increase the depth of the BiGRU encoder from 1 layer to 2 layers and introduce dropout (0.3) to prevent overfitting while maintaining the necessary capacity for hierarchical sequence modeling. This aligns the architecture with the "Current Best" configuration referenced in the lessons. I will also slightly increase the number of training epochs in the run script to accommodate the deeper model.
