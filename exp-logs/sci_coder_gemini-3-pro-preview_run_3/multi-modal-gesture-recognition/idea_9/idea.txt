Based on the progression of experiments and the specific insights from the "Lessons Learned," I propose the **View-Invariant Attentive Refinement Network (VI-ARN)**.

This solution structurally evolves the successful "Kinematically-Consistent Iterative Refinement" architecture (Idea 7) by addressing two remaining bottlenecks: the spatial variance caused by user orientation (User Independence) and the lack of explicit inter-class dependency modeling in the refinement stages.

# Model
The architecture is a **Three-Stage Cascaded Network** augmented with **Channel Attention** mechanisms.

1.  **Stage 1: Sequence Encoder (Bi-GRU)**
    *   **Input:** An **Early Fusion** vector consisting of View-Aligned Kinematically Consistent Skeleton Features (Position, Velocity, Acceleration) and Audio MFCCs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Bi-directional GRU (Bi-GRU)**. This is retained for its superior sample efficiency and ability to capture local temporal context on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Output:** Initial frame-wise class probabilities ($P_1$) over 21 classes.

2.  **Stage 2 & 3: Attentive Gated Refinement**
    *   **Input:** Strictly the **class probabilities** from the previous stage, enforcing the **Information Bottleneck** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Architecture:** Stacked **Gated Dilated Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCNs)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
    *   **Structural Innovation (Channel Attention):** Within each residual block of the TCN, a **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Block** is inserted after the Gated Activation.
        *   *Mechanism:* The SE block performs global average pooling over the temporal window to capture the global distribution of active classes, then recalibrates the channel (class) weights. This allows the refinement stage to learn global class correlations (e.g., suppressing "Start" gestures if "End" gestures are not present), enhancing the smoothing process beyond simple local temporal consistency.

# Data
The data pipeline is fundamentally restructured to explicitly solve the "User Independent" challenge via geometric alignment.

1.  **Canonical View Alignment (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Before any augmentation or feature extraction, perform **Frame-wise Skeleton Alignment**.
    *   Calculate the rotation angle $\theta$ required to align the vector connecting the **Left Hip** and **Right Hip** to the global X-axis.
    *   Apply this rotation to all joint coordinates. This normalizes the user's orientation relative to the camera, reducing intra-class variance caused by users standing at different angles.

2.  **Kinematically Consistent Augmentation:**
    *   Apply random scaling and minor rotations to the **Aligned Positions**.
    *   Mathematically derive **Velocity** and **Acceleration** from these *augmented* positions. This ensures physical consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029, solution_lesson_node_00032).

3.  **Windowing:**
    *   Use **Sliding Windows of 64 frames** to match the atomic gesture duration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

# Training
The training strategy employs **Deep Supervision** with a strictly calibrated loss landscape.

1.  **Cascaded Loss Function:**
    *   The total loss is calculated at the output of **all three stages** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   $L_{total} = \sum_{s=1}^{3} (L_{cls}^{(s)} + \lambda_{smooth} L_{smooth}^{(s)})$.
2.  **Loss Components:**
    *   **Weighted Cross-Entropy:** For gesture classification, with a weight of **0.2** for the background class to counteract imbalance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010). We strictly avoid Focal Loss due to the noisy nature of the data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Log-Space Smoothing:** A Truncated Mean Squared Error (MSE) loss applied to the log-probabilities of adjacent frames for the refinement stages ($P_2, P_3$). This penalizes rapid label fluctuations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
3.  **Optimizer:** **Adam** optimizer. We avoid AdamW as standard Adam has proven superior for this recurrent backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Process test sequences using sliding windows with 50% overlap, averaging the probabilities in the overlapping regions.
2.  **Prediction:** Extract the final probabilities from **Stage 3**.
3.  **Decoding:**
    *   Apply `argmax` to obtain frame labels.
    *   Perform **Run-Length Encoding** to collapse consecutive identical labels.
    *   **No Heuristic Filtering:** We rely entirely on the model's learned smoothness and the SE-blocks' context awareness, avoiding the destructive interference of post-hoc median filters (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
