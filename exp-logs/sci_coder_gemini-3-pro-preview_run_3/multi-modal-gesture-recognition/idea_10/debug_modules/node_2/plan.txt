The issue is that the `_load_data` method relies on `self.mfcc_transform` to process audio features, but `self.mfcc_transform` is currently initialized in the `__init__` method *after* `_load_data` is called. This leads to an `AttributeError` because the attribute does not exist when the helper method attempts to access it. To fix this, I will move the initialization of `self.mfcc_transform` to occur before the call to `self._load_data` within `__init__`. Cite debug_lesson_13.
