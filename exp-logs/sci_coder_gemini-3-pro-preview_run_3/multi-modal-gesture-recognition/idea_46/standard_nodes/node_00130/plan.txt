Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Optimize Temporal Context (Cite Lesson 00057):** Increase the `WINDOW_SIZE` from 64 to 128. The current model has a Receptive Field (RF) of ~63 frames. Lesson 00057 teaches that the TCN dilation span should be significantly smaller than the window size (e.g., `max_span < window_size / 2`) to avoid boundary artifacts. With a window of 64 and max dilation span of 32, the model was on the edge. Increasing the window to 128 places the RF comfortably within the window, ensuring the central predictions rely on valid data context.
2.  **Reduce Sampling Redundancy (Cite Lesson 00053):** Increase `STRIDE` from 32 to 64. This maintains a 50% overlap ratio with the new window size but reduces the total number of highly correlated training samples, preventing overfitting to redundant patterns and speeding up training.
3.  **Adjust Training Duration:** Increase `EPOCHS` from 25 to 40 in `runfile.py`. Since the number of training samples (windows) decreases due to the larger stride, and the model now has to learn from longer temporal contexts, slightly more epochs are required for convergence.
