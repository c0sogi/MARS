To resolve the `torch.OutOfMemoryError` caused by the memory-intensive DeBERTa-Large model and Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP), I will implement two key optimizations: **Mixed Precision Training (AMP)** and **Gradient Checkpointing**.

1.  **Mixed Precision (AMP)**: I will modify `library/engine.py` to wrap the forward pass in `torch.amp.autocast('cuda')` and use `torch.amp.GradScaler` to scale gradients. This significantly reduces memory usage for activations.
2.  **Gradient Checkpointing**: I will enable gradient checkpointing on the model backbone in `runfile.py`. Crucially, I will enforce `use_reentrant=False` to prevent the "backward through graph a second time" error (Cite `debug_lesson_7`) and explicitly disable `use_cache` to avoid conflicts with the checkpointing mechanism (Cite `debug_lesson_5`).

These changes will reduce the memory footprint, allowing the model to train with AWP on the available hardware.
