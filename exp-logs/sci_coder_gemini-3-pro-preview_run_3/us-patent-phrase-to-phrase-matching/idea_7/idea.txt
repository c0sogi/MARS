Based on the accumulated lessons and the available computational resources, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the previous "Hierarchical Context-Aware Cross-Encoder" by introducing **Multi-Layer Representation Aggregation** and **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** stabilization, while retaining the successful components of DAPT, AWP, and Metric-Aware Loss.

# Model
*   **Backbone:** We will employ **`microsoft/deberta-v3-large`** as the core encoder. Its pre-trained disentangled attention mechanism has proven superior for capturing semantic nuances (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Architecture:** We will persist with the **Cross-Encoder** design (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008), feeding the context and phrase pairs as a single sequence to enable deep token-level interaction.
*   **Multi-Layer Attention Pooling (Structural Innovation):** Instead of relying solely on the final layer's output, we will implement a **Multi-Layer Pooling** mechanism. We will extract the hidden states from the **last 4 layers** of the transformer. An **Attention Pooling** layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) will be applied to each of these layers independently to generate four distinct sentence embeddings. These embeddings will be concatenated to form the final representation.
    *   *Rationale:* While the final layer captures high-level abstract semantics, slightly lower layers often retain more granular lexical and syntactic information. Aggregating these provides the regression head with a "vertical slice" of the model's understanding, helping to distinguish between close synonyms and antonyms (which often share high-level semantic contexts but differ in specific lexical polarity).
*   **Heads:** The concatenated output will pass through **Multi-Sample Dropout (MSD)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014) before feeding into two parallel heads:
    1.  **Regression Head:** Predicts the continuous similarity score.
    2.  **Classification Head:** Predicts the discrete score bucket (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1).

# Data
*   **Hierarchical Context Expansion:** We will map every `context` code to its full lineage of descriptions (Section > Class > Subclass) and inject this into the input sequence (e.g., `[CLS] Section Desc [SEP] Class Desc [SEP] Subclass Desc [SEP] Anchor [SEP] Target [SEP]`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Domain-Adaptive Pre-training (DAPT) Corpus:** We will compile a corpus containing all unique texts from the training set, test set, and CPC context descriptions to adapt the model's vocabulary to the patent domain.

# Training
The training pipeline will consist of two distinct phases:

**Phase 1: Domain-Adaptive Pre-training (DAPT)**
*   We will train the `deberta-v3-large` backbone on the unlabelled domain corpus using a **Masked Language Modeling (MLM)** objective. This ensures the embeddings are aligned with technical patent terminology before supervised learning begins.

**Phase 2: Robust Supervised Fine-Tuning with EMA**
*   **Loss Function:** We will optimize a composite objective: $L_{Total} = L_{MSE} + \lambda_1 L_{CE} + \lambda_2 (1 - \rho)$. This combines the geometric precision of MSE, the decision-boundary sharpness of Cross-Entropy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014), and the direct maximization of the Pearson correlation ($\rho$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Regularization:** We will apply **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) to flatten the loss landscape.
*   **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA) (Structural Innovation):** We will maintain a "shadow" copy of the model parameters that is updated as an exponential moving average of the active weights during training (e.g., $\theta_{EMA} = 0.999 \cdot \theta_{EMA} + 0.001 \cdot \theta_{train}$).
    *   *Rationale:* AWP introduces noise to the weights to find robust minima. EMA acts as a continuous temporal ensemble, smoothing out these high-frequency perturbations and yielding a final model that generalizes significantly better than any single snapshot.
*   **Optimization:** We will use AdamW with **Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) to protect the pre-trained backbone features.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will use the **EMA weights** (not the final training weights) to generate predictions on the test set.
*   **Prediction:** Inputs will be formatted with the hierarchical context strings. The model will output raw regression scores.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be clipped to the [0, 1] range.
