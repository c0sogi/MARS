The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` during `loss.backward()` indicates a conflict in the autograd graph management, specifically caused by **Gradient Checkpointing**. As explained in **debug_lesson_1**, this optimization technique, while useful for saving memory, often causes graph traversal issues (such as "freed tensor" or "double backward" errors) when combined with complex architectures or training loops like Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP). Since the provided NVIDIA A100 40GB GPU has ample VRAM to accommodate the model without checkpointing, the most stable fix is to disable this optimization in the configuration. Cite debug_lesson_1.
