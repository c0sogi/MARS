The execution failure is caused by **Gradient Checkpointing**, which is enabled in `library/model.py`. While this technique saves memory, it complicates the computational graph and, in this specific environment (likely due to library versions or the specific model architecture), causes a `RuntimeError` during the backward pass indicating that the graph is being traversed incorrectly ("backward through the graph a second time"). Since the available hardware (NVIDIA A100 40GB) has sufficient VRAM for the configured batch size, gradient checkpointing is unnecessary. I will disable it by commenting out the `gradient_checkpointing_enable` call in `library/model.py` to resolve the autograd conflict (Cite debug_lesson_1).
