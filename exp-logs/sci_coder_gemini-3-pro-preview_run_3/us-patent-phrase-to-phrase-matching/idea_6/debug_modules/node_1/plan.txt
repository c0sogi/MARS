The range of FP16 is approximately $[-65504, 65504]$, so `-1e9` causes an overflow when cast to half-precision. To fix this, I will replace `-1e9` with `-1e4`, which is sufficiently negative to ensure the softmax output is zero for masked tokens while remaining within the representable range of FP16.
