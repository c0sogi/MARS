The `AttributeError: 'list' object has no attribute 'to'` arises because the `CustomCollator` inserts an `"ids"` key (containing a list of strings) into the batch dictionary, but the training and validation loops in `library/engine.py` blindly iterate over all items and attempt to call `.to(device)` on them. Lists do not have a `.to()` method. The fix involves modifying the loops in `train_fn` and `valid_fn` to skip the `"ids"` key when moving tensors to the GPU.
