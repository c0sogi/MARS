Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the approach by implementing a **Multi-Backbone Ensemble** to resolve the capacity-vs-data trade-off, correcting the preprocessing flaw identified in previous experiments, and enhancing the inference pipeline with **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**.

### 1. Model: Dual-Backbone Ensemble (EfficientNet-B2 & B3)
**Architecture:**
I will construct an ensemble of two distinct models to capture complementary feature representations:
1.  **Model A (Retention):** **EfficientNet-B2** backbone.
    *   *Reasoning:* This architecture is the proven "sweet spot" for this dataset, offering the optimal balance of convergence speed and feature extraction capability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).
2.  **Model B (Structural Innovation):** **EfficientNet-B3** backbone.
    *   *Reasoning:* Previous attempts with B3 failed due to **isotropic padding**, which reduced information density (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**). By correcting the preprocessing (see Data section), B3 allows us to leverage higher model capacity than B2 without encountering the severe optimization difficulties of B4 (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
3.  **Head (Retention):** Both backbones will utilize **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** followed by a **Linear ArcFace Head** (Batch Normalization $\rightarrow$ Dropout $\rightarrow$ Linear $\rightarrow$ Batch Normalization).
    *   *Reasoning:* I will strictly avoid non-linear activations (like PReLU) in the projection head to prevent embedding manifold distortion (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00029**) and avoid learnable pooling (GeM) to ensure stability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).

### 2. Data: High-Density Anisotropic Geometry
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Resolution:** Both models will accept **512x512** inputs.
    *   *Reasoning:* High resolution is a primary bottleneck for resolving fine-grained fluke details (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).
*   **Resizing Strategy (Crucial Correction):** I will apply **Anisotropic Resizing ("Squashing")** to force the image to 512x512, ignoring aspect ratio.
    *   *Reasoning:* I will explicitly reject the padding strategy used in previous B3 experiments. Maximizing the pixel area of the whale fluke is more critical than preserving geometric proportions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**).
*   **Augmentation:** I will strictly employ **Geometric Augmentations** (rotation, scaling, shear, flip).
    *   *Reasoning:* I will exclude occlusion-based augmentations (Cutout/Random Erasing) to preserve unique features in singleton classes (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Class Handling:** Training will be restricted to known `Id`s; `new_whale` images are excluded (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**).

### 3. Training: Independent Aggressive Optimization
**Strategy:**
*   **Loss Function:** **ArcFace Loss** ($m=0.5, s=30$).
    *   *Reasoning:* Fixed-margin ArcFace is superior to Triplet Loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**) and more stable than adaptive margins (AdaFace) in small-batch regimes (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a learning rate of **3e-4** and a batch size of **16** for *each* model.
    *   *Reasoning:* This aggressive optimization strategy is necessary to overcome the angular margin penalty and prevent underfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).
*   **Protocol:** The two models will be trained independently.

### 4. Evaluation: TTA-Enhanced Manifold Inference
**Pipeline:**
1.  **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, generate embeddings for the original and a horizontally flipped version using both Model A and Model B. Average the embeddings to produce a robust descriptor.
2.  **Ensemble Fusion:** Concatenate the L2-normalized embeddings from Model A and Model B to form the final query vector.
3.  **Query Expansion (QE) & Re-ranking:** Perform **Query Expansion** (refining the query with its neighbors) followed by **k-Reciprocal Re-ranking** (ranking based on Jaccard similarity of neighbor sets).
    *   *Reasoning:* This exploits the local density of the manifold to retrieve matches that are semantically similar but distant in pure cosine space, significantly boosting open-set performance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**).
4.  **Open-Set Thresholding:** Apply a distance threshold to the re-ranked scores. If the top match is too distant, predict `new_whale`.
5.  **Validation:** Use the retrieval-based protocol on known identities only (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).
