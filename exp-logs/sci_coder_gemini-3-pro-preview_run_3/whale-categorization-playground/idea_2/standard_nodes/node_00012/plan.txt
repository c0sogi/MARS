To optimize the solution, I will implement the following changes based on the provided lessons and the available compute resources (A100 GPU):

1.  **Backbone Upgrade**: I will upgrade the backbone from `EfficientNet-B2` to `EfficientNet-B4`. As noted in **Lesson 10 (Prioritize Modern Backbones)**, the capacity of the feature extractor is often the bottleneck in fine-grained recognition. `EfficientNet-B4` offers a better trade-off between accuracy and computational cost, and its native resolution (~380px) aligns perfectly with our 384x384 input size.
2.  **Batch Size & ArcFace Scale**: I will increase the `BATCH_SIZE` from 16 to 32, leveraging the 40GB VRAM of the A100 GPU. Larger batches provide more stable gradients for the ArcFace head. Concurrently, I will increase the ArcFace scale parameter `s` from 30.0 to 64.0. A higher scale factor is commonly used in large-scale face recognition (like InsightFace) to produce sharper probability distributions, aiding convergence when class cardinality is high (4000+ classes).
3.  **Regularization**: I will add `CoarseDropout` (a variant of Cutout) to the training augmentations. This forces the model to learn distributed features across the whale fluke rather than relying on a single distinctive mark, improving robustness to occlusion and noise.
