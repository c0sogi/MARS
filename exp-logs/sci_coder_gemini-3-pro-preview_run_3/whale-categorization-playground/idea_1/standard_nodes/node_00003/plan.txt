The previous execution failed with a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` because the GPU (approximately 16GB available capacity) could not handle the memory requirements of the training configuration. Specifically, a `BATCH_SIZE` of 32 combined with an image resolution of 256x256 and the Triplet Loss architecture (which processes three images per sample: Anchor, Positive, Negative) exceeded the available memory. DenseNet121 is also known to be memory-intensive due to feature map concatenation.

To resolve this, I will modify `library/config.py` to:
1.  Reduce `BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to 16.
2.  Reduce `IMG_HEIGHT` and `IMG_WIDTH` from 256 to 224 (the standard resolution for DenseNet).

These changes will significantly reduce the memory footprint and allow the model to train within the 16GB limit.
