# Optimized Strategy: U-Net with ConvNeXt-Small on Multi-Order Temporal Composites

### Model
I propose upgrading the model architecture to a **U-Net with a ConvNeXt-Small backbone**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Backbone Scaling):** Previous experiments confirmed that modern, isotropic backbones (ConvNeXt-Tiny) significantly outperform traditional ones (EfficientNet) due to their larger effective receptive fields (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014). Furthermore, it was established that the encoder's representation power is the primary driver of performance, outweighing decoder complexity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018). Therefore, I will scale from ConvNeXt-Tiny to **ConvNeXt-Small**. This increases the depth of the critical third stage (from 9 to 27 blocks) and the channel width, providing the necessary capacity to resolve fine-grained spatial details that the Tiny variant misses.
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first projection layer of the backbone will be structurally modified to accept **9 input channels** (an increase from 6), enabling the ingestion of the expanded temporal context described below.
*   **Decoder:** A standard symmetric U-Net decoder will be used, utilizing skip connections from the ConvNeXt encoder stages (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Data
I will evolve the feature engineering paradigm from simple motion detection to **Multi-Order Temporal Encoding**:
*   **Multi-Order Temporal Input:** Instead of the previous 6-channel input (Current Frame + 1st Order Difference), I will construct a **9-channel tensor** to capture the *trajectory* and *acceleration* of contrail formation. The input will consist of:
    1.  **Channels 1-3:** "Ash" False-Color Composite at time $t$ (Current Frame).
    2.  **Channels 4-6:** Temporal Difference ($Ash_t - Ash_{t-1}$). Captures instantaneous motion/formation (Velocity) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    3.  **Channels 7-9:** Temporal Difference ($Ash_{t-1} - Ash_{t-2}$). Captures the history of motion (Acceleration).
    This expanded context helps the model distinguish between transient, growing contrails and consistent, moving cirrus clouds.
*   **Statistical Alignment:** I will apply **ImageNet-style normalization** (subtracting mean, dividing by standard deviation) to the entire 9-channel tensor. This ensures the input distribution aligns with the pretrained backbone's internal weights, which was critical for the success of the previous best model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Augmentation:** Strong geometric augmentations (ShiftScaleRotate, Flips) will be applied to prevent the higher-capacity ConvNeXt-Small backbone from overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

### Training
The training protocol is designed to support the heavier backbone without falling into the optimization traps identified in previous lessons:
*   **Optimization Budget:** I will train for **40 epochs** on the **full dataset** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011). I will explicitly manage the batch size (utilizing Gradient Accumulation if necessary) to ensure the total number of gradient updates is not reduced, avoiding under-optimization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Loss Function:** A **Hybrid Dice Loss + Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) Loss** will be used to prevent the model from optimizing for the background class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Precision:** I will strictly use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to reduce the memory footprint of the 9-channel ConvNeXt-Small model and accelerate training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Stabilization:**
    *   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing with `T_max=40` to maximize exploration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
    *   **Online Top-K Checkpointing:** I will maintain a priority queue of the **Top-5** checkpoints to conserve disk space (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
    *   **Convergence-Aware Averaging:** The final model will be an average of the Top-5 checkpoints, strictly restricted to the latter half of training (e.g., Epoch > 20) to ensure stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** The inference pipeline will include **Test Time Augmentation**. The model will predict on the original image and geometric transformations (flips), and the results will be averaged to marginalize over prediction variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
