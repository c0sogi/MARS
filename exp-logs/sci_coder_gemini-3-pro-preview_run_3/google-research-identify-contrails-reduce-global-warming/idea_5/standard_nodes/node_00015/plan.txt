The strategy for this iteration focuses on scaling up the model capacity and improving inference robustness, building on the success of the ConvNeXt architecture (Cite Lesson 14).

1.  **Backbone Upgrade**: I will upgrade the backbone from `convnext_tiny` to `convnext_small`. The "small" variant offers significantly more parameters (~50M vs ~28M) and deeper feature extraction capabilities, which should help in resolving the subtle, thin structures of contrails that lighter models might miss.
2.  **Extended Training**: To accommodate the slower convergence rate of the larger backbone and avoid under-fitting (Cite Lesson 2, Lesson 9), I will increase the training duration from 40 to 48 epochs. This ensures the optimizer has sufficient steps to refine the weights of the deeper network.
3.  **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**: I will implement a 3-view Test-Time Augmentation (Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip) during the final validation and submission phases. By averaging predictions across these views, the model can smooth out equivariant errors and exploit the rotation invariance of the satellite imagery, providing a "free" boost to the Dice score without retraining.
