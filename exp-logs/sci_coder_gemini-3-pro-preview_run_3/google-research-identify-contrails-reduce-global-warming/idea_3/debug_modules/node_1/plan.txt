The error observed in the execution traceback (`AssertionError: Unexpected mask shape: torch.Size([256, 256, 1])`) is caused by the `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2` transform in `library/dataset.py`. By default, `ToTensorV2` does not transpose masks, leaving them in the channel-last format `(H, W, C)` (specifically `256x256x1` in this case), whereas PyTorch models and the subsequent assertion expect the channel-first format `(C, H, W)` (i.e., `1x256x256`).

To resolve this, I will explicitly set `transpose_mask=True` when initializing `ToTensorV2` in the dataset's transform composition. This ensures that the mask dimensions are permuted correctly to match the expected tensor layout Cite debug_lesson_2.
