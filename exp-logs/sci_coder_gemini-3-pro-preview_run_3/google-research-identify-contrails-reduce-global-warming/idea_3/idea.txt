# Deeply Nested U-Net++ with EfficientNet-B4 and Convergence-Aware Ensembling

### Model
I propose replacing the standard U-Net architecture with a **U-Net++ architecture utilizing an EfficientNet-B4 backbone**.
*   **Structural Innovation (U-Net++):** Unlike the standard U-Net used in previous iterations, U-Net++ introduces nested, dense skip pathways between the encoder and decoder sub-networks. These nested pathways reduce the semantic gap between the feature maps of the encoder and decoder, which is critical for preserving the high-frequency spatial information required to segment thin, linear structures like contrails.
*   **Backbone Upgrade:** I will scale the encoder from EfficientNet-B0 to **EfficientNet-B4**. Previous experiments demonstrated that adapting pretrained backbones significantly outperforms custom architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008) and that increased capacity combined with augmentation drives performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002). The B4 variant offers a larger receptive field and richer feature extraction capabilities than B0, allowing the model to better distinguish contrails from complex cloud backgrounds.
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer of the EfficientNet-B4 encoder will be structurally modified to accept **6 input channels**, enabling the direct ingestion of the physics-informed tensor.

### Data
I will strictly retain the high-performing feature engineering and augmentation pipeline:
*   **Physics-Informed Input:** The model will be trained on the **6-channel tensor** comprising the "Ash" false-color composite (Bands 11, 13, 14, 15) and the temporal difference ($Ash_t - Ash_{t-1}$). This explicitly encodes the physical properties (optical depth, temperature) and temporal dynamics required for the task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Augmentation:** To prevent the higher-capacity EfficientNet-B4 model from overfitting, I will apply the strong geometric augmentation suite (ShiftScaleRotate, Horizontal/Vertical Flips) validated in previous experiments (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

### Training
The training protocol is designed to maximize convergence stability and exploit the benefits of the heavier architecture:
*   **Extended Schedule with Cosine Annealing:** The model will be trained for **35 epochs** using the **AdamW** optimizer and a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. Extending the schedule allows the optimizer to explore the loss landscape more thoroughly and prevents premature convergence to suboptimal local minima, a behavior observed when `T_max` is too short (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Online Top-K Checkpointing:** I will implement the resource-efficient **Online Top-K Checkpointing** strategy to maintain a priority queue of the best 5 models during training, deleting inferior checkpoints on the fly to conserve disk space (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Convergence-Aware Weight Averaging:** To address the issue of corrupting the model with under-trained weights, I will refine the averaging strategy. While I will save the Top-5 checkpoints, the final weight averaging will strictly enforce a **Convergence Constraint**: only checkpoints saved *after* the first 50% of the training epochs (e.g., Epoch > 17) will be eligible for averaging. This ensures that we only ensemble models residing within the final basin of attraction, preventing the feature dilution caused by mixing early-stage and late-stage weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Type-Safe Implementation:** The averaging function will explicitly filter for floating-point parameters to protect integer buffers like Batch Normalization statistics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** The test set will be processed into the standard 6-channel format.
*   **Prediction:** The final **Convergence-Aware Averaged Model** will be used to generate probability maps.
*   **Post-Processing:** A sigmoid activation followed by a threshold of 0.5 will produce the binary masks, which will be converted to Run-Length Encoding (RLE) for submission.
