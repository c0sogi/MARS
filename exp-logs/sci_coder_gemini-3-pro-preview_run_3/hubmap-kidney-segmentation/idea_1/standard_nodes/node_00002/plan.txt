The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU VRAM was exhausted during the training process, specifically within the FPN decoder's upsampling operations. This is caused by the combination of a large `TILE_SIZE` (1024) and `BATCH_SIZE` (16), which creates large intermediate tensors that exceed the 16GB GPU capacity.

To fix this, I will:
1.  **Reduce Memory Footprint**: Update `library/config.py` to lower `TILE_SIZE` to 768 and `BATCH_SIZE` to 8. This significantly reduces the memory required for activations and gradients.
2.  **Manage Memory Fragmentation**: Update `library/inference.py` and `library/train.py` to explicitly call `torch.cuda.empty_cache()` at strategic points (after inference on a slide and after each training epoch) to release unused cached memory back to the allocator.
