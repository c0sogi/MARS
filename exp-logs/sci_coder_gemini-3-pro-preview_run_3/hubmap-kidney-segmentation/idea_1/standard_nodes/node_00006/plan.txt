The `CUDA out of memory` error is caused by the `BATCH_SIZE` (16) and `TILE_SIZE` (1024) being too large for the available GPU memory, leading to a ~1GB allocation attempt for a single feature map during the forward pass. To fix this, I will reduce `BATCH_SIZE` to 8 and `TILE_SIZE` to 512 in `library/config.py`. Additionally, to address the issue of stale modules in the persistent environment preventing configuration changes from taking effect (Cite debug_lesson_1), I will modify `runfile.py` to explicitly reload the `library` modules before importing them.
