The issue is that the final validation metric is being calculated by averaging the Dice scores of individual image tiles (excluding background regions) from a subsampled dataset, rather than evaluating the Dice score on the fully reconstructed validation images. This introduces bias and does not represent the true performance of the model on the task.

To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to:
1.  **Remove Validation Subsampling**: Ensure the validation process considers the entire validation set.
2.  **Implement Full Image Reconstruction**: Replace the tile-based validation loop with an image-based inference loop using `predict_slide` (imported from `library.inference`). This function stitches predicted tiles back into a full-resolution mask.
3.  **Compute Image-Level Dice**: Calculate the Dice coefficient between the reconstructed prediction mask and the full ground truth mask for each validation image, then average these scores to produce the final metric.

I will also update the imports to include `predict_slide` and `rle_decode`.
