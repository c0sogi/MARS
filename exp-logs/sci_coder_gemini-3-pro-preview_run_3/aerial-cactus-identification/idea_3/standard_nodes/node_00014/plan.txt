Based on the lessons learned and the analysis of the previous solution, I will implement the following optimization strategy:

1.  **Enhanced Data Augmentation**: I will increase the `RandomRotation` range from 15 degrees to 180 degrees in `library/data_loader.py`. Aerial imagery possesses full rotational invariance, and restricting rotation limits the model's ability to learn orientation-independent features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00013).
2.  **Optimizer Switch**: I will replace `Adam` with `AdamW` in `runfile.py`. AdamW decouples weight decay from the gradient update, often leading to better generalization performance for deep models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013). Correspondingly, I will increase `WEIGHT_DECAY` in `library/config.py` to `0.01`, a standard value for AdamW.
3.  **Extended Training Schedule**: I will increase `RUN_EPOCHS` to 75 in `runfile.py` and `EARLY_STOPPING_PATIENCE` to 25 in `library/config.py`. Strong regularization techniques like Mixup slow down convergence, requiring more epochs to reach optimal performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
4.  **Threshold Update**: I will update the submission threshold in `runfile.py` to `0.9999694825433514` to ensure we only submit if we beat the current best ensemble.
