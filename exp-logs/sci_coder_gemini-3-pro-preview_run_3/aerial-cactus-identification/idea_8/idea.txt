Based on the empirical lessons learned, I propose a **Stem-Adapted Pretrained Heterogeneous Stacking Ensemble**.

This strategy fundamentally resolves the conflict between "training from scratch for low resolution" (Idea 7) and "transfer learning for robustness" (Idea 3/5). It leverages the superior generalization of ImageNet-pretrained weights `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026` while structurally correcting the resolution mismatch that caused previous architectures to fail `Cite solution_lesson_node_00027`.

# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** of three distinct architectures. Unlike previous iterations that used "CIFAR-scale" models trained from scratch, this approach uses **ImageNet-pretrained backbones** adapted via a specific "Stem Surgery" protocol to handle 32x32 inputs without information loss.

1.  **Base Architectures (The "Adapted Triad"):**
    *   **SE-ResNeXt50:** Chosen for its high capacity and channel-wise attention, which was identified as a key component of the best-performing solutions `Cite solution_lesson_node_00027`.
    *   **DenseNet121:** Chosen for its feature reuse and proven stability under stem modification `Cite solution_lesson_node_00027`.
    *   **ResNet34:** Chosen as a lighter, highly stable residual anchor that complements the heavier models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00027`.
    *   *Exclusion:* We explicitly exclude EfficientNet, as it was proven brittle to stem modification `Cite solution_lesson_node_00027`.

2.  **Stem Surgery & Initialization Protocol:**
    *   For all three models, we will replace the standard ImageNet "stem" (7x7 convolution, stride 2, followed by max pooling) with a **single 3x3 convolution with stride 1**. This preserves the full 32x32 spatial resolution `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.
    *   **Crucial Innovation:** We will **randomly initialize** this new stride-1 stem layer, while loading **pretrained ImageNet weights** for all subsequent layers. This avoids the distribution shift caused by forcing stride-2 weights into a stride-1 context `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`, while still retaining the robust feature extractors learned from massive data `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.

3.  **Stacking Meta-Learner:**
    *   Predictions will be fused using a **Logistic Regression Meta-Learner** trained on **Out-Of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation. This allows the ensemble to learn optimal weights based on validated reliability `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`.

# Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   Images will be converted to floating-point tensors and normalized to [0, 1].
    *   Data will be split into **5 Stratified Folds** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`.

2.  **Augmentation Strategy:**
    *   **Geometric (Lossless):** We will strictly limit geometric transformations to **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**. We will exclude affine transformations (rotation/shear) to avoid interpolation artifacts that degrade 32x32 images `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.
    *   **Photometric:** We will apply **ColorJitter** (brightness and contrast) to resolve the model's sensitivity to lighting variations `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
    *   **Regularization:** We will apply **Mixup augmentation** ($\alpha=1.0$) to the training batches. This encourages the high-capacity pretrained models to adapt smoothly to the new domain without overfitting `Cite solution_lesson_node_00010`.

# Training
1.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with weight decay to ensure stable regularization during the fine-tuning of pretrained weights `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.
    *   **Hardware Optimization:** We will explicitly set `torch.backends.cudnn.benchmark = True` to maximize training throughput for the fixed 32x32 input size `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.

2.  **Procedure:**
    *   We will train 15 models total (3 architectures $\times$ 5 folds).
    *   **Convergence Management:** We will employ a **relaxed Early Stopping patience** (e.g., 20+ epochs) to allow sufficient time for the models to converge on the Mixup-interpolated objective `Cite solution_lesson_node_00011`.
    *   **Checkpointing:** We will use **`copy.deepcopy(model.state_dict())`** when saving the best model weights to ensure the stored state is immutable `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference (Base Models):**
    *   We will employ **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** using the original, horizontally flipped, and vertically flipped versions of each test image. These predictions will be averaged to enforce rotational invariance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.

2.  **Fusion (Stacking):**
    *   The OOF predictions generated during the training phase will be used to train the Logistic Regression meta-learner.
    *   The TTA-averaged test predictions from all 15 base models will be fed into this meta-learner to produce the final `has_cactus` probability.
