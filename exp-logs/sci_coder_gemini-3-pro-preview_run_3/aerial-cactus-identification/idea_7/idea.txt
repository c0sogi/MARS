Based on the empirical lessons learned, specifically the superiority of shallow, wide architectures for low-resolution data (`solution_lesson_node_00024`) and the instability of adapting pre-trained weights to modified stems (`solution_lesson_node_00023`), I propose a **Lightweight Heterogeneous Triad with Stacking**.

This strategy evolves the previous ensemble approach by strictly replacing "ImageNet-scale" backbones (which are overly deep and inefficient for 32x32 images) with "CIFAR-scale" custom architectures. It also explicitly rejects the Transformer architecture due to its proven instability on this dataset (`solution_lesson_node_00022`).

### Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Stacking Ensemble** consisting of three architecturally distinct base models. Each model represents a specific inductive bias (Residual, Concatenative, and Inverted Residual) but is structurally optimized for the 32x32 resolution.

1.  **Base Architectures (The "Lightweight Triad"):**
    *   **Custom Wide SE-ResNet:** A shallow but wide Residual Network (e.g., Depth 16, Widen Factor 4) augmented with Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks. This replaces deep ResNets/ResNeXts, matching model complexity to the low information density of the images `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.
    *   **Custom DenseNet-BC:** A compact Densely Connected Network (e.g., Growth Rate 12, Depth 40). This maximizes feature reuse and gradient flow without the parameter bloat of standard DenseNet-121 `Cite solution_lesson_node_00012`.
    *   **Modified EfficientNet-B0:** Utilizes Inverted Residual blocks (MBConv). Crucially, the initial "stem" (downsampling layer) will be replaced with a **single 3x3 convolution with stride 1** to preserve spatial resolution `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.
    *   **Initialization Strategy:** For the Modified EfficientNet, the new stride-1 stem will be **randomly initialized** rather than copying weights from a pre-trained stride-2 stem. This prevents the distribution shift that caused convergence failure in previous attempts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.

2.  **Stacking Meta-Learner:**
    *   The predictions from these three models will be fused using a **Logistic Regression Meta-Learner**. This learner will be trained on **Out-Of-Fold (OOF)** predictions generated via 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation, allowing the ensemble to learn optimal weights based on validation reliability `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   Images will be converted to floating-point tensors and normalized to the range [0, 1].
    *   The dataset will be split into **5 Stratified Folds** to ensure consistent class distribution across training and validation.

2.  **Augmentation Strategy:**
    *   **Geometric (Lossless):** We will strictly limit geometric transformations to **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**. We will exclude affine transformations (rotation/shear) to avoid interpolation artifacts that degrade 32x32 images `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.
    *   **Photometric:** We will apply **ColorJitter** (brightness and contrast) to resolve the model's proven sensitivity to lighting variations `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
    *   **Regularization:** We will apply **Mixup augmentation** ($\alpha=1.0$) to the training batches. This encourages the models to learn smoother decision boundaries and prevents overfitting `Cite solution_lesson_node_00010`.

### Training
1.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with weight decay to ensure stable regularization during the late stages of convergence `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.
    *   **Hardware Optimization:** We will explicitly set `torch.backends.cudnn.benchmark = True`. Since input sizes are fixed at 32x32, this enables the cuDNN autotuner to select the most efficient convolution kernels `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.

2.  **Procedure:**
    *   We will train 15 models total (3 architectures $\times$ 5 folds).
    *   **Convergence Management:** Due to the use of Mixup, we will employ a relaxed **Early Stopping** patience (e.g., 20+ epochs) to allow sufficient time for the models to converge on the interpolated objective `Cite solution_lesson_node_00011`.
    *   **Checkpointing:** We will use **`copy.deepcopy(model.state_dict())`** when saving the best model weights to ensure the stored state is immutable and not overwritten by subsequent optimizer steps `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference (Base Models):**
    *   For the Test Set, we will employ **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Predictions will be generated for the original, horizontally flipped, and vertically flipped versions of each image, then averaged. This enforces the rotational invariance required for aerial imagery `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.

2.  **Fusion (Stacking):**
    *   The OOF predictions generated during the training phase will be used to train the Logistic Regression meta-learner.
    *   The TTA-averaged test predictions from all 15 base models will be fed into this meta-learner to produce the final `has_cactus` probability. This ensures the ensemble optimally weights the contribution of the Residual, Dense, and Inverted Residual features.
