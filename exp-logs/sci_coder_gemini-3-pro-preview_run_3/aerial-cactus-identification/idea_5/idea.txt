Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous ideas and the empirical lessons learned, I propose the following optimized strategy. This approach represents a **Triple-Architecture Heterogeneous Stacking Ensemble**, evolving the previous dual-model approach by incorporating a third orthogonal architectural paradigm (Inverted Residuals) and refining the training pipeline with superior optimization and regularization techniques.

# Model
To maximize the Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC), I propose a **Stacked Heterogeneous Ensemble** composed of three distinct base architectures. This design leverages "orthogonal inductive biases"—combining models that process features in fundamentally different ways to ensure their errors are uncorrelated `Cite solution_lesson_node_00012`.

1.  **Base Architectures (The "Orthogonal Triad"):**
    *   **Modified Wide SE-ResNet:** Focuses on residual learning with channel-wise attention (Squeeze-and-Excitation).
    *   **Modified DenseNet-BC:** Focuses on feature reuse and gradient flow via dense concatenations.
    *   **Modified EfficientNet-B0 (New):** Introduces the **Inverted Residual** block (MBConv) structure, which expands features into high-dimensional space before filtering. This adds a third, distinct feature extraction paradigm to the ensemble.
    *   **Crucial Adaptation:** For *all three* architectures, the standard "stem" (initial downsampling layers) will be replaced with a **single 3x3 convolution with stride 1**. This preserves the full 32x32 spatial resolution of the input, preventing the premature loss of fine-grained details typical in standard ImageNet backbones `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.

2.  **Stacking Meta-Learner:**
    *   Instead of simple averaging (which risks dilution), predictions will be fused using a **Logistic Regression Meta-Learner**. This learner will be trained on the **Out-Of-Fold (OOF)** predictions generated via 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`.
    *   **Greedy Forward Selection:** To strictly adhere to the lesson that "quality trumps quantity," we will validate that adding the EfficientNet component improves the OOF AUC before including it in the final stack, preventing the "ensemble dilution" observed with weaker models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00013`.

# Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   Images will be converted to floating-point tensors and normalized to the [0, 1] range.
    *   Data will be split into **5 Stratified Folds** to ensure consistent class distribution.

2.  **Augmentation Strategy:**
    *   **Geometric (Lossless Only):** We will strictly limit geometric transformations to **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**. We will **exclude** affine transformations like rotations or shears, as they introduce interpolation artifacts that degrade performance on low-resolution (32x32) images `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.
    *   **Photometric:** We will apply **ColorJitter** (brightness and contrast) to resolve the model's sensitivity to lighting variations identified in failure analyses `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
    *   **Regularization:** We will apply **Mixup augmentation** ($\alpha=1.0$) to the training batches. This encourages the models to learn smoother decision boundaries and prevents overfitting despite their high capacity `Cite solution_lesson_node_00010`.

# Training
1.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** We will switch to **AdamW** (with weight decay) instead of Adam. This decouples weight decay from gradient updates, providing more stable regularization for models converging near metric saturation `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.
    *   **Hardware Optimization:** We will explicitly set `torch.backends.cudnn.benchmark = True`. Since input sizes are fixed (32x32), this enables the cuDNN autotuner to select the most efficient convolution kernels, improving training throughput and numerical precision `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.

2.  **Procedure:**
    *   Each of the 15 models (3 architectures $\times$ 5 folds) will be trained independently.
    *   **Extended Convergence:** Due to the use of Mixup and AdamW, we will significantly increase the **patience** for early stopping (e.g., to 30+ epochs) and the total epoch limit. This ensures the models have sufficient time to converge on the complex interpolated objective `Cite solution_lesson_node_00011`.
    *   **Checkpointing:** We will use **`copy.deepcopy(model.state_dict())`** when saving the best model weights to ensure the stored state is immutable and not overwritten by subsequent optimizer steps `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference (Base Models):**
    *   For the Test Set, we will employ **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Predictions will be generated for the original, horizontally flipped, and vertically flipped versions of each image, then averaged. This enforces the rotational invariance required for aerial imagery `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.

2.  **Fusion (Stacking):**
    *   The OOF predictions from the training phase will be used to train the Logistic Regression meta-learner.
    *   The TTA-averaged test predictions from all base models will be fed into this meta-learner to produce the final probability. This ensures the ensemble optimally weights the contribution of the Residual, Dense, and Inverted Residual features based on their proven reliability.
