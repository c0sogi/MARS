To fix this:
1.  In `library/feature_extraction.py`, I will explicitly pass `format='aims'` to the `read()` function to correctly parse the geometry files (Cite debug_lesson_3).
2.  In `library/model_handler.py`, I will move the `early_stopping_rounds` parameter from the `.fit()` method to the `XGBRegressor` constructor in `__init__` to comply with the modern XGBoost API.
