Based on the success of **Bond Valence Sums (BVS)** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00060}) and **Distributional Aggregation** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00054}), I propose a **Vector-Valence & Weighted-Topology Fingerprinting** strategy.

This approach addresses the primary limitation of the scalar BVS descriptor: it captures chemical bond saturation but ignores **directional symmetry breaking** (e.g., off-center distortions characteristic of Ga/In oxides). By calculating the **Bond Valence Vector Sum (BVVS)**, we can explicitly quantify these local dipoles. Furthermore, to resolve the "noise" issue with bond angles (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}), this strategy introduces **Valence-Weighted Angular Fingerprints**, where bond angles are weighted by the strength of their constituent bonds before aggregation, ensuring the model focuses on the electronically significant framework rather than weak steric contacts.

# Model
-   **Architecture**: **XGBoost Regressor** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}). Tree ensembles remain the superior architecture for this dataset given the tabular nature of the engineered features.
-   **Input Features**: A concatenated feature vector representing the material's stability, symmetry, and topology:
    1.  **Global Stability**: **Global Instability Index (GII)** (RMS of BVS strain) and explicit Physical Descriptors (Volume, Density).
    2.  **Chemo-Symmetry Fingerprints**: Chemically resolved percentiles of **Scalar BVS** (Valence) and **Vector BVVS** (Distortion).
    3.  **Weighted Topological Fingerprints**: Percentiles of **Valence-Weighted Bond Angles**.
    4.  **Geometric Fingerprints**: Element-resolved Radial Distribution Functions (RDF) and Effective Coordination Numbers (ECoN).
    5.  **Tabular Metadata**: Atomic composition and Spacegroup (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00058}).

# Data
1.  **Geometric Feature Extraction (Raw Geometry)**:
    -   Load crystal structures from raw `.xyz` files using the `ase` library (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00018}).
    -   **Bond Valence Analysis**:
        -   Calculate bond valences $s_{ij} = \exp((R_{0} - d_{ij}) / 0.37)$ for all Metal-Oxygen pairs using standard $R_0$ parameters.
        -   **Scalar BVS**: $V_i = \sum_j s_{ij}$.
        -   **Vector BVVS**: $\vec{V}_i = \sum_j s_{ij} \hat{r}_{ij}$. Calculate the magnitude $|\vec{V}_i|$ to quantify directional distortion.
        -   **GII**: Calculate the root-mean-square difference between calculated $V_i$ and ideal oxidation states (+3 for Al/Ga/In, -2 for O) across the unit cell.
    -   **Weighted Topology**:
        -   Calculate bond angles $\theta_{ijk}$ for all Metal-Oxygen-Metal and Oxygen-Metal-Oxygen triplets.
        -   Weight each angle by the product of the bond valences: $w_{ijk} = s_{ij} \cdot s_{jk}$.
        -   Construct a distribution of these weighted angles and compute **Percentiles** (Min, 25%, 50%, 75%, Max). This filters out geometric noise from weak interactions.
    -   **Geometric Baseline**:
        -   Compute **Effective Coordination Number (ECoN)** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00057}).
        -   Compute **Element-Resolved RDFs** normalized by atom count (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00033}).
    -   **Aggregation**: Group atom-level metrics (BVS, BVVS, ECoN) by element type and compute **Percentiles** to capture the distribution of local states (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00054}).

2.  **Preprocessing**:
    -   **Target Transformation**: Apply the natural logarithm transformation $z = \log(1 + y)$ to the target variables (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}).
    -   **Feature Cleaning**: Drop constant columns to prevent feature dilution (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}).

# Training
-   **Algorithm**: Train separate XGBoost regressors for formation energy and bandgap energy.
-   **Optimization Strategy**:
    -   **Shrinkage**: Use a **low learning rate** (e.g., $\eta \approx 0.01$) and a **high number of estimators** (e.g., 3,000) to maximize generalization (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00004}).
    -   **Stochastic Subsampling**: Set row (`subsample`) and column (`colsample_bytree`) subsampling to $\approx 0.6 - 0.7$. This forces the model to utilize the diverse structural views (Vector vs. Scalar vs. Radial) rather than overfitting to specific features (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00004}).
-   **Loss Function**: Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the log-transformed targets.

# Evaluation
-   **Inference**:
    1.  Process the test set `.xyz` files to generate the Vector-Valence and Weighted-Topology features.
    2.  Generate predictions in the log-transformed space using the trained XGBoost models.
    3.  Apply the inverse transformation $\hat{y} = \exp(z) - 1$ to obtain the final energy predictions.
