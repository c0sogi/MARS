Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Hybrid Geometric-Sublattice Fingerprinting** strategy.

This approach synthesizes the strengths of the most successful explicit descriptors (RDF and Local Moments) with the power of pre-trained deep learning, while specifically addressing the "feature dilution" and "noise" issues that caused previous hybrid and GNN-based attempts to fail. The core innovation is **Sublattice-Resolved Embedding Pooling**, which captures the distinct electronic environments of the Anion (Oxygen) and Cation (Metal) sublattices without generating the sparse, missing-value features that degraded tree-model performance in prior element-wise schemes.

# Model
-   **Architecture**: **XGBoost Regressor** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003). This remains the most robust predictor for this dataset.
-   **Input Features**: A "Multi-View" feature vector constructed by concatenating:
    1.  **Explicit Physical Descriptors**: Unit Cell Volume and Mass Density.
    2.  **Explicit Structural Fingerprints**: 
        *   **RDF**: Element-resolved pairwise distance histograms.
        *   **CR-LEM**: Chemically-Resolved Local Environment Moments (Coordination/Angle statistics).
    3.  **Implicit Sublattice Fingerprints (The Innovation)**: Dense statistical summaries of pre-trained GNN node embeddings, aggregated specifically by **Sublattice** (Anion vs. Cation) rather than by individual element or globally.
    4.  **Tabular Metadata**: Atomic composition and Spacegroup.

# Data
1.  **Explicit Feature Extraction (Raw Geometry)**:
    -   Load crystal structures from raw `.xyz` files using `ase`.
    -   **Physical Descriptors**: Calculate **Unit Cell Volume** and **Mass Density** directly from the atomic objects to ensure fidelity to the simulation constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
    -   **Radial Features (RDF)**: Compute element-resolved Radial Distribution Functions using a moderate cutoff (e.g., 6.0 Å). Normalize by **atom count** to decouple topology from density (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    -   **Local Environment Moments (CR-LEM)**: Calculate the **Mean** and **Standard Deviation** of the **Coordination Number** and **Bond Angle Variance** for each element type using a fixed radial cutoff (e.g., 3.0 Å). This captures 3-body geometric constraints robustly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037, solution_lesson_node_00042).

2.  **Implicit Feature Extraction (Sublattice Pooling)**:
    -   Convert structures into graphs and pass them through a **Pre-trained MatGL (M3GNet)** model to obtain node-level embeddings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    -   **Sublattice Aggregation**: Instead of global pooling (too vague) or element-wise pooling (too sparse/diluted, Cite solution_lesson_node_00016), partition the node embeddings into two groups:
        1.  **Anion Sublattice (Oxygen)**: Compute Mean and Std Dev of embeddings for all O atoms. This captures the "view from the anion" of the local disorder.
        2.  **Cation Sublattice (Metals)**: Compute Mean and Std Dev of embeddings for all Al/Ga/In atoms combined.
    -   This results in a dense, fixed-size, chemically meaningful representation of the electronic environment that is never missing (since all crystals have O and Metals).

3.  **Preprocessing**:
    -   **Target Transformation**: Apply $z = \log(1 + y)$ to the targets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    -   **Feature Cleaning**: Drop constant columns.

# Training
-   **Algorithm**: Train separate XGBoost regressors for formation energy and bandgap energy.
-   **Optimization Strategy**:
    -   **Shrinkage**: Use a low learning rate ($\eta \approx 0.01$) and high number of estimators ($n \approx 3000$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    -   **Stochastic Subsampling**: Set `subsample` and `colsample_bytree` to $\approx 0.6 - 0.7$. This forces the model to utilize the diverse feature sets (Geometric vs. Sublattice) without overfitting to the high-dimensional embeddings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
-   **Loss Function**: Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the log-transformed targets.

# Evaluation
-   **Inference**:
    1.  Process test set `.xyz` files to generate Physical, RDF, CR-LEM, and Sublattice Embedding features.
    2.  Predict log-scale values using the trained XGBoost models.
    3.  Apply the inverse transformation $\hat{y} = \exp(z) - 1$ to obtain final energy predictions.
