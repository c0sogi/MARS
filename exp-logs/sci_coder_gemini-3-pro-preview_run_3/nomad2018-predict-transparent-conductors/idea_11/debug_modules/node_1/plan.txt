The execution failed because `ase.io.read` attempted to parse the geometry files as standard XYZ format (due to the `.xyz` extension), but the files actually contain FHI-aims formatted data with comment headers, causing a `ValueError`. To fix this, I will explicitly specify `format='aims'` in the `ase.io.read` call (Cite debug_lesson_3). Additionally, I will address a known incompatibility with XGBoost v1.6+ by moving the `early_stopping_rounds` parameter from the `fit` method to the `XGBRegressor` constructor in `library/model_wrapper.py` (Cite debug_lesson_1).
