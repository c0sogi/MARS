The execution failure was caused by the model checkpoint not being saved during the training loop. Specifically, the variable `best_acc` was initialized to `0.0`, and since the validation accuracy in the demo run was also `0.0`, the condition `val_acc > best_acc` evaluated to `False`, skipping the save operation. This is a direct instance of the issue described in `debug_lesson_1`. To fix this, I will initialize `best_acc` to `-1.0` in both `library/trainer.py` and `library/model.py` (Cite debug_lesson_1), ensuring that the model is saved at least once even if the accuracy is zero.
