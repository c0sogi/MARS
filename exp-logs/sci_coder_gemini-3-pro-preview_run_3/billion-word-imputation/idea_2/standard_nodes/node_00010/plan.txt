The `InvalidMetricValueError` is caused by the grading system failing to parse the validation metric from the logs because it was printed too early. The subsequent submission generation process produces excessive logs (thousands of progress bar updates over 30 minutes), causing the metric output to be truncated from the log buffer. To fix this, I will reprint the `Final Validation Metric` at the very end of `runfile.py` to ensure it appears as the last output. Additionally, I will reduce the verbosity of the `tqdm` progress bar in `library/predictor.py` by setting a `mininterval`, preventing log buffer saturation.
