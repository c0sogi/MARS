The failure in `runfile.py` is caused by unintended broadcasting during the calculation of absolute error in the `analyze_failures` function. The model predictions `preds` have a shape of `(Batch_Size, 1)`, while the targets `targets` are flattened to `(Batch_Size,)`. Subtracting these tensors results in a matrix of shape `(Batch_Size, Batch_Size)` instead of an element-wise difference. This causes `np.concatenate` to fail when processing the final batch, which has a different size than the preceding batches (dimension mismatch on axis 1). The fix is to flatten `preds` to `(Batch_Size,)` using `.view(-1)` before subtraction to ensure element-wise operations.
