Based on the comprehensive analysis of the experimental history, specifically the critical failure of **Aggressive Bottlenecks** in gating (Lesson 110) and the dominance of **Backbone Capacity** (Lesson 103), I propose the following optimized strategy.

This strategy represents the **High-Capacity Wide-Gated Synthesis**. It rejects the compromise of reducing gate capacity to save parameters (which caused Idea 64 to fail) and rejects reducing backbone capacity to fit complex gates (which caused previous attempts to fail). Instead, it implements a **4-Layer High-Capacity Backbone** interleaved with **Wide Stabilized MLP Gates**, made trainable through rigorous normalization protocols.

# Proposed Strategy: High-Capacity Wide-Gated Decoupled BiGRU (HC-WG-BiGRU)

This strategy introduces a **4-Layer Bidirectional GRU** with a **High-Capacity Hidden Dimension** (384 per direction, 768 total). It interleaves this backbone with a **Wide Stabilized Decoupled Structural Injection** module. Unlike previous iterations that bottlenecked the gating MLP to reduce compute, this strategy maintains a wide hidden dimension within the gate to ensure the model has sufficient capacity to distinguish signal from noise in the sparse structural graph, while using **Internal Normalization** and **Post-Normalization** to ensure stability.

### Model

1.  **Input Representation:**
    *   **Tensor Shape:** $(N, 107, 14)$. Sequence dimension is preserved. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
    *   **Feature Channels:** Strictly **One-Hot Encoding** for nucleotide (4), secondary structure (3), and predicted loop type (7). No learned embeddings. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.
    *   **Purity:** No concatenation of paired features at the input. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.

2.  **Convolutional Stem:**
    *   **1D Convolutional Layer** (kernel size 3, filters 256, activation GELU).
    *   **Purpose:** Projects sparse inputs into a dense embedding space and aggregates local k-mers to initialize the backbone. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.

3.  **High-Capacity 4-Layer Backbone:**
    *   The backbone consists of **4 Blocks** (maximizing capacity, addressing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**). Each block contains a **Bidirectional GRU** followed by the **Structural Interaction Module** (except the final block).
    *   **BiGRU Layer:** Hidden dimension of **384** (Total 768). We explicitly maintain this high capacity, as narrowing the backbone caused severe degradation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00103** and **Cite solution_lesson_node_00063**.
    *   **Wide Decoupled Interaction Module (Structural Innovation):**
        *   **Context:** For every position $i$ paired with index $j$.
        *   **Gather:** Retrieve $h_j$ directly (Point-to-Point). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00058**.
        *   **Input Zero-Masking:** If $i$ is unpaired, explicitly force $h_j = 0$. Strictly avoid self-loops. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**.
        *   **Decoupled Message (Bias-Refined):** $m_{ij} = \text{GELU}(W_{msg} \cdot h_j + b_{msg})$. We do **not** concatenate $h_i$ here. Crucially, for unpaired bases ($h_j=0$), this term becomes the bias $b_{msg}$, serving as a learnable "loop embedding" to allow self-refinement. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00080** and **Cite solution_lesson_node_00085**.
        *   **Wide Stabilized MLP Gate:**
            *   **Wide Projection:** $z_{wide} = W_{in} \cdot [h_i; h_j]$. We project the 1536-dim context to a **Wide Hidden Dimension** (e.g., 768), explicitly avoiding the aggressive bottleneck (96-dim) that caused failure in Lesson 110. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00110**.
            *   **Internal Normalization:** $z_{norm} = \text{LayerNorm}(z_{wide})$. Stabilizes the MLP internals. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00075**.
            *   Activation: $z_{act} = \text{GELU}(z_{norm})$.
            *   **Output:** $logits = W_{out} \cdot z_{act}$. Projects to 768 dimensions for channel-wise gating.
            *   **No Logit Norm:** $g_{ij} = \sigma(logits)$. Allows full saturation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00078**.
        *   **Injection:** $h_{res} = h_i + g_{ij} \odot m_{ij}$.
        *   **Post-Normalization:** $h_{out} = \text{LayerNorm}(h_{res})$. Ensures stability for the deep 4-layer stack. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.

4.  **Output Head:**
    *   Linear projection to 5 target values.
