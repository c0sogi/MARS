The `InvalidMetricValueError` is caused by data leakage in the stacking ensemble's Level 2 training process. The Meta-Learner is currently trained on the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions for the entire dataset (which includes the validation set) and then evaluated on the validation subset of that same data. This allows the Meta-Learner to implicitly "see" the validation labels during training. To fix this, I will decouple the Meta-Learner's training data from the hold-out evaluation set (Cite debug_lesson_11) by modifying `train_meta_learner` to accept a mask that excludes validation samples, and updating the run script to generate and pass this mask.
