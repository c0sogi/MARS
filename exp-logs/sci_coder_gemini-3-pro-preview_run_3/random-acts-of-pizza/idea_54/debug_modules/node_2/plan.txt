This triggers NumPy's broadcasting logic, which fails due to shape mismatches (e.g., `(50, 384)` vs `(50, 8)`). To fix this, I will modify `library/feature_engineering.py` to explicitly convert all dense matrices to sparse CSR format before stacking them.
