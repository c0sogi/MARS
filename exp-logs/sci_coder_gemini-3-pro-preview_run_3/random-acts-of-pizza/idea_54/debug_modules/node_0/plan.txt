### Implementation Strategy

1.  **Configuration Patching**: We first import the `library.config` module and modify the hyperparameters of the models (e.g., reducing `n_estimators` to 10, setting `early_stopping_rounds` to 2) to ensure the script executes in a matter of seconds rather than hours. We also patch `N_FOLDS` in the `library.inference_engine` to match our demonstration setting of 2 folds.
2.  **Data Loading & Subsampling**: We use `library.data_loader` to load the full training and test sets. To demonstrate the pipeline quickly, we subset the data to 50 training samples and 20 test samples. We also apply the provided cleaning function.
3.  **Feature Engineering**: We instantiate `FeatureFactory` and fit it on the small training subset. We then generate feature matrices for both train and test sets, explicitly disabling cache loading (`load_cache=False`) to verify the feature generation logic runs correctly.
4.  **Training Pipeline**:
    *   **Level 1**: We use `HybridTrainer` to train the base learners using a 2-fold stratified split. This generates Out-of-Fold (OOF) predictions and saves the base models (both volatile fold-specific models and stable full-retrain models) to the working directory.
    *   **Level 2**: We train the meta-learner (Logistic Regression) on the OOF predictions generated in Level 1.
5.  **Inference Pipeline**: We use `HybridPredictor` to load the saved models and generate probability predictions for the test subset. We then generate a submission file.
6.  **Verification**: Throughout the script, assertions are used to validate data shapes, the existence of generated feature keys, and the creation of the final submission file.
