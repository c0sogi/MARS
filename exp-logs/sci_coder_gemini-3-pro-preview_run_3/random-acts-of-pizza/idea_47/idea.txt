Based on the comprehensive empirical analysis of the "Lessons Learned," particularly the critical discovery of **Target Leakage via Post-Hoc Edits** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062), the inadvertent pruning of valid **Domain-Specific History** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062), and the robustness of **CV-Bagging Inference** (implied by Cite solution_lesson_node_00036), I propose a **Restored-History Hex-View Stacking Ensemble with CV-Bagging**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Pent-View" architecture by:
1.  **Strictly Correcting Data Hygiene:** Using edit-aware text to eliminate leakage.
2.  **Restoring Valid Signals:** Explicitly re-introducing RAOP-specific interaction counters that were wrongly discarded in previous cleanup attempts.
3.  **Implementing CV-Bagging:** Replacing the fragile "Final Retraining" step with an ensemble-of-ensembles approach to resolve the conflict between early stopping and data utilization.

# Model

The architecture is a **Stacking Ensemble** composed of **six** "Level 1" base learners, organized into four topology-matched branches. These learners feed into a "Level 2" Logistic Regression meta-learner.

**Level 1: Base Learners**

1.  **Sparse Lexical Branch (Text Modality)**
    *   **Lexical Bagger:** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **TF-IDF vectors of the Concatenated Title and Edit-Aware Body** combined with the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Concatenating Title + Body maximizes signal (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044). We apply regularization (`min_samples_leaf=2`) to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

2.  **Sparse Behavioral Branch (History Modality)**
    *   **Community Bagger:** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **TF-IDF vectors of the Subreddit History** combined with the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* We treat user history as a "Bag-of-Concepts." We strictly limit the vocabulary to the **Top 1,000** subreddits to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042) and maintain the superiority of sparse representations for categorical sets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

3.  **Dense Semantic Branch (Text Modality)**
    *   **Semantic Booster (XGB):** An **XGBoost Classifier** trained on **Frozen Compact Embeddings (Title + Body)** combined with Metadata.
        *   *Retention:* Captures non-linear signals in the continuous semantic space (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018). We use `scale_pos_weight` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) and maintain high capacity (2,000 estimators).
    *   **Semantic Bagger (RF):** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **Frozen Compact Embeddings (Title + Body)** combined with Metadata.
        *   *Retention:* Provides structural diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025). We apply **Modality-Specific Regularization** (`max_depth=12`, `min_samples_leaf=4`) to prevent memorization of dense noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).

4.  **Contextual Branch (Metadata Modality)**
    *   **Metadata Anchor (Linear):** A **Logistic Regression** model trained on the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Acts as a high-bias regularizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
    *   **Temporal Booster (Non-Linear):** A **LightGBM Classifier** trained on the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Captures non-linear temporal drift via raw timestamps (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).

**Level 2: Meta-Learner**
*   **Logistic Regression:** Calibrates the ensemble weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

# Data

The data pipeline enforces strict leakage prevention, robust typing, and **Positive Feature Selection**.

1.  **Leakage Prevention:**
    *   **Source Text:** Use `request_text_edit_aware` exclusively. Do not touch `request_text` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).
    *   **Exclusion:** Drop all columns suffixed with `_at_retrieval`.
2.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Augmented Global Metadata:** Explicitly **Allow-List** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030) a curated set of dense features:
        *   **Raw Timestamp:** `unix_timestamp_of_request_utc` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
        *   **User Stats:** Account Age, Karma, Total Post Counts.
        *   **Restored RAOP History:** Explicitly include `requester_number_of_posts_on_raop_at_request`, `requester_number_of_comments_in_raop_at_request`, and `requester_days_since_first_post_on_raop_at_request`. These are valid pre-request signals of community participation (reciprocity) that were inadvertently pruned in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).
        *   **Exclusion:** Explicitly exclude derived text length features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
    *   **Text Concatenation:** Join `request_title` and `request_text_edit_aware` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
    *   **Sparse Features:** TF-IDF (Unigrams/Bigrams) with `sublinear_tf=True` and `min_df=5` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
    *   **Dense Features:** Frozen `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` Embeddings (384-dim) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
3.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Imputation:** Median imputation.
    *   **Scaling:** `StandardScaler` applied to dense inputs.
    *   **No Caching:** Recompute features on-the-fly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

# Training

The training procedure implements a **CV-Bagging Inference Protocol** to resolve the conflict between early stopping and data utilization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

1.  **Fold Generation:** 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation.
2.  **Ensemble Training (Per Fold):**
    *   Instead of training one final model per learner, we persist the models trained on **each of the 5 folds**.
    *   **XGBoost / LightGBM:** Train on the 4 training folds, using the **1 validation fold** for **Early Stopping**. This ensures every model is optimally tuned without "peeking" at a hold-out set or guessing iteration counts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
    *   **RF / Linear:** Train on the 4 training folds.
    *   **Meta-Learner:** Train on the OOF predictions.
3.  **No Blind Retraining:** We do *not* retrain a single model on the full dataset. This avoids the risk of underfitting (due to small nested validation sets) or overfitting (due to fixed iteration counts).

# Evaluation

1.  **Multi-Model Inference:** For each test sample, generate predictions using **all 5 saved fold-models** for each base learner.
2.  **Bagging:** Average the 5 predictions for each base learner. This effectively utilizes 100% of the training data for inference while maintaining rigorous early stopping for every component.
3.  **Stacking:** Feed the bagged probabilities to the Meta-Learner for the final prediction.
