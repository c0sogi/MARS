Cite debug_lesson_10. Additionally, when training without a validation set (e.g., during final retraining on the full dataset), we must explicitly ensure `early_stopping_rounds` is disabled (set to `None`) to avoid validation errors. Cite debug_lesson_14.

Instead of adding `early_stopping_rounds` to the `fit_params` dictionary passed to `fit()`, I will use `self.model.set_params()` to dynamically configure the parameter based on the presence of an `eval_set`.
