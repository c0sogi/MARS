The issue is a `TypeError` caused by passing `early_stopping_rounds` to the `fit` method of `XGBClassifier`. In the installed version of XGBoost (3.0.5), this parameter is no longer supported in `fit` and must be passed to the model constructor (`__init__`) instead.

I will fix this in `library/models.py` by:
1.  Modifying `DenseBooster.__init__` to stop removing `early_stopping_rounds` from `self.params`. This ensures it is passed to `XGBClassifier(**self.params)`.
2.  Modifying `DenseBooster.fit` to stop adding `early_stopping_rounds` to `fit_params`.
