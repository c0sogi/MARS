The issue is a case of **Data Leakage** in the stacking ensemble. The Level 2 Meta-Learner is currently trained on the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions and labels of the entire "Union Dataset," which includes the hold-out validation set. Consequently, the model "sees" the validation labels during training, making the subsequent evaluation on that same validation set invalid (as indicated by the `InvalidMetricValueError`).

To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to isolate the validation data. I will explicitly define the training indices and use them to slice the OOF predictions and target labels passed to the Meta-Learner's `fit` method. This ensures the Meta-Learner is trained solely on the training partition, while the validation partition remains unseen until the inference/evaluation step, complying with **debug_lesson_12**.
