The issue is that the pipeline performs Cross-Validation on the combined "Union Dataset" (Training + Validation) to generate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions, which are then used to evaluate the model on the Validation set. This constitutes data leakage because the "validation" OOF predictions, while technically held out from the specific fold that predicted them, are generated by models trained on other parts of the validation set. To fix this, I will decouple the validation and submission phases. Phase 1 will train models strictly on the Training set and evaluate on the Validation set (Cite debug_lesson_11). Phase 2 will retrain the entire pipeline on the Union Dataset to maximize data for the final submission, but only if the Phase 1 validation metric meets the threshold. I will also introduce dynamic cache naming to prevent collisions between these phases (Cite debug_lesson_7).
