Based on the comprehensive empirical analysis of the "Lessons Learned," particularly the breakthrough success of **Textual Field Concatenation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044), the superiority of **Sparse Representations** for categorical sets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029), and the need to propagate behavioral signals to dense learners without dimensionality issues (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028), I propose a **Pent-View Stacking Ensemble with Bayesian Community Profiling**.

This solution structurally evolves the robust "Pent-View" architecture (Idea 33) by introducing a **Bayesian Community Profiling** layer. This innovation transforms the high-dimensional sparse subreddit history into a dense, target-encoded scalar (the "Community Success Score"), allowing the dense semantic learners (XGBoost/RF) to access behavioral signals that were previously "siloed" in the sparse branch (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).

# Model

The architecture is a **Stacking Ensemble** composed of **five** "Level 1" base learners, organized into three topology-matched branches. These learners feed into a "Level 2" Logistic Regression meta-learner.

**Level 1: Base Learners**

1.  **Sparse Lexical Branch (Text Modality)**
    *   **Lexical Bagger:** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **TF-IDF vectors of the Concatenated Title and Body** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044) combined with the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Random Forests on sparse TF-IDF consistently outperform dense methods for capturing specific high-impact keywords (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013). We apply regularization (`min_samples_leaf=2`) to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

2.  **Sparse Behavioral Branch (History Modality)**
    *   **Community Bagger:** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **TF-IDF vectors of the Subreddit History** combined with the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* We treat user history as a "Bag-of-Concepts" document. We strictly limit the vocabulary to the **Top 1,000** subreddits to prevent overfitting to rare communities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).

3.  **Dense Semantic Branch (Text Modality)**
    *   **Semantic Booster:** An **XGBoost Classifier** trained on **Frozen Compact Embeddings (Title + Body)** combined with **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Innovation:* By including the **Community Success Score** (from Bayesian Profiling) in the metadata, this model can now learn non-linear interactions between the semantic tone of the request and the historical generosity of the user's communities (e.g., "Sad story + High-Risk Community = Low Probability").
        *   *Retention:* We use `scale_pos_weight` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) and maintain high capacity (2,000 estimators) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Semantic Bagger:** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **Frozen Compact Embeddings (Title + Body)** combined with **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Provides structural diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025). We apply **Modality-Specific Regularization** (`max_depth=12`, `min_samples_leaf=4`) to prevent memorization of dense noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).

4.  **Contextual Branch (Metadata Modality)**
    *   **Metadata Anchor:** A **Logistic Regression** model trained on the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Acts as a high-bias regularizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

**Level 2: Meta-Learner**
*   **Logistic Regression:** Calibrates the ensemble weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

# Data

The data pipeline enforces strict leakage prevention, robust typing, and **Positive Feature Selection**.

1.  **Leakage Prevention:**
    *   Source Text: Use `request_text_edit_aware` to remove post-hoc edits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   Exclusion: Drop all columns suffixed with `_at_retrieval`.
2.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Bayesian Community Profiling (Structural Innovation):** Implement **Target Encoding** for the `requester_subreddits_at_request` field.
        *   For each subreddit, calculate the probability of success ($P(Pizza|Subreddit)$) based on training data.
        *   For each user, compute the **Community Success Score** as the average probability of the subreddits they have posted in.
        *   *Note:* This must be computed inside the Cross-Validation loop (OOF) to prevent target leakage.
    *   **Augmented Global Metadata:** Explicitly **Allow-List** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030):
        *   **Raw Timestamp:** `unix_timestamp_of_request_utc` to capture temporal drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
        *   **User Stats:** Account Age, Karma, Post Counts.
        *   **Community Success Score:** The new dense behavioral feature.
        *   **Exclusion:** Explicitly exclude derived text length features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
    *   **Sparse Features:** TF-IDF (Unigrams/Bigrams) on Concatenated Text (Title+Body) with `sublinear_tf=True` and `min_df=5` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
    *   **Dense Features:** Frozen `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` Embeddings (384-dim).
3.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Imputation:** Median imputation.
    *   **Scaling:** `StandardScaler` applied to dense inputs.
    *   **No Caching:** Recompute features on-the-fly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

# Training

The training procedure implements the **Validation-Guided Retraining Protocol** with **Nested Target Encoding**.

1.  **Fold Generation:** 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation.
2.  **Level 1 Training (OOF Generation):**
    *   **Target Encoding:** For each fold, fit the Subreddit Target Encoder on the Training split and transform the Validation split. Handle unseen subreddits by defaulting to the global mean.
    *   **RF / Linear:** Train on 4 folds, predict on 5th. Use `class_weight='balanced'`.
    *   **XGBoost:** Train on 4 folds, predict on 5th. Use `scale_pos_weight`.
3.  **Level 2 Training:** Train Meta-Learner on the OOF matrix.
4.  **Final Retraining:**
    *   **Target Encoding:** Fit on the **Full Training Set**.
    *   **RF / Linear:** Retrain on the **Full Training Set** (Train + Validation) to maximize data usage (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
    *   **XGBoost:** Retrain on the **Training Set only**, explicitly passing the **Validation Set** as the `eval_set` to trigger **Early Stopping**. This prevents "blind overfitting" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).

# Evaluation

1.  Process the test set to generate all feature views.
2.  Apply the fitted Target Encoder to generate the `Community Success Score` for test samples.
3.  Pass specific views to the five base learners to obtain probability scores.
4.  Feed aggregated probabilities to the Meta-Learner for the final prediction.
