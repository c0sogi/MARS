The `ValueError: empty vocabulary` is caused by applying standard English stop-word removal to subreddit names, which are categorical identifiers rather than natural language sentences. This filters out valid subreddits that coincide with common English words (e.g., "funny", "news"), resulting in an empty vocabulary when the dataset is small (as in debug mode). To fix this, I will remove `stop_words="english"` from the `community_tfidf` vectorizer initialization in `library/features.py`, treating the subreddit list as a collection of tags without filtering Cite debug_lesson_2.
