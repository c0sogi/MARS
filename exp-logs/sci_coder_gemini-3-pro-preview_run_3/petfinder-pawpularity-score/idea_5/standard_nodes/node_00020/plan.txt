The implementation strategy follows a robust "Validate then Submit" workflow. First, we utilize the `FeatureExtractor` to process images through the frozen backbones (Swin, EfficientNet, DINOv2), leveraging caching to optimize runtime. We then perform a strict hold-out validation: we train the Stacking Ensemble (Level 1 Base Models + Level 2 Meta-Learner) on the training split and evaluate it on the validation split. The `CrossValidator` is used to generate unbiased Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions for the Meta-Learner training, while `IndependentPCA` ensures dimensionality reduction is fit solely on the training data to prevent leakage. After computing the RMSE and performing failure analysis (correlating errors with metadata), we check against the threshold. If successful, we merge the training and validation sets, re-run the cross-validation to update the meta-learner inputs, and use `FinalTrainer` to retrain the entire pipeline on the full dataset for the final submission.
