Given the high quality of the embeddings extracted from "Large" backbones (Swin-L, EffNet-L, DINOv2-L, CLIP-L), the feature space is robust enough to support a higher penalty parameter. I will increase `C` to `20.0` to reduce bias and allow the model to fit the complex manifold of "Pawpularity" more aggressively.
2.  **Enhance Ensemble Diversity (Cite Lesson 00011):** The `Ridge` regression model contributed negligible signal (Coefficient ~0.005) compared to SVR and LightGBM. I will replace it with an **ExtraTreesRegressor**. Extra Trees introduce a randomized tree-based inductive bias that is distinct from the gradient boosting of LightGBM and the kernel-based approach of SVR. Using `max_features="sqrt"` makes it particularly effective for the high-dimensional (approx. 2000 dim) concatenated feature space.
