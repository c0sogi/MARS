The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU VRAM was exhausted during the forward pass. This occurred because the `BATCH_SIZE` of 64 generated intermediate activation maps that exceeded the 16GB GPU capacity, particularly due to the Siamese architecture processing two image streams per sample. To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` from 64 to 32 in `library/config.py`. This reduction will decrease the memory required for activations, ensuring the training process fits within the hardware limits (Cite debug_lesson_1).
