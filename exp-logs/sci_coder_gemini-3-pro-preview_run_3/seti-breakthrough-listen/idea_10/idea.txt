Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, specifically the failure of the "modernized" Idea 9 due to distribution shifts and backbone fragility, and the success of the robust baseline in Idea 8, I propose the **Siamese EfficientNet-B0 with Adaptive Difference and GeM Pooling**.

This solution structurally evolves the successful Idea 8 by integrating the advanced feature interaction mechanisms (Adaptive Difference and GeM Pooling) that were theoretically sound in Idea 9 but failed due to confounding implementation errors (ImageNet normalization and fragile backbone). By applying these innovations to the **robust EfficientNet-B0 backbone** with **raw data**, we can isolate the benefits of the improved architecture.

### Model
**Architecture: Siamese EfficientNet-B0 with Adaptive Scaling and GeM Pooling**

1.  **Backbone:** We will revert to the **EfficientNet-B0** backbone initialized with ImageNet weights. This model uses Batch Normalization, which has proven robust to the raw spectrogram statistics, unlike the LayerNorm-based ConvNeXt or EfficientNet-V2 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00030`, `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
    *   **Dual Inputs:** The model processes two streams via shared weights:
        *   **Stream A (Signal):** "On-Target" frames (Indices 0, 2, 4) stacked depth-wise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00003`).
        *   **Stream B (Reference):** "Off-Target" frames (Indices 1, 3, 5) stacked depth-wise.
    *   **Deep Feature Extraction:** Features are extracted from the final convolutional stage (Stride 32) to ensure semantic abstraction (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00026`).

2.  **Adaptive Feature Difference Module:**
    *   Standard subtraction ($F_{on} - F_{off}$) assumes the noise floor is identical in both observations. To account for non-stationary RFI intensity, we introduce a **Learnable Channel Scaling Vector** $w$ of shape $(1, C, 1, 1)$.
    *   We compute the difference as $F_{diff} = F_{on} - (w \odot F_{off})$. This allows the network to adaptively normalize the "Off" features to match the "On" noise profile before subtraction (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).

3.  **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling Head:**
    *   We replace the fixed GAP+GMP strategy of Idea 8 with **GeM Pooling** applied to all three feature maps: $F_{on}$, $F_{off}$, and $F_{diff}$.
    *   **Operation:** $f = (\frac{1}{HW} \sum F(x,y)^p)^{1/p}$, where $p$ is a learnable parameter initialized to 3.
    *   **Rationale:** GeM structurally generalizes the "Source-Stream Max Pooling" lesson (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`). It allows the model to continuously tune the pooling sensitivity per channel, behaving like Max Pooling for sparse "needle" features and Average Pooling for diffuse textures, without the dimensionality explosion of concatenating both fixed pools.

4.  **Classifier:**
    *   The GeM-pooled vectors ($v_{on}, v_{off}, v_{diff}$) are concatenated.
    *   A **Single Linear Layer** maps the result to the output logit (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
1.  **Full Dataset:** Train on the entire training set (~43,000 samples) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00024`).
2.  **Native Resolution Padding:** Pad the input arrays from `(273, 256)` to `(288, 256)` (nearest multiple of 32). Do **not** resize, preserving fine-grained spectral resolution (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).
3.  **No Normalization:** Feed the raw, standardized data directly. Do **not** apply ImageNet normalization, as this was the primary cause of failure in Idea 9 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00031`).
4.  **Synchronized Augmentation:**
    *   Apply **Horizontal Flip** (Time Reversal) and **Vertical Flip** (Frequency Inversion) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`).
    *   Ensure identical transformations are applied to both Stream A and Stream B to maintain pixel-wise correspondence (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).
    *   Apply **Mixup** ($\alpha=0.2$) for regularization.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss`.
*   **Optimizer:** `AdamW` with learning rate `1e-3` and weight decay `1e-2`.
*   **Scheduler:** `CosineAnnealingLR` with `T_max=15` epochs.
*   **Strategy:** Train for 15 epochs. The combination of the robust backbone and learnable pooling/scaling should converge efficiently.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Process the test set using the padding and splitting logic.
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** Perform inference on 4 variations: Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, and Horizontal+Vertical Flip. Average the predictions to maximize geometric invariance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`).
