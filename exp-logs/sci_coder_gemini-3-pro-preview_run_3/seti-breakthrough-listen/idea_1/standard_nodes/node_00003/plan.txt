My optimization strategy focuses on enabling the model to learn the global spatial structure of the technosignatures (which often drift across time/frequency) without destroying information through rigid subtraction.

1.  **Data Representation (Vertical Stacking):** Instead of feeding the 6 cadence panels as separate channels (which failed in the baseline) or subtracting them (which fails for drifting signals per **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**), I will vertically stack the 6 panels into a single tall image `(1, 1638, 256)`. This transforms the problem into detecting a "broken line" pattern, which 2D CNNs are naturally good at, addressing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
2.  **Model Architecture (ResNet18d):** I will replace the shallow custom CNN with a `timm` implementation of `resnet18d`. This provides a deeper, pretrained backbone capable of capturing complex spatial dependencies that the shallow network missed.
3.  **Hyperparameters:** I will increase the number of epochs to 8 and reduce the batch size to 32 to accommodate the larger input dimensions and ensure stable convergence.
