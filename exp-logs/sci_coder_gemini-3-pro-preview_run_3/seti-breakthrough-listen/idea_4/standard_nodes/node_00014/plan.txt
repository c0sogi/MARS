I will optimize the solution by scaling up the training process and refining the input representation.
1.  **Full Dataset Training**: The previous solution demonstrated that the Siamese architecture is effective (AUC ~0.70) even on a small subset (15k samples). I will now train on the full dataset (43k samples) to maximize the model's ability to generalize and distinguish faint signals.
2.  **Resolution Optimization**: I will increase the input image size from `(224, 224)` to `(256, 256)`. The raw spectrograms have a time dimension of 256. Matching this dimension prevents interpolation artifacts along the time axis, preserving the structural integrity of the "needle" signals (Cite Lesson 00006).
3.  **Enhanced Augmentation**: I will add `VerticalFlip` (frequency reversal) to the training pipeline. Since technosignatures can exhibit positive or negative Doppler drift and appear at any frequency, the problem is invariant to frequency direction. This augmentation effectively doubles the diversity of the training data.
