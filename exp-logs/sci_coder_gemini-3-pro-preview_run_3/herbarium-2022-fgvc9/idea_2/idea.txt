# Proposed Strategy: EfficientNetV2 Scaling with Mixup and Test-Time Augmentation

## Model
We propose a structural upgrade from the lightweight MobileNetV3 to the **EfficientNetV2-Small** architecture. 
- **Architecture**: EfficientNetV2-Small is chosen for its superior parameter efficiency and training speed on modern accelerators (A100) compared to V1. It utilizes **Fused-MBConv** blocks in the early stages to reduce memory access overhead, which is critical when scaling up from the previous baseline.
- **Capacity**: With approximately 22 million parameters (vs. ~5.4M in MobileNetV3), this model provides the necessary capacity to model the fine-grained decision boundaries required for **15,501 distinct species**, addressing the high-cardinality nature of the task.
- **Head**: The classification head will be a single linear layer projecting the 1280-dimensional feature embedding to the 15,501 class logits.

## Data
We will enhance the data pipeline to support the increased model capacity and further regularize the training process.
- **Resolution**: We will increase the input image resolution from 224x224 to **260x260** (or higher if memory permits, e.g., 300x300). Higher resolution is vital for plant classification to capture minute textural details (e.g., leaf venation, flower patterns) that distinguish closely related species.
- **Augmentation**: We will explicitly retain the **Strong Data Augmentation** strategy (RandomResizedCrop, ShiftScaleRotate, ColorJitter) which was proven effective in preventing overfitting. Cite solution_lesson_node_00004.
- **Regularization (Mixup)**: We will introduce **Mixup** (or CutMix) augmentation. This technique trains the model on convex combinations of pairs of examples and their labels. This encourages the model to behave linearly in-between training examples, improving generalization and robustness, particularly for the tail classes in our high-cardinality dataset.

## Training
The training process is designed to maximize convergence on the A100 GPU within the time limit.
- **Loss Function**: We will use **Cross-Entropy Loss** with **Label Smoothing** (0.1). Cite solution_lesson_node_00004. This loss will be adapted to handle the soft targets generated by the Mixup augmentation.
- **Optimizer & Scheduler**: We will retain the **AdamW** optimizer and the **OneCycleLR** scheduler, as these have demonstrated rapid convergence in the previous experiments. Cite solution_lesson_node_00002.
- **Duration**: We will train for approximately **12-15 epochs**. The A100's throughput allows for this extended duration even with the larger EfficientNetV2 backbone, ensuring the model has sufficient time to learn from the strong augmentations.

## Evaluation
To maximize the **Macro F1 score**, we will improve the inference stability.
- **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**: Instead of a single center crop, we will employ TTA (e.g., averaging predictions from the original image and a horizontally flipped version, or 5-crop averaging). This reduces prediction variance and is particularly beneficial for improving the F1 score on hard-to-classify examples.
- **Resolution**: Inference will be performed at the same or slightly higher resolution than training (e.g., 260x260) to ensure feature consistency.
