# Proposed Strategy: Corrected Hierarchical EfficientNetV2 with Progressive Resolution

We propose a robust evolution of the categorization pipeline that integrates the structural benefits of taxonomic hierarchy with a disciplined training regimen derived from the failure analysis of previous experiments. This strategy specifically corrects the data sampling error from **Idea 5** while retaining the successful regularization and scheduling components from **Idea 4**.

## Model
We will employ a **Hierarchical Multi-Task EfficientNetV2-Small** architecture.
- **Backbone:** We replace the MobileNetV3 used in the baseline with **EfficientNetV2-Small**. This architecture offers a superior balance of parameter efficiency and feature capacity, necessary for distinguishing 15,501 classes, without the convergence difficulties of the ConvNeXt model attempted in **Idea 3**.
- **Hierarchical Heads:** Instead of a single flat classification layer, the model will utilize **three parallel linear heads** branching from the final feature extractor. These heads will correspond to the dataset's taxonomic structure:
    1.  **Family Head** (Auxiliary): Predicts ~270 families.
    2.  **Genus Head** (Auxiliary): Predicts ~2,564 genera.
    3.  **Species Head** (Primary): Predicts 15,501 species.
    This structure forces the model to learn taxonomically consistent features, using the coarser-grained labels (Family/Genus) to stabilize the learning of the fine-grained Species task, effectively regularizing the tail classes.

## Data
- **Preprocessing:** We will merge the `train_metadata.json` with the image list to generate the ground truth labels for Family and Genus for every training image.
- **Dataset Integrity:** We will train on the **full dataset (532,576 images)**. We explicitly reject the random subsampling strategy that caused the failure in **Idea 5**, as reducing sample density destroys performance in high-cardinality tasks. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**.
- **Augmentation:** We will implement **Strong Data Augmentation** (RandomResizedCrop, ShiftScaleRotate, ColorJitter). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
- **Regularization Exclusion:** We will **exclude Mixup and CutMix**. While beneficial in long regimes, these techniques obscure the training signal and caused convergence failure in restricted compute scenarios. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.

## Training
We will implement a **Progressive Resolution Training** strategy to maximize both convergence speed and fine-grained accuracy.
- **Loss Function:** We will use a **Weighted Multi-Task Cross-Entropy Loss** with **Label Smoothing (0.1)** applied to all heads. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**. The total loss will be $L_{total} = L_{species} + 0.1 L_{genus} + 0.1 L_{family}$.
- **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** combined with the **OneCycleLR** scheduler to ensure rapid convergence. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.

**Two-Stage Pipeline:**
1.  **Stage 1 (Feature Learning):** Train on **224x224** resolution for **12 epochs**. This phase leverages the high throughput of lower resolution images to learn robust general features and converge quickly. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
2.  **Stage 2 (Fine-Grained Refinement):** Fine-tune the model on **320x320** resolution for **8 epochs**. This higher resolution is critical for resolving minute details (e.g., leaf venation) required for species identification, while the pre-trained weights from Stage 1 prevent the "cold start" issues associated with high-resolution training.

## Evaluation
- **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the **Species Head** at **320x320** resolution.
- **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will apply **Horizontal Flip TTA** (averaging predictions of the original and flipped image) to reduce variance and improve the F1 score on hard examples.
