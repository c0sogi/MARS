# Proposed Strategy: Hierarchical Multi-Task EfficientNetV2

## Model
We propose a **Hierarchical Multi-Task Learning** architecture to structurally evolve the flat classification approaches of previous ideas.
- **Backbone**: We will utilize **EfficientNetV2-Small**. This backbone offers a superior trade-off between parameter efficiency and feature capacity compared to the MobileNetV3 used in **Idea 1** and **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**, while being significantly faster and easier to converge than the ConvNeXt-Tiny attempted in **Idea 3**.
- **Hierarchical Heads**: Instead of a single classification head, the model will feature **three parallel linear heads** branching from the feature extractor. These heads will correspond to the taxonomic hierarchy provided in the dataset:
    1.  **Family Head**: Predicts one of ~270 families.
    2.  **Genus Head**: Predicts one of ~2,500 genera.
    3.  **Species Head**: Predicts one of 15,501 species (Primary Task).
- **Rationale**: Explicitly supervising the model on higher-level taxonomic ranks (Family/Genus) acts as a structural regularizer. It forces the model to learn robust, shared features (e.g., "this looks like an Orchid") before distinguishing fine-grained species details, helping to address the high-cardinality and tail-class issues without the convergence risks of Mixup.

## Data
- **Resolution**: We will standardize inputs to **256x256** pixels. This is a moderate increase from the 224x224 used in the successful **Idea 1**, allowing for better detail resolution, but avoids the excessive computational cost and memory instability observed with 384x384 in **Idea 3** and **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
- **Augmentation**: We will strictly adhere to the **Strong Data Augmentation** policy (RandomResizedCrop, ShiftScaleRotate, ColorJitter). As demonstrated in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**, these geometric and color invariances are critical for preventing overfitting in long training runs.
- **Preprocessing**: We will join the training metadata with the taxonomic structure (Family/Genus) to generate the auxiliary labels required for the multi-task heads.

## Training
- **Loss Function**: We will use a **Weighted Multi-Task Cross-Entropy Loss**. The total loss will be $L_{total} = L_{species} + \lambda_1 L_{genus} + \lambda_2 L_{family}$, where $\lambda$ are small coefficients (e.g., 0.1).
- **Regularization**: We will apply **Label Smoothing (0.1)** to all heads. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**. Crucially, we will **exclude Mixup and CutMix**. As learned in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**, these heavy regularizers can destroy the training signal when the model capacity or training duration is constrained, leading to convergence failure.
- **Optimizer & Scheduler**: We will utilize **AdamW** with the **OneCycleLR** scheduler. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**. This combination provides the rapid convergence necessary to maximize the utility of our compute budget.
- **Duration**: We will train for **15-20 epochs**. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004** proved that extending training duration is the most reliable way to scale performance in high-cardinality tasks, provided overfitting is controlled by strong augmentation.

## Evaluation
- **Inference**: Predictions will be generated solely from the **Species Head**.
- **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**: We will employ a simple **Horizontal Flip TTA** (averaging predictions of the original and flipped image) to reduce variance and boost the Macro F1 score on hard examples.
