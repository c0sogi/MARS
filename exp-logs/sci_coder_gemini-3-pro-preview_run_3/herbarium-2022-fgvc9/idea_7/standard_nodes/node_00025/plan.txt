The issue is a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` caused by the configured batch sizes exceeding the available GPU VRAM (approx. 16GB). The traceback shows the allocation failing during the forward pass of Stage 1 with a batch size of 128. To fix this, I will reduce `STAGE1_BATCH_SIZE` from 128 to 32 and `STAGE2_BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to 16 in `library/config.py`. This scales the memory usage to fit within the hardware constraints, as recommended in the error analysis. Cite debug_lesson_7.
