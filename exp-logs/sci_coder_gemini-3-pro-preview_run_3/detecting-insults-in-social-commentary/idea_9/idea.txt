Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose a **Robust Semi-Supervised Heterogeneous Ensemble**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by integrating **Self-Training (Pseudo-Labeling)** with **Adversarial Training (AWP)**. It synthesizes the architectural diversity of the "Heterogeneous Ensemble" (Idea 8) with the data augmentation benefits of "Pseudo-Labeling" (Idea 6/Lesson 35), while correcting the implementation constraints that previously caused Adversarial Weight Perturbation to fail (Lesson 30).

# Model
The solution employs a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** to maximize linguistic coverage and error diversity **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}**.
1.  **Backbones:** We will utilize two distinct architectures:
    *   **RoBERTa-Large:** Retained for its stability and high performance on this specific dataset **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}**.
    *   **DeBERTa-v3-Large:** Included for its superior disentangled attention mechanisms **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}**.
2.  **Aggregation:** Both backbones will utilize **Mean Pooling** of the final hidden states. We explicitly retain this parameter-free approach over complex heads like Attention Pooling to prevent overfitting **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00013}**.
3.  **Regularization:**
    *   **Layer Freezing:** We will freeze the embeddings and the bottom 6 encoder layers to improve efficiency and preserve general linguistic features **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}**.
    *   **Dropout:** Fixed at **0.2** for all models **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}**.

# Data
1.  **Preprocessing:** Decode unicode-escaped text to ensure character integrity.
2.  **Tokenization:** Use a **Maximum Sequence Length of 160** tokens. This is critical to capture the context of longer comments, which are otherwise truncated **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}**.
3.  **Dataset Construction:**
    *   **Initial Training Set:** Explicitly concatenate the Train and Validation sets into a single corpus **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00021}**.
    *   **Augmented Training Set (Stage 2):** This set will comprise the Initial Training Set merged with **Pseudo-Labeled Test Data** generated in Stage 1.

# Training
The training pipeline is a **Two-Stage Self-Training Workflow** designed to address data scarcity and generalization.

**Stage 1: Teacher Ensemble Training**
*   **Objective:** Train high-quality "Teacher" models to label the test data.
*   **Procedure:** Train **3 independent seeds** of RoBERTa-Large and **3 independent seeds** of DeBERTa-v3-Large on the *Initial Training Set*.
*   **Stability:** Strictly maintain an **Effective Batch Size of 32** via Gradient Accumulation **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027}**.
*   **Pseudo-Labeling:** Generate predictions on the unlabeled Test Set using the average of the 6 Teacher models. Filter samples with high confidence (e.g., probability > 0.95 or < 0.05), assign hard labels (0 or 1), and merge them into the *Augmented Training Set* **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00035}**.

**Stage 2: Robust Student Training with AWP**
*   **Objective:** Train "Student" models on the larger, augmented dataset using advanced regularization.
*   **Procedure:** Train a new set of **3 RoBERTa** and **3 DeBERTa** models on the *Augmented Training Set*.
*   **Structural Innovation (Adversarial Weight Perturbation):** During this stage, we will enable **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP)**.
    *   *Correction from Previous Failures:* Previous attempts at AWP failed because the computational cost forced a reduction in training epochs (fixed at 2), preventing convergence **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00030}**. Leveraging the 24-hour runtime budget, we will run these models for sufficient epochs (e.g., 4-5) to allow the adversarial regularization to stabilize the loss landscape on the augmented manifold.
*   **Optimization:** AdamW optimizer with a linear scheduler. The scheduler steps will be decoupled from the epoch count to prevent schedule compression **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}**.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The test set will be processed by the **6 Student Models** trained in Stage 2 (3 RoBERTa + 3 DeBERTa).
2.  **Prediction:** The final submission will be the **arithmetic mean** of the probability outputs from these 6 models. This combines the benefits of architectural diversity, data augmentation via self-training, and adversarial robustness.
