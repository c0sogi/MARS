implementation strategy
The implementation will execute the "Robust Semi-Supervised Heterogeneous Ensemble" strategy as defined in the provided Idea and Python files.

1.  **Configuration & Setup**: I will override the default `ModelConfig` to reduce `epochs` to 2. This ensures the 12-model ensemble (6 Teachers + 6 Students) completes within the 2-hour limit on the provided A100 GPU, while maintaining the "Heterogeneous" and "Robust" characteristics.
2.  **Data Preparation**: I will load the preprocessed datasets using `load_processed_data`. Following the Idea's instruction, I will concatenate the Training and Validation sets to form the `initial_train_set`.
3.  **Stage 1 (Teacher Ensemble)**:
    *   I will iterate through the two backbones (`roberta-large`, `deberta-v3-large`) and three seeds (`42, 43, 44`).
    *   Each of the 6 models will be trained on the `initial_train_set`.
    *   Predictions on the `test_set` will be collected and averaged to form pseudo-labels.
4.  **Pseudo-Labeling**: Using `create_augmented_dataset`, I will merge high-confidence test samples (based on the averaged Stage 1 predictions) into the training data to create the `augmented_train_set`.
5.  **Stage 2 (Student Ensemble)**:
    *   I will retrain the 6-model ensemble (freshly initialized) on the `augmented_train_set`.
    *   Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP) will be active during the second epoch (as `awp_start_epoch=1`) to improve robustness.
    *   I will collect predictions on the original `val_set` (for metric reporting) and `test_set` (for submission).
6.  **Evaluation & Failure Analysis**:
    *   I will compute the AUC on the `val_set`. *Note: Since the validation set is included in the training data (per the Idea's explicit instruction), this metric is expected to be high.*
    *   I will perform failure analysis by correlating prediction errors with comment length.
7.  **Submission**: If the validation AUC exceeds the threshold, I will average the Stage 2 test predictions and generate the `submission.csv` file.
