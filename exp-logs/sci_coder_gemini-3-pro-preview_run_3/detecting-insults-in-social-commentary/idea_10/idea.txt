Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the progression of previous ideas, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Adversarial Knowledge Distillation**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution from "Self-Training with Hard Labels" (Lesson 35) to **Knowledge Distillation with Soft Targets**, integrated with a corrected implementation of **Adversarial Weight Perturbation** (Lesson 30). It addresses the core constraint of data scarcity by leveraging the full information content of the unlabeled test set (including uncertainty) rather than just high-confidence snapshots.

### Model
The solution will employ a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** to maximize linguistic coverage and error diversity **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}**.
1.  **Backbones:** We will utilize two distinct architectures:
    *   **RoBERTa-Large:** Retained for its proven stability and high performance on this dataset **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}**.
    *   **DeBERTa-v3-Large:** Retained for its superior disentangled attention mechanisms **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}**.
2.  **Aggregation:** Both backbones will utilize **Mean Pooling** of the final hidden states. We explicitly retain this parameter-free approach over complex heads like Attention Pooling to prevent overfitting **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00013}**.
3.  **Regularization:**
    *   **Layer Freezing:** We will freeze the embeddings and the bottom 6 encoder layers to improve efficiency and preserve general linguistic features **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}**.
    *   **Dropout:** Fixed at **0.2** for all models to combat overfitting on small data **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}**.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:** Decode unicode-escaped text to ensure character integrity.
2.  **Tokenization:** Use a **Maximum Sequence Length of 160** tokens. This is critical to capture the context of longer comments, which are otherwise truncated **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}**.
3.  **Dataset Construction:**
    *   **Labeled Corpus:** Explicitly concatenate the Train and Validation sets into a single corpus **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00021}**.
    *   **Distillation Corpus:** This consists of the unlabeled Test set paired with **Soft Targets** (probability distributions) generated by the Teacher Ensemble. Unlike Hard Pseudo-Labeling which discards uncertainty **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00035}**, this retains the "dark knowledge" (e.g., a comment being 60% insulting vs 90%) to guide the Student models.

### Training
The training pipeline is a **Teacher-Student Knowledge Distillation Workflow** enhanced with Adversarial Training.

**Stage 1: Teacher Ensemble Training**
*   **Objective:** Train high-quality "Teacher" models to generate soft targets.
*   **Procedure:** Train **3 independent seeds** of RoBERTa-Large and **3 independent seeds** of DeBERTa-v3-Large on the *Labeled Corpus*.
*   **Stability:** Strictly maintain an **Effective Batch Size of 32** via Gradient Accumulation to prevent the instability observed with smaller batches **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027}**.
*   **Target Generation:** Generate probability predictions (Soft Targets) for the entire unlabeled Test Set using the arithmetic mean of the 6 Teacher models.

**Stage 2: Adversarial Student Distillation**
*   **Objective:** Train "Student" models that learn from both the ground truth and the Teacher's probability landscape, while being robust to feature noise.
*   **Procedure:** Train a new set of **3 RoBERTa** and **3 DeBERTa** models (Students).
*   **Loss Function:** Use a **Hybrid Loss**: Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) for the *Labeled Corpus* and Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence for the *Distillation Corpus*. This allows the students to learn from the full test set distribution, not just high-confidence samples.
*   **Structural Innovation (Adversarial Weight Perturbation):** We will enable **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP)** for the Student models.
    *   *Correction:* Previous attempts failed because training was restricted to 2 epochs **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00030}**. We will run these models for **4-5 epochs**, utilizing the full 24-hour budget to allow the adversarial regularization to stabilize the loss landscape.
*   **Optimization:** AdamW optimizer with a linear scheduler, decoupled from the epoch count to prevent schedule compression **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The test set will be processed by the **6 Student Models** trained in Stage 2 (3 RoBERTa + 3 DeBERTa).
2.  **Prediction:** The final submission will be the **arithmetic mean** of the probability outputs from these 6 models. This combines the benefits of architectural diversity **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}**, soft-target knowledge transfer, and adversarial robustness.
