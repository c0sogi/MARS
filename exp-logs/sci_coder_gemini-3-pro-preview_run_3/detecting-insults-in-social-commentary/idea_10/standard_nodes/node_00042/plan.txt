The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU VRAM (~16GB) is insufficient for training the `deberta-v3-large` model with a micro-batch size of 16, particularly due to the additional memory overhead required by Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP). To resolve this, I will reduce the `train_batch_size` to 8 and increase `accumulation_steps` to 4. This modification lowers the peak memory footprint of activations during the forward pass while preserving the target effective batch size of 32. Cite debug_lesson_5. Additionally, I will introduce explicit garbage collection (`gc.collect()`) in the cleanup phases of the training loops to ensure that memory from previous models is fully released, preventing fragmentation-induced OOMs in subsequent seed runs.
