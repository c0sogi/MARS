Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00027` and `solution_lesson_node_00013` suggest that a complex pipeline often fails to outperform a well-tuned baseline if the base models are suboptimal. By focusing on a pure supervised ensemble of 3 models trained with the optimized batch size, we aim to reliably exceed the threshold without the noise and overhead of pseudo-labeling.
