1.  **Greedy First-Match Decoding (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`)**: I will modify the decoding logic in `library/trainer.py`. Instead of selecting the answer span with the highest confidence score within a window (which often biases towards short, high-probability noise), I will select the **first valid span** detected in the sequence. 2.  **Seed Ensembling (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00008`)**: To mitigate the high variance inherent in training on small datasets (~1k samples), I will modify `runfile.py` to train the model three times with different random seeds (42, 43, 44). During inference, I will average the output probabilities (soft voting) from all three models before decoding. This stabilizes the performance and ensures the solution reliably meets the metric threshold.

I will also refactor `library/trainer.py` to expose methods for retrieving raw probabilities (`get_probs`) and decoding from them (`decode_from_probs`), facilitating the ensemble logic.

        """
