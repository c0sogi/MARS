Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces **Question-Context Task-Adaptive Pretraining (QC-TAPT)** and **Stratified K-Fold Ensembling** as structural innovations to maximize data utility and model adaptation, while rigorously adhering to the engineering constraints identified in previous failures.

# Model
**Architecture:**
I will utilize the **`xlm-roberta-base`** model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004) as the backbone, architected as a **Token Classifier** (projecting hidden states to `B-ANS`, `I-ANS`, `O`). This model is chosen for its superior multilingual capacity compared to distilled variants.

**Structural Innovation: Question-Context Task-Adaptive Pretraining (QC-TAPT)**
Standard TAPT adapts the model only to the document distribution. I propose a structural evolution to adapt the model to the specific *input pair structure* of the QA task.
1.  **Data Construction:** I will concatenate the `Question` and `Context` (separated by the `</s>` token) for all samples in both the **Train and Test** sets.
2.  **MLM Adaptation:** I will fine-tune the `xlm-roberta-base` model using a **Masked Language Modeling (MLM)** objective on these pairs. This allows the model to learn the interaction patterns between questions and Hindi/Tamil contexts before supervised fine-tuning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Sliding Window:** The text will be tokenized and split into overlapping windows of **384 tokens** with a **stride of 128**.
*   **Labeling (Strict Containment):** I will strictly employ **Strict Containment** for label generation. A window will only be labeled with `B-ANS`/`I-ANS` if it contains the **entire** ground truth span. This prevents the structural noise and model collapse observed with "Soft Overlap" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Full Distribution:** I will retain **all** generated windows for training. I will **not** perform any negative downsampling, ensuring the model learns to suppress false positives by seeing the full background distribution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Coupled Metadata:** I will implement a custom `collate_fn` to bundle necessary metadata (e.g., `offset_mapping`, `input_ids`) directly into the training batches (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

# Training
**Structural Innovation: Stratified K-Fold Ensembling**
To address the high variance of low-resource training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008) while maximizing the utility of the small dataset (~800 samples), I will replace the single train/val split with a **Stratified K-Fold** approach (e.g., K=3).
*   **Procedure:** I will train 3 independent models, each on a different 2/3 subset of the data. This ensures that across the ensemble, the system learns from **100% of the available training data**, unlike a fixed split which permanently hides 20% of the data from the model.

**Hyperparameters:**
*   **Batch Size:** I will strictly utilize a **Batch Size of 16**. This is the critical "sweet spot" that balances gradient update frequency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015) with gradient stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Loss Function:** I will use **Standard Cross-Entropy Loss**, avoiding aggressive class weighting that was shown to degrade precision (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Duration:** Each fold will be fine-tuned for **10 epochs** to accommodate the "delayed onset" of convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
1.  **Global Confidence Aggregation (Per Model):** For a given document, the model will process **all** overlapping windows. It will collect all candidate spans and select the single span with the **highest confidence score** across the entire document. This filters out low-confidence false positives often generated by greedy "first-match" strategies in partial contexts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Self-Contained Decoding:** Answer strings will be decoded directly from the predicted token IDs using the tokenizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
3.  **Majority Voting (Ensemble):** The final prediction will be determined by a **Majority Vote** on the exact string outputs from the K fold models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
