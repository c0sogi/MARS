To optimize the solution, I will implement a **Differential Learning Rate** strategy for domain-specific fine-tuning, as recommended in Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00027`. This involves unfreezing the top layers of the EfficientNet-B0 backbone (specifically the last two blocks and the convolution head) to adapt high-level semantic features to the medical domain while preserving low-level filters. I will assign a lower learning rate (`1e-4`) to the backbone and a higher learning rate (`1e-3`) to the task-specific head. Additionally, I will increase the capacity of the fusion network (projection and hidden dimensions) and extend the training duration to 50 epochs to ensure convergence Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027}.
