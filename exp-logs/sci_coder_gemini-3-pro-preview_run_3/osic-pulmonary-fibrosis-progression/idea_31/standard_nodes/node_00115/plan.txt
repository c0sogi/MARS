My optimization strategy focuses on three key improvements derived from the lessons learned:

1.  **Architecture Refinement (Linear Residual Stream)**: I will simplify the "Clinical Anchor" stream (Stream A) from a 2-layer MLP to a single Linear layer. This implements a **Linear Residual Stream** (Cite Lesson 00052) that is **Over-parameterized** (Cite Lesson 00060). This allows the model to easily learn the dominant linear autoregressive trend ($FVC \approx Baseline + Rate \times Time$) without the optimization bottlenecks or non-linear distortions introduced by the previous MLP.

2.  **Scheduler Correction**: I will modify the `Trainer` class to initialize the `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler using `Config.EPOCHS` (the actual training duration) instead of `Config.T_MAX` (a static constant). This fixes a bug where the learning rate schedule was decoupled from the runtime, ensuring the model completes its full decay cycle (Cite Lesson 00100).

3.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**: I will increase the training duration from 15 to 30 epochs in `runfile.py`. The previous run's learning curve indicated that 15 epochs were insufficient for the loss to stabilize, and the corrected scheduler requires this extended horizon to reach the sharp minima required for high-precision regression.
