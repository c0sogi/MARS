To improve the model's performance, I will implement a targeted optimization focusing on the **Clinical Stream** architecture and the training schedule.

1.  **Linearize the Clinical Stream (Cite Lesson 00052, 00060)**: The current `ClinicalStream` uses a non-linear MLP (`Linear -> ReLU -> Linear`). Since the input features (specifically `Baseline_FVC`) are Z-score standardized, they often take negative values. The `ReLU` activation blocks these negative signals, hindering the model from learning the dominant linear relationship (Identity Mapping: $FVC_{target} \approx FVC_{baseline}$). I will replace this MLP with a single, over-parameterized `nn.Linear` projection. This allows the strong linear priors to propagate directly to the fusion layer without distortion, acting as a robust residual connection.

2.  **Align Scheduler and Epochs (Cite Lesson 00009)**: I will increase the training duration from 20 to 30 epochs and synchronize the Cosine Annealing scheduler (`T_MAX`) to match. The previous run stopped early, but the linear stream modification simplifies the optimization landscape, likely requiring a different convergence trajectory. Extending the schedule ensures the visual stream has sufficient time to fine-tune the backbone features.
