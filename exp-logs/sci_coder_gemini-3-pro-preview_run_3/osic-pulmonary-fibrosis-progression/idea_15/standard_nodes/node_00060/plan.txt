Based on the analysis of the previous run and the provided lessons, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Configuration (`library/config.py`)**:
    *   **Backbone & Resolution**: Switch from `efficientnet_b2` (260px) to `efficientnet_b0` (224px). The dataset is small (126 patients), and the larger backbone likely introduces capacity mismatch and overfitting risks. **Cite Lesson 00054**.
    *   **Uncertainty Floor**: Reduce `EPSILON` from `0.1` to `1e-6`. The previous value (~80ml) acted as a hard architectural floor during training, dampening gradients for overconfident predictions. We should allow the model to predict lower uncertainty during training and clip only at inference. **Cite Lesson 00014**.
    *   **Epochs**: Increase `EPOCHS` to 30 to ensure sufficient convergence for the fine-tuning of the backbone. **Cite Lesson 00009**.

2.  **Model Architecture (`library/model.py`)**:
    *   **Dropout Removal**: Remove the `Dropout` layer immediately preceding the final output layer in the `deep_stream`. Dropout here destabilizes the calibration of the uncertainty head (`sigma`). **Cite Lesson 00007**.
    *   **Initialization**: Change the initialization of the sigma bias from `3.0` to `-0.5`. The previous value initialized the model with massive uncertainty (~2400ml), which suppresses gradients (due to the $1/\sigma$ term in NLL) and causes the model to get stuck in a high-uncertainty local minimum. Initializing at `-0.5` (scaled) results in a starting sigma of $\approx 0.5$ standard deviations, which is a neutral and learnable starting point. **Cite Lesson 00001**.

3.  **Execution (`runfile.py`)**:
    *   Update the epoch override to match the new configuration.
