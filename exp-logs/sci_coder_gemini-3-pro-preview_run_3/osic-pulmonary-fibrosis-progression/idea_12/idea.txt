Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the **Residual-Skipped Time-Conditioned Network (RSTC-Net)**.

This solution represents a structural evolution that synthesizes the superior "Point-Wise" temporal modeling (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**) with a robust mechanism to preserve strong scalar predictors (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**). It resolves the conflict between the need for complex cross-modal interactions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**) and the risk of "drowning out" the critical baseline signal in deep networks (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).

### 1. Model
The architecture is a hybrid CNN-MLP that utilizes a **Wide-and-Deep** design pattern to predict lung function ($\mu$) and confidence ($\sigma$) for a specific time point $t$.

*   **Image Branch (Fine-Tuned Content-Adaptive 2.5D):**
    *   **Input:** Three slices per patient selected via the **Content-Adaptive Heuristic** (Apical, Middle, Basal) to ensure anatomical consistency (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
    *   **Backbone:** **EfficientNet-B2**. We unfreeze the top two convolutional blocks to allow domain adaptation while keeping lower layers frozen (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**).
    *   **Feature Extraction:** Features are extracted via **Global Average Pooling** from the final layer to prioritize deep semantic signals (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**). We avoid attention modules to prevent overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).
    *   **Projection:** The concatenated vector (3 slices) is projected to 128 dimensions via a Linear layer (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**).

*   **Temporal & Tabular Inputs:**
    *   **Relative Time ($t_{rel}$):** We calculate $t_{rel} = \text{Weeks} - \text{Baseline\_Week}$ and scale it by 0.01. We **do not** Z-score this variable, strictly preserving the zero at the baseline to maintain the physical origin of the trajectory (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
    *   **Tabular Features:** `Baseline FVC`, `Age`, `Sex`, `SmokingStatus`. These are Z-score standardized (except categorical) but **not** passed through normalization layers (BN/LN) to preserve their absolute magnitude (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**).

*   **Dual-Path Fusion Architecture (Structural Innovation):**
    *   **Path 1: Deep Interaction Branch:**
        *   Input: Concatenation of [Image Projection, Tabular Features, $t_{rel}$].
        *   Structure: A Multi-Layer Perceptron (Linear $\to$ ReLU $\to$ Linear).
        *   Purpose: This branch learns complex non-linear interactions (e.g., "Fibrosis accelerates decline") (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**).
    *   **Path 2: Wide Skip Connection:**
        *   Input: Concatenation of [Tabular Features, $t_{rel}$].
        *   Structure: Identity Mapping (Direct pass-through).
        *   Purpose: This ensures the final layer has direct access to the strong linear predictors (`Baseline FVC` and Time), preventing them from being obscured by the high-dimensional image noise in the Deep Branch (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).

*   **Final Head:**
    *   **Input:** Concatenation of [Deep Branch Output, Wide Branch Output].
    *   **Layer:** A single Linear layer projecting to $\mu$ and $\sigma$.
    *   **Constraints:** No Dropout (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**) and No Batch Norm (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).

### 2. Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Slice Selection:** Select the "Anchor" slice (Max Lung Area) and two boundary slices (50% area threshold) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
    *   **Target Scaling:** The target `FVC` is **Z-score standardized** during training to resolve the magnitude gap (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).
    *   **Input Normalization:** `Baseline FVC` and `Age` are standardized. `Weeks` is converted to $t_{rel}$.

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** Modified Laplace Log Likelihood ($L = \frac{|y - \mu|}{\sigma} + \ln(\sigma)$).
*   **Uncertainty Constraints:**
    *   We use `sigma = softplus(raw_output) + epsilon`. We **do not** enforce the metric's 70ml clip in the architecture (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Optimizer & Schedule:**
    *   **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing**.
    *   **Differential Learning Rates:** `1e-4` for the Unfrozen Backbone, `1e-3` for the Head (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**).
    *   **Duration:** **50 Epochs** to allow for uncertainty calibration (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**).

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    *   For each test `Patient_Week`, calculate $t_{rel}$.
    *   Predict $\mu_{scaled}$ and $\sigma_{scaled}$.
*   **Inverse Transformation:**
    *   $\mu_{final} = \mu_{scaled} \times \sigma_{target} + \mu_{target}$.
    *   $\sigma_{final} = \sigma_{scaled} \times \sigma_{target}$ (Scale only) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).
*   **Post-Processing:**
    *   Apply the hard clip $\sigma_{final} = \max(\sigma_{final}, 70)$ strictly for the submission file.
