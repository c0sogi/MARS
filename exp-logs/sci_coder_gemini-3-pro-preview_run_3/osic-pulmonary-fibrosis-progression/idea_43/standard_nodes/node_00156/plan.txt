To improve the model's performance, I will implement three targeted optimizations based on the provided lessons:

1.  **Correct Target Normalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00155):** I will modify `library/data.py` to calculate the target normalization statistics (`fvc_mean`, `fvc_std`) using the *entire* history of FVC measurements in the training set, rather than just the baseline measurements. This prevents the covariate shift caused by normalizing declining values with baseline statistics.
2.  **Remove Zero-Initialization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00118):** I will modify `library/model.py` to remove the zero-initialization of the residual stream's output layer (`stream_b_out`). Since the residual stream involves a learnable projection (MLP) rather than a fixed identity, zero-initialization forces the gradient flow through an unoptimized bottleneck at the start of training.
3.  **Remove Dropout from Residual Stream (Cite solution_lesson_node_00126):** I will modify `library/model.py` to remove `Dropout` layers from the deep interaction stream (`stream_b_mlp`). As the image stream provides a weak residual correction to the strong linear baseline, stochastic regularization like Dropout can drown out the signal, causing the model to ignore the image features.
