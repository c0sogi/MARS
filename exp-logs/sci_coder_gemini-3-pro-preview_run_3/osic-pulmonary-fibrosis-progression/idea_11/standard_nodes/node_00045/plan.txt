The following modifications implement a targeted optimization strategy based on the provided lessons and failure analysis.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Downgrade Backbone & Resolution**: Switch from `tf_efficientnet_b2_ns` (260x260) to `tf_efficientnet_b0_ns` (224x224). The dataset is small (~176 patients), and a smaller backbone reduces overfitting risks and aligns with the principle of matching architectural complexity to dataset scale (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
2.  **Rebalance Modalities**: Reduce the image projection dimension (`N_FEATURES`) from 128 to 64. This prevents the high-dimensional image features from drowning out the critical low-dimensional tabular signals (like Baseline FVC and Time) in the fusion layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
3.  **Fix Metric Logging Bug**: Correct the broadcasting error in `library/train.py` where `(Batch, 1)` targets were subtracted from `(Batch,)` predictions, causing incorrect training logs.
4.  **Extend Training Schedule**: Increase `Config.EPOCHS` in `runfile.py` from 15 to 30. Frozen-backbone architectures with learnable projection heads require more iterations to fully converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
