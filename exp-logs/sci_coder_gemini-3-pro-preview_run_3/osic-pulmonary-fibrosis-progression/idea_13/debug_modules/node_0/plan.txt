### Implementation Strategy

1.  **Configuration Setup**: We will import the `Config` class and override specific attributes (e.g., `EPOCHS`, `BATCH_SIZE`, `MAX_TRAIN_SAMPLES`) to ensure the demonstration runs quickly and uses a minimal subset of the data.
2.  **Data Pipeline Verification**:
    *   We will manually load the training metadata and augment it with baseline information using `add_baseline_info`.
    *   We will instantiate and fit the `TabularPreprocessor` and `TargetScaler` to verify feature engineering and target normalization logic.
    *   We will create an instance of `OSICDataset` and retrieve a single sample to assert the correctness of tensor shapes (`image`, `tabular`, `t_rel`, `target`) and types.
3.  **Model Architecture Verification**:
    *   We will instantiate the `DSPRNet` model and move it to the configured device.
    *   We will perform a forward pass with a dummy batch derived from the dataset sample to verify that the model outputs `mu` (prediction) and `sigma` (confidence) with the correct shapes.
    *   We will assert that the predicted `sigma` is positive.
4.  **Training & Validation Loop**:
    *   We will use `get_dataloaders` to generate training and validation loaders based on the reduced dataset.
    *   We will execute `train_one_epoch` to simulate a training step and verify that the loss is computed and returned.
    *   We will execute `validate` to ensure the metric calculation and inverse transformation logic work as expected.
5.  **Metric Logic Check**:
    *   We will isolate the `MetricMonitor` and test it with a known "perfect prediction" case to ensure the Laplace Log Likelihood formula is implemented correctly (accounting for clipping).
