As noted in **Lesson 32** and **Lesson 72**, a batch size of 8 is critical for the stability of Batch Normalization/Layer Normalization in volumetric models, whereas micro-batches (size 2) lead to instability. I will also increase `EPOCHS` to 20 to ensure convergence, as the previous 3-epoch run was insufficient.

The current implementation sums the patient loss (weight 1) and the mean vertebral loss (weight 1), resulting in a total weight mass of 2. As per **Lesson 43**, the target threshold of ~0.0643 corresponds to a weighted *average* (dividing by the sum of weights), not the weighted sum. Normalizing the metric is essential for valid comparison against the threshold.
3.  **Full Dataset**: I will remove the `DEBUG_DATA_SIZE` override in `runfile.py` to train on the full dataset instead of a 200-sample subset, reducing the risk of overfitting to a small split.
