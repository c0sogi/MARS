Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Dual-Resolution ConvNeXt Multi-Task MIL Network**.

This solution represents a structural evolution designed to solve the "Small Feature" problem (fractures are fine-grained) while adhering to the stability constraints of small-batch training. It synthesizes the **LayerNorm-Native Architecture** (Lesson 48), **Multi-Task Learning** (Lesson 45), and **High-Fidelity Data Representation** (Lesson 42) into a cohesive pipeline.

### 1. Model
**Architecture:** Dual-Stream 2.5D ConvNeXt-Tiny with Multi-Task Instance Aggregation
*   **Backbone:** **ConvNeXt-Tiny** (pretrained on ImageNet).
    *   *Strategic Retention:* We retain the **Layer Normalization-native** design of ConvNeXt. This explicitly addresses the "Micro-Batch Instability" observed with ResNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022), allowing us to train stable models even if the effective batch size drops to 4 due to the increased resolution of the dual-stream input. We avoid manually replacing normalization layers, which destroys pretrained weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00048).
*   **Input (Structural Innovation):** **Dual-Resolution 2.5D Stacks**.
    *   For each sampled slice location $z$, the model processes two inputs sharing the same backbone:
        1.  **Global Stream:** The full 512x512 slice (stacked $z-1, z, z+1$) resized to **256x256**. This provides anatomical context.
        2.  **Local Stream:** A **Center Crop** of 256x256 from the original, non-resized image.
    *   *Justification:* Fracture detection relies on high-frequency details that are often lost during resizing. By processing a non-resampled center crop (where the spine is located), we preserve the original pixel fidelity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042) without the computational cost of processing full 512x512 volumes.
*   **Feature Fusion:** Concatenate the feature vectors from the Global and Local streams ($D_{fused} = 2 \times D_{backbone}$).
*   **Context Module:** **Non-Linear 1D Convolution**.
    *   Structure: `Conv1d(k=3, padding=1) -> LayerNorm -> GELU`.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* We retain the 1D Convolution to leverage spatial continuity along the Z-axis (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014). We enforce Non-Linearity and Normalization to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027) but avoid complex multi-scale blocks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028) or residual connections (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
*   **Heads:** **Multi-Task Instance Classifiers**.
    *   The model projects the fused, contextualized features to **8 distinct logits** per slice: [C1...C7, Patient_Overall].
    *   *Strategic Retention:* We use **Multi-Task Learning** (dedicated patient head) rather than logical aggregation (`max()`), as this stabilizes training by allowing independent gradient flow for the global label (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).
*   **Aggregation:** **Global Max Pooling** applied per channel.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* Max Pooling is superior to Attention or Average Pooling for detecting sparse anomalies (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019, Cite solution_lesson_node_00047) and superior to embedding-level aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### 2. Data
*   **Ingestion Pipeline:**
    *   **Sorting:** Sort slices strictly by `ImagePositionPatient` z-coordinate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Calibration:** Apply `RescaleSlope` and `RescaleIntercept` to convert to Hounsfield Units (HU) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Windowing:** Apply **Standard Bone Window** (Level: 400, Width: 1800). Do not use High-Density windows (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Caching:** Preprocess and cache both Global (resized) and Local (cropped) 2.5D stacks as **`uint8`** to maximize I/O throughput (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Sampling:** Uniformly sample **64 slices** per exam. (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Volumetric-Consistent:** Apply the exact same geometric transform to every slice in the bag (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Single-Step Affine:** Fuse rotation, scaling, and shifting into a single affine operation to minimize resampling blur (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
    *   **Non-Destructive:** Explicitly avoid `Cutout` or `Dropout` on inputs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Implicitly Weighted Multi-Task Loss**.
    *   $L = \text{mean}(\text{BCE}_{C1..C7}) + \text{BCE}_{\text{Patient}}$.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* This formulation naturally creates the **1:7 weighting ratio** required by the metric without explicit scalar multipliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Batch Size 4-8**.
    *   *Note:* The increased memory of the Dual-Stream input may force a smaller batch size. The ConvNeXt backbone (LayerNorm) ensures stability even at $N=4$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Scheduler:** **Decoupled Cosine Annealing**. Set `T_max` to **1.5x the number of epochs**.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* Prevents premature learning rate decay (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Regularization:** Do **not** use Dropout in the classification heads (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    *   Load test scans using the sorted/calibrated pipeline.
    *   Generate Global (resized) and Local (center crop) inputs for 64 sampled slices.
    *   Pass through the Dual-Stream ConvNeXt -> Fusion -> Context -> Multi-Task Heads.
    *   Aggregate via Global Max Pooling to obtain 8 probabilities.
    *   Submit the 7 vertebral probabilities and the distinct `patient_overall` probability directly.
