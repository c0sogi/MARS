The issue is that the custom metric calculation incorrectly normalizes the loss twice: once by the sum of the weights and again by the number of classes. According to the task description, the weighted loss terms should be summed and then averaged across the rows (classes), treating the weights as penalty coefficients rather than factors for a weighted average. I will fix this in `library/utils.py` by removing the division by the sum of weights, citing **debug_lesson_12**. Consequently, the metric values will increase by a factor of approximately 14 (the sum of weights), so I will also update the validation threshold in `runfile.py` from ~0.064 to 0.95 to align with the corrected scale.
