The `KeyError: 'exp_avg'` occurs because the `SAM` optimizer forces the base optimizer (`AdamW`) to share its state dictionary. When `SAM` stores the original parameters (`"old_p"`) in this shared state, `AdamW` detects that the state is non-empty and skips the lazy initialization of its internal buffers (like `exp_avg`). To fix this, I will decouple the state management (Cite debug_lesson_8) by removing the shared state assignment in `__init__`. Additionally, I will override `state_dict` and `load_state_dict` to delegate these operations to the base optimizer, ensuring that momentum buffers are correctly saved and loaded without interfering with `SAM`'s transient state.
