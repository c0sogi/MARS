The solution implements a heterogeneous ensemble of three CNN architectures (ResNet-18, EfficientNet-B0, DenseNet-121) trained across 5 folds using Iterative Stratification. To ensure robustness and generalization on this small dataset, the training process utilizes Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) with a constant learning rate in the final phase, coupled with Gradient Accumulation to simulate a larger effective batch size. The pipeline performs full cross-validation to compute the AUC metric and conducts failure analysis by correlating prediction errors with input signal characteristics.
