My optimization strategy focuses on refining the ensemble composition and training duration based on the empirical failure of the previous run and relevant lessons learned.

1.  **Remove the MLP Component**: The MLP model trained on Bag-of-Audio-Words features achieved a validation AUC of ~0.33, which is worse than random guessing and significantly lower than the CNN components (~0.82-0.86). Including a significantly weaker model in an unweighted ensemble dilutes the overall performance. I will remove the MLP to prevent this contamination, relying solely on the robust heterogeneous CNN ensemble (ResNet18, EfficientNet-B0, DenseNet121). **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00048}**.

2.  **Restore Training Duration**: The previous run overrode the configuration to limit training to 20 epochs. Given the use of Mixup augmentation ($\alpha=0.4$) and the small dataset size, 20 epochs is insufficient for the models to converge effectively. I will remove this override to allow the models to train for the full 50 epochs specified in the config, ensuring better feature learning and convergence. **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00040}**.

3.  **Cleanup**: I will remove the associated data loading and initialization logic for the MLP to streamline the execution.
