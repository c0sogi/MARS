Increasing the height to 64 provides better frequency resolution and ensures that the feature maps retain more spatial information before the final pooling layer.
2.  **Upgrade to ResNet34**: I will replace `resnet18` with `resnet34` in `library/model.py`. Given the strong regularization (SpecAugment) and balanced data sampling, the model can benefit from the increased depth of ResNet34 to learn more complex hierarchical features without overfitting. 3.  **Optimize Training with Scheduler**: In `library/trainer.py`, I will switch the optimizer to `AdamW` (which handles weight decay more effectively) and introduce a `ReduceLROnPlateau` learning rate scheduler. This will allow the model to lower the learning rate when validation loss plateaus, enabling fine-grained weight updates to converge to a better minima.
