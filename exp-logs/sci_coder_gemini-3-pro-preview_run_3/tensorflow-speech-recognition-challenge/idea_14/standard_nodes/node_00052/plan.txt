2.  **Extend Training Schedule:** The previous run showed consistent improvement up to epoch 20 without plateauing. Increase `num_epochs` from 20 to 40 and `early_stopping_patience` from 5 to 10 to allow the model to fully converge.
3.  **Update Configuration:** Reflect these hyperparameters in `library/config.py` and `runfile.py`.
