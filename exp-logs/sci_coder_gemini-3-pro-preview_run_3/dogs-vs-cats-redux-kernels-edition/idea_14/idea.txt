Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons" and "Previous Ideas," I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with On-the-Fly Cross-Model Distillation**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Current Best" (Idea 8) by addressing the recurring bottleneck: the underperformance of the Transformer component (the "Third Pillar"). Previous attempts to replace Swin-Tiny with other Transformers (ViT, MaxViT) failed due to overfitting or optimization complexity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034, solution_lesson_node_00038). This solution introduces **Knowledge Distillation** to explicitly stabilize the training of the Transformer backbone using the robust CNNs as teachers, ensuring the ensemble benefits from architectural diversity without suffering from the convergence issues of Transformers on medium-sized datasets.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Triple Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of:
1.  **ResNet-50:** The "Standard CNN" anchor. Provides robust, texture-based feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** The "Modern CNN". Captures semantic context via large kernels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **MaxViT-Tiny:** The "Multi-Axis Transformer". **Structural Innovation.** We re-introduce MaxViT to replace Swin-Tiny. Unlike Swin's local window attention, MaxViT employs **Block (Local) and Grid (Global) Attention**, offering a superior global context mechanism.
    *   **Correction:** We explicitly strip away the "bells and whistles" (SWA, Multi-Sample Dropout) that confounded previous MaxViT attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034, solution_lesson_node_00037). We rely on **Distillation** to handle regularization.

**Initialization:**
*   ResNet-50: **`IMAGENET1K_V2`** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   ConvNeXt/MaxViT: Best available pre-trained weights (e.g., `in1k`).

### Data
**Preprocessing (Decoupled Pipelines):** Maintain the **Decoupled Input Pipeline** to maximize fidelity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Pipeline A (Teachers: ResNet, ConvNeXt):** Resize images to **256x256**. This preserves fine spatial details proven to improve CNN performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Pipeline B (Student: MaxViT):** Resize images to **224x224**. This aligns with the native grid structure of the Transformer, avoiding interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Interpolation:** Strictly use **Bicubic Interpolation** for all pipelines (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

**Augmentation:** Apply **Context-Preserving Augmentation**.
*   Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to ensure the subject is never cropped out (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   Apply `ColorJitter` with intensity $\ge 0.2$ to handle lighting variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Training
**Strategy:** Implement a **Two-Stage Distillation Pipeline**.

**Stage 1: Independent Teacher Training**
*   Train **ResNet-50** and **ConvNeXt-Small** independently on the full training set.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with Cosine Annealing.
*   **Loss:** BCEWithLogitsLoss (No Label Smoothing) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Duration:** 8-10 epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

**Stage 2: Distilled Student Training**
*   Train **MaxViT-Tiny** while freezing the trained ResNet-50 and ConvNeXt-Small to act as **Teachers**.
*   **Mechanism:** For each training batch, pass the images through the frozen Teachers (using Pipeline A resizing) to generate logits. Average these logits to form a "Soft Target."
*   **Loss Function:** Combine Hard and Soft losses:
    $$L = \alpha \cdot \text{BCE}(y_{pred}, y_{true}) + (1-\alpha) \cdot \text{KLDiv}(y_{pred}, y_{teacher})$$
    (e.g., $\alpha=0.5$).
*   **Rationale:** This transfers the robust, converged feature representations of the CNNs into the Transformer. It prevents the MaxViT from overfitting (a common failure mode, Cite solution_lesson_node_00038) while allowing it to learn the residual errors via its distinct Multi-Axis attention mechanism.

### Evaluation
**Inference & Aggregation:**
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** Generate probabilities using original and horizontally flipped images for all three models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Ensemble Averaging:** Calculate the final probability by taking the **arithmetic mean** of the outputs from the ResNet-50, ConvNeXt-Small, and the Distilled MaxViT-Tiny. This leverages the structural diversity of the architectures while ensuring the Transformer is fully stabilized.
