Based on the accumulated lessons, specifically the feasibility of **K-Fold Cross-Validation** within the compute budget (implied by Lesson 00031 runtime vs. 24h limit), the critical importance of **Convergence** (Lesson 00042), and the sensitivity of Log Loss to **Calibration** (Lesson 00012), I propose a **Stratified K-Fold Heterogeneous Ensemble with Decoupled Resolutions and OOF Temperature Scaling**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by:
1.  **Full Data Utilization:** Transitioning from a single train/val split to a **5-Fold Cross-Validation** pipeline. Unlike previous failed attempts (Idea 17), we will utilize the 24-hour budget to train every fold to full convergence, ensuring the ensemble learns from 100% of the data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
2.  **Calibration Innovation:** Introducing a **Temperature Scaling** stage. Instead of complex stacking (which overfits, Cite solution_lesson_node_00023) or simple averaging (which ignores calibration), we will optimize a scalar temperature parameter for each model using its Out-Of-Fold (OOF) logits. This directly minimizes Log Loss by correcting the overconfidence inherent in modern neural networks.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of three distinct backbones, replicated across **5 Stratified Folds** (Total 15 models). We retain the "Golden Trio" identified in Lesson 00040 for their maximized inductive bias diversity:
1.  **ResNet-50:** The "Standard CNN" anchor (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** The "Modern CNN" with large kernels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **MaxViT-Tiny:** The "Multi-Axis Transformer" with global grid attention (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).

**Initialization:** Initialize all models with the highest quality pre-trained weights available (e.g., `IMAGENET1K_V2` for ResNet) to adhere to the lesson that modern recipes outperform legacy weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing (Decoupled Pipelines):** Implement **Resolution Diversity** to maximize feature extraction scales without forcing a "lowest common denominator" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028, solution_lesson_node_00040).
1.  **Pipeline A (ResNet-50):** Resize to **256x256**.
2.  **Pipeline B (ConvNeXt-Small):** Resize to **288x288**.
3.  **Pipeline C (MaxViT-Tiny):** Resize to **224x224** (Native resolution).
*   **Interpolation:** Strictly use **Bicubic Interpolation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

**Augmentation:** Apply **Context-Preserving Augmentation** across all pipelines.
*   Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to prevent subject loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   Apply `ColorJitter` with intensity $\ge 0.2$ to handle lighting variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Training
**Strategy:** **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**.
*   Train an independent instance of each architecture on each fold. This ensures 100% data utilization and generates unbiased OOF predictions for calibration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
*   **Static Resolution:** Strictly avoid progressive resizing to ensure stable convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).

**Optimization (Asynchronous Schedules):**
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing** scheduler (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Function:** **BCEWithLogitsLoss** *without* Label Smoothing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Schedules:** Apply architecture-specific durations to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042):
    *   **ResNet-50 & ConvNeXt-Small:** Train for **8 epochs** per fold.
    *   **MaxViT-Tiny:** Train for **15 epochs** per fold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

### Evaluation
**Inference & Calibration Pipeline:**
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** Generate raw logits using original and horizontally flipped images for all 15 models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Quality Gating:** Calculate the OOF Log Loss for each model. Discard any model whose OOF loss exceeds a safety threshold (e.g., > 0.05) to prevent "Poison Pills" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
3.  **Temperature Scaling (Structural Innovation):**
    *   For each accepted model $m$, learn a single scalar parameter $T_m > 0$ that minimizes the Negative Log Likelihood (Log Loss) of the model's OOF logits divided by $T_m$.
    *   Apply this optimized $T_m$ to the model's Test Set logits: $P_{calibrated} = \sigma(Logits_{test} / T_m)$.
    *   **Rationale:** This aligns the model's confidence with its empirical accuracy on unseen data, explicitly optimizing the submission metric without the risk of overfitting associated with vector-based stackers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
4.  **Aggregation:** Calculate the final probability by taking the **arithmetic mean** of the *calibrated* probabilities from all passing models.
