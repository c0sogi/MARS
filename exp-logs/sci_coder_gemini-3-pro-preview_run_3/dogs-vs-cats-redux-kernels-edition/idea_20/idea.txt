Based on the accumulated lessons, specifically the potential of **K-Fold Cross-Validation** when fully converged (Lesson 00042), the danger of **"Poison Pill" models** in ensembles (Lesson 00043), and the failure of complex **Stacking** meta-learners (Lesson 00023), I propose a **Stratified K-Fold Heterogeneous Ensemble with OOF-Optimized Weighting**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution from a single-split simple average (Current Best) to a **Full-Coverage Cross-Validation** pipeline. Unlike previous failed attempts, this approach ensures full convergence for every fold and replaces the naive arithmetic mean with a **Data-Driven Weight Optimization** stage. This explicitly minimizes Log Loss using the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions, providing superior calibration without the overfitting risks of a Logistic Regression stacker.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of three distinct backbones, replicated across **5 Stratified Folds** (Total 15 models). We retain the "Golden Trio" identified in Lesson 00040 for their maximized inductive bias diversity:
1.  **ResNet-50:** The "Standard CNN" anchor (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** The "Modern CNN" with large kernels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **MaxViT-Tiny:** The "Multi-Axis Transformer" with global grid attention (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).

**Initialization:** Initialize all models with the highest quality pre-trained weights available (e.g., `IMAGENET1K_V2` for ResNet) to adhere to the lesson that modern recipes outperform legacy weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing (Decoupled Pipelines):** Implement **Resolution Diversity** to maximize feature extraction scales without forcing a "lowest common denominator" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028, solution_lesson_node_00040):
1.  **Pipeline A (ResNet-50):** Resize to **256x256**.
2.  **Pipeline B (ConvNeXt-Small):** Resize to **288x288**.
3.  **Pipeline C (MaxViT-Tiny):** Resize to **224x224** (Native resolution).

**Augmentation:** Apply **Context-Preserving Augmentation** across all pipelines.
*   Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to prevent subject loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   Apply `ColorJitter` with intensity $\ge 0.2$ to handle lighting variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   Strictly use **Bicubic Interpolation** for all resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Training
**Strategy:** **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**.
*   Train an independent instance of each architecture on each fold. This ensures the ensemble learns from 100% of the training data and generates unbiased OOF predictions for every image (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

**Optimization (Asynchronous Schedules):**
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing** scheduler (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Function:** **BCEWithLogitsLoss** *without* Label Smoothing, to avoid the "loss floor" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Schedules:** Apply architecture-specific durations to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042):
    *   **ResNet-50 & ConvNeXt-Small:** Train for **8 epochs** per fold.
    *   **MaxViT-Tiny:** Train for **15 epochs** per fold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

### Evaluation
**Inference & Aggregation:**
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** Generate probabilities using original and horizontally flipped images for all 15 models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Quality Gating:** Calculate the OOF Log Loss for each model. Discard any model whose OOF loss exceeds a safety threshold (e.g., > 0.05) to prevent the "Poison Pill" effect (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
3.  **OOF-Optimized Weighting (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Instead of a simple mean or a trained meta-learner (which failed in Lesson 00023), use **Constrained Convex Optimization** (e.g., SLSQP) to find the scalar weights $w_1, ..., w_N$ that minimize the Log Loss of the weighted average prediction on the **OOF dataset**.
    *   **Constraint:** $\sum w_i = 1$ and $w_i \ge 0$.
    *   This mathematically guarantees the ensemble is calibrated to the specific Log Loss metric on unseen data, leveraging the diversity of the 15 models optimally.
4.  **Final Prediction:** Apply the optimized weights to the Test Set predictions.
