The issue stems from the initialization of `best_acc` to `0.0` combined with a strict inequality check (`val_acc > best_acc`) in the `fit_classifier` function. In the demo run, the classifier achieved exactly `0.0` accuracy in the first epoch. Consequently, the condition `0.0 > 0.0` evaluated to `False`, causing the training loop to skip saving the model checkpoint and trigger early stopping immediately. By initializing `best_acc` to `-1.0`, we ensure that an accuracy of `0.0` is treated as an improvement, guaranteeing that the model is saved at least once.
