The `CropSizeError` occurs because the `A.RandomScale` augmentation can shrink the image dimensions below the required crop size of `(1024, 1024)` defined in `A.RandomCrop`, even if the original image was padded initially. To fix this, I will insert `A.PadIfNeeded` into the transformation pipeline in `library/dataset.py` immediately before `A.RandomCrop`. This ensures that the image is always large enough for the crop operation, regardless of prior scaling or rotation.
