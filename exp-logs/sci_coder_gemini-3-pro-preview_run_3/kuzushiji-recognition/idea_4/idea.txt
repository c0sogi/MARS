Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and previous attempts, I propose a **Native-Resolution Patch-Based Cascade** strategy. This solution addresses the critical resolution bottleneck identified in previous iterations while avoiding the data starvation and convergence pitfalls that caused the failure of the high-complexity architectures.

### Model
I propose a robust **Two-Stage Recognition Pipeline** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) that decouples localization from classification, structurally optimized for high-resolution document processing.

1.  **Stage 1: Native-Resolution Detector (Patch-Based CenterNet)**
    *   **Architecture:** I will utilize a **ResNet-34** backbone with a Feature Pyramid Network (FPN). Unlike previous successful attempts that resized the full page to $1024 \times 1024$ (causing small characters to vanish), this model will be designed to accept fixed-size **patches** (e.g., $1024 \times 1024$) cropped directly from the *original resolution* images.
    *   **Rationale:** This preserves the pixel density required to distinguish small characters, directly addressing the downscaling bottleneck (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011). By using ResNet-34 instead of the heavier HRNet attempted in Idea 3, we maintain computational feasibility and convergence stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Heads:** Standard CenterNet heads: Heatmap (Textness), Offset (Sub-pixel refinement), and Size (Width/Height).

2.  **Stage 2: High-Capacity Classifier (ResNet-50)**
    *   **Architecture:** I will upgrade the classifier from ResNet-34 to **ResNet-50**.
    *   **Rationale:** As the detector's recall improves via native-resolution processing, the system will encounter more "marginal" or difficult characters. To prevent the "Double Penalty" (False Positive + False Negative) caused by misclassifying these detections, the classifier's capacity must be increased to match the detector's improved throughput (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).

### Data
The data pipeline will be fundamentally shifted from "Global Resizing" to "Patch-Based Sampling."

1.  **Detector Data (Patch Sampling):**
    *   **Input:** Instead of resizing images, generate training samples by extracting random $1024 \times 1024$ crops from the full-resolution training images.
    *   **Volume:** Crucially, I will use the **Full Dataset** for training. I will explicitly avoid the data subsampling that caused the regression failure in previous tiled approaches (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Augmentation:**
        *   **Scale:** Apply aggressive random scaling ($\pm 30\%$) to the patches to force the model to learn scale invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
        *   **Rotation:** Strictly limit rotation to $\pm 5^\circ$ to respect the vertical structure of the text (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
        *   **Geometric:** Apply random shifts and flips (where appropriate) to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

2.  **Classifier Data:**
    *   **Input:** Extract character crops using Ground Truth bounding boxes.
    *   **Sampling:** Use **Class-Balanced Sampling** across the **Full Dataset**. I will not cap the total number of samples, ensuring that even rare classes (in the long tail of the 3,848 classes) have sufficient representation for the deep classifier to converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
1.  **Detector Training:**
    *   **Duration:** Train for **30+ epochs**. The regression heads (Size and Offset) require significantly longer to converge than the heatmap head. I will not stop early based on total loss but will ensure the regression loss plateaus (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   **Monitoring:** I will monitor **Heatmap Recall** and **Size L1 Loss** independently to ensure the model is optimizing for the business metric (finding centers) rather than just minimizing a proxy loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

2.  **Classifier Training:**
    *   Train independently using Cross-Entropy Loss with the class-balanced sampler.

### Evaluation
To leverage the patch-based training during inference:

1.  **Tiled Inference:**
    *   Split each high-resolution test image into overlapping tiles (e.g., $1024 \times 1024$ with 25% overlap).
    *   Run the Stage 1 Detector on each tile to locate characters at native resolution.
2.  **Global NMS:**
    *   Project the tile-relative coordinates back to the global image space.
    *   Apply **Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS)** to merge duplicate detections from overlapping regions.
3.  **Crop & Classify:**
    *   Use the refined global bounding boxes to extract crops from the original image.
    *   Pass these crops to the Stage 2 ResNet-50 to obtain the final Unicode labels.
