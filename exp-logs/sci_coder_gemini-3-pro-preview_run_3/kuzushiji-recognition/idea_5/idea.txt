Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and previous attempts, I propose a **Global-Context Scale-Preserved Pipeline with Background Suppression**. This solution structurally evolves the "Decoupled Two-Stage" approach (Idea 2) by addressing the "Double Penalty" phenomenon (Lesson 00020) and the geometric preprocessing failures (Lesson 00022).

# Model

I propose a **Two-Stage Verification Pipeline** that decouples global localization from fine-grained recognition and verification.

1.  **Stage 1: Global Context Detector (CenterNet ResNet-34)**
    *   **Architecture:** I will retain the **ResNet-34 FPN** backbone for the detector. This specific depth provides the optimal balance between feature hierarchy and convergence speed, overcoming the underfitting seen in lighter models while avoiding the training instability of heavier backbones like HRNet on this dataset size (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
    *   **Heads:** The model will utilize three heads: **Heatmap** (Textness), **Offset** (Sub-pixel refinement), and **Size** (Width/Height regression).

2.  **Stage 2: Verification Classifier (ResNet-50 with Background Class)**
    *   **Structural Innovation:** I will upgrade the classifier to a **ResNet-50** to provide the capacity needed for 3,848 classes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). Crucially, I will modify the output layer to predict **3,849 classes** (3,848 Characters + 1 Background).
    *   **Rationale:** Previous solutions suffered from a "Double Penalty" where false positives from the detector were forced into a character class, counting as both a False Positive and potentially masking a False Negative (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020). By explicitly modeling "Background/Noise," the classifier acts as a verification filter, rejecting low-quality detections that pass the Stage 1 threshold.

# Data

The data pipeline will be strictly bifurcated to respect the "Decoupled Resolution" principle (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).

1.  **Detector Data (Global Context & Aspect Ratio):**
    *   **Preprocessing:** Input images will be resized such that the longest side is 1024 pixels, and the shorter side is **padded with zeros** to create a $1024 \times 1024$ square. This strictly preserves the aspect ratio, preventing the geometric distortion that caused regression failures in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Augmentation:** I will apply **Scale Augmentation** ($\pm 30\%$) to force the model to learn scale invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011). However, **Rotation** will be strictly limited to $\pm 5^\circ$ to respect the vertical structure of the text columns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

2.  **Classifier Data (High-Fidelity Crops):**
    *   **Source:** Crops will be extracted from the **Original High-Resolution Images**, not the downscaled detector inputs, ensuring maximum detail for character recognition (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Negative Sampling:** In addition to ground truth character crops, I will generate **Background Crops** (random patches or non-text regions) to train the 3,849th class.
    *   **Sampling:** I will use **Class-Balanced Sampling** on the full dataset to ensuring rare characters (and the background class) are adequately represented (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

# Training

1.  **Detector Training:**
    *   **Duration:** The detector will be trained for **30+ epochs**. I will explicitly monitor the **Size (Regression) Loss** and ensure it plateaus before stopping, as this task converges significantly slower than the heatmap task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   **Metric:** I will track **Heatmap Recall** rather than just total loss to avoid the "Proxy Metric Trap" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

2.  **Classifier Training:**
    *   The classifier will be trained using Cross-Entropy Loss. The inclusion of the "Background" class transforms the problem from pure classification to **Detection-Verification**, allowing the model to learn the decision boundary between "Text" and "Noise."

# Evaluation

1.  **Global Detection:** Resize the test image (preserving aspect ratio with padding) and pass it through the Stage 1 Detector.
2.  **Coordinate Mapping:** Map the detected center points and bounding boxes from the $1024 \times 1024$ padded space back to the **original high-resolution image** coordinates.
3.  **High-Res Cropping:** Extract crops from the original image using the predicted bounding boxes.
4.  **Classification & Suppression:** Pass crops to the Stage 2 ResNet-50.
    *   If the predicted class is **Background (Index 3848)**, discard the prediction.
    *   Otherwise, submit the predicted character label and the Stage 1 center coordinates.
