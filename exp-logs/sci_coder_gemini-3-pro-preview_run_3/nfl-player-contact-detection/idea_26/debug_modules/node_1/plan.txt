The issue stems from a mismatch between the column names in the right DataFrame (which are suffixed with `_p1` or `_p2`) and the keys specified in the `right_on` argument of `pd.merge`. The `add_suffix` method renames all columns, including the join keys `game_play` and `step`, to `game_play_p1` and `step_p1`. Consequently, attempting to merge on the original names raises a `KeyError`. Additionally, to prevent potential type mismatches (Cite debug_lesson_13) and schema pollution (Cite debug_lesson_11), I will explicitly cast `nfl_player_id` to string and ensure the redundant suffixed join keys are dropped after the merge.
