Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned," specifically the critical need for **Temporal Window Flattening** over instantaneous derivatives (Cite solution_lesson_node_00086), the necessity of **Hybrid Context** (Raw + Invariant) for ground impacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091, solution_lesson_node_00055), and the power of **Cross-Modal Consistency** for interactions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080), I propose the **Hybrid-Context Dual-Stream GBDT with Cross-Modal Consistency Pyramids**.

This solution structurally evolves the previous architectures by integrating the **Consistency Logic** of Idea 26 with the **Robust Temporal Flattening** of Idea 28. It posits that "Contact" is best detected by verifying the consistency between Physical Closure (Tracking) and Visual Looming (Video) over a temporal window for interactions, while "Impact" is best detected by matching the shape of Ego-Centric Dynamics within a Field-Centric Context.

# Model

The architecture consists of two specialized **XGBoost** classifiers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016) operating on physically disentangled feature spaces with asymmetric complexity constraints.

1.  **Stream A: The Interaction Model (Consistency & Verification)**
    *   **Scope:** Predicts contact between two players (`player2 != 'G'`).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Conditional Early-Fusion GBDT** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Structural Innovation:** **Cross-Modal Consistency Pyramids**.
        *   Instead of treating Tracking and Visuals as independent signals, this stream explicitly models their **Divergence** over time.
        *   It flattens the difference between **Physical Closure Rate** (Tracking) and **Visual Looming Rate** (Video) at exponential lags. This allows the tree to identify "Phantom Approaches" (where tracking shows proximity but video shows no looming due to depth mismatch) without relying on instantaneous, noisy derivatives (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080).
    *   **Base Features:** Fuses **Relational Scalars** (for robust proximity), **System Energy** (for collision intensity), and **Visual Consensus** (for view disambiguation).

2.  **Stream B: The Impact Model (Hybrid Context & Dynamics)**
    *   **Scope:** Predicts contact between a player and the ground (`player2 == 'G'`).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Uni-Modal Hybrid GBDT**.
    *   **Feature Strategy:** **Hybrid Context + Ego-Dynamics**.
        *   Combines **Field-Centric Anchors** (Raw $x, y, v, a$) to capture environmental priors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091) with **Ego-Centric Dynamics** (Surge/Sway Jerk) to capture the invariant signature of a fall (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).
        *   Crucially, these are **Flattened** over the temporal window (Cite solution_lesson_node_00086) to match the "shape" of the impact.
    *   **Constraint:** Explicitly blocks all visual features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

# Data

**Preprocessing:**
*   **Pipeline Integrity:** Implement **hash-based cache invalidation** derived from the full feature configuration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00093).
*   **Strict Schema Validation:** Raise a `RuntimeError` if any expected feature column is missing or zero-filled at the end of the pipeline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00088).
*   **Validation Mapping:** Explicitly map the `validation` mode to read from `train_player_tracking.csv` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).

**Feature Engineering - Stream A (Interaction):**
1.  **Relational Primitives:** Calculate **Euclidean Distance** and **Closure Rate** (Finite Difference of Distance) at sparse exponential lags ($t, t\pm1, t\pm2, t\pm4, t\pm8, t\pm15$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042, solution_lesson_node_00080).
2.  **Visual Consensus:** Flatten `Sideline_IoU`, `Endzone_IoU`, `Max_IoU`, `Min_IoU`, and `IoU_Diff` at sparse lags ($t, t\pm4, t\pm8, t\pm15$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).
3.  **Cross-Modal Consistency (The Innovation):**
    *   Calculate **Visual Looming Rate**: Finite difference of `Max_IoU`.
    *   Calculate **Consistency Metric**: `Normalized_Closure_Rate` - `Visual_Looming_Rate`.
    *   **Flatten** this metric over the temporal window. A sustained high value indicates a perspective artifact.
4.  **System Energy:** Flattened pyramids of Absolute Speed and Scalar Acceleration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00075).
5.  **Imputation:** Use **Left Join + Sentinel** (`-999`) for missing visuals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

**Feature Engineering - Stream B (Impact):**
1.  **Hybrid Context:**
    *   **Field-Centric:** Flattened pyramids of `x`, `y`, `speed`, `acceleration` (scalar), `direction`, `orientation`. Retains robust raw data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
    *   **Ego-Centric Physics:** For every timestep:
        *   Project Velocity $v$ onto Orientation $\hat{o}$ $\rightarrow$ $v_{surge}, v_{sway}$.
        *   Differentiate $v_{surge/sway}$ $\rightarrow$ $a_{surge}, a_{sway}$.
        *   Differentiate $a_{surge/sway}$ $\rightarrow$ $j_{surge}, j_{sway}$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).
    *   **Flattening:** Flatten these derived sequences to capture the trajectory of the fall (Cite solution_lesson_node_00086).
2.  **Exclusion:** Explicitly block all visual features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

# Training

*   **Sampling:** Apply **Targeted Majority Undersampling**. Retain **100%** of the positive class and randomly subsample the negative class to a 10:1 ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Algorithm:** Train both streams using **XGBoost** with the `gpu_hist` tree method (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Asymmetric Regularization:**
    *   **Stream A:** **Standard Depth** (`max_depth=6`) to model complex cross-modal interactions.
    *   **Stream B:** **Shallow Depth** (`max_depth=6` to `8`). By providing the robust, flattened Ego-Centric trajectories and explicit Jerk, we avoid the need for deep trees that overfit sensor noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00070, solution_lesson_node_00045).
*   **Objective:** Minimize **Binary Logistic Loss**.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:**
    *   Route test samples to Stream A or Stream B based on the `contact_id`.
    *   Stream A receives the "Consistency + Energy + Visual" vector. Stream B receives the "Hybrid Context + Ego-Dynamics" vector.
2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Thresholding:** Decouple the decision boundary from the probability. Perform a linear search on the Validation set to find the optimal probability threshold **independently for each stream** that maximizes the Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Submission:** Apply these specific thresholds to the Test set predictions.
