To optimize the Dual-Stream GBDT pipeline, I will focus on refining the feature sets for both streams based on the failure analysis and lessons learned. For Stream A (Interaction), I will introduce explicit relative geometric features (`cos_sim_v`, `cos_sim_o`) and `time_to_collision` to better resolve trajectory alignments and looming dynamics, addressing the limitations of relying solely on visual IoU (Cite Lesson 00074). For Stream B (Impact), I will enforce strict coordinate invariance by removing absolute field-centric features (`x`, `y`, `direction`, `orientation`) and reducing the model depth from 8 to 6. This aims to force the model to learn from translation-invariant ego-centric dynamics (`jerk`, `surge`, `sway`) and prevent overfitting to location-specific noise (Cite Lesson 00075, Lesson 00070).
