The issue is that the `early_stopping_rounds` parameter is passed to the `fit` method of `XGBClassifier`, which causes a `TypeError` in recent versions of XGBoost where this parameter has been moved to the constructor. To fix this, I will modify `library/model_trainer.py` to pass `early_stopping_rounds` during the initialization of `XGBClassifier` and remove it from the `fit` call (Cite debug_lesson_18).
