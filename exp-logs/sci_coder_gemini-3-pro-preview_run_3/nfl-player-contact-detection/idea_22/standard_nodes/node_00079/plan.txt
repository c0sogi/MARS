To optimize the solution, I will focus on three key improvements derived from the failure analysis and lessons learned:

1.  **Fix Overfitting via Early Stopping**: The training logs indicated that both models continued training well past their convergence points (Validation LogLoss increased while training continued). I will explicitly pass `early_stopping_rounds` to the XGBoost `fit` method to halt training when generalization performance degrades.
2.  **Enhance Stream A (Interaction) Features**: I will add `cos_rel_dir` (cosine similarity of velocity vectors) to explicitly capture the relative trajectory alignment. This addresses the need for "Relative Geometric Features" to distinguish between collision courses and parallel paths (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00074}).
3.  **Enhance Stream B (Impact) Features**: I will add `angular_velocity` (rate of change of orientation) to the ego-dynamics feature set. This feature is a critical indicator of loss of control (tumbling) and ground impact, as identified in previous failure analyses (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00031}, {solution_lesson_node_00050}).

These changes are targeted to improve the model's generalization and signal-to-noise ratio without increasing architectural complexity.
