The `TypeError: XGBClassifier.fit() got an unexpected keyword argument 'early_stopping_rounds'` indicates that the installed version of XGBoost (3.0.5) adheres to the updated Scikit-Learn wrapper API, where training parameters like `early_stopping_rounds` must be passed to the model constructor (`__init__`) rather than the `fit` method. This issue is explicitly described in **Cite debug_lesson_5**. To fix this, I will modify `library/model_trainer.py` to pass `early_stopping_rounds` during the initialization of `XGBClassifier` and remove it from the `fit` call.
