Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned," specifically the need for **Hybrid Context** in ground impacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091), the superiority of **Finite-Difference Positional Differentials** for interactions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080), and the critical importance of **Pipeline Integrity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00088, solution_lesson_node_00054), I propose the **Physically-Consistent Hybrid-Context Dual-Stream GBDT**.

This solution structurally evolves the previous architectures by formalizing a **Three-Layer Feature Hierarchy** (Raw Anchor $\rightarrow$ Derived Physics $\rightarrow$ Temporal Trajectory). It corrects the "Over-Simplification" errors of previous iterations (e.g., stripping absolute coordinates) and implements the "Cross-Modal Verification" logic to gate perspective artifacts in the interaction stream.

# Model

The architecture consists of two specialized **XGBoost** classifiers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016) operating on physically disentangled feature spaces with asymmetric complexity constraints.

1.  **Stream A: The Interaction Model (Consistency & Energy)**
    *   **Scope:** Predicts contact between two players (`player2 != 'G'`).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Conditional Early-Fusion GBDT** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Philosophy:** **Verification & Energy**. Interactions are defined by the convergence of trajectories. This stream fuses **Relational Scalars** (for robust proximity), **System Energy** (for collision intensity), and **Cross-Modal Verification** (comparing Physical Closure to Visual Looming to detect artifacts).

2.  **Stream B: The Impact Model (Hybrid Context & Dynamics)**
    *   **Scope:** Predicts contact between a player and the ground (`player2 == 'G'`).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Uni-Modal Hybrid GBDT**.
    *   **Philosophy:** **Hybrid Context**. Ground impacts are invariant to field position but statistically correlated with it. This stream combines **Field-Centric Anchors** (Raw Data) with **Finite-Difference Ego-Dynamics** (Invariant Physics), flattened over time to capture the "shape" of the fall (Cite solution_lesson_node_00086).

# Data

**Preprocessing:**
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   Implement **Configuration-Hashed Cache Invalidation** to prevent stale data loading (Cite solution_lesson_node_00093).
    *   Enforce **Strict Schema Validation** at the end of the pipeline; raise a `RuntimeError` if any feature is missing/zero-filled (Cite solution_lesson_node_00088).
    *   Explicitly map the `validation` mode to read from `train_player_tracking.csv` to ensure correct data merging (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).

**Feature Engineering - Stream A (Interaction):**
1.  **Relational Primitives:** Calculate **Euclidean Distance** and **Closure Rate** (Finite Difference of Distance: $-\Delta d / \Delta t$) at sparse exponential lags ($t, t\pm1... t\pm15$). Explicitly reject vector projections (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080, solution_lesson_node_00042).
2.  **System Energy:** Flattened pyramids of **Absolute Speed** and **Scalar Acceleration** for both players. This restores the energy context lost in invariant models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00075).
3.  **Visual Consensus & Verification:**
    *   Calculate `Sideline_IoU` and `Endzone_IoU` at sparse lags.
    *   Compute **Consensus Metrics**: `Max_IoU`, `Min_IoU`, and `IoU_Diff` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).
    *   **Cross-Modal Verification:** Calculate the difference between `Normalized_Closure_Rate` and `Visual_Looming_Rate` ($\Delta \text{Max\_IoU} / \Delta t$). Flatten this to allow the tree to identify perspective artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080).
    *   **Imputation:** Use **Left Join + Sentinel** (`-999`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

**Feature Engineering - Stream B (Impact):**
1.  **Hybrid Context:**
    *   **Field-Centric Anchor:** Flattened pyramids of `x`, `y`, `speed`, `acceleration` (scalar), `direction`, `orientation`. Retains environmental priors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091).
    *   **Ego-Centric Dynamics:** For every timestep:
        *   Project Velocity $v$ onto Orientation $\hat{o}$ $\rightarrow$ $v_{surge}, v_{sway}$.
        *   Differentiate $v_{surge/sway}$ $\rightarrow$ $a_{surge}, a_{sway}$.
        *   Differentiate $a_{surge/sway}$ $\rightarrow$ $j_{surge}, j_{sway}$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).
    *   **Flattening:** Flatten these derived sequences to capture the trajectory of the fall (Cite solution_lesson_node_00086).
2.  **Exclusion:** Explicitly block all visual features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

# Training

*   **Sampling:** Apply **Targeted Majority Undersampling**. Retain **100%** of the positive class and randomly subsample the negative class to a 10:1 ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Algorithm:** Train both streams using **XGBoost** with the `gpu_hist` tree method (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Asymmetric Regularization:**
    *   **Stream A:** **Standard Depth** (`max_depth=6`) to model complex cross-modal interactions.
    *   **Stream B:** **Shallow Depth** (`max_depth=6` to `8`). By providing the robust, flattened Ego-Centric trajectories and explicit Jerk, we avoid the need for deep trees that overfit sensor noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00070, solution_lesson_node_00045).
*   **Objective:** Minimize **Binary Logistic Loss**.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:**
    *   Route test samples to Stream A or Stream B based on the `contact_id`.
    *   Stream A receives the "Verification + Energy + Visual" vector. Stream B receives the "Hybrid Context + Ego-Dynamics" vector.
2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Thresholding:** Decouple the decision boundary from the probability. Perform a linear search on the Validation set to find the optimal probability threshold **independently for each stream** that maximizes the Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Submission:** Apply these specific thresholds to the Test set predictions.
