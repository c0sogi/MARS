The optimization strategy focuses on two key areas: data quantity and feature enrichment, leveraging insights from previous lessons.

1.  **Increase Data Quantity via Undersampling Adjustment**: The `UNDERSAMPLE_RATIO` will be increased from 10.0 to 20.0. By providing the XGBoost model with twice as many "negative" (non-contact) examples, we aim to improve the model's ability to distinguish between actual contact and close-proximity "near misses," which the failure analysis identified as the primary source of error (high correlation with `distance`).
2.  **Enrich Feature Space (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027)**: We will explicitly add `cos_sim_orient` (Orientation Similarity) and `pose_motion_align` (for both players) to the Stream A (Player-Player) feature set. Lesson 27 highlighted that these features—capturing the alignment between players' facing directions and their movement vectors—are critical for distinguishing controlled athletic maneuvers from the instability characteristic of contact, yet they were missing from the Stream A configuration in the previous iteration.
3.  **Update Submission Threshold**: The submission logic is updated to reflect the new benchmark MCC of `0.6565613438092561`.
