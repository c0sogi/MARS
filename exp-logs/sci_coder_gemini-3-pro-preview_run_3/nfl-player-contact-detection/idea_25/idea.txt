Based on the progression of empirical findings—specifically the need to disambiguate **Perspective Artifacts** in interactions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074) and the importance of **Rotational Dynamics** in impacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068)—I propose the **Cross-Modal Consistency Dual-Stream GBDT with Rotational-Difference Dynamics**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Differential-Physics" architecture (Idea 24) by introducing a **Cross-Modal Alignment** layer to Stream A (to detect visual illusions) and enriching Stream B with **Rotational Energy** features (to better distinguish controlled cuts from uncontrolled tumbles).

# Model

The architecture consists of two specialized **XGBoost** classifiers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016) operating on physically disentangled feature spaces.

1.  **Stream A: The Interaction Model (Consistency & Convergence)**
    *   **Scope:** Predicts contact between two players (`player2 != 'G'`).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Conditional Early-Fusion GBDT** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Structural Innovation:** **Cross-Modal Trajectory Alignment**.
        *   Previous iterations treated Tracking and Visuals as independent signals. This model explicitly engineers the **Divergence** between them.
        *   **Logic:** If `Closure_Rate` (Tracking) is high but `IoU_Derivative` (Visual) is near zero, it implies the players are passing at different depths (z-axis) relative to the camera—a "Phantom Approach."
        *   **Features:** `Visual_Kinematic_Ratio` (Closure Rate / IoU Change) and `View_Disagreement_Trend` (Rate of change of `|Sideline - Endzone|` IoU).
    *   **Base Features:** Retains **Translational Differentials** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080), **System Energy** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00075), and **Visual Consensus Pyramids** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).

2.  **Stream B: The Impact Model (Rotational Inertia)**
    *   **Scope:** Predicts contact between a player and the ground (`player2 == 'G'`).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Uni-Modal Invariant GBDT**.
    *   **Structural Innovation:** **Rotational Energy Profile**.
        *   Augments the Finite-Difference pipeline with **Lateral Kinetic Energy** terms ($0.5 \cdot v_{sway}^2$).
        *   **Rationale:** Controlled athletic cuts involve high acceleration but controlled sway. Uncontrolled impacts (tumbles) often exhibit high **Sway Energy** combined with high **Sway Jerk**. Explicitly providing the energy term helps the tree distinguish "hard cut" from "blowout" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).
    *   **Constraint:** Maintains **Strict Invariance** (No `x`, `y`, `visuals`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00075, solution_lesson_node_00034).

# Data

**Preprocessing:**
*   **Pipeline Integrity:** Implement **hash-based cache invalidation** derived from the full feature configuration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Validation Mapping:** Explicitly map the `validation` mode to read from `train_player_tracking.csv` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).

**Feature Engineering - Stream A (Interaction):**
1.  **Cross-Modal Alignment (The Innovation):**
    *   Calculate **Visual Looming Rate**: Finite difference of `Max_IoU` over the window ($\Delta \text{IoU} / \Delta t$).
    *   Calculate **Physical Closure Rate**: Finite difference of `Distance` ($\Delta d / \Delta t$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080).
    *   **Alignment Features:** `Looming_Closure_Product` (Do they agree?) and `Looming_Closure_Ratio` (Magnitude mismatch).
2.  **Base Primitives:**
    *   **System Energy:** Flattened pyramids of Absolute Speed/Accel (Cite solution_lesson_node_00075).
    *   **Relational Scalars:** Distance, Relative Speed (Scalar) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).
    *   **Visual Consensus:** Max/Min/Diff IoU at sparse lags (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).
    *   **Imputation:** Left Join + Sentinel (`-999`) for visuals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

**Feature Engineering - Stream B (Impact):**
1.  **Rotational-Difference Dynamics:**
    *   Project Velocity ($v$) onto Orientation ($\hat{o}$) $\rightarrow$ $v_{surge}, v_{sway}$.
    *   **Rotational Energy:** Calculate $v_{sway}^2$ and $v_{surge}^2$.
    *   **Ego-Jerk:** Differentiate $v \rightarrow a \rightarrow j$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).
    *   Flatten these features to expose the "Energy-Jerk" phase space.
2.  **Invariant Baseline:** Scalar Speed/Accel (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
3.  **Exclusion:** Block all absolute coordinates and visual features.

# Training

*   **Sampling:** Apply **Targeted Majority Undersampling**. Retain **100%** of the positive class and randomly subsample the negative class to a 10:1 ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Algorithm:** Train both streams using **XGBoost** with the `gpu_hist` tree method.
*   **Asymmetric Regularization:**
    *   **Stream A:** Standard depth (`max_depth=6`) to model complex cross-modal interactions.
    *   **Stream B:** **Shallow Depth** (`max_depth=6` to `8`) to prevent overfitting sensor noise, relying on the robust `Ego-Jerk` and `Sway-Energy` signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00070).
*   **Objective:** Minimize **Binary Logistic Loss**.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:**
    *   Route test samples to Stream A or Stream B based on the `contact_id`.
    *   Stream A receives the "Consistency + Convergence" vector. Stream B receives the "Rotational Energy + Dynamics" vector.
2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Thresholding:** Decouple the decision boundary from the probability. Perform a linear search on the Validation set to find the optimal probability threshold **independently for each stream** that maximizes the Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Submission:** Apply these specific thresholds to the Test set predictions.
