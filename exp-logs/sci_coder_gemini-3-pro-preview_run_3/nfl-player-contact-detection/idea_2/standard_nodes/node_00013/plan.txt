Subtracting these two arrays triggers NumPy to broadcast them into an `(N, N)` matrix, requiring terabytes of memory for the large validation set. To fix this, I will flatten `y_true` to ensure both arrays are 1D vectors of shape `(N,)`, allowing for the intended element-wise subtraction.
