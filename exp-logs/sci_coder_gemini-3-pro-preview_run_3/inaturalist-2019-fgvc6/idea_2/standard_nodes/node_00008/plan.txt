I will implement a strategy focused on maximizing performance at high resolution while avoiding the pitfalls of class balancing on this specific metric.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Remove Stage 3 (Class-Balanced Fine-Tuning):** As identified in **Lesson 00002**, class-balanced sampling improved rare class performance but degraded the overall Top-1 Error (from 22.89% to 23.19%) because it sacrificed accuracy on frequent classes. Since the metric is micro-average error, I will remove this stage.
2.  **Optimize Stage 2 (High-Resolution Training):** Leveraging **Lesson 00007**, which highlights the importance of input resolution, I will extend the training duration of the 384x384 stage from 2 to 5 epochs. This allows the model more time to adapt to the higher resolution features. I will also enable **Label Smoothing (0.1)** in this stage (previously 0.0) to provide better regularization during this longer fine-tuning period.
3.  **Extend Stage 1:** I will increase Stage 1 training from 3 to 5 epochs to ensure a more robust initial representation, utilizing the available 4-hour runtime (estimated total runtime ~3.2 hours).
