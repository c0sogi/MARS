To optimize the performance of the DnCNN denoising model, I will focus on increasing the **data density** available for training.

### Strategy
1.  **Increase Data Density (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013):** The previous solution used a stride of 20 for extracting 50x50 patches. By reducing the stride to 10, we will increase the number of training patches by approximately a factor of 4. This increased density will provide the model with significantly more examples of local image structures and noise patterns, helping it to better resolve the fine details of the text where the failure analysis showed it was struggling (negative correlation with intensity).
2.  **Adjust Training Duration:** Since the effective dataset size per epoch will quadruple, maintaining 30 epochs would quadruple the training time. To balance performance and runtime, I will reduce the number of epochs to 20. This still results in significantly more total gradient updates (approx. 2.6x) compared to the previous run, ensuring sufficient convergence while keeping execution time well within limits.
3.  **Update Threshold:** I will update the validation threshold to the current best RMSE (`0.011577641381826402`) to ensure a submission is generated only if we achieve an improvement.
