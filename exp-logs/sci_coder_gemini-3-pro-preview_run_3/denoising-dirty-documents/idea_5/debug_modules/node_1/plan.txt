The execution failure is primarily caused by the inclusion of the `verbose` argument in the initialization of `torch.optim.lr_scheduler.ReduceLROnPlateau`, which has been deprecated and removed in recent PyTorch versions. Cite debug_lesson_2. Furthermore, `library/model.py` invokes its `main()` function at the module level, causing the training logic (and the associated error) to execute immediately when the module is imported by `runfile.py`. To resolve these issues, I will remove the `verbose=True` argument from the scheduler initialization in both `library/model.py` and `library/train.py`, and I will wrap the execution of `main()` in `library/model.py` within a standard `if __name__ == "__main__":` guard.
