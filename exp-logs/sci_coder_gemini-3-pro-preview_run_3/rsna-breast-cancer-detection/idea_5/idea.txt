# Optimized Strategy: Transformer-Enhanced Multi-View MIL with Auxiliary Multi-Task Learning

This proposal introduces a structural evolution by upgrading the aggregation mechanism from simple attention to a **Transformer-based Relational Module** and incorporating **Auxiliary Multi-Task Learning**. This approach leverages the spatial correlation between different mammographic views (CC and MLO) and utilizes rich metadata (density, biopsy) to regularize the model, while strictly adhering to the calibration protocols derived from the lessons learned.

### Model
*   **Backbone Architecture**: Retain **EfficientNetV2-Small** as the feature extractor for each image instance. It provides an optimal balance of parameter efficiency and texture recognition capability.
*   **Structural Innovation: Transformer MIL Aggregator**:
    *   Instead of the Gated Attention mechanism (which computes a weighted sum), implement a **Transformer Encoder** layer to aggregate features from the "bag" of images (e.g., CC and MLO views of the same breast).
    *   **Mechanism**: The feature vectors from the backbone are treated as a sequence. A learnable `[CLS]` token is appended to the sequence. Self-attention layers allow the model to capture non-linear relationships between views (e.g., a suspicious mass in the MLO view reinforcing a subtle opacity in the CC view).
    *   The final representation of the `[CLS]` token serves as the holistic "breast embedding."
*   **Structural Innovation: Multi-Task Heads**:
    *   **Primary Head**: A classification layer on the breast embedding to predict `cancer`.
    *   **Auxiliary Heads**: Separate classification layers to predict `density` (4 classes) and `biopsy` (binary). These targets are strongly correlated with cancer risk and provide dense supervisory signals to the backbone, preventing overfitting to the rare cancer class.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing**:
    *   **ROI-Aware Cropping**: Apply automated thresholding (e.g., Otsu's method) to generate a bounding box around the breast tissue and crop out the background. This maximizes the effective resolution of the tissue.
    *   **Bag Construction**: Group images by `patient_id` and `laterality` to form bags. Each bag contains all available views for a specific breast.
*   **Augmentation**: Apply spatial (flip, rotate) and intensity (brightness, contrast) augmentations to individual images within the bag.

### Training
*   **Strategic Retention: Balanced Bag Sampling**:
    *   Utilize a sampler that constructs batches with a **50/50 ratio** of positive (cancer) to negative bags. This ensures the model receives consistent gradient updates for the minority class, which is essential for learning discriminative features. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}.
*   **Multi-Task Loss Function**:
    *   Train the model end-to-end using a weighted sum of losses:
        $$ L_{total} = L_{cancer} + \lambda_1 L_{density} + \lambda_2 L_{biopsy} $$
    *   This forces the shared backbone to learn features that are not just discriminative for cancer but also descriptive of tissue structure (density) and pathology (biopsy).

### Evaluation
*   **Strategic Retention: Analytical Prior Correction**:
    *   Since the model is trained on a balanced distribution ($P_{train}=0.5$) but evaluated on the natural distribution ($P_{test} \approx 0.02$), the raw output probabilities will be uncalibrated.
    *   Apply **Analytical Log-Odds Correction** to the cancer logits before the sigmoid activation:
        $$ \text{Logit}_{corrected} = \text{Logit}_{pred} + \log\left(\frac{P_{test}}{1 - P_{test}}\right) - \log\left(\frac{P_{train}}{1 - P_{train}}\right) $$
    *   This mathematically adjusts the decision boundary to align with the true prevalence, ensuring the **Probabilistic F1 (pF1)** score is maximized. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}.
*   **Inference**: The model processes the bag of images for a target `prediction_id` and outputs a single, calibrated probability.
