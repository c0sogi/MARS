The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurring in the second fold (Fold 1) suggests that GPU memory from the first fold (Fold 0) is not being fully released. Specifically, the `checkpoint` variable, which loads the model weights onto the GPU for inference at the end of the fold loop, persists in the local scope of the loop. Since it is not explicitly deleted in the cleanup block, the VRAM occupied by the previous fold's weights remains allocated while the script attempts to initialize the next fold's model, leading to an OOM crash. I will fix this by adding `checkpoint` to the `del` statement at the end of the fold iteration to ensure all GPU tensors are freed before the next fold begins (Cite debug_lesson_6).
