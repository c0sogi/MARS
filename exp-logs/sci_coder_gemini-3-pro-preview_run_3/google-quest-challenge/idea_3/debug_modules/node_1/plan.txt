To fix this, I will explicitly convert `alphas` to a mutable list in `library/ridge_head.py` before passing it to the model. Additionally, I will update the default configuration in `library/config.py` and the override in `runfile.py` to define `ridge_alphas` as a list, ensuring consistency and preventing this type error.
