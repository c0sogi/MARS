To improve the model's performance, I will implement a targeted upgrade to the ensemble architecture by replacing the `roberta-base` stream with `microsoft/deberta-v3-base`. DeBERTa V3 is a stronger backbone for natural language understanding tasks due to its disentangled attention mechanism and relative position embeddings, which should provide higher quality embeddings for the regression head.

I will maintain the Late Fusion strategy (averaging predictions) and the Segment-Aware Cross-Encoder architecture, as these have proven effective (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015, solution_lesson_node_00012). I will also adjust the learning rate for the DeBERTa stream to `1.5e-5` to ensure stable fine-tuning, as DeBERTa can be sensitive to higher learning rates.
