The issue is that the `train_loop` function in `library/engine.py` initializes `best_score` to `0.0` and only saves the model if the validation score is strictly greater than `best_score`. In the demonstration run, the model achieved a MAP@5 of `0.0` (common in early training or random initialization), so the condition `0.0 > 0.0` evaluated to `False`, preventing the model from being saved. To fix this, I will initialize `best_score` to `-1.0` so that even a score of `0.0` triggers the initial save.
