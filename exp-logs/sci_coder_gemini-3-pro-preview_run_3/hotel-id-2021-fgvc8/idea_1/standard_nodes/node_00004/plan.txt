To improve the model's performance on the long-tail hotel identification task, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Architecture Upgrade**: I will switch the backbone from ResNet18 to **ResNet50**. The deeper network provides higher capacity to distinguish between the 7,770 fine-grained classes.
2.  **Data Augmentation**: I will introduce `RandomResizedCrop` and `RandomHorizontalFlip` to the training transforms. This increases the effective diversity of the training data, which is a crucial distribution-aware intervention for tail classes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
3.  **ArcFace Tuning**: I will increase the ArcFace scale parameter `s` from 30.0 to **64.0**. A larger scale is necessary for high-cardinality datasets to ensure sufficient gradient magnitude and angular separation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
4.  **Learning Rate Schedule**: I will add a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler to the training loop in `runfile.py` to refine the weights as training progresses.
5.  **Training Duration**: I will increase the number of epochs to 12 to allow the model to converge with the new augmentations and larger architecture.
