These innovations aim to maximize the statistical robustness of the Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) classifier by strictly enforcing its theoretical assumptions regarding sample density and normality.

# Proposed Solution: Hyper-Densified Global-Gaussianized LDA Ensemble

## Model
The architecture utilizes a **Dual-Stream Early Fusion** design driven by **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)**, incorporating a fundamental shift in feature engineering to align deep representations with the classifier's inductive bias.

1.  **Dual-Stream Feature Extraction**:
    *   **Global Geometry Stream**: We will utilize **DINOv2 (ViT-Large)** to capture self-supervised geometric priors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Local Texture Stream**: We will utilize **ConvNeXt Large** to capture high-frequency margin details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
2.  **Independent Subspace Reduction**: We will apply **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** to each visual stream *independently*, retaining **99% of the variance** for each. This ensures that the distinct manifold structures of the ViT and CNN embeddings are preserved without one dominating the other (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052).
3.  **Global Gaussianization (Innovation)**: Unlike previous iterations that only normalized the tabular data, we will apply a **Quantile Transformer (Output distribution = Normal)** to **ALL** feature streams: the PCA-reduced DINOv2 embeddings, the PCA-reduced ConvNeXt embeddings, and the 192 tabular features.
    *   *Rationale*: LDA assumes class-conditional Gaussian densities. While PCA decorrelates features, it does not guarantee normality (especially for ReLU-activated deep features). Explicitly warping the deep feature space to a Gaussian distribution strictly satisfies the classifier's assumptions, maximizing discriminative efficiency (Evolution of Lesson 00021).
4.  **Early Fusion**: The Gaussianized vectors from all streams are concatenated into a single feature vector (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
5.  **Classifier**: A **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** model with **Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage**. We rely on this stable classifier to dominate the few-shot regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).

## Data
We introduce **Hyper-Densification**, a structural evolution of the "Manifold Densification" topology, to provide an extremely dense estimate of the class covariance structures.

1.  **Multi-View Extraction**: For every image (Train and Test), we will extract features for **36 equidistant rotations** (0°, 10°, 20°, ..., 350°) instead of the previous 12.
2.  **Training Topology (Hyper-Densification)**: For the training set, we will generate **9 distinct "Orthogonal Centroids"** per image by averaging mutually exclusive sets of 4 orthogonal views:
    *   Centroid 1: Average of {0°, 90°, 180°, 270°}
    *   Centroid 2: Average of {10°, 100°, 190°, 280°}
    *   ...
    *   Centroid 9: Average of {80°, 170°, 260°, 350°}
    *   *Impact*: This increases the effective training sample size from $N$ to $9N$ (approx. 6,300 samples). This dense "cloud" of valid, variance-aligned centroids allows the LDA to estimate a highly robust covariance matrix, significantly mitigating the HDLSS instability (Evolution of Lesson 00053).
3.  **Inference Topology**: For validation and test sets, we generate a **single canonical centroid** per image (Centroid 1: Average of {0°, 90°, 180°, 270°}). This ensures the model is evaluated on the exact variance profile ($\sigma^2/4$) it was trained on (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).

## Training
We will employ **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation (K=10)** to construct an ensemble, ensuring robustness against data split variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).

1.  **Feature Caching**: Execute the 36-view extraction pipeline once for the entire dataset using the GPU.
2.  **Ensemble Construction**:
    *   Split the unique image IDs into 10 stratified folds.
    *   For each fold $k$:
        *   **Expand**: Construct the **Hyper-Densified Training Set** ($9 \times$ samples) from the training IDs.
        *   **Fit Transformers**: Fit the Independent PCAs and the Global Quantile Transformer on this densified training set.
        *   **Train Classifier**: Train the LDA model (Ledoit-Wolf) on the transformed densified set.
        *   **Store**: Save the pipeline as member $k$ of the ensemble.

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: For each test image:
    *   Generate the canonical centroid (Average of 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°) for both visual streams.
    *   Pass these centroids and the raw tabular features through all 10 saved ensemble pipelines.
    *   Each pipeline applies its specific Independent PCA, Global Quantile transformation, and LDA prediction.
2.  **Aggregation**: Compute the **arithmetic mean** of the probability vectors from all 10 models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
3.  **Formatting**: Apply probability clipping ($[10^{-15}, 1-10^{-15}]$) and format the submission CSV.
