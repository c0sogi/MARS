The `CUDA out of memory` error is caused by an excessively large effective batch size (384 images due to 12-view expansion) overwhelming the GPU memory during the simultaneous inference of two large models. To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 32 to 4, scaling it inversely to the internal data expansion factor (Cite debug_lesson_14). Additionally, I will modify `library/feature_extraction.py` to serialize the model execution: DINOv2 features will be computed and immediately moved to the CPU before ConvNeXt inference begins, preventing the GPU from holding inputs/outputs for both models simultaneously (Cite debug_lesson_7). I will also enable Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) to further reduce the memory footprint.
