The issue is **Data Leakage** caused by merging the training and validation datasets in `CrossValidator`. This allows models to train on data that is subsequently used for final evaluation, resulting in invalidly optimistic metrics.

To fix this, I will modify `library/trainer.py` to:
1.  Replace `_prepare_full_dataset` with `_prepare_training_dataset`, which loads only the training split (`metadata/train.csv`).
2.  Update `run_cv` to use this new method and perform cross-validation strictly on the training data.

This ensures the hold-out validation set remains unseen until the final inference step (Cite debug_lesson_14).
