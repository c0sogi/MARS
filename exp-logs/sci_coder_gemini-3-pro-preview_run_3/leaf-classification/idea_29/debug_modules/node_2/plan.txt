The `ValueError` arises because the model, when trained on a small subset of data (debug mode), only observes a subset of the total classes (39 out of 56). Consequently, `predict_proba` returns a matrix with fewer columns than expected by the global `LabelEncoder`. To resolve this, I will modify `library/trainer.py` and `library/predictor.py` to detect this mismatch. When detected, I will expand the prediction matrix to the full label space by initializing a zero-filled matrix and mapping the model's output columns to their corresponding indices in the global schema, as described in `debug_lesson_2`.
