Strategy:
1.  **Refine Training Data Quality**: In the `fit` method, explicitly filter out training examples where the `selected_text` cannot be exactly located within the `text` after normalization. This prevents the model from training on noisy `(0, 0)` targets (CLS token) caused by preprocessing mismatches, ensuring that `targets_start` and `targets_end` always point to valid spans (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
2.  **Optimize Inference Decoding**: Replace the greedy decoding strategy (independent `argmax` for start and end) with a **Joint Probability Maximization** approach. This searches for the span `(start, end)` with `start <= end` that maximizes the product of probabilities $P(start) \times P(end)$. This guarantees valid span boundaries and captures the most likely coherent span rather than just the strongest individual boundary markers.
3.  **Regularization**: Increase the dropout rate in the `TweetModel` from 0.1 to 0.15 to mitigate the overfitting observed in the positive/negative classes during failure analysis.
4.  **Configuration**: Update the model save path to ensure a fresh training run (bypassing the cache of the previous suboptimal model) and update the baseline threshold in the runfile.
