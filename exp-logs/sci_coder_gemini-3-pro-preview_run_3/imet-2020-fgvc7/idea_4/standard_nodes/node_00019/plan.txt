To improve the model's performance within the 4-hour time limit, I will extend the training duration of the ensemble members from 2 epochs to 5 epochs. The previous solution (Lesson 00018) demonstrated that an ensemble of short-trained models outperforms a single converged model, but 2 epochs is likely too short for optimal feature learning given the high cardinality of the dataset. Increasing the epochs to 5 allows the `OneCycleLR` scheduler to anneal the learning rate more gradually, avoiding premature convergence (Cite Lesson 00003).

This change will increase the total runtime to approximately 2.5 hours, which is safely within the 4-hour constraint. I will also force the inference pipeline to ignore cached predictions from previous runs to ensure the validation and test outputs reflect the newly trained weights. Finally, I will update the submission threshold to the current best score to ensuring only improved solutions are submitted.
