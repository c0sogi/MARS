Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous successes (e.g., **Dual-Query Attention**, **Global Consistency**, **Top-K Indicators**) and the critical failure identified in Lesson 00073 (e.g., **Missing Skip Connections in Gated Fusion**), I propose a **Hybrid Ensemble with Community-Enhanced Skip-Gated MLP and Global-Consistency Augmented Trees**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Skip-Gated Dual-Query MLP" (Idea 40) and the "Community-Aware MLP" (Idea 39). The core innovation is the **Integration of Top-K Community Signals into the Skip-Gated Mechanism**. Lesson 00073 proved that gating without skip connections destroys strong signals; Lesson 00055 proved that Top-K indicators are the most effective representation of history for tabular models. By feeding these Top-K indicators into the MLP's metadata branch *with* a skip connection, we allow the neural network to learn both multiplicative interactions (e.g., "Trust text more if user is active in *r/Loans*") and additive biases (e.g., "Being active in *r/RandomActsOfPizza* increases probability"). Simultaneously, the MLP is augmented with **Global Persona Injection** (User History Centroid), ensuring it sees the user's "average" semantic location alongside the "relevant" attention context.

# Model

The solution employs an ensemble of two distinct, highly optimized learners fused via robust averaging.

**1. Stream A: Global-Consistency Augmented Random Forest**
*   **Goal**: Equip the Random Forest with a robust, multi-view representation of user history that prioritizes "Persona Consistency" and specific high-frequency behaviors.
*   **Architecture**: A Random Forest Classifier with balanced class weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Input**:
    *   **High-Fidelity TF-IDF**: Concatenated Title+Body TF-IDF with continuous frequency weights and a large vocabulary (e.g., 5,000) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Full-Spectrum Metadata**: **Raw Magnitudes** (e.g., account age) + **Engineered Ratios** (e.g., upvote ratio) + **Text Meta-Features** (e.g., caps ratio) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Top-K Binary Community Indicators**: Binary flags (0/1) for the top $K=50$ most frequent subreddits. This provides specific, high-signal splits for common communities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
    *   **Global Consistency Scalars**:
        *   Cosine Similarity between *Request Title* and *User History Centroid*.
        *   Cosine Similarity between *Request Body* and *User History Centroid*.
        *   **Rationale**: Comparing against the *average* behavior (Global Consistency) is more predictive of credibility than finding the single best match (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Sentiment Scores**: VADER polarity and subjectivity scores to capture emotional tone.
*   **Configuration**: **Low Regularization** (`min_samples_leaf=1`) to preserve fine-grained sparse signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).

**2. Stream B: Community-Enhanced Skip-Gated MLP**
*   **Goal**: Model the complex interaction between Request, History, and Community Context while preserving direct predictive paths.
*   **Architecture**: A neural network with multiple branches and a Skip-Connection Gated Fusion mechanism.
    *   **Branch 1 (Title Semantics)**: Processes **Raw SBERT embedding** of Title (No early projection) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051).
    *   **Branch 2 (Body Semantics)**: Processes **Raw SBERT embedding** of Body.
    *   **Branch 3 (Dual-Query History Attention)**:
        *   **Input**: Sequence of **Raw SBERT embeddings** for user history.
        *   **Heads**: Topic Context (Query=Title) and Narrative Context (Query=Body) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059).
        *   **Masking**: Apply **Explicit Additive Masking** ($-\infty$ to padding) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).
    *   **Branch 4 (Global Persona Injection - Structural Innovation)**:
        *   Input: The **User History Centroid Vector** (384-dim).
        *   **Rationale**: Explicitly providing the "average user embedding" allows the model to learn semantic biases (e.g., "Users centered in Altruism are credible") distinct from the specific attention context.
    *   **Branch 5 (Community-Enhanced Metadata)**:
        *   **Input**: Concatenation of **Numerical Metadata** (Arcsinh-transformed) AND **Top-K Binary Community Indicators**.
    *   **Skip-Gated Fusion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073)**:
        *   **Semantic Vector**: [Title, Body, Attended_Contexts, Global_Centroid, Alignment_Scalars].
        *   **Gate**: Sigmoid(Dense(Branch 5)).
        *   **Gated Semantic**: Semantic Vector $\odot$ Gate.
        *   **Final Fusion**: Concatenate [**Gated Semantic**, **Branch 5**].
        *   **Rationale**: This allows Top-K flags and Metadata to act as both *Gates* (modulating text) and *Direct Features* (additive signal).
    *   **Regularization**: **Dropout Only**. Remove Batch Normalization to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).

# Data

1.  **Leakage Prevention**: Strictly apply `get_feature_intersection` to restrict inputs to columns present in both train and test sets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **Semantic Feature Engineering**:
    *   Compute SBERT embeddings for Title, Body, and all User Subreddits.
    *   **Centroid**: Mean of user subreddit embeddings.
    *   **Consistency Scalars**: Dot-product between Title/Body embeddings and History Centroid (Cite solution_lesson_node_00061).
3.  **Top-K Profiling**:
    *   Identify top 50 subreddits by frequency in `train`.
    *   Generate binary flags for train and test.
4.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Stream A (RF)**: Impute NaNs with median.
    *   **Stream B (MLP)**: Apply **Arcsinh Transformation** followed by **StandardScaler** to numerical metadata (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025). Keep Top-K flags binary.

# Training

1.  **Random Forest**: Trained with 500 estimators, balanced class weights, and `min_samples_leaf=1` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).
2.  **MLP**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with weight decay.
    *   **Regularization**: Dropout (e.g., 0.5 on embeddings, 0.2 on dense).
    *   **Protocol**: **High Patience** (e.g., 15 epochs) and sufficient total epochs (e.g., 50) to allow the Dual-Attention and Gating layers to stabilize (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **Independence**: Train both models independently on the full training set.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test data through dual pipelines (Consistency/Top-K/TF-IDF for RF, Sequence-History/Top-K-Metadata/SBERT for MLP).
2.  **Ensemble**: Calculate final probability as **Simple Weighted Average** (0.5/0.5) of the two models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
