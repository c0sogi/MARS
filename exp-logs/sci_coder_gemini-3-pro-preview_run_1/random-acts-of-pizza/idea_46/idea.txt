Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous successes (e.g., **Dual-Query Attention**, **Global Consistency**, **Top-K Indicators**, **Skip Connections**) and the critical failures identified in Lessons 00073 and 00079 (e.g., **Gate Contamination**, **Missing Skips**), I propose a **Hybrid Ensemble with Orthogonal Skip-Gated MLP and Interaction-Projected Trees**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Skip-Gated MLP" (Idea 40) and the "Global-Consistency Augmented Trees" (Idea 35). The core innovation is the strict enforcement of **Orthogonal Information Flow** in the neural network: the "Control Signal" (Gate) is derived *exclusively* from low-dimensional numerical metadata to ensure stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00079), while the "Skip Connection" carries the **Top-K Community Indicators** to provide additive bias without poisoning the gate. Simultaneously, the Random Forest is upgraded with **Explicit Interaction Features**, projecting the Neural Network's "Gating" logic (Consistency $\times$ Credibility) into a tree-compatible format.

# Model

The solution employs an ensemble of two distinct, highly optimized learners fused via robust averaging.

**1. Stream A: Interaction-Projected Random Forest**
*   **Goal**: Equip the Random Forest with explicit features that mimic the "Credibility Gating" logic, allowing it to split on the *interaction* between a user's trustworthiness and their semantic consistency.
*   **Architecture**: A Random Forest Classifier with balanced class weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Input**:
    *   **High-Fidelity TF-IDF**: Concatenated Title+Body TF-IDF with continuous frequency weights and a large vocabulary (e.g., 5,000) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Full-Spectrum Metadata**: **Raw Magnitudes** (e.g., account age) + **Engineered Ratios** (e.g., upvote ratio) + **Text Meta-Features** (e.g., caps ratio) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Top-K Binary Community Indicators**: Binary flags (0/1) for the top $K=50$ most frequent subreddits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
    *   **Global Consistency Scalars**: Cosine Similarity between *Request* (Title/Body) and *User History Centroid* (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Sentiment Scores**: VADER polarity and subjectivity scores.
    *   **Interaction-Projected Features (Structural Innovation)**:
        *   Explicitly generate interaction terms between **Consistency Scalars** and **Key Credibility Metrics**.
        *   $I_1 = \text{Title\_Consistency} \times \log(1 + \text{Account\_Age})$
        *   $I_2 = \text{Body\_Consistency} \times \text{Upvote\_Ratio}$
        *   **Rationale**: This projects the neural network's inductive bias (that credibility modulates the relevance of content) into the tree's feature space, preventing the tree from treating these orthogonal signals as independent.
*   **Configuration**: **Low Regularization** (`min_samples_leaf=1`) to preserve fine-grained sparse signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).

**2. Stream B: Orthogonal Skip-Gated Dual-Query MLP**
*   **Goal**: Model the modulation of semantic content by user credibility while keeping the control signal clean and preserving direct predictive paths.
*   **Architecture**: A neural network with multiple branches and a Skip-Connection Gated Fusion mechanism.
    *   **Branch 1 (Title Semantics)**: Processes **Raw SBERT embedding** of Title (No early projection) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051).
    *   **Branch 2 (Body Semantics)**: Processes **Raw SBERT embedding** of Body.
    *   **Branch 3 (Dual-Query History Attention)**:
        *   **Input**: Sequence of **Raw SBERT embeddings** for user history.
        *   **Heads**: Topic Context (Query=Title) and Narrative Context (Query=Body) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059).
        *   **Masking**: Apply **Explicit Additive Masking** ($-\infty$ to padding) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).
    *   **Branch 4 (Global Persona Injection)**:
        *   **Input**: The **User History Centroid Vector** (384-dim).
        *   **Destination**: Concatenate to the **Semantic Vector** (Data Path).
        *   **Rationale**: Provides a baseline "Average Persona" context alongside the specific "Attended Context" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Branch 5 (Metadata & Community)**:
        *   **Control Source**: Numerical Metadata (Arcsinh-transformed).
        *   **Skip Source**: Concatenation of [Numerical Metadata, Top-K Community Indicators].
    *   **Orthogonal Skip-Gated Fusion (Structural Innovation)**:
        *   **Semantic Vector**: [Title, Body, Attended_Contexts, Global_Centroid, Alignment_Scalars].
        *   **Control Gate**: Sigmoid(Dense(**Control Source Only**)). *Crucially, restricts the gate to low-dim metadata to prevent contamination* (Cite solution_lesson_node_00079).
        *   **Gated Semantic**: Semantic Vector $\odot$ Control Gate.
        *   **Final Fusion**: Concatenate [**Gated Semantic**, **Skip Source**].
        *   **Rationale**: This ensures the gate represents pure reliability, while the skip connection preserves the additive signals of metadata and specific community memberships (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).
    *   **Regularization**: **Dropout Only**. Remove Batch Normalization to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).

# Data

1.  **Leakage Prevention**: Strictly apply `get_feature_intersection` to restrict inputs to columns present in both train and test sets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **Semantic Feature Engineering**:
    *   Compute SBERT embeddings for Title, Body, and all User Subreddits.
    *   **Centroid**: Mean of user subreddit embeddings.
    *   **Consistency**: Dot-product between Title/Body and Centroid (Cite solution_lesson_node_00061).
3.  **Top-K Profiling**:
    *   Identify top 50 subreddits by frequency in `train`.
    *   Generate binary flags for train and test.
4.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Stream A (RF)**: Impute NaNs with median.
    *   **Stream B (MLP)**: Apply **Arcsinh Transformation** followed by **StandardScaler** to numerical metadata (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025). Keep Top-K flags binary.

# Training

1.  **Random Forest**: Trained with 500 estimators, balanced class weights, and `min_samples_leaf=1` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).
2.  **MLP**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with weight decay.
    *   **Regularization**: Dropout (e.g., 0.5 on embeddings, 0.2 on dense).
    *   **Protocol**: **High Patience** (e.g., 15 epochs) and sufficient total epochs (e.g., 50) to allow the Dual-Attention and Gating layers to stabilize (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **Independence**: Train both models independently on the full training set.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test data through dual pipelines (Interaction/Top-K/TF-IDF for RF, Sequence-History/Persona/Metadata for MLP).
2.  **Ensemble**: Calculate final probability as **Simple Weighted Average** (0.5/0.5) of the two models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
