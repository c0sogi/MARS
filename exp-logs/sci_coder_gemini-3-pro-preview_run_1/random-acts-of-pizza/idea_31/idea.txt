Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous successes (e.g., **Dual-Query Attention**, **Alignment Injection**, **Gated Fusion**) and specific failures (e.g., **Batch Normalization Instability**, **Single-Scalar Alignment in Trees**, **History Feature Deprivation**), I propose a **Hybrid Ensemble with Dual-View Consistency Features and Simplified Regularization**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Alignment-Injected MLP" (Idea 29/Lesson 00061) and the "Low-Bias Top-K Random Forest" (Idea 27). It addresses the **Random Forest's "Semantic Blindness"** (Lesson 00061) not just by restoring Top-K indicators, but by explicitly projecting the **Dual-Query** logic (Lesson 00059) into dense scalar features for the tree. Simultaneously, it stabilizes the Neural Network by stripping the **Batch Normalization** layers that caused overfitting (Lesson 00062), reverting to the robust **Dropout-only** regime.

# Model

The solution employs an ensemble of two distinct, highly optimized learners fused via robust averaging.

**1. Stream A: Dual-View Consistency Random Forest**
*   **Goal**: Equip the Random Forest with the same structural understanding of "Topic vs. Narrative" consistency that drove the MLP's success, without forcing it to process embeddings directly.
*   **Architecture**: A Random Forest Classifier with balanced class weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Input**:
    *   **High-Fidelity TF-IDF**: Concatenated Title+Body TF-IDF with continuous frequency weights and a large vocabulary (e.g., 5,000) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Full-Spectrum Metadata**: **Raw Magnitudes** (e.g., account age) + **Engineered Ratios** (e.g., upvote ratio) + **Text Meta-Features** (e.g., caps ratio) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Top-K Binary Community Indicators**: Binary flags for the top $K=50$ most frequent subreddits. This restores the specific history signals essential for tree performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
    *   **Dual-View Alignment Scalars (Structural Innovation)**:
        *   Instead of a single "Request vs. History" similarity score (which failed to save the RF in Lesson 00061), generate two distinct features:
            1.  **Topic Consistency**: Cosine Similarity between *Title Embedding* and *History Centroid*.
            2.  **Narrative Consistency**: Cosine Similarity between *Body Embedding* and *History Centroid*.
        *   **Rationale**: This allows the tree to split differently on users who match the *topic* of their history (e.g., "Gamer asking in Gaming") versus those who match the *narrative style* (e.g., "Storyteller posting long text"), mirroring the Dual-Query MLP's logic.

**2. Stream B: Simplified Dual-Query Alignment-Injected MLP**
*   **Goal**: Maximize the extraction of relevant historical context while strictly controlling model complexity to prevent the overfitting observed in Lesson 00062.
*   **Architecture**: A neural network with multiple branches and a Gated Fusion mechanism.
    *   **Branch 1 (Title Semantics)**: Processes the **Raw SBERT embedding** of the Request Title (No early projection) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051).
    *   **Branch 2 (Body Semantics)**: Processes the **Raw SBERT embedding** of the Request Body.
    *   **Branch 3 (Dual-Query History Attention)**:
        *   **Input**: Sequence of **Raw SBERT embeddings** for the user's subreddit history.
        *   **Head A (Topic Context)**: **Dot-Product Attention** (Query=Title, Key=History) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059).
        *   **Head B (Narrative Context)**: **Dot-Product Attention** (Query=Body, Key=History).
        *   **Masking**: Apply **Explicit Additive Masking** ($-\infty$ to padding) before Softmax (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).
    *   **Alignment Injection**: Concatenate the cosine similarity scalars ($S_{topic}$, $S_{narrative}$) to the fusion layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00061).
    *   **Branch 4 (Metadata)**: Processes "Full-Spectrum" metadata via **Arcsinh Transformation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Gated Fusion**: The Metadata Branch generates a sigmoid **Credibility Gate** that modulates the concatenated semantic vector (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Regularization (Correction)**: **Remove Batch Normalization**. Rely exclusively on **Dropout** (e.g., 0.5 on embeddings, 0.2 on dense layers) to prevent the training instability and overfitting observed in Lesson 00062.

# Data

1.  **Leakage Prevention**: Strictly apply `get_feature_intersection` to restrict inputs to columns present in both train and test sets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **Feature Engineering**:
    *   **Dual-View Alignment**: Compute SBERT embeddings for Title, Body, and Mean History. Calculate `title_sim` and `body_sim` for every sample.
    *   **Top-K Profiling**: Generate binary flags for the top 50 subreddits.
    *   **Magnitudes & Ratios**: Preserve raw counts and generate normalized rates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Dynamic Appending**: Programmatically add all new features to the active list (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
3.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Stream A (RF)**: Impute NaNs with median.
    *   **Stream B (MLP)**: Apply **Arcsinh Transformation** followed by **StandardScaler** to all numerical metadata (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

# Training

1.  **Random Forest**: Trained with 500 estimators, balanced class weights, and **minimal regularization** (`min_samples_leaf=1`) to preserve the sparse Top-K signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).
2.  **MLP**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with weight decay.
    *   **Regularization**: **Dropout Only** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).
    *   **Protocol**: Use **High Patience** (e.g., 15 epochs) and sufficient total epochs (e.g., 50) to allow the Dual-Attention and Gating layers to stabilize (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **Independence**: Train both models independently on the full training set.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test data through the dual pipelines (Top-K/Dual-Alignment/TF-IDF for RF, Sequence-History/SBERT for MLP).
2.  **Ensemble**: Calculate the final probability as the **Simple Weighted Average** (0.5/0.5) of the two models. This ensures robustness and prevents the collapse associated with biased weighting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
