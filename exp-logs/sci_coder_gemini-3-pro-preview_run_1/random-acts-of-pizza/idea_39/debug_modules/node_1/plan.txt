To fix this, I will explicitly pass `allow_pickle=True` to the `np.load` calls in `library/features.py` where the cache is loaded. I will also preemptively apply the same fix to `library/model_nn.py`, which performs a similar load operation for inference, ensuring the pipeline runs to completion.
