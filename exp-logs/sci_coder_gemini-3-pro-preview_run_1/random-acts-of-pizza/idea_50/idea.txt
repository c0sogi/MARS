Based on the progression of empirical lessons—specifically the success of **FiLM Fusion** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00085), **Dual-Query Attention** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059), and **Explicit Interaction Modeling** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00085)—and the identified gaps in **Sentiment Utilization** and **Dissonance Modeling**, I propose a **Hybrid Ensemble with Coherence-Interaction Trees and Sentiment-Conditioned FiLM MLP**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Orthogonal FiLM-Conditioned MLP" (Idea 49). The core innovation is the integration of **Sentiment and Tone** directly into the neural network's control logic (FiLM Gate), creating a "Tone-Aware" modulation that filters semantic content based on emotional credibility. Simultaneously, the Random Forest is upgraded with **Dissonance Interaction Features**, explicitly modeling the divergence between a user's topical consistency (Title) and narrative consistency (Body) as a signal for deception or anomaly.

# Model

The solution employs an ensemble of two distinct, highly optimized learners fused via robust averaging.

**1. Stream A: Coherence-Interaction Random Forest**
*   **Goal**: Equip the Random Forest with explicit features that capture "Behavioral Coherence" (alignment between Title, Body, and History) and its interaction with user credibility.
*   **Architecture**: A Random Forest Classifier with balanced class weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Input**:
    *   **High-Fidelity TF-IDF**: Concatenated Title+Body TF-IDF with continuous frequency weights and a large vocabulary (e.g., 5,000) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Full-Spectrum Metadata**: **Raw Magnitudes** (e.g., account age) + **Engineered Ratios** (e.g., upvote ratio) + **Text Meta-Features** (e.g., caps ratio) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Top-K Binary Community Indicators**: Binary flags (0/1) for the top $K=50$ most frequent subreddits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
    *   **Coherence Profile (Structural Innovation)**:
        *   **External Topic Consistency**: Cosine Similarity between *Request Title* and *User History Centroid*.
        *   **External Narrative Consistency**: Cosine Similarity between *Request Body* and *User History Centroid*.
        *   **Internal Semantic Consistency**: Cosine Similarity between *Request Title* and *Request Body*.
        *   **Semantic Dissonance**: Absolute difference between External Topic and External Narrative Consistency ($|Sim_{title} - Sim_{body}|$). Captures "Style Shifts" (e.g., User is on-topic but off-style).
    *   **Dissonance-Interaction Block (Structural Innovation)**:
        *   Explicitly generate cross-products between **Coherence/Dissonance** metrics and **Credibility** metrics.
        *   $I_1 = \text{Semantic\_Dissonance} \times \log(1 + \text{Account\_Age})$
        *   $I_2 = \text{Internal\_Consistency} \times \text{Upvote\_Ratio}$
        *   **Rationale**: Explicitly models the hypothesis that "High Dissonance is only acceptable from High Credibility users" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00085).
*   **Configuration**: **Low Regularization** (`min_samples_leaf=1`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).

**2. Stream B: Sentiment-Conditioned FiLM MLP**
*   **Goal**: Model the modulation of semantic content by user credibility *and* emotional tone using a theoretically grounded affine transformation.
*   **Architecture**: A neural network with multiple branches and a FiLM Fusion mechanism.
    *   **Branch 1 (Title Semantics)**: Processes **Raw SBERT embedding** of Title (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051).
    *   **Branch 2 (Body Semantics)**: Processes **Raw SBERT embedding** of Body.
    *   **Branch 3 (Dual-Query History Attention)**:
        *   **Input**: Sequence of **Raw SBERT embeddings** for user history.
        *   **Heads**: Topic Context (Query=Title) and Narrative Context (Query=Body) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059).
        *   **Masking**: Apply **Explicit Additive Masking** ($-\infty$ to padding) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).
    *   **Branch 4 (Global Persona Injection)**: Concatenate **User History Centroid** to the Semantic Vector (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Branch 5 (Tone-Aware Control Signal - Structural Innovation)**:
        *   **Input**: Concatenation of:
            1.  **Numerical Metadata** (Arcsinh-transformed).
            2.  **Top-K Binary Indicators**.
            3.  **VADER Sentiment Scalars** (Pos, Neg, Neu, Compound) for Title and Body.
        *   **Rationale**: By including Sentiment in the control signal, the FiLM gate can learn to suppress "Angry" or "Hyper-Subjective" requests from low-credibility users, while allowing them for high-credibility users. This maintains orthogonality (no high-dim embeddings) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00079).
    *   **Orthogonal FiLM Fusion**:
        *   **Data ($x$)**: [Title, Body, Attended_Contexts, Global_Centroid, Alignment_Scalars].
        *   **Control ($z$)**: Branch 5 Output.
        *   **Modulation**: $Output = (1 + \text{Dense}(z)) \odot x + \text{Dense}(z)$.
        *   **Skip**: Concatenate [Output, Branch 5] (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).
    *   **Regularization**: **Dropout Only** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).

# Data

1.  **Leakage Prevention**: Strictly apply `get_feature_intersection` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **Semantic Feature Engineering**:
    *   **SBERT**: Embeddings for Title, Body, Subreddits.
    *   **Centroid**: Mean of user subreddit embeddings.
    *   **Coherence**: Dot-products for Title-History, Body-History, Title-Body.
    *   **Sentiment**: VADER scores for Title and Body.
3.  **Top-K Profiling**: Binary flags for top 50 subreddits.
4.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Stream A (RF)**: Impute NaNs with median.
    *   **Stream B (MLP)**: Apply **Arcsinh Transformation** followed by **StandardScaler** to numerical metadata. Keep Top-K and Sentiment as is.

# Training

1.  **Random Forest**: Trained with 500 estimators, balanced class weights, and `min_samples_leaf=1` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).
2.  **MLP**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with weight decay.
    *   **Regularization**: Dropout (e.g., 0.5 on embeddings, 0.2 on dense).
    *   **Protocol**: **High Patience** (e.g., 15 epochs) and sufficient total epochs (e.g., 50) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **Independence**: Train both models independently on the full training set.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test data through dual pipelines (Coherence/Interaction/Top-K/TF-IDF for RF, Sequence-History/Tone-Aware-Control/SBERT for MLP).
2.  **Ensemble**: Calculate final probability as **Simple Weighted Average** (0.5/0.5) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
