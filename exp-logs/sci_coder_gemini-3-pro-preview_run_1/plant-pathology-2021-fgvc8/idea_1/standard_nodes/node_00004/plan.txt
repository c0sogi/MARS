I will implement a strategy to further scale the model's performance by increasing the input resolution and introducing advanced regularization.

1.  **Resolution Scaling**: I will increase `IMG_SIZE` from 320 to 384. As noted in **Lesson 2**, scaling up resolution helps the model discriminate fine-grained disease patterns. 384x384 is also closer to the native resolution of `EfficientNetV2-M`.
2.  **Mixup Regularization**: To prevent overfitting with the increased resolution and model capacity, I will implement **Mixup** augmentation. This encourages the model to learn linear interpolations between classes, which is particularly effective for multi-label problems with overlapping features (Cite Lesson 1 & 2 regarding regularization needs).
3.  **Training Adjustments**:
    *   Increase `EPOCHS` from 12 to 18 to allow the model to converge with the Mixup regularization (which typically slows training error reduction but improves generalization).
    *   Decrease `BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to 24 to ensure memory safety on the GPU given the 1.44x increase in pixel count ($384^2 / 320^2$).
