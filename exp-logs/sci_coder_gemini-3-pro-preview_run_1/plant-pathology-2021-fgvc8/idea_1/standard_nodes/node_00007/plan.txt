To improve the model's performance, I will implement a strategy focusing on **scaling input resolution**, **regularization**, and **inference optimization**.

1.  **Scaling Resolution**: I will increase the input image size from 320x320 to **384x384**. As noted in **Lesson 00002**, scaling up input resolution and model capacity is a reliable method to improve performance on fine-grained classification tasks.
2.  **Label Smoothing**: The previous training logs showed signs of slight overfitting (Train Loss < Val Loss). I will introduce **Label Smoothing (0.05)** in the training loop. This prevents the model from becoming over-confident and often improves generalization.
3.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**: I will implement a simple TTA strategy (averaging predictions from the original image and a horizontally flipped version) during both the final validation assessment and test inference. This typically boosts the F1 score by smoothing out prediction noise.
4.  **Extended Training**: To accommodate the larger image size and the softer targets from label smoothing, I will increase the training duration from 12 to **15 epochs**.
