The issue is caused by the debug sampling strategy in `library/data_manager.py`. The code `df.iloc[-Config.DEBUG_ROWS:]` selects the last 50,000 transactions, which, given the high volume of data, correspond only to the final 1-2 days of the dataset. The validation split logic defines the "Target" period as the last 7 days and the "Train" period as the weeks prior. Since the sampled data falls entirely within the "Target" window, the resulting `train_df` (data prior to the last 7 days) becomes empty, triggering the `AssertionError`.

To fix this, I will update the debug sampling logic to select a subset of unique users who are active in the tail of the dataset (ensuring they have target data) and retain their full transaction history. This guarantees that the debug dataset contains both the historical data needed for training and the recent data needed for validation.
