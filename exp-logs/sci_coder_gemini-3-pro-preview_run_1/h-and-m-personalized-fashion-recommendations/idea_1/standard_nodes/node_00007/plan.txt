Optimization Strategy:
1.  **Extend History Horizon**: Increase `HISTORY_WEEKS` from 5 to 10. This allows the Item-Item Collaborative Filtering (CF) matrix to learn from a richer set of co-occurrences (70 days of data vs 35), improving the quality of learned item similarities.
2.  **Decouple Repurchase from CF Seeds**: Introduce a `REPURCHASE_WINDOW_DAYS` (set to 35 days, matching the previous baseline's history length).
    -   **Repurchase Step**: Only items purchased in the last 35 days are candidates for immediate repurchase. This prevents recommending stale items from 2 months ago just because they are in the extended "history".
    -   **CF Step**: Use the full 10-week history as "seeds" to find similar items. This ensures that users who haven't bought anything in the last month but were active before still get personalized recommendations based on their older preferences (Cite Lesson 3 & 5), rather than falling back to generic global trends.
3.  **Tune CF Neighbors**: Increase `CF_NEIGHBORS` from 20 to 40 to leverage the denser data from the extended history.
