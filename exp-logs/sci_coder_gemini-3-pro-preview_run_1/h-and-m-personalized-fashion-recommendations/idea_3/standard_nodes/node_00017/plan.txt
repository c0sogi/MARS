The strategy is to transition from a **Weighted Linear Ensemble** back to a **Strict Cascade** architecture, as previous lessons (`solution_lesson_node_00016`, `solution_lesson_node_00003`) demonstrated that enforcing a strict hierarchy of signals (Repurchase > Collaborative Filtering > Popularity) outperforms linear blending in this domain.

To implement this efficiently without rewriting the vectorized inference engine:
1.  **Config Update**: I will set the ensemble weights to orders of magnitude apart (e.g., Repurchase=`1e9`, Behavioral=`1e6`, Trend=`1.0`). This "soft cascade" leverages the floating-point dynamic range to ensure that any signal from a higher tier strictly dominates any signal from a lower tier, effectively mimicking a hard cascade while retaining the speed of matrix operations.
2.  **Disable Visual Component**: I will set the Visual weight to `0.0` and conditionally skip its computation. Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00016` indicated that visual features added noise and computational overhead without improving the cascade baseline.
3.  **Remove Tuning Overhead**: I will remove the `run_validation_and_tuning` function and replace it with a direct `run_validation`. Since we are adopting a deterministic cascade strategy, online hyperparameter tuning of linear weights is unnecessary and potentially counterproductive.
