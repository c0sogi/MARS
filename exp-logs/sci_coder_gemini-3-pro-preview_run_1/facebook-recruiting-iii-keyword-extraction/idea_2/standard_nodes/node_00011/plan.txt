To improve the model's performance, I will implement a **Dual-Input Deep Averaging Network (DAN)**. This approach separates the "Title" and "Body" text fields into distinct input streams rather than concatenating them.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Separate Encoders (Cite Lesson 4):** Titles in Stack Exchange are short, high-density signals, whereas Bodies are long and noisy. Concatenating them dilutes the Title's signal (averaging ~10 title words with ~150 body words). By using separate `EmbeddingBag` operations for Title and Body (sharing the same vocabulary/embeddings), the model can learn to weight the Title representation more heavily.
2.  **Increased Capacity (Cite Lesson 5):** I will concatenate the two resulting embedding vectors (Title Embedding + Body Embedding) and feed them into a larger hidden layer (1024 units instead of 512). This increases the model's capacity to capture interactions between the specific problem statement (Title) and the details (Body).
3.  **Refined Initialization (Cite Lesson 6):** I will ensure the padding index (0) in the embedding layer is explicitly initialized to zero to prevent noise accumulation from padding tokens.

This targeted architectural change leverages the structural properties of the data without sacrificing the efficiency of the averaging-based approach.
