To accommodate this larger batch size within the GPU memory limits while maintaining the 640x640 resolution, I will enable **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** training.
2.  **Model Regularization and Generalization**: I will implement **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** of the model weights (`decay=0.9995`). EMA acts as a robust ensemble of the model's recent states, typically leading to better generalization and stability on the validation set compared to the raw weights at the final step.
3.  **Training Duration**: The previous run showed consistent improvement up to epoch 6. I will increase `num_epochs` to 12 to allow the model (and its EMA shadow) to converge further, taking advantage of the available 4-hour runtime.
4.  **Numerical Stability**: To ensure the **Generalized Mean (GeM)** pooling layer remains stable under mixed precision (where `pow` operations can be risky), I will force the GeM calculation to execute in `float32`.
