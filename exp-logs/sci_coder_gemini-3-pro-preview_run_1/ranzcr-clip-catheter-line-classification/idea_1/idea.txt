# Solution: MobileNetV3 Baseline with Weighted Loss

### Model
We will utilize the **MobileNetV3-Large** architecture, a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) specifically optimized for efficiency and low latency. This model serves as a lightweight alternative to the heavier backbones (like EfficientNet or Swin Transformer) proposed in other architectures.
- **Backbone:** The network will be initialized with weights pre-trained on ImageNet to leverage learned feature extractors.
- **Head:** The standard classification head will be replaced with a simplified output block consisting of a Global Average Pooling layer followed immediately by a single dense layer with 11 output neurons.
- **Activation:** A Sigmoid activation function will be applied to the outputs to generate independent probabilities for each of the 11 target labels.

### Data
To ensure rapid training while maintaining the ability to detect thin catheter lines, we will adopt a fixed-size preprocessing pipeline:
- **Resizing:** All input images will be resized to a fixed resolution of **512x512** pixels. This resolution offers a balanced trade-off between retaining fine details (crucial for lines/tubes) and minimizing computational overhead.
- **Channel Adaptation:** As the pre-trained backbone expects 3-channel input, the single-channel grayscale X-rays will be converted to RGB by replicating the channel dimension three times.
- **Normalization:** Images will be normalized using the standard ImageNet mean and standard deviation values.
- **Augmentation:** To keep the baseline simple and fast, we will strictly limit augmentation to random horizontal flips (if anatomically appropriate) or minimal random rotations, avoiding computationally expensive transformations.

### Training
The training process focuses on handling the class imbalance identified in the data analysis without complex sampling strategies:
- **Loss Function:** We will use **Binary Cross Entropy with Logits Loss**. Crucially, we will implement a **positive weighting scheme** (pos_weight) for each class, calculated based on the inverse frequency of positive labels in the training set. This ensures that rare classes (like `ETT - Abnormal`) contribute sufficiently to the gradient updates.
- **Optimizer:** The **Adam** optimizer will be used for its robustness and fast convergence, with a standard learning rate (e.g., 1e-3 or 1e-4).
- **Strategy:** The model will be trained end-to-end. We will use a standard training loop with Early Stopping monitoring the validation AUC to prevent overfitting and minimize wasted compute time.

### Evaluation
- **Inference:** Test images will undergo the same preprocessing (resizing to 512x512, channel replication, and normalization) as the training set.
- **Prediction:** The images will be passed through the trained MobileNetV3 model. The resulting logits will be transformed via the Sigmoid function to produce the final probability scores for submission. No Test Time Augmentation (TTA) will be used to maximize inference speed.
