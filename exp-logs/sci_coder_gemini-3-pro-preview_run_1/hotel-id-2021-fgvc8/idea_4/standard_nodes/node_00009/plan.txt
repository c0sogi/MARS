My optimization strategy focuses on extending the training duration and stabilizing the curriculum learning schedule, leveraging the superior per-epoch performance of the GeM-augmented EfficientNet-B0 architecture observed in the previous run.

1.  **Extend Training Duration**: The previous solution achieved a MAP@5 of 0.5635 in just 5 epochs, showing a strong upward trend. I will increase `TOTAL_EPOCHS` to 12. This provides sufficient time for the ArcFace loss to optimize the embedding space, which is critical for margin-based metric learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
2.  **Stabilize Curriculum**: I will increase `WARMUP_EPOCHS` from 1 to 2. This ensures the backbone learns robust features via standard Softmax before the strict angular constraints of ArcFace are applied, preventing instability caused by strong augmentations like `RandomResizedCrop` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
3.  **Maintain Configuration**: I will retain the batch size of 64 to preserve implicit regularization for long-tail classes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004) and the GeM pooling layer for effective fine-grained feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

The estimated runtime for 12 epochs is approximately 2 hours, well within the 4-hour limit.
