Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the **Noisy Student ResNet-34 with Stochastic Depth and TTA-Ensemble Distillation**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in the Student's regularization scheme—**Stochastic Depth** combined with **Head Dropout**—to create a robust "Noisy Student." This addresses the need for the Student to be sufficiently regularized to learn a generalized manifold from the Teacher's pseudo-labels, while strictly adhering to the architectural constraints (Vanilla ResNet-34) and high-fidelity resolution (640px) identified as optimal in previous lessons.

### Model
*   **Backbone:** Utilize the **ResNet-34** architecture via the `timm` library, initialized with ImageNet weights.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* ResNet-34 is the proven optimal backbone for this micro-dataset, outperforming both MLP (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006) and SE-ResNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00056).
    *   *Structural Innovation (Stochastic Depth):* Configure the model with a **Drop Path Rate of 0.1**. This technique randomly drops residual branches during training, effectively training an ensemble of shallower sub-networks. This acts as a powerful regularizer, preventing the model from over-relying on specific depth paths and improving generalization on small data.
*   **Classification Head:** Modify the head to include a **Dropout Layer (p=0.5)** before the final projection. This "Head Dropout" serves as the noise injection mechanism required for effective Noisy Student training.
*   **Input Adaptation:** Use **Channel Replication** (grayscale to RGB) to preserve natural image statistics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

### Data
*   **Source:** `supplemental_data/spectrograms`.
*   **Preprocessing (High-Fidelity Alignment):**
    *   **Resolution:** Resize images to **256 pixels (Height) x 640 pixels (Width)**. This preserves the native frequency resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) and captures fine-grained temporal morphology (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
    *   **Normalization:** Explicitly apply **ImageNet Mean and Standard Deviation normalization** to maintain the transfer learning contract (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   **Dynamic Loading:** Implement purely dynamic in-memory loading without disk caching (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
    *   **Deterministic Seeding:** Explicitly pass a fixed seed to the NumPy `default_rng()` generator within augmentation classes to prevent hidden variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Unstructured Cutout:** Apply random rectangular masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Horizontal Flip:** Enable random time inversion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
    *   **Conservative Mixup:** Apply Mixup with **alpha = 0.2** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).

### Training
I will implement a **Two-Stage Distillation Pipeline** with extended schedules:

1.  **Stage 1: Teacher Ensemble Training**
    *   Train **3 independent Vanilla ResNet-34 models** (No Stochastic Depth, No Head Dropout) on the labeled training set (Fold 0).
    *   **Schedule:** Train for **50 epochs**. Activate **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** for the final 25% of epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Safety:** Explicitly disable Early Stopping during the SWA phase (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).
    *   **Loss:** BCEWithLogitsLoss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

2.  **Stage 2: TTA-Ensemble Distillation (Teacher $\to$ Noisy Student)**
    *   **Pseudo-Label Generation:** Generate predictions for the Test Set (Fold 1) using the 3 Teachers. Crucially, apply **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** (Horizontal Flips) during inference and average the results. This creates smoother, higher-quality targets than single-pass inference.
    *   **Sanitization:** Assert no `NaN` values in the merged targets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
    *   **Student Training:** Train the **Noisy Student** (ResNet-34 with Stochastic Depth + Head Dropout) on the **Combined Dataset** (Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test).
    *   **Schedule:** 50 epochs with SWA active in the final 30% (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess test images to **256 x 640**, apply **ImageNet Normalization**, and perform a single forward pass using the final **SWA Student Model**.
*   **Output:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the logits to generate probabilities. This leverages the finding that a single model trained on maximal data outperforms cross-validation ensembles (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
