Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the **Heterogeneous Non-Attentive Ensemble Distillation with SWA**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the successful Idea 11 (High-Fidelity Distillation). It addresses the limitations of the Homogeneous Teacher Ensemble (Idea 11) by introducing **Architectural Diversity** (ResNet + DenseNet) to improve pseudo-label quality. Crucially, it strictly avoids the "Global Channel Attention" mechanism (SE-ResNet) that caused the failure of Idea 16, adhering to the finding that such mechanisms suppress co-occurring classes in multi-label tasks (Lesson 00056).

# Model
*   **Student Architecture:** Maintain the **Vanilla ResNet-34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* This backbone is retained as the optimal choice for the student, having demonstrated the capacity to outperform its teachers (AUC 0.959) while avoiding the overfitting risks of larger models (Lesson 00006) and the suppression effects of attention blocks (Lesson 00056).
*   **Teacher Ensemble (Structural Innovation):** Construct a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** of 4 models to generate pseudo-labels:
    *   **2x ResNet-34:** To provide the robust, proven baseline performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
    *   **2x DenseNet-121:** To introduce architectural diversity. DenseNet-121 is highly parameter-efficient (~7M params) and uses dense connectivity (feature reuse), which typically yields different error patterns than residual networks. Crucially, it **does not** use Squeeze-and-Excitation (Global Channel Attention), making it safe for this multi-label task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00056).
*   **Classification Head:** Use a standard **Linear Layer** projecting to 19 classes. Strictly avoid complex aggregation heads (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and structural noise like Head Dropout (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063).

# Data
*   **Source:** `supplemental_data/spectrograms`.
*   **Preprocessing (High-Fidelity Alignment):**
    *   **Resolution:** Resize images to **256 pixels (Height) x 640 pixels (Width)**. This preserves native frequency resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) and captures the fine-grained temporal morphology required for high performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041). All models (Teachers and Student) will operate at this resolution to prevent knowledge dilution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Normalization:** Explicitly apply **ImageNet Mean and Standard Deviation normalization** to maintain the transfer learning contract (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).
    *   **Input Adaptation:** Use **Channel Replication** (grayscale to RGB) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   **Dynamic Loading:** Implement purely dynamic in-memory loading without disk caching (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
    *   **Deterministic Seeding:** Explicitly pass a fixed seed to the NumPy `default_rng()` generator within augmentation classes to prevent hidden variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Unstructured Cutout:** Apply random rectangular masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Horizontal Flip:** Enable random time inversion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
    *   **Conservative Mixup:** Apply Mixup with **alpha = 0.2** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).

# Training
I will implement a **Single-Stage Distillation Pipeline** with extended schedules. I will explicitly avoid Multi-Generational (Iterative) Distillation, as previous attempts showed it degrades performance on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).

1.  **Stage 1: Heterogeneous Teacher Training**
    *   Train the 4 Teachers (2x ResNet-34, 2x DenseNet-121) on the labeled training set (Fold 0).
    *   **Schedule:** Train for **50 epochs** to ensure full convergence with Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
    *   **SWA Protocol:** Activate **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** for the final 25% of epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Safety:** Explicitly disable Early Stopping during the SWA phase (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).
    *   **Loss:** BCEWithLogitsLoss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

2.  **Stage 2: Robust Pseudo-Label Generation**
    *   Generate predictions for the Test Set (Fold 1) using all 4 Teachers.
    *   **TTA:** Apply **Test-Time Augmentation** (Horizontal Flips) during inference and average the results.
    *   **Ensemble Averaging:** Average the predictions across the heterogeneous ensemble. This combines the stability of ResNet with the feature-reuse efficiency of DenseNet to create high-quality soft targets.
    *   **Sanitize:** Assert no `NaN` values (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).

3.  **Stage 3: Student Training**
    *   Train the single **ResNet-34 Student** on the **Combined Dataset** (Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test).
    *   **Schedule:** 50 epochs with SWA active in the final 30% (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess test images to **256 x 640**, apply **ImageNet Normalization**, and perform a single forward pass using the final **SWA Student Model**.
*   **Output:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the logits to generate probabilities. This leverages the finding that a single model trained on maximal data outperforms cross-validation ensembles (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
