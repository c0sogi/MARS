Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and the specific "Lessons Learned," I propose the **High-Fidelity Aligned Distillation with Robust Augmentation and Dual-Stage SWA**.

This strategy synthesizes the success of the SWA-Distillation pipeline (Idea 8) with the high-resolution potential identified in Idea 10, while explicitly correcting the augmentation failures that caused Idea 10 to regress (Lesson 00039). It introduces a structural innovation by aligning the entire pipeline—Teachers and Student—to a high-fidelity resolution (640px) while enforcing the robust regularization required to support this increased dimensionality.

### Model
*   **Backbone:** Maintain the **ResNet-34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights. This remains the optimal backbone for this micro-dataset, balancing capacity and regularizability **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006, solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Input Adaptation:** Use **Channel Replication** (copying the single-channel spectrogram to R, G, and B inputs) to preserve natural image statistics expected by the pretrained weights **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
*   **Classification Head:** Use a standard **Linear Layer** projecting to the 19 species classes, strictly avoiding complex aggregation heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.

### Data
*   **Source:** `supplemental_data/spectrograms`.
*   **Preprocessing (High-Fidelity Alignment):**
    *   **Vertical Dimension:** Fix height at **256 pixels** to strictly preserve native frequency resolution **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
    *   **Horizontal Dimension:** Resize images to a fixed width of **640 pixels**. This resolution was identified as the "sweet spot" where individual teacher models achieved peak performance (AUC 0.9544) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**, provided that the student is also aligned to this resolution to avoid knowledge dilution.
*   **Pipeline Integrity:** Implement **Purely Dynamic In-Memory Loading** without caching pre-processed tensors to disk **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
*   **Augmentation (Correction of Idea 10):**
    *   **Reintroduction of Horizontal Flip:** Explicitly include random time inversion. Increasing resolution to 640px increases feature sparsity; removing this augmentation caused regression in previous high-res attempts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
    *   **Unstructured Cutout:** Apply random, unstructured rectangular masks (Cutout) instead of structured SpecAugment (time/frequency strips). Unstructured masking preserves partial features of short, transient bird calls better than full-strip masking **Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**.
    *   **Conservative Mixup:** Apply Mixup with **alpha = 0.2** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**.

### Training
I will implement a **Three-Stage Pipeline** with Dual-Stage SWA and extended schedules:

1.  **Stage 1: High-Fidelity Teacher Ensemble with SWA**
    *   Train **3 independent ResNet-34 models** on the labeled training set (Fold 0) at **256x640** resolution.
    *   **SWA Protocol:** Train for an extended schedule (e.g., 40 epochs). Activate **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** for the final 25% of epochs to create robust "Super-Teachers" **Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**.
    *   **Optimization:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** and a stable learning rate of **3e-4** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.

2.  **Stage 2: Sanitized Pseudo-Label Generation**
    *   Generate predictions for the unlabeled Test Set (Fold 1) using the 3 SWA Teachers.
    *   Average the probabilities to create robust soft targets.
    *   **Sanitize:** Explicitly assert that the merged targets contain no `NaN` values before proceeding **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.

3.  **Stage 3: Resolution-Aligned Student with SWA**
    *   Train a single **ResNet-34 Student** on the **Combined Dataset** (Labeled Train + Sanitized Pseudo-Labeled Test).
    *   **Input Resolution:** **256x640**. This aligns the student's capacity with the teachers' high-fidelity features, preventing the degradation observed when distilling high-res teachers into low-res students **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.
    *   **SWA Protocol:** Train for an extended duration (e.g., 50 epochs) and activate SWA for the final 30% of epochs to ensure convergence before averaging **Cite solution_lesson_node_00029**.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess test images to the high-fidelity resolution (**256 x 640**), normalize, and perform a single forward pass using the final **SWA Student Model**.
*   **Output:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the logits to generate probabilities. This leverages the finding that a single model trained on maximal data outperforms cross-validation ensembles **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
