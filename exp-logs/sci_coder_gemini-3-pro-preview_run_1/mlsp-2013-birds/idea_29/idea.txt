Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the **Corrected Augmentation-Stratified ResNet-34 Ensemble Distillation with Calibrated SWA**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of Idea 28. It retains the innovative **Augmentation Stratification** (inducing ensemble diversity through distinct regularization policies rather than architectural changes) and **Temperature Scaling** (calibrating the distillation signal). Crucially, it rigorously corrects the fatal optimization error identified in Lesson 00084 (the use of SGD) by reverting to the proven **AdamW** optimizer, while strictly adhering to the high-fidelity resolution and stability constraints established in successful prior attempts.

# Model
*   **Backbone:** Utilize the **Vanilla ResNet-34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* This backbone is retained as the optimal choice for this micro-dataset. It significantly outperforms MLPs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**, avoids the suppression of co-occurring classes observed with SE-ResNet architectures **Cite solution_lesson_node_00056**, and avoids the weight incompatibility issues of ResNet-34d/Anti-aliased variants **Cite solution_lesson_node_00079, solution_lesson_node_00081**.
    *   *Homogeneity:* Both Teachers and Student will use this identical architecture to ensure inductive bias alignment **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.
*   **Classification Head:** Use a standard **Linear Layer** projecting to the 19 species classes, strictly avoiding complex aggregation heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007** or structural noise like Dropout **Cite solution_lesson_node_00063**.

# Data
*   **Source:** `supplemental_data/spectrograms`.
*   **Preprocessing (High-Fidelity Alignment):**
    *   **Resolution:** Resize images to **256 pixels (Height) x 640 pixels (Width)**. This resolution strictly preserves the native frequency resolution **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013** and captures the fine-grained temporal morphology required for high performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00041**.
    *   **Normalization:** Explicitly apply **ImageNet Mean and Standard Deviation normalization**. This is mandatory to uphold the transfer learning contract **Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**.
    *   **Input Adaptation:** Use **Channel Replication** (grayscale to RGB) to preserve natural image statistics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   **Dynamic Loading:** Implement purely dynamic in-memory loading without disk caching to prevent stale data issues **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
    *   **Deterministic Seeding:** Explicitly pass a fixed seed to the NumPy `default_rng()` generator within augmentation classes to prevent hidden variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**.

# Training
I will implement a **Single-Stage Homogeneous Ensemble Distillation Pipeline** with extended schedules.

**Stage 1: Augmentation-Stratified Teacher Ensemble (Structural Innovation)**
*   Train **3 independent ResNet-34 models** on the labeled training set (Fold 0).
*   **Stratification Strategy:** Induce diversity by varying the regularization policy for each teacher, decorrelating their errors without altering the architecture:
    *   **Teacher 1 (Linearity Bias):** High Input Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$), Low Cutout.
    *   **Teacher 2 (Occlusion Robustness):** Low Input Mixup ($\alpha=0.1$), High Unstructured Cutout **Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**.
    *   **Teacher 3 (Balanced):** Standard Mixup ($\alpha=0.2$) and Cutout.
*   **Optimizer (Critical Correction):** Use **AdamW** with a learning rate of `3e-4`. This corrects the failure of Idea 28, where SGD failed to converge on this sparse, regularized dataset **Cite solution_lesson_node_00084**.
*   **SWA Protocol:** Train for **50 epochs** (to ensure convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**) with **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** active in the final 25% **Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**.
*   **Safety:** Explicitly disable Early Stopping during the SWA phase **Cite solution_lesson_node_00050**.

**Stage 2: Calibrated Pseudo-Label Generation**
*   Generate predictions for the Test Set (Fold 1) using the 3 Teachers.
*   **TTA:** Apply **Test-Time Augmentation** (Horizontal Flips) and average the results.
*   **Temperature Scaling:** Divide logits by **$T=1.5$** before applying Sigmoid. This softens the probability distribution, preventing the student from overfitting to the teachers' overconfidence while preserving inter-class correlations.
*   **Ensemble Averaging:** Average the calibrated probabilities across the 3 teachers.
*   **Sanitize:** Assert no `NaN` values **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.

**Stage 3: Student Training**
*   Train a single **ResNet-34 Student** on the **Combined Dataset** (Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test).
*   **Config:** Use the "Balanced" augmentation policy ($\alpha=0.2$) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**.
*   **Schedule:** 50 epochs with SWA active in the final 30% **Cite solution_lesson_node_00029**.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess test images to **256 x 640**, apply **ImageNet Normalization**, and perform a single forward pass using the final **SWA Student Model**.
*   **Output:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the logits to generate probabilities. This leverages the finding that a single model trained on maximal data outperforms cross-validation ensembles **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
