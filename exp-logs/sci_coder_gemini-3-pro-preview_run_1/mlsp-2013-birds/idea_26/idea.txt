Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the **Deep-Stem ResNet-34 (Non-Anti-Aliased) Ensemble Distillation with SWA**.

This strategy introduces a precise structural innovation in the backbone architecture: **ResNet-34d (Deep Stem) with Anti-Aliasing Disabled**. It synthesizes the findings from Lesson 00041 (importance of temporal morphology) and Lesson 00079 (detriment of BlurPool/Anti-aliasing). By isolating the "Deep Stem" component—which replaces the aggressive 7x7 downsampling of vanilla ResNet with stacked 3x3 convolutions—we preserve the fine-grained transient details of bird calls without introducing the shift-invariance (blurring) that caused previous attempts to fail. This is integrated into the proven Single-Stage Ensemble Distillation pipeline with High-Fidelity resolution and SWA.

# Model
*   **Backbone (Structural Innovation):** Utilize the **ResNet-34d** architecture (via `timm`), but explicitly **disable Anti-Aliasing (BlurPool)**.
    *   *Deep Stem (The Gain):* Standard ResNet-34 uses a 7x7 stride-2 convolution followed by Max Pooling, which aggressively downsamples the input 4x, destroying short-duration signal transients (chirps). The "Deep Stem" replaces this with three consecutive 3x3 convolutions. This preserves the spectrotemporal morphology of the signal in the critical early layers **Cite solution_lesson_node_00041**.
    *   *No BlurPool (The Fix):* Unlike Idea 25, we explicitly set `antialiased=False`. Lesson 00079 proved that BlurPool (Low-Pass Filtering) smoothes out the sharp spectral edges required to separate overlapping calls. By removing it, we retain the "Deep Stem" benefits without the "Blurring" penalty **Cite solution_lesson_node_00079**.
    *   *Homogeneity:* Use this architecture for both Teachers and Student to ensure capacity alignment **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.
*   **Classification Head:** Use a standard **Linear Layer** projecting to the 19 species classes, strictly avoiding complex aggregation heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.
*   **Input Adaptation:** Use **Channel Replication** (copying the single-channel spectrogram to R, G, and B inputs) to preserve natural image statistics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.

# Data

    *   **Resolution:** Resize images to **256 pixels (Height) x 640 pixels (Width)**. This resolution strictly preserves the native frequency resolution **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013** and captures the fine-grained temporal morphology required for high performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00041**.
    *   **Normalization:** Explicitly apply **ImageNet Mean and Standard Deviation normalization**. This is mandatory to uphold the transfer learning contract **Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**.

*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Unstructured Cutout:** Apply random rectangular masks instead of structured SpecAugment **Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**.
    *   **Horizontal Flip:** Enable random time inversion **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
    *   **Conservative Mixup:** Apply **Input-Level Mixup** with **alpha = 0.2** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**. Strictly avoid Manifold Mixup **Cite solution_lesson_node_00070**.

# Training
I will implement a **Single-Stage Homogeneous Ensemble Distillation Pipeline** with extended schedules. I will explicitly avoid iterative distillation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00065** and heterogeneous ensembles **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.

1.  **Stage 1: Deep-Stem Teacher Ensemble**
    *   Train **3 independent ResNet-34d (No AA) models** on the labeled training set (Fold 0).
    *   **Schedule:** Train for **50 epochs** to ensure full convergence with Mixup **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**.
    *   **SWA Protocol:** Activate **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** for the final 25% of epochs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**.
    *   **Optimization:** Use a high, constant learning rate during the SWA phase **Cite solution_lesson_node_00078**.
    *   **Safety:** Explicitly disable Early Stopping during the SWA phase **Cite solution_lesson_node_00050**.
    *   **Loss:** BCEWithLogitsLoss **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

2.  **Stage 2: Robust Pseudo-Label Generation**
    *   Generate predictions for the Test Set (Fold 1) using the 3 Teachers.
    *   **TTA:** Apply **Test-Time Augmentation** (Horizontal Flips) during inference and average the results.
    *   **Ensemble Averaging:** Average the predictions across the 3 teachers to create low-variance soft targets.
    *   **Sanitize:** Assert no `NaN` values **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.

3.  **Stage 3: Student Training**
    *   Train a single **ResNet-34d (No AA) Student** on the **Combined Dataset** (Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test).
    *   **Schedule:** 50 epochs with SWA active in the final 30% **Cite solution_lesson_node_00029**.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess test images to **256 x 640**, apply **ImageNet Normalization**, and perform a single forward pass using the final **SWA Student Model**.
*   **Output:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the logits to generate probabilities. This leverages the finding that a single model trained on maximal data outperforms cross-validation ensembles **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
