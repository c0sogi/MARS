Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, the primary cause of the performance regression (from ~0.95 AUC to ~0.86 AUC) is the aggressive **Learning Rate** of `1e-3`. Lesson 25 explicitly highlights that for small, sparse datasets (N=208), a learning rate of `1e-3` can destabilize optimization and lead to suboptimal convergence compared to `3e-4`.

My strategy is to:
1.  **Optimize Hyperparameters**: Reduce `LEARNING_RATE` from `1e-3` to `3e-4` in `library/config.py` (Cite Lesson 25).
2.  **Adjust SWA LR**: Proportionally reduce `SWA_LR` from `5e-4` to `2e-4` to ensure the Stochastic Weight Averaging phase refines the weights rather than disrupting them.
3.  **Optimize Resources**: Increase `NUM_WORKERS` to 6 to better utilize the available 12 vCPUs.

The rest of the pipeline (SE-ResNet34, Mixup, SWA, Distillation, Resolution 640px) aligns with the best practices identified in the lessons (Lessons 28, 29, 39, 41, 45).
