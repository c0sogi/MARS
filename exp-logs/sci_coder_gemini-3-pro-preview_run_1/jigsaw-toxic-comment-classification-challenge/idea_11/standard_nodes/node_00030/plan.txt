The `RuntimeError: unscale_() has already been called on this optimizer` occurs because `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` is invoked twice within a single gradient accumulation cycle: first during the Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP) step and again during the standard optimization step. PyTorch's `GradScaler` is stateful and forbids multiple unscaling operations per step without an intervening update.

To fix this, I will remove the `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` call inside the AWP block in `library/engine.py`. This is a safe modification because the AWP perturbation calculation (`grad / norm`) is scale-invariant—the scaling factor cancels out when dividing the gradient by its norm. This resolves the double-unscale violation while preserving the adversarial training logic (Cite debug_lesson_7).
