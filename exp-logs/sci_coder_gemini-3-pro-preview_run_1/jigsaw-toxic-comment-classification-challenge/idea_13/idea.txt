# Solution: Deeply Supervised DeBERTa-v3 with Domain Adaptation and Self-Distillation

## Model
I propose a **Deeply Supervised Architecture** that modifies the gradient flow to ensure both high-level semantic understanding and low-level lexical adaptation.

1.  **Backbone (Strategic Retention):** We will utilize **DeBERTa-v3-base**. This model outperforms RNNs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and offers superior context understanding via disentangled attention. We explicitly select the "Base" variant to ensure we can train on the **full dataset** within the 24-hour limit, avoiding the performance pitfalls of subsampling required by "Large" models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
2.  **Deep Supervision (Structural Innovation):** To address the finding that lower layers often fail to adapt to toxic vocabulary (slang, profanity) when using standard fine-tuning or LLRD (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009), we will attach a temporary **Auxiliary Classification Head** to the middle layer of the encoder (e.g., Layer 6).
    *   **Mechanism:** This head will consist of a simple Global Max Pooling layer followed by a dense projection. During training, we will minimize a combined loss: $L_{total} = L_{main} + \lambda \cdot L_{aux}$.
    *   **Rationale:** This forces the lower half of the network to learn discriminative toxic features directly, ensuring the "lexical gap" is bridged without the optimization penalties of Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay.
3.  **Main Classification Head (Strategic Retention):** The primary output will be derived from a **Weighted Aggregation of the Last 4 Layers**, providing access to both deep semantics and surface-level signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009). We will apply the proven **Hybrid Pooling** strategy to this aggregated representation:
    *   **Global Max Pooling:** To extract the strongest local signal (e.g., a specific slur) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    *   **Linear Attention Pooling:** To compute a context-aware weighted average using a simple linear projection ($w^T h_t$) for efficient convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   These vectors are concatenated and passed through **Multi-Sample Dropout** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

## Data
1.  **Tokenization (Strategic Retention):** We will use the **DeBERTa-v3 Tokenizer** (SentencePiece). This preserves punctuation and special characters (e.g., "!!", "f**k") as distinct tokens, which are critical features for toxicity detection often lost in standard cleaning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
2.  **Unsupervised Corpus:** We will concatenate text from `train.csv` and `test.csv` to create a domain-specific corpus.
3.  **Distillation Dataset:** For the final stage, we will combine the labeled training set with the test set, assigning **soft labels** (probability vectors) to the test samples based on predictions from the intermediate "Teacher" model.

## Training
I propose a **Three-Stage Training Pipeline** to maximize adaptation and robustness.

**Stage 1: Domain-Adaptive Pre-training (DAPT)**
*   **Objective:** Fine-tune the DeBERTa-v3 backbone on the combined (train + test) corpus using **Masked Language Modeling (MLM)** for 3 epochs.
*   **Rationale:** This adapts the embeddings and lower layers to the specific distribution of Wikipedia comments before supervised learning begins (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

**Stage 2: Deeply Supervised Teacher Training (5-Fold CV)**
*   **Strategy:** Fine-tune the DAPT backbone on the labeled training set.
*   **Optimization:** Use **AdamW** with a **Uniform Learning Rate** (rejecting LLRD to allow lower layers to update fully, Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Robustness:** Implement **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP)** starting from epoch 2. This perturbs weights to maximize loss during training, forcing the model to find flatter, more robust minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Loss:** $L_{total} = L_{BCE}(Main) + 0.3 \cdot L_{BCE}(Aux)$.
*   **Scheduling:** Use **OneCycleLR** with a duration of 5 epochs per fold. This extended duration is critical to allow the complex architecture (Deep Supervision + Multi-Sample Dropout) to converge, avoiding the underfitting seen with compressed schedules (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006, solution_lesson_node_00016).

**Stage 3: Student Self-Distillation**
*   **Strategy:** Train a new "Student" model (same architecture, initialized from DAPT) on the **combined dataset** (Train + Soft-Labeled Test).
*   **Objective:** Minimize the KL-Divergence between the Student's predictions and the Teacher's soft labels.
*   **Rationale:** This exploits the manifold structure of the unlabeled test data to smooth decision boundaries, leveraging the Teacher's robustness to guide the Student.

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Generate predictions for the test set using the **Stage 3 (Student)** models. The Auxiliary Head is discarded during inference.
2.  **Ensembling:** Average the probabilities from the 5 folds.
3.  **Submission:** Format the averaged probabilities into the required CSV structure.
