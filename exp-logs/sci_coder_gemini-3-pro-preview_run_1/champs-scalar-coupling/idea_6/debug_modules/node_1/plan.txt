The issue stems from the `_downcast_dtypes` function in `data_loader.py` converting the `atom` column to a `category` dtype. When `map(ATOM_RADII)` is applied to these categorical columns in `feature_engineering.py`, Pandas preserves the categorical dtype for the resulting `rad_i` and `rad_j` columns. Since arithmetic addition is not defined for Categorical series, the expression `df_pairs["rad_i"] + df_pairs["rad_j"]` raises a `TypeError`. The fix is to explicitly cast the mapped radius columns to `np.float32` to ensure they are treated as numerical arrays suitable for arithmetic operations.
