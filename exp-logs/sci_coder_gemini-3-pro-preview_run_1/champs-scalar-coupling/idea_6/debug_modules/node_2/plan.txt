The `TypeError` occurs because `df_nodes.fillna(0)` attempts to insert the integer `0` into the `molecule_name` column, which has been downcasted to a Pandas `Categorical` type. Since `0` is not a valid category in the string-based `molecule_name` column, Pandas raises an error. To resolve this, I will modify `compute_node_features` to apply `fillna(0)` exclusively to numeric columns. This ensures that the generated feature columns (which may contain `NaN`s from the left join) are correctly zero-filled, while the categorical identifier columns are preserved without modification (Cite debug_lesson_8).
