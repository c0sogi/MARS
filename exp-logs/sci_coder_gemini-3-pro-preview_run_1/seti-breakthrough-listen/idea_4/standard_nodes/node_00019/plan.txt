To optimize performance, I will upgrade the backbone from **ResNet34** to **ResNet50** to increase model capacity (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00017}). To manage the increased parameter count in the fusion head (due to ResNet50's 2048-channel output), I will introduce a **1x1 convolution bottleneck** to reduce channels before the spatial mixing layer. Additionally, I will implement **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** using horizontal flips (frequency flips) during validation and inference to improve robustness and prediction accuracy. Finally, I will reduce the training epochs from 12 to 10 to ensure the heavier model completes within the 4-hour runtime limit.
