**Optimization Strategy:**
1.  **Preserve Temporal Information (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**: I will modify the input configuration and data loader to feed all 6 cadence panels (A-B-A-C-A-D) directly into the model as separate channels, rather than averaging them. This allows the CNN to learn the temporal evolution and drift of the signal across the sequence.
2.  **Data Preprocessing**: I will replace the "ON minus OFF" subtraction with instance-level normalization of the full 6-channel stack. This ensures the model receives the raw intensity patterns relative to the noise floor without losing information.
3.  **Conditional Submission**: I will update the execution script to strictly follow the requirement of only generating a submission if the validation AUC improves upon the baseline (0.506).
