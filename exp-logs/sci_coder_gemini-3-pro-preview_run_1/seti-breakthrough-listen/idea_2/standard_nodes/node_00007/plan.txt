To improve the model's performance, I will implement a strategy that preserves the temporal dynamics of the signal, addressing the failure of the previous channel-stacking approach (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00006`). Instead of collapsing the 6 cadence panels into channels (which washes out the signal in the first convolution), I will vertically concatenate them into a single continuous spectrogram of shape `(1638, 256)`. This maps the temporal dimension to the spatial vertical axis, allowing the 2D CNN to learn the "needle" trajectory (Doppler drift) and the "ON-OFF" modulation pattern as spatial features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00001`).

Here is the implementation plan:
1.  **`library/config.py`**: Update `IN_CHANNELS` to 1 and `IMAGE_HEIGHT` to 1638 (6 * 273). Increase `EPOCHS` to 20 to allow convergence on the larger spatial input.
2.  **`library/dataset.py`**: Modify `__getitem__` to vertically concatenate the 6 panels of the loaded `.npy` file. Update the fallback logic and data augmentation to operate on the new `(1638, 256)` shape (flipping/rolling the correct axes).
3.  **`library/model.py`**: No changes required in code logic, as it dynamically builds the first layer based on `IN_CHANNELS` from the config.
