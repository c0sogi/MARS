The strategy focuses on correcting the architectural and training hyperparameters that led to suboptimal performance in the previous run.

1.  **Architecture Optimization**: I will revert the U-Net architecture from 4 levels to 3 levels and reduce the patch size from 320x320 to 160x160. 2.  **Convergence Optimization**: I will increase the training duration from 150 to 1000 epochs. This ensures that the ensemble models fully converge, leveraging the Cosine Annealing scheduler effectively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **Code Adaptation**: The `UNet` class will be updated to support the 3-level depth, and padding logic in training/inference scripts will be adjusted to use a divisor of $2^3=8$ instead of 16.
4.  **Threshold Update**: The submission threshold will be updated to the best-known metric (0.01187) to ensure only a superior solution is submitted.
