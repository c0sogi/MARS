Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose the **Signal-Aligned Reflection-Padded Ensemble**.

This strategy synthesizes the **Signal Inversion** paradigm (which accelerates convergence by aligning sparsity with CNN inductive biases) with the robust **Deep Context Architecture** and **Reflection Padding** strategies that achieved the current state-of-the-art. It explicitly resolves the implementation flaws of previous attempts (Idea 11) by ensuring that the inverted signal is handled with boundary-preserving padding and trained to full convergence.

# Model
**Architecture: 4-Level U-Net (Context Specialist)**
We will utilize the **Standard 4-Level U-Net** (Encoder: 32 $\to$ 64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 512 filters) as the sole backbone.
*   **Rationale:** Previous experiments confirmed that for this specific task, "Context is King." A 4-level depth is required to capture the global structure of text lines, and this depth necessitates a large input patch to maintain a viable bottleneck resolution ($20 \times 20$ pixels) (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}). We explicitly reject the "Heterogeneous Ensemble" approach (Idea 11) because the shallower "Diversity Stream" was empirically proven to degrade performance (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00039}).
*   **Upsampling:** We will strictly employ **Bilinear Upsampling followed by Convolution** to avoid checkerboard artifacts (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00009}).
*   **Input Channels:** The model will accept 1 channel (grayscale) and output 1 channel (clean signal).

# Data
**Preprocessing: Signal Alignment & Boundary Continuity**
We will implement a specialized data pipeline that aligns the image statistics with the network's activation dynamics while preserving boundary continuity.

1.  **Signal Inversion (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Upon loading, all images will be transformed: $I_{processed} = 1.0 - I_{raw}$.
    *   **Rationale:** This maps the background (white) to 0 and text (black) to 1. This aligns the dominant background class with the "inactive" state of ReLU activations and zero-padding, which was shown to reduce error by ~50% in early training stages (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00038}).

2.  **Reflection Padding (Correction):**
    *   We will strictly use **Reflection Padding** for all boundary handling (both internal padding for downsampling and augmentation).
    *   **Rationale:** Idea 11 failed because it combined Signal Inversion with Constant Padding, creating artificial "noise-to-silence" edges. Reflection padding extends the noise distribution, ensuring the CNN sees consistent statistics at the boundaries (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00039}).

3.  **Large Patch Extraction:**
    *   We will train on **$320 \times 320$ pixel random crops**.
    *   **Rationale:** This maximizes the physical input window to support the 4-level network's receptive field (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}).

4.  **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** Random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and 90-degree rotations (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}).
    *   **Determinism:** Use a `worker_init_fn` to seed data loaders for full reproducibility (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}).

# Training
**Strategy: Massive Converged Bagging**
We will exploit the efficiency gains from Signal Inversion to train a larger, fully converged ensemble within the 24-hour limit.

*   **Ensemble Scale:** We will train **10 independent instances** of the 4-Level U-Net using different random seeds.
    *   **Rationale:** Seed averaging significantly reduces variance (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00028}). With the estimated training time of ~25 minutes per model, training 10 models fits comfortably within the budget.
*   **Full Convergence:** Each model will be trained for **1000 epochs**.
    *   **Rationale:** We prioritize training to full convergence over early stopping to minimize bias and resolve high-frequency details (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00012}).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** Adam (LR 1e-3, Batch Size 16) to maximize update frequency (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}).
    *   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing with `T_max=1000` (decoupled from stopping) to ensure the learning rate decays fully to zero (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}).
    *   **Loss Function:** Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the **inverted signal**.

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Inverted Inference:** Test images will be inverted ($1.0 - I$) and reflection-padded to be multiples of 16.
*   **D4 Group Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, we will generate predictions for **8 geometric views** (Original, Rot90, Rot180, Rot270, and their horizontal flips). These will be inversely transformed and averaged (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00028}).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The TTA-averaged outputs from all **10 fully converged models** will be averaged together.
*   **Re-Inversion:** The final prediction will be inverted back ($1.0 - I_{pred}$) to the original intensity space for submission.
