The error arises because the `verbose` parameter was removed from the `torch.optim.lr_scheduler.ReduceLROnPlateau` constructor in recent PyTorch versions, causing a `TypeError` during initialization. To resolve this, I will remove the `verbose=True` argument from the scheduler instantiation in `library/train.py`, as recommended in the debug lessons. Cite debug_lesson_1.
