The error is caused by the inclusion of the `verbose` argument in the initialization of `torch.optim.lr_scheduler.ReduceLROnPlateau` in `library/train.py`. In recent versions of PyTorch, the `verbose` parameter has been deprecated and removed from the constructor (logging is now handled differently or silently). To fix this, I will remove the `verbose=True` argument from the scheduler initialization.
