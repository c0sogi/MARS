Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the identification of specific failure modes in previous "Signal Inversion" attempts, I propose the **Bounded Inverted-Signal Deep Ensemble**.

This strategy synthesizes the efficiency of **Signal Alignment** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00038}) with the structural robustness of the **Deep Context U-Net** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}), while explicitly correcting the padding and activation flaws (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00039}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00040}) that caused previous iterations to fail.

# Model
**Architecture: Bounded Deep U-Net**
We will employ the **Standard 4-Level U-Net** (Encoder: 32 $\to$ 64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 512 filters) as the backbone.
*   **Depth & Capacity:** We retain the 4-level depth to capture the global structural context of text lines, which was proven superior to shallow networks when supported by adequate input resolution (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}).
*   **Upsampling:** We strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling followed by Convolution** to avoid the checkerboard artifacts introduced by Transposed Convolutions (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00009}).
*   **Bounded Activation (Structural Correction):** Crucially, the final output layer will use a **Sigmoid Activation** function.
    *   *Rationale:* Previous attempts at Signal Inversion (Idea 12) failed because they utilized linear outputs for regression, leading to "overshoot" errors and instability at the bounds (0 and 1). Sigmoid structurally enforces these physical constraints, ensuring the model focuses on the signal transition rather than maintaining the background floor (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00040}).

# Data
**Preprocessing: Inverted Signal with Boundary Continuity**
We will implement a specialized data pipeline that aligns the sparse text signal with the network's inductive biases while preserving statistical continuity at the boundaries.

1.  **Signal Inversion:** Upon loading, all images will be inverted: $I_{processed} = 1.0 - I_{raw}$.
    *   *Rationale:* This maps the dominant background (white) to 0 and the sparse text (black) to 1. This aligns the data with the "inactive" state of ReLU activations and zero-padding, which has been shown to accelerate convergence and reduce early-stage error (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00038}).
2.  **Reflection Padding (Structural Correction):** We will strictly use **Reflection Padding** for all boundary handling (augmentations and internal downsampling).
    *   *Rationale:* Previous Inversion attempts (Idea 11) failed due to Constant Zero-Padding, which created artificial "noise-to-silence" discontinuities at the image edges. Reflection padding extends the noise distribution, ensuring the CNN sees consistent statistics (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00039}).
3.  **Large Patch Extraction:** We will train on **$320 \times 320$ pixel random crops**.
    *   *Rationale:* This large input window ensures the 4-level network maintains a bottleneck resolution of $20 \times 20$ pixels, preserving the spatial fidelity required for accurate text reconstruction (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}).
4.  **Augmentation:** We will apply random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and 90-degree rotations (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}), using a `worker_init_fn` to ensure deterministic reproducibility (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}).

# Training
**Strategy: Converged Seed-Averaging Ensemble**
We will train a robust ensemble of **10 independent models** using the corrected pipeline.
*   **Full Convergence:** Each model will be trained for **1000 epochs**. We prioritize training to full convergence over early stopping to minimize bias and resolve high-frequency details (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00012}).
*   **Optimization Velocity:** We will use the **Adam** optimizer with a **high learning rate (1e-3)** and **small batch size (16)** to maximize the frequency of gradient updates (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}).
*   **Scheduler:** We will use **Cosine Annealing** with `T_max=1000`. This decouples the scheduler from stopping criteria, ensuring the learning rate decays fully to zero to settle into sharp minima (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}).
*   **Loss Function:** Mean Squared Error (MSE) computed on the **inverted** signal.

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Inverted Inference:** Test images will be inverted ($1.0 - I$) and reflection-padded to be multiples of 16.
*   **D4 Group Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, we will generate predictions for **8 geometric views** (Original, Rot90, Rot180, Rot270, and their horizontal flips). These will be inversely transformed and averaged. This significantly reduces variance compared to single-view inference (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00028}).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The TTA-averaged outputs from all **10 fully converged models** will be averaged together.
*   **Re-Inversion:** The final aggregated prediction will be inverted back ($1.0 - I_{pred}$) to the original intensity space for submission.
