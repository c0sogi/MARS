The issue is that the metric verification logic in `runfile.py` uses synthetic data that fails to populate all identity subgroups required by the Jigsaw metric. The metric calculates a generalized mean (soft minimum) of AUCs across 9 identity groups. In the original code, only the first identity column is populated with a positive example. The remaining 8 identities are empty or mono-class in the subset, causing the metric calculator to assign them a default score of 0.5. These default scores drag the aggregated "perfect" score down from 1.0 to ~0.646, triggering the assertion error.

To fix this, I will update the data generation in `runfile.py` to ensure that **every** identity column has the necessary diversity (Toxic vs. Non-Toxic, Identity Present vs. Absent) to calculate a valid AUC of 1.0. This involves creating four distinct rows (Toxic+Identity, Non-Toxic+Identity, Toxic+NoIdentity, Non-Toxic+NoIdentity) and applying this pattern to all identity columns simultaneously.
