The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the default behavior of gradient checkpointing in the `JigsawModel`. By default, `gradient_checkpointing_enable()` often activates PyTorch's legacy "re-entrant" checkpointing, which can lead to autograd graph conflicts when used with complex Transformer backbones during the backward pass. To resolve this, I will explicitly disable re-entrant checkpointing and enforce the modern hook-based implementation by passing `gradient_checkpointing_kwargs={"use_reentrant": False}` to the configuration method.
