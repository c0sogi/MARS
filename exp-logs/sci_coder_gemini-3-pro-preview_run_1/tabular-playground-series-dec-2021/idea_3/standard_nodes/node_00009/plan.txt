The solution implements a Hybrid Stacking Strategy using XGBoost and a Tabular ResNet. To satisfy the "Fast Baseline" requirement and the runtime constraints, the solution subclasses the provided `EnsembleRunner` to create two distinct execution modes: one for validation (training on a subsample of `train.csv` and predicting on `val.csv`) and one for submission (training on a subsample of `train.csv` + `val.csv` and predicting on `test.csv`). This approach ensures strict adherence to the hold-out validation requirement while optimizing for speed by subsampling the large dataset and reducing training epochs/rounds.
