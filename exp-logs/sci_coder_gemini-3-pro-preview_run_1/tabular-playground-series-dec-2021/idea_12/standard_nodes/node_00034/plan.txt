To optimize the solution, I will implement a strategy based on **Full Dataset Training** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`) combined with a scalable **Landmark-based k-NN** approach.

1.  **Full Dataset Training**: I will modify `runfile.py` to remove the subsampling of the training data. Training on the full 2.8 million samples is crucial for maximizing model performance on this large dataset, as indicated by previous lessons.
2.  **Landmark k-NN Reference**: To prevent the k-NN feature generation from becoming a computational bottleneck ($O(N^2)$ complexity) when scaling to the full dataset, I will sample a fixed "Landmark" set (100,000 samples) from the training data. This subset will serve as the reference manifold for calculating density and class probability features for the Training, Validation, and Test sets. This approximates the manifold structure efficiently without requiring the full training set as a reference.
3.  **Throughput Optimization**: I will increase the `KNN_BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` to 4096 to maximize GPU memory utilization and throughput during the feature extraction phase.
