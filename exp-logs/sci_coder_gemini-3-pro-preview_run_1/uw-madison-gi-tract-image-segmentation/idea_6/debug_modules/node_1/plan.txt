The `TimeoutError` during validation is caused by the computationally expensive 3D Hausdorff distance calculation on dense volumetric masks. The function `directed_hausdorff` has a complexity roughly proportional to the product of the number of points in the two sets. For 3D segmentation masks, using all foreground voxels results in massive point clouds, causing the operation to hang. The fix is to optimize the metric calculation by extracting only the **surface voxels** (contours) of the predicted and ground truth masks using morphological erosion. Computing the Hausdorff distance on the surface points is mathematically sufficient for this metric and significantly reduces the number of points, ensuring the validation completes within the time limit.
