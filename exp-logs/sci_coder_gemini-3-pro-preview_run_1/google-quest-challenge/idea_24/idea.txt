Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the **Multi-Scale Partitioned-Pooling RoBERTa Dual-Encoder**.

This strategy synthesizes the high capacity of the `roberta-base` backbone (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00077**) with the structural advantages of **Partitioned Pooling** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00101**) and **Residual Fusion** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00100**). Crucially, it introduces two **Structural Innovations**—**Multi-Scale Layer Aggregation** and **Salience Interaction Fusion**—to improve the robustness of the embeddings and capture keyword-level matching signals that were previously ignored.

# Model
*   **Backbone Architecture:** Utilize **`roberta-base`** in a **Symmetric (Shared-Weight)** configuration.
    *   *Rationale:* This backbone (12 layers) provides the necessary capacity to model complex hierarchical dependencies (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00077**). Using shared weights ensures the Question and Answer embeddings reside in the same metric space, maximizing the validity of geometric interaction features (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00080**).
*   **Multi-Scale Layer Aggregation (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Instead of relying solely on the final hidden layer (Layer 12), which can be volatile during fine-tuning, compute a **learnable weighted average of the last 4 hidden layers** (Layers 9-12) for both branches.
    *   *Benefit:* This creates a "Multi-Scale" representation that fuses high-level semantic abstractions (Layer 12) with more robust syntactic/structural features from lower layers, smoothing the optimization landscape and improving generalization.
*   **Partitioned & Global Pooling:**
    *   **Question Branch:** Apply **Partitioned Average Pooling** to the *aggregated* representation to extract $u_{title}$ (Intent) and $u_{body}$ (Context) using tokenizer masks (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00101**). Also extract **Global Max Pooling** ($u_{max}$) to capture salient keywords across the entire question.
    *   **Answer Branch:** Extract **Global Average Pooling** ($v_{avg}$) and **Global Max Pooling** ($v_{max}$).
*   **Salience-Enhanced Interaction Fusion (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Compute explicit geometric interactions (Product and Difference) not just on the semantic (Average) vectors, but also on the **Salience (Max)** vectors.
    *   **Intent Matching:** $I_{intent} = [u_{title} \odot v_{avg}, |u_{title} - v_{avg}|]$.
    *   **Context Matching:** $I_{context} = [u_{body} \odot v_{avg}, |u_{body} - v_{avg}|]$.
    *   **Salience Matching:** $I_{salience} = [u_{max} \odot v_{max}, |u_{max} - v_{max}|]$.
    *   *Rationale:* While previous lessons advised against Max-Pool interactions due to noise (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**), the **Residual Head** allows the model to selectively utilize these "Keyword Overlap" signals (crucial for Answer Relevance) while bypassing noise via the skip connection.
    *   **Fusion:** Concatenate all raw vectors and interaction terms: $F = [u_{title}, u_{body}, v_{avg}, u_{max}, v_{max}, I_{intent}, I_{context}, I_{salience}]$.
    *   Apply **Layer Normalization** to $F$ (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
*   **Residual Interaction Head:**
    *   Utilize the **Residual Projection Block** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00100**).
    *   Architecture: $Output = \text{Linear}(\text{Concat}(F, \text{Dropout}(\text{ReLU}(\text{Linear}(F)))))$.
    *   **Monolithic Output:** Predict all 30 targets simultaneously (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Question Stream:** Use `tokenizer(question_title, question_body)`. This leverages the tokenizer's native separator insertion (`[CLS] Title [SEP] Body [SEP]`) to preserve semantic structure (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00070**).
    *   **Answer Stream:** Use `tokenizer(answer)`. Do not inject the Title here to avoid redundancy and truncation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00063**).
    *   **Mask Generation:** Generate auxiliary binary masks identifying the `title` token range and `body` token range for the Question branch.
*   **Tokenization:** Use the `roberta-base` tokenizer.
*   **Dynamic Padding:** Pad sequences to the longest length in the batch to optimize throughput (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).

# Training
*   **Phantom Scheduling (Strategic Retention):**
    *   **Configuration:** Initialize the Learning Rate Scheduler with a total duration of **7 epochs**.
    *   **Execution:** Explicitly terminate training after **3 epochs**.
    *   *Rationale:* `roberta-base` peaks at Epoch 3 on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00077**). This strategy preserves the optimal gentle decay profile while preventing overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00078**).
*   **Gradient Accumulation:** Set physical batch size to **8** and accumulate gradients (steps=2) to achieve an **effective batch size of 16** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
*   **Differential Learning Rates:** Apply a high learning rate (`1e-3`) to the Head/Aggregation Weights and a low learning rate (`2e-5`) to the Backbone (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
*   **Head Warmup:** Freeze the backbone for the first epoch. Train only the head and aggregation weights (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00050**).
*   **Continuous Schedule:** Do not reset the optimizer after warmup (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Pass the test set through the model, applying the multi-scale aggregation and partitioned pooling logic.
*   **Prediction:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the 30 output logits.
*   **Validation:** Monitor Mean Column-wise Spearman's Correlation, expecting peak performance at Epoch 3.
