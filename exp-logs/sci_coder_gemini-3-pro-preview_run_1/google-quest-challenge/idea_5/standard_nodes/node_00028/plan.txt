Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Architectural Upgrade to MLP Head**: I will replace the simple linear projection head in `QuestModel` with a Non-Linear Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) consisting of `Linear -> ReLU -> Dropout -> Linear`. As noted in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**, when fusing heterogeneous features (Average Pooling, Max Pooling, Product, Difference), a simple linear layer lacks the capacity to effectively mix these signals. An MLP allows the model to learn a unified representation of the interactions before the final classification.

2.  **Extended Training Schedule**: I will increase the number of epochs from 5 to 6. Given the additional parameters in the MLP head and the observation that regression tasks on noisy data often benefit from longer convergence times (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**), this slight extension will help maximize performance without risking overfitting, while staying well within the runtime limits (previous run was ~8 mins).

3.  **Preserved Best Practices**: I will maintain the `microsoft/deberta-v3-small` backbone (efficient and powerful), the Dual-Branch Siamese structure with Hybrid Pooling (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**), and the explicit interaction heuristics (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**), as these are foundational to the model's efficiency.
