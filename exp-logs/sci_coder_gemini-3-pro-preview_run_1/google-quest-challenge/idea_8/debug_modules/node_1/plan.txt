The `ImportError` occurs because the `AdamW` optimizer was deprecated and removed from the `transformers` library in favor of the standard PyTorch implementation. As explained in the debug notes, `AdamW` must now be imported directly from `torch.optim` (Cite debug_lesson_1). I will correct the import statements in both `library/train.py` and `runfile.py` to source `AdamW` from `torch.optim` while retaining the scheduler and tokenizer imports from `transformers`.
