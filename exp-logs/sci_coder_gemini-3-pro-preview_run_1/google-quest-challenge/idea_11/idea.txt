Based on the extensive analysis of previous experiments and the "Lessons Learned," I propose the following optimized strategy.

# Model
I propose a **Task-Adapted DistilRoBERTa Dual-Encoder with Residual Fusion**. This architecture introduces a structural evolution in how the pre-trained backbone is utilized and how features are propagated to the output, addressing the "Capacity vs. Trainability" trade-off (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**) and the "Feature Vanishing" problem in non-linear heads (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**).

*   **Backbone Architecture:** Utilize **`distilroberta-base`**.
    *   *Rationale:* This backbone (82M params) is the empirically verified "Right-Sized" model for this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**). It avoids the instability of "Starved Batches" required by larger models (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**) and the capacity limitations of smaller ones.
*   **Structural Innovation 1: Deep Encoder Adaptation (Re-initialization):**
    *   Explicitly **re-initialize the weights of the last 2 Transformer layers** of the backbone using the original pre-training initialization scheme (Normal, $\mu=0, \sigma=0.02$) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00051**).
    *   *Mechanism:* Instead of fine-tuning the generic Masked Language Modeling features of the top layers (which are often ill-suited for regression), this converts the top of the network into a "Task-Specific Transition Zone." These layers are treated as scratch-initialized parameters, allowing them to learn high-level regression features from the ground up while relying on the robust linguistic features of the frozen lower layers.
*   **Dual-Branch Structure:** Maintain the Siamese design, processing "Question" and "Answer" streams independently (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).
*   **Masked Hybrid Pooling:** Apply **Masked Global Average and Max Pooling** to the output of the re-initialized encoder (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**).
*   **Interaction-Aware Fusion:**
    1.  Compute explicit interactions (Element-wise Product and Absolute Difference) *only* on the **Average Pooled** vectors (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
    2.  Construct the Fused Vector $F = [u_{avg}, u_{max}, v_{avg}, v_{max}, u_{avg} \odot v_{avg}, |u_{avg} - v_{avg}|]$.
    3.  Apply **Layer Normalization** to $F$ (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
*   **Structural Innovation 2: Residual Fusion Head:**
    *   Replace the standard MLP with a **Residual Projection Block**.
    *   **Path A (Non-Linear):** $H_{proj} = \text{Dropout}(\text{GELU}(\text{Linear}(F)))$. (Projects to hidden dim, e.g., 768).
    *   **Path B (Skip):** $F$ (The raw fused vector).
    *   **Output:** $\text{Linear}(\text{Concat}(F, H_{proj})) \to 30 \text{ Targets}$.
    *   *Rationale:* This architecture forces the model to retain access to the raw engineered features (like explicit interactions) at the final decision layer, preventing the "vanishing signal" observed in deep non-linear heads (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**) while avoiding the information bottleneck of narrow projection heads (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00048**).

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Concatenate `question_title` and `question_body` for the Question input; use `answer` for the Answer input.
*   **Metadata:** **Exclude** categorical metadata injection. Previous experiments proved that explicit embeddings overfit (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**) and text injection causes shortcut learning (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**). Rely solely on the text content.
*   **Tokenization:** Use the `distilroberta-base` tokenizer.
*   **Dynamic Padding:** Pad sequences to the longest length in the batch to optimize throughput (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).

# Training
*   **Differential Learning Rates & Grouping:**
    *   **Group 1 (High LR `1e-3`):** The **Residual Fusion Head** AND the **Re-initialized Top 2 Transformer Layers**. These are effectively "new" parameters and require a high learning rate to escape random initialization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
    *   **Group 2 (Low LR `2e-5`):** The frozen/pretrained lower layers of the backbone.
*   **Parameter Regularization:** Explicitly exclude `bias` and `LayerNorm` parameters from weight decay (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).
*   **Progressive Unfreezing Strategy:**
    *   **Epoch 1 (Adaptation):** Freeze the lower backbone layers (0-3). Train *only* the Re-initialized layers (4-5) and the Head. This stabilizes the task-specific features without distorting the pre-trained linguistic base (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00050**).
    *   **Epoch 2-5 (Fine-tuning):** Unfreeze the entire model. Continue training with a linear decay schedule.
*   **Continuous Schedule:** Do not reset the optimizer/scheduler between Epoch 1 and 2; allow momentum to carry over (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).
*   **Batch Size:** Use **16**. This fits in memory and provides the necessary gradient noise for generalization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
*   **Loss Function:** Monolithic Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss for all 30 targets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Pass the test set through the trained model.
*   **Prediction:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the 30 output logits.
*   **Validation:** Monitor Mean Column-wise Spearman's Correlation. Accept the model if it exceeds the previous best (`~0.408`) plus a small noise margin (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).
