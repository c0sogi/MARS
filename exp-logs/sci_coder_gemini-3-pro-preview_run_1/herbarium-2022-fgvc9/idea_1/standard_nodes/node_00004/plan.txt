Based on the lessons learned, specifically `solution_lesson_node_00003`, I will transition from a Linear Probing strategy to a **Fine-Tuning** strategy with **Differential Learning Rates**. The previous linear probe achieved a Macro F1 of ~0.058, while fine-tuning has been shown to reach ~0.343.

My optimization strategy involves:
1.  **Unfreezing the Backbone**: Allowing the ResNet18 feature extractor to update its weights to better capture the fine-grained differences between the 15,501 plant species.
2.  **Differential Learning Rates**: Using a smaller learning rate (`1e-4`) for the backbone to preserve ImageNet features and a larger rate (`1e-3`) for the classification head to learn the new decision boundaries (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00003`).
3.  **Data Augmentation**: Implementing `RandomResizedCrop` and `HorizontalFlip` in the training pipeline to improve model generalization.
4.  **Hyperparameter Tuning**: Increasing training to 4 epochs, which is estimated to fit within the 4-hour runtime limit on the A100 GPU (estimated ~2.5 hours).
