To improve performance on the fine-grained iNaturalist 2019 dataset, I will upgrade the model architecture from EfficientNet-B0 to **EfficientNet-B1**, which offers higher capacity for distinguishing between the 1,010 species. I will also increase the input resolution to **240x240** (native for B1) and extend training to **12 epochs** to ensure convergence. Furthermore, I will implement a dynamic resizing strategy in the validation pipeline (resizing to `img_size / 0.875`) to maintain the optimal crop ratio and preserve aspect ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007). Finally, I will update the submission threshold to the new target.
