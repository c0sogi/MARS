The `RuntimeError: expected scalar type Long but found Byte` occurs because the `SegmentationDataset` loads segmentation masks as `uint8` (Byte) arrays (generated by the fallback logic), and `albumentations` preserves this data type when converting to tensors. However, the `nn.CrossEntropyLoss` used in the training loop strictly requires target tensors to be of type `int64` (Long). To resolve this, I will explicitly cast the mask tensor to `long()` within the `__getitem__` method of `SegmentationDataset` after augmentations are applied. This ensures the data type matches the loss function's requirements, applying the lesson on validating tensor types for data fallbacks (Cite {debug_lesson_14}).
