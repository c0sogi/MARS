Based on the provided lessons and previous ideas, I propose the **Dual-Resolution Anatomical Recurrent Network**. This solution structurally evolves the "Anatomically-Grouped" concept (Idea 6) by integrating the "Dual-Branch" recommendation from Lesson 00002 and refining the aggregation mechanism to handle anatomical uncertainty via soft attention.

# Model

The architecture employs a **Global-Local Dual Stream** design to reconcile the need for high-resolution fracture detection with global anatomical context, aggregated by a robust recurrent network.

1.  **Stage 1: Global Context & Localization Stream (2D U-Net)**
    *   **Input:** Downsampled axial slices (e.g., 256x256) covering the full field of view.
    *   **Task:** Multi-class segmentation (Background, C1-C7).
    *   **Outputs:**
        1.  **ROI Coordinates:** Center-of-mass for the spinal column.
        2.  **Anatomical Probability Map:** A soft probability vector ($P_{C1}, ..., P_{C7}$) for each slice, preserving uncertainty at vertebral boundaries.
        3.  **Global Context Vector:** A compressed feature vector (e.g., from the U-Net bottleneck) capturing global spinal alignment and gross deformities **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.

2.  **Stage 2: Local Fracture Stream (2.5D CNN)**
    *   **Input:** **High-Resolution Crops** (e.g., 256x256) extracted from the **original, full-resolution** (512x512) images centered on the Stage 1 coordinates. This avoids the "low-pass filter" effect of global downsampling **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Composition:** A 4-channel tensor: 3 consecutive CT slices (RGB) + the Binary Bone Mask (Alpha) derived from Stage 1.
    *   **Backbone:** A robust 2D CNN (e.g., EfficientNet-V2) that outputs a dense **Local Fracture Embedding**.

3.  **Stage 3: Dual-Stream Soft-Attention Aggregator (Bi-GRU)**
    *   **Input:** A sequence representing the patient scan. At each step $t$, the input is the concatenation of the **Local Fracture Embedding**, the **Global Context Vector**, and the **Anatomical Probability Map**.
    *   **Core:** A **Bidirectional GRU** processes the sequence to model Z-axis consistency **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00009)**.
    *   **Anatomical Attention Heads:** Instead of hard pooling, the model uses 7 specific output heads for C1-C7. The prediction for vertebra $k$ is generated by aggregating the GRU hidden states using the **Stage 1 Probability $P_{Ck}$** as the attention weight. This ensures that the C1 prediction is derived specifically from slices probabilistically identified as C1, while allowing boundary slices to contribute to adjacent vertebrae.
    *   **Patient Head:** A separate Global Attention head aggregates the entire sequence to predict `patient_overall` **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00008)**.

# Data

1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Reorientation:** Parse NIFTI headers to reorient sagittal segmentations to the axial plane to match DICOMs.
    *   **Windowing:** Apply "bone window" (Center: 400, Width: 1800) to all images.
    *   **Dual-Resolution Generation:**
        *   Generate downsampled full-slice images for Stage 1.
        *   Generate high-resolution crops from the **original** images for Stage 2 **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.

2.  **Labeling:**
    *   **Stage 2:** Use `train_bounding_boxes.csv` to create binary slice-level targets (Fracture vs. Background).
    *   **Stage 3:** Use `train.csv` for patient-level multi-label targets.

# Training

The training is split into three phases to decouple feature learning from metric optimization **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.

1.  **Phase 1 (Global Stream):** Train the Stage 1 U-Net on the segmentation subset using Dice Loss + Cross Entropy.

2.  **Phase 2 (Local Stream):** Train the Stage 2 CNN on the slice-level bounding box targets.
    *   **Loss:** Use **Unweighted Binary Cross Entropy (BCE)**. We explicitly **exclude** the competition's heavy `patient_overall` weights here to force the encoder to be precise and conservative, avoiding the amplification of False Positives **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.

3.  **Phase 3 (Aggregator):** Freeze Stage 1 and Stage 2. Pre-compute the sequence of Local Embeddings, Global Vectors, and Anatomical Probabilities for all training patients. Train the Stage 3 Bi-GRU on these sequences.
    *   **Loss:** Use the **Weighted Multi-label Logarithmic Loss**. Since the RNN aggregates evidence across the volume and uses Anatomical Attention to route signals, it can safely optimize the weighted metric without the risk of amplifying single-slice noise inherent to Max Pooling **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00008)**.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference Pipeline:**
    *   **Step 1:** Pass the test scan through Stage 1 to obtain ROI coordinates, Global Context Vectors, and Anatomical Probabilities.
    *   **Step 2:** Extract high-resolution crops and pass them through Stage 2 to get Local Fracture Embeddings.
    *   **Step 3:** Construct the sequence (Local + Global + Probabilities) and feed it to the Stage 3 Bi-GRU.
    *   **Step 4:** The Bi-GRU uses Soft Anatomical Attention to output probabilities for C1-C7 and Global Attention for `patient_overall`.
