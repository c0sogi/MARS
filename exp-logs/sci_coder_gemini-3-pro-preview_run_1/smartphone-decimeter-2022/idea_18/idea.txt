Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Azimuthal-Channel 1D ResUNet with Squeeze-and-Excitation**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in feature representation by transforming the satellite constellation into a **"Sky Panorama"**—encoding directional signal quality into the input channels of the network. This allows the model to explicitly learn the geometry of signal blockage (e.g., "weak signals in the East = longitudinal bias") while leveraging the proven efficiency of the 1D U-Net backbone and the feature selection capabilities of Attention mechanisms.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D SE-ResUNet with ASPP**
The model is a sequence-to-sequence convolutional network designed to map a time-series of geometric signal profiles to coordinate corrections.
*   **Input:** A time-series tensor where the channel dimension encodes both global statistics and directional sectors (Azimuthal Bins).
*   **Encoder:** A stack of **1D Residual Blocks** for deep feature extraction without gradient degradation.
*   **Channel Attention (Structural Innovation):** Each Residual Block incorporates a **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** module. This allows the network to dynamically recalibrate the importance of specific directional sectors (channels) based on the global context (e.g., suppressing the "North" channels if the vehicle is moving South and those signals are irrelevant or noisy).
*   **Bottleneck:** An **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module captures multi-scale temporal dependencies, handling both instantaneous multipath spikes and slow atmospheric drifts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Decoder:** Upsampling layers concatenated with **Skip Connections** from the encoder to preserve high-frequency temporal details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Heads:** Multi-scale output heads are attached to the decoder for Deep Supervision.

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss from clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Azimuthal Feature Engineering (Innovation):**
    *   **Global Stats:** Calculate Mean, **Standard Deviation**, **Minimum**, and Maximum for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees` across all satellites (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020, solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Sky Panorama:** Divide the azimuth (0-360°) into $K$ fixed sectors (e.g., 8 bins of 45°). For each sector, calculate:
        *   `Max_Cn0`: The strength of the best signal in that direction.
        *   `Mean_Elevation`: The average height of satellites in that direction.
        *   `Sat_Count`: The number of satellites in that direction.
    *   *Note:* This creates a structured geometric representation (e.g., 8 sectors * 3 features = 24 channels) that preserves the *directionality* of signal health, unlike global averages.
    *   **Metadata:** Include global `SatCount` and aggregated `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
3.  **Translation Invariance:** Absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` are strictly excluded to prevent map-memorization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
4.  **Target Transformation:** Regression targets are transformed from Degrees to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)** is used for its robustness to outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Scaled Deep Supervision:** The total loss is a weighted sum of the final and auxiliary heads. Crucially, the ground truth targets for auxiliary heads are **downsampled (Average Pooled)** to match the temporal resolution of the intermediate layers, preventing gradient conflicts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).
*   **Optimization:** AdamW optimizer with **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule and **Gradient Clipping** to ensure stability given the increased architectural complexity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
*   **Validation:** `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` to prevent data leakage.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed "Panorama" feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
