I will modify `library/data_loader.py` to forward-fill and backward-fill missing GNSS values (specifically WLS coordinates and signal metrics) before converting ECEF to LLA, and ensure all input features are free of NaNs before model training. Finally, I will update `library/model.py` to include a `nn.BatchNorm1d` layer at the input of `SensorFusionTCN` to normalize features, preventing exploding gradients.
