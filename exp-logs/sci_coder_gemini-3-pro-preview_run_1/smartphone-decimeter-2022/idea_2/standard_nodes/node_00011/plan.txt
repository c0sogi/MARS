Based on the lessons learned, specifically `solution_lesson_node_00010` ("Signal Quality vs. Kinematics in Residual Correction"), I will optimize the solution by removing kinematic features derived from IMU and WLS velocity proxies. These features were shown to correlate with error magnitude but likely confused the model rather than helping it correct positions. Instead, I will focus exclusively on GNSS signal quality metrics (`Cn0`, `Elevation`, `SatCount`, `Uncertainty`) and Altitude, which quantify measurement integrity.

Additionally, citing `solution_lesson_node_00003`, I will increase the training duration to 30 epochs to ensure convergence, as 5 epochs was insufficient for the model to fully learn the residual patterns. I will also streamline the data loader to skip IMU processing, improving efficiency.
