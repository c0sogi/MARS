The `ZeroDivisionError` in `library/trainer.py` occurs because the validation dataset is empty, resulting in `total_samples` being zero during metric calculation. This emptiness is caused by a cache collision in `library/utils.py`: `process_target_residuals` saves computed residuals to a fixed filename (`target_residuals.parquet`). When the script runs, it first caches residuals for the training split. When it subsequently attempts to load residuals for the validation split, it loads the cached training residuals. Since the training and validation splits contain disjoint sets of drives, merging the validation metadata with training residuals results in an empty DataFrame.

To fix this, I will:
1.  Modify `library/utils.py` to generate a unique cache filename for `target_residuals` based on a hash of the `drive_id`s in the input metadata. This ensures that different data splits do not overwrite or read each other's cached residuals (Cite debug_lesson_3).
2.  Update `library/trainer.py` to explicitly handle the case where `total_samples` is zero in the `validate` method to prevent the division by zero error.
