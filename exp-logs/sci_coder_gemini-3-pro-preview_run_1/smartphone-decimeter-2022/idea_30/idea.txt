Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Phase-Aware Stratified 1D SE-ResUNet with Decimated Deep Supervision**.

This strategy synthesizes the most effective feature engineering paradigm (**Stratified Aggregation** from Cite solution_lesson_node_00063) with the robust **1D ResUNet** backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018). It introduces **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** blocks to dynamically handle the sparsity of high-precision signals and rigorously applies **Decimated Deep Supervision** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060) to ensure stable training of the deep architecture, correcting the gradient conflicts observed in previous multi-scale attempts.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D SE-ResUNet with ASPP**
The model is a sequence-to-sequence convolutional network designed to map a time-series of stratified sensor features to coordinate corrections.
*   **Backbone:** A **1D Residual U-Net**. The Encoder and Decoder are symmetric, built from **1D Residual Blocks**.
*   **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE):** Each Residual Block incorporates an **SE Module**. This allows the network to perform channel-wise attention. Crucially, this enables the model to dynamically "switch off" channels corresponding to the **High-Precision Stratum** when those signals are unavailable (zero-filled) or "turn up" the **Low-Quality Stratum** channels when the vehicle enters an urban canyon and relies on poor signals.
*   **ASPP Bottleneck:** The deepest layer utilizes **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** with dilation rates (e.g., 1, 6, 12, 24). This captures multi-scale temporal context, distinguishing between instantaneous multipath spikes and long-term atmospheric drifts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Decimated Auxiliary Heads (Structural Innovation):** Intermediate decoder layers are equipped with auxiliary output heads. Unlike standard Deep Supervision, the ground truth targets for these heads are **decimated (subsampled)** to match the temporal resolution of the layer (e.g., every 4th ground truth point for a 1/4 scale head) rather than average-pooled. This forces the encoder to learn valid instantaneous states (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss from clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Phase-Aware Stratification:** For each timestamp, partition visible satellites into three strata to ensure **Feature Completeness** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033) and signal discrimination:
    *   **Stratum 1 (Global):** All visible satellites.
    *   **Stratum 2 (High-Precision):** Satellites with `SignalType` in {L5, E5a, B2a} **OR** `AccumulatedDeltaRangeState` indicating **Valid Carrier Phase**. This groups the most reliable "anchors".
    *   **Stratum 3 (Low-Quality):** Satellites with Elevation < 30° or `Cn0DbHz` < 20. This groups likely noise sources.
3.  **Feature Aggregation:** For each stratum, compute robust statistics: **Mean, Standard Deviation, Minimum, and Maximum** for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
4.  **Geometric Context:** Concatenate these with global metadata (`SatCount`, `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters`) and the **Signal-Weighted Azimuth Centroid**. Crucially, decompose the azimuth into **Sine and Cosine** components to avoid numerical discontinuities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00064).
5.  **Translation Invariance:** Strictly exclude absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
6.  **Target Transformation:** Convert regression targets from Degrees to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. The total loss is a weighted sum: $L_{total} = L_{final} + \lambda \sum L_{aux\_decimated}$. MAE is used for robustness to heavy-tailed outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Gradient Clipping** and a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule. This addresses the instability observed with complex architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
*   **Validation Strategy:** A `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` is used to ensure the model is evaluated on entirely unseen trips.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed stratified feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
