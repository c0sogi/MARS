Based on the lessons learned, I propose the **Stratified 1D Attention ResUNet with Decimated Deep Supervision**.

This strategy synthesizes the most effective feature engineering paradigm (**Stratified Aggregation** from Cite solution_lesson_node_00063) with a sophisticated architectural mechanism (**Attention Gates** from Cite solution_lesson_node_00015) that was previously hindered by incorrect training targets. By applying the critical fix of **Decimated Deep Supervision** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060), we can now successfully leverage Attention Gates to dynamically filter noise from the stratified inputs, distinguishing between "clean" L5 signals and "noisy" multipath reflections.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D Residual U-Net with Attention Gates and ASPP**
The model is a sequence-to-sequence Encoder-Decoder network.
*   **Stratified Input:** The network accepts a multi-channel time-series tensor where channels represent statistics from different satellite strata (Global, High-Precision, High-Risk).
*   **Residual Encoder:** A series of **1D Residual Blocks** followed by Max Pooling layers to extract hierarchical features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **ASPP Bottleneck:** An **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling** module captures multi-scale temporal context, distinguishing between instantaneous signal spikes and long-term atmospheric drifts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Attention-Gated Decoder:** Upsampling layers are connected to the encoder via **Attention Gates** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
    *   *Mechanism:* Instead of simple concatenation, the gate uses the upsampled decoder feature (context) to compute a spatial attention map that weights the encoder features (local details). This suppresses irrelevant noise (e.g., multipath jitter encoded in the "High-Risk" stratum) while passing through relevant signal transients, refining the reconstruction.
*   **Decimated Auxiliary Heads:** Intermediate decoder layers have auxiliary output heads for Deep Supervision.

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Stratified Feature Aggregation:** For each timestamp, partition satellites into three strata (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063):
    *   **Stratum 1 (Global):** All visible satellites.
    *   **Stratum 2 (High-Precision):** Satellites with `SignalType` in {L5, E5a, B2a} or Valid Carrier Phase.
    *   **Stratum 3 (High-Risk):** Satellites with Elevation < 30°.
    *   For each stratum, compute **Mean, Standard Deviation, Minimum, and Maximum** for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
3.  **Geometric Context:** Concatenate these with global metadata (`SatCount`, `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters`) and the **Signal-Weighted Azimuth Centroid** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
4.  **Translation Invariance:** Strictly exclude absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
5.  **Target Transformation:** Convert regression targets to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. The total loss is a weighted sum of the final output and auxiliary outputs.
*   **Decimated Deep Supervision (Critical Fix):** For the auxiliary heads operating at lower temporal resolutions (e.g., 1/2, 1/4 scale), the ground truth target is **decimated (subsampled)** to match the resolution, rather than average-pooled. This forces the encoder to learn valid instantaneous states, stabilizing the training of the Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Gradient Clipping** and **Cosine Annealing** to manage the increased complexity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Feed the preprocessed stratified feature sequence for a full test drive into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** Convert Cartesian offsets to angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** Add the predicted offsets to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
