The core idea is to augment the input feature space of the Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) with metadata that quantifies the quality and uncertainty of the baseline WLS solution. Specifically, I will add:
1.  **`SatCount`**: The number of satellites/signals used in the epoch. A higher count generally implies a more robust geometric fix.
2.  **`RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters`**: The aggregated uncertainty of the raw pseudorange measurements.

This requires modifying the data loading pipeline to extract and aggregate these fields from `device_gnss.csv`, and updating the model definitions to accept 5 input channels (Altitude, CN0, Elevation, SatCount, Uncertainty) instead of the previous 3. I will also update the cache directory to `./working/idea_3/` to ensure fresh feature extraction.
