Based on the accumulated lessons, specifically the importance of **Feature Completeness** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033), the efficiency of **1D U-Nets** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018), and the critical correction regarding **Deep Supervision Targets** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060), I propose the **Stratified-Signal 1D ResUNet with Decimated Deep Supervision**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in feature representation by **stratifying** the satellite signals into distinct quality groups (Global, High-Precision L5, and Low-Elevation) before aggregation. This allows the model to explicitly contrast "clean" vs. "noisy" signal populations within the channel dimension, rather than blurring them into a single global average. It also rigorously applies the corrected Deep Supervision methodology to ensure stable training of the deep architecture.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D Residual U-Net with ASPP**
The backbone is a sequence-to-sequence Encoder-Decoder network designed to map a time-series of stratified signal features to coordinate corrections.
*   **Multi-Channel Input:** The input tensor is constructed by concatenating feature vectors from three distinct satellite strata (detailed in Data).
*   **Residual Encoder:** A series of **1D Residual Blocks** (ResNet style) followed by Max Pooling layers. This extracts hierarchical features while preventing gradient degradation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **ASPP Bottleneck:** An **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling** module processes the compressed features with multiple dilation rates. This captures both local signal spikes (multipath) and global trajectory drifts (atmospheric bias) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) while maintaining the local inductive bias of CNNs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Residual Decoder:** Upsampling layers concatenated with **Skip Connections** from the encoder to preserve high-frequency temporal details.
*   **Decimated Auxiliary Heads (Structural Innovation):** Intermediate decoder layers are equipped with auxiliary output heads. Unlike standard Deep Supervision which compares these low-resolution outputs to *averaged* ground truth, these heads are trained against **decimated (subsampled)** ground truth targets. This forces the encoder to learn valid instantaneous states rather than smoothed approximations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Output Head:** A final projection layer predicts the 2D residual vector $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ in meters.

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss from clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Stratified Feature Aggregation (Innovation):** For each timestamp, partition visible satellites into three overlapping strata:
    *   **Stratum 1 (Global):** All visible satellites.
    *   **Stratum 2 (High-Precision):** Satellites transmitting on L5/E5a/B2a frequencies (physically more robust to multipath).
    *   **Stratum 3 (High-Risk):** Satellites with Elevation < 30 degrees (highest probability of non-line-of-sight).
    *   For *each* stratum, compute robust statistics: **Mean, Standard Deviation, Minimum, and Maximum** for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   If a stratum is empty (e.g., no L5 satellites), fill with zero/mask values.
3.  **Geometric Context:** Concatenate these stratified features with global metadata (`SatCount`, `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters`) and the **Signal-Weighted Azimuth Centroid** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033) to capture constellation orientation.
4.  **Translation Invariance:** Strictly exclude absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
5.  **Target Transformation:** Convert regression targets from Degrees to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. The total loss is a weighted sum: $L_{total} = L_{final} + \lambda \sum L_{aux}$.
    *   Crucially, $L_{aux}$ compares the downsampled prediction to the **decimated** ground truth (e.g., taking every 2nd or 4th point) rather than the average, ensuring the model learns to predict precise residuals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule and **Gradient Clipping** to ensure stability given the increased channel depth (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
*   **Validation Strategy:** A `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` is used to ensure the model is evaluated on entirely unseen trips.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed stratified feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
