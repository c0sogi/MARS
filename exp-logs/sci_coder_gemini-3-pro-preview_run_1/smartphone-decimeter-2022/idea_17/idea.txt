Based on the lessons learned, I propose the **Cascaded 1D ResUNet with Scaled Deep Supervision**.

This strategy structurally evolves the successful **1D U-Net** approach (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) by implementing a **Cascaded (Two-Stage)** architecture to explicitly decompose the error correction into "Global Drift" and "Local Noise" components. It addresses the training instability of complex models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042) and the gradient conflicts of previous multi-scale attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037) by rigorously applying **Scaled Deep Supervision** and gradient stabilization techniques.

### Model
**Architecture: Two-Stage Cascaded 1D Residual U-Net**
The model consists of two sequential U-Net modules.
1.  **Stage 1 (Coarse Correction):** A deep **1D Residual U-Net** with an **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** bottleneck.
    *   *Purpose:* To capture long-term dependencies and correct low-frequency errors (e.g., atmospheric bias, clock drift) using the wide receptive field of ASPP (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   *Structural Innovation:* **Scaled Deep Supervision**. Auxiliary output heads are attached to the intermediate decoder layers. Crucially, the ground truth targets for these auxiliary heads are **downsampled (Average Pooled)** to match the temporal resolution of the specific layer. This prevents the "conflicting gradient" failure observed previously (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).
2.  **Stage 2 (Fine Refinement):** A shallower 1D U-Net.
    *   *Input:* Concatenation of the original input features and the residual prediction from Stage 1.
    *   *Purpose:* To predict the high-frequency residual errors (e.g., multipath noise) that the deeper Stage 1 model might smooth over.
3.  **Final Output:** The sum of the Stage 1 and Stage 2 predictions.

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Align raw GNSS logs and ground truth by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss from clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Feature Completeness:** Construct a dense feature vector for each timestamp that captures all physical degrees of freedom (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033):
    *   **Signal Distribution:** Compute **Mean, Standard Deviation, Minimum, and Maximum** for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees`. The "Minimum" is vital for detecting weak links (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Geometric Orientation:** Compute **Mean Sine** and **Mean Cosine** of `SvAzimuthDegrees` to encode constellation shape.
    *   **Signal-Weighted Geometry:** Calculate the weighted average of satellite unit vectors, weighted by their signal strength ($Cn0$). This creates a "Center of Signal Mass" feature.
    *   **Metadata:** `SatCount` and aggregated `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
3.  **Translation Invariance:** Strictly exclude absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` to prevent map memorization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
4.  **Target Transformation:** Convert regression targets from Degrees (Lat/Lon) to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)** is used for all heads due to its robustness to outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). The total loss is a weighted sum: $L_{total} = L_{Stage2} + \lambda \sum L_{Stage1\_Aux}$.
*   **Optimization Stabilization:** To prevent the training instability observed in complex architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042), employ:
    *   **Gradient Clipping:** To cap outliers in the backward pass.
    *   **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate:** To smooth convergence.
*   **Validation Strategy:** Use `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` to ensure evaluation on unseen trips.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Feed the preprocessed feature sequence for a full test drive into the cascaded model.
2.  **Reconstruction:** The model outputs Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$. Convert these back to angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** Add the predicted offsets to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
