The `ValueError: Expected more than 1 value per channel when training` occurs because the training dataset in the demo has 3 samples and the batch size is 2, resulting in a final batch of size 1. The model's Batch Normalization layers (specifically in the ASPP module after global pooling) require at least two samples to compute variance. To fix this, I will set `drop_last=True` in the `DataLoader` instantiation within `runfile.py`. This ensures that incomplete batches are discarded during training, preventing the model from encountering a batch size of 1. This solution applies **Cite debug_lesson_16**.
