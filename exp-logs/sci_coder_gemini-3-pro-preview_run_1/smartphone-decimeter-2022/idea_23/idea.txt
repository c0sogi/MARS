Based on the lessons learned, I propose the **Stratified 1D SE-ResUNet with Phase-Aware Signal Aggregation**.

This strategy structurally evolves the successful **Stratified 1D ResUNet** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063) by integrating **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** modules into the backbone. This allows the network to dynamically recalibrate the importance of different signal strata (e.g., boosting L5 channels when available, suppressing them when empty/noisy) based on the context of the drive window. Additionally, it enhances feature completeness by incorporating **Carrier Phase Validity** metrics, a critical indicator of signal integrity previously unused.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D Squeeze-and-Excitation Residual U-Net**
The model is a sequence-to-sequence Encoder-Decoder network designed to map a time-series of stratified sensor features to coordinate corrections.
*   **SE-Residual Encoder:** The contracting path consists of stacked **1D Residual Blocks**, where each block is equipped with a **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** module.
    *   *Mechanism:* The SE block aggregates global temporal information (Global Average Pooling) to compute channel-wise importance weights. This allows the model to selectively emphasize specific signal strata (e.g., "High Precision L5") or feature types (e.g., "Elevation Min") depending on the driving environment (e.g., Open Sky vs. Urban Canyon).
*   **ASPP Bottleneck:** The deepest layer utilizes **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** with varying dilation rates. This captures multi-scale temporal context, distinguishing between instantaneous multipath spikes and long-term atmospheric drifts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Residual Decoder:** Upsampling layers concatenated with **Skip Connections** from the encoder to preserve high-frequency temporal details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Decimated Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary output heads are attached to intermediate decoder layers. The loss for these heads is computed against **decimated (subsampled)** ground truth targets, ensuring the encoder learns valid instantaneous states rather than smoothed averages (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)** to prevent data loss from clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Phase-Aware Stratified Aggregation (Innovation):** For each timestamp, partition visible satellites into three strata: (1) **Global** (All), (2) **High-Precision** (L5/E5a/B2a bands), and (3) **High-Risk** (Elevation < 30°). For each stratum, compute a dense feature vector:
    *   **Signal Statistics:** Mean, Standard Deviation, **Minimum**, and Maximum for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Phase Integrity (New):** Calculate the **count and fraction** of satellites with valid Carrier Phase (derived from `AccumulatedDeltaRangeState` bit 0). Valid phase lock is a strong proxy for clean, line-of-sight signals.
    *   If a stratum is empty, fill with zero/mask values.
3.  **Geometric Context:** Concatenate these stratified features with global metadata (`SatCount`, `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters`) and the **Signal-Weighted Azimuth Centroid** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
4.  **Translation Invariance:** Strictly exclude absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
5.  **Target Transformation:** Convert regression targets from Degrees to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)** is used for all heads due to its robustness against heavy-tailed outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). The total loss is a weighted sum of the final and auxiliary head losses.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Gradient Clipping** and a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule to ensure stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
*   **Validation Strategy:** A `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` is used to ensure the model is evaluated on entirely unseen trips.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed phase-aware stratified feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
