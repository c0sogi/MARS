The `ValueError` during training is caused by the `BatchNorm1d` layer receiving a batch of size 1. This occurs because the training dataset size (3 trips in the demo) is not divisible by the batch size (2), resulting in a final batch with a single sample. Batch Normalization requires at least two samples to calculate variance. To fix this, I will set `drop_last=True` in the training `DataLoader` within `library/train.py`, which ensures that incomplete batches are discarded during training.
