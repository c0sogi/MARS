The `RuntimeError` is caused by a channel mismatch in the decoder blocks of the U-Net architecture. In the forward pass, the upsampled features are concatenated with the skip connections from the encoder. The number of channels after concatenation is the sum of the upsampled channels and the skip connection channels.

1.  **Stage 4:** Upsampled `d4` (256 channels) + Skip `x4` (512 channels) = 768 channels. The `dec4` block was expecting 512.
2.  **Stage 3:** Upsampled `d3` (128 channels) + Skip `x3` (256 channels) = 384 channels. The `dec3` block was expecting 256.
3.  **Stage 2:** Upsampled `d2` (64 channels) + Skip `x2` (128 channels) = 192 channels. The `dec2` block was expecting 128.

I will update the initialization of `self.dec4`, `self.dec3`, and `self.dec2` in `library/model.py` to accept the correct number of input channels, ensuring consistency with the concatenation operations in the forward pass (Cite debug_lesson_8).
