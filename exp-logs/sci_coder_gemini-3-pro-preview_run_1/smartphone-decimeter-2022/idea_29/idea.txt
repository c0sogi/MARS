Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **High-Resolution 1D Residual Network (HR-1D-ResNet) with Phase-Aware Stratified Aggregation**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation by replacing the serial Encoder-Decoder (U-Net) backbone with a **Parallel Multi-Resolution** architecture. While U-Nets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) successfully capture global context via downsampling, they risk losing the precise high-frequency information required to correct instantaneous multipath spikes. The HR-1D-ResNet maintains a high-resolution stream throughout the entire forward pass, fusing it with parallel low-resolution streams to capture global drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) without the information bottleneck of a U-Net. This is paired with **Phase-Aware Stratification** to explicitly separate high-integrity signals from noise.

### Model
**Architecture: High-Resolution 1D ResNet (HR-1D-ResNet)**
The model processes the entire drive sequence simultaneously using parallel convolutional streams at different temporal resolutions.
*   **Stem:** A standard 1D Convolutional layer projects the input features into the latent space.
*   **Parallel Streams:** The network branches into three parallel streams that operate continuously:
    1.  **High-Res Stream ($T$):** Maintains the original 1Hz temporal resolution. This stream focuses on detecting and correcting sharp, local anomalies (e.g., multipath spikes).
    2.  **Med-Res Stream ($T/4$):** Downsampled by factor 4. Captures neighborhood context and maneuvers.
    3.  **Low-Res Stream ($T/16$):** Downsampled by factor 16. Captures long-term biases like atmospheric delay or clock drift.
*   **Multi-Scale Fusion:** At regular intervals (e.g., every 4 residual blocks), "Fusion Units" exchange information between streams.
    *   *High-to-Low:* Strided convolutions downsample features to inject local details into global context.
    *   *Low-to-High:* Bilinear upsampling followed by 1x1 convolutions injects global context into the local stream.
*   **Output Head:** The final prediction is generated solely from the **High-Res Stream**, ensuring no loss of temporal precision. A projection layer outputs the 2D residual vector $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$.

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss from clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Phase-Aware Stratified Aggregation:** For each timestamp, visible satellites are partitioned into three distinct strata to ensure **Feature Completeness** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033) and signal discrimination (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063):
    *   **Stratum 1 (Global):** All visible satellites.
    *   **Stratum 2 (High-Integrity):** Satellites with `SignalType` in {L5, E5a, B2a} **OR** `AccumulatedDeltaRangeState` indicating **Valid Carrier Phase**. This groups the most reliable "anchors".
    *   **Stratum 3 (Low-Quality):** Satellites with Elevation < 30° or `Cn0DbHz` < 25. This groups likely noise sources.
    *   For each stratum, compute robust statistics: **Mean, Standard Deviation, Minimum, and Maximum** for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees`. The "Minimum" is critical for identifying weak links (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
3.  **Geometric Context:** Concatenate these stratified features with global metadata (`SatCount`, `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters`) and the **Signal-Weighted Azimuth Centroid** (decomposed into Sine/Cosine) to capture constellation orientation without absolute heading (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033, solution_lesson_node_00069).
4.  **Translation Invariance:** Absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` are strictly excluded (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
5.  **Target Transformation:** Regression targets are transformed from Degrees to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)** is used for robustness to outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Multi-Stream Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary loss heads are attached to the Med-Res and Low-Res streams. Crucially, the ground truth targets for these heads are **decimated (subsampled)** to match their respective resolutions ($T/4, T/16$) rather than averaged. This forces the low-res streams to learn valid instantaneous state corrections, stabilizing the fusion process (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Gradient Clipping** and **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule to handle the multi-branch gradient flow (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed stratified feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the High-Res stream.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
