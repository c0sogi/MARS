The `CUDA error: out of memory` during model initialization indicates that the GPU memory is exhausted. This is caused by the high memory requirements of the current configuration (`IMAGE_SIZE=768`, `BATCH_SIZE=16`) combined with the resource fragmentation typical of a persistent execution environment (Cite `debug_lesson_5`). Additionally, the `IMAGE_SIZE` of 768 unnecessarily upsamples the pre-processed data, which is natively 512x512. To resolve this, I will reduce `IMAGE_SIZE` to 512 and `BATCH_SIZE` to 8 in `library/config.py` to significantly lower the memory footprint. I will also add explicit garbage collection in `runfile.py` before clearing the CUDA cache to ensure "zombie" tensors are released (Cite `debug_lesson_2`).
