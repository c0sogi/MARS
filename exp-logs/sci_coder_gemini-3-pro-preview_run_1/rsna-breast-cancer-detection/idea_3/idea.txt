Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the specific lessons learned, I propose the following optimized strategy.

### Model
**Architecture: Stochastic Metadata-Embedded EfficientNet-B2**

*   **Backbone:** Upgrade the backbone to **EfficientNet-B2**. The previous use of B0 was sufficient for convergence, but B2 offers the increased parameter count and receptive field necessary to resolve fine-grained microcalcifications in the higher-resolution inputs required for this task.
*   **Input Strategy (Early Fusion):** Retain the **Spatial Channel Expansion** technique. The input tensor will be $3 \times H \times W$, comprising:
    1.  **Image Channel:** The preprocessed mammogram.
    2.  **Age Channel:** A spatially broadcasted map of the patient's age.
    3.  **Implant Channel:** A spatially broadcasted map of the binary implant status.
    *   *Cite Lesson 00002:* This structure allows the convolutional filters to learn context-aware features (entangling demographics with texture) from the first layer, which is superior to Late Fusion.
*   **Structural Innovation: Stochastic Modality Dropout:** Introduce a custom **Channel Dropout Layer** immediately after the input construction. During training, this layer will randomly zero out the **Age** and **Implant** channels with a probability of $p=0.5$, while leaving the Image channel intact.
    *   *Rationale:* Previous iterations failed because the model engaged in "shortcut learning," relying heavily on the strong demographic prior (Age) and ignoring the complex visual signal, evidenced by the high error-age correlation (*Cite Lesson 00012, Lesson 00014*). By intermittently removing the metadata, the network is forced to minimize loss using *only* the visual features for half the training steps, thereby robustly learning to identify tumor patterns without becoming an "age regressor."

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**

*   **Image Pipeline:**
    *   Load DICOMs using `rasterio`.
    *   Apply a threshold-based **ROI Crop** to remove the background.
    *   Resize images to **768x768**. This resolution balances the capacity of the B2 backbone with the need to preserve small lesion details.
*   **Metadata Normalization:**
    *   Apply **Standard Scaling** (Zero Mean, Unit Variance) to the Age feature before spatial broadcasting.
    *   *Cite Lesson 00005:* This ensures the metadata channel has unit variance, preventing it from being overpowered by image features (as seen with MinMax scaling) or dominating gradients due to magnitude.
*   **Augmentation Strategy:**
    *   Apply **Moderate Geometric Augmentations** (Shift, Scale, Rotate, Flip). *Cite Lesson 00007.*
    *   **Strictly Limit Photometric Augmentation:** Use only very mild brightness/contrast shifts or disable them entirely.
    *   *Cite Lesson 00008:* Aggressive photometric augmentation destroys the tissue density signal, which is the primary diagnostic feature in mammography.

### Training
**Procedure**

*   **Loss Function:** Use `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with `pos_weight` set to approximately **47.0** (the inverse class frequency).
    *   *Cite Lesson 00003:* In this highly imbalanced regime (<3% positive), aggressive positive weighting is required to maintain Probabilistic Recall; conservative weights cause the model to collapse to the majority class.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a learning rate of 1e-4 and a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
*   **Gradient Strategy:** **Disable Gradient Clipping**.
    *   *Cite Lesson 00009:* Clipping gradients when using a high `pos_weight` neutralizes the weighting mechanism, leading to slow convergence and poor minority class prioritization.
*   **Duration:** Train for 8-10 epochs. The use of Modality Dropout increases the difficulty of the training task, requiring slightly more iterations for the visual features to converge compared to the baseline.

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**

*   **State:** Disable the Stochastic Modality Dropout layer (always provide full metadata channels) to maximize predictive power during inference.
*   **Aggregation:** For each `prediction_id` (representing a breast), generate predictions for all associated images (views). Compute the **Max** probability across these views to determine the final cancer likelihood, aligning with clinical screening logic.
