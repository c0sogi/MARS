Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous failures and successes, I propose the **Resolution-Aligned Time-Preserving EfficientNet-BiGRU**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Current Best" ResNet-based approach by transitioning to an **EfficientNet** backbone to leverage its superior channel-wise attention capabilities. Crucially, it rectifies the specific implementation flaws (Loss Weighting, Input Truncation, and Resolution Mismatch) that caused the previous EfficientNet attempt (Idea 5) to fail, synthesizing lessons from across the development history.

### Model
The architecture is a **Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network (CRNN)** designed to align the backbone's feature extraction capacity with the temporal dynamics of the target signal.

1.  **Backbone: Time-Preserving EfficientNet-B0**
    *   **Architecture**: Replace ResNet-18 with **EfficientNet-B0**. The key structural advantage is the inclusion of **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** blocks, which perform dynamic channel-wise attention. This allows the model to selectively emphasize frequency bands relevant to the whale call while suppressing noise, a capability lacking in standard ResNet blocks.
    *   **Initialization**: Initialize with **ImageNet pre-trained weights**. Programmatically average the weights of the first convolutional layer (originally 3 channels) to 1 channel to adapt to the spectrogram input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Time-Preservation (Structural Innovation)**: Standard EfficientNet downsamples inputs by 32x. We will modify the strides of the final three stages (MBConv blocks) to be **Asymmetric `(2, 1)`** (Frequency, Time). This compresses the frequency dimension to extract abstract features while limiting temporal downsampling to a factor of **4x**. This ensures the downstream RNN receives a sequence of sufficient length (~50 steps) to detect the "up-sweep" motion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

2.  **Temporal Modeling: Bi-Directional GRU**
    *   The output feature maps are pooled along the frequency dimension and permuted to a sequence format $(T \times C)$. This sequence is fed into a **Bi-directional GRU**, preserving the component proven most effective for modeling the specific temporal contour of the right whale call (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

3.  **Aggregation: Attention Pooling**
    *   Utilize a learnable **Attention Pooling** layer to compute a weighted sum of the GRU outputs, focusing the classification on the specific timeframe of the call.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Input Window**: Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms covering the **full 2.0-second duration** of the clip. Do not truncate to 1.0s, ensuring the signal is never discarded (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Resolution Alignment**: Tune the spectrogram hop length (e.g., ~10ms) to produce an input width of approximately **200-250 time frames**. Combined with the 4x backbone downsampling, this yields a sequence of ~50-60 steps for the RNN. This avoids the "Signal Dilution" caused by excessive resolution in Idea 5 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

2.  **Augmentation**:
    *   **Mixup**: Apply Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$) to training batches to regularize the model and accelerate convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **SpecAugment**: Apply Frequency and Time Masking.
    *   **Constraint**: Strictly limit the **Time Mask width to a maximum of 200ms** (approx. 20 frames). This ensures the augmentation does not occlude the entire short-duration whale call (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training
1.  **Loss Function**: Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss**.
    *   **Critical Fix**: Apply a **Positive Class Weight** (e.g., 9.0) to explicitly handle the 1:9 class imbalance. This corrects the "Loss Misalignment" failure of Idea 5 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Target Handling**: Compute loss against the **mixed soft targets** generated by Mixup.
2.  **Optimizer**: Use **Adam** with a standard learning rate.
3.  **Scheduler**: Implement **ReduceLROnPlateau** to decay the learning rate when Validation AUC stagnates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: Process test clips into 2.0s Log-Mel Spectrograms using the aligned resolution parameters.
2.  **Prediction**: Pass inputs through the model. The output of the Attention Pooling layer is passed through a Sigmoid activation to generate the probability.
3.  **Metric**: Monitor **Validation AUC** on unmixed data during training to judge convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
