To optimize the performance of the Right Whale Detection model, I will implement a strategy focused on **reducing variance** and **enhancing robustness**, leveraging the available computational budget (3 hours) and insights from the provided lessons.

**Optimization Strategy:**

1.  **Expanded Ensemble (Cite Lesson 00066):** The previous solution identified run-to-run variance as a limiting factor in the high-precision regime (>0.995 AUC). I will increase the ensemble size from 5 to 10 seeds. This will stabilize the decision boundary and statistically improve the final metric by averaging out stochastic noise.
2.  **Amplitude Jitter (Cite Lesson 00006):** I will introduce a new data augmentation technique: Amplitude Jitter. By adding random scalar shifts to the Log-Mel Spectrograms (simulating volume changes), the model will learn to be invariant to signal loudness, improving generalization.
3.  **Refined SpecAugment (Cite Lesson 00011):** I will slightly increase the `TIME_MASK_PARAM` from 10 (160ms) to 12 (192ms). This pushes the regularization closer to the safe limit (200ms) relative to the whale call duration, encouraging the model to rely on distributed temporal features without occluding the entire signal.
4.  **Configuration Consistency:** I will consolidate the hyperparameters (Batch Size 128, Epochs 12) directly into `Config` to ensure consistent execution across the expanded ensemble.
