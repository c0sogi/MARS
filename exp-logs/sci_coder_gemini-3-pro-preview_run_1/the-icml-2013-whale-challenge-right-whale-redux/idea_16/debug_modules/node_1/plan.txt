The execution failure is caused by the inclusion of the `verbose` parameter in the initialization of the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler. In recent versions of PyTorch (2.2+), this parameter has been deprecated and removed, leading to a `TypeError` when provided. To resolve this, I will remove the `verbose=False` argument from the scheduler instantiation in both `library/trainer.py` and `library/model.py`, as the default behavior now aligns with the intention of not printing updates automatically. Cite debug_lesson_2.
