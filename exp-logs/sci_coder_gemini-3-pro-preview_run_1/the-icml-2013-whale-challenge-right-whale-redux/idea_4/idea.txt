Based on the provided lessons and previous ideas, I propose the **Time-Preserving EfficientNet-BiGRU** architecture. This solution structurally evolves the feature extraction backbone from a standard ResNet to an EfficientNet, introducing channel-wise attention mechanisms while strictly preserving the temporal resolution and augmentation constraints identified as critical in the lessons.

### Model
The architecture is a **Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network (CRNN)** designed to leverage advanced spectral feature extraction with robust temporal modeling.

1.  **Backbone: Time-Preserving EfficientNet-B0**
    We replace the ResNet-18 backbone with an **EfficientNet-B0**.
    *   **Structural Innovation**: EfficientNet utilizes **Inverted Residual Blocks (MBConv)** equipped with **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** modules. Unlike the standard convolutions in ResNet, the SE blocks explicitly model channel interdependencies, allowing the network to dynamically recalibrate the importance of specific feature maps. This enables the model to selectively emphasize frequency bands containing the whale call while suppressing noise-dominant channels at every stage of the hierarchy.
    *   **Time-Preservation**: Standard EfficientNet backbones downsample inputs by a factor of 32, which destroys the temporal structure of short audio clips (Cite Lesson 00012). We will modify the architecture to use **Asymmetric Strides**. For the final three stages of the network, we set the stride to `(2, 1)` (Frequency, Time). This compresses the frequency dimension to extract abstract features while maintaining a temporal sequence length of ~50-60 steps, sufficient to capture the "up-sweep" motion.
    *   **Initialization**: The backbone will be initialized with **ImageNet pre-trained weights**. We will programmatically average the weights of the first convolutional layer (originally 3 channels) to adapt it to the 1-channel spectrogram input (Cite Lesson 00020).

2.  **Temporal Modeling: Bi-Directional GRU**
    The output feature maps are pooled along the frequency dimension and permuted to a sequence format $(T \times C)$. This sequence is fed into a **Bi-directional GRU**. We retain this component as it has proven highly effective at modeling the specific temporal contour (shape) of the right whale up-call (Cite Lesson 00001).

3.  **Aggregation: Attention Pooling**
    The GRU output sequence is aggregated using an **Attention Pooling** layer. This allows the model to learn a weighted sum of the time steps, focusing the classification decision on the specific segment containing the call and ignoring background noise.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   Convert audio clips to **Log-Mel Spectrograms** with **128 Mel bins** (to support the deeper backbone), a window size of 25ms, and a hop length of 10ms.
    *   Normalize spectrograms using the dataset-level mean and standard deviation.

2.  **Augmentation**:
    *   **Mixup**: Apply Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$) to training batches. This regularizes the model and has been shown to drastically accelerate convergence when combined with pre-trained weights (Cite Lesson 00020).
    *   **SpecAugment**: Apply Frequency and Time Masking.
    *   **Constraint**: Strictly limit the **Time Mask width to a maximum of 200ms** (approx. 20 frames). This ensures the augmentation does not occlude the entire short-duration whale call, preventing the introduction of label noise (Cite Lesson 00011).

### Training
*   **Loss Function**: Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss**.
*   **Target Handling**: Compute the loss against the **mixed soft targets** generated by Mixup.
*   **Metric Validity**: Do not rely on training metrics calculated against original hard labels, as they are misleading under Mixup. Monitor progress solely via **Validation AUC** or calculate training metrics against binarized mixed targets (Cite Lesson 00022).
*   **Optimizer**: Use **Adam** with a standard learning rate.
*   **Scheduler**: Implement **ReduceLROnPlateau** to decay the learning rate when Validation AUC stagnates (Cite Lesson 00006).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference**: Convert test clips to Log-Mel Spectrograms using the training parameters (128 bins).
*   **Prediction**: Pass the spectrograms through the Time-Preserving EfficientNet-BiGRU model.
*   **Output**: Apply a Sigmoid activation to the final output of the Attention Pooling layer to generate the probability `p` for the submission file.
