1.  **Mixup Augmentation**: I will introduce Mixup in the training loop. Mixup trains the model on convex combinations of pairs of examples and their labels ($x = \lambda x_i + (1-\lambda) x_j$, $y = \lambda y_i + (1-\lambda) y_j$). This acts as a strong regularizer, encouraging the model to behave linearly in-between training examples and improving generalization on unseen data. I will add `USE_MIXUP` and `MIXUP_ALPHA` to the configuration and modify the `train_one_epoch` function.
2.  **Pretrained Backbone**: Instead of initializing the `TimePreservingResNet` with random weights, I will load weights pretrained on ImageNet (`IMAGENET1K_V1`). Although the domain (spectrograms) differs from natural images, the low-level filters (edge/curve detectors) learned from ImageNet provide a superior initialization compared to random weights. I will adapt the first convolutional layer (3 channels -> 1 channel) by averaging the pretrained weights across the channel dimension.
3.  **Extended Training**: To accommodate the increased regularization from Mixup, I will increase the number of epochs from 25 to 40.
