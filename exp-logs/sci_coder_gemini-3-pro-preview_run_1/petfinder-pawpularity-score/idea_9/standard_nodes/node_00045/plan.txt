The issue is that the validation metric is being calculated using predictions from a meta-learner that was trained on the validation targets, constituting data leakage. Specifically, the `MetaLearner` is trained on the full set of Level-0 OOF predictions (which includes the validation set) and then evaluated on that same set. As identified in the error analysis, this measures training error rather than generalization error.

To fix this, I will implement cross-validation at the meta-learner level. I will modify `train_level1` in `library/stacking_engine.py` to perform K-Fold cross-validation to generate "clean" OOF predictions for the meta-learner, ensuring that the prediction for any sample comes from a model that did not see that sample during training. I will then update `runfile.py` to use these valid predictions for the final metric calculation. Cite debug_lesson_4.
