# Proposed Strategy: Stratified Tri-Paradigm Stacking with Interaction-Aware Meta-Learning

This strategy synthesizes the successful components of previous experiments—specifically the "Tri-Paradigm" backbone selection and "Heterogeneous Expert" diversity—while introducing a structural innovation at the Meta-Learner level to explicitly model the conditional reliability of experts based on image metadata.

# Model

### 1. Feature Extraction (Backbones)
I will utilize the three high-capacity backbones identified as optimal for their orthogonal inductive biases (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`), strictly adhering to their native resolutions to avoid preprocessing artifacts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`):
*   **Semantic Expert:** **SigLIP So400M (384px)**. Retained for its superior performance on subjective/aesthetic tasks compared to CLIP (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`).
*   **Geometric Expert:** **DINOv2 ViT-Large (518px)**. Retained for its self-supervised understanding of object layout and structure.
*   **Textural Expert:** **ConvNeXt Large (224px)**. Retained for capturing high-frequency textures (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).

### 2. Level-0 Heterogeneous Experts
For *each* of the three backbones, I will train four distinct regression experts to maximize algorithmic diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`):
*   **Linear:** **Ridge Regression** (Maps global trends).
*   **Kernel:** **SVR (RBF Kernel)** (Captures non-linear manifolds).
*   **Bagging:** **ExtraTrees Regressor** (Reduces variance).
*   **Boosting:** **LightGBM Regressor** (Reduces bias and models sequential splits).

This results in **12 Level-0 Experts** (3 Backbones $\times$ 4 Algorithms).

### 3. Level-1 Innovation: Interaction-Aware Bayesian Meta-Learner
Previous solutions concatenated metadata at the Level-0 stage. However, a single expert may struggle to completely discount its visual features when a specific flag (e.g., "Blur" or "Occlusion") is present.

I propose moving the metadata interaction logic to the **Level-1 Meta-Learner** to create a **Context-Aware Ensemble**.
*   **Input Construction:** Instead of training the Meta-Learner solely on the matrix of Expert Predictions ($P$), I will construct an expanded design matrix containing:
    1.  **Raw Predictions ($P$):** The 12 expert scores.
    2.  **Metadata ($M$):** The 12 binary flags.
    3.  **Interaction Terms ($P \times M$):** The element-wise product of every expert prediction with every metadata flag.
*   **Algorithm:** **Bayesian Ridge Regression**.
    *   *Rationale:* This effectively implements a "Soft Mixture of Experts." The model can learn weights such as $w_{DINO} + w_{DINO \times Blur} \cdot I(Blur)$. If $w_{DINO \times Blur}$ is negative, the ensemble automatically downweights the DINOv2 expert when the image is blurry, shifting reliance to other experts. Bayesian Ridge is selected for its ability to handle the resulting high-dimensional, correlated feature space via Automatic Relevance Determination (ARD) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).

# Data

### Preprocessing
1.  **Automated Transformation:** Use the specific `AutoImageProcessor` for SigLIP, DINOv2, and ConvNeXt to ensure exact alignment with pre-training statistics (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`).
2.  **Feature-Space Augmentation:** Apply **Feature-Space Augmentation Averaging** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`) by averaging embeddings of original and horizontally flipped images.

### Feature Engineering for Level-0
I will apply expert-specific preprocessing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`):
*   **For Ridge/SVR:** Concatenate [Embeddings, Metadata] and apply **StandardScaler** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
*   **For ExtraTrees/LightGBM:** Apply **PCA** to embeddings (reducing to ~64 components) before concatenating with raw metadata.

# Training

1.  **Stratified Full-Dataset Cross-Validation:** I will merge the training and validation sets and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** (binning the continuous target) to generate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions. This ensures the Level-0 experts and the Meta-Learner are trained on representative distributions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00054`, `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
2.  **Rigorous Optimization:**
    *   **Ridge:** `RidgeCV` with alpha search up to 50,000 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **SVR:** Grid search for `C` and `epsilon`.
    *   **LightGBM:** Tune `learning_rate`, `num_leaves`, and regularization to prevent overfitting PCA features.
    *   **ExtraTrees:** Fixed robust settings (500 estimators).
    *   *Note:* I will prioritize this tuning over adding more backbones/views (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`).

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:** Process test images through the three backbones (using `AutoImageProcessor` and flip-averaging).
2.  **Level-0 Prediction:** Transform features (Scaling/PCA) and pass to the 12 trained Level-0 experts (averaged across folds).
3.  **Level-1 Prediction:** Construct the Interaction-Aware feature matrix ($P \oplus M \oplus P \times M$) and feed it to the trained Bayesian Ridge Meta-Learner.
4.  **Validation:** Perform **Nested Cross-Validation** on the Meta-Learner to calculate the final RMSE, ensuring the reported metric reflects true generalization error (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00029`).
