# Proposed Strategy: Stratified Tri-Paradigm Stacking with Interaction-Aware Meta-Learning

This strategy synthesizes the "Tri-Paradigm" backbone selection (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`, `solution_lesson_node_00052`), the "Heterogeneous Expert" diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`), and the "Stratified Validation" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00054`) into a unified pipeline. The key structural innovation is the introduction of an **Interaction-Aware Meta-Learner**, which explicitly models the conditional reliability of experts based on image metadata, addressing the limitation where global weights fail to account for specific image degradations (e.g., Blur, Occlusion).

# Model

### 1. Feature Extraction (Backbones)
I will utilize three high-capacity backbones representing orthogonal pre-training paradigms, strictly adhering to their native resolutions via `AutoImageProcessor` to avoid preprocessing artifacts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`):
*   **Semantic Expert:** **SigLIP So400M (384px)**. Selected for its superior performance on subjective tasks compared to CLIP, providing dense, discriminative features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`).
*   **Geometric Expert:** **DINOv2 ViT-Large (518px)**. Retained for its self-supervised understanding of object layout and structure (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`).
*   **Textural Expert:** **ConvNeXt Large (224px)**. Retained for capturing high-frequency local textures (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).

### 2. Level-0 Heterogeneous Experts
For *each* of the three backbones, I will train four distinct regression experts to maximize algorithmic diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`):
*   **Linear Expert:** **Ridge Regression** (Maps global linear trends).
*   **Kernel Expert:** **SVR (RBF Kernel)** (Captures non-linear manifolds).
*   **Bagging Expert:** **ExtraTrees Regressor** (Reduces variance and models conditional logic) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).
*   **Boosting Expert:** **LightGBM Regressor** (Reduces bias and models sequential interactions) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

This results in **12 Level-0 Experts** (3 Backbones $\times$ 4 Algorithms).

### 3. Level-1 Innovation: Interaction-Aware Bayesian Meta-Learner
Standard stacking learns a static weight for each expert (e.g., $0.3 \times \text{ConvNeXt}$). However, a texture expert like ConvNeXt may be unreliable if the image is blurry. To address this, I will construct an **Interaction-Aware Design Matrix** for the Meta-Learner containing:
1.  **Raw Predictions ($P$):** The 12 expert scores.
2.  **Metadata ($M$):** The 12 binary flags.
3.  **Interaction Terms ($P \times M$):** The element-wise product of every expert prediction with every metadata flag.

The Meta-Learner will be a **Bayesian Ridge Regressor**. This model is uniquely suited to handle the resulting high-dimensional, correlated feature space (12 predictions + 12 metadata + 144 interactions = 168 features) by automatically inferring regularization parameters (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`). This allows the ensemble to dynamically downweight specific experts based on the presence of metadata flags (e.g., learning a negative coefficient for $\text{ConvNeXt} \times \text{Blur}$).

# Data

### Preprocessing
1.  **Automated Transformation:** Use the specific `AutoImageProcessor` for SigLIP, DINOv2, and ConvNeXt to ensure exact alignment with pre-training statistics (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`).
2.  **Feature-Space Augmentation:** Apply **Feature-Space Augmentation Averaging** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`). Compute embeddings for both the original and horizontally flipped images and average them to enforce invariance.

### Feature Engineering for Level-0
I will apply expert-specific preprocessing to the features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`):
*   **For Ridge/SVR:** Concatenate [Image Embedding, Metadata] and apply **StandardScaler** to the full vector (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
*   **For ExtraTrees/LightGBM:** Apply **PCA** to the image embeddings (reducing to ~64 components) before concatenating with raw binary metadata. This mitigates the curse of dimensionality for tree-based splits.

# Training

1.  **Stratified Full-Dataset Cross-Validation:** I will merge the training and validation sets and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** (binning the continuous `Pawpularity` target) to generate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions. This ensures the Level-0 experts are trained on representative distributions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00054`, `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
2.  **Rigorous Optimization:**
    *   **Ridge:** `RidgeCV` with alpha search up to 50,000 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **SVR:** Grid search for `C` and `epsilon`.
    *   **LightGBM:** Tune `learning_rate`, `num_leaves`, and regularization to prevent overfitting PCA features.
    *   **ExtraTrees:** Fixed robust settings (500 estimators).

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:** Process test images through the three backbones (using `AutoImageProcessor` and flip-averaging).
2.  **Level-0 Prediction:** Transform features (Scaling/PCA) and pass to the 12 trained Level-0 experts (averaged across folds).
3.  **Level-1 Prediction:** Construct the Interaction-Aware feature matrix ($P \oplus M \oplus P \times M$) and feed it to the trained Bayesian Ridge Meta-Learner.
4.  **Validation:** Perform **Nested Cross-Validation** on the Meta-Learner to calculate the final RMSE, ensuring the reported metric reflects true generalization error (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00029`).
