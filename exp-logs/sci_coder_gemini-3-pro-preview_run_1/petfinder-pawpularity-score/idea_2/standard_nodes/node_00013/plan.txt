To optimize the solution, I will implement a strategy focused on increasing the representational capacity of the visual features while simultaneously preventing overfitting through stronger regularization.

1.  **Backbone Upgrade**: I will replace the `swin_tiny_patch4_window7_224` backbone with `swin_large_patch4_window7_224`. As noted in **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00006}**, backbone capacity dominates the performance ceiling of linear probing baselines. The larger model (197M params vs 28M) will extract significantly richer semantic features from the pet images.
2.  **Hyperparameter Expansion**: The previous execution hit the upper boundary of the regularization search space (`alpha=2000`). With the move to Swin Large, the embedding dimension increases from 768 to 1536, doubling the number of features in the linear layer. This increases the risk of overfitting (**Cite {solution_lesson_node_00004}**). Therefore, I will significantly expand the `alphas` search grid in `RidgeCV` up to 50,000 to ensure the optimal regularization strength is found (**Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}**).
3.  **Efficiency**: I will increase the `batch_size` to 64 to better utilize the A100 GPU with the larger model.
