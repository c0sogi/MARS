The `RuntimeError` is caused by the use of `-1e9` as a masking constant, which falls outside the range of 16-bit floating-point numbers (FP16) used during mixed precision training (`autocast`). This triggers an overflow when the tensor is cast to half precision. To fix this, I will replace `-1e9` with `-1e4`, a value that fits within the FP16 dynamic range while still being sufficiently negative to ensure Softmax probabilities for masked tokens are effectively zero.
