The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` occurs during the backward pass when Gradient Checkpointing is enabled. As identified in the error analysis and **Debug Lesson 3**, this error persists even after ensuring inputs require gradients, indicating a deeper incompatibility with the recomputation graph (likely due to freed buffers or specific interactions with the attention pooling mechanism). Since the demo uses a small model (`deberta-v3-xsmall`) and runs on an A100, gradient checkpointing is unnecessary for memory efficiency. The fix is to explicitly disable gradient checkpointing on the model's backbone immediately after initialization in `runfile.py`, removing the problematic recomputation step.
