The `RuntimeError: unscale_() has already been called on this optimizer since the last update()` occurs because `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` is invoked twice within the same training step: first inside the Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP) block to calculate perturbation magnitudes, and second immediately after, inside the gradient clipping block. PyTorch's `GradScaler` strictly forbids calling `unscale_` more than once per optimizer update cycle.

To fix this, I will modify `library/engine.py` to introduce a flag `awp_triggered` that tracks whether unscaling has already occurred during the AWP step. In the subsequent gradient clipping block, I will check this flag and only call `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` if it hasn't been called yet. This prevents the redundant call while ensuring gradients are properly unscaled for clipping, applying the solution from **debug_lesson_4**.
