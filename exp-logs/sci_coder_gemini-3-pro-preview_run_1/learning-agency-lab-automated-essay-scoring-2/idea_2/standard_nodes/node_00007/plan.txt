To fix this, I will reduce the batch sizes to 4 for training and 8 for validation. Additionally, I will modify `runfile.py` to explicitly reload the `library.config` module using `importlib` to ensure the configuration changes are picked up in the persistent execution environment (Cite debug_lesson_1).
