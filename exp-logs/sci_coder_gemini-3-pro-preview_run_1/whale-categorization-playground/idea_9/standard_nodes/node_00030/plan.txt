The previous implementation caused a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` because the configured `BATCH_SIZE` of 64 required more VRAM than the actual 15.77 GiB available on the GPU, contradicting the expected 40GB capacity. To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` from 64 to 32 in `library/config.py`. This reduction scales down the memory footprint of intermediate activations during the forward and backward passes, ensuring the training loop fits within the physical hardware constraints (Cite debug_lesson_3).
