My strategy focuses on addressing the "Undertrained Ensemble Trap" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026) and refining the geometric augmentation to be more conservative for the ArcFace metric learning objective (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

1.  **Extended Training Duration**: The previous execution trained for only 8 epochs, and the loss curves indicated that the models (especially the DenseNets) were still converging. I will increase `MAX_EPOCHS` to 25 and `EARLY_STOPPING_PATIENCE` to 6 in `runfile.py`. This ensures that the ensemble members reach their optimal performance before being aggregated, preventing the aggregation of undertrained models.
2.  **Conservative Augmentation**: I will modify `library/dataset.py` to reduce the `rotate_limit` from 20 to 15 degrees. Aggressive geometric distortions can disrupt the alignment required for ArcFace to learn tight clusters, especially for singleton classes where the model relies on a few samples to define the class center.
