My optimization strategy focuses on reverting the experimental Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) and aggressive augmentation changes that degraded performance in the previous solution, while retaining the robust DenseNet121 + ArcFace baseline.

1.  **Remove SWA**: The previous solution's use of SWA degraded the MAP@5 score from 0.655 to 0.608. Metric learning losses like ArcFace require precise embedding geometries that weight averaging can disrupt. I will revert to standard training with Early Stopping. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00035}.
2.  **Conservative Augmentation**: I will reduce the rotation limit from 20 degrees to 15 degrees. Previous experiments showed that aggressive geometric augmentation (rotation > 15) harmed performance in this few-shot regime. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00014}.
3.  **Label Smoothing**: I will retain `LABEL_SMOOTHING = 0.1`. While one lesson suggested incompatibility, another highlighted it as critical for singleton-heavy datasets to prevent overfitting (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00024}). Given the high singleton count (76%), I will prioritize the regularization benefit.
4.  **Training Schedule**: I will reduce `TOTAL_EPOCHS` to 15. The baseline consistently peaked around epoch 12, making the extended 22-epoch run unnecessary and potentially harmful without SWA.
