To optimize the solution, I will switch the backbone from EfficientNet-B2 to **ResNet18** and reduce the input resolution to **256x256**, addressing the overfitting and diminishing returns observed with higher resolutions and complex models (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`). I will replace the standard Cross Entropy Loss with **Cross Entropy with Label Smoothing (0.1)** and implement **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)** using horizontal flips during both validation and inference to enhance generalization in this few-shot classification task (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`). Additionally, I will adjust the batch size to 64 and epochs to 12 to suit the lighter architecture.
