Based on the analysis of previous experiments and the specific failure modes identified in the lessons, I propose the **Hypercolumn Deep Residual U-Net with Two-Stage Lovasz Optimization**.

This strategy synthesizes the robust feature extraction of the **Deep Residual U-Net** (from the current best solution) with the multi-scale aggregation of **Hypercolumns** (resurrecting a promising architectural concept from Idea 3 that failed due to unrelated pipeline errors). It integrates the rigorous **Two-Stage Metric Optimization** pipeline to safely leverage the Lovasz-Hinge loss.

### 1. Model: Hypercolumn Deep Residual U-Net
I will evolve the architecture from a standard U-Net to a **Hypercolumn** design. This addresses the need for pixel-perfect boundary precision by allowing the final classifier to explicitly combine high-level semantic features with low-level edge information, rather than relying solely on the final decoder block.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** I will retain the **Custom Deep Residual Encoder** initialized with **64 filters**. This backbone has proven to possess the necessary channel capacity `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020` and depth `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021` for the task, avoiding the overfitting risks of pre-trained models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be placed in the decoder blocks to recalibrate features `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.
    *   **Upsampling:** I will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling** to avoid the checkerboard artifacts and performance degradation caused by Transpose Convolutions `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Structural Innovation (Hypercolumns):** Instead of predicting the mask only from the final decoder block, I will upsample the output feature maps of *all* decoder blocks (after scSE) to the input resolution ($128 \times 128$) and concatenate them along the channel dimension. A final $1 \times 1$ convolution will process this aggregated "Hypercolumn" tensor. This ensures that the decision boundary is informed simultaneously by the semantic context of deep layers and the spatial precision of shallow layers.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

### 2. Data: Maximum Utilization and Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle irregular dimensions without edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will explicitly avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model, preventing the performance loss observed when fragmenting the small dataset `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve the edge sharpness required for the high-IoU metric `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

### 3. Training: Two-Stage Cyclic Curriculum
This pipeline corrects the implementation errors of Idea 5 (Batch-wise Dice, Premature Lovasz) while leveraging the stability of the current best approach.

*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**. The schedule will consist of **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs). Long, constant cycles allow the model to escape local minima and converge robustly `Cite solution_lesson_node_00022`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Phase 1 (Cycles 1 & 2, Epochs 0-100):**
    *   **Loss:** **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice Loss**. Crucially, the Dice loss will be calculated **per image** and averaged. This ensures that empty masks (39% of data) are correctly penalized, fixing the batch-wise aggregation failure `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Goal:** Establish a robust feature extractor and converge to a stable minimum.
*   **Phase 2 (Cycle 3, Epochs 101-150):**
    *   **Loss:** Switch to **Lovasz-Hinge Loss** (weighted with BCE). This switch will occur *only* after the model has fully converged in Phase 1, avoiding the instability of premature optimization `Cite solution_lesson_node_00018`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary heads will be attached to intermediate decoder levels and trained with **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice** throughout all phases to enforce hierarchical feature learning `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.

### 4. Evaluation: Snapshot Ensembling and Metric Alignment
*   **Snapshot Ensembling:** I will ensemble the predictions from the **Best Model of Cycle 2** (Robust BCE/Dice Baseline) and the **Best Model of Cycle 3** (Lovasz Refined). This combines the generalist capabilities of the stable phase with the boundary precision of the fine-tuned phase.
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** The "Best Model" for each cycle will be selected based strictly on the **Validation mAP**, not the loss `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.
*   **Inference:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`. Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
