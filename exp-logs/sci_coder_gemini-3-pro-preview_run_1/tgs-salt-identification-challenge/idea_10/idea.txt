Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Classification-Gated Deep Residual U-Net with Global Context and Snapshot Ensembling**.

This strategy introduces two key structural innovations: a **Global Context Block (GCB)** to capture scene-level dependencies (addressing the receptive field limitations of U-Nets without the artifacts of ASPP) and a **Parallel Classification Head** to explicitly model and gate the "Empty Mask" probability (addressing the 39% class imbalance). It integrates these into the high-capacity backbone and robust training pipeline established in previous successes.

### 1. Model: Classification-Gated Deep Residual U-Net
I will retain the high-capacity **Deep Residual U-Net** backbone `Cite solution_lesson_node_00034` but enhance the bottleneck and inference logic.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** A custom Deep Residual Encoder initialized with **64 filters** and expanding to **1024 filters** at the bottleneck. This preserves the channel capacity required for high-texture segmentation `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`.
*   **Bottleneck Innovations:**
    *   **Global Context Block (GCB):** I will insert a lightweight Global Context Block at the bottleneck. Unlike ASPP (which failed due to dilation artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033`) or Hypercolumns (which failed due to unrefined aggregation `Cite solution_lesson_node_00030`), GCB uses global attention pooling to model long-range dependencies (e.g., the global presence of salt structures) efficiently.
    *   **Auxiliary Classification Head:** A parallel branch (Global Average Pooling $\rightarrow$ Dense Layer $\rightarrow$ Sigmoid) will be attached to the bottleneck to predict a binary "Salt Present" vs. "Empty" label.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be placed in the decoder blocks to recalibrate features during upsampling `Cite solution_lesson_node_00007`.
    *   **Upsampling:** **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by a convolution, strictly avoiding Transpose Convolutions `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary segmentation heads at resolutions $32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$, and $128 \times 128$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

### 2. Data: Maximum Utilization and Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle the $101 \times 101$ dimensions without introducing edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only, strictly avoiding interpolation-heavy transformations `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

### 3. Training: Consistent Compound Loss with Classification Aux
This pipeline combines the stability of the "Consistent Compound Loss" with the explicit supervision of the classification head.

*   **Loss Function:**
    $$L_{total} = L_{seg} + 0.05 \times L_{cls}$$
    *   $L_{seg} = L_{BCE} + L_{Dice\_Sample} + 0.1 \times L_{Lovasz}$.
    *   **Sample-Wise Dice:** Calculated per image to correctly penalize errors on empty masks `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Consistency:** Lovasz-Hinge is included from the start (weighted) to avoid the instability of hard switching `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033`.
    *   $L_{cls}$: Binary Cross Entropy on the classification head.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Schedule:** **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs) to allow for deep convergence and robust restarts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00022`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Checkpointing:** Save the "Best Model" (based on **Validation mAP**) from the end of **Cycle 2** and **Cycle 3** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.

### 4. Evaluation: Gated Snapshot Ensembling
*   **Snapshot Ensembling:** I will average the segmentation predictions of the **Best Model from Cycle 2** and the **Best Model from Cycle 3** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00035`.
*   **Classification Gating:** The final averaged mask probability map will be multiplied by the ensemble's predicted **Classification Probability**.
    $$P_{final} = P_{mask} \times P_{class}$$
    This explicitly suppresses noise in images where the model is confident there is no salt, directly addressing the "Empty Mask" failure mode.
*   **TTA:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
