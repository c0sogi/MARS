Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and the specific failure modes identified in the lessons, I propose the **Context-Aware Deep Residual U-Net (ResUNet-PPM) with Two-Stage Metric Optimization**.

This strategy synthesizes the robust feature extraction of the DeepResUNet (from Idea 4) with a global context module (PPM) and a corrected, rigorous implementation of the Lovasz-Hinge fine-tuning pipeline (correcting the failures of Idea 5).

### 1. Model: Context-Aware Deep Residual U-Net (ResUNet-PPM)
I will retain the successful **Deep Residual U-Net** backbone but introduce a structural enhancement to the bottleneck to capture global context, while strictly adhering to the "parameter-free upsampling" lesson.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** A custom Deep Residual Encoder initialized with **64 filters**. This preserves the channel capacity required for high-texture segmentation `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020` and avoids the overfitting risks of pre-trained ResNets `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`.
*   **Structural Innovation (Pyramid Pooling Module):** I will insert a **Pyramid Pooling Module (PPM)** at the bottleneck (between the encoder and decoder). Standard U-Nets suffer from limited receptive fields in the deepest layers. The PPM will pool features at multiple bin sizes (e.g., $1\times1$, $2\times2$, $3\times3$, $6\times6$) and concatenate them. This allows the model to reason about the global scene (e.g., "is this a salt dome or a sediment layer?") before reconstruction, addressing the lack of global context in pure FCNs.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be placed in the decoder blocks to recalibrate features during upsampling `Cite solution_lesson_node_00007`.
    *   **Upsampling:** I will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by a convolution, replacing the Transpose Convolutions that caused checkerboard artifacts and performance degradation in previous attempts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input level `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

### 2. Data: Reflection Padding and Sample-Wise Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle the $101 \times 101$ dimensions without edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve edge precision `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.

### 3. Training: Corrected Two-Stage Metric Optimization
This training pipeline corrects the specific implementation errors that caused the failure of Idea 5 (Batch-wise Dice and Premature Switching).

*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**. The schedule will use **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs) to allow for deep convergence and robust restarts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00022`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Phase 1 (Cycles 1 & 2, Epochs 0-100):**
    *   **Loss:** **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice Loss**. Crucially, the Dice loss will be calculated **per image** and averaged, rather than over the flattened batch. This ensures that empty masks (39% of data) contribute correctly to the loss, fixing the "Batch-Wise" bug `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Goal:** Establish a robust feature extractor and converge to a stable minimum.
*   **Phase 2 (Cycle 3, Epochs 101-150):**
    *   **Loss:** Switch to **Lovasz-Hinge Loss** (with a small BCE regularizer). This switch will occur *only* after the model has fully converged in Phase 1. This directly optimizes the Jaccard index (IoU) for the final fine-tuning, adhering to the lesson on stable transition `Cite solution_lesson_node_00018`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary heads will be used with decaying weights, but only trained with BCE+Dice throughout to maintain gradient stability `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.

### 4. Evaluation: Snapshot Ensembling and Metric Selection
*   **Snapshot Ensembling:** I will ensemble the predictions from the **Best Model of Cycle 2** (Robust BCE/Dice Baseline) and the **Best Model of Cycle 3** (Lovasz Refined). This combines the generalist capabilities of the stable phase with the boundary precision of the fine-tuned phase `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Checkpointing:** The "Best Model" for each cycle will be selected based strictly on the **Validation mAP**, not the loss `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.
*   **Inference:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`. Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
