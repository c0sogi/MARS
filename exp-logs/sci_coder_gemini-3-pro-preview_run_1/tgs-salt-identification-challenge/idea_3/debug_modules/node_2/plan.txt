The `RuntimeError` during inference verification is caused by a mismatch in model architecture between the training and verification phases. The `run_fold` function in `library/train.py` hardcodes `base_filters=32`, whereas `runfile.py` instantiates the verification model with `base_filters=16` (Cite debug_lesson_2). To fix this, I will update `run_fold` to accept a `base_filters` argument and pass `base_filters=16` from `runfile.py` to ensure the saved checkpoint matches the verification model's structure.
