My implementation strategy focuses on simplifying the training pipeline to ensure robust convergence before considering metric-specific optimizations, as suggested by the lessons.

1.  **Extend Training Duration**: I will increase the number of epochs from 15 to 60. The previous solution showed signs of under-training (best metric on last epoch or shortly before switching losses), and `solution_lesson_node_00011` highlights that models for this task require significantly more epochs to resolve fine boundary details.
2.  **Stable Loss Function**: I will remove the two-stage training strategy (switching to Lovasz-Hinge loss). `solution_lesson_node_00018` indicates that premature switching to Lovasz loss can destabilize training. Instead, I will train exclusively with the stable `BCEDiceLoss` for the entire duration to maximize the benefits of the extended training schedule.
3.  **Refined Scheduling**: I will modify the learning rate scheduler to monitor `val_loss` (minimization) instead of `val_map` (maximization). As per `solution_lesson_node_00006`, continuous loss functions provide better gradients for fine-tuning at the tail end of convergence than discrete thresholded metrics.
4.  **Metric-Based Checkpointing**: While the scheduler optimizes for loss, I will retain the logic to save the best model based on the competition metric (`val_map`), ensuring we select the model that performs best on the actual task (`solution_lesson_node_00016`).
