Based on the analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **High-Capacity Deep Residual U-Net with Consistent Compound Loss and Snapshot Ensembling**.

This strategy synthesizes the successful **Snapshot Ensembling** (from Idea 8) with a restoration of **Model Capacity** (addressing the regression in Idea 7) and a novel **Consistent Compound Loss** formulation (addressing the stability failures of Idea 6 and 8).

### 1. Model: High-Capacity Deep Residual U-Net
I will employ the **Deep Residual U-Net (DeepResUNet)** architecture, but I will explicitly restore the bottleneck capacity that was reduced in previous ensemble attempts.

*   **Backbone (High-Capacity Encoder):** A custom Deep Residual Encoder initialized with **64 filters**. Crucially, the encoder will extend to a bottleneck of **1024 filters**. Previous experiments showed that reducing this capacity (to 512) degraded performance, even if ensembling masked the loss `Cite solution_lesson_node_00034`. Maintaining high channel width is essential for capturing the complex textural features of salt `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Residual Blocks:** Symmetrical residual blocks to facilitate gradient flow.
    *   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules placed *only* in the decoder to recalibrate features during upsampling `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.
    *   **Upsampling:** **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by a convolution. I will strictly avoid Transpose Convolutions to prevent checkerboard artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary prediction heads at resolutions $32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$, and $128 \times 128$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

### 2. Data: Maximum Utilization and Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle the $101 \times 101$ dimensions without introducing edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve edge precision `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

### 3. Training: Consistent Compound Loss with Cyclic Schedule
This pipeline introduces a **Consistent Compound Loss** to solve the "Hard Switch" instability observed in previous Lovasz experiments `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033`.

*   **Loss Function:** Instead of switching loss functions mid-training (which caused catastrophic forgetting), I will use a weighted compound loss from the start:
    $$L = L_{BCE} + L_{Dice\_Sample} + 0.1 \times L_{Lovasz}$$
    *   **Sample-Wise Dice:** The Dice component will be calculated **per image** and averaged, ensuring empty masks are correctly penalized `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Consistency:** Including a small weight for Lovasz-Hinge from the beginning allows the model to optimize the IoU metric continuously without the shock of a sudden objective change `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033`.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Schedule:** **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs). This provides multiple convergence points for the ensemble `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Checkpointing:** I will save the "Best Model" (based on **Validation mAP**) from the end of **Cycle 2** and **Cycle 3**. Metric-based selection is critical `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.

### 4. Evaluation: Snapshot Ensembling
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will implement **Snapshot Ensembling** by averaging the predictions of the **Best Model from Cycle 2** and the **Best Model from Cycle 3**. This leverages the diversity of the local minima visited by the cyclic schedule to improve generalization without the cost of training multiple independent models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00035`.
*   **TTA:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
