Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Deep Residual U-Net with Extended Homogeneous Lovasz-Ensemble**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Homogeneous Lovasz-Cycle" concept (Idea 16). It specifically addresses the **Ensemble Disparity** failure (`Cite solution_lesson_node_00050`) and the **Calibration/Sharpness** trade-off (`Cite solution_lesson_node_00047`) by extending the cyclic curriculum to generate **three** independent, metric-optimized snapshots. By enforcing homogeneity in the loss function across the majority of the training duration and applying a rigorous **Quality Gate**, this approach maximizes the variance-reduction benefits of ensembling without diluting prediction quality.

### 1. Model: Deep Residual U-Net with scSE and Deep Supervision
I will employ the **Deep Residual U-Net** architecture, which has consistently proven to be the most robust backbone, striking the optimal balance between capacity and generalization.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** A custom **Deep Residual Encoder** initialized with **64 filters** and expanding to a bottleneck of **512 filters**. I will explicitly avoid the 1024-filter capacity, as `Cite solution_lesson_node_00049` demonstrated that this increased capacity hindered convergence on this dataset. The residual structure enables deep feature extraction without gradient degradation `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be placed in the decoder blocks. This is chosen over Coordinate Attention, which underperformed on these low-resolution inputs `Cite solution_lesson_node_00055`. Attention is restricted to the decoder to preserve encoder feature integrity `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.
    *   **Upsampling:** I will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by a convolution, avoiding Transpose Convolutions to eliminate checkerboard artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary prediction heads will be attached at resolutions $32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$, and $128 \times 128$. These will be trained with **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice** throughout all phases to ensure stable gradient flow `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

### 2. Data: Maximum Utilization and Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle the $101 \times 101$ dimensions without introducing edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will explicitly avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve the edge sharpness required for the high-IoU metric `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

### 3. Training: Extended Homogeneous Lovasz-Cycle Curriculum
This training pipeline is designed to produce **three** high-quality, metric-optimized checkpoints for ensembling.

*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Schedule:** **4 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 200 epochs). The extended duration allows for multiple independent convergences `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Phase 1 (Cycle 1, Epochs 0-50):**
    *   **Loss:** **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice Loss**. Dice is calculated per-image to correctly penalize empty masks `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Goal:** Establish robust structural convergence and a stable feature extractor.
*   **Phase 2 (Cycles 2, 3, & 4, Epochs 51-200):**
    *   **Loss Substitution:** Switch the main head loss to **BCE + Lovasz-Hinge Loss**. I will **substitute** the Dice term with Lovasz (rather than adding it) to avoid gradient conflict `Cite solution_lesson_node_00053`.
    *   **Timing:** The switch occurs exactly at the Cycle 1 boundary (Epoch 50). The learning rate restart at this point facilitates the transition to the new loss landscape, avoiding the instability of intra-cycle switching `Cite solution_lesson_node_00052`.
    *   **Goal:** By training Cycles 2, 3, and 4 with Lovasz, we generate three independent models that are all optimized for the Jaccard index. This addresses the "Weak+Strong" ensemble failure `Cite solution_lesson_node_00050`.
*   **Checkpointing:** Save the "Best Model" from **Cycle 2**, **Cycle 3**, and **Cycle 4** based strictly on the **Validation mAP** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.

### 4. Evaluation: Gated Homogeneous Ensemble
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will ensemble (average) the predictions of the Best Models from Cycles 2, 3, and 4.
*   **Quality Gating:** Before averaging, I will apply a strict quality gate. I will identify the single best model (highest mAP). Any other model in the set (C2, C3, C4) that has a Validation mAP more than **0.5% lower** than the best model will be excluded from the ensemble. This explicitly prevents the degradation caused by averaging "weak" models with "strong" ones `Cite solution_lesson_node_00050`.
*   **TTA:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
