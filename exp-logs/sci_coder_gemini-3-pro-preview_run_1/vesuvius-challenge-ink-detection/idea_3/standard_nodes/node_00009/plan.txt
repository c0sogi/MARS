The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` during the backward pass indicates that the GPU memory is saturated by the activations required for gradient computation, which is common in 3D volumetric models with large inputs ($65 \times 512 \times 512$). As identified in the error analysis and **debug_lesson_1**, the `BATCH_SIZE` of 4 is too large for the available 40GB GPU memory.

To fix this, I will:
1.  **Reduce Batch Size**: Modify `library/config.py` to set `BATCH_SIZE` to 1.
2.  **Implement Gradient Accumulation**: To maintain the effective batch size of 4 (ensuring similar convergence properties), I will add `GRAD_ACCUM_STEPS = 4` to the configuration and update the training loop in `library/engine.py` to accumulate gradients over 4 steps before updating weights. This directly applies the strategy from **Cite debug_lesson_1**.
