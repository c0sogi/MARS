The issue is a coordinate system mismatch between the model's predictions and the ground truth labels during validation. The model predicts coordinates in the resized $1024 \times 1024$ input space (processed with padding and scaling), while the ground truth labels in `val_df` remain in the original high-resolution image space. This results in zero intersection between predictions and targets, leading to an F1 score of 0.0.

To fix this, I will update `library/trainer.py` and `runfile.py` to apply the inverse geometric transformation to the predicted coordinates before they are formatted for evaluation. This involves subtracting the padding and dividing by the scale factor, using the original image dimensions (`orig_h`, `orig_w`) provided in the batch.
