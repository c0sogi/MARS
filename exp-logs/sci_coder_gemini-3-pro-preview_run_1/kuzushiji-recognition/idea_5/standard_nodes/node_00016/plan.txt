The `ValueError` reported by `albumentations` indicates that some ground truth bounding boxes in the training set extend beyond the image boundaries (e.g., `x_max > image_width`). This causes the augmentation pipeline to fail during coordinate validation. To fix this, we need to sanitize the bounding box data within the `__getitem__` method of the `KuzushijiDataset` class. Specifically, we will clip the bounding box coordinates to ensure they stay strictly within the `[0, width]` and `[0, height]` range of the image before passing them to the transform pipeline. This prevents the "dirty" data from crashing the training loop.
