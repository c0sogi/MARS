The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` is caused by the massive memory footprint of the dense prediction heads in the CenterNet architecture. With 4,782 classes and a $1024 \times 1024$ input (resulting in a $256 \times 256$ output grid), a single output tensor for a batch of 8 requires approximately 9.3 GB of VRAM. During the forward and backward passes, multiple intermediate tensors of this size are allocated, exceeding the GPU's capacity (Cite debug_lesson_1). To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` from 8 to 4 and set the `PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF` environment variable to `expandable_segments:True` to mitigate memory fragmentation.
