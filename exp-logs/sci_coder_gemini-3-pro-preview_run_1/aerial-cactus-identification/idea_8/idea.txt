Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous iterations and the extracted lessons, I propose a solution that maximizes robustness and generalization by transitioning from a single-model paradigm to a **Cross-Validation Ensemble** trained with **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)**. This approach structurally enforces the discovery of flat minima in the loss landscape and marginalizes out data split variances.

# Model
I propose a **Deeply Supervised RepVGG Ensemble**.

*   **Backbone Architecture:** The core model will be the **RepVGG-style** network that proved highly efficient and accurate.
    *   **Conservative Downsampling:** The network will utilize a "conservative stem" (3x3 convolution, stride 1, no pooling) to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage. This addresses the critical lesson that aggressive downsampling destroys signal in low-resolution imagery **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
    *   **Kernel Sizing:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels**, avoiding the performance degradation observed with larger 7x7 kernels on small feature maps **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
    *   **Deep Supervision:** An **Auxiliary Classification Head** will be attached after the second stage. This enforces discriminative feature learning early in the network and stabilizes the training of the ensemble members.
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** The multi-branch training topology (3x3 + 1x1 + Identity) will be retained to aid convergence, but will be mathematically fused into single 3x3 convolutional layers for inference, ensuring the ensemble remains computationally feasible **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To eliminate I/O bottlenecks and maximize the throughput of the ensemble training, the entire dataset will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Pipeline Structure:** I will implement **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**. Instead of a single 80/20 split, the training data will be divided into 5 folds. Five separate models will be trained, each using 4 folds for training and 1 for validation. This ensures the model learns from 100% of the available data across the ensemble.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**, exploiting the rotational invariance of aerial photography **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00010)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$) to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
*   **Optimizer Innovation (SAM):** I will introduce **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)**. This optimization algorithm wraps the base optimizer (AdamW) and performs two forward-backward passes per step to simultaneously minimize the loss value and the *loss sharpness*. By explicitly seeking a "flat minimum" in the loss landscape, SAM produces models that are significantly more robust to distribution shifts than those trained with standard weight averaging (SWA) or simple SGD.
*   **Loss Function:** A weighted sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from the Main and Auxiliary heads will be used.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be employed for each fold.
*   **Duration:** Each fold will be trained for **30 epochs**. This duration is explicitly chosen to accommodate the convergence dynamics of Mixup and ensure the Cosine Annealing schedule follows a gradual decay curve **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, all 5 trained models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their blocks into efficient single-path networks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will implement a **Double-Ensemble Strategy**:
    1.  **Model Ensemble:** Predictions will be generated by all 5 models from the cross-validation folds.
    2.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For *each* model, I will utilize **4-view TTA** (averaging predictions of the original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, and 180-degree rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
*   **Aggregation:** The final probability for each test image will be the average of these 20 predictions (5 models $\times$ 4 views), providing the ultimate safeguard against variance and noise.
