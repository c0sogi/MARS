Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, I propose a solution that introduces **Structural Re-parameterization** to combine the training stability of residual networks with the inference efficiency of plain convolutional networks.

# Model
I propose a **Custom Re-parameterizable CNN (RepVGG-style)** tailored for 32x32 imagery.

*   **Structural Innovation (Re-parameterization):** The architecture will decouple the training-time topology from the inference-time topology.
    *   **Training Phase:** Each block will consist of a multi-branch design: a 3x3 convolution, a parallel 1x1 convolution, and an identity branch (where dimensions match). This multi-branch structure mimics the residual connections of the successful ResNet (Idea 1), facilitating gradient flow and convergence.
    *   **Inference Phase:** After training, these parallel branches will be mathematically fused (re-parameterized) into a single 3x3 convolutional layer and bias. This results in a strictly feed-forward stack of layers (like VGG) with no residual addition operations or multi-path branching at runtime, optimizing memory access and inference speed.
*   **Strategic Retention (Conservative Downsampling):** The network will utilize a "conservative stem" (3x3 convolution, stride 1, no pooling) to preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage. This strictly adheres to the finding that aggressive downsampling destroys signal in low-resolution thumbnails **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Strategic Retention (Kernel Sizing):** The architecture will exclusively use 3x3 kernels. This avoids the performance degradation observed with larger 7x7 kernels (ConvNeXt) on small feature maps, ensuring local spatial hierarchies are preserved **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be normalized using the dataset's channel-wise mean and standard deviation.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**. This leverages the rotational invariance of aerial photography to improve robustness without the computational overhead of hard-coded equivariant layers **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00010)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is retained to close the generalization gap and ensure perfect classification without drowning out the signal, as observed when comparing against aggressive Mixup ($\alpha=1.0$) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** will be used to handle the continuous soft targets generated by Mixup.
*   **Optimizer:** The **AdamW** optimizer will be employed for effective weight decay handling.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be used.
*   **Duration:** The training will run for **30 epochs**. This duration is explicitly retained to accommodate the convergence dynamics of Mixup and ensure the Cosine Annealing schedule follows a gradual decay curve, preventing the performance regression observed with shorter 15-epoch schedules **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, the model's multi-branch blocks will be structurally re-parameterized (collapsed) into single convolutional layers.
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will utilize **4-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**. The final prediction will be the average of the probabilities for the original image, a horizontal flip, a vertical flip, and a 180-degree rotation. This leverages geometric symmetries to marginalize out noise and maximize the robustness of the predictions **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
