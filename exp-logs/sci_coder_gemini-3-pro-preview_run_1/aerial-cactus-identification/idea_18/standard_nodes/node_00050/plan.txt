The validation data is loaded into local variables (`val_ids`, etc.) and immediately merged into the training arrays, but the reference to the distinct validation set is lost. To fix this, I will explicitly assign the loaded validation data to instance attributes (`self.val_ids`, `self.val_imgs`, etc.) in `load_all_data` before the merge operation. I will also initialize these attributes to `None` in the `__init__` method to establish a stable class structure (Cite debug_lesson_12).
