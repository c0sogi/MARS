To optimize the solution, I will focus on enhancing the model's robustness and generalization capabilities without altering the effective `CactusResNet` architecture, which is already tailored for 32x32 inputs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

**Strategy:**
1.  **Augmentation**: I will add `RandomVerticalFlip` to the training transformations in `library/dataset.py`. Since the dataset consists of aerial photos, the vertical orientation is arbitrary, and this augmentation will effectively double the diversity of the training data.
2.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**: I will modify `library/engine.py` to implement 4-view TTA (Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, and 180-degree Rotation) in both the `evaluate` and `predict` functions. Averaging predictions across these views reduces variance and typically boosts the ROC AUC score, especially in high-performance regimes.
3.  **Optimization**: In `runfile.py`, I will switch from `Adam` to `AdamW` to better handle weight decay. I will also increase the training epochs from 15 to 20 to allow the model to fully converge with the increased data augmentation. Finally, I will implement the conditional submission logic to ensure a submission is generated only if the validation score exceeds the previous best.
