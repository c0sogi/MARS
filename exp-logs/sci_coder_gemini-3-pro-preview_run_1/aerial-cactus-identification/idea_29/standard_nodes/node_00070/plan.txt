The `ValueError` in `library/engine.py` is caused by a shape mismatch between the model predictions and the target labels during the calculation of the Binary Cross Entropy loss. Specifically, the `torch.sum` operation in the weighted ensemble logic uses `keepdim=True`, producing a prediction tensor of shape `(Batch, 1, 1)` (retaining the reduced expert dimension as a singleton), whereas the target labels have shape `(Batch, 1)`. To fix this, I will change `keepdim=True` to `keepdim=False` in both `train_router_epoch` and `validate_router`. This ensures the summation reduces the dimension completely, resulting in a `(Batch, 1)` tensor that matches the target shape.
