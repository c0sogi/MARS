The `RuntimeError` is caused by a broadcasting mismatch between the router weights tensor `(Batch, Experts)` and the stacked expert predictions tensor `(Batch, Experts, Output_Dim)`. PyTorch aligns dimensions from the right, causing the batch dimension of the weights to misalign with the experts dimension of the predictions. To fix this, I will explicitly unsqueeze the weights tensor to `(Batch, Experts, 1)` in both `library/engine.py` and `runfile.py` to ensure correct broadcasting (Cite debug_lesson_17). Additionally, I will flatten the final predictions and error arrays in `runfile.py` to ensure compatibility with `np.corrcoef` and Pandas DataFrame construction (Cite debug_lesson_9).
