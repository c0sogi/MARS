Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous iterations and the extracted lessons, I propose a solution that structurally evolves the **Self-Ensembling** concept by integrating it with **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** within a **Stratified Cross-Validation** framework. This approach explicitly synthesizes the efficiency of RepVGG, the robustness of ensembling, and the critical finding that explicit metadata injection is redundant when the visual backbone is sufficiently capable.

# Model
I propose a **Stratified Multi-Head RepVGG Ensemble**.

*   **Backbone Architecture:** The core model will be a **Custom RepVGG**.
    *   **Training Topology:** Each block will utilize a multi-branch design (3x3 convolution, 1x1 convolution, and identity connection) to maximize gradient flow and training stability **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
    *   **Conservative Downsampling:** The network will utilize a "conservative stem" (3x3 convolution, stride 1, no pooling) to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage. This adheres to the lesson that aggressive downsampling destroys signal in low-resolution imagery **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
    *   **Kernel Sizing:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels**, avoiding the performance degradation observed with larger kernels (e.g., ConvNeXt 7x7) on small feature maps **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Permanent Internal Ensemble):** Instead of using a single output head or discarding auxiliary heads after training, this model will feature **two permanent classification heads**:
    1.  **Texture Head:** Attached after the intermediate stage (Stage 2). This captures low-level, high-frequency features (crucial for detecting spines and compression artifacts).
    2.  **Semantic Head:** Attached after the final stage (Stage 3). This captures abstract shape and global context.
    By retaining both heads for inference, the model functions as a "Self-Ensemble," leveraging the "Implicit Learning" capacity of the CNN to handle data quality variations without the overhead of explicit metadata injection **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00054)**.
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** For inference, the multi-branch backbone blocks will be mathematically fused into single 3x3 convolutional layers **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To eliminate I/O bottlenecks, the entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors before training begins. This leverages the small dataset size to maximize GPU throughput **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**, exploiting the rotational invariance of aerial photography **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is selected to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
I propose a **Stratified SWA Cross-Validation Pipeline**.
*   **Strategy:** I will employ **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**. This ensures the solution is robust across the entire data distribution and mitigates the variance observed with single splits **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029, solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
*   **Optimizer Strategy (SWA):** I will utilize **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** to find flat minima in the loss landscape.
    *   **Phase 1 (Convergence):** Train with **AdamW** and Cosine Annealing for **25 epochs** to converge to the optimal region **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.
    *   **Phase 2 (Exploration):** Continue for **10 epochs** with a cyclic learning rate, capturing weight snapshots at the end of each cycle.
    *   **Phase 3 (Averaging):** Average the snapshots to create the final model for that fold. This improves generalization without the 2x computational cost of SAM **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.
*   **Loss Function:** The training loss will be the sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from both the Texture Head and the Semantic Head.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, all 5 fold models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their backbone blocks into efficient single-path networks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will utilize a **Hierarchical Ensemble Strategy**:
    1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each fold model, predictions will be generated for 4 views (original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, 180-degree rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Internal Aggregation:** For each view, the prediction is the average of the Texture Head and Semantic Head.
    3.  **Fold Aggregation:** The final probability for each test image will be the average of the predictions across all 5 folds.
    This results in averaging $5 \text{ (Folds)} \times 2 \text{ (Heads)} \times 4 \text{ (Views)} = 40$ predictions per image, maximizing robustness against noise and variance.
