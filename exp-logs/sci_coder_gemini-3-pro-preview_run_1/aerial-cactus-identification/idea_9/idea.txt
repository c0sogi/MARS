Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, I propose an optimized strategy that evolves the solution into a **Self-Ensembling RepVGG trained with Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)**. This approach synthesizes the architectural efficiency of RepVGG, the signal preservation of conservative downsampling, and the robustness of advanced optimization and ensembling.

# Model
I propose a **Self-Ensembling RepVGG** architecture.
*   **Backbone Design:** The network will be constructed using **RepVGG Blocks**. During training, these blocks utilize a multi-branch topology (3x3 convolution, 1x1 convolution, and identity connection) to maximize gradient flow. For inference, they are structurally re-parameterized into single 3x3 convolutional layers **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Conservative Downsampling:** The network will feature a "conservative stem" (3x3 convolution, stride 1, no pooling) to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage. This prevents the signal destruction caused by aggressive downsampling in low-resolution imagery **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Kernel Sizing:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels**, avoiding the performance degradation observed with larger kernels on small feature maps **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
*   **Internal Self-Ensembling:** Unlike standard architectures that use a single output head, this model will feature **two classification heads**:
    1.  **Auxiliary Head:** Attached after the intermediate stage (Stage 2).
    2.  **Main Head:** Attached after the final stage (Stage 3).
    Crucially, instead of discarding the auxiliary head after training (as in standard Deep Supervision), both heads will be retained. Their outputs will be averaged during inference, creating an efficient "internal ensemble" that combines features from different abstraction depths without the cost of a second backbone.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To eliminate I/O bottlenecks, the entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors before training begins. This leverages the small dataset size to maximize GPU throughput **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**. Images will be normalized using the dataset's channel-wise mean and standard deviation.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**, exploiting the rotational invariance of aerial photography **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is selected to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
I propose a **SAM-Optimized Cross-Validation Pipeline**.
*   **Cross-Validation:** The training data will be split into **5 Stratified Folds**. A separate model will be trained on each fold to ensure the final solution utilizes 100% of the data and is robust to split variance.
*   **Optimizer (SAM):** I will utilize **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)**. This optimizer wraps the base optimizer (AdamW) and performs two forward-backward passes per step to simultaneously minimize the loss value and the *loss sharpness*. This explicitly seeks a flat minimum in the loss landscape, ensuring the model generalizes better than standard SGD/Adam.
*   **Loss Function:** The loss will be the sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from both the Main Head and the Auxiliary Head. This enforces deep supervision while training the internal ensemble members.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be employed for **30 epochs** per fold to ensure gradual convergence **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** Before inference, all trained models will have their RepVGG blocks mathematically fused into single-layer structures to maximize inference speed **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will utilize a **Hierarchical Ensemble Strategy**:
    1.  **TTA:** For every image, predictions will be generated for 4 views (original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, 180-degree rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Internal Ensemble:** For each view, the model will output the average of the Main Head and Auxiliary Head probabilities.
    3.  **Cross-Fold Ensemble:** The final probability will be the average across all 5 fold models.
    This results in a final prediction averaged over $5 \text{ (Models)} \times 2 \text{ (Heads)} \times 4 \text{ (Views)} = 40$ logical inferences, providing maximum robustness.
