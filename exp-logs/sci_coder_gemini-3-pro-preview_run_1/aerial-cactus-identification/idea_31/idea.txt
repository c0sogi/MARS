Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose a solution that returns to a **Pure Vision** strategy but maximizes robustness through **Heterogeneous Deeply-Supervised Stacking**.

This approach explicitly rejects the "Metadata Injection" and "Auxiliary Quality Regression" paths, which were proven to be redundant (Lesson 00054) or uncorrelated distractors (Lesson 00072). Instead, it focuses on perfecting the visual representation learning by combining the efficiency of **RepVGG**, the stability of **ResNet**, and the regularization of **Deep Supervision** and **SWA**.

# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Deeply-Supervised Stacking Ensemble**.

### 1. Diverse Deeply-Supervised Base Learners
The ensemble will consist of two distinct backbone architectures. This ensures diversity in the error profiles (VGG-style vs. Residual-style) while adhering to the strict constraints required for 32x32 imagery.

*   **Base A: CactusRepVGG-DS (The Efficient Specialist)**
    *   **Backbone:** A custom RepVGG architecture utilizing multi-branch blocks (3x3 + 1x1 + Identity) during training for maximum gradient flow **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
    *   **Deep Supervision:** An **Auxiliary Classification Head** will be attached after the intermediate stage (Stage 2). This forces the early layers to learn discriminative features, addressing the vanishing gradient problem without the overhead of complex attention mechanisms.
*   **Base B: CactusResNet-DS (The Residual Baseline)**
    *   **Backbone:** A standard ResNet architecture.
    *   **Deep Supervision:** Similarly, an Auxiliary Classification Head will be attached after the intermediate stage to enforce robust feature learning.

**Universal Structural Constraints:**
Both architectures will strictly adhere to the following design principles:
*   **Conservative Downsampling:** Both stems will use a single 3x3 convolution with stride 1 and **no pooling**. This strictly preserves the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage, preventing the signal destruction caused by aggressive downsampling **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Small Kernel Constraint:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels**, avoiding the performance degradation observed with larger kernels (e.g., 7x7) on small thumbnail images **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.

### 2. Meta-Learner
A **Logistic Regression** meta-learner will be trained to aggregate the predictions from the base learners. This corrects for architectural biases and provides a statistically robust consensus **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To eliminate the I/O bottleneck identified in previous runs, the entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors before training begins. This leverages the small dataset size to maximize GPU throughput **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**, exploiting the rotational invariance of aerial photography **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is selected to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
I propose a **Stratified SWA Cross-Validation Pipeline**.
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** will be performed for *each* of the two architectures (10 models total). This generates the unbiased Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions required to train the Meta-Learner **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** will be used for effective weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be employed for **30 epochs** per fold. This duration ensures sufficient convergence for Mixup **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** SWA will be activated during the final 10 epochs (using a cyclic learning rate) to locate flat minima in the loss landscape. This improves generalization robustness without the 2x computational cost of SAM **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.
*   **Loss Function:** A weighted sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from the Main Head and the Auxiliary Head ($L_{total} = L_{main} + 0.4 \times L_{aux}$). This leverages the Deep Supervision to stabilize training.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, the RepVGG models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their multi-branch blocks into efficient single-path networks. The Auxiliary Heads for both architectures will be discarded, leaving only the optimized Main Heads **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:**
    1.  **TTA:** Each of the 10 trained models (2 archs $\times$ 5 folds) will generate predictions using **4-view Test Time Augmentation** (original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, 180-degree rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Stacking:** The aggregated OOF predictions from the base learners will be fed into the **Logistic Regression Meta-Learner** to produce the final probability. This approach maximizes robustness by combining architectural diversity (ResNet vs. RepVGG), data diversity (5-Fold CV), and geometric invariance (TTA).
