Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, I propose a solution that structurally evolves the training pipeline by integrating **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** with the highly efficient **RepVGG** architecture. This approach aims to secure the robustness of the "perfect" solution by explicitly optimizing for a flat minimum in the loss landscape, ensuring the model generalizes well even against unseen variations.

# Model
I propose a **Custom RepVGG** architecture, retaining the successful design from Idea 5.
*   **Backbone Design:** The network will be composed of **RepVGG Blocks**. During training, these blocks utilize a multi-branch topology (3x3 convolution, 1x1 convolution, and identity connection) to maximize gradient flow.
*   **Kernel Sizing:** The model will exclusively use **3x3 kernels**, avoiding the performance degradation observed with larger kernels on small images **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
*   **Conservative Downsampling:** The architecture will feature a "conservative stem" (3x3 convolution, stride 1, no pooling) to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage, preventing signal loss **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** The design allows the multi-branch blocks to be mathematically fused into single 3x3 convolutional layers for inference, decoupling training complexity from inference speed **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To eliminate I/O bottlenecks, the entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors before training begins. This leverages the small dataset size to maximize GPU throughput **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**, exploiting the rotational invariance of aerial imagery **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is retained to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
I propose a **Multi-Stage SWA Training Pipeline** to structurally enforce generalization.
*   **Phase 1 (Convergence):** The model will be trained using **AdamW** and a standard learning rate schedule (e.g., Cosine Annealing) to converge to a high-performance region.
*   **Phase 2 (Stochastic Weight Averaging):** Instead of terminating, the training will enter an SWA phase. The learning rate will switch to a cyclic or constant schedule to traverse the region around the minimum. At the end of each cycle, a "snapshot" of the model weights will be captured. The final model will be constructed by **averaging these weight snapshots** in parameter space. This creates a solution centered in a flat minimum, which is theoretically more robust than any single point estimate.
*   **Phase 3 (BatchNorm Update):** Since the averaged weights are not the result of a direct forward pass, a final single pass over the cached training data will be performed to re-calculate and freeze the Batch Normalization statistics (running mean and variance).
*   **Duration:** The total duration will be sufficient (e.g., 25+ epochs) to allow for both initial convergence and the SWA exploration, respecting the need for gradual decay **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, the SWA-averaged model will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing the multi-branch blocks into single layers **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will utilize **4-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**. The final prediction will be the average of probabilities for the original image, a horizontal flip, a vertical flip, and a 180-degree rotation. This combines the weight-space ensemble of SWA with the image-space ensemble of TTA for maximum robustness **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
