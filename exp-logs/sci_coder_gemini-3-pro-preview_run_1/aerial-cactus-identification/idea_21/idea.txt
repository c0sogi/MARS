Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous iterations and the extracted lessons, I propose a solution that synthesizes the efficiency of **Structural Re-parameterization**, the robustness of **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**, and the multi-scale feature extraction of **Self-Ensembling**. This approach evolves the "Self-Ensembling" concept (Idea 9) by replacing the computationally expensive SAM optimizer with the more efficient SWA (Lesson 00029) and expanding the internal ensemble to capture texture-level features explicitly.

# Model
I propose a **Multi-Scale Self-Ensembling RepVGG**.

*   **Backbone Architecture:** The core model will be a **Custom RepVGG**.
    *   **Training Topology:** Each block will utilize a multi-branch design (3x3 convolution, 1x1 convolution, and identity connection) to maximize gradient flow and training stability **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
    *   **Conservative Downsampling:** The network will utilize a "conservative stem" (3x3 convolution, stride 1, no pooling) to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage. This adheres to the critical lesson that aggressive downsampling destroys signal in low-resolution imagery **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
    *   **Kernel Sizing:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels**, avoiding the performance degradation observed with larger kernels (e.g., ConvNeXt 7x7) on small feature maps **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Multi-Scale Self-Ensembling):** Instead of a single output head or a discarded auxiliary head, this model will feature **three permanent classification heads** attached at different depths of the network (e.g., after Stage 1, Stage 2, and Stage 3).
    *   **Rationale:** The "Early" head forces the model to make predictions based on low-level texture features (crucial for distinguishing spines from compression artifacts), while the "Late" head captures abstract shape information. By retaining all heads, we create an efficient "internal ensemble" that leverages representations from multiple levels of abstraction without the cost of multiple backbones.
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** For inference, the multi-branch backbone blocks will be mathematically fused into single 3x3 convolutional layers. The three heads remain active, and their outputs are averaged to produce the final model prediction **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To eliminate the I/O bottleneck identified in previous runs, the entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors before training begins. This leverages the small dataset size to maximize GPU throughput **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**, exploiting the rotational invariance of aerial photography **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is selected to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal, as observed when comparing against aggressive Mixup ($\alpha=1.0$) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
I propose a **Stratified SWA Cross-Validation Pipeline**.
*   **Strategy:** I will employ **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**. Unlike previous single-split attempts which are prone to variance ("lucky splits"), this ensures the solution is robust across the entire data distribution **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
*   **Optimizer Strategy (SWA):** I will utilize **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**.
    *   **Phase 1:** Train with **AdamW** and Cosine Annealing for **25 epochs** to converge to the optimal region **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.
    *   **Phase 2:** Continue for **10 epochs** with a cyclic learning rate, capturing weight snapshots at the end of each cycle.
    *   **Phase 3:** Average the snapshots to create the final model for that fold. This locates a "flat minimum" in the loss landscape, improving generalization without the 2x computational cost of SAM **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.
*   **Loss Function:** The training loss will be the unweighted sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from all three heads (Early, Middle, Late). This enforces discriminative learning at all stages.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, all 5 fold models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their backbone blocks into efficient single-path networks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will utilize a **Hierarchical Ensemble Strategy**:
    1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each fold model, predictions will be generated for 4 views (original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, 180-degree rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Internal Aggregation:** For each view, the prediction is the average of the 3 internal heads (Early, Middle, Late).
    3.  **Fold Aggregation:** The final probability for each test image will be the average of the predictions across all 5 folds.
    *   **Total Robustness:** This results in averaging $5 \text{ (Folds)} \times 3 \text{ (Heads)} \times 4 \text{ (Views)} = 60$ predictions per image, maximizing stability against noise and variance.
