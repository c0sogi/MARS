Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a solution that evolves the successful RepVGG-style architecture (Idea 5) by integrating **Deep Supervision** into the training topology. This structural innovation aims to enforce robust feature learning in the early stages of the network while retaining the inference efficiency of the re-parameterized model.

# Model
I propose a **Shallow RepVGG with Deep Supervision**.

*   **Backbone Architecture (RepVGG-style):** The core architecture will consist of a "stem" followed by three stages of **RepVGG Blocks**.
    *   **Training Topology:** Each block will utilize a multi-branch design (3x3 convolution, 1x1 convolution, and identity connection) to improve gradient flow and convergence properties.
    *   **Kernel Sizing:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels**. This adheres to the finding that larger kernels (e.g., 7x7 in ConvNeXt) degrade performance on 32x32 thumbnails by blurring local spatial hierarchies **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
    *   **Conservative Downsampling:** The stem will use a 3x3 convolution with a stride of 1 and no pooling. This preserves the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage, preventing the signal destruction caused by aggressive downsampling **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Deep Supervision):** I will attach an **Auxiliary Classification Head** (consisting of Global Average Pooling and a Linear Layer) after the second stage of the network.
    *   **Purpose:** This auxiliary head provides direct supervision to the intermediate layers, forcing the network to learn discriminative features earlier in the pipeline and acting as a strong regularizer against overfitting.
*   **Inference Topology:** Before inference, the model will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**. The multi-branch RepVGG blocks will be mathematically fused into single 3x3 convolutions **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**, and the Auxiliary Head will be **pruned** (discarded). This results in a highly efficient, linear chain of layers (VGG-style) for deployment.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To maximize training throughput, the entire dataset (train and test images) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors before training begins. This eliminates disk I/O and JPEG decoding overhead **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**. Images will be normalized using the dataset's channel-wise mean and standard deviation.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**. This exploits the rotational invariance of aerial photography to improve robustness without the computational cost of equivariant architectures **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00010)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is selected to close the generalization gap to perfection without drowning out the signal, as observed with more aggressive Mixup settings **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** The model will be trained using a weighted sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** losses from the Main Head and the Auxiliary Head (e.g., $L_{total} = L_{main} + 0.4 \times L_{aux}$). This handles the soft targets generated by Mixup while enforcing the deep supervision constraint.
*   **Optimizer:** The **AdamW** optimizer will be used for its effective weight decay handling.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be employed.
*   **Duration:** The training will run for **30 epochs**. This duration is explicitly chosen to accommodate the convergence dynamics of Mixup and to ensure the Cosine Annealing schedule follows a gradual decay curve, preventing the performance regression observed with shorter schedules **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** Predictions will be generated using the re-parameterized (fused) main model, ensuring maximum inference speed.
*   **Robustness:** I will utilize **4-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**. The final probability will be the average of predictions for the original image, a horizontal flip, a vertical flip, and a 180-degree rotation. This leverages geometric symmetries to marginalize out noise and ensure the highest possible AUC **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
