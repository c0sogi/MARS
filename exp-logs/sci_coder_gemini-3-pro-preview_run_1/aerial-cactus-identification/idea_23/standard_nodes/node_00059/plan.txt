By removing the merge step, I ensure strict isolation between the training process and the final evaluation, as recommended in **Cite debug_lesson_11**.

The original script reduced the total epochs to 10 but left the SWA start epoch at the default of 20, causing the SWA model to never update and retain random weights. Adjusting the start epoch ensures the SWA mechanism activates within the reduced training duration.
