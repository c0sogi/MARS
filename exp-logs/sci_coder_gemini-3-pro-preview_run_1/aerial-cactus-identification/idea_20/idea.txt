Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the extracted lessons, I propose a solution that synthesizes the efficiency of **Structural Re-parameterization**, the robustness of **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**, and the statistical stability of **Cross-Validation**. This approach explicitly rejects the "Metadata Injection" path (which proved redundant and computationally expensive in Lesson 00054) in favor of maximizing the performance of the pure vision baseline.

# Model
I propose a **Deeply Supervised Homogeneous RepVGG Ensemble**.

*   **Backbone Architecture:** The core model will be a **Custom RepVGG**.
    *   **Training Topology:** Each block will utilize a multi-branch design (3x3 convolution, 1x1 convolution, and identity connection) to maximize gradient flow and training stability **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
    *   **Conservative Downsampling:** The network will utilize a "conservative stem" (3x3 convolution, stride 1, no pooling) to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage. This adheres to the critical lesson that aggressive downsampling destroys signal in low-resolution imagery **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
    *   **Kernel Sizing:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels**, avoiding the performance degradation observed with larger kernels (e.g., ConvNeXt 7x7) on small feature maps **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
*   **Deep Supervision:** An **Auxiliary Classification Head** will be attached after the second stage. This ensures discriminative features are learned early in the network and improves gradient propagation during the initial convergence phase.
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** For inference, the multi-branch blocks will be mathematically fused into single 3x3 convolutional layers, and the auxiliary head will be discarded. This results in a highly efficient, linear feed-forward network **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To eliminate the I/O bottleneck identified in previous runs, the entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors before training begins. This leverages the small dataset size to maximize GPU throughput **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**, exploiting the rotational invariance of aerial photography **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is selected to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal, as observed when comparing against aggressive Mixup ($\alpha=1.0$) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
I propose a **Stratified SWA Cross-Validation Pipeline**.
*   **Strategy:** I will employ **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**. Unlike previous single-split attempts which are prone to variance ("lucky splits"), this ensures the solution is robust across the entire data distribution **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
*   **Optimizer Strategy (SWA):** Instead of the computationally expensive Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM), I will utilize **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**.
    *   **Phase 1:** Train with **AdamW** and Cosine Annealing for **25 epochs** to converge to the optimal region.
    *   **Phase 2:** Continue for **10 epochs** with a cyclic learning rate, capturing weight snapshots at the end of each cycle.
    *   **Phase 3:** Average the snapshots to create the final model for that fold. This locates a "flat minimum" in the loss landscape, improving generalization without the 2x cost of SAM **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.
*   **Loss Function:** A weighted sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from the Main Head and the Auxiliary Head to handle soft Mixup targets.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, all 5 fold models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their blocks into efficient single-path networks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will utilize a **Double-Ensemble Strategy**:
    1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each fold model, predictions will be generated for 4 views (original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, 180-degree rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Fold Aggregation:** The final probability for each test image will be the average of the predictions across all 5 folds. This marginalizes out both model variance (via SWA/Ensembling) and data variance (via TTA), providing the most robust possible prediction.
