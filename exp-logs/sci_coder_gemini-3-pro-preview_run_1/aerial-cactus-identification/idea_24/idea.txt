Based on the accumulated lessons, specifically the critical pivot in **Lesson 00054** (which proved that explicit metadata injection is redundant and computationally expensive) and **Lesson 00029** (which highlighted the need for robustness against data quality variance), I propose a solution that maximizes the performance of the pure vision baseline.

The strategy is to evolve the efficient RepVGG architecture into a **Stratified Multi-Head Self-Ensembling Network**, trained via **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**. This approach leverages the implicit feature learning capabilities of the CNN to handle data quality issues (texture vs. semantics) without the architectural debt of metadata fusion.

# Model
I propose a **Multi-Head Self-Ensembling RepVGG**.

*   **Backbone Architecture:** The core feature extractor will be a **Custom RepVGG**.
    *   **Conservative Downsampling:** The network will utilize a "conservative stem" (3x3 convolution, stride 1, no pooling) to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage. This explicitly prevents the signal destruction caused by aggressive downsampling in low-resolution imagery **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
    *   **Kernel Sizing:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels**, avoiding the performance degradation observed with larger kernels (e.g., 7x7) on small feature maps **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
    *   **Training Topology:** Each block will utilize a multi-branch design (3x3 convolution, 1x1 convolution, and identity connection) to maximize gradient flow and training stability **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Internal Self-Ensemble):** Instead of a single output head, the model will feature **two permanent classification heads**:
    1.  **Texture Head (Mid-Level):** Attached after the intermediate stage (Stage 2). This forces the network to make predictions based on local texture and edge patterns (crucial for distinguishing spines from compression artifacts).
    2.  **Semantic Head (High-Level):** Attached after the final stage (Stage 3). This captures global shape and context.
    *   **Rationale:** By retaining and averaging these heads, the model acts as an internal ensemble that leverages the CNN's implicit ability to learn meta-features from pixels **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00054)**, without the complexity of external metadata injection.
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** For inference, the multi-branch backbone blocks will be mathematically fused into single 3x3 convolutional layers to ensure maximum speed **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** To eliminate I/O bottlenecks, the entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors before training begins **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** The pipeline will apply **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip**, exploiting the rotational invariance of aerial photography **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** I will apply **Mild Mixup Regularization** (Beta distribution $\alpha=0.2$). This specific intensity is selected to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal, as observed when comparing against aggressive Mixup **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
I propose a **Stratified SWA Cross-Validation Pipeline**.
*   **Strategy:** I will employ **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**. This ensures the solution is robust across the entire data distribution and mitigates the variance observed with single splits **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
*   **Optimizer Strategy (SWA):** I will utilize **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**.
    *   **Phase 1 (Convergence):** Train with **AdamW** and Cosine Annealing for **25 epochs** to converge to the optimal region.
    *   **Phase 2 (Exploration):** Continue for **10 epochs** with a cyclic learning rate, capturing weight snapshots at the end of each cycle.
    *   **Phase 3 (Averaging):** Average the snapshots to create the final model for that fold. This locates a "flat minimum" in the loss landscape, improving generalization without the 2x computational cost of SAM **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.
*   **Loss Function:** The training loss will be the unweighted sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from both the Texture Head and the Semantic Head.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, all 5 fold models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their backbone blocks into efficient single-path networks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will utilize a **Hierarchical Ensemble Strategy**:
    1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each fold model, predictions will be generated for 4 views (original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, 180-degree rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Internal Aggregation:** For each view, the prediction is the average of the Texture Head and Semantic Head probabilities.
    3.  **Fold Aggregation:** The final probability for each test image will be the average of the predictions across all 5 folds.
