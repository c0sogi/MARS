1.  **Extend Training Duration (Cite Lesson 00145):** The previous run was artificially limited to 10 epochs, achieving a metric of 0.087. The best previous solution (GCINet) required ~40 epochs to reach 0.057. Although BAMPNet converges faster, 10 epochs is insufficient to reach the global optimum. I will remove the hardcoded epoch limit in `runfile.py` and rely on the default 50 epochs with early stopping.
2.  **Optimize Batch Size:** I will increase `BATCH_SIZE` from 8 to 16 in `library/config.py`. This utilizes the available A100 GPU more efficiently and provides more stable gradient estimates for the small dataset.
3.  **Refine Class Balancing (Cite Lesson 00095):** The failure analysis shows a high penalty for short sequences, often due to deletion errors (predicting background instead of a gesture). I will aggressively down-weight the background class from 0.5 to 0.35 in `runfile.py` to encourage the model to predict active gestures.
4.  **Balance Auxiliary Loss:** I will reduce the `LOSS_WEIGHT_BOUNDARY` from 0.5 to 0.3 in `library/config.py`. While the boundary head helps early convergence, it is an auxiliary task; reducing its weight ensures the model prioritizes the primary classification metric as it fine-tunes.
