Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, the primary cause of the suboptimal performance (Levenshtein Error Rate ~0.207) is the use of **explicit loss masking** for padded frames. While masking is theoretically sound for preventing gradient dilution, **Lesson 00076** demonstrates that for this specific task, **Supervised Padding** (training the model to predict the "Background" class on padded frames) is significantly superior. It anchors the model's null-state predictions, reducing insertion errors and improving stability.

Additionally, the training duration was artificially capped at 30 epochs in the `runfile.py`, whereas **Lesson 00040** and **Lesson 00012** suggest that convergence to the optimal error rate (~0.0825) typically requires around 40-50 epochs with the current scheduler.

Therefore, the optimization strategy is:
1.  **Modify `library/train.py`**: Remove the manual masking logic and switch the `CrossEntropyLoss` reduction to `'mean'`. This enforces supervision on padded regions (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00076}).
2.  **Modify `runfile.py`**: Increase the training epochs from 30 to `Config.NUM_EPOCHS` (50) to ensure the model reaches the convergence plateau (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00040}).
