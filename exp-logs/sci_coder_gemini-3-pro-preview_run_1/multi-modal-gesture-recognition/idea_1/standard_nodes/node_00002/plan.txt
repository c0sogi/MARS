The issue identified is that the training script `runfile.py` is configured to load cached data (`load_cached_data=True`), but the existing cache contains an incomplete subset of the dataset (only 8 training samples). This causes the model to train on insufficient data and fail to generalize. To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to set `load_cached_data=False` during the initialization of the training and validation datasets, as well as during the inference step. This will force the data loader to ignore the stale cache and process the full dataset from the metadata files.
