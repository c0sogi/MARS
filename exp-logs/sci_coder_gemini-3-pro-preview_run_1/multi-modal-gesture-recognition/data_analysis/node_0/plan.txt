The strategy involves loading the generated training metadata and performing a comprehensive analysis across the different modalities present in the dataset (Tabular/Metadata, Video, Audio, and Skeleton/Structured).

1.  **Setup and Data Loading**: Initialize the environment with fixed random seeds and load the `train.csv` metadata.
2.  **Target Analysis**: Parse the label sequences to analyze the frequency and distribution of the 20 gesture categories. We will calculate class imbalance ratios and analyze the distribution of sequence lengths (number of gestures per video).
3.  **Multimodal Input Analysis**:
    *   **Video (RGB & Depth)**: Iterate through the training samples to extract video dimensions (width, height) and frame counts. To estimate pixel statistics (mean/std) efficiently without exceeding the time limit, we will process a random subset of videos and sample frames within them.
    *   **Audio**: Use `soundfile` to inspect audio files for duration, sample rates, and channel counts.
    *   **Structured/Skeleton (MAT files)**: Load the `.mat` files to analyze the skeleton data (joint coordinates) and, crucially, extract the temporal segmentation (start/end frames) of gestures to calculate the duration distribution for each gesture class.
4.  **Feature Relationships**: Analyze correlations between metadata (e.g., total video duration) and target properties (e.g., number of gestures). We will also examine if specific gestures tend to be longer or shorter.
5.  **Reporting**: Output the findings in the specified format, ensuring all floating-point numbers are formatted to 4 decimal places.
