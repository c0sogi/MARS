The strategy focuses on two key optimizations derived from the provided lessons: improving the neural model's context robustness and expanding the neuro-symbolic routing logic.

1.  **Data Augmentation for Context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012):** The previous solution significantly downsampled "easy" plain text (5%) to speed up training. However, Lesson 12 warns that "starving" the model of this background data degrades its ability to learn contextual embeddings, which are crucial for inflecting complex tokens (like numbers) correctly based on surrounding words. I will increase the `plain_subset_ratio` from `0.05` to `0.25` in `runfile.py`. To accommodate the larger dataset within the time limit, I will increase the `batch_size` to `256` (utilizing the A100 GPU) and `epochs` to `7` to ensure convergence.

2.  **Expanded Routing Logic (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010):** The current router only sends tokens containing digits to the neural model. This misses "long tail" cases like foreign acronyms (e.g., "TV", "DVD") or Latin script, which require transliteration but are often treated as identity mappings by the N-gram fallback. I will rename and update `is_digit_token` to `should_route_to_neural` in `library/utils.py`, modifying the regex to capture both digits and Latin characters (`[\d]|[a-zA-Z]`). This ensures these edge cases are routed to the neural generator instead of the symbolic fallback.
