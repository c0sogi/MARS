The issue is caused by the use of Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) in the `predict` function. Inside the `autocast` context, the model outputs logits in `float16` (half-precision). When `torch.softmax` is applied directly to these `float16` logits, the limited numerical precision results in probability distributions that do not sum exactly to 1.0, causing the integrity check in `runfile.py` to fail.

To fix this, I will modify `library/train.py` to explicitly cast the logits to `float32` (full precision) before applying the softmax function in both the `predict` and `validate` functions. This ensures the calculated probabilities are precise enough to satisfy the summation constraint.
