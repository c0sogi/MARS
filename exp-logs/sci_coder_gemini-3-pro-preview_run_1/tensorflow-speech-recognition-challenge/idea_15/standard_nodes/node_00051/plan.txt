My implementation strategy focuses on three key optimizations derived from the lessons learned:

1.  **Enhance Temporal Resolution**: I will modify `library/model.py` to set `output_stride=8` (instead of 16) for the Dilated EfficientNet-B2. This doubles the temporal resolution of the feature maps fed into the Attentive Pooling layer, allowing the model to better distinguish short, transient phonemes critical for command recognition (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

2.  **Optimize Training Schedule and Data**: I will update `runfile.py` to increase the training duration to 30 epochs and restore the target sample count to 2000. This ensures the model has sufficient iterations and data density to converge fully, addressing the underfitting observed in the previous "Fast Baseline" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00048, solution_lesson_node_00026).

3.  **Disable SWA**: I will disable Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) in `runfile.py` and `library/engine.py`. The previous failure analysis indicated that SWA caused performance degradation due to "Learning Rate Shock" when transitioning from a decayed learning rate to the high SWA learning rate. Instead, I will rely on the best checkpoint from the standard cosine annealing schedule (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).
