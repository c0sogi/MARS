My optimization strategy involves removing the Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) and the "Fast Baseline" configuration overrides to restore the model's convergence stability and data volume.

1.  **Remove SWA (Cite solution_lesson_node_00048):** The previous solution applied SWA starting at epoch 11 of 15. The logs showed the loss was still decreasing significantly at this point, meaning the model was in a transient phase. Averaging weights during this phase results in a "smeared" model that underperforms the best single checkpoint (0.9850 vs 0.9852). I will remove SWA and rely on standard checkpointing of the best model.
2.  **Restore Training Duration and Data Volume:** The previous `runfile.py` included an override block that reduced `epochs` to 15 and `target_sample_count` to 1000. This constrained the model's ability to converge and reduced the diversity of the training data. I will remove these overrides and set `epochs` to 30 in `config.py`. This is sufficient to reach convergence (previous run reached 98.5% in 10 epochs) while staying well within the 4-hour time limit (estimated runtime ~20-25 mins).
3.  **Optimize Scheduler:** I will adjust the Cosine Annealing scheduler to span the full 30 epochs (`T_max=30`) instead of being cut short by the SWA start epoch.
