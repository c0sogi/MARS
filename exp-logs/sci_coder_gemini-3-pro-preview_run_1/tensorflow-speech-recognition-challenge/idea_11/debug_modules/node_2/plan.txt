The `TypeError` indicates that `torchaudio.transforms.FrequencyMasking` was initialized with an unexpected keyword argument `p`. While `TimeMasking` in recent `torchaudio` versions accepts a `p` argument (specifying the maximum proportion of the signal that can be masked), `FrequencyMasking` does not support this parameter. The inclusion of `p=0.5` in the `FrequencyMasking` constructor is invalid and must be removed.
