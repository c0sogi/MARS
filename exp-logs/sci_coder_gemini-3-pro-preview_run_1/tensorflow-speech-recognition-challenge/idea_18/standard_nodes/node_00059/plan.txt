To optimize the performance and ensure the model beats the baseline, I will implement a strategy focused on **Input-Space Regularization (Mixup)** while simplifying the training pipeline to remove **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Re-enable Mixup Training**: The previous failure analysis (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058) identified that while Mixup parameters were defined, the mixing logic was missing from the training loop. I will implement Mixup with `alpha=1.0` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016) in `train_one_epoch` to prevent overfitting and improve generalization.
2.  **Remove SWA & Reduce Epochs**: Given the time constraints and the risk of "Learning Rate Shock" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050), I will revert to the robust baseline strategy of standard training. I will reduce the epoch count from 50 to **30** (matching the successful baseline Idea 7) and remove SWA. This ensures the training completes well within the 1-hour limit while relying on the proven efficacy of Mixup and SpecAugment.
3.  **Load Best Model**: Instead of using the final SWA model, the pipeline will explicitly load the checkpoint with the highest validation accuracy for final inference and submission.

**Code Changes:**
-   `library/config.py`: Reduce `EPOCHS` to 30.
-   `library/trainer.py`: Implement Mixup logic in `train_one_epoch`, remove `AveragedModel` (SWA) logic, and ensure the best model is loaded at the end of training.
-   `runfile.py`: Update the validation step to use `trainer.model` instead of `trainer.swa_model`.
