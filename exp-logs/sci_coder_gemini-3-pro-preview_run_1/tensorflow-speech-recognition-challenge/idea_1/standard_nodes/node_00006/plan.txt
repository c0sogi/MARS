My optimization strategy focuses on two key areas: improving the spectral resolution of the input features and tuning the regularization strength to better balance bias and variance.

1.  **Enhance Spectral Resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005):**
    I will increase the `N_FFT` parameter from 512 to 1024 in `library/config.py`. As noted in the lessons, a higher Mel filterbank density (128 bands) requires sufficient FFT bins to avoid aliasing and loss of detail. `N_FFT=512` provides only 257 unique bins, which is marginally sufficient for 128 Mel bands, especially in lower frequencies. Increasing it to 1024 (513 bins) ensures robust feature extraction.

2.  **Optimize Mixup Regularization:**
    I will reduce the Mixup `alpha` parameter from 1.0 to 0.4 in `library/trainer.py`. The previous training logs showed that the Training Accuracy (~90%) was consistently lower than the Validation Accuracy (~95%), a sign that the `alpha=1.0` setting (sampling from a uniform distribution) was creating training samples that were too difficult or noisy, leading to underfitting. Reducing `alpha` to 0.4 (sampling from a bell-shaped distribution peaked at 0 and 1) provides a more balanced regularization that retains the benefits of Mixup without overwhelming the model.

3.  **Signal Cleaning:**
    I will add `f_min=20.0` to the `MelSpectrogram` transform in `library/dataset.py` to filter out sub-audible low-frequency noise (DC offset/rumble) that contributes no linguistic information.
