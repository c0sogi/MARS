Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose a solution that structurally evolves the training paradigm from **Supervised Learning** to **Semi-Supervised Self-Training (Noisy Student)**. This approach leverages the massive unlabeled test set to bridge the generalization gap for unseen speakers, while strictly adhering to the robust architectural and data engineering protocols validated in previous experiments.

# Model
**Architecture: Dilated EfficientNet-B2 with Attentive Pooling**
We will retain the highly effective architecture from the "Current Best" solution, as architectural changes (e.g., ConvNeXt, Transformers, FPNs) have consistently underperformed or introduced instability.
*   **Backbone:** **EfficientNet-B2** initialized with ImageNet weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer will be modified to accept **1-channel input** by averaging the pretrained RGB weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016, Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
*   **Resolution Preservation:** We will strictly apply **Dilated Convolutions** (stride=1, dilation=2) in the final backbone stage. This preserves a temporal resolution of ~6-7 frames, which is critical for the attention mechanism to distinguish short phonemes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009, Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Head:** We will use **Attentive Pooling** to dynamically weight active speech segments and suppress background noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Output:** The model will output probabilities for **31+ Fine-Grained Classes** (all unique command words) rather than the compressed 12-class target set (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **High-Fidelity Spectrograms:** Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms with **128 Mel bands**, **N_FFT of 1024** (64ms), and a **Hop Length of 160** (10ms) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005, Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Normalization:** We will **strictly avoid** Instance Normalization to preserve the absolute energy differences between silence and speech (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).

**Augmentation:**
*   **Waveform Noise Injection:** Inject background noise into raw waveforms (SNR 10-30dB) to force invariance to environmental conditions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Dynamic Silence Synthesis:** Randomly crop background noise files to generate diverse 'silence' samples (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Regularization:** Apply **SpecAugment** (Time/Freq masking) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003) and **Mixup** with **$\alpha = 1.0$** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

**Balancing:**
*   **Variance-Aware Balancing:** Target commands will be upsampled to ~2,000, while the diverse auxiliary classes (the "Unknown" source) will be kept at their natural counts (~9,000 total) to ensure the negative class retains high variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).

# Training
**Strategy: Noisy Student Self-Training**
We will implement a two-stage training pipeline to exploit the large unlabeled test set (150,000+ files) for domain adaptation.

1.  **Stage 1 (Teacher):** Train the Dilated EfficientNet-B2 on the **Labeled Training Set** for **50 epochs** using **AdamW** and **Cosine Annealing** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
2.  **Stage 2 (Pseudo-Labeling):** Use the trained Teacher to predict labels for the **Unlabeled Test Set**.
    *   Apply a high confidence threshold (e.g., **0.95**) to filter reliable predictions.
    *   Retain the **Fine-Grained Labels** (e.g., predicting "bed", "bird" on the test set) to maintain the precision of the decision boundaries.
3.  **Stage 3 (Student):** Initialize a **fresh** Dilated EfficientNet-B2 (Student). Train this Student on the combined dataset (**Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test**).
    *   The Student learns from a much larger, diverse dataset (exposing it to unseen speakers/microphones).
    *   The strong noise injection (Waveform Noise, SpecAugment, Mixup) ensures the Student becomes more robust than the Teacher (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

# Evaluation
**Inference:**
*   Use the **Student Model** for final predictions.
*   Process test clips into Log-Mel Spectrograms (128 Mels, 1024 FFT).
*   **Post-Hoc Mapping:** Map the fine-grained predictions to the final submission format (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028):
    *   Target Labels (`yes`, `no`...) $\rightarrow$ Target Labels.
    *   `silence` $\rightarrow$ `silence`.
    *   Auxiliary Labels (`bed`, `bird`...) $\rightarrow$ `unknown`.
