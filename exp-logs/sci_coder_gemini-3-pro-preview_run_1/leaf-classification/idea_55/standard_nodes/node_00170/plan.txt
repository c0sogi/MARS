My optimization strategy focuses on **Feature Parsimony** and **Redundancy Elimination** to improve the conditioning of the OAS covariance estimator. Based on **Lesson 150** (Deterministic Redundancy) and **Lesson 158** (Signal Dilution), the previous solution's inclusion of raw absolute measures (`area`, `perimeter`, `major/minor_axis`) alongside their derived ratios (`roundness`, `aspect_ratio`) introduced multicollinearity and noise that destabilized the high-precision model. Furthermore, **Lesson 118** highlights that `equivalent_diameter` is a superior absolute scale descriptor compared to raw Area for Gaussian-based models, and **Lesson 140** emphasizes that a compact set of robust geometric ratios outperforms a "complete" but noisy set.

Therefore, I will:
1.  **Refine the Geometric Feature Set**: Reduce the extracted features to a parsimonious triad of macro-geometric descriptors (`solidity`, `extent`, `aspect_ratio`) plus `eccentricity`, `roundness`, and `equivalent_diameter`. I will remove the raw absolute measures and the experimental `mean_thickness` (Cite Lesson 120, 140, 150).
2.  **Update Caching**: Change the cache directory to ensure the model trains on the new feature set (Cite Lesson 116).
