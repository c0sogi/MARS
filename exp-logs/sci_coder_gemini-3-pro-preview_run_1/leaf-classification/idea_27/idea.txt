Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons—specifically the "near-perfect" separation achieved by the **OAS-LDA backbone** [Cite solution_lesson_node_00047], the numerical precision floor imposed by **`float32`** [Cite solution_lesson_node_00057], and the potential over-regularization of shrinkage estimators in high-confidence regimes—I propose the **Dual-Covariance Precision-Interpolated Discriminant**.

This strategy introduces a **Precision Matrix Interpolation** as a structural innovation. We hypothesize that while OAS provides the optimal *Mean Squared Error* estimate of the covariance, it may over-shrink the "flat" (low-variance) directions that are critical for maximizing the classification margin in perfectly separable data. By ensembling the **Conservative OAS Estimator** with the **Aggressive Empirical Estimator** (regularized only by microscopic jitter), we construct a composite decision boundary that balances structural robustness with the extreme sharpness required to minimize Log Loss.

# Model
The architecture is a **Linear Discriminant Ensemble** that operates by averaging the decision boundaries derived from two distinct covariance estimators before inference.

1.  **Expert A (Conservative):** A Linear Discriminant utilizing **Oracle Approximating Shrinkage (OAS)**.
    *   *Role:* Provides a robust, well-conditioned estimate of the covariance structure, protecting against noise and singularities [Cite solution_lesson_node_00047].
2.  **Expert B (Aggressive):** A Linear Discriminant utilizing **Empirical Covariance** with **Micro-Jitter**.
    *   *Role:* Estimates the covariance matrix using the raw training data with minimal regularization (`shrinkage=1e-6`). Since the pooled sample size ($N=712$) significantly exceeds the feature dimensionality ($P=192$), the empirical covariance is full-rank. This expert captures the exact sharpness of the training manifold, maximizing confidence in the "flat" directions that OAS might dampen.
3.  **Linearized Logit Fusion (Innovation):**
    *   Instead of averaging probabilities (which dampens confidence), we average the **Linear Logits** (or equivalently, interpolate the weight matrices).
    *   $W_{final} = 0.5 \cdot W_{OAS} + 0.5 \cdot W_{Empirical}$
    *   $b_{final} = 0.5 \cdot b_{OAS} + 0.5 \cdot b_{Empirical}$
    *   *Rationale:* This effectively interpolates the **Precision Matrices** ($\Sigma^{-1}$) of the two experts, creating a solution that is sharper than OAS but more stable than Empirical.

# Data
1.  **Input:** The solution will exclusively use the 192 pre-extracted tabular features (`margin`, `shape`, `texture`).
2.  **Deterministic Ordering:** We hardcode the feature list to prevent implicit permutation [Cite solution_lesson_node_00029].
3.  **Inductive Preprocessing Pipeline:**
    *   **High-Precision Loading:** Data is loaded and processed in **`float64`** [Cite solution_lesson_node_00046].
    *   **Transformation:** Apply **Yeo-Johnson Power Transformation** (`standardize=False`) followed by **Standard Scaling**.
    *   **Inductive Fit:** Fitted **only on the Training set** [Cite solution_lesson_node_00041].
    *   **No Quantization:** Data remains in `float64` throughout to avoid the $10^{-7}$ metric floor [Cite solution_lesson_node_00057].

# Training
1.  **Common Statistics:**
    *   Compute empirical class means $\mu_k$ and priors $\pi_k$ on the transformed training set ($N=712$).
    *   Compute centered residuals $R = X - \mu_y$.
2.  **Expert A (OAS):**
    *   Estimate $\Sigma_{OAS}$ using `OAS(assume_centered=True).fit(R)` [Cite solution_lesson_node_00061].
    *   Compute $W_{OAS} = \Sigma_{OAS}^{-1} \mu$ and $b_{OAS}$ using the Linear Formulation [Cite solution_lesson_node_00055].
3.  **Expert B (Empirical):**
    *   Estimate $\Sigma_{Emp}$ using the empirical covariance of $R$, adding a tiny diagonal jitter ($10^{-6} \cdot I$) to ensure numerical stability during inversion.
    *   Compute $W_{Emp} = \Sigma_{Emp}^{-1} \mu$ and $b_{Emp}$ using the Linear Formulation.
4.  **Fusion:**
    *   Compute the interpolated parameters $W_{final}$ and $b_{final}$ in **`float64`**.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Preprocess the test data using the fixed `float64` pipeline.
    *   **Linear Scoring:** Compute the logits $Z = X_{test} W_{final}^T + b_{final}$ in **`float64`**.
    *   **Probability:** Apply Softmax to $Z$ in **`float64`**.
2.  **Robustness:** This strategy leverages the high separability of the dataset. By allowing the Empirical expert to influence the decision boundary, we recover the discriminative signal in low-variance subspaces that OAS might over-regularize, pushing the probabilities closer to 1.0 and breaking the log-loss floor, while the OAS component ensures the solution remains well-conditioned.
