Based on the lessons learned, specifically the tension between noise filtering in estimation and numerical precision in inference, I will optimize the `LinearizedOASClassifier`.

The strategy involves a hybrid precision approach:
1.  **Covariance Estimation in `float32`**: I will cast the calculated residuals (`R`) to `float32` before fitting the `OAS` estimator. This acts as a regularization step, filtering out high-frequency numerical noise generated by the Yeo-Johnson transformation, which can lead to overfitting in the shrinkage parameter estimation (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00042`, `solution_lesson_node_00049`).
2.  **Inference in `float64`**: I will cast the resulting precision matrix back to `float64` immediately after estimation. This ensures that the calculation of weights, biases, and the final dot products remains in double precision, preventing the model from hitting the `float32` machine epsilon floor (~1e-7) which would otherwise cap the Log Loss performance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00057`).

This targeted change resolves the conflict between needing quantization for robust estimation and high precision for logarithmic scoring.
