Based on **Lesson 41 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041)**, the previous attempt to use **Transductive Preprocessing** (fitting transformations on both Train and Test data) caused a performance degradation from ~1.47e-08 to ~1.85e-08. The inclusion of Test data in the unsupervised fit likely introduced sampling variance that decoupled the feature normalization from the training label distribution.

To optimize performance and recover the best historical result, I will:
1.  **Switch to Inductive Preprocessing**: Modify `library/preprocessing.py` to fit the `PowerTransformer` and `StandardScaler` **strictly on the Training set**. This ensures the feature space is perfectly aligned with the supervised signals.
2.  **Update Configuration**: Change the working directory to `idea_14` to avoid loading cached transductive features.
3.  **Enforce Strict Threshold**: Update `runfile.py` to use the strict validation threshold (`1.4705366241156435e-08`) for submission generation.
