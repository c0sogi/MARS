Based on the empirical findings and lessons learned, the primary bottleneck preventing the previous solution from meeting the strict validation threshold (`1.21e-09`) is the **precision quantization** of the model weights and inference pipeline.

Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00057` explicitly states that `float32` precision imposes a "loss floor" of approximately $10^{-7}$ for logarithmic metrics because the model cannot resolve probabilities closer to 1.0 than $1.0 - \epsilon$. The previous run's result (`1.19e-07`) corresponds exactly to this machine epsilon floor. Furthermore, Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00045` advises using `float64` for algorithms dependent on sensitive matrix decompositions like LDA in high-dimensional spaces.

Therefore, the optimization strategy is to **remove the "Dual-Precision Gate"** in `library/model.py`. I will modify the `LinearizedOASDiscriminant` to maintain `float64` precision for both the stored weights (`W_`, `b_`) and the inference calculations (`predict_proba`).
