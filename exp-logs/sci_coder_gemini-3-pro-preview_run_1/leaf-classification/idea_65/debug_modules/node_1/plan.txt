The issue is that the naive subsampling strategy in "Debug Mode" (slicing the first 50 rows) results in disjoint sets of classes between the training and validation datasets. Because the dataset has high cardinality (99 species), a random slice of 50 rows in the training set captures a different subset of species than the slice in the validation set. This causes the `LabelEncoder` (fitted on the training subset) to fail when transforming the validation subset, as it encounters "unseen labels" like `'Populus_Adenopoda'`.

To fix this, I will modify the debug logic in `library/features.py` to first filter both the training and validation dataframes to a shared subset of classes (the first 5 unique species found in the training data) before applying the row count limit. This ensures that every class present in the validation subset is also present in the training subset, preventing the `ValueError`. This applies the principle from `debug_lesson_1` ("Filter Classes, Don't Just Slice Rows").
