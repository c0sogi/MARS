**Rationale:**
1.  **Feature Parsimony (Cite solution_lesson_node_00198):** Previous experiments demonstrated that a compact set of 5 robust geometric features (Area, Eccentricity, Solidity, Extent, Aspect Ratio) outperformed larger sets containing redundant or noisy descriptors. `major_axis_length`, while informative, is an absolute dimension that correlates with `area` and relies on `fitEllipse`, potentially introducing boundary fitting noise without adding significant orthogonal signal compared to the integral `area`.
2.  **Metric Calculation Stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00184):** The "Current Best" solution achieved its low error by passing raw softmax probabilities directly to `log_loss`. Manual normalization and clipping steps, even if theoretically correct, introduce additional floating-point operations that can perturb values at the `1e-14` scale.
