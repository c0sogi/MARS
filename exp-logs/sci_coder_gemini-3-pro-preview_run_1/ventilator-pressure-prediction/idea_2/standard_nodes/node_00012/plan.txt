**Implementation Strategy**
I will enhance the physics-informed feature engineering by explicitly modeling the volume-compliance relationship ($V/C$) and volume-resistance interaction, as suggested by the equation of motion $P = \dot{V}R + V/C$ (Cite Lesson 00005). Specifically, I will add `u_in_cumsum / C` and `u_in_cumsum * R` to the continuous features. Additionally, I will increase the model capacity by doubling the CNN filters (64 $\to$ 128) and the regression head size (128 $\to$ 256) to better capture local dynamics. Finally, I will extend the training duration from 10 to 50 epochs in `runfile.py` to allow the model to fully converge, as the previous logs showed the loss was still decreasing.
