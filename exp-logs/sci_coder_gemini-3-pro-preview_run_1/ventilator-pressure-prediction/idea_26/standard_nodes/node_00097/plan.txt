Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Prevent Import Side-Effect**: Wrap the `main()` call in `library/train.py` with `if __name__ == "__main__":` to prevent the unintended initial training run, saving computational resources.
2.  **Optimize Architecture for Convergence and Speed**: In `library/config.py`, reduce `MODEL_DIM` to 512 and `HIDDEN_DIM` to 256. This creates a **Uniform Hidden Dimension** architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00085) and avoids the detrimental **Bottleneck-to-Wide Projection** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073). This lighter model allows for faster iteration and better optimization stability.
3.  **Extend Training Horizon**: In `runfile.py`, increase `Config.EPOCHS` from 10 to 35. Given the reduced model capacity and the importance of training duration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028), this ensures the model fully converges within the time limit.
