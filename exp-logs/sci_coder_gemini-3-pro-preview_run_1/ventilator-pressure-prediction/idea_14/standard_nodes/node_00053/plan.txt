My optimization strategy focuses on stabilizing the training of the high-capacity architecture and extending the optimization horizon to ensure convergence.

1.  **Architecture Stabilization**: I will reduce the Feed-Forward Network (FFN) expansion factor in the `CompositeBlock` from 4x to 2x. As noted in **Lesson 52** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052), high expansion factors in unnormalized residual blocks can lead to optimization instability and performance degradation. A modest expansion (2x) retains the benefits of channel mixing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040) without destabilizing the gradients in the absence of Layer Normalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).

2.  **Extended Optimization Horizon**: I will increase the training duration to 30 epochs. The previous execution was limited to 6 epochs for demonstration, but high-precision regression on physical signals requires a longer training schedule to fully converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028). 30 epochs strikes a balance between the 20-25 epochs used in the best historical runs and the available runtime.

3.  **Configuration Cleanup**: I will remove the debug overrides in `runfile.py` to ensure the model trains for the full 30 epochs on the complete dataset, utilizing the optimized configuration.
