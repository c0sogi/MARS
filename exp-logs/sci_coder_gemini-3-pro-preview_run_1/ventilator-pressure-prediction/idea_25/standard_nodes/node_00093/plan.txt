Additionally, `library/data.py` relies on `Config.FEATURE_ORDER` to organize the input features, which is also missing from the configuration. This desynchronization between the configuration and the dependent modules (model and data) caused the `AttributeError`.

To fix this, I will add the `FEATURE_ORDER` list (defining the 14 expected features) and the `CONTEXT_FEATURE_INDICES` list (pointing to the indices of `R` and `C`) to the `Config` class. This restores the data contract required by the refactored architecture. Cite debug_lesson_3.
