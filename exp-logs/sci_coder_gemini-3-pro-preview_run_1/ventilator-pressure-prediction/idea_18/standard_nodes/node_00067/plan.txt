**Optimization Strategy:**

1.  **Filtered Context Injection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066)**:
    -   The previous solution injected the *entire* input feature vector (`x_orig`) into every LSTM block. Lesson 66 highlights that re-injecting dynamic, high-frequency inputs (like `u_in`, lags, derivatives) into deep layers introduces noise and bypasses the temporal hierarchy.
    -   I will modify the architecture to inject **only** the static, global system parameters (`R` and `C`) into the deep layers. This allows the model to condition its dynamics on the physical constraints without being overwhelmed by low-level signal noise.
    -   I will update `VentilatorModel` to extract these specific features and `CompositeBlock` to accept this filtered context.

2.  **Full Training Regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00061, solution_lesson_node_00028)**:
    -   The previous run was a "Fast Baseline" using only 30k samples and 15 epochs. Lesson 61 emphasizes that high-capacity models cannot be properly evaluated or trained on reduced regimes.
    -   I will remove the data subsampling to train on the full dataset (~54k breaths).
    -   I will increase the number of epochs from 15 to 40 to ensure full convergence, as Lesson 28 suggests that "training longer" is often necessary for schedule-dependent optimizers (OneCycleLR) to reach the global minimum.

3.  **Config Updates**:
    -   Define `CONTEXT_FEATURES` (`R`, `C`) in the configuration.
    -   Update hyperparameters to support the extended training.
