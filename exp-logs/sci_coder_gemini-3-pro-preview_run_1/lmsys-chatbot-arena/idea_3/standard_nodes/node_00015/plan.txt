The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` was caused by the high memory footprint of the Siamese DeBERTa model, which processes two sequences per sample, combined with a batch size of 8 and a sequence length of 1024 on a 16GB GPU. To fix this, I will reduce the `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` to 4, `VALID_BATCH_SIZE` to 8, and `MAX_LENGTH` to 512 in `runfile.py`. This adjustment ensures the memory usage fits within the hardware limits while accounting for the architectural overhead of the dual-branch network (Cite debug_lesson_3).
