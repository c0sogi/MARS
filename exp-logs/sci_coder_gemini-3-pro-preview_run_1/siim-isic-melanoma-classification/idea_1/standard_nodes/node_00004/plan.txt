The issue is that the `train_transform` pipeline in `library/dataset.py` applies geometric augmentations (like `RandomHorizontalFlip`) directly to a NumPy array (loaded via OpenCV), but these transforms expect a PIL Image or a PyTorch Tensor. To fix this, I will add `transforms.ToPILImage()` at the beginning of the `train_transform` composition to convert the NumPy array to a PIL Image before the augmentations are applied.
