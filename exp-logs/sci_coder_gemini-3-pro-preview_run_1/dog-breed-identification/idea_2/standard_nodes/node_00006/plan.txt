To optimize the solution, I will focus on mitigating the overfitting observed in Phase 2 and smoothing the transition between Head Adaptation and Fine-Tuning. The failure analysis and training logs indicated that while the `ConvNeXt-Tiny` backbone provides excellent features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005), the fine-tuning phase was too aggressive, causing the model to deviate from the optimal weights found in Phase 1.

My strategy involves:
1.  **Regularization**: Introducing `Dropout` (p=0.5) to the classifier head. The previous solution showed signs of severe overfitting (Train Loss ~0.03 vs Val Loss ~0.26). Dropout will help generalize better.
2.  **Gentler Fine-Tuning**: Reducing the learning rates for Phase 2 significantly (`1e-6` for backbone, `1e-4` for head). This ensures that we refine the features without destroying the representations learned during pre-training and Phase 1.
3.  **Extended Head Adaptation**: Increasing `PHASE1_EPOCHS` to 6 to ensure the head is fully converged before unfreezing the backbone.
4.  **Updated Threshold**: Updating the submission threshold in `runfile.py` to the previous best validation metric (0.2182).
