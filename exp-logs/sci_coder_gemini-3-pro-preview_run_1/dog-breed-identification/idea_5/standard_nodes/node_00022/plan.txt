I will optimize the solution by reverting from a heterogeneous ensemble to a single, fully converged `ConvNeXt Base` model. This decision is based on **Lesson 00020**, which demonstrates that a single strong model trained to convergence outperforms an under-trained ensemble, and **Lesson 00021**, which highlights that naively ensembling a weaker model (Swin Transformer) with a stronger one (ConvNeXt) degrades performance.

To implement this:
1.  **Configuration (`library/config.py`)**: I will restrict the model architecture to `convnext_base.fb_in1k` and increase the fine-tuning epochs (`EPOCHS_FINE`) from 12 to 20 to ensure the model has sufficient time to minimize log loss (Cite Lesson 00020).
2.  **Execution Script (`runfile.py`)**: I will remove the hardcoded epoch overrides that were artificially shortening training time. I will also simplify the training loop to focus on the single model, removing the ensemble aggregation logic (Cite Lesson 00021).
