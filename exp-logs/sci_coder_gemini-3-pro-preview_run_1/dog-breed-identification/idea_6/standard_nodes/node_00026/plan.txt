To optimize performance, I will implement a robust **5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensemble** strategy combined with enhanced data augmentation.

### Strategy

1.  **Enhanced Data Augmentation (Cite Lesson 2)**: I will introduce `ColorJitter` to the training transforms. While Lesson 2 warns against aggressive spatial augmentations (like `RandomResizedCrop`) for fine-grained tasks, mild color perturbations help the model generalize to different lighting conditions without distorting the critical geometric features of the dog breeds.
2.  **5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensemble (Cite Lesson 20)**: I will modify `runfile.py` to train on all 5 folds instead of just a single fold. Lesson 20 highlights that ensembling is a powerful way to reduce Log Loss. By training 5 models on different subsets of the data and averaging their predictions, we reduce the variance of the final predictions and improve generalization.
3.  **Global OOF Evaluation**: The validation metric will be calculated using Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions across the entire dataset. This provides a more reliable estimate of model performance than a single validation split.
4.  **Ensemble Submission**: If the OOF metric meets the threshold, the submission will be generated by averaging the predictions of all 5 trained SWA models (Cite Lesson 24 for SWA benefits).

This strategy fits comfortably within the time limit (approx. 2.5 hours for 5 folds) and directly targets Log Loss minimization.
