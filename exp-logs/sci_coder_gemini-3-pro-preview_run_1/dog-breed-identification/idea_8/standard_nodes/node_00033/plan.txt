To optimize the solution, I will focus on a single, high-performance architecture (ConvNeXt Base) rather than a heterogeneous ensemble, as the Swin Transformer proved to be a "weak link" that degraded performance and consumed significant compute (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00020).

My strategy involves:
1.  **Resolution Upgrade**: Increasing the input resolution to 256x256 (via a 274px resize and 256px crop) to preserve fine-grained spatial details, which was identified as a critical factor for this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
2.  **Regularization**: Re-introducing Dropout (0.5) to the classification head to prevent overfitting, which was missing in the previous run (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
3.  **Training Schedule**: Slightly extending the fine-tuning phase (Phase 2) from 6 to 8 epochs to ensure full convergence of the single strong model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
4.  **Evaluation**: Maintaining the robust 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation strategy to ensure reliable performance estimation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026), but simplifying the inference pipeline to a direct average of the 5 folds, removing the meta-learner overhead.

I will implement these changes by modifying `library/config.py`, `library/training.py`, and `runfile.py`.
