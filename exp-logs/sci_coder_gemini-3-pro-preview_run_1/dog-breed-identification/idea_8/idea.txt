Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose a **Regime-Adaptive Heterogeneous Stacking Strategy**.

This solution introduces a structural innovation by acknowledging that different model architectures (CNNs vs. Transformers) require fundamentally different training regimes to converge on small, fine-grained datasets. Instead of forcing a unified pipeline, we will implement specialized training tracks that are combined via a learned meta-model to maximize log-loss calibration.

# Model
We will construct a **Stacked Ensemble** composed of two distinct Level-1 expert architectures and a Level-2 meta-learner:

1.  **Expert A (CNN): ConvNeXt-Base** (Pre-trained on ImageNet-1k).
    *   **Role:** Captures local geometric features (texture, shape) where it has proven highly effective `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.
    *   **Configuration:** Linear head with Dropout `Cite solution_lesson_node_00006`.
2.  **Expert B (Transformer): Swin-Base** (Pre-trained on ImageNet-1k).
    *   **Role:** Captures global context and long-range dependencies.
    *   **Innovation:** To address the "Weak Link" failure `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021` and the "Overfitting" of high-capacity models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`, this model will undergo a distinct, heavily regularized training regime (detailed below).
3.  **Meta-Learner:** **Logistic Regression**.
    *   **Role:** Instead of simple averaging (which dilutes strong predictions with weak ones), this model will learn the optimal linear combination of the experts' Out-Of-Fold (OOF) logits, ensuring probability calibration `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.

# Data
We will enforce the **High-Resolution Geometric Pipeline** for both experts to preserve breed-specific morphology.
*   **Preprocessing:** `Resize(274)` (integer argument) followed by `CenterCrop(256)`. This strictly preserves aspect ratio, preventing the geometric distortion that caused previous failures `Cite solution_lesson_node_00018`.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Expert A (CNN):** Only **Random Horizontal Flips**. We avoid aggressive spatial/pixel augmentation here to prevent underfitting `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
    *   **Expert B (Swin):** **Random Horizontal Flips** + **Mixup/CutMix** (during specific training phases).

# Training
We will implement **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026` for both experts. Crucially, we will employ **Regime-Adaptive Training**:

**Regime A (For ConvNeXt - The "Precision" Track):**
Designed for fast convergence and high precision on local features.
1.  **Phase 1:** Head Adaptation (Frozen Backbone).
2.  **Phase 2:** Fine-Tuning with **Discriminative LRs** (`1e-6` backbone, `1e-4` head) `Cite solution_lesson_node_00006`. No Mixup/CutMix.
3.  **Phase 3:** **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** to find a flat minimum `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

**Regime B (For Swin - The "Robustness" Track):**
Designed to regularize the high-capacity transformer and fix the "Weak Link" issue.
1.  **Phase 1:** Head Adaptation.
2.  **Phase 2:** **Regularized Fine-Tuning**. Enable **Mixup** and **CutMix**. Crucially, we will **extend the training epochs** (e.g., 2x duration of Regime A) to allow the model to converge on these soft targets, addressing the underfitting observed in previous short-run attempts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00029`.
3.  **Phase 3:** **Cooldown (Augmentation Annealing)**. **Disable Mixup/CutMix** and fine-tune for a few epochs with a low learning rate. This "sharpens" the predictions, as soft targets penalize Log Loss. This directly resolves the conflict between regularization and the metric `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
4.  **Phase 4:** **SWA** on the cooled model.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Apply **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)** (Original + Flip) for all models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.
2.  **OOF Generation:** Generate Out-Of-Fold predictions for the entire training set using the 10 trained models (5 ConvNeXt + 5 Swin).
3.  **Stacking:** Train the **Logistic Regression** meta-learner on the OOF logits to minimize Log Loss.
4.  **Submission:** Pass the test set images through all 10 models, aggregate predictions using the trained meta-learner, and submit.
