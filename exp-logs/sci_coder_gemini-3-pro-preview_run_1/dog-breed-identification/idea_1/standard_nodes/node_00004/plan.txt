The issue is that the validation metric was being calculated using `CrossEntropyLoss` with label smoothing enabled, which mathematically results in a higher loss value than the standard Multi Class Log Loss (Cross Entropy with hard labels) required by the competition. This caused the validation score to appear artificially high (worse), failing to beat the baseline threshold and skipping the submission. To fix this, I will instantiate two separate loss functions in `runfile.py`: `train_criterion` with label smoothing for training regularization, and `val_criterion` without label smoothing for accurate metric tracking during validation and final evaluation.
