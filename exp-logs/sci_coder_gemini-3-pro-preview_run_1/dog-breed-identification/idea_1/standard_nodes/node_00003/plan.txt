2.  **Data Augmentation**: I will replace `CenterCrop` with `RandomCrop` in the training pipeline. Per **Lesson 00002**, aggressive spatial augmentations like `RandomResizedCrop` can be detrimental in fine-grained tasks, but `RandomCrop` (after resizing) is a mild, deterministic transformation that preserves aspect ratios while exposing the model to more diverse views of the subject.
3.  **Conditional Execution**: I will modify the submission logic to only generate predictions if the validation metric improves upon the baseline of 0.7155.
