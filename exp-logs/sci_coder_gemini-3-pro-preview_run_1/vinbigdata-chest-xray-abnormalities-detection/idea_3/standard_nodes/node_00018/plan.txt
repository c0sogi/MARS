The `RuntimeError` indicates that `torch.nn.BCELoss` is being used within an `autocast` context, which is unsafe for mixed precision training due to numerical instability. To fix this, I will replace `BCELoss` with `BCEWithLogitsLoss`, which combines the sigmoid activation and cross-entropy loss into a single stable operation, as recommended in `debug_lesson_5`. This involves modifying the model to output raw logits for the global classification head, updating the loss function, and explicitly applying the sigmoid activation during inference in the engine.
