2.  **Robust Data Loading with Rasterio (Cite debug_lesson_4):** The `Warning: Could not load image` logs indicate that the custom DICOM parser is failing to decode the images, likely due to compression formats (like JPEG 2000) that the manual binary parser and standard OpenCV cannot handle. I will modify `library/utils.py` to include `rasterio` as a robust decoding backend. This "defense-in-depth" strategy layers `rasterio` (which uses GDAL) before the OpenCV and manual fallbacks, ensuring valid pixel data is loaded even when `pydicom` is unavailable.
