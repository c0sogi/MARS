The issue is that the pipeline was executed in `DEBUG` mode, which restricted the dataset to only 2,000 samples, leading to an `InvalidMetricValueError` because the validation metric must be calculated on the full validation set. Additionally, the training loss was `inf`, indicating that the learning rate (`1e-3`) is too high for the CodeBERT model, causing numerical instability. Furthermore, simply disabling debug mode is insufficient because the `FeatureExtractor` would load the stale, partial feature cache generated during the debug run.

To fix this:
1.  In `library/config.py`, I will lower the `LEARNING_RATE` to `2e-5` to ensure stable training and update the `BATCH_SIZE` to `16`.
2.  In `runfile.py`, I will disable `Config.DEBUG`, increase `NUM_EPOCHS` to 5, and increase `NUM_WORKERS`.
3.  In `runfile.py`, I will set `load_cached_data=False` when calling `extract_and_save_features` to explicitly invalidate the stale cache and force processing of the full dataset (Cite debug_lesson_1).
