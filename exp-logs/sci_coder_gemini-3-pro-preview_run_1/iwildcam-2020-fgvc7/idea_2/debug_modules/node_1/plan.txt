The `CUDA error: device-side assert triggered` during the `F.cross_entropy` calculation is caused by a mismatch between the model's expected class indices (0 to 184) and the dataset's raw category IDs (which are sparse integers up to 675). The loss function expects targets in the range `[0, NUM_CLASSES-1]`, but receives raw IDs that exceed this range, causing an out-of-bounds access on the GPU.

To fix this:
1.  **Label Encoding**: I will modify `library/dataset.py` to create a mapping from raw `category_id`s to contiguous indices `[0, 184]` based on the training metadata. The `CameraTrapDataset` will use this mapping to transform labels during training and validation.
2.  **Inverse Mapping**: I will modify `library/engine.py` to use the inverse mapping during submission generation, converting the model's predicted indices back to the original category IDs.
3.  **Metric Initialization**: I will also update the `best_acc` initialization in `library/engine.py` to `-1.0` to ensure the first epoch's model is saved even if accuracy is `0.0`, as per `debug_lesson_1`.
