Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Dual-Resolution Spectral Energy Stacking Strategy**. This approach structurally evolves the solution by introducing **Sub-band Energy Features** to address the magnitude-insensitivity of previous spectral descriptors (Lesson 00037) and by implementing a **Dual-Resolution Vision Backbone** to simultaneously capture impulsive shocks and harmonic tremors, strictly adhering to the global scaling requirements for energy preservation.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Heterogeneous Stacking Ensemble** designed to maximize the capture of absolute signal energy and multi-scale temporal structures.

1.  **Branch A: Energy-Aware Tabular Regressor (LightGBM)**
    *   **Architecture:** A Gradient Boosting Machine (LightGBM) optimized for a high-dimensional, expert-engineered feature space.
    *   **Structural Innovation (Sub-band Energy & Crest Factor):** To address the failure of normalized spectral features (Lesson 00037), we will explicitly engineer **Log-Subband Energies**. We divide the frequency spectrum into $N$ distinct bands (e.g., 0-2Hz, 2-5Hz, etc.) and calculate the sum of squared magnitudes (Energy) for each band. This provides the model with magnitude-dependent spectral descriptors (e.g., "how loud is the tremor at 5Hz?") rather than scale-invariant shapes. Additionally, we include **Crest Factor** (Peak-to-RMS ratio) to explicitly quantify signal impulsiveness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).
    *   **Feature Policy:** We retain the **Multi-View Robustness Policy**:
        *   **Time-Domain:** Retain full statistics (including Min/Max/Kurtosis) to preserve extreme event magnitudes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
        *   **Frequency-Domain:** Use **Robust Statistics Only** (Mean, Std, Q05, Q95) for FFT/MFCCs to avoid noise artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).

2.  **Branch B: Dual-Resolution Global-Scaled Vision Model (EfficientNet)**
    *   **Architecture:** An **EfficientNet-B0** CNN modified to accept **20 input channels** (2 views per sensor).
    *   **Structural Innovation (Dual-Resolution Spectrograms):** Seismic signals contain both short, impulsive events (shocks) and long, harmonic events (tremors). Standard spectrograms trade off time vs. frequency resolution. We will generate **two** spectrograms for each sensor:
        1.  **Wide-Band View:** Short window length (high time resolution) to capture impulsive shocks.
        2.  **Narrow-Band View:** Long window length (high frequency resolution) to capture harmonic structures.
        These are stacked channel-wise, allowing the CNN to learn from both resolutions simultaneously.
    *   **Normalization:** We strictly apply **Global Log-Max Scaling** (normalizing by a fixed global constant derived from training) to preserve the absolute energy differences between segments, avoiding the destructive effects of Instance Standardization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).

3.  **Meta-Learner:**
    *   A **Ridge Regression** model (Unconstrained) trained on Out-of-Fold (OOF) predictions. This allows the ensemble to optimally weight the orthogonal contributions of the Tabular and Vision branches without artificial constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).

# Data
The data pipeline implements strict safeguards against stale data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) and enforces specific feature engineering constraints.

1.  **Branch A Preprocessing (Tabular):**
    *   **Sub-band Energy:** Calculate `log(sum(fft_magnitude[band]**2))` for ~10 linearly spaced frequency bands.
    *   **Impulsiveness:** Calculate Crest Factor (`max(abs(x)) / rms(x)`) and Shape Factor (`rms(x) / mean(abs(x))`).
    *   **Robust MFCCs:** Extract MFCCs restricted to **Coefficients 1-13** (Low-Order) to capture timbre, aggregated using only robust statistics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Energy & Structure:** Include **Absolute Quantiles** (`quantile(abs(x))`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014) and **Raw Zero-Crossing Rate** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Spatial:** Correlation Matrix between all sensors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Exclusion:** Explicitly avoid raw gradients (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020) and beamforming aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

2.  **Branch B Preprocessing (Vision):**
    *   **Generation:** For each of the 10 sensors, generate two Log-Mel Spectrograms (Short-Window + Long-Window) and stack them into a `(20, Freq, Time)` tensor.
    *   **Normalization:** Apply $X_{norm} = \frac{\log(X + 1)}{\log(M_{global} + 1)}$, where $M_{global}$ is the maximum value observed in a subset of the training data.
    *   **Target Scaling:** Apply **Log-Scaling** (`np.log1p`) to the `time_to_eruption` target (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

# Training
1.  **Strategy:** 5-Fold Cross-Validation for both branches to generate aligned OOF predictions.
2.  **Tabular Branch:** Train LightGBM with early stopping based on validation MAE.
3.  **Vision Branch:** Train using **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing**. Extend training to **30+ epochs** to ensure convergence on the complex dual-resolution features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
4.  **Meta-Learner:** Train Ridge Regression on the OOF predictions against the ground truth.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Generate the Energy-Aware Feature Matrix and Dual-Resolution Spectrograms for the test set.
    *   Predict with LightGBM (Branch A).
    *   Predict with EfficientNet (Branch B) and apply **Inverse Target Transformation** (`np.expm1`).
2.  **Submission:** Input both prediction vectors into the trained Ridge Meta-Learner to produce the final `time_to_eruption` estimate.
