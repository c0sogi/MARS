The execution failure is caused by an arithmetic overflow when converting the neural network's log-scale predictions back to the original time scale. During early training epochs, the model produces large output values which, when passed to `np.expm1()`, exceed the maximum representable value for `float32`, resulting in infinity and crashing the metric calculation. To fix this, I will clamp the log-predictions to a safe upper bound (85.0) before applying the inverse transformation, preventing the overflow while preserving the logic for valid predictions (Cite debug_lesson_5). I will apply this safeguard in the training validation loop, the final validation step, and the inference function.
