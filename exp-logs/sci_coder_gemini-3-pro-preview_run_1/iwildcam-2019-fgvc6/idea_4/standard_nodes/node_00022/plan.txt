Based on the failure analysis of the previous solution, which showed a positive correlation (+0.31) between prediction error and class rarity, the model is currently under-weighting rare classes. The previous square-root dampening (exponent 0.5) was too strong, while linear weighting (exponent 1.0) is known to be too weak (causing false positives). I will adjust the class weight dampening exponent to **0.8** to better balance the loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).

Additionally, I will increase the input resolution to **256x256** to better resolve small animals and extend the training to **10 epochs** to ensure convergence, as the available compute time (4 hours) permits this increased load.
