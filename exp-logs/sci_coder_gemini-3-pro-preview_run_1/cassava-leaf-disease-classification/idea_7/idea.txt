Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the success of the previous experiments, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the approach from a single-model baseline to a **Hybrid Architectural Ensemble**, upgrades the backbone to **ConvNeXt V2**, and integrates the advanced training paradigms (SWA, LLRD, Progressive Resizing) validated in the lessons.

# Model
- **Architecture (Structural Innovation):** Implement a **Hybrid Ensemble** consisting of two distinct architectures to maximize feature diversity and robustness **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**:
    1.  **ConvNeXt V2-Small:** An evolution of the successful ConvNeXt backbone that incorporates **Global Response Normalization (GRN)** to enhance inter-channel feature competition and is initialized with **Masked Autoencoder (FCMAE)** weights for superior fine-grained feature recognition. This addresses the need for high capacity without the overfitting risks of "Base" models **Cite solution_lesson_node_00024**.
    2.  **Swin Transformer V2-Tiny:** A hierarchical Vision Transformer that utilizes window-based attention. This complements the CNN-based ConvNeXt by capturing long-range global dependencies and semantic context that local convolutions might miss.
- **Pooling:** Apply **Generalized Mean Pooling (GeM)** to the output feature maps of both models (flattening the spatial dimensions of ConvNeXt and the patch tokens of Swin). This learnable pooling layer adaptively focuses on salient high-frequency details (disease spots) rather than averaging them out **Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**.
- **Head:** Equip both models with a **Multi-Sample Dropout** classification head to accelerate convergence and improve generalization by averaging logits from multiple parallel dropout masks **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.

# Data
- **Preprocessing (Progressive Resizing):**
    - **Phase 1 (Coarse):** Resize images to **384x384** for the initial training phase to maximize throughput and learn structural features.
    - **Phase 2 (Fine):** Resize images to **512x512** for the fine-tuning and SWA phases. This leverages the proven accuracy gains from high-resolution training for fine-grained classification **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
- **Augmentation:**
    - **Mixing:** Apply **MixUp** and **CutMix** to synthesize novel training samples and enforce linear decision boundaries, effectively handling the class imbalance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
    - **Contextual Cropping:** Constrain `RandomResizedCrop` to a scale of `(0.3, 1.0)` to ensure that the model trains on semantically valid crops rather than ambiguous macro-textures **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
    - **Determinism:** Explicitly seed the `worker_init_fn` in DataLoaders to ensure reproducibility **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.

# Training
- **Optimizer Strategy:** Use **AdamW** with **Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD)** for both models. Apply a decay factor (e.g., 0.8) to progressively lower the learning rate for layers closer to the input. This preserves the robust pre-trained features of the backbones while allowing the GeM pooling and heads to adapt aggressively **Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**.
- **Loss Function:** Use **Soft Target Cross Entropy** to handle the continuous target distributions generated by MixUp and CutMix. Do not use explicit Label Smoothing, as MixUp provides sufficient data-dependent smoothing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.
- **Regularization:** Set **Stochastic Depth (Drop Path)** rate to `0.0` (or very low) for these "Small/Tiny" architectures. Given the strong data augmentation (MixUp/CutMix), standard drop path rates lead to over-regularization and underfitting **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.
- **Batch Size:** Use **Gradient Accumulation** to ensure the effective batch size remains $\ge 32$ per update, preserving Batch Normalization stability **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
- **Schedule (SWA Pipeline):** Train each model independently with the following schedule:
    1.  **Warmup:** 1 epoch at 384x384 with backbone frozen **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
    2.  **Base Training:** ~10 epochs at 384x384 with LLRD.
    3.  **High-Res Fine-Tuning:** ~5 epochs at 512x512.
    4.  **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** ~5 additional epochs at 512x512 using a cyclical learning rate. Save snapshots at the end of each cycle and average them to create the final weights for that model **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.

# Evaluation
- **Inference:**
    1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test image, generate predictions for the original, horizontally flipped, and vertically flipped versions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
    2.  **Ensemble Aggregation:** Compute the arithmetic mean of the TTA-averaged probabilities from the **ConvNeXt V2-Small (SWA)** model and the **Swin V2-Tiny (SWA)** model. This structural ensemble mitigates the stochastic variance of single-model training and leverages the diverse inductive biases of CNNs and Transformers to maximize accuracy **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
