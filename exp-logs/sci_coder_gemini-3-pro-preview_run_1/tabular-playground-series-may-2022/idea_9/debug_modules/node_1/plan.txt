The error is caused by an incorrect expectation in the test script `runfile.py` regarding the number of numerical features. The `generate_dummy_data` function creates 31 original feature columns (`f_00` through `f_30`). The pipeline excludes `f_27` (treating it as a sequence) and adds one engineered feature (`unique_characters`), resulting in a total of 31 numerical features ($31 - 1 + 1 = 31$). The assertions in `runfile.py` incorrectly expect 32 features.

I will update `runfile.py` to assert 31 numerical features instead of 32 in the data shape verification, dataset item verification, batch shape verification, and model output shape verification.
