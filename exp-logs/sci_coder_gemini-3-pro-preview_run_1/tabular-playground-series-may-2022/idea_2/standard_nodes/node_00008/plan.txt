The model should be able to learn these sequence properties (like "uniqueness") directly from the embeddings if the representation is rich enough, without the noise of a hard-coded scalar feature.

My optimization plan involves:
1.  **Removing the `unique_char_count` feature** from the preprocessing pipeline to force the model to rely on the learnable embeddings, thereby simplifying the input space and avoiding potential redundancy (Cite Lesson 00007).
2.  **Increasing the `EMBEDDING_DIM` from 32 to 64** to provide a higher-capacity representation for the characters, ensuring the model has enough bandwidth to learn complex patterns (like uniqueness) that were previously manually engineered.
3.  **Increasing the MLP capacity** (Hidden Units from `[512, 256, 128]` to `[1024, 512, 256]`) to better model the non-linear interactions between the richer flattened embeddings and the numerical features.
4.  **Updating the Cache Directory** to ensure the data is re-processed without the removed feature.
