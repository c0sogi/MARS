Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Dual-Stream Denoising Network (DSDN)**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** by explicitly decoupling the modeling of "Main Effects/Positional Rigidity" from "Complex Non-Linear Interactions" using a hybrid dual-stream architecture. It synthesizes the superior interaction modeling of the **Denoising Granular Transformer** (Lesson 00026, 00032) with the positional stability of **Flattened MLP Baselines** (Lesson 00009), while correcting the critical objective function flaw that caused the failure of previous hybrid attempts (Lesson 00043).

# Model
I propose a **Dual-Stream Architecture** that processes the input through two parallel branches before late fusion.

1.  **Shared Input Layer (Strategic Retention):**
    *   **Sequence:** The `f_27` string is tokenized and mapped to **Entity Embeddings** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
    *   **Numerical:** All continuous features (including engineered ones) are projected into distinct tokens using **Linear Feature Tokenization** ($x \cdot \mathbf{w} + \mathbf{b}$), preserving magnitude information (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).

2.  **Stream 1: The Deep Denoising Expert (Transformer):**
    *   **Purpose:** To capture complex, non-linear cross-feature interactions and the joint data manifold.
    *   **Input:** A Unified Sequence of `[CLS]`, `Numerical Tokens`, and `Sequence Embeddings` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Structure:**
        *   **Positional Encodings:** Added to sequence tokens to preserve order (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
        *   **Masking:** We apply **Embedding-Level Masking** (replacing vectors with a learnable `[MASK]` token) to 15% of inputs. This is superior to input-level mean imputation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
        *   **Backbone:** A stack of **Transformer Encoder Layers** utilizing **GEGLU** feed-forward blocks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029) and instantiated via `nn.ModuleList` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
        *   **Readout:** We extract the **[CLS] token**, which aggregates global context better than pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Auxiliary Heads:** Two reconstruction heads are attached to the feature tokens to predict the original values of **both** numerical (MSE) and sequence (Cross-Entropy) inputs. This corrects the failure of Idea 11 by ensuring the deep branch receives dense supervision from all modalities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).

3.  **Stream 2: The Wide Anchoring Expert (MLP):**
    *   **Purpose:** To capture simple main effects and enforce positional rigidity for the short sequence.
    *   **Input:** A concatenation of **Raw Standardized Numerical Features** and **Flattened Sequence Embeddings**.
    *   **Why:** Flattening sequence embeddings is superior to Transformers for modeling fixed-position logic in short sequences (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009). This branch receives **clean (unmasked) data** to serve as a stable anchor for the classification task.
    *   **Structure:** A Residual MLP block (Linear $\to$ BN $\to$ GELU $\to$ Linear) projecting to the fusion dimension.

4.  **Fusion Head:**
    *   The `[CLS]` vector (from Stream 1) and the Residual vector (from Stream 2) are concatenated.
    *   The combined vector is passed to a final **High-Capacity MLP** to predict the `target`.

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   We will explicitly calculate the **count of unique characters** (`unique_characters`) from `f_27`. This global statistic is a critical signal (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Dynamic Selection:** We will use `df.select_dtypes` to automatically capture all numerical columns (original + engineered) to ensure pipeline integrity and prevent silent feature dropping (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Numerical:** Standardized (Z-score) for the Wide branch and Linear Tokenizer.
    *   **Sequence:** Tokenized into integers for the Embedding layer.

# Training
1.  **Loss Function:**
    *   $$L = L_{BCE}(y, \hat{y}) + \lambda \cdot (L_{MSE}(x_{num}, \hat{x}_{num}) + L_{CE}(x_{seq}, \hat{x}_{seq}))$$
    *   **Label Smoothing (0.01)** is applied to the BCE loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   Crucially, the reconstruction loss applies to **masked tokens only** but covers **all modalities** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
2.  **Optimizer:** **AdamW** for all parameters.
3.  **Scheduler:** **OneCycleLR** with an extended duration (e.g., **35 epochs**) to stretch the annealing phase for optimal convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
4.  **Batch Size:** **512**. We explicitly avoid larger batch sizes (e.g., 2048) as they have been shown to degrade generalization in this high-precision regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).

# Evaluation
For the test set, **no masking** is applied. The clean data is passed through both the Deep (Transformer) and Wide (MLP) branches. The final prediction is generated from the fused output of the Classification Head. The Reconstruction Heads are ignored during inference.
