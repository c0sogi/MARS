My optimization strategy involves three key changes based on the provided lessons:
1.  **Disable Adversarial Training (FGM):** I will disable Fast Gradient Method (FGM) training. While FGM can improve robustness, it requires a significantly longer training horizon to converge effectively. Given the time constraints and the evidence that standard training achieved higher scores (0.6003) than under-converged adversarial training (0.5360), reverting to standard training is safer and more efficient (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
2.  **Remove Hard Negative Mining:** I will modify the data preparation to use purely Random Negative Sampling instead of prioritizing "boundary negatives" (Hard Negative Mining). In low-resource settings, hard negatives often introduce label noise (ambiguous contexts) that degrades performance compared to the cleaner signal from random negatives (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
3.  **Increase Training Epochs:** I will increase the training duration from 5 to 7 epochs. Differential Learning Rates (DLR) slow down the adaptation of the pre-trained backbone, requiring more epochs to reach optimal convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
