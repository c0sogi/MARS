Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **ResNet18-D Multi-Task U-Net with GeM Pooling**.

This strategy introduces two targeted structural innovations—**Deep Stem (ResNet-D)** and **Generalized Mean Pooling (GeM)**—to address the specific limitations of spatial downsampling and signal dilution, while strictly adhering to the lightweight backbone and robust training protocols that have empirically outperformed heavier, more complex architectures.

# Model
**Architecture:** **ResNet18-D U-Net with GeM Pooling**
*   **Backbone (Structural Innovation):** Replace the standard ResNet18 with **ResNet18-D** (via `timm`).
    *   *Rationale:* Standard ResNet18 uses a 7x7 stride-2 convolution in the input stem, which aggressively discards 75% of the spatial information immediately. ResNet18-D replaces this with three consecutive 3x3 convolutions. This "Deep Stem" preserves high-frequency spatial details critical for the segmentation of subtle opacities, addressing the "coarse mask" issues observed in previous experiments, without the optimization instability of heavier backbones like EfficientNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012, solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Classification Head (Structural Innovation):** Replace the standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) with **Generalized Mean Pooling (GeM)** attached to the bottleneck.
    *   *Rationale:* GAP dilutes the signal of focal "Typical" opacities by averaging them with the large background. Global Max Pooling captures focal points but ignores diffuse "Atypical" textures. GeM introduces a learnable parameter $p$ ($f = (\frac{1}{|X|}\sum x^p)^{1/p}$), allowing the model to dynamically tune its focus between focal and global features. This enhances the discriminative power of the head while respecting the "Shallow Head" constraint (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008) and avoiding the overfitting caused by multi-scale aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
*   **Decoder (Exploitation):** Retain the standard **U-Net Decoder** with symmetric, direct skip connections and **Bilinear Upsampling**.
    *   *Rationale:* Direct concatenation is strictly preferred over Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015) or FPN (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021) to preserve gradient flow for diffuse targets. Bilinear upsampling is preferred over PixelShuffle to avoid checkerboard artifacts and ensure spatial smoothness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force global context learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Consistency Constraint:** Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" that degraded performance in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **No MixUp:** Strictly avoid MixUp regularization, as it degrades dense prediction performance on short schedules (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).

# Training
**Strategy**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax). Since the study labels are mutually exclusive, Softmax provides a structural constraint that regularizes the model better than independent Sigmoids (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively. Avoid Dice Loss due to instability on empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** (1.0 for Classification, 10.0 for Segmentation). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the shared encoder to learn generalizable spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW.
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing** to dynamically adjust the learning rate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Batch Size & Scaling:** Maximize the batch size (e.g., 32) and strictly apply the **Linear Scaling Rule** to the learning rate to prevent iteration deficits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Duration:** Train for **20 epochs** to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on the **Composite Validation mAP** (averaging study and image metrics), mirroring the competition metric exactly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Prediction Formatting:** Output probabilities for **all 4 study classes** (not just ArgMax) to maximize the area under the precision-recall curve (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Apply horizontal flip TTA during inference and average the predictions to improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1". This couples the validation logic with the inference constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
