Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **DropBlock-Regularized ResNet34 Multi-Task U-Net**.

This strategy introduces a structural evolution by upgrading the backbone to ResNet34 (to address the capacity limitations of ResNet18) while integrating **DropBlock** (a structural regularization mechanism) to prevent the overfitting observed with heavier models. It strictly retains the "Standard Stem" and robust training protocols that have empirically outperformed more complex architectural variants.

# Model
**Architecture:** **ResNet34 U-Net with DropBlock Regularization**
*   **Backbone (Capacity & Robustness):** Upgrade the backbone to **ResNet34** (pre-trained on ImageNet).
    *   *Rationale:* Previous lessons established that **ResNet18** lacks the capacity to distinguish subtle "Atypical" textures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008), while **EfficientNet** and **ResNet18-D** failed due to optimization instability and the loss of the structural regularization provided by a coarse input stem (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012, solution_lesson_node_00041, solution_lesson_node_00050). ResNet34 provides the necessary depth for texture modeling while retaining the **Standard 7x7 Convolutional Stem**, which acts as a critical low-pass filter for medical image noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).
*   **Structural Innovation (Feature Regularization):** Integrate **DropBlock** layers into the 3rd and 4th residual groups of the encoder.
    *   *Rationale:* While **CoarseDropout** effectively regularizes the input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004), deep networks can still overfit by latching onto focal feature artifacts. DropBlock extends the Cutout concept to the feature maps, dropping contiguous spatial regions of activations. This forces the model to learn spatially distributed, redundant representations for the "amorphous" opacities, acting as a "Contextual Booster" without the signal loss of GeM (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046) or the optimization risks of Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Decoder (Exploitation):** Use the standard **U-Net Decoder** with **Bilinear Upsampling** and direct symmetric skip connections.
    *   *Rationale:* Direct concatenation is strictly preferred over FPN aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021) or Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015). Bilinear upsampling is chosen over PixelShuffle to avoid checkerboard artifacts and ensure the spatial smoothness required for mask generation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
*   **Classification Head (Exploitation):** Use **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** attached to the final bottleneck, followed by a **Single Linear Layer**.
    *   *Rationale:* GAP is superior to GeM for diffuse opacity detection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046). The head is restricted to the deepest layer to prevent overfitting from low-level features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035) and kept shallow to prevent task decoupling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force global context learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Consistency Constraint:** Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" that degraded performance in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

# Training
**Strategy**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax) to enforce the mutual exclusivity of the study labels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively. Strictly avoid Dice Loss due to its instability on empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** (1.0 for Classification, 10.0 for Segmentation). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the shared encoder to learn generalizable spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW.
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing** to dynamically adjust the learning rate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Conservative Learning Rate:** Initialize with a conservative base learning rate (e.g., `1e-4`) and strictly apply the **Linear Scaling Rule** if batch size increases. This prevents the catastrophic overfitting observed when aggressively fine-tuning pre-trained backbones on small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041, solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Duration:** Train for **20 epochs** to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on the **Composite Validation mAP** (averaging study and image metrics), mirroring the competition metric exactly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Prediction Formatting:** Output probabilities for **all 4 study classes** (not just ArgMax) to maximize the area under the precision-recall curve (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Apply horizontal flip TTA during inference and average the predictions to improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1". This couples the validation logic with the inference constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
