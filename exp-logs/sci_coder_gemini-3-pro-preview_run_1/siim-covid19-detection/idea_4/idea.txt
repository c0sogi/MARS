Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Constrained-Head EfficientNet-B4 U-Net with Deep Supervision**.

This strategy addresses the failure of previous heavier models (Idea 3) by strictly constraining the classification head's capacity and enforcing stronger gradient flow through the segmentation path. It combines the high-capacity feature extraction of EfficientNet with the robust training protocols established in the ResNet18 baseline.

# Model
**Architecture:** **EfficientNet-B4 U-Net with Deep Supervision**
*   **Backbone (Encoder):** Upgrade to **EfficientNet-B4** (pre-trained on ImageNet). This provides a deeper, more semantically rich feature hierarchy than ResNet18, capable of capturing the subtle texture differences required for "Atypical" vs. "Indeterminate" classification.
*   **Decoder:** A U-Net decoder with **Deep Supervision**. Instead of computing the segmentation loss only at the final output resolution, auxiliary segmentation heads will be attached to the decoder feature maps at 1/2 and 1/4 resolutions. This forces the deeper layers of the backbone to retain spatial information and improves gradient flow, preventing the "feature collapse" often seen when training deep backbones on limited data.
*   **Constrained Classification Head:** Crucially, the study-level classification head will be a **Single Linear Layer** attached to the Global Average Pooling of the bottleneck. I will explicitly **avoid** using an MLP (Dense-ReLU-Dropout-Dense) here. This constraint forces the shared encoder to learn robust, linearly separable features rather than allowing the head to memorize global labels, addressing the overfitting observed in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion regions (~10% of image size) to force global context learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004). Crucially, ensure that **mask_fill_value=0** is set for the segmentation targets. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" that degraded performance in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

# Training
**Strategy:**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax) instead of BCE. Since the study labels (Negative, Typical, Indeterminate, Atypical) are mutually exclusive, Softmax provides a structural constraint that regularizes the model better than independent Sigmoids (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Weighted sum of **BCE** and **Dice Loss** to optimize both pixel fidelity and geometric overlap.
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a strict **1:10 weighting ratio** (1.0 for Classification, 10.0 for Segmentation). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the encoder to learn generalizable spatial features and preventing the classification head from dominating the gradient updates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Schedule:** Train for **20 epochs** to allow the heavier EfficientNet-B4 backbone sufficient time to converge, as shorter schedules have proven insufficient for maximizing backbone potential (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** The best model will be selected based on the **Validation mAP**, not the validation loss. This avoids the pitfall of "metric divergence" where pixel-level loss improves while object-level detection degrades (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Predict Study Class and Segmentation Mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1".
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the segmentation mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
