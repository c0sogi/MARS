Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Stochastic ResNet34 Multi-Task U-Net**.

This strategy introduces a structural evolution by upgrading the backbone to **ResNet34** to address the capacity limitations of ResNet18, while integrating **Stochastic Depth (DropPath)** to solve the overfitting and convergence issues that caused heavier backbones to fail in previous experiments. It strictly adheres to the robust "Standard Stem" and U-Net decoder protocols that have empirically outperformed more complex architectural variants.

# Model
**Architecture:** **ResNet34 U-Net with Stochastic Depth**
*   **Backbone (Capacity & Regularization):** Upgrade the backbone to **ResNet34** (pre-trained on ImageNet) and integrate **Stochastic Depth (DropPath)** into the residual blocks.
    *   *Rationale:* Previous lessons established a conflict: **ResNet18** lacks the capacity to distinguish subtle "Atypical" textures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008), but heavier backbones like **EfficientNet** and **ResNet34** (with DropBlock) failed due to overfitting or spatial signal destruction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012, solution_lesson_node_00052). **Stochastic Depth** randomly drops entire residual paths (layers) during training. Unlike DropBlock (which occludes spatial regions and destroys segmentation targets, Cite solution_lesson_node_00052), Stochastic Depth preserves spatial integrity while implicitly training an ensemble of shallower networks. This acts as a powerful structural regularizer, preventing the overfitting observed with heavier models while allowing the full depth (texture capacity) to be utilized during inference.
*   **Input Stem (Exploitation):** Retain the **Standard 7x7 Convolutional Stem**.
    *   *Rationale:* Strictly avoid "Deep Stems" (ResNet-D) or Anti-Aliased Downsampling. The standard stem acts as a necessary low-pass filter for medical image noise, whereas Deep Stems introduce high-frequency artifacts that degrade segmentation performance on diffuse opacities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050, solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Decoder (Exploitation):** Use the standard **U-Net Decoder** with **Bilinear Upsampling** and direct symmetric skip connections.
    *   *Rationale:* Direct concatenation is strictly preferred over FPN aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021) or Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015). Bilinear upsampling is chosen over PixelShuffle to avoid checkerboard artifacts and ensure spatial smoothness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039). Avoid Deep Supervision (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Classification Head (Exploitation):** Use **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** attached to the final bottleneck, followed by a **Single Linear Layer**.
    *   *Rationale:* GAP is superior to GeM for diffuse opacity detection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046). The head is restricted to the deepest layer to prevent overfitting from low-level features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035) and kept shallow to prevent task decoupling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force global context learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Consistency Constraint:** Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" that degraded performance in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Exclusion:** Strictly avoid MixUp regularization, as it degrades dense prediction performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).

# Training
**Strategy**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax) to enforce the mutual exclusivity of the study labels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively. Strictly avoid Dice Loss due to its instability on empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** (1.0 for Classification, 10.0 for Segmentation). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the shared encoder to learn generalizable spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW.
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing** to dynamically adjust the learning rate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Batch Size & Scaling:** Maximize the batch size (e.g., 32) and strictly apply the **Linear Scaling Rule** to the learning rate to prevent iteration deficits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Duration:** Train for **20-25 epochs**. The heavier backbone and Stochastic Depth regularization may require slightly more iterations to converge than ResNet18, but the extended schedule ensures the loss landscape is fully explored (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on the **Composite Validation mAP** (averaging study and image metrics), mirroring the competition metric exactly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Prediction Formatting:** Output probabilities for **all 4 study classes** (not just ArgMax) to maximize the area under the precision-recall curve (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Apply horizontal flip TTA during inference and average the predictions to improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1". This couples the validation logic with the inference constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
