Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Attention-Gated ResNet18 Multi-Task U-Net**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in the decoder (Attention Gates) to improve the localization of amorphous opacities while strictly adhering to the lightweight backbone constraint and robust training protocols identified in previous experiments.

### Model
**Architecture:** **Attention-Gated U-Net with ResNet18 Encoder**
*   **Backbone (Encoder):** Retain the **ResNet18** backbone. Previous experiments demonstrated that heavier backbones (EfficientNet-B3/B4) overfit the coarse bounding-box annotations, while ResNet18 acts as a necessary structural regularizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Decoder (Structural Innovation):** Replace the standard U-Net decoder with an **Attention-Gated Decoder**. In a standard U-Net, skip connections simply concatenate encoder features with decoder features. For this task, I will introduce **Attention Gates** at the skip connections. These gates use the coarser, semantic gating signal from the lower layer to suppress irrelevant background regions in the skip connection features before concatenation. This explicitly helps the model focus on the "amorphous" opacity shapes and ignore the noise from healthy lung tissue, addressing the localization challenges without increasing the backbone's capacity.
*   **Classification Head:** Use a **Single Linear Layer** attached to the Global Average Pooling of the bottleneck. I will strictly avoid MLP structures to prevent the head from decoupling from the encoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force global context learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004). Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" issues observed in Idea 3 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

### Training
**Strategy:**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax). Since the study labels are mutually exclusive (Negative, Typical, Indeterminate, Atypical), Softmax provides a better structural constraint than independent Sigmoids (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively. I will strictly avoid Dice Loss, as it proved unstable and detrimental due to the high prevalence of empty masks (negative cases) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** (1.0 for Classification, 10.0 for Segmentation). This forces the shared encoder to prioritize the dense, spatial features required for segmentation, which regularizes the classification task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Schedule:** Train for **20 epochs**. This extended schedule is necessary to allow the model to fully converge, as shorter runs (5-15 epochs) leave performance on the table (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on **Validation mAP**, not validation loss, to avoid the "metric divergence" trap (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For positive cases, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction.
