Based on the empirical lessons and previous iterations, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the baseline by upgrading the feature extractor to a more sophisticated architecture while strictly adhering to the data augmentation and training protocols proven to maximize performance.

# Model
**Architecture:** **Multi-Task U-Net with EfficientNet-B3 Encoder**
I propose replacing the ResNet18 backbone with an **EfficientNet-B3** encoder. While the ResNet18-based Multi-Task U-Net proved that segmentation is a viable proxy for detection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001), the "Atypical" and "Indeterminate" classes rely on subtle textural features that a lightweight ResNet may struggle to capture. EfficientNet-B3 utilizes MBConv blocks with Squeeze-and-Excitation optimization, providing a stronger "structural innovation" to capture these complex feature hierarchies without becoming computationally prohibitive on the A100 GPU.

-   **Encoder:** EfficientNet-B3 (pre-trained on ImageNet), removing the classification head to serve as the feature extractor.
-   **Decoder:** A standard U-Net decoder that aggregates features from the EfficientNet stages (via skip connections) to recover spatial resolution.
-   **Heads:**
    1.  **Classification Head:** A Global Average Pooling layer connected to the encoder's bottleneck, followed by a dense layer for the 4-class study prediction.
    2.  **Segmentation Head:** A final convolution on the decoder output to produce a pixel-wise opacity probability map.

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
-   **Resolution:** Resize all images to 512x512 pixels.
-   **CoarseDropout (Cutout):** Apply `CoarseDropout` with **large occlusion dimensions** (scaling the hole size relative to the image, e.g., ~10% of image size) to force the model to learn global context rather than local textures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
-   **Label Consistency:** Crucially, when applying CoarseDropout, ensure that the **ground truth segmentation mask is also zeroed out** in the occluded regions. This prevents the "label noise" issue where the model is penalized for not detecting opacities in blacked-out pixels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

# Training
**Strategy:**
-   **Hybrid Loss Function:** To address the divergence between the surrogate pixel loss and the object-level metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006), the segmentation loss will be a weighted sum of **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE)** and **Dice Loss**.
    -   $L_{seg} = L_{BCE} + L_{Dice}$
    -   BCE optimizes pixel-level probability fidelity.
    -   Dice Loss directly optimizes the Intersection over Union (IoU), ensuring that the model prioritizes the geometric overlap required for high mAP.
-   **Extended Convergence:** The model will be trained for **20 epochs** to ensure the heavier EfficientNet backbone fully converges, as previous experiments showed that shorter schedules (5-15 epochs) leave performance on the table (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
-   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Model selection will be strictly based on the validation **mAP**, not the validation loss, to avoid the pitfall of metric divergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
-   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate the study-level classification.
    2.  Generate the segmentation mask.
    3.  **Logical Gating:** If the study prediction is "Negative for Pneumonia" (with high confidence), override the image-level output to "none". Otherwise, threshold the mask and use **contour extraction** to generate bounding boxes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
