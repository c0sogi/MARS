The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` is caused by an excessively large `BATCH_SIZE` (2048) relative to the model's output dimensionality (Vocab Size: 60,000). As detailed in the error analysis, the final projection layer produces a tensor of shape `(Batch, Seq_Len, Vocab)`, which requires ~75GB of memory for the requested batch, far exceeding the A100's 40GB capacity.

To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to 256. This reduction accounts for the `B × L × V` expansion in the final layer, ensuring the logits and their gradients fit within VRAM even when batches contain longer sequences. This solution directly applies the knowledge from **Cite {debug_lesson_1}** regarding output tensor explosion in sequence tasks with large vocabularies. I will update both the default configuration in `library/config.py` and the override in `runfile.py`.
