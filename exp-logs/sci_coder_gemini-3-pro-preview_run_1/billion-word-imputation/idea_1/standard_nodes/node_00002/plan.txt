The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` is caused by the `BATCH_SIZE` of 256 being too large for the GPU memory, specifically due to the large size of the output logits tensor (Batch × Seq_Len × Vocab_Size) and its associated gradients during backpropagation. To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` from 256 to 64 in both the default configuration (`library/config.py`) and the execution script (`runfile.py`). This will significantly lower the memory requirements per step, preventing the OOM error.
