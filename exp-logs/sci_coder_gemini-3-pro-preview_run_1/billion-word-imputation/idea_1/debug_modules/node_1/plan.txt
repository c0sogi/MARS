The issue is located in the `__init__` method of the `InfillingDataset` class in `library/dataset.py`. The variable `self.ids` is reset to `None` immediately after the `_load_data()` method is called. Since `_load_data()` is responsible for populating `self.ids` when the split is "test", this reset effectively wipes out the loaded IDs, causing the subsequent check `if self.ids is None` to fail and raise a `RuntimeError`.

To fix this, I will move the initialization `self.ids = None` to before the call to `self._load_data()`. This ensures `self.ids` is properly initialized but allows `_load_data()` to populate it with the correct values without being overwritten.
