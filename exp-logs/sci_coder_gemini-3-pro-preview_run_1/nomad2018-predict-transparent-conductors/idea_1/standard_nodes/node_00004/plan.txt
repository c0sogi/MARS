To fix the `RuntimeError` caused by a dimension mismatch when concatenating atomic and lattice embeddings, I will modify `library/architecture.py`. The issue arises because `global_max_pool` and `global_mean_pool` infer the batch size from the `batch` index tensor, which may fail to account for empty graphs (graphs with zero atoms) if they occur at the end of a batch or if indices are missing. By explicitly passing `size=num_graphs` to the pooling functions, we ensure the output tensor always matches the expected batch size. Additionally, I will add a check to replace any extremely small values (resulting from max pooling over empty sets) with zero to prevent numerical instability.
