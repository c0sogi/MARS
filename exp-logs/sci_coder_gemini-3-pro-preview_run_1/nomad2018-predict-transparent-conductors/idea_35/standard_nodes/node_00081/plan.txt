The `TypeError` encountered during execution is caused by the inclusion of the `verbose` parameter in the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler initialization, which has been deprecated and removed in recent versions of PyTorch (Cite debug_lesson_1). To fix this, I will remove the `verbose` argument from the scheduler's constructor in both `runfile.py` and `library/train.py`.
