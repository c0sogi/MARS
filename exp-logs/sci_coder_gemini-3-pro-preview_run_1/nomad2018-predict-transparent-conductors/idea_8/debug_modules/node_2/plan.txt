The `KeyError: 'id'` in the trainer's prediction loop occurs because the `collate_materials` function in `library/data_loader.py` aggregates sample IDs under the key `"ids"`, while the `Trainer` expects the key `"id"` (matching the individual sample key). To fix this, I will rename the key in the `collate_materials` return dictionary from `"ids"` to `"id"`, ensuring consistency between the dataset items and the batched output as recommended in **Cite debug_lesson_4**.
