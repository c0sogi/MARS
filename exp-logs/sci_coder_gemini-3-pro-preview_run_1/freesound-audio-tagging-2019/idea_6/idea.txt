Based on the lessons learned from previous attempts, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a structural evolution by transitioning to a **ConvNeXt** backbone (a modern "pure CNN" architecture) and enhancing the aggregation mechanism to **Multi-Head Attention Pooling**, while retaining the successful data augmentation and regularization strategies identified in previous successful runs.

# Model
**Architecture: ConvNeXt-Nano with Multi-Head Attention Pooling**

*   **Backbone (Structural Innovation):** We will replace the EfficientNet backbone with **ConvNeXt-Nano** initialized with **ImageNet pretrained weights** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    *   *Rationale:* While EfficientNet-B3 was the "sweet spot" in terms of parameter count (~12M) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019), ConvNeXt (approx. 15M parameters for Nano) represents a fundamental architectural evolution. It incorporates design choices from Vision Transformers (large 7x7 kernels, Layer Normalization, GELU activations) while maintaining the inductive biases of a fully convolutional network. This larger receptive field is particularly beneficial for capturing the continuous spectral structures in audio spectrograms, potentially offering better feature extraction than the 3x3/5x5 kernels of EfficientNet.
*   **Input Adaptation:** We will employ **Input Repetition**, repeating the single-channel Log-Mel spectrogram three times to match the RGB input expected by the pretrained ConvNeXt backbone. This preserves the structural integrity of the pretrained filters, which is superior to modifying the first layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Normalization:** A **learnable Batch Normalization layer** will be inserted immediately before the backbone to dynamically adapt the statistics of the audio spectrograms to the distribution expected by the ImageNet weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Aggregation Head (Structural Innovation):** We will upgrade the standard Attention Pooling to **Multi-Head Attention Pooling**.
    *   *Rationale:* Standard Attention Pooling learns a single weight vector over time (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007). By using multiple parallel attention heads (e.g., 4), the model can learn distinct temporal attention masks. This allows it to simultaneously focus on disjoint sound events (e.g., a continuous "Rain" background and a transient "Door Slam") and aggregate them into a richer representation before classification.
*   **Regularization:** We will incorporate **Multi-Sample Dropout** in the classification head.
    *   *Rationale:* Retained from the "Idea 5" attempt, this technique creates an internal ensemble by applying multiple dropout masks and averaging the results. This improves generalization and stability for single-stage training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Spectrograms:** Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms with **128 Mel bins** to capture high-fidelity frequency details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Training Input:** Perform **random 5-second crops** on the audio data. This acts as implicit data augmentation and maximizes signal density (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001, Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

**Augmentation:**
*   **SpecAugment:** Apply time and frequency masking to force the model to learn robust spectral features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Continuous Mixup:** Apply Mixup augmentation to **100% of training batches** with $\alpha=0.4$. This strong regularization is essential for generalization and preventing memorization of the noisy labels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

# Training
*   **Strategy:** Perform **Single-Stage Training** on the combined dataset (Curated + Noisy).
    *   *Rationale:* Previous experiments showed that a robust single-stage model with aggressive augmentation outperforms complex multi-stage distillation pipelines when the teacher is trained on the same noisy data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Schedule:** Train for an **Extended Duration (e.g., 30 epochs)**.
    *   *Rationale:* The use of continuous Mixup (100%) and the shift to the ConvNeXt architecture creates a harder optimization task. A longer schedule is required to overcome the initial underfitting phase and reach the model's higher performance ceiling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014, Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Optimizer:** Use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing learning rate scheduler.
*   **Loss Function:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` for multi-label classification.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference Process:** Perform inference on the **full-length audio clips** (approx. 13s).
    *   *Rationale:* Since the model is fully convolutional with a global Multi-Head Attention Pooling layer, it can process variable-length inputs in a single forward pass. This ensures no sound events are missed due to cropping, while the attention mechanism automatically focuses on the relevant segments (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001, Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
