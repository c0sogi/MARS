# Anatomically-Anchored Weight-Inflated 2.5D Slab Network (AA-WIIS-Net)

This solution introduces a **Structural Innovation** in the data sampling paradigm, moving from "File-Index Heuristics" to **"Anatomically-Anchored Sampling"**. It combines this with the robust **Weight-Inflated Thick-Slab** architecture to solve the "Middle Slice Fallacy" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) and "Small Data" constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051) without the alignment errors of cross-modality projection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

### Model
*   **Backbone:** We utilize **EfficientNet-B0**. This architecture is retained for its parameter efficiency and proven stability on this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Input Structure:** The network accepts a **9-channel** input tensor $(224 \times 224 \times 9)$, representing a "Thick Slab" of multi-modal MRI data.
    *   Channels 0-2: **FLAIR** slab (3 consecutive slices: $z-1, z, z+1$).
    *   Channels 3-5: **T1wCE** slab (3 consecutive slices: $z-1, z, z+1$).
    *   Channels 6-8: **T2w** slab (3 consecutive slices: $z-1, z, z+1$).
*   **Initialization (Weight Inflation):**
    *   To utilize 9 channels without destroying ImageNet priors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) or using unstable learnable adapters (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025), we mathematically adapt the backbone's first convolutional layer.
    *   The original weights $W_{rgb}$ are expanded to $W_{new}$ by dividing the energy of the original RGB channels across the corresponding modality triplets (e.g., $W_{new}[:, 0:3, :, :] = W_{rgb}[:, 0, :, :] / 3$). This preserves the pretrained feature detectors while enabling immediate volumetric texture learning.

### Data
The innovation lies in the **Anatomically-Anchored Independent Sampling Pipeline**:

1.  **Independent Anatomical Anchoring:**
    *   Instead of relying on the "Median File Index" (which fails when scans are shifted/off-center, Cite solution_lesson_node_00002), we compute the **Brain ROI** for *each modality independently*.
    *   We scan the file list to identify the `start` and `end` indices of non-zero pixel data (actual brain tissue).
    *   We strictly perform this *per modality* to respect varying Fields-of-View, avoiding the misalignment caused by cross-modality projection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
2.  **Stratified Anatomical Expansion:**
    *   From the identified Brain ROI, we deterministically extract **3 Slabs** at relative anatomical depths: **40%, 50%, and 60%**.
    *   Each slab consists of 3 consecutive slices $[z-1, z, z+1]$.
    *   **Crucially**, we treat these 3 slabs as **independent training examples**, assigning the patient's global label to each. This triples the effective dataset size, providing the "Critical Mass" needed for generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051) and avoiding the signal dilution of Siamese pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
3.  **High-Fidelity Preprocessing:**
    *   **Precision:** All operations use **float32** to preserve the MRI dynamic range (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
    *   **Normalization:** We apply **Independent Channel Min-Max Scaling** to $[0, 1]$ *before* stacking. This prevents high-intensity modalities from suppressing low-intensity ones (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023). We avoid ImageNet mean/std normalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) with Logits.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a learning rate of $1e-4$.
*   **Regularization:**
    *   **Aggressive Weight Decay:** Set to **$1e-2$** to prevent overfitting on the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Dropout:** Enforce a rate of **0.3**. We prioritize Dropout over Label Smoothing to encourage robust feature redundancy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
    *   **Augmentation:** Apply **Elastic Transformations** and **Grid Distortions** to the 9-channel tensor. The same geometric transform is applied to all 9 channels simultaneously to preserve alignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Strategy:** **GroupKFold Cross-Validation** (5 folds) grouped by **Subject ID**. This ensures all anatomical slabs from a single patient reside in the same fold to prevent data leakage (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For each test subject, deterministically generate the same 3 Anatomically-Anchored Slabs (40%, 50%, 60% of Brain ROI).
*   **Consensus Aggregation:** Pass each 9-channel slab through the AA-WIIS-Net to obtain 3 probability scores. The final prediction is the **mean** of these scores. This acts as a deterministic ensemble, capturing volumetric context and providing robustness against positioning errors without the noise of stochastic sampling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
