# Tri-Stream Modality-Specific Volumetric Network (TMSV-Net)

This solution introduces a **Structural Innovation** in how 2D pretrained backbones are utilized for 3D medical data. Previous approaches naively mapped MRI modalities (FLAIR, T1wCE, T2w) to the R-G-B channels of a single network (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002). However, MRI modalities are semantically distinct physical measurements, unlike the highly correlated color channels of natural images. Conversely, spatially adjacent depth slices ($z-1, z, z+1$) exhibit high spatial correlation, mimicking the structure of natural RGB channels.

Therefore, this architecture **inverts the standard mapping**: it uses the "RGB" channels to encode **Local Volumetric Depth** (Slab) and uses separate **Parallel Network Streams** to handle distinct **Modalities**.

### Model
The architecture consists of **three parallel streams**, each dedicated to a single MRI modality, followed by a fusion head.
1.  **Backbones:** Three independent **EfficientNet-B0** encoders (initialized with ImageNet weights).
    *   **Stream 1 (FLAIR):** Processes a 3-channel "Slab" of FLAIR slices ($z-1, z, z+1$).
    *   **Stream 2 (T1wCE):** Processes a 3-channel "Slab" of T1wCE slices ($z-1, z, z+1$).
    *   **Stream 3 (T2w):** Processes a 3-channel "Slab" of T2w slices ($z-1, z, z+1$).
    *   *Rationale:* This mapping preserves the pretrained filters' ability to detect local gradients and edges (now representing volumetric texture) without forcing them to process uncorrelated multi-modal data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
2.  **Fusion Head:**
    *   The Global Average Pooling output from each stream (1280-dim) is concatenated (3840-dim).
    *   **Dropout:** A high dropout rate of **0.5** is applied to the fused vector to prevent co-adaptation of streams (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Classifier:** A final Dense layer outputs the probability of `MGMT_value`.

### Data
The pipeline employs **Brain-Centric Geometric Sampling** and **Deterministic Data Expansion** to address the "Middle Slice Fallacy" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) and "Small Data" constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

1.  **Brain-Centric ROI:** For each subject, we compute the bounding box of the non-zero pixel values to define the "Brain Volume," discarding empty neck/skull space (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
2.  **Deterministic Data Expansion (Training):** Instead of a single sample per patient, we extract **3 distinct samples** centered at **45%, 50%, and 55%** of the Brain Volume depth.
    *   Each sample consists of 3 "Slabs" (one per modality).
    *   Each "Slab" comprises 3 consecutive slices: $[d-1, d, d+1]$.
    *   These are treated as independent training examples, effectively tripling the dataset size while maintaining deterministic label assignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
3.  **High-Fidelity Preprocessing:**
    *   **Precision:** All operations use **float32**. We strictly avoid casting to `uint8` to preserve the MRI dynamic range (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
    *   **Normalization:** We apply **Independent Slab Normalization**. Each 3-channel slab is Min-Max scaled to $[0, 1]$ based on its own statistics. This prevents high-intensity modalities (like T2w) from suppressing low-intensity ones (like T1wCE) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023) and avoids domain-inappropriate ImageNet standardization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

### Training
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a learning rate of $1e-4$.
*   **Regularization:**
    *   **Weight Decay:** Set to **$1e-2$** (aggressive) to counteract the increased parameter count of the three-stream architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Augmentation:** Apply **Elastic Transformations** and **Grid Distortions** to the slabs. Crucially, the same geometric transformation is applied to all 3 streams simultaneously to preserve anatomical alignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Validation:** Employ **5-Fold Cross-Validation** grouped by Subject ID. We strictly evaluate on a locked hold-out fold to avoid the "Lucky Epoch" bias observed in single-split runs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For each test subject, we generate the same 3 samples (Slabs at 45%, 50%, 55% depth).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The subject's final prediction is the **mean** of the probabilities output by the model for these 3 spatial locations. This provides robustness against tumor positioning variability without the complexity of attention mechanisms.
