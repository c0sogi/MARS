# Verified Anatomically-Anchored (VAA) Network

This solution introduces a **Structural Innovation** in the data sampling and augmentation pipeline: **Independent Anatomical Anchoring**. It replaces the naive "Median File Index" heuristic (which is vulnerable to scan positioning variability) with a content-based Center-of-Mass selection. Crucially, it couples this with a **Spatially-Constrained Augmentation** strategy to preserve the strong spatial priors established by the anchoring, and enforces **Strict Pipeline Integrity** to prevent the silent failures that invalidated previous attempts.

### Model
*   **Architecture:** We utilize **EfficientNet-B0** initialized with ImageNet weights. This backbone is retained for its parameter efficiency and proven stability on this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Input Structure:** The network accepts a standard **3-channel** 2D input tensor $(224 \times 224 \times 3)$.
    *   **Channel 1:** **FLAIR** slice at the Anatomical Center.
    *   **Channel 2:** **T1wCE** slice at the Anatomical Center.
    *   **Channel 3:** **T2w** slice at the Anatomical Center.
    *   *Rationale:* We strictly adhere to **Early Fusion** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031) and avoid stacking volumetric depth into channels, as 2D kernels cannot model the spatial displacement of deep slabs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00106). By focusing on the single most informative anatomical plane, we avoid the signal dilution observed in Siamese/Multi-View architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Data
The pipeline employs **Independent Content-Based Anchoring** and **Strict Integrity Verification**.

1.  **Independent Anatomical Anchoring (The Innovation):**
    *   Instead of selecting the middle file index (which often captures neck or air), we calculate the **Z-axis Center of Mass (CoM)** of the brain tissue.
    *   For **each modality independently**, we load the volume, threshold pixels $>0$ to identify the Brain ROI, and select the slice index closest to the CoM of the non-zero pixels.
    *   *Benefit:* This ensures the input is always anchored to the biological center of the brain, implicitly handling differences in Field-of-View (FOV) and scan positioning without the misalignment errors caused by cross-modality geometric projection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
2.  **High-Fidelity Preprocessing:**
    *   **Precision:** Use **float32** to preserve the 12-16 bit dynamic range of the MRI sensors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
    *   **Normalization:** Apply **Independent Channel Min-Max Scaling** to $[0, 1]$ *before* stacking. This prevents high-intensity modalities (like T2w) from suppressing low-intensity ones (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023). We avoid ImageNet mean/std normalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
3.  **Integrity Check:** We implement a strict assertion that the loaded dataset length matches the expected subject count (~523) and that the CoM metadata is successfully computed for every sample. We explicitly **remove any legacy fallback logic** (e.g., defaulting to `len//2` on error) to prevent the silent pipeline failures identified in Lesson 92.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) with Logits.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a learning rate of $1e-4$.
*   **Regularization:**
    *   **Aggressive Weight Decay:** Set to **$1e-2$** to prevent overfitting on the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Dropout:** Enforce a rate of **0.3** in the classifier. We prioritize Dropout over Label Smoothing to encourage robust feature redundancy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
*   **Spatially-Constrained Augmentation:**
    *   We apply **Elastic Transformations**, **Grid Distortions**, **Rotations**, and **Flips**.
    *   **Strictly Exclude:** Random Translations (Shift) and Scaling.
    *   *Rationale:* Since we are using Anatomical Anchoring, the tumor is spatially localized relative to the brain center. Random translation augmentation destroys this strong spatial prior, forcing the model to relearn localization from scratch, which degrades performance in small-data regimes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065, solution_lesson_node_00102).
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Cross-Validation** grouped by Subject ID. This ensures the training set size (~418 subjects) remains above the critical mass required for generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For each test subject, calculate the Per-Modality CoM and deterministically generate the 3-channel input.
*   **Prediction:** Pass the image through the VAA network.
*   **Submission:** Average the probabilities from the 5 fold models. This leverages the robustness of the content-based anchoring without the need for stochastic test-time ensembles (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
