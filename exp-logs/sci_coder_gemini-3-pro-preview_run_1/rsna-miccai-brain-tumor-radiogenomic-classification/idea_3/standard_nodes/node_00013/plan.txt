To optimize the solution, I will implement a **Multi-Slice 2.5D** approach. Instead of relying on a single middle slice, which may miss the tumor center (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002), I will extract 3 adjacent slices (Middle-1, Middle, Middle+1) for each of the 3 modalities (FLAIR, T1wCE, T2w). I will maintain the aggressive regularization (Weight Decay 1e-2) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010) and default dropout settings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) found effective in previous iterations.
