However, given the high density of rows per play (approximately 20,000 rows/play due to the combinatorial nature of contact pairs), this sample size captures less than one full play. The subsequent logic requires at least 3 unique plays to split into training and validation sets, causing a `ValueError`.

To fix this, I will increase the `nrows` parameter for both the metadata and tracking data loads. Reading 100,000 rows of metadata will ensure we capture approximately 5 full plays, satisfying the requirement for the split. I will also increase the tracking data limit to 200,000 rows to ensure sufficient coverage for the selected plays.
