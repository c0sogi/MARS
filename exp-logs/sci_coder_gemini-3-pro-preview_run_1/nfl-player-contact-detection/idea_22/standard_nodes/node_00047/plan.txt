To resolve the `ValueError: Unknown label type: continuous` caused by training scikit-learn Classifiers on soft labels (probabilities from temporal smoothing), I will modify `library/models.py`. Specifically, I will replace `LGBMClassifier` and `XGBClassifier` with `LGBMRegressor` and `XGBRegressor`, respectively. When configured with binary objectives (which are already present in `Config`), these regressors accept continuous targets and output probabilities directly via `predict()`, bypassing the strict label validation of the classifier wrappers. I will also update the `predict_proba` methods to return the direct output of `predict()` instead of slicing class probabilities.
