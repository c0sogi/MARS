The issue is caused by passing a negative integer (`-1`) to the `verbose_eval` parameter in `xgb.train`. While LightGBM accepts negative integers to suppress logging, XGBoost strictly requires `verbose_eval` to be either a boolean (`False`) or a positive integer representing the logging period. Passing `-1` triggers an `AssertionError` in XGBoost's internal callback validation (Cite debug_lesson_3).

To fix this, I will modify the `XGBExpert.fit` method in `library/model_zoo.py` to sanitize the `verbose_eval` parameter. If the configured value is an integer less than or equal to 0, it will be converted to `False` before being passed to `xgb.train`.
