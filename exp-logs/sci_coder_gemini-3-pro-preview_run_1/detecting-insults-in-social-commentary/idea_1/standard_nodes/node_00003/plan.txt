The strategy involves two main optimizations:
1.  **Feature Engineering**: I will lower the `min_df` threshold from 3 (words) and 5 (chars) to 2 for both in the `DualTfidfVectorizer`. This change allows the model to incorporate rarer tokens and character patterns, which is crucial for detecting obfuscated insults (e.g., "id!ot" or typos). This directly applies the lesson on the robustness of Character N-grams (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) by increasing the resolution of the character-level features. I will also update the cache directory to ensure these new features are computed.
2.  **Model Architecture**: I will replace the standalone Logistic Regression with a **Voting Classifier** that ensembles **Logistic Regression** and a **Calibrated Linear SVM**. Linear SVMs are highly effective for high-dimensional sparse text data, and ensembling them with Logistic Regression typically yields a more robust decision boundary and higher AUC than either model alone.
