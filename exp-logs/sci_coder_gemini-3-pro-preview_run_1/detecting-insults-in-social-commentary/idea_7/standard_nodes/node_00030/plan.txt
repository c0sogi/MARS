The `ValueError: Input contains NaN` was caused by a gradient scaling mismatch in the training loop when combining Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP) with Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP). Specifically, `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` was being called prematurely inside the AWP block, and potentially redundantly, leading to incorrect gradient updates and exploding weights (Cite debug_lesson_6). Additionally, the gradient clipping threshold was set too high (1000), effectively disabling it.

To fix this:
1.  In `library/config.py`, I will reduce `MAX_GRAD_NORM` to `1.0` to enforce effective gradient clipping.
2.  In `library/model.py`, I will modify `train_one_epoch` to ensure `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` is called exactly once per step, specifically after the AWP backward pass and before gradient clipping. This ensures that the optimizer step receives correctly scaled gradients.
