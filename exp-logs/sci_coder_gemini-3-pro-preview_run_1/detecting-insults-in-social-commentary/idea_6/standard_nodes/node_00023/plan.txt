The strategy is to transition from a single-model approach with Domain Adaptation (MLM) to a **Stratified 5-Fold Ensemble** strategy, as empirical lessons indicate that ensembling offers superior robustness and performance for small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022). I will disable the MLM stage to save computational resources and instead allocate them to training five distinct models on stratified folds of the combined training and validation data. The final predictions will be an average of the 5 models, and the validation metric will be the OOF (Out-Of-Fold) AUC. I will also update the working directory to ensure a clean training run.
