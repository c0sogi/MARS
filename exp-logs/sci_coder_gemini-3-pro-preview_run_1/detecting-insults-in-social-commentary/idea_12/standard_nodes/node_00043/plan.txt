The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` originating from `torch.utils.checkpoint.py` indicates a conflict between the Gradient Checkpointing mechanism and the DeBERTa-v3 architecture (likely due to its specific attention implementation or relative position embeddings). As noted in `debug_lesson_9`, this is a known issue where the re-entrant backward pass of checkpointing fails to manage the computational graph correctly for this model. To resolve this, I will disable gradient checkpointing in `library/architecture.py`. This ensures training stability, and the available 40GB A100 GPU is sufficient to handle the model with the current batch size of 2 without this optimization.
