Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous attempts and the derived lessons, I propose the **Hybrid-Input SWA-ResNet Ensemble with Schedule-Mapped Full-Fit Training**.

This solution introduces two structural innovations. First, it implements **Input-Level Ensemble Diversity** by training models on complementary feature representations (Statistical vs. Physical) to capture different aspects of the SAR signal. Second, it employs a **Schedule Mapping Protocol** that translates the adaptive convergence trajectory found during cross-validation into a deterministic schedule for full-data training, resolving the conflict between calibration accuracy and optimizer stability identified in Lessons 00055 and 00090.

# Model
*   **Backbone Architecture:** We will utilize the **ResNet-18** architecture pretrained on ImageNet. We strictly adhere to the finding that shallow, dense convolutional networks are superior to deeper variants for this small, noisy dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Pooling Strategy:** We will use standard **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**. We explicitly reject Dual-Stream (Max+Avg) pooling, as Max Pooling amplifies speckle noise in SAR imagery (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Late Fusion:** We will retain the **Late Fusion** strategy. The scalar `inc_angle` will be normalized and concatenated with the 512-dimensional GAP feature vector (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Minimalist Classification Head:** The fused vector will be passed to a **Minimalist Head** consisting of Batch Normalization, **Dropout (rate 0.5)**, and a single Linear layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Hybrid Ensemble Structure (Structural Innovation):** The ensemble will consist of 5 models split into two variants to maximize diversity:
    *   **Variant A (Statistical - 3 Models):** Uses the standard composite input [Band 1, Band 2, Mean].
    *   **Variant B (Physical - 2 Models):** Uses a physically motivated composite [Band 1, Band 2, Difference]. The difference channel ($Band1_{dB} - Band2_{dB}$) represents the **Cross-Polarization Ratio**, a critical physical discriminator between volume scattering (ice) and surface scattering (ships/ocean) that is often obscured by simple averaging.

# Data
*   **Independent Band Normalization:** We will apply **Global Min-Max Normalization** to each channel independently using training set statistics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042, solution_lesson_node_00037).
    *   For Variant B, the Difference channel is computed from the raw dB values first, then normalized globally.
*   **Bicubic Upsampling:** The 75x75 images will be upsampled to **224x224** using **Bicubic Interpolation** to preserve high-frequency texture details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
*   **Augmentation:** We will employ a robust geometric augmentation pipeline:
    *   **Discrete:** `RandomRotate90` for cardinal invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Continuous:** `ShiftScaleRotate` ($\pm 20^{\circ}$) to smooth the decision boundary (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Flips:** Horizontal and Vertical Flips.
    *   We will **not** use Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030) or Pseudo-Labeling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00081).

# Training
We will implement a **Two-Phase Schedule-Mapped Protocol** to ensure optimal convergence on the full dataset.

**Phase 1: Calibration (Adaptive Trajectory Discovery)**
*   **Objective:** Determine the natural convergence epoch ($E_{conv}$) and the stable final learning rate ($LR_{final}$).
*   **Method:** Run **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** (`lr=2e-4`, `weight_decay=0.01`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077).
*   **Scheduler:** `ReduceLROnPlateau` with a **Gentle Decay Factor (0.5)** and **High Patience (10 epochs)**. This allows the model to find the optimal stopping point organically without premature freezing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00070, solution_lesson_node_00065).
*   **Selection:** Identify $E_{conv}$ as the epoch that minimizes the **TTA-Validation Loss** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

**Phase 2: Production (Schedule-Mapped Full-Fit)**
*   **Objective:** Train 5 independent models (3x Variant A, 2x Variant B) on **100% of the training data**.
*   **Schedule Mapping (Structural Innovation):** Instead of reusing the plateau scheduler (which fails without a validation set), we map the trajectory to a **Cosine Annealing Schedule**.
    *   Configure `CosineAnnealingLR` with $T_{max} = E_{conv}$, starting at `2e-4` and decaying to $LR_{final}$.
    *   **Rationale:** This forces the model to follow the exact convergence profile discovered in Phase 1 but in a deterministic manner suitable for full-data training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
*   **SWA Phase:** Immediately continue training for 12 additional epochs using **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** with a constant learning rate equal to $LR_{final}$. This ensures a smooth transition without the "shock" of hard resets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00090, solution_lesson_node_00049).
*   **Loss:** **BCEWithLogitsLoss** with **Label Smoothing** ($\epsilon=0.05$).

# Evaluation
*   **Exhaustive Closed-Group TTA:** For every test image, we will generate predictions using the **Klein Four-Group**: Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, and Rotate180. This ensures the prediction is mathematically invariant to cardinal symmetries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the TTA-averaged probabilities from all 5 models.
