To improve the solution's performance and robustness, I will implement a **5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensemble** strategy combined with the **SAM optimizer** and **GeM Pooling**.

**Rationale:**
1.  **Training Duration**: The previous run used 25 epochs, which is likely insufficient for full convergence on this dataset (Lesson 00095). I will increase `PHASE1_EPOCHS` to 50 to allow the model to reach a deeper minimum.
2.  **Ensembling**: Instead of relying on a single model or a full-retrain strategy (which risks overfitting or underfitting due to fixed epoch selection, Lesson 00041), I will train 5 independent models on stratified folds. Averaging their predictions (Ensembling) significantly reduces variance and improves generalization (Lesson 00023, Lesson 00049).
3.  **Architecture**: I will retain the **SAM + GeM Pooling** configuration, as it demonstrated superior single-model performance (0.1770) compared to standard training (Lesson 00093).
4.  **SWA**: Stochastic Weight Averaging will be applied to each fold's model to further flatten the loss landscape and improve robustness.

The `runfile.py` will be refactored to execute this 5-fold loop, accumulate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions for a reliable global validation metric, and generate the submission by averaging the test predictions from all 5 trained models.
