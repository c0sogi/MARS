The previous experiments demonstrated that ResNet-18 is more robust to the speckle noise inherent in this small radar dataset than EfficientNet, which relies on depthwise separable convolutions.

I will also rename the model class to `IcebergModel` to be generic and update the `DROPOUT_RATE` to `0.5`, which is more standard for ResNet architectures compared to EfficientNet. I will retain the **AdamW** optimizer with `WEIGHT_DECAY=0.01` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`) and the **Late Fusion** strategy (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00001`). Finally, I will restore the epoch count to 20 in the runfile to ensure full convergence.
