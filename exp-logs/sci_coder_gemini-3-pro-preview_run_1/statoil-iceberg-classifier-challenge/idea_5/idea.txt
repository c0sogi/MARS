# Proposed Solution: Stratified ResNet-18 Ensemble with Dual-Stream Pooling

This solution structurally evolves the successful ResNet-18 approach (Idea 2) by integrating it into a robust **Stratified K-Fold Ensemble** framework. It introduces a novel **Dual-Stream Pooling** mechanism to better capture radar signal characteristics and strictly adheres to the optimization protocols identified in the lessons to avoid the instability seen in previous ensemble attempts (Ideas 3 and 4).

## Model
*   **Backbone Architecture:** We will utilize the **ResNet-18** architecture pretrained on ImageNet. We explicitly reject deeper models (like ResNet-34) and depthwise-separable architectures (like EfficientNet) based on their proven instability and poor performance on this specific noisy, small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012, solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Dual-Stream Pooling (Structural Innovation):** Standard ResNet implementations use Global Average Pooling (GAP), which smooths out features. In SAR imagery, strong specular reflections (bright pixels) are critical indicators of hard targets like ships or icebergs. We will replace the final GAP layer with a **Dual-Stream Pooling** layer that computes both the **Global Average** and **Global Maximum** of the feature maps. These two vectors will be concatenated (resulting in a 1024-dimensional feature vector) to ensure the model retains both texture information and peak intensity signals.
*   **Late Fusion:** We will apply **Late Fusion** for the incidence angle. The scalar `inc_angle` will be normalized and concatenated with the Dual-Stream feature vector before passing to the classifier. This allows the model to calibrate the visual signals based on the viewing geometry (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Classification Head:** The fused vector will pass through a block consisting of Batch Normalization, **High Dropout (0.5)**, and a final Dense layer. The high dropout is retained to prevent overfitting during fine-tuning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

## Data
*   **Input Construction:** We will construct a **3-channel composite image** using Band 1 (HH), Band 2 (HV), and their arithmetic mean.
*   **Preprocessing:** The native 75x75 images will be **upsampled to 224x224** using bicubic interpolation to match the receptive field of the pretrained backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004). Pixel values will be Min-Max scaled.
*   **Augmentation:** We will employ **standard geometric augmentation** (Random Horizontal Flips, Random Vertical Flips, and Random Rotations). We prioritize these input-space transformations to teach spatial invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006), but we avoid combining them with excessive output regularization to prevent the over-constraining observed in Idea 4 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

## Training
*   **Pipeline Strategy:** We will implement **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation**. This ensures that our predictions are robust to data selection bias, addressing the variance issues seen in single-split models.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with a `weight_decay` of **0.01**. This corrects the under-regularization observed when using default Adam settings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Scheduling:** We will use a **ReduceLROnPlateau** scheduler with an **aggressive decay factor of 0.1** (reducing LR by 10x upon plateau) and a conservative base learning rate (e.g., `1e-4`). This ensures the model settles into sharp local minima, avoiding the oscillation caused by gentle decay schedules (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011, solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Loss Function:** We will use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** with **Label Smoothing ($\epsilon=0.05$)**. This improves probability calibration for the Log Loss metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Checkpointing:** For each fold, we will save the model weights using `copy.deepcopy()` at the epoch with the lowest validation loss to ensure the restored state is immutable and correct (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

## Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test sample, we will predict on the original image and its horizontally/vertically flipped versions, averaging the results (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the TTA-averaged probabilities from all **5 independent models** trained in the cross-validation loop.
