Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the derived lessons, I propose the **Distilled Full-Data ResNet-18 with Continuous Rotation**.

This solution introduces a **Two-Stage Knowledge Distillation Pipeline** as the primary structural innovation. It directly addresses the "Data Scarcity vs. Ensembling" trade-off identified in **Lesson 00020**, which established that for this specific dataset, a single model trained on 100% of the data outperforms an ensemble where each member only sees 80%. By using a Stratified K-Fold ensemble as a "Teacher" to generate soft targets for a "Student" model trained on the full dataset, we combine the regularization benefits of ensembling with the data efficiency of full-set training.

# Model
*   **Backbone Architecture:** We will utilize the **ResNet-18** architecture pretrained on ImageNet. We strictly adhere to the finding that shallow, dense convolutional networks are superior to deeper variants (ResNet-34) or depthwise-separable architectures (EfficientNet) for small, noisy radar datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Pooling & Feature Extraction:** We will use standard **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**. We explicitly reject Dual-Stream (Max+Avg) pooling, as Max Pooling amplifies speckle noise in SAR imagery (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Late Fusion:** We will retain the **Late Fusion** strategy. The scalar `inc_angle` will be normalized and concatenated with the 512-dimensional GAP feature vector (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Minimalist Classification Head:** The fused vector will be passed to a **Minimalist Head** (Batch Normalization $\to$ Dropout 0.5 $\to$ Linear). We explicitly avoid complex MLP heads to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

# Data
*   **Global Normalization:** We will implement **Global Min-Max Scaling** using statistics from the entire training set. We explicitly reject per-sample normalization to preserve the absolute physical magnitude of the radar backscatter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Input Construction:** A **3-channel composite image** (Band 1, Band 2, Mean) will be constructed and upsampled to **224x224** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Continuous Geometric Augmentation:** We will apply **Continuous Rotation** ($\pm 20^{\circ}$) alongside discrete **RandomRotate90** and flips. This is critical for teaching the model true rotational invariance, which discrete transforms alone failed to achieve (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031, solution_lesson_node_00028). We will **not** use Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).

# Training
The training will proceed in two distinct stages:

**Stage 1: Teacher Ensemble (Stratified K-Fold)**
*   Train a **Stratified 5-Fold Ensemble** of ResNet-18 models.
*   **Objective:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with **Label Smoothing** ($\epsilon=0.05$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Optimization:** **AdamW** with `weight_decay=0.01` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and **ReduceLROnPlateau** (factor 0.1) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Checkpointing:** Use **TTA-Aware Checkpointing** (saving the model that minimizes validation loss *after* TTA) to ensure the saved weights are optimized for the inference strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Output:** Generate **Out-Of-Fold (OOF)** predictions for the entire training set. These serve as the "Soft Targets."

**Stage 2: Student Model (Full-Data Distillation)**
*   Initialize a fresh ResNet-18 model and train it on **100% of the training data**.
*   **Loss Function:** A composite Distillation Loss: $L = \alpha L_{BCE}(y_{true}, p) + (1-\alpha) L_{KL}(y_{soft}, p)$, where $y_{soft}$ are the OOF probabilities from Stage 1. This forces the student to learn the robust decision boundary of the ensemble while benefiting from the full dataset size (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Optimization:** Use the same robust AdamW + Scheduler settings as Stage 1.

# Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image, horizontal flips, and vertical flips using the **Stage 2 Student Model**. We explicitly use exhaustive TTA (all views) rather than a reduced subset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Submission:** The final submission is the TTA-averaged output of the single Student Model.
