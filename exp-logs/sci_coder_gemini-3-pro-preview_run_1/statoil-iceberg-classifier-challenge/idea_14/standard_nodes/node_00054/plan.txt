My strategy is to optimize the solution by reverting the detrimental semi-supervised learning components (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053) and focusing on a robust, supervised Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) ensemble (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).

Specific changes:
1.  **Configuration (`library/config.py`)**: 
    -   Remove Semi-Supervised Learning (SSL) hyperparameters.
    -   Adjust the SWA schedule to `SWA_START_EPOCH=20` and `TOTAL_EPOCHS=30`. This ensures sufficient gradient steps (approx. 960 steps) for convergence, aligning with the successful duration observed in previous experiments (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    -   Lower `LEARNING_RATE` from `1e-3` to `5e-4` to improve stability during fine-tuning, striking a balance between the conservative `1e-4` recommended in Lesson 00009 and the exploration needed for SWA.

2.  **Training Engine (`library/engine.py`)**: 
    -   Remove the Early Stopping trigger in the standard training phase. Previous logs showed early stopping prematurely terminating models (at epoch 15) before they could enter the SWA phase. By enforcing the fixed schedule, we ensure all ensemble members benefit from SWA (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).

3.  **Execution Script (`runfile.py`)**: 
    -   Remove the entire "Cycle 2" (Student training) and Pseudo-Labeling logic.
    -   Train a single ensemble of 5 models on the labeled training set.
    -   Use this ensemble for the final validation and submission.
