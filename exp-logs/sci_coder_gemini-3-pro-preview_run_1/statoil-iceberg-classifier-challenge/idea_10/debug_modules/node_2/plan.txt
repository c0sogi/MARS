The error `ValueError: Expected more than 1 value per channel when training` occurs because the training dataset size is not divisible by the batch size, leaving a final batch containing a single sample. Batch Normalization requires at least two samples to calculate variance, causing the training to crash on the last step of the epoch.

To fix this, I will modify `library/data.py` to set `drop_last=True` in the training `DataLoader` constructors. This ensures that any incomplete final batch is discarded, preventing single-sample batches from reaching the model during training (Cite debug_lesson_1).
