The execution failure is due to a breaking API change in `albumentations` version 2.0.8. The `RandomResizedCrop` transform now requires a `size` argument (a tuple of `(height, width)`) instead of separate `height` and `width` arguments, as indicated by the `ValidationError: ... size Field required`. This change typically applies to other resizing transforms in version 2.0+ as well.

To fix this, I will update `library/dataset.py` to replace `height=img_size, width=img_size` with `size=(img_size, img_size)` for both `A.RandomResizedCrop` and `A.Resize`.
