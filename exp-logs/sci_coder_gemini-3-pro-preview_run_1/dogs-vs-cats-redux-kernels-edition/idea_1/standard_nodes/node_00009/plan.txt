The `RuntimeError` occurs because `torch.nn.BCELoss` is being executed inside a `torch.cuda.amp.autocast` context in `library/engine.py`. PyTorch's mixed precision (AMP) implementation explicitly forbids `BCELoss` inside autocast regions due to numerical instability when computing logarithms on float16 probabilities. While `BCEWithLogitsLoss` is safe, the validation function manually computes probabilities for Test Time Augmentation (TTA) and then applies `BCELoss`.

To fix this, I will modify `library/engine.py` to move the `BCELoss` calculation outside the `autocast` block and explicitly cast the inputs to `float32`. This ensures the loss calculation happens in full precision, satisfying PyTorch's requirements.
