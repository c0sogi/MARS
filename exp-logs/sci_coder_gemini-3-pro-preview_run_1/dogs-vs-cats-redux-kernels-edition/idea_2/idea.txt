Based on the lessons learned, I propose a solution that structurally evolves from a single-model training loop to a **Cross-Validated Ensemble Pipeline**. This approach maximizes the utility of the available training data and robustness of predictions while adhering to the specific constraints identified in previous experiments.

# Model
I propose using an **EfficientNetV2-M (Medium)** architecture, which significantly increases model capacity compared to the previously tested B0, B1, and B2 variants. This decision leverages the finding that increasing capacity improves performance when paired with appropriate regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Structural Innovation (Ensembling):** Instead of training a single instance, the solution will implement a **5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensemble**. The training data will be split into 5 stratified folds. Five separate instances of the EfficientNetV2-M model will be trained, each using 4 folds for training and 1 for validation. This ensures that the final prediction for the test set is an average of 5 models that have collectively seen 100% of the training data, reducing variance and bias compared to a single hold-out split.
*   **Head Design:** Each model will retain the simple Global Average Pooling and Linear classification head structure, as complex heads are unnecessary for this domain (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** The input resolution will be increased to **384x384**. Previous experiments showed that increasing resolution (from 224 to 256) yielded performance gains (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003); 384 is the native resolution for the "M" variant and will allow the model to capture finer texture details (fur, whiskers).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ the **Heavy Augmentation** strategy identified as successful in previous steps, specifically utilizing `RandomResizedCrop`, `ShiftScaleRotate`, and `ColorJitter` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003). This strong data augmentation is critical to prevent the larger "M" model from overfitting.
*   **Regularization Constraints:** Crucially, I will **exclude Mixup and CutMix** augmentations. As observed, these techniques overly complicate the training signal for this high-overlap domain, delaying convergence and degrading performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

# Training
*   **Strategy:** The training will proceed in a loop over the 5 folds.
*   **Optimization:** Each fold will be trained using the **AdamW** optimizer with **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to manage the memory footprint of the larger backbone.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler will be used to ensure smooth convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Duration:** Training will be limited to a short duration (e.g., 3-5 epochs per fold) with strict early stopping. Since the domain overlap between ImageNet and the target is high, the feature extractors require minimal adjustment, and prolonged training risks overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

# Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** To further boost robustness without retraining, inference will be performed on both the original test image and a **horizontally flipped** version. The predictions for these two views will be averaged.
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the mean of the TTA-averaged probabilities from all 5 fold models. This smooths out individual model idiosyncrasies and provides a well-calibrated probability estimate.
