The `KeyError: np.int64(53222)` occurs because the `debug` mode in `get_dataloaders` naively slices the first 2000 training images and 500 validation images. Since the dataset has high cardinality (over 64,000 classes) and is shuffled, the validation slice contains species (`category_id`) that are not present in the training slice. Consequently, the `cat2idx` mapping (built only from the training slice in debug mode) fails when the validation dataloader attempts to look up a missing category.

To fix this, I will modify the `debug` logic in `library/dataset.py` to:
1.  Identify the top 50 most frequent categories in the training set.
2.  Filter both the training and validation dataframes to include only these categories.
3.  Slice the filtered dataframes to the desired debug sizes.

This ensures that the validation set is a strict subset of the training set's categories, preventing the `KeyError` while also providing a dense enough subset for meaningful debug training.
