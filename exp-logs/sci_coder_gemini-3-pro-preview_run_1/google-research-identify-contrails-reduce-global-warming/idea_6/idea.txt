# Model
**Architecture: U-Net++ with Dilated ResNet34 Encoder**
I propose replacing the standard U-Net architecture with **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net)**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Nested Skips):** Unlike the standard U-Net used in previous best attempts, U-Net++ employs dense, nested skip pathways with intermediate convolution blocks. These dense connections reduce the semantic gap between the encoder and decoder feature maps, allowing the optimizer to deal with an easier learning task. This is particularly effective for segmenting fine, sparse structures like contrails where the signal can be easily lost in long skip connections.
*   **Backbone:** We will use a **ResNet34 encoder pretrained on ImageNet**. This leverages robust, learned feature detectors to accelerate convergence compared to training from scratch. Cite solution_lesson_node_00014
*   **Resolution Preservation:** We will modify the ResNet34 backbone to have a **maximum downsampling factor of 16x** (Output Stride 16). We will achieve this by replacing the stride in the final encoder stage (`layer4`) with dilated convolutions. This prevents the thin spatial signal of contrails from being compressed into an unrecoverable 32x bottleneck. Cite solution_lesson_node_00015

# Data
**Preprocessing: High-Contrast Ash Composite**
We will utilize the physics-informed Ash composite but strictly adhere to the normalization bounds that maximize contrast.
*   **Input Representation:** 3-Channel Ash False-Color Composite.
    *   Red: Band 15 $-$ Band 14 (Optical Depth)
    *   Green: Band 14 $-$ Band 11 (Particle Phase)
    *   Blue: Band 14 (Temperature)
    Cite solution_lesson_node_00001
*   **Normalization:** We will apply **Standard Meteorological Normalization Bounds** (e.g., Blue/Temp channel: 243K to 303K; Red/Diff channel: -4K to 2K). We explicitly reject the "Wide" normalization bounds attempted in previous ideas. "Standard" bounds effectively saturate (clip) the background and extreme outliers, creating a high-contrast binary-like signal for the contrails, which is critical for model performance. Cite solution_lesson_node_00023
*   **Augmentation:** We will employ **only discrete geometric transformations** (Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, Random 90-degree Rotation). We will strictly exclude interpolation-based transforms (scaling, shifting, shearing) to prevent the "smearing" of single-pixel contrail features. Cite solution_lesson_node_00012

# Training
**Strategy: Metric-Driven Stability**
The training loop is designed to prioritize the minority class and ensure refinement based on the actual evaluation metric.
*   **Loss Function:** A weighted composite of **Focal Loss (0.75)** and **Dice Loss (0.25)**.
    *   **Focal Configuration:** We will set **$\alpha=0.75$** (or at least 0.5) to ensure the sparse positive class is heavily prioritized in the gradient, correcting the imbalance issue. Cite solution_lesson_node_00010
    *   **Stability:** The higher weight on Focal Loss provides stable pixel-wise gradients, preventing the instability associated with pure Dice optimization on sparse targets. Cite solution_lesson_node_00006
*   **Scheduler:** We will use `ReduceLROnPlateau` monitoring the **Validation Dice Score** (not Validation Loss). This ensures that the learning rate decay—and the subsequent fine-tuning phase—is triggered only when the segmentation quality actually stagnates. Cite solution_lesson_node_00018
*   **Duration:** The model will be trained for **30+ epochs** to allow sufficient time for the scheduler to decay the learning rate and fine-tune the weights. Cite solution_lesson_node_00002
