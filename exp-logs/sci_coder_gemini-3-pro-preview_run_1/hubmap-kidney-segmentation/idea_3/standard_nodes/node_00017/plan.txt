The `RuntimeError: Trying to resize storage that is not resizable` is caused by the DataLoader attempting to collate a batch containing tensors of different shapes. Specifically, the dataset contains a mix of 1-channel (grayscale) and 3-channel (RGB) images. When `HuBMAPDataset` loads a 1-channel image, it produces a tensor of shape `(1, H, W)`, while 3-channel images produce `(3, H, W)`. The default collate function fails when trying to stack these into a single batch tensor.

To fix this, I will modify `library/data.py` and `library/predict.py` to check the channel count of the loaded image tile immediately after reading it with `rasterio`. If the image has only 1 channel, I will replicate it 3 times to ensure a consistent `(3, H, W)` shape for all inputs. This ensures that the batch dimensions are uniform, resolving the collation error (Cite debug_lesson_1).
