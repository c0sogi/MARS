# Baseline Approach: LinkNet with ResNet-18 Encoder

## Model
I propose using a **LinkNet architecture** equipped with a **ResNet-18 backbone**. 
*   **Design**: LinkNet is a lightweight semantic segmentation architecture explicitly designed for efficiency. Unlike the U-Net (Model 1), which concatenates feature maps from the encoder to the decoder, LinkNet uses **element-wise addition**. This design choice significantly reduces the number of parameters in the decoder and speeds up the forward pass while preserving spatial information required for accurate boundary detection.
*   **Backbone**: The ResNet-18 encoder provides a robust, pre-trained feature extractor that is deeper and more powerful than simple custom CNNs but much faster and less memory-intensive than the EfficientNet (Model 1) or Transformer (Model 2) variants.

## Data
*   **Preprocessing (Tiling)**: The source images are too large to process directly. The images will be divided into fixed-size tiles (e.g., 1024x1024 pixels).
*   **Sampling Strategy**: Since glomeruli (FTUs) occupy a very small fraction of the total tissue area, a random sampling of tiles would lead to severe class imbalance. I will employ a **positive-oversampling strategy** for training:
    1.  Extract all tiles that contain at least one pixel of a glomerulus mask.
    2.  Randomly sample an equal number of "background-only" tiles to ensure the model learns to distinguish tissue background from FTUs.
*   **Normalization**: Pixel values will be normalized using standard ImageNet mean and standard deviation to align with the pre-trained backbone.

## Training
*   **Loss Function**: A composite **BCE-Dice Loss** will be used. Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) provides smooth gradients for pixel-level classification, while the Dice Loss directly optimizes the overlap metric, counteracting the issue where the background dominates the loss signal.
*   **Optimizer**: The **Adam** optimizer will be used for its adaptive learning rate capabilities, ensuring stable convergence without extensive tuning.
*   **Augmentation**: To prevent overfitting on the small number of training images (10 WSIs), simple geometric augmentations (horizontal/vertical flips and 90-degree rotations) will be applied on the fly during training.

## Evaluation
*   **Inference**: The model will process test images using a **sliding window approach** with a fixed overlap (e.g., 25%) to mitigate edge artifacts at tile boundaries.
*   **Reconstruction**: Predictions from overlapping windows will be averaged (or max-voted) to reconstruct the full-size probability map.
*   **Post-processing**: A global threshold of 0.5 will be applied to the probability map to generate the binary mask. Finally, the mask will be flattened into the required Run-Length Encoding (RLE) format for submission.
