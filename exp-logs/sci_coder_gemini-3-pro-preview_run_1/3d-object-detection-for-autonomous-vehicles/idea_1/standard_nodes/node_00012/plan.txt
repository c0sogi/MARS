Based on the provided lessons, I will implement the following optimizations to improve the 3D object detection model:

1.  **Equalize Regression Weights (Cite Lesson 7)**: In `library/config.py`, I will increase the loss weights for bounding box dimensions (`WH_WEIGHT`), Z-coordinate (`Z_WEIGHT`), and rotation (`ROT_WEIGHT`) from `0.1` to `1.0`. This forces the model to prioritize geometric precision, which is critical for the 3D IoU metric.
2.  **Optimize Architecture (Cite Lesson 8)**: In `library/model.py`, I will remove the "Super-Resolution Refinement" block (`sr_up` and `sr_refine`). Processing features at a higher resolution (stride 2) than the output (stride 4) was found to be computationally wasteful and detrimental to performance.
3.  **Extend Training Schedule (Cite Lesson 9)**: In `library/config.py`, I will increase `NUM_EPOCHS` to 25 and `PATIENCE` to 5. Regression tasks require more iterations to converge than classification, and the previous schedule was too short. I will also remove the epoch override in `runfile.py`.
4.  **Ensure Reproducibility (Cite Lesson 10)**: In `library/train.py`, I will implement a `worker_init_fn` for the PyTorch DataLoader to ensure that data augmentation randomness is deterministic across worker processes.
