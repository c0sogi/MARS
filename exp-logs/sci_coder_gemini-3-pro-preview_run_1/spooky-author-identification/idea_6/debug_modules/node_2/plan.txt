The `AssertionError` in `runfile.py` occurs because the `oof_preds` array is not being populated for some folds, leaving rows of zeros that fail the `sum(axis=1) == 1.0` check. This happens because the validation metric (`score`) becomes `NaN` due to numerical instability in the DeBERTa-v3 model when trained with Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) on small batch sizes. Consequently, the condition `if score < best_loss:` evaluates to `False` (since `NaN < inf` is False), causing the OOF update block to be skipped entirely (Cite debug_lesson_5). To fix this, I will disable AMP in `library/transformer_expert.py` by removing `GradScaler` and `autocast`, reverting to standard FP32 training to ensure numerical stability.
