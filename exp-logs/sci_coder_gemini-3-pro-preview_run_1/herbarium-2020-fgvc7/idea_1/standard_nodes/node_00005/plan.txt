**Optimization Strategy:**
1.  **Upgrade Backbone**: I will replace `resnet18` with `resnet50` in `library/model.py`. I will also use the improved `IMAGENET1K_V2` pre-trained weights for better initialization. The dynamic adjustment of the final fully connected layer in the existing code will automatically handle the increased feature dimension (2048 vs 512).
2.  **Hyperparameter Tuning**: In `library/config.py`, I will:
    -   Increase `NUM_EPOCHS` from 4 to 5 to allow the larger model to converge, estimating a total runtime of ~3 hours.
    -   Decrease `BATCH_SIZE` from 256 to 128 to ensure training stability and fit comfortably within GPU memory with the larger model.
3.  **Update Submission Threshold**: In `runfile.py`, I will update the submission condition to generate predictions only if the validation F1 score exceeds the previous best of `0.4585`.
