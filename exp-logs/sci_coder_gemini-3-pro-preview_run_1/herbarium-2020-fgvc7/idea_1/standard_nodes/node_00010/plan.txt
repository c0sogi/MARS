2.  **Extended Training**: Increase `NUM_EPOCHS` from 5 to 6. With the faster epoch time from the larger batch size, we can afford an extra epoch to ensure convergence, which is necessary to compensate for the reduced number of gradient updates per epoch (due to the larger batch size) and the added regularization.
3.  **Regularization**:
    *   **Label Smoothing**: Add `label_smoothing=0.1` to the `CrossEntropyLoss`. This prevents the model from becoming over-confident on the training data and improves generalization on fine-grained classes.
    *   **Data Augmentation**: Enhance the training pipeline with `ShiftScaleRotate` and `RandomBrightnessContrast`. This introduces necessary invariance to geometric and lighting variations, helping the model generalize better to the test set.
