Given the goal to maximize performance within a reasonable timeframe, standard training is preferred here.

4.  **Increase Training Epochs**: I will increase the epoch count from 20 to 35 in `runfile.py`. With Mixup disabled and a constant learning rate, this extended budget will ensure the model fully converges to a sharp minimum.
