Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Disable Mixup/CutMix**: The previous solution used Mixup with a short training budget (20 epochs), which likely hindered convergence as noted in **Lesson 00011**. I will set `MIXUP_PROB` to 0.0 in `library/config.py` to allow the model to learn faster on the raw images.
2.  **Enhance Data Augmentation**: The rotation limit was restricted to 15 degrees. **Lesson 00016** highlights that reducing augmentation intensity (specifically rotation) degrades performance on small datasets. I will increase the rotation limit to 30 degrees in `library/dataset.py`.
3.  **Synchronize Learning Rate Scheduler**: The `runfile.py` overrides the epoch count to 20, but the scheduler in `library/train.py` was using `Config.T_0` (default 50). This mismatch prevents the learning rate from decaying properly. I will modify `library/train.py` to explicitly set `T_0` equal to `Config.EPOCHS`, ensuring the cycle completes within the training budget (**Lesson 00015**).
