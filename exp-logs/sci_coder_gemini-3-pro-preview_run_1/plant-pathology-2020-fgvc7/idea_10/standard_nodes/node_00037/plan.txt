To optimize the model's performance and exceed the validation threshold, I will implement a strategy focused on enhancing data augmentation and refining the optimization schedule, supported by the provided lessons.

1.  **Augmentation Strategy**: I will increase the rotation limit in the data augmentation pipeline from 15 degrees to 30 degrees. As noted in **Lesson 00030**, matching the augmentation strategy to the domain's physical invariances (apple leaves are rotationally invariant) is critical, and the previous reduction to 15 degrees likely degraded performance compared to the baseline.
2.  **Optimization Schedule**: I will switch the learning rate scheduler from `CosineAnnealingLR` to `CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts`. **Lesson 00003** highlights that this scheduler contributed to the superior performance of the ResNet34 baseline. I will ensure the cycle length (`T_0`) is synchronized with the total epochs (**Lesson 00015**).
3.  **Extended Training Budget**: I will increase the calibration epochs from 15 to 25. Stronger augmentations act as regularization and typically require more training steps to converge (**Lesson 00011**). Given the available runtime (4 hours) and the previous run's short duration (11 minutes), this increase is well within the computational budget.
