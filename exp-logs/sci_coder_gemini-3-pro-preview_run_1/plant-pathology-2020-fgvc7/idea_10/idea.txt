Based on the detailed analysis of previous attempts and the lessons learned, I propose a solution titled **"Proxy-Validated Full-Data Training"**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in the training workflow: separating the **Hyperparameter Calibration Phase** from the **Production Training Phase**. This approach resolves the critical conflict between the need to maximize training data on this small dataset (Lesson 25) and the need for reliable validation to prevent overfitting (Lesson 9, 31).

# Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is selected as the optimal backbone. It has consistently outperformed shallower (ResNet18) and deeper/complex (ResNeXt, EfficientNet) architectures on this specific dataset, offering the best balance of capacity and convergence speed (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003, solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer with 4 outputs. I will explicitly avoid complex heads like Multi-Sample Dropout or GeM Pooling, which were shown to cause underfitting due to over-regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).

# Data
*   **Resolution:** All images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels. This resolution ensures pipeline consistency between training and inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and avoids the batch size instability associated with higher resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a robust `albumentations` pipeline including **HorizontalFlip**, **ShiftScaleRotate**, and **RandomBrightnessContrast**.
    *   **Strategic Retention:** I will explicitly include **VerticalFlip**. Previous experiments demonstrated that omitting this augmentation on rotationally invariant data (leaves) significantly harms performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
    *   **Exclusion:** I will exclude Mixup/CutMix to avoid the "convergence tax" that previously led to underfitting within short training budgets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Dataset Usage (Structural Innovation):**
    *   **Phase 1 (Calibration):** Use standard Stratified K-Fold splits.
    *   **Phase 2 (Production):** Use **100% of the available training data**. This maximizes the discriminative signal, addressing the data scarcity issue where ensembling fragmented the dataset too much (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** I will use **Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss** with weights inversely proportional to class frequencies. This is essential to maximize the Macro-Averaged AUC by prioritizing the minority `multiple_diseases` class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer:** The **Adam** optimizer will be used.
*   **Workflow (Structural Innovation):**
    1.  **Calibration Phase:** Run a **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** for a fixed budget (e.g., 15 epochs). The purpose is *not* to save these models, but to calculate the average validation AUC across folds for each epoch. I will identify the **Global Optimal Epoch ($E_{opt}$)** where the mean validation AUC peaks. This provides a statistically robust stopping criterion without wasting data in the final model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
    2.  **Production Phase:** Re-initialize the model and train it on the **Full Dataset (100%)** for exactly **$E_{opt}$** epochs. This applies the "Early Stopping" logic derived from the proxy validation to the full-data model, preventing the overfitting risks of blind training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Scheduler:** I will use a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
    *   **Strategic Retention:** The cycle length (`T_max`) will be strictly synchronized with the number of epochs (set to 15 in Phase 1, and $E_{opt}$ in Phase 2). This ensures the learning rate decays fully to the fine-tuning range, correcting the synchronization errors that caused previous failures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the single model trained on the full dataset.
*   **TTA Exclusion:** I will **explicitly disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Previous experiments showed that TTA degraded performance compared to the baseline, likely by introducing uncertainty via unnatural views (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). By validating on single views in Phase 1 and predicting on single views in Phase 2, I ensure **Validation-Inference Parity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
