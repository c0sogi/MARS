Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose a solution titled **"Full-Coverage Stratified 5-Fold Ensemble with Soft-Target Optimization"**.

This strategy structurally evolves the pipeline by shifting from a **Fixed-Split Seed Ensemble** (which wastes 20% of the training data) to a **Stratified 5-Fold Ensemble** (which utilizes 100% of the data in the aggregate). It explicitly corrects the configuration errors—specifically regarding learning rate schedules, augmentation intensity, and loss formulation—that caused previous cross-validation attempts to underperform.

# Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is selected as the optimal architecture. It has consistently outperformed shallower (ResNet18) models due to better feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003) and deeper/complex models (ResNeXt, EfficientNet) which suffered from overfitting and optimization difficulties on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer. I will explicitly avoid complex heads like Multi-Sample Dropout or GeM Pooling, which were shown to cause underfitting due to over-regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Initialization Safeguard:** I will implement a mandatory **Initial Loss Test**. Before training begins, the loss of the first batch must be verified to be significantly lower than random guessing ($-\ln(1/4) \approx 1.38$). This prevents the "silent failure" of transfer learning observed when libraries fail to load cached weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).

# Data
*   **Resolution:** Images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels. This ensures pipeline consistency between training and inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and avoids the batch size instability associated with higher resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a robust `albumentations` pipeline.
    *   **Geometric Intensity (Structural Correction):** I will explicitly include **VerticalFlip** and **ShiftScaleRotate** with a wide rotation limit ($\pm 45^\circ$). Previous experiments demonstrated that omitting vertical flips or restricting rotations on rotationally invariant data (leaves) significantly reduces ensemble diversity and performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030, solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Exclusion:** I will exclude Mixup/CutMix to avoid the "convergence tax" that previously led to underfitting within short training budgets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Topology (Structural Innovation):** I will implement **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation**. Unlike the Fixed-Split Seed Averaging (Idea 16) which leaves 20% of the data unseen, this approach ensures that every image in the dataset is used for training in 4 out of 5 models. This maximizes the total information content captured by the ensemble (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025), while maintaining a validation set size (~20%) large enough to provide a stable signal for Early Stopping, avoiding the instability of 10-Fold CV (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).

# Training
*   **Loss Function (Structural Innovation):** I will use **Soft-Target Cross-Entropy Loss**. Instead of converting the one-hot encoded targets into hard integer indices (via `argmax`), I will pass the floating-point probabilities directly to the loss function. This preserves the subtle uncertainty in the labels and prevents the models from becoming overconfident ("peaky"), which is critical for maximizing the gain from ensembling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00067). I will also apply class weighting to prioritize the minority `multiple_diseases` class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer:** The **Adam** optimizer will be used.
*   **Scheduler:** I will use **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Synchronization:** The cycle length (`T_0`) will be **strictly synchronized** with the total number of epochs (e.g., 20). This ensures the learning rate decays fully to the fine-tuning range, correcting the mismatch that caused previous CV attempts to under-converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Checkpointing:** I will implement **Metric-Based Early Stopping**. The model weights for each fold will be saved *only* when the validation AUC improves, ensuring we use the peak performance model rather than the final epoch's state (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

# Evaluation
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probability outputs from all **5 Fold Models**. This combines the data diversity of Cross-Validation with the initialization diversity of Seed Averaging.
*   **TTA Exclusion:** I will **explicitly disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Previous experiments showed that TTA degraded performance compared to the baseline, likely by introducing uncertainty via unnatural views (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). By validating on single views and predicting on single views, I ensure **Validation-Inference Parity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
