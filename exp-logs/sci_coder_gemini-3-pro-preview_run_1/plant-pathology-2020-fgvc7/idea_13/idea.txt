Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose a solution titled **"Calibrated Full-Data Seed Ensemble with Discriminative Fine-Tuning"**.

This strategy structurally evolves the pipeline by resolving the conflict between data scarcity (Lesson 25) and the need for rigorous validation (Lesson 9). It introduces **Discriminative Fine-Tuning** to stabilize transfer learning and employs a **Two-Phase Calibration-Production Workflow** to maximize data utility without sacrificing stopping precision.

### Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights, specifically using the `torchvision` library to ensure reliable weight loading.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is selected as the optimal backbone. It has consistently outperformed both shallower (ResNet18) and deeper/complex (ResNeXt, EfficientNet) architectures on this specific dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003, solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer. I will explicitly avoid complex heads like Multi-Sample Dropout or GeM Pooling, which were shown to cause underfitting due to over-regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Initialization Verification (Strategic Retention):** I will implement an **Initial Loss Test** at the start of training. If the initial loss is close to $-\ln(1/4) \approx 1.38$, the run will abort, ensuring we do not train from scratch due to library caching issues (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).

### Data
*   **Resolution:** Images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels. This ensures pipeline consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and avoids the batch size instability associated with higher resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a robust `albumentations` pipeline including **ShiftScaleRotate** and **RandomBrightnessContrast**.
    *   **Strategic Retention:** I will explicitly include **VerticalFlip** and **HorizontalFlip**. Lesson 30 demonstrated that omitting vertical flips on rotationally invariant data (leaves) significantly harms performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
    *   **Exclusion:** I will exclude Mixup/CutMix to avoid the "convergence tax" that previously led to underfitting within short training budgets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Dataset Usage (Structural Innovation):** The final models will be trained on **100% of the available training data**, avoiding the performance degradation caused by fragmenting small datasets into folds (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

### Training
I will implement a **Discriminative Fine-Tuning** strategy within a **Two-Phase Workflow**:

**Technique: Discriminative Fine-Tuning (Structural Innovation)**
To address the stability issues observed in transfer learning (Lesson 43) and optimization (Lesson 5), I will apply different learning rates to the backbone and the head.
*   **Backbone LR:** `1e-4`. This conservative rate preserves the pre-trained feature extractors, preventing "catastrophic forgetting" of the ImageNet features which are critical for this small dataset.
*   **Head LR:** `1e-3`. This higher rate allows the new classification layer to converge rapidly.

**Phase 1: Proxy Calibration (5-Fold CV)**
*   **Purpose:** To statistically determine the **Global Optimal Epoch ($E_{opt}$)**.
*   **Procedure:** Run a Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation using the Discriminative Fine-Tuning strategy.
*   **Loss:** **Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss** with weights inversely proportional to class frequencies (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing** synchronized to the epoch budget (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Outcome:** Identify the epoch $E_{opt}$ where the mean validation AUC peaks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

**Phase 2: Production Training (Seed Ensemble)**
*   **Purpose:** To train the final models using the maximum available signal.
*   **Procedure:** Train **5 separate ResNet34 models** on **100% of the training data**.
*   **Diversity:** Each model will use a different random seed for initialization and data shuffling. This **Seed Ensembling** provides variance reduction similar to K-Fold but allows every model to see every training image (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Duration:** Train each model for exactly $E_{opt}$ epochs.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated by averaging the probability outputs of the 5 Seed-Ensembled models.
*   **TTA Exclusion:** I will **explicitly disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Previous experiments showed that TTA degraded performance compared to the baseline, likely by introducing uncertainty via unnatural views (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). By validating on single views in Phase 1 and predicting on single views in Phase 2, I ensure **Validation-Inference Parity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
