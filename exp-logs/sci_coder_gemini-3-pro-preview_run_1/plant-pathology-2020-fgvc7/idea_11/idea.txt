Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose a solution titled **"Full-Data Seed Ensembling with Proxy Calibration"**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation by decoupling the **Calibration Phase** (used to determine stopping criteria and inference strategies) from the **Production Phase** (used to train the final models). This approach directly resolves the conflict between the need for rigorous validation (Lesson 9) and the performance penalty incurred by fragmenting small datasets for ensembling (Lesson 25).

### Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is selected as the core architecture. It has consistently proven to be the most robust and efficient for this specific dataset, outperforming both shallower (ResNet18) and deeper/complex (ResNeXt, EfficientNet) alternatives (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003, solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer. I will explicitly avoid complex heads like Multi-Sample Dropout or GeM Pooling, which were shown to cause underfitting due to over-regularization on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Ensemble Topology (Structural Innovation):** Instead of a standard K-Fold Ensemble (where each model sees only 80% of the data), I will implement **Seed Ensembling**. The final submission will consist of $N$ distinct ResNet34 models, each trained on **100% of the training data** but initialized with different random seeds. This maximizes the discriminative signal available to every model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025) while still reducing variance through averaging.

### Data
*   **Resolution:** Images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels. This ensures pipeline consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and avoids the batch size instability associated with higher resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a robust `albumentations` pipeline including **ShiftScaleRotate** and **RandomBrightnessContrast** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Strategic Retention:** I will explicitly include **VerticalFlip** and **HorizontalFlip**. Lesson 30 demonstrated that omitting vertical flips on rotationally invariant data (leaves) significantly harms performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
    *   **Exclusion:** I will exclude Mixup/CutMix to avoid the "convergence tax" that previously led to underfitting within short training budgets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training
I will implement a two-stage training workflow:

**Phase 1: Proxy Calibration (5-Fold CV)**
*   **Purpose:** To statistically determine the **Global Optimal Epoch ($E_{opt}$)** and verify the efficacy of Test-Time Augmentation (TTA).
*   **Procedure:** Run a Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation.
*   **Loss:** **Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss** with weights inversely proportional to class frequencies to maximize Macro-AUC (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** Adam optimizer with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**. The cycle length will be strictly synchronized with the total epoch budget to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Outcome:** Calculate the mean validation AUC per epoch across all folds. Identify the epoch $E_{opt}$ where performance peaks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

**Phase 2: Production Training (Full-Data Seed Ensemble)**
*   **Purpose:** To train the final high-performance models using the maximum available data.
*   **Procedure:** Train 5 separate ResNet34 models on **100% of the training data**.
*   **Duration:** Train each model for exactly $E_{opt}$ epochs (derived from Phase 1). This applies the "Early Stopping" logic without needing a validation set during production training.
*   **Diversity:** Each model will use a different random seed for initialization and data shuffling, creating the necessary diversity for the ensemble.

### Evaluation
*   **Conditional TTA (Structural Innovation):** During Phase 1, I will evaluate the Out-of-Fold (OOF) predictions with and without Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal/Vertical flips).
    *   **Decision Rule:** I will apply TTA in the final submission *only if* it improves the metric in Phase 1. This prevents the performance degradation observed when blindly applying TTA (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) and ensures Validation-Inference Parity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Aggregation:** The final predictions will be the arithmetic mean of the probabilities output by the 5 Full-Data Seed models.
