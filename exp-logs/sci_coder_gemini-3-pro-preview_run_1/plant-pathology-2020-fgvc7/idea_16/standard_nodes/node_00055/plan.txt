I will optimize the solution by replacing the "Decoupled Calibration" strategy with a **Seed Averaging Ensemble with Early Stopping** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00032`, `solution_lesson_node_00054`). The previous approach of estimating an optimal epoch on a subset of data and applying it blindly to the full dataset led to under-training. Instead, I will train 5 independent models on the training set, using the validation set to monitor performance and save the best checkpoint for each seed. This ensures each model in the ensemble is optimally converged. Additionally, I will increase the rotation augmentation to +/- 45 degrees (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00030`) to better match the rotational invariance of leaf images, and strictly synchronize the scheduler's `T_0` parameter with `MAX_EPOCHS` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).
