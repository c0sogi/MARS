The issue identified is **Data Leakage**, where the hold-out validation set was merged with the training data during both the calibration and production phases. This allowed the model to train on data it would later be evaluated on, resulting in an invalidly high performance metric. To fix this, I will modify `library/data.py` to strictly isolate the hold-out validation set. Specifically, I will update `calculate_class_weights` and `get_loaders` to load only the training metadata (`train_metadata.csv`), ensuring the validation metadata (`val_metadata.csv`) is never seen by the model during training or calibration, adhering to **Cite debug_lesson_2**.
