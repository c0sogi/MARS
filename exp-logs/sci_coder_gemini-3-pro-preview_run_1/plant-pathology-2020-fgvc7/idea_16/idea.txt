Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose a solution titled **"Decoupled Calibration with Full-Data Seed Ensembling"**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** by bifurcating the pipeline into two distinct stages: a **Calibration Stage** (used solely to derive the optimal stopping epoch via Cross-Validation) and a **Production Stage** (used to train the final ensemble on 100% of the data). This approach resolves the fundamental conflict between the need for rigorous validation on small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) and the performance penalty incurred by fragmenting that data into folds (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

# Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is selected as the "sweet spot" architecture. It has consistently outperformed shallower (ResNet18) models due to better feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003) and deeper/complex models (ResNeXt, EfficientNet) which suffered from overfitting and optimization difficulties on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer. I will explicitly avoid complex heads like Multi-Sample Dropout or GeM Pooling, which were shown to cause underfitting due to over-regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Initialization Safeguard:** I will implement a mandatory **Initial Loss Test**. Before training begins, the loss of the first batch must be verified to be significantly lower than random guessing ($-\ln(1/4) \approx 1.38$). This prevents the "silent failure" of transfer learning observed when libraries fail to load cached weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).

# Data
*   **Resolution:** Images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels. This ensures pipeline consistency between training and inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and avoids the batch size instability and overfitting risks associated with higher resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a robust `albumentations` pipeline including **ShiftScaleRotate** and **RandomBrightnessContrast** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Strategic Retention:** I will explicitly include **VerticalFlip** and **HorizontalFlip**. Previous experiments demonstrated that omitting vertical flips on rotationally invariant data (leaves) significantly harms performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
    *   **Exclusion:** I will exclude Mixup/CutMix. These techniques impose a "convergence tax" that requires longer training schedules than available, previously leading to underfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Dataset Usage:** The Production Stage will utilize **100% of the available training data** (merging train and validation sets). This maximizes the discriminative signal, addressing the data scarcity issue where standard K-Fold ensembling fragmented the dataset too much (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

# Training
I will implement a **Two-Stage Decoupled Workflow**:

**Stage 1: Calibration (Meta-Parameter Extraction)**
*   **Purpose:** To statistically determine the **Global Optimal Epoch ($E_{opt}$)**.
*   **Procedure:** Run a Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation.
*   **Optimization:** Use the **Adam** optimizer with a **Uniform Learning Rate** (e.g., `1e-4`) across all layers. I will avoid Discriminative Learning Rates, which caused instability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
*   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**, strictly synchronizing the cycle length (`T_0`) with the total epoch budget to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Loss:** **Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss** with weights inversely proportional to class frequencies to maximize Macro-AUC (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Outcome:** Calculate the mean validation AUC per epoch across all folds. Identify the epoch $E_{opt}$ where performance peaks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031). Crucially, I will **not** gate the next stage based on this score (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).

**Stage 2: Production (Full-Data Seed Ensemble)**
*   **Purpose:** To train the final high-performance models using the maximum available data.
*   **Procedure:** Train **5 separate ResNet34 models** on **100% of the training data**.
*   **Diversity:** Each model will use a different random seed for initialization and data shuffling. This **Seed Ensembling** provides the variance reduction benefits of ensembling without the data fragmentation of K-Fold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Duration:** Train each model for exactly $E_{opt}$ epochs (derived from Stage 1). This applies the "Early Stopping" logic to the full-data models without requiring a validation set during production.

# Evaluation
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final predictions will be the arithmetic mean of the probability outputs from the 5 Full-Data Seed models.
*   **TTA Exclusion:** I will **explicitly disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Previous experiments showed that TTA degraded performance compared to the baseline, likely by introducing uncertainty via unnatural views (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). By validating on single views in Stage 1 and predicting on single views in Stage 2, I ensure **Validation-Inference Parity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
