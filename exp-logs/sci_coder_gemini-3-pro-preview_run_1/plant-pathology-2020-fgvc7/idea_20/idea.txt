Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose a solution titled **"Stratified Shuffle-Split Ensemble with Soft-Target Optimization"**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** in the ensemble topology. It evolves from the "Fixed-Split Seed Averaging" (which risks overfitting to a specific data subset) and "K-Fold Cross-Validation" (which fragments data into unstable validation sets, Cite solution_lesson_node_00058) to a **Stratified Shuffle-Split** approach. This ensures that every model trains on a large, data-rich subset (80%) while validating on a statistically stable hold-out (20%), with the ensemble as a whole covering the entire dataset variance.

# Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is selected as the optimal architecture. It has consistently outperformed shallower (ResNet18) models due to better feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003) and deeper/complex models (ResNeXt, EfficientNet) which suffered from overfitting and optimization difficulties on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer. I will explicitly avoid complex heads like Multi-Sample Dropout or GeM Pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Initialization Safeguard:** I will implement a mandatory **Initial Loss Test**. Before training begins, the loss of the first batch must be verified to be significantly lower than random guessing ($-\ln(1/4) \approx 1.38$). This prevents the "silent failure" of transfer learning observed when libraries fail to load cached weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).

# Data
*   **Resolution:** Images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels. This ensures pipeline consistency between training and inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and avoids the batch size instability associated with higher resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a robust `albumentations` pipeline to maximize ensemble diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Geometric:** **VerticalFlip**, **HorizontalFlip**, and **ShiftScaleRotate** with a wide rotation limit ($\pm 45^\circ$). Including vertical flips is critical for rotationally invariant data like leaves (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
    *   **Photometric:** **RandomBrightnessContrast**.
    *   **Exclusion:** I will exclude Mixup/CutMix to avoid the "convergence tax" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Topology (Structural Innovation):** I will implement a **Stratified Shuffle-Split Ensemble**.
    *   Instead of a single fixed split (Idea 16) or disjoint K-Folds (Idea 17), I will generate **5 independent Stratified Splits** (80% Train, 20% Validation).
    *   **Rationale:** This combines the benefits of **Seed Averaging** (large, stable validation sets for robust Early Stopping, Cite solution_lesson_node_00058) with the benefits of **Bagging** (diversity in training data, Cite solution_lesson_node_00063). Each model sees a different 80% of the data, ensuring the ensemble covers the entire dataset manifold without the fragmentation penalties of 10-Fold CV.

# Training
*   **Loss Function (Structural Innovation):** I will use **Weighted Soft-Target Cross-Entropy Loss**.
    *   **Soft Targets:** I will pass the one-hot encoded targets as **float tensors** (probabilities) to the loss function rather than converting them to hard integer indices. This preserves the subtle uncertainty in the labels and prevents the models from becoming overconfident ("peaky"), which is critical for maximizing the gain from ensembling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00067).
    *   **Weighting:** I will apply inverse class frequency weights to prioritize the minority `multiple_diseases` class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer:** The **Adam** optimizer will be used.
*   **Scheduler:** I will use **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Synchronization:** The cycle length (`T_0`) will be **strictly synchronized** with the total number of epochs (e.g., 15). This ensures the learning rate decays fully to the fine-tuning range (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Checkpointing:** I will implement **Metric-Based Early Stopping**. The model weights for each split will be saved *only* when the validation AUC improves (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

# Evaluation
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probability outputs from all **5 Shuffle-Split Models**.
*   **TTA Exclusion:** I will **explicitly disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Previous experiments showed that TTA degraded performance compared to the baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). By validating on single views and predicting on single views, I ensure **Validation-Inference Parity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
