The `RuntimeError` during initialization verification is caused by the high variance of logits produced by the `xavier_uniform_` initialization of the classification head when fed with features from the pretrained backbone. This leads to confident but random predictions that, combined with class weighting, result in an initial loss significantly higher than the expected random guessing baseline. To resolve this, I will modify the `AppleResNet34` class in `library/model.py` to initialize the weights of the final fully connected layer to zero. This forces the model to output uniform probabilities initially, ensuring the loss matches the theoretical baseline and passing the verification check (Cite debug_lesson_7).
