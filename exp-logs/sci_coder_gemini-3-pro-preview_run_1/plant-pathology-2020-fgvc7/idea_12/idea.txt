Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose a solution titled **"Full-Data Knowledge Distillation with Proxy Calibration"**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation by implementing a **Self-Distillation** pipeline. It resolves the conflict between the need for rigorous K-Fold validation (Lesson 9) and the performance penalty incurred by fragmenting the small dataset for ensembling (Lesson 25). Instead of submitting an ensemble of models trained on partial data, we will use the ensemble's "Out-of-Fold" (OOF) predictions to supervise a single "Student" model trained on 100% of the data.

### Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is selected as the core architecture. It has consistently outperformed both the shallower ResNet18 and the deeper/complex ResNeXt and EfficientNet architectures on this specific dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003, solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer. I will explicitly avoid complex heads like Multi-Sample Dropout or GeM Pooling, which were shown to cause underfitting due to over-regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Student-Teacher Topology (Structural Innovation):** The solution utilizes the exact same architecture for both the "Teacher" (the K-Fold models) and the "Student" (the final Full-Data model). This "Born-Again" distillation strategy allows the Student to absorb the generalization capability of the ensemble while benefiting from the full dataset.

### Data
*   **Resolution:** Images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels. This ensures pipeline consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and avoids the batch size instability associated with higher resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a robust `albumentations` pipeline including **ShiftScaleRotate** and **RandomBrightnessContrast**.
    *   **Strategic Retention:** I will explicitly include **VerticalFlip** and **HorizontalFlip**. Lesson 30 demonstrated that omitting vertical flips on rotationally invariant data (leaves) significantly harms performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
    *   **Exclusion:** I will exclude Mixup/CutMix to avoid the "convergence tax" that previously led to underfitting within short training budgets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training
I will implement a two-stage training workflow:

**Phase 1: Calibration & Teacher Generation (5-Fold CV)**
*   **Procedure:** Run a Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation.
*   **Loss:** **Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss** with weights inversely proportional to class frequencies (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** Adam optimizer with **Cosine Annealing**. The cycle length will be strictly synchronized with the total epoch budget (e.g., 15 epochs) to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Outputs:**
    1.  **Global Optimal Epoch ($E_{opt}$):** The epoch where the mean OOF AUC peaks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    2.  **OOF Logits:** The raw prediction logits for every image in the training set, generated when that image was in the validation fold. These serve as the "Soft Targets".

**Phase 2: Full-Data Distillation (Student Training)**
*   **Procedure:** Train a single ResNet34 model on **100% of the training data**.
*   **Duration:** Train for exactly $E_{opt}$ epochs.
*   **Loss Function (Structural Innovation):** The Student will be trained using a combined objective:
    $$L = \alpha \cdot \text{WeightedCE}(y_{true}, \hat{y}_{student}) + (1-\alpha) \cdot \text{KLDiv}(\sigma(z_{teacher}/\tau), \sigma(z_{student}/\tau))$$
    *   **Weighted Hard Loss:** Ensures the model prioritizes the minority class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    *   **Soft Distillation Loss:** Uses the OOF logits ($z_{teacher}$) from Phase 1. This transfers the "dark knowledge" (class relationships) learned by the ensemble to the single student model, acting as a powerful regularizer without the data fragmentation of ensembling.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the single **Student Model**.
*   **TTA Exclusion:** I will **explicitly disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Previous experiments showed that TTA degraded performance compared to the baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). By relying on the robust features learned via distillation, we avoid the risk of introducing variance through unnatural augmented views.
