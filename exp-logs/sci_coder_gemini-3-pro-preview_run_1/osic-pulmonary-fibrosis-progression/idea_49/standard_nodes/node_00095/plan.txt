The `RuntimeError` is caused by `pydicom` failing to decompress JPEG Lossless encoded DICOM files due to missing external dependencies (`gdcm`, `pylibjpeg`) in the environment. To resolve this, I will wrap the `pixel_array` access in a `try-except` block to gracefully skip slices that cannot be decoded (Cite `debug_lesson_2`). Additionally, I will handle the case where all slices for a patient fail by returning a blank placeholder and, crucially, saving this placeholder to the cache to prevent the DataLoader from repeatedly attempting the failing operation in subsequent epochs (Cite `debug_lesson_8`).
