To improve the model's performance and generalization on this small dataset (126 patients), I will implement **Data Augmentation** for the MIP images and increase **Regularization**.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Data Augmentation**: I will integrate `albumentations` into the `LungDataset`. Since the dataset is small, the model is prone to overfitting (as seen in the previous run where the best epoch was 1). Augmentations like rotation, flipping, and brightness contrast will artificially increase the diversity of the training data, encouraging the model to learn robust features rather than memorizing specific patient scans. This aligns with standard practices for medical image analysis on limited data.
2.  **Regularization**: I will increase the Dropout rate in the model's fusion head from 0.2 to 0.5. This stronger regularization will further prevent the model from relying too heavily on specific features.
3.  **Hyperparameters**: I will increase the number of epochs to 30 and the scheduler patience to 5. With augmentation, the model will likely take longer to converge but should reach a better optimum.
4.  **Submission Logic**: I will add the required conditional check to generate the submission file only if the validation metric improves upon the baseline (-7.4969).
