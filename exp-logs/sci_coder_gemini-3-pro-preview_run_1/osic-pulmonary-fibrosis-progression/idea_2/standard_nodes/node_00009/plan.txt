My optimization strategy focuses on improving feature extraction and training stability while adhering to the lessons learned about small-cohort convergence.

1.  **Backbone & Resolution Upgrade**: I will switch the backbone from `efficientnet_b0` to `efficientnet_b1` and increase the input resolution from 224 to 240. This provides a slight increase in model capacity and spatial resolution to better capture fibrosis patterns in the Tri-Slab MIPs (Cite Lesson 6).
2.  **Scheduler Alignment**: I will synchronize the Cosine Annealing `T_MAX` with the actual number of training epochs (10). In the previous solution, `T_MAX` was 30 while training ran for 10 epochs, preventing the learning rate from decaying fully and hindering convergence (Cite Lesson 8).
3.  **Gradient Clipping**: I will add gradient clipping to the training loop. The Laplace Log Likelihood loss contains exponential terms which can cause gradient spikes; clipping ensures stable updates.
4.  **Cache Refresh**: I will update the cache directory to `idea_3` to ensure the dataset is re-processed at the new 240px resolution.
