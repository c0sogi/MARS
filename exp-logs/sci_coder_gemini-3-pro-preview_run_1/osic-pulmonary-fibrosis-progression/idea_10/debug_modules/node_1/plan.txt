The `RuntimeError` is caused by variable-sized tensors in the `DataLoader` batch. Specifically, the Coronal view (`img_cor`) is generated by projecting the CT scan along the Y-axis, which results in an image where the height corresponds to the scan depth (number of slices). Since patients have different numbers of CT slices, `img_cor` has a variable height (e.g., 258 vs 52), causing `torch.stack` to fail when collating the batch.

To fix this, I will modify `library/data.py` to explicitly resize both the Axial and Coronal images to the fixed `Config.IMG_SIZE` (224x224) within the `__getitem__` method, ensuring all tensors have consistent dimensions before they are passed to the transforms and the `DataLoader`. This applies **debug_lesson_3** (Enforce Tensor Shapes Explicitly at the Dataset Boundary).
