### **Model: Dual-Axis Tri-Slab Network**

**Architecture Design**
This solution introduces a **multi-view fusion architecture** to resolve the limitations of single-view projections. While the previous "Tri-Slab" approach successfully encoded depth (Z-axis) into color channels Cite solution_lesson_node_00006, it still collapsed the spatial distribution of fibrosis along the Z-axis into a single pixel location, relying entirely on the channel dimension to represent "depth."

The new model consists of two parallel Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) branches (EfficientNet-B0 backbones) that process the CT scan from orthogonal perspectives:
1.  **Axial Branch (Channel-Encoded Depth):** Receives the **Axial Tri-Slab** input (as defined in Idea 2). Here, the Z-axis (patient height) is split into three chunks, and their MIPs are mapped to RGB channels. This view excels at visualizing the cross-sectional extent of scarring.
2.  **Coronal Branch (Spatial-Encoded Depth):** Receives a **Coronal Tri-Slab** input. To generate this, the CT volume is sliced along the Y-axis (Front-to-Back) into three sections. The MIPs are computed projecting through the Y-axis, resulting in images where the vertical pixel axis corresponds to the patient's Z-axis (height). This allows the CNN to explicitly learn spatial patterns of **basal vs. apical** fibrosis distribution (a key clinical indicator) using its standard spatial filters, rather than relying solely on channel correlations.

**Fusion & Prediction Head**
The feature vectors from both backbones are concatenated with the encoded tabular features (Age, Sex, SmokingStatus). The fused vector is fed into a parametric regression head that predicts three patient-specific scalars Cite solution_lesson_node_00004:
*   $\alpha$ (Decline Rate): The linear slope of FVC change.
*   $\sigma_{base}$ (Base Confidence): Uncertainty at the baseline visit.
*   $\sigma_{growth}$ (Uncertainty Growth): The rate at which confidence degrades over time.

### **Data: Preprocessing and Preparation**

**Dual-View Generation**
For each patient, the 3D CT volume is reconstructed from the DICOM slices.
1.  **Axial Tri-Slab:** The volume is divided along the Z-axis (Depth) into three equal overlapping slabs. The Maximum Intensity Projection (MIP) of each slab is computed to form the Red, Green, and Blue channels of the Axial input Cite solution_lesson_node_00003.
2.  **Coronal Tri-Slab:** The volume is divided along the Y-axis (Anterior-Posterior) into three equal slabs. The MIP is computed for each slab (projecting onto the X-Z plane). These are stacked to form the Coronal input.
3.  **Normalization:** Both images are resized to the native resolution of the backbone ($224 \times 224$) Cite solution_lesson_node_00009 and normalized. No intensity-based augmentations (e.g., brightness/contrast) are applied, preserving the physical Hounsfield Unit density signal Cite solution_lesson_node_00005.

### **Training: Procedure and Strategy**

*   **Augmentation:** Apply **Spatial Augmentation** (Horizontal Flip, slight Shift/Scale/Rotate) independently to both the Axial and Coronal views. This forces the model to learn anatomical invariance and regularizes the small dataset Cite solution_lesson_node_00007.
*   **Loss Function:** The model is trained end-to-end using the **Modified Laplace Log Likelihood** on the parametric trajectory:
    $$ \text{Loss} = \text{Metric}(\text{True\_FVC}, \text{Base\_FVC} + \alpha t, \sigma_{base} + \sigma_{growth}|t|) $$
*   **Optimization:** Use the AdamW optimizer with Cosine Annealing. Given the small cohort size (126 patients), training will use a strict **Early Stopping** protocol (monitoring validation metric) to prevent the overfitting observed in longer schedules Cite solution_lesson_node_00008.

### **Evaluation: Prediction Generation**

1.  **Inference:** For each test patient, generate both Axial and Coronal Tri-Slab images. Pass them through the dual-branch model to predict $\alpha$, $\sigma_{base}$, and $\sigma_{growth}$.
2.  **Trajectory Construction:** For every required `Patient_Week` in the submission:
    *   Calculate the time delta $t$ (Week - Baseline_Week).
    *   **FVC:** $\text{Baseline\_FVC} + \alpha \times t$
    *   **Confidence:** $\sigma_{base} + \sigma_{growth} \times |t|$
    *   Clip the confidence at 70 ml as per the metric definition.
