Based on the extensive analysis of previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **High-Fidelity Holistic Dual-Axis Network**, a structural evolution that synthesizes the most effective fusion paradigms while strictly enforcing "Isotropic Information Flow" to prevent the compression of high-bandwidth visual signals.

# Model: High-Fidelity Holistic Dual-Axis Network

**Architecture Design**
The model is designed to maintain the full dimensionality of the visual features throughout the fusion process, correcting the bottlenecks identified in previous iterations where visual features were compressed or discarded.

1.  **Independent Visual Backbones:**
    *   Two separate **EfficientNet-B0** backbones (initialized with ImageNet weights) process the orthogonal views.
    *   **Axial Branch:** Processes the Axial Tri-Slab input.
    *   **Coronal Branch:** Processes the Coronal Tri-Slab input.
    *   **Feature Extraction:** We apply **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** to the output feature maps of each backbone to obtain robust global descriptors $V_{ax}$ and $V_{cor}$. Crucially, we maintain the **native backbone dimensionality** (1280-dim) and do **not** project these down, avoiding the information loss observed in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
    *   *Rationale:* Independent backbones allow specialization for distinct anatomical views (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014), while GAP provides regularization suitable for small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).

2.  **Up-Projected Tabular Embedding:**
    *   A Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) encodes the clinical metadata (Age, Sex, SmokingStatus, and **Percent** - Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Structural Innovation:** Instead of compressing images to match the tabular data, we **project the tabular embedding UP** to match the visual dimensionality (1280-dim), creating vector $V_{tab}$. This ensures the fusion mechanism operates in the high-fidelity space of the CT scan features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).

3.  **Symmetric Attention with Holistic Readout:**
    *   **Tokenization:** We stack the vectors into a sequence: $[V_{ax}, V_{cor}, V_{tab}]$.
    *   **Fusion:** A **Multi-Head Self-Attention** layer processes this sequence, allowing symmetric contextualization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Holistic Readout:** Instead of selecting a single token (like the tabular token or a [CLS] token) which creates a bottleneck, we apply **Mean Pooling across the entire output sequence**. This ensures that the rich, high-dimensional visual residuals are preserved in the final representation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).

4.  **Prior-Preserving Skip Connection:**
    *   We concatenate the pooled fusion vector with the **original raw tabular features** (or a low-dim embedding) via a skip connection.
    *   *Rationale:* This guarantees the regression head has direct access to the strong scalar priors (Baseline FVC, Percent), preventing them from being "washed out" by the high-dimensional fusion process (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

5.  **Constrained Parametric Head:**
    *   The head predicts three scalars: $\alpha$ (Slope), $\sigma_{base}$ (Base Confidence), and $\sigma_{growth}$ (Uncertainty Growth) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    *   **Constraint:** We apply **Softplus** activation to the $\sigma$ outputs to strictly enforce positivity via architecture, avoiding "dead gradients" associated with loss-based clipping (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).

# Data: Fixed Overlapping Orthogonal Tri-Slabs

**Preprocessing**
1.  **Dual-View Generation:** For each patient, generate two inputs to capture 3D spatial heterogeneity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Axial Tri-Slab (RGB):** Split the CT volume along the Z-axis (Depth) into three slabs with **fixed boundaries** (0-33%, 33-66%, 66-100%) and **15% overlap**. Compute the Maximum Intensity Projection (MIP) of each slab and map to RGB channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006). We strictly avoid stochastic jitter to preserve semantic spatial consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   **Coronal Tri-Slab (RGB):** Split along the Y-axis (Anterior-Posterior) into three overlapping slabs, compute MIPs, and map to RGB.
2.  **Resolution:** Resize all images to **224x224** (native resolution) to strictly avoid overfitting caused by upscaling artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

**Augmentation**
1.  **Spatial Only:** Apply random horizontal flips, shifts, and slight rotations. This is critical for regularizing the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **No Intensity Changes:** Strictly **disable** brightness/contrast augmentations to preserve the physical Hounsfield Unit density signal (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

# Training: Robust Metric Optimization

**Loss Function**
*   **Metric:** Modified Laplace Log Likelihood.
*   **Robustness:** We explicitly **retain the error clipping** (thresholding absolute error at 1000 ml) within the loss calculation. This acts as a necessary filter for large outliers in the noisy medical data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **No Auxiliary Loss:** We optimize the metric directly to avoid interference with the probabilistic objective (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

**Strategy**
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with Cosine Annealing.
*   **Early Stopping:** Use a strict patience (e.g., 5-8 epochs). Training on this small cohort exhausts the generalizable signal rapidly, and we avoid weight averaging (SWA) which was shown to degrade performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00030).

# Evaluation: Parametric Inference

For every `Patient_Week` in the test set:
1.  Generate the Fixed Overlapping Axial and Coronal Tri-Slab images.
2.  Pass inputs and baseline metadata through the **High-Fidelity Holistic Network**.
3.  Obtain $\alpha, \sigma_{base}, \sigma_{growth}$.
4.  Calculate predictions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004):
    *   $$ \text{FVC} = \text{Baseline\_FVC} + \alpha \times (\text{Week} - \text{Baseline\_Week}) $$
    *   $$ \text{Confidence} = \sigma_{base} + \sigma_{growth} \times |\text{Week} - \text{Baseline\_Week}| $$
5.  Clip the Confidence at 70 ml as required by the metric.
