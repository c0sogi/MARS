I will implement the `runfile.py` script to execute the **Two-Stage Retrieve-and-Rank Pipeline**.

**Implementation Strategy:**

1.  **Data Loading**: I will utilize `get_time_split_data` to load the historical training data (`train_df`) and the hold-out validation set (`val_df`).
2.  **Dataset Construction (Stage 1 & 2)**: I will use `RankerDatasetBuilder` to construct the training dataset for the ranker. This involves fitting the retrieval model on `train_df`, generating candidates for users in `val_df`, and merging these candidates with user/item metadata and visual similarity features.
3.  **Model Training**: I will split the constructed dataset into training and validation subsets (80/20 split by customer) to train the `LGBMRanker` and monitor for overfitting.
4.  **Validation**: I will evaluate the trained ranker on the validation subset by predicting scores, selecting the top 12 items per user, and computing the **MAP@12** metric against the ground truth from `val_df`.
5.  **Failure Analysis**: I will compute the absolute error of the model's predictions on the validation set and calculate the Pearson correlation between this error and the input features to identify sources of poor performance.
6.  **Submission**: If the validation metric exceeds the specified threshold, I will retrain the retrieval model on the full history (training + validation), generate candidates for the test set, predict their rankings, and generate the `submission.csv` file.
