With 160 training samples, a batch size of 16, and 1 epoch, there are only 10 total steps. Given `pct_start=0.1`, the warmup phase is calculated to be extremely short (likely rounding to 0 or 1 step depending on implementation details), causing a division by zero when the scheduler attempts to compute the progress percentage within that phase.

To fix this, I will increase `Config.EPOCHS` from 1 to 5 in the `patch_config` function within `runfile.py`. This increases the total training steps to 50, ensuring the scheduler's warmup phase is sufficiently long to avoid the calculation error.
