To optimize the solution, I will apply the insights from **Lesson 00008** (Prioritize Data Throughput) and **Lesson 00007** (Cyclic Schedules for Time-Constrained Learning). The previous solution trained on a repeated subset of 800,000 images for 2 epochs (1.6M total samples seen). I will modify the strategy to train on a much larger subset of **3,200,000 unique images** for only **1 epoch**.

To maintain the same computational budget (number of optimizer steps) and ensure efficient GPU utilization on the A100, I will increase the `BATCH_SIZE` from 256 to **512**.
- **Baseline:** 800,000 samples / 256 batch $\times$ 2 epochs $\approx$ 6,250 steps.
- **New Strategy:** 3,200,000 samples / 512 batch $\times$ 1 epoch $\approx$ 6,250 steps.

This change ensures the model sees 4x more unique data points within the same training time, which is critical for high-cardinality classification tasks.
