# Optimized Strategy: ConvNeXt-Tiny with Full-Scale Data and Metadata Indexing

### Model
I propose replacing the ResNet backbone with **ConvNeXt-Tiny**.
*   **Structural Innovation:** Shift from the traditional ResNet architecture to **ConvNeXt**, a modern convolutional network design inspired by Vision Transformers. ConvNeXt-Tiny offers a superior accuracy-to-computation ratio compared to ResNet-50, effectively capturing more complex visual features and long-range dependencies without a significant increase in parameter count (~28M vs. ~25M) or training latency.
*   **Head:** The classification head will be a standard linear layer projecting the flattened features to the 5270 category logits.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing (Training):**
    *   **Full Dataset Utilization:** We will train on the **entire labeled dataset** (approximately 12 million images). As demonstrated previously, maximizing the volume of unique samples is the primary driver of performance in this high-cardinality task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Metadata Indexing:** We will continue to use the lightweight CSV metadata index to perform efficient random-access reads directly from the monolithic BSON file, ensuring the pipeline remains compute-bound (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    *   **Flattening:** Images will be treated as independent samples during training to maximize throughput (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Preprocessing (Evaluation):**
    *   Validation and Test loaders will group images by `product_id` to enable multi-view aggregation.

### Training
*   **Strategy:**
    *   **One-Cycle Policy:** We will utilize the **`OneCycleLR`** scheduler. This is critical for achieving convergence on the massive 12-million-image dataset within the 24-hour time limit (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Label Smoothing:** We will apply **Label Smoothing** (e.g., 0.1). With the full dataset available, the model will see sufficient examples to benefit from this regularization without underfitting, addressing the overconfidence issues seen in high-cardinality classification (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    *   **Precision:** Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) will be employed to maximize training speed on the A100 GPU.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will retain the **Late Fusion** strategy. The model will predict probabilities for every image associated with a product, and these vectors will be averaged to produce the final consensus prediction. This method effectively leverages the multi-view nature of the data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
