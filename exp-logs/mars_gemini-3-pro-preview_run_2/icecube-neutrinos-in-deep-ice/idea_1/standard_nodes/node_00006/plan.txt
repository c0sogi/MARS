The `pyarrow.lib.ArrowMemoryError` during the second epoch of training indicates that the system ran out of memory or memory became too fragmented to satisfy a re-allocation request. This was caused by **cache thrashing** in the `IceCubeDataset` when used with a `DataLoader`.

Although the metadata was sorted to ensure sequential access, the sparse sampling (200,000 events from ~600 files) results in approximately 333 events per file. With a `BATCH_SIZE` of 1024, a single batch spans roughly 3 to 4 different Parquet files. The previous `cache_limit=1` forced the dataset to constantly load and evict files *within the construction of a single batch*, causing massive memory churn (allocation/deallocation cycles) and fragmentation. With 4 workers operating simultaneously, this overwhelmed the memory allocator.

To fix this, I will increase the `cache_limit` in `runfile.py` from 1 to 4. This ensures that each worker can hold the entire "working set" of files (approx. 4) required to construct a batch in memory, eliminating the intra-batch thrashing. The estimated memory usage ($4 \text{ workers} \times 4 \text{ files} \times 1.5\text{ GB} \approx 24\text{ GB}$) is well within the 220 GB system limit.
