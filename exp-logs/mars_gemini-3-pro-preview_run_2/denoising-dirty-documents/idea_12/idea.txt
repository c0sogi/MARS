Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the specific nature of the task (denoising scanned text), I propose the **Coordinate Sub-Pixel ResUNet (CoSP-ResUNet)**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** by replacing the standard Transposed Convolution upsampling with **Sub-Pixel Convolution (PixelShuffle)** and implementing **Coordinate-Attended Skip Connections**. This addresses the "High-Frequency Reconstruction" challenge (Lesson 00020) and the "Noise Propagation" issue (Lesson 00030) while strictly adhering to the capacity and inductive bias protocols proven essential in Lessons 00021 and 00031.

### Model: Coordinate Sub-Pixel ResUNet (CoSP-ResUNet)
The architecture is a U-Net that fundamentally redesigns the decoder and skip pathways to maximize edge sharpness and minimize noise re-injection.

*   **Structural Innovation 1: Sub-Pixel Upsampling (PixelShuffle)**
    *   **Mechanism:** Instead of Transposed Convolutions (which can introduce checkerboard artifacts) or Bilinear Interpolation (which blurs edges), we will use **Sub-Pixel Convolutions**. This involves a standard convolution to expand the channel dimension to $C \times r^2$ (where $r$ is the upscaling factor, e.g., 2), followed by a **PixelShuffle** operation that rearranges elements from the channel dimension into the spatial dimensions ($H \times W \times C \cdot r^2 \rightarrow rH \times rW \times C$).
    *   **Rationale:** This technique, widely used in Super-Resolution, is mathematically more efficient and preserves high-frequency gradients better than Transposed Convolutions. It directly addresses the need for sharp text reconstruction identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**, without the blurring side effects of fixed interpolation.
*   **Structural Innovation 2: Coordinate-Attended Skip Connections**
    *   **Mechanism:** In standard U-Nets, noisy features from the encoder are directly concatenated with the decoder. Instead of using complex Attention Gates (which failed in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**), we will simply apply a **Coordinate Attention (CA)** module to the encoder features *before* they are concatenated.
    *   **Rationale:** This acts as a lightweight filter. The CA module uses its global vertical/horizontal pooling to identify and emphasize structural text features while suppressing random background noise in the skip connection (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**). This prevents the "re-injection" of noise into the decoder.
*   **Backbone:** We retain the **Residual Blocks** with **3x3 Convolutions**, **Batch Normalization**, and **SiLU** activations. This maintains the strong local inductive bias and convergence stability required for this task (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).
*   **Global Context:** We retain the **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module at the bottleneck to capture multi-scale context (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**).
*   **Capacity:** We strictly maintain a base filter count of **64** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00021**).
*   **Global Residual Learning:** The network is designed to predict the **noise residual** ($Clean = Input - Prediction$) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).

### Data: High-Density Contextual Processing
*   **Preprocessing:** Images are loaded in grayscale and normalized to [0, 1].
*   **Patch Size:** We will strictly use **128x128** patches. This ensures sufficient spatial context for the Coordinate Attention and ASPP modules (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
*   **Sampling Density:** We will employ **High-Density Sampling**, extracting **100 random patches per image per epoch**. This provides the rich gradient signal necessary to train the PixelShuffle layers and attention mechanisms (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply aggressive geometric augmentations (random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and discrete 90-degree rotations) to exploit the rotational symmetry of the noise (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).

### Training: Regularized Residual Optimization
*   **Objective:** Minimize **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** on the predicted noise residual. We avoid Deep Supervision to prevent optimization conflicts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with an aggressive **weight decay of 1e-2**. This strong regularization is essential to prevent overfitting given the high capacity of the model (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler will be used to gradually reduce the learning rate, ensuring convergence to a sharp minimum (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Duration:** We will train for **100 epochs**. We explicitly acknowledge the "convergence tax" of this sophisticated architecture (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).

### Evaluation: Robust Ensemble Inference
*   **Tiled Inference with Overlap:** Test images will be processed using a sliding window with **50% overlap**. Predictions in overlapping regions will be averaged to eliminate boundary artifacts and ensure every pixel is predicted with centered context (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image and its geometric transformations (flips/rotates) and average the results. This ensemble approach reduces variance and improves robustness (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).
