The `NameError` is caused by `library/model.py` referencing an undefined class `FlatCNN` (instead of the defined `UNet`) within its `train_model` and `predict_and_submit` functions. Furthermore, `library/model.py` contains an unconditional call to `run()` at the end of the file, which causes these broken functions to execute immediately when `runfile.py` imports `UNet`, leading to a crash. I will fix this by updating the class references to `UNet` and removing the unconditional execution logic, allowing `runfile.py` to control the training flow properly.
