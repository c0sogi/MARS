Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose a strategy that synthesizes the superior architectural topology of the **Nested U-Net (UNet++)** with the robust training regime and high capacity of the current best **ResUNet**.

This solution addresses the "Convergence Tax" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019) and "Capacity vs. Complexity" trade-off (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021) by combining the advanced architecture with the necessary computational budget and training duration.

### Model: Full-Capacity Deeply Supervised ResUNet++
I propose replacing the standard U-Net backbone with a **ResUNet++ (Nested U-Net)**. This architecture introduces nested, dense skip pathways that bridge the semantic gap between the encoder and decoder sub-networks, unlike the simple skip connections of the previous best model.

*   **Backbone:** The fundamental building blocks will remain **Residual Blocks** enhanced with **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** mechanisms and **SiLU** activations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Nested Skip Pathways:** We will implement dense convolutional blocks on the skip pathways. This ensures that the decoder receives features at varying semantic levels, facilitating easier gradient flow and more precise reconstruction of fine text details.
*   **Capacity Retention:** Crucially, we will **maintain the base filter count at 64**, rejecting the previous failed attempt to reduce width to 32 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021). We prioritize model width (capacity) to ensure the network has sufficient representational power to model high-frequency noise.
*   **Upsampling:** We will utilize **Transposed Convolutions** (Learnable Upsampling) rather than bilinear interpolation to preserve sharp text edges (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Global Residual Learning:** The network will predict the **noise residual** rather than the clean image (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

### Data: High-Density Patching with Symmetry
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be normalized to [0, 1].
*   **Patch Sampling:** We will employ a **High-Density Sampling Strategy**, extracting **100 random patches per image per epoch** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014). This ensures the complex ResUNet++ model receives a rich and varied gradient signal, which is necessary to overcome the "convergence tax" of its architectural complexity.
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply aggressive geometric augmentations, including random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and discrete 90-degree rotations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010), to exploit the rotational symmetry of the noise patterns.

### Training: Deep Supervision with Aggressive Regularization
*   **Deep Supervision:** We will employ **Deep Supervision**, computing the loss at multiple decoder levels (e.g., the output of the nested branches at different depths). The total loss will be a weighted sum of the MSE losses from these heads. This combats vanishing gradients in the deep encoder and forces intermediate layers to learn meaningful features.
*   **Loss Function:** Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the predicted residuals.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with an aggressive **weight decay of 1e-2** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015). This strong regularization is essential to prevent the high-capacity, complex architecture from overfitting the small dataset.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be used to gradually reduce the learning rate from a conservative maximum (e.g., 1e-4) to a fine-tuning minimum (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Duration:** We will train for **100 epochs** or more. We explicitly reject "fast baseline" approaches for this architecture, acknowledging that complex nested networks require more iterations to converge than standard U-Nets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Evaluation: Multi-Head Ensemble and Tiled TTA
*   **Tiled Inference:** Test images will be processed using overlapping tiles to prevent boundary artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will average predictions from the original image and its geometric transformations (flips/rotates) to reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Output Selection:** While Deep Supervision is used for training, for inference we will utilize the output from the final, deepest branch ($L_4$), which typically offers the highest restoration fidelity.
