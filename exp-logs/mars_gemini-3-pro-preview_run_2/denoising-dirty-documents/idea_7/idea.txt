Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and previous ideas, I propose the **Deeply Supervised Attention-Gated Coordinate ResUNet (DS-AG-CAC-ResUNet)**.

This strategy synthesizes the architectural strengths of **Coordinate Attention** and **Attention Gates** (from Idea 6 and Lesson 00026) with the robust training stability of **Deep Supervision** (from Idea 3), while strictly adhering to the capacity and data density protocols proven essential in Lessons 00014, 00019, and 00021.

### Model: Deeply Supervised Attention-Gated Coordinate ResUNet
I propose a unified architecture that addresses the receptive field, noise propagation, and convergence challenges simultaneously.

*   **Backbone:** The core structure is a **ResUNet** with a base capacity of **64 filters**. We explicitly retain this width to ensure sufficient representational power, avoiding the capacity bottleneck observed when narrowing the network (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Coordinate Attention (CA) Residual Blocks:** We replace standard Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks with **Coordinate Attention** modules within the encoder and decoder. Unlike global pooling, CA preserves vertical and horizontal positional information, which is critical for distinguishing structural text strokes from random noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
*   **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP):** An ASPP module is placed at the bottleneck to capture multi-scale context (fine noise vs. broad background) without losing resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
*   **Attention Gates (AGs):** We incorporate **Attention Gates** into the skip connections. These gates use the decoder's features to learn a soft mask, filtering the encoder's features before concatenation. This suppresses the "re-injection" of noise from the encoder while allowing structural information to pass through, refining the input for the decoder.
*   **Deep Supervision:** We introduce **Deep Supervision** by attaching auxiliary prediction heads to the output of each decoder level (e.g., at resolutions 1/8, 1/4, 1/2). This combats the vanishing gradient problem in deep networks and forces intermediate layers to learn semantically meaningful noise representations, which improves the effectiveness of the Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Upsampling:** We utilize **Transposed Convolutions** (Learnable Upsampling) to preserve sharp edges, rejecting fixed bilinear interpolation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Activation:** **SiLU (Swish)** is used throughout for smooth gradient propagation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

### Data: High-Density Contextual Sampling
*   **Patch Size:** We will strictly use **128x128** patches. This large context is non-negotiable for the Attention mechanisms and ASPP to function correctly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Sampling Density:** We will employ **High-Density Sampling**, extracting **100 random patches per image per epoch**. This ensures the complex model receives a sufficiently rich gradient signal to overcome the "convergence tax" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply aggressive geometric augmentations (random horizontal/vertical flips and discrete 90-degree rotations) to exploit the rotational symmetry of the noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training: Residual Optimization with Deep Supervision
*   **Objective:** The model will be trained to predict the **noise residual** ($Clean = Input - Prediction$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Function:** We will use a **Multi-Scale MSE Loss**, calculated as the weighted sum of Mean Squared Errors from the final output and all auxiliary Deep Supervision heads. This ensures gradients are injected directly into deeper layers.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with an aggressive **weight decay of 1e-2**. This strong regularization is required to prevent the high-capacity architecture from overfitting the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler will be used to gradually reduce the learning rate from 1e-4 to 1e-6 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Duration:** We will train for a minimum of **100 epochs**. We explicitly acknowledge the "convergence tax" of this complex architecture and reject "fast baseline" shortcuts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Evaluation: Robust Ensemble Inference
*   **Branch Selection:** For inference, we will discard the auxiliary heads and use only the output from the final, highest-resolution branch.
*   **Tiled Inference with Overlap:** Test images will be processed using a sliding window with **50% overlap**. Predictions in overlapping regions will be averaged to eliminate boundary artifacts and ensure every pixel is predicted with centered context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image and its 7 geometric transformations (flips/rotates) and average the results to maximize robustness and reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
