The `RuntimeError` is caused by a spatial dimension mismatch between the upsampled feature maps in the decoder and the corresponding skip connections from the encoder. This occurs because `MaxPool2d` rounds down odd dimensions during downsampling, while `ConvTranspose2d` doubles the dimensions during upsampling, resulting in a loss of 1 pixel for odd-sized inputs (Cite debug_lesson_5). To fix this, I will modify `library/model.py` to explicitly import `torch.nn.functional` and use `F.interpolate` in the `forward` method to resize the upsampled tensors to match the skip connection dimensions before concatenation at each decoder stage.
