Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Attention-Gated Context-Aware Coordinate ResUNet (AG-CAC-ResUNet)**.

This solution synthesizes the architecturally superior components identified in Lesson 00026 (Coordinate Attention, ASPP) with a new structural innovation (Attention Gates) to address the specific problem of noise propagation in U-Net skip connections, while strictly adhering to the capacity and training protocols proven necessary in Lessons 00019 and 00021.

### Model: Attention-Gated Context-Aware Coordinate ResUNet (AG-CAC-ResUNet)
I propose a structural evolution of the ResUNet that integrates **Coordinate Attention**, **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)**, and **Attention Gates**.

*   **Backbone:** The core architecture remains a U-Net with **Residual Blocks** to facilitate deep gradient flow. We explicitly retain the **64-filter base capacity** and **Transposed Convolutions** for upsampling, rejecting capacity reduction or fixed interpolation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Coordinate Attention (CA):** We will incorporate **Coordinate Attention** modules within the residual blocks, replacing Squeeze-and-Excitation. Unlike global pooling, CA aggregates features along the horizontal and vertical directions separately. This preserves the precise positional information required to distinguish fine text strokes from random noise specks, a capability proven to accelerate convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
*   **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP):** We will retain the **ASPP** module at the bottleneck. This captures multi-scale context (both fine noise and broad background texture) without losing resolution, addressing the receptive field limitations of standard CNNs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
*   **Structural Innovation: Attention Gates (AGs):** In a standard U-Net, skip connections directly concatenate encoder features (which contain high-frequency noise) with decoder features. I propose inserting **Attention Gates** into the skip connections.
    *   *Mechanism:* The AG uses the decoder's upsampled feature map as a gating signal to weigh the encoder's feature map. It learns a soft mask that highlights relevant structural features (text) and suppresses irrelevant background regions (noise) *before* the concatenation occurs. This prevents the "re-injection" of noise into the reconstruction path.
*   **Activation:** We will use **SiLU (Swish)** activations throughout, as they provide smoother gradient propagation than ReLU (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Global Residual Learning:** The network will predict the **noise residual** ($Clean = Input - Prediction$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

### Data: High-Density Contextual Patching
*   **Patch Size:** We will strictly use **128x128** patches to ensure sufficient spatial context for the Attention Gates and ASPP to function effectively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Sampling Density:** We will employ **High-Density Sampling**, extracting **100 random patches per image per epoch**. This ensures the model is exposed to a rich variety of noise patterns and text alignments, which is critical for training complex attention mechanisms (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply aggressive geometric augmentations (random horizontal/vertical flips and 90-degree rotations) to exploit the rotational symmetry of the noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training: Robust Optimization Regime
*   **Objective:** Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the predicted noise residual.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with an aggressive **weight decay of 1e-2**. This strong regularization is essential to prevent the increased parameter count of the AG-CAC-ResUNet from overfitting the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler will be used to gradually reduce the learning rate, ensuring convergence to a sharp minimum (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Duration:** We will train for **100 epochs**. We explicitly reject "fast baseline" approaches, acknowledging that architectural sophistication incurs a "convergence tax" and requires adequate training time to realize its benefits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Evaluation: Ensemble Inference
*   **Tiled Inference with Overlap:** Test images will be processed using a sliding window approach with significant overlap (e.g., 50%). Predictions in overlapping regions will be averaged to eliminate boundary artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image and its geometric transformations (flips/rotates) and average the results to reduce variance and improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
