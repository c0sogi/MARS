Based on the analysis of the provided lessons and the failure modes of previous high-complexity architectures (ConvNeXt, Deep Supervision), I propose the **Coordinate Res2Net U-Net (CoRes2Net-UNet)**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** by integrating **Res2Net** modules into the U-Net backbone. This addresses the critical trade-off between the need for a large receptive field (Lesson 00001, 00007) and the necessity of strong local inductive biases to prevent overfitting in low-data regimes (Lesson 00031).

### Model: Coordinate Res2Net U-Net (CoRes2Net-UNet)
The architecture is a U-Net that fundamentally redesigns the convolutional building blocks to achieve multi-scale feature extraction at a granular level, without the optimization risks of large kernels.

*   **Structural Innovation: Res2Net Blocks**: We will replace standard Residual Blocks with **Res2Net Blocks**.
    *   *Mechanism:* Inside each block, the feature maps are split into multiple groups (subsets). These groups are connected in a hierarchical residual style (e.g., the output of one 3x3 convolution is added to the input of the next).
    *   *Rationale:* This effectively creates a combination of receptive fields (e.g., 3x3, 5x5, 7x7, 9x9) within a single block using only standard 3x3 filters. This solves the **Receptive Field Limitation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) while retaining the **Local Inductive Bias** of 3x3 convolutions that was proven essential to prevent the overfitting seen in ConvNeXt (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Coordinate Attention (CA):** We will integrate a **Coordinate Attention** module at the end of each Res2Net block.
    *   *Rationale:* Unlike global pooling, CA aggregates features along vertical and horizontal directions separately. This allows the model to distinguish between random noise specks and the structural strokes of text (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
*   **Global Context:** We retain the **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module at the bottleneck to capture the broadest context (background vs. text block) without losing resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
*   **Upsampling:** We utilize **Transposed Convolutions** (Learnable Upsampling) in the decoder to ensure sharp edge reconstruction, explicitly rejecting fixed bilinear interpolation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Activations:** We replace standard ReLUs with **SiLU (Swish)** activations throughout the network to facilitate smoother gradient propagation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Capacity:** We strictly maintain a base filter count of **64** and use **Batch Normalization** to ensure convergence stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Global Residual Learning:** The network is designed to predict the **noise residual** ($Clean = Input - Prediction$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

### Data: High-Density Contextual Processing
*   **Preprocessing:** Images are loaded in grayscale and normalized to [0, 1].
*   **Patch Size:** We will strictly use **128x128** patches. We reject smaller patches to ensure the Res2Net's multi-scale receptive fields have sufficient spatial context to operate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Sampling Density:** We will employ **High-Density Sampling**, extracting **100 random patches per image per epoch**. This provides the rich gradient signal necessary to train the complex internal connectivity of the Res2Net blocks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply aggressive geometric augmentations (random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and discrete 90-degree rotations) to exploit the rotational symmetry of the noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training: Regularized Residual Optimization
*   **Objective:** The model will be trained to predict the **noise residual**. This simplifies the optimization landscape by focusing on the high-frequency artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Function:** Standard **Mean Squared Error (MSE)**. We explicitly reject Deep Supervision auxiliary heads to avoid the optimization conflicts and blurring observed in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with an aggressive **weight decay of 1e-2**. This strong regularization is crucial to prevent the increased parameter efficiency of Res2Net from overfitting the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler will be used to gradually reduce the learning rate, ensuring convergence to a sharp minimum (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Duration:** We will train for a minimum of **100 epochs** to pay the "convergence tax" required by the sophisticated architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Evaluation: Robust Ensemble Inference
*   **Tiled Inference with Overlap:** Test images will be processed using a sliding window with **50% overlap**. Predictions in overlapping regions will be averaged to eliminate boundary artifacts and ensure every pixel is predicted with centered context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image and its 7 geometric transformations (flips/rotates) and average the results. This ensemble approach reduces variance and improves robustness against random noise patterns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
