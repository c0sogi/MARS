The `TypeError` encountered during execution is caused by the inclusion of the `verbose=True` argument in the initialization of `ReduceLROnPlateau` in `library/train.py`. This argument was deprecated and subsequently removed in recent versions of PyTorch (2.2+), shifting the responsibility of logging to the user. To fix this, I will remove the `verbose` argument from the scheduler's constructor. Additionally, to preserve the intended functionality of tracking learning rate changes, I will add a manual log of the current learning rate within the training loop. Cite debug_lesson_1.
