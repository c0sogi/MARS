Based on the accumulated lessons and the available computational resources (24 hours, A100 GPU), I propose a **Heterogeneous High-Capacity Ensemble** strategy. This approach evolves the solution from a single-architecture baseline to a diverse ensemble that leverages complementary inductive biases (Convolutional vs. Transformer) while strictly adhering to the convergence and calibration principles established in previous experiments.

### Model
I propose constructing a heterogeneous ensemble consisting of two distinct high-capacity architectures: **ConvNeXt-Small** and **Swin-Small**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Heterogeneity):** Previous successful iterations relied on a single CNN architecture (`ConvNeXt-Tiny`). By combining **ConvNeXt** (which excels at local feature extraction via convolutions) and **Swin Transformer** (which captures long-range global dependencies via shifted-window attention), we create an ensemble where the errors are less correlated, leading to superior log loss reduction compared to homogeneous ensembles.
*   **Capacity Upgrade:** We will upgrade from the "Tiny" variants to "Small". This provides the necessary parameter depth to model complex features, addressing the capacity limitations observed in earlier ensemble attempts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.
*   **Feasibility:** We explicitly choose "Small" over "Base" or "Large" to ensure that we can train all ensemble members to full convergence within the 24-hour limit, avoiding the under-training pitfalls of larger models **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** All images will be resized to **224x224** pixels. We explicitly reject the 384x384 resolution used in Idea 4. Lesson 15 demonstrated that higher resolution drastically increases computational cost and hinders convergence within a fixed budget. Sticking to 224x224 allows us to allocate our compute towards more training epochs and ensemble members **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Splitting:** We will employ **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** for *each* architecture. This ensures that every training image is utilized for learning and provides robust out-of-fold predictions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly maintain the optimized augmentation pipeline:
    *   **RandomResizedCrop:** With a minimum scale of **0.8**. This prevents the semantic noise introduced by aggressive cropping (e.g., 0.5) while still forcing feature invariance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
    *   **Mixup:** Applied with `alpha=0.2` to enforce soft decision boundaries and probability calibration, which is critical for minimizing Log Loss **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
    *   **HorizontalFlip:** For basic geometric invariance.

### Training
*   **Strategy:** We will train a total of **10 models** (5 folds $\times$ 2 architectures).
*   **Optimization:** Each model will be trained using the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate** scheduler.
*   **Convergence & Duration:** Each fold will be trained for **20 epochs**. This extended duration (double the previous best) ensures that the larger "Small" models fully converge. We will effectively disable early stopping (setting patience to the full duration) to allow the scheduler to fully decay the learning rate and settle into a global minimum **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
*   **Checkpointing:** We will save the weights with the lowest validation log loss for each fold **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.

### Evaluation
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, each of the 10 models will predict probabilities for both the original and a horizontally flipped version. These two predictions will be averaged to reduce individual model variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final submission probability will be the arithmetic mean of the TTA-averaged predictions from all 10 models (5 ConvNeXt-Small + 5 Swin-Small). This double-averaging strategy maximizes stability and leverages the diversity of the heterogeneous architectures to minimize Log Loss.
