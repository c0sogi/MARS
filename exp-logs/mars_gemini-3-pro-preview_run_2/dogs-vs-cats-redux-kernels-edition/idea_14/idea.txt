Based on the empirical lessons and the progression of previous experiments, I propose a **Tri-Modal Heterogeneous Stacking Strategy with Intra-Fold Model Soups**.

This solution structurally evolves the heterogeneous ensemble concept (Idea 10/Lesson 23) by integrating a third distinct inductive bias (MBConv) and applying "Model Soups" (Idea 12/Lesson 26) to the **full dataset**. It explicitly corrects the data truncation error that caused the failure in Lesson 26 and addresses the convergence issues of larger models identified in Lesson 15.

### Model
I propose a **Tri-Modal Heterogeneous Ensemble** composed of three high-capacity backbones, each representing a distinct neural architecture paradigm to maximize error decorrelation:
1.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** Represents modern pure Convolutional architectures **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.
2.  **Swin-Small:** Represents Hierarchical Vision Transformers, capturing global dependencies via shifted-window attention **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.
3.  **EfficientNetV2-Small:** Represents **MBConv (Mobile Inverted Bottleneck)** architectures with extensive Squeeze-and-Excitation. This adds a third, distinct feature representation to the ensemble, further diversifying the decision boundaries beyond the Bi-Modal (CNN+ViT) approach.

**Structural Innovation (Intra-Fold Model Soups):**
Instead of selecting a single "best" checkpoint, we will implement **Intra-Fold Model Soups**. For every fold of each architecture, we will average the weights of the checkpoints saved from the final 3 epochs of training. This creates a "Soup" model that sits in a flatter minimum of the loss landscape, improving generalization and calibration without increasing inference latency **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** All images will be resized to **224x224** pixels. We strictly adhere to this resolution to ensure we can train all 15 models (3 architectures $\times$ 5 folds) to full convergence within the 24-hour budget, avoiding the under-training pitfalls of higher resolutions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Dataset:** We will utilize the **full training dataset** (18,000+ images). We explicitly reject the data truncation used in the failed Lesson 26 experiment, as maximizing data volume is the primary driver of performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **RandomResizedCrop:** We will strictly enforce a minimum scale of **0.8**. This prevents the semantic noise introduced by aggressive cropping, ensuring the subject remains the focus **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
    *   **Regularization:** We will apply **Mixup** (`alpha=0.2`) and **CutMix** (`alpha=1.0`) stochastically. This is critical for preventing overconfidence and ensuring the probability calibration required for minimizing Log Loss **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.

### Training
*   **Strategy:** We will implement **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** for each of the 3 architectures, resulting in **15 trained models**. This ensures robust Out-Of-Fold (OOF) generation for the meta-learner **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Optimization:** We will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate** scheduler.
*   **Convergence:** Each fold will be trained for **20 epochs**. This extended duration ensures that the "Small" capacity models fully converge. We will **disable early stopping** (or set patience to the full duration) to allow the scheduler to fully decay the learning rate, ensuring the models settle into the global minimum **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
*   **Checkpointing:** We will save checkpoints at epochs 18, 19, and 20 to construct the Model Soup for each fold.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For the test set, we will generate predictions using the 15 "Soup" models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each Soup model, we will average the predictions of the original and a horizontally flipped version to further reduce variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
*   **Stacking:** The TTA-averaged predictions from the 15 base models will be fed into a **Logistic Regression** meta-learner (trained on the OOF logits) to optimize the final ensemble weights specifically for Log Loss **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.
