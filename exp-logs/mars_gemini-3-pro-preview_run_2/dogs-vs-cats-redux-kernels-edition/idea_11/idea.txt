Based on the progression of experiments and the available computational resources, I propose a **Semi-Supervised Heterogeneous Stacking** strategy. This approach structurally evolves the solution from a purely supervised ensemble (Idea 10/Lesson 23) to a semi-supervised framework that leverages the unlabeled test data to refine the decision boundaries and improve calibration.

### Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Stacked Ensemble** composed of **ConvNeXt-Small** and **Swin-Small** backbones.
*   **Base Learners:** We retain the heterogeneous pairing of a CNN (`ConvNeXt-Small`) and a Vision Transformer (`Swin-Small`). This combination has proven to yield uncorrelated errors, maximizing ensemble utility **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**. We stick to the "Small" capacity to ensure full convergence within the compute budget **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Meta-Learner:** We will use a **Logistic Regression** meta-model to stack the predictions. This simple linear combination of logits is effective for optimizing Log Loss by correcting calibration biases in the base models **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.

### Data
*   **Resolution:** All images will be processed at **224x224** pixels. We explicitly avoid higher resolutions to prioritize training iterations and ensemble size **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly maintain the optimized pipeline:
    *   **RandomResizedCrop:** Minimum scale of **0.8** to preserve semantic integrity **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
    *   **Regularization:** **Mixup** and **CutMix** will be applied to prevent overfitting and improve calibration **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.
*   **Pseudo-Labeling (Structural Innovation):** We will implement a **Self-Training** loop. After the initial supervised training phase, we will generate soft probability predictions for the entire test set. These test images, paired with their predicted soft labels, will be added to the training pool. This allows the models to learn the specific data distribution of the test set (transductive learning), effectively increasing the dataset size and density.

### Training
*   **Strategy (Two-Stage Pipeline):**
    *   **Stage 1 (Supervised):** We will train a **5-Fold Cross-Validation** ensemble (5 ConvNeXt + 5 Swin) on the original labeled data.
    *   **Stage 2 (Semi-Supervised):** We will re-train the entire 5-Fold ensemble (from scratch) on the **Combined Dataset** (Original Labeled + Soft-Labeled Test). The loss function will be **Soft-Target Cross-Entropy** to allow the model to learn from the calibrated uncertainty of the Stage 1 predictions.
*   **Convergence:** Each fold in both stages will be trained for **20 epochs**. This extended duration ensures the "Small" models reach the global minimum, correcting the under-training observed in previous experiments **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Optimization:** We will use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate** scheduler.
*   **Patience:** Early stopping will be **disabled** (or patience set to 20) to allow the scheduler to fully decay the learning rate, ensuring robust convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the **Stage 2** models (trained on the expanded dataset).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will apply horizontal flip TTA for each base model **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
*   **Aggregation:** The TTA-averaged predictions from the Stage 2 base models will be fed into the Logistic Regression meta-learner (trained on Stage 2 OOF predictions) to produce the final submission.
