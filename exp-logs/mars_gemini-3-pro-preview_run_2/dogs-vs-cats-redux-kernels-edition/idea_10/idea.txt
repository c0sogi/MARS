Based on the empirical insights from the provided lessons, I propose a **Heterogeneous Stacking Strategy with Dual-Mix Regularization**. This approach evolves the solution from a simple averaging ensemble (Idea 9) to a meta-learning framework, while introducing advanced regularization to further improve the calibration required for the Log Loss metric.

### Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Stacked Ensemble** composed of two distinct high-capacity backbones: **ConvNeXt-Small** and **Swin-Small**.
*   **Base Learners:** We will utilize `ConvNeXt-Small` (Convolutional) and `Swin-Small` (Transformer). This combination leverages complementary inductive biases—ConvNeXt excels at local feature extraction, while Swin captures global dependencies. We retain the "Small" capacity level to ensure sufficient modeling power without the convergence issues of larger variants **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Meta-Learner (Structural Innovation):** Instead of using a simple arithmetic mean for aggregation, we will implement **Stacking**. We will train a **Logistic Regression** meta-model on the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions of the base learners. This allows the system to mathematically optimize the ensemble weights specifically for minimizing Log Loss, correcting any residual calibration errors in the individual models **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.

### Data
*   **Resolution:** All images will be processed at **224x224** pixels. We explicitly reject higher resolutions (e.g., 384x384) to avoid the computational bottlenecks and under-training observed in previous high-resolution experiments **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Dual-Mix Regularization (Structural Innovation):** We will upgrade the regularization pipeline to include both **Mixup** and **CutMix**. While Mixup (linear interpolation) proved effective **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**, CutMix (patch replacement) forces the model to focus on less discriminative parts of the object, preventing it from over-relying on specific features (like dog faces). We will apply these stochastically (e.g., 50% probability for each) during training.
*   **Augmentation Constraints:** We will strictly enforce a minimum scale of **0.8** for `RandomResizedCrop`. This ensures that the semantic integrity of the subject is preserved, avoiding the performance degradation caused by aggressive cropping **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.

### Training
*   **Strategy:** We will employ **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** for both base architectures. This generates the unbiased OOF predictions required to train the meta-learner effectively **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Optimization:** We will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate** scheduler.
*   **Convergence:** Each fold will be trained for **20 epochs** with **Early Stopping disabled** (or patience set to the full duration). This ensures the learning rate fully decays and the models settle into a global minimum, preventing the premature termination that can occur with cosine schedules **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For the test set, we will generate predictions using all 10 base models (5 ConvNeXt + 5 Swin).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will apply horizontal flip TTA for each base model to reduce variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
*   **Aggregation:** The TTA-averaged predictions from the base models will be fed into the trained Logistic Regression meta-learner to generate the final calibrated probabilities for submission.
