To optimize the solution, I will implement the following changes focusing on improving generalization and reducing overfitting on the small dataset:

1.  **Upgrade Backbone to `roberta-base`**: I will replace `distilroberta-base` with `roberta-base`. The deeper architecture (12 layers vs 6) provides richer linguistic features, which is crucial given the small training set (Cite Lesson 00006).
2.  **Layer Freezing**: To counter the increased risk of overfitting with a larger model, I will freeze the embeddings and the first 6 layers of the `roberta-base` encoder. This retains the robust low-level features while allowing the upper layers to adapt to the specific task, effectively balancing model capacity with dataset size (Cite Lesson 00007, Lesson 00002).
3.  **Increased Regularization**: I will increase the dropout rate from 0.2 to 0.3 to further regularize the classification head.
4.  **Fresh Cache**: I will update the `WORKING_DIR` to ensure the dataset is re-tokenized using the `roberta-base` tokenizer.
