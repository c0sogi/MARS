The previous implementation encountered a `CUDA out of memory` error because the Siamese network architecture processes the heavy DeBERTa backbone twice per sample (once for the question and once for the answer) within a single forward pass. This creates two sets of activations that must be stored simultaneously for backpropagation, effectively doubling the memory footprint compared to a standard model. To resolve this, I will reduce the `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` from 8 to 4 and the `VALID_BATCH_SIZE` from 16 to 8 in `library/config.py`, ensuring the activation graph fits within the available GPU memory.
