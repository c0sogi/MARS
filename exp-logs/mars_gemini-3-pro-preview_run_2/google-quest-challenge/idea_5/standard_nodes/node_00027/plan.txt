The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by enabling **Gradient Checkpointing** on a shared backbone (`self.backbone`) that is executed twice within a single `forward` pass (once for the Intrinsic View and once for the Relational View). As explained in **debug_lesson_5**, this is fundamentally incompatible because the backward pass of the second execution frees the computational graph required by the first execution.

To fix this, I will:
1.  **Disable Gradient Checkpointing**: Remove the call to `gradient_checkpointing_enable()` in `library/model.py` to resolve the graph reuse error.
2.  **Reduce Batch Size**: Lower the `train_batch_size` from 8 to 4 in `library/config.py`. Since disabling checkpointing significantly increases memory usage (by storing all intermediate activations), this step is necessary to prevent a potential `CUDA out of memory` error, applying the strategy from **debug_lesson_1**.
