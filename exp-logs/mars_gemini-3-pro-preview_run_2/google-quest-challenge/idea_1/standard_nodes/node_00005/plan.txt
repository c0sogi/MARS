I will optimize the solution by refining the feature representation and the training process.

1.  **Disentangle Semantics and Length (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004 & Data Analysis)**: The current `DualAveNet` uses Global Average Pooling on padded sequences. This implicitly scales the embedding magnitude by the sequence length (shorter sequences = smaller vectors due to zero-padding dilution). While the data analysis showed that length (`answer_len_word`) is a top predictor, entangling it with semantic content is suboptimal. I will switch to **Masked Global Average Pooling** (True Mean) to obtain a length-invariant semantic centroid, and explicitly concatenate **Normalized Length Features** to the interaction layer. This allows the MLP to learn the impact of "verbosity" and "content" independently.
2.  **Learning Rate Scheduler**: To improve convergence and potentially escape local minima on this small dataset, I will integrate a `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler into the `Trainer`.
