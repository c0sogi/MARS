# Proposed Strategy: Causal-Aware Siamese DeBERTa Network

## Model
I propose upgrading the backbone to **DeBERTa-v3-base** and implementing a **Causal-Aware Multi-Head Topology** to respect the logical independence of question attributes from answer content.

1.  **Backbone (DeBERTa-v3-base):** We will replace DistilRoBERTa with **DeBERTa-v3-base**. DeBERTa's disentangled attention mechanism and relative position embeddings provide significantly stronger natural language understanding capabilities than RoBERTa variants, which is crucial for capturing the subtle nuances of the 30 subjective labels.
2.  **Siamese Encoder:** We retain the shared-weight Siamese structure. The *Question* (title + body) and *Answer* are processed independently by the backbone to generate token embeddings.
3.  **Pooling:** We apply **Mean Pooling** to the final hidden states of both streams to obtain fixed-size representations $u$ (Question) and $v$ (Answer). We explicitly avoid learnable pooling mechanisms to prevent overfitting (Cite Lesson 00004).
4.  **Causal-Aware Split Heads (Structural Innovation):** Instead of feeding a single fused vector into one classifier for all 30 targets, we split the classification into two logically distinct heads:
    *   **Question Head:** Receives **only** the Question embedding $u$ to predict the 21 `question_` targets. This structurally enforces the causal constraint that a question's intrinsic properties (e.g., `question_well_written`) are independent of the answer provided.
    *   **Answer Interaction Head:** Receives the fused interaction vector $[u, v, |u - v|, u \cdot v]$ to predict the 9 `answer_` targets. This preserves the explicit interaction modeling (Cite Lesson 00005) required for targets that measure relevance and satisfaction.

## Data
1.  **Tokenization:** Use the `microsoft/deberta-v3-base` tokenizer. This uses a SentencePiece-based vocabulary which may handle the technical jargon in StackExchange data differently than RoBERTa's BPE.
2.  **Input Formatting:**
    *   Stream 1: `question_title` + `[SEP]` + `question_body`
    *   Stream 2: `answer`
    *   We will truncate inputs to a maximum length of 512 tokens.
3.  **Preprocessing:** Data will be pre-tokenized and cached to maximize training throughput.

## Training
1.  **Full Fine-Tuning:** We will fine-tune all layers of the DeBERTa backbone. We will **not** freeze the encoder, as fixed representations have been proven insufficient for these specific subjective targets (Cite Lesson 00008).
2.  **Loss Function:** The total loss will be the sum of the Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) losses from the Question Head and the Answer Interaction Head.
3.  **Optimization:** Use the AdamW optimizer with **Differential Learning Rates**: a lower rate (e.g., 1e-5) for the backbone to preserve linguistic knowledge, and a higher rate (e.g., 1e-4) for the custom heads.
4.  **Early Stopping:** We will monitor the **Mean Column-wise Spearman's Correlation** on the validation set. Training will be halted if this metric does not improve for 2 consecutive epochs, anticipating the rapid convergence and potential for overfitting observed in previous experiments (Cite Lesson 00007).

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The test set will be processed through the Siamese DeBERTa model.
2.  **Prediction Assembly:** The 21 predictions from the Question Head and the 9 predictions from the Answer Interaction Head will be concatenated to form the final 30-column prediction vector for each `qa_id`.
3.  **Submission:** Probabilities will be saved to the submission file format, ensuring the column order matches `sample_submission.csv`.
