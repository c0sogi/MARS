To optimize the performance of the Causal-Aware Siamese DeBERTa model, I will implement a targeted regularization and training schedule adjustment strategy based on the provided lessons and empirical findings.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Regularization (Dropout):** The current model architecture lacks explicit dropout before the classification heads. Given the small dataset size (~4,400 samples) and the complexity of the interaction features, the model is prone to overfitting as training progresses. I will add a `Dropout` layer (rate 0.15) before the Question and Answer heads. This aligns with **Lesson 2**, which emphasizes the need for regularization and simplicity on small datasets to prevent overfitting noise.
2.  **Extended Training Schedule:** The previous run showed the model improving up to Epoch 6 without plateauing. **Lesson 15** highlights that Siamese networks with interaction heads (`[u, v, |u-v|, u*v]`) require significantly more updates to align the latent spaces compared to standard fine-tuning. Therefore, I will increase the maximum `EPOCHS` to 10 and `PATIENCE` to 3 to allow the model to fully converge.
3.  **Batch Size Optimization:** To improve gradient estimation stability and utilize the A100 GPU more effectively, I will increase `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` from 4 to 8 and `VALID_BATCH_SIZE` from 16 to 32.
