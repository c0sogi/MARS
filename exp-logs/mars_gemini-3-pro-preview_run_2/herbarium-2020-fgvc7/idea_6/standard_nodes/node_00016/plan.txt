The `CUDA out of memory` error indicates that the batch size of 128 is too large for the EfficientNet-B3 model given the available GPU memory. To resolve this, I will aggressively reduce the batch sizes for both training phases (Phase 1 to 32, Phase 2 to 16) as suggested by the error analysis and **Cite debug_lesson_3**. Additionally, I will set the `PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF` environment variable to `expandable_segments:True` to better handle memory fragmentation.
