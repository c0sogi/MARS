The "REPL child process died unexpectedly" error indicates that the training process is crashing due to shared memory exhaustion (`/dev/shm`), which is common when using a large `BATCH_SIZE` and many `NUM_WORKERS` with PyTorch DataLoaders in containerized environments. Additionally, using OpenCV inside DataLoader workers can cause threading deadlocks. To fix this, I will reduce `BATCH_SIZE` to 64 and `NUM_WORKERS` to 4 in `library/config.py` to lower memory usage. I will also disable OpenCV's internal multithreading in `library/dataset.py` by calling `cv2.setNumThreads(0)` to prevent concurrency conflicts.
