The `TypeError` in the `DataLoader` occurs because `HerbariumDataset` returns `None` for the genus and family labels when a species ID is not found in the taxonomy mapping. The execution logs indicate that the `TaxonomyManager` loaded a stale or incomplete cached mapping containing only 100 species, whereas the training subset includes species from the full dataset (32,093 classes). This incomplete mapping causes lookups to fail for most images.

To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to initialize `TaxonomyManager` with `load_cached_data=False`. This forces the regeneration of the taxonomy mapping from the raw metadata, ensuring all species are correctly mapped to their genus and family IDs, thereby preventing `None` values in the batch and resolving the collision error (Cite debug_lesson_4).
