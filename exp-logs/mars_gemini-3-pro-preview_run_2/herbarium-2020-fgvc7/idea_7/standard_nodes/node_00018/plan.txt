The previous implementation encountered a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` because the batch sizes (128 for Phase 1 and 64 for Phase 2) were too large for the available 16GB GPU memory, causing an allocation failure during the forward pass of the EfficientNet-B3 backbone. To fix this, I will aggressively reduce the batch sizes to 32 and 16 respectively, ensuring the model fits within VRAM limits (Cite debug_lesson_3). I will also reduce the number of data loader workers from 12 to 4 to prevent potential shared memory exhaustion (Cite debug_lesson_1).
