My optimization strategy involves three targeted changes to improve the model's ability to capture aesthetic quality and generalize better:

1.  **Backbone Upgrade (Cite Lesson 00007)**: I will switch the backbone from `swin_large_patch4_window7_224` (ImageNet-1k) to `swin_large_patch4_window7_224.ms_in22k` (ImageNet-22k). As noted in Lesson 00007, leveraging larger, higher-quality pre-trained representations is often more effective than architectural tweaking on small datasets. The 22k pre-training provides a richer feature space for the regression head.
2.  **Augmentation Refinement**: I will remove `ColorJitter` from the training transforms. The "Pawpularity" score is likely sensitive to the photo's original lighting, saturation, and contrast. Randomly altering these properties (as done in the previous solution) introduces noise that conflicts with the ground truth aesthetic score.
3.  **Head Capacity Expansion**: I will increase the hidden layer size of the fusion MLP from 128 to 512. The Swin Large backbone produces a 1536-dimensional embedding. Compressing this immediately to 128 dimensions creates a significant bottleneck; a wider layer allows the model to retain more visual information when fusing it with the metadata.
