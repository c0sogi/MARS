By transitioning from a Linear Probe (frozen backbone) to **Fine-Tuning** (unfrozen backbone), we allow the ResNet-18 model to adapt its feature extraction layers specifically for the "Pawpularity" task, rather than relying solely on generic ImageNet features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). To support this:
1.  **Differential Learning Rates**: I am implementing a lower learning rate (`1e-4`) for the backbone to preserve pre-trained patterns and a higher rate (`1e-3`) for the head.
2.  **Data Augmentation**: To prevent overfitting as model capacity increases, I am adding `RandomHorizontalFlip`, `RandomAffine`, and `ColorJitter` to the training transforms.
3.  **Hyperparameters**: I am increasing `NUM_EPOCHS` to 20 to ensure convergence during fine-tuning.
