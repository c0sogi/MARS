The implementation strategy involves a Tri-Model Stacking Ensemble. We will train three diverse architectures (Swin Transformer, ConvNeXt, and BEiT) using a Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation scheme on the training set. This generates Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions which are then used to train a Ridge Regression Meta-Learner. The entire ensemble is then evaluated on a separate hold-out validation set. Finally, if the validation performance meets the threshold, predictions are generated for the test set. To ensure the solution fits within the time limit, we limit the training to 1 epoch per fold.
