# Proposed Solution: 2.5D Dual-Attention Network with Spatially-Guided Feature Aggregation

## Model
The proposed architecture is a hierarchical attention network that applies attention mechanisms at both the **intra-slice (spatial)** and **inter-slice (temporal)** levels. This design addresses the limitation of standard Global Average Pooling, which dilutes focal fracture signals by averaging them with healthy background tissue, by introducing a spatially-guided aggregation step anchored by dense supervision.

1.  **2.5D Multi-Scale Backbone:**
    *   **Input:** The model accepts **2.5D stacks** (slices $z-1, z, z+1$) to capture local volumetric continuity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
    *   **Feature Extraction:** An **EfficientNet-B4** serves as the backbone. To preserve both semantic context and fine-grained texture details necessary for fracture detection, feature maps are extracted from both the **penultimate (P4)** and **final (P5)** blocks and concatenated (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

2.  **Structural Innovation: Spatially-Guided Attention Pooling:**
    *   Instead of reducing feature maps to vectors via Global Average Pooling, the model employs a **Spatial Attention Module**.
    *   A lightweight convolutional block projects the concatenated P4/P5 feature maps to a single-channel **Spatial Attention Map**.
    *   The final embedding for each slice is computed as the **spatially weighted sum** of the feature maps using this attention map. This forces the model to "focus" on the specific region of the slice containing the fracture before compressing the data into a vector.

3.  **Sequence Modeling:**
    *   **Bi-LSTM with Positional Embeddings:** The sequence of spatially-focused embeddings is processed by a Bidirectional LSTM to model anatomical continuity along the Z-axis. Learnable **Positional Embeddings** are injected to provide explicit anatomical references (e.g., distinguishing C1 from C7) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

4.  **Dual-Level Output Heads:**
    *   **Primary Heads (Temporal Attention):** 8 Independent Class-Specific Attention Heads (one for each vertebra C1-C7 and Patient Overall) aggregate the LSTM outputs to predict study-level probabilities. This retains the successful "disentangled" aggregation strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   **Auxiliary Head 1 (Slice Supervision):** A linear layer on the LSTM output predicts the presence of a fracture on each specific slice (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   **Auxiliary Head 2 (Spatial Supervision):** The **Spatial Attention Map** (from step 2) is directly supervised to align with ground-truth bounding boxes.

## Data
1.  **Preprocessing & Sampling:**
    *   **2.5D Stacking:** Inputs are constructed by stacking 3 adjacent slices.
    *   **High-Density Sampling:** The loader samples a sequence of **64 to 96 slices**. This prioritizes Z-axis coverage over model complexity, avoiding the sampling pitfalls observed with Transformer approaches (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
    *   **Volumetric Augmentation:** Geometric transformations (rotation, shift, scale) are applied **identically across the entire volume** within the `__getitem__` method to preserve physical alignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00010).

2.  **Hierarchical Target Generation:**
    *   **Slice Targets:** Binary vectors indicating if a slice falls within a fracture bounding box (derived from `train_bounding_boxes.csv`).
    *   **Spatial Masks:** For slices with bounding boxes, a binary 2D mask is generated corresponding to the box coordinates. This provides the ground truth for the Spatial Attention Module.

## Training
1.  **Tri-Level Loss Function:**
    *   The total loss is a weighted sum: $L_{total} = L_{study} + \lambda_1 L_{slice} + \lambda_2 L_{spatial}$.
    *   **Study Loss ($L_{study}$):** Weighted Multi-Label Logarithmic Loss applied to the Primary Heads. A **Positive Class Weight > 2.0** is used to maintain sensitivity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029), avoiding the collapse observed with misconfigured Focal Loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031). Metric weights will be normalized to ensure stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
    *   **Slice Loss ($L_{slice}$):** Masked Binary Cross Entropy applied to the Auxiliary Slice Head (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   **Spatial Loss ($L_{spatial}$):** **Masked Dice Loss** applied to the Spatial Attention Map. This term is only computed for slices that have ground-truth bounding boxes. It explicitly trains the backbone to localize fractures.

2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Gradient Accumulation:** Employed to maintain a stable effective batch size (e.g., 8-16 studies) despite the memory overhead of the EfficientNet-B4 backbone and spatial attention maps (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with cosine annealing scheduler.

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Test studies are loaded, preprocessed into 2.5D stacks, and passed through the backbone, Spatial Attention Module, and LSTM.
    *   The **Primary Heads** (Class-Specific Attention) generate the final submission probabilities.
    *   The Auxiliary heads (Slice and Spatial) are discarded; their sole purpose is to structure the latent space and focus the feature extractor during training.
