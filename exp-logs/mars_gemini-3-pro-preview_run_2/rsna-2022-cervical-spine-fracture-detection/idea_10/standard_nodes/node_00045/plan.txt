The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` was caused by the model attempting to process an excessively large effective batch size of high-resolution images simultaneously. Specifically, flattening the batch and sequence dimensions resulted in processing $2 \times 96 = 192$ images of size $512 \times 512$ in a single pass through the EfficientNet backbone, exceeding the GPU's memory capacity (Cite debug_lesson_1).

To resolve this, I will:
1.  **Reduce Memory Footprint in Config:** Lower `IMAGE_SIZE` to 384 and `BATCH_SIZE` to 1 in `library/config.py`, while increasing `ACCUMULATION_STEPS` to 16 to maintain training stability.
2.  **Implement Chunked Inference in Model:** Modify `library/model.py` to process the flattened sequence in smaller chunks (e.g., 16 slices at a time) during the backbone feature extraction. This reduces peak memory usage by avoiding the simultaneous allocation of activations for the entire sequence.
