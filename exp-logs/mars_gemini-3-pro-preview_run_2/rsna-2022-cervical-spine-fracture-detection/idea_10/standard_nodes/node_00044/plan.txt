The error occurs because stacking 96 slices of 512x512 images vertically creates an image with a height of 49,152 pixels, which exceeds OpenCV's `SHRT_MAX` limit (32,767) used in `warpAffine`. To fix this, I will modify the data loading logic to stack slices along the channel dimension instead of the spatial dimension. This results in an image of shape (512, 512, 288), which fits within OpenCV's limits while maintaining the ability to apply consistent geometric augmentations across the sequence. This approach aligns with the principle of managing tensor dimensions to avoid resource exhaustion (Cite debug_lesson_1).
