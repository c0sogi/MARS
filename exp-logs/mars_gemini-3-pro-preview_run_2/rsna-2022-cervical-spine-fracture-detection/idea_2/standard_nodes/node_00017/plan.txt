The optimization strategy focuses on three key areas derived from the lessons learned and failure analysis:

1.  **Backbone Capacity Upgrade**: I will replace `resnet18` with `resnet34`. The previous solution showed that 2.5D sequential modeling works well, but the model may benefit from a stronger feature extractor to resolve subtle fracture patterns, utilizing the available computational budget (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).
2.  **Targeted Loss Weighting**: I will implement specific weights in the loss function.
    *   **Class Weights**: Assign a weight of `2.0` to `patient_overall` and `1.0` to others, aligning with the task description that the overall label is weighted more highly.
    *   **Positive Weight**: Apply a `pos_weight` of `2.0` to the Binary Cross Entropy loss. This directly addresses the high correlation (0.83) between prediction error and positive fracture cases found in the failure analysis, forcing the model to be more sensitive to fractures (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`).
3.  **Extended Training**: Increase `EPOCHS` from 10 to 12 to accommodate the slightly larger backbone and ensure convergence with the new loss landscape.
