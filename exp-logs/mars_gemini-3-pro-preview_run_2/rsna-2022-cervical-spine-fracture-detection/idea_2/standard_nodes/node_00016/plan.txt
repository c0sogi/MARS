To improve the model's performance and address the high false-negative rate (indicated by the 0.83 correlation between error and the positive class), I will implement the following targeted optimizations:

1.  **Architecture Upgrade**: Switch the backbone from `resnet18` to `tf_efficientnet_b0_ns` (Noisy Student). EfficientNet generally provides better feature extraction for medical imaging tasks compared to ResNet18.
2.  **Regularization**: Increase `WEIGHT_DECAY` from `1e-5` to `1e-2` and `LSTM_DROPOUT` from `0.2` to `0.3`. As noted in **Lesson 00008**, stronger regularization is crucial for small datasets (161 studies) to mitigate the rapid overfitting observed in previous runs.
3.  **Handling Class Imbalance**: Introduce a `POS_WEIGHT` (set to 2.0) in the training loss function. The failure analysis showed the model struggles to identify fractures (positive cases). Weighting positive samples during training will force the model to prioritize sensitivity.
4.  **Metric Consistency**: While I will use the weighted loss for *training* to improve learning, I will continue to use the unweighted loss for *validation* and model selection. This ensures the final reported metric is directly comparable to the baseline threshold of `0.3812`.
