To resolve the `torch.OutOfMemoryError` caused by the large effective batch size (Batch Size × Sequence Length) exceeding GPU memory (Cite debug_lesson_1), I will reduce the input resolution and batch size in `library/config.py`. To prevent numerical instability caused by Batch Normalization on single-patient batches (where slices are highly correlated), I will freeze the Batch Normalization layers in the backbone during training (Cite debug_lesson_12). Additionally, I will enable gradient checkpointing in the backbone to further reduce memory usage.
