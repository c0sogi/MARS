# Proposed Solution: Calibrated 2.5D Multi-Level Network with Supervised Attention Guidance

## Model
The proposed architecture is a **2.5D Multi-Level Sequence Network** enhanced with **Supervised Attention Guidance**. This design explicitly addresses the trade-off between sensitivity and calibration while leveraging sparse annotations safely.

1.  **2.5D Multi-Level Backbone:**
    *   **Input:** The model processes **2.5D stacks** (slices $z-1, z, z+1$) to capture local volumetric continuity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
    *   **Feature Extraction:** An **EfficientNet-B4** backbone is employed. To resolve both high-level semantic context and fine-grained fracture textures, feature maps are extracted from both the **penultimate (P4)** and **final (P5)** blocks.
    *   **Aggregation:** Standard **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** is applied independently to the P4 and P5 maps, and the resulting vectors are concatenated. This ensures robust multi-scale representation without the gradient instability or signal gating associated with custom learnable pooling layers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052, solution_lesson_node_00035).

2.  **Anatomically-Aware Sequence Modeling:**
    *   **Bi-LSTM:** The sequence of multi-level embeddings is processed by a Bidirectional LSTM to model Z-axis anatomical continuity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
    *   **Positional Embeddings:** Learnable positional vectors are injected into the LSTM outputs to provide explicit spatial references (e.g., top vs. bottom), enabling the model to distinguish visually similar vertebrae (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

3.  **Supervised Class-Specific Attention:**
    *   **Heads:** The model utilizes **8 Independent Attention Heads** (C1-C7, Patient Overall). Each head computes attention weights over the sequence to aggregate a study-level embedding (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   **Structural Innovation (Guidance):** Unlike standard MIL where attention is learned implicitly, here the attention weights are explicitly supervised. For the subset of training data with bounding boxes, the "C1 Head" is penalized if it fails to attend to the slices containing a C1 fracture. This anchors the model's focus without introducing auxiliary classification heads that can destabilize training on small datasets (Addressing solution_lesson_node_00047).

## Data
1.  **Preprocessing & Sampling:**
    *   **High-Density Sampling:** The loader samples a sequence of **96 slices**. This prioritizes Z-axis coverage to ensure small fractures are not skipped, adhering to the finding that sampling density outweighs model complexity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
    *   **2.5D Stacking:** Inputs are constructed by stacking 3 adjacent slices.

2.  **Dataset-Encapsulated Augmentation:**
    *   Geometric transformations (rotation, shift, scale) are sampled *once* per study and applied identically to every slice in the stack within the `Dataset.__getitem__` method. This preserves the physical alignment of the spine required for the sequence model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00010).

3.  **Attention Target Generation:**
    *   For studies with bounding boxes (`train_bounding_boxes.csv`), a "Ground Truth Attention Mask" is generated. This is a Gaussian distribution centered on the Z-indices of the fracture, normalized to sum to 1.

## Training
1.  **Calibrated Dual-Objective Loss:**
    *   The total loss is $L_{total} = L_{study} + \lambda L_{attn}$.
    *   **Study Loss ($L_{study}$):** Weighted Multi-Label Logarithmic Loss applied to the study-level predictions. **Crucially**, this uses **NO Positive Class Weighting** (`pos_weight=1.0`). Training on the natural class balance ensures the predicted probabilities are well-calibrated, minimizing the Log Loss metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
    *   **Attention Loss ($L_{attn}$):** Kullback-Leibler Divergence (KLD) or MSE between the predicted attention weights of the relevant fracture head and the Ground Truth Attention Mask. This is **masked** and only applied to positive samples with bounding boxes. It forces the model to learn correct localization features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035) without the noise of a full auxiliary classifier.

2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Gradient Accumulation:** Employed to simulate a stable batch size (e.g., 16 studies) despite the memory footprint of the EfficientNet-B4 backbone and dense sampling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Test studies are loaded, preprocessed into 2.5D stacks (96 slices), and passed through the pipeline.
    *   The **Multi-Level GAP** ensures fine details are captured.
    *   The **Class-Specific Attention Heads**, guided by the supervised training, accurately focus on their respective anatomical regions.
    *   The final output probabilities are well-calibrated (due to the unweighted loss), maximizing the competition metric.
