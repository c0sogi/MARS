The issue is caused by **Batch Normalization statistics corruption** due to the model processing small batches of highly correlated data (sequential CT slices from the same patient). As explained in `debug_lesson_12`, when the batch size is small and samples are correlated, the running variance in BN layers collapses to near zero during training. During validation, the model uses these corrupted statistics, causing activations to explode to infinity/NaN.

To fix this, I will override the `train` method in `CervicalFractureNet` to force all `BatchNorm2d` layers in the backbone to remain in evaluation mode (`eval()`) even when the model is training. This ensures the model uses robust pretrained statistics instead of updating them with the biased training batches, preventing the numerical instability.
