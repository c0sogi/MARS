The OOM error is caused by the excessively large effective batch size ($2 \text{ batches} \times 96 \text{ slices} = 192 \text{ images}$) being processed simultaneously by the EfficientNet backbone, which overwhelms the GPU memory (Cite debug_lesson_1). To resolve this, I will implement gradient checkpointing combined with chunking in the `CervicalFractureNet` forward pass. This approach splits the sequence into smaller mini-batches (chunks of 32 slices) and processes them sequentially through the backbone while discarding intermediate activations, drastically reducing memory usage during training (Cite debug_lesson_10).
