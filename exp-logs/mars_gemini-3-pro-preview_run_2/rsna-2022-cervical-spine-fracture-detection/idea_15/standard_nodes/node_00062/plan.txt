The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the effective input size to the backbone (Batch Size × Sequence Length) is too large for the GPU memory (Cite debug_lesson_1). With `BATCH_SIZE=4` and `SEQ_LEN=96`, the model attempts to process 384 images simultaneously. To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to 1 and increase `ACCUMULATION_STEPS` to 16 in `library/config.py` to maintain the effective batch size while reducing peak memory usage.

However, training with a batch size of 1 on sequences of correlated slices (from the same patient) causes Batch Normalization layers to compute degenerate statistics, leading to instability (Cite debug_lesson_12). Therefore, I will also modify `library/model.py` to freeze the Batch Normalization layers in the backbone (forcing them to use pre-trained statistics) and enable gradient checkpointing to further conserve memory.
