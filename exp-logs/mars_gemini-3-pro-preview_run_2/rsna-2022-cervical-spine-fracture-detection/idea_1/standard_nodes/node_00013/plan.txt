To improve the model's performance, I will implement a **Bidirectional LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)** layer between the CNN feature extractor and the Attention Aggregation mechanism. This change addresses the limitation of the current "bag-of-slices" approach by explicitly modeling the spatial continuity and sequential dependencies of the spine along the Z-axis.

Additionally, I will optimize the following hyperparameters:
1.  **`NUM_SLICES`**: Increase from 64 to **96**. This improves the Z-axis resolution, allowing the model to capture finer anatomical details and smaller fractures that might be missed with coarser sampling.
2.  **`DROPOUT`**: Increase from 0.5 to **0.6**. The addition of the LSTM layer increases the parameter count, so slightly higher regularization is needed to prevent the rapid overfitting observed in previous runs.
3.  **`EPOCHS`**: Increase from 10 to **12** to allow sufficient convergence time for the recurrent layers.

These changes build upon the existing successful strategies of Embedding-Space Aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005) and Dataset-Encapsulated Augmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
