The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` is caused by an excessive effective batch size when processing 3D volumes. The model collapses the batch and sequence dimensions (`Batch * Seq_Len`), resulting in 192 high-resolution images ($2 \times 96$) being passed to the EfficientNet-B4 backbone simultaneously, which exceeds the GPU's memory capacity (Cite debug_lesson_1). Additionally, reducing the batch size to 1 to fix the OOM introduces a risk of Batch Normalization failure due to highly correlated slices from a single patient (Cite debug_lesson_12).

To fix this:
1.  **Reduce Memory Footprint:** In `library/config.py`, I will reduce `IMAGE_SIZE` to `(256, 256)` and `BATCH_SIZE` to `1`.
2.  **Maintain Optimization Stability:** I will increase `GRADIENT_ACCUMULATION_STEPS` to `16` in `library/config.py` to preserve the effective batch size of 16.
3.  **Prevent BN Corruption:** I will override the `train()` method in `library/model.py` to freeze the backbone (`self.backbone.eval()`), ensuring robust ImageNet statistics are used instead of unstable single-patient statistics (Cite debug_lesson_12).
