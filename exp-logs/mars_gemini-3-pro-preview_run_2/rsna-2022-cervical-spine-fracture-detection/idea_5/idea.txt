# Proposed Solution: 2.5D Anatomically-Guided Attention Network with Auxiliary Supervision

## Model
The proposed architecture evolves the successful **Class-Specific Attention** design (Idea 4) by integrating **Auxiliary Anatomical Supervision** and refining the loss landscape to mitigate false positives.

1.  **2.5D Backbone with Auxiliary Branch:**
    The core feature extractor remains a **2.5D EfficientNet-B4** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015), processing stacks of adjacent slices ($z-1, z, z+1$) to capture local volumetric context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
    *   **Structural Innovation (Auxiliary Head):** A lightweight classification branch is attached to the backbone's output. This head predicts the **anatomical level** of the current slice (Multi-class: C1, C2... C7, or Background). This explicitly forces the encoder to learn features that distinguish vertebral levels, rather than relying solely on implicit positional embeddings.

2.  **Feature Fusion & Sequential Modeling:**
    The feature vector from the backbone is concatenated with the **predicted anatomical probabilities** from the auxiliary head. This "Anatomically-Augmented" feature sequence is passed to a **Bidirectional LSTM** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014), ensuring the sequence model is explicitly aware of which vertebra it is processing at every step.

3.  **Disentangled Attention Aggregation:**
    The model retains the **8 Independent Class-Specific Attention Heads** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029). These heads aggregate the LSTM outputs to form study-level embeddings for each target (C1-C7, Overall). The explicit anatomical cues injected in the previous step allow these heads to focus even more precisely on their respective regions (e.g., the C1 head will strongly attend to slices predicted as "C1" by the aux head).

## Data
1.  **Target Generation for Auxiliary Task:**
    Prior to training, "Silver Standard" slice-level labels are generated using the provided `segmentations/` (converting 3D NIFTI masks to per-slice presence vectors) and `train_bounding_boxes.csv` (mapping box Z-coordinates to labels). This creates a subset of training data with explicit slice-level ground truth.

2.  **Dataset-Encapsulated Augmentation:**
    Geometric augmentations (rotation, shift, scale) are applied consistently across the 2.5D volume within the `__getitem__` method to preserve physical alignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00010).

3.  **Input Pipeline:**
    The model uses **2.5D Stacking** (3 channels) and uniform sampling of the Z-axis (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).

## Training
1.  **Hybrid Loss Function:**
    *   **Fracture Classification (Main Task):** Replaces the static weighted BCE (which caused high False Positives in Idea 4) with **Focal Loss**. Focal Loss dynamically down-weights easy negatives and focuses on hard examples, improving sensitivity without the aggressive over-prediction observed previously.
    *   **Anatomical Localization (Auxiliary Task):** Uses **Cross-Entropy Loss** on the auxiliary head's predictions. This loss is **masked** and only computed for the subset of samples where slice-level ground truth (from segmentations/boxes) is available.

2.  **Gradient Accumulation:**
    To accommodate the EfficientNet-B4 backbone and the additional auxiliary branch memory overhead, **Gradient Accumulation** is employed to maintain a stable effective batch size of 8-16 studies (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    The test data is processed through the full pipeline. The Auxiliary Head generates internal anatomical features that guide the LSTM and Attention heads, but its direct output is discarded.
2.  **Prediction:**
    The 8 Class-Specific Attention Heads output the final probabilities for the submission.
