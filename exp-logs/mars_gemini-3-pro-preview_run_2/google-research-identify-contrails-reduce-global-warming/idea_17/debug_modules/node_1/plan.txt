The issue is located in the `validate` function within `library/train.py`. The loop `for images, masks, _ in enumerate(loader):` incorrectly attempts to unpack the output of `enumerate` (which yields `(index, batch)`) into three variables. Since `enumerate` only produces two items per iteration, this raises a `ValueError`. The fix is to remove `enumerate` and iterate directly over `loader`, which correctly yields the three-element batch `(images, masks, record_ids)` defined in the dataset.
