Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Cascaded ResNet18 U-Net with End-to-End Deep Supervision**.

This strategy introduces a **Multi-Stage Refinement Architecture**. It structurally evolves the "Current Best" baseline by stacking two U-Net models in sequence. This addresses the core difficulty of sparse segmentation: the trade-off between detection (finding the object) and delineation (defining its precise shape). The first stage acts as a high-recall detector, while the second stage functions as a shape refiner, leveraging the initial probability map to resolve disjoint segments and suppress false positives without the risks of hard gating.

### Model
The architecture consists of two identical **ResNet18 U-Net** sub-networks connected in series.

*   **Stage 1 (Detector):**
    *   **Backbone:** ResNet18 encoder. This retains the optimal capacity/efficiency balance established in previous experiments (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`, `solution_lesson_node_00005`).
    *   **Context Bridge:** An **ASPP (Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling)** module is placed at the bottleneck to capture isotropic global context (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`, `solution_lesson_node_00025`).
    *   **Decoder:** Uses **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by convolutions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00008`) and **SCSE Attention** blocks (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).
    *   **Output:** Produces a 1-channel probability map $P_{stage1}$.
*   **Stage 2 (Refiner):**
    *   **Input:** Accepts a **7-channel tensor**: The original 6 input channels concatenated with the detached probability map $P_{stage1}$ from Stage 1. This "soft attention" guide allows the network to focus its capacity on refining regions identified as probable contrails.
    *   **Architecture:** Identical to Stage 1 (ResNet18 + ASPP + SCSE).
    *   **Output:** Produces the final probability map $P_{final}$.
*   **Structural Rationale:** Unlike simply deepening the backbone (which failed in `solution_lesson_node_00005`), this "Stacked Hourglass" approach restores the spatial resolution to $256 \times 256$ between stages. This allows the model to re-process the spatial features with a renewed "global view," effectively correcting the disconnects and noise that single-pass models struggle with.

### Data
*   **Input Engineering:** We generate the **6-channel input** for Stage 1:
    *   **Channels 1-3:** "Ash" False Color Composite of the labeled frame ($t=4$), explicitly encoding optical depth and temperature (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00001`).
    *   **Channels 4-6:** The temporal difference ($t=4$ minus $t=3$). We reject symmetric future differencing to avoid redundancy (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00006`).
*   **Augmentation:** We employ a strict **Affine Transformation** pipeline (Rotation, Scale, Shift, Flip). We explicitly **exclude** elastic, grid, or optical distortions, as these warp the linear morphology of contrails into curve-like shapes that resemble natural clouds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).
*   **Dataset:** We train on the **full dataset** to ensure the model learns to suppress the "long tail" of background clutter (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** We employ a **Deeply Supervised Hybrid Loss**:
    *   $L_{total} = L_{Hybrid}(P_{stage1}, Y) + L_{Hybrid}(P_{final}, Y)$.
    *   $L_{Hybrid} = L_{BCE} + L_{BatchDice}$.
    *   **Batch-Level Dice:** We compute the Dice score over the **entire flattened batch** (treating the batch as a single volume). This stabilizes gradients and aligns with the global metric (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`, `solution_lesson_node_00018`).
    *   The auxiliary loss on Stage 1 ensures it learns to be a competent segmenter independently, providing high-quality guidance to Stage 2.
*   **Optimization:** We use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
*   **Batch Size:** We utilize a **moderate batch size** (e.g., 32) to ensure frequent gradient updates (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`).
*   **Duration:** We train for **30 epochs**. We explicitly avoid shortening the training horizon to prevent underfitting on fine-grained details (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** We implement **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**.
    *   For each test image, we generate predictions for the original, horizontally flipped, vertically flipped, and 180-degree rotated versions.
    *   The four probability maps are averaged before thresholding. This ensemble-like approach smooths out prediction noise and boosts the Global Dice score (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00024`).
*   **Post-Processing:** We apply a standard pixel-level threshold of **0.5** to $P_{final}$. We explicitly reject "Hard Gating" mechanisms (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`).
*   **Formatting:** The final binary mask is run-length encoded.
