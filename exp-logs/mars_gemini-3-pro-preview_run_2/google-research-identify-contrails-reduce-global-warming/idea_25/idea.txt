Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons and the identification of specific failure modes in previous attempts (specifically the scaling issues in Idea 22 and the information loss in Idea 15), I propose the **Raw-Physics ConvNeXt U-Net with Focal-Batch Optimization**.

This strategy synthesizes the robust **Large-Kernel Architecture** of the "Current Best" solution (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`) with a **Pure Raw-Physics Input Strategy** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`) and a **Hard-Example Mining Loss** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`). It explicitly corrects the "Heterogeneous Scaling" flaw (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00058`) that prevented previous physics-informed models from converging.

### Model
The architecture is a **U-Net** variant designed to maximize the isotropic receptive field and preserve feature linearity, utilizing a modern ConvNeXt backbone.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** We utilize **ConvNeXt-Tiny** (initialized with ImageNet weights).
    *   **Retention:** We retain the **7x7 Depthwise Convolutions**. As established, this large isotropic receptive field enforces a strong linearity prior, which is superior for contrail segmentation compared to standard 3x3 CNNs (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`) or dilated backbones (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
    *   **Stem:** We modify the first convolutional layer to accept **6 input channels** (initializing weights by averaging the standard RGB weights) but retain the standard patchify structure to preserve the pretrained spatial priors.
*   **Context Bridge:** We retain the **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module at the bottleneck. This ensures the model captures multi-scale global context, which is critical for distinguishing contrails from background cirrus (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`) and performs better than axis-aligned pooling (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00025`).
*   **Decoder (Sequential Refinement):**
    *   **Structure:** We employ a standard **Sequential U-Net Decoder** (Stride 32 $\to$ 16 $\to$ 8 $\to$ 4 $\to$ 2 $\to$ 1). We explicitly reject "Pyramid Fusion" heads (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00046`) and interpolation bottlenecks (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00039`), ensuring learned upsampling reaches the pixel level to resolve thin boundaries.
    *   **Blocks:** We use **ConvNeXt Blocks (7x7)** in the decoder to preserve the linearity of features during reconstruction (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`).
    *   **Upsampling:** We use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by convolutions, strictly avoiding Transposed Convolutions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00008`).
    *   **Attention:** We retain **SCSE (Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation)** blocks after each concatenation to adaptively suppress noise in the skip connections (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`). We explicitly reject Attention Gates (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00055`) and Input Injection (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

### Data
*   **Input Engineering (Pure Raw Physics):** We generate a **6-channel input tensor** that completely replaces the "Ash" heuristic with raw sensor data to prevent information loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`).
    *   **Channels 1-3 (Static):** **Raw Brightness Temperatures** for Bands 11, 14, and 15 at $t=4$. (Replacing the processed Ash composite).
    *   **Channels 4-6 (Dynamic):** **Raw Band Differences** ($t=4$ minus $t=3$) for Bands 11, 14, and 15.
    *   **Preprocessing (Homogeneous Scaling):** We apply **Min-Max Normalization** to all 6 channels individually, scaling them to the range $[0, 1]$ based on the dataset's global statistics. This corrects the critical failure of Idea 22, ensuring that the static and dynamic features share the same numerical distribution for stable optimization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00058`).
*   **Augmentation:** We employ a strict **Affine Transformation** pipeline (Rotation, Scale, Shift, Flip). We explicitly **exclude** elastic, grid, or optical distortions, as these warp the linear morphology of contrails into curve-like shapes that resemble natural clouds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).
*   **Dataset:** We train on the **full dataset** (18,673 images) to ensure the model learns to suppress the "long tail" of background clutter (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`).

### Training
*   **Loss Function (Focal-Batch Hybrid):** We employ a **Focal + Batch-Dice Loss**: $L_{total} = L_{Focal} + L_{BatchDice}$.
    *   **Focal Loss:** We replace the standard BCE with **Focal Loss** ($\gamma=2.0$). This directly addresses the "False Positive" bottleneck (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`) by down-weighting easy background examples and forcing the model to focus on hard negatives (cirrus clouds) and sparse positives (contrails). This was previously attempted but failed due to scaling issues; we re-introduce it now that the input scaling is corrected.
    *   **Batch-Level Dice:** We compute the Dice score over the **entire flattened batch** (treating the batch as a single volume). This stabilizes gradients and aligns with the global metric (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`, `solution_lesson_node_00018`).
    *   We explicitly avoid Deep Supervision (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00040`).
*   **Optimization:** We use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
*   **Batch Size:** We utilize a **Batch Size of 32**. This is critical to ensure the statistical stability of the Batch Dice loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00043`).
*   **Duration:** We train for **30 epochs**. We explicitly avoid shortening the training horizon to prevent underfitting on fine-grained details (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** We implement **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**.
    *   For each test image, we generate predictions for the original, horizontally flipped, vertically flipped, and 180-degree rotated versions.
    *   The four probability maps are averaged before thresholding. This ensemble-like approach smooths out prediction noise and boosts the Global Dice score (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00024`).
*   **Post-Processing:** We apply a standard pixel-level threshold of **0.5**. We explicitly reject "Hard Gating" mechanisms (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`).
