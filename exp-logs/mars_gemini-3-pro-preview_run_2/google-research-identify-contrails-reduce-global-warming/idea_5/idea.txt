Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and previous attempts, I propose the **Context-Enhanced ResNet18 U-Net with ASPP and Hybrid Batch-Dice Loss**.

This strategy synthesizes the robust baseline of the "Current Best" solution with a structural evolution in the network's bottleneck to capture long-range dependencies (linearity) and a corrected loss formulation that aligns with the global metric without sacrificing stability.

### Model
I propose a **ResNet18 U-Net** architecture modified with an **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** bridge.

*   **Backbone:** The encoder will be **ResNet18**. As established in **Lesson 2** and **Lesson 5**, this backbone provides the optimal balance of spatial fidelity and capacity for simple geometric features, whereas lighter models (MobileNet) fail to suppress noise and deeper models (ResNet34) add computational cost without accuracy gains.
*   **Structural Innovation (ASPP Bridge):** I will replace the standard convolutional bottleneck with an **ASPP module**. Standard U-Nets struggle to capture the global context required to distinguish long, linear contrails from short, disjointed cirrus streaks. ASPP employs parallel dilated convolutions with different rates to capture multi-scale context without losing resolution. This acts as a "linearity detector" at the deepest feature level.
*   **Decoder:** The decoder will utilize **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by convolutions. I explicitly reject Transposed Convolutions to prevent checkerboard artifacts and ensure better probability calibration (Cite **Lesson 8**).
*   **Feature Refinement:** I will integrate **SCSE (Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation)** blocks after each concatenation step in the decoder. This allows the model to adaptively suppress noise in the skip connections—a useful component from Idea 3 that failed previously only due to the flawed loss function and gating strategy (Cite **Lesson 14**).
*   **Input Head:** The model will accept the **6-channel input tensor** (Ash Composite + Temporal Difference) validated in **Lesson 1** and **Lesson 6**.

### Data
*   **Input Engineering:** We will generate the **6-channel input**:
    *   **Channels 1-3:** The "Ash" False Color Composite of the labeled frame ($t=4$), explicitly encoding optical depth and temperature (Cite **Lesson 1**).
    *   **Channels 4-6:** The temporal difference ($t=4$ minus $t=3$). We explicitly reject symmetric future differencing ($t+1$) as it adds redundancy (Cite **Lesson 6**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will employ a strict **Affine Transformation** pipeline (Rotation, Scale, Shift, Flip). We will explicitly **exclude** elastic, grid, or optical distortions, as these warp the linear morphology of contrails into curve-like shapes that resemble natural clouds, creating adversarial noise (Cite **Lesson 4**).
*   **Dataset:** The model will be trained on the **full dataset** to ensure it learns to suppress the "long tail" of background clutter (Cite **Lesson 2**).

### Training
I propose a **Hybrid Batch-Level Loss** strategy to correct the instability observed in Idea 4 and the metric misalignment in Idea 3.

*   **Loss Function:** $L_{total} = L_{BCE} + L_{BatchDice}$.
    *   **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE):** Included to provide smooth, convex gradients for pixel-level classification, preventing the instability and degradation observed when using pure Dice loss (Cite **Lesson 18**).
    *   **Batch-Level Dice:** Instead of averaging Dice scores across samples (which fails on empty masks), we will compute the Dice score over the **entire flattened batch**. This treats the batch as a single volume, stabilizing gradients and directly optimizing the Global Dice metric (Cite **Lesson 14**).
*   **Optimization:** We will use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
*   **Duration:** Training will run for a minimum of **20 epochs**. We explicitly avoid shortening the training horizon, as this leads to underfitting on fine-grained details (Cite **Lesson 7**).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** The model will output a pixel-wise probability map.
*   **Post-Processing:** We will apply a standard threshold of **0.5**. We explicitly reject "Hard Gating" (Idea 3) and "Soft Gating" (Idea 4) mechanisms, as they introduce single points of failure that irreversibly reduce recall (Cite **Lesson 9**, **Lesson 18**).
*   **Formatting:** The resulting binary mask will be run-length encoded.
