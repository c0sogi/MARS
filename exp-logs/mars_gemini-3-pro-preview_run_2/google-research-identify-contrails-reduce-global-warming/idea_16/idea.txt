Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons and the specific failure modes of previous attempts, I propose the **Isotropic Large-Kernel ConvNeXt U-Net with Decoupled Spatiotemporal Input and Pyramid Fusion**.

This strategy synthesizes the "Linearity Prior" of large-kernel architectures (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`) with the "Gradient Shortcut" efficiency of multi-scale fusion (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00044`), while correcting the critical feature engineering flaw identified in the most recent experiments (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`).

### Model
The architecture is a **U-Net** variant designed to maximize the isotropic receptive field and preserve feature linearity at all scales, while ensuring efficient gradient flow.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** We utilize **ConvNeXt-Tiny** (initialized with ImageNet weights).
    *   **Structural Retention:** We retain the **7x7 Depthwise Convolutions**. As established, this large isotropic receptive field enforces a strong linearity prior, which is superior for contrail segmentation compared to standard 3x3 CNNs (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`) or Transformer backbones (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00034`).
    *   **Downsampling:** We maintain the standard stride-32 downsampling path to generate robust semantic features, explicitly rejecting dilated backbones which degrade performance on thin objects (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
*   **Context Bridge:** We retain the **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module at the bottleneck. This ensures the model captures multi-scale global context, which is critical for distinguishing contrails from background cirrus (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`), and performs better than axis-aligned Strip Pooling (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00025`).
*   **Decoder (Large-Kernel Reconstruction):**
    *   We employ a symmetric decoder where each stage utilizes **ConvNeXt Blocks (7x7)**. This ensures that the "linearity prior" is maintained during the upsampling process, preventing the fragmentation of thin lines that occurs with small 3x3 kernels (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`).
    *   We use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by convolutions, strictly avoiding Transposed Convolutions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00008`).
    *   We retain **SCSE Attention** blocks after each concatenation to adaptively suppress noise in the skip connections (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).
*   **Structural Innovation: Lightweight Pyramid-Fusion Head:**
    *   To address the "thin feature disappearance" problem (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00039`) without incurring the memory penalty of Hypercolumns (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00043`), we implement the **Project-then-Fuse** strategy (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00044`).
    *   We extract feature maps from Decoder Stride 4, Stride 2, and Stride 1.
    *   These maps are projected to a low channel count (e.g., 8) via 1x1 convolutions, bilinearly upsampled to Stride 1, and concatenated.
    *   The final prediction is generated from this fused tensor. This acts as a gradient shortcut, accelerating convergence and improving data efficiency.

### Data
*   **Input Engineering (Decoupled Spatiotemporal Features):** We generate a **6-channel input tensor** that explicitly decouples spectral and temporal signals, addressing the information loss observed in previous "Ash Difference" attempts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`).
    *   **Channels 1-3 (Static Spectral):** "Ash" False Color Composite of the labeled frame ($t=4$). This encodes the physical properties (optical depth, particle phase, temperature) using the standard difference formula (Band 15-14, 14-11, 14).
    *   **Channels 4-6 (Dynamic Physical):** The **Raw Band Differences** ($t=4$ minus $t=3$) for Bands 11, 14, and 15. By differencing the raw brightness temperatures *before* any non-linear scaling or compositing, we preserve the full dynamic range of the cooling signal associated with contrail formation.
*   **Augmentation:** We employ a strict **Affine Transformation** pipeline (Rotation, Scale, Shift, Flip). We explicitly **exclude** elastic, grid, or optical distortions, as these warp the linear morphology of contrails into curve-like shapes that resemble natural clouds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).
*   **Dataset:** We train on the **full dataset** (18,673 images) to ensure the model learns to suppress the "long tail" of background clutter (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** We employ a **Hybrid Loss**: $L_{total} = L_{BCE} + L_{BatchDice}$.
    *   **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE):** Included to provide smooth, convex gradients for pixel-level classification.
    *   **Batch-Level Dice:** We compute the Dice score over the **entire flattened batch** (treating the batch as a single volume). This stabilizes gradients and aligns with the global metric (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`, `solution_lesson_node_00018`).
    *   We apply this loss only to the final fused output, avoiding the performance constraints of Deep Supervision (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00040`).
*   **Optimization:** We use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
*   **Batch Size:** We utilize a **Batch Size of 32**. The lightweight nature of the Pyramid-Fusion head (compared to Hypercolumns) makes this feasible, ensuring we avoid the instability of small batches (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`, `solution_lesson_node_00043`).
*   **Duration:** We train for **30 epochs**. We explicitly avoid shortening the training horizon to prevent underfitting on fine-grained details (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** We implement **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**.
    *   For each test image, we generate predictions for the original, horizontally flipped, vertically flipped, and 180-degree rotated versions.
    *   The four probability maps are averaged before thresholding. This ensemble-like approach smooths out prediction noise and boosts the Global Dice score (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00024`).
*   **Post-Processing:** We apply a standard pixel-level threshold of **0.5**. We explicitly reject "Hard Gating" mechanisms (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`).
