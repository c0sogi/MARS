Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons and the specific challenges of segmenting thin, linear features in satellite imagery, I propose the **Dilated ResNet18 U-Net with Output Stride 8**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in the encoder to prevent the spatial collapse of fine-grained features, synthesizing the robustness of the "Current Best" baseline with the resolution-preserving properties of dilated convolutions.

### Model
The architecture is a **U-Net** variant that fundamentally alters the encoder's downsampling strategy to maintain high-resolution feature maps deep in the network.

*   **Backbone (Dilated ResNet18):** We utilize **ResNet18** as the encoder backbone (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`).
    *   **Structural Innovation:** We enforce an **Output Stride of 8** (feature map size 1/8th of input) instead of the standard 32 (1/32nd).
    *   To achieve this, we remove the strided downsampling in **Layer 3** and **Layer 4** of the ResNet.
    *   We replace the standard convolutions in these layers with **Dilated Convolutions** (Dilation Rate = 2 for Layer 3, Rate = 4 for Layer 4).
    *   **Rationale:** Standard backbones downsample a 256x256 image to an 8x8 feature map. For a contrail that is 1-2 pixels wide, this aggressive reduction often destroys the spatial signal before the decoder can recover it. By maintaining a 32x32 resolution at the bottleneck, we preserve the spatial integrity of thin features without the optimization instability of Deformable Convolutions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00032`) or the orientation bias of Strip Pooling (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00025`).
*   **Context Bridge (ASPP):** We employ an **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module at the bottleneck. This is specifically designed to work with dilated encoders (as in DeepLab) to capture multi-scale context at a consistent resolution (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).
*   **Decoder:** We adapt the U-Net decoder to the modified encoder.
    *   Since the bottleneck is already at 1/8th resolution, the decoder requires fewer upsampling stages.
    *   We use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by convolutions, strictly avoiding Transposed Convolutions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00008`).
    *   We incorporate **SCSE (Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation)** blocks after each concatenation to adaptively suppress noise in the skip connections (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).
*   **Input Head:** The model accepts the **6-channel input tensor** (Ash Composite + Temporal Difference) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00001`).

### Data
*   **Input Engineering:** We generate the **6-channel input**:
    *   **Channels 1-3:** "Ash" False Color Composite of the labeled frame ($t=4$), explicitly encoding optical depth and temperature.
    *   **Channels 4-6:** The temporal difference ($t=4$ minus $t=3$). We reject symmetric future differencing to avoid redundancy (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00006`).
*   **Augmentation:** We employ a strict **Affine Transformation** pipeline (Rotation, Scale, Shift, Flip). We explicitly **exclude** elastic, grid, or optical distortions, as these warp the linear morphology of contrails into curve-like shapes that resemble natural clouds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).
*   **Dataset:** We train on the **full dataset** to ensure the model learns to suppress the "long tail" of background clutter (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** We employ a **Hybrid Loss**: $L_{total} = L_{BCE} + L_{BatchDice}$.
    *   **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE):** Provides smooth, convex gradients for pixel-level classification.
    *   **Batch-Level Dice:** We compute the Dice score over the **entire flattened batch** (treating the batch as a single volume). This stabilizes gradients and aligns with the global metric, avoiding the instability of sample-level averaging (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`) and the degradation caused by pure Dice loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00018`).
*   **Optimization:** We use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
*   **Batch Size:** We utilize a **moderate batch size** (e.g., 32) to ensure frequent gradient updates (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`).
*   **Duration:** We train for **30 epochs**. We explicitly avoid shortening the training horizon to prevent underfitting on fine-grained details (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** We implement **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**.
    *   For each test image, we generate predictions for the original, horizontally flipped, vertically flipped, and 180-degree rotated versions.
    *   The four probability maps are averaged to smooth out prediction noise and improve robustness (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00024`).
*   **Post-Processing:** We apply a standard pixel-level threshold of **0.5**. We explicitly reject "Hard Gating" or "Soft Gating" mechanisms (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`).
*   **Formatting:** The final binary mask is run-length encoded.
