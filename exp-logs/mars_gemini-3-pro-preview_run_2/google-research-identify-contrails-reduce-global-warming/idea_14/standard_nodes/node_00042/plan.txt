The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the current batch size of 32 consumes more VRAM than the 40GB A100 GPU can provide, specifically during the memory-intensive hypercolumn aggregation step in the `ConvNeXtHyperUNet` model. As identified in the error analysis, the activation maps at full resolution ($256 \times 256$) with the expanded channel dimensions in the ConvNeXt block create a massive memory footprint. To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 32 to 8, which will significantly lower the memory requirements per iteration and allow the training to proceed without crashing.
