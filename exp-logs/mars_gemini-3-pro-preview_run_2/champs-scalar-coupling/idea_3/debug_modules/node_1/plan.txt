The issue stems from the heuristic formula `(n + l/2)*pi` used to estimate the roots of spherical Bessel functions; this approximation is inaccurate for high angular momenta ($l$) and low radial indices ($n$), resulting in search intervals where the function does not change sign. This triggers a `ValueError` in `brentq`. The fix replaces this heuristic with a robust incremental scanning approach that iterates through the domain with a safe step size (0.5) to reliably identify intervals where the sign changes, guaranteeing a valid bracket for the root-finding algorithm.
