To fix this, I will rename the `notebook_id` column to `id` in two places within `library/pipeline.py`:
1.  In `_postprocess_submission`, which generates the predicted cell orders.
2.  In `train_pipeline`, where the ground truth cell orders (`df_gt_order`) are constructed for validation.

This ensures that the downstream evaluation logic receives the expected schema, addressing the `KeyError: 'id'` as per **Cite debug_lesson_6**.
