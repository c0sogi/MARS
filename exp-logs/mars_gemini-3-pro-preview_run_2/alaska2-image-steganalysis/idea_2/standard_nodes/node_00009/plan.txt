The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` was caused by the effective batch size exceeding the GPU's memory capacity. The `AlaskaDataset` in training mode returns pairs of images (Cover and Stego), which are flattened in `train_one_epoch`, effectively doubling the configured `train_batch_size` from 32 to 64. Processing 64 images at $512 \times 512$ resolution with the EfficientNetV2-S backbone consumed approximately 39.36 GiB of VRAM, causing the allocation failure on the 40GB A100 GPU. To resolve this, I will reduce the `train_batch_size` to 16 (effective batch size of 32) and the `val_batch_size` to 32 in `library/config.py` to ensure the model fits within the available memory.
