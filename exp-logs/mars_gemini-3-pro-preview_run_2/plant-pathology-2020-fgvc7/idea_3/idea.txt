Based on the analysis of the previous ideas and the empirical lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy.

### Model
I propose upgrading the backbone to the **EfficientNetV2-M (Medium)** architecture.
*   **Structural Evolution:** While the previous EfficientNet-B4 (Idea 2) successfully applied "Compound Scaling" `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`, the V2 family introduces Fused-MBConv layers and improved progressive learning, offering a better trade-off between parameter efficiency and training speed. The "Medium" variant significantly increases model capacity (approx. 54M parameters vs. 19M for B4) to better capture complex, fine-grained disease patterns, fitting within the "moderately heavier" computational budget.
*   **Head Design:** The classification head will remain a Global Average Pooling layer followed by a Dropout layer and a Linear layer with 4 outputs, consistent with previous successful designs.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Resolution:** We will increase the input resolution to **480x480 pixels**. This aligns with the native resolution of the EfficientNetV2-M architecture, adhering to the principle of matching input resolution to model capacity `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **CutMix Regularization:** We will replace the "CoarseDropout" strategy with **CutMix**. This represents a structural evolution of the "Aggressive Information Dropping" lesson `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`. While CoarseDropout replaces regions with black pixels to force distributed feature learning `Cite solution_lesson_node_00006`, CutMix replaces them with patches from other training images. This maintains the benefit of occlusion (forcing the model to look beyond the most discriminative spot) while preserving pixel information and providing stronger regularization against overfitting.
    *   **Constraints:** We will continue to exclude global photometric distortions (Brightness/Contrast) to preserve color semantics `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** We will implement a **Weighted Soft-Target Cross-Entropy Loss**.
    *   **Synthesis:** Since CutMix generates non-binary labels (e.g., 0.6 Rust, 0.4 Healthy), we must use a soft-target loss. Crucially, to retain the success of handling imbalance, we will calculate the loss as a weighted sum of the individual losses for the mixed images, using the **inverse class frequency weights** derived in the baseline `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** to support the new architecture, as established in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.
*   **Reproducibility:** We will strictly enforce determinism by passing a `worker_init_fn` to the DataLoaders to seed workers individually, ensuring that hyperparameter comparisons are not clouded by stochastic data loading noise `Cite solution_lesson_node_00009`.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will apply **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)** by averaging predictions from the original image and a horizontally flipped version `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Metric:** The model will be evaluated using the Mean Column-wise ROC AUC.
