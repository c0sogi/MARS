# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** comprising **EfficientNetV2-L** and **ConvNeXt-Base**, enhanced with **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**.
*   **Structural Innovation (SWA):** We will integrate **Stochastic Weight Averaging** into the training pipeline. Standard optimization on small datasets (~1,300 images) often converges to sharp local minima, leading to high variance and poor calibration `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`. SWA averages the weights of the model across multiple points in the trajectory of SGD, approximating the wider, flatter minimum of the loss landscape. This significantly improves generalization and, crucially, model calibration, directly addressing the instability observed in previous iterations.
*   **Backbones:**
    *   **EfficientNetV2-L:** Leverages compound scaling and Fused-MBConv layers for maximum feature extraction capacity `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.
    *   **ConvNeXt-Base:** Provides a distinct inductive bias (Transformer-style large kernels) to capture features complementary to the EfficientNet architecture.
*   **Head Design:** Both models will utilize the **Multi-Label Decomposition** head (outputting binary "Is Rust" and "Is Scab" logits) `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`. This allows the model to learn shared representations from the "Multiple Diseases" class.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Resolution:** We will match input resolutions to the native design of each backbone to maximize efficiency `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`: **480x480** for EfficientNetV2-L and **384x384** for ConvNeXt-Base.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **CoarseDropout:** We will apply scaled CoarseDropout (e.g., max hole 100x100) to force distributed feature learning `Cite solution_lesson_node_00006`. We will strictly enforce **minimum hole dimensions** (e.g., 16x16) to prevent trivial occlusions `Cite solution_lesson_node_00005`.
    *   **Exclusions:** We will exclude CutMix due to the risk of label noise on localized pathologies `Cite solution_lesson_node_00013` and avoid global photometric distortions `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.

# Training
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Cross-Validation** will be performed for both architectures.
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **SWA Schedule:** We will use a standard learning rate schedule (Cosine Annealing) for the initial 75% of epochs, then switch to a constant or cyclical learning rate to collect SWA weights for the final 25% of epochs.
    *   **Loss Function:** **Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) Loss** with **Positive Class Weights**. Weights will be calculated based on the inverse frequency of "Rust" and "Scab" occurrences (including those in the "Multiple" class) to handle imbalance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`.
    *   **Stability:** We will enforce a batch size of at least **16** to ensure stable Batch Normalization statistics `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.
    *   **Reproducibility:** A `worker_init_fn` will be used to seed DataLoaders `Cite solution_lesson_node_00009`.

# Evaluation
*   **Metric Calculation (Correction):** We will strictly avoid concatenating Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions to calculate the global AUC, as this is susceptible to calibration drift `Cite solution_lesson_node_00030`. Instead, we will calculate the ROC AUC for each fold independently and report the **mean of the fold AUCs** to accurately assess model performance.
*   **Inference (Rank-Calibrated Ensemble):**
    *   **TTA:** We will apply Test Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) for all models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
    *   **Rank Averaging:** To generate the final submission, we will **rank-normalize** the predictions of each model (converting probabilities to ranks 0-1) before averaging them. This ensures that the final ensemble is robust to differences in calibration between the EfficientNet and ConvNeXt models, preserving the relative ordering required for the ROC AUC metric `Cite solution_lesson_node_00030`.
    *   **Reconstruction:** The aggregated rank scores for "Rust" ($S_r$) and "Scab" ($S_s$) will be mapped to the 4-class format:
        *   `Healthy`: $(1 - S_r) \times (1 - S_s)$
        *   `Rust`: $S_r \times (1 - S_s)$
        *   `Scab`: $(1 - S_r) \times S_s$
        *   `Multiple`: $S_r \times S_s$
