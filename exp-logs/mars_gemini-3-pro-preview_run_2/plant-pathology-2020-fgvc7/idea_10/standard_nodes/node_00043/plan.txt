The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the physical batch size of 16 is too large for the GPU memory when training the `EfficientNetV2-L` model at 480x480 resolution. To resolve this, I will reduce the `batch_size` to 4 and increase `gradient_accumulation_steps` to 4 in `library/config.py`, effectively maintaining the same training dynamics while fitting within VRAM (Cite debug_lesson_5). Additionally, since the script runs in a persistent environment where modules may already be cached, I will update `runfile.py` to explicitly reload all dependent modules (`library.utils`, `library.dataset`, etc.) after reloading the configuration, ensuring they pick up the updated `CFG` values (Cite debug_lesson_8).
