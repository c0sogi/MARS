# Model: High-Fidelity Selective-Injection BiLSTM (HFSI-BiLSTM)

I propose a structural evolution that refines the "Injection" mechanism to strictly enforce the "Signal Fidelity" principle (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) while retaining the efficiency of "Bottlenecking" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).

*   **Selective-Injection Block (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Previous best solutions (Lesson 00045) generated an injection payload by projecting the *entire* dual-path signal (Identity + Context) through a bottleneck. This inadvertently compresses the raw physical signals (Flow, R, C) into a latent representation, potentially degrading their numerical precision.
    *   I propose a **Selective-Injection Mechanism** that treats the two signal paths differently:
        1.  **Fidelity Path (Identity):** The raw input features (Flow, Resistance, Compliance, Derivatives) are passed through an **Identity** mapping. They are **never** projected or compressed. This guarantees that the deep layers have access to the exact floating-point magnitudes required for the Equation of Motion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
        2.  **Context Path (Bottlenecked GLU):** The raw inputs are processed through a **Gated Linear Unit (GLU)** to extract non-linear "Physics Context" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035). The output of this GLU is then projected down to a low-dimensional bottleneck (e.g., 64 units) to control model capacity and compute cost (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).
    *   **Fusion:** The uncompressed Fidelity Path (~15 dims) and the compressed Context Path (64 dims) are concatenated to form the **Injection Payload**.
    *   **Deterministic Constraint:** We strictly **exclude Dropout** from this entire block to ensure the injection serves as a stable ground truth (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Deep Recurrent Backbone:**
    *   The core consists of **4 layers of Bidirectional LSTMs**.
    *   **Deep Injection:** The "Injection Payload" is concatenated to the input of **every** LSTM layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Inter-Layer Connectivity:** We use **Layer Normalization** and **Dropout** *between* LSTM layers to stabilize dynamics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023), but explicitly reject complex inter-layer gating to maintain high gradient flow (Cite solution_lesson_node_00048).
    *   **Shallow Front-End:** We explicitly reject Deep Front-End MLPs to prevent overfitting to instantaneous correlations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).

# Data: Robust Physics-Fidelity Engineering

We will implement the refined feature engineering pipeline that strictly adheres to the calculus of the physical system and robust statistical principles.

*   **Robust Scaling:**
    *   We will apply **RobustScaler** (Median/IQR) to all continuous inputs. This handles the ~14% outliers in the control signal `u_in` significantly better than Standard Scaling, accelerating convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
*   **Time-Weighted Integration:**
    *   We will calculate the time delta `dt` and generate the volume feature as `u_in_cumsum = cumsum(u_in * dt)`. This corrects the integration error of early baselines (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
*   **Physics Interaction Terms:**
    *   We will calculate `R * u_in` (Resistive Pressure) and `u_in_cumsum / C` (Elastic Pressure) using the time-weighted volume (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Explicit Dynamics:**
    *   We will augment the input with **Lag features** and **Finite Differences** (1st and 2nd derivatives of `u_in`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Signal Preservation:**
    *   Lung Resistance (`R`) and Compliance (`C`) will be treated strictly as **continuous numerical features**. We reject embeddings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) and CNN stems (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

# Training: Stretched-Horizon Convergence Protocol

*   **Loss Function:** We will use a **Weighted L1 Loss**, assigning a weight of **1.0** to the inspiratory phase and **0.1** to the expiratory phase. This focuses capacity on the metric while maintaining the temporal context required for stable hidden states (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Stretched Horizon:** We will set the training duration to **200 epochs** and explicitly set the scheduler's `T_max=200`.
    *   Deep recurrent models on this task exhibit "long-tail convergence" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   Stretching the scheduler horizon keeps the learning rate higher for longer, preventing premature convergence to shallow local minima and yielding superior final accuracy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
