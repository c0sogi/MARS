The previous solution utilized a highly effective architecture (RGI-BiLSTM) which achieved a Validation MAE of ~0.204 in just 20 epochs. However, Lesson 00029 ("Long-Tail Convergence") and Lesson 00005 ("Convergence Horizon") explicitly state that deep recurrent models for physical dynamics require significantly longer training (100-150 epochs) to converge to the optimal error range (< 0.192). The previous run was artificially limited to 20 epochs.

My strategy is as follows:
1.  **Extend Training to 150 Epochs (Cite Lesson 00029):** I will remove the 20-epoch override in `runfile.py` and set the default `EPOCHS` to 150 in `library/config.py`. This allows the model to exploit the "long tail" of convergence.
2.  **Increase Regularization (Cite Lesson 00023, 00037):** To prevent overfitting during this extended training period, I will increase `WEIGHT_DECAY` to 0.05 and `DROPOUT` to 0.2. Lesson 00037 emphasizes that deep injection architectures require robust regularization.
3.  **Maximize Throughput (Cite Lesson 00017):** I will increase `BATCH_SIZE` to 512 to better utilize the A100 GPU, ensuring the 150-epoch run completes well within the 4-hour time limit.
