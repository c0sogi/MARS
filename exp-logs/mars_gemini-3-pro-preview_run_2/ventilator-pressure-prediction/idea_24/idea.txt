# Model: Uncompressed Physics-Context Deep-Injection BiLSTM

    *   I propose a **Wide-Bandwidth Injection Mechanism** composed of two parallel paths:
        1.  **Path A (Explicit Fidelity):** An **Identity** mapping of the raw input features, explicitly augmented with physics interaction terms ($R \cdot u_{in}$, $Vol/C$) and finite differences. This ensures deep layers have direct access to the exact numerical magnitudes required for the Equation of Motion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018, solution_lesson_node_00076).
        2.  **Path B (Wide Context):** A **Wide Monolithic Gated Linear Unit (GLU)** (e.g., 256 units) that processes *all* input features.     *   **Fusion:** Path A and Path B are concatenated to form the Injection Payload. We strictly **exclude Dropout** from this payload to ensure it serves as a deterministic ground truth (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).

# Data: Segregated Physics-Fidelity Pipeline

We will implement a corrected feature engineering pipeline that addresses the "Silent Peril" of scaling binary flags while strictly enforcing the inclusion of physics-based features.

*   **Segregated Robust Scaling (Critical Correction):**
    *   We will explicitly **segregate** the scaling logic.
    *   **Continuous Features** ($u_{in}$, $R$, $C$, Time, Derivatives, Interactions): Apply **RobustScaler** (Median/IQR) to handle the ~14% outliers in control signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
    *   **Binary Features** ($u_{out}$): Pass through **Raw** (unscaled).
    *   **Rationale:** Lesson 00062 demonstrated that scaling the binary flag $u_{out}$ inverts the validation logic, leading to deceptive metrics. This correction is mandatory for valid learning.
*   **Explicit Physics Interactions:**
    *   We will explicitly calculate and include the interaction terms: `R * u_in` (Resistive Pressure) and `u_in_cumsum / C` (Elastic Pressure). Lesson 00076 proved that removing these in favor of latent learning degrades performance significantly.
*   **Time-Weighted Integration:**
    *   Calculate volume as `u_in_cumsum = cumsum(u_in * dt)` to correct integration error (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
*   **Finite Difference Dynamics:**
    *   We will include **1st and 2nd Finite Differences** ($x_t - x_{t-1}$) of the control input. We explicitly reject "Iterated Finite Differences" (acceleration of acceleration) as they amplify noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068) and reject "Future Lags" to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055). We also prefer these explicit differences over raw lags (Cite solution_lesson_node_00087).
*   **Full Dataset:**
    *   We will use the **full training dataset**, rejecting any subsetting strategies, as data volume is the primary driver of performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00086).
