To optimize the solution, I will implement the following changes:

1.  **Categorical Embeddings for Physical Attributes**: Replace the continuous `R` and `C` input features with One-Hot Encoded vectors. The physical system behaves differently across these discrete settings (Resistance 5, 20, 50; Compliance 10, 20, 50), and treating them as continuous variables forces the model to learn a linear interpolation that may not exist. Explicitly encoding these states allows the LSTM to condition its dynamics on the specific lung configuration, enhancing the physics-based modeling approach (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
2.  **Increased Model Capacity**: Scale the Bi-LSTM architecture from 2 layers of 256 units to **4 layers of 384 units**. The previous solution trained very quickly (2 minutes), leaving significant computational budget within the 4-hour limit. Increasing depth and width allows the model to capture more complex non-linear temporal dependencies and reduce the bias observed in the failure analysis.
3.  **Configuration Updates**: Update `INPUT_DIM` to 16 (reflecting the switch from 2 continuous to 6 binary features) and adjust the submission threshold in `runfile.py` to the current best metric.
