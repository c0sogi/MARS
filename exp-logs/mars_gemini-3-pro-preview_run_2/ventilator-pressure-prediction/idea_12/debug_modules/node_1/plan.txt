The loop `for inputs, targets, u_out in enumerate(loader):` attempts to unpack the tuple returned by `enumerate` (which consists of `(index, batch)`) into three variables, causing a `ValueError` because there are only two values to unpack. Additionally, the subsequent line `inputs, targets, u_out = inputs` is incorrect and redundant, as it attempts to unpack the loop variable (which would be the index or a tensor, depending on the unpacking) again.

The fix involves removing `enumerate` to iterate directly over the `DataLoader` (which yields the batch tuple `(inputs, targets, u_out)` directly) and removing the erroneous unpacking line inside the loop.
