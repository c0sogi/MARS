The strategy focuses on unleashing the model's potential by extending the training horizon and optimizing for the A100 hardware, while enhancing the architectural capacity for physical context injection.

1.  **Extend Training Horizon**: I will increase `EPOCHS` from the debug value of 15 to 180. Deep Recurrent Neural Networks modeling continuous physical dynamics exhibit "long-tail" convergence, where significant error reduction occurs late in training (Cite Lesson 00029, 00043).
2.  **Maximize Throughput**: I will increase `BATCH_SIZE` from 512 to 1024. The available A100 GPU has sufficient memory, and higher throughput allows for more gradient updates within the time limit without degrading convergence quality (Cite Lesson 00017).
3.  **Enhance Context Injection**: I will increase the `GLU_PROJECTION_DIM` from 128 to 256. This ensures that the injected static physics attributes (Resistance, Compliance) constitute a larger, more significant fraction of the input vector to deep LSTM layers, preventing the physical signal from being diluted by the recurrent state (Cite Lesson 00028).
4.  **Enable Full Execution**: I will modify `runfile.py` to remove the "Fast Baseline" override, allowing the model to train for the full duration required to meet the submission threshold.
