# Model: Wide-and-Deep Physics-Informed BiLSTM

I propose a **Wide-and-Deep** architecture that synthesizes the strengths of the "Unified" and "Dual-Stream" approaches while addressing their respective limitations.

*   **Rationale:** Lesson 00024 demonstrated that separating "Instantaneous" (Resistive) and "Recurrent" (Elastic) dynamics accelerates early learning. However, Lesson 00025 proved that a hard-coded additive sum ($P = P_{elastic} + P_{resistive}$) limits the model's ability to capture non-linear interactions, causing the Unified architecture to eventually outperform it.
*   **Structural Innovation:** We will implement a **Non-Linear Fusion** of the two streams. Instead of summing the outputs, we will concatenate the high-level features from both a Deep Recurrent Stream and a Wide Instantaneous Stream, feeding them into a final Dense Regression Head. This allows the model to dynamically learn how to weight the "memory" (LSTM) vs. the "instantaneous physics" (MLP) for each time step, effectively implementing a learned gating mechanism.

**Architecture Details:**
1.  **Deep Recurrent Stream (The "Deep" Path):**
    *   **Backbone:** A 4-layer **Bidirectional LSTM**.
    *   **Input Injection:** We will concatenate the raw input features to the input of *every* LSTM layer. This ensures deep layers retain access to high-fidelity physical signals (Cite Lesson 00021).
    *   **Layer Normalization:** Applied after every LSTM layer to stabilize the hidden state dynamics (Cite Lesson 00023).
2.  **Instantaneous Stream (The "Wide" Path):**
    *   **Backbone:** A 3-layer **Residual MLP** (Feed-Forward Network) operating on each time step independently.
    *   **Purpose:** This provides a direct, low-latency path for the model to map instantaneous features (Flow, Resistance, Interactions) to Pressure, bypassing the "soft" filtering of the LSTM gates (Cite Lesson 00024).
3.  **Fusion Head:**
    *   The outputs of the Deep Stream (sequence of hidden states) and the Wide Stream (sequence of features) are **concatenated** at each time step.
    *   A final **Dense Layer** projects this combined representation to the scalar pressure prediction.

# Data: Physics-Preserving Feature Engineering

We will strictly adhere to the feature engineering protocols that preserve signal fidelity and encode physical laws.

*   **Physics-Based Integration:** Explicitly generate `u_in_cumsum` to represent Air Volume, relieving the LSTM of the integration task (Cite Lesson 00003).
*   **Equation of Motion Interactions:** Explicitly calculate the interaction terms `R * u_in` (Resistive Pressure) and `u_in_cumsum / C` (Elastic Pressure) (Cite Lesson 00004).
*   **Explicit Dynamics:** Augment inputs with **Lag features** and **Finite Differences** (1st and 2nd derivatives of `u_in`) to provide explicit velocity/acceleration data to the Wide Stream (Cite Lesson 00008).
*   **Signal Fidelity:** Treat Lung Resistance (`R`) and Compliance (`C`) strictly as **continuous numerical features**. We explicitly reject categorical embeddings and CNN stems to preserve the ordinal magnitude information required for the physics calculations (Cite Lesson 00018).
*   **Normalization:** Apply Robust Scaling to all inputs.

# Training: Stabilized Convergence Protocol

*   **Loss Function:** Use **Weighted L1 Loss**, assigning a weight of **1.0** to the inspiratory phase (target) and **0.1** to the expiratory phase. This focuses capacity on the metric while maintaining the temporal context required for the LSTM states (Cite Lesson 00021).
*   **Optimizer:** Use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Throughput & Duration:** By avoiding computationally expensive blocks like Transformers (Cite Lesson 00012) or CNN stems (Cite Lesson 00017), we maximize training throughput. We will utilize this efficiency to train for **50+ epochs**, ensuring the deep recurrent backbone fully converges (Cite Lesson 00005).
