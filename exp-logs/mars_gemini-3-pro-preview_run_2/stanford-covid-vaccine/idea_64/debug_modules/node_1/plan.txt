The `AssertionError` is caused by the numerical stability epsilon (`1e-8`) used in the `MaskedMCRMSELoss` class. When the Mean Squared Error (MSE) is zero (perfect prediction), the loss calculates as $\sqrt{0 + 10^{-8}} = 10^{-4}$. This value ($0.0001$) exceeds the strict threshold of $10^{-6}$ expected by the verification script. To resolve this, I will reduce the epsilon to `1e-16`, which results in a bias of $\sqrt{10^{-16}} = 10^{-8}$, satisfying the test requirements while preserving gradient stability at zero.
