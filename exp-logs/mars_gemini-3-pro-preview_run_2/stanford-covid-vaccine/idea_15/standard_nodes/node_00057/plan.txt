Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, I will implement the following optimization strategy:

1.  **Simplify Structural Feature Injection (Parsimony):** I will reduce the `STACKING_NEIGHBORS` parameter from 3 to 1. The previous solution gathered latent features from the pairing partner *and* its two neighbors. According to **Lesson 23**, high-dimensional context windows often introduce noise that outweighs their information gain. By restricting the latent gathering to just the direct partner, we rely on the Dilated TCN backbone to handle local context (neighbors) implicitly, while explicitly bridging the critical long-range dependency.
2.  **Update Data Processing & Cache:** I will modify `library/data.py` to generate neighbor indices based on the new `STACKING_NEIGHBORS` setting. Crucially, I will update the cache keys in `library/config.py` to `_v2` to prevent the model from loading stale data with the wrong shape (**Cite Lesson 32**).
3.  **Refine Training Duration:** I will set `EPOCHS` to 25 (up from the baseline run's 15) to ensure the model has sufficient time to converge, while keeping the total runtime well within limits.
