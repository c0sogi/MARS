Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the progression of previous ideas, I propose the **Stacked Interaction Dense Network**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation: a **Post-Interaction Refinement Module**. While previous successful models (Idea 8) gathered structural features and immediately passed them to the RNN, this approach recognizes that RNA stability is governed not just by the existence of base pairs, but by the **stacking interactions** between adjacent pairs. By inserting a localized convolutional block *after* the latent gather step, we allow the model to explicitly learn the thermodynamics of "neighboring pairs" on the 1D grid before global aggregation, strictly adhering to all data engineering and regularization constraints established in the lessons.

# Model

1.  **Input Representation (Partner-Aware):**
    *   **Standard Features:** One-Hot Encoding for `sequence`, `structure`, and `predicted_loop_type` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Explicit Partner Identity:** We **retain** the explicit injection of the **Partner Base Identity** (one-hot vector of the paired base) at the input level. As established in **Lesson 00052**, removing this input cripples the encoder's ability to form basic chemical representations.

2.  **Backbone (Dense Dilated TCN):**
    *   **Architecture:** A stack of **Single-Layer Dilated Residual Blocks** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) utilizing **Dense Connections** (concatenating outputs of all prior blocks). This preserves the full multi-scale history (Cite solution_lesson_node_00047).
    *   **Configuration:** Exponentially increasing dilation rates ($d=1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32$).
    *   **Capacity & Regularization:** Growth Rate **64**, **Dropout 0.1** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037, solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Convolution:** Standard Convolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

3.  **Latent Interaction (Symmetric & Linear):**
    *   **Projection:** A **Linear 1x1 Convolution** compresses the dense history to **32 channels**. We avoid non-linear bottlenecks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00076) and maintain a compact dimension (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).
    *   **Gather:** For position $i$, gather the 32-dim vector of partner $j$. Use **Zero-Masking** for unpaired bases (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071).
    *   **Fusion:** Concatenate Local (32) + Partner (32) $\to$ **64 channels**. This maintains symmetry (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073) and uses simple concatenation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).

4.  **Innovation: Stacking Refinement Module:**
    *   **Concept:** The fused 64-channel vector at index $i$ represents the "Pair State" of interaction $(i, j)$. To model stacking, we must relate pair $(i, j)$ to pair $(i+1, k)$.
    *   **Mechanism:** We process the fused sequence with a **Mini-DenseNet**:
        *   Layer 1: Conv1d(k=3, d=1), Growth 32, Dropout 0.1.
        *   Layer 2: Conv1d(k=3, d=2), Growth 32, Dropout 0.1.
    *   **Output:** Concatenate [Input(64), L1(32), L2(32)] $\to$ **128 channels**.
    *   **Rationale:** This explicitly models the local environment of the *interaction itself*, capturing stacking energies efficiently on the 1D grid without the redundancy of gathering neighbors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).

5.  **Global Aggregation (BiGRU):**
    *   **Bidirectional GRU** processes the refined 128-channel features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Constraint:** Hidden size = `input_dim // 2` = **64** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

6.  **Output Head:**
    *   Linear projection to the 5 target variables.
