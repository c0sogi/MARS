Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the extracted lessons, I propose the **Calibrated Multi-Task Single-Instance Network (CMT-SIN)**.

This strategy synthesizes the architectural strengths of Multi-Task Learning (which improved feature quality in Idea 6) with the robust calibration of Single-Instance Learning (Idea 3), while introducing a specific optimization strategy to resolve the numerical instability that caused previous failures.

### Model
**Architecture: Fine-Tuned EfficientNetV2 with Auxiliary Regularization**
1.  **Visual Backbone:** The model will utilize an **EfficientNetV2-Small** initialized with ImageNet weights. Crucially, this backbone will be **completely unfrozen and fine-tuned** end-to-end to adapt to the specific grayscale texture statistics of mammography (e.g., microcalcifications), correcting the failure of frozen embeddings Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}.
2.  **Pooling & Fusion:** Feature maps will be aggregated using **Generalized Mean Pooling (GeM)**, which effectively highlights small lesions. The resulting visual embedding will be concatenated with a processed metadata vector (Age, Implant, View, etc.) derived from a parallel MLP branch.
3.  **Multi-Task Heads:** The network will branch into three output heads to leverage all available training signals:
    *   **Primary Head (Cancer):** Predicts malignancy.
    *   **Auxiliary Head 1 (BIRADS):** Predicts the BIRADS assessment score.
    *   **Auxiliary Head 2 (Density):** Predicts breast density.
    *   *Rationale:* While Idea 6 failed due to sampling issues, it demonstrated that auxiliary tasks reduced the model's reliance on metadata (Age). Retaining this structure improves feature representation.

### Data
1.  **Single-Instance Learning (SIL):** The pipeline will strictly adhere to the SIL paradigm. Patient-level labels (Cancer, BIRADS, Density) will be broadcast to every individual image (view). This **dense supervision** strategy is retained as it was proven to outperform the sparse supervision of Multi-Instance Learning (MIL) in this domain Cite {solution_lesson_node_00005}.
2.  **Byte-Level Ingestion:** The robust byte-level image extractor (scanning binary streams for JPEG/JPEG2000 headers and decoding via `cv2.imdecode`) will be used to handle DICOM files without `pydicom`.
3.  **High-Resolution Input:** Images will be resized to **640x640** or higher. This resolution is necessary to capture subtle pathologies and is made computationally feasible by the efficient V2 backbone and Mixed Precision training.

### Training
1.  **Optimization Objective: Focal Loss:**
    *   Instead of using `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with a massive `pos_weight` (which caused NaN divergence in Idea 5) or Balanced Sampling (which destroyed calibration in Idea 6), the model will use **Focal Loss** for the primary cancer target.
    *   *Rationale:* Focal Loss addresses class imbalance mathematically by down-weighting easy negatives rather than linearly scaling up positives. This avoids the large scalar multipliers that break **Mixed Precision (AMP)** training Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}.
2.  **Sampling Strategy: Natural Distribution:**
    *   The training will use a **Standard Random Sampler** rather than a Weighted Random Sampler.
    *   *Rationale:* Training on the natural distribution ensures the model learns the true prior probability of cancer (~2%). This preserves **probability calibration**, which is critical for the Probabilistic F1 metric, avoiding the calibration drift observed when oversampling Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}.
3.  **Precision:** **Mixed Precision (AMP)** will be enabled to maximize batch size and resolution. The Focal Loss calculation will be explicitly wrapped to run in `float32` to ensure numerical stability.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The model will iterate through the test set. Auxiliary heads will be ignored, and only the probability from the **Primary Cancer Head** will be extracted.
2.  **Aggregation:** Predictions will be grouped by `prediction_id`. The final submission score will be the **maximum** probability across all images (views) associated with that ID. This aligns with the empirical finding that robust single-instance predictions aggregated via max-pooling outperform complex learned aggregation mechanisms Cite {solution_lesson_node_00005}.
