The optimization strategy involves switching from `distilbert-base-multilingual-cased` to `google/muril-base-cased`, a model specifically pre-trained on Indian languages (including Hindi and Tamil), which should provide better representations for this task. Since MuRIL is a BERT-based model, it utilizes `token_type_ids` (segment embeddings) to distinguish between the question and the context, whereas DistilBERT does not. Therefore, the pipeline is updated to extract, pass, and utilize `token_type_ids` throughout the data loading, modeling, and inference stages. Additionally, the training duration is extended to 5 epochs and the learning rate is adjusted to 2e-5 to accommodate the new model, while the regression gate threshold is updated to the current best metric (0.2522) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
