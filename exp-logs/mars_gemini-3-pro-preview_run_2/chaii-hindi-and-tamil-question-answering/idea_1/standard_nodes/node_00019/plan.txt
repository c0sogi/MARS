To improve the stability and performance of the QA model, I will implement an **Ensemble Strategy** using **Seed Averaging** (Cite Lesson 00008). Given the small dataset size (~800 samples), single-run metrics are highly stochastic. By training three identical models with different random seeds and averaging their logits during inference, I can reduce variance and improve the generalization of the final predictions.

Additionally, I will increase the training duration from 3 to 4 epochs (Cite Lesson 00003). The training logs indicated that the loss was still decreasing and validation scores were improving at the end of epoch 3, suggesting that the model could benefit from slightly more training steps to fully converge with the linear learning rate scheduler.

I will also update the output directory to ensure a clean execution environment for the new ensemble.

Here are the changes:

1.  **`library/config.py`**: Add `SEEDS = [42, 43, 44]`, increase `EPOCHS` to 4, and update `OUTPUT_DIR`.
2.  **`library/engine.py`**:
    *   Modify `run_training` to iterate over `Config.SEEDS`, training and saving a separate model for each seed.
    *   Add `validate_ensemble` to evaluate the combined performance of the trained models.
    *   Update `generate_submission` to load all models and average their logits for test predictions.
3.  **`runfile.py`**: Update `perform_failure_analysis` to load the ensemble models and perform averaged inference for the final validation check.

    Validates an ensemble of models by averaging logits.
    """
    for model in models:
        model.eval()

    all_end_logits = []
