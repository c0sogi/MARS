The following optimization strategy addresses the underfitting observed in the previous solution (Validation Jaccard ~0.44 vs Threshold ~0.59) by prioritizing training capacity over expensive regularization.

1.  **Disable Adversarial Training (FGM) and Increase Epochs**: The previous solution reduced training to 2 epochs to accommodate the computational cost of FGM. As noted in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**, this trade-off led to underfitting. I will disable FGM and increase the epoch count to 3. This restores the setup that previously achieved high scores (~0.59) while keeping the runtime well within the 4-hour limit (removing FGM roughly halves the time per epoch).
2.  **Optimize Learning Rate**: I will slightly increase the base learning rate from `1.5e-5` to `2.5e-5`. Since we are removing the regularizing effect of FGM and using Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD), a slightly higher rate for the task heads will assist in faster convergence and better adaptation to the specific answer spans.
3.  **Increase Maximum Answer Length**: Data analysis indicates the maximum answer length in the training set is 51 words. The current inference default of 40 is too restrictive and likely truncates valid long answers. I will increase `max_answer_len` to 60.
