To ensure convergence, I will increase the number of epochs to 5.

Additionally, I will address Feature-Architecture Compatibility (**Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00016**) by removing `token_type_ids` (segment embeddings) from the data pipeline and model inputs, as DistilBERT does not support them.
