To improve the model's performance and stability, I will implement the following changes:

1.  **Switch Optimizer in Stage 1**: I will replace the L-BFGS optimizer with **Adam** in the `train_stage1_warmup` function. L-BFGS is numerically unstable in mini-batch regimes, which caused the gradient explosion and `NaN` values in the previous run. Switching to a first-order optimizer like Adam is necessary for standard mini-batch training (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`).
2.  **Correct Learning Rate**: I will reduce the Stage 1 learning rate from `1.0` to `1e-3`. A learning rate of `1.0` is appropriate for L-BFGS but catastrophic for Adam; adjusting this ensures the optimizer-hyperparameter coupling is respected (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00016`).
3.  **Train on Full Dataset**: I will remove the `debug_size=20000` limit in `runfile.py`. Given the 4-hour time limit and A100 GPU, training on the full dataset (~144k images) is feasible and will significantly improve generalization compared to the subsampled baseline.
4.  **Update Threshold**: I will update the submission threshold to `0.3674631922482031` as required.
