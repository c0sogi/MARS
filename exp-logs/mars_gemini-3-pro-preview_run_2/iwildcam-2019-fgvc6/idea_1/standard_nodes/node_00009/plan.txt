To address the rapid overfitting and domain shift issues observed in the previous solution, I will implement a targeted optimization strategy focusing on feature robustness and regularization.

**Optimization Strategy:**

1.  **Feature Simplification (Ablation)**: I will modify the feature extractor to use **only Global Average Pooling (GAP)**, discarding Global Max Pooling. Max pooling is highly sensitive to local spatial outliers (e.g., bright background spots), which likely contributed to the model latching onto location-specific features. Reducing the dimensionality from 4096 to 2048 also acts as a structural regularizer. I will update `IDEA_NAME` to `idea_2` to force a fresh feature extraction.
2.  **Feature Standardization**: I will introduce `StandardScaler` to normalize the features (zero mean, unit variance) before training. L-BFGS optimization performs significantly better on well-conditioned problems, and standardization ensures that the L2 penalty is applied uniformly across all feature dimensions.
3.  **Regularization**: I will add **L2 regularization** (`weight_decay=1e-2`) to the L-BFGS optimizer. The previous solution lacked explicit regularization, leading to immediate overfitting. A weight decay of `0.01` provides a strong penalty against large weights, encouraging the model to learn more distributed, generalizable patterns.
4.  **Optimizer Settings**: I will maintain the learning rate at `1.0` for L-BFGS, directly applying the finding from **Lesson 00008** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008) that reducing the learning rate for L-BFGS on convex problems can be detrimental.

This approach combines better feature engineering with proper optimization hygiene for linear models.
