The `torch.optim.LBFGS` optimizer raises a `TypeError` because it does not accept `weight_decay` in its constructor, unlike other PyTorch optimizers. To resolve this, I will remove the `weight_decay` argument from the `LBFGS` initialization in `library/model.py`. To maintain the intended L2 regularization, I will manually add the regularization term ($\frac{1}{2} \lambda \|w\|^2$) to the loss function within the optimization `closure`. Cite debug_lesson_3.
