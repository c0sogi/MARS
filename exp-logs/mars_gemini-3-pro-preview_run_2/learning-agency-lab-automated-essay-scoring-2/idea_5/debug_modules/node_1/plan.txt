The execution failure `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the default behavior of Gradient Checkpointing in PyTorch/Transformers, which uses a "reentrant" strategy (`use_reentrant=True`). This strategy dynamically reconstructs the computation graph during the backward pass, which often conflicts with complex training loops involving Gradient Accumulation or Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP), as the engine may attempt to access tensors that have already been freed.

To fix this, I will explicitly disable reentrant checkpointing by passing `gradient_checkpointing_kwargs={"use_reentrant": False}` when enabling it in `library/modeling.py`. This forces the use of the newer, non-reentrant implementation that is fully integrated with PyTorch's autograd engine, preventing the graph retention errors (Cite debug_lesson_4).
