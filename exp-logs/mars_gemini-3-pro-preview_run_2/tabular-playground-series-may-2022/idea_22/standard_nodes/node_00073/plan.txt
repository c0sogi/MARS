Optimization Strategy:
1.  **Context-Aware Initialization**: I will modify `SustainedDepthHybridNet` in `library/model.py` to apply `kaiming_init_weights` exclusively to the `backbone` and `head` sub-modules. This prevents the global application of Kaiming initialization (optimized for ReLU/GLU) from disrupting the Transformer encoder, which relies on Xavier initialization for its attention mechanisms to maintain gradient stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00072).
2.  **Positional Embedding Noise**: I will update `TransformerStream` in `library/model.py` to initialize `pos_embed` with `torch.randn` instead of zeros. This random noise breaks symmetry at the start of training, allowing the model to learn position-sensitive features more rapidly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00072).
3.  **Extended Optimization Schedule**: I will increase the training duration in `runfile.py` from 15 to 35 epochs. The previous solution was cut off while still improving, and maximizing the time spent in the final learning rate decay stage is crucial for reaching the target AUC > 0.997 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).
