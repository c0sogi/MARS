Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, the primary optimization strategy is to simplify the feature fusion mechanism. Specifically, **Lesson 78** highlights that replacing a simple Linear Projection with a "Gated Stem" (Linear -> GLU) at the fusion point leads to overfitting and performance degradation. Additionally, **Lesson 74** and **Lesson 73** reinforce the importance of using a dedicated, simple linear stem immediately after concatenation, without intermediate normalization or complex non-linearities.

Therefore, I will modify the model architecture to replace the `GatedStemHybridNet` with a `HybridResFunnel` that employs a standard `nn.Linear` stem. This reduces parameter count at the critical fusion interface and prevents the memorization of noise. I will retain the successful training hyperparameters (40 epochs, StepLR, Batch Size 1024) and the "Sustained Depth" backbone topology (2 blocks per stage) as these have proven robust in previous iterations.
