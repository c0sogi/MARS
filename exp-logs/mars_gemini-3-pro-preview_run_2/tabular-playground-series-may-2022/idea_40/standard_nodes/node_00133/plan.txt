I will optimize the solution by addressing critical architectural and training deficiencies identified in the lessons.

1.  **Architecture Fix (Transformer FFN):** I will replace the `DirectSwiGLU` block in the Transformer encoder with a `TransformerFFN` block. The current `DirectSwiGLU` lacks the final output projection layer, which prevents proper feature mixing and degrades performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00132). The `TransformerFFN` will implement the standard `Linear -> SwiGLU -> Linear` structure.
2.  **Fusion Refinement:** I will remove the `BatchNorm1d` (alignment norm) applied to the flattened Transformer output. Concatenating the raw embeddings (which are initialized with unit variance) with the Z-scored continuous features is superior to applying an intermediate normalization that might distort the semantic signal (Cite solution_lesson_node_00124).
3.  **Initialization Fix:** I will explicitly initialize the `bias` of the output `head` with a uniform distribution, overriding the global zero-bias initialization. This ensures the output layer has the necessary initial variance for robust convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00130).
4.  **Training Schedule:** I will increase the training epochs from 3 to 15 in `runfile.py` and adjust the scheduler to decay every 5 epochs. This allows the model to benefit from multi-stage annealing within the 6-minute time limit (Cite solution_lesson_node_00128).

class TransformerFFN(nn.Module):
    """
    Standard SwiGLU FFN with Output Projection: Linear(d->4d) -> SwishGate -> Linear(2d->d)
    Used for the Transformer to ensure proper feature mixing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00132).
    """

    def __init__(self, dim, hidden_dim=None):
        super().__init__()
        if hidden_dim is None:
            hidden_dim = 4 * dim
        self.w12 = nn.Linear(dim, 2 * hidden_dim)
        self.w3 = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, dim)

        x12 = self.w12(x)
        x1, x2 = x12.chunk(2, dim=-1)
        hidden = F.silu(x1) * x2
