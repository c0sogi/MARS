Based on the comprehensive history of experiments and the derived empirical lessons, I propose the implementation of a **Modality-Scaled Hybrid SwiGLU Network**.

This strategy represents a structural synthesis that optimizes the **Fusion Interface** between the high-variance Post-Norm Transformer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00114) and the unit-variance continuous features. It introduces **Learnable Modality Scaling** to explicitly resolve the distribution mismatch identified in Lesson 69 without the overfitting risks of LayerScale (Cite solution_lesson_node_00115) or the destructive effects of Batch Normalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074). It retains the state-of-the-art **SwiGLU-ResFunnel** backbone and **Post-Norm Transformer** configuration.

# Model

The architecture is a dual-stream network that fuses a scaled categorical sequence with a scaled continuous stream into a deep, SwiGLU-based residual funnel.

1.  **Stream 1: Categorical Sequence (Post-Norm Transformer)**
    *   **Input**: The `f_27` feature is decomposed into 10 distinct character tokens.
    *   **Embedding**: Tokens are passed through a shared embedding layer (dimension 32).
        *   **Initialization**: Explicitly initialize with **Unit Variance** (`std=1.0`) to ensure robust signal propagation in the Post-Norm architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00114).
    *   **Positional Encoding**: **Learnable Absolute Positional Embeddings** are added.
        *   **Initialization**: Explicitly initialize with **Low Variance Random Noise** (`std=0.02`) to break symmetry without introducing noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00081).
    *   **Encoder**: The sequence is processed by a **Standard Transformer Encoder** (2 layers, 4 heads).
        *   **Configuration**: Use **Post-Normalization** (`norm_first=False`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00095).
        *   **Activation**: Explicitly set `activation="gelu"` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).
        *   **Regularization**: Apply **Low Dropout (0.1)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00048).
    *   **Output**: The resulting sequence is flattened.

2.  **Stream 2: Continuous Preservation**
    *   **Input**: The 30 numerical features are standardized.
    *   **Preservation**: Features are maintained in their **raw, normalized form** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).

3.  **Fusion Layer: Learnable Modality Scaling (Structural Innovation)**
    *   **Mechanism**: Introduce two learnable scalar parameters, $\lambda_{seq}$ and $\lambda_{cont}$.
        *   $F_{seq} = \lambda_{seq} \cdot \text{Flatten}(\text{Transformer}(x_{seq}))$
        *   $F_{cont} = \lambda_{cont} \cdot x_{cont}$
    *   **Initialization**: Initialize $\lambda_{seq} = 0.1$ and $\lambda_{cont} = 1.0$.
    *   **Rationale**: The Post-Norm Transformer output has unbounded variance that grows with depth, while continuous features are Z-scored (~1.0). Direct concatenation allows the sequence features to dominate the initial gradients (Cite solution_lesson_node_00069). By initializing the sequence scaler to a small value, we explicitly balance the variance contribution of both modalities at the start of training, allowing the Linear Stem to learn optimal mixing weights without transient instability.
    *   **Concatenation**: Concatenate the scaled vectors $F_{seq}$ and $F_{cont}$.
    *   **Linear Stem**: Pass through a **Linear Projection Layer** to map to the backbone width (512) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).

4.  **Backbone: LayerNorm SwiGLU ResFunnel**
    *   **Topology**: The backbone is organized into 3 stages (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00084).
    *   **Sustained Depth**: Each stage consists of **3 stacked Residual Blocks** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00070).
    *   **Blocks**: Use **Pre-LayerNorm Direct SwiGLU Residual Blocks**.
        *   **Structure**: $x_{out} = x + \text{DropPath}(\text{Dropout}(\text{SwiGLU}(\text{Linear}(\text{LayerNorm}(x)))))$.
        *   **Normalization**: Use **LayerNorm** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00087).
        *   **Activation**: **SwiGLU** (Swish-Gated Linear Unit) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00090).
    *   **Transitions**: Use **Pre-Norm Transitions** (`LayerNorm -> Linear`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00088).
    *   **Regularization**: Linear **Stochastic Depth** schedule (0.0 to 0.2) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00086) + **Dropout (0.35)**.

5.  **Output Head**
    *   **Minimalist Design**: Single `Linear` layer with Sigmoid activation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).

# Data

1.  **Decomposition**: The string feature `f_27` is strictly split into 10 integer-encoded columns.
2.  **Normalization**: Continuous features (`f_00` - `f_30`) are Z-score normalized (StandardScaler).
3.  **Splitting**: Use the metadata-defined stratified validation split.
4.  **Volume**: Use the **Full Training Dataset** (640,000 samples) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).

# Training

1.  **Optimizer**: Use **AdamW**.
2.  **Strict Decoupled Weight Decay**:
    *   **Group 1 (Decay 1e-2)**: Weights of Linear layers, Embeddings, and Attention projections.
    *   **Group 2 (Decay 0.0)**: Biases, **LayerNorm parameters**, **Positional Embeddings**, and the new **Modality Scalars** ($\lambda_{seq}, \lambda_{cont}$). Explicitly exclude the scalars to prevent them from decaying to zero (Cite solution_lesson_node_00098).
3.  **Initialization**:
    *   **SwiGLU Blocks**: Use **Kaiming (He) Uniform** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00090).
    *   **Transformer**: Use **Xavier (Glorot)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00082).
4.  **Optimization Granularity**:
    *   **Batch Size**: 1024 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
    *   **Scheduler**: Aggressive Step Decay (factor 0.1 every 10 epochs) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
5.  **Duration**: Train for **40 epochs** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).
6.  **Metric**: Monitor **Validation AUC** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
