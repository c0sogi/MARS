The issue is an `ArrayMemoryError` caused by unintended broadcasting during the calculation of prediction errors in `runfile.py`. Specifically, `y_true` has shape `(N,)` while `y_pred` has shape `(N, 1)` (coming from the model's output). When subtracting these arrays (`y_true - y_pred`), NumPy broadcasts them into an `(N, N)` matrix (160,000 x 160,000), which requires approximately 191 GB of memory, exhausting the available RAM.

To fix this, I will flatten `y_pred` to shape `(N,)` immediately after concatenation. This ensures the subtraction operation occurs element-wise between two 1D arrays, resulting in an error vector of shape `(N,)` as intended.
