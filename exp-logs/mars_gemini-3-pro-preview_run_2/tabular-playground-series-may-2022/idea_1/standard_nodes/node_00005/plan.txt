A larger embedding dimension will allow the model to learn more nuanced representations of these characters and their interactions with the continuous features.
2.  **Modernize Activation Function**: I will replace the standard `ReLU` activation with `SiLU` (Swish). SiLU has been shown to perform better in deep networks for tabular data, as it is smooth and non-monotonic, allowing for better gradient flow through the deep MLP layers.
3.  **Optimize Training Dynamics**: I will introduce a `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler. The previous training logs showed that while the loss plateaued early, the AUC continued to improve. A cosine scheduler will gradually anneal the learning rate, helping the model settle into a more robust minimum in the final epochs.
4.  **Extend Training**: I will ensure the training runs for 30 epochs (up from 25) to allow the scheduler to complete its full cycle and maximize the benefit of the learning rate decay.
