My optimization strategy focuses on correcting architectural flaws identified in the lessons and tuning hyperparameters for better generalization.

1.  **Architecture Refinement (Late Fusion)**: I will replace the "Early Fusion" strategy (injecting a continuous state token into the Transformer) with "Late Fusion". **Cite solution_lesson_node_00093** ("Efficacy of Late Fusion over Context-Token Injection") explicitly states that forcing continuous features into the sequence encoder degrades performance. The new model will process the character sequence independently via the Transformer and fuse it with continuous features only at the backbone input.
2.  **Depth Adjustment**: I will reduce `BLOCKS_PER_STAGE` from 3 to 2. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00075** ("Diminishing Returns of Depth") indicates that moderate depth (e.g., 2 blocks) is often superior to deeper networks for this dataset, saving computation and reducing overfitting risks.
3.  **Training Schedule**: I will increase `EPOCHS` from 20 to 35. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00067** ("Optimization Schedule Sensitivity") suggests that extending the high-learning-rate phase allows for better convergence in high-precision tasks. The `LR_STEP_SIZE` of 10 combined with 35 epochs ensures a robust decay schedule (decays at 10, 20, 30).

I will rename the model class to `LateFusionSwishGatedResFunnel` to reflect these changes.
