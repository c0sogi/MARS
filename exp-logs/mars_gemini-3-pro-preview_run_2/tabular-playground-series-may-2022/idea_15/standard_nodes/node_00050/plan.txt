Therefore, the optimization strategy involves:
1.  **Reverting Normalization in Backbone**: Modifying `ResFunnelBlock` to use `nn.BatchNorm1d` instead of `nn.LayerNorm`. This aligns with the finding that Batch Normalization is essential for deep tabular networks (Cite Lesson 00049).
2.  **Reverting Transformer FFN**: Replacing the `GatedTransformerBlock` with a standard `TransformerBlock` that uses a conventional Feed-Forward Network (Linear -> ReLU -> Dropout -> Linear -> Dropout). This reverts the "Gated GLU" change in the Transformer stream which was part of the regression (Cite Lesson 00049).
