To optimize the model's performance, I will implement an **Early Fusion** strategy by replacing the independent processing stems for continuous and sequence features with a direct concatenation approach, as recommended in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00105**. The previous solution's use of independent stems likely delayed the learning of cross-modal interactions. Additionally, I will revert the training duration to 40 epochs (removing the 10-epoch constraint used for the fast baseline) to ensure full convergence, as high-precision tasks require extended training schedules.
