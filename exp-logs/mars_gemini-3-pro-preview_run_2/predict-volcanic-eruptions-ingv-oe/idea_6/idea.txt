Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Channel-Adaptive Hybrid EfficientNet** architecture.

This strategy evolves the successful "Current Best" approach (ResNet18 + GAP) by upgrading the backbone to an architecture that explicitly models sensor interdependencies, while stripping away the destabilizing components (Attention Pooling, Multi-View Stacking) that caused failures in Ideas 4 and 5.

### Model
The architecture is a dual-branch network designed to fuse channel-aware visual feature extraction with robust global statistics:

1.  **Branch 1: Channel-Adaptive Spectrogram Encoder**
    *   **Backbone:** **EfficientNet-B0**. I propose replacing the ResNet18 backbone with EfficientNet-B0. While ResNet treats all channels equally in its convolution operations, EfficientNet natively incorporates **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** blocks. These blocks explicitly model the interdependencies between the 10 seismic sensors, allowing the network to dynamically recalibrate the importance of each channel (e.g., upweighting a sensor picking up a tremor while suppressing a noisy one) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
    *   **Pooling:** Use **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**. We explicitly reject the Attention Pooling mechanism used in Idea 4. As established in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**, the target variable (time to eruption) represents a cumulative global state, which is best captured by averaging the total energy of the feature maps rather than filtering for specific localized events.
    *   **Initialization:** Initialize with **ImageNet weights**. The first convolutional layer will be adapted by averaging the RGB weights and replicating them 10 times to preserve learned spatial filters and avoid the performance degradation of training from scratch **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.

2.  **Branch 2: Full Statistical MLP**
    *   **Input:** A vector of engineered statistics for all 10 sensors. Crucially, we will **retain the full feature set** (Mean, Std, Skew, Kurtosis, Quantiles, and NaN counts). We will not reduce the feature set as attempted in Idea 5, which led to performance degradation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
    *   **Architecture:** A Multi-Layer Perceptron (Dense $\rightarrow$ BN $\rightarrow$ ReLU $\rightarrow$ Dropout) to preserve explicit access to global signal magnitudes and data quality metrics.

3.  **Fusion Head:**
    *   The embeddings from the EfficientNet GAP layer and the MLP are concatenated.
    *   A final regression block predicts the scaled target value.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Imputation:** Fill `NaN` values in the raw sensor data with **zeros**. We explicitly avoid linear interpolation to prevent the generation of artificial spectral ramps that confuse the CNN **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
    *   **Spectrogram Generation:** Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms with **High Resolution**:
        *   **Mel Bins:** 128. This is critical to match the spatial hierarchy of the backbone, as 64-bin inputs were shown to be a bottleneck **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
        *   **Scaling:** Apply **`AmplitudeToDB(top_db=80)`**. This normalizes the signal relative to its peak energy, ensuring robustness to varying gain levels **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
        *   **Single View:** We will use a single, optimized spectrogram representation (e.g., N_FFT=1024) rather than stacking multi-resolution views, to avoid the overfitting and instability observed in Idea 5 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.
    *   **Target Scaling:** Apply **StandardScaler** to the `time_to_eruption` target variable to ensure convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

### Training
1.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Loss Function:** Mean Absolute Error (MAE) on the scaled target.
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with weight decay.
    *   **Scheduler:** Implement **`ReduceLROnPlateau`**. This is mandatory to achieve high precision; constant learning rates were identified as a primary cause of stagnation in Idea 5 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
2.  **Regularization:**
    *   **Augmentation:** Apply **SpecAugment** (Time and Frequency Masking).
    *   **Constraint:** Configure masking **conservatively** (masking <15% of the spectrum). Aggressive masking was shown to destroy critical contiguous signal structures in scientific data **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.
    *   **Patience:** Set Early Stopping patience to **20 epochs**. This high tolerance is required to allow the optimizer to navigate the noisy validation landscape typical of seismic data **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Generate 128-bin, dB-scaled spectrograms (zero-filled) and the full statistical feature vector for the test set.
2.  **Prediction:** Pass inputs through the trained Channel-Adaptive Hybrid model.
3.  **Inverse Scaling:** Apply the inverse transform (multiply by training std, add training mean) to the model outputs to restore the `time_to_eruption` to its original scale.
4.  **Submission:** Format the `segment_id` and restored predictions into the required CSV file.
