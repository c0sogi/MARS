1.  In `library/features.py`, I will clamp the Mel Spectrogram values to a small epsilon (`1e-10`) before the log-scale conversion to prevent infinite values. I will also modify `get_statistics` to replace `NaN` outputs from skew and kurtosis calculations with `0.0`.
