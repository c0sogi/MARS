Attention mechanisms can struggle with global state estimation in noisy signals where the "total energy" is more predictive than specific localized events.
    *   **Cite Lesson 00011**: ResNet's architecture is well-suited for capturing the "texture" of spectrograms, which is critical for this task.
    *   I will modify `library/config.py` to switch the backbone to `resnet18` and disable attention pooling.
    *   I will update `library/model.py` to handle the `resnet18` naming convention (`conv1` instead of `conv_stem`) and implement standard GAP.

2.  **Increase Training Duration**:
    *   **Cite Lesson 00007**: The previous run used only 6 epochs, which is insufficient for a hybrid architecture with SpecAugment to converge. The lesson suggests 40 epochs are necessary to reach the optimal performance of ~2.08M MAE (or better with higher resolution).
    *   I will update `library/config.py` to set `NUM_EPOCHS = 40` and ensure `runfile.py` uses this configuration instead of a hardcoded value.

3.  **Refactoring**:
    *   I will rename the model class from `AttentionPooledHybridEfficientNet` to `VolcanoHybridModel` to reflect its generic nature and update all references in `train.py`, `predict.py`, and `runfile.py`.
