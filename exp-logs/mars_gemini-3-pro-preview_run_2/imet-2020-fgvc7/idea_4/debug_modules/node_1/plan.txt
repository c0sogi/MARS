The execution traceback indicates a `ValidationError` originating from `albumentations`, specifically complaining that the `size` field is required during the initialization of transforms like `RandomResizedCrop`. This is a known breaking change in `albumentations` version 2.0+, where separate `height` and `width` arguments have been replaced by a single `size` tuple argument (Cite debug_lesson_1). To fix this, I will update the `A.Resize` and `A.RandomResizedCrop` instantiations in `library/dataset.py` to use the `size=(Config.IMAGE_SIZE, Config.IMAGE_SIZE)` argument instead of separate dimensions.
