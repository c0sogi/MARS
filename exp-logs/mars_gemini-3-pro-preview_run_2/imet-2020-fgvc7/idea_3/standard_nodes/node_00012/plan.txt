To improve the performance of the multi-label artwork attribute classification, I will implement the following optimization strategy:

1.  **Extend Training Duration**: The previous solution achieved a strong baseline but was limited to only 3 epochs ("Fast Baseline"), resulting in underfitting as evidenced by the steep downward trajectory of the training loss. I will increase the training epochs to 8. This allows the Heterogeneous Ensemble (ConvNeXt + Swin) sufficient time to converge and learn the complex feature distributions of the 3,474 classes (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`).
2.  **Enhance Regularization**: To support the longer training schedule and prevent overfitting, I will increase the intensity of the data augmentation. specifically, I will widen the `RandomResizedCrop` scale from `(0.9, 1.0)` to `(0.75, 1.0)`. This forces the model to learn more robust features from varying scales of the artwork.
3.  **Optimize System Throughput**: I will increase `num_workers` from 8 to 10 to better utilize the available 12 vCPUs, ensuring that data loading does not become a bottleneck during the extended training.
4.  **Update Execution Logic**: I will remove the debug override in `runfile.py` that forced the epoch count to 3 and update the submission threshold to the current best validation score (`0.6117`).
