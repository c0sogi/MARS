Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Iterative-Gaussianized Corrected-Morphology Ensemble (IGCME)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Gaussianized" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the failure of "Spectral Gaussianization" (Lesson 104) and "Non-Parametric Gaussianization" (Lesson 76) by introducing **Iterative Manifold Engineering** and **Constrained Rank Normalization**. Furthermore, it integrates the critical **Polarity Correction** for image data (Lesson 104) to ensure the physical morphometric signal is valid.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that vary in **Gaussianization Topology**, **Covariance Estimation**, and **Feature View**, allowing the Greedy Selector to mathematically determine the optimal density estimation surface.

### 1. The Multi-Topology Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Topology A: Marginal Parametric Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with Shrinkage Library (**OAS**, **Fixed**).
    *   **Mechanism:** Gaussianizes each feature independently.
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline which is robust but ignores non-Gaussian dependencies between features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Topology B: Iterative Parametric Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ `PCA(whiten=False)` $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Mechanism:**
        1.  **Stabilize:** Apply initial Power Transform to marginals.
        2.  **Rotate:** Apply PCA (without whitening) to align data with principal axes of variation.
        3.  **Re-Gaussianize:** Apply a second Power Transform to the Principal Components.
    *   **Rationale:** Lesson 104 failed because "Whitening" (scaling by inverse singular values) amplified noise in the small-sample regime. By using PCA *only* for rotation and letting the second PowerTransformer handle the scaling/distribution, we approximate **Multivariate Normality** (correcting dependencies) without the instability of spectral scaling (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).

*   **Topology C: Constrained Non-Parametric Experts**
    *   **Pipeline:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=50)`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Lesson 76 failed because `n_quantiles` matched the sample size ($N \approx 700$), leading to overfitting. By strictly constraining `n_quantiles` to 50 (~7% of data), we create a **Regularized Rank-Based Normalizer**. This handles skewed features that Yeo-Johnson misses, protected by the low-rank constraint (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering (Polarity-Corrected):**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Morphometric View (Innovation):** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check the corner pixels of the image. If the mean corner value is 1 (white), invert the image so the leaf is always the foreground (value 1). This fixes the invalid shape descriptors generated in previous attempts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariant descriptors) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
    *   **Combined View:** Concatenate Global and Morphometric matrices (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Topology Generation:** Apply the three preprocessing pipelines (Marginal, Iterative, Constrained-Rank) to generate transformed versions of the Global and Combined views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full Cartesian product of (Topologies $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators $\times$ Views) on the 80% split.
        *   *Shrinkage Estimators:* **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`), **Fixed** (`[0.001, 0.01, 0.1]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values, PCA components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometric features for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts (applying the specific Topology transform and `float64` casting).
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
