Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Multi-Scale Discriminative-Interaction Precision-Generative Ensemble (MS-DIPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) and "Precision-Generative" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`) concepts. It addresses the limitation that while the Global Linear LDA model is a "Super-Expert" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`), it inherently misses **non-linear interactions** (e.g., quadratic dependencies). Since standard QDA fails due to sample size constraints (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`) and Kernel methods fail due to noise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00061`), I propose using **Supervised Discriminative Bottlenecks**. By projecting features onto their most class-discriminative axes (using LDA as a transformer) *before* polynomial expansion, we can model quadratic interactions in a dense, optimized subspace. This is applied at both a **Global** scale and a **Stratified** (Component-wise) scale to capture different resolutions of biological dependency.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Topologies** and **Interaction Scales**, allowing the ensemble to exploit Linear, Rotational, Physical, and Discriminative-Interaction signals simultaneously.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Group A: Global Linear Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
    *   **Topologies:**
        1.  **Marginal:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`. (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`)
        2.  **Rotational:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`. This aligns data with principal axes without the noise amplification of Whitening (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, modeling global linear correlations with robust covariance estimation.

*   **Group B: Physical Polynomial Experts (The Domain Signal)**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
        *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
    *   **Rationale:** Captures non-linear physical constraints (e.g., $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) from raw images, treating them as an orthogonal view (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

*   **Group C: Global Discriminative-Interaction Experts (Holistic Non-Linearity)**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **LDA_Transformer(n_components=15)** $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Explicitly models quadratic interactions between global features. The initial **LDA Transformer** projects data onto the 15 most **class-discriminative** axes. The subsequent polynomial expansion generates $\approx 135$ interaction features. Since $D < N$, the final LDA solver can stably find quadratic decision boundaries in this optimized subspace (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00064` context).

*   **Group D: Stratified Discriminative-Interaction Experts (Component Non-Linearity)**
    *   **Input:** Split Global View into **Margin**, **Shape**, and **Texture** subsets.
    *   **Pipeline:**
        1.  **Stratified Projection:** Apply `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **LDA_Transformer(n=9)** to each subset independently.
        2.  **Concatenation:** Combine the three projected vectors ($9 \times 3 = 27$ dims).
        3.  **Interaction Expansion:** Apply **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)**.
        4.  **Re-Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Models interactions *between* the discriminative essences of different biological domains (e.g., "Margin Discriminant 1" $\times$ "Texture Discriminant 1"). This captures complex cross-domain dependencies that the Global Linear model misses, without the noise of raw feature interactions.

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Stratified Views:** Slice Global into Margin (0-63), Shape (64-127), Texture (128-191).
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipelines:** Apply the specific transformation sequences (Marginal, Rotational, Global-Interaction, Stratified-Interaction, Poly-Physical) to generate the feature views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (PowerTransformer, PCA, LDA-Transformer) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Groups A-D $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values, n_components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
