Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Full-Rank Stratified-Manifold Precision-Generative Ensemble (FR-SMPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) and "Stratified-Rotational" (Idea 63) concepts. It directly addresses the critical failure identified in Lesson 129: that dimensionality reduction (bottlenecks)—even when discriminative—degrades performance in high-dimensional, small-sample regimes compared to full-rank regularization.

Instead of compressing feature groups to force interactions (which causes information loss), this solution constructs a library of **Full-Rank Manifold Views**. We apply **Stratified Rotational Alignment**—rotating each biological domain (Margin, Shape, Texture) into its own principal axes *without truncation*—to provide the Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) solver with optimally Gaussianized manifolds. Additionally, we integrate **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** modeled via **Regularized QDA**, exploiting the low dimensionality of the physical view to capture non-linear shape constraints without the instability of global QDA.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Topologies** and **Feature Scopes**, strictly maintaining **Double Precision** and **Full Rank** (no bottlenecks).

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

**Group A: Global Statistical Anchors (The Baseline)**
*   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
*   **Topologies:**
    1.  **Marginal:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`. Stabilizes variance feature-wise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
    2.  **Global Rotational:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`. Aligns the global data with its principal axes *without* the noise amplification of Whitening (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).
    3.  **Robust:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=50)`. Strictly constrained rank-based normalization (~7% of $N$) to handle skew without the overfitting observed in Lesson 76 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).
*   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
*   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, modeling global linear correlations.

**Group B: Stratified Rotational Experts (The Refinement)**
*   **Input:** Split Global View into **Margin**, **Shape**, and **Texture** subsets.
*   **Pipeline:**
    1.  **Independent Rotation:** Apply `Power` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `Power` to each 64-feature subset independently. Crucially, **do not truncate components** (keep all 64).
    2.  **Concatenation:** Recombine the three transformed views into a Full-Rank (192-dim) matrix.
*   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
*   **Rationale:** Prevents high-variance feature groups (e.g., Texture) from dominating the rotation of lower-variance groups (e.g., Margin). By aligning each biological manifold independently, we ensure that the LDA solver receives a representation where *all* domains are optimally Gaussianized, avoiding the information loss of bottlenecks (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00129`, `solution_lesson_node_00109`).

**Group C: Physical Non-Linear Experts (The Domain Signal)**
*   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
    *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
*   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` (Yeo-Johnson).
*   **Algorithm:** **Regularized Quadratic Discriminant Analysis (QDA)** (`reg_param` grid `[0.1, 0.5]`).
*   **Rationale:** Unlike the high-dimensional global view, the physical view is low-dimensional ($D \approx 11$). This allows us to stably estimate **class-specific covariance matrices** (QDA), capturing non-linear shape constraints (heteroscedasticity) that Linear models miss, without the overfitting seen in Global QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`, `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global/Stratified Views:** Load 192 features; slice into Global, Margin, Shape, Texture.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Application:** Apply the specific transformation sequences to generate the candidate feature matrices.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Power, PCA, Quantile) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full library of experts (Groups A-C $\times$ Shrinkage/Regularization Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
