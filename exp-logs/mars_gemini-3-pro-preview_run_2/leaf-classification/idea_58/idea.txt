Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Stratified-Topology Precision-Generative Ensemble (STPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) and "Component-Interaction" (Idea 57) concepts. It addresses the limitation that previous solutions applied advanced preprocessing topologies (like Rotational Alignment) only to the Global view, potentially ignoring that distinct biological traits (Margin, Shape, Texture) may require different geometric corrections. By systematically applying a matrix of **Preprocessing Topologies** (Marginal, Rotational, Robust) to **Stratified Feature Subsets**, and integrating **Discriminative Interaction Bottlenecks**, we construct a library of experts that can model the unique manifold structure of each biological domain. This is strictly managed by **Greedy Forward Selection** to prevent overfitting.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by the Cartesian product of **Feature Scopes** and **Preprocessing Topologies**.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

**Dimension A: Feature Scopes**
We define five distinct views of the data:
1.  **Global:** All 192 provided features.
2.  **Physical:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars). (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`, `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
3.  **Margin:** The 64 margin features.
4.  **Shape:** The 64 shape features.
5.  **Texture:** The 64 texture features.

**Dimension B: Preprocessing Topologies**
For the Global and Semantic (Margin, Shape, Texture) scopes, we apply three distinct geometric transformations:
1.  **Marginal Topology:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Role:** Stabilizes variance feature-wise. The proven baseline (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
2.  **Rotational Topology:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Role:** Aligns the subspace with its principal axes *without* noise-amplifying whitening. This approximates Multivariate Normality better than marginal scaling (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`). Applying this to *strata* (e.g., Texture) aligns the texture manifold independently of shape.
3.  **Robust Topology:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=50)`.
    *   **Role:** Strictly constrained rank-based normalization (using ~7% of $N$). Handles skewed distributions that Yeo-Johnson misses without overfitting (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).

**Dimension C: Interaction Topologies**
We introduce specialized experts to capture non-linear dependencies:
1.  **Physical-Polynomial:** Apply `Poly(degree=2)` to the Physical view. Captures non-linear geometric constraints.
2.  **Stratified Discriminative-Interaction:** For each Semantic Scope (Margin, Shape, Texture):
    *   **Pipeline:** `Power` $\rightarrow$ **LDA_Transformer(n=12)** $\rightarrow$ **Poly(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `Power`.
    *   **Role:** Projects the component onto its 12 most class-discriminative axes, then models quadratic interactions. This creates a "Bottleneck QDA" expert that captures complex intra-domain patterns (e.g., non-linear texture interactions) without the dimensionality curse (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`, `solution_lesson_node_00064`).

**Estimator:** All pipelines feed into **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global/Semantic Views:** Load 192 features; slice into Global, Margin, Shape, Texture.
    *   **Physical View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Application:** Apply the specific Topologies to the specific Scopes to generate the candidate feature matrices.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Power, PCA, LDA-Transformer, Quantile) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full library of experts (Scopes $\times$ Topologies $\times$ Shrinkage) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
