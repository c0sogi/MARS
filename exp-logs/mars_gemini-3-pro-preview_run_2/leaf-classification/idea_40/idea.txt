Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Spectral-Gaussianized Dynamic Generative Ensemble (SGDGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Gaussianized" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the fundamental limitation of the "Marginal Gaussianization" used in previous baselines (applying `PowerTransformer` to features independently), which does not guarantee the **Multivariate Normality** required by LDA. By introducing a **Spectral Gaussianization** stream—which decorrelates the data via Whitening PCA *before* applying the Power Transform—we provide the generative models with a feature space that better approximates the joint distribution assumptions. This is managed alongside the proven Marginal and Constrained-Rank streams by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that vary in **Gaussianization Topology** (Marginal vs. Spectral vs. Rank-Based) and **Covariance Estimation**, allowing the Greedy Selector to mathematically determine the optimal density estimation surface.

### 1. The Multi-Topology Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Topology A: Marginal Parametric Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Preprocessing:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with Shrinkage Library (OAS, Ledoit-Wolf, Fixed).
    *   **Mechanism:** Gaussianizes each feature independently.
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Topology B: Spectral Parametric Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Preprocessing:** `StandardScaler` $\rightarrow$ `PCA(whiten=True)` $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Mechanism:** First decorrelates the data and scales to unit variance (Whitening), then applies the Power Transform to the **Principal Components**.
    *   **Rationale:** LDA assumes the data follows a Multivariate Gaussian distribution. Marginal Gaussianization (Topology A) only guarantees that individual features are Gaussian; it does not correct non-Gaussian dependencies *between* features. By rotating the data to its principal axes (where components are uncorrelated) and *then* Gaussianizing, we achieve a superior approximation of **Multivariate Normality**, potentially unlocking performance gains blocked by the "Marginal" assumption.

*   **Topology C: Constrained Non-Parametric Experts**
    *   **Preprocessing:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=50)`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Strictly constrained rank-based normalization. Handles skewed distributions that Yeo-Johnson cannot fit, with `n_quantiles` constrained to ~7% of $N$ to prevent the overfitting observed in Lesson 76 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss. This ensures that if the Spectral view is superior, it is prioritized; if the Marginal view is sufficient (Lesson 90), the ensemble converges to it (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Combined View:** Load raw binary images. Extract **Deterministic** Morphometrics (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars) and concatenate with Global features. We include this to allow the selector to resolve the conflict between Lesson 50 (Geometry helps) and Lesson 90 (Geometry is redundant).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Topology Generation:** Apply the three preprocessing pipelines (Marginal, Spectral, Constrained-Rank) to generate transformed versions of the Global and Combined views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Scaler, PCA, PowerTransformer) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full Cartesian product of (Topologies $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators $\times$ Views) on the 80% split.
        *   *Shrinkage Estimators:* OAS (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`), Ledoit-Wolf, Fixed (`[0.001, 0.01, 0.1]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values, PCA components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Morphometric features for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts (applying the specific Topology transform and `float64` casting).
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
