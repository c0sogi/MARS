This strategy outlines the implementation of a Precision-Covariance Multi-Resolution Ensemble (PCMRE) pipeline for leaf classification. The process begins by initializing the configuration and ensuring reproducibility. We then instantiate the `LeafDataManager` to load metadata and generate features, specifically extracting "Macro" features (Hu moments and geometric properties) from the binary images and combining them with pre-extracted shape, margin, and texture features. A `ModelFactory` generates a library of experts ranging from Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) with various shrinkage parameters to Quadratic Discriminant Analysis (QDA) and Gaussian Naive Bayes, each assigned to specific feature views (Global, Macro, or Combined). An `EnsembleSelector` is then used to perform Greedy Forward Selection on the validation set to determine the optimal composition of experts. Finally, the selected experts are retrained on the full training and validation data, and their predictions on the test set are aggregated to produce the final submission file. Assertions are placed throughout to verify data integrity, model availability, and output validity.
