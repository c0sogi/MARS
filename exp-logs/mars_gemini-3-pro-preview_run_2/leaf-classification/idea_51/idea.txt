Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Discriminative-Subspace Projected-Generative Ensemble (DSPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Idea 45, Lesson 109) and "Precision-Generative" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the limitation of Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) in capturing **non-linear decision boundaries** in high-dimensional space without succumbing to the instability of QDA (Lesson 94). By introducing a **Discriminative-Subspace Topology**—which projects global features onto the most class-discriminative axes (using LDA as a transformer) *before* applying polynomial expansion—we create a "Bottleneck Quadratic" expert. This expert models interactions specifically on the axes that matter for classification, integrated into a library managed by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Topologies**, allowing the ensemble to exploit Marginal, Rotational, Physical, and Discriminative-Interaction signals simultaneously.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Topology A: Marginal Statistical Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, utilizing the full high-dimensional statistical signal with robust covariance estimation (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Topology B: Rotational Statistical Experts (The Alignment)**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Aligns the data with its principal axes of variation *without* the noise-amplifying effects of Whitening (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`). This provides a superior approximation of the Multivariate Normal assumption required by LDA.

*   **Topology C: Discriminative-Subspace Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **LDA(n_components=15)** $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Explicitly models quadratic interactions between global features. Unlike PCA (Variance-based), the initial **LDA Transformer** projects data onto the 15 most **class-discriminative** axes. The subsequent polynomial expansion generates $\approx 135$ interaction features. Since $D < N$, the final LDA solver can stably find quadratic decision boundaries in this optimized subspace, bridging the gap between Linear LDA and unstable QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`).

*   **Topology D: Polynomial Physical Experts (The Domain Signal)**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
        *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
    *   **Rationale:** Captures non-linear physical constraints (e.g., $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) from the raw images, treating them as an orthogonal view (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`, `solution_lesson_node_00109`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipelines:** Apply the specific transformation sequences (Marginal, Rotational, Discriminative-Subspace, Poly-Physical) to generate the feature views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (PowerTransformer, PCA, LDA-Transformer) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Topologies A-D $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values, n_components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
