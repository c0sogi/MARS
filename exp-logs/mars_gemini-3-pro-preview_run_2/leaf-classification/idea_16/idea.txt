Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Dynamic Multi-View Ensemble Selection (DMVES)** strategy.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Multi-View" (Idea 10) and "Soft Voting" (Idea 2, 6, 15) approaches. It addresses the critical tension between **Ensemble Diversity** (needing different views/models) and **Ensemble Dilution** (Lesson 9, where weak models degrade the average). Instead of "Blind Soft Voting" or "Overfit Stacking" (Lesson 34), it employs **Greedy Forward Selection** to mathematically construct the optimal sub-ensemble from a pool of calibrated experts.

# Model
The solution utilizes a two-stage architecture: **Expert Generation** and **Dynamic Selection**.

1.  **Stage 1: Expert Pool Generation (9 Candidates)**
    We generate a diverse pool of estimators by varying both the **Feature View** and the **Inductive Bias**.
    *   **View Definitions:**
        *   **Global View:** All 192 features.
        *   **Local Views:** `Margin` (64 feats), `Shape` (64 feats), `Texture` (64 feats).
    *   **Estimator Types:**
        *   **Discriminative Linear:** `LogisticRegressionCV` with L2 regularization. Trained on *all 4 views* (Global, Margin, Shape, Texture). This allows for **Decoupled Regularization**, where the Texture view can be regularized heavily (high $C$) while the Shape view is regularized lightly, optimizing signal extraction per domain (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00047`).
        *   **Generative Linear:** `LDA` with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage. Trained on *all 4 views*. This provides sample-efficient density estimation (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00006`).
        *   **Discriminative Non-Linear (Global Only):** A **Random Forest Classifier**.
            *   **Correction:** To avoid the calibration failure of previous non-linear models (SVM, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`), this model is wrapped in `CalibratedClassifierCV` (Isotonic Regression). This adds a distinct tree-based inductive bias to capture interactions while ensuring Log Loss optimization.

2.  **Stage 2: Greedy Ensemble Selection**
    *   Instead of averaging all 9 models (Risk of Dilution) or training a Meta-Learner (Risk of Stacking Overfitting, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00034`), we employ **Greedy Forward Selection** (Caruana et al.) on the validation set.
    *   **Mechanism:** Start with the single best model (lowest Val Log Loss). Iteratively add the model from the pool that maximizes the improvement in the ensemble's Val Log Loss. This empirically determines the optimal mixing weights without learning parameters, adhering to the lesson "Evaluate based on contribution, not standalone performance" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Selection:** Utilize the **192 extracted features** exclusively. We reject raw images (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00049`) and coarse geometric scalars (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`) to maintain the high signal-to-noise ratio of the provided descriptors.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Global Scaling:** Apply `StandardScaler` to all feature views. Explicitly avoid non-linear transformers (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00003`).
    *   **View Slicing:** Programmatically slice the 192 columns into the Global, Margin, Shape, and Texture subsets.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to strictly prevent leakage and maximize data usage (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase A: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Train all 9 candidates on the **Training Set** (80%).
    *   **Logistic Regression Config:** `LogisticRegressionCV`, `scoring='neg_log_loss'` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00031`), `Cs=np.logspace(-4, 4, 100)` (Dense Grid, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00047`), `solver='lbfgs'`.
    *   **LDA Config:** Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage.
    *   **RF Config:** `n_estimators=500`, wrapped in `CalibratedClassifierCV(cv=3)`.
    *   Generate probability predictions on the **Validation Set** (20%).
    *   Run **Greedy Forward Selection** on the Validation predictions to identify the optimal subset of models and their integer weights (e.g., 2x Global-LR + 1x Shape-LDA).

2.  **Phase B: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected models on the **Combined Train + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., optimal `C`) found in Phase A to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Process the test set to generate the required feature views.
    *   Pass the features through the retrained selected models.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined by the Greedy Selection step.
    *   This ensures the final submission is a robust, calibrated ensemble that includes weak views (e.g., Texture) *only* if they mathematically improve the Log Loss.
