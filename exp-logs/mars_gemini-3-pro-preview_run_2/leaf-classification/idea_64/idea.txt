Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Factorized-Bottleneck Precision-Generative Ensemble (FBPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) and "Discriminative-Subspace" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00064` context) concepts. It addresses the limitation that the "Super-Expert" Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) model (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`) inherently misses **non-linear interactions** between biological traits (e.g., how "Margin" serration couples with "Texture" entropy). Since global polynomial expansion is computationally infeasible ($D \gg N$) and standard QDA is unstable (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`), I propose a **Factorized-Bottleneck Topology**. This approach projects semantic feature groups into low-dimensional discriminative subspaces *before* computing quadratic interactions, creating a dense, stable "Interaction View" managed by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Topologies** and **Interaction Scopes**, allowing the ensemble to exploit Global Linear, Physical Non-Linear, and Factorized Interaction signals simultaneously.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Group A: Global Statistical Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
    *   **Topologies:**
        1.  **Marginal:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`. Stabilizes variance feature-wise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
        2.  **Rotational:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`. Aligns data with principal axes without the noise amplification of Whitening, approximating Multivariate Normality (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).
        3.  **Robust:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=50)`. Strictly constrained rank-based normalization (~7% of $N$) to handle skew without overfitting (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, modeling global linear correlations.

*   **Group B: Physical Polynomial Experts (The Domain Signal)**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
        *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
    *   **Role:** Captures non-linear physical constraints (e.g., $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) from raw images (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

*   **Group C: Factorized-Bottleneck Interaction Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** Split features into four semantic groups: **Margin** (64), **Shape** (64), **Texture** (64), and **Physical** (11).
    *   **Pipeline:**
        1.  **Discriminative Bottleneck:** Apply `Power` $\rightarrow$ **LDA_Transformer(n_components=5)** to each group independently. This projects each domain onto its 5 most class-discriminative axes.
        2.  **Concatenation:** Combine the projected vectors ($5 \times 4 = 20$ dims).
        3.  **Interaction Expansion:** Apply **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)**. This generates $\approx 230$ quadratic terms representing interactions *within* and *between* biological domains (e.g., $Texture\_Discriminant \times Physical\_Discriminant$).
        4.  **Re-Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Explicitly models quadratic dependencies in a dense, optimized subspace. Since $D_{poly} \approx 230 < N \approx 700$, the final LDA solver can stably find quadratic decision boundaries, bridging the gap between Linear LDA and unstable QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`, `solution_lesson_node_00109`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global/Semantic Views:** Load 192 features; slice into Global, Margin, Shape, Texture.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Application:** Apply the specific transformation sequences to generate the candidate feature matrices.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Power, PCA, LDA-Transformer, Quantile) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full library of experts (Groups A-C $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values, n_components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
