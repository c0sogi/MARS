Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Rotational-Polynomial Precision-Generative Ensemble (RP-PGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Gaussianized" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the failure of "Spectral Gaussianization" (Lesson 104) by replacing noise-amplifying Whitening with **Variance-Preserving Rotation**. Furthermore, it resolves the tension between "Physical Features Help" (Lesson 50) and "QDA Overfits" (Lesson 94) by applying **Polynomial Expansion** to **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Lesson 106), allowing robust Linear solvers to capture non-linear shape constraints.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that vary in **Gaussianization Topology** (Marginal vs. Rotational) and **Feature Complexity** (Linear vs. Polynomial), allowing the Greedy Selector to mathematically determine the optimal density estimation surface.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Group A: Marginal Statistical Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
    *   **Preprocessing:** **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, utilizing the high-dimensional statistical signal with robust covariance estimation (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Group B: Rotational Statistical Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Preprocessing:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Lesson 104 failed because "Whitening" (scaling by inverse singular values) amplified noise. Here, we use PCA **strictly for Rotation**. By aligning the data with its principal axes, we satisfy the "uncorrelated" assumption of the LDA shrinkage target (Identity Matrix) more effectively than in the original space. The second PowerTransformer ensures the Principal Components themselves are Gaussianized. This approximates **Multivariate Normality** without the instability of spectral scaling (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).

*   **Group C: Polynomial Physical Experts (The Synergy)**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
        *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
    *   **Preprocessing:** **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
    *   **Rationale:** Captures non-linear physical constraints (e.g., $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) that linear models miss. By using Polynomial Expansion + LDA, we model quadratic boundaries using a stable linear solver, avoiding the overfitting of QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`) while exploiting the domain-specific signal (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Physical View:** Load raw binary images. Apply **Polarity Correction** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`). Extract **Hu Moments** and **Geometric Scalars** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipelines:** Apply the specific transformation sequence (Marginal, Rotational, Polynomial) to the respective views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Marginal LDA, Rotational LDA, Poly-Physical LDA) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
