Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Stratified-Covariance Projected-Generative Ensemble (SCPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Multi-Resolution" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the final theoretical gap in the previous solutions: the reliance on **Linear (Shared-Covariance)** boundaries for the high-dimensional global features. While LDA is a "Super-Expert" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`), it assumes all species have identical shape variance (homoscedasticity). By introducing **Principal Component QDA (PC-QDA)** experts, we allow the ensemble to model **Class-Specific Covariance** (heteroscedasticity) on the global feature set, strictly managed by dimensionality reduction and Greedy Selection.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that span the **Covariance Complexity Spectrum**—from Diagonal to Shared to Class-Specific—applied to appropriate projections of the data.

### 1. The Stratified Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`) and utilize **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson) preprocessing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Tier 1: Shared-Covariance Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 features) and Combined View (192 + Morphometrics).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with a library of shrinkage estimators: **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`), **Ledoit-Wolf** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`), and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Provides robust, low-variance density estimation assuming homoscedasticity.

*   **Tier 2: Global Class-Specific Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 features).
    *   **Architecture:** **PCA** (Retain Top $K$ Components, e.g., $K=40$) $\rightarrow$ **Regularized Quadratic Discriminant Analysis (QDA)**.
    *   **Rationale:** Standard QDA fails on the global view because $P=192$ exceeds the sample size per class. By projecting the Gaussianized features into a dense Principal Subspace where $D < N_{class}$, we enable the stable estimation of **class-specific covariance matrices**. This allows the model to capture non-linear quadratic boundaries derived from the *main* texture/shape signal, not just the auxiliary macro features.
    *   **Configuration:** `reg_param` grid `[0.1, 0.5, 0.9]` to ensure stability.

*   **Tier 3: Macro Class-Specific Experts**
    *   **Input:** **Macro-Resolution View** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Regularized QDA**.
    *   **Role:** Captures heteroscedasticity in the explicit physical shape descriptors (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`, `solution_lesson_node_00029`).

*   **Tier 4: Diagonal Covariance Anchors**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Gaussian Naive Bayes (GNB)**.
    *   **Role:** High-bias failsafe assuming feature independence.

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss. This ensures that the quadratic experts (Tier 2/3) are included only if their capacity to model variance outweighs their estimation noise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Macro-Resolution:** Load raw binary images. Extract **Deterministic** Morphometrics: **Hu Moments** (7 invariant descriptors) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity). We explicitly reject Monte-Carlo augmentation (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00089`) and ImageNet features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00049`).
    *   **Micro-Resolution:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Combined:** Concatenate Macro and Micro matrices.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64`. This is mandatory for minimizing Log Loss near the theoretical floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')` to all views. This forces the skewed geometric scalars and non-normal histograms to conform to the Gaussian assumption of the generative models (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (LDA, PC-QDA, Macro-QDA, GNB) on their respective views using the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage, regularization, PCA components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Macro-Resolution features for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts (applying `PowerTransformer`, `PCA` where applicable, and `float64` casting).
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
