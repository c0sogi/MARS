My optimization strategy focuses on refining the expert library to exploit the high linear separability of the Gaussianized features, based on Lessons 00063, 00072, and 00081.

1.  **Expand LDA Shrinkage Grid (Cite Lesson 00081):** The previous solution's success with `LDA_Global_OAS` and the lesson's finding that fixed shrinkage (e.g., 0.01) can outperform analytical methods (Ledoit-Wolf/OAS) suggests that a denser grid of shrinkage values is critical for hitting the log-loss floor. I will expand the `LDA_SHRINKAGE_GRID` in `library/config.py` to include a wider range of fixed values.
2.  **Introduce Combined Feature View (Cite Lesson 00063):** Lesson 00063 emphasizes concatenating distinct feature sets into a single global matrix for LDA to leverage cross-correlations. I will modify `library/config.py` to generate experts trained on the `combined` view (Global + Morphological) and update `runfile.py` to handle this new view.
3.  **Remove QDA Experts (Cite Lesson 00072):** Given the small dataset size and the effectiveness of LDA on Gaussianized data, QDA experts are likely to overfit and were not selected in previous iterations. I will remove them to save computational resources and focus on the robust linear experts.
