Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Factorized-Interaction Precision-Generative Ensemble (FIPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) and "Discriminative-Subspace" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00064`, context) concepts. It addresses the fundamental limitation that the Global Linear "Super-Expert" (LDA) cannot capture **non-linear interactions** between biological traits (e.g., how "Margin" serration relates to "Texture" entropy). Since Global Polynomial expansion is computationally infeasible ($D \gg N$) and standard QDA is unstable (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`), I propose a **Factorized Interaction Topology**. This explicitly models cross-domain dependencies by projecting semantic feature groups into low-dimensional discriminative bottlenecks *before* computing interactions. This is integrated into a **Multi-Basis Library** managed by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by the Cartesian product of **Feature Scopes** (Global, Physical, Factorized) and **Gaussian Bases** (Parametric, Rotational, Robust).

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

**Dimension A: Feature Scopes**
1.  **Global Linear Scope (The Baseline):**
    *   **Input:** The 192 provided features.
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, modeling global linear correlations (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`).
2.  **Physical Polynomial Scope (The Domain Signal):**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
        *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
    *   **Expansion:** `PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)`.
    *   **Role:** Captures non-linear physical constraints (e.g., $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) from raw images (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
3.  **Factorized Interaction Scope (The Innovation):**
    *   **Mechanism:**
        1.  **Decomposition:** Split Global features into **Margin**, **Shape**, and **Texture**.
        2.  **Discriminative Bottleneck:** Train three independent **LDA Transformers** to project each group onto its top $k=10$ class-discriminative axes ($z_m, z_s, z_t$).
        3.  **Interaction Synthesis:** Compute pairwise interactions ($z_m \otimes z_s$, $z_m \otimes z_t$, $z_s \otimes z_t$) using polynomial expansion.
    *   **Role:** Explicitly models quadratic dependencies *between* biological domains (e.g., "Serrated Margin" coupled with "Rough Texture") in a dense, stable subspace ($D \approx 300 < N$), bridging the gap between Linear LDA and unstable QDA.

**Dimension B: Gaussian Bases (Preprocessing)**
For each Scope, we apply three distinct geometric transformations to generate "Orthogonal Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`):
1.  **Marginal Basis:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`. Stabilizes variance feature-wise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
2.  **Rotational Basis:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`. Aligns data with principal axes *without* the noise amplification of Whitening, approximating Multivariate Normality (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).
3.  **Robust Basis:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=50)`. Strictly constrained rank-based normalization (~7% of $N$) to handle skew without the overfitting observed in Lesson 76 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).

**Estimator:** All pipelines feed into **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global/Component Views:** Load 192 features; slice into Global, Margin, Shape, Texture.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Application:** Apply the Cartesian product of Scopes and Bases to generate the candidate feature matrices.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Power, PCA, LDA-Transformer, Quantile) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full library of experts (Scopes $\times$ Bases $\times$ Shrinkage) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
