Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Probabilistic-Morphology Augmented Generative Library (PMAGL)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Diverse-Covariance" (Idea 23) and "Monte-Carlo" (Idea 25) concepts. It addresses the inherent measurement noise in binary shape extraction by shifting from static feature engineering to **Probabilistic Feature Engineering**. Furthermore, it introduces **View-Specific Complexity** by allowing the ensemble to utilize higher-capacity models (Regularized QDA) specifically on the low-dimensional morphological view, where the sample-to-feature ratio permits it, while retaining the robust Linear LDA baseline for the high-dimensional global view.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of experts that vary in **Inductive Bias** (Linear vs. Quadratic), **Covariance Estimation** (Analytical vs. Fixed), and **Feature View** (Global vs. Probabilistic-Morphological).

### 1. Expert Library Generation
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`) and utilize **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson) preprocessing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Group A: The Global Linear Anchors**
    *   **Input:** Provided 192 features (Margin, Shape, Texture).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with a spectrum of shrinkage estimators: **Ledoit-Wolf** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`), **OAS**, and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01, 0.1]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Provides robust, low-variance density estimation on the high-dimensional view.

*   **Group B: The Probabilistic Morphological Experts (Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** Newly engineered **Probabilistic Morphometric Features** (Mean and Std of geometric descriptors over augmentations).
    *   **Algorithms:**
        *   **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf, Fixed).
        *   **Regularized Quadratic Discriminant Analysis (QDA):** Unlike the global view ($P=192 \gg N_{class}$), this view has low dimensionality ($P \approx 20$). This allows us to deploy **QDA** (with regularization `reg_param`) to model class-specific covariance matrices (heteroscedasticity) and capture non-linear shape constraints, which LDA misses.
    *   **Role:** Exploits the physical signal with higher model complexity, protected by the low dimensionality of this specific view.

*   **Group C: The Synergistic Experts**
    *   **Input:** Concatenation of Global + Probabilistic Morphometric features.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf, Fixed).
    *   **Role:** Captures cross-covariance between texture histograms and shape stability.

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss. This ensures that if the QDA experts are unstable, they are discarded; if they provide valuable curvature, they are included (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering (Structural Innovation: Probabilistic Morphometrics):**
    *   **Source:** Raw binary images.
    *   **Monte-Carlo Augmentation:** For each image, generate $K=10$ affine perturbations (Rotation, Scale, Shear).
    *   **Extraction:** Compute **Hu Moments** (7 invariant descriptors) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) for all $K$ variations (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
    *   **Aggregation:** Calculate the **Mean** and **Standard Deviation** of these descriptors. The "Mean" captures the robust shape, while the "Std" captures the **stability** of the shape under perturbation, adding a novel signal orthogonal to static descriptors.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Precision:** Cast all data to `float64` to prevent numerical noise in density calculations (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')` to all views. This is critical for maximizing the performance of both LDA and QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (LDA variants on all views; QDA variants on Morph view) using the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Apply the Monte-Carlo Augmentation pipeline to the Test images to generate Probabilistic Morphometric features.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
    *   This strategy combines the stability of the Linear Baseline with the robustness of Probabilistic Features and the flexibility of View-Specific QDA.
