The execution failure in `runfile.py` is caused by aggressive subsampling in the `demo_models` function. The code randomly selects only 50 samples to train the models, but the dataset contains 99 unique plant species. This violates the requirements for cross-validation (specifically `LogisticRegressionCV` with `cv=2`), as it is mathematically impossible to split classes with 0 or 1 sample into training and validation folds. This leads to the `ValueError: cannot reshape array...` when the internal cross-validation loop attempts to aggregate results from inconsistent folds (Cite debug_lesson_2).

To fix this, I will remove the subsampling logic in `demo_models` and use the full `X_sample` (712 rows) provided to the function. This dataset size is small enough for the demo (especially with the patched `max_iter` and `cv` parameters) and ensures that the class distribution remains sufficient for the `cv=2` split (Cite debug_lesson_1).
