Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Hierarchical Discriminative-Manifold Ensemble (HDME)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Factorized-Interaction" (Idea 59) and "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Idea 45, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) concepts. It addresses the limitation that applying transformations solely to the "Global" view allows high-variance features (e.g., Texture) to dominate the manifold structure, potentially obscuring subtle signals in other domains (Margin/Shape).

By introducing **Stratified Rotational Alignment**—aligning the principal axes of each semantic feature group independently—and coupling this with a **Hierarchical Interaction Topology** that models both intra-domain and inter-domain non-linearities via discriminative bottlenecks, we construct a library of experts that mathematically spans the full complexity of the biological signal. This is strictly managed by **Greedy Forward Selection** to prevent overfitting.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Topologies** and **Interaction Hierarchies**.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

**Group A: Global Statistical Anchors (The Baseline)**
*   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
*   **Topologies:**
    1.  **Marginal:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`. Stabilizes variance feature-wise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
    2.  **Global Rotational:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`. Aligns the global data with its principal axes without the noise amplification of Whitening (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).
*   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
*   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, modeling global linear correlations.

**Group B: Stratified Rotational Experts (The Refinement)**
*   **Input:** Split Global View into **Margin**, **Shape**, and **Texture**.
*   **Topology:** Apply the **Rotational Pipeline** (`Power` $\rightarrow$ `PCA(NoWhiten)` $\rightarrow$ `Power`) to *each subset independently*, then concatenate.
*   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
*   **Rationale:** Prevents high-variance groups (Texture) from distorting the rotation of lower-variance groups (Margin). This ensures each biological domain is optimally Gaussianized within its own manifold before density estimation.

**Group C: Hierarchical Interaction Experts (The Non-Linearity)**
*   **Level 1: Intra-Domain Interactions:**
    *   **Pipeline:** For each subset (Margin, Shape, Texture), apply `Power` $\rightarrow$ **LDA_Transformer(n=10)** $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `Power`.
    *   **Rationale:** Projects each domain onto its discriminative axes and models local quadratic patterns (e.g., complex texture gradients).
*   **Level 2: Inter-Domain Interactions:**
    *   **Pipeline:** Concatenate pairwise LDA-projected subsets (e.g., Margin+Texture) $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `Power`.
    *   **Rationale:** Models coupling between domains (e.g., how Margin serration interacts with Texture entropy) in a dense subspace ($D \approx 200 < N$), bridging the gap to QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`).
*   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage.

**Group D: Physical Polynomial Experts (The Domain Signal)**
*   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
    *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
*   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
*   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
*   **Rationale:** Captures non-linear physical constraints orthogonal to the histograms (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global/Stratified Views:** Load 192 features; slice into Global, Margin, Shape, Texture.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Application:** Apply the specific transformation sequences to generate the candidate feature matrices.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Power, PCA, LDA-Transformer) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full library of experts (Groups A-D $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values, n_components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
