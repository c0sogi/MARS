Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Deep-Feature Augmented Multi-View Ensemble**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Multi-View" concept (Idea 10) and the "Linear Baseline" success (Idea 13) by introducing a **Deep Representation Learning** branch. Instead of relying solely on the provided handcrafted features (which are excellent but limited), we will leverage the raw images using Transfer Learning to extract high-level semantic embeddings. These embeddings will be treated as a distinct "view" of the data, processed by robust linear solvers, and ensembled with the successful handcrafted baseline.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Soft-Voting Ensemble** composed of three estimators operating on two distinct feature views. This design combines the proven reliability of the handcrafted features with the rich, uncompressed signal from the raw images, all managed by calibrated linear models.

1.  **View 1: Handcrafted Features (The Anchor)**
    *   **Input:** The 192 extracted features (Margin, Shape, Texture).
    *   **Estimator A: Discriminative Linear:** A **Logistic Regression** model with L2 regularization.
        *   **Configuration:** Uses `LogisticRegressionCV` with a **dense, broad search grid** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00047`, `solution_lesson_node_00037`) and explicitly optimizes `neg_log_loss` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00031`).
    *   **Estimator B: Generative Linear:** A **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** model.
        *   **Role:** Provides superior sample efficiency and density estimation, acting as a stabilizer (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00006`).
        *   **Configuration:** Uses **Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage**.

2.  **View 2: Deep Visual Embeddings (The Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** Raw binary leaf images.
    *   **Feature Extractor:** A pre-trained **ResNet18** (on ImageNet).
        *   **Rationale:** While the provided features are strong, they are handcrafted summaries. A CNN backbone can capture complex global geometries and local textures that histograms miss. We use a pre-trained model as a fixed feature extractor to avoid the overfitting risks of training a CNN from scratch on $N \approx 700$ samples (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00001`).
    *   **Preprocessing:** `Resize(224, 224)` $\rightarrow$ `ResNet18 (AvgPool Output)` $\rightarrow$ `StandardScaler` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00003`) $\rightarrow$ **PCA** (Retain 95% Variance).
        *   **PCA Role:** The raw embeddings (512 dims) are high-dimensional relative to the sample size. PCA densifies this signal and removes noise, ensuring the downstream linear solver works in a favorable $N/D$ regime.
    *   **Estimator C: Discriminative Linear:** A **Logistic Regression** model with L2 regularization.
        *   **Configuration:** Identical rigorous setup as Estimator A (Dense Grid, Log Loss scoring). We apply a linear solver on top of the deep embeddings to maintain the robust, calibrated behavior established in previous lessons.

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Handcrafted View:** Load the 192 features from CSVs.
    *   **Deep View:** Load raw images, convert to 3-channel (duplicate grayscale), and pass through the ResNet18 backbone (in evaluation mode) to extract 512-dimensional vectors.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Global Scaling:** Apply `StandardScaler` to both views independently. Explicitly avoid non-linear transformers like `QuantileTransformer` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00003`).
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** All Scalers and PCA transformers must be fit on the training data and applied to the test data.

# Training
The models will be trained independently on the **combined Training and Validation sets** to maximize sample utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Logistic Regression (Estimators A & C):**
    *   **Estimator:** `LogisticRegressionCV` with **3-fold Cross-Validation** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **Metric Alignment:** Explicitly set `scoring='neg_log_loss'` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00031`).
    *   **Search Grid:** Use a **dense and broad logarithmic grid** for `Cs` (e.g., `np.logspace(-4, 4, 50)`). This synthesizes the need for a broad search (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00037`) with the need for high density to pinpoint the sharp optimum (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00047`).
    *   **Solver:** `lbfgs` with L2 regularization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00005`).

2.  **LDA (Estimator B):**
    *   Train with **Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage**.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   **View 1:** Process test CSV features through Estimators A and B.
    *   **View 2:** Process test images through the ResNet18 $\rightarrow$ PCA pipeline, then through Estimator C.
2.  **Ensembling:**
    *   Compute the final predictions using **Soft Voting** (averaging the probability vectors of A, B, and C).
    *   This strategy combines the proven handcrafted baseline with a powerful new visual representation, using simple averaging to avoid the "dilution" of pruning (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`) and the overfitting of Stacking (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00034`).
