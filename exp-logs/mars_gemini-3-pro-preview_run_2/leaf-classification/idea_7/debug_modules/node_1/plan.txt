The error occurs because the `runfile.py` script aggressively subsamples the training data to just 100 rows for a dataset with 99 unique classes. This leaves many classes with only a single sample (or zero), making it impossible to perform 2-fold cross-validation (`cv=2`) inside `LogisticRegressionCV`. This causes the internal solvers to produce coefficient matrices of inconsistent shapes across folds, leading to the `ValueError` (Cite debug_lesson_2). To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to use the full training dataset (which is small, ~900 rows) instead of a subset, ensuring that all classes have sufficient samples (minimum 5 per class) to support the cross-validation process.
