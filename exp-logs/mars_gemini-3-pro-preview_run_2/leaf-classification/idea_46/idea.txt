Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Compressed-Interaction Precision-Generative Ensemble (CIPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" concept (Idea 45, Lesson 109) and the "Precision-Generative" baseline (Idea 30). It addresses the remaining limitation of the Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) "Super-Expert": its inability to model **non-linear interactions** between the provided global features (e.g., specific combinations of "Margin" and "Texture" defining a species) due to the high dimensionality ($P=192$).

By introducing a **Compressed-Interaction View**—which projects the global features into a dense latent space before applying polynomial expansion—we allow the robust Linear solver to model quadratic decision boundaries on the global signal without hitting the "Curse of Dimensionality" or the stability issues of QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`). This is integrated into a library of experts managed by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Pipelines**, allowing the ensemble to exploit Linear, Rotational, and Quadratic signal structures simultaneously.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **View A: Marginal Statistical Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, utilizing the full high-dimensional statistical signal with robust covariance estimation (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **View B: Rotational Statistical Experts (The Alignment)**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Aligns the data with its principal axes of variation *without* the noise-amplifying effects of Whitening (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`). This provides a superior approximation of the Multivariate Normal assumption required by LDA.

*   **View C: Polynomial Physical Experts (The Domain Signal)**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
    *   **Rationale:** Captures non-linear physical constraints (e.g., $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) from the raw images. Polarity correction ensures these features are valid (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).

*   **View D: Compressed-Interaction Global Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(n_components=25)** $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Explicitly models quadratic interactions between global features. Direct expansion of 192 features would yield ~18,000 dimensions, which is infeasible ($D \gg N$). By compressing to the top ~25 Principal Components first, we generate a dense interaction space of $\approx 350$ features. This ratio ($D < N$) allows the regularized LDA to find stable quadratic boundaries in the global data, bridging the gap between Linear LDA and unstable QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00064` context).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipelines:** Apply the specific transformation sequences (Marginal, Rotational, Poly-Physical, Compressed-Interaction) to generate the four feature views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (PowerTransformer, PCA) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Views A, B, C, D $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values, PCA components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
