The issue is that the `GreedyEnsembleSelector.fit` method performs data leakage by retraining the selected models on the combined training and validation dataset ("Phase 2") immediately after selection. Consequently, when `runfile.py` subsequently evaluates the model on the validation set, it uses models that have already seen the validation labels, resulting in an invalid, near-zero log loss. To fix this, I will decouple the selection phase from the retraining phase. I will modify `GreedyEnsembleSelector` to only perform selection in `fit()` (leaving models trained on the training set) and move the retraining logic to a new `refit()` method (Cite debug_lesson_3). I will then update `runfile.py` and `library/pipeline.py` to call `refit()` only after the validation metric has been computed.
