The issue stems from the random subsampling of the training data to 200 samples in `runfile.py`. With 99 classes and only 200 samples, many classes end up with 0 or 1 sample in the subset. This causes `LogisticRegressionCV` (configured with `cv=2`) to fail because it cannot form valid training/validation splits where every class is present in the training fold, leading to inconsistent coefficient shapes and the observed reshape error. As noted in the error analysis, the full dataset (~900 samples) is small enough to process quickly. To fix this, I will remove the subsampling logic and use the full training set, ensuring all classes are adequately represented for cross-validation. Cite debug_lesson_1.
