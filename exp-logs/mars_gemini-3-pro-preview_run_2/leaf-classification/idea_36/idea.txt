Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Multi-Basis Gaussianized Hybrid Ensemble (MB-GHE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Gaussianized" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the "Metric Floor Paradox" (Lesson 90 vs. Lesson 81) and the "Non-Parametric Overfitting" (Lesson 76) by formalizing **Normality** not as a single preprocessing step, but as a **Hyperparameter Space**. By generating multiple "Gaussian Bases" (Parametric vs. Regularized Non-Parametric) and applying both **Generative** (LDA) and **Discriminative** (Logistic Regression) solvers to each, we allow the Greedy Selector to mathematically determine the optimal projection and decision boundary for the 99-class manifold.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that vary in **Gaussian Basis**, **Inductive Bias**, and **Feature View**.

### 1. The Multi-Basis Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

**Dimension A: The Gaussian Bases (Preprocessing)**
Instead of a single transformer, we project the data into three distinct latent spaces:
1.  **Parametric Basis:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Role:** Stabilizes variance using a smooth, global power law. Proven robust baseline (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
2.  **Coarse Non-Parametric Basis:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=20)`.
    *   **Role:** A highly constrained rank-based transform. It handles outliers/skew that Yeo-Johnson misses but uses very few quantiles to prevent the overfitting observed in Lesson 76 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076` context).
3.  **Fine Non-Parametric Basis:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=100)`.
    *   **Role:** A balanced transform that captures more local density variations while still regularizing heavily compared to $N \approx 700$.

**Dimension B: The Inductive Biases (Estimators)**
For *each* Basis and *each* View, we train:
1.  **Generative Experts (LDA):**
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Models joint probability $P(X, Y)$. Efficient and calibrated.
2.  **Discriminative Experts (LogReg):**
    *   **Algorithm:** **Logistic Regression** with L2 regularization.
    *   **Configuration:** `LogisticRegressionCV` with a **Dense Logarithmic Grid** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00047`) and explicitly optimizing `neg_log_loss` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00031`).
    *   **Role:** Models conditional probability $P(Y|X)$. Re-introducing this (from Lesson 6) *after* Gaussianization allows the linear boundaries to operate in the idealized space where linear separation is most effective.

**Dimension C: The Feature Views**
1.  **Global View:** The 192 provided features.
2.  **Macro View:** Explicit Morphological Descriptors (Hu Moments + Scalars). (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss. This ensures that if the Parametric OAS-LDA is sufficient (Lesson 90), the ensemble converges to it; if the Discriminative Coarse-Quantile expert adds orthogonal signal, it is incorporated (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Macro View:** Load raw binary images. Extract **Deterministic** Morphometrics: **Hu Moments** (7 invariant descriptors) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity). We explicitly reject Monte-Carlo augmentation (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00089`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Basis Generation:** Apply the three transforms (Parametric, Coarse-Quantile, Fine-Quantile) to generate transformed versions of the Global and Macro views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full Cartesian product of (Bases $\times$ Estimators $\times$ Views) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage, C, n_quantiles) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Macro features for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts (applying the specific Basis transform and `float64` casting).
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
