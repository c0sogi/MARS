To improve the model's performance, I will upgrade the backbone architecture from **ResNet18** to **ResNet50**. ResNet50 offers a deeper and more expressive feature extractor, which is beneficial for distinguishing between the subtle disease patterns in Cassava leaves. I will use the `IMAGENET1K_V2` weights for better initialization.

Additionally, I will introduce **Label Smoothing** (smoothing factor 0.1) to the Cross Entropy Loss. This technique helps prevent the model from becoming overconfident on the noisy labels present in the Cassava dataset and acts as a regularizer.

I will also adjust the hyperparameters: increasing the **Batch Size** to 64 to utilize the A100 GPU more efficiently and increasing the **Epochs** to 15 to ensure the larger model has sufficient time to converge.

These changes build upon the previous success of using automated augmentation (RandAugment) by providing a more capable model to learn from the diverse augmented data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
