The implementation strategy follows the Progressive Resolution Training approach outlined in the idea.

1.  **Setup & Data Loading**: We initialize the environment (seed, logger) and load the training and validation metadata. We combine them to perform a custom 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation, ensuring we use all available data robustly.
2.  **Progressive Training Loop**: For each fold, we execute a two-phase training process:
    *   **Phase 1 (Coarse)**: Train at 224x224 resolution with higher MixUp regularization to learn robust global features.
    *   **Phase 2 (Fine)**: Fine-tune at 384x384 resolution with reduced regularization to resolve fine-grained disease patterns.
    *   We use `AdamW` optimizer and `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler, resetting for each phase to adapt to the new resolution dynamics.
    *   We utilize **Model EMA** throughout to maintain stable weights.
3.  **Validation & Analysis**: After training, we evaluate the EMA model on the validation fold. We aggregate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions to compute the final validation metric. We then perform failure analysis by correlating prediction error magnitudes with file sizes to identify potential complexity-related biases.
4.  **Submission**: If the final validation accuracy exceeds the threshold (0.9076), we load the best models from all 5 folds. We perform inference on the test set using Test Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) and ensemble the predictions to generate the final submission.
