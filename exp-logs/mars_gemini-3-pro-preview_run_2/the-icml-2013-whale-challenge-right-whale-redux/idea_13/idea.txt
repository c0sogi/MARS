Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the extracted lessons, I propose a **Multi-Resolution Stacked Ensemble with Loss-Guided Convergence**.

This strategy evolves the successful Stacking approach (Idea 12) by introducing **Input Resolution Diversity** to the base learners. It explicitly addresses the "Resolution vs. Smearing" trade-off identified in Lesson 00033 and implements the "Loss-based Early Stopping" protocol from Lesson 00055 to maximize the calibration and stackability of the ensemble members.

# Model
We will construct a **Two-Stage Stacked Ensemble** driven by a heterogeneous set of base learners operating on different temporal views of the data.

**Level 0: Multi-Resolution Base Learners**
We will train three distinct model variations to serve as the foundation of the stack.
1.  **Model A (Standard Temporal Resolution):** **EfficientNet-B0** initialized with **Noisy Student weights** (`ns_jft_in1k`).
    *   *Input:* Spectrograms with standard hop length.
    *   *Role:* Capitalizes on the massive pre-training of Noisy Student weights **Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**.
2.  **Model B (Standard Temporal Resolution):** **ResNet-34** initialized with ImageNet weights.
    *   *Input:* Spectrograms with standard hop length.
    *   *Role:* Provides architectural diversity (Legacy ResNet vs. MBConv) to decorrelate errors **Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**.
3.  **Model C (High Temporal Resolution):** **EfficientNet-B0** (Noisy Student).
    *   *Input:* Spectrograms with **halved hop length** (Double Temporal Resolution).
    *   *Role:* Structural Innovation. By doubling the temporal resolution, this model captures transient acoustic details that might be smeared in the standard view, adhering to the principle of compensating for high frequency resolution with high overlap **Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**.

**Common Components:**
*   **Pooling:** All models will use **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** to maximize detection of transient signals **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
*   **Adaptation:** All first convolutional layers will be adapted to **1-channel input** via weight averaging **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

**Level 1: Meta-Learner**
*   A **Logistic Regression** model will be trained on the concatenated Out-Of-Fold (OOF) probabilities from Models A, B, and C. This learns the optimal weighted combination of the diverse views **Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**.

# Data
The data pipeline is bifurcated to generate two distinct "views" of the audio signal, while strictly maintaining physical fidelity.

1.  **Spectrogram Generation (Common Parameters):**
    *   `n_fft=1024`: Ensures high frequency resolution (>4:1 bin-to-mel ratio) to prevent aliasing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.
    *   `n_mels=128`, `normalized=False`: Preserves environmental spectral tilt **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
2.  **Resolution Streams:**
    *   **Stream 1 (Standard):** `hop_length=64`. Resulting native resolution approx. $128 \times 63$. Used for Models A and B.
    *   **Stream 2 (High-Res):** `hop_length=32`. Resulting native resolution approx. $128 \times 126$. Used for Model C.
3.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Native Resolution:** Inputs are fed to models at their generated dimensions. No resizing to 224x224 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**.
    *   **Instance Standardization:** Zero-Mean, Unit-Variance standardization applied per clip **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
4.  **Augmentation:**
    *   **SpecAugment** (Time/Freq masking) is applied **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**. Mixup is excluded **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

# Training
We implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** workflow with a specific focus on probability calibration.

1.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with low weight decay (`1e-4`) to prevent over-constraining the transfer learning **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
    *   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing to ensure convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**.
    *   **Batch Size:** 128 to stabilize Weighted Sampling **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
2.  **Convergence Strategy (Crucial Update):**
    *   We will use **Early Stopping monitoring Validation Loss** (not AUC).
    *   *Justification:* Lesson 00055 demonstrated that maximizing AUC in later epochs often degrades probability calibration (increasing loss), which hurts the performance of the stacking meta-learner. Minimizing Loss ensures the base learners provide reliable, calibratable probabilities **Cite solution_lesson_node_00055**.

# Evaluation
1.  **Base Prediction:**
    *   Process Test clips through Stream 1 (Hop 64) for Models A & B.
    *   Process Test clips through Stream 2 (Hop 32) for Model C.
    *   Generate probability predictions from all 15 trained base models (3 architectures $\times$ 5 folds).
2.  **Stacking:**
    *   Feed the averaged fold predictions (or concatenated OOF-aligned predictions) into the trained Level 1 Logistic Regression model.
    *   This yields a final prediction that integrates architectural diversity with multi-resolution signal analysis.
