1.  **Simplify Ensemble Architecture**: I will remove the High-Resolution EfficientNet-B0 (Hop 32) from the ensemble. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00056** indicates that increasing temporal resolution provided diminishing returns and the model was highly correlated with the standard resolution version. Removing it reduces computational overhead without sacrificing significant performance.
2.  **Reintroduce Weighted Sampling**: I will modify the `DataLoader` to use a `WeightedRandomSampler`. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00056** and **solution_lesson_node_00004** highlight that removing the sampler in the previous iteration caused a performance regression due to the 90:10 class imbalance. Balancing the batches is critical for stability and convergence.
3.  **Optimize Training Schedule**: I will increase the training epochs from 5 to 12 in `runfile.py`. Since the ensemble size is reduced (from 3 models to 2), the computational budget allows for longer training per model to ensure they reach better convergence before early stopping, addressing the under-training observed in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00055**.
