Based on the analysis of previous attempts and the extracted lessons, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble of Scaled Legacy Architectures (EfficientNet-B2 & ResNet-50)**. This strategy addresses the specific implementation flaws that caused the failure of the previous ensemble attempt (Idea 6) and the scaling attempt (Idea 5), while retaining the structural innovations that delivered the current best performance (Idea 4).

### Model
We will construct an ensemble of two robust, "legacy" Convolutional Neural Networks. This combines the high-performance feature extraction of EfficientNet with the architectural distinctness of ResNet to maximize variance reduction.

1.  **Backbone A: EfficientNet-B2**
    *   **Evolution:** We scale up from the EfficientNet-B0 used in the current best solution (Idea 4) to **EfficientNet-B2**. This increases model capacity to capture more subtle spectral features. We explicitly reject the multi-channel "Delta" inputs used in Idea 5, as they were found to degrade performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00021**.
2.  **Backbone B: ResNet-50**
    *   **Evolution:** We scale up from the ResNet-34 used in Idea 6 to **ResNet-50**. Lesson 00013 confirms that legacy architectures like ResNet are significantly more robust and easier to train on this dataset than modern alternatives like ConvNeXt **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
3.  **Common Structural Components:**
    *   **Pooling:** Both models will replace Global Average Pooling with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. This is critical for retaining the strong activation of transient whale calls that would otherwise be diluted by background noise **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
    *   **Input Adaptation:** Both models will be modified to accept **1-channel input**. The weights for the first convolutional layer will be initialized by **averaging the weights of the original pretrained RGB channels** to preserve learned spatial filters **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

### Data
The data pipeline is strictly standardized to correct the signal processing errors found in Idea 2, Idea 3, and Idea 6.

1.  **Spectrogram Generation:**
    *   **FFT Parameters:** We will set `n_fft=1024` and `hop_length=64`. This ensures a high ratio of FFT bins to Mel bands (>4:1) to prevent aliasing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** and provides sufficient temporal resolution to resolve short call contours **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Mel Filterbank:** We will set `normalized=False`. This preserves the natural spectral tilt of the environmental noise, maximizing contrast for the model **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Instance-Wise Standardization:** We will apply **Zero-Mean, Unit-Variance standardization** to each spectrogram individually. This was the critical missing component that caused the failure of the ensemble in Idea 6 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
    *   **Input Channels:** We will use **Single-Channel Log-Mel Spectrograms** only, avoiding manual feature engineering like Deltas **Cite solution_lesson_node_00021**.
3.  **Augmentation:**
    *   We will use **SpecAugment** (Time and Frequency Masking) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**. We will explicitly **exclude Mixup**, as it degrades the detection of transient signals **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

### Training
The training protocol is designed to ensure stability and full convergence, addressing the under-fitting observed in Idea 6.

1.  **Sampling & Batching:** We will use a **WeightedRandomSampler** to balance the classes and maximize the batch size to **128** (or hardware limit) to stabilize Batch Normalization statistics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
2.  **Optimization:** We will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
3.  **Convergence:** We will extend the patience for early stopping and the maximum epoch count to ensure both larger models reach their global performance peak, avoiding the premature stopping that handicapped the previous ensemble **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Test clips will be processed using the exact same pipeline (1024/64 STFT, Instance Standardization).
2.  **Ensembling:** We will perform **Soft Voting** by averaging the probability outputs of the EfficientNet-B2 and ResNet-50 models. This combines the predictions of two structurally distinct, fully converged, and correctly normalized models to achieve a score superior to any single architecture.
