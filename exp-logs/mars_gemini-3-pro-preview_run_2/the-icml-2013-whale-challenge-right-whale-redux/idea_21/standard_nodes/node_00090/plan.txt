The issue is data leakage in the evaluation of the Stacking Meta-Learner. The original code trained the meta-learner (Logistic Regression) on the validation set's predictions and labels, and then evaluated it on the exact same data to compute the final metric. This "resubstitution" leads to an invalid, optimistically biased score. To fix this, I will modify `library/stacking.py` to implement K-Fold Cross-Validation within `train_meta_learner`. This ensures that the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions used for the metric calculation are generated by a model that has not seen the labels of those specific samples during training (Cite debug_lesson_12). I will then update `runfile.py` to use these valid OOF predictions and the corresponding AUC score returned by the updated function.
