Implementation strategy:
1.  **Setup & Configuration**: Initialize the environment using `library.config` and `library.utils`. Adjust `Config.EPOCHS` to 10 to ensure the "fast baseline" completes within the 2-hour limit while preserving the Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) phases (5 epochs annealing + 5 epochs SWA). Ensure `Config.DEBUG` is False to use the full dataset for maximum performance.
2.  **Data Preparation**: Trigger `library.data_loader.load_data` to ensure all spectrograms are processed and cached.
3.  **Training (Stratified K-Fold)**: Iterate through 5 folds. For each fold, retrieve train/val loaders and execute `library.trainer.run_fold`. Collect the trained SWA models and validation predictions (OOF).
4.  **Validation & Metrics**: Aggregate OOF predictions to compute the final Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC). Print this metric with full precision.
5.  **Failure Analysis**: Compute the correlation between the prediction error magnitude and simple spectrogram statistics (Mean, Std, Max) derived from the validation set to identify error patterns.
6.  **Submission**: If the final AUC exceeds the strict threshold of `0.9959177895986835`, perform ensemble inference on the test set. Average the probabilities from all 5 SWA models and generate `submission.csv`.
