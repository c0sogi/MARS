# Proposed Strategy: EfficientNet-B0 with Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling and Mixup

This strategy evolves the previous ResNet-based approach by adopting a more parameter-efficient architecture equipped with attention mechanisms and a specialized pooling layer designed for detecting transient acoustic events.

### Model
The core architecture will transition from ResNet-18 to **EfficientNet-B0**.
- **Backbone:** EfficientNet-B0 is selected for its use of Inverted Residual blocks and Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) modules. The SE blocks explicitly model channel interdependencies, allowing the network to selectively emphasize frequency bands relevant to whale calls while suppressing noise.
- **Input Adaptation:** To accommodate single-channel spectrograms, the first convolutional layer of the pretrained EfficientNet (originally designed for 3-channel RGB images) will be modified to accept 1-channel input. The weights for this new kernel will be initialized by averaging the weights of the original pretrained RGB channels **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
- **Pooling Layer (Structural Innovation):** Standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) will be replaced with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. While GAP treats all time-frequency bins equally, GeM Pooling (which is a learnable interpolation between Max and Average pooling) allows the model to focus on salient "peaks" of activation. This is structurally superior for this task because whale calls are transient events (short duration) within the 2-second clip; GeM prevents the strong signal of the call from being diluted by the surrounding background noise.

### Data
The data processing pipeline remains centered on 2D time-frequency representations but incorporates advanced regularization.
- **Preprocessing:** Raw audio waveforms will be converted into **Log-Mel Spectrograms**.
- **Augmentation:**
    1.  **SpecAugment:** Time and Frequency masking will be applied to the spectrograms during training to force the model to learn robust, distributed features rather than memorizing specific artifacts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    2.  **Mixup:** We will introduce **Mixup augmentation**, which generates synthetic training examples by taking convex combinations of pairs of inputs and their labels. This encourages the model to behave linearly in-between training examples, smoothing the decision boundary and improving calibration.

### Training
The training procedure is designed to handle class imbalance and maximize stability.
- **Sampling & Batching:** A **WeightedRandomSampler** will be used to oversample the minority class (whale calls), ensuring a balanced effective distribution. To stabilize the gradients from this weighted sampling and the Batch Normalization layers within EfficientNet, we will maximize the **batch size to 128** (or higher if memory permits) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
- **Loss Function:** **Binary Cross Entropy with Logits Loss** will be used. This loss is compatible with the soft labels generated by Mixup augmentation.
- **Optimizer:** The AdamW optimizer will be used in conjunction with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler to ensure convergence to a flat minimum.

### Evaluation
- **Inference:** Test set recordings will undergo the same Log-Mel Spectrogram conversion.
- **Prediction:** The trained EfficientNet-B0 model will perform a forward pass. Due to the GeM pooling, the model will naturally highlight the presence of whale calls even if they are short. The final sigmoid output will be recorded as the probability for the submission file.
