Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the extracted lessons, I propose a **Stacked Generalization Ensemble of Physically-Aligned Legacy Architectures**.

This strategy evolves the solution from a simple "Soft Voting" ensemble (Idea 11) to a **Two-Stage Stacking Pipeline**. It addresses the limitation of unweighted averaging identified in Lesson 00037 by learning the optimal combination of base learners, while strictly adhering to the "Golden Recipe" of signal processing parameters that proved critical in Idea 4.

# Model
We will construct a **Two-Stage Stacked Ensemble**.

**Level 0: Base Learners (Heterogeneous Legacy Encoders)**
We will employ two distinct, robust CNN architectures to generate diverse feature representations.
1.  **EfficientNet-B0:** Retained for its proven high performance (Idea 4) and effective Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks. We use standard ImageNet weights **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**.
2.  **ResNet-34:** Introduced to provide architectural diversity. As a "Legacy" model, it offers greater stability and robustness than modern Transformers or ConvNeXt on this dataset **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
3.  **Common Modifications:**
    *   **Pooling:** Both models will utilize **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** to maximize the detection of transient whale calls in noise **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
    *   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer of both models will be adapted to **1-channel input**, initialized by averaging the original RGB weights **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

**Level 1: Meta-Learner**
Instead of simple averaging, we will train a **Logistic Regression** meta-learner. This model takes the probability outputs of the Level 0 models as input and predicts the final label. This allows the system to learn a **weighted combination** of the base models, assigning higher importance to the stronger predictor and correcting systematic biases, addressing the limitations of soft voting **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.

# Data
The data pipeline is rigorously standardized to preserve physical signal fidelity, correcting the errors of previous ensemble attempts.

1.  **Spectrogram Generation:**
    *   **FFT Parameters:** `n_fft=1024` and `hop_length=64`. This ensures the FFT bins exceed Mel bands (>4:1) to prevent aliasing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** and provides high temporal resolution to resolve short call contours **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Mel Filterbank:** `n_mels=128`, `normalized=False`. We explicitly disable area normalization to preserve the environmental "Pink" noise spectral tilt **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Native Resolution:** Inputs will be fed to the models at their **native resolution** (approx. $128 \times 63$). We will **NOT** resize to $224 \times 224$, avoiding interpolation artifacts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**.
    *   **Instance Standardization:** We will apply **Zero-Mean, Unit-Variance standardization** to each clip individually. This is critical for convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
3.  **Augmentation:**
    *   **SpecAugment** (Time/Freq masking) will be used **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**. Mixup is explicitly excluded **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

# Training
We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** workflow to train the Level 0 models and generate inputs for the Level 1 meta-learner.

1.  **Level 0 Training (Base Models):**
    *   **Strategy:** Train 5 folds of EfficientNet-B0 and 5 folds of ResNet-34.
    *   **Optimization:** Use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate Scheduler** to ensure effective fine-tuning **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**.
    *   **Regularization:** Use a low weight decay of `1e-4` **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
    *   **Sampling:** Use a **WeightedRandomSampler** with a **Batch Size of 128** to stabilize Batch Normalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
    *   **Convergence:** Use **Early Stopping** (patience ~5-7 epochs) to capture the peak of generalization, explicitly avoiding SWA in the overfitting regime **Cite solution_lesson_node_00042**.
2.  **Level 1 Training (Meta-Learner):**
    *   **Input:** The **Out-Of-Fold (OOF)** probability predictions from the Level 0 models.
    *   **Procedure:** Train the Logistic Regression model on the concatenated OOF predictions to minimize Log Loss. This provides a robust estimate of generalization performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00047**.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Process Test clips using the exact "Golden Recipe" pipeline (1024/64 STFT, Native Res, Instance Norm).
    *   Generate predictions from all 10 trained Level 0 models (5 EfficientNet + 5 ResNet).
2.  **Stacking:**
    *   Feed the averaged fold predictions (or individual fold predictions) into the trained Level 1 Logistic Regression model to generate the final probability. This yields a calibrated, variance-reduced submission.
