To optimize the model's performance, I will address the "Signal Dilution" issue identified in the failure analysis and the provided lesson (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002). The previous model showed a negative correlation between audio duration and error, suggesting that zero-padding combined with Global Average Pooling (GAP) was diluting features for shorter clips.

My strategy is as follows:
1.  **Modify `library/model.py`**: Replace the Global Average Pooling (GAP) layer with **Global Max Pooling (GMP)**. Max pooling captures the most prominent features in the spectrogram and is invariant to the zero-padding used for variable-length audio clips, effectively solving the signal dilution problem.
2.  **Modify `runfile.py`**:
    *   Increase `Config.MAX_EPOCHS` from 5 to 40 to allow the model to converge properly, as the previous run was a short baseline.
