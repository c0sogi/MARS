Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a solution that structurally evolves the baseline by upgrading the backbone architecture and introducing a learnable aggregation layer, while strictly adhering to the proven training and data processing recipes.

### Model
**Backbone Upgrade: EfficientNet-B0**
I propose replacing the MobileNetV2 backbone with **EfficientNet-B0**. While MobileNetV2 is efficient, EfficientNet utilizes Compound Scaling to balance depth, width, and resolution, providing a stronger feature extraction capability for complex patterns without an excessive computational penalty.
*   **Initialization:** The model will be initialized with **ImageNet-1k V2 (NoisyStudent or DEFAULT)** weights to ensure robust feature recognition capabilities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer will be modified to accept 1-channel audio input by **summing the pre-trained RGB weights** along the channel dimension, preserving the spatial structure of the learned filters (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

**Structural Innovation: Attention Pooling Head**
To address the issue of "Signal Dilution" in variable-length clips, I will replace the standard Global Average/Max Pooling with a **Multi-Head Attention Pooling (or Gated Linear Unit) layer**.
*   Instead of simply averaging or taking the max over the time dimension (which can be sensitive to noise or diluted by silence), this layer learns to assign importance weights to different time frames. This allows the model to dynamically focus on the active sound events and ignore background noise or zero-padded regions, providing a more sophisticated solution to the padding problem than simple masking (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

### Data
**Preprocessing**
*   **Format:** Log-Mel Spectrograms (128 bands) derived from audio resampled to 32kHz.
*   **Normalization Order:** Crucially, **instance-wise normalization** (zero mean, unit variance) will be applied to the raw spectrogram **before** any masking or augmentation is performed. This ensures the statistical distribution of the input remains stable and is not skewed by the artificial values introduced by masking (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

**Augmentation**
*   **SpecAugment:** After normalization, **SpecAugment** will be applied with aggressive parameters (Frequency Masking $F=30$, Time Masking $T=30$) to force the model to learn distributed spectral features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Mixup:** We will employ **Mixup** with a **Uniform distribution ($\alpha=1.0$)**. This encourages the model to learn disentangled representations for overlapping sound events, which is superior to the bimodal distribution used in vision (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### Training
**Optimization Strategy**
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** combined with **Cosine Annealing** to effectively navigate the loss landscape and prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Batch Size:** The batch size will be kept moderate (e.g., **64**) rather than large (128) to maintain the implicit regularization noise that aids generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

**Convergence Management**
*   **Dynamic Termination:** We will set a high `MAX_EPOCHS` (e.g., 60) and rely on an **Early Stopping** mechanism to determine the optimal training duration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Patience Synchronization:** The Early Stopping patience will be set significantly higher (e.g., 10-15 epochs) than the Learning Rate Scheduler's patience (e.g., 3-5 epochs). This ensures the model has sufficient time to converge to a better local minimum after the learning rate decays (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated on the test set using the trained EfficientNet-B0 model.
*   **Variable Length Handling:** The Attention Pooling layer will naturally handle variable-length test clips by computing attention weights over the specific duration of each file, ensuring that the final prediction is based on the most relevant segments of the audio.
