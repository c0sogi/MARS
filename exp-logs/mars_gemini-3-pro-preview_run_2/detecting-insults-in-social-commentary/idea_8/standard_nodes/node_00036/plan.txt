The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the interaction between Gradient Checkpointing and the autograd engine during the backward pass in the DeBERTa model. To resolve this, I will disable gradient checkpointing in both the configuration class and the execution script. Since disabling checkpointing increases memory usage, I will also reduce the batch size from 8 to 4 to prevent Out-Of-Memory errors (Cite debug_lesson_3). I will apply these changes explicitly in `runfile.py` as well to ensure they take effect even if the module is cached in the environment (Cite debug_lesson_6).
