The previous run demonstrated that `roberta-large` (AUC ~0.95) significantly underperformed `microsoft/deberta-v3-large` (AUC ~0.96) and diluted the ensemble's overall performance. Focusing on the single strongest model is preferred over averaging with a weaker one (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

Adversarial Weight Perturbation doubles the computational cost per step, which previously forced a reduction of training epochs to 2. By disabling AWP, we can afford to train the superior `deberta-v3-large` model for the full 4 epochs within the time limit. Ensuring the large model fully converges is more beneficial than applying expensive regularization to an under-trained model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

3.  **Restore Full Training Duration**: I will remove the hardcoded `Config.epochs = 2` override in `runfile.py`. This allows the configuration setting (`epochs = 4`) to take effect, ensuring the `deberta-v3-large` model has sufficient iterations to minimize loss and refine the decision boundary (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
