The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the batch size of 8 is too large for the actual GPU capacity (~16GB), leading to memory exhaustion during the memory-intensive attention operations of the DeBERTa-v3-large model. To address this, I will reduce the batch sizes to 4 and enable Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) to significantly lower memory consumption. I will also update the training engine to use `torch.cuda.amp.GradScaler` and `autocast`, ensuring the model fits within the hardware constraints without resorting to gradient checkpointing, which previously caused instability.
