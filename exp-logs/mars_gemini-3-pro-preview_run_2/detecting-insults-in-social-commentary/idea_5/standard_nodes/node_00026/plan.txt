The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the interaction between Gradient Checkpointing and the specific computation graph of the DeBERTa model (likely involving the Multi-Sample Dropout head or internal tensor reuse). As noted in the error analysis and **Cite debug_lesson_3**, enabling gradient checkpointing can trigger this error when the backward pass attempts to recompute activations that rely on freed tensors. To fix this, I will disable gradient checkpointing in `library/model.py`. Since this increases memory usage, I will also proactively reduce the batch size and increase gradient accumulation steps in `library/config.py` to prevent Out-Of-Memory errors, adhering to **Cite debug_lesson_2**.
