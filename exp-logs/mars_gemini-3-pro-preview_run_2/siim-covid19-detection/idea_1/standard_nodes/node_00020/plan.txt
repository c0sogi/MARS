The training failed with a `NaN` loss due to numerical instability caused by an excessively high learning rate (`0.005`) for fine-tuning a pre-trained Faster R-CNN model, compounded by the lack of gradient clipping. This instability was anticipated in the notes of a previous debugging session (Cite debug_lesson_3), which observed that high learning rates or unscaled loss terms could lead to such failures. To fix this, I will reduce the learning rate to `0.0005` in `library/config.py` and implement gradient clipping in `library/engine.py` to prevent exploding gradients during backpropagation.
