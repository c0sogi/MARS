Based on the lessons learned, particularly **Lesson 00012** (Synergy of Data Scale) and **Lesson 00014** (Synchronize Learning Rate Decay), the optimization strategy focuses on scaling the training to the full dataset while employing a concise, aggressive learning rate schedule.

1.  **Full Dataset Training**: We will modify `runfile.py` to remove the sampling limits (`MAX_TRAIN_SAMPLES = None`), allowing the model to learn from the full diversity of the training set. This directly addresses the finding in **Lesson 00012** that scaling to the full dataset significantly improves performance (from ~0.28 to ~0.36+ mAP).
2.  **Optimized LR Schedule**: We will configure the training for **7 epochs** with a learning rate decay step at **epoch 3**. **Lesson 00014** demonstrated that a short training horizon with an early decay (allowing for a "fine-tuning" phase) outperforms longer schedules where early stopping might cut off training before the decay phase occurs.
3.  **Threshold Update**: We will update the submission threshold in `runfile.py` to the current best score (`0.411098...`) to ensure we only generate a submission if we surpass the previous best model.

The configuration in `library/config.py` will be updated to reflect these defaults for consistency.
