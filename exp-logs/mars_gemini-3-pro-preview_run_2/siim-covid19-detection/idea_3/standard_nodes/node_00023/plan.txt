Based on the lessons learned, particularly `solution_lesson_node_00022` ("Capacity-Budget Mismatch in Model Selection"), the previous attempt failed because it used a computationally expensive architecture (`ResNeXt101-32x8d`) and high resolution (`1024x1024`) which forced a drastic reduction in training data (subset of 1500 images) and epochs to fit within the time limit. The "Current Best Solution" identified in the lessons achieved significantly higher performance (`mAP ~0.433`) using a lighter `ResNet101` backbone on the full dataset at `800x800` resolution.

Therefore, I will optimize the solution by:
1.  **Reverting to ResNet101**: Replacing the heavy `ResNeXt` backbone with `ResNet101` to improve throughput and convergence speed (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`).
2.  **Training on Full Dataset**: Removing the subsetting logic to train on all ~4500 images, as data scale is critical for performance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00012`).
3.  **Optimizing Resolution and Batch Size**: Reducing image size to `800x800` and increasing batch size to `8`, balancing detail with training speed.
4.  **Enhancing RPN Capacity**: Increasing RPN training proposals to `3000` to handle dense opacities, as identified in failure analysis (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00005`, `solution_lesson_node_00013`).
5.  **Adjusting Schedule**: Training for `10` epochs with learning rate decay at epoch `6` to ensure proper fine-tuning before convergence (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`).
