I will optimize the solution by addressing the capacity-budget mismatch identified in Lesson 00022. The previous solution used a computationally expensive `ResNeXt101` backbone at high resolution (1024x1024), which forced the use of a small data subset (1500 images) and few epochs (3) to meet time constraints, resulting in poor performance on small objects.

1.  **Backbone & Resolution**: I will revert to a `ResNet101` backbone and reduce the image resolution to `800x800`. This lighter configuration allows for faster iterations and fits within the compute budget while maintaining high capacity for feature extraction. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00022}.
2.  **Data Scale**: I will train on the **full training dataset** (~4500 images) instead of a subset. Scaling up the data is crucial for the model to learn robust features for complex scenes, as seen in the failure analysis where the subset-trained model failed on dense/small objects. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00012}.
3.  **Training Schedule**: I will increase the training duration to **10 epochs** and adjust the learning rate decay to occur at epoch 6. This ensures the model has sufficient time to converge and benefits from a fine-tuning phase at a lower learning rate before training ends. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00014}.

I will implement these changes by modifying `library/config.py` to update hyperparameters, `library/model.py` to switch the backbone, and `runfile.py` to remove the subsetting logic.
