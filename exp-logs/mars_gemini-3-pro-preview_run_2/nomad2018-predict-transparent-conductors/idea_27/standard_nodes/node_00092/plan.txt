As identified in **Lesson 90**, normalizing only the update branch (`BN(Conv(x)) + x`) allows the residual signal to dominate and wash out structural information. The corrected order (`BN(Conv(x) + x)`) ensures that the combined signal is normalized, preserving the contribution of the graph convolution.
