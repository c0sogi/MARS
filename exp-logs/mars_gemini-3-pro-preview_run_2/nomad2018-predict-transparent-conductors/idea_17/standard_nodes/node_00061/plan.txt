To optimize the performance of the Learnable-Residual Crystal Graph Convolutional Neural Network (LRCGCNN), I will implement the following targeted changes based on the provided lessons:

1.  **Optimize Model Capacity (Lessons 4, 5, 19):** I will reduce the embedding dimensions (`ATOM_FEA_LEN` and `H_FEA_LEN`) from 192 to 128. While Lesson 5 suggested scaling up, Lesson 4 and Lesson 19 indicate that for small datasets (~2000 samples), excessive width can lead to overfitting or inefficient optimization. 128 is a robust middle ground that performed best in Lesson 19. I will also set the number of interaction layers (`N_CONV`) to 4, balancing depth and over-smoothing risks.

2.  **Refine Pooling Strategy (Lesson 33):** I will replace the Dual Pooling (Mean + Max) with **Global Mean Pooling** only. As per Lesson 33, intensive physical properties like formation energy and bandgap are better modeled by average feature aggregation rather than max pooling, which can be sensitive to local outliers. This also reduces the parameter count in the readout heads.

3.  **Enhance Training Schedule (Lesson 60):** The previous run was truncated at 20 epochs, preventing convergence. I will increase `EPOCHS` to 150 in the configuration and remove the override in `runfile.py`.

4.  **Learning Rate Scheduling:** I will introduce a `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler in `runfile.py` to dynamically adjust the learning rate when validation loss plateaus, facilitating finer convergence.
