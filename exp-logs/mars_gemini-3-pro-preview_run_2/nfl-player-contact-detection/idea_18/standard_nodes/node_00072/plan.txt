    *   **Visual Stream**: Add **Logarithmic Centroid Distance** to the visual pair features. Similar to how `log_distance` helped the kinematic stream, applying a non-linear transformation to the visual distance will improve resolution near the contact boundary (Cite Lesson 00005).
