I will implement a "Best View Selection" strategy for the visual features, replacing the concatenation of Sideline and Endzone views with a dynamic selection based on player visibility (bounding box area). This directly addresses the lesson learned in `solution_lesson_node_00074` regarding the superiority of Max-Pooling over Concatenation for multi-view fusion. Additionally, I will scale up the training process to utilize the full dataset, as the previous execution time allows for significantly more data to be processed (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`).
