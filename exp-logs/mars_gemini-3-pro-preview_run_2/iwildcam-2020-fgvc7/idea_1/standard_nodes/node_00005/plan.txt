3.  **Optimization & Regularization**: I will switch the optimizer from `Adam` to `AdamW` to utilize decoupled weight decay. I will also incorporate `Label Smoothing` (0.1) into the CrossEntropy loss and add a `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler. This combination is known to improve generalization for fine-grained classification tasks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
