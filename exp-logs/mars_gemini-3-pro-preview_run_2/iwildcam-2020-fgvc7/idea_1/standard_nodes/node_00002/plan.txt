**Strategy:**
1.  **Increase Input Resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001):** The failure analysis showed a strong negative correlation between classification error and image dimensions. Even with cropping, the model struggles with low pixel density on the subject. I will increase `IMG_SIZE` from `(224, 224)` to `(448, 448)` to provide the classifier with higher-fidelity features.
2.  **Fine-tune Backbone:** Instead of using a frozen ResNet50, I will unfreeze the backbone to allow the model to adapt its feature extraction layers to the specific domain of camera trap images, which differs significantly from ImageNet.
3.  **Full Dataset Training:** I will scale up training from a 20,000-image subset to the full training set (~122k images) to maximize generalization.
4.  **Data Augmentation:** To prevent overfitting during fine-tuning, I will introduce random horizontal flips and color jittering to the training pipeline.
5.  **Hyperparameter Tuning:** I will adjust the learning rate to `1e-4` (suitable for fine-tuning) and increase epochs to 8, while reducing the batch size to 32 to accommodate the larger image resolution within GPU memory limits.
