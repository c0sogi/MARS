Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the specific lessons learned—particularly the imperative to prioritize model width (Lesson 49), the failure of dense shortcuts (Lesson 63), and the convergence benefits of attention (Lesson 12)—I propose the **Custom Wide SE-ResNeXt with Multi-Scale Aggregation**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution that maximizes **Representational Capacity** (Width) while maintaining computational efficiency. By transitioning from a standard ResNet to a **ResNeXt** (Grouped Convolution) architecture, we can scale the channel dimensions beyond the previous "Wide" limits without incurring the prohibitive parameter costs of dense convolutions. This creates a "Super-Wide" backbone that strictly adheres to the successful topology identified in prior lessons.

# Model
I propose a **Custom Wide SE-ResNeXt Architecture**.

*   **Backbone (Wide ResNeXt):** We will implement a 3-stage CNN based on the ResNeXt design pattern (Split-Transform-Merge).
    *   **High Cardinality (Width):** We will significantly increase the channel dimensions to `[64, 128, 256]` (doubling the previous best "Wide" configuration of `[32, 64, 128]`). We enable this massive capacity increase by utilizing **Grouped Convolutions** (e.g., Cardinality = 32) within the 3x3 layers. This directly addresses the lesson that **Model Width is the primary driver of performance** in this domain **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00049)**.
    *   **Shortcuts:** We will strictly utilize **1x1 convolutions** for projection shortcuts during downsampling. We explicitly reject the 3x3 shortcuts proposed in Idea 19, as recent evidence proved they degrade performance **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00063)**.
    *   **Attention:** We will integrate **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Blocks** within each residual unit. While Lesson 13 noted they were overhead for standard ResNets, they are essential in Grouped architectures to facilitate global information flow across separated channel groups and have been proven to **accelerate convergence** **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00012)**.
    *   **Spatial Preservation:** The network will downsample the input from 32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8. We will strictly preserve the 8x8 resolution to prevent the loss of fine-grained spatial details **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.

*   **Head (Multi-Scale Aggregation):** We will implement a concatenation-based aggregation head.
    *   We will extract feature maps from the end of **Stage 2** (16x16) and **Stage 3** (8x8).
    *   We will apply **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** to both. We explicitly reject Covariance Pooling as the high channel count of this Wide model makes second-order statistics computationally expensive and potentially unstable **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00053)**.
    *   The concatenated vector will be passed to a single dense classification layer. This ensures that the massive semantic capacity of the deep stages is complemented by the texture-rich features of the mid-stages **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling to prevent interpolation artifacts **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.

# Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy is chosen to eliminate stochastic residual ranking errors **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)** while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00008)**.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)**.
*   **Training Duration:** We will utilize a reduced training schedule (e.g., 15-20 epochs). The combination of the "Super-Wide" capacity and SE blocks facilitates rapid convergence, making long training runs unnecessary.
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

# Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds. This maximizes robustness and minimizes stochastic noise.
