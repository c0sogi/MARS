Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Custom Wide ResNet with Deeply Supervised Pyramidal Heads**.

This strategy represents a structural synthesis of the "Wide Capacity" paradigm (which achieved the highest absolute metrics) and the "Multi-Scale" paradigm (which improved feature robustness), while explicitly avoiding the architectural pitfalls of U-Nets and Second-Order Pooling identified in recent lessons.

### Model
I propose a **Custom Wide ResNet with Deeply Supervised Pyramidal Heads**.

*   **Backbone (Wide ResNet):** We will utilize the **Wide Channel Configuration** (`[32, 64, 128]`) established as the performance champion in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**.
    *   **Structure:** A 3-stage residual network designed for 32x32 inputs.
    *   **Kernels:** We will exclusively use **3x3 convolutions**, as they provide the optimal receptive field for this resolution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**).
    *   **Spatial Preservation:** The network will downsample 32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8. We strictly preserve the 8x8 resolution to avoid the information loss associated with aggressive downsampling (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).
    *   **Attention:** We will **exclude** SE blocks, as they add latency without improving the saturated metric of wide models (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).

*   **Structural Innovation (Deeply Supervised Pyramidal Heads):** Instead of concatenating feature vectors (which increases the classifier's dimensionality) or using a decoder (which adds redundant upsampling, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**), we will attach **independent classification heads** to the outputs of Stage 2 and Stage 3.
    *   **Head A (Mid-Level):** Attached to the Stage 2 output ($16 \times 16$, 64 channels). It applies **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** followed by a dense layer. This head explicitly targets mid-level texture features (e.g., spines) at a higher spatial resolution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).
    *   **Head B (High-Level):** Attached to the Stage 3 output ($8 \times 8$, 128 channels). It applies **GAP** followed by a dense layer. This head captures high-level semantic context.
    *   **Pooling:** We strictly use GAP for both heads, rejecting Global Covariance Pooling (GCP) as computationally expensive and unnecessary for this high-signal task (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**).

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** We will use a balanced joint loss: $L_{total} = 0.5 \cdot L_{HeadA} + 0.5 \cdot L_{HeadB}$. This enforces Deep Supervision, ensuring that the backbone learns features that are discriminative at both the texture level (Stage 2) and the semantic level (Stage 3).
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy eliminates stochastic residual ranking errors (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**) while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**).
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference Aggregation:** For each image, the model will output two probabilities ($P_A$ and $P_B$). The final prediction for a single model instance will be the arithmetic mean: $P = (P_A + P_B) / 2$. This effectively acts as an internal ensemble, leveraging multi-scale information without the parameter overhead of concatenation.
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).
*   **Final Submission:** The final probability will be the average of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds.
