Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the progression of previous experiments—specifically the dominance of **Wide Architectures** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00064), the inference efficiency of **Structural Re-parameterization** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00103), and the texture modeling capabilities of **Hierarchical Multi-Scale Receptive Fields** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011)—I propose the **Custom Ultra-Wide SE-RepRes2NeXt with Multi-Scale Aggregation**.

This strategy represents the ultimate structural synthesis. It integrates the **Representational Capacity** of an Ultra-Wide ResNeXt, the **Granular Texture Modeling** of Res2Net, and the **Inference Speed** of RepVGG. By replacing standard convolutions with **Re-parameterizable Grouped Convolutions** within a **Hierarchical Res2Net** structure, we can afford a massive channel capacity (`[96, 192, 384]`) that saturates the performance metric while maintaining a feasible inference budget.

# Model
I propose a **Custom Ultra-Wide SE-RepRes2NeXt Architecture**.

*   **Backbone (Ultra-Wide RepRes2NeXt):** We will implement a 3-stage backbone designed for 32x32 inputs.
    *   **Channel Configuration:** We will scale the width to **"Ultra-Wide"** dimensions: `[96, 192, 384]`. This massive capacity is the primary driver of performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049, Cite solution_lesson_node_00064).
    *   **Outer Structure (Res2Net):** Within each bottleneck block, we will employ the **Res2Net** strategy: splitting the feature map into subsets (scales) and cascading them ($y_i = x_i + K_i(y_{i-1})$). This creates a granular, multi-scale receptive field using only 3x3 kernels, explicitly modeling the diverse texture sizes (spines vs. vegetation) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
    *   **Inner Convolution (RepNeXt):** Crucially, the convolution $K_i$ inside the Res2Net cascade will be a **RepNeXt Block**.
        *   **Training:** It will consist of parallel branches: **Grouped $3 \times 3$ Convolution** (Cardinality=32), **Grouped $1 \times 1$ Convolution**, and **Identity**. This facilitates robust gradient flow.
        *   **Inference:** It will be mathematically fused into a single **Grouped $3 \times 3$ Convolution**. This eliminates the overhead of residual additions, allowing us to afford the Res2Net cascade and Ultra-Wide channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00103).
    *   **Attention:** We will integrate **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Blocks** at the output of each residual unit to adaptively recalibrate the rich multi-scale features, accelerating convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   **Downsampling:** We will use a re-parameterizable downsampling block (Parallel Stride-2 $3 \times 3$ and Stride-2 $1 \times 1$). This applies the optimal $3 \times 3$ strided projection for low-resolution convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
    *   **Spatial Preservation:** The network will downsample 32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8, strictly preserving the 8x8 resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

*   **Head (Multi-Scale Aggregation):** We will implement a concatenation-based aggregation head.
    *   We will extract feature maps from the end of **Stage 2** (16x16) and **Stage 3** (8x8) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
    *   We will apply **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** to both. We explicitly reject Covariance Pooling or GeM, as standard GAP is sufficient for this high-signal task and avoids statistical instability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).
    *   The concatenated vector will be passed to a single dense classification layer.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling to prevent interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy eliminates stochastic residual ranking errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Training Duration:** We will utilize a reduced training schedule (e.g., 20 epochs). The combination of the "Ultra-Wide" capacity, SE blocks, and RepVGG-style gradient flow facilitates rapid convergence.
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** Prior to inference, every RepNeXt block within the Res2Net structure will be fused from its multi-branch training form to its single-kernel inference form. This significantly increases throughput (Cite solution_lesson_node_00103).
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds.
