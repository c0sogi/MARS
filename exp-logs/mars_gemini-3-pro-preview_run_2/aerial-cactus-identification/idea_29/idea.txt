Based on the progression of experiments and the specific lessons learned—specifically the dominance of the "Wide SE-ResNeXt" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**), the efficiency of "Multi-Scale Aggregation" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**), and the critical need to preserve spatial fidelity in low-resolution imagery (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**)—I propose the **Custom Wide Anti-Aliased Coordinate-Res2NeXt with Multi-Scale Aggregation**.

This strategy represents the ultimate structural synthesis. It combines the **Representational Capacity** of the Wide ResNeXt, the **Granular Multi-Scale Receptive Fields** of Res2Net, and the **Spatial Sensitivity** of Coordinate Attention. Crucially, it introduces **Anti-Aliased Downsampling (BlurPool)** to strictly enforce shift invariance—a critical property for aerial imagery often lost in standard strided convolutions.

# Model
I propose a **Custom Wide Anti-Aliased Coordinate-Res2NeXt Architecture**.

*   **Backbone (Wide Res2NeXt):** We will implement a 3-stage backbone that integrates **Res2Net Hierarchical Connections** within a **ResNeXt Grouped Convolution** topology.
    *   **Width & Cardinality:** We will strictly adhere to the **"Super-Wide" Channel Configuration** (`[64, 128, 256]`) identified as optimal in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**. To maintain this high capacity efficiently, we will use **Grouped Convolutions** (e.g., Cardinality = 32).
    *   **Res2NeXt Block:** Within each residual bottleneck, we will split the feature map into subsets (scales) and cascade them. This creates a multi-scale receptive field using only **3x3 kernels**, explicitly modeling the diverse texture sizes (spines vs. vegetation) without the computational inefficiency of large kernels **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00011)**.
    *   **Shortcuts:** We will strictly utilize **1x1 convolutions** for projection shortcuts, rejecting 3x3 shortcuts **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00063)**.

*   **Structural Innovation 1 (Coordinate Attention):** We will replace standard Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) blocks with **Coordinate Attention**.
    *   **Rationale:** Standard SE blocks use Global Average Pooling, which compresses all spatial information into a single scalar. For 32x32 images, spatial cues are scarce and valuable. Coordinate Attention factorizes channel attention into two parallel 1D feature encoding processes (pooling along X and Y). This preserves precise positional information while capturing long-range dependencies, enhancing the model's ability to localize fine-grained cactus features **(Evolution of Cite solution_lesson_node_00012)**.

*   **Structural Innovation 2 (Anti-Aliased Downsampling):** We will replace standard strided convolutions (stride 2) with a **Stride-1 Convolution followed by BlurPool**.
    *   **Rationale:** Standard downsampling on small grids (32x32) violates the sampling theorem, causing aliasing and loss of shift invariance. By applying a low-pass filter (BlurPool) before subsampling, we ensure that the features remain robust to small shifts and translations, which is critical for aerial object detection **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.

*   **Head (Multi-Scale Aggregation):** We will retain the concatenation-based aggregation head.
    *   We will extract feature maps from the end of **Stage 2** (16x16) and **Stage 3** (8x8) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
    *   We will apply **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** to both. We explicitly reject Covariance Pooling as GAP is sufficient for this high-signal task and avoids statistical instability **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00053)**.
    *   The concatenated vector will be passed to a single dense classification layer.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling to prevent interpolation artifacts **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.

# Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy eliminates stochastic residual ranking errors **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)** while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00008)**.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)**.
*   **Training Duration:** We will utilize a reduced training schedule (e.g., 20 epochs). The combination of the "Super-Wide" capacity **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00064)** and the enhanced attention mechanism facilitates rapid convergence.
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

# Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds. This maximizes robustness and minimizes stochastic noise.
