The `RecursionError` is caused by the `WideRepVGG.switch_to_deploy` method iterating over `self.modules()`. In PyTorch, `self.modules()` yields all modules in the network recursively, **including the module itself (`self`)** as the first element. Consequently, when `WideRepVGG` attempts to switch to deploy mode, it finds itself in the module list, calls `self.switch_to_deploy()`, and triggers an infinite recursion loop. The fix is to explicitly skip `self` during the iteration to ensure the method only operates on its sub-modules (like `RepVGGBlock`).
