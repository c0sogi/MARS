The `RuntimeError` is caused by a dimension mismatch in the `Res2NetBottleneck` when `stride > 1`. In the original code, the initial 1x1 convolution (`conv1`) uses `stride=1`, keeping the feature maps at the input resolution, while the subsequent 3x3 convolutions in the hierarchical loop use `stride=2`. This results in the accumulating tensor `sp` being downsampled while the next split `spx[i+1]` remains at the original resolution, causing the addition `spx[i+1] + sp` to fail.

To fix this, I will implement the "Res2Net v1b" modification: apply the stride at the initial 1x1 convolution so that all feature splits are downsampled immediately. The 3x3 convolutions will then operate with `stride=1`, and the separate pooling step for the identity branch becomes unnecessary.
