Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Configuration**: I will override the `Config` parameters `EPOCHS` to 5 and `MAX_TRAIN_SAMPLES` to 5000. This ensures the training pipeline runs quickly as a "fast baseline" while still exercising the full model architecture.
2.  **Data Loading**: I will instantiate `CactusDataset` and `DataLoader` for the full validation and test sets. These will be used for the final ensemble evaluation and submission generation.
3.  **Training Loop**: I will iterate through the 5 seeds defined in `Config`. For each seed, I will execute `run_training` (which handles training and internal validation).
4.  **Ensemble Inference**: Inside the loop, I will load the best model for the current seed and perform inference on the full validation and test sets using `inference.predict`. I will accumulate the probabilities to compute the ensemble average later.
5.  **Aggregation**: I will average the accumulated probabilities across all seeds to get the final robust predictions.
6.  **Validation**: I will compute the ROC AUC score on the averaged validation predictions and print it in the required format `Final Validation Metric: <value>`.
7.  **Failure Analysis**: I will calculate the absolute error between the ensemble predictions and the ground truth labels. Then, I will compute image meta-features (Brightness, Contrast, Channel Means) from the raw validation images and calculate their Pearson correlation with the error magnitude.
8.  **Submission**: I will save the averaged test predictions to `submission.csv`.
