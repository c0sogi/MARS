Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the progression of previous experiments—specifically the dominance of "Wide" architectures (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**), the inference efficiency of "Structural Re-parameterization" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00103**), and the proven benefit of "Multi-Scale Aggregation" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**)—I propose the **Custom Ultra-Wide SE-RepNeXt with Spatial Feature Fusion**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Multi-Scale" concept. While Lesson 16 demonstrated that concatenating pooled feature vectors improves performance, it discards spatial correspondence between scales. My proposal introduces a **Spatial Feature Fusion Head** that projects mid-level features to the final resolution *before* pooling. This allows the network to model local interactions between texture details (Stage 2) and semantic shapes (Stage 3) while leveraging the massive capacity of the Ultra-Wide RepNeXt backbone.

# Model
I propose a **Custom Ultra-Wide SE-RepNeXt with Spatial Feature Fusion**.

*   **Backbone (Ultra-Wide RepNeXt):** We will implement a 3-stage backbone using **SE-RepNeXt Blocks**.
    *   **Channel Configuration:** We will strictly adhere to the **"Ultra-Wide" Channel Configuration** (`[96, 192, 384]`). This massive representational capacity is identified as the primary driver of performance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**).
    *   **Training Topology (Multi-Branch):** Each block will consist of parallel branches: a **Grouped $3 \times 3$ Convolution** (Cardinality = 32), a **Grouped $1 \times 1$ Convolution**, and an **Identity Mapping**. This ensures robust gradient flow during training.
    *   **Inference Topology (Fused):** Before inference, we will mathematically fuse the branches into a single **Grouped $3 \times 3$ Convolution**, eliminating the memory access overhead of residual additions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00103**).
    *   **Attention:** We will integrate **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Blocks** after the fused convolution in each block to accelerate convergence (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
    *   **Spatial Preservation:** The network will downsample 32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8 (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).

*   **Head (Spatial Feature Fusion):** Instead of concatenating global vectors (Lesson 16), we will fuse features spatially.
    *   **Projection:** We will take the **Stage 2** feature map ($16 \times 16$, 192 channels) and apply a re-parameterizable **Stride-2 $3 \times 3$ Convolution** to project it to $8 \times 8$.
    *   **Fusion:** We will concatenate this projected map with the **Stage 3** feature map ($8 \times 8$, 384 channels) along the channel dimension.
    *   **Mixing:** We will apply a **$1 \times 1$ Convolution** to mix the multi-scale features.
    *   **Pooling:** We will apply **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** to the fused map. We explicitly reject Covariance Pooling as GAP is sufficient and statistically stable (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**).
    *   **Classification:** The pooled vector will be passed to a single dense layer.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling to prevent interpolation artifacts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).

# Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy eliminates stochastic residual ranking errors (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**) while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**).
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
*   **Training Duration:** We will utilize a reduced training schedule (e.g., 20 epochs). The combination of the "Ultra-Wide" capacity, SE blocks, and RepVGG-style gradient flow facilitates rapid convergence.
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** Prior to inference, the model (including the backbone and the head's projection layers) will be converted from its training form (multi-branch) to its inference form (single-branch) to maximize throughput (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00103**).
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds.
