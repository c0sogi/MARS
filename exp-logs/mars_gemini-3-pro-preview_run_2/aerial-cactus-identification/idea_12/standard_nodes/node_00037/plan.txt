To optimize the solution, I will simplify the pooling mechanism and extend the training duration. First, I will replace the Learnable Generalized Mean Pooling (GeM) with standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) in the `NarrowSEResNet` architecture. While GeM is powerful, standard GAP is often sufficient and more stable for global classification tasks on small images, reducing model complexity without sacrificing performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005). I will retain the Multi-Scale Feature Aggregation strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016) and the Narrow SE-ResNet backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019, solution_lesson_node_00012). Second, I will increase the number of training epochs from 15 to 20. The previous loss curves indicated that the model was still improving; extending the training with the Cosine Annealing scheduler will help push convergence further (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

class GeM(nn.Module):
    """
    Generalized Mean Pooling (GeM) with learnable parameter p per channel.
    f = (1/|Omega| * sum(x^p))^(1/p)
    """

    def __init__(self, channels, p=GEM_P_INIT, eps=1e-6):
        super(GeM, self).__init__()
        # Learnable p per channel: shape (1, C, 1, 1) for broadcasting
        self.p = nn.Parameter(torch.ones(1, channels, 1, 1) * p)
        self.eps = eps

    def forward(self, x):
        # x: (B, C, H, W)
        # Clamp x to avoid NaN in power operation
        x = x.clamp(min=self.eps)

        # Calculate x^p
        x_pow = x.pow(self.p)
