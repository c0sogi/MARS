Based on the progression of experiments and the specific lessons learned—specifically the success of the Wide SE-ResNeXt (Idea 20) and the importance of channel capacity (Lesson 49)—I propose the **Custom Wide Split-Attention Network (ResNeSt) with Multi-Scale Aggregation**.

This strategy represents the next logical evolutionary step in the "Wide + Grouped + Attention" lineage. While Idea 20 successfully combined Grouped Convolutions (ResNeXt) with Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE), these components operated sequentially (Convolution $\to$ Attention). The **Split-Attention** architecture structurally unifies these operations, enabling the network to apply attention *within* channel groups (cardinals). This allows for more fine-grained feature selection and cross-channel interaction modeling compared to the global recalibration of SE blocks, while maintaining the massive representational capacity of the "Wide" configuration.

# Model
I propose a **Custom Wide Split-Attention Network**.

*   **Backbone (Wide Split-Attention):** We will implement a 3-stage backbone using **Split-Attention Blocks** (Radix-2).
    *   **Structure:** Instead of standard residual blocks, each block splits the input channels into $k$ groups (cardinals), and further splits each group into $r$ branches (radices). These branches undergo transformations and are then fused via a Softmax-based attention mechanism that adaptively selects the most informative features from each branch. This generalizes the "SE-ResNeXt" concept into a single, unified operation.
    *   **Width:** We will strictly adhere to the **"Super-Wide" Channel Configuration** (`[64, 128, 256]`) identified as optimal in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**. The Split-Attention mechanism is highly parameter-efficient, allowing us to maintain this large capacity without exploding the computational budget.
    *   **Shortcuts:** We will strictly utilize **1x1 convolutions** for projection shortcuts during downsampling. We explicitly reject 3x3 shortcuts, as empirical evidence showed they degrade performance **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00063)**.
    *   **Spatial Preservation:** The network will downsample 32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8. We strictly preserve the 8x8 resolution to avoid the loss of fine-grained spatial details **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.

*   **Head (Multi-Scale Aggregation):** We will retain the concatenation-based aggregation head that proved effective for wide models.
    *   We will extract feature maps from the end of **Stage 2** (16x16) and **Stage 3** (8x8).
    *   We will apply **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** to both. We explicitly reject Covariance Pooling as the high channel count makes second-order statistics computationally expensive and potentially unstable **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00053)**.
    *   The concatenated vector will be passed to a single dense classification layer. This ensures the classifier has direct access to both the texture-rich mid-level features and the semantic high-level features **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling to prevent interpolation artifacts **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.

# Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy is chosen to eliminate stochastic residual ranking errors **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)** while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00008)**.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)**.
*   **Training Duration:** We will utilize a reduced training schedule (e.g., 20 epochs). The combination of the "Wide" capacity **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00049)** and the convergence acceleration of attention mechanisms **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00012)** ensures the model will saturate the metric rapidly.
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

# Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds. This maximizes robustness and minimizes stochastic noise.
