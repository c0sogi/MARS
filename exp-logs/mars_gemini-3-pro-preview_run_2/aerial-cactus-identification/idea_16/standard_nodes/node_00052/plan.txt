To optimize the solution, I will implement a "Narrow Multi-Scale ResNet" strategy. Based on Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00016` and `solution_lesson_node_00019`, for low-resolution 32x32 images, a narrower network (starting with 16 channels instead of 32) combined with multi-scale feature aggregation is sufficient to achieve perfect accuracy while significantly reducing computational cost (training time reduced by ~50%). I will also reduce the training duration from 20 to 12 epochs, as the previous wider model converged to near-perfect metrics by epoch 7-10 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`). Additionally, I will disable the automatic execution of the training pipeline in `library/model.py` to prevent unintended side effects during import.
