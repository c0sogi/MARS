Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose an optimized strategy that evolves the architecture into a **Custom Multi-Scale ResNet**. This approach addresses the potential loss of fine-grained texture details in the final network stages while strictly adhering to the stability and resolution constraints identified in prior runs.

### Model
I propose a **Custom Multi-Scale ResNet**. This architecture retains the successful backbone of the previous best solution but introduces a structural innovation in the classification head to explicitly leverage the multi-hierarchical nature of the features.

*   **Backbone (Retained):** We will use the **Custom Lightweight ResNet** backbone. It consists of an initial convolution followed by three residual stages. We will strictly use **3x3 convolutions** (avoiding large kernels, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**) and maintain the shallow downsampling schedule (32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8) to preserve spatial resolution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).
*   **Structural Innovation (Multi-Scale Aggregation):** Instead of performing Global Average Pooling (GAP) solely on the final 8x8 feature map, we will implement **Multi-Scale Feature Aggregation**. We will apply GAP to the output of **Stage 2 (16x16)** and **Stage 3 (8x8)** independently. These two feature vectors will be concatenated to form a fused representation before passing to the final classification layer.
    *   **Rationale:** Cacti identification in low-resolution aerial imagery relies heavily on high-frequency textures (spines, vegetation patterns) which are most prominent in earlier stages, as well as semantic shapes found in later stages. By explicitly providing the classifier with features from multiple resolutions, we improve the model's ability to discriminate based on texture without relying solely on the potentially over-abstracted final layer.
*   **Head:** The concatenated feature vector will be passed to a single dense layer. We will **exclude Dropout**, as the structural regularization of the residual backbone and the high-signal nature of the data make it unnecessary and potentially harmful (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will not perform any resizing or upscaling (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy. We will apply only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques have been proven to degrade performance on this specific dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).

### Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of the Custom Multi-Scale ResNet using different random seeds. We choose this over heterogeneous ensembling because the ResNet backbone is proven to be stable and metric-saturating, whereas mixing in other architectures (like DenseNet) introduces instability without performance gains (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**).
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use the **AdamW** optimizer combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination is critical for eliminating residual ranking errors and ensuring robust convergence across all ensemble members (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

### Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds. This maximizes robustness and minimizes stochastic noise (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
