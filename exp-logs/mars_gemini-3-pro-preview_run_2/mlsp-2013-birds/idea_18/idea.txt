Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Selective Signal-Noise Mixup**.

This strategy introduces a **Data-Centric Structural Innovation** by redefining the Mixup augmentation logic. Instead of randomly mixing any two samples (which often creates confusing "Bird A + Bird B" overlaps, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**), this approach explicitly pairs **Labeled (Bird)** samples with **Unlabeled (Background/Noise)** samples. This "Selective Mixup" acts as a targeted data generator, placing known bird calls into diverse background environments, thereby multiplying the effective training data (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**) without introducing the label ambiguity that degraded performance in previous iterations.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity, which has proven superior to weight averaging techniques (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones trained in parallel:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** Retained for its proven robustness on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** Integrated to capture fine-grained features; optimization issues are resolved via the weighted loss formulation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** Retained for feature reuse, acting as a structural regularizer when constrained to lower input resolutions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).

**Head Design: Multi-Sample Dropout**
All backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head. The Global Average Pooling output will pass through 5 parallel dropout layers (with different random masks), each followed by a shared fully connected layer. The outputs are averaged during the forward pass. This "ensemble within a model" accelerates convergence and provides internal regularization (**Cite Idea 13**).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Multi-Resolution Strategy (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**):**
    *   **ResNet18 / EfficientNet-B0:** Resize to **224 (Freq) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
    *   **DenseNet121:** Resize to **160 (Freq) x 320 (Time)**. This lower resolution forces the deeper model to learn macro-scale patterns, preventing it from overfitting to high-frequency noise (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

**Augmentation Strategy (Structural Innovation)**
*   **Selective Signal-Noise Mixup:**
    *   *Mechanism:* Partition the training batch into "Signal" (samples with $\ge 1$ label) and "Noise" (samples with 0 labels, which constitute ~43% of the data).
    *   *Operation:* Instead of mixing random pairs, strictly mix **Signal + Noise**.
    *   *Rationale:* Standard Mixup often combines two different birds, creating unrealistic, high-entropy samples that confuse the model (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**). By mixing birds only with background noise, we synthetically generate "clean" training examples of birds in varying environmental conditions, directly addressing data scarcity without ambiguity.
*   **Spectrogram Time-Rolling:** Apply random circular shifts along the time axis (**Cite Idea 16**) to exploit translation invariance.
*   **Standard Augs:** Retain **SpecAugment** and **RandomBrightnessContrast** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).

### Training
**Procedure: Noise-Augmented Optimization**
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**. Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with Learning Rate **`1e-3`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**) and conservative Weight Decay **`1e-4`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**).
    *   **Schedule:** Use a **Constant Learning Rate** to ensure escape from local minima in the noisy loss landscape (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
    *   **Duration:** Target **1000 Total Update Steps** per fold (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**).
*   **Reproducibility:** Use `worker_init_fn` to enforce RNG diversity across data loader workers (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**).
*   **Validation:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).

### Evaluation
**Inference: TTA & Grand Ensemble**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants (Original + 3 Time-Roll shifts) and average their predictions (**Cite Idea 16**).
*   **Aggregation:** Apply TTA to all 15 trained models (5 Folds × 3 Architectures) and average their outputs. This combines the stability of bagging, the diversity of heterogeneous architectures, and the robustness of Selective Mixup to maximize AUC.
