Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Out-of-Fold (OOF) Self-Distillation**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution from a "Single-Stage Supervised" pipeline to a **"Two-Stage Self-Distillation"** paradigm. It directly addresses the "Micro-Data" variance bottleneck (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**) and the failure of Semi-Supervised Learning on noisy test data (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00061**). By distilling the "dark knowledge" (inter-class correlations and sample ambiguity) from the ensemble's own Out-Of-Fold predictions back into a new generation of models, we create a richer, smoothed supervision signal that stabilizes training and improves generalization without relying on external or noisy unlabeled data.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity, which has proven superior to weight averaging techniques (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones trained in parallel:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** Retained for its proven robustness on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** Retained to capture fine-grained features; optimization issues are resolved via weighted loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** Retained for feature reuse, acting as a structural regularizer when constrained to lower input resolutions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).

**Head Design: Multi-Sample Dropout**
All backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head (**Cite Idea 13**). This "ensemble within a model" accelerates convergence and provides internal regularization, which is critical for the student models to effectively absorb the distilled knowledge.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Multi-Resolution Strategy (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**):**
    *   **ResNet18 / EfficientNet-B0:** Resize to **224 (Freq) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
    *   **DenseNet121:** Resize to **160 (Freq) x 320 (Time)** to force macro-scale feature learning (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: Two-Stage OOF Self-Distillation**

**Stage 1: Teacher Generation (Standard Supervised)**
*   Train the complete Heterogeneous Ensemble using **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).
*   **Loss:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with **`pos_weight`** to handle class imbalance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Augmentation:** **Spectrogram Time-Rolling** (Circular Shift) (**Cite Idea 16**), **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**), and **SpecAugment**.
*   **Optimization:** **AdamW** (LR `1e-3`, Decay `1e-4`) with **Constant Schedule** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**, **solution_lesson_node_00040**).
*   **Output:** Generate **Out-Of-Fold (OOF) Probabilities** for the entire training set. These serve as the "Soft Labels" for Stage 2.

**Stage 2: Student Distillation (Born-Again Ensemble)**
*   Re-initialize a fresh Heterogeneous Ensemble (Student) with the same architecture.
*   Train on the same **Iterative Stratified K-Folds**, but modify the supervision signal.
*   **Distillation Loss:** Use a composite loss function:
    $$ L = \gamma \cdot \text{BCE}_{\text{weighted}}(\text{Target}, \text{Label}) + (1-\gamma) \cdot \text{BCE}_{\text{soft}}(\text{Target}, \text{Teacher\_OOF}) $$
    *   *Rationale:* The Hard Labels (Ground Truth) provide the correct class targets. The Soft Labels (Teacher OOF) provide information about class ambiguity and difficulty (e.g., "this empty recording looks 10% like a bird"). Learning this "dark knowledge" prevents the student from becoming overconfident on noisy/ambiguous samples, effectively smoothing the decision boundary for the micro-dataset.
*   **Augmentation & Optimization:** Identical to Stage 1.

### Evaluation
**Inference: Distilled Bagging with TTA**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants (Original + 3 Time-Roll shifts) and average their predictions (**Cite Idea 16**).
*   **Aggregation:** Apply TTA to all 15 trained **Student** models (from Stage 2) and average their outputs.
*   This approach combines the structural diversity of the heterogeneous architecture, the temporal robustness of Time-Rolling, and the smoothed generalization of Self-Distillation to maximize AUC.
