Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Time-Extended Resolution**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by correcting the optimization failures of the previous ensemble attempt (Idea 7) and introducing a new input topology (Rectangular Resolution) to address the temporal blurring inherent in square resizing. It strictly retains the successful components of the "Current Best" solution (Idea 2) while implementing the critical corrections for micro-dataset training identified in the failure analysis.

### Model
**Architecture: Heterogeneous Rectangular Ensemble**
*   **Dual-Backbone Design:** The solution will utilize two distinct architectures trained in parallel:
    1.  **ResNet18:** Retained for its proven robustness on this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    2.  **DenseNet121:** Re-introduced to leverage feature reuse (dense connectivity), which is theoretically superior for small data but failed previously due to hyperparameter misalignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Input Topology (Structural Innovation):** Instead of the standard square input (224x224), the models will accept **Rectangular Inputs of 224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**.
    *   *Rationale:* Compressing a 10-second audio clip into 224 pixels width blurs short, high-frequency bird calls. Doubling the temporal dimension to 448 preserves signal integrity without the complexity of tiling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027) or the noise amplification of engineered channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030). Both ResNet and DenseNet utilize Global Average Pooling (GAP), allowing them to adapt natively to this non-square aspect ratio.
*   **Head:** Both backbones will terminate in a **Global Average Pooling** layer followed by a single fully connected layer with 19 outputs and **Sigmoid** activation. We strictly avoid Dual Pooling to prevent noise amplification (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Splitting**
*   **Input Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure the model trains on denoised signal representations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012, solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Adaptation:**
    *   **Resize:** Resize images to **224x448**.
    *   **Pseudo-RGB:** Replicate the single-channel grayscale spectrogram 3 times to create a 3-channel input. This is preferred over engineering Delta channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Splitting:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)**. This is mandatory to mitigate the stochastic variance of single splits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Mixup:** Apply **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)**. This provides necessary regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) without creating the ambiguous samples that degraded performance at $\alpha=1.0$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Photometric:** Apply **RandomBrightnessContrast** to handle recording gain variations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Geometric:** Apply **SpecAugment** (Time/Frequency Masking).

### Training
**Procedure: Step-Based Convergence Regime**
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**.
    *   *Rationale:* Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable. Omitting this was the primary failure mode in Idea 3 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Optimization Strategy (Correction):**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a conservative weight decay of **`1e-4`**. This corrects the aggressive decay (`1e-2`) that caused underfitting in Idea 7 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Duration:** Define training by **Total Update Steps** (target: ~1000 steps per fold), rather than epochs. Given the micro-dataset size (~165 samples/fold), standard epoch counts result in insufficient gradient updates. We will significantly increase the epoch count (e.g., to 60-80) to ensure the model converges (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Reproducibility:** Use a `worker_init_fn` in DataLoaders to ensure deterministic augmentation sequences (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Evaluation
**Inference: Heterogeneous Bagging**
*   **Process:** For each test recording, resize to 224x448 and generate predictions using all trained models (5 ResNet18 models + 5 DenseNet121 models).
*   **Aggregation:** Average the probability outputs of all 10 models.
    *   *Rationale:* Averaging predictions from architecturally diverse models reduces generalization error more effectively than single-architecture ensembles, stabilizing the final output against the noise inherent in the small training set (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
