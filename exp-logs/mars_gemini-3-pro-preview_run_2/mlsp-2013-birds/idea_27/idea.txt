Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous "Born-Again" Ensemble with Iterative Weighted Self-Distillation**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Distillation" concept introduced in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00085**. While the previous best solution successfully stabilized the high-capacity DenseNet121 by distilling knowledge from stable anchors, it left the anchors themselves (ResNet18, EfficientNet-B0) in their "Generation 0" state, limited by the noise and sparsity of the hard labels (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**). This new strategy implements a **Recursive Distillation** pipeline where the *entire* ensemble is re-trained ("born again") using the smoothed soft-targets from the previous generation. This maximizes the performance of all components, not just the unstable ones.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** Proven robustness on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** Captures fine-grained features; optimized via weighted loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** Maximizes feature reuse (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**). Its optimization instability is resolved via the distillation pipeline (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00085**).

**Head Design:**
All backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head (**Cite Idea 13**). The Global Average Pooling output passes through 5 parallel dropout layers (with different random masks), each followed by a shared fully connected layer. The outputs are averaged during the forward pass to accelerate convergence and provide internal regularization.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Rectangular Resolution:** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls better than square resizing (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**) while avoiding the overfitting risks of excessive resolution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights, avoiding the pitfalls of engineered Delta channels (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: Two-Generation Weighted Self-Distillation**

**Generation 0: Teacher Training**
*   Train the **Stable Anchors** (ResNet18 and EfficientNet-B0) using **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).
*   **Loss:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with **`pos_weight`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Augmentation:** **Cyclic Time-Rolling** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**), **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**), and **SpecAugment**.
*   **Optimization:** **AdamW** (LR `1e-3`, Decay `1e-4`) with **Constant Schedule** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
*   **Output:** Aggregate the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions from both anchors to create a robust **Ensemble Soft Target** for the entire training set.

**Generation 1: Student Training (Born-Again)**
*   Train the **Full Ensemble** (ResNet18, EfficientNet-B0, *and* DenseNet121).
    *   *Note:* We re-train fresh copies of the anchors alongside the DenseNet.
*   **Loss (Structural Innovation):** Implement **Weighted Self-Distillation**.
    *   $$ L = \text{BCE}(\text{Target}, \text{Pred}, \text{pos\_weight}) + \lambda \cdot \text{BCE}(\text{Gen0\_OOF}, \text{Pred}, \text{pos\_weight}) $$
    *   *Correction:* We explicitly apply `pos_weight` to the distillation term. This ensures the students learn the "dark knowledge" (uncertainty) regarding rare classes without being overwhelmed by the majority negative class, correcting the failure mode of unweighted distillation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00080**).
*   **Rationale:** This allows the Anchors to improve themselves (Self-Distillation) by learning from the smoothed ensemble average, while simultaneously stabilizing the DenseNet.

### Evaluation
**Inference: Cyclic TTA & Grand Bagging**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants: [Original, Time-Roll(25%), Time-Roll(50%), Time-Roll(75%)].
    *   *Rationale:* Averaging predictions across these cyclic shifts stabilizes the output against framing bias and exploits the model's learned temporal invariance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
*   **Aggregation:** Apply TTA to all 15 trained **Generation 1** models (5 ResNet + 5 EfficientNet + 5 DenseNet) and average their outputs. This combines the stability of bagging, the diversity of heterogeneous architectures, and the smoothed generalization of Born-Again Distillation to maximize AUC.
