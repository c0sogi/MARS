Based on the accumulated lessons and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Tri-Architecture Heterogeneous Ensemble with Asymmetric Loss and Test-Time Augmentation**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Current Best" solution (Idea 11). It retains the successful "Maximally Diverse" backbone configuration (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**) but fundamentally upgrades the optimization objective from static weighting to **Asymmetric Loss (ASL)**. This directly targets the class imbalance bottleneck identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00020** with a dynamic mechanism superior to fixed weights. Furthermore, it formalizes **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** to mitigate the micro-data variance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**) by exploiting temporal invariance.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity, which has proven superior to weight averaging techniques (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones trained in parallel:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** Retained for its proven robustness and stability on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** Retained to capture fine-grained features; optimization issues are resolved via the aggressive learning rate strategy (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** Retained for feature reuse, which is critical for maximizing information extraction from small datasets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).

**Head Design:**
All three backbones will terminate in a **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** layer followed by a single fully connected layer with 19 outputs. We strictly avoid "Dual Pooling" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00024**) and "GeM Pooling" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**) to prevent noise amplification and overfitting.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Rectangular Resolution (Exploitation):** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls better than square resizing (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**) while avoiding the overfitting risks of excessive resolution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights, avoiding the pitfalls of engineered Delta channels (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: Asymmetric Optimization Regime**
*   **Loss Function (Structural Innovation):** Replace `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with **Asymmetric Loss (ASL)**.
    *   *Mechanism:* ASL dynamically down-weights "easy negative" samples (background silence) while maintaining strong gradients for "hard negatives" (confusing artifacts) and positive samples.
    *   *Rationale:* **Cite solution_lesson_node_00020** established that handling imbalance is more critical than architecture. While `pos_weight` (used previously) applies a static scalar, ASL adapts to the model's confidence during training, preventing the overwhelming volume of easy negatives from washing out the gradients of rare positive signals.
*   **Optimization Strategy:**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with Learning Rate **`1e-3`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**) and conservative Weight Decay **`1e-4`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**).
    *   **Schedule:** Use a **Constant Learning Rate**. This is required to escape local minima in the noisy loss landscape of micro-datasets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
    *   **Duration:** Target **1000 Total Update Steps** per fold (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Standard Mixup:** Apply **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)**. We explicitly reject "Selective Signal-Noise Mixup" in favor of standard global mixing to learn inter-class boundaries (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00070**).
    *   **Spectrogram Time-Rolling:** Apply random circular shifts along the time axis during training to exploit translation invariance.
    *   **Reproducibility:** Use `worker_init_fn` to enforce RNG diversity across data loader workers (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**).
*   **Validation:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).

### Evaluation
**Inference: TTA & Grand Bagging**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) (Structural Innovation):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants: [Original, Time-Roll(25%), Time-Roll(50%), Time-Roll(75%)].
    *   *Rationale:* This exploits the cyclic nature of the 10-second loop. Averaging predictions across these shifts stabilizes the output against framing bias, effectively quadrupling the inference-time "views" of the data.
*   **Aggregation:**
    1.  Apply TTA for each model instance.
    2.  Average the TTA outputs across all 15 trained models (5 Folds × 3 Architectures).
    3.  This "Grand Ensemble" combines the stability of bagging, the diversity of heterogeneous architectures, and the robustness of ASL and TTA to maximize AUC.
