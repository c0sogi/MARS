Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Corrected Tri-Architecture Heterogeneous Ensemble with Aggressive Domain Adaptation**.

This strategy represents the structural evolution of the "Heterogeneous Ensemble" (Idea 8). It successfully integrates the **EfficientNet-B0** architecture—which previously failed due to optimization alignment errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040) and loss formulation issues (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)—to create a "Maximally Diverse" ensemble. By combining the three primary CNN inductive biases (Residual, Dense, and Inverted Residual) under the specific "Rectangular Resolution" and "Aggressive Fine-Tuning" protocols proven effective for this micro-dataset, this solution maximizes feature extraction capability while mitigating the high variance of small-data training.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
The solution will utilize three distinct CNN architectures trained in parallel to maximize the diversity of learned features (Inductive Bias Diversity):
1.  **ResNet18 (Exploitation):** Retained for its proven robustness and stability on this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012). It contributes a **Residual** bias.
2.  **DenseNet121 (Exploitation):** Retained for its **Dense** connectivity, which maximizes feature reuse and gradient flow in low-data regimes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
3.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Structural Innovation/Correction):** We integrate EfficientNet-B0 to contribute an **Inverted Residual (MBConv)** bias.
    *   *Correction:* Previous attempts to use EfficientNet failed due to low learning rates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040) and unweighted loss functions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020). We explicitly correct these by aligning its training protocol with the aggressive adaptation strategy used for the other backbones.

**Head Design:**
All three backbones will terminate in a **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** layer followed by a single fully connected layer with 19 outputs and **Sigmoid** activation.
*   *Constraint:* We strictly avoid "Dual Pooling" (Max+Avg) to prevent noise amplification (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024) and "GeM Pooling" to avoid overfitting learnable parameters on this micro-dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Splitting**
*   **Input Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure the model trains on denoised signal representations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Rectangular Resolution (Exploitation):** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**.
    *   *Rationale:* We strictly adhere to the 1:2 aspect ratio. This preserves the temporal fidelity of short bird calls better than square resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) while avoiding the overfitting risks of excessive resolution (e.g., 224x896) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
*   **Pseudo-RGB:** Replicate the single-channel grayscale spectrogram 3 times to create a 3-channel input. This adapts the data to the pretrained weights better than engineering Delta channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Splitting:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** to mitigate stochastic variance and ensure balanced class distribution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
**Procedure: Aggressive Domain Adaptation**
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**.
    *   *Rationale:* Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable for this imbalanced dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Optimization Strategy (Crucial Correction):**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with conservative weight decay (`1e-4`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Learning Rate:** Set to **`1e-3`** for *all* backbones, including EfficientNet.
        *   *Rationale:* Previous failures with EfficientNet were driven by a conservative `1e-4` LR. A high LR is required to aggressively adapt the ImageNet-pretrained filters to the fundamentally different audio spectrogram domain within the limited training budget (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
    *   **Scheduler:** Use a **Constant Learning Rate** (or very slow decay).
        *   *Rationale:* Aggressive schedules like Cosine Annealing decay too quickly for micro-datasets, preventing escape from local minima. A sustained rate is required (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Duration:** Define training by **Total Update Steps** (target ~1000 steps per fold) to ensure convergence regardless of batch size (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Mixup:** Apply **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)**. This provides necessary regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) without creating the destructive ambiguity of aggressive Mixup ($\alpha=1.0$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Photometric:** Apply **RandomBrightnessContrast** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Geometric:** Apply **SpecAugment**.

### Evaluation
**Inference: Grand Heterogeneous Ensemble**
*   **Process:** For each test recording, resize to 224x448 and generate predictions using all trained models from the cross-validation loop (5 ResNet + 5 DenseNet + 5 EfficientNet = 15 models).
*   **Aggregation:** Average the probability outputs of all 15 models.
    *   *Rationale:* This combines the stability of bagging (averaging across folds) with the maximum available diversity of architectural inductive biases, providing the most robust defense against the noise and variance inherent in training on ~200 samples (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
