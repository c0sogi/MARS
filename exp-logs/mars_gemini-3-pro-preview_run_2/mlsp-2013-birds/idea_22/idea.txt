Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Anchor-Guided Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Heterogeneous Ensemble" concept. It specifically targets the **instability of the DenseNet121 backbone** identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00073** and the general **micro-data variance** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**). By combining **Out-Of-Fold (OOF) Distillation** (to smooth targets) with **Sharpness-Aware Minimization** (to smooth the loss landscape), we create a rigorous optimization environment that allows the high-capacity DenseNet to converge reliably, maximizing the ensemble's diversity.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones trained in parallel:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** The "Stable Anchor," proven robust on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** The "Stable Anchor," optimized via weighted loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** The "High-Capacity Student." This model is theoretically superior for small data due to feature reuse (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**) but has historically failed due to optimization instability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).

**Head Design:**
All backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head (**Cite Idea 13**). The Global Average Pooling output passes through 5 parallel dropout layers, averaged to accelerate convergence and provide internal regularization.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Rectangular Resolution:** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**) while avoiding the overfitting risks of excessive resolution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: Two-Stage Anchor-Guided SAM Optimization**

**Stage 1: Train Stable Anchors (Standard Optimization)**
*   Train **ResNet18** and **EfficientNet-B0** using **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)**.
*   **Loss:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with **`pos_weight`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Augmentation:** **Cyclic Time-Rolling** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**), **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**), and **SpecAugment**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** (LR `1e-3`) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**).
*   **Output:** Generate **Out-Of-Fold (OOF) Soft Probabilities** for the entire training set.

**Stage 2: Train DenseNet121 (SAM + Distillation)**
*   Train **DenseNet121** on the same folds using the **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)** optimizer.
    *   *Structural Innovation:* SAM simultaneously minimizes loss value and loss sharpness (geometry). This explicitly prevents the model from converging to the sharp, unstable minima that caused DenseNet's previous failures on this micro-dataset.
*   **Composite Loss:**
    $$ L = \text{BCE}_{\text{weighted}}(\text{Target}, \text{Label}) + \lambda \cdot \text{KLDiv}(\text{Target}, \text{Anchor\_OOF}) $$
    *   *Rationale:* The Anchors provide stable "dark knowledge" (soft targets) that guides the DenseNet. Combined with SAM, this ensures the model finds a flat, generalizable solution.
*   **Augmentation:** Identical to Stage 1.

### Evaluation
**Inference: TTA & Grand Bagging**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants (Original, Roll-25%, Roll-50%, Roll-75%) and average their predictions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
*   **Aggregation:** Apply TTA to all 15 trained models (5 ResNet + 5 EfficientNet + 5 SAM-Stabilized DenseNet) and average their outputs.
*   This strategy leverages the stability of anchors and the geometric regularization of SAM to successfully integrate the DenseNet, maximizing the ensemble's AUC.
