Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Multi-Instance Learning (MIL) ResNet18 Ensemble with Temporal Tiling**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation by shifting from "Global Image Classification" (resizing the entire 10-second spectrogram to a square) to a **Time-Distributed Multi-Instance** approach. This directly addresses the resolution loss inherent in previous methods and handles the temporal sparsity of bird calls (where a bird may only vocalize for a fraction of the clip) without adding the parameter complexity that caused previous failures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Model
**Architecture: Time-Distributed ResNet18 with Max-Logit Aggregation**
*   **Backbone:** I will retain the **ResNet18** backbone (pretrained on ImageNet). This model has proven to be the most robust feature extractor for this dataset, outperforming tabular methods (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) and heavier architectures like EfficientNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Structural Innovation (Tiling):** Instead of processing the entire spectrogram as a single image, the model will process the input as a "bag" of **3 overlapping temporal tiles** (e.g., covering 0-4s, 3-7s, and 6-10s).
    *   *Rationale:* Resizing a wide 10-second spectrogram to a standard 224x224 square distorts the spectral shape of bird calls (making them unnaturally thin) and blurs high-frequency details. Tiling preserves the native aspect ratio and time-frequency resolution of the signals.
*   **Aggregation Head:** The ResNet18 backbone (with shared weights) will extract logits for each of the 3 tiles independently. These logits will be aggregated using a **Max-Pooling** operation across the temporal dimension *before* the final Sigmoid activation.
    *   *Rationale:* This implements a parameter-free Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) strategy. If a bird is present in *any* of the tiles, the max operator ensures the recording is labeled positive, while ignoring the silence/noise in the other tiles. This avoids the noise amplification observed with Dual Pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Splitting**
*   **Input:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to minimize stationary noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Tiling Strategy:** During preprocessing, slice each recording's spectrogram into 3 fixed, overlapping segments along the time axis.
*   **Stratified K-Fold Split:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)**. This is mandatory to mitigate the high variance observed in single-split training runs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) and ensure rare species are distributed evenly.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Mixup:** Apply Mixup with a calibrated **$\alpha=0.4$**. This provides necessary regularization for the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) without creating the overly ambiguous samples that degraded performance when $\alpha=1.0$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **SpecAugment:** Apply time/frequency masking to individual tiles.
    *   **Photometric:** Include RandomBrightnessContrast to handle gain variations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

### Training
**Procedure: Weighted Loss with Cross-Validation**
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**.
    *   *Rationale:* Explicitly weighting positive classes is critical. The removal of `pos_weight` was the primary cause of failure in the EfficientNet experiment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020). The weights will be calculated as `negative_count / positive_count` for each class in the training fold.
*   **Binary Relevance:** The model outputs 19 independent probabilities, adhering to the Binary Relevance principle (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Reproducibility:** Use a `worker_init_fn` in the DataLoader to ensure deterministic augmentation sequences, preventing the stochastic instability observed previously (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Evaluation
**Inference: Tiled Ensemble Averaging**
*   **Process:** For each test recording, generate the 3 temporal tiles and pass them through the trained model to obtain the max-aggregated prediction.
*   **Ensemble:** Repeat this for all 5 models trained in the K-Fold loop.
*   **Final Prediction:** Average the probability outputs of the 5 models. This bagging approach stabilizes predictions against the noise inherent in the small training set (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
