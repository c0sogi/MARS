Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Class-Weighted Knowledge Distillation**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Heterogeneous Ensemble" concept. It specifically addresses the **instability of the DenseNet121 backbone** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073) and the **catastrophic failure of previous distillation attempts** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080). By implementing a **Two-Stage Pipeline** with a **Corrected Weighted Loss**, we leverage the stability of robust anchors (ResNet/EfficientNet) to guide the high-capacity DenseNet, ensuring it converges effectively on the micro-dataset without succumbing to class imbalance.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** The "Stable Anchor," proven robust on this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** The "Stable Anchor," optimized via weighted loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** The "Student." This model is theoretically superior for small data due to feature reuse (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) but has historically failed due to optimization instability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).

**Head Design:**
All backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head (Cite Idea 13). The Global Average Pooling output passes through 5 parallel dropout layers (with different random masks), each followed by a shared fully connected layer. The outputs are averaged during the forward pass to accelerate convergence.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Rectangular Resolution:** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) while avoiding the overfitting risks of excessive resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).

### Training
**Procedure: Two-Stage Class-Weighted Distillation**

**Stage 1: Train Stable Anchors**
*   Train **ResNet18** and **EfficientNet-B0** using **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Loss:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with **`pos_weight`** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Augmentation:** **Cyclic Time-Rolling** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073), **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022), and **SpecAugment**.
*   **Optimization:** **AdamW** (LR `1e-3`, Decay `1e-4`) with **Constant Schedule** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040, solution_lesson_node_00036).
*   **Output:** Generate **Out-Of-Fold (OOF) Soft Probabilities** for the entire training set.

**Stage 2: Train Student (DenseNet121)**
*   Train **DenseNet121** on the same folds using the OOF predictions from Stage 1 as targets.
*   **Structural Innovation (The Correction):** Implement a **Class-Weighted Distillation Loss**.
    *   *Failure Analysis:* Previous distillation attempts failed because the soft-target loss term was unweighted, causing the student to learn the teacher's bias toward the majority class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080).
    *   *Correction:* Apply the **same `pos_weight`** to the distillation term as the supervised term.
    *   $$ L = \text{BCE}(\text{Target}, \text{Pred}, \text{pos\_weight}) + \lambda \cdot \text{BCE}(\text{Anchor\_OOF}, \text{Pred}, \text{pos\_weight}) $$
    *   *Rationale:* This forces the DenseNet to respect the Anchors' "dark knowledge" (uncertainty) *specifically regarding the rare bird species*, stabilizing its convergence without being overwhelmed by the negatives.

### Evaluation
**Inference: Cyclic TTA & Grand Bagging**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants: [Original, Time-Roll(25%), Time-Roll(50%), Time-Roll(75%)].
    *   *Rationale:* Averaging predictions across these cyclic shifts stabilizes the output against framing bias and exploits the model's learned temporal invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).
*   **Aggregation:** Apply TTA to all 15 trained models (5 ResNet + 5 EfficientNet + 5 Stabilized DenseNet) and average their outputs. This combines the stability of the Anchors, the feature diversity of the stabilized DenseNet, and the temporal robustness of TTA to maximize AUC.
