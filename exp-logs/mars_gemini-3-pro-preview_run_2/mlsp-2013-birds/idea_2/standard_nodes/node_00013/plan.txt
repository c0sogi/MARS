To improve the model's performance on this small dataset, I will implement a strategy focused on enhancing the visual representation of the audio and improving generalization through advanced regularization.

1.  **Higher Resolution Spectrograms**: I will increase the input image size from `(224, 224)` to `(256, 512)`. The original spectrograms have a height of 256 and a width of ~1246. Resizing to `(256, 512)` preserves the full frequency resolution (height) and significantly reduces temporal compression compared to square inputs. This aligns with the lesson that treating audio as an image problem requires preserving the structural topology of the spectrogram (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
2.  **EfficientNet Backbone**: I will replace `resnet18` with `efficientnet_b0`. EfficientNet architectures generally provide better parameter efficiency and feature extraction capabilities for transfer learning, which is crucial when training data is scarce.
3.  **Mixup Augmentation**: I will implement Mixup in the training loop. By training on linear combinations of images and labels, Mixup acts as a strong regularizer, encouraging the model to behave linearly in-between training examples. This is highly effective for preventing overfitting on small datasets.
4.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**: I will increase the number of epochs to 40 and patience to 10, as Mixup increases the difficulty of the training task, requiring more iterations to converge.
