Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Semi-Supervised Heterogeneous Ensemble with Multi-Resolution Distillation**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution from a purely supervised paradigm (which has saturated at ~0.91 AUC due to data scarcity, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00042**) to a **Semi-Supervised Self-Training pipeline**. By treating the test set as unlabeled data and leveraging the high-quality predictions of our heterogeneous ensemble to generate soft targets, we effectively expand the training set size. This directly addresses the primary bottleneck of "Micro-Data" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**) while retaining the architectural robustness of the "Tri-Architecture" and "Multi-Resolution" designs (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**).

### Model
**Architecture: Heterogeneous Tri-Backbone Ensemble**
We retain the highly successful **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize inductive bias diversity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**):
1.  **ResNet18** (Residual Bias): Robust feature extractor for rectangular inputs (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
2.  **DenseNet121** (Dense Bias): Maximizes feature reuse (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
3.  **EfficientNet-B0** (Inverted Residual Bias): Adds diversity; optimization issues are resolved via weighted loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).

**Head Design:**
All backbones will utilize **Multi-Sample Dropout** heads (**Cite Idea 13**). This "ensemble within a model" accelerates convergence and provides internal regularization, which is critical when training on the expanded but potentially noisy pseudo-labeled dataset.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure denoised inputs (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Multi-Resolution Strategy (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**):**
    *   **ResNet18 / EfficientNet-B0:** Resize to **224 (Freq) x 448 (Time)** to preserve temporal fidelity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
    *   **DenseNet121:** Resize to **160 (Freq) x 320 (Time)** to act as an architectural regularizer (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: Two-Stage Self-Distillation Loop**

**Stage 1: Teacher Training (Supervised)**
*   Train the complete heterogeneous ensemble (5-Fold CV) on the labeled **Train Set** only.
*   **Loss:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with **`pos_weight`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Optimization:** **AdamW** (LR `1e-3`, Decay `1e-4`) with a **Constant Schedule** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**, **solution_lesson_node_00040**).
*   **Augmentation:** **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**, **solution_lesson_node_00022**), **SpecAugment**, and **RandomBrightnessContrast**.
*   **Reproducibility:** Use `worker_init_fn` to enforce RNG diversity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**).

**Stage 2: Pseudo-Labeling (Inference)**
*   Pass the **Test Set** through the trained Teacher Ensemble.
*   Average the predictions to generate **Soft Pseudo-Labels** (probabilities between 0 and 1).
*   *Innovation:* Do not apply hard thresholding. Use the raw soft probabilities to preserve the teacher's uncertainty (Distillation).

**Stage 3: Student Training (Semi-Supervised)**
*   Re-initialize a fresh Heterogeneous Ensemble (Student).
*   Train on the **Union of Labeled Train Data + Pseudo-Labeled Test Data**.
*   **Loss Formulation:**
    *   For Labeled Data: Standard Weighted BCE (Ground Truth).
    *   For Test Data: **Soft-Target BCE**. The loss is calculated against the soft pseudo-labels. This allows the model to learn from the "dark knowledge" (inter-class correlations) captured by the Teacher.
*   **Sampling:** Use a balanced sampler or weighted loader to ensure the model sees a mix of ground-truth and pseudo-labeled data in every batch.

### Evaluation
**Inference: Student Bagging**
*   Generate final predictions using the **Student Ensemble** (trained in Stage 3).
*   Average the outputs of all Student models (ResNet + DenseNet + EfficientNet across all folds).
*   This approach leverages the expanded effective dataset size to refine decision boundaries, particularly for rare classes where the Teacher might have found confident patterns in the test set.
