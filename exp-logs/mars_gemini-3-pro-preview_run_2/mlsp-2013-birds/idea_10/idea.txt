Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Tri-Architecture Heterogeneous Ensemble with Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling and Rectangular Resolution**.

This strategy represents a structural synthesis that reintegrates promising components (EfficientNet, GeM Pooling) that previously failed due to isolated configuration errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020), embedding them within the robust "Rectangular Resolution" framework (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) that has proven most effective for this micro-dataset.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble with GeM Heads**
The solution will utilize three distinct CNN architectures trained in parallel to maximize inductive bias diversity. This "Grand Ensemble" approach stabilizes predictions against the high variance of the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
1.  **ResNet18 (Exploitation):** Retained for its proven stability and robustness on this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
2.  **DenseNet121 (Exploitation):** Retained for its feature reuse capabilities, which are critical in low-data regimes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
3.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Structural Innovation/Correction):** We re-introduce EfficientNet to leverage its inverted residual blocks. Its previous failure was explicitly linked to the lack of class weighting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020), which we will correct here.

**Head Innovation: Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**
Instead of the standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) or the noise-sensitive Dual Pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024), all three backbones will terminate in a **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** layer.
*   *Rationale:* Bird calls are sparse events. GAP dilutes the signal with background noise, while Max Pooling amplifies high-intensity noise artifacts. GeM Pooling introduces a learnable parameter $p$ that interpolates between the two, allowing the model to automatically tune its focus on signal-rich regions while suppressing noise. This component failed in "Idea 3" solely due to the unweighted loss function (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020), warranting its re-evaluation in this corrected pipeline.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Input Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Rectangular Resolution (Exploitation):** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**.
    *   *Rationale:* We strictly adhere to the 1:2 aspect ratio. This preserves the temporal fidelity of short bird calls better than square resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) while avoiding the overfitting risks of excessive resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
*   **Pseudo-RGB:** Replicate the single-channel grayscale spectrogram 3 times. This adapts the data to the pretrained weights better than engineering Delta channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Mixup:** Apply **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)**. This provides necessary regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) without creating the ambiguous samples that degraded performance at $\alpha=1.0$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Photometric:** Apply **RandomBrightnessContrast** to handle recording gain variations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Geometric:** Apply **SpecAugment** (Time/Frequency Masking).

### Training
**Procedure: Corrected Optimization Regime**
*   **Splitting:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** to mitigate stochastic variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**.
    *   *Rationale:* Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable. This is the critical correction that enables the use of GeM Pooling and EfficientNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Optimization Strategy:**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with conservative weight decay (`1e-4`) to prevent underfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Scheduler:** Use a **Constant Learning Rate** (or very slow decay).
        *   *Rationale:* Given the micro-dataset size, aggressive schedules like Cosine Annealing decay the learning rate too quickly. A sustained learning rate is required to escape local minima in the noisy loss landscape (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Duration:** Define training by **Total Update Steps** (target ~1000 steps), ignoring standard epoch counts to ensure convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

### Evaluation
**Inference: Grand Ensemble**
*   **Process:** For each test recording, resize to 224x448 and generate predictions using all trained models (5 ResNet + 5 DenseNet + 5 EfficientNet = 15 models).
*   **Aggregation:** Average the probability outputs of all 15 models.
    *   *Rationale:* This combines the stability of bagging (averaging across folds) with the diversity of heterogeneous architectures (averaging across inductive biases), providing the ultimate defense against the noise and variance inherent in training on ~200 samples (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
