To fix this, I will:
1.  Manually shuffle the dataframe `df_dev` before splitting to ensure randomized folds, preserving the intent of the original `shuffle=True` argument.
2.  Remove the `shuffle` and `random_state` arguments from the `IterativeStratification` constructor call.
