Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Tri-Architecture Multi-Resolution Ensemble with Multi-Sample Dropout**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Corrected Tri-Architecture Ensemble" (Idea 11) by integrating the **Multi-Resolution** concept (from Idea 12) and **Multi-Sample Dropout** (from Idea 6). This approach maximizes architectural diversity while introducing two layers of regularization—input resolution variance and internal ensembling—to further stabilize training on this micro-dataset without the saturation or runtime penalties observed in previous attempts.

### Model
**Architecture: Heterogeneous Multi-Resolution Ensemble with Internal Bagging**
The solution will utilize three distinct CNN backbones trained in parallel, leveraging different input resolutions and head structures to maximize feature decorrelation:

1.  **Backbone A (Residual Bias): ResNet18** (Pretrained on ImageNet).
    *   **Input Resolution:** **224 (Freq) x 448 (Time)**.
    *   *Role:* Retained for its proven robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) and ability to capture fine-grained temporal transients using the rectangular aspect ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
2.  **Backbone B (Inverted Residual Bias): EfficientNet-B0** (Pretrained on ImageNet).
    *   **Input Resolution:** **224 (Freq) x 448 (Time)**.
    *   *Role:* Retained to contribute the MBConv inductive bias, which adds diversity to the ensemble (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052).
3.  **Backbone C (Dense Bias): DenseNet121** (Pretrained on ImageNet).
    *   **Input Resolution (Structural Innovation):** **160 (Freq) x 320 (Time)**.
    *   *Role:* By feeding the deepest model (DenseNet) a lower-resolution input, we force it to learn coarser, macro-scale signal patterns. This acts as an architectural regularizer, preventing the high-capacity model from overfitting to high-frequency noise in the micro-dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035, solution_lesson_node_00052).

**Head Innovation: Multi-Sample Dropout**
Instead of a standard single Dropout layer, all three backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head.
*   *Mechanism:* The Global Average Pooling output is passed through 5 parallel dropout layers (with different random masks), each followed by a shared Fully Connected layer. The outputs are averaged.
*   *Rationale:* This creates an "ensemble within a model," significantly accelerating convergence and reducing variance on small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028). Note: This technique was part of Idea 6 but was obscured by the failure of "Delta" inputs; we re-integrate it here with the proven "Pseudo-RGB" inputs.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Splitting**
*   **Input Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Adaptation:**
    *   **Pseudo-RGB:** Replicate the single-channel grayscale spectrogram 3 times (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
    *   **Resizing:** Resize inputs to 224x448 for ResNet/EfficientNet and 160x320 for DenseNet.
*   **Splitting:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Mixup:** Apply **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)**. This is critical for regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) but must not be aggressive (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Photometric:** Apply **RandomBrightnessContrast** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Geometric:** Apply **SpecAugment**.
*   **Loader:** Implement a `worker_init_fn` to explicitly seed worker processes, ensuring true stochastic diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).

### Training
**Procedure: Aggressive Adaptation with Weighted Loss**
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**. Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **Learning Rate `1e-3`** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040) and **Weight Decay `1e-4`** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Schedule:** Use a **Constant Learning Rate**. This is required to escape local minima in the noisy loss landscape of small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
*   **Duration:** Target **1000 Total Update Steps** per fold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

### Evaluation
**Inference: Multi-Resolution Grand Ensemble**
*   **Process:** For each test recording:
    1.  Resize to 224x448 and predict using the 5 ResNet18 and 5 EfficientNet-B0 models.
    2.  Resize to 160x320 and predict using the 5 DenseNet121 models.
*   **Aggregation:** Average the probability outputs of all 15 models. This combines the stability of bagging (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018), the diversity of heterogeneous architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052), and the regularization of multi-resolution inputs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
