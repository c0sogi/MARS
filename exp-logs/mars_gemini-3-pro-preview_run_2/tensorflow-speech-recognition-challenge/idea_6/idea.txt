Based on the analysis of the previous attempts and the specific empirical lessons learned, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves from the EfficientNet-based approach to a **ConvNeXt** architecture, explicitly correcting the implementation errors (signal processing and initialization) that caused previous failures, while rejecting the Transformer-based approach due to its inefficiency with native audio resolutions.

### 1. Model: ConvNeXt-Tiny with Native Resolution Support
We will transition from the EfficientNet-B0 backbone to a **ConvNeXt-Tiny** architecture. This represents a structural innovation by adopting a "modernized CNN" design that incorporates Transformer-like inductive biases (specifically large **7x7 kernels**) while retaining the translation invariance and efficiency of standard convolutions **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.

*   **Large Kernel Advantage**: Unlike EfficientNet (which relies on 3x3/5x5 kernels), ConvNeXt's 7x7 kernels provide a wider effective receptive field. This is critical for capturing phonetic context and temporal dependencies in speech without needing the recurrent layers that were discarded in earlier iterations.
*   **Corrected Input Adaptation**: We will modify the first projection layer to accept 1-channel spectrograms. Crucially, we will initialize the weights of this layer by **summing** the original RGB weights across the channel dimension (not averaging them). This preserves the activation magnitude expected by the pretrained network, preventing the signal collapse observed in previous attempts **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00019)**.
*   **Native Resolution Processing**: Unlike the Swin Transformer attempt which required wasteful resizing to 224x224, ConvNeXt is fully convolutional. We will feed the spectrograms at their **native resolution** (approx. 128 $\times$ 100), drastically reducing computational overhead and avoiding interpolation artifacts **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Attention Pooling Head**: We will replace the standard Global Average Pooling with a learnable **Attention Pooling** layer. This allows the model to dynamically weight the relevant time frames (speech) and suppress the background noise, ensuring the global feature vector is not diluted by silence **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00011)**.

### 2. Data: High-Fidelity Spectrograms & Balanced Pipeline
The data processing will be strictly aligned with the signal dynamics of short speech commands.

*   **Spectro-Temporal Alignment**: We will generate Log-Mel Spectrograms with a window size of **25ms** (`n_fft=400` at 16kHz) and a hop length of **10ms**. This precise configuration resolves the rapid temporal transitions of phonemes (preventing the "smearing" caused by the 64ms window in Idea 3) while maintaining adequate frequency resolution **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
*   **Instance Normalization**: We will apply Instance Normalization to each spectrogram sample to standardize contrast and volume, improving robustness to recording variations **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00011)**.
*   **Weighted Random Sampling**: We will utilize a `WeightedRandomSampler` to ensure every training batch contains a balanced distribution of the 12 classes, neutralizing the severe 17:1 imbalance caused by the "unknown" class **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Calibrated SpecAugment**: We will apply Time and Frequency masking. The mask parameters will be calculated to be a conservative fraction (<20%) of the native input dimensions (approx 100 time steps) to act as a regularizer without destroying the semantic signal **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.

### 3. Training: Stable Convergence Strategy
*   **Loss Function**: We will use Cross-Entropy Loss with **Label Smoothing (0.1)**. This prevents the high-capacity ConvNeXt model from becoming overconfident and encourages the learning of robust, clustered feature representations **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00013)**.
*   **Optimizer**: We will use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler.
*   **Regularization**: We will **exclude Mixup**. Given the effectiveness of SpecAugment and the structural capacity of ConvNeXt, Mixup would unnecessarily slow down convergence within the limited training budget **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)**.

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference**: Test audio clips will be processed using the same high-resolution parameters (25ms window, 10ms hop) and Instance Normalization.
*   **Prediction**: The model will output class probabilities via Softmax. The label corresponding to the highest probability will be selected. The `unknown` label is predicted naturally if its probability is highest.
