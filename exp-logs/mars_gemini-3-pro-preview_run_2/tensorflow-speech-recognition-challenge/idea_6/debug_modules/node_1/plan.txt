The `ZeroDivisionError` occurs in `library/train.py` because `main_epochs` (which is passed as `T_max` to `CosineAnnealingLR`) evaluates to 0 when the total number of training epochs (`epochs`) equals the number of warmup epochs (`config.WARMUP_EPOCHS`). In the demo run, both are set to 2, resulting in `T_max = 2 - 2 = 0`. The `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler performs a division by `T_max`, causing the crash.

To fix this, I will modify `library/train.py` to ensure `main_epochs` is at least 1 using `max(1, epochs - warmup_epochs)`. This prevents `T_max` from being 0 while preserving the intended scheduler behavior for longer training runs.
