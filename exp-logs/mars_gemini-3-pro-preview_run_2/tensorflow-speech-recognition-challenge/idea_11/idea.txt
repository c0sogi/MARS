Based on the analysis of previous attempts and the synthesized lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy.

### 1. Model: EfficientNet-B0 with RGB-Dynamic Feature Stacking
I propose a structural evolution in **Feature Representation** and **Input Adaptation**. Previous successful iterations adapted the model to the data (collapsing RGB weights to 1-channel). This strategy adapts the data to the model, leveraging the pretrained 3-channel architecture to capture signal dynamics explicitly.

*   **Backbone (EfficientNet-B0)**: We will utilize the **EfficientNet-B0** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
    *   **Structural Innovation (3-Channel Input)**: Instead of summing the pretrained weights to accept a single-channel spectrogram **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00019)**, we will retain the original **3-channel input layer**. We will feed the model a composite image consisting of the **Static Spectrogram**, **Delta (Velocity)**, and **Delta-Delta (Acceleration)** coefficients. This maps the spectral dynamics of speech (critical for distinguishing short commands) directly to the R-G-B channels the network is optimized to process.
*   **Head (Single-Head Attention Pooling)**: We will employ a **Single-Head Attention Pooling** layer. This mechanism acts as a learned "Voice Activity Detector," allowing the model to localize the command within the 1-second clip and suppress background noise, which is superior to Multi-Head variants for single-event detection **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00035)**.

### 2. Data: Dynamic Features & Robust Normalization
The data pipeline is designed to maximize signal fidelity and robustness, correcting the specific normalization error that caused the failure of Idea 10.

*   **Waveform Augmentation (Noise Injection)**: We will re-introduce **Background Noise Mixing**. We will dynamically mix random segments from the `_background_noise_` folder into the training clips with a randomized Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR). This forces the Attention mechanism to actively learn to suppress non-stationary noise.
*   **Feature Extraction (Spectral Oversampling)**: We will generate Log-Mel Spectrograms using **Spectral Oversampling**: a window size of **25ms** (400 samples) and an FFT size (`n_fft`) of **1024**. This interpolates the frequency spectrum, reducing quantization noise **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00034)**. We will maintain a **10ms** hop length to preserve temporal resolution **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
*   **Dynamic Channel Generation**: We will compute the first and second derivatives (Delta and Delta-Delta) of the Log-Mel spectrogram along the time dimension and stack them to form a `(3, n_mels, time)` tensor.
*   **Instance Normalization**: We will apply **Instance Normalization** to the 3-channel tensor (standardizing each channel of every sample to zero mean and unit variance). This is the critical correction to Idea 10, ensuring the input distribution matches the pretrained backbone's expectations **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00038)**.
*   **Calibrated SpecAugment**: We will apply Time and Frequency masking to the 3-channel image. Mask parameters will be calibrated to <20% of dimensions **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.
*   **Sampling**: We will strictly employ a `WeightedRandomSampler` to balance the 12 classes **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.

### 3. Training: Optimization for Generalization
*   **Loss Function**: We will use Cross-Entropy Loss with **Label Smoothing (0.1)** to prevent overconfidence and encourage robust clustering **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00013)**.
*   **Batch Size**: We will use a small batch size of **32**. This ensures a high frequency of gradient updates, critical for convergence on this dataset size **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00031)**.
*   **Optimizer**: We will use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler.
*   **Regularization**: We will **exclude Mixup** to prioritize faster convergence, relying on Noise Injection and SpecAugment **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)**.

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference**: Test audio clips will be processed through the full pipeline: Noise Injection is disabled, but **Spectral Oversampling**, **Delta Generation**, and **Instance Normalization** are applied.
*   **Prediction**: The model will output class probabilities via Softmax. The label corresponding to the highest probability will be selected.
