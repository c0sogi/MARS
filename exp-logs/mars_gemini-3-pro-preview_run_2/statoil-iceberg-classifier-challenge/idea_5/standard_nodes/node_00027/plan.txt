My optimization strategy focuses on three key areas: Feature Engineering, Regularization, and Inference Robustness.

1.  **Feature Engineering (Polarization Difference):** I will modify the input data representation by replacing the third channel (previously the arithmetic mean of Band 1 and Band 2) with the **difference** (`Band 1 - Band 2`). In SAR imagery, the difference between HH and HV bands corresponds to the polarization ratio, which is a powerful physical discriminator between volume scatterers (icebergs) and surface scatterers (ships). By scaling this channel independently, we maximize the contrast of this specific feature (Cite Lesson 00025).
2.  **Regularization (Increased Dropout):** To address the observed overfitting (Train Loss 0.15 vs. Val Loss 0.19) and the "velocity" of overfitting, I will increase the Dropout rate in the model's fusion head from 0.2 to **0.5**. This aggressive regularization helps flatten the loss curvature and improves generalization (Cite Lesson 00002).
3.  **Inference (Test-Time Augmentation):** I will implement Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) in the prediction loop. By averaging predictions from the original image and its horizontally flipped version, we can reduce variance and improve the robustness of the final probabilities without additional training cost.

I will also update the cache filename to ensure the new feature engineering is applied and adjust the training patience to accommodate the slower convergence expected with higher dropout.
