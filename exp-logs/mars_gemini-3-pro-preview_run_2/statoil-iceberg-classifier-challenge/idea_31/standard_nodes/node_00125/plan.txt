My strategy focuses on optimizing the "Robust Dual-Path Wide-Body Network" (RDP-WBN) by addressing two key lessons regarding architectural bottlenecks and regularization in small-data regimes.

1.  **Relaxing the Readout Bottleneck (Cite Lesson 00055):** The current model compresses 256 feature channels down to 48 in the spatial readout path before flattening. This aggressive compression (5.3x) risks discarding discriminative spatial features. I will increase `READOUT_PATH_A_FILTERS` from 48 to 64 to preserve more spatial fidelity entering the fusion head.

Lesson 00124 indicates that high dropout (>0.5) combined with other regularizers can suppress learning on small datasets (~1600 samples). I will reduce `DROPOUT_RATE` to 0.3 to allow the model to capture weaker but informative signals.
3.  **Refining Training Duration:** I will increase the training epochs from 50 to 60 in `runfile.py` to accommodate the potentially different convergence dynamics of the lower dropout rate, while relying on the existing Early Stopping mechanism (Patience 15) to prevent overfitting.
4.  **Updating Evaluation Threshold:** I will update the submission threshold in `runfile.py` to the current best validation score (0.15744) to ensure we only generate a submission if performance improves.
