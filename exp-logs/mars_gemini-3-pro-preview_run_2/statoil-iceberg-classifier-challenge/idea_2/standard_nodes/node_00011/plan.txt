The `TypeError` is caused by the `verbose` parameter being passed to `torch.optim.lr_scheduler.ReduceLROnPlateau`, which has been deprecated and removed in the installed version of PyTorch. To fix this, I will remove the `verbose=True` argument from the scheduler initialization in both `runfile.py` and `library/train.py`. Furthermore, to restore the functionality of logging learning rate changes (Cite debug_lesson_1), I will update `library/model.py` to manually check for and print changes to the learning rate after the scheduler step.
