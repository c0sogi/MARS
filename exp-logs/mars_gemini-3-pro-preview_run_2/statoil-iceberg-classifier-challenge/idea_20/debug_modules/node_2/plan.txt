The training process crashed with a `ValueError` because the final batch of the epoch contained a single sample, which prevents Batch Normalization layers from calculating statistics (variance). This occurred because the dataset size was not perfectly divisible by the batch size. Following the guidance in **debug_lesson_3**, I will modify the training `DataLoader` in `library/data_loader.py` to include `drop_last=True`. This ensures that any incomplete final batch is discarded, guaranteeing that the model always receives batches with more than one sample during training.
