1.  **Remove the "Detail" Readout Stream (1x1 Conv):** As identified in **Lesson solution_lesson_node_00179**, using 1x1 convolutions in the final readout of SAR classification models tends to overfit to speckle noise (pixel-level variance) rather than capturing robust semantic features. I will revert to a **Dual-Path Readout** (Spatial Context + Global Invariance) as recommended in **Lesson solution_lesson_node_00123**.
2.  **Increase Batch Size:** I will increase the `BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to 64. **Lesson solution_lesson_node_00179** and **Lesson solution_lesson_node_00157** highlight that larger batch sizes improve the stability of Batch Normalization layers, which is critical for wide architectures on small datasets.
3.  **Adjust Training Epochs:** To accommodate the larger batch size (which results in fewer updates per epoch) and the simplified, more robust architecture, I will increase the number of training epochs in `runfile.py` from 25 to 50.
