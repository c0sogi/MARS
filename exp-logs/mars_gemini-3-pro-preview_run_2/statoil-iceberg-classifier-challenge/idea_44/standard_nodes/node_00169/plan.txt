The previous implementation suffered from data leakage because the training pipeline loaded the entire dataset from `train.json` and performed cross-validation on it, ignoring the designated training split defined in `metadata/train.csv`. This meant the hold-out validation set (`metadata/val.csv`) was inadvertently included in the training folds. To fix this, I will modify `library/data_loader.py` to filter the loaded data against `metadata/train.csv` before creating folds (Cite debug_lesson_10). Additionally, I will add weight decay regularization and increase patience in `library/config.py` and `library/train.py` to improve generalization on the clean dataset (Cite debug_lesson_11), and adjust the submission threshold in `runfile.py` to ensure a submission is generated.
