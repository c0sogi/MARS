Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Contracted Spatial Pyramid Hybrid Network (CSP-HN)**.

This solution represents a structural evolution of the "Attention-Augmented Shallow Hybrid" architecture. It addresses the specific tension between the need for **spatial retention** (Lesson 21) and the risk of **over-parameterization** in the dense head (Lesson 41) by introducing a **Channel-Contracting Bottleneck** followed by a **Spatial Pyramid Pooling (SPP)** module.

# Model

The architecture maintains the successful **Split-Branch Topology** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) but redesigns the interface between the visual backbone and the classification head.

1.  **Visual Branch (Backbone & Bottleneck)**:
    *   **Backbone**: Retain the **Custom 4-Layer Shallow CNN** identified as superior to deep ResNets for 75x75 SAR imagery (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016, solution_lesson_node_00024).
    *   **Attention**: Integrate **Convolutional Block Attention Modules (CBAM)** after each convolutional block to dynamically weight signal channels and spatial peaks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   **Pooling**: Strictly employ **Max Pooling** throughout the backbone to preserve high-intensity backscatter signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Structural Innovation 1: Channel Contraction Bottleneck**: Before the final pooling/flattening stage, insert a **1x1 Convolutional Layer** to explicitly reduce the channel depth (e.g., from 128 to 32). This creates a compact feature bottleneck, significantly reducing the parameter count of the subsequent dense layers to prevent overfitting, addressing the specific failure mode of previous wider architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
    *   **Structural Innovation 2: Spatial Pyramid Pooling (SPP)**: Instead of a simple Flatten or Global Average Pool, apply **Max Pooling** at three distinct spatial scales on the contracted feature map:
        1.  **4x4 (Identity)**: Retains the coarse spatial grid to resolve location-specific features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
        2.  **2x2**: Captures mid-level regional intensity.
        3.  **1x1 (Global)**: Captures the single strongest signal peak in the image.
    *   **Output**: Flatten and concatenate these three vectors. This multi-granular representation ensures the model is robust to iceberg size variations while maintaining the "Peak-Preserving" property of Max Pooling.

2.  **Metadata Branch**:
    *   A dedicated Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) to process the scalar `inc_angle` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

3.  **Fusion Head**:
    *   Concatenate the SPP visual vector and the metadata vector.
    *   Process through a classification head with Batch Normalization, ReLU, and **Moderate Dropout (0.2)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

# Data

1.  **Input Construction**: Construct 3-channel images using `Band_1`, `Band_2`, and the arithmetic mean `(Band_1 + Band_2) / 2`.
2.  **Normalization**: Apply **Independent Per-Channel Min-Max Scaling**. Calculate min/max statistics for HH, HV, and Avg channels separately on the training set and scale each to [0, 1] to maximize contrast (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
3.  **Augmentation**:
    *   **Rotational Invariance**: Apply random rotations of **0°, 90°, 180°, or 270°** combined with random **Horizontal Flips** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
    *   **Constraints**: Exclude Vertical Flips to avoid mathematical redundancy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
    *   **Implementation**: Use **native tensor operations** for efficiency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
    *   **Exclusion**: Do not use Mixup, as it induces underfitting in this small-data regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

# Training

1.  **Strategy**: Implement **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation**. Train 5 independent instances of the CSP-HN. This is critical to reduce variance and avoid selection bias (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029, solution_lesson_node_00032).
2.  **Optimization**:
    *   **"Low and Slow"**: Initialize Adam with a conservative learning rate (e.g., **2e-4**) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Scheduler**: Use `ReduceLROnPlateau` to decay the learning rate when validation loss stagnates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
3.  **State Management**: Use `copy.deepcopy(model.state_dict())` in the Early Stopping callback to strictly preserve the best weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
4.  **Regularization**: Only apply Weight Decay if Validation Loss > Training Loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test images into the 3-channel, per-channel scaled format.
2.  **Ensemble Prediction**: Pass the test data through all 5 trained CSP-HN models.
3.  **Aggregation**: Compute the **mean** of the predicted probabilities to generate the final submission. This ensemble approach is the valid metric for performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
