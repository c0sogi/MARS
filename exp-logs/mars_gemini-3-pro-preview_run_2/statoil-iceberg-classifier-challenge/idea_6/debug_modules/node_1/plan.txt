The `ValueError` is caused by the training `DataLoader` producing a final batch of size 1 when the dataset size is not divisible by the batch size. The Batch Normalization layers in the `A2SHN` model require more than one sample to compute statistics during training. To fix this, I will update the `DataLoader` initialization in `library/utils.py` to include `drop_last=True`, which ensures that incomplete batches are discarded during training.
