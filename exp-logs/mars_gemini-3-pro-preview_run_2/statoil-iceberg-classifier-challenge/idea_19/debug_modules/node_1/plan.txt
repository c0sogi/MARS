The runtime error `ValueError: Expected more than 1 value per channel when training` occurs because the dataset size is not perfectly divisible by the batch size, leaving a single sample in the final batch. Batch Normalization layers require at least two samples to compute statistics during training. To resolve this, I will modify the training `DataLoader` in `library/data_loader.py` to include `drop_last=True`, which discards the incomplete final batch. Cite debug_lesson_3.
