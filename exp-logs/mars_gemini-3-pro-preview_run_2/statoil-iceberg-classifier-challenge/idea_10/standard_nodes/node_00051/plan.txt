I will optimize the solution by implementing a "Wide-Contracting" architecture and adjusting training hyperparameters to favor a "Low and Slow" convergence strategy.

1.  **Architecture Update (Wide-Contracting Backbone)**:
    *   I will replace the "Multi-Level Constrained-Width" architecture with a wider backbone (`[64, 128, 128, 64]`) as suggested by **Lesson 00049**, which highlights that backbone width is more critical than complex feature aggregation for small datasets.
    *   I will enforce "Channel Contraction" in the final convolutional stage (reducing filters from 128 to 64) before flattening. This reduces the dimensionality of the dense head input, preventing overfitting caused by parameter explosion (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00041**).
    *   I will remove the multi-level fusion logic, which was found to be ineffective/redundant (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**).

2.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**:
    *   **Patience**: Increase from 15 to 20 epochs to allow the model to navigate noisy gradients without premature stopping (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
    *   **Dropout**: Increase from 0.2 to 0.4 to provide stronger regularization given the increased backbone capacity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).
    *   **Epochs**: Increase the training limit in `runfile.py` to 100 to accommodate the extended patience and slower convergence.
