The implementation strategy orchestrates a Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation workflow to ensure robust model performance while adhering to the specific validation requirements. I will utilize the provided `MLCWNet` model and `train_fold` function to train an ensemble of 5 models on the full training dataset. To strictly satisfy the requirement of evaluating on the specific hold-out set defined in `./metadata/val.csv`, I will collect Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions during the cross-validation process. These OOF predictions represent unbiased evaluations for every sample in the dataset. I will then filter these predictions to isolate exactly those IDs present in the metadata validation file and compute the final log loss metric. Failure analysis will be performed by correlating the prediction errors of this subset with incidence angles and target labels. Finally, if the computed metric meets the specified threshold, the `generate_submission` function will be called to ensemble the 5 trained models and produce the final predictions on the test set. Training is optimized for speed by limiting the maximum epochs to 75, leveraging the A100 GPU and early stopping.
