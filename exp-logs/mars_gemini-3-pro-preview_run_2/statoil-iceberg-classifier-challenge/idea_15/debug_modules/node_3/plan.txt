The error `ValueError: Expected more than 1 value per channel when training` is caused by the final batch of the training epoch containing a single sample (due to the dataset size not being divisible by the batch size). Batch Normalization layers require at least two samples to calculate variance during training (Cite debug_lesson_3). To fix this, I will update the `train_loader` in `library/data_loader.py` to set `drop_last=True`, ensuring that any incomplete final batch is discarded.
