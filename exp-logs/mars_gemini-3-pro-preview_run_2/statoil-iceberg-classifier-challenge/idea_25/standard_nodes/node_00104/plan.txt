My optimization strategy focuses on refining the model architecture to align with the successful "DPCNet" principles identified in the lessons, specifically regarding channel progression and spatial preservation.

1.  **Channel Contraction (Cite Lesson 41, 49):** I will modify the network to use a filter progression of `[64, 128, 128, 64]` instead of a constant `128`. This maintains capacity in the middle layers while explicitly contracting the channel dimension in the final convolutional block. This structural bottleneck reduces the risk of overfitting in the dense layers without sacrificing feature extraction power.
2.  **Spatial Preservation (Cite Lesson 21):** I will remove the `quadrant_pool` (AdaptiveMaxPool 2x2) layer. Instead, I will flatten the final 4x4 feature map directly. Aggressive pooling at the end of the network destroys valuable spatial geometry in small 75x75 images; retaining the 4x4 grid allows the dense layers to utilize coarse location information.
