Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, specifically the superior performance of **Gated Activation Units** with **Explicit Supervision** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00099), the failure of **Hard Binary Boundaries** due to ambiguity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077), and the failure of **Symmetric/Multi-Scale Schedules** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00101, solution_lesson_node_00102), I propose the **Geometric Soft-Gated Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (GSG-CRCN)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the current state-of-the-art RSG-CRCN by introducing **Geometric Feature Expansion (Bone Vectors)** to explicitly model pose shape invariant of position, and **Gaussian Soft-Boundary Supervision** to resolve the conflict between the need for boundary supervision and the inherent ambiguity of transition frames.

# Model
**Architecture: Geometric Soft-Gated Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (GSG-CRCN)**
The model consists of three sequentially connected stages trained end-to-end with Deep Supervision.

1.  **Stage 1: Geometric Recurrent Encoder**
    *   **Feature Expansion (Structural Innovation):** In addition to Joint Positions ($P$) and Temporal Velocities ($V$), we explicitly compute **Bone Vectors** ($B$). For every connected pair of joints (e.g., Elbow-Wrist), we calculate the 3D vector difference.
        *   *Motivation:* Raw positions are dependent on the camera frame. Bone vectors provide a translation-invariant representation of body shape and orientation, enhancing the signal-to-noise ratio of the input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Bi-Directional LSTM** (2 layers, 256 hidden units). We retain this backbone for its superior inductive bias for continuous temporal dynamics compared to Transformers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Input:** Concatenated vector $[P, V, B, Audio_{MFCC}]$.
    *   **Heads:** The LSTM output is projected into two distinct streams:
        *   **Class Probabilities ($P_{cls}$):** 21 channels (Softmax).
        *   **Boundary Probability ($P_{bnd}$):** 1 channel (Sigmoid).
    *   **Output:** Concatenated tensor $[P_{cls}, P_{bnd}]$.

2.  **Inter-Stage Masking:**
    *   We explicitly multiply the concatenated output tensor by the binary sequence mask to zero out padding, preventing noise propagation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

3.  **Stage 2: Soft-Gated Refinement (Gated MS-TCN)**
    *   **Block Architecture:** We employ **Gated Activation Blocks** ($\tanh(W_f x) \odot \sigma(W_g x)$). The gating mechanism ($\sigma$) dynamically regulates information flow.
        *   *Innovation:* By supervising this with **Soft Targets** (see Training), we allow the gates to open gradually during transitions, matching the continuous nature of the sigmoid activation, rather than forcing a binary hard-switch which caused instability in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077).
    *   **Configuration:** 10 dilated layers with **Monotonically Increasing Dilation** ($1, 2, \dots, 512$). We strictly avoid symmetric or multi-scale schedules to ensure stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00101, solution_lesson_node_00102). We maintain **256 channels** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078) and explicitly **exclude Instance Normalization** to preserve sparse signal magnitude (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Input:** The masked tensor $[P_{cls}, P_{bnd}]$ from Stage 1.
    *   **Output:** Refined $[P'_{cls}, P'_{bnd}]$.

4.  **Stage 3: Soft-Gated Sharpening**
    *   **Backbone:** Identical to Stage 2.
    *   **Output:** Final predictions.

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Normalization (Crucial):** Explicitly **center** the skeleton data relative to the HipCenter and **scale the units from millimeters to meters** (multiply by 0.001). This prevents saturation of the Gated Units (Cite solution_lesson_node_00084).
*   **Feature Selection:** Strictly limit inputs to **12 Upper-Body Joints** and **Audio MFCCs** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Defensive Loading:** Explicitly handle matrix dimension transpositions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00048).

**Augmentation:**
*   **Physically Consistent Geometric Augmentation:** Implement the temporally correlated noise pipeline.
    1.  Generate Gaussian noise and apply a **Temporal Low-Pass Filter**.
    2.  Add this smooth noise to the **Raw 3D Joint Positions**.
    3.  **Derive** both **Temporal Velocity** and **Bone Vectors** from the augmented positions.
    *   This ensures all geometric features remain physically consistent, avoiding the jitter caused by independent noise injection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059, solution_lesson_node_00064).

**Evaluation Data:**
*   **Deterministic Split:** Instantiate the validation set with **augmentation disabled** (`is_train=False`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

# Training
**Loss Function:**
*   **Multi-Stage Deep Supervision:** Minimize $L_{total} = \sum_{s=1}^{3} (L_{cls}^{(s)} + L_{bnd}^{(s)} + L_{smooth}^{(s)})$.
*   **Classification Loss:** Weighted Cross-Entropy with a strict ratio of **0.1 (Background) : 1.0 (Gesture)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Soft-Boundary Supervision (Structural Innovation):** Instead of binary targets, we generate **Gaussian Soft-Targets** for the boundary head (placing a Gaussian kernel at each transition frame). We minimize the **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** between the predicted boundary probability and this soft target.
    *   *Motivation:* This provides the **Explicit Supervision** required to stabilize the Gated TCN (Cite solution_lesson_node_00082) while acknowledging the **Ambiguity** of exact transition frames (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077), preventing the model from overfitting to annotation noise.
*   **Unclamped Probability-Space Smoothing:** Apply **Truncated Mean Squared Error (T-MSE)** to the **Softmax probabilities**. We do **not clamp** the loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00087) and do not use Log-Softmax (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Strict Masking:** Apply sequence masks to all loss terms (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

**Optimization:**
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with learning rate `1e-3` and weight decay `1e-4` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00067).
*   **Strategy:** Prioritize output smoothness (T-MSE weight) over aggressive parameter regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
1.  **Forward Pass:** Generate predictions using the Class Probability output of **Stage 3**.
2.  **Label-Space Smoothing:** Apply a **Median Filter** to the discrete class labels (post-argmax). This merges fragmented segments more effectively than probability filtering (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).
3.  **Boundary Protection:** Use **Nearest-Neighbor Padding** during filtering to prevent the deletion of valid gestures at sequence edges (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
4.  **Decoding:** Collapse consecutive repeated labels and remove the background class.
