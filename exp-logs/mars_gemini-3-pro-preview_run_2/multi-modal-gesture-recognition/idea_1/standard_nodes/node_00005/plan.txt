To improve the model's performance, I will implement a three-pronged optimization strategy focusing on data robustness, training stability, and output refinement:

1.  **Data Augmentation (Robustness)**: I will inject Gaussian noise into the skeletal and velocity features during training. Since the model relies on "Explicit Dynamics" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004), adding noise to these features helps the model generalize better to sensor variations and prevents overfitting to specific trajectory artifacts.
2.  **Prediction Smoothing (Refinement)**: Frame-wise classification often suffers from "jitter" (e.g., `A-A-B-A-A`), which leads to high insertion error rates in the Levenshtein metric. I will implement a median filter on the raw frame predictions before the "collapse repeats" step. This temporal smoothing enforces a prior that gestures have a minimum duration.
3.  **Learning Rate Scheduling (Stability)**: I will integrate a `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler in the training loop. This allows the model to fine-tune its weights with a smaller learning rate once the validation loss plateaus, potentially escaping local minima and achieving a lower error rate.

I will also increase the number of epochs to allow the scheduler sufficient time to operate.
