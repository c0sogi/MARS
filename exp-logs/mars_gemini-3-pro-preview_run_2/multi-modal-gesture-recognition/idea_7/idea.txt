Based on the extensive analysis of previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Iterative Deeply-Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (IDC-RCN)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the successful "Cascaded" approach (Idea 5) by introducing **iterative refinement** (attempted in Idea 6 but failed due to implementation errors). It rigorously corrects the flaws of Idea 6 by restoring model capacity, fixing the smoothing loss formulation, and enforcing strict masking.

# Model
**Architecture: Iterative Deeply-Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (IDC-RCN)**
The model consists of three sequential stages trained end-to-end, designed to progressively refine predictions from "feature extraction" to "coarse smoothing" to "boundary sharpening".

1.  **Stage 1: Recurrent Encoder (Bi-LSTM)**
    *   **Role:** Feature extraction and initial prediction. We retain the **Bi-LSTM** backbone with **256 hidden units** (preserving capacity) because it possesses the inductive bias for continuous dynamics that Transformers lacked (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Input:** Frame-wise feature vector (Upper-Body Skeleton + Velocity + Audio).
    *   **Output:** Initial frame-wise Softmax probabilities ($P_0$).

2.  **Stage 2: Coarse Refinement (Deep TCN)**
    *   **Role:** Correction of local errors (jitter). This stage takes $P_0$ as input.
    *   **Structure:** A Single-Stage Temporal Convolutional Network (SS-TCN) with **10 dilated residual layers** ($dilation=1, 2, \dots, 512$). We explicitly maintain this depth to ensure the **receptive field** covers the entire sequence, preventing the "local overfitting" observed when depth was reduced (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Masking:** We apply **strict sequence masking** to the input to zero out padding, preventing noise propagation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   **Output:** Refined probabilities ($P_1$).

3.  **Stage 3: Fine Refinement (Deep TCN)**
    *   **Role:** Boundary sharpening. This stage takes $P_1$ as input and applies another pass of dilated convolutions.
    *   **Structure:** Identical to Stage 2 (10 layers).
    *   **Output:** Final probabilities ($P_2$).

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Feature Selection:** We will strictly limit skeletal inputs to the **12 Upper-Body Joints** (Head, Shoulders, Elbows, Wrists, Hands, Spine, HipCenter) and **Audio MFCCs**. We explicitly **discard lower-body joints** to eliminate the noise that caused previous failures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Dynamics & Normalization:** Joint coordinates will be normalized relative to the `HipCenter` and concatenated with their **temporal velocity** (first derivative) to explicitly encode motion dynamics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

**Validation:**
*   **Deterministic Split:** The validation dataset will be instantiated with **augmentation disabled** (`is_train=False`). This ensures that metric improvements are real and not artifacts of random noise, enabling reliable checkpoint selection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

# Training
**Loss Function:**
*   **Multi-Stage Supervision:** We minimize the sum of losses from all three stages: $L_{total} = L_{stage1} + L_{stage2} + L_{stage3}$.
*   **Aggressive Class Weighting:** For the Cross-Entropy component, we use the strict ratio: **0.1 for Background and 1.0 for Gestures**. This is critical to prevent background collapse (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Probability-Space Smoothing (T-MSE):** We apply the **Truncated Mean Squared Error (T-MSE)** loss to the outputs of Stage 2 and Stage 3. Crucially, we compute this on the **Softmax probabilities**, *not* the Log-Softmax, to ensure gradient stability and avoid the optimization issues observed with log-space smoothing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Strict Masking:** All loss terms (Cross-Entropy and T-MSE) will be computed using **sequence-length masks** to exclude padding regions entirely from the gradient calculation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

**Optimization:**
*   **Regularization:** Gaussian noise will be applied to skeletal inputs during training to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Monitoring:** We will monitor **Validation Loss** for early stopping (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
1.  **Forward Pass:** Pass the test sequence through the full IDC-RCN. Use the output of **Stage 3** ($P_2$) as the final prediction stream.
2.  **Temporal Smoothing:** Apply a **Median Filter** to the predictions. Crucially, use **Nearest-Neighbor Padding** (replicating boundary values) instead of zero-padding to prevent the deletion of valid gestures at the sequence edges (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
3.  **Decoding:** Apply greedy decoding (argmax), collapse consecutive repeated labels, and remove the background class.
