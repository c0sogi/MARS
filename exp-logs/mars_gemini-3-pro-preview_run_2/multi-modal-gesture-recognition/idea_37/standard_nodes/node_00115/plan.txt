    *   Load the best saved model weights.

    *   Decode predictions and targets for each sample.
    *   Calculate the error rate per sample and compute its correlation with `sequence_length` and `num_gestures`.
    *   Print these correlations to identify systematic error patterns.

7.  **Submission**:
