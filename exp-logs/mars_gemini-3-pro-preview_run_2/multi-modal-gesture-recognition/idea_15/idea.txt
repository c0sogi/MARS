Based on the extensive empirical analysis and the specific failure modes of previous attempts (particularly the potential of Gated Units in Idea 14 vs. the rigorous stability of Idea 13), I propose the **Supervised Gated-Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (SG-CRCN)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the current best solution (Idea 13) by replacing the standard Residual TCN blocks with **Gated Activation Units**. It explicitly corrects the implementation flaws that caused the previous Gated TCN attempt (Idea 14) to fail—namely, the lack of input normalization and the omission of boundary supervision—by integrating the rigorous data and training pipeline of Idea 13.

# Model
**Architecture: Supervised Gated-Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (SG-CRCN)**
The model consists of three sequentially connected stages trained end-to-end with Deep Supervision.

1.  **Stage 1: Multi-Task Recurrent Encoder**
    *   **Backbone:** A **Bi-Directional LSTM** (2 layers, 256 hidden units). We retain this backbone for its superior inductive bias for continuous temporal dynamics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025) and high capacity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Input:** Frame-wise feature vector (Upper-Body Skeleton + Velocity + Audio), fused via concatenation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).
    *   **Heads:** The LSTM output is projected into two distinct streams:
        *   **Class Probabilities ($P_{cls}$):** 21 channels (Softmax).
        *   **Boundary Probability ($P_{bnd}$):** 1 channel (Sigmoid).
    *   **Output:** Concatenated tensor $[P_{cls}, P_{bnd}]$.

2.  **Inter-Stage Masking:**
    *   We explicitly multiply the concatenated output tensor by the binary sequence mask to zero out padding, preventing noise propagation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

3.  **Stage 2: Supervised Gated Refinement (Gated MS-TCN)**
    *   **Structural Innovation:** We replace the standard Dilated Residual Blocks with **Gated Activation Blocks** to better control the flow of information and the boundary signal.
        *   **Mechanism:** For input $X$, the block computes $Z = \tanh(W_f * X) \odot \sigma(W_g * X)$, where $W_f$ is the filter convolution and $W_g$ is the gate convolution. This gating mechanism allows the network to dynamically "open" or "close" information flow based on the boundary confidence ($P_{bnd}$), offering a more expressive refinement mechanism than simple ReLUs.
    *   **Configuration:** 10 dilated layers ($1, 2, \dots, 512$) with **256 channels** to maintain capacity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078). We **exclude Instance Normalization** to preserve the magnitude of the sparse probability inputs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Input:** The masked tensor $[P_{cls}, P_{bnd}]$ from Stage 1.
    *   **Output:** Refined Class Probabilities ($P'_{cls}$) and Refined Boundary Probability ($P'_{bnd}$).

4.  **Stage 3: Fine Sharpening (Gated MS-TCN)**
    *   **Backbone:** Identical to Stage 2.
    *   **Output:** Final predictions ($P''_{cls}, P''_{bnd}$).

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Normalization (Crucial):** Explicitly **center** the skeleton data relative to the HipCenter and **scale** the units from millimeters to meters (e.g., $\times 0.001$). This prevents the numerical instability and saturation of Gated units observed in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00084).
*   **Feature Selection:** Strictly limit inputs to **12 Upper-Body Joints** and **Audio MFCCs** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Target Generation:** Generate ground-truth boundary labels ($Y_{bnd}$) where $1$ indicates a frame-to-frame label change.

**Augmentation:**
*   **Physically Consistent Smooth Noise:** Implement the temporally correlated noise pipeline.
    1.  Generate Gaussian noise and apply a **Temporal Low-Pass Filter**.
    2.  Add this smooth noise to the **Raw 3D Joint Positions**.
    3.  **Derive** the Temporal Velocity from the augmented positions.
    *   This ensures velocity features remain physically consistent, avoiding the jitter caused by independent noise injection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059, solution_lesson_node_00064). We avoid aggressive geometric rotations to preserve semantic directionality (Cite solution_lesson_node_00067).

**Validation:**
*   **Deterministic Split:** Instantiate validation with **augmentation disabled** (`is_train=False`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

# Training
**Loss Function:**
*   **Multi-Stage Deep Supervision:** Minimize $L_{total} = \sum_{s=1}^{3} (L_{cls}^{(s)} + L_{bnd}^{(s)} + L_{smooth}^{(s)})$.
*   **Classification Loss:** Weighted Cross-Entropy (Ratio 0.1 Background : 1.0 Gesture) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Boundary Loss (Crucial):** Weighted Binary Cross-Entropy on the boundary heads. This provides the **explicit supervision** required to stabilize the Gated TCN, preventing the convergence failure observed in Idea 14 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00082, solution_lesson_node_00073).
*   **Probability-Space Smoothing:** Apply **Truncated Mean Squared Error (T-MSE)** to the **Softmax probabilities** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Strict Masking:** Apply sequence masks to all loss terms (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

**Optimization:**
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with learning rate `1e-3` and weight decay `1e-4` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00067).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
1.  **Forward Pass:** Generate predictions using the Class Probability output of **Stage 3**.
2.  **Label-Space Smoothing:** Apply a **Median Filter** to the discrete class labels (post-argmax) to effectively merge fragmented segments (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).
3.  **Boundary Protection:** Use **Nearest-Neighbor Padding** during filtering (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
4.  **Decoding:** Collapse consecutive repeated labels and remove the background class.
