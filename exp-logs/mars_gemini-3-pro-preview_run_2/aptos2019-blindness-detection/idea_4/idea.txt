Based on the empirical lessons learned, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the previous "Dual-Backbone Ensemble" (Idea 3) into a **Multi-Scale Heterogeneous Ensemble**. This approach explicitly addresses the trade-off between input resolution and model capacity identified in the failure analyses, ensuring the system captures both fine-grained lesions and global retinal structures.

# Model
I propose a **Multi-Scale Heterogeneous Ensemble** that leverages architectural and resolution diversity to maximize feature extraction capability.
*   **Architectural Diversity:** The ensemble will consist of two distinct backbones:
    1.  **EfficientNet-B5:** Selected for its high capacity and proven performance in resolving fine details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    2.  **ConvNeXt-Base:** Selected to introduce a "Transformer-style" inductive bias, which processes texture and global shape differently than standard CNNs, reducing ensemble correlation.
*   **Resolution Diversity (Structural Innovation):** Unlike previous iterations that standardized input size, this strategy assigns specific resolutions to each backbone to target different feature frequencies:
    1.  **EfficientNet-B5 @ 512x512:** This stream is dedicated to **high-frequency feature extraction**. The higher resolution (increased from 456) minimizes the information loss caused by downsampling, addressing the correlation between error and image dimensions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    2.  **ConvNeXt-Base @ 384x384:** This stream focuses on **robust global feature extraction**. The moderate resolution allows for a larger effective batch size and regularization, stabilizing the ensemble against noise.
*   **Pooling (GeM):** Both architectures will utilize **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** ($p=3$) instead of Global Average Pooling. This ensures that even at different scales, the models remain sensitive to sparse, high-activation anomalies like microaneurysms (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Regression Head:** Both streams will terminate in a single linear neuron outputting a continuous scalar, maintaining the regression formulation that aligns with the Quadratic Weighted Kappa metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

# Data
The data pipeline is designed to maximize the "effective" information content of the images while preventing domain shifts.
*   **Preprocessing (Circle Crop):** Apply a **Circle Crop** to all images to remove uninformative black borders. This ensures that the pixel budget (512x512 or 384x384) is spent entirely on retinal tissue (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** Apply aggressive geometric augmentations (Random Horizontal/Vertical Flip, Rotation up to 30°) to prevent the high-capacity models from overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Photometric (Stochastic CLAHE):** Apply Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) with a probability of **$p=0.5$**. This ensures the model learns to handle both enhanced and raw contrast distributions, preventing the train-inference distribution mismatch caused by deterministic augmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

# Training
The training procedure prioritizes stability and coverage of the data distribution.
*   **Cross-Validation:** Implement **5-Fold Cross-Validation** for both architectures. This results in 10 total models (5 EfficientNet + 5 ConvNeXt), ensuring that predictions are robust to data selection noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** to directly optimize the proxy for the Quadratic Weighted Kappa metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Scheduler:** Use a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate Scheduler** without aggressive early stopping. This allows the models to traverse the loss landscape effectively and settle into better global minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Checkpointing:** Save model weights based on the **best Validation Kappa** score rather than the final epoch, safeguarding against stochastic optimization noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

# Evaluation
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** Generate predictions using all 10 trained models. Crucially, average the **continuous regression scores** from all models *before* rounding. This "soft voting" stabilizes the predictions against the quantization noise inherent in ordinal classification (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each model, average the prediction of the original image and its horizontally flipped version.
*   **Post-processing:** Round the final averaged continuous score to the nearest integer to produce the diagnosis labels (0-4).
