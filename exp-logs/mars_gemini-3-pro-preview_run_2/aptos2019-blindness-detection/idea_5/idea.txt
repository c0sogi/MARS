Based on the empirical lessons learned and the progression of previous ideas, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the "Multi-Scale Heterogeneous Ensemble" (Idea 4) by introducing a **Transformer-based backbone** (Swin Transformer V2) to replace the ConvNeXt stream and implementing **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** to address the optimization noise inherent in the dataset.

# Model
I propose a **Hybrid CNN-Transformer Ensemble** that maximizes architectural diversity to capture complementary features of diabetic retinopathy.
*   **Stream 1 (CNN Backbone):** Retain **EfficientNet-B5** initialized with ImageNet weights. This backbone is proven to effectively resolve fine-grained details when coupled with high-resolution inputs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Stream 2 (Transformer Backbone):** Replace the ConvNeXt backbone with **Swin Transformer V2 (Base)**. While ConvNeXt mimics Transformer behavior using convolutions, Swin V2 utilizes **shifted window attention**, a mechanism explicitly designed to model long-range dependencies and adapt to high-resolution inputs. This allows the model to attend to "global" retinal structures and "local" lesions (like microaneurysms) using a fundamentally different inductive bias than the CNN stream, reducing ensemble correlation.
*   **Pooling (GeM):** Both streams will utilize **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** ($p=3$). This ensures that the models remain sensitive to sparse, high-activation anomalies (lesions) rather than washing them out with average pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Regression Head:** Both streams will terminate in a single linear neuron outputting a continuous scalar, maintaining the regression formulation that aligns with the Quadratic Weighted Kappa metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

# Data
The data pipeline preserves the successful resolution scaling and stochastic preprocessing strategies.
*   **Preprocessing (Circle Crop & Multi-Scale):** Apply **Circle Cropping** to remove uninformative black borders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010). To maintain the "Multi-Scale" benefit of Idea 4, we will assign distinct resolutions:
    *   **EfficientNet-B5 @ 512x512:** Maximizes the detection of fine-grained lesions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    *   **Swin Transformer V2 @ 384x384:** Balances computational load while leveraging the Transformer's ability to extract robust features at moderate resolutions.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** Apply aggressive augmentations (Flip, Rotation up to 30°) to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Photometric (Stochastic CLAHE):** Apply CLAHE with a probability of **$p=0.5$**. This ensures the model learns to handle both enhanced and raw contrast distributions, preventing the train-inference mismatch observed when $p=1.0$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

# Training
The training procedure introduces a new optimization phase to stabilize convergence on the noisy ordinal labels.
*   **Cross-Validation:** Implement **5-Fold Cross-Validation** for both architectures (10 models total) to ensure robust coverage (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** to directly optimize the proxy for Quadratic Weighted Kappa (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** Introduce **SWA** in the final 20% of training epochs. Instead of relying solely on a single "best" checkpoint which may be subject to optimization noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006), SWA averages the weights of the model at different points in the trajectory. This finds a flatter, wider minimum in the loss landscape, leading to better generalization on the subjective/noisy grading labels.
*   **Scheduler:** Use a standard **Cosine Annealing Scheduler** for the initial phase (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003), transitioning to a constant learning rate for the SWA phase.

# Evaluation
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** Generate predictions using all 10 trained models (5 EfficientNet + 5 Swin V2). Average the **continuous regression scores** from all models *before* rounding. This "soft voting" stabilizes the predictions against quantization noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Average the predictions of the original image and its horizontal flip for each model.
*   **Post-processing:** Round the final averaged continuous score to the nearest integer to produce the diagnosis labels (0-4).
