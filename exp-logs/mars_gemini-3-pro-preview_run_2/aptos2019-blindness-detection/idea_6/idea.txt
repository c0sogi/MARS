Based on the progression of previous experiments and the critical failure analysis of Idea 5, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution realizes the **Hybrid CNN-Transformer Ensemble** proposed in Idea 5 by introducing a **Precision-Safe Pooling Mechanism** to resolve the catastrophic numerical instability that caused the previous failure. Additionally, it incorporates **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** to better handle the noisy ordinal labels.

# Model
I propose a **Numerically Stable Hybrid Ensemble** that combines the inductive biases of Convolutional Neural Networks and Transformers to capture both local retinal textures and global geometric dependencies.

*   **Stream 1: EfficientNet-B5 (CNN Backbone):**
    *   **Role:** Specialized in extracting high-frequency, local texture features such as microaneurysms and hemorrhages.
    *   **Pooling:** Utilizes **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** ($p=3$) to focus on sparse, high-activation lesions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Stream 2: Swin Transformer V2 Base (Transformer Backbone):**
    *   **Role:** Specialized in modeling long-range dependencies and global shape features using shifted window attention. This complements the CNN by identifying broader pathological patterns (e.g., exudate clusters) that may be missed by local convolutions.
    *   **Structural Innovation (Precision-Safe GeM):** To enable the use of GeM pooling with this architecture without the numerical divergence observed in Idea 5, the GeM layer will be explicitly wrapped in a **Float32 Context**. This ensures that the power operations ($x^p$ and $x^{1/p}$) are calculated in full precision, preventing the overflow and gradient explosion (NaNs) caused by `float16` execution in Mixed Precision training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Head:** Both streams terminate in a single linear neuron outputting a continuous scalar, adhering to the regression formulation that aligns with the Quadratic Weighted Kappa metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

# Data
The data pipeline employs a **Multi-Scale Strategy** to maximize the effective resolution for each architecture while preventing domain shifts.

*   **Preprocessing (Circle Crop & Scale):**
    *   All images undergo **Circle Cropping** to remove uninformative black borders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **EfficientNet-B5** inputs are resized to **512x512** to maximize the detection of fine-grained lesions, addressing the correlation between error and downsampling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    *   **Swin Transformer V2** inputs are resized to **384x384**, balancing computational load while leveraging the Transformer's robust global feature extraction.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** Aggressive transformations (Random Flip, Rotation up to 30°) are applied to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Photometric (Stochastic CLAHE):** Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) is applied with a probability of **$p=0.5$**. This ensures the model learns to handle both enhanced and raw contrast distributions, preventing the train-inference distribution mismatch observed when $p=1.0$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

# Training
The training procedure focuses on stability and finding robust minima in the noisy optimization landscape.

*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** is used to directly optimize the proxy for the Quadratic Weighted Kappa metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Cross-Validation:** A **5-Fold Cross-Validation** scheme is implemented for both architectures (10 models total) to ensure predictions are robust to data selection noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** In the final 20% of training epochs, **SWA** is activated. This technique averages the weights of the model at different points in the trajectory to find a flatter, wider minimum in the loss landscape, improving generalization on the subjective grading labels and mitigating the risk of "lucky" narrow minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Optimization:** The Adam optimizer is used with a **Cosine Annealing Scheduler**. Crucially, **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** is enabled for the backbones to save memory, but the GeM layers are forced to run in FP32 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

# Evaluation
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** Predictions are generated using all 10 trained models (5 CNN + 5 Transformer). The **continuous regression scores** are averaged *before* rounding. This "soft voting" stabilizes the predictions against the quantization noise inherent in ordinal classification (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each model, the prediction is the average of the original image and its horizontal flip.
*   **Post-processing:** The final averaged continuous score is rounded to the nearest integer to produce the diagnosis labels (0-4).
