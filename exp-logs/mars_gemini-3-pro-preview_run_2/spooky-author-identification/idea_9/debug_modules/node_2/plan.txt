The `AssertionError: Probabilities do not sum to 1` is caused by numerical precision limitations when performing the Softmax operation on `float16` tensors generated within the `torch.amp.autocast` context. The resulting probabilities drift slightly from summing to 1.0, exceeding the strict tolerance. To resolve this, I will modify `library/engine.py` to cast the logits to `float32` **before** applying `torch.softmax`. This ensures the normalization is performed with sufficient precision (Cite debug_lesson_6).
