The `AssertionError: Probabilities do not sum to 1` occurs because the model inference runs within a `torch.amp.autocast` context, producing logits in half-precision (Float16). When `torch.softmax` is applied directly to these Float16 logits, the resulting probabilities suffer from precision loss and do not sum strictly to 1.0 within the required tolerance ($10^{-5}$). To fix this, the logits must be cast to single-precision (Float32) *before* applying the softmax function, ensuring accurate normalization.
