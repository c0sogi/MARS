# Solution Strategy: Adversarily-Regularized Hybrid Stacking with Layer-Adaptive Fine-Tuning

## Model
The architecture represents a structural evolution of the "Single-Backbone Multi-View" approach. It focuses on maximizing the generalization capability of the strongest learner through advanced regularization and optimization dynamics, while strictly respecting the "Late Fusion" paradigm to avoid the modality gap failures observed in previous iterations.

*   **Level 1: Base Estimators (Dual-Branch Architecture)**
    *   **Branch A: The Adversarial Neural Expert**
        *   **Backbone:** We will utilize **`microsoft/deberta-v3-large`** exclusively, as it is the proven superior learner for this task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
        *   **Structural Innovation (Head):** We will implement a **Weighted Layer & Dual-Pooling Head** followed by **Multi-Sample Dropout**.
            1.  **Weighted Layer Pooling:** We extract hidden states from the **last 4 layers** and compute a learnable weighted average. This captures a hierarchy of linguistic features, from syntax (lower) to semantics (higher).
            2.  **Dual Pooling:** The averaged representation is processed by both **Mean Pooling** (global tone) and **Max Pooling** (salient signals), and the results are concatenated.
            3.  **Multi-Sample Dropout:** The fused vector is passed through 5 parallel dropout masks. The outputs are averaged before the final classifier. This acts as an internal ensemble to smooth the loss landscape (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Branch B: The Classical Statistical Expert**
        *   We retain the "shallow" stack to capture explicit lexical signals, but we strictly avoid fusing these features into the neural network (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
        *   **Models:**
            1.  **Logistic Regression** & **Multinomial Naive Bayes** (Sparse TF-IDF).
            2.  **XGBoost** (Dense SVD-Projected) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

*   **Level 2: Meta-Learner**
    *   A **Logistic Regression** model will aggregate the probability distributions from the Neural branch and the Classical models.

## Data
The data pipeline is strictly regulated to maximize signal density and computational efficiency.

*   **Classical Preprocessing:**
    *   **Hybrid N-Grams:** We will generate **Word N-grams (1-3)** and **Character N-grams (2-5)**. This combination is essential for capturing both semantic content and morphological style (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    *   **Noise Pruning:** We will enforce **`min_df=2`** to remove singleton noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Dimensionality Reduction:** We will apply **Truncated SVD** (100 components) to the TF-IDF matrix specifically for the XGBoost input.

*   **Neural Preprocessing:**
    *   **Sequence Length:** We will set **`max_length=85`**. Analysis confirms this covers the vast majority of the data distribution, preventing the batch instability associated with excessive padding (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

## Training
The training strategy introduces **Adversarial Training** and **Layer-Wise Learning Rate Decay** to push the performance of the single backbone beyond standard fine-tuning limits.

*   **Optimization Dynamics:**
    *   **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP):** We will implement AWP (or FGM) in the training loop. This technique injects small, worst-case perturbations to the model weights during the backward pass, forcing the model to find a flatter minimum in the loss landscape. This significantly improves generalization on small datasets.
    *   **Layer-Wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD):** We will apply multiplicative decay (e.g., 0.9x) to the learning rates of the Transformer layers. Lower layers (containing general syntactic knowledge) will be updated less aggressively than top layers (task-specific semantics), preventing catastrophic forgetting.
*   **Cross-Validation:** We will strictly enforce **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation**. We will not sacrifice training data volume to fit multiple weaker backbones (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Regularization:**
    *   **Aggressive Early Stopping:** We set **`patience=1`** to prevent overfitting to specific folds (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Batch Stability:** We use **Gradient Accumulation** to maintain an effective batch size of $\ge 16$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Artifact Caching:** All features and OOF predictions will be cached using **configuration-hash filenames** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Test data is processed through the Neural pipeline (DeBERTa w/ AWP & Dual-Pooling) and the Classical pipeline (TF-IDF/SVD).
*   **Aggregation:** Predictions from all base models are concatenated and fed to the Meta-Learner.
*   **Safety:** Final probabilities are clipped to \\([10^{-15}, 1-10^{-15}]\\).
