# Solution Strategy: Multi-Architecture Hybrid Stacking with Diverse Pooling

## Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Stacking Ensemble** that expands the neural capacity of the system while enforcing architectural diversity to decorrelate errors.

*   **Level 1: Base Estimators (Tri-Branch Architecture)**
    *   **Branch A: Classical Statistical (Retained):** We will preserve the highly effective "shallow" models that capture explicit lexical and morphological signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008). This includes **Logistic Regression** and **Multinomial Naive Bayes** on sparse TF-IDF features, and **XGBoost** on SVD-projected dense features.
    *   **Branch B: Disentangled Attention (Primary Neural):** We will utilize **`microsoft/deberta-v3-large`** as the primary deep learner. Its disentangled attention mechanism is proven to be superior for this task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009). We will use the standard `[CLS]` token for classification.
    *   **Branch C: Absolute Attention with Mean Pooling (Secondary Neural):** We will introduce **`roberta-large`** as a complementary neural backbone. Unlike DeBERTa, RoBERTa uses absolute positional embeddings. Crucially, instead of the `[CLS]` token, we will use **Mean Pooling** over the final hidden states. This forces the model to aggregate stylistic information across the entire sequence, providing a distinct "view" of the data compared to the specific token focus of Branch B.

*   **Level 2: Meta-Learner**
    *   A **Logistic Regression** model will aggregate the probability distributions from all five Level 1 models (3 Classical, 2 Neural). This allows the system to balance the high-precision lexical matching of Branch A with the diverse semantic and contextual reasoning of Branches B and C.

## Data
The data pipeline will be optimized to maximize signal density while respecting computational constraints.

*   **Text Preprocessing:**
    *   **Hybrid N-Grams:** We will generate **Word N-grams (1-3)** and **Character N-grams (2-5)** for Branch A to capture sub-word stylistic markers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    *   **Noise Pruning:** We will strictly enforce **`min_df=2`** for TF-IDF vectorization to remove singleton noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Sequence Length Optimization:** For the Neural Branches, we will set **`max_length=85`**. Analysis shows the mean sentence length is ~27 words; a limit of 85 covers >99% of the data. This avoids the computational waste and batch instability caused by the excessive padding (128+) observed in previous failures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

*   **Dimensionality Reduction:**
    *   **Truncated SVD:** We will apply SVD (100 components) specifically for the XGBoost model to enable tree-based learning on dense vectors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

## Training
The training process focuses on preventing overfitting and ensuring efficient resource utilization.

*   **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation:** A 5-fold split will be used across all models to generate consistent Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions for the meta-learner.
*   **Aggressive Early Stopping:** For both Transformer models (Branches B and C), we will set **`patience=1`**. This forces the models to stop immediately upon the first sign of validation loss degradation, preventing the "memory" of specific fold noise and ensuring robust OOF generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Batch Size Stability:** By restricting `max_length` to 85, we will maintain a **Batch Size >= 16** (using Gradient Accumulation if necessary). This ensures stable gradient estimation and Batch Normalization statistics, correcting the instability issues seen when batch sizes were sacrificed for sequence length (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Configuration-Aware Caching:** All intermediate features and OOF predictions will be cached using hash-based filenames to ensure reproducibility and speed (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Test data will be processed through all three branches. The Classical models will predict based on TF-IDF/SVD features, while the two Transformers will predict based on their respective tokenizations.
*   **Ensembling:** The probability vectors from all base models will be concatenated and fed into the trained Meta-Learner.
*   **Post-Processing:** Final predictions will be clipped to the range \\([10^{-15}, 1-10^{-15}]\\) to ensure metric stability.
