The previous implementation caused a dimension mismatch error during inference because the `OneHotEncoder` was re-fitted on the full training dataset in `get_test_data`, whereas it was originally fitted on a smaller subset (due to `Config.DEBUG_DATA_SIZE`) in `get_train_val_data`. This resulted in the encoder recognizing more categories (and thus creating more feature columns) during testing than the model was trained on.

To fix this, I will modify `library/data_preparation.py` to serialize (save) the fitted preprocessors (`StandardScaler` and `OneHotEncoder`) during the training phase and reload them during the testing phase. This ensures that the feature transformation logic remains identical between training and inference, preventing shape mismatches. This applies **debug_lesson_8** (Serialize and Reuse Fitted Preprocessors).
