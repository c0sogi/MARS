The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the physical batch size of 256 exceeds the 16GB VRAM capacity of the available GPU, despite the `expandable_segments` setting (Cite debug_lesson_7). To fix this while preserving the convergence stability of the original batch size, I will implement **Gradient Accumulation** (Cite debug_lesson_8). This involves reducing the `BATCH_SIZE` in `Config` to 32 and introducing `GRAD_ACCUM_STEPS = 8` (maintaining an effective batch size of 256). I will then update the `Trainer` class in `library/trainer.py` to scale the loss, accumulate gradients over multiple steps, and update the optimizer/scheduler only after the accumulated steps, ensuring the memory footprint is minimized without altering the training dynamics.
