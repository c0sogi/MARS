The execution failure was caused by a **CUDA Out of Memory (OOM)** error. The root cause is the excessively large `BATCH_SIZE` (512) combined with the memory-intensive output of the `generation_head` in the `BiLSTMDualHead` model. This layer produces a tensor of shape `(batch_size, seq_len, vocab_size)` (512 × 128 × 50,000), which requires approximately 12.2 GB of memory for the tensor alone, and another ~12 GB for its gradients during backpropagation, exceeding the A100 GPU's capacity.

To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` from 512 to 64 in both `library/config.py` (the default) and `runfile.py` (the override). This will reduce the memory footprint of the problematic tensors by a factor of 8, ensuring the training fits comfortably within the 40GB GPU memory.
