Based on the lessons learned from the previous experiments—specifically the superiority of sequence tagging for localization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004), the effectiveness of aligning with pre-training objectives (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007), and the necessity of probabilistic beam search to prevent error propagation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009)—I propose a **Three-Stage "Generate-and-Discriminate" Pipeline**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation by adding a third **Discriminative Verification** stage. While the previous best solution (Idea 6) relied on a "Joint Score" of localization and in-filling probabilities, it lacked a mechanism to evaluate the *global coherence* of the resulting sentence. The new stage explicitly learns to distinguish between "correctly reconstructed" and "subtly flawed" sentences, acting as a final quality gate.

### Model
The solution consists of three specialized Transformer models arranged in a cascade:

1.  **Stage 1: The Structural Locator (DeBERTa-v3-Base)**
    *   **Role:** Identify potential gap locations.
    *   **Architecture:** A DeBERTa-v3 encoder with a **Token Classification Head**. It utilizes disentangled attention to effectively model relative positions and syntactic breaks.
    *   **Output:** Instead of a single hard prediction, it outputs the **Top-K (e.g., K=5)** most probable gap indices for each sentence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

2.  **Stage 2: The Semantic In-Filler (RoBERTa-Large)**
    *   **Role:** Generate candidate words for the proposed locations.
    *   **Architecture:** A RoBERTa-Large model using its native **Masked Language Modeling (MLM) Head**.
    *   **Operation:** For each of the $K$ indices from Stage 1, it inserts a `<mask>` token and predicts the most likely word $w$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007). This produces $K$ distinct "candidate sentences."

3.  **Stage 3: The Contextual Verifier (DeBERTa-v3-Large)**
    *   **Role:** Re-rank candidates based on global fluency and coherence.
    *   **Architecture:** A DeBERTa-v3-Large model with a **Sequence Classification Head** (Binary).
    *   **Operation:** It takes a complete candidate sentence as input and outputs a scalar probability indicating whether the sentence is "Real/Correct" or "Corrupted/Incorrect." This allows the system to reject candidates that are locally probable (high MLM score) but globally nonsensical.

### Data
We will leverage the large `train_v2.txt` dataset to create specialized training sets for each stage, using **Hard Negative Mining** for the Verifier.

1.  **For Locator & In-Filler:**
    *   Sample 2 million sentences.
    *   **Synthetic Corruption:** Uniformly remove one word.
    *   **Labels:** Binary sequence tags for the Locator; original masked words for the In-Filler.

2.  **For the Verifier (Crucial Step):**
    *   We construct a "Contrastive" dataset of **Triples**: `(Original, Negative_Pos, Negative_Word)`.
    *   **Positive:** The original, valid sentence (Label 1).
    *   **Hard Negative (Position):** The sentence with the *correct* word inserted at a *slightly wrong* location (e.g., neighbor indices).
    *   **Hard Negative (Word):** The sentence with a *wrong* word (sampled from the In-Filler's top predictions) inserted at the *correct* location.
    *   This forces the Verifier to learn fine-grained decision boundaries that the previous stages might miss.

### Training
Training is decoupled to maximize efficiency and performance:

1.  **Stage 1 (Locator):** Trained with **Focal Loss** to handle the extreme class imbalance (1 gap vs. ~25 non-gaps).
2.  **Stage 2 (In-Filler):** Fine-tuned with standard **Cross-Entropy Loss** on the masked tokens.
3.  **Stage 3 (Verifier):** Trained with **Binary Cross-Entropy Loss** on the balanced dataset of Positives and Hard Negatives. We will use **Label Smoothing** (0.1) to prevent overconfidence and improve generalization.

### Evaluation
Inference follows a **Beam Search with Discriminative Reranking** procedure:

1.  **Candidate Generation:** The Locator proposes the Top-$K$ ($K=5$) insertion indices.
2.  **Hypothesis Expansion:** The In-Filler generates the best word for each index, creating 5 complete candidate sentences.
3.  **Scoring:**
    *   Calculate the **Joint Score** for each candidate $c$:
        $$ S(c) = \log(P_{\text{loc}}) + \log(P_{\text{MLM}}) + \lambda \cdot \log(P_{\text{Verifier}}) $$
    *   $\lambda$ is a hyperparameter (e.g., 2.0) weighting the Verifier's judgment.
4.  **Selection:** Submit the candidate with the highest total score. This ensures the final answer is syntactically sound, semantically precise, and globally coherent.
