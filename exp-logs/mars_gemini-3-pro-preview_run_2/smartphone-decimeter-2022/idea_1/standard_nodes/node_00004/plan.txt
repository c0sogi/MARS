To optimize the solution, I will implement a strategy focused on improving generalization and preventing the overfitting observed in the previous run (Train Loss ~1.7 vs Val Loss ~79). The primary issue identified is the use of absolute coordinates (`WlsLat`, `WlsLon`) as input features, which causes the model to memorize location-specific errors in the training set that do not transfer to the validation set.

Here is the plan:
1.  **Feature Engineering**:
    *   **Remove Absolute Coordinates**: Eliminate `WlsLat`, `WlsLon`, and `WlsAlt` from the input features.
    *   **Add Relative Dynamics**: Introduce `ScaledDeltaLat` and `ScaledDeltaLon` (differences between consecutive WLS positions, scaled to roughly meters). This allows the model to perceive the local trajectory shape (velocity/acceleration) and learn smoothing patterns without overfitting to specific locations.
    *   **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}**: I will continue to use the `TARGET_SCALE_FACTOR` (100,000) for both the targets and the new delta features to ensure numerical stability and compatibility with network initialization.

2.  **Model Architecture & Hyperparameters**:
    *   **Increase Window Size**: Expand `WINDOW_SIZE` from 5 to 11 (approx. +/- 5 seconds context) to provide the model with more trajectory history for effective smoothing.
    *   **Reduce Model Complexity**: Shrink hidden layers to `[128, 64]` and increase `DROPOUT_RATE` to 0.3 to further regularize the model and force it to learn generalizable signal-error relationships.
    *   **Batch Size**: Increase to 2048 for stable gradient estimation and faster training.

3.  **Code Changes**:
    *   Modify `library/config.py` to update feature lists and hyperparameters.
    *   Modify `library/data_loader.py` to compute the new delta features during processing.
