Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous iterations and the available computational resources (24 hours, NVIDIA A100), I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the approach into a **Memory-Resident Homogeneous Bagged Ensemble**. This strategy leverages the high-throughput capabilities of in-memory training to deploy a massive ensemble of fully converged, high-performance models, correcting the "weak learner" and "underfitting" flaws of previous ensemble attempts.

# Model
I will implement a **Homogeneous Bagged Ensemble** consisting of **10 independent ConvNeXt-Tiny** models (2 runs of 5-Fold Cross-Validation).
*   **Backbone Selection**: I will strictly utilize the **ConvNeXt-Tiny** architecture. This backbone has proven to be the "sweet spot" for this task, offering superior data efficiency compared to ResNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) while avoiding the "training inertia" and underfitting observed with larger variants like ConvNeXt-Small (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Head Design (Correction)**: I will use **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015) and explicitly retain the backbone's native `LayerNorm` before the classifier to ensure stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013). Crucially, I will **exclude** Multi-Sample Dropout, as previous experiments showed that structural regularization (DropPath) in the backbone is superior to head-based regularization for this architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
*   **Ensemble Structure**: Unlike previous heterogeneous attempts where weaker models (EfficientNet) degraded performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027), this ensemble will be **homogeneous**. I will train 10 identical high-performance ConvNeXt-Tiny models (using different random seeds for data splitting and initialization) to reduce variance via Bagging without diluting prediction quality.

# Data
The data pipeline will be implemented as a **Memory-Resident Isotropic Pipeline** to maximize throughput and enforce strict geometric invariance.
1.  **In-Memory Caching**: I will load the entire dataset (~140k images) into system RAM at the start. This eliminates disk I/O latency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026), enabling the training of 10 fully converged models within the 24-hour limit.
2.  **Isotropic Augmentation Pipeline**:
    *   **Geometric Completeness**: I will apply **Random Horizontal/Vertical Flips** *combined* with **Continuous Random Rotation** ($[-180^{\circ}, 180^{\circ}]$). This corrects the critical failures of previous solutions that omitted flips (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026) or used discrete rotations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021), ensuring the model learns the full Dihedral symmetry of the data.
    *   **Invariant Intensity**: I will apply **ColorJitter** to **100%** of the training images. This corrects the "probabilistic intensity" error, forcing the model to learn features invariant to stain variations rather than overfitting to specific color histograms (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Augment-then-Crop**: All augmentations will be applied to the full **96x96px** patch before performing the **Contextual Crop to 64x64px**. This prevents rotation artifacts and ensures intensity statistics are derived from the global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00005).
3.  **Normalization**: Use **dataset-specific mean and standard deviation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

# Training
The training strategy prioritizes **Full Convergence** for every model in the ensemble, utilizing the time saved by RAM caching to avoid the "schedule compression" errors of previous attempts.
*   **Duration**: Each of the 10 models will be trained for **30 epochs**. This extended duration ensures the models overcome training inertia and fully converge, correcting the underfitting observed in previous ensemble attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Regularization**: I will apply **Mixup** (alpha=0.2) combined with high **Weight Decay** (0.05) and **Stochastic Depth (DropPath)**. This combination is essential for modern architectures to generalize well (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010, solution_lesson_node_00032).
*   **Optimization**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
    *   **EMA**: I will implement **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** with a decay rate tuned to the longer schedule (e.g., $\beta=0.995$), preventing the initialization bias observed in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
    *   **Validation**: I will validate on the **full hold-out set** for each fold to ensure the checkpoint selection is statistically representative (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

# Evaluation
The evaluation process maximizes robustness by integrating model-level bagging with geometric marginalization.
*   **Inference**: Predictions will be generated using the **EMA weights** of all 10 models.
*   **TTA**: I will implement **8-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)** for each model, averaging predictions across the original image and its 7 Dihedral variants (rotations and flips). This marginalizes out orientation-specific noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Aggregation**: The final submission will be the average of **80 probabilities** per image (10 models $\times$ 8 TTA views).
