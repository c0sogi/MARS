Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous iterations and the available computational resources (24 hours, NVIDIA A100), I propose an optimized solution that structurally evolves the approach from a single-architecture ensemble to a **Heterogeneous Deep Ensemble**. This strategy leverages the diversity of inductive biases between modern ConvNets and EfficientNets to minimize generalization error, while strictly adhering to the proven data processing and regularization recipes.

### Model
I will implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of two distinct backbone architectures: **ConvNeXt-Tiny** and **EfficientNetV2-Small**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Heterogeneity)**: Instead of relying solely on ConvNeXt, I will introduce **EfficientNetV2-Small** as a complementary backbone. While ConvNeXt modernizes standard CNNs with Transformer-like design choices (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009), EfficientNetV2 utilizes Fused-MBConv blocks and neural architecture search. Ensembling these distinct architectures decorrelates prediction errors more effectively than ensembling identical models, leading to a more robust decision boundary.
*   **Pooling & Head Design**: Both architectures will utilize **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**. Previous experiments confirmed that for this texture-based tumor detection task, GAP outperforms focal pooling methods like GeM (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015). Crucially, I will instantiate these models with their **default classification heads** (modifying only the output dimension) to ensure that essential normalization layers (e.g., `LayerNorm` in ConvNeXt) are preserved, preventing the convergence instability observed when customizing heads (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Model EMA**: I will apply **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** to the weights of all models in the ensemble. This creates a "temporal ensemble" for each fold, stabilizing the model against the noise introduced by aggressive regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

### Data
The data pipeline will strictly enforce the "Augment-then-Crop" paradigm to maximize context usage and minimize artifacts.
1.  **Normalization**: I will use **dataset-specific mean and standard deviation** to align with the pathology scans' dynamic range (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
2.  **Augmentation Pipeline**:
    *   **Global Augmentation**: Apply geometric (flips, rotations) and intensity (ColorJitter) augmentations to the full **96x96px** source patch. This ensures intensity statistics are derived from the global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005) and prevents rotation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    *   **Contextual Crop**: Perform a **Center Crop to 64x64px** as the final step. This preserves the 32x32px ROI and a 16px context buffer while removing the irrelevant outer rim, preventing spatial label noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
3.  **Regularization**: I will apply **Mixup** (alpha=0.2) to the training batches. This encourages the models to learn linear interpolations between classes, which has been proven to outperform Label Smoothing for these architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
The training strategy is designed to fully utilize the 24-hour runtime to ensure maximum convergence, correcting the underfitting observed in previous attempts.
*   **Ensemble Structure**: I will train **10 independent models** (5-Fold Cross-Validation for ConvNeXt-Tiny + 5-Fold Cross-Validation for EfficientNetV2-Small).
*   **Optimization**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
    *   **Learning Rate**: Use a **conservative learning rate** (e.g., 2e-4) to preserve pre-trained feature extractors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Weight Decay**: Use high weight decay (e.g., 0.05) to complement Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Duration**: Each model will be trained for **25 epochs**. This extended duration ensures the models overcome "training inertia" and fully converge, addressing the failure mode where schedule compression degraded performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Evaluation
The evaluation process maximizes robustness by integrating architectural, spatial, and geometric ensembling.
*   **Inference**: Predictions will be generated using the **EMA weights** of all 10 models.
*   **TTA**: I will implement **8-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**, averaging predictions across the original image and its 7 Dihedral variants (rotations and flips) to exploit rotational invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Aggregation**: The final submission will be the average of **80 probabilities** per image (10 models $\times$ 8 TTA views). This extensive averaging marginalizes out model-specific bias and orientation noise, resulting in a highly calibrated prediction.
