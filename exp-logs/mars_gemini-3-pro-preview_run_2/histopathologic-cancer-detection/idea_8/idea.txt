Based on the analysis of the previous ideas and the specific lessons learned from the "New Solutions," I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the approach by implementing an **Isotropic Heterogeneous Ensemble**. This strategy combines the diversity of multiple architectures with the "Isotropic" data processing paradigm, correcting the critical flaw where previous ensembles failed because they relied on discrete rotations rather than the continuous rotational invariance inherent to the domain.

### Model
I will implement a **Heterogeneous Deep Ensemble** consisting of two distinct backbone architectures: **ConvNeXt-Tiny** and **EfficientNetV2-Small**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Heterogeneity)**: I will combine **ConvNeXt-Tiny** (which utilizes isotropic, Transformer-style blocks) with **EfficientNetV2-Small** (which utilizes Fused-MBConv blocks). This heterogeneity ensures that the ensemble captures diverse feature representations—ConvNeXt excels at global context via large kernels, while EfficientNet optimizes local texture extraction.
*   **Head Design (Correction)**: Both architectures will use **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**. I will explicitly retain the backbone-specific normalization layers (e.g., `LayerNorm` for ConvNeXt) before the classifier to prevent the convergence instability observed when customizing heads (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013). This adheres to the finding that GAP outperforms focal pooling (GeM) for this texture-based task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Model EMA**: I will apply **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** to the weights of all models. This creates a "temporal ensemble" that stabilizes the decision boundary against the noise introduced by Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

### Data
The data pipeline will be fundamentally restructured to enforce **Continuous Isotropic Invariance**, addressing the finding that discrete ensembling is inferior to continuous augmentation in this domain (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
1.  **Normalization**: Use **dataset-specific mean and standard deviation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
2.  **Isotropic Augmentation Pipeline**:
    *   **Step 1 (Global Context)**: Load the full **96x96px** source patch.
    *   **Step 2 (Continuous Rotation)**: Apply **Random Rotation** sampling from the full continuous circle ($[-180^{\circ}, 180^{\circ}]$). This is a critical evolution from previous attempts that used discrete 90-degree rotations; it forces the model to learn true rotational invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Step 3 (Intensity)**: Apply **ColorJitter** to the rotated 96x96px patch to derive statistics from the global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
    *   **Step 4 (Contextual Crop)**: Perform a **Center Crop to 64x64px**. This preserves the 32x32px ROI and a 16px context buffer while removing artifacts introduced by rotation at the corners (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001, solution_lesson_node_00004).
3.  **Regularization**: Apply **Mixup** (alpha=0.2) to the training batches to improve data efficiency and generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009, solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
The training strategy prioritizes **Full Convergence** over iteration speed, utilizing the A100 GPU to support a computationally intensive schedule that previous attempts compromised on.
*   **Ensemble Structure**: I will train **10 independent models** (5-Fold Cross-Validation for ConvNeXt + 5-Fold Cross-Validation for EfficientNet).
*   **Duration**: Each model will be trained for **25 epochs**. This corrects the "schedule compression" error, ensuring the models overcome training inertia and fully converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00012, solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Optimization**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
    *   **Learning Rate**: Use a **conservative learning rate** (e.g., 2e-4) to preserve pre-trained features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Weight Decay**: Use high weight decay (e.g., 0.05) to complement Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Evaluation
The evaluation process maximizes robustness by integrating architectural diversity with geometric marginalization.
*   **Inference**: Predictions will be generated using the **EMA weights** of all 10 models.
*   **TTA**: I will implement **8-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**, averaging predictions across the original image and its 7 Dihedral variants (rotations and flips). This marginalizes out any remaining orientation-specific noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Aggregation**: The final submission will be the unweighted average of probabilities from all models and TTA views.
