Based on the provided lessons and the performance analysis of the previous solution, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Focus on ConvNeXt-Tiny**: The heterogeneous ensemble with `tf_efficientnetv2_s` proved inefficient, as the EfficientNet model struggled to converge (AUC ~0.55) compared to ConvNeXt (AUC ~0.83) within the limited training time. I will remove EfficientNet to allocate all computational resources to the performing model. Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`.
2.  **Ensure Convergence**: The previous run was artificially limited to 3 epochs, leading to underfitting. I will increase the training duration to 15 epochs, which was identified in `solution_lesson_node_00015` as a sufficient duration to reach high performance (~0.9889 AUC). I will remove the hardcoded epoch override in `runfile.py`. Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`.
3.  **Maintain Proven Techniques**: I will retain the 5-fold Cross-Validation, Mixup regularization, EMA, and 8-view TTA, as these have been empirically validated in `solution_lesson_node_00002`, `solution_lesson_node_00006`, and `solution_lesson_node_00010`.
