To combat the overfitting observed in previous runs (where validation loss drifted high), I will increase **weight decay** and add **CoarseDropout** augmentation. Crucially, I will maintain the 3-slice input configuration (12 channels) to avoid the "input inflation" issues highlighted in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**. Finally, I will implement the conditional submission logic in the runfile.
