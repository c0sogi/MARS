Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, the primary cause of the performance degradation was the increase in input dimensionality (20 channels) and the associated heuristic weight replication, which broke the integrity of the pre-trained features (Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00127). Additionally, the weight decay was likely too aggressive for this small dataset (Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00126).

My optimization strategy is as follows:
1.  **Revert Input Dimensionality**: Reduce the input from 20 channels (5 slices per modality) to 12 channels (3 slices per modality). This ensures that `Channels per Group (12/4) = 3`, matching the pre-trained ImageNet weights exactly.
2.  **Fix Initialization**: Replace the "Central Replication" initialization with a direct 1:1 copy of the pre-trained weights, preserving the spatial filters learned on RGB images (Cite 00127).
3.  **Adjust Regularization**: Reduce `WEIGHT_DECAY` from `1e-2` to `1e-3` to prevent over-regularization in this low-data regime (Cite 00126).
