My strategy is to revert the "Pseudo-3D" architecture from the 5-slice configuration (Idea 24) back to the more robust 3-slice configuration (Idea 21), while retaining the improvements in data processing (Edge Clamping, Integral ROI).

1.  **Configuration (`library/config.py`)**: Reduce `NUM_SLICES` from 5 to 3 and set `STRIDE` to `[-5, 0, 5]`. This aligns the input channels (12 total, 3 per group) with the pre-trained EfficientNet stem filters ($K \times K \times 3$), avoiding the need for heuristic weight scaling and reducing overfitting risks (Cite Lesson 68).
2.  **Model (`library/model.py`)**: Simplify the `_init_stem_weights` method. Since the input channels per group (3) now exactly match the pre-trained weights, I will directly copy the full bank of 32 pre-trained filters to the new grouped stem. This preserves the diversity of feature detectors learned on ImageNet (Cite Lesson 23).
3.  **Execution (`runfile.py`)**: Increase the training epochs slightly (to 15) to allow for convergence, as the simpler model may be more stable than the previous overfitting one.
