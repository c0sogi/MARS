To optimize the performance of the MGMT promoter methylation prediction model, I will implement a strategy focused on **stabilizing the training process** and **preserving weak signals**, drawing directly from the provided lessons.

The current solution suffers from severe instability (Validation Loss exploding to 4.17) and likely underfitting due to aggressive regularization. My changes are:

1.  **Reduce Weight Decay (Cite Lesson 00003)**: The current `WEIGHT_DECAY` is `1e-2`, which is excessively high for this weak-signal task. Lesson 00003 demonstrated that aggressive decay (`>1e-4`) can extinguish the predictive signal entirely. I will reduce this to `1e-4`.
2.  **Robust Initialization (Cite Lesson 00006)**: I will modify `library/model.py` to decouple the bias initialization from the weight shape check. As noted in Lesson 00006, ensuring the pretrained bias is preserved regardless of the convolution grouping strategy contributes to a more stable optimization landscape.
3.  **Learning Rate Scheduler (Cite Lesson 00007)**: To further combat the observed instability and loss explosion, I will introduce a `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler in `library/train.py`. Lesson 00007 highlighted that this scheduler effectively stabilizes the loss curve.
4.  **Training Duration**: I will increase the training epochs from 5 to 10 in `runfile.py` to allow the scheduler to complete a meaningful cycle and the model to converge with the lower weight decay.
