My optimization strategy focuses on two key areas derived from the "Lessons Learned" to improve model generalization and convergence:

1.  **Refining Geometric Augmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091):**
    The previous solution used zero-padding (`cv2.BORDER_CONSTANT`) during image rotation. As identified in Lesson 91, this introduces artificial high-frequency edges (black borders) that can confuse the model and distort feature statistics, especially when fine-tuning pre-trained networks. I will switch this to **Reflection Padding** (`cv2.BORDER_REFLECT`), which preserves local texture consistency at the boundaries.

2.  **Extending Training Duration:**
    The previous run was limited to a hardcoded 10 epochs for speed, achieving an AUC of ~0.614. To allow the model to fully converge and take advantage of the improved augmentation, I will update the execution script to use the configured `NUM_EPOCHS` (15), relying on the existing early stopping mechanism to prevent overfitting if convergence occurs earlier.
