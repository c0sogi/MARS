Based on the analysis of the provided lessons and the current solution's performance, the primary cause of the low validation AUC (0.486) and overfitting is the **Dual-Scale Input Strategy** (Stride 2 + Stride 5). **Lesson 00073** explicitly warns that adding "global context" slices to local slices increases dimensionality and dilutes the signal in small datasets, leading to performance degradation compared to a focused single-stride approach. Additionally, **Lesson 00127** highlights the importance of matching the input channels per group to the pre-trained weights (3 channels) for optimal initialization.

My strategy is to:
1.  **Simplify the Input:** Revert from the 24-channel Dual-Scale input to a 12-channel Single-Scale input using a fixed stride of 5 (Cite **solution_lesson_node_00073** and **solution_lesson_node_00110**).
2.  **Align Grouped Convolutions:** Reduce the number of stem groups from 8 to 4 (one per modality). This ensures `Channels per Group = 3` (12 channels / 4 groups), allowing for a direct, mathematically consistent copy of the pre-trained ImageNet weights (Cite **solution_lesson_node_00127** and **solution_lesson_node_00076**).
3.  **Optimize Training:** Increase the number of epochs from 5 to 10 in `runfile.py` to allow the simplified, more robust model to converge, as the previous run showed underfitting on the validation set despite high training AUC.
