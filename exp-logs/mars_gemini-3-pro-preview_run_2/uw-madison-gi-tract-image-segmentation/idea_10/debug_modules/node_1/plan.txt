The issue is that the `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2` transform does not automatically transpose multi-channel masks (e.g., from `(H, W, C)` to `(C, H, W)`) unless explicitly configured. This results in the masks remaining in channel-last format, causing a shape mismatch assertion failure in the training loop where PyTorch expects channel-first tensors. As detailed in **debug_lesson_3**, the fix is to explicitly set `transpose_mask=True` when initializing `ToTensorV2` for segmentation tasks involving multi-channel masks. Cite debug_lesson_3.
