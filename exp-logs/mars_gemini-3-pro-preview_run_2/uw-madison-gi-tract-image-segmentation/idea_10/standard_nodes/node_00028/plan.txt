The primary issue causing the `InvalidSubmissionError` is that the segmentation masks are being submitted in the model's output resolution (256x256) rather than the original image resolution, which varies across the dataset. The evaluation system expects predictions to match the original scan dimensions. To fix this, I will modify `generate_submission` in `library/train.py` to resize the predicted masks back to their original height and width before RLE encoding (Cite debug_lesson_2).

Additionally, the post-processing logic incorrectly groups all slices for a patient (`case`) together, ignoring that a patient may have multiple scans on different `day`s with different resolutions. This causes dimension mismatch errors when constructing 3D volumes. I will update `refine_predictions` in `library/post_processing.py` to group by both `case` and `day` to ensure dimension homogeneity within each volume (Cite debug_lesson_4).
