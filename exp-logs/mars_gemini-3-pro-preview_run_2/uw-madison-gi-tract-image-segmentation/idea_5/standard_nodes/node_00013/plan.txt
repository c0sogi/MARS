The issue is that the **3D Hausdorff Distance** calculation in `library/utils.py` fails to normalize the Z-axis (depth) dimension, whereas the X and Y dimensions are normalized to the `[0, 1]` range. The current implementation uses a spacing of `1.0` for the Z-axis, meaning the distance is calculated in "number of slices" for depth but "fraction of image" for height/width. This inhomogeneous scaling (Cite debug_lesson_4) causes the Hausdorff distance to exceed `1.0` when segmentation errors occur across slices, leading to large negative values in the final metric `0.4*Dice + 0.6*(1 - HD)`. The fix is to normalize the Z-axis spacing by dividing `1.0` by the volume depth, ensuring the entire 3D volume is mapped to a unit cube `[0, 1]^3`.
