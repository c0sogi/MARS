### Implementation Strategy

1.  **Configuration Override**: To ensure the "fast baseline" completes within the 2-hour limit, I will override the default `Config` parameters. I will enable `DEBUG` mode but increase the `DEBUG_SAMPLE_SIZE` to 6000 samples to provide sufficient training data while keeping runtime low. I will also reduce the number of epochs for both stages to 5.
2.  **Training**: I will sequentially initialize and run the `Trainer` for the 'coarse' stage (Global Localization) and then the 'fine' stage (Local Refinement). This will save the best model checkpoints to the working directory.
3.  **Validation & Metric Calculation**:
    *   I will load the validation metadata and group it by case and day.
    *   I will instantiate the `InferenceEngine` (which loads the trained models) and use it to predict segmentation masks for each validation case.
    *   I will construct 3D volumes for both the Ground Truth and Predictions.
    *   I will compute the Mean Dice and 3D Hausdorff Distance according to the competition metric (0.4 * Dice + 0.6 * Hausdorff).
    *   I will print the `Final Validation Metric: <value>` with full precision.
4.  **Failure Analysis**:
    *   During the validation loop, I will record error metrics (1 - Dice) for each slice/case along with metadata features (slice index, pixel spacing, etc.).
    *   I will calculate and print the correlation between these features and the error magnitude to identify failure modes.
5.  **Submission**:
    *   If the calculated validation metric exceeds the threshold of 0.438, I will execute the `run_inference()` function to generate the final `submission.csv` for the test set.
