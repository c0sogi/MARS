To optimize the performance of the DeBERTa-v3-large model, I will implement **Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD)**. This technique assigns progressively lower learning rates to the earlier layers of the Transformer backbone, preserving the pre-trained features while allowing the top layers and the task-specific head to adapt more aggressively. This is particularly effective for fine-tuning large models on smaller datasets to prevent overfitting and catastrophic forgetting.

Additionally, I will increase the number of training epochs from 2 to 3 to allow the lower layers (which will now have very small learning rates) sufficient time to converge. I will also slightly increase the base learning rate to `2.5e-5` to compensate for the decay applied to the backbone.

The changes are confined to `runfile.py`.

        head_params = [(n, p) for n, p in param_optimizer if "backbone" not in n]
        optimizer_parameters.append({
            "params": [p for n, p in head_params if not any(nd in n for nd in no_decay)],
            "weight_decay": weight_decay,
            "lr": base_lr
        })
        optimizer_parameters.append({
            "params": [p for n, p in head_params if any(nd in n for nd in no_decay)],
            "weight_decay": 0.0,

        # 2. Backbone Parameters
        num_layers = 24
