Based on the lessons learned, specifically **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00009**, I will switch from **Early Fusion** (concatenating embeddings) to **Late Fusion** (ensembling predictions). The previous solution's performance degradation (0.2922 Log Loss) compared to the single backbone baseline (0.1229) confirms that concatenating heterogeneous features (CNN + ViT) confuses the linear classifier. By training separate classifiers for each backbone and averaging their probabilities, we allow each model to optimally utilize its specific feature representation.

Here is the implementation strategy:
1.  **Modify `library/feature_engine.py`**: Update the extraction logic to return and save CNN and ViT embeddings separately, rather than fusing them.
2.  **Modify `runfile.py`**: Update the workflow to train two separate Logistic Regression models (one for CNN, one for ViT) and ensemble their predictions by averaging probabilities.
