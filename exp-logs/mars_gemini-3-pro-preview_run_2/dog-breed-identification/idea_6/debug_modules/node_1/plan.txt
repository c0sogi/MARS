The `TimeoutError` observed during the execution of `runfile.py` is caused by a deadlock between PyTorch and Scikit-Learn's `joblib` backend. PyTorch initializes the OpenMP runtime in the main process, and when `LogisticRegressionCV` attempts to fork new processes with `n_jobs=-1`, the child processes inherit a corrupted or locked OpenMP state, leading to a hang. To fix this, I will modify `library/config.py` to set `n_jobs` to `1`, disabling multiprocessing for the classifier and ensuring serial execution. This avoids the conflict and resolves the root cause of the timeout.
