The `AssertionError: Mask tensor shape incorrect` occurs because the `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2` transform defaults to transposing images (HWC to CHW) but leaves masks in their original HWC format unless explicitly instructed otherwise. Since the dataset code expands the mask dimensions to `(H, W, 1)` before augmentation, the resulting tensor remains `(H, W, 1)`, causing a mismatch with the expected `(1, H, W)` format in the DataLoader and model. To resolve this, I will update the `ToTensorV2` initialization in `library/dataset.py` to include `transpose_mask=True`, ensuring masks are correctly transposed to the channel-first format.
