### Model: ResNet34-LinkNet with ASPP Bottleneck

I propose a structural evolution of the architecture that retains the efficiency of the LinkNet decoder but significantly enhances the encoder's capability to capture multi-scale context—a critical requirement for distinguishing salt textures from sediment.

*   **Backbone:** Retain the **ResNet34** encoder pretrained on ImageNet. This backbone is "right-sized" for the dataset scale and resolution, avoiding the optimization inertia and overfitting observed with heavier backbones like SE-ResNeXt50 Cite solution_lesson_node_00012, solution_lesson_node_00002.
*   **Structural Innovation (ASPP Bottleneck):** Insert an **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module at the bottleneck, between the encoder's final layer and the decoder. Standard CNNs often struggle to capture the global context required to identify large salt bodies in seismic images. ASPP utilizes parallel dilated convolutions (with varying rates) to expand the receptive field and aggregate multi-scale features without losing spatial resolution. This enriches the feature map entering the decoder with global context information.
*   **Depth Injection:** Inject the depth metadata immediately after the ASPP module. Project the scalar depth (normalized) into a low-dimensional embedding (e.g., 32 channels) and concatenate it with the ASPP output. This adheres to the proven strategy of bottleneck injection via concatenation rather than heavy convolutional fusion Cite solution_lesson_node_00001, solution_lesson_node_00009.
*   **Decoder:** Utilize the **LinkNet** decoder. Its additive skip connections are computationally efficient and have been proven to outperform the denser, concatenation-heavy U-Net++ for this specific task Cite solution_lesson_node_00008.

### Data

*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Depth Normalization:** Apply **Standard Scaling** (zero mean, unit variance) to the depth values. This is superior to Min-Max scaling for neural network convergence Cite solution_lesson_node_00010.
    *   **Image Formatting:** Convert images to single-channel grayscale and pad to 128x128 using reflection padding.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Elastic Transformations:** Apply **ElasticTransform** with the tuned parameters (`alpha` $\approx$ 120, `sigma` $\approx$ 6) to simulate the organic plasticity of salt Cite solution_lesson_node_00003, solution_lesson_node_00004.
    *   **Affine Transformations:** Include **ShiftScaleRotate**. Geometric invariance is mandatory for seismic data, and removing these rigid transforms has been shown to degrade performance Cite solution_lesson_node_00010.
    *   **Regularization Strength:** Apply non-rigid augmentations with a moderate probability ($p=0.2$) to act as a regularizer without shifting the training distribution too aggressively Cite solution_lesson_node_00007.

### Training

*   **Loss Function (Structural Change):** Adopt the **Lovasz-Hinge Loss** combined with Binary Cross Entropy (BCE). While previous iterations used Dice Loss, Lovasz-Hinge is a surrogate that directly optimizes the Jaccard index (IoU), aligning the training objective mathematically with the competition metric (mAP at IoU thresholds). This addresses the "Metric Optimization" gap in previous ideas.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing learning rate scheduler.
*   **Training Horizon:** Train for **50 epochs**. The addition of the ASPP module increases model complexity slightly, and previous lessons indicate that truncating training (e.g., to 20 epochs) prevents models from reaching peak performance Cite solution_lesson_node_00006, solution_lesson_node_00011.

### Evaluation

*   **Inference:**
    *   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Predict on the original image and a horizontally flipped version, averaging the results. This provides a reliable boost to metric stability Cite solution_lesson_node_00002.
    *   **Thresholding:** Optimize the binarization threshold based on the validation set's mAP score rather than using a fixed 0.5 cutoff.
*   **Post-Processing:** Center crop predictions to 101x101 and convert to Run-Length Encoding (RLE).
