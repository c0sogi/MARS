The issue is that the `best_score` variable in the training loops is initialized to `0.0`. If the model achieves a validation mAP of exactly `0.0` (common in the first epoch with random weights or small subsets), the condition `if val_score > best_score` evaluates to `False` (`0.0 > 0.0` is False). Consequently, the model checkpoint is never saved, causing the subsequent assertion `os.path.exists(teacher_save_path)` to fail. To fix this, `best_score` should be initialized to `-1.0` in both `fit_teacher` and `fit_student` to ensure the initial model state is saved even if the metric is zero.
