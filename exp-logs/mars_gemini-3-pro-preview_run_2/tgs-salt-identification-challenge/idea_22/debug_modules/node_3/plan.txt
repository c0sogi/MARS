The issue is that the training loops in `library/engine.py` initialize `best_score` to `0.0`. During the demonstration run on a small subset, the validation mAP for the teacher model was exactly `0.0`. Since the saving condition is strictly `if val_score > best_score`, the condition `0.0 > 0.0` evaluated to `False`, preventing the model checkpoint from being saved and causing the subsequent file existence assertion to fail. To fix this, I will initialize `best_score` to `-1.0` in both `fit_teacher` and `fit_student` functions, ensuring that even a score of `0.0` triggers the initial checkpoint save.
