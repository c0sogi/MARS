The `CUDA out of memory` error occurred because the `swinv2_large` model requires significantly more memory for intermediate activations (attention maps) than the `convnext_large` model, causing it to exceed the available ~16GB VRAM with a batch size of 16. To resolve this while preserving the optimization behavior, I will decouple the physical batch size from the effective batch size. I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` from 16 to 4 and proportionally increase the `GRAD_ACCUM_STEPS` from 2 to 8. This maintains the target effective batch size of 32 (4 * 8 = 32) ensuring the training dynamics remain unchanged while fitting the model into memory. Cite debug_lesson_2.
