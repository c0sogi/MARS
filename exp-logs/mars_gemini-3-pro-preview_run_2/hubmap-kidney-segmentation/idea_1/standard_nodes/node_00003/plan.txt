The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the `BATCH_SIZE` of 16, combined with the large `TILE_SIZE` of 1024 and the `FPNResNet18` architecture, requires more VRAM than the available ~16 GB (despite the 40 GB A100 specification in the description, the environment seems to be restricted or using a slice). The model attempted to allocate a 4 GB tensor during the upsampling phase, which failed as the GPU was already fully utilized.

To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 16 to 4. This will significantly decrease the memory required for storing intermediate activations during the forward and backward passes, allowing the model to train within the available hardware limits.
