The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU VRAM (approx. 16GB) is insufficient to handle a batch size of 16 with $1024 \times 1024$ images, particularly during the upsampling phase where activation maps become very large. To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 16 to 4, which will linearly reduce the memory footprint of the activations and fit within the hardware constraints.
