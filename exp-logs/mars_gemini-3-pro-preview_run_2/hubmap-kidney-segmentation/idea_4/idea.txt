Based on the lessons learned, I propose the following optimized strategy. This approach introduces **Multi-Task Learning (MTL)** to leverage the anatomical context without creating the brittle dependencies that caused previous failures.

### Model: Multi-Task FPN with ResNet-34
I propose a **Multi-Task Feature Pyramid Network (FPN)** with a **ResNet-34** backbone.

*   **Backbone:** We will use **ResNet-34** as the encoder. Heavier backbones like ResNeXt-50 have been proven to overfit and degrade performance on this small dataset **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Multi-Task Heads):** Instead of using anatomical masks as *inputs* (which caused the "Hard Dependency" failure in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**), we will use them as **auxiliary training targets**. The network will have two parallel segmentation heads sharing the same encoder and FPN neck:
    1.  **Primary Head:** Predicts the binary **Glomerulus Mask**.
    2.  **Auxiliary Head:** Predicts the binary **Anatomical Cortex Mask**.
    This "Soft Integration" forces the shared encoder to learn robust features that distinguish the renal cortex (where glomeruli reside) from the medulla and background, effectively acting as a regularizer. Crucially, because this is an output branch, the anatomical data is required *only during training*. At inference time, the auxiliary head is discarded, eliminating the risk of failure if test-set anatomical annotations are missing or imperfect.

### Data: Representative Sampling and Target Generation
*   **Target Generation:** We will preprocess the training data to generate two masks for every image: the standard Glomerulus mask and the Anatomical Cortex mask (derived from the anatomical structure JSONs).
*   **Representative Undersampling:** We will employ **Representative Undersampling** for the training tiles. We will retain 100% of tiles containing glomeruli and randomly sample exactly **20% of the background-only tiles**. This fixed-rate strategy outperforms strict 1:1 balancing by providing the model with sufficient "negative context" to suppress false positives in empty regions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply **Color Augmentation** (HueSaturationValue, RandomBrightnessContrast) in addition to geometric flips/rotations. This is essential for generalizing across the staining variations observed between different tissue preparation pipelines **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.

### Training: Stabilized Multi-Objective Optimization
*   **Loss Function:** We will use a weighted sum of losses: $L_{total} = L_{glom} + \lambda L_{cortex}$.
    *   $L_{glom}$ will be the **Hybrid BCEDiceLoss** (Binary Cross Entropy + Dice). This combination stabilizes gradients while optimizing the segmentation metric, preventing vanishing gradients on sparse targets **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   $L_{cortex}$ will be Binary Cross Entropy, as the cortex is a large, non-sparse region.
*   **Scheduler:** We will use a **Compressed Cosine Annealing Schedule** with a maximum of **20 epochs**. Accelerating the learning rate decay helps the model settle into a robust minimum and reduces the metric volatility observed with longer, flatter schedules **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
*   **Checkpointing Strategy:** We will implement a **Warmup Period** of 5 epochs where Early Stopping is disabled **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**. Crucially, we will **defer "Best Model" saving** until after this warmup period. This prevents the system from locking in under-converged weights from transient "lucky spikes" in validation metrics that often occur early in training **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.

### Evaluation: Primary-Head Inference
*   **Inference Process:** We will perform inference using a sliding window approach (1024x1024 tiles, 50% overlap) with Gaussian kernel smoothing to merge predictions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
*   **Decoupled Prediction:** We will strictly use the output from the **Primary Head (Glomerulus)** for the final submission. The Auxiliary Head is ignored. This ensures the solution remains robust and independent of any auxiliary metadata or masks during the testing phase **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
