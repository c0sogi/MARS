The `KeyError` encountered during execution is caused by a mismatch between the positional indices generated by Scikit-Learn's `KFold` (which are 0-based integers) and the non-contiguous, label-based index of the `learner_df` DataFrame (which retains original indices from subsampling). When `transform_learner` attempts to use `df.loc[val_idx]`, it fails because the positional indices do not exist as labels in the DataFrame. To resolve this, I will reset the index of `learner_df` to a sequential `RangeIndex` immediately before feature transformation in `process_data`, ensuring compatibility with `KFold`.
