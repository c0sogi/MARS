The training succeeded only because it loaded stale cached data containing the required columns. When the validation step ran on fresh data, it crashed with `KeyError: 'base_margin'`.

1.  **Revert `library/feature_engine.py`**: Restore the creation of `base_margin` and the calculation of `residual` in `FeatureEngineer.process` to align with the model's expectations.
