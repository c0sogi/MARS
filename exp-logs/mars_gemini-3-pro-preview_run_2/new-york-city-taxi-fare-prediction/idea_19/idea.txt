Based on the analysis of the provided lessons and the evolution of previous experiments, I propose a **Hierarchical Distributional Dual-Hygiene Gradient Boosting** strategy. This approach structurally synthesizes the **Multi-Scale Hierarchy** of Idea 15 (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00072}) with the **Variance-Awareness** of Idea 18 (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00071}), while strictly enforcing the **Dual-Hygiene** and **Safety** protocols that prevented the failures of Idea 16 and 17.

# Model
I will utilize the **XGBoost Regressor** as the backbone, leveraging its proven superiority for spatial tabular tasks Cite {solution_lesson_node_00030}.
*   **Structural Innovation (Hierarchical Distributional Priors):** Instead of providing only the "Expected Price" (Mean) or relying on a single spatial resolution, I will construct a **Pyramid of Distributional Moments**.
    *   **Moments:** For every route, the model will be fed the **Mean** (1st Moment), **Standard Deviation** (2nd Moment), and **Count** (Confidence).
        *   **Rationale:** Explicitly encoding the Standard Deviation allows the model to quantify "Price Risk." A route with high variance (e.g., traffic-prone airport runs) signals the model to look for temporal triggers (like Rush Hour) to predict heavy-tail outliers, whereas a low-variance route implies a fixed rate regardless of time. This addresses the "Heavy Tail" prediction problem safely Cite {solution_lesson_node_00018}.
    *   **Hierarchy:** These moments will be generated at three simultaneous resolutions: **Micro** (Geohash L7, ~150m), **Meso** (Geohash L6, ~1km), and **Macro** (Geohash L5, ~5km). This allows the tree to dynamically shift focus from fine-grained memorization to regional smoothing based on data density, preventing the "sparse data" failure of Idea 18 Cite {solution_lesson_node_00072}.
    *   **Integration:** These will be supplied as **Input Features** (Soft Integration), not Base Margins, to avoid the catastrophic error propagation seen in Idea 16 Cite {solution_lesson_node_00055}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00070}.

# Data
The data pipeline employs a **Dual-Hygiene Strategy** combined with **K-Fold Vectorized Subtraction** to maximize signal quality and prevent leakage.

1.  **Input Sanitization (Safety First):**
    *   Strictly clamp all pickup and dropoff coordinates in the **Training, Validation, and Test sets** to the NYC bounding box immediately upon loading. This mitigates the risk of linear extrapolation from corrupt GPS data, which destroyed Idea 16 Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00070}.

2.  **Dual-Hygiene Preprocessing (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00066}):**
    *   **Wisdom Set (Full 55M):** Apply **Strict Filtering** (Fare between $2.50-$200, Fare/Km < $10). This dataset is used *exclusively* to generate clean, robust statistical priors (Mean, Std, Count).
    *   **Learner Set (Subsample 5M):** Apply **Loose Filtering** (Fare > $2.50). Retain valid high-fare outliers (e.g., >$500) to ensure the model learns to predict the "Heavy Tail" required for the RMSE metric Cite {solution_lesson_node_00018}.

3.  **Feature Engineering (K-Fold Vectorized Subtraction):**
    *   **Global Aggregation:** Compute `Sum_Fare`, `Sum_FareSq`, and `Count` for all three Geohash levels (L5, L6, L7) using the **Wisdom Set**.
    *   **Training Encoding:** For the Learner Set, use **K-Fold Vectorized Subtraction** (Global Stats - Fold Stats) to derive the Hierarchical Moments.
        *   *Correction:* Unlike Idea 18 which failed due to LOO leakage, this uses **K-Fold** separation. The statistics for Fold $k$ are derived strictly from the other $K-1$ folds, ensuring no target leakage Cite {solution_lesson_node_00071}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00045}.
    *   **Factorized Temporal Rate:** Include the `Mean_Fare_Per_Km` aggregated by `(Geohash5, Hour)` to capture time-of-day pricing dynamics orthogonal to the spatial route Cite {solution_lesson_node_00065}.
    *   **Inductive Bias:** Retain explicit geometric features (Haversine, Manhattan, Rotated Coordinates) Cite {solution_lesson_node_00046}.

# Training
1.  **Objective Function:** Use **`reg:squarederror`** (Standard L2 Loss).
    *   **Rationale:** We are training on a stable subsample with robust, clean Hierarchical Priors. The optimization landscape is stable enough to use L2 loss, which aligns perfectly with the RMSE metric. We avoid robust losses (Huber) as they fail to prioritize the high-fare outliers explicitly retained in the Learner target Cite {solution_lesson_node_00037}.
2.  **Target:** Train on the **raw `fare_amount`** (Loose Filtered). Do not log-transform, as this biases the RMSE Cite {solution_lesson_node_00025}.

    *   Map coordinates to Geohashes (L5, L6, L7).
    *   Retrieve `Mean`, `StdDev`, and `Count` priors from the **Global Wisdom Aggregates** (no subtraction needed).
2.  **Prediction:** Generate predictions using the XGBoost model.
