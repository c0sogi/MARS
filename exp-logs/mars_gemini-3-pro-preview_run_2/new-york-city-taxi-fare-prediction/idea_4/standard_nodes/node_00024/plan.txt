We also clean the validation set to prevent subsequent failures during the prediction/transform phase, while leaving the test set untouched (or assuming it's clean/handled separately) to preserve submission integrity. This approach aligns with **Cite debug_lesson_7** by distinguishing between necessary feature cleaning (validity) and target-based filtering (bias).
