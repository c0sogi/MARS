The `HotelDataset` class expects all validation classes to exist in the `class_to_idx` mapping generated from the training data, leading to a `ValueError` when an unknown ID is encountered. To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to select samples from the most frequent classes for the training subset and then strictly filter the validation subset to only include hotel IDs that are present in that training subset.
