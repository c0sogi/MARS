The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurred because the batch size of 24, configured for an expected A100 (40GB) GPU, exceeded the capacity of the actual 16GB GPU provided in the environment, specifically when the backbone was unfrozen and gradients were computed (Cite debug_lesson_3). To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to 8 in both `library/config.py` and `runfile.py`, ensuring the training process fits within the available VRAM.
