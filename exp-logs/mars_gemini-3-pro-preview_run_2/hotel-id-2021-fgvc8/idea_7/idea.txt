# Idea: ConvNeXt-Small with Sub-Center ArcFace and Graph-Regularized Retrieval

### Model
To address the need for robust feature extraction without incurring the computational penalty that led to subsampling in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019), I propose a structural shift to a **ConvNeXt** architecture.
*   **Backbone**: Use **ConvNeXt-Small** initialized with ImageNet weights. ConvNeXt integrates Vision Transformer design principles (isotropic stages, large kernel sizes, LayerNorm) into a CNN framework. This structure is empirically superior to standard CNNs (ResNet/EfficientNet) for capturing the global context and texture patterns vital for hotel identification, while being computationally light enough to train on the **full dataset** within the time limit.
*   **Pooling**: Employ **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** to spatially aggregate features, focusing on high-activation regions (salient objects) rather than background noise.
*   **Head**: Implement a **Sub-Center ArcFace Head** ($K=3$). This retains the critical ability to handle multi-modal classes (e.g., grouping "bathrooms" and "facades" under one ID) which single-center models fail to do (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Data
*   **Coverage**: We will utilize the **Full Training Dataset** (approx. 70k images). We explicitly reject data subsampling, as previous experiments demonstrated that model complexity cannot compensate for the loss of manifold density and tail-class examples (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Resolution**: Input images will be resized to **384 $\times$ 384** pixels to resolve fine-grained architectural details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Augmentation**: Apply a robust `albumentations` pipeline (HorizontalFlip, ColorJitter, ShiftScaleRotate, CoarseDropout) to enforce invariance to lighting and occlusion.

### Training
*   **Loss Function**: Use **Sub-Center ArcFace Loss**. Crucially, **Label Smoothing must be disabled**. The angular margin requires sharp, sparse gradient signals to compact clusters; smoothing dilutes this signal and prevents convergence in high-cardinality regimes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Duration**: Train for a minimum of **18 epochs**. Margin-based objectives require extended training phases to iteratively compact intra-class clusters. Short "fast baseline" runs result in underfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Optimizer**: Use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing scheduler to stabilize the optimization of the randomly initialized sub-centers.

### Evaluation
The inference pipeline utilizes the **Train-with-Proxy, Infer-with-Instances** paradigm (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015) but introduces **Graph-Based Regularization** to denoise the embedding space.
1.  **Gallery Construction**: Generate embeddings for the **entire training set** using the trained backbone (discarding the head).
2.  **Database Augmentation (DBA)**: Refine the Gallery embeddings. For each image in the Gallery, identify its $k$ nearest neighbors within the Gallery and replace its embedding with a weighted average of itself and these neighbors. This "smooths" the manifold, reducing the impact of outliers and noisy samples.
3.  **Query Expansion (QE)**: Refine the Test (Query) embeddings. For each test image, retrieve the top $k$ matches from the refined Gallery, average their embeddings with the query vector, and perform a second, final retrieval.
4.  **Prediction**: Return the top 5 `hotel_id`s based on the cosine similarity scores from the final retrieval step. This approach leverages the density of the fully trained manifold to correct individual prediction errors.
