The issue arises because the `UserPersonaTransformer` uses a hardcoded `min_df=2` in its `TfidfVectorizer`, which filters out terms appearing fewer than 2 times. In the small dummy dataset used in `runfile.py`, several rows contain terms that appear only once (e.g., "askreddit", "news"), resulting in all-zero vectors. When these zero vectors are normalized, they remain zero, causing the assertion `np.allclose(norms, 1.0)` to fail.

To fix this, I will modify `UserPersonaTransformer` to accept a `min_df` parameter (defaulting to 2 to preserve production behavior) and update the demonstration in `runfile.py` to use `min_df=1`. This ensures the transformer adapts correctly to the small size of the demonstration subset, preventing the generation of invalid zero-norm vectors (Cite debug_lesson_3).
