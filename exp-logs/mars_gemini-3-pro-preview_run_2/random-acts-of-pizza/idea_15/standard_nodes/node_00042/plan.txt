I will optimize the solution by reverting to a simpler, more robust architecture that avoids the pitfalls of dimensionality reduction on small datasets. Specifically, I will:

1.  **Replace the Text Encoder**: Switch from the 768-dimensional `all-mpnet-base-v2` to the 384-dimensional `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` to reduce the feature-to-sample ratio and mitigate the curse of dimensionality (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
2.  **Remove Partial Least Squares (PLS)**: Eliminate the PLS projection step. Instead, I will concatenate the dense, L2-normalized text embeddings directly with the metadata features. This avoids information loss associated with compressing embeddings into low-dimensional latent spaces on small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
3.  **Simplify Feature Fusion**: Use Early Fusion by concatenating raw embeddings with RankGauss-scaled metadata (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
4.  **Constrain Hyperparameters**: Cap the inverse regularization strength `C` in the Logistic Regression search grid to prevent the selection of under-regularized models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

This approach focuses on linear probing of the high-quality embedding space, which has proven more effective than complex non-linear projections in this data-scarce regime.
