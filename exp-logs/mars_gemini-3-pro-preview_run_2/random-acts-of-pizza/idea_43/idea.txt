Based on the comprehensive failure analysis and the synthesis of lessons learned—specifically the breakthrough success of **Multi-Field Embeddings** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077), the importance of **Global Context** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039), and the critical need to preserve the **"Hook" Signal** (Title) against signal dilution in global views—I propose the **Hook-Augmented Multi-Field Asymmetric Dual-Backbone Ensemble (HAMF-ADBE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Multi-Field" approach (Idea 39) by introducing a dedicated **Deep Semantic Hook View**. While Idea 39 successfully separated Title and Body for the anchor model (MiniLM), it relied on a single "Global" view for the auxiliary model (MPNet). In this Global view, the short, critical Title ("The Hook") is often overwhelmed by the longer Body ("The Narrative") during the self-attention mechanism. By explicitly extracting and compressing the MPNet embedding of the *Title alone*, we inject a dense, high-intelligence signal representing the "Deep Meaning of the Hook," allowing the linear ensemble to weight the request's core appeal independently of its narrative context.

# Model
The architecture is a **Bagged Ensemble of Logistic Regression Classifiers** operating on a fused, multi-resolution feature space designed to maximize signal clarity for distinct rhetorical components.

1.  **Field-Specific Anchors (High-Resolution):**
    *   **Title Anchor:** `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` applied to `request_title` (384d).
    *   **Body Anchor:** `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` applied to `request_text_edit_aware` (384d).
    *   **Rationale:** Retains the structural independence of the "Hook" and "Narrative" using the most sample-efficient backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077, Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

2.  **Contextual Auxiliaries (Low-Resolution):**
    *   **Global Context:** `sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2` applied to the *concatenation* of Title and Body (768d).
        *   **Transformation:** **PCA (50 components)**.
        *   **Rationale:** Captures holistic context and cross-field interactions (e.g., consistency between title and body) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
    *   **Deep Hook (The Innovation):** `sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2` applied to `request_title` *only* (768d).
        *   **Transformation:** **PCA (20 components)**.
        *   **Rationale:** Explicitly preserves the deep semantic nuances of the Title. In the Global view, the Title's tokens are a small fraction of the input; this view ensures the "Deep Hook" signal is available as a distinct, dense feature block, robust to being washed out by the Body (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046).

3.  **Classifier:** **BaggingClassifier** wrapping **Logistic Regression** (Ridge).
    *   **Rationale:** We preserve the high-bias linear core (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005) and use Bagging to reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

# Data
We construct five distinct feature views and fuse them via **Early Fusion** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020):

1.  **View 1: Title Anchor (384 dims)**
    *   **Processing:** Encode `request_title` with `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (Frozen) -> **L2 Normalization** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
2.  **View 2: Body Anchor (384 dims)**
    *   **Processing:** Encode `request_text_edit_aware` with `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (Frozen) -> **L2 Normalization**.
3.  **View 3: Global Context (50 dims)**
    *   **Processing:** Encode Combined Text with `all-mpnet-base-v2` (Frozen) -> **PCA (50)** -> **L2 Normalization**.
4.  **View 4: Deep Hook (20 dims)**
    *   **Processing:** Encode `request_title` with `all-mpnet-base-v2` (Frozen) -> **PCA (20)** -> **L2 Normalization**.
    *   **Rationale:** Normalization after PCA ensures this view aligns with the scale of the anchors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
5.  **View 5: Robust Metadata (~10 dims)**
    *   **Features:** All numerical metadata, **explicitly including** `unix_timestamp_of_request` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Scaling:** Apply **QuantileTransformer (output_distribution='normal')** (RankGauss) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

# Training
1.  **Pipeline Efficiency:**
    *   **Pre-computation:** Compute raw embeddings for all backbones (MiniLM-Title, MiniLM-Body, MPNet-Global, MPNet-Title) **outside** the cross-validation loop (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Cache Validation:** Implement strict row-count checks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

2.  **Stratified Cross-Validation:**
    *   Use 5-Fold Stratified CV.
    *   **Encapsulation:** The PCA (Views 3 & 4) and QuantileTransformer (View 5) must be fitted **inside** the cross-validation loop (on training folds only) to prevent statistical leakage (Cite solution_lesson_node_00047).

3.  **Context-Aware Hyperparameter Tuning:**
    *   Perform Grid Search within each fold.
    *   **Crucial:** Tune the hyperparameters (`C`, `class_weight`) of the **Full Bagged Ensemble**, not just the base learner (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Search Space:**
        *   `C`: High-Regularization Regime (`1e-4` to `10.0`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
        *   `class_weight`: `['balanced', None]` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
        *   `Penalty`: **L2 (Ridge)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).

4.  **Ensemble Strategy:**
    *   Train a Bagged Ensemble (`n_estimators=20`) on each fold using the best parameters.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Generate raw embeddings for the test set.
    *   For each of the 5 trained fold-pipelines:
        *   **Anchors:** L2 Normalize MiniLM Title/Body.
        *   **Auxiliaries:** Project Global/Title MPNet using fold's PCA models -> L2 Normalize.
        *   **Metadata:** Transform using fold's RankGauss.
        *   **Fusion:** Concatenate all views (~850 dims) and predict.
2.  **Prediction:**
    *   **Average the probability outputs** from all 5 fold-ensembles. This **CV-Bagging** strategy maximizes stability and generalization, ensuring the model is robust to fold-specific noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
