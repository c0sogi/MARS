The error arises because the demonstration script (`runfile.py`) aggressively subsamples the dataset (to 50 training samples total), resulting in training folds of only 35 samples during 2-fold cross-validation. However, the global configuration (`Config.PCA_GLOBAL_COMPONENTS`) remains at its production default of 50. Since PCA requires the number of components to be less than or equal to the number of samples, this causes a `ValueError` (Cite debug_lesson_8). To fix this, I will patch `Config.PCA_GLOBAL_COMPONENTS` and `Config.PCA_HOOK_COMPONENTS` in `runfile.py` to values smaller than the fold size (e.g., 10 and 5) specifically for the demonstration execution.
