I will implement a strategy focused on dimensionality reduction and feature scale alignment to improve generalization on this small dataset.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Revert to Compact Embeddings**: I will switch from the 768-dimensional `all-mpnet-base-v2` back to the 384-dimensional `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`. As noted in **Lesson 00017**, high-dimensional embeddings on small datasets (<2500 samples) can lead to the curse of dimensionality, where the model struggles to find robust decision boundaries.
2.  **Align Feature Scales**:
    - I will replace `QuantileTransformer` (RankGauss) with `RobustScaler` for numerical features. **Lesson 00006** indicates that `RobustScaler` effectively handles the extreme outliers in this dataset (e.g., karma scores) without distorting the distribution of the majority data, which is crucial for linear models.
    - To prevent regularization bias, I will normalize the text embeddings (L2) and then scale them by $\sqrt{D}$ (where $D=384$). This ensures that the variance of the embedding features ($\approx 1$) matches the variance of the `RobustScaler` output (IQR $\approx 1$), preventing the model from disproportionately penalizing the embedding signal (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
3.  **Fresh Workspace**: I will update the `WORKING_DIR` to `optimization_1` to ensure that cached features are regenerated with the new embedding model and scaling logic.
