My strategy is to revert the regression caused by fine-tuning and mismatched scaling in the previous solution, while retaining the benefits of high-dimensional embeddings.

1.  **Use Frozen Embeddings**: I will disable the fine-tuning of the Sentence Transformer. Fine-tuning on this small dataset (~2,300 samples) caused overfitting and manifold collapse (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014). I will use the pre-trained `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` model directly.
2.  **Align Feature Scales**: I will switch from `RobustScaler` back to `StandardScaler` for numerical features and enforce unit-normalization on the text embeddings. This ensures that the L2 regularization in the Logistic Regression treats both feature modalities (dense embeddings and tabular metadata) equitably (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
3.  **Explicit Signal Extraction**: I will add a simple `text_len_word` feature. Analysis showed successful requests tend to be longer, and explicit length features can provide a strong linear signal that might be diffused in high-dimensional embeddings.
4.  **Regularization**: I will maintain the aggressive regularization grid (including `1e-4`, `1e-3`) to handle the high dimensionality of the combined feature space (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
