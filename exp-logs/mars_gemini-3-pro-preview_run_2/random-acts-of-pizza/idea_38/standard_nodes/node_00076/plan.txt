The issue is that the `Trainer.train` method merges the training and validation splits into a single dataset before performing cross-validation. This causes data leakage because the models are trained on samples that are later used for the final hold-out evaluation in `runfile.py`. To fix this, I will modify `library/trainer.py` to use only the `df_train_split` (and its corresponding embeddings) for the cross-validation loop, ensuring the validation set remains completely unseen during training. This strictly enforces the separation between training and hold-out validation sets (Cite debug_lesson_9).
