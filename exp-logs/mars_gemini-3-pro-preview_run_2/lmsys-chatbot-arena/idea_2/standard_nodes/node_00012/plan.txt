To optimize the solution, I will upgrade the model backbone from `microsoft/deberta-v3-xsmall` (22M params) to `microsoft/deberta-v3-small` (44M params). This increases the model's capacity to capture semantic nuances in the text while remaining computationally efficient enough to train within the time limit. This builds upon the proven Siamese Dual-Encoder architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) and Mean Pooling strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008) established in previous iterations.

I will also increase the batch sizes in `runfile.py` to 16 (train) and 32 (validation) to better utilize the 40GB NVIDIA A100 GPU, ensuring that the training process remains fast despite the larger model size. The training duration is kept at 2 epochs to avoid the overfitting observed in longer runs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
