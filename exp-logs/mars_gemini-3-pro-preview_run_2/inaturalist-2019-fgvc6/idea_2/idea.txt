Based on the lessons learned from the previous experiments, I propose a **High-Fidelity Compound Scaling** strategy. This approach shifts from rapid prototyping with lightweight models to maximizing performance using a higher-capacity architecture that aligns network depth, width, and input resolution.

### Model
I propose upgrading the architecture from EfficientNet-B0 to **EfficientNet-B4**.
*   **Rationale**: The previous experiments with EfficientNet-B0 showed that the model continued to improve with longer training and more data, suggesting it had not yet overfitted or reached the limit of the dataset's complexity. EfficientNet-B4 offers significantly higher parameter capacity (~19M vs ~5.3M), allowing it to capture the subtle, fine-grained features required to distinguish between the 1,010 species.
*   **Compound Scaling**: Unlike previous attempts that fixed resolution at 224x224, this solution will respect the EfficientNet compound scaling principles by increasing the input resolution to **380x380** (the native resolution for B4). This higher resolution is critical for fine-grained classification, as it preserves small visual cues (e.g., insect wing patterns or plant textures) that are lost at lower resolutions.

### Data
*   **Dataset**: We will utilize the **Full Training Dataset** (~186,000 images) without downsampling. As established in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**, preserving the natural frequency distribution of the "head" classes is essential for maximizing global accuracy, and aggressive downsampling degrades performance.
*   **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Training**: Images will be resized and randomly cropped to **380x380** pixels. We will apply standard augmentations (RandomHorizontalFlip) and **Label Smoothing** (e.g., 0.1) to the target labels. Label smoothing acts as a regularizer, preventing the larger model from becoming overconfident on noisy examples or ambiguous species, which is common in fine-grained datasets.
    *   **Evaluation**: Images will be resized such that the shorter side is 380 pixels (or slightly larger, e.g., 448), followed by a CenterCrop to **380x380**.

### Training
*   **Procedure**: We will train the model for **20 epochs**. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005** demonstrated that the model was still converging at epoch 12 and that extending the training duration (and thus the scheduler's decay profile) yields better generalization.
*   **Loss Function**: We will use **Cross Entropy Loss with Label Smoothing**.
*   **Optimization**: We will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a Cosine Annealing learning rate scheduler.
*   **Compute Optimization**: To handle the increased computational load of the B4 model and 380x380 resolution within the 24-hour limit, we will utilize **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** on the NVIDIA A100 GPU.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference**: Predictions will be generated using a single forward pass on the center-cropped (380x380) test images.
*   **Submission**: The top predicted category will be selected based on the highest softmax probability.
