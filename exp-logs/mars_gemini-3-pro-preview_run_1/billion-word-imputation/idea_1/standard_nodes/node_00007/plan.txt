The issue is caused by **Output Tensor Explosion** (Cite {debug_lesson_1}), where the memory required for the model's output logits and gradients `(Batch_Size, Sequence_Length, Vocab_Size)` exceeds the A100's capacity. With `Batch=256` and `Vocab=60,000`, even moderate sequence lengths consume excessive VRAM during the backward pass.

To fix this, I will:
1.  **Reduce Batch Size**: In `runfile.py`, lower `Config.BATCH_SIZE` from 256 to 64 to directly reduce the tensor dimensions.
2.  **Truncate Sequences**: In `library/dataset.py`, enforce `Config.MAX_SEQ_LEN` for training data. This prevents outlier long sentences from causing memory spikes, as the `collate_fn` pads the entire batch to the length of the longest sequence.
