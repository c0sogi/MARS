The `ValueError` in `runfile.py` stems from a shape mismatch between the ground truth `target` tensor `(Batch_Size, 1)` and the model's `pred_fvc` output `(Batch_Size,)`. When subtracted, PyTorch silently broadcasts these to a `(Batch_Size, Batch_Size)` matrix, calculating the difference between every prediction and every target in the batch. This results in the `errors` list containing arrays of inconsistent lengths (due to the smaller final batch), causing `np.array(errors)` to crash. This also implies the training loss was computing an invalid cross-product average.

To fix this, I will explicitly flatten the `target` tensor to shape `(Batch_Size,)` using `.view(-1)` in both the training/evaluation loops (`library/engine.py`) and the failure analysis loop (`runfile.py`). This ensures element-wise operations are performed correctly, resolving the crash and the logical error in training (Cite debug_lesson_9).
