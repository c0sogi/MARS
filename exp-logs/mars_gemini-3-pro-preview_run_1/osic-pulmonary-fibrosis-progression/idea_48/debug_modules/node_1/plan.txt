The `AssertionError` is caused by an incorrect dimension expansion of the `weeks` tensor, which leads to unintended broadcasting during the trajectory calculation. The `LungDataset` returns `weeks` as a tensor of shape `(1,)`, which the `DataLoader` collates into a batch of shape `(B, 1)`. The code in `library/train.py` and `runfile.py` redundantly applies `.unsqueeze(1)`, changing the shape to `(B, 1, 1)`. When subtracted from `base_week` (shape `(B, 1)`), this triggers broadcasting that results in a tensor of shape `(B, B, 1)` and a final output of `(B, 2*B, 1)` (e.g., `(4, 8, 1)`), violating the expected shape `(B, 2)`. I will remove the `.unsqueeze(1)` calls to maintain the correct rank-2 tensor shape `(B, 1)`, ensuring element-wise operations as per **Cite debug_lesson_12**.
