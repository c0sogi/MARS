The issue is that the `TabularPreprocessor` (specifically its internal `OneHotEncoder`) is being fitted on the truncated "debug" dataset (20 samples) rather than the full training dataset. This small subset fails to capture all unique categories for the `SmokingStatus` feature, resulting in fewer one-hot encoded columns (6 features instead of the expected 7) and causing a dimension mismatch in the model.

To fix this, I will reorder the operations in `get_dataloaders` within `library/data.py`. I will ensure that `prepare_training_data` and `preprocessor.fit()` are executed on the **full** dataframes *before* applying the debug slicing. This guarantees that the encoder learns the complete schema and generates consistent feature vectors regardless of the subset size used for training (Cite debug_lesson_1).
