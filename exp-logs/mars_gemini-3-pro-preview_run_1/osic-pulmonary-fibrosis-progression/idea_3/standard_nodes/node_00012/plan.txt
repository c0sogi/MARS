In the current implementation, if the prediction error exceeds 1000ml, the gradient with respect to the FVC prediction becomes zero (due to the `min` operation), causing the model to stop learning for difficult samples ("Gradient Starvation"). 2.  **Regularization via Dropout**: I will add `Dropout` layers (p=0.2) to the tabular MLP and the feature fusion head of the `DualAxisTriSlabModel`. This addresses the rapid overfitting seen in **Lesson 8**, where validation performance peaked early and then degraded.
3.  **Extended Training**: I will increase the training duration from 12 to 20 epochs to allow the regularized model sufficient time to converge.
