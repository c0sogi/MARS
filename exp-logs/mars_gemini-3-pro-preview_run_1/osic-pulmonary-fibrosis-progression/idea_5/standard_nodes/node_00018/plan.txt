To improve the model's performance, I will implement a **Tabular Skip Connection** in the `AttentionFusedDualAxisNet` architecture. While the current solution fuses image and tabular features using self-attention (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017), the high dimensionality of the image embeddings (1280) can potentially dilute the signal from the low-dimensional but highly predictive tabular features (like `Percent` and `Age`). By injecting the tabular embedding directly into the final prediction head (concatenating it with the attention-fused context vector), I ensure the model retains direct access to these strong clinical priors. Additionally, I will increase the optimizer's `weight_decay` to `0.05` to provide stronger regularization, which is critical for preventing overfitting on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
