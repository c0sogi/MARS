Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous successes (e.g., **Dual-Query Attention**, **Global Consistency**, **Top-K Indicators**, **Gated Fusion**) and specific failures (e.g., **Semantic Averaging**, **Signal Dilution via Aggregation**, **Batch Normalization Instability**), I propose a **Hybrid Ensemble with Dual-View Consistency Trees and Community-Aware Gated MLP**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Global-Consistency Augmented" approach (Idea 35) and the "Dual-Query MLP" (Idea 27/29). The core innovation addresses the **Information Gap in the Credibility Gate**. Previous gating mechanisms relied solely on generic numerical metadata (e.g., account age, karma) to determine user trustworthiness. However, credibility is often context-specific (e.g., participation in "Verified Giver" communities like `r/Loans`). I propose injecting **Top-K Community Indicators** directly into the MLP's gating branch, allowing the model to learn community-specific trust biases. Simultaneously, I refine the Random Forest by projecting the **Dual-Query** insight (Title vs. Body) into dense **Global Consistency Scalars**, ensuring the tree model benefits from the same structural decomposition as the neural network.

# Model

The solution employs an ensemble of two distinct, highly optimized learners fused via robust averaging.

**1. Stream A: Dual-View Consistency Random Forest**
*   **Goal**: Equip the Random Forest with a robust, multi-view representation of user history that captures "Global Persona Consistency" across both Topic (Title) and Narrative (Body) dimensions, alongside specific high-signal community indicators.
*   **Architecture**: A Random Forest Classifier with balanced class weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Input**:
    *   **High-Fidelity TF-IDF**: Concatenated Title+Body TF-IDF with continuous frequency weights and a large vocabulary (e.g., 5,000) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Full-Spectrum Metadata**: **Raw Magnitudes** (e.g., account age) + **Engineered Ratios** (e.g., upvote ratio) + **Text Meta-Features** (e.g., caps ratio) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Top-K Binary Community Indicators**: Binary flags (0/1) for the top $K=50$ most frequent subreddits. This provides specific, high-signal splits for common communities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
    *   **Dual-View Global Consistency (Structural Innovation)**:
        *   Calculate **Topic Consistency**: Cosine Similarity between *Request Title* and *User History Centroid* (Mean of all past subreddit embeddings).
        *   Calculate **Narrative Consistency**: Cosine Similarity between *Request Body* and *User History Centroid*.
        *   **Rationale**: This projects the "Dual-Query" insight (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059) into the tree model using the robust "Global Consistency" metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066), allowing the tree to distinguish users who align with their history topically vs. stylistically.
    *   **Sentiment Scores**: VADER polarity and subjectivity scores.
*   **Configuration**: **Low Regularization** (`min_samples_leaf=1`) to preserve fine-grained sparse signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).

**2. Stream B: Community-Aware Dual-Query MLP**
*   **Goal**: Enhance the "Credibility Gate" by providing it with explicit community membership signals, stabilized by a Dropout-Only regime.
*   **Architecture**: A neural network with multiple branches and a Gated Fusion mechanism.
    *   **Branch 1 (Title Semantics)**: Processes **Raw SBERT embedding** of Title (No early projection) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051).
    *   **Branch 2 (Body Semantics)**: Processes **Raw SBERT embedding** of Body.
    *   **Branch 3 (Dual-Query History Attention)**:
        *   **Input**: Sequence of **Raw SBERT embeddings** for user history.
        *   **Head A (Topic Context)**: **Dot-Product Attention** (Query=Title, Key=History) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059).
        *   **Head B (Narrative Context)**: **Dot-Product Attention** (Query=Body, Key=History).
        *   **Masking**: Apply **Explicit Additive Masking** ($-\infty$ to padding) before Softmax (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).
    *   **Alignment Injection**: Concatenate scalar similarities ($S_{topic}$, $S_{narrative}$) between Queries and Attended Contexts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00061).
    *   **Branch 4 (Community-Aware Metadata - Structural Innovation)**:
        *   **Input**: Concatenation of **Numerical Metadata** (Arcsinh-transformed) AND **Top-K Binary Community Indicators** (50 flags).
        *   **Rationale**: By feeding community flags into the metadata branch, the **Credibility Gate** can learn to open or close based on specific community memberships (e.g., "Trust users from r/Borrow"), augmenting the generic signals from account age/karma (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Gated Fusion**: Branch 4 generates a sigmoid gate modulating the concatenated semantic vector.
    *   **Regularization**: **Dropout Only**. Remove Batch Normalization to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062).

# Data

1.  **Leakage Prevention**: Strictly apply `get_feature_intersection` to restrict inputs to columns present in both train and test sets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **Semantic Feature Engineering**:
    *   Compute SBERT embeddings for Title, Body, and all User Subreddits.
    *   **Centroid**: Mean of user subreddit embeddings.
    *   **Consistency Scalars**: Dot-product between Title/Body embeddings and History Centroid.
3.  **Top-K Profiling**:
    *   Identify top 50 subreddits by frequency in `train`.
    *   Generate binary flags for train and test.
4.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Stream A (RF)**: Impute NaNs with median.
    *   **Stream B (MLP)**: Apply **Arcsinh Transformation** followed by **StandardScaler** to numerical metadata (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025). Keep Top-K flags as binary (0/1).

# Training

1.  **Random Forest**: Trained with 500 estimators, balanced class weights, and `min_samples_leaf=1` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).
2.  **MLP**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with weight decay.
    *   **Regularization**: Dropout (e.g., 0.5 on embeddings, 0.2 on dense).
    *   **Protocol**: **High Patience** (e.g., 15 epochs) and sufficient total epochs (e.g., 50) to allow the Dual-Attention and Gating layers to stabilize (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **Independence**: Train both models independently on the full training set.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test data through dual pipelines (Dual-Consistency/Top-K/TF-IDF for RF, Sequence-History/Top-K-Metadata/SBERT for MLP).
2.  **Ensemble**: Calculate final probability as **Simple Weighted Average** (0.5/0.5) of the two models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
