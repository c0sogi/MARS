The issue is caused by the `DualQueryAttention` mechanism receiving all-zero attention masks for users with no subreddit history. When the mask is entirely zero (indicating all padding), the attention scores are set to negative infinity, leading to a division by zero in the Softmax calculation ($e^{-\infty}/\sum e^{-\infty} = 0/0$), which produces `NaN` values that propagate through the model.
