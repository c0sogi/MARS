To improve the model's performance, I will implement a strategy focusing on enhanced feature engineering, better text representation, and a stronger classifier, while strictly adhering to leakage prevention principles.

1.  **Feature Engineering**: I will extract temporal features (`request_hour`, `request_day`) from the Unix timestamp and content features (`text_len_words`) from the request text. These derived features provide dense signals that tree-based models can easily exploit.
2.  **Text Representation**: I will replace the Bag-of-Words approach with **TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency)** including bigrams. This reduces the impact of frequent but uninformative words and captures local context better than simple counts.
3.  **Model Upgrade**: I will replace the Random Forest classifier with **XGBoost**. XGBoost generally yields higher AUC on tabular data due to its gradient boosting framework and better handling of class imbalance via `scale_pos_weight`.
4.  **Leakage Prevention**: I will ensure all new features are generated consistently for both train and test sets before the intersection check, maintaining the robustness described in the lesson (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
