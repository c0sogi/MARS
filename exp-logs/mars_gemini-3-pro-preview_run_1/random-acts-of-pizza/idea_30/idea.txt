Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous successes (e.g., **Dual-Query Attention**, **Alignment Injection**, **Gated Fusion**) and specific failures (e.g., **Orthogonal Scoping/Removing History from Trees**, **Aggregated Target Encoding**, **Regularization Sensitivity**), I propose a **Hybrid Ensemble with Alignment-Augmented Top-K Trees and Dual-Query Alignment-Injected MLP**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Alignment-Injected MLP" (Idea 29/Lesson 00061) and the "Low-Bias Top-K Random Forest" (Idea 27). It directly addresses the failure in Lesson 00061—where the Random Forest collapsed because it was deprived of history features—by restoring **Top-K Community Indicators** to the tree model. Furthermore, it unifies the feature engineering strategy by injecting the **Scalar Alignment Score** (proven effective for the MLP) into the Random Forest as well, providing the tree with a dense, high-level summary of "Request-History Consistency" that it cannot easily infer from raw features.

# Model

The solution employs an ensemble of two distinct, highly optimized learners fused via robust averaging.

**1. Stream A: Alignment-Augmented Top-K Random Forest**
*   **Goal**: Equip the Random Forest with a robust, multi-view representation of user history that combines specific community signals (Top-K) with holistic consistency signals (Alignment), avoiding the noise of full sparsity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045) or the signal loss of aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
*   **Architecture**: A Random Forest Classifier with balanced class weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Input**:
    *   **High-Fidelity TF-IDF**: Concatenated Title+Body TF-IDF with continuous frequency weights and a large vocabulary (e.g., 5,000) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Full-Spectrum Metadata**: **Raw Magnitudes** (e.g., account age) + **Engineered Ratios** (e.g., upvote ratio) + **Text Meta-Features** (e.g., caps ratio) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021, solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Sentiment Scores**: VADER sentiment and subjectivity scores to capture emotional tone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   **Top-K Binary Community Indicators (Restored)**:
        *   Binary flags (0/1) for the top $K=50$ most frequent subreddits. This restores the high-signal specific indicators that were fatal to remove in Lesson 00061.
    *   **Semantic Alignment Scalar (Structural Innovation)**:
        *   The **Cosine Similarity** between the *Request SBERT Embedding* and the *User History Centroid*.
        *   **Rationale**: Lesson 00061 proved this scalar is highly predictive. Feeding it to the RF gives the tree a dense, interpretable feature representing "How consistent is this request with the user's past behavior?" without requiring the tree to process embeddings.

**2. Stream B: Dual-Query Alignment-Injected MLP**
*   **Goal**: Maximize the extraction of relevant historical context by querying history separately against the *Topic* and *Narrative*, and explicitly quantifying the quality of those matches.
*   **Architecture**: A neural network with multiple branches and a Gated Fusion mechanism.
    *   **Branch 1 (Title Semantics)**: Processes the **Raw SBERT embedding** of the Request Title (No early projection) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051).
    *   **Branch 2 (Body Semantics)**: Processes the **Raw SBERT embedding** of the Request Body.
    *   **Branch 3 (Dual-Query History Attention)**:
        *   **Input**: Sequence of **Raw SBERT embeddings** for the user's subreddit history.
        *   **Head A (Topic Context)**: **Dot-Product Attention** (Query=Title, Key=History). Output: $C_{topic}$.
        *   **Head B (Narrative Context)**: **Dot-Product Attention** (Query=Body, Key=History). Output: $C_{narrative}$.
        *   **Masking**: Apply **Explicit Additive Masking** ($-\infty$ to padding) before Softmax (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).
    *   **Alignment Injection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00061)**:
        *   Compute scalars: $S_{topic} = \text{CosSim}(\text{Title}, C_{topic})$ and $S_{narrative} = \text{CosSim}(\text{Body}, C_{narrative})$.
        *   Concatenate these scalars to the fusion layer to explicitly gate the context quality.
    *   **Branch 4 (Metadata)**: Processes "Full-Spectrum" metadata via **Arcsinh Transformation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Gated Fusion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028)**:
        *   Concatenate [Title, Body, $C_{topic}$, $C_{narrative}$, $S_{topic}$, $S_{narrative}$].
        *   Generate a sigmoid **Credibility Gate** from Branch 4.
        *   Modulate the concatenated semantic vector by this gate.

# Data

1.  **Leakage Prevention**: Strictly apply `get_feature_intersection` to restrict inputs to columns present in both train and test sets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **Top-K Profiling (Pre-processing)**:
    *   Identify top 50 subreddits in `train`.
    *   Generate binary columns for both train and test.
3.  **Alignment Calculation**:
    *   Compute SBERT embeddings for Request (Title+Body) and User History (Mean of Subreddits).
    *   Calculate Cosine Similarity for every sample to generate the `alignment_score` feature for RF.
4.  **Feature Engineering**:
    *   **Sentiment**: VADER polarity/subjectivity scores.
    *   **Magnitudes & Ratios**: Preserve raw counts and generate normalized rates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Dynamic Appending**: Programmatically add all new features to the active list (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
5.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Stream A (RF)**: Impute NaNs with median.
    *   **Stream B (MLP)**: Apply **Arcsinh Transformation** followed by **StandardScaler** to all numerical metadata (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

# Training

1.  **Random Forest**: Trained with 500 estimators, balanced class weights, and **minimal regularization** (`min_samples_leaf=1`) to preserve the sparse Top-K signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).
2.  **MLP**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with weight decay.
    *   **Regularization**: **Dropout** on SBERT embeddings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
    *   **Protocol**: Use **High Patience** (e.g., 15 epochs) and sufficient total epochs (e.g., 50) to allow the Dual-Attention and Gating layers to stabilize (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **Independence**: Train both models independently on the full training set.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test data through the dual pipelines (Top-K/Alignment/TF-IDF for RF, Sequence-History/SBERT for MLP).
2.  **Ensemble**: Calculate the final probability as the **Simple Weighted Average** (0.5/0.5) of the two models. This ensures robustness and prevents the collapse associated with biased weighting or stacking on small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020, solution_lesson_node_00018).
