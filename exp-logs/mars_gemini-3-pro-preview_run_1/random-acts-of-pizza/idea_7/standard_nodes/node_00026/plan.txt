Based on the failure analysis of the previous solution and the lessons learned (specifically **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00024** and **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00025**), the primary optimization strategy is to replace the `StandardScaler` in the MLP preprocessing pipeline with a Log-Modulus transformation (`np.arcsinh`). The previous attempt to use Standard Scaling on sparse and heavy-tailed social data led to gradient explosion and model collapse. `np.arcsinh` will compress the feature magnitudes logarithmically while preserving the sparsity (zeros remain zeros) and avoiding the extreme value projection caused by centering sparse data.
