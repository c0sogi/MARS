The `InvalidSubmissionError` indicating a length mismatch between the submission and the answers is caused by the `TaggerDataset` truncating sentences that exceed `Config.MAX_SEQ_LEN` (currently 128). Since the EDA revealed sentences up to 233 tokens long, the pipeline silently drops tokens at the end of these long sentences during inference, resulting in missing rows in the submission file (Cite debug_lesson_2). To fix this, I will update `library/config.py` to increase `MAX_SEQ_LEN` to 300 to accommodate the longest sentences and reduce `BATCH_SIZE` to 64 to ensure GPU memory stability with the larger tensors.
