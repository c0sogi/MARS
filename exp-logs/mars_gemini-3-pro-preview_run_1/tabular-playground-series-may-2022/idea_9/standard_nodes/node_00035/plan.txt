The `BCEWithLogitsLoss` class in PyTorch does not accept a `label_smoothing` argument in its constructor, unlike `CrossEntropyLoss`. This mismatch caused the `TypeError` observed in the traceback. To resolve this, I will remove the argument from the initialization and implement label smoothing manually on the target tensor before passing it to the loss function. This aligns with `debug_lesson_2` by ensuring the code strictly adheres to the framework's expected interface.
