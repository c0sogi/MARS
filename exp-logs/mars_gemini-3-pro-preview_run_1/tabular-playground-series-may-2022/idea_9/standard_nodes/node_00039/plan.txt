Additionally, `library/data_factory.py` contains legacy logic for swap noise that references the soon-to-be-removed `SWAP_PROB`.

To fix this, I will:
1.  Update `library/config.py` to replace `SWAP_PROB` with `MASK_PROB` (Cite debug_lesson_1).
2.  Update `library/data_factory.py` to remove the obsolete swap noise logic, ensuring it doesn't reference the removed `SWAP_PROB` attribute and aligns with the new GPU-based masking strategy.
