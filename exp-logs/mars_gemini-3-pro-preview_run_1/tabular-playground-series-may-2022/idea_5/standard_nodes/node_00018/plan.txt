2.  **Capacity Scaling (Cite Lesson 8)**: To accommodate the additional feature and prevent the explicit signal from dominating the learned embeddings, I will increase the `EMBED_DIM` from 128 to 192.
3.  **Regularization**: The previous execution logs showed validation loss increasing in the final epochs (0.085 -> 0.092) while AUC plateaued, indicating overfitting. I will increase `WEIGHT_DECAY` (to `5e-2`) and `DROPOUT` (to `0.2`) to combat this.
4.  **Training Schedule**: I will increase `EPOCHS` to 35 to allow the model to converge properly under the increased regularization.
