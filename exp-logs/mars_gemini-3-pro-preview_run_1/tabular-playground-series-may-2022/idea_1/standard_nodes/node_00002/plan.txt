To improve the model's performance, I will transition from a linear baseline to a Gradient Boosting model (LightGBM). The previous failure analysis showed that prediction errors were uncorrelated with input features, indicating that the linear model had fully exploited the main effects and that the remaining error was due to unmodeled non-linear interactions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). LightGBM is well-suited to capture these interactions.

I will also optimize the feature engineering for the tree-based model by switching the encoding of the `f_27` character sequence from One-Hot Encoding (which creates high dimensionality) to Ordinal Encoding. Finally, I will train on the full dataset and enforce the submission threshold requirement.
