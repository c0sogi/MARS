I will optimize the solution by implementing a **Hybrid Transformer with Global Average Pooling (GAP) and Positional Encodings**. This addresses the shortcomings of the previous "Flattening" approach which failed to generalize, while reintroducing the critical positional information lost when pooling.

1.  **Reintroduce Positional Encodings**: I will add a learnable positional embedding to the sequence input. Since the Transformer is permutation-invariant, and Global Average Pooling aggregates across time, explicit positional information is necessary for the model to distinguish sequence patterns (e.g., "A at pos 1" vs "A at pos 5"). Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`.
2.  **Switch to Global Average Pooling (GAP)**: I will replace the `torch.flatten` operation with `torch.mean`. Flattening the transformer output resulted in a high-dimensional input to the MLP, leading to overfitting. GAP acts as a regularizer, forcing the Transformer to compress semantic information into a fixed-size vector. Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`.
3.  **Optimize Architecture Depth & Capacity**: I will reduce the Transformer depth from 4 layers to 2 layers and resize the MLP to `[512, 256, 128]`. This matches the capacity to the reduced dimensionality of the pooled output, preventing overfitting on the short sequence length (10). Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`.
4.  **Extend Training Schedule**: I will increase the training epochs to 22. This stretches the OneCycleLR schedule, allowing for a more gradual learning rate decay and better convergence into the loss minimum. Cite `solution_lesson_node_00013`.
