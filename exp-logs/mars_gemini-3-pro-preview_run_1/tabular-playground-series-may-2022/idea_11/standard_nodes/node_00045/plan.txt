I will re-introduce the numerical reconstruction loss (MSE) to the multi-task objective. 2.  **Embedding-Level Masking:** Instead of masking numerical features by replacing input values with zero (or mean), I will implement masking in the embedding space using a learnable `[MASK]` token. This prevents the model from conflating "missing" data with "zero-valued" data and provides a clearer signal to the attention mechanism, as recommended by **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**.
3.  **Optimize Training Efficiency:** To accommodate the additional computational overhead of the numerical reconstruction head within the time limit, I will increase the `BATCH_SIZE` from 512 to 1024.
