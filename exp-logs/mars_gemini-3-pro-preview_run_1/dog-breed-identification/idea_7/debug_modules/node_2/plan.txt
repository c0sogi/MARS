The issue stems from the use of an unrepresentative training subset (16 images, 14 breeds) to generate the `label_map`. The validation subset contains breeds not present in this small training subset. When `load_data` processes the validation images, it fails to look up the breed in the incomplete `label_map`, causing a `KeyError` which is caught, resulting in the image being skipped. This leads to an empty validation dataset and the subsequent `AssertionError`.

To fix this, we will modify `runfile.py` to generate a complete `label_map` from the original full metadata (which is already loaded as `train_meta_orig`). We will then bypass `get_datasets` and manually call `load_data` for each subset, explicitly passing this complete `label_map`. This ensures all breeds are recognized during data loading (Cite debug_lesson_4).
