The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU memory (approximately 16GB available) is insufficient to accommodate the memory footprint of the `convnext_base` model with a `batch_size` of 64 during the fine-tuning phase, where gradients for the entire backbone must be stored. To resolve this, I will reduce the `batch_size` in `library/config.py` from 64 to 16. This reduction will decrease the memory required for intermediate activations by approximately 4x, allowing the training to proceed within the hardware constraints.
