The `RuntimeError` occurs in the `evaluate` method because `valid_loss` is a 1D tensor containing all individual loss values for the batch (after masking), not a scalar mean. The `.item()` method cannot be called on a multi-element tensor. In the training loop, `.mean()` is called first for backpropagation, making `.item()` valid, but the evaluation loop skips this reduction. To fix this, we should sum the elements of `valid_loss` directly to accumulate the total loss.
