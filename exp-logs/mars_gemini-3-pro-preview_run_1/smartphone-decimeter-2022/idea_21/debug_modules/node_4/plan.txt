To fix the `AssertionError: Processed training dataframe is empty!`, I will modify `library/data_processing.py`. The error is caused by two issues: timestamp misalignment preventing merges, and indiscriminate zero-filling of missing WLS coordinates causing invalid targets.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Normalize Timestamps:** In `process_dataset`, I will round timestamps to the nearest second (`UnixTimeMillis_rounded`) for both GNSS features and Ground Truth/Metadata before merging. This ensures 1Hz alignment (Cite `debug_lesson_4`).
2.  **Robust Aggregation:** In `process_gnss_log`, I will change the aggregation of WLS coordinates from `.first()` to `.mean()` to handle rows with missing data within the same epoch (Cite `debug_lesson_6`).
3.  **Selective Fillna:** I will restrict `fillna(0)` to feature columns only. WLS coordinate columns will retain `NaN` values if missing, and rows with `NaN` WLS will be dropped to prevent calculating targets relative to (0,0,0) (Cite `debug_lesson_25`).
