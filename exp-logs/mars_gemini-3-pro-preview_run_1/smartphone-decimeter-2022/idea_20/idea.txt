Based on the lessons learned, I propose the **Kinematic-Geometric 1D ResUNet with Scaled Deep Supervision**.

This strategy structurally evolves the successful **1D U-Net** approach (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) by integrating **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** to capture multi-scale temporal context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) and augmenting the feature space with **Raw Kinematic Statistics** to address the limitations of static snapshots. It strictly employs **Scaled Deep Supervision** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037) to ensure training stability.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D Residual U-Net with ASPP**
The model is a sequence-to-sequence convolutional network designed to map a time-series of sensor features to coordinate corrections.
*   **Residual Encoder:** The contracting path consists of stacked **1D Residual Blocks** followed by Max Pooling. This allows the network to learn deep, hierarchical representations of signal error dynamics without gradient degradation.
*   **Multi-Scale Bottleneck:** The deepest layer utilizes **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)**. This module applies parallel 1D convolutions with varying dilation rates (e.g., 1, 6, 12, 24) to the feature map. This allows the model to simultaneously capture instantaneous signal spikes (e.g., multipath) and long-term trajectory drifts (e.g., atmospheric bias), preserving the local inductive bias that Transformers lacked (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Residual Decoder:** The expanding path uses upsampling layers concatenated with **Skip Connections** from the encoder to preserve high-frequency temporal details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Scaled Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary output heads are attached to intermediate decoder layers. Crucially, the ground truth targets for these heads are **downsampled (Average Pooled)** to match the temporal resolution of the specific layer. This prevents the gradient conflicts observed in previous attempts where low-resolution layers were forced to predict high-frequency noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss caused by exact matching failures due to clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Robust Feature Engineering:** For each timestamp, aggregate raw satellite data into a dense vector. We ensure **Feature Completeness** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033) and add a kinematic dimension:
    *   **Signal Statistics:** Mean, **Standard Deviation**, **Minimum**, and Maximum for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees`. The "Minimum" is critical for detecting weak links (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Geometric Orientation:** Mean Sine and Cosine of `SvAzimuthDegrees` to capture constellation shape.
    *   **Raw Kinematics (Innovation):** Calculate Mean and Standard Deviation of `PseudorangeRateMetersPerSecond` (Doppler shift). Unlike derived position deltas which corrupted the model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010), raw Doppler is a fundamental signal integrity metric that helps distinguish between receiver motion and multipath-induced signal jitter.
    *   **Reliability Metadata:** `SatCount` and aggregated `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
3.  **Translation Invariance:** Absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` are strictly excluded from the input to prevent map-memorization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
4.  **Target Transformation:** Regression targets are transformed from Degrees (Lat/Lon) to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline to ensure physical consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)** is used for the final and auxiliary heads due to its robustness against heavy-tailed outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). The total loss is a weighted sum of the multi-scale losses.
*   **Optimization:** AdamW optimizer with **Gradient Clipping** and a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule. This addresses the instability observed with complex architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
*   **Validation Strategy:** A `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` is used to ensure the model is evaluated on entirely unseen trips.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
