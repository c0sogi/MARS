The issue is a `TypeError` caused by passing the deprecated `verbose=True` argument to the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler constructor in `runfile.py` and `library/trainer.py`. Newer versions of PyTorch have removed this argument. To fix this, I will remove `verbose=True` from the scheduler initialization in both files. This aligns with the guidance to remove deprecated arguments from scheduler instantiation. Cite debug_lesson_4.
