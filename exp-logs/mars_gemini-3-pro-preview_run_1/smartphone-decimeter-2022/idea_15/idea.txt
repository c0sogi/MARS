Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Attention-Gated 1D ResUNet with Multi-Scale Deep Supervision**.

This strategy evolves the successful **1D U-Net** backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) by integrating **Attention Gates** to refine feature fusion and implementing a **Multi-Scale Deep Supervision** strategy that correctly handles target resolution (addressing the failure in Cite solution_lesson_node_00037). It also ensures **Feature Completeness** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033) by introducing geometric signal moments.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D Attention Residual U-Net**
The model is a sequence-to-sequence Encoder-Decoder network designed to predict trajectory residuals.
*   **Residual Encoder:** The contracting path consists of **1D Residual Blocks** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005) followed by Max Pooling. This extracts hierarchical features ranging from local signal noise to global trajectory drifts.
*   **ASPP Bottleneck:** The deepest layer utilizes **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)**. Parallel convolutions with varying dilation rates capture multi-scale temporal context, allowing the model to simultaneously distinguish instantaneous multipath errors from slow atmospheric biases (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Attention-Gated Skip Connections (Structural Innovation):** Standard skip connections are replaced with **Attention Gates**. These gates use the upsampled features from the decoder (gating signal) to weight the features coming from the encoder. This suppresses irrelevant background noise in the high-frequency encoder features, allowing the decoder to focus only on the relevant signal transients (e.g., specific noise spikes) required for reconstruction.
*   **Multi-Scale Heads:** The model includes auxiliary output heads at intermediate decoder resolutions to facilitate Deep Supervision.

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss caused by clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Robust Feature Engineering:** For each timestamp, aggregate raw satellite data into a dense vector:
    *   **Signal Statistics:** Mean, **Standard Deviation**, **Minimum**, and Maximum for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees`. The "Minimum" and "Std" are critical for detecting instability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020, solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Geometric Completeness:** Mean Sine and Cosine of `SvAzimuthDegrees` to capture constellation orientation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Geometric Signal Moments (New):** Compute the "Center of Signal Mass" by calculating the mean of Azimuth unit vectors weighted by Signal Strength ($Cn0$). This explicitly couples signal quality with direction, helping the model resolve vector errors.
    *   **Metadata:** `SatCount` and aggregated `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
3.  **Translation Invariance:** Absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` are strictly excluded from inputs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
4.  **Target Transformation:** Targets are converted from Degrees to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)** is used for robustness against outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Multi-Scale Deep Supervision (Correction):** The total loss is a weighted sum of the final output loss and auxiliary head losses. Crucially, **the Ground Truth target is downsampled (e.g., via Average Pooling)** to match the temporal resolution of each auxiliary head. This prevents the conflicting gradients observed when forcing low-resolution layers to predict high-frequency noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with weight decay.
*   **Validation:** `GroupShuffleSplit` by `drive_id` to prevent leakage.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** Predicted Cartesian offsets are converted back to angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** These offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
