Based on the lessons learned, I propose the **Sector-Aware 1D ResUNet**.

This strategy introduces a **distinct feature engineering paradigm** by incorporating **Directional Signal Aggregation**. Previous successful models relied on global aggregates (e.g., mean signal strength), effectively ignoring the *direction* of the error source (e.g., a blockage to the North vs. the South). By binning satellite signals into azimuthal sectors, this solution provides the model with the geometric context required to map signal degradation to specific vector corrections, while retaining the highly efficient 1D U-Net backbone.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D Residual U-Net**
The model employs a fully convolutional sequence-to-sequence architecture, chosen for its superior efficiency and local inductive bias compared to Transformers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Residual Encoder:** A series of downsampling blocks, each containing **1D Residual Units** (two 1D convolutions with a skip connection) followed by Max Pooling. This allows the network to learn deep, hierarchical representations of the signal dynamics without gradient degradation.
*   **Bottleneck:** A stack of dilated residual blocks to capture long-term trajectory dependencies (global context) such as atmospheric drift.
*   **Residual Decoder:** A series of upsampling blocks that concatenate features from the Encoder via **Skip Connections** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018). This preserves the high-frequency local signal details necessary for precise timestamp-level corrections.
*   **Output Head:** A final projection layer outputs the 2D residual vector $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ in meters.

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz). This prevents the massive data loss associated with exact timestamp matching (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Sector-Aware Feature Engineering (Innovation):**
    *   **Global Statistics:** Calculate **Mean, Minimum, and Maximum** for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees` across all visible satellites (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020). Include `SatCount` and aggregated `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
    *   **Directional Aggregation:** Divide the sky into 4 azimuthal quadrants (North-East, South-East, South-West, North-West). For each quadrant, calculate the **Mean Cn0DbHz** and **Mean SvElevationDegrees** of the satellites within it. This explicitly encodes the *geometry* of the signal environment, allowing the model to infer the direction of multipath errors.
3.  **Translation Invariance:** Absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` are strictly excluded from the input to prevent map-memorization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
4.  **Target Transformation:** Regression targets are transformed from Degrees (Lat/Lon) to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline to ensure physical consistency in the loss function (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. This is selected for its robustness to the heavy-tailed outlier distributions inherent in urban GNSS data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with weight decay is used to regularize the residual blocks.
*   **Validation Strategy:** A `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` is used to ensure the model is evaluated on entirely unseen trips, preventing data leakage.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed feature sequence (Global + Sector stats) for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission coordinates.
