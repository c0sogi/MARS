Based on the lessons learned, I propose the **Geometric-Aware 1D ResUNet with Scaled Deep Supervision**.

This strategy addresses the limitations of previous iterations by synthesizing the efficiency of the 1D U-Net backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) with a corrected Deep Supervision implementation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037), stabilization techniques for complex architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042), and a complete geometric feature set (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).

### Model
**Architecture: 1D Residual U-Net with ASPP**
The model is a sequence-to-sequence convolutional network designed to map a time-series of sensor features to a time-series of coordinate corrections.
*   **Residual Encoder:** The contracting path consists of stacked **1D Residual Blocks** followed by Max Pooling. This allows the network to learn deep, hierarchical representations of signal error dynamics without suffering from gradient degradation.
*   **Multi-Scale Bottleneck:** The deepest layer utilizes **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)**. This module applies parallel 1D convolutions with varying dilation rates to the feature map. This allows the model to simultaneously capture instantaneous signal spikes (e.g., multipath) and long-term trajectory drifts (e.g., atmospheric bias) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012), preserving the local inductive bias that Transformers lacked (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Residual Decoder:** The expanding path uses upsampling layers concatenated with **Skip Connections** from the encoder to preserve high-frequency temporal details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Scaled Deep Supervision (Structural Innovation):** Auxiliary output heads are attached to intermediate decoder layers. Crucially, the ground truth targets for these heads are **downsampled** (via Average Pooling) to match the temporal resolution of the specific layer. This resolves the gradient conflicts observed in previous attempts where low-resolution layers were forced to predict high-frequency noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss caused by exact matching failures due to clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Geometric Feature Engineering:** To ensure **Feature Completeness** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033), the input vector for each timestamp includes:
    *   **Signal Statistics:** Mean, **Standard Deviation**, **Minimum**, and Maximum for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Geometric Moments:** Calculate the **Signal-Weighted Centroid** of the visible constellation. This is computed as the weighted average of satellite unit vectors (derived from Azimuth/Elevation), weighted by their signal strength ($Cn0$). This explicitly encodes the directionality of signal blockage (e.g., "strong signals only from the West").
    *   **Reliability Metadata:** `SatCount` and aggregated `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
3.  **Translation Invariance:** Absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` are strictly excluded from the input to prevent map-memorization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
4.  **Target Transformation:** Regression targets are transformed from Degrees (Lat/Lon) to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)** is used for the final and auxiliary heads due to its robustness against heavy-tailed outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). The total loss is a weighted sum of the multi-scale losses.
*   **Optimization Stabilization:** To address the instability observed with complex architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042), the training will utilize **Gradient Clipping** and a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate** schedule. This ensures that the gradients from the deep supervision heads do not destabilize the early training phase.
*   **Validation Strategy:** A `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` is used to ensure the model is evaluated on entirely unseen trips.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
