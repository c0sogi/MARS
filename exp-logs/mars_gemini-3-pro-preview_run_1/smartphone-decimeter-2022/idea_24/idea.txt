Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Dual-Stream 1D ResUNet with Decimated Deep Supervision**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** by decoupling the processing of "Standard" and "High-Precision" signals into separate encoder streams. This allows the network to learn distinct feature extractors for noisy, multipath-prone signals (L1/Global) versus clean, high-integrity signals (L5/Phase-Locked), fusing them only at the bottleneck. It integrates this with the critical **Decimated Deep Supervision** technique (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060) to ensure stable training of the deep architecture.

### Model
**Architecture: Dual-Encoder 1D Residual U-Net**
The model uses a sequence-to-sequence architecture with two parallel encoders feeding into a shared decoder.
*   **Dual Encoders:**
    *   **Stream A (Context Stream):** Processes statistics derived from *all* visible satellites. This ensures the model always has a continuous view of the environment, even in poor conditions.
    *   **Stream B (Precision Stream):** Processes statistics derived *only* from high-quality signals (L5 frequency bands and/or satellites with Valid Carrier Phase). This stream provides sparse but highly accurate "anchor" features.
    *   Both encoders use stacks of **1D Residual Blocks** to extract hierarchical features without gradient degradation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Fusion Bottleneck:** Features from both encoders are concatenated and processed by an **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module. This captures multi-scale temporal context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012), allowing the model to correlate local noise in Stream A with stable trends in Stream B.
*   **Shared Decoder:** Upsampling layers that concatenate fused features from the bottleneck with skip connections from *both* encoders.
*   **Decimated Deep Supervision (Key Retention):** Auxiliary output heads are attached to intermediate decoder layers. The loss for these heads is computed against **decimated (subsampled)** ground truth targets, forcing the encoder to learn valid instantaneous states rather than smoothed averages (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss from clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Stratified Feature Engineering:** For each timestamp, generate two distinct feature sets:
    *   **Set A (All Signals):** Robust statistics (Mean, **Standard Deviation**, **Minimum**, Maximum) for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees` computed across all satellites (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020, solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Set B (High Quality):** The same statistics computed only for satellites with `SignalType` in {L5, E5a, B2a} OR `AccumulatedDeltaRangeState` indicating valid phase. If no such satellites exist, fill with zeros.
3.  **Reliability Metadata:** Include `SatCount` and aggregated `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters` in both streams (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
4.  **Translation Invariance:** Strictly exclude absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` to prevent map memorization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
5.  **Target Transformation:** Regression targets are transformed from Degrees to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. The total loss is a weighted sum of the final output loss and the auxiliary head losses. MAE is selected for its robustness to the heavy-tailed outliers common in urban GNSS data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Gradient Clipping** and a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule. This addresses the training instability often observed with complex, multi-branch architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
*   **Validation Strategy:** A `GroupShuffleSplit` based on `drive_id` is used to ensure the model is evaluated on entirely unseen trips, preventing data leakage.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The preprocessed dual-stream feature sequence for a full test drive is fed into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** These Cartesian offsets are converted back into angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** The predicted offsets are added to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
