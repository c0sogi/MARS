Standard `fillna(0)` operations do not replace `inf`. To fix this, I will modify `library/preprocessing.py` to explicitly replace `inf` and `-inf` with `NaN` immediately after loading the raw CSV data. Additionally, I will add a check to filter out any rows with non-finite target values (`target_e`, `target_n`) to ensure training stability (Cite debug_lesson_11).
