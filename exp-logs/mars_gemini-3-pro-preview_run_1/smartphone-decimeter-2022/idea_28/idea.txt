Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments, I propose the **Directional-Stratified 1D SE-ResUNet with Decimated Deep Supervision**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution by synthesizing the two most successful feature engineering paradigms—**Stratified Aggregation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063) and **Geometric Completeness** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)—into a unified **"Sky Dashboard"** representation. While previous models treated satellite signals as either a global average or a global stratified list, this approach explicitly bins signals by both **Direction (Azimuth)** and **Quality (Frequency/Phase)**. This allows the model to leverage the local inductive bias of CNNs to learn complex spatio-temporal error patterns, such as "High-precision L5 signals are blocked in the North-East sector," which are invisible to global aggregators.

### Model
**Architecture: 1D Residual U-Net with SE-Blocks and ASPP**
The model is a sequence-to-sequence convolutional network designed to map a time-series of geometric signal profiles to coordinate corrections.
*   **Input:** A multi-channel time-series tensor. The channel dimension is structured to represent the sky geometry (e.g., 4 Azimuth Sectors $\times$ 2 Quality Strata $\times$ $N$ Statistics).
*   **Encoder:** A stack of **1D Residual Blocks** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) to extract hierarchical features.
    *   **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Modules:** Integrated into each residual block. These modules dynamically recalibrate channel weights, allowing the network to suppress noisy sectors (e.g., "Ignore South-L1") and emphasize clean ones (e.g., "Focus on North-L5") based on the temporal context.
*   **ASPP Bottleneck:** An **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling** module processes the deepest features with multiple dilation rates. This captures multi-scale dependencies, distinguishing between instantaneous multipath spikes and long-term atmospheric drifts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Residual Decoder:** Upsampling layers concatenated with **Skip Connections** from the encoder to preserve high-frequency temporal details.
*   **Decimated Auxiliary Heads:** Intermediate decoder layers are equipped with auxiliary output heads for Deep Supervision.

### Data
**Preprocessing Pipeline**
1.  **Temporal Quantization:** Raw GNSS logs and ground truth are aligned by **rounding timestamps to the nearest second (1Hz)**. This prevents data loss from clock jitter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Directional-Stratified Feature Engineering (Innovation):**
    *   **Binning:** Divide the sky into 4 Azimuthal Sectors (NE, SE, SW, NW).
    *   **Stratification:** Within each sector, partition satellites into two strata:
        *   **High-Quality:** Satellites with `SignalType` in {L5, E5a, B2a} **OR** Valid Carrier Phase (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063).
        *   **Standard:** All other satellites (L1, etc.).
    *   **Aggregation:** For each Sector-Stratum pair (8 total groups), compute robust statistics: **Mean, Standard Deviation, Minimum, and Maximum** for `Cn0DbHz` and `SvElevationDegrees`. The "Minimum" is critical for detecting weak links (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Handling Sparsity:** If a bin is empty, fill with zero/mask values.
3.  **Global Context:** Concatenate these directional features with global metadata (`SatCount`, `RawPseudorangeUncertaintyMeters`) and the **Signal-Weighted Azimuth Centroid** (decomposed into Sin/Cos) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033, solution_lesson_node_00064).
4.  **Translation Invariance:** Strictly exclude absolute `Latitude` and `Longitude` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
5.  **Target Transformation:** Convert regression targets from Degrees to **Meters (East-North)** relative to the WLS baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. The total loss is a weighted sum of the final output loss and auxiliary head losses. MAE is selected for its robustness to outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Decimated Deep Supervision:** For the auxiliary heads operating at lower temporal resolutions, the ground truth target is **decimated (subsampled)** to match the resolution, rather than average-pooled. This forces the encoder to learn valid instantaneous states, preventing the gradient conflicts observed in previous multi-scale attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Optimization:** AdamW optimizer with **Gradient Clipping** and a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule to ensure stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
*   **Batch Size:** Maintain a healthy batch size (e.g., 32) to ensure Batch Normalization stability, prioritizing this over extreme network width (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Feed the preprocessed directional-stratified feature sequence for a full test drive into the model. The model outputs the sequence of Cartesian residuals $(\Delta North, \Delta East)$ from the final high-resolution head.
2.  **Reconstruction:** Convert Cartesian offsets to angular offsets $(\Delta Lat, \Delta Lon)$ using local WGS84 approximations.
3.  **Correction:** Add the predicted offsets to the baseline WLS coordinates provided in the test set to generate the final submission.
