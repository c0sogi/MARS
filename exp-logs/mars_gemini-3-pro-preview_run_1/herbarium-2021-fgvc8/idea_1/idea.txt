# Baseline Solution: ResNet-18 with Label Smoothing

### Model
I propose a standard **ResNet-18** architecture as the baseline model. This choice prioritizes training speed and memory efficiency over the deeper architectures (like ResNet-50 or EfficientNet) used in other approaches.
*   **Backbone**: A ResNet-18 network pre-trained on ImageNet will serve as the feature extractor. Its smaller embedding dimension (512 compared to 2048 in ResNet-50) significantly reduces the number of parameters in the final classification layer, which is crucial given the high cardinality (64,500 classes) of the dataset.
*   **Head**: The standard final fully connected layer will be replaced with a single linear projection mapping the 512-dimensional features to the 64,500 species categories, followed by a Softmax activation.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing**: All images will be resized to **224x224** pixels. This resolution is standard for ResNet architectures and ensures high throughput during training compared to larger resolutions (e.g., 384x384).
*   **Normalization**: Pixel values will be normalized using the standard ImageNet mean and standard deviation to align with the pre-trained weights.
*   **Augmentation**: To maintain speed, only minimal augmentations will be applied: random horizontal flips and random crops during training.

### Training
*   **Loss Function**: We will use **Cross-Entropy Loss with Label Smoothing** (e.g., factor of 0.1). Label smoothing is a computationally free regularization technique that prevents the model from becoming over-confident in its predictions, which is particularly beneficial for datasets with a massive number of classes and potential label noise.
*   **Optimizer**: The **AdamW** optimizer will be used for its ability to handle weight decay correctly and converge faster than standard SGD.
*   **Scheduler**: A **One-Cycle Learning Rate Policy** will be employed. This scheduler ramps the learning rate up and then down during a single training cycle, allowing for "super-convergence," which significantly reduces the number of epochs required to reach a competitive baseline score.
*   **Precision**: Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) will be utilized to further accelerate training and reduce GPU memory usage.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference**: Test images will be resized to 224x224 (without cropping) and normalized.
*   **Prediction**: The model will perform a single forward pass. The class index corresponding to the maximum logit (argmax) will be selected as the predicted `category_id`. This avoids the computational overhead of k-NN search or ensemble voting.
