3.  **Schedule Optimization (Cite Lesson 00005)**: I will increase the training `epochs` to 25 and match the Cosine Annealing `T_0` parameter to 25. This ensures a single, complete cosine cycle where the learning rate decays from `1e-4` to `1e-6` over the full duration, allowing the model to settle into a sharper minimum. I will also remove the epoch override in `runfile.py`.

4.  **Training Stability**: I will increase the early stopping `patience` from 5 to 10 in `library/training.py`. This prevents the training from stopping prematurely during the middle of the cosine cycle, ensuring the model benefits from the low learning rate phase at the end of the schedule.
