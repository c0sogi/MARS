**Strategy:**
1.  **Model Simplification**: I will switch from `resnext50_32x4d` to `resnet34`. As noted in **Lesson 00007**, simpler backbones like ResNet34 often outperform complex ones on small datasets and offer smoother optimization landscapes.
2.  **Disable Mixup**: I will disable Mixup regularization. **Lesson 00011** highlights that heavy regularization imposes a "convergence tax" and caused underfitting in the previous short run. Removing it will accelerate learning.
3.  **Constant Learning Rate**: I will remove the `CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts` scheduler and use a constant learning rate of `1e-4`. **Lesson 00005** and **Lesson 00008** indicate that for short fine-tuning budgets, a constant learning rate prevents premature decay and ensures better weight adaptation.
4.  **Increase Epochs**: I will increase the training epochs from 10 to 15. This modest increase, combined with the removal of Mixup and the use of a lighter model, ensures we reach convergence without exceeding the time limit (**Lesson 00003**).
