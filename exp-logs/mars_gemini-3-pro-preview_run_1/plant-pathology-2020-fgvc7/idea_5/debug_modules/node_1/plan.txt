The issue is that `roc_auc_score` with `average='macro'` returns `nan` (Not a Number) instead of raising a `ValueError` when the validation subset is small and missing certain classes. This `nan` value causes the training loop to fail to update the `best_metric` (since `nan > -inf` is False), resulting in the best AUC remaining at `-inf` and triggering an assertion error. The fix involves explicitly checking if the calculated score is `nan` in `library/utils.py` and returning a neutral score of 0.5 in that case to ensure the training process continues correctly.
