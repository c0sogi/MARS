Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, I propose a solution titled **"Stratified Bagging Ensemble with Out-of-Bag (OOB) Early Stopping"**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** in the data topology: replacing standard Cross-Validation or Fixed-Splits with **Bootstrap Aggregation (Bagging)**. This approach resolves the conflict between the need for maximum training data (Lesson 25) and the requirement for stable validation signals (Lesson 58), while leveraging the proven robustness of Seed Averaging (Lesson 55).

# Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is selected as the optimal architecture, having consistently outperformed shallower (ResNet18) and deeper (ResNeXt, EfficientNet) models on this specific dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003, solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer. I will explicitly avoid complex heads like Multi-Sample Dropout or GeM Pooling, which caused underfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Initialization Safeguard:** I will implement a mandatory **Initial Loss Test**. Before training, the loss of the first batch must be verified to be significantly lower than random guessing ($-\ln(1/4) \approx 1.38$) to ensure transfer learning is active (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).

# Data
*   **Topology (Structural Innovation):** Instead of K-Fold or Fixed-Split validation, I will implement **Stratified Bagging**. I will generate **5 independent training sets** by sampling with replacement from the full original dataset.
    *   **Training Data:** Each "Bag" will contain the same number of images as the original dataset ($N=1310$), maximizing the data volume available to the model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Validation Data:** For each model, the validation set will consist of the **Out-of-Bag (OOB)** samples (approximately 36.8% of the data, or ~480 images). This is significantly larger than the 20% split (~260 images) or 10-Fold split (~130 images) used previously, providing a much more stable signal for Early Stopping (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).
    *   **Stratification:** The sampling will be performed per-class to ensure the minority `multiple_diseases` class is preserved in both the Bag and the OOB set.
*   **Resolution:** Images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels to ensure pipeline consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and batch size stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a robust `albumentations` pipeline including **ShiftScaleRotate** and **RandomBrightnessContrast**.
    *   **Strategic Retention:** I will explicitly include **VerticalFlip** and **HorizontalFlip**. Lesson 30 demonstrated that omitting vertical flips on rotationally invariant data (leaves) significantly harms performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
    *   **Exclusion:** I will exclude Mixup/CutMix to avoid the "convergence tax" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** I will use **Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss** with weights inversely proportional to class frequencies. This is essential to maximize the Macro-Averaged AUC by prioritizing the minority class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimizer:** The **Adam** optimizer will be used.
*   **Scheduler:** I will use **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Synchronization:** The cycle length (`T_0`) will be **strictly synchronized** with the total number of epochs (e.g., 15). This ensures the learning rate decays fully to the fine-tuning range (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Checkpointing:** I will implement **Metric-Based Early Stopping** using the **OOB Validation AUC**. The model weights will be saved only when the OOB metric improves (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** The final submission will be the **Ensemble Average** of the probabilities output by the 5 Bagged models. This combines the variance reduction of ensembling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055) with the diversity introduced by bootstrapping.
*   **TTA Strategy:** I will **explicitly disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Previous experiments showed TTA degraded performance compared to single-view inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). By validating on single-view OOB samples and predicting on single-view test samples, I ensure **Validation-Inference Parity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
