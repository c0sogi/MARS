Based on the analysis of the provided lessons, I propose a solution titled **"SAM-Optimized Stratified Shuffle-Split Ensemble"**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** in the optimization and data topology layers. It replaces the standard Adam optimizer with **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)** to directly target generalization on this small dataset, and evolves the ensemble topology from a "Fixed-Split" approach to a **Stratified Shuffle-Split** to maximize data coverage without the instability of K-Fold Cross-Validation.

# Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Backbone:** ResNet34 is retained as the optimal architecture, having consistently outperformed deeper (ResNeXt) and more complex (EfficientNet) models on this specific dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a single fully connected layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Initialization:** I will implement a mandatory **Initial Loss Test** to verify that pre-trained weights are correctly loaded before training proceeds (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).

# Data
*   **Topology (Structural Innovation):** I will implement a **Stratified Shuffle-Split Ensemble**.
    *   Unlike the "Fixed-Split" strategy (Idea 16) which leaves 20% of the data unseen by any model, and "Bagging" (Idea 18) which reduces unique training samples via replacement (Cite solution_lesson_node_00063), this approach generates **5 unique random splits** (80% Train, 20% Validation).
    *   Each model trains on a different 80% subset, ensuring the ensemble as a whole covers the entire dataset manifold (addressing Lesson 25) while maintaining a large, stable validation set for robust Early Stopping (addressing Lesson 58).
*   **Resolution:** Images will be processed strictly at **256x256** pixels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ a **Conservative** `albumentations` pipeline to maximize diversity without destroying fine-grained features.
    *   **Geometric:** **VerticalFlip**, **HorizontalFlip** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030), and **Rotate** ($\pm 45^\circ$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Constraint (Strategic Retention):** I will strictly limit `ShiftScaleRotate` scaling to **$\pm 5\%$**. Previous attempts with the default 10% scaling degraded performance by obscuring small disease lesions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077).
    *   **Exclusion:** Mixup/CutMix are excluded to avoid the convergence tax (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

# Training
*   **Optimizer (Structural Innovation):** I will implement **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)** wrapping the **Adam** base optimizer.
    *   *Rationale:* Standard optimizers seek the lowest loss value, often finding "sharp" minima that generalize poorly on small datasets. SAM simultaneously minimizes loss value and loss *sharpness* (geometry), finding flat minima that are more robust to domain shifts. This acts as a powerful regularizer, complementing the Shuffle-Split topology.
    *   **Configuration:** Uniform learning rate (avoiding discriminative rates, Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
*   **Loss Function:** I will use **Weighted Soft-Target Cross-Entropy**.
    *   **Soft Targets:** Targets will be passed as float probabilities to preserve uncertainty and improve ensemble diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00067).
    *   **Normalization:** The loss will be normalized by the **sum of weights** in the batch, not the batch size, to ensure stability with class weighting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00076).
    *   **Weighting:** Inverse class frequency weights will be applied (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**, with the cycle length (`T_0`) strictly synchronized to the total number of epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Seeds:** I will use widely spaced random seeds (e.g., based on years) to minimize RNG correlation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00067).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probability outputs from all **5 Shuffle-Split Models**.
*   **TTA Exclusion:** I will **explicitly disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Previous experiments confirmed that TTA degrades performance on this dataset compared to single-view inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). Validating and predicting on single views ensures **Validation-Inference Parity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
