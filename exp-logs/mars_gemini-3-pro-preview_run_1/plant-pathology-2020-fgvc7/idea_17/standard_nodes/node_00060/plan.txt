To improve performance and stability on this small dataset, I will switch from the 10-Fold Cross-Validation strategy back to a **Seed Averaging Ensemble** on a fixed validation split. As noted in **Lesson {solution_lesson_node_00058}**, high-K cross-validation on small datasets fragments the validation data, leading to unstable early stopping signals. Furthermore, **Lesson {solution_lesson_node_00059}** highlights that OOF metrics often underestimate the performance of an ensemble; by validating the ensemble directly on a fixed hold-out set, we can better estimate the final test performance. I will retain the robust augmentation pipeline (including Vertical Flip, **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00030}**) and the synchronized Cosine Annealing scheduler (**Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}**) which have proven effective.
