The issue stems from the Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) utility attempting to perform standard floating-point division on integer tensors (specifically `num_batches_tracked` in BatchNorm layers). In PyTorch, in-place division (`div_`) of an integer tensor by a number results in a float, which causes a `RuntimeError` when trying to write back into the original integer tensor. To fix this, I will modify `library/utils.py` to check if a tensor is floating-point before dividing. If it is an integer tensor, I will use floor division (`rounding_mode='floor'`) to maintain the integer data type.
