Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, I propose a solution centered on a **ResNet34-based Stratified K-Fold Ensemble with Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**. This strategy structurally evolves the pipeline from a single train/validation split to a robust ensemble framework, directly addressing the small dataset size and high variance while adhering to the architectural constraints identified in the lessons.

# Model
I will utilize the **ResNet34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
*   **Rationale:** Previous experiments demonstrated that ResNet34 provides a superior balance of capacity and generalization compared to the shallower ResNet18 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003), while avoiding the optimization difficulties and overfitting observed with EfficientNet-B3 on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Head Design:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a fully connected layer with 4 outputs, ensuring the model remains lightweight and easy to optimize.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** All images will be resized to **256x256** pixels. This resolution will be strictly enforced during both training and inference to prevent the performance degradation caused by resolution mismatch (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Augmentation:** I will employ the `albumentations` pipeline established in the previous successful iteration, including **ShiftScaleRotate** and **RandomBrightnessContrast**. This matches the increased capacity of the ResNet34 backbone with sufficient data variability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Splitting Strategy (Innovation):** Instead of a single hold-out set, I will implement **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation (K=5)**. This ensures that every image in the dataset is used for both training and validation across different folds, maximizing the utility of the limited data (~1,300 images) and reducing the variance associated with a single random split.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** I will use **Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss**, where weights are inversely proportional to class frequencies. This is critical to maximizing the Macro-Averaged AUC metric by preventing the model from ignoring the minority `multiple_diseases` class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Optimization:** The model will be trained using the **Adam** optimizer with a **constant learning rate** (e.g., 1e-4). I will explicitly avoid aggressive learning rate decay schedules (like Cosine Annealing) which were shown to hamper convergence in short training regimes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Procedure:** The training loop will be executed 5 times (once for each fold). In each iteration, the model will be reset and trained on $K-1$ folds while validating on the remaining fold. The best checkpoint for each fold (based on Validation AUC) will be saved.

# Evaluation
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) (Innovation):** During inference, I will introduce a TTA procedure. For each test image, the model will predict on the original image and a horizontally flipped version. The predictions will be averaged to smooth out noise and improve robustness.
*   **Ensembling:** The final submission will be generated by averaging the TTA-enhanced probability outputs from all **5 models** (one from each fold). This ensemble approach is expected to significantly improve the stability and ranking accuracy (AUC) compared to any single model.
