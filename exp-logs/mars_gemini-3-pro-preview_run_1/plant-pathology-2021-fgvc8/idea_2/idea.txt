Based on the progression of empirical findings and the specific failure modes identified in previous iterations, I propose the following optimized strategy.

### Model
I propose replacing the EfficientNetV2 backbone with a **ConvNeXt-Small** architecture.
*   **Structural Innovation:** Unlike the MBConv-based EfficientNet used previously, ConvNeXt adopts a hierarchical structure inspired by Vision Transformers (using large 7x7 kernels, LayerNorm, and fewer activation layers) while retaining the inductive biases of CNNs. This architecture is designed to better capture the global context of disease patterns (e.g., distinguishing widespread "rust" from localized "frog_eye_leaf_spot") compared to the local-texture focus of standard CNNs.
*   **Capacity:** ConvNeXt-Small has a parameter count (~50M) comparable to the EfficientNetV2-Medium used in **Lesson 00002**, ensuring we maintain the high model capacity that was proven effective, but with a more modern feature extraction paradigm.

### Data
*   **Resolution:** The input resolution will be increased to **384x384 pixels**. This continues the successful scaling strategy observed in **Lesson 00002**, where higher resolution (320px) improved fine-grained detection.
*   **Regularization Pipeline (Mixup & CutMix):** I will implement a **Mixup and CutMix** strategy directly into the data loading pipeline.
    *   **Correction of Previous Failure:** This explicitly addresses **Lesson 00004**, where a defensive configuration prevented Mixup from running. This solution will make Mixup/CutMix a mandatory step in the batch collation process, ensuring the model trains on convex combinations of image pairs.
    *   **Handling Complexity:** This technique acts as the "Aggressive Regularization" required when scaling up model capacity (Cite **Lesson 00002**), preventing overfitting on the larger 384px inputs.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** The model will be trained using **BCEWithLogitsLoss** (Cite **Lesson 00001**). However, the target handling will be structurally modified to accept **soft, continuous labels** (e.g., 0.7 Scab, 0.3 Rust) generated by the Mixup/CutMix process, rather than strict binary {0,1} vectors.
*   **Optimization:** Training will utilize the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Precision:** **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** will be strictly enforced to manage the memory footprint of the ConvNeXt backbone at 384x384 resolution, ensuring the solution fits within the GPU memory budget (Cite **Lesson 00002**).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Test images will be resized to 384x384 pixels.
*   **Prediction:** The model will output logits which are passed through a Sigmoid activation. A threshold of **0.5** will be applied to generate the final binary labels. The "complex" class will be treated as a standard class during prediction, consistent with the successful multi-label formulation established in **Lesson 00001**.
