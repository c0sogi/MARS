To optimize performance, I will switch the model architecture from Swin Transformer Tiny back to **EfficientNetV2-Medium** and increase the input resolution to **384x384**. This aligns with previous empirical findings that resolution and CNN inductive biases (captured by EfficientNet) are critical for this fine-grained disease detection task Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}, {solution_lesson_node_00011}.

I will also re-introduce **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** (specifically horizontal flipping) during both the final inference and the validation loop. While some previous experiments showed mixed results with TTA, the most recent analysis suggests that removing it contributed to performance degradation Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}. TTA stabilizes predictions and is generally beneficial for maximizing F1 scores in multi-label settings.

The training schedule will be set to 15 epochs with a batch size of 24 to ensure convergence while fitting within the 4-hour runtime limit on the A100 GPU.
