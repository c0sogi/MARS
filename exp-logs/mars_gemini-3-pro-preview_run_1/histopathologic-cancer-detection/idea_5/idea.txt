Based on the accumulated empirical evidence, specifically the superiority of **DenseNet121** over **ConvNeXt** (Lesson 16) and the critical importance of **Stem Modification** (Lesson 3/8) and **Full Data Training** (Lesson 14), I propose a solution that pushes performance through a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** strategy.

# Model: Heterogeneous Ensemble with Resolution-Preserving Stems

I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** composed of two structurally distinct architectures: **DenseNet121** and **ResNet50**.

1.  **DenseNet121 (Exploitation):** We retain this architecture as it has proven to be the top performer (AUC 0.9849). Its feature concatenation mechanism effectively preserves the high-frequency textural details required for this task. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
2.  **ResNet50 (Exploration/Diversity):** We introduce ResNet50 to complement the DenseNet. While DenseNet relies on concatenation, ResNet relies on residual addition. This distinct inductive bias allows the ResNet to capture different feature representations, and ensembling these diverse predictions typically yields a lower-variance, higher-performance system than any single model.

**Crucial Structural Modification:**
For **both** models, I will apply the **Stem Modification** strategy. The standard input stems (typically a $7 \times 7$ stride-2 convolution followed by pooling) will be replaced with a **single $3 \times 3$ convolution with stride 1 and padding 1**.
*   This prevents the immediate $4\times$ downsampling of the $48 \times 48$ input, preserving the spatial resolution entering the deep stages. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
*   The rest of the networks will be initialized with **ImageNet pretrained weights** to leverage transfer learning. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

# Data: Hard Attention and Stain-Preserving Augmentation

**Preprocessing:**
I will continue to use **Hard Attention via ROI Cropping**, extracting the central **48x48 pixel region** from the source images. This aligns the input with the ground truth definition while removing background noise. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

**Dataset Usage:**
I will train both models on the **full training dataset**. I will explicitly avoid subsampling, as high-capacity models require the full data distribution to learn robust invariants and avoid the overfitting observed in previous experiments. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.

**Augmentation:**
I will apply random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and 90-degree rotations. I will strictly limit color augmentation to **mild brightness and contrast adjustments**, explicitly excluding hue and saturation jittering to preserve the H&E stain biomarkers. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.

# Training: Independent Training with Volatility-Resistant Schedule

Each model in the ensemble will be trained independently using the following protocol:
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** for improved weight decay handling.
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule over 20 epochs.
*   **Strategy:** I will maintain a **relaxed Early Stopping patience of 6 epochs**. This ensures that the training process does not terminate prematurely during the volatile high-learning-rate phases. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.

# Evaluation: TTA-Ensemble

For the final submission, I will implement a two-stage inference strategy:
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test image, both the DenseNet121 and ResNet50 will generate predictions for 4 views (original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, rot90). These 4 predictions will be averaged for each model. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
2.  **Ensemble Averaging:** The final submission probability will be the simple average of the TTA-smoothed output from the DenseNet121 and the TTA-smoothed output from the ResNet50. This combines the robustness of TTA with the diversity of the ensemble.
