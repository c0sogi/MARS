# Model: ConvNeXt-Tiny with Adapted Resolution Stem
I propose replacing the DenseNet/ResNet backbones with a **ConvNeXt-Tiny** architecture. ConvNeXt represents a structural evolution in CNN design, adopting Vision Transformer (ViT) principles—such as large kernel sizes ($7 \times 7$), inverted bottlenecks, and Layer Normalization—to capture long-range dependencies and textural context more effectively than standard convolutions.

To ensure this modern backbone functions on our small input, I will apply the **Partial Transfer Learning with Stem Modification** strategy. The standard ConvNeXt "patchify" stem (a $4 \times 4$ convolution with stride 4) is too aggressive for our data, as it would instantly reduce a $48 \times 48$ input to $12 \times 12$. I will replace this stem with a finer-grained $3 \times 3$ convolution (stride 1, padding 1) initialized with random weights, while loading **ImageNet pretrained weights** for all subsequent stages. This preserves the $48 \times 48$ spatial resolution entering the first stage, allowing the model's deep layers to process high-fidelity features.

Crucially, I will maintain a **relaxed Early Stopping patience** of 6 epochs. This ensures that the training process does not terminate prematurely during the volatile high-learning-rate phases, allowing the model to reach the stable convergence zone where performance is maximized.

# Evaluation: Test Time Augmentation (TTA)
For the final inference, I will employ **Test Time Augmentation**. The model will predict probabilities for the original crop and its geometric transformations (horizontal flip, vertical flip, and 90-degree rotation). The final submission score will be the average of these four predictions, reducing variance and improving robustness.
