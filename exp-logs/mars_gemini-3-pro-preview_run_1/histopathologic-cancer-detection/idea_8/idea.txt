# Model: Converged Heterogeneous Ensemble with GeM Pooling

I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** composed of two distinct DenseNet architectures: **DenseNet121** and **DenseNet169**.

*   **Architectural Rationale:** I retain the **DenseNet121** as the core component due to its proven top performance (AUC 0.9849), confirming that dense feature concatenation is structurally superior to residual addition or large-kernel convolutions for capturing fine-grained tumor textures. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**. I introduce **DenseNet169** to add depth and capacity diversity to the ensemble, creating a robust "DenseNet Family" system.
*   **Structural Innovation (GeM Pooling):** For both architectures, I will replace the standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) layer with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. The task requires detecting "at least one pixel" of tumor tissue, which conceptually aligns with Max Pooling, while the texture classification aligns with Average Pooling. GeM pooling introduces a learnable parameter $p$ that dynamically interpolates between Max and Average pooling, allowing the model to better capture sparse tumor signals within the patch than a fixed GAP layer.
*   **Stem Modification:** To prevent the immediate loss of spatial fidelity, I will replace the standard input stem of both models ($7 \times 7$ stride-2 convolution + max pooling) with a **single $3 \times 3$ convolution with stride 1 and padding 1**. This ensures the deep layers process a feature map that retains the full $48 \times 48$ spatial resolution of the input. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
*   **Initialization:** Both models will be initialized with **ImageNet pretrained weights** (excluding the modified stem) to leverage learned feature detectors. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

# Data: Hard Attention and Stain-Preserving Augmentation

*   **Preprocessing:** I will apply **Hard Attention via ROI Cropping**, extracting the central **48x48 pixel region** from the source images. This focuses the model on the relevant 32x32 target area plus a minimal 8-pixel context border, removing irrelevant background noise. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Dataset Usage:** I will utilize the **full training dataset** for all folds. I will strictly avoid subsampling, as high-capacity models require the full data distribution to learn robust invariants and avoid overfitting. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.
*   **Augmentation:** I will apply random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and 90-degree rotations. Crucially, I will restrict color augmentation to **mild brightness and contrast adjustments only**, explicitly **excluding hue and saturation jittering**. This preserves the H&E stain color signatures that are critical for identifying tumor tissue. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.

# Training: Fully Converged 5-Fold Cross-Validation

To explicitly correct the under-training failure observed in previous ensemble attempts, I will implement a **Fully Converged 5-Fold Cross-Validation** strategy for *each* architecture.

*   **Protocol:** I will train 5 independent DenseNet121 models and 5 independent DenseNet169 models (10 models total), each on stratified folds of the full dataset.
*   **Convergence:** Each model will be trained for **20 epochs**. I will maintain a **relaxed Early Stopping patience of 6 epochs** to ensure the models survive volatile transient phases and reach true convergence, prioritizing the quality of the base learners over raw speed. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007** and **Cite solution_lesson_node_00021**.
*   **Optimization:** I will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. I will also introduce **Label Smoothing (0.05)** to the Binary Cross-Entropy loss to prevent overconfidence and improve generalization on noisy boundary cases.

# Evaluation: TTA-Ensemble

For the final submission, I will employ a robust inference pipeline that marginalizes out both model variance and orientation noise:

1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test image, every model in the ensemble (10 total) will generate predictions for 4 views: original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, and 90-degree rotation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
2.  **Global Averaging:** The final prediction will be the simple average of the 40 probability outputs (2 architectures $\times$ 5 folds $\times$ 4 TTA views). This maximizes the Area Under the ROC Curve by smoothing out stochastic errors across architectures, data splits, and orientations.
