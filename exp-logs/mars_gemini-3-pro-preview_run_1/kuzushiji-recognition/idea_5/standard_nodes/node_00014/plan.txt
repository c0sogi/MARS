The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` is caused by the aggressive `BATCH_SIZE` of 6 set in `runfile.py`. As noted in the error analysis, the model's classification head produces a high-cardinality dense output tensor of shape `(Batch, 4782, 256, 256)`. With 4,782 classes, this single tensor requires approximately 7 GB of VRAM for a batch of 6. Combined with the memory footprint of the Swin-Base backbone (~35 GB), the total requirement exceeds the A100's 40 GB capacity. Following `debug_lesson_1`, I will reduce the batch size to 4 to linearly scale down the memory usage of both the activations and the dense output tensors, ensuring the training fits within the available GPU memory.
