The `NaN` loss is caused by the `_init_weights` method in `ConvNeXtCenterNet` iterating over `self.modules()`, which inadvertently re-initializes the pre-trained `ConvNeXt` backbone with Kaiming Normal initialization. This destroys the backbone's learned weights and destabilizes the network. Additionally, it overwrites the crucial zero-initialization of the `offset_mask_conv` layers in the `DeformableConvBlock`, causing the deformable convolutions to start with random offsets, further leading to numerical instability.

The fix involves rewriting `_init_weights` to explicitly initialize only the newly added components: the FPN lateral connections and the specific layers within the detection heads, while leaving the backbone and the `offset_mask_conv` layers untouched.
