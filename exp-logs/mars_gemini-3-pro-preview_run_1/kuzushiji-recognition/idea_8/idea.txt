Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the successful ResNet50 baseline by integrating Deformable Convolutions directly into the backbone, addressing the receptive field limitations without incurring the convergence penalties of heavy Transformers.

# Optimized Strategy: Deformable ResNet-50 CenterNet

## Model
I propose a **Deformable ResNet-50 (ResNet-50-DCN)** backbone integrated into the CenterNet anchor-free framework.

*   **Backbone (ResNet-50-DCN):**
    *   **Structural Innovation:** While the standard **ResNet-50** provided the best balance of capacity and convergence speed **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00016**, it suffers from a fixed geometric receptive field. This causes failures on large, cursive characters where the geometric center falls on empty whitespace **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
    *   To solve this without switching to slow-converging Transformers (like Swin-B), I will replace the standard $3 \times 3$ convolutions in the deeper stages (Stage 3 and Stage 4) of the ResNet-50 with **Deformable Convolutions (DCNv2)**. This structural change allows the backbone to learn dynamic offsets, effectively expanding its receptive field to "reach out" and aggregate features from the ink strokes of a character, even when the center point is in whitespace.
*   **Neck (FPN):** A Feature Pyramid Network will be used to fuse features from the deformable backbone (strides 4, 8, 16, 32). This ensures robust detection across the extreme scale variations of Kuzushiji characters.
*   **Heads:** The model will utilize the standard anchor-free CenterNet heads (Heatmap, Regression, Classification). The enhanced backbone features render complex DCN heads less critical, simplifying the detection end.

## Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Resolution:** Images will be resized to `1024x1024` with padding to preserve the aspect ratio.
    *   **Normalization:** Crucially, pixel values will be **normalized using the standard ImageNet mean and standard deviation**. This strict adherence to the pretrained model's input statistics is required to prevent the model failure observed when normalization was omitted **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric Only:** The pipeline will strictly utilize **Geometric Augmentations** (Random Scaling, Random Shifting, and Random Rotation).
    *   **Exclusion:** I will explicitly **exclude** all photometric augmentations (Gaussian noise, blur, color jitter). As determined in previous experiments, these distortions degrade the high-frequency edge information (strokes) that constitutes the primary signal for character recognition **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.

## Training
*   **Loss Functions:**
    *   **Heatmap:** Penalty-Reduced Focal Loss to handle the foreground-background imbalance.
    *   **Classification:** Cross-Entropy Loss (calculated only at positive ground truth center locations).
    *   **Regression:** L1 Loss for offsets and bounding box sizes.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Duration:** Training will be set to **35-40 epochs**. Since the ResNet-50-DCN adds minimal computational overhead compared to the standard ResNet-50 (and significantly less than Swin-B), we can afford a full training schedule to ensure the high-cardinality classification head converges **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Forward pass test images through the Deformable ResNet-50 CenterNet.
*   **Decoding:** Apply 3x3 Max Pooling to the heatmap to extract local peaks. Select the top 1,200 peaks with confidence > 0.1.
*   **Submission:** Map the predicted center coordinates from the model space back to the original image dimensions. This anchor-free approach ensures direct alignment with the evaluation metric **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
