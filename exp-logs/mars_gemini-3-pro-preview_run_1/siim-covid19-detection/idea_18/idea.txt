Based on the accumulated lessons and the need to address the specific "texture discrimination" weakness (Typical vs. Atypical) without incurring the overfitting penalties of heavy architectures, I propose the **Mean-Variance Pooled ResNet34 U-Net with Stochastic Depth**.

This strategy evolves the baseline by upgrading the backbone capacity (ResNet34) and introducing a statistical pooling head (Mean+Variance) to capture texture, while using Stochastic Depth to strictly enforce the regularization that failed in previous heavier attempts.

# Model
**Architecture:** **ResNet34 U-Net with Stochastic Depth and Dual-Pooling Head**
*   **Backbone (Capacity & Regularization):** Upgrade the backbone to **ResNet34** (pre-trained on ImageNet).
    *   *Rationale:* Previous lessons established that **ResNet18** lacks the capacity to distinguish subtle "Atypical" textures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008), while heavier backbones failed due to overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012). To resolve this, I will apply **Stochastic Depth (DropPath)** to the residual blocks of the ResNet34 encoder. Unlike DropBlock (which destroys spatial features, Cite solution_lesson_node_00052), Stochastic Depth randomly drops entire residual paths during training, implicitly training an ensemble of shallower networks. This provides the necessary structural regularization to prevent overfitting while allowing the full depth of ResNet34 to be utilized for texture extraction during inference.
*   **Input Stem (Exploitation):** Retain the **Standard 7x7 Convolutional Stem**.
    *   *Rationale:* Strictly avoid "Deep Stems" or Anti-Aliased Downsampling. The standard stem acts as a necessary low-pass filter for medical image noise, preserving the segmentation performance on diffuse opacities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050, solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Classification Head (Structural Innovation):** Replace the standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) with a **Dual-Pooling Head (Mean + Standard Deviation)**.
    *   *Mechanism:* Compute both the Global Average ($\mu$) and the Global Standard Deviation ($\sigma$) of the bottleneck feature maps. Concatenate these vectors and pass them through a **Single Linear Layer**.
    *   *Rationale:* "Atypical" and "Indeterminate" appearances are distinguished by their texture (intensity variation) rather than just presence. GAP captures mean intensity but discards variance. GeM pooling failed because it focused on high-intensity artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046). By explicitly encoding the standard deviation, we provide the classifier with a direct statistical measure of texture contrast without adding the depth or complexity that causes task decoupling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00035).
*   **Decoder (Exploitation):** Use the standard **U-Net Decoder** with **Bilinear Upsampling** and direct symmetric skip connections.
    *   *Rationale:* Direct concatenation is strictly preferred over FPN aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021) or Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force global context learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Consistency Constraint:** Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing label noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Exclusion:** Strictly avoid MixUp regularization, as it degrades dense prediction performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).

# Training
**Strategy**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax) to enforce mutual exclusivity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively. Strictly avoid Dice Loss due to instability on empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** (1.0 for Classification, 10.0 for Segmentation). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the shared encoder to learn generalizable spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW.
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing** to dynamically adjust the learning rate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Batch Size & Scaling:** Maximize batch size (e.g., 32) and strictly apply the **Linear Scaling Rule** to the learning rate to prevent iteration deficits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Duration:** Train for **20 epochs** to ensure full convergence of the heavier backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on the **Composite Validation mAP** (averaging study and image metrics), mirroring the competition metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Prediction Formatting:** Output probabilities for **all 4 study classes** (not just ArgMax) to maximize the area under the precision-recall curve (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Apply horizontal flip TTA during inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
