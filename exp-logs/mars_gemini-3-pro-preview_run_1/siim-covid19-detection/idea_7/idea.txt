Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Deeply Supervised ResNet18 Multi-Task U-Net**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the baseline. It retains the lightweight backbone and U-Net architecture that proved superior to heavier models (FPN, EfficientNet) but introduces **Deep Supervision** to the decoder. This structural change addresses the gradient flow limitations of the standard U-Net, forcing the shared encoder to maintain high spatial fidelity across all resolutions without incurring the overfitting penalties of complex backbones.

### Model
**Architecture:** **Deeply Supervised ResNet18 U-Net**
*   **Backbone (Encoder):** Retain the **ResNet18** backbone (pre-trained on ImageNet). Previous experiments proved that heavier backbones (EfficientNet-B3/B4) and FPN architectures overfit the coarse bounding box annotations and underperform due to optimization difficulties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012, solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Decoder:** Use a standard **U-Net Decoder** with symmetric skip connections. Direct concatenation is strictly preferred over Attention Gates or FPN aggregation, as the latter methods degraded performance on diffuse opacity targets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015, solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Structural Innovation (Deep Supervision):** Modify the decoder to output segmentation masks at multiple resolutions, not just the final layer. Attach auxiliary segmentation heads (1x1 convolutions) to the decoder feature maps at **1/2** and **1/4** resolutions.
    *   *Rationale:* In a standard Multi-Task U-Net, the gradient signal from the segmentation loss must propagate all the way from the final pixel output back to the early encoder layers. Deep Supervision injects gradients directly into intermediate layers, forcing the encoder to learn robust, scale-invariant spatial features. This acts as a structural regularizer, improving the "Atypical" vs. "Indeterminate" feature extraction without increasing the parameter count of the classification head.
*   **Classification Head:** Maintain the **Single Linear Layer** attached to the Global Average Pooling of the bottleneck. Avoid MLP structures to prevent the head from decoupling from the encoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force global context learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Consistency Constraint:** Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets during CoarseDropout. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" that degraded performance in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

### Training
**Strategy:**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax) to enforce the mutual exclusivity of the study labels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively for all segmentation heads (final and auxiliary). Strictly avoid Dice Loss, as it causes instability on the high prevalence of empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Deep Supervision Weighting:** Calculate the total segmentation loss as a weighted sum: $L_{seg} = L_{final} + 0.5 \cdot L_{aux1} + 0.25 \cdot L_{aux2}$.
*   **Global Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** ($L_{total} = 1.0 \cdot L_{cls} + 10.0 \cdot L_{seg}$). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the shared encoder to learn generalizable spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** Adam or AdamW.
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing** to dynamically adjust the learning rate, which has been proven to improve convergence over constant schedules (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Batch Size:** Use the maximum feasible batch size (e.g., 32) and apply **Linear Scaling** to the learning rate to prevent the "iteration deficit" observed in previous scaling attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Duration:** Train for **20 epochs** to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on the **Composite Validation mAP** (averaging study and image metrics), mirroring the competition metric. This prevents "metric divergence" where a model optimizes loss but fails on the specific detection task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006, solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Apply horizontal flip TTA during inference and average the predictions to improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and the final resolution segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction.
