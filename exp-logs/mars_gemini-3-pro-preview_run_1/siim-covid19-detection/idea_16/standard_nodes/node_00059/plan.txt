Here is the implementation strategy and the complete `runfile.py` script.

### Implementation Strategy

1.  **Configuration Override**: To adhere to the "fast baseline" requirement while ensuring sufficient convergence to meet the score threshold, I will override the default `Config` to use 6 epochs and a larger batch size of 32 (leveraging the A100 GPU).
2.  **Training**: I will instantiate the provided `Trainer` class and execute the `fit()` method. This handles the training loop, validation at each epoch, EMA updates, and checkpointing of the best model.
3.  **Validation & Metric Calculation**:
    -   After training, I will reload the best model checkpoint.
    -   I will run a dedicated validation pass to calculate the exact composite mAP (Study mAP + Image mAP / 2).
    -   I will print the required string: `Final Validation Metric: <value>`.
4.  **Failure Analysis**:
    -   During the validation pass, I will track the cross-entropy loss per sample as a proxy for "error magnitude".
    -   I will compute correlations between this error and ground-truth features (specifically, the number of bounding boxes and the total area of opacities) to identify systematic weaknesses.
5.  **Inference & Submission**:
    -   If the validation metric exceeds the threshold (`0.49944536565378`), I will proceed to test set inference.
    -   **TTA**: I will apply Horizontal Flip Test-Time Augmentation. Predictions from the original and flipped images will be averaged.
    -   **Gating**: I will enforce the logical constraint: if the predicted study label is "Negative for Pneumonia", the image-level prediction is forced to "none".
    -   **Formatting**: I will construct the prediction strings for both study (all class probabilities) and image (bounding boxes) levels, matching the sample submission format, and save to `./submission/submission.csv`.
