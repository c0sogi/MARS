Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Anti-Aliased ResNet18 Multi-Task U-Net with EMA**.

This strategy introduces a structural evolution to the backbone—**Anti-Aliased Downsampling (BlurPool)**—to address the specific trade-off between spatial fidelity and noise robustness that caused previous architectural variants (Deep Stems, PixelShuffle) to fail. It combines this with **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** to stabilize the optimization trajectory, strictly adhering to the robust training protocols identified in the lessons.

# Model
**Architecture:** **Anti-Aliased ResNet18 U-Net**
*   **Backbone (Structural Innovation):** Replace the standard ResNet18 backbone with an **Anti-Aliased ResNet18**.
    *   *Mechanism:* Replace standard strided convolutions and max-pooling layers in the encoder with **BlurPool** (MaxBlurPool) operations.
    *   *Rationale:* Previous experiments revealed a critical conflict: standard ResNet18 downsampling causes aliasing (loss of shift-invariance), while "Deep Stems" (ResNet-D) introduce high-frequency noise that degrades performance on diffuse opacities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050). Anti-Aliased downsampling inserts a low-pass filter (blur) before subsampling. This strictly preserves the semantic structure required for "Atypical" texture recognition and the spatial shift-invariance needed for precise segmentation, without the artifact introduction of Deep Stems.
*   **Decoder (Exploitation):** Retain the standard **U-Net Decoder** with symmetric, direct skip connections and **Bilinear Upsampling**.
    *   *Rationale:* Direct concatenation is strictly preferred over Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015) or FPN aggregation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021). Bilinear upsampling is preferred over PixelShuffle to avoid checkerboard artifacts and ensure the spatial smoothness required for mask generation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
*   **Classification Head (Exploitation):** Use **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** attached to the final bottleneck, followed by a **Single Linear Layer**.
    *   *Rationale:* GAP is superior to GeM for diffuse opacity detection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046). The head is restricted to the deepest layer to prevent overfitting from low-level features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035) and kept shallow to prevent task decoupling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force the model to learn global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Consistency Constraint:** Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" that degraded performance in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Exclusion:** Strictly avoid MixUp regularization, as it degrades dense prediction performance on short schedules (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).

# Training
**Strategy**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax) to enforce the mutual exclusivity of the study labels (Negative, Typical, Indeterminate, Atypical) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively. Strictly avoid Dice Loss due to its instability on empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** (1.0 for Classification, 10.0 for Segmentation). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the shared encoder to learn generalizable spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW.
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing** to dynamically adjust the learning rate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Weight Averaging (New):** Implement **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** of model weights during training. This stabilizes the optimization on the small dataset and improves generalization, complementing the extended training schedule.
    *   **Conservative Learning Rate:** Initialize with a conservative base learning rate (e.g., `1e-4`) and strictly apply the **Linear Scaling Rule** if batch size increases (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020, solution_lesson_node_00041).
    *   **Duration:** Train for **20 epochs** to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on the **Composite Validation mAP** (averaging study and image metrics), mirroring the competition metric exactly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Prediction Formatting:** Output probabilities for **all 4 study classes** (not just ArgMax) to maximize the area under the precision-recall curve (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Apply horizontal flip TTA during inference and average the predictions to improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1". This couples the validation logic with the inference constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
