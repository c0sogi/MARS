Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **ASPP-Enhanced Multi-Task ResNet18 U-Net**.

This strategy addresses the central conflict identified in the lessons: the need for **richer global context** (to distinguish "Atypical" vs. "Typical" and segment large opacities) versus the failure of **heavy backbones** (EfficientNet) and **complex aggregators** (FPN/Attention) to generalize on this dataset. By integrating Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP) into the robust ResNet18 baseline, we effectively expand the receptive field to capture multi-scale context without the optimization instability of deeper networks.

### Model
**Architecture:** **ResNet18 U-Net with Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)**
*   **Backbone (Encoder):** Retain the **ResNet18** backbone (pre-trained on ImageNet). This is strictly chosen over heavier backbones (EfficientNet-B3/B4) to prevent the overfitting and optimization difficulties observed with limited data and coarse labels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Context Module (Structural Innovation):** Insert an **ASPP (Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling)** module at the bottleneck (between the encoder and decoder).
    *   *Rationale:* Previous attempts to increase capacity (EfficientNet) or aggregate features (FPN) failed because they disrupted the spatial integrity required for segmentation or overfit the noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021). ASPP uses parallel dilated convolutions to capture multi-scale context (essential for distinguishing diffuse "Atypical" patterns from focal "Typical" ones) while preserving the single-scale spatial resolution that the U-Net decoder handles best. This acts as a "contextual booster" for the lightweight backbone.
*   **Decoder:** Use the standard **U-Net Decoder** with symmetric skip connections from the ResNet18 encoder layers. Direct concatenation is preserved, avoiding the signal loss caused by Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Heads:**
    *   **Classification:** Apply Global Average Pooling (GAP) to the **ASPP output feature map**, followed by a **Single Linear Layer**. This ensures the study classifier benefits from the expanded receptive field of the ASPP module while adhering to the "Shallow Head" constraint to prevent task decoupling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    *   **Segmentation:** The decoder processes the ASPP features and outputs the pixel-wise mask via a final 1x1 convolution.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force the model to rely on the global context provided by the ASPP module rather than local textures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Consistency Constraint:** Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets during augmentation. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" that degraded performance in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

### Training
**Strategy:**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax) to enforce the mutual exclusivity of the study labels (Negative, Typical, Indeterminate, Atypical) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively. Strictly avoid Dice Loss, as it causes instability due to the high prevalence of empty masks (negative cases) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Deep Supervision:** **Do not use.** We rely on the single final output to avoid forcing intermediate layers to overfit coarse bounding box annotations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** ($L_{total} = 1.0 \cdot L_{cls} + 10.0 \cdot L_{seg}$). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the shared encoder to learn generalizable spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW.
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing** to dynamically adjust the learning rate, improving convergence over constant schedules (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Batch Size & Scaling:** Maximize the batch size (e.g., 32) and strictly apply the **Linear Scaling Rule** to the learning rate to prevent the "iteration deficit" observed in previous scaling attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Duration:** Train for **20 epochs** to ensure full convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on the **Composite Validation mAP** (averaging study and image metrics). This ensures the selected model performs well on the actual competition metric, avoiding "metric divergence" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Apply horizontal flip TTA during inference and average the predictions to improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1". This couples the validation logic with the inference constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
