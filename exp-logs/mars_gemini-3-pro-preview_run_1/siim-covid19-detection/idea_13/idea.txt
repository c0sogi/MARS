Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **ResNet18-D U-Net with Learnable GeM Pooling**.

This strategy introduces a structural evolution to the backbone and pooling mechanism to address the specific textural challenges of "Atypical" vs. "Indeterminate" classification, while strictly correcting the optimization flaws that caused previous iterations of this architecture to fail.

# Model
**Architecture:** **ResNet18-D U-Net with Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**
*   **Backbone (Structural Innovation):** Replace the standard ResNet18 with **ResNet18-D** (Deep Stem).
    *   *Rationale:* Standard ResNet18 uses a 7x7 stride-2 convolution in the input stem, which aggressively discards 75% of the spatial information immediately. ResNet18-D replaces this with three consecutive 3x3 convolutions. This "Deep Stem" preserves high-frequency spatial details critical for distinguishing subtle textural differences (Atypical vs. Indeterminate) and generating precise segmentation masks, without the optimization instability and overfitting risks of heavier backbones like EfficientNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041, solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Classification Head (Structural Innovation):** Replace Global Average Pooling (GAP) with **Learnable GeM Pooling**.
    *   *Rationale:* GAP dilutes the signal of small, focal opacities (Typical) by averaging them with the large healthy background. GeM introduces a learnable parameter $p$, allowing the model to dynamically tune its focus between focal salient features (approximating Max Pooling) and global textures (approximating Average Pooling). This enhances the discriminative power of the head while adhering to the "Shallow Head" constraint (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Decoder (Exploitation):** Retain the standard **U-Net Decoder** with symmetric, direct skip connections.
    *   *Rationale:* Direct concatenation is strictly preferred over Attention Gates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015), FPN (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021), or Deep Supervision (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027) to preserve the gradient flow for diffuse targets and avoid overfitting coarse annotations.

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Input:** Resize all images to **512x512** pixels.
*   **Label-Consistent CoarseDropout:** Apply `CoarseDropout` with large occlusion dimensions (~10% of image size) to force the model to learn global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Consistency Constraint:** Crucially, set **`mask_fill_value=0`** for the segmentation targets. This ensures that when an opacity is occluded in the image, it is also removed from the ground truth mask, preventing the "label noise" that degraded performance in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Exclusion:** Strictly avoid MixUp regularization, as it degrades dense prediction performance on short schedules (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).

# Training
**Strategy**
*   **Loss Function:**
    *   **Study-Level:** Use **CrossEntropyLoss** (Softmax) to enforce the mutual exclusivity of the study labels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Image-Level:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** exclusively. Strictly avoid Dice Loss due to instability on empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Loss Weighting:** Apply a **1:10 weighting ratio** (1.0 for Classification, 10.0 for Segmentation). This prioritizes the dense prediction task, forcing the shared encoder to learn generalizable spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW.
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Cosine Annealing** to dynamically adjust the learning rate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Batch Size & Scaling:** Maximize the batch size (e.g., 32) and strictly apply the **Linear Scaling Rule** to the learning rate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Conservative Learning Rate:** Crucially, use a conservative base learning rate (e.g., `1e-4`) rather than aggressive scaling. Previous attempts with ResNet18-D failed specifically because a 5x learning rate increase caused catastrophic overfitting; the architectural upgrade requires a stable optimization trajectory to succeed (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** Select the best model based on the **Composite Validation mAP** (averaging study and image metrics), mirroring the competition metric exactly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Prediction Formatting:** Output probabilities for **all 4 study classes** (not just ArgMax) to maximize the area under the precision-recall curve (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Apply horizontal flip TTA during inference and average the predictions to improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Gated Prediction:**
    1.  Generate study-level class and segmentation mask.
    2.  **Logical Gating:** If the predicted study class is "Negative for Pneumonia", explicitly override the image-level prediction to "none 1 0 0 1 1" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
    3.  **Box Extraction:** For non-negative studies, threshold the probability mask and generate bounding boxes via contour extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
