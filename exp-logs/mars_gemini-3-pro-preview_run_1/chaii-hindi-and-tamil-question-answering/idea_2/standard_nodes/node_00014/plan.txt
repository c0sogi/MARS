To optimize the solution, I will implement the following changes based on the provided lessons and data analysis:

1.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**:
    *   **Increase `max_answer_length` to 100**: The data analysis revealed answers up to 51 words long. The previous limit of 30 tokens was likely truncating correct answers, hurting recall.
    *   **Increase `max_length` to 512**: To better handle the long document contexts (mean ~1600 words) and minimize the chance of the answer being split across windows or separated from its context (Cite Lesson 1).
    *   **Increase `doc_stride` to 256**: To maintain efficient sliding window coverage with the larger window size.
    *   **Increase `epochs` to 7**: The previous training curve showed monotonic improvement up to the final epoch, suggesting the model had not yet converged.

2.  **Ensemble Strategy (Cite Lesson 13)**:
    *   Given the high run-to-run variance observed in small-sample fine-tuning, I will train an ensemble of 3 models using different random seeds.
    *   I will modify `runfile.py` to train these models sequentially and `trainer.py` to average their logits during inference. This is a robust method to boost performance and stability without changing the underlying architecture.

def eval_fn(model, data_loader, raw_examples, features_df, device):

    Evaluates the model on the validation set using Jaccard score.

    """
