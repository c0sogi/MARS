My optimization strategy focuses on addressing the underfitting observed in the previous run (Validation Jaccard ~0.558) by extending the training duration and refining the optimization schedule, while managing the computational budget.

1.  **Disable Adversarial Training (FGM)**: As noted in **Lesson 00034**, adversarial training significantly increases the computational cost per step and requires a much longer training horizon to converge. Given the time constraints, standard training allows for more epochs and better convergence than under-trained adversarial training.
2.  **Increase Learning Rate for Heads**: I will increase the base learning rate to `5e-5`. Following **Lesson 00031**, using a higher learning rate for the randomly initialized task heads (while decaying it for the backbone via LLRD) is crucial for rapid convergence in differential fine-tuning.
3.  **Extend Training Epochs**: I will increase the number of epochs from 3 to 7. **Lesson 00032** demonstrates that differential learning rate schedules require more epochs to align the backbone with the heads, and 7 epochs was the sweet spot for the best performing model (0.6003 Jaccard).
