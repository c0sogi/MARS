Based on the empirical findings in **Lesson 00024**, the switch from the generalist `xlm-roberta-base` to the region-specific `google/muril-base-cased` resulted in a significant performance degradation (Validation Jaccard dropped from ~0.48 to ~0.43). The lesson advises prioritizing high-capacity generalist multilingual models for low-resource extractive QA. Therefore, I will revert the model architecture to `xlm-roberta-base`.

Additionally, I will address **Lesson 00010**, which highlighted performance bottlenecks and potential errors when loading nested structures (like `offset_mapping`) from Parquet caches as Numpy arrays. I will add an explicit sanitization step in the `QADataset` class to ensure these are converted to native Python lists before tensor creation.

Finally, I will update the `IDEA_NAME` to ensure a clean execution environment without cache conflicts.
