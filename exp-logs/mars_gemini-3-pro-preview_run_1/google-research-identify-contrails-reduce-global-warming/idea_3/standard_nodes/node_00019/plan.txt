Based on the lessons learned, I will implement a targeted optimization strategy focusing on resolution preservation, loss balancing, and metric-driven scheduling.

1.  **Resolution Preservation (Cite Lesson 00015):** The previous solution used a standard ResNet34 encoder which downsamples the input by 32x (8x8 bottleneck). For thin contrails, this spatial compression is too aggressive. I will modify the `DilatedResNetUNet` to use dilated convolutions in the final encoder stage (`replace_stride_with_dilation=[False, False, True]`), maintaining a 16x downsampling factor (16x16 bottleneck) similar to the successful custom U-Net from Idea 1.
2.  **Loss Rebalancing (Cite Lesson 00006):** I will adjust the loss weights to prioritize Focal Loss (0.75) over Dice Loss (0.25). This stabilizes gradients in the presence of extreme class imbalance, preventing the model from collapsing to an all-background solution or oscillating due to unstable Dice gradients.
3.  **Metric-Coupled Scheduling (Cite Lesson 00018):** I will switch the learning rate scheduler to monitor the `val_dice` metric (maximizing it) rather than `val_loss`. This ensures learning rate decay occurs only when the segmentation quality actually plateaus, allowing for fine-tuning of decision boundaries.
4.  **Extended Training:** I will increase the training duration from 3 to 20 epochs to allow the model sufficient time to converge, as the previous 3-epoch run was insufficient for high performance.
