The `TypeError` is caused by the inclusion of the `verbose` argument in the `ReduceLROnPlateau` constructor, which has been deprecated and removed in recent PyTorch versions. I will remove `verbose=True` from the scheduler initialization in `library/train.py` to resolve this compatibility issue. The current learning rate is already manually logged later in the training loop, so removing this argument will not result in a loss of information. Cite debug_lesson_2.
