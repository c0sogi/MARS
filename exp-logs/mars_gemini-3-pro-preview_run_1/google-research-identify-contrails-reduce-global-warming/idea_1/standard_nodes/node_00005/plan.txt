The optimization strategy focuses on two key areas: loss function alignment and training dynamics. First, I will rebalance the loss weights to an equal 1:1 ratio between Focal Loss and Dice Loss (0.5 each). This directly addresses the regression observed in the previous solution where down-weighting the metric-aligned Dice loss degraded performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004). Second, I will introduce a `ReduceLROnPlateau` learning rate scheduler to dynamically decay the learning rate when the validation Dice score plateaus, helping the model converge to a better optimum. To accommodate this fine-tuning, I will increase the training duration from 15 to 20 epochs.
