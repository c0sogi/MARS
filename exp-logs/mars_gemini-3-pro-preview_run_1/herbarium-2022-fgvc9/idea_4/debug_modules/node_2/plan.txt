The issue is caused by the initialization of `self.best_f1` to `0.0` in the `Trainer` class combined with the strict inequality check `if val_f1 > self.best_f1`. In the demonstration run, the model achieves a validation F1 score of exactly `0.0`. Since `0.0 > 0.0` is `False`, the model checkpoint is never saved. This leads to a `FileNotFoundError` when `generate_submission` subsequently attempts to load `best_model.pth`. The fix is to initialize `self.best_f1` to `-1.0` so that an F1 score of `0.0` is recognized as an improvement, ensuring the model is saved at least once.
