Based on the lessons learned, I will implement a high-capacity fine-tuning strategy to significantly improve the Macro F1 score.

1.  **Architecture Upgrade**: I will replace the ResNet-18 backbone with **ResNet-50** initialized with **IMAGENET1K_V2** weights. The previous solution underfitted (F1 ~0.325), and Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00006` demonstrates that scaling to ResNet-50 with modern weights addresses the capacity bottleneck for 15,501 classes, boosting F1 to >0.60.
2.  **Scheduler Integration**: I will add a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate Scheduler** to the `Trainer` and ensure `scheduler.step()` is called at the end of each epoch (after validation). This optimizes the convergence trajectory during the short 4-epoch schedule (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00006`, `solution_lesson_node_00008`).
3.  **Conservative Augmentation**: I will adjust the `RandomResizedCrop` scale from `(0.6, 1.0)` back to `(0.8, 1.0)`. Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00005` showed that aggressive cropping (0.6) caused a performance regression in this fine-grained task by removing diagnostic features.
4.  **Hyperparameters**: I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to **128** to accommodate the larger ResNet-50 model within the GPU memory limits while maintaining the same training duration (~2 hours, well within the 4-hour limit).
