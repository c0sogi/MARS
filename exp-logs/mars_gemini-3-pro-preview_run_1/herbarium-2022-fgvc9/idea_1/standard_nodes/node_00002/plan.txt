I will optimize the solution by transitioning from **Linear Probing** to **Fine-Tuning** the ResNet-18 backbone. This allows the model to learn domain-specific features for the 15,501 plant species, addressing the limitation of generic ImageNet features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). To support this, I will implement **Differential Learning Rates** (lower for the backbone, higher for the head) to preserve pre-trained knowledge while adapting the classifier. Additionally, I will introduce **Data Augmentation** (RandomResizedCrop, HorizontalFlip) to improve generalization and increase the number of training epochs to 4 to ensure convergence within the time limit.
