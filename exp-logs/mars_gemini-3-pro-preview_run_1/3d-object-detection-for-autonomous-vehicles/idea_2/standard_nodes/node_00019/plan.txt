The issue is that the validation metric calculation in `runfile.py` compares predicted bounding boxes, which are in the **Sensor (Ego) Frame**, directly against ground truth annotations, which are in the **Global World Frame**. This coordinate frame mismatch results in zero Intersection over Union (IoU) and a metric of 0.0. To fix this, I will modify `library/dataset.py` to expose the `sensor_to_global` transformation matrix for validation samples. Then, I will update `runfile.py` to apply this transformation to the predictions before evaluation, aligning them with the ground truth.
