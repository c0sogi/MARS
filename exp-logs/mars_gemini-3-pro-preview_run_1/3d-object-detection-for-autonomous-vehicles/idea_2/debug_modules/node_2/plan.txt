The `RuntimeError` is caused by a tensor shape mismatch in the DLA backbone. The current `INPUT_SIZE` of `(250, 250)` results in an odd feature map dimension (125) at Level 1. When this is downsampled to Level 2 (stride 2), the convolutional path produces a dimension of 63, while the max-pooling path produces 62, causing the element-wise addition to fail. To fix this, I will update `INPUT_SIZE` in `library/config.py` to `(256, 256)`. This value is divisible by the network's total stride (32), ensuring that all feature map dimensions remain even and consistent throughout the architecture.
