Based on the lessons learned, specifically **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00024**, the primary bottleneck in the previous solution was the computational cost of high-resolution (384x384) training, which forced an early stop (2 epochs) and prevented convergence. The lesson demonstrates that prioritizing **throughput** (iterations) over **resolution** yields better results under strict time constraints. A lower resolution (224x224) allows for a larger batch size and significantly more epochs within the same runtime budget, enabling the model to learn more effective decision boundaries.

Therefore, the optimization strategy is:
1.  **Reduce Input Resolution**: Revert `IMG_SIZE` from 384 to 224 in `library/config.py`.
2.  **Increase Batch Size**: Increase `BATCH_SIZE` from 16 to 64 to fully utilize the A100 GPU with the smaller image size, further improving throughput.
3.  **Increase Training Duration**: Update `runfile.py` to train for the full 10 epochs defined in `Config`, rather than the hardcoded 2 epochs.

This approach aims to recover the performance of the "Current Best Solution" (0.642 F1) referenced in the lessons by allowing the model to converge.
