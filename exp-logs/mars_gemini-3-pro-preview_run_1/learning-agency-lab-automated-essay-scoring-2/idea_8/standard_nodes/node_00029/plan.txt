The execution failed with a `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` during the supervised training phase. This is a known conflict between the default "reentrant" implementation of gradient checkpointing in the `transformers` library and PyTorch's Mixed Precision (`autocast`) engine. As explained in **Cite debug_lesson_5**, the reentrant version obscures the computation graph from the autograd engine during the backward pass when mixed precision is active. The fix is to explicitly force the use of the non-reentrant implementation by passing `gradient_checkpointing_kwargs={"use_reentrant": False}` when enabling gradient checkpointing. I will apply this fix to the `EssayModel` in `library/modeling.py` where the error occurred, and also to `library/pretraining.py` to ensure consistency and prevent future failures in the pre-training stage.
