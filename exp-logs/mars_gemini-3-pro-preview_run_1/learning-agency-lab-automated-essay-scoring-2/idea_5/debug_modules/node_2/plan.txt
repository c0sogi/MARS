The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` during the MLM pre-training phase is caused by the use of gradient checkpointing (enabled via `TrainingArguments`) without ensuring that the model's input embeddings require gradients. As explained in **debug_lesson_2**, when gradient checkpointing is active, the inputs to the checkpointed segments must have `requires_grad=True` to maintain the autograd graph connection during the backward pass recomputation. I will fix this by calling `model.enable_input_require_grads()` in `library/pretraining.py` immediately after initializing the model.
