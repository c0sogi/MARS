The `RuntimeError` during the backward pass is caused by a conflict between **Gradient Checkpointing** and the model's caching mechanism (`use_cache=True`). Gradient checkpointing requires re-computing the forward pass during backpropagation, but caching stores intermediate states that become invalid or freed, leading to the error. To fix this, I will explicitly set `use_cache=False` in the model configuration for both the MLM model in `library/pretraining.py` and the supervised `EssayScorer` in `library/modeling.py`.

Additionally, I will address the potential FP16 overflow issue identified in the error analysis. In `library/modeling.py`, the masking value `-1e9` exceeds the range of 16-bit floating-point numbers (approx. -65,504). I will replace it with `-1e4` to ensure numerical stability while maintaining effective masking (Cite debug_lesson_1).
