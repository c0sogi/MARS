The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` occurs because the custom training loop uses gradient checkpointing without explicitly ensuring that the model's input embeddings require gradients. When `gradient_checkpointing_enable()` is used outside of the Hugging Face `Trainer`, the input embeddings (which are the inputs to the checkpointed encoder) often remain detached from the computation graph (i.e., `requires_grad=False`). This prevents the autograd engine from properly tracking the inputs needed for the re-computation step during the backward pass.

To fix this, I will modify `library/model.py` to explicitly call `enable_input_require_grads()` on the backbone model immediately after enabling gradient checkpointing. This ensures the embeddings are treated as differentiable tensors, maintaining the necessary graph connections for checkpointing to work correctly in a custom loop (Cite debug_lesson_2).
