This script performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the training dataset provided in the `./metadata` directory. It focuses on the text modality of the essay data.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Setup & Loading**: The script sets random seeds for reproducibility and loads the `train.csv` from the metadata directory to ensure data integrity and avoid leakage from the test set.
2.  **Target Analysis**: It analyzes the `score` column, calculating the distribution of scores (class balance) and statistical properties like skewness and kurtosis to understand the target variable's nature (ordinal/categorical).
3.  **Text Analysis**:
    *   **Lengths**: It computes character counts and word counts for every essay to understand the distribution of essay lengths.
    *   **Vocabulary**: It uses `CountVectorizer` to extract the vocabulary from the training corpus, reporting the total unique vocabulary size and the count of rare words (appearing only once), which serves as a proxy for Out-Of-Vocabulary (OOV) potential.
4.  **Feature Relationships**: It investigates the relationship between the extracted meta-features (text lengths) and the target score. This includes calculating Pearson correlation coefficients and aggregating mean word counts by score class to see if longer essays tend to receive higher scores.
5.  **Output**: The results are printed to standard output in a clean, formatted text report.
