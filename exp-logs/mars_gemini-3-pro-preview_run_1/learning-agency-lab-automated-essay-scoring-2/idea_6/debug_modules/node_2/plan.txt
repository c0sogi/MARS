The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` occurs because the custom training loop uses gradient checkpointing without ensuring that the model's input embeddings require gradients. In PyTorch's gradient checkpointing, if the inputs do not require gradients, the computation graph can become disconnected or intermediate buffers needed for the re-computation step during the backward pass may be prematurely freed. To fix this, we must explicitly enable gradient requirements for the inputs of the backbone model. This is a necessary step when using gradient checkpointing outside of the Hugging Face `Trainer` (which handles this automatically). I will update `library/model.py` to call `enable_input_require_grads()` when checkpointing is enabled, citing `debug_lesson_2`.
