The `AssertionError` in `runfile.py` is caused by the failure to generate a model checkpoint during training. In `library/trainer.py`, the `best_score` variable is initialized to `0.0`. The demo training run achieves a validation MAP@5 score of exactly `0.0`. The logic `if val_score > best_score` evaluates to `False` (`0.0 > 0.0`), preventing the `save_path` block from executing. To ensure that a checkpoint is saved even if the initial performance is at the metric's lower bound, `best_score` must be initialized to a value strictly less than `0.0` (e.g., `-1.0`).
