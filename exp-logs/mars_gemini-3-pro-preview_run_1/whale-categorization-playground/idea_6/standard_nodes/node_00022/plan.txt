Based on the lessons learned, specifically `solution_lesson_node_00020` and `solution_lesson_node_00016`, I will revert the backbone from `densenet169` to `densenet121`. The compact nature of DenseNet121 with its dense feature reuse has proven more effective for this high-cardinality, few-shot task than deeper models.

Additionally, I will optimize the hyperparameters:
1.  **Augmentation**: Reduce `AUG_ROTATION` from 20 to 15 degrees. `solution_lesson_node_00014` highlights that aggressive geometric distortions degrade metric learning performance.
2.  **ArcFace Scale**: Increase `ARCFACE_SCALE` from 30.0 to 45.0. With over 4,000 classes, a larger scale factor helps distribute embeddings more effectively on the hypersphere, potentially improving the margin between classes.
