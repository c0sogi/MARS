1.  **Revert to Single Model Training**: I will switch from a 3-model ensemble to a single model. The previous ensemble members were undertrained (15 epochs) due to time constraints and performed worse than a single well-trained model. Training one model allows me to increase the epoch count to ensure convergence (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00026`).
2.  **Remove Label Smoothing**: I will set `LABEL_SMOOTHING` to 0.0. Lessons indicate that label smoothing degrades performance when used with ArcFace on datasets dominated by singleton classes, as it dilutes the gradient signal needed to enforce margins (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00013`, `solution_lesson_node_00024`).
3.  **Disable Dropout**: I will set `DROPOUT` to 0.0. Margin-based losses like ArcFace act as strong regularizers; adding high dropout caused underfitting in previous experiments (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00030`).
4.  **Conservative Augmentation**: I will reduce the rotation limit to 15 degrees and reduce the probability of geometric augmentations (ShiftScaleRotate) from 1.0 to 0.5. Aggressive, "always-on" geometric distortions were found to be detrimental in few-shot metric learning (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`, `solution_lesson_node_00030`).
5.  **Increase Training Duration**: I will increase the training epochs in `runfile.py` from 15 to 25 to allow the single model to fully converge.
