Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** applied to a **DenseNet121** backbone. This structural innovation addresses the high variance inherent in singleton-heavy classification and the "undertrained ensemble" trap observed in previous attempts, offering the generalization benefits of ensembling within a single training trajectory.

# Model
I propose a **DenseNet121** architecture equipped with a **Projected ArcFace Head**, optimized via **Stochastic Weight Averaging**.

*   **Backbone:** **DenseNet121**.
    *   *Rationale:* This architecture is empirically proven to outperform modern isotropic designs (ConvNeXt) and deeper variants (DenseNet169) in this specific few-shot, fine-grained regime due to its efficient feature reuse **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016, solution_lesson_node_00020**.
*   **Pooling:** **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**.
    *   *Rationale:* We explicitly retain GAP and reject specialized pooling (GeM), which was shown to overfit to background artifacts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**.
*   **Neck (Structural Retention):** **Projection Head**.
    *   *Structure:* `Linear (Features -> 512)` $\to$ `Batch Normalization`.
    *   *Rationale:* This learnable bottleneck is critical for adapting the semantic features of the backbone to the geometric constraints of the ArcFace loss. Omitting it leads to severe underfitting **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Head:** **ArcFace (Additive Angular Margin)**.
    *   *Rationale:* Superior to Cross-Entropy for high-cardinality classification by enforcing intra-class compactness **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.

# Data
*   **Splitting Strategy:** Strictly maintain the **Singleton-Inclusive Split**. All classes with only 1 sample must be in the training set to prevent zero-shot validation errors **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Resolution:** Fixed **320x320**.
    *   *Rationale:* This resolution strikes the optimal balance, avoiding the information loss of 224x224 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005** and the overfitting risks of 448x448 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** **Conservative** Affine transformations (Rotation $\pm 20^\circ$, Scale 0.9-1.1) and Horizontal Flips. Aggressive distortions are avoided to preserve feature integrity **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.
    *   **Photometric:** Brightness and Contrast adjustments only. Explicitly **exclude** Saturation and Hue jitter, as chromatic noise degrades performance in this domain **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.

# Training
I propose a **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** training protocol. This replaces the standard "train until early stopping" or "train N separate models" approaches.

1.  **Phase 1 (Convergence):** Train the model using the **AdamW** optimizer and a standard **Cosine Annealing** scheduler for **12 epochs**.
    *   *Rationale:* Previous lessons indicate the model reaches peak performance around epoch 12 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.
2.  **Phase 2 (SWA):** Continue training for an additional **10 epochs** with a **Cyclic Learning Rate**. At the end of each cycle, capture a "snapshot" of the model weights and update a running average of the weights.
    *   *Rationale:* SWA finds a flatter minimum in the loss landscape, which significantly improves generalization and robustness for few-shot classes without the computational cost of training multiple independent models.
3.  **Regularization Strategy:**
    *   **Enable Label Smoothing (0.1):** Critical for preventing overfitting on singleton classes **Cite solution_lesson_node_00024**.
    *   **Disable Dropout:** Explicitly remove Dropout from the projection neck. Previous experiments showed that combining Dropout with Label Smoothing leads to underfitting in this architecture **Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**.

# Evaluation
*   **Protocol:** Implement **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** using Horizontal Flips for **both** the validation loop and final inference.
    *   *Rationale:* Consistency between validation and inference is crucial for accurate model selection **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
*   **Inference:**
    *   Use the final **SWA-averaged weights** for inference.
    *   Generate embeddings/logits for the original and flipped test images.
    *   Average the results and select the top 5 `Id`s.
