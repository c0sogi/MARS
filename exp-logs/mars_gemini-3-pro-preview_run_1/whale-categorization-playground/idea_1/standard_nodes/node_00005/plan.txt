To optimize the solution, I will upgrade the model architecture and training configuration to leverage the available compute resources (A100 GPU) and improve fine-grained classification performance.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Model Upgrade**: Replace the `ResNet18` backbone with `ResNet50` in `library/model.py`. The deeper architecture is better suited for capturing the subtle texture details of whale flukes.
2.  **Regularization**: Add `weight_decay=1e-4` to the Adam optimizer in `library/model.py`. This is essential to prevent the larger ResNet50 model from overfitting the dataset, especially given the class imbalance.
3.  **Resolution Increase**: In `runfile.py`, increase `IMAGE_SIZE` from 224 to 384. Higher resolution inputs allow the model to see finer details critical for distinguishing similar whales.
4.  **Training Schedule**: Increase `EPOCHS` to 20 and `PATIENCE` to 5 in `runfile.py` to allow the larger model sufficient time to converge, supported by the Cosine Annealing scheduler (Cite **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00003**).
5.  **Data Loading**: Increase `num_workers` to 8 in `runfile.py` to ensure the GPU is not starved of data due to the increased image decoding/resizing load (384x384).
