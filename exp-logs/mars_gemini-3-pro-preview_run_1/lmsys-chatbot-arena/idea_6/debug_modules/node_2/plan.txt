The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU ran out of memory during the forward pass, specifically when processing the second branch of the Siamese network while holding the activations of the first branch in memory. To fix this, I will implement three optimizations: 1) Enable gradient checkpointing in the DeBERTa backbone to trade compute for memory, 2) Refactor the `forward` method in `library/model.py` to process the two branches sequentially and immediately delete the heavy backbone outputs (`out_a`) before processing the second branch, and 3) Reduce the batch sizes in `runfile.py` and `library/config.py` to ensure the model fits within the 16GB VRAM limit.
