To optimize the solution, I will implement a three-pronged strategy focusing on maximizing the utility of the patch-based training and reducing inference variance:

1.  **Extended Training with Cosine Annealing**: Since the dataset consists of random crops extracted from a small number of images, the effective dataset size is virtually infinite. The previous 200 epochs were insufficient to fully exploit this. I will increase `NUM_EPOCHS` to 1500 and switch the scheduler from `ReduceLROnPlateau` to `CosineAnnealingLR`. This allows the model to see many more variations of noise and texture while smoothly decaying the learning rate to fine-tune the weights.
2.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**: To further reduce the reconstruction error (RMSE), I will implement 4-fold TTA (Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, and 180-degree Rotation) during both the final validation check and the submission generation. This averages out random prediction errors and enforces equivariance.
3.  **Performance Consistency**: By applying TTA in the validation loop of `runfile.py`, the metric used to decide whether to overwrite the submission will accurately reflect the enhanced inference capability.
