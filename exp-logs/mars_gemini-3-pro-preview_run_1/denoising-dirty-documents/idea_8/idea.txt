Based on the analysis of the provided lessons and the specific constraints of the text denoising task, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces an **ASPP-Enhanced Shallow U-Net** to resolve the "Depth-Resolution vs. Crop Diversity" conflict identified in Lessons 26 and 27, while leveraging the robust training and inference protocols established in Lesson 28.

# Model
**Architecture: ASPP-Enhanced Shallow U-Net**
I propose a structural innovation that integrates **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** into the bottleneck of the successful **3-Level Shallow U-Net**.
*   **Backbone:** We will utilize the **3-Level Encoder-Decoder** structure (32 $\to$ 64 $\to$ 128 filters).
    *   *Rationale:* Lesson 18 demonstrated that a 3-level network (RMSE 0.0122) outperforms a 4-level network (RMSE 0.0176) on this small dataset. Lesson 26 identified that 4-level networks fail because the bottleneck resolution ($10 \times 10$ pixels for 160px patches) is too coarse to preserve spatial fidelity.
*   **ASPP Bottleneck (Structural Innovation):** We will replace the standard convolutional bottleneck with an **ASPP block** containing parallel convolutions with different dilation rates (e.g., 1, 2, 4, 8).
    *   *Rationale:* This resolves the conflict between **Capacity** and **Resolution**. It allows the model to expand its receptive field to match that of a deeper network (addressing the capacity limit noted in Lesson 24) without performing an additional downsampling operation. This preserves the critical $20 \times 20$ bottleneck resolution required for accurate text reconstruction (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}) while capturing the broader context needed to distinguish noise from signal.
*   **Upsampling:** We will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling followed by Convolution** to avoid checkerboard artifacts (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00009}).
*   **Prediction Target:** The model will predict the **Clean Image** directly, avoiding the "Residual Learning" approach which failed due to insufficient abstraction power in previous experiments (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00023}).

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Optimal Patch Size:** We will train on **$160 \times 160$ pixel random crops**.
    *   *Rationale:* This size is the optimal trade-off. It is large enough to provide necessary spatial context (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}) but small enough relative to the image size ($~540 \times 360$) to allow for massive **crop diversity**. This avoids the failure of Lesson 27, where 320px patches restricted randomness and caused overfitting (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027}).
*   **Aggressive Geometric Augmentation:** We will apply random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and 90-degree rotations to exploit the rotational invariance of the noise patterns (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}).
*   **Deterministic Loading:** We will implement a `worker_init_fn` to seed the data loader workers, ensuring that the random cropping is fully reproducible (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}).

# Training
**Strategy: Fully Converged Seed-Averaging Ensemble**
We will train **5 independent instances** of the ASPP-Enhanced Shallow U-Net on the full training set, using different random seeds.
*   **Full Convergence:** Each model will be trained for **1000 epochs**.
    *   *Rationale:* Lesson 12 proved that training a single model to convergence is superior to ensembling undertrained models. Lesson 16 showed that 1000 epochs are necessary for the loss to settle into a sharp minimum. With the lightweight 3-level architecture, this is computationally feasible within the 24-hour limit.
*   **Optimization Velocity:** We will use the **Adam** optimizer with a **high learning rate (1e-3)** and **small batch size (16)** to maximize the frequency of weight updates (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}).
*   **Scheduler:** We will use **Cosine Annealing** with `T_max=1000`. This decouples the scheduler from early stopping, ensuring the learning rate decays fully to zero to refine fine-grained details (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}).
*   **Loss Function:** Mean Squared Error (MSE).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **D4 Group Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, we will generate predictions for **8 geometric views** (Original, Rot90, Rot180, Rot270, and their horizontal flips). These will be inversely transformed and averaged.
    *   *Rationale:* Lesson 28 demonstrated that extending TTA from 4 views to 8 views (the full D4 symmetry group) provides a critical reduction in variance, pushing the RMSE lower than any single model could achieve (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00028}).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The TTA-averaged outputs from all **5 fully converged models** will be averaged together to produce the final pixel intensities. This combines the low bias of the converged ASPP models with the variance reduction of the ensemble.
