Based on the analysis of the provided lessons and the failure modes of previous ideas, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution synthesizes the superior generalization of the **Shallow U-Net**, the convergence benefits of **Long Training Schedules**, and the variance reduction of **Ensembling**, while explicitly avoiding the architectural pitfalls (Deep Supervision, Residual Prediction, Transposed Convolutions) identified in earlier experiments.

# Model
**Architecture: Shallow U-Net (3-Levels)**
We will utilize the **Shallow U-Net** architecture (Encoder: 32 $\to$ 64 $\to$ 128 filters) rather than deeper variants.
*   **Rationale:** Previous experiments proved that on this small dataset (92 images), a 3-level network (RMSE 0.0122) significantly outperforms a standard 4-level deep U-Net (RMSE 0.0176) by acting as a structural regularizer and preventing overfitting (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00018}).
*   **Upsampling:** We will strictly employ **Bilinear Upsampling followed by Convolution** in the decoder to avoid the checkerboard artifacts introduced by Transposed Convolutions (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00009}).
*   **Prediction Target:** The model will predict the **Clean Image (Signal)** directly. We will explicitly reject the "Residual Learning" (predicting noise) approach, as previous attempts showed that shallow networks lack the capacity to abstract the noise manifold effectively (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00023}).
*   **No Deep Supervision:** We will use a single output head, as auxiliary losses were found to over-constrain the decoder in restoration tasks (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00017}).

# Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Large Patch Training:** We will train on **random crops of $160 \times 160$ pixels** extracted from the native resolution images. This larger patch size provides the critical spatial context required for the model to distinguish text from noise, which was a key factor in achieving the current best score (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}).
*   **Aggressive Geometric Augmentation:** We will apply random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and 90-degree rotations to exploit the rotational invariance of the noise patterns (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}).
*   **Deterministic Pipeline:** We will implement a `worker_init_fn` to seed the data loader workers, ensuring that the random cropping and augmentation are fully reproducible and eliminating the non-deterministic variance observed in previous runs (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}).

# Training
**Strategy: Converged 5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensemble**
We will implement a **5-Fold Cross-Validation** pipeline where we train 5 independent instances of the Shallow U-Net, each on a different 80% subset of the training data.
*   **Full Convergence (Structural Innovation):** Unlike previous ensemble attempts that sacrificed training duration for model count (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00012}), we will train **each of the 5 models for the full 1000 epochs**.
    *   *Feasibility:* With the lightweight Shallow U-Net, 1000 epochs takes approximately 15 minutes. Training 5 folds will take ~75 minutes, fitting easily within the 24-hour budget.
    *   *Benefit:* This combines the low bias of fully converged models (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}) with the variance reduction of ensembling (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00005}), ensuring the final prediction utilizes patterns from 100% of the available training data.
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** Adam with a **high learning rate (1e-3)** and **small batch size (16)** to maximize optimization velocity (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}).
    *   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing** with `T_max=1000` (decoupled from early stopping). This ensures the learning rate decays fully to zero, allowing the model to settle into a sharp minimum (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}).
    *   **Loss Function:** Standard **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** on the pixel intensities.

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, we will generate predictions for 8 geometric views (Original, Rot90, Rot180, Rot270, and their horizontal flips). These will be inversely transformed and averaged (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00006}).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The TTA-averaged outputs from all **5 fully converged fold models** will be averaged together to produce the final submission. This robust aggregation strategy minimizes the stochastic risk of a single training run and maximizes the generalization capability of the solution.
