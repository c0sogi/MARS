To address the issues of **insufficient training** and **data leakage** identified in the error analysis, I will make two targeted changes:

1.  **Fix Data Leakage**: In `library/dataset.py`, I will modify the `get_kfold_loaders` function to load only `TRAIN_METADATA` instead of combining `TRAIN_METADATA` and `VAL_METADATA`. This ensures that the validation set used for the final failure analysis in `runfile.py` remains strictly unseen during the training of the folds. I will also rename the cache file to `"train_only_cache.npz"` to prevent loading the previously cached dataset that contained the leaked data.

2.  **Increase Training Epochs**: In `runfile.py`, I will update the training call to use the full `EPOCHS` (1000) defined in the configuration, rather than the reduced 50 epochs. This will allow the `ResidualShallowUNet` sufficient time to converge and achieve the low RMSE required by the threshold.
