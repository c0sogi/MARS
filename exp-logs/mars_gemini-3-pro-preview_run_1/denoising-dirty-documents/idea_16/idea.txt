Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the identification of the "Context-Diversity-Resolution Trilemma" (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}), I propose the following optimized strategy.

This solution introduces the **Dual-Scale Resolution-Preserved Deep Ensemble**. It structurally resolves the conflict between the need for **Deep Network Capacity** (Lesson 24), **High Spatial Resolution** (Lesson 26), and **Data Diversity** (Lesson 27) by decoupling the network's depth from its downsampling factor and deploying a scale-specialized ensemble.

# Model
**Architecture: Resolution-Preserved Deep U-Net**
We will design a custom U-Net backbone that maintains the high capacity of a deep network without suffering from the "resolution collapse" that typically plagues deep networks on small patches.
*   **Backbone Structure:** We will utilize the channel depth of a **4-Level U-Net** (Encoder: 32 $\to$ 64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 512 filters) to ensure sufficient capacity for modeling complex text structures (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00024}).
*   **Strided-Dilation Modification (Structural Innovation):** Crucially, in the final Encoder block (Block 4), we will set the convolution **Stride to 1** (instead of 2) and **Dilation to 2**.
    *   **Rationale:** This restricts the total downsampling factor to **8x** (equivalent to a 3-Level network) while preserving the 4-Level depth. This ensures that even on small $160 \times 160$ input patches, the bottleneck resolution remains a safe **$20 \times 20$ pixels** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}), effectively decoupling depth from resolution loss.
*   **Upsampling:** We will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling followed by Convolution** to avoid checkerboard artifacts (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00009}).
*   **Bounded Activation:** The final output layer will use a **Sigmoid Activation** to structurally enforce the $[0, 1]$ pixel intensity bounds, preventing regression overshoot (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00040}).

# Data
**Preprocessing: Dense Signal with Dual-Scale Patching**
We will implement a heterogeneous data pipeline that leverages the resolution-preserved architecture to maximize both context and diversity.
*   **Dense Representation:** We will use the original image intensities (Text=0, Background=1). We explicitly reject "Signal Inversion" as it was proven to degrade performance by creating sparse activation maps (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00041}).
*   **Reflection Padding:** We will strictly use **Reflection Padding** for all boundary handling to maintain statistical continuity at the edges (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00039}).
*   **Dual-Scale Streams:**
    *   **Stream A (Context Specialists):** Trained on **$320 \times 320$ pixel crops**. Thanks to the modified architecture, these models will enjoy a massive **$40 \times 40$ bottleneck**, maximizing global context (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}).
    *   **Stream B (Diversity Specialists):** Trained on **$160 \times 160$ pixel crops**. Thanks to the modified architecture, these models will maintain a safe **$20 \times 20$ bottleneck** (avoiding the collapse seen in standard 4-level nets) while benefiting from massive combinatorial crop diversity (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027}).
*   **Augmentation:** Random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and 90-degree rotations (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}), using a `worker_init_fn` for determinism (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}).

# Training
**Strategy: Converged Heterogeneous Bagging**
We will train a robust ensemble of **10 independent models** (5 instances of Stream A, 5 instances of Stream B) using different random seeds.
*   **Full Convergence:** Each model will be trained for **1000 epochs**. We prioritize training to full convergence to minimize bias and resolve high-frequency details (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00012}).
*   **Optimization Velocity:** We will use the **Adam** optimizer with a **high learning rate (1e-3)** and **small batch size (16)** to maximize the frequency of gradient updates (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}).
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing** with `T_max=1000`. This decouples the scheduler from stopping criteria, ensuring the learning rate decays fully to zero (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}).
*   **Loss Function:** Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the dense original signal.

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**
*   **Padding:** Test images will be reflection-padded to be multiples of 8 (matching the modified downsampling factor).
*   **D4 Group Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, we will generate predictions for **8 geometric views** (Original, Rot90, Rot180, Rot270, and their horizontal flips). These will be inversely transformed and averaged. This significantly reduces variance (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00028}).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The TTA-averaged outputs from all **10 fully converged models** (5 Context + 5 Diversity) will be averaged together. This combines the global coherence of the Context Stream with the robust, diversity-trained texture fidelity of the Diversity Stream, with both streams utilizing a high-capacity, resolution-safe backbone.
