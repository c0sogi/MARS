Based on the progression of previous experiments and the provided lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **Bias-Targeted Self-Training Pipeline** as the primary structural innovation.

The core insight driving this evolution is that the **Pairwise Ranking Loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)** is highly effective but bottlenecked by the scarcity of "Bias Trap" examples (specifically *Non-Toxic + Identity Mention*) in the labeled training set. By leveraging the **Multi-Task Identity Head (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004)** to "mine" these rare examples from the massive unlabeled corpus, we can artificially densify the difficult subgroups, providing the ranking objective with the necessary volume of hard negatives to optimally reshape the decision boundary.

# Proposed Solution: DeBERTa-v3-Large with Bias-Targeted Self-Training and Adversarial Robustness

### Model
**Architecture:**
We utilize **DeBERTa-v3-Large** as the backbone. Its disentangled attention mechanism is critical for distinguishing the *content* of an identity term from its *context* (e.g., neutral mention vs. slur).
**Multi-Task Learning (MTL) Heads:**
The encoder feeds into three distinct classification heads **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004)**:
1.  **Primary Toxicity Head:** Predicts the main `target`.
2.  **Auxiliary Identity Head:** Predicts the presence of identity attributes (e.g., `male`, `muslim`).
3.  **Auxiliary Identity Attack Head:** Predicts the `identity_attack` subtype.
*Innovation:* In this pipeline, the Auxiliary Identity Head serves a dual purpose: it acts as a regularizer during training (disentangling representations) and as a **"Scout"** during the self-training phase to discover unlabeled bias traps.

### Data
**Preprocessing:**
1.  **Corpus Generation:** Concatenate `comment_text` from Train and Test sets to create a large corpus for Domain-Adaptive Pretraining.
2.  **Bias-Targeted Data Mining (Structural Innovation):**
    *   We treat the Test Set and the unlabeled portion of the Train Set as a "Mining Pool".
    *   We use a preliminary version of the model (the "Scout") to predict **Identity Attributes** and **Toxicity** on this pool.
    *   We identify **"Hard Negative Candidates"**: Samples where the Scout predicts a **High Identity Probability** (e.g., > 0.5) but **Low Toxicity Probability** (e.g., < 0.1).
    *   These samples are added to the training set with pseudo-labels (Toxicity=0, Identity=1). This explicitly addresses the data scarcity that limits the effectiveness of the Ranking Loss.
3.  **Stratified Weighted Sampling:** We utilize a **Weighted Random Sampler** that heavily oversamples both the original and the newly mined "Bias Trap" subgroups. This ensures every batch contains a dense mixture of valid pairs for the ranking objective **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)**.
4.  **Bias-Centric Sample Weights:** We assign high sample weights (e.g., 5.0) to these subgroups to force the pointwise loss to focus on them **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.

### Training
The training proceeds in a **Three-Stage Pipeline**:

**Stage 1: Domain-Adaptive Pretraining (DAPT)**
We perform Masked Language Modeling (MLM) on the combined Train + Test corpus. This adapts the DeBERTa-Large backbone to the specific linguistic distribution (slang, obfuscations) of the competition data **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00010)**.

**Stage 2: Scout Training & Mining**
We quickly fine-tune a lighter version of the model (e.g., DeBERTa-v3-Base) or the DAPT model for a single epoch to act as the "Scout". We run inference on the unlabeled data to generate the **Bias-Augmented Dataset** described in the Data section.

**Stage 3: Robust Metric Optimization (Final Training)**
We fine-tune the DAPT-adapted DeBERTa-v3-Large on the **Bias-Augmented Dataset** using a **Hybrid Loss** and **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP)**.
*   **Loss Function:** $L_{total} = L_{BCE\_Weighted} + \lambda_{rank} L_{Rank} + \lambda_{aux} L_{Aux}$.
    *   **Weighted Pointwise Loss:** Focuses on absolute error using bias weights **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Pairwise Margin Ranking Loss:** We mine hard pairs $(x_{pos}, x_{neg})$ within each batch. Thanks to the Bias-Targeted Mining in Stage 2, the batch now contains a rich supply of "Non-Toxic Identity" examples ($x_{neg}$), allowing this loss to aggressively penalize the model for ranking neutral identity mentions higher than toxic background comments **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)**.
*   **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP):** We inject worst-case noise into the weights during training to flatten the loss landscape. This prevents the model from overfitting to the pseudo-labels or specific identity tokens, ensuring robust generalization **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00010)**.

### Evaluation
**Inference:**
Predictions are generated using the **Primary Toxicity Head** of the final DeBERTa-v3-Large model. The auxiliary heads and mining logic are training-time constructs used to shape the encoder's representation and decision boundary.
