This solution evolves the Multi-Task Learning approach (Idea 5) by shifting from a purely pointwise classification paradigm to a **hybrid pairwise ranking** paradigm. This directly targets the rank-based nature of the competition metric (ROC-AUC) and the specific "Bias AUC" failure modes.

# Proposed Solution: DeBERTa-v3 with Hybrid Pairwise Ranking and Semantic Disentanglement

### Model
**Architecture:**
We utilize **DeBERTa-v3-base** as the backbone. This model's disentangled attention mechanism is superior for distinguishing between the *content* of an identity term (e.g., "gay") and the *context* (e.g., neutral vs. toxic), addressing the root cause of the BPSN bias.
**Structural Innovation (Triangulated Multi-Task Heads):**
The encoder feeds into three distinct classification heads:
1.  **Primary Toxicity Head:** A linear layer predicting the main `target`.
2.  **Auxiliary Identity Head:** A multi-label classification head predicting the presence of identity attributes. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
3.  **Auxiliary Identity Attack Head:** A head predicting the `identity_attack` subtype.
*Rationale:* Retaining the auxiliary heads forces the shared encoder to maintain a rich representation of identity (to satisfy the auxiliary tasks) separate from the toxicity decision, preventing the model from collapsing identity into a simple toxicity proxy.

### Data
**Preprocessing:**
1.  **Tokenization:** Use the DeBERTa-v3 tokenizer on `comment_text`.
2.  **Bias-Centric Weight Generation:** We strictly retain the successful weighting strategy. Assign a sample weight of **5.0** to examples in the "Bias Trap" subgroups (Non-Toxic + Identity Mention, Toxic + Identity Mention) and **1.0** to others. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
3.  **Stratified Batch Sampling (New):** Instead of random sampling, use a custom sampler that ensures each training batch contains a dense mixture of "Bias Trap" examples. This is crucial for the pairwise training strategy described below, as it ensures valid pairs (e.g., Toxic vs. Non-Toxic+Identity) exist within every batch.

### Training
**Procedure:**
We introduce a **Hybrid Pointwise-Pairwise Loss** function: $L_{total} = L_{BCE} + \lambda_{rank} L_{Rank} + \lambda_{aux} L_{Aux}$.

1.  **Weighted Pointwise Loss ($L_{BCE}$):** We apply **Weighted Binary Cross-Entropy** on the soft toxicity targets using the pre-calculated bias weights. This ensures the model minimizes the absolute error on difficult subgroups. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
2.  **Auxiliary Loss ($L_{Aux}$):** Standard BCE on the Identity and Identity Attack heads to enforce semantic disentanglement. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
3.  **Bias-Aware Ranking Loss ($L_{Rank}$):** This is the key structural innovation. We apply a **Margin Ranking Loss** to pairs of examples $(x_{pos}, x_{neg})$ mined dynamically from the batch.
    *   **BPSN Pairs:** Select $x_{pos} \in \text{Toxic (No Identity)}$ and $x_{neg} \in \text{Non-Toxic (Identity)}$. Minimize $\max(0, \text{margin} - (Score(x_{pos}) - Score(x_{neg})))$.
    *   *Rationale:* The competition metric (AUC) is rank-based. Pointwise loss optimizes probability, but Ranking loss directly optimizes the relative ordering required to maximize the BPSN/BNSP metrics. This explicitly penalizes the model if it ranks a neutral identity comment higher than a toxic background comment.

### Evaluation
**Inference:**
The test set is processed through the fine-tuned DeBERTa backbone. We extract predictions **only from the Primary Toxicity Head** for the submission. The auxiliary heads and ranking logic are training-time constraints used to shape the encoder's representation and decision boundary.
