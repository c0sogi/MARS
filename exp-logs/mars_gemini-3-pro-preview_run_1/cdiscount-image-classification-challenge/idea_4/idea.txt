Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous attempts and the lessons learned, I propose a **Deeply Supervised Hierarchical ResNet-50** strategy. This approach structurally evolves the hierarchical concept by integrating it into the backbone's intermediate layers (Deep Supervision) and rigorously applies the data-centric and optimization protocols that yielded the best baseline results.

### Model
I will adopt a **Deeply Supervised Multi-Task Architecture** designed to align the semantic hierarchy of the labels with the feature abstraction hierarchy of the network.

*   **Backbone:** I will upgrade the feature extractor to **ResNet-50**. This provides the necessary capacity to disentangle the 5,270 fine-grained categories, addressing the potential underfitting of the ResNet-18 baseline while remaining computationally feasible within the 24-hour limit.
*   **Multi-View Aggregation:** I will retain the **Global Max Pooling** strategy to aggregate features from multiple product images. This method is computationally efficient and was proven effective for this specific task, outperforming complex attention mechanisms when data volume is the constraint **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Deep Hierarchical Supervision (Structural Innovation):** Instead of branching all classification heads from the final layer (as in Idea 2), I will attach auxiliary classifiers to intermediate stages of the backbone to enforce a coarse-to-fine feature learning trajectory:
    1.  **Coarse Head (Level 1):** Attached to the output of **ResNet Stage 3**. This forces the middle layers to capture high-level generic concepts (e.g., "SPORT" vs. "FURNITURE").
    2.  **Intermediate Head (Level 2):** Attached to the output of **ResNet Stage 4** (alongside the main head).
    3.  **Fine Head (Level 3 - Target):** Attached to the output of **ResNet Stage 4**.
    *   This structure mitigates the vanishing gradient problem and ensures that the shared features in the backbone are robust and semantically meaningful at multiple levels of abstraction.

### Data
*   **Full Dataset Training:** I will train on the **entire training dataset (7M+ images)**. Lesson 10 demonstrated that architectural sophistication cannot compensate for data pruning; therefore, maximizing data coverage is the primary priority **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**.
*   **Metadata Indexing:** I will utilize the CSV-based metadata index to perform random-access reads from the BSON files, preventing the I/O bottleneck associated with extracting millions of small files **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be decoded, resized to 180x180, and normalized. I will strictly avoid complex augmentations like ColorJitter, which were shown to hinder convergence under short training schedules **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.

### Training
*   **Hardware Saturation:** I will set the batch size to **512** (or higher, if VRAM permits) and use **12 data loader workers**. This ensures the A100 GPU remains fully saturated and stabilizes Batch Normalization statistics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
*   **Optimization Strategy:**
    *   **Scheduler:** I will use **OneCycleLR** with a short duration of **3 epochs**. Given the dataset size, this provides sufficient gradient updates for convergence while keeping runtime manageable (estimated ~3-4 hours for ResNet-50) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   **Linear Scaling Rule:** The peak learning rate will be scaled linearly with the batch size to ensure effective weight updates despite the reduced iteration count **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
*   **Loss Function:** The total loss will be a weighted sum: $L_{total} = L_{fine} + 0.3 L_{mid} + 0.1 L_{coarse}$. I will apply **Label Smoothing (0.1)** to the fine-grained head to prevent overfitting on the noisy target labels **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** The model will process the test set using the `test_metadata.csv` index.
*   **Prediction:** For each product, the images will be passed through the backbone, aggregated via Global Max Pooling, and the final prediction will be taken from the **Fine Head (Level 3)**. The auxiliary heads will be discarded during inference.
