To optimize the solution, I will implement the following changes based on the lessons learned and the goal of improving accuracy within the time constraints:

1.  **Simplify Classification Head**: I will revert the `MultiViewResNet` classifier from a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) to a simple Linear layer. As noted in **solution_lesson_node_00003**, a simple linear probe is often more sample-efficient and yields better convergence when fine-tuning backbones compared to complex custom heads.
2.  **Enable Label Smoothing**: I will update the loss function to use `CrossEntropyLoss` with `label_smoothing=0.1`. This regularization technique helps prevent overfitting and improves generalization on high-cardinality classification tasks (Cite **solution_lesson_node_00003**).
3.  **Remove Expensive Augmentation**: I will remove `ColorJitter` from the training transformations. This reduces the CPU overhead for data loading and removes a source of variance that may require longer training times to overcome (Cite **solution_lesson_node_00003**).
4.  **Increase Training Epochs**: I will increase the number of epochs from 1 to 2. Since the previous 1-epoch run took approximately 1 hour, 2 epochs will comfortably fit within the 4-hour limit and allow the model to benefit from the improved regularization and simplified architecture.
