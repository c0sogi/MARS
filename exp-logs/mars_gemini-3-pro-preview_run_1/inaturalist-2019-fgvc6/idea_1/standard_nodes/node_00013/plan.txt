1.  **Model Upgrade (Better Weights)**: Switch from `torchvision`'s EfficientNet-B0 to `timm`'s `tf_efficientnet_b0.ns_jft_in1k`. This allows for better performance with fewer training epochs.
2.  **Scheduler Optimization**: Replace `CosineAnnealingLR` with `OneCycleLR`. OneCycleLR is designed for "super-convergence," allowing the model to reach a better optimum significantly faster, which is essential given the reduced epoch budget.
3.  **Resource Utilization**: Increase `NUM_WORKERS` from 4 to 12 to maximize data loading throughput on the available vCPUs.
4.  **Time Management**: Reduce `EPOCHS` from 10 to 6. Based on previous runtimes (~12 mins/epoch), 6 epochs will fit safely within the remaining ~85 minutes.
5.  **Normalization Adjustment**: Update the data transformation pipeline to use Inception-style mean/std (0.5, 0.5, 0.5) as required by the `tf_efficientnet` architecture.
