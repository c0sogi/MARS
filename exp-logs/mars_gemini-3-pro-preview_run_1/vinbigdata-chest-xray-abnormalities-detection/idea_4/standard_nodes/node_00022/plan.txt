To resolve this, I will:
1.  Reduce `BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to 8 in both `library/config.py` and `runfile.py` to respect hardware constraints (Cite `debug_lesson_3`).
2.  Modify `library/model.py` to output raw logits for the global classification head instead of sigmoid probabilities.
3.  Update `library/loss.py` to use `BCEWithLogitsLoss` instead of `BCELoss`, removing the manual clamping (Cite `debug_lesson_5`).
4.  Update `library/inference.py` and `library/train.py` to explicitly apply `torch.sigmoid` to the logits when probabilities are required for accuracy calculation or inference output.
