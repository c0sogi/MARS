The `IndexError` in `perform_failure_analysis` is caused by the assumption that the indices of the filtered DataFrame `c_gts` are contiguous. The expression `i - c_gts.index[0]` attempts to map the DataFrame's original index `i` to the 0-based index of the `max_ious` array. However, because `c_gts` is a filtered subset of `gt_df`, its indices are non-contiguous (e.g., `[0, 5, 12]`), causing the calculated index to exceed the bounds of `max_ious` (which has size equal to the number of rows in `c_gts`). The fix is to use `enumerate` to generate a guaranteed 0-based sequence `idx` that perfectly aligns with the `max_ious` array, decoupling the array access from the DataFrame's original indices.
