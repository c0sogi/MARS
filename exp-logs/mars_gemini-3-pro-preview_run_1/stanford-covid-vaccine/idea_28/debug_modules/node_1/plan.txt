The `StopIteration` error arises because the `debug_sample_size` of 32 in `runfile.py` is smaller than the configured `BATCH_SIZE` of 64. Since the training `DataLoader` uses `drop_last=True`, the single incomplete batch is discarded, resulting in an empty iterator. I will fix this by importing `BATCH_SIZE` from `library.config` into `runfile.py` and setting `debug_size` equal to `BATCH_SIZE`. This ensures the test case is consistent with the application's configuration and produces at least one valid batch. Cite debug_lesson_5.
