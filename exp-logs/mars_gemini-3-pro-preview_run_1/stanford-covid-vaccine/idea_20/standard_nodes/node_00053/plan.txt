The `TypeError` encountered during execution is caused by the inclusion of the `verbose` argument in the initialization of `torch.optim.lr_scheduler.ReduceLROnPlateau`. As noted in `debug_lesson_1`, this parameter was deprecated and removed in PyTorch 2.0+, making it incompatible with the installed version (2.8.0). To resolve this, I will remove the `verbose` keyword argument from the scheduler initialization in both `runfile.py` and `library/train.py`. The logging of learning rates is already handled manually within the training loop, so removing this argument does not result in a loss of functionality.
