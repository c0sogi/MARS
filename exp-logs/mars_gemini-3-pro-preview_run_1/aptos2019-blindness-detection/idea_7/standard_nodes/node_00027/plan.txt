Based on the lessons learned, specifically **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00026**, the use of **Generalized Mean Pooling (GeM)** with aggressive image downsampling (squashing large fundus images to 512x512) amplifies resizing artifacts, leading to a higher correlation between prediction error and image dimensions. The lesson suggests that **Global Average Pooling** acts as a low-pass filter, providing more robustness in this scenario. Additionally, **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00020** indicates that models on this dataset tend to peak around Epoch 7, so I will increase the training duration from 5 to 10 epochs to ensure full convergence. I will also switch the backbone from `convnext_small` to `convnext_tiny` as the lighter model previously achieved higher scores (0.923 vs 0.918) when paired with Average Pooling.

class RetinopathyModel(nn.Module):

        super().__init__()
