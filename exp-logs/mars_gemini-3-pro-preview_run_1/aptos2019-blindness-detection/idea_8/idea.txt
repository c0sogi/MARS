Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **distinct feature engineering paradigm** (Graham's Normalization) to address the root causes of the "large image error" and "domain shift" identified in the failure analyses, while stabilizing the high-capacity architecture with **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**.

# Model
*   **Backbone Architecture:** **ConvNeXt-Small**.
    *   *Rationale:* This architecture balances high representational capacity with the training stability provided by Layer Normalization, avoiding the batch-size pitfalls of EfficientNet `Cite solution_lesson_node_00007`. It outperformed lighter variants in the previous best attempt `Cite solution_lesson_node_00028`.
*   **Pooling Mechanism:** **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**.
    *   *Rationale:* We retain GeM pooling to capture the high-activation signals of sparse lesions (microaneurysms) which are often washed out by standard Average Pooling `Cite solution_lesson_node_00028`.
*   **Head Architecture:** **Ordinal Head with Multi-Sample Dropout**.
    *   *Structure:* GeM Pooling $\rightarrow$ Multi-Sample Dropout $\rightarrow$ Linear Layer (4 outputs).
    *   *Rationale:* We preserve the Rank-Consistent Ordinal Regression approach `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`. The Multi-Sample Dropout provides ensemble-like regularization within a single forward pass, crucial for preventing overfitting on this small dataset `Cite solution_lesson_node_00028` `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.
*   **Structural Innovation (Training Dynamics):** **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**.
    *   *Method:* Maintain a shadow copy of the model weights that is updated as a moving average of the training weights after each step.
    *   *Rationale:* Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00026` revealed that GeM pooling can amplify noise/artifacts from "squashing" resizing. EMA acts as a temporal ensemble, smoothing out these high-frequency fluctuations and providing a more robust set of weights for the final inference, effectively counteracting the noise sensitivity of the GeM layer.

# Data
*   **Feature Engineering Paradigm:** **Graham’s Color Normalization**.
    *   *Method:* Apply the transformation `Image = 4 * Image - 4 * GaussianBlur(Image) + 128` to every image before resizing.
    *   *Rationale:* This acts as a band-pass filter, removing low-frequency luminosity variations (addressing the **Domain Shift** identified in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`) and enhancing high-frequency local textures (lesions). This directly addresses the **Resolution Bottleneck** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00005` by increasing the signal-to-noise ratio of the pathological features *before* the destructive downsampling occurs.
*   **Preprocessing:** **Simple Resizing ("Squashing") to 512x512**.
    *   *Rationale:* Despite the geometric distortion, "squashing" maximizes the information density of the input tensor compared to padding or cropping `Cite solution_lesson_node_00010` `Cite solution_lesson_node_00009`. The Graham's Normalization step will make the model more robust to this distortion by highlighting the texture anomalies regardless of their aspect ratio.
*   **Augmentation:** **Strict Geometric Invariance**.
    *   *Techniques:* Random Vertical Flips, Random Horizontal Flips, and Random 90-degree Rotations.
    *   *Constraint:* **No Photometric (Color/Hue) Augmentations.**
    *   *Rationale:* We rely on Graham's Normalization to handle lighting invariance. Introducing random Hue/Saturation shifts destroys the specific color markers (red hemorrhages vs. yellow exudates) critical for diagnosis `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** **BCEWithLogitsLoss** summed across the 4 ordinal units.
    *   *Rationale:* Ensures numerical stability via the Log-Sum-Exp trick `Cite solution_lesson_node_00018`.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **Weight Decay = 0.05**.
    *   *Rationale:* We strictly adhere to the high weight decay requirement for ConvNeXt to maintain feature robustness, as reducing it was shown to degrade performance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00027`.
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing Scheduler**.
    *   *Rationale:* Essential for convergence stability in high-capacity models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00012`.
*   **System Configuration:** `num_workers=8` to optimize stochastic shuffling `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** **4-View Test Time Augmentation (TTA)** using **EMA Weights**.
    *   *Method:* Generate predictions for [Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, Rotate 180] using the **EMA model copy**. Average the sigmoid probabilities.
    *   *Rationale:* TTA marginalizes geometric variance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00028`, while using EMA weights provides a more stable estimate than the final training snapshot.
*   **Decoding:** Sum the probabilities to obtain a continuous score (0-4) and **round to the nearest integer** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`.
