Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **Multi-Scale Feature Aggregation** architecture to address the spatial resolution loss inherent in deep CNNs, ensuring that fine-grained lesions (microaneurysms) are captured even after aggressive downsampling.

# Model
*   **Backbone Architecture:** **ConvNeXt-Base**.
    *   *Rationale:* We retain the "Base" variant as it offers the necessary high representational capacity to utilize the A100 GPU effectively, while its Layer Normalization design ensures stability without the batch-size constraints of EfficientNet `Cite solution_lesson_node_00037` `Cite solution_lesson_node_00007`.
*   **Structural Innovation:** **Multi-Scale Dual-Stream Pooling**.
    *   *Concept:* Instead of utilizing only the final feature map (Stage 4, typically 1/32 spatial resolution), we will extract feature maps from **both Stage 3 (1/16 resolution) and Stage 4**.
    *   *Mechanism:* Apply **Dual-Stream Pooling (Concatenated GAP + GeM)** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00037` independently to the feature maps from Stage 3 and Stage 4.
    *   *Rationale:* Diabetic Retinopathy is characterized by minute lesions that can be spatially merged or lost in the deepest, lowest-resolution layer of the network. By tapping into Stage 3, we access features with double the spatial resolution (e.g., 32x32 vs 16x16), allowing the GeM pooling to detect sparse lesions that might otherwise vanish. Concatenating these multi-scale features provides the classifier with both high-resolution local texture signals and low-resolution global semantic context.
*   **Classification Head:** **Ordinal Head with Multi-Sample Dropout**.
    *   *Structure:* Concatenate [Stage3_GAP, Stage3_GeM, Stage4_GAP, Stage4_GeM] $\rightarrow$ LayerNorm $\rightarrow$ Multi-Sample Dropout $\rightarrow$ Linear Layer (4 outputs).
    *   *Rationale:* We preserve the Rank-Consistent Ordinal Regression logic `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001` and the ensemble-like regularization of Multi-Sample Dropout `Cite solution_lesson_node_00028`.

# Data
*   **Resolution:** **512x512 pixels**.
    *   *Rationale:* High resolution is critical for minimizing the information loss bottleneck `Cite solution_lesson_node_00005`.
*   **Preprocessing:** **Simple Resizing ("Squashing")**.
    *   *Method:* Resize source images directly to 512x512, disregarding aspect ratio.
    *   *Rationale:* "Squashing" maximizes the information density of the input tensor compared to padding `Cite solution_lesson_node_00010`. The proposed Multi-Scale Dual-Stream Pooling is specifically designed to be robust to the artifacts this introduces by capturing features at multiple granularities.
*   **Augmentation:** **Strict Geometric Invariance**.
    *   *Techniques:* Random Vertical Flips, Random Horizontal Flips, and Random 90-degree Rotations.
    *   *Constraint:* **No Photometric (Hue) Augmentations.**
    *   *Rationale:* We strictly avoid Hue/Saturation shifts to preserve the pathological color markers `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

# Training
*   **Regularization Strategy:** **Mixup**.
    *   *Method:* Apply Mixup (linear interpolation of images and labels) with $\alpha=0.4$ during training.
    *   *Rationale:* With the increased capacity of the Multi-Scale ConvNeXt-Base model and the relatively small dataset, overfitting is a primary risk. Mixup acts as a strong regularizer that encourages the model to behave linearly in-between classes. Since we use Ordinal Regression (binary targets), the mixed targets (e.g., 0.5) are naturally handled by the BCE loss, smoothing the decision boundaries.
*   **Training Dynamics:** **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**.
    *   *Method:* Maintain a shadow copy of the model weights updated via moving average.
    *   *Rationale:* EMA is critical to smooth out the high-frequency noise in the GeM features and the gradients from the "squashed" inputs `Cite solution_lesson_node_00031`.
*   **Loss Function:** **BCEWithLogitsLoss**.
    *   *Rationale:* Summed across the 4 ordinal units. Ensures numerical stability `Cite solution_lesson_node_00018`.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **Weight Decay = 0.05**.
    *   *Rationale:* Adhere to the high weight decay requirement for ConvNeXt `Cite solution_lesson_node_00027`.
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing Scheduler**.
    *   *Rationale:* Essential for convergence stability `Cite solution_lesson_node_00012`.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** **4-View Test Time Augmentation (TTA) with EMA Weights**.
    *   *Method:* Generate predictions for [Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, Rotate 180] using the **EMA model**. Average the sigmoid probabilities.
    *   *Rationale:* TTA marginalizes geometric variance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00028`, while EMA weights provide the most stable estimate.
*   **Decoding:** Sum the probabilities to obtain a continuous score (0-4) and **round to the nearest integer** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`.
