Based on the lessons learned, specifically `solution_lesson_node_00043`, the strategy to improve performance involves increasing the regularization strength and extending the training duration. The ConvNeXt Base model is a high-capacity architecture that can easily overfit on the small Diabetic Retinopathy dataset (~2.6k samples). Increasing the **Stochastic Depth (Drop Path)** rate forces the model to learn more robust features by randomly dropping paths in the residual blocks. Consequently, this increased regularization slows down convergence, necessitating a longer training schedule (15 epochs instead of 10) to reach the optimal performance peak. Additionally, leveraging the 40GB A100 GPU allows us to increase the batch size to 24, improving the stability of optimization (`solution_lesson_node_00007`) and training throughput.
