To improve the model's performance, I will implement a strategy focused on enhancing feature resolution and increasing model capacity, addressing the limitations identified in the failure analysis and lessons learned.

1.  **Refined Temporal Features**: I will replace the coarse `months_since_2009` feature with a high-resolution `time_delta` (total seconds since 2009). This continuous monotonic index will allow the model to capture fine-grained temporal trends (e.g., daily inflation, specific event impacts) more effectively than monthly bins (Cite Lesson 5).
2.  **Expanded Landmark Features**: Failure analysis showed a high correlation between errors and specific locations. I will expand the set of distance features to include major NYC hubs (Times Square, Penn Station, Grand Central, World Trade Center) in addition to airports. This provides the model with explicit "anchors" to better approximate location-specific pricing rules (Cite Lesson 2).
3.  **Increased Model Capacity**: Given the dataset size (44M rows) and the addition of new features, I will increase `num_leaves` from 256 to 384 and `min_child_samples` to 200. To ensure stable convergence with this higher complexity, I will reduce the `learning_rate` to 0.015 and increase the maximum boosting rounds.
