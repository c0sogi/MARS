### Model: ResNet34-Based U-Net++ with scSE Attention
I propose a structural evolution from the simple custom U-Net to a **U-Net++ architecture backed by a ResNet34 encoder**. This design addresses the need for stronger feature extraction while introducing a more sophisticated decoding pathway to handle the variable shapes of salt bodies.

*   **Encoder (Backbone):** Replace the custom lightweight encoder with a **ResNet34** pre-trained on ImageNet. The residual connections in ResNet facilitate the training of deeper networks and provide richer semantic feature extraction compared to the previous simple convolutional blocks.
*   **Decoder Topology (U-Net++):** Instead of the standard U-Net skip connections, employ the **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net)** architecture. This structure introduces nested, dense convolutional blocks on the skip pathways. These dense connections bridge the semantic gap between the encoder and decoder feature maps, allowing the optimizer to deal with an easier learning task when fusing features.
*   **Attention Mechanism:** Integrate **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules within the decoder nodes. As established, these modules effectively recalibrate features to focus on salt textures and suppress background noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Depth Integration:** Continue to fuse the depth information at the input level. The scalar depth value will be normalized and repeated spatially to form a dense channel concatenated with the image input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

### Data
*   **Input Adaptation:** Preprocess the 101x101 images by applying **Reflection Padding** to reach a resolution of 128x128. This ensures compatibility with the deep downsampling operations of the ResNet encoder (divisible by 32) and avoids the edge artifacts introduced by zero-padding (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Augmentation:** Maintain the use of Random Horizontal Flips during training to provide basic regularization without distorting the seismic structures.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** Use a composite of **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) and Dice Loss** to optimize for both pixel-wise accuracy and region overlap.
*   **Optimization Strategy:**
    *   **Scheduler:** Implement a `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler that monitors **Validation Loss** rather than IoU. This ensures the learning rate is reduced to refine probability confidence and boundary sharpness even after the threshold-based metrics have plateaued (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
    *   **Duration:** Configure the training for a significantly higher number of epochs (e.g., 100) with a patience of 20-25 epochs. This extended duration is critical to allow the model to enter the "fine-tuning" phase where boundary details are resolved (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010, solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Evaluation
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, generate predictions for both the original image and a horizontally flipped version. Average the probability maps before applying the threshold. This reduces variance and improves the robustness of the segmentation masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Post-Processing:** The 128x128 output predictions will be **center-cropped** back to the original 101x101 dimensions before thresholding and RLE encoding (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
