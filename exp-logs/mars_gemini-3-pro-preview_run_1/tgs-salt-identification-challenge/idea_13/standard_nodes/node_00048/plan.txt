Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, I will implement the following optimization strategy:

1.  **Revert to Probabilistic Loss for Ensembling**: I will remove the curriculum learning schedule that switches to `LovaszHingeLoss`. According to **Lesson 47** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00047), metric-direct losses like Lovasz-Hinge sharpen predictions and eliminate the "soft" uncertainty required for effective ensembling. By using `BCEDiceLoss` consistently throughout training, the model preserves calibrated probabilities, maximizing the benefit of the Snapshot Ensemble.

2.  **Retain High-Capacity Architecture**: I will keep the `DeepResUNet` with 1024 filters in the bottleneck. The previous experiment showed this architecture produced a superior individual model (mAP 0.8350) compared to the 512-filter variant. The failure of the previous attempt was due to the loss function interfering with the ensemble, not the capacity itself.

3.  **Maintain Snapshot Ensembling**: I will continue to use the cyclic cosine annealing schedule (150 epochs, 3 cycles) and ensemble the best checkpoints from cycles 2 and 3, as this provides robust generalization without additional training cost (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
