### Proposed Strategy: Context-Aware Deep Residual U-Net (ResUNet-ASPP) with Corrected Lovasz-Cycle Optimization

This strategy synthesizes the robust feature extraction of the **Deep Residual U-Net** (from Idea 4) with a new **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module to address the limited receptive field of standard U-Nets. Furthermore, it implements a **Corrected Lovasz-Cycle** training pipeline that specifically fixes the implementation errors (Batch-wise Dice, Premature Switching) identified in previous attempts.

#### 1. Model: Deep Residual U-Net with ASPP and scSE
I will retain the successful "Deep Residual" backbone but introduce a structural innovation at the bottleneck to capture multi-scale global context, which is critical for distinguishing salt from sediment in ambiguous regions.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** A custom **Deep Residual Encoder** initialized with **64 filters**. This preserves the channel capacity required for high-texture segmentation `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020` and avoids the overfitting risks of pre-trained models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`.
*   **Structural Innovation (ASPP):** I will insert an **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module at the bottleneck (between the encoder and decoder). Unlike the failed Hypercolumns approach (which aggregated unrefined features), ASPP uses parallel dilated convolutions (rates 1, 6, 12, 18) to capture multi-scale context *before* the decoding phase. This expands the receptive field without losing spatial resolution, addressing the context limitation of the pure U-Net.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be placed in the decoder blocks to recalibrate features during upsampling `Cite solution_lesson_node_00007`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.
    *   **Upsampling:** I will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling** to avoid the checkerboard artifacts caused by Transpose Convolutions `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary prediction heads will be attached to intermediate decoder levels ($32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$) to enforce hierarchical feature learning `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

#### 2. Data: Sample Integrity and Maximum Utilization
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle irregular dimensions without edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will explicitly avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve edge precision `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

#### 3. Training: Corrected Lovasz-Cycle Curriculum
This pipeline specifically corrects the "Batch-wise Dice" and "Premature Lovasz" errors that caused previous failures, while leveraging the "Long Cycle" stability.

*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**. The schedule will consist of **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs) to allow for deep convergence `Cite solution_lesson_node_00022`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Phase 1 (Cycles 1 & 2, Epochs 0-100):**
    *   **Loss:** **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice Loss**. Crucially, the Dice loss will be calculated **per image** and averaged. This ensures that empty masks (39% of data) are correctly penalized, fixing the batch-wise aggregation failure `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Goal:** Establish a robust feature extractor and converge to a stable minimum.
*   **Phase 2 (Cycle 3, Epochs 101-150):**
    *   **Loss:** Switch to **Lovasz-Hinge Loss** (combined with BCE). This switch will occur *only* after the model has fully converged in Phase 1. This directly optimizes the Jaccard index (IoU) for the final fine-tuning, adhering to the lesson on stable transition `Cite solution_lesson_node_00018`.
*   **Metric-Based Checkpointing:** The "Best Model" for each cycle will be selected based strictly on the **Validation mAP**, not the loss `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.

#### 4. Evaluation: Snapshot Ensembling
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will implement **Snapshot Ensembling** by averaging the predictions of the **Best Model from Cycle 2** (Robust BCE/Dice Baseline) and the **Best Model from Cycle 3** (Lovasz Refined). This captures the diversity of the optimization landscape without the data reduction of K-Fold CV.
*   **TTA:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
