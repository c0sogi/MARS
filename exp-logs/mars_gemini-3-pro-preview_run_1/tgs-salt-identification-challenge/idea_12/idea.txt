Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **High-Capacity Deep Residual U-Net with Stochastic Depth and Consistent Compound Loss**.

This strategy is designed to resolve the conflict between the need for high model capacity (identified in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00034`) and the risk of overfitting on small images (identified in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`). It synthesizes the robust **Snapshot Ensembling** pipeline with a **Consistent Compound Loss** to fix the optimization instability observed in previous attempts.

### 1. Model: High-Capacity Deep Residual U-Net with Stochastic Depth
I will employ a custom **Deep Residual U-Net** that restores the high channel capacity of the bottleneck while introducing structural regularization to prevent the overfitting observed with heavy backbones.

*   **Backbone (High-Capacity Encoder):** A custom Deep Residual Encoder initialized with **64 filters** and expanding to a bottleneck of **1024 filters**. Previous lessons demonstrated that reducing this capacity (e.g., to 512) degrades performance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00034`, as high channel width is essential for capturing complex seismic textures `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`.
*   **Structural Innovation (Stochastic Depth):** To manage this high capacity without incurring the overfitting penalties seen in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`, I will implement **Stochastic Depth (DropPath)** within the residual blocks. This technique randomly drops entire residual paths during training (with probability increasing with depth), acting as a powerful regularizer that promotes robust feature learning across the ensemble of sub-paths.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Attention:** I will utilize **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules in the decoder. I am reverting to scSE (proven effective in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00007`) rather than unproven attention mechanisms, ensuring stable feature recalibration.
    *   **Upsampling:** I will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by a convolution, avoiding Transpose Convolutions to prevent checkerboard artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary prediction heads will be attached at resolutions $32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$, and $128 \times 128$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

### 2. Data: Maximum Utilization and Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle the $101 \times 101$ dimensions without introducing edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will explicitly avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model, preventing the performance drop observed when fragmenting the small dataset `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve the edge sharpness required for the high-IoU metric `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

### 3. Training: Consistent Compound Loss with Cyclic Schedule
This pipeline addresses the "Hard Switch" instability `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033` and the "Batch-wise Dice" error `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.

*   **Loss Function:** I will use a **Consistent Compound Loss** throughout the entire training duration.
    $$L = L_{BCE} + L_{Dice\_Sample} + 0.1 \times L_{Lovasz}$$
    *   **Sample-Wise Dice:** The Dice component will be calculated **per image** and averaged. This ensures that empty masks (39% of data) are correctly penalized, fixing the batch-wise aggregation failure `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Consistency:** Including a small, constant weight for Lovasz-Hinge from the start allows the model to optimize the IoU metric continuously without the shock of a sudden objective change `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033`.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Schedule:** **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs). The constant cycle length allows the model to settle into robust minima before restarting `Cite solution_lesson_node_00022`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Checkpointing:** I will save the "Best Model" for each cycle based strictly on the **Validation mAP**, not the loss `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.

### 4. Evaluation: Snapshot Ensembling
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will implement **Snapshot Ensembling** by averaging the predictions of the **Best Model from Cycle 2** and the **Best Model from Cycle 3**. This leverages the diversity of the local minima visited by the cyclic schedule to improve generalization without the cost of training multiple independent models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00035`.
*   **TTA:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ before thresholding `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
