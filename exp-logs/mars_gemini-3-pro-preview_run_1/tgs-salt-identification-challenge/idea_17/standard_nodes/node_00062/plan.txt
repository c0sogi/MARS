2.  **Optimize Training Schedule**: I will modify `runfile.py` to extend the training duration from 20 epochs to **120 epochs** (4 cycles of 30 epochs). This utilizes the "Final Epoch Heuristic" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`) to ensure the model is not under-trained. The multi-cycle schedule enables "Snapshot Ensembling" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00035`), allowing us to ensemble diverse models from the ends of Cycles 2, 3, and 4.
3.  **Preserve Curriculum Strategy**: The schedule maintains the strategy of switching from BCE+Dice to BCE+Lovasz exactly at the cycle boundary (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`) and substituting the coarse metric proxy with the direct one (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00053`).
