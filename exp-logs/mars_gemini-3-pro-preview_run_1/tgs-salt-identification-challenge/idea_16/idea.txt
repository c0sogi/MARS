### Proposed Strategy: Deep Residual U-Net with Homogeneous Lovasz-Cycle Ensembling

Based on the analysis of previous experiments, I propose a strategy that specifically addresses the **Ensemble Disparity** failure identified in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00050`. The previous best attempt failed because it ensembled a "weak" Dice-optimized model (Cycle 2) with a "strong" Lovasz-optimized model (Cycle 3). This new strategy extends the **Lovasz Curriculum** to cover the final *two* cycles, ensuring that both snapshots in the ensemble are fine-tuned for the competition metric, thereby maximizing the variance-reduction benefits of **Snapshot Ensembling** without diluting prediction quality.

#### 1. Model: Deep Residual U-Net with scSE
I will retain the architecture that has consistently proven most robust, avoiding the over-parameterization and complexity traps identified in recent lessons.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** A custom **Deep Residual Encoder** initialized with **64 filters** and expanding to a bottleneck of **512 filters**. I will explicitly avoid the 1024-filter capacity, as `Cite solution_lesson_node_00049` showed this degrades performance on small images. The residual structure enables deep feature extraction `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be placed in the decoder. This is chosen over Coordinate Attention, which was shown to underperform on these low-resolution inputs `Cite solution_lesson_node_00055`. Attention is restricted to the decoder to preserve encoder feature integrity `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.
    *   **Upsampling:** **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by a convolution. This is strictly preferred over Transpose Convolutions to eliminate checkerboard artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary prediction heads will be attached at resolutions $32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$, and $128 \times 128$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

#### 2. Data: Maximum Utilization and Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle the $101 \times 101$ dimensions without introducing edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will explicitly avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve the edge sharpness required for the high-IoU metric `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

#### 3. Training: Homogeneous Lovasz-Cycle Curriculum
This training pipeline is designed to produce **two** high-quality, metric-optimized checkpoints for ensembling, rather than just one.

*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Schedule:** **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs). The restart mechanism allows the model to escape local minima `Cite solution_lesson_node_00022`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Phase 1 (Cycle 1, Epochs 0-50):**
    *   **Loss:** **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice Loss**. Dice is calculated per-image to correctly penalize empty masks `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Goal:** Establish robust structural convergence.
*   **Phase 2 (Cycles 2 & 3, Epochs 51-150):**
    *   **Loss Substitution:** Switch to **BCE + Lovasz-Hinge Loss**. I will **substitute** the Dice term with Lovasz (rather than adding it) to avoid gradient conflict `Cite solution_lesson_node_00053`.
    *   **Timing:** The switch occurs exactly at the Cycle 1 boundary (Epoch 50). The learning rate restart at this point facilitates the transition to the new loss landscape, avoiding the instability of intra-cycle switching `Cite solution_lesson_node_00052`.
    *   **Goal:** By training *both* Cycle 2 and Cycle 3 with Lovasz, we generate two independent models that are both optimized for the Jaccard index.
*   **Checkpointing:** Save the "Best Model" from **Cycle 2** and **Cycle 3** based strictly on the **Validation mAP** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.

#### 4. Evaluation: Homogeneous Snapshot Ensembling
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will ensemble (average) the predictions of the **Best Model from Cycle 2** and the **Best Model from Cycle 3**.
    *   **Innovation:** Unlike previous attempts where Cycle 2 was a "weak" Dice model and Cycle 3 was a "strong" Lovasz model (leading to degradation `Cite solution_lesson_node_00050`), this strategy ensures both members are "strong" Lovasz models. This allows the ensemble to benefit from the diversity of the cyclic minima `Cite solution_lesson_node_00035` without suffering from quality disparity.
*   **TTA:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
