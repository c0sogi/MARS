2.  **Implementing Decaying Deep Supervision Weights**: Instead of equal weighting (0.5), I will weight the auxiliary heads based on their resolution/proximity to the output (Main: 1.0, Aux3: 0.5, Aux2: 0.25, Aux1: 0.1) to prioritize high-resolution features.
3.  **Increasing Batch Size to 64**: To better utilize the A100 GPU and provide more stable gradient estimates for the Cosine Annealing scheduler.
