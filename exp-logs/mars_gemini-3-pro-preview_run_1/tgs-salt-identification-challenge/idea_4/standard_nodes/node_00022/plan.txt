My optimization strategy focuses on extending the training duration and refining the optimization trajectory, leveraging the insights from the provided lessons.

1.  **Extended Training Duration (Cite Lesson 00011)**: The previous solution achieved a high metric (0.801) in just 50 epochs, with the best model likely saved near the end. I will increase the training epochs to **150** to allow the model to fully converge and refine segmentation boundaries.
2.  **Cosine Annealing Scheduler (Cite Lesson 00004)**: I will replace the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler with `CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts`. This scheduler periodically resets the learning rate, helping the model escape local minima and explore the loss landscape more effectively, which is critical for maximizing the mAP metric.
3.  **AdamW Optimizer**: I will switch from `Adam` to `AdamW` to decouple weight decay from gradient updates, which generally yields better generalization in prolonged training runs.
4.  **Deep Supervision Weights**: I will assign explicit weights `[1.0, 0.5, 0.25]` to the deep supervision heads (High-Res, Med-Res, Low-Res) to prioritize the optimization of the final high-resolution output while still benefiting from the gradient flow of auxiliary heads.
5.  **Batch Size**: I will increase the batch size to **96** to improve gradient stability and utilization of the A100 GPU.
