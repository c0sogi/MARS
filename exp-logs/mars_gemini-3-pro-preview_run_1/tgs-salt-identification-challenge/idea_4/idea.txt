### Proposed Strategy: Deeply Supervised Residual U-Net

Based on the analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose a structural evolution that enhances the successful "Custom Lightweight" paradigm with **Residual Learning** and **Deep Supervision**. This approach addresses the need for better gradient flow and multi-scale feature discrimination without incurring the over-parameterization penalties of heavy pre-trained backbones.

#### 1. Model: Custom Residual U-Net with Deep Supervision
I will replace the plain convolutional blocks of the previous best model with **Residual Blocks** and introduce **Deep Supervision** to the decoder.

*   **Backbone (Residual Custom Encoder):** Instead of using a pre-trained ResNet34 (which led to overfitting, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**) or a plain convolutional encoder, I will construct a custom encoder where each stage consists of a **Residual Block** (two $3 \times 3$ convolutions with a skip connection: $y = f(x) + x$).
    *   **Width:** The network will initialize with **64 filters** in the first layer and double at each downsampling step. This preserves the channel capacity necessary for high-texture segmentation, avoiding the underfitting observed when reducing width (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
    *   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input level (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).
*   **Decoder (Attentive Reconstruction):** The decoder will mirror the encoder but will be equipped with **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules. These will be placed *only* in the decoder to refine features during upsampling without disrupting the encoder's feature extraction (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**).
*   **Structural Innovation (Deep Supervision):** I will attach auxiliary prediction heads ($1 \times 1$ convolutions) to the output of the last three decoder blocks (e.g., at resolutions $32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$, and $128 \times 128$). During training, the loss will be calculated as a weighted sum of these multi-scale outputs. This forces the deeper layers to learn semantically discriminative features directly, improving gradient flow and boundary precision.

#### 2. Data: Reflection Padding and Maximum Data Utilization
*   **Preprocessing:** Input images ($101 \times 101$) will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle irregular dimensions without introducing edge artifacts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will abandon the 5-Fold Cross-Validation strategy. Instead, I will use a single, stratified Train/Validation split (e.g., 90% Train, 10% Val). This maximizes the effective training set size for the single model, addressing the performance degradation caused by data fragmentation in previous ensemble attempts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).

#### 3. Training: Long-Duration Convergence with Metric-Decoupled Logic
*   **Loss Function:** A composite **BCE + Dice Loss** will be applied to all Deep Supervision heads. I will strictly avoid switching to unstable surrogate losses like Lovasz-Hinge during training (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).
*   **Optimization Strategy:**
    *   **Scheduler:** The `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler will monitor **Validation Loss**. This ensures the learning rate decays to refine probability confidence even after the discrete IoU metric has plateaued (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).
    *   **Checkpointing:** Conversely, the "Best Model" checkpoint will be saved based on the **Validation mAP (Metric)**. This ensures that the selected weights are optimized for the specific competition criteria, not just the proxy loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).
    *   **Duration:** Training will be extended to **100 epochs**. This accounts for the "long tail" of convergence where boundary sharpening occurs, ensuring the model is not terminated while still improving (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**).

#### 4. Evaluation: Robust Inference
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Inference will be performed on the original image and a horizontally flipped version, with predictions averaged to reduce variance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**).
*   **Post-Processing:** The final $128 \times 128$ prediction (from the highest resolution head) will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ before thresholding (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).
