Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **High-Capacity Deep Residual U-Net with Coordinate Attention and Consistent Lovasz-Snapshot Ensembling**.

This strategy synthesizes the proven high-capacity backbone `Cite solution_lesson_node_00034` and snapshot ensembling `Cite solution_lesson_node_00035` with two structural evolutions: **Coordinate Attention** (to refine feature recalibration) and **Stochastic Depth** (to regularize the high-capacity encoder). Furthermore, it implements a **Consistent Compound Loss** to resolve the optimization instability observed in previous Lovasz experiments `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033`.

### 1. Model: High-Capacity Deep Residual U-Net with Coordinate Attention
I will retain the **Deep Residual U-Net** architecture but upgrade its internal components to maximize representational power while maintaining precise localization.

*   **Backbone (High-Capacity Encoder with Stochastic Depth):**
    *   **Structure:** A custom Deep Residual Encoder initialized with **64 filters** and expanding to a bottleneck of **1024 filters**. Maintaining this high channel width is critical for capturing complex seismic textures `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00034`.
    *   **Regularization:** To manage this high capacity and prevent the overfitting observed in heavy models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00015`, I will incorporate **Stochastic Depth (DropPath)** within the residual blocks. This randomly drops entire residual paths during training, acting as a structural regularizer that promotes robust feature learning.
*   **Decoder (Coordinate Attention):**
    *   **Attention Evolution:** I will replace the scSE modules with **Coordinate Attention (CA)** blocks in the decoder. While scSE `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017` treats spatial attention globally, CA factorizes channel attention into two parallel 1D feature encoding operations (X and Y directions). This allows the model to capture long-range dependencies along one spatial direction while preserving precise positional information along the other, which is structurally superior for delineating stratified salt boundaries.
    *   **Upsampling:** I will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by a convolution, avoiding Transpose Convolutions to prevent checkerboard artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary heads will be attached at resolutions $32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$, and $128 \times 128$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

### 2. Data: Maximum Utilization and Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle the $101 \times 101$ dimensions without edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will explicitly avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve the edge sharpness required for the high-IoU metric `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

### 3. Training: Consistent Compound Loss with Cyclic Schedule
This pipeline solves the "Hard Switch" instability `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033` and the "Batch-wise Dice" error `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.

*   **Loss Function:** I will use a **Consistent Compound Loss** throughout the entire training duration, rather than switching losses mid-stream.
    $$L = L_{BCE} + L_{Dice\_Sample} + 0.05 \times L_{Lovasz}$$
    *   **Sample-Wise Dice:** Calculated **per image** and averaged. This ensures that empty masks (39% of data) are correctly penalized `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`.
    *   **Consistency:** Including a small, constant weight for Lovasz-Hinge from the start allows the model to optimize the IoU metric continuously without the shock of a sudden objective change `Cite solution_lesson_node_00033`.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Schedule:** **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs). The constant cycle length allows the model to settle into robust minima before restarting `Cite solution_lesson_node_00022`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Checkpointing:** I will save the "Best Model" for each cycle based strictly on the **Validation mAP**, not the loss `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.

### 4. Evaluation: Snapshot Ensembling
*   **Inference Strategy:** I will implement **Snapshot Ensembling** by averaging the predictions of the **Best Model from Cycle 2** and the **Best Model from Cycle 3**. This leverages the diversity of the local minima visited by the cyclic schedule to improve generalization without the cost of training multiple independent models `Cite solution_lesson_node_00035`.
*   **TTA:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each snapshot before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
