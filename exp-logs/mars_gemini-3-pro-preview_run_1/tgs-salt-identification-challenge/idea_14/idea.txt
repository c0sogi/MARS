Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Deep Residual U-Net with Intra-Cycle Lovasz Curriculum and Quality-Gated Ensembling**.

This strategy addresses the critical failure mode identified in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00050`, where naively ensembling a "strong" metric-optimized model with a "weak" baseline model degraded performance. It synthesizes the **Deep Residual Backbone** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021` with a novel **Intra-Cycle Curriculum** that ensures *every* snapshot in the ensemble is fine-tuned for the competition metric, while maintaining the diversity benefits of cyclic restarts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00035`.

### 1. Model: Deep Residual U-Net with scSE and Bilinear Upsampling
I will employ the robust **Deep Residual U-Net** architecture, strictly adhering to the capacity and component choices proven to work, while avoiding the over-parameterization that caused previous failures.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** A custom Deep Residual Encoder initialized with **64 filters** and expanding to a bottleneck of **512 filters**. I will explicitly avoid the 1024-filter bottleneck used in Idea 9/12, as `Cite solution_lesson_node_00049` demonstrated that this increased capacity hindered convergence without adding value for small input images.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be placed in the decoder blocks. This is retained from `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017` as it effectively highlights salt textures during upsampling.
    *   **Upsampling:** I will strictly use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by a convolution, avoiding Transpose Convolutions to prevent checkerboard artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Auxiliary prediction heads will be attached at resolutions $32 \times 32$, $64 \times 64$, and $128 \times 128$ to enforce hierarchical feature learning `Cite solution_lesson_node_00021`.
*   **Input Fusion:** Depth information (`z`) will be fused as a dense channel at the input `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.

### 2. Data: Maximum Utilization and Integrity
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be padded to $128 \times 128$ using **Reflection Padding** to handle the $101 \times 101$ dimensions without introducing edge artifacts `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Data Partitioning:** I will use a single **90/10 Stratified Split** (Train/Val). I will explicitly avoid K-Fold Cross-Validation to maximize the training signal for the single model `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
*   **Augmentation:** I will restrict augmentation to **Horizontal Flips** only. I will strictly avoid interpolation-heavy transformations (rotation, scaling) to preserve the edge sharpness required for the high-IoU metric `Cite solution_lesson_node_00024`.

### 3. Training: Intra-Cycle Lovasz Curriculum
This is the core structural innovation. Instead of a global curriculum (which created one strong and one weak cycle in Idea 13), I will apply a **Curriculum Loss Schedule within each restart cycle**. This ensures that every saved snapshot is a "strong learner" optimized for the IoU metric.

*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts**.
    *   **Schedule:** **3 constant cycles of 50 epochs** (Total: 150 epochs). The restart mechanism allows the model to escape local minima `Cite solution_lesson_node_00022`, `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Loss Strategy:**
    *   **Cycle 1 (Epochs 0-50):** Train with **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice Loss** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00025`. This stabilizes the feature extractor.
    *   **Cycle 2 (Epochs 51-100) & Cycle 3 (Epochs 101-150):**
        *   **Phase A (First 35 epochs of cycle):** Train with **BCE + Sample-Wise Dice**. The high learning rate from the restart allows the model to explore a new basin of attraction using calibrated probabilistic losses.
        *   **Phase B (Last 15 epochs of cycle):** Linearly ramp up **Lovasz-Hinge Loss** (to a weight of 0.5). This fine-tunes the specific local minimum for the Jaccard metric just before the cycle ends `Cite solution_lesson_node_00045`.
*   **Checkpointing:** Save the "Best Model" from **Cycle 2** and **Cycle 3** based strictly on the **Validation mAP** `Cite solution_lesson_node_00016`.

### 4. Evaluation: Quality-Gated Snapshot Ensembling
I will implement a gating mechanism to prevent the degradation observed in `Cite solution_lesson_node_00050`.

*   **Inference Strategy:**
    1.  Load the Best Model from Cycle 2 and the Best Model from Cycle 3.
    2.  **Quality Gate:** Calculate the difference in their Validation mAP.
        *   **If $|mAP_{C2} - mAP_{C3}| < 0.005$:** Average the predictions (Ensemble). This implies both models are "strong learners" and averaging will reduce variance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00035`.
        *   **Else:** Use only the predictions of the higher-scoring model. This avoids diluting a strong model with a significantly weaker one `Cite solution_lesson_node_00050`.
*   **TTA:** Test-Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) will be applied to each model before averaging `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.
*   **Post-Processing:** Predictions will be center-cropped to $101 \times 101$ `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
