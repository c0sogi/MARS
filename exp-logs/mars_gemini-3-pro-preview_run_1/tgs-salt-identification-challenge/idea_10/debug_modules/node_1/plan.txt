The execution failure stems from two issues: an unintended execution of the training loop in `library/model.py` upon import, and a tensor dimension mismatch where masks remain in "Channel Last" format `(H, W, C)` instead of the expected "Channel First" format `(C, H, W)`.

To resolve this:
1.  **Prevent Unintended Execution**: I will wrap the `main()` call in `library/model.py` with `if __name__ == "__main__":`. This ensures the script only runs when executed directly, not when imported by `runfile.py`.
2.  **Fix Shape Mismatch**: I will modify `SaltDataset.__getitem__` in both `library/model.py` and `library/dataset.py`. The `albumentations` library's `ToTensorV2` does not automatically transpose masks with a channel dimension. I will explicitly enforce the shape contract by permuting the mask from `(H, W, 1)` to `(1, H, W)` before returning it (Cite debug_lesson_6).
