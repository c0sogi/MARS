To optimize performance, I will implement a strategy based on **Lesson 13** and **Lesson 12**. The previous attempt failed because embedding the target token within the full sentence (Global Context) introduced too much noise, making it difficult for the model to isolate the transformation rules. I will revert to a **Local Context Window** approach, which explicitly concatenates the target with its immediate neighbors (`left_context <sep> target <sep> right_context`). This provides a dense, high-signal input. Additionally, I will adjust the `plain_inclusion_ratio` to 0.1 (10%) to ensure the neural model receives sufficient background data to learn robust embeddings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012), and I will increase the number of epochs to 10 to ensure convergence on this cleaner dataset.
