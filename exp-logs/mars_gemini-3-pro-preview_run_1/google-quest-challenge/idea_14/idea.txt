Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the following optimized strategy.

# Model
I propose a **Title-Contextualized DistilRoBERTa Dual-Encoder**.

This architecture introduces a **Structural Innovation** in the input paradigm of the Siamese network. It bridges the gap between the "Context Window" benefits of Dual-Encoders (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**) and the "Interaction Depth" benefits of Cross-Encoders (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**), specifically leveraging the insight that concatenating related text segments improves token-level dependency modeling (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00059**).

*   **Backbone Architecture:** Utilize **`distilroberta-base`**.
    *   *Rationale:* This backbone (82M params) is the empirically verified "Right-Sized" model, balancing capacity and trainability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**) while avoiding the optimization instability of larger models (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
*   **Asymmetric Input Structure (Structural Innovation):**
    *   **Branch 1 (Question):** Input `[CLS] Title [SEP] Body [SEP]`. This preserves the standard question representation.
    *   **Branch 2 (Contextualized Answer):** Input `[CLS] Title [SEP] Answer [SEP]`.
    *   *Mechanism:* By injecting the Question Title into the Answer branch, we allow the Answer encoder to perform **Cross-Attention** between the Answer tokens and the Title tokens *within* the branch. This directly models the relevance of the answer to the question's core intent without sacrificing the sequence length available for the Answer body (unlike full Q+A concatenation).
*   **Target-Masked Hybrid Pooling:**
    *   **Branch 1:** Apply **Masked Global Average and Max Pooling** to the entire sequence to generate $u_{avg}, u_{max}$ (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**).
    *   **Branch 2:** Apply **Masked Pooling** restricted *only* to the **Answer tokens**. We explicitly mask out the Title tokens during this pooling step.
    *   *Rationale:* This ensures that the resulting vector $v_{avg}$ represents the *Answer* (contextualized by the Title) rather than a mix of Title and Answer. This prevents the fusion layer's interaction terms (e.g., $|u - v|$) from being dominated by the identical Title information present in both branches.
*   **Interaction-Aware Fusion:**
    *   Compute explicit interactions (Element-wise Product and Absolute Difference) *only* on the Average Pooled vectors ($u_{avg}, v_{avg}$) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
    *   Concatenate: $[u_{avg}, u_{max}, v_{avg}, v_{max}, u_{avg} \odot v_{avg}, |u_{avg} - v_{avg}|]$.
    *   Apply **Layer Normalization** to the fused vector to stabilize heterogeneous feature scales (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
*   **Monolithic Simple Head:**
    *   Pass the normalized vector through a simple MLP (`Linear` $\to$ `ReLU` $\to$ `Dropout` $\to$ `Linear`) to predict all 30 targets.
    *   *Rationale:* This retains the ability to learn inter-label correlations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**) while avoiding the optimization pitfalls of complex heads (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**).

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Stream A:** Concatenate `question_title` and `question_body`.
    *   **Stream B:** Concatenate `question_title` and `answer`.
*   **Tokenization:** Use the `distilroberta-base` tokenizer.
    *   Generate `attention_mask` (standard padding mask).
    *   Generate `special_token_mask` or segment IDs to identify the "Answer" portion of Stream B for the Target-Masked Pooling.
*   **Dynamic Padding:** Pad sequences to the longest length in the batch to optimize throughput (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).

# Training
*   **Layer-Wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD):**
    *   Implement LLRD (e.g., decay factor 0.95) to assign progressively lower learning rates to the bottom layers of the backbone.
    *   *Rationale:* This respects the hierarchy of pre-trained features (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**), allowing the top layers to adapt to the "Contextualized Answer" task while preserving the robust linguistic foundations of the lower layers.
*   **Differential Learning Rates:** Apply a high base learning rate (`1e-3`) to the Head and a low base learning rate (`2e-5`) to the top layer of the Backbone (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
*   **Parameter Grouping:** Explicitly exclude `bias` and `LayerNorm` parameters from weight decay (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).
*   **Batch Size:** Use **16**. This fits in memory and provides the necessary gradient noise for robust generalization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
*   **Schedule:** Train for **5 epochs** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**) using a **Continuous Schedule** (Linear Decay with 10% Warmup). Do not reset the optimizer/scheduler; allow momentum to carry over (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00060**).
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Process the test set using the Asymmetric Input generation (Title+Body, Title+Answer).
*   **Prediction:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the 30 output logits.
*   **Validation:** Monitor Mean Column-wise Spearman's Correlation. Accept the model if it exceeds the previous best (`~0.408`) plus a small noise margin (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).
