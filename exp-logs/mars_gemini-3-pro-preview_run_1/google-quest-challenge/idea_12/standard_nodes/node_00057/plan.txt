The strategy involves implementing a **Continuous Learning Schedule** as recommended in `solution_lesson_node_00019`. The previous solution used a two-stage process where the optimizer and scheduler were reset after the warmup epoch. This reset discards the optimizer's momentum and forces a second warmup phase, which can hinder convergence. 

The new approach will:
1.  Modify `library/engine.py` to allow `get_optimizer_params` to include parameters even if they are currently frozen (`requires_grad=False`). This ensures the optimizer tracks the backbone parameters from the start, even during the warmup phase when they are not updated.
2.  Modify `runfile.py` to use a single, continuous training loop. The backbone will be frozen initially for the warmup epoch, but the optimizer and scheduler will be initialized once for the entire duration. After the warmup epoch, the backbone will be unfrozen, allowing the training to continue seamlessly with preserved momentum and scheduler state.

This change optimizes the fine-tuning process by maintaining continuity in the optimization trajectory.
