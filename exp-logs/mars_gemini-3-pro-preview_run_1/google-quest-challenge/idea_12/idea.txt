Based on the extensive empirical evidence gathered from previous experiments, I propose the **DistilRoBERTa Dual-Encoder with Query-Aware Attention Pooling**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation—**Query-Aware Pooling**—to bridge the gap between the efficiency of Dual-Encoders and the interaction depth of Cross-Encoders, without incurring the computational cost or context leakage risks of the latter.

### Model
**Architecture:** `distilroberta-base` Dual-Encoder.
*   **Rationale:** This backbone strikes the optimal balance between capacity and trainability for this dataset size (~4,400 samples). It avoids the "Starved Batch" instability of `deberta-v3-base` (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**) and the capacity bottlenecks of `deberta-v3-small` (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
*   **Dual-Branch Structure:** Process "Question" (Title + Body) and "Answer" independently to preserve the full 512-token context for each and protect intrinsic question features from leakage (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**).

**Structural Innovation: Query-Aware Attention Pooling**
Instead of relying solely on static global pooling, we implement a lightweight attention mechanism to dynamically extract relevant answer information conditioned on the question.
1.  **Question Branch:**
    *   Output: Sequence of hidden states $Q_{seq}$ and global vector $q_{avg}$ (Masked Average).
    *   Pooling: Extract $q_{avg}$ and $q_{max}$ (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**).
2.  **Answer Branch:**
    *   Output: Sequence of hidden states $A_{seq}$.
    *   **Attention Mechanism:** Treat $q_{avg}$ as the **Query** and $A_{seq}$ as the **Keys/Values**. Compute dot-product attention scores (masked for padding), apply Softmax, and compute the weighted sum of $A_{seq}$ to generate $a_{attn}$.
    *   *Benefit:* This creates a representation of the Answer that is specifically aligned with the Question's intent, capturing relevance signals that global averaging misses, without the cost of a full Cross-Encoder.
    *   Pooling: Also extract standard $a_{avg}$ and $a_{max}$ to capture intrinsic answer qualities.

**Fusion & Prediction:**
*   **Interaction-Aware Fusion:**
    *   Compute explicit interactions (Product and Difference) between the semantic centroids: ($q_{avg}, a_{avg}$) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
    *   Compute **Aligned Interactions** between the question and the attention-pooled answer: ($q_{avg}, a_{attn}$).
    *   Concatenate all features: $[q_{avg}, q_{max}, a_{avg}, a_{max}, a_{attn}, \text{Interactions}]$.
*   **Normalization:** Apply **Layer Normalization** to the high-dimensional fused vector to homogenize the feature scales (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
*   **Bottleneck Head:** Project the fused vector to the backbone dimension (768) via a Linear layer, followed by ReLU and Dropout ($p=0.1$). Avoid aggressive bottlenecks (<768) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00048**) or complex activations (GELU/LayerNorm) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**).
*   **Monolithic Output:** Predict all 30 targets simultaneously to leverage inter-label correlations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Concatenate `question_title` and `question_body` for the Question input; use `answer` for the Answer input.
*   **Feature Selection:** Rely solely on text. Do not inject metadata or use auxiliary embeddings, as these have proven to cause overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**).
*   **Dynamic Padding:** Pad sequences to the longest length in the batch to optimize throughput (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).

### Training
*   **Batch Size:** Use **16**. This fits comfortably in memory with `distilroberta` and provides the necessary gradient noise for robust generalization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
*   **Differential Learning Rates:** Apply a high learning rate (`1e-3`) to the scratch-initialized Head (including the Attention pooling) and a low learning rate (`2e-5`) to the Backbone (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
*   **Parameter Grouping:** Explicitly exclude `bias` and `LayerNorm` parameters from weight decay (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).
*   **Schedule:**
    *   **Epoch 1 (Head Warmup):** Freeze the backbone completely. Train only the head to align the random weights (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00050**).
    *   **Epochs 2-5 (Fine-tuning):** Unfreeze the backbone. Use a continuous linear decay schedule. Do **not** reset the optimizer or scheduler; allow momentum to carry over (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).
*   **Initialization:** Initialize the custom head weights using a Normal distribution ($\mu=0, \sigma=0.02$) to match the backbone's pre-trained statistics (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00051**). Do not re-initialize backbone layers (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**).
*   **Loss:** Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Pass the test set through the trained model.
*   **Prediction:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the 30 output logits.
*   **Validation:** Monitor Mean Column-wise Spearman's Correlation. Accept the model if it exceeds the previous best (`~0.408`) plus a small noise margin (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).
