To improve the model's performance, I will implement a strategy focusing on better text representation, enhanced feature interaction, and optimized training dynamics.

1.  **Refined Tokenization**: I will replace the simple whitespace tokenizer with a regex-based tokenizer (`re.findall(r'\w+', ...)`). This will strip punctuation attached to words (e.g., "word." becomes "word"), reducing vocabulary sparsity and improving embedding utilization.
2.  **Interaction Features**: Building on the Dual-Branch DAN architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001), I will explicitly model the relationship between the Question and Answer branches. I will augment the MLP input by concatenating the **element-wise product** ($q \odot a$) and **absolute difference** ($|q - a|$) of the pooled embeddings. This allows the network to better capture semantic alignment and discrepancies.
3.  **Learning Rate Scheduling**: I will introduce a `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler to the `Trainer`. This will dynamically lower the learning rate when validation Spearman correlation plateaus, allowing the model to settle into a better optimum.
4.  **Configuration Updates**: I will point the cache to a new directory to force data reprocessing, and increase `EPOCHS` and `PATIENCE` to accommodate the scheduler.
