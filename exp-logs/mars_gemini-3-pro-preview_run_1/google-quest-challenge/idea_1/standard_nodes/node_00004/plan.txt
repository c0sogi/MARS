To optimize the model's performance, I will implement a targeted enhancement to the Dual-Branch Deep Averaging Network (DAN) architecture by introducing **explicit interaction features** between the Question and Answer embeddings.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Interaction Features:** In addition to concatenating the independent Question and Answer representations, I will compute and concatenate their element-wise product (`q_avg * a_avg`) and absolute difference (`|q_avg - a_avg|`). This standard technique in semantic text similarity (often used in Siamese networks) allows the MLP to directly leverage the alignment and discrepancy between the question and answer, improving the model's ability to learn "interaction logic" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
2.  **Hyperparameter Tuning:** I will moderately increase the `EMBEDDING_DIM` (128 -> 160) and `HIDDEN_DIM` (256 -> 320) to accommodate the richer feature space. I will also increase `EPOCHS` (15 -> 20) to allow the model to fully converge with the added complexity.
3.  **Conditional Submission:** I will update `runfile.py` to strictly enforce the requirement that a submission is generated *only* if the validation score exceeds the previous best of ~0.2875.
