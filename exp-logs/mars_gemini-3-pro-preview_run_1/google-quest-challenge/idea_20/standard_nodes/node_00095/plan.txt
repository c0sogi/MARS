The `AttributeError` occurs because the training engine (`library/engine.py`) attempts to access attributes (`embeddings`, `shared_layers`, `q_branch`, `a_branch`) that do not exist in the `SiameseDualEncoder` defined in `library/model.py`. The model instead uses `base_model` and `head`. Furthermore, this incorrect freezing logic is duplicated in both `set_backbone_freezing` and `train_one_epoch`, causing the error to persist if only one is fixed (Cite debug_lesson_15). I will update both locations in `library/engine.py` to correctly identify the backbone as `model.base_model` and the head as `model.head`.
