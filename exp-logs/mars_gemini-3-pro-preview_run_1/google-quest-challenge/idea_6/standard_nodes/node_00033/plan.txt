To optimize the solution, I will implement a **Dual-Encoder (Siamese) Architecture** using `distilroberta-base`. This approach addresses the shortcomings of the previous Single-Encoder (Cross-Encoder) model, specifically the context leakage between Question and Answer (Cite Lesson 00030) and the truncation of long inputs (Cite Lesson 00032). By processing Question and Answer in separate branches, we effectively double the context window (512+512 tokens) and preserve the integrity of intrinsic features. I will also revert the backbone to `distilroberta-base` as larger models like `deberta-v3` have shown diminishing returns on this small dataset (Cite Lesson 00023).

Here are the specific changes:
1.  **Config**: Switch `model_name` to `"distilroberta-base"` and update the working directory.
2.  **Data**: Modify `process_text_data` and `Collate` to tokenize Question and Answer separately, producing independent `q_input_ids` and `a_input_ids`.
3.  **Model**: Update `QuestModel` to perform two forward passes (one for Q, one for A) and then fuse the pooled representations.
4.  **Engine/Runfile**: Update training and evaluation loops to handle the new dual-input batch structure.
