As advised in the error analysis and referencing `debug_lesson_3` (Cite debug_lesson_3), enabling gradient checkpointing on a shared backbone can lead to backward pass errors. Therefore, the correct fix is to reduce the training batch size to fit the computation graph within the available memory.
