To optimize the performance of the Global-Context Anchored Input-Injected Network (GCA-IIN), I will implement the following targeted changes based on the provided lessons and failure analysis:

1.  **Increase Background Class Weight (0.5 $\to$ 0.8):**
    The failure analysis of the previous solution showed a strong negative correlation ($-0.85$) between the number of gestures and the error rate. This indicates that the model struggles primarily with sparse sequences (containing few gestures and mostly background), likely by hallucinating gestures (insertion errors).
    *Cite solution_lesson_node_00070:* "Maintaining a higher weight for the 'Background' class is crucial to prevent the hallucination of gestures during transition periods." By increasing the penalty for misclassifying background frames, we suppress these insertion errors.

2.  **Increase Dropout (0.3 $\to$ 0.4):**
    The previous model converged rapidly (Epoch 19) on this small dataset (~300 sequences). To improve generalization and prevent overfitting to the specific training noise, I will slightly increase the dropout rate.
    *Cite solution_lesson_node_00023:* Structural regularization is key for small datasets.

3.  **Extend Training Budget (30 $\to$ 40 Epochs):**
    With increased regularization (Dropout) and a stricter loss landscape (Higher Background Weight), convergence may take slightly longer. I will increase the epoch limit to ensure the model has sufficient time to settle.
