To optimize the Context-Gated Residual GRU (CGR-GRU) model, I will implement a strategy focused on **robustness and regularization** to improve generalization on the small dataset.

1.  **Architectural Refinement**: I will increase the kernel size of the initial temporal convolution in the `InputStem` from 5 to 7. This aligns with **Lesson 00011**, which demonstrated that a larger receptive field at the feature extraction stage improves performance by capturing broader local context.
2.  **Enhanced Data Augmentation**: I will introduce **Random Time Masking** in the `CollateFn`. By zeroing out contiguous blocks of frames (5-15 frames) during training, I force the GRU to rely on temporal context to interpolate missing data, improving robustness against sensor noise and occlusion.
3.  **Optimization & Regularization**:
    *   **Class Weighting**: I will increase the `BG_CLASS_WEIGHT` from 0.5 to 0.7. This moderate increase helps suppress insertion errors (fragmentation) which are detrimental to the Levenshtein metric, without causing excessive deletions (**Lesson 00011**).
    *   **Scheduler**: I will switch from `ReduceLROnPlateau` to `CosineAnnealingLR`. This proactive decay schedule often yields better convergence in fixed-budget training runs compared to reactive plateaus (**Lesson 00031**).
    *   **Weight Decay**: I will explicitly set `WEIGHT_DECAY` to 0.05 (up from default 0.01) to impose stronger structural regularization (**Lesson 00023**).
    *   **Training Budget**: I will slightly extend `NUM_EPOCHS` to 60 and `EARLY_STOPPING_PATIENCE` to 20 to allow the Cosine Annealing schedule to complete its descent.
