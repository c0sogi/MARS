To optimize the performance of the Context-Gated Residual GRU (CGR-GRU) model, I will focus on enriching the input representation and enhancing the data augmentation strategy.

1.  **Explicit Motion Features (Velocity):** I will modify the data loader to compute the **velocity** (first-order temporal difference) of the skeleton joints and concatenate it with the positional data. This increases the skeleton input dimension from 60 to 120. Explicit velocity features provide the RNN with direct dynamic cues, reducing the burden of learning these derivatives from noisy raw positions (Cite Lesson 00005).
2.  **Time Masking Augmentation:** I will introduce **Time Masking** in the `CollateFn`. By randomly zeroing out contiguous blocks of time steps in the training data, I force the model to rely on temporal context and become robust to signal interruptions, complementing the existing Random Channel Masking (Cite Lesson 00013).
3.  **Regularization & Configuration:** To accommodate the richer feature set and prevent overfitting, I will increase the `DROPOUT` rate from 0.3 to 0.4 (Cite Lesson 00023). I will also update the `SKELETON_INPUT_DIM` in the configuration and point the cache to a new directory to ensure stale features are not loaded.
