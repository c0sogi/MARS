Based on the provided lessons, specifically **Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00030`**, I will remove the Self-Training (Teacher-Student) component of the pipeline. The lesson highlights that Hard Pseudo-Labeling often degrades performance in high-performing baselines by reinforcing errors, especially when using threshold-based labeling. Reverting to a robust **5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensemble** on the labeled data (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00016`) is the recommended strategy to maximize robustness and performance.

I will also remove progress bars (`tqdm`) to comply with the instruction to "Only print the required information".

Here are the changes:

1.  **`library/config.py`**: Remove pseudo-labeling configuration parameters.
2.  **`library/model.py`**: Remove `tqdm` from training and inference loops.
3.  **`runfile.py`**: Refactor the main execution loop to perform a single stage of 5-Fold Cross-Validation, removing the Teacher-Student logic.
