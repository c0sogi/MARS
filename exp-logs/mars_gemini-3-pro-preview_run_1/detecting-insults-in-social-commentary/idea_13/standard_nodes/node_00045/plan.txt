The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the use of Gradient Checkpointing (`gradient_checkpointing_enable()`) in the `HybridModel`. As noted in the error analysis and **debug_lesson_9**, this optimization technique often conflicts with the autograd engine in complex architectures like DeBERTa-v3, especially when used in conjunction with Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP). Since the pipeline already employs AMP for memory efficiency, Gradient Checkpointing is unnecessary and causing instability. I will disable it in `library/models.py` to resolve the crash.
