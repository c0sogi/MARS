Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Target-Transformed Stratified Tri-Paradigm Stacking Ensemble**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in the **Modeling of the Target Variable** to address the bounded nature of the "Pawpularity" score (1-100). It combines this with the successful "Tri-Paradigm" backbone selection (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`, `solution_lesson_node_00052`), "Heterogeneous Expert" diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`), and "Stratified Validation" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00054`).

# Model

### 1. Feature Extraction (Backbones)
I will utilize three high-capacity backbones representing orthogonal pre-training paradigms, adhering to their native resolutions via `AutoImageProcessor` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`). I will upgrade the textural expert to its modern V2 variant to leverage masked autoencoder pre-training.

*   **Semantic Expert:** **SigLIP So400M (384px)**. Retained for its superior performance on subjective tasks compared to CLIP (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`).
*   **Geometric Expert:** **DINOv2 ViT-Large (518px)**. Retained for its self-supervised understanding of object layout and structure (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`).
*   **Textural Expert:** **ConvNeXt V2 Large (Native)**. **(Upgrade)** Replaces ConvNeXt V1. ConvNeXt V2 utilizes Fully Convolutional Masked Autoencoder (FCMAE) pre-training, providing richer textural and shape features than standard supervised pre-training (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).

### 2. Level-0 Heterogeneous Experts with Target Transformation
For *each* of the three backbones, I will train four distinct regression experts. **Crucially, I will decouple the target variable representation based on the estimator's inductive bias:**

*   **Manifold-Linear Experts (Ridge & SVR):**
    *   **Innovation:** These models assume Gaussian residuals and struggle with bounded intervals (1-100). I will train them on **Logit-Transformed Targets**.
    *   *Transformation:* Map $y \in [1, 100]$ to $y' \in [0, 1]$, clip to $[0.001, 0.999]$, then apply $z = \log(y' / (1-y'))$. This maps the bounded score to an unbounded space $(-\infty, \infty)$, linearizing the problem for the solver.
    *   **Algorithms:** **Ridge Regression** (Linear) and **SVR (RBF Kernel)** (Non-Linear).
*   **Partitioning Experts (Trees):**
    *   These models are invariant to monotonic transformations and handle non-linear boundaries naturally. They will be trained on the **Raw Targets** to preserve interpretability and avoid transformation artifacts.
    *   **Algorithms:** **ExtraTrees Regressor** (Bagging) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`) and **LightGBM Regressor** (Boosting) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

### 3. Level-1 Meta-Learner
The predictions from the 12 experts will be aggregated by a **Bayesian Ridge Regressor** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).
*   *Note:* Predictions from Ridge/SVR will be **Inverse-Transformed** (Sigmoid -> Scale) back to the [1, 100] domain before being passed to the Meta-Learner.

# Data

### Preprocessing
1.  **Automated Transformation:** Use `AutoImageProcessor` for SigLIP, DINOv2, and ConvNeXt V2 to ensure exact alignment with pre-training statistics (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`).
2.  **Feature-Space Augmentation:** Apply **Feature-Space Augmentation Averaging** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`) by averaging embeddings of original and horizontally flipped images.

### Feature Engineering for Level-0
I will apply expert-specific preprocessing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`):
*   **For Ridge/SVR:** Concatenate [Image Embedding, Metadata] and apply **StandardScaler** to the full vector (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
*   **For ExtraTrees/LightGBM:** Apply **PCA** to the image embeddings (reducing to ~64 components) before concatenating with raw binary metadata.

# Training

1.  **Stratified Full-Dataset Cross-Validation:** I will merge the training and validation sets and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** (binning the continuous `Pawpularity` target) to generate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions. This ensures the experts are trained on representative distributions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00054`, `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Ridge:** `RidgeCV` with alpha search up to 50,000 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **SVR:** Grid search for `C` and `epsilon`.
    *   **LightGBM:** Tune `learning_rate`, `num_leaves`, and regularization.
    *   **ExtraTrees:** Fixed robust settings (500 estimators).

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:** Process test images through the three backbones (using `AutoImageProcessor` and flip-averaging).
2.  **Level-0 Prediction:**
    *   Generate predictions from Ridge/SVR (in Logit space), then apply **Inverse Sigmoid** transformation to map back to [1, 100].
    *   Generate predictions from ExtraTrees/LightGBM (already in [1, 100]).
3.  **Level-1 Prediction:** Feed the 12 calibrated predictions to the Bayesian Ridge Meta-Learner.
4.  **Validation:** Perform **Nested Cross-Validation** on the Meta-Learner to calculate the final RMSE (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00029`).
