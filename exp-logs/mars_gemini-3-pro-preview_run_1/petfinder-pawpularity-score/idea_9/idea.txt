Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Multi-Scale Tri-Paradigm Stacking Ensemble with Heterogeneous Experts**. This strategy synthesizes the successful "Tri-Paradigm" backbone selection (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`) and "Heterogeneous Expert" stacking (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`, `solution_lesson_node_00027`) while addressing the geometric distortion issues of previous attempts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00032`) and maximizing data utility (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00033`).

# Model

### 1. Feature Extraction (Backbones)
I will utilize the three high-capacity backbones identified as optimal for their orthogonal inductive biases (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`), retaining standard pre-trained weights (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00016`):
*   **Semantic Expert:** **CLIP ViT-L/14** (Captures text-aligned subjective concepts).
*   **Geometric Expert:** **DINOv2 ViT-Large** (Captures object structure/layout).
*   **Textural Expert:** **ConvNeXt Large** (Captures high-frequency details).

### 2. Multi-Scale Input Strategy (Structural Innovation)
To address the "padding artifacts" failure of Idea 7 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00032`) while capturing the "Subject Focus" signal, I will implement a **Dual-View Feature Extraction** strategy based on cropping rather than padding:
*   **Global View:** The full image resized (warped) to the target resolution. This captures the entire context.
*   **Local View (Central Crop):** A central crop covering ~75-80% of the image area, resized to the target resolution. This focuses on the subject (pet) and removes background clutter, providing a "zoomed-in" feature set that is non-linearly distinct from the global view, unlike the redundant linear projections in Idea 8 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00038`).

### 3. Level-0 Heterogeneous Experts
For each of the 6 feature streams (3 Backbones $\times$ 2 Views), I will train three distinct regression experts to maximize algorithmic diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`):
*   **Linear Expert:** **Ridge Regression**. Efficiently maps global trends (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00001`).
*   **Kernel Expert:** **SVR (RBF Kernel)**. Captures non-linear manifolds.
*   **Partitioning Expert:** **ExtraTrees Regressor**. Captures conditional logic and interactions with binary metadata (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).

This results in **18 Level-0 Experts** (3 Backbones $\times$ 2 Views $\times$ 3 Algorithms).

### 4. Level-1 Meta-Learner
The predictions from these 18 experts will be aggregated by a **Bayesian Ridge Regressor**. This model is chosen for its robustness to collinearity (expected between Global and Local views of the same backbone) and its ability to automatically infer regularization parameters (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).

# Data

### Preprocessing
1.  **Image Transformation:** Generate **Global (Warped)** and **Local (Central Crop)** versions of each image. Normalize pixel values according to backbone-specific requirements. Strictly avoid padding (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00032`).
2.  **Feature-Space Augmentation:** Apply **Feature-Space Augmentation Averaging** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`). Compute embeddings for both original and horizontally flipped versions of both views and average them.

### Feature Engineering for Experts
I will apply expert-specific preprocessing to the concatenated vector of [Image Embedding, Metadata]:
*   **For Ridge/SVR:** Apply **StandardScaler** to the full vector to ensure proper regularization weighting (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
*   **For ExtraTrees:** Apply **PCA** to the image embeddings (reducing to ~64 components) before concatenating with raw binary metadata. This mitigates the curse of dimensionality for tree splits (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).

# Training

1.  **Full-Dataset Cross-Validation:** I will merge the training and validation sets and perform **5-Fold Cross-Validation** on the entire labeled dataset. This ensures the Level-0 experts see the maximum diversity of data and the Meta-Learner is trained on OOF predictions from the full distribution (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
2.  **Level-0 Optimization:**
    *   **Ridge:** Use `RidgeCV` with an expanded alpha search range (up to 50,000) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **SVR/ExtraTrees:** Train on the specific feature subsets (Scaled vs. PCA) defined above.
3.  **Level-1 Optimization:** Train the Bayesian Ridge Meta-Learner on the matrix of OOF predictions from the 18 experts.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:** Process test images through the 3 backbones (Global + Local views, with flip-averaging) to generate 6 embedding sets.
2.  **Prediction:** Transform features (Scaling/PCA) and pass to the 18 trained Level-0 experts (averaged across folds). Feed these base predictions to the Level-1 Meta-Learner for the final score.
3.  **Validation:** Perform **Nested Cross-Validation** on the Meta-Learner (generating "Meta-OOF" predictions) to calculate the final RMSE. This ensures the reported metric reflects true generalization error (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00029`).
