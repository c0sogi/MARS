Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Quad-Paradigm Stacking Ensemble with Zero-Shot Aesthetic Injection**. This strategy evolves the previous "Tri-Paradigm" approach by introducing a fourth estimator paradigm (Instance-Based Learning), leveraging the text-encoding capabilities of Vision-Language Models for feature engineering, and optimizing the resolution and validation strategies.

# Model

### 1. Feature Extraction (Backbones)
I will utilize three high-capacity backbones to capture orthogonal feature representations (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`), upgrading the Semantic expert to a higher resolution to mitigate resizing loss.
*   **Semantic Expert:** **CLIP ViT-L/14@336px** (via `sentence-transformers`). I will use the 336px resolution variant if available (or standard 224px otherwise) to capture finer facial details. This model aligns visual features with language concepts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).
*   **Geometric Expert:** **DINOv2 ViT-Large** (via `timm`). Captures object structure and layout via self-supervision.
*   **Textural Expert:** **ConvNeXt Large** (via `timm`). Captures high-frequency details and local textures (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).

### 2. Zero-Shot Aesthetic Injection (Structural Innovation)
To explicitly target the subjective "Pawpularity" metric, I will exploit the text-encoder of the CLIP model. I will define a set of **Aesthetic Prompts** (e.g., *"a cute pet"*, *"an adorable photo"*, *"a high quality image"*, *"a blurry photo"*). For every image, I will compute the **Cosine Similarity** between its CLIP image embedding and these pre-computed text embeddings. These scalar "Zero-Shot Scores" will be concatenated with the image embeddings and metadata, injecting explicit human-prior knowledge into the feature space.

### 3. Level-0 Heterogeneous Experts
For each of the three backbones, I will train four distinct regression experts to maximize algorithmic diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`):
*   **Linear Expert:** **Ridge Regression**. Maps global linear trends.
*   **Kernel Expert:** **SVR (RBF Kernel)**. Captures non-linear manifolds.
*   **Partitioning Expert:** **ExtraTrees Regressor**. Captures conditional logic and interactions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).
*   **Retrieval Expert (New):** **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) Regressor**. Unlike the other parametric models, KNN relies on local density estimation in the feature space. It operates on the premise that if a test image is semantically close to a training image (in CLIP/DINO space), it likely shares a similar engagement score.

### 4. Level-1 Meta-Learner
The predictions from these 12 experts (3 Backbones $\times$ 4 Estimators) will be aggregated by a **Bayesian Ridge Regressor**. This model is robust to collinearity (expected among experts from the same backbone) and automatically infers regularization parameters (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).

# Data

### Preprocessing
1.  **Image Transformation:** Images will be **resized (warped)** to the specific input resolution of each backbone (e.g., 336x336 for CLIP, 224x224 for others). I will strictly avoid padding, as it introduces artifacts that degrade frozen backbone performance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00032`).
2.  **Feature-Space Augmentation:** I will apply **Feature-Space Augmentation Averaging** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`). Embeddings will be computed for both the original and horizontally flipped images and averaged to enforce invariance.

### Feature Engineering for Experts
I will apply expert-specific preprocessing to the concatenated vector of [Image Embedding, Metadata, Zero-Shot Scores]:
*   **For Ridge/SVR:** Apply **StandardScaler** to the full vector to ensure proper regularization weighting between high-variance embeddings and binary metadata (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
*   **For ExtraTrees/KNN:** Apply **PCA** (Principal Component Analysis) to the image embeddings (reducing to ~64 components) before concatenating with metadata/scores. This mitigates the "curse of dimensionality" for distance-based (KNN) and split-based (Trees) models (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).

# Training

1.  **Full-Dataset Cross-Validation:** I will merge the training and validation sets and perform **5-Fold Cross-Validation** on the entire labeled dataset. This ensures the Level-0 experts see the maximum diversity of data and the Meta-Learner is trained on OOF predictions from the full distribution (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
2.  **Hyperparameter Optimization:**
    *   **Ridge:** Use `RidgeCV` with an expanded alpha search range (up to 50,000) to prevent boundary clipping (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **KNN:** Optimize `n_neighbors` (e.g., 5 to 50) and distance metric (Cosine vs Euclidean).
    *   **SVR/ExtraTrees:** Fixed robust hyperparameters or limited grid search to manage runtime.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:** Test images will be processed through the three backbones (with flip-averaging) to generate embeddings and Zero-Shot Scores.
2.  **Prediction:** The features will be transformed (Scaling/PCA) and passed to the 12 trained Level-0 experts (averaged across folds). These base predictions will be fed to the Level-1 Meta-Learner for the final score.
3.  **Validation:** I will perform **Nested Cross-Validation** on the Meta-Learner (generating "Meta-OOF" predictions) to calculate the final RMSE. This ensures the reported metric reflects true generalization error and not overfitting to the Level-0 OOFs (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00029`).
