Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned," I propose a **Paradigm-Specific Augmented Stacking Ensemble**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in **Feature-Space Augmentation**: instead of applying a uniform augmentation strategy across all backbones, I will tailor the test-time augmentation (TTA) to the specific inductive bias of each expert paradigm (Semantic, Geometric, Textural). This maximizes the signal quality for each backbone type while preserving the successful "Tri-Paradigm" architecture (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`, `solution_lesson_node_00052`) and "Heterogeneous Expert" diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Model

### 1. Feature Extraction (Backbones)
I will utilize three high-capacity backbones with orthogonal pre-training objectives. Crucially, I will standardize the "Textural" expert to a higher resolution to match the Semantic expert, ensuring fine-grained detail capture across the pipeline.

*   **Semantic Expert:** **SigLIP So400M (384px)**. Retained for its superior dense feature representation for subjective tasks (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`).
*   **Geometric Expert:** **DINOv2 ViT-Large (518px)**. Retained for its self-supervised understanding of object layout and structure (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`).
*   **Textural Expert:** **ConvNeXt Large (384px, ImageNet-22k)**.
    *   *Upgrade:* I will select the **384px** resolution variant of ConvNeXt V1 pre-trained on **ImageNet-22k**. This addresses the resolution mismatch in previous experiments and strictly adheres to the lesson that pre-training scale (22k classes) outweighs architectural novelty (V2 1k) for this task (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00061`).

### 2. Level-0 Heterogeneous Experts
For *each* of the three backbones, I will train four distinct regression experts to maximize algorithmic diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`):
*   **Linear:** **Ridge Regression** (Maps global trends).
*   **Kernel:** **SVR (RBF Kernel)** (Captures non-linear manifolds).
*   **Bagging:** **ExtraTrees Regressor** (Reduces variance).
*   **Boosting:** **LightGBM Regressor** (Reduces bias and models sequential interactions).

### 3. Level-1 Meta-Learner
The 12 predictions will be aggregated by a **Bayesian Ridge Regressor**. I will strictly avoid adding interaction terms or auxiliary features, keeping the meta-learner simple to prevent overfitting (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00058`).

# Data

### 1. Structural Innovation: Paradigm-Specific Feature Augmentation
Instead of a uniform "Flip-Averaging" strategy (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`), I will implement an augmentation strategy that respects the spatial sensitivity of each backbone:
*   **For SigLIP & DINOv2 (ViTs):** Apply **Horizontal Flip Averaging** only. These models rely on global positional embeddings and semantic composition. Aggressive cropping (e.g., corners) destroys the global layout ("Near", "Group") that these models excel at capturing.
*   **For ConvNeXt (CNN):** Apply **5-Crop Averaging** (Center, Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Left, Bottom-Right). As a CNN with translation invariance, ConvNeXt excels at local texture analysis. Averaging embeddings from 5 spatial crops ensures the model captures high-frequency details ("Blur", "Focus") across the entire frame, not just the center, effectively increasing the sample density for texture features.

### 2. Preprocessing
*   **Automated Transformation:** Use `AutoImageProcessor` for each backbone to ensure correct normalization and resizing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`). For the ConvNeXt 5-crops, I will derive the crop coordinates manually but apply the processor's standard resize/normalize functions to the cropped regions.
*   **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Ridge/SVR:** Concatenate [Embeddings, Metadata] and apply **StandardScaler** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
    *   **ExtraTrees/LightGBM:** Apply **PCA** to embeddings (~64 components) before concatenating with raw metadata (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).

# Training

1.  **Stratified Full-Dataset Cross-Validation:** I will merge the training and validation sets and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** (binning the continuous target) to generate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions. This ensures representative training distributions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00054`, `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
2.  **Target Representation:** Train all experts on **Raw Targets**. I will strictly avoid Logit/Sigmoid transformations, as previous experiments proved they degrade performance on this bounded metric (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00060`).
3.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Ridge:** `RidgeCV` with alpha search up to 50,000 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **SVR:** Grid search for `C` and `epsilon`.
    *   **LightGBM:** Tune `learning_rate`, `num_leaves`, and `reg_alpha`/`lambda`.
    *   **ExtraTrees:** Fixed robust settings (500 estimators).
    *   *Strategy:* Prioritize this tuning over adding more experts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`).

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:**
    *   Process SigLIP/DINOv2 with Flip-Averaging.
    *   Process ConvNeXt with 5-Crop Averaging.
2.  **Prediction:** Transform features (Scaling/PCA) and pass to the 12 trained Level-0 experts (averaged across folds). Feed these base predictions to the Level-1 Meta-Learner.
3.  **Validation:** Perform **Nested Cross-Validation** on the Meta-Learner to calculate the final RMSE, ensuring the reported metric reflects true generalization error (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00029`).
