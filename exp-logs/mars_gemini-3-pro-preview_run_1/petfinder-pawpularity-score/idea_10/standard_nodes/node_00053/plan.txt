My optimization strategy focuses on **maximizing data utility for the stacking ensemble** by implementing **Full-Dataset Cross-Validation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033). Instead of training Level-0 experts solely on the training set (~80% of data) and validating on a static hold-out set, I will merge the training and validation sets into a single combined dataset. I will then perform 5-Fold Cross-Validation on this combined set to generate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions for all samples.

This approach increases the training data available to the base models by approximately 25%, improving their generalization capability. The Level-1 Meta-Learner will then be trained on these higher-quality OOF predictions. To satisfy the requirement of reporting a validation metric on the specific hold-out set, I will extract the subset of OOF predictions corresponding to the original validation samples and compute the RMSE on that subset. This ensures the metric remains comparable (and likely better due to stronger base models) while leveraging the full dataset for training. I will also introduce a `cache_suffix` to the training pipeline to prevent conflicts with the previous training-set-only cache.
