Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose a **Native Multi-Resolution Tri-Paradigm Stacking Ensemble**.

This strategy evolves the successful "Tri-Paradigm" approach (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`) by upgrading the semantic backbone to a modern **SigLIP** architecture and orchestrating a **Multi-Resolution** feature extraction strategy. This addresses the failure of manual multi-scale cropping (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`) by leveraging the native input resolutions of distinct backbones to capture fine, global, and textural details without artificial preprocessing artifacts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`).

# Model

### 1. Feature Extraction: Native Multi-Resolution Backbones
Instead of forcing all models to a standard 224x224 resolution—which loses fine detail—or manually cropping—which introduces optimization overhead—I will utilize three backbones that natively operate at distinct resolutions and paradigms:

*   **Fine-Grained Semantic Expert:** **SigLIP So400M (Patch 14, 384px)**.
    *   *Innovation:* Replaces CLIP ViT-L/14. SigLIP (Sigmoid Loss for Language Image Pre-training) offers superior image-text alignment for subjective tasks (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`) and operates at a higher native resolution (**384x384**). This allows it to capture finer facial details ("Eyes", "Focus") that are lost at 224px.
*   **Global Geometric Expert:** **DINOv2 ViT-Large (518px)**.
    *   *Configuration:* I will utilize the **518x518** resolution variant of DINOv2. As a self-supervised model, it excels at understanding object layout and geometry. The high resolution ensures that the "Subject Focus" and "Near" signals are preserved with high fidelity.
*   **Local Textural Expert:** **ConvNeXt Large (224px)**.
    *   *Retention:* Retained at standard **224x224** resolution. CNNs are efficient at capturing high-frequency textures (fur, blur) which are scale-invariant enough to be captured at lower resolutions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).

### 2. Level-0 Heterogeneous Experts
To maximize diversity without exploding the model count (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`), I will train three distinct estimators for *each* of the three backbones (Total = 9 Experts). This adheres to the "Heterogeneous Expert" strategy (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`):
*   **Linear Expert:** **Ridge Regression**. Maps global linear trends.
*   **Kernel Expert:** **SVR (RBF Kernel)**. Captures non-linear embedding manifolds.
*   **Partitioning Expert:** **ExtraTrees Regressor**. Captures conditional logic and interactions between visual features and binary metadata (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).

### 3. Level-1 Meta-Learner
The 9 predictions will be aggregated by a **Bayesian Ridge Regressor**, which automatically handles the correlation between experts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).

# Data

### Preprocessing
1.  **Automated Transformation:** I will strictly utilize the `AutoImageProcessor` associated with each specific backbone (SigLIP, DINOv2, ConvNeXt) to handle resizing, cropping, and normalization. This ensures the input data distribution exactly matches the pre-training protocol, avoiding the performance degradation caused by manual resizing or padding (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`, `solution_lesson_node_00032`).
2.  **Feature-Space Augmentation:** I will apply **Feature-Space Augmentation Averaging** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`). Embeddings will be computed for both the original and horizontally flipped images and averaged to enforce invariance.

### Feature Engineering
I will apply expert-specific transformations to the concatenated vector of [Image Embedding, Metadata]:
*   **For Ridge/SVR:** Apply **StandardScaler** to the full vector to ensure proper regularization weighting (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
*   **For ExtraTrees:** Apply **PCA** to the image embeddings (reducing to ~64 components) before concatenating with raw binary metadata. This mitigates the curse of dimensionality for tree splits (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).

# Training

1.  **Full-Dataset Cross-Validation:** I will merge the training and validation sets and perform **5-Fold Cross-Validation** on the entire labeled dataset to generate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions. This maximizes data utility for the base learners (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
2.  **Rigorous Optimization:** Unlike the failed multi-scale attempt (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`), keeping the expert count to 9 allows for full hyperparameter tuning within the time limit:
    *   **Ridge:** `RidgeCV` with alpha search up to 50,000 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **SVR:** Grid Search for `C` and `epsilon`.
    *   **ExtraTrees:** 500 estimators with `max_depth` tuning.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:** Test images will be processed through the three backbones (SigLIP, DINOv2, ConvNeXt) using their respective `AutoImageProcessor` and flip-averaging.
2.  **Prediction:** Features will be transformed (Scaling/PCA) and passed to the 9 Level-0 experts (averaged across folds). Base predictions will be aggregated by the Level-1 Meta-Learner.
3.  **Validation:** I will perform **Nested Cross-Validation** on the Meta-Learner to calculate the final RMSE, ensuring the metric reflects true generalization error (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00029`).
