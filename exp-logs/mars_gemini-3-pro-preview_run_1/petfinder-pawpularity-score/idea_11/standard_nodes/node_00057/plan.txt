To improve the solution's performance, I will implement the following changes based on the lessons learned:

1.  **Increase Cross-Validation Folds**: I will increase `N_FOLDS` from 5 to 10 in `Config`. This increases the effective training set size for the Level-0 experts from 80% to 90% of the available data, improving their generalization capability and the quality of the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions used to train the meta-learner (Cite Lesson 54).
2.  **Optimize Meta-Learner with Non-Negativity**: I will replace the `BayesianRidge` meta-learner with a `Ridge` regressor that enforces **positive coefficients** (`positive=True`), wrapped in a `GridSearchCV` to optimize `alpha`. Enforcing non-negativity prevents the ensemble from learning subtractive relationships between highly correlated experts, which is a common source of overfitting in stacking (Cite Lesson 19).
3.  **Expand Hyperparameter Search**: I will extend the Support Vector Regression (SVR) `C` parameter grid to include `100.0` and `500.0`. The previous upper bound of `50.0` may have been too restrictive for the high-dimensional embedding space, potentially clipping the optimal regularization strength (Cite Lesson 10).
4.  **Refine Boosting**: I will decrease the `learning_rate` of the LightGBM expert to `0.005` and increase `n_estimators` to `4000`. This allows the boosting model to converge more gradually, typically resulting in better generalization on the residuals (Cite Lesson 56).

    We use Ridge with positive=True to enforce additive contributions from experts,
    preventing overfitting due to collinearity. We use GridSearchCV to find the optimal alpha.
