Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned," I propose a **Stratified Tri-Paradigm Stacking Ensemble with Gradient Boosting Experts**.

This strategy structurally evolves the previous "Tri-Paradigm" approach by:
1.  **Algorithmic Expansion:** Introducing **Gradient Boosting (LightGBM)** as a fourth Level-0 expert paradigm to capture non-linear feature-metadata interactions that Ridge (Linear), SVR (Kernel), and ExtraTrees (Bagging) might miss.
2.  **Validation Correction:** Implementing **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation** on the merged dataset to resolve the distribution mismatch observed in previous random splitting attempts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00054`).
3.  **Backbone Optimization:** Standardizing on the **SigLIP** architecture for semantic features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`).

# Model

### 1. Feature Extraction (Backbones)
I will utilize three high-capacity backbones to capture orthogonal feature representations (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`), utilizing their native resolutions via `AutoImageProcessor` to avoid preprocessing artifacts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`):
*   **Semantic Expert:** **SigLIP So400M (384px)**. This replaces CLIP. SigLIP's sigmoid loss training produces denser, more discriminative features for subjective tasks (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`).
*   **Geometric Expert:** **DINOv2 ViT-Large (518px)**. Retained for its superior self-supervised understanding of object layout and geometry (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00022`).
*   **Textural Expert:** **ConvNeXt Large (224px)**. Retained for capturing high-frequency local textures (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).

### 2. Level-0 Heterogeneous Experts
For *each* of the three backbones, I will train four distinct regression experts. This "Quad-Expert" setup maximizes algorithmic diversity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00023`):
*   **Linear Expert:** **Ridge Regression**. Maps global linear trends.
*   **Kernel Expert:** **SVR (RBF Kernel)**. Captures non-linear manifolds based on embedding distance.
*   **Bagging Expert:** **ExtraTrees Regressor**. Captures conditional logic via randomized decision trees (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).
*   **Boosting Expert (New):** **LightGBM Regressor**. Unlike ExtraTrees (which reduces variance), LightGBM reduces bias and can learn subtler, sequential interactions between the visual features and metadata. It will operate on the same PCA-reduced feature set as ExtraTrees.

### 3. Level-1 Meta-Learner
The predictions from these 12 experts (3 Backbones $\times$ 4 Algorithms) will be aggregated by a **Bayesian Ridge Regressor**, which automatically handles the correlation between experts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).

# Data

### Preprocessing
1.  **Automated Transformation:** Use the specific `AutoImageProcessor` for SigLIP, DINOv2, and ConvNeXt to ensure exact alignment with pre-training statistics (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00048`).
2.  **Feature-Space Augmentation:** Apply **Feature-Space Augmentation Averaging** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`). Compute embeddings for original and horizontally flipped images and average them.

### Feature Engineering for Experts
I will apply expert-specific preprocessing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`):
*   **For Ridge/SVR:** Concatenate [Image Embedding, Metadata] and apply **StandardScaler** to the full vector (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
*   **For ExtraTrees/LightGBM:** Apply **PCA** to the image embeddings (reducing to ~64 components) before concatenating with raw binary metadata. This mitigates the curse of dimensionality for tree-based splits.

# Training

1.  **Stratified Full-Dataset Cross-Validation:** I will merge the training and validation sets and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** on the entire labeled dataset.
    *   *Correction:* Unlike the failed random K-Fold attempt (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00054`), I will bin the continuous `Pawpularity` target (e.g., into 10-15 bins) and use these bins to stratify the folds. This ensures that the Level-0 experts are trained on representative distributions, maximizing data utility (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
2.  **Hyperparameter Optimization:**
    *   **Ridge:** `RidgeCV` with alpha search up to 50,000 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **SVR:** Grid Search for `C` and `epsilon`.
    *   **ExtraTrees:** Fixed robust settings (e.g., 500 estimators).
    *   **LightGBM:** Tune `learning_rate`, `num_leaves`, and `reg_alpha`/`reg_lambda` to prevent overfitting on the PCA features.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:** Process test images through the three backbones (using `AutoImageProcessor` and flip-averaging).
2.  **Prediction:** Transform features (Scaling/PCA) and pass to the 12 trained Level-0 experts (averaged across the 5 stratified folds). Feed these base predictions to the Level-1 Meta-Learner.
3.  **Validation:** Perform **Nested Cross-Validation** on the Meta-Learner to calculate the final RMSE, ensuring the reported metric reflects true generalization error (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00029`).
