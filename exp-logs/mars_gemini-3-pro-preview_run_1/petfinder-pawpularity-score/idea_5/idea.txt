# Proposed Strategy: Tri-Paradigm Stacking Ensemble (Semantic, Geometric, Textural)

## Model
To address the "Ensemble Saturation" observed when combining architecturally similar backbones (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00018`) and to maximize the "Subjective" signal identified as critical (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`), I propose a **Tri-Paradigm Stacking Ensemble**. This approach evolves the previous stacking strategy by selecting Level-0 experts with fundamentally orthogonal training objectives and inductive biases, rather than relying on multiple ImageNet-supervised models.

1.  **Level-0 Experts (Feature Extractors):**
    *   **Semantic Expert (Vision-Language):** **CLIP ViT-L/14**. Retained as the primary expert. Its contrastive language-image pre-training aligns best with the subjective "Pawpularity" score (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).
    *   **Geometric Expert (Self-Supervised):** **DINOv2 ViT-Large**. This replaces the ImageNet-supervised Swin Transformer. Unlike supervised models that focus on class discriminability, DINOv2 is trained via self-supervision to understand object geometry, layout, and parts. This introduces a novel signal for assessing features like "Near", "Action", and "Occlusion" that standard classifiers miss, effectively diversifying the ensemble beyond ImageNet features.
    *   **Textural Expert (CNN):** **ConvNeXt Large**. Retained to provide the Convolutional inductive bias, ensuring high-frequency details and local textures are captured (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`), which complements the global attention of the Transformers.

2.  **Level-1 Meta-Learner:**
    *   The predictions from the three experts will be aggregated using a **Ridge Regression** meta-learner (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`). This "Late Fusion" approach prevents the high-dimensional feature spaces from interfering with each other.

## Data
1.  **Preprocessing:** Images will be resized and normalized according to the specific requirements of each backbone (e.g., CLIP, DINOv2, and ConvNeXt specific transforms).
2.  **Feature-Space Augmentation:** For all three backbones, I will apply **Feature-Space Augmentation Averaging** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`). Embeddings will be computed for both the original and horizontally flipped images and averaged to enforce invariance.
3.  **Metadata Fusion:** The 12 binary metadata features will be concatenated with the image embeddings *within* each Level-0 expert's feature vector.
4.  **Normalization:** Crucially, **StandardScaler** will be applied to the concatenated feature matrix (image embeddings + metadata) for each expert before training the linear heads. This ensures the binary metadata is properly weighted against the high-variance embeddings (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).

## Training
1.  **Cross-Validation:** I will employ **5-Fold Cross-Validation** to generate unbiased Out-of-Fold (OOF) predictions for the training set.
2.  **Level-0 Training:** Within each fold, three independent **RidgeCV** models will be trained on the features from CLIP, DINOv2, and ConvNeXt. I will ensure the hyperparameter search space for `alpha` is sufficiently wide to avoid boundary clipping (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
3.  **Level-1 Training:** The OOF predictions from the three experts will form a new `(N_samples, 3)` training matrix. The Meta-Regressor will be trained on this low-dimensional data to minimize the final RMSE.

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Test images will be processed through the three backbones (with flip averaging) to generate embeddings.
2.  **Prediction:**
    *   The embeddings will be passed to the trained Level-0 Ridge models (averaged across folds) to generate three base predictions.
    *   These base predictions will be fed into the Level-1 Meta-Learner to produce the final `Pawpularity` score.
3.  **Validation:** The stratified validation set will be used to verify that replacing the supervised Swin Transformer with the self-supervised DINOv2 improves the ensemble's performance.
