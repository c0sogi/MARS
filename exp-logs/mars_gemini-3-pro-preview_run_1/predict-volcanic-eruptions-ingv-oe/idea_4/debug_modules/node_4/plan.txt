The execution failure is caused by a numerical overflow in the `CNNTrainer.validate` method. The EfficientNet model, when trained with a very small batch size (as in the demo configuration), can produce unstable, large predictions. When these predictions are passed to `np.expm1` to invert the `log1p` scaling, they result in infinity for `float32` (overflow), causing `mean_absolute_error` to crash. To fix this, I will clamp the model predictions to a safe upper bound (32.0) before applying the exponential transform, ensuring the values remain within the representable range of `float32` while covering the valid target range. This applies the principle of sanitizing outputs to handle mathematical edge cases (Cite debug_lesson_1). I will apply this fix to both the `validate` and `predict` methods in `library/training_engines.py`.
