The issue is caused by an arithmetic overflow in the `exp_transform_target` function within `library/utils.py`. The Vision model (EfficientNet) predicts log-transformed targets, and during the initial training epochs, it may output large values. When `np.expm1` is applied to these large values (specifically anything greater than ~88.7 for float32), it results in infinity (`inf`), causing the subsequent Mean Absolute Error calculation to crash with a `ValueError`.

To fix this, I will modify `library/utils.py` to clamp the input values passed to `np.expm1` to a safe upper bound (85.0). This ensures the result remains within the representable range of `float32` while being significantly larger than any valid target value (which maxes out around `exp(18)`).
