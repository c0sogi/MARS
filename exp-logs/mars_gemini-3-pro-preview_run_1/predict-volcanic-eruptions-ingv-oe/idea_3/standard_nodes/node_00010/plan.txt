The strategy focuses on addressing the critical failure mode of the 1D-ResNet branch observed in the previous run: the lack of target scaling for regression on large-magnitude values.

1.  **Target Scaling for ResNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009)**:
    -   In `runfile.py`, I will implement standard scaling (z-score normalization) for the target variable `time_to_eruption` within the ResNet cross-validation loop.
    -   The targets for the training fold will be used to compute the mean and standard deviation.
    -   Both training and validation targets will be scaled before being passed to the `SeismicDataset`.
    -   During inference (both for OOF validation and Test predictions), the model's outputs will be inverse-transformed (scaled back) using the same mean and standard deviation to match the original target magnitude.

2.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**:
    -   I will update `runfile.py` to move from "Fast Baseline" settings to "Optimized" settings.
    -   `Config.EPOCHS` will be increased from 5 to 20 to allow the ResNet sufficient time to converge now that the gradients will be stable.
    -   `Config.LGBM_PARAMS["n_estimators"]` will be increased to 5000 to maximize the performance of the LightGBM branch.

3.  **Configuration Update**:
    -   I will also update `library/config.py` to reflect the improved default epoch count.

This approach transforms the ensemble from a "Good Model + Failed Model" average into a robust combination of a feature-based Gradient Boosting model and a raw-signal Deep Learning model.
