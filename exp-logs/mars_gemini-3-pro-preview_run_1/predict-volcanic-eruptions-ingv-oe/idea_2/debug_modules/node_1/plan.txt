The issue is caused by `NaN` values persisting in the feature matrix, primarily due to two reasons: (1) sensor columns that are entirely `NaN` or constant result in `NaN`s during preprocessing (mean of `NaN` is `NaN`) or feature calculation (correlation/skew on constant data), and (2) potential missing segments during the merge of metadata and features. The fix involves ensuring all `NaN`s are filled with 0 during data loading, sanitizing the extracted feature dictionary to replace `NaN`/`Inf` with 0, and filling any remaining `NaN`s in the `FeatureManager` after merging features with metadata.
