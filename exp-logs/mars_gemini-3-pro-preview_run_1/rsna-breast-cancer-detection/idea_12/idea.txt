Based on the progression of empirical findings—specifically the promise of Siamese architectures in mitigating demographic bias (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021) and the potential for spatial misalignment to degrade difference-based detection—I propose the **Flow-Aligned Pyramid Siamese Network**.

This strategy addresses the critical limitation of previous "Symmetry-Difference" proposals: the assumption that left and right breasts are spatially perfectly aligned. By introducing a **Spatial Alignment Module**, we ensure that the difference operation effectively cancels out symmetric tissue patterns (and the embedded demographic signals) even in the presence of anatomical or positional shifts, while preserving the "Early Fusion" benefits.

### Model
**Architecture: Flow-Aligned Pyramid Siamese EfficientNet-B2**

*   **Siamese Backbone:** Utilize **EfficientNet-B2** with shared weights. This backbone processes both the **Target Image** and the **Contralateral Image** (opposing breast, same view) to extract feature pyramids.
*   **Input Strategy (Early Fusion):**
    *   Retain the **Spatial Channel Expansion** technique (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   Input tensors are $3 \times 768 \times 768$, comprising the **Image**, a spatially broadcasted **Age** map, and a spatially broadcasted **Implant** map.
    *   **Standard Scaling** is applied to the Age metadata before broadcasting to prevent gradient domination (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Spatial Alignment Module (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Instead of performing a rigid subtraction ($F_{target} - F_{contra}$), which is sensitive to misalignment, the network estimates a **Dense Displacement Field (Flow)** at the semantic bottleneck (P5).
    *   A lightweight convolutional block predicts a flow field $\Delta$ that maps the Contralateral features to the Target features.
    *   This flow is upsampled and applied to warp the Contralateral features at all pyramid levels (P3, P4, P5) using a **Spatial Transformer** operation.
*   **Aligned Difference Operation:**
    *   Compute the difference between the Target features and the *Warped* Contralateral features: $D_i = F_{target, i} - \text{Warp}(F_{contra, i}, \Delta)$.
    *   *Mechanism:* Since the Age and Implant maps are spatially constant, the warping operation preserves their values. Consequently, the subtraction $F_{target} - \text{Warp}(F_{contra})$ still mathematically cancels out the demographic "Age Shortcut" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014), while the alignment ensures that symmetric tissue textures are also suppressed, leaving only asymmetric lesions.
*   **Head:** Concatenate the Global Average Pooled (GAP) vectors from the Target features ($F_{target}$) and the Difference features ($D$) across all scales, followed by a dense classification layer.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**

*   **Paired Data Loading:**
    *   Load pairs of (Target, Contralateral) images. If a physical contralateral image is missing, substitute a zero-tensor.
    *   **Fail Loudly:** The data loader must **raise an exception** immediately if a file is missing or corrupt. Do not use silent fallbacks (e.g., black images) for I/O errors, as this causes model collapse (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Synchronized Geometric Augmentation:** Apply identical random spatial transformations (Flip, Rotate, Shift) to both images to maintain the intrinsic anatomical correspondence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Disable Photometric Augmentation:** Do not use brightness or contrast shifts. Preserving absolute pixel intensity (density) is critical for this modality (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

### Training
**Procedure**

*   **Objective:** **Single-Task Learning (STL)**.
    *   Train exclusively on the binary cancer target. Do not use auxiliary heads for Breast Density.
    *   *Rationale:* Auxiliary tasks on global features are "easier" to learn and exacerbate age bias, leading to negative transfer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Loss Function:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with `pos_weight` set to **47.0**.
    *   Aggressive positive weighting is required to maintain Probabilistic Recall in this 1:47 imbalance regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Gradient Strategy:** **Disable Gradient Clipping**.
    *   Clipping gradients neutralizes the high `pos_weight`, preventing the necessary large updates for the minority class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW (LR 1e-4) with Cosine Annealing.

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline**

*   **Pair Construction:** For each row in `test.csv`, identify and load the matching contralateral view.
*   **Prediction:** Pass the pair through the Flow-Aligned Siamese network.
*   **Aggregation:** Group predictions by `prediction_id` (breast level). Compute the **Max** probability across all available views (CC and MLO) for that breast to generate the final submission.
