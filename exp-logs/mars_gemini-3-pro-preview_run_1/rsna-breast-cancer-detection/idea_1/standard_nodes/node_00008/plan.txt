To optimize the solution, I will implement **photometric data augmentation** as suggested in **Lesson 00007**. By introducing `RandomBrightnessContrast` to the image pipeline, we encourage the model to rely less on absolute pixel intensities (which can vary by machine and density) and more on the structural features and the un-augmented metadata channels (Age, Implant). This acts as a regularizer and has been shown to improve the Probabilistic F1 score.

Additionally, I will increase the number of training epochs from 5 to 10. Since data augmentation increases the effective size of the dataset and makes the training task harder, more epochs are generally required for convergence. Given the 24-hour time limit and the previous runtime of ~30 minutes, this is well within the compute budget.

Here are the changes:

1.  **`library/dataset.py`**: Add `albumentations.RandomBrightnessContrast` to the training transforms.
2.  **`library/config.py`**: Increase default `EPOCHS` to 10.
3.  **`runfile.py`**: Update `TRAIN_EPOCHS` to 10 and set the `BASELINE_SCORE` to the new target of `0.04436662048101425`.
