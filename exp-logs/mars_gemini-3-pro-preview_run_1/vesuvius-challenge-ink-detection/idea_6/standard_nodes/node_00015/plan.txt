The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU memory (40GB) was exhausted when training with `BATCH_SIZE = 32`. The model processes high-resolution inputs ($65 \times 512 \times 512$) and maintains the spatial resolution ($512 \times 512$) throughout its layers without downsampling. Storing the activations for backpropagation with such a large batch size requires significantly more memory than available.

To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 32 to 2. This drastic reduction is necessary to ensure the model's activations fit within the GPU memory, aligning with the guidance in **Cite debug_lesson_1** to use a minimal batch size for high-resolution volumetric data. I am selecting 2 instead of 1 because the model uses `BatchNorm2d` layers, which require a batch size greater than 1 during training to compute statistics.
