With thousands of files failing, this created an I/O bottleneck that slowed down the script significantly. To fix this, I will remove the `print` statement from the exception handler, effectively silencing the error logging while preserving the fallback behavior (returning a black image), as suggested by the error analysis and preventing timeouts caused by excessive logging (Cite 4).
