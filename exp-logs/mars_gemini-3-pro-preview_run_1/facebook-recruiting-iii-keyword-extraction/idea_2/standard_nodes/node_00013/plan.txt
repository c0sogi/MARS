To optimize the model's performance, I will implement a **separate embedding stream architecture** for the Title and Body fields (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`). Instead of concatenating the raw text, which dilutes the strong signal from the Title, I will process them independently through the `EmbeddingBag` layer and concatenate their resulting vectors. Additionally, I will **increase the hidden layer dimension** from 512 to 1024 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00012`) to provide the necessary capacity for the dense layers to process the richer, concatenated representation. These changes require updates to the configuration, tokenizer, dataset, model, and training loops to handle the dual-input stream.
