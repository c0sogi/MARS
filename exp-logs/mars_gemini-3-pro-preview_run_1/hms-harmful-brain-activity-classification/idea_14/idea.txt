Based on the synthesis of the provided lessons, I propose the **Cyclic-Subset Coordinate-Guided Fusion Network**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Current Best" approach (Idea 10). It retains the highly effective **Coordinate-Injected Dual-Stream** architecture but introduces a novel **Cyclic-Subset Training Pipeline** to resolve the critical conflict between data diversity **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}** and training stability **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00014}**. Additionally, it strictly enforces the architectural constraints identified in the failure analysis of previous ideas (e.g., asymmetric attention, frequency preservation).

### 1. Model
The architecture is a **Dual-Stream Network** designed to fuse high-resolution temporal dynamics with coordinate-aware global spectral context.

*   **Stream A: Raw EEG Encoder (Waveform Stream)**
    *   **Backbone:** Utilize the **Multi-Scale 1D Convolutional (Inception)** architecture.
    *   **Function:** Processes the 50-second raw EEG window (20 channels) using parallel convolutional branches with varying kernel sizes (e.g., 3, 5, 7, 9).
    *   **Rationale:** This component is explicitly preserved to extract phase and frequency-specific features directly from the waveform, which has been proven superior to spectrograms for short-window analysis **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}**.
    *   **Constraint:** Process all 20 channels jointly to capture full spatial dependencies, avoiding the performance degradation of manual channel splitting **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00005}**.

*   **Stream B: Coordinate-Aware Spectrogram Encoder (Context Stream)**
    *   **Backbone:** Use a lightweight **EfficientNet-B0** to fit the compute budget **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00013}**.
    *   **Input Construction:** Construct a **5-channel input tensor**. The first 4 channels are the standard recording regions. The **5th channel is a Coordinate Map**—a linear gradient encoding the relative temporal distance from the center of the labeled event.
    *   **Rationale:** This injection breaks translation invariance, allowing the CNN to effectively locate the event within the 10-minute context **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}**.
    *   **Tokenization:** The output feature map $(C, H, W)$ is flattened into a sequence of tokens $(C, H \times W)$. **Crucially, do not pool the frequency dimension**, preserving the spectral semantic identity **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00009}**.

*   **Fusion: Asymmetric Cross-Attention**
    *   **Mechanism:** Implement a **Single-Layer Cross-Attention** block **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00013}**.
    *   **Directionality:** Configure strictly as **EEG queries Spectrogram** ($Q=EEG, K=Spec, V=Spec$).
    *   **Rationale:** This allows the randomly initialized EEG stream to extract stable features from the pretrained Spectrogram backbone. Avoid bidirectional attention, which was shown to destabilize training **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00012}**. Avoid hard masking, allowing the model to learn soft alignments **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}**.

### 2. Data
*   **Raw EEG:** Extract the central 50-second window. Downsample to **100 Hz** to maintain the Nyquist frequency while reducing sequence length **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}**. Apply channel-wise instance normalization.
*   **Spectrograms:** Load the full 10-minute spectrogram. Resize to a standard resolution (e.g., $512 \times 512$).
*   **Coordinate Map:** Generate the 5th channel by creating a matrix where each column's value corresponds to its normalized temporal distance (e.g., $[-1, 1]$) from the `spectrogram_label_offset_seconds` **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}**.
*   **Augmentation:** Apply **SpecAugment** (masking) to the spectrogram channels (excluding the coordinate channel) and **Channel Dropout** to the EEG. Do not add redundant cropped inputs **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00004}**.

### 3. Training
*   **Strategy: Cyclic-Subset Training (Pipeline Innovation)**
    *   **Problem:** Lesson 00010 states that full data coverage is required for generalization, but Lesson 00014 states that training on small subsets for multiple epochs is required for stability and convergence within the time limit.
    *   **Solution:** Implement a **Cyclic Pipeline**. Divide the full training set into $N$ disjoint folds (e.g., 4 folds of ~25% data).
        *   **Epoch 1:** Train on Fold 1.
        *   **Epoch 2:** Train on Fold 2.
        *   **Epoch 3:** Train on Fold 3.
        *   **Epoch 4:** Train on Fold 4.
    *   **Benefit:** This approach ensures the model is exposed to the **Full Dataset** (satisfying Lesson 00010) while maintaining the **optimization stability** of a smaller epoch size (satisfying Lesson 00014). It avoids the overfitting risk of static subsampling and the underfitting risk of a single massive epoch.
*   **Loss Function:** **Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence** **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a **OneCycle Learning Rate** scheduler to facilitate super-convergence **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}**.
*   **Regularization:** Apply **Modality Dropout** (randomly zeroing out one stream during training) to force the fusion layer to be robust.

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For each test sample, generate the 50-second EEG and the 5-channel Spectrogram.
*   **Alignment:** If `spectrogram_label_offset_seconds` is missing in the test metadata, assume the test EEG is centered within the spectrogram (offset = 0) to generate the coordinate map.
*   **Prediction:** Pass the inputs through the network. The attention outputs are Global Average Pooled and processed via Softmax to produce probabilities. Ensure the output sums to 1.0.
