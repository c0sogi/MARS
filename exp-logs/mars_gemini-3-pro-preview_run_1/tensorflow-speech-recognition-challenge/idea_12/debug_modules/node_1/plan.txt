The `AssertionError` in `runfile.py` occurs because `SpeechCommandDataset` returns an energy tensor of shape `(1, T)` (resulting in a batch of `(B, 1, T)`), whereas the verification script expects a 1D energy tensor per sample `(T)` (resulting in a batch of `(B, T)`). This is a case of audio datasets incorrectly returning tensors with singleton dimensions (Cite debug_lesson_7). To fix this, I will modify `library/dataset.py` to fully squeeze the energy tensor to `(T)`. Consequently, I must also update `library/trainer.py` to explicitly unsqueeze the energy tensor back to `(B, 1, T)` before passing it to the model, ensuring the model receives the expected 3D input shape.
