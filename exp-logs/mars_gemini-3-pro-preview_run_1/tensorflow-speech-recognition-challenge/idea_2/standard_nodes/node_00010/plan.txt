1.  **Fix Spectrogram Hop Length**: I will modify `library/config.py` to change `hop_length` from `512` to `160`. The previous setting resulted in ~31 time frames, which caused feature map collapse after the 32x downsampling of the ConvNeXt backbone. A hop length of 160 yields ~100 frames, satisfying the requirement $T \ge 2 \times \text{Stride}$ (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`).
