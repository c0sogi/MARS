The error analysis indicates that the validation metric is invalid because the "Quadratic Gating" mechanism, which filters out "easy negatives" (players far apart), was incorrectly applied to the validation set. This filtering removed a large portion of the validation data (True Negatives), distorting the confusion matrix and the resulting Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC). As per **debug_lesson_3** ("Do Not Apply Training-Specific Data Filtering to Validation Sets"), validation must be performed on the complete, unaltered distribution.

To fix this, I will modify `library/feature_extractor.py` to ensure that gating is strictly limited to the training phase (`mode == "train"`).
