The implementation strategy involves the following steps:

1.  **Configuration for Speed**: Before initializing the pipeline, I will modify the `MODEL_PARAMS` in `library.config` to reduce the number of estimators (trees) and adjust the `TrainConfig` to reduce the sampling ratio of anchor negatives. This ensures the solution runs within the "fast baseline" time constraints while maintaining the architectural integrity.
2.  **Pipeline Execution**: I will utilize `library.training.run_training_pipeline` to orchestrate the end-to-end process: loading data, mining hard negatives using the "Scout" models, constructing the "Expert" dataset, training the Tri-Ensemble, and optimizing the decision threshold.
3.  **Validation & Assessment**: After training, I will explicitly reload the validation set to calculate the Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC). I will print this metric in the required format (`Final Validation Metric: <value>`).
4.  **Failure Analysis**: I will compute the absolute error between the model's predicted probabilities and the ground truth on the validation set. Then, I will calculate the Pearson correlation between this error signal and each input feature to identify which features are most associated with model failure.
5.  **Conditional Submission**: I will compare the calculated MCC against the threshold (0.6865). If the model performs sufficiently well, I will trigger `library.inference.generate_submission` to produce the final `submission.csv`.
