However, the `generate_features` method passes the logical split "val" directly to `load_tracking` when processing the validation set, causing a `ValueError`. Since the validation set is a subset of the training data, its tracking information resides in the training tracking file.

To fix this, I will modify `library/feature_engine.py` to map the logical split "val" to the physical source "train" before calling `load_tracking`.
