This allows the tree-based model to handle these cases explicitly (via default directions) rather than being confused by arbitrary distance values.

Given the 4-hour time limit, I will increase the training sample size from 500,000 to 2,000,000 rows in `runfile.py`. This will expose the model to more rare contact events and variance.

3.  **Hyperparameter Tuning**: To accommodate the larger dataset and prevent the rapid overfitting observed previously (early stopping at 21 iterations), I will lower the `learning_rate` to 0.02 and increase `n_estimators` in `library/config.py`.
