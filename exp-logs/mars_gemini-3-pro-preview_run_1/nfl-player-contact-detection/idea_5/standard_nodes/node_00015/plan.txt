This strategy implements a **Unified Heterogeneous Ensemble** (LightGBM + XGBoost) trained on a topologically-enriched dataset. To satisfy the "Fast Baseline" requirement (2-hour limit), the pipeline samples the training data to 250,000 interactions while maintaining full validation and test sets for accurate evaluation. The solution leverages GPU acceleration for inference and training, performs failure analysis by correlating errors with input features, and optimizes the decision threshold to maximize the Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC).
