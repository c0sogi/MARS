This violates the principle of tracking schema changes across sequential transformations (Cite debug_lesson_24).

To fix this, I will modify the exclusion lists in the dictionary comprehensions for both `p1_track` and `p2_track` to explicitly exclude `nfl_player_id` from the automatic suffixing. This ensures the column retains its original name, allowing the subsequent specific rename operation to function correctly and provide the expected join keys (`nfl_player_id_1` and `join_id_2`) for the merge operations.
