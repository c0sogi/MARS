While LightGBM interprets negative values as "silent", XGBoost requires either a positive integer (representing the logging period) or `False` to disable logging; passing `-1` triggers an assertion that the period must be greater than 0. To fix this, I will modify `XGBWrapper.fit` in `library/model_zoo.py` to sanitize the `verbose_eval` argument, explicitly converting any integer less than or equal to 0 to `False` before passing it to `xgb.train`.
