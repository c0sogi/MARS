The issue involves two distinct problems causing the pipeline failure. First, in `library/models.py`, the LightGBM model attempts to use an `early_stopping` callback unconditionally, which raises a `ValueError` when no validation set is provided (as is the case during the "Scout" training phase). Second, in `runfile.py`, the generation of the mini-dataset overwrites the training tracking data with validation tracking data because they share the same output filename. Since the train and validation splits contain disjoint sets of plays, this results in the training metadata having no corresponding tracking data.

To fix these issues:
1.  **In `library/models.py`**: Modify `TriEnsemble.fit` to conditionally append the `early_stopping` callback only when `X_val` and `y_val` are explicitly provided.
2.  **In `runfile.py`**: Update `run_demo` to generate the mini-tracking data for training and validation into separate temporary files, then merge them into the final `mini_train_tracking.csv`. This prevents the validation data generation from overwriting the training data (Cite debug_lesson_21).
