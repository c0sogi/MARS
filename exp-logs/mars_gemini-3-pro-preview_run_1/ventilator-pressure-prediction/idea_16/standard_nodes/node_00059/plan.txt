My implementation strategy focuses on correcting critical data preprocessing errors and restoring the model architecture to a proven high-performance configuration, guided by the provided lessons.

1.  **Fix Data Preprocessing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058):** The most critical fix is to exclude `u_out` from the `RobustScaler` in `library/features.py`. Scaling this binary control variable breaks the loss masking logic (converting 0/1 to floats like -1.0/0.0), causing the model to optimize on the irrelevant expiratory phase.
2.  **Optimize Hyperparameters (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052):** I will reduce the `EXPANSION_FACTOR` in `library/config.py` from 4 to 2. High expansion in unnormalized residual blocks leads to optimization instability. I will also set `EPOCHS` to 20 to ensure sufficient convergence.
3.  **Restore Training Data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057):** I will remove the code in `runfile.py` that subsamples the training data to 15,000 breaths. The previous failure analysis showed high correlation with `R`, indicating underfitting due to lack of data/training.
4.  **Simplify Architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054):** In `library/model.py`, I will remove `weight_norm` from the `CompositeBlock` to rely on standard additive residuals, which are more robust for this depth.
