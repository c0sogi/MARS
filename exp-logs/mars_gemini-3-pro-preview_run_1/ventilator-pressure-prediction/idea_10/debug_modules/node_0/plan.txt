### Implementation Strategy

1.  **Configuration Override**: To ensure the demonstration runs quickly and fits within the time/compute constraints, I will modify the `Config` class attributes at runtime. I will enable `DEBUG` mode, reduce the `DEBUG_SAMPLE_SIZE` to a small number of breaths (e.g., 200), set `EPOCHS` to 2, and reduce the `BATCH_SIZE`.
2.  **Data Preparation**: I will utilize `library.features.prepare_datasets` to process the raw metadata. I will assert that the returned numpy arrays have the expected shapes corresponding to the reduced dataset size and sequence length.
3.  **Dataset & DataLoader**: I will use `library.dataset.get_dataloaders` to generate PyTorch DataLoaders. I will fetch a single batch to verify the tensor shapes and types (float32).
4.  **Model Initialization & Forward Pass**: I will instantiate the `DeepSupervisedVentilatorModel`. I will perform a forward pass with the sample batch in both `train` (returning a tuple of outputs) and `eval` (returning a single output) modes to verify the architecture's behavior.
5.  **Loss Calculation**: I will verify the `CompositeLoss` (for training) and `MaskedL1Loss` (for validation) by computing the loss on the sample batch and asserting the result is a valid scalar.
6.  **Training Loop**: I will manually execute the `train_epoch` and `validate_epoch` functions from `library.train` for a single epoch to demonstrate the training workflow and ensure the loss decreases or remains valid.
7.  **Inference**: I will save the trained model state to the path defined in `Config.MODEL_PATH` and then call `library.inference.predict`. I will verify that the generated submission DataFrame has the correct length and columns.
8.  **Reproducibility**: I will use `library.utils.seed_everything` at the start.
