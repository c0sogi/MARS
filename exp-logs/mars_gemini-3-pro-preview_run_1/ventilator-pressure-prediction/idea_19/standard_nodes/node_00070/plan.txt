The previous solution (Idea 19) significantly underperformed (MAE 0.47) compared to the historical best (Idea 10, MAE 0.21) because it reduced the model's capacity to enforce a "Strict Identity" residual connection. Specifically, it bottlenecked the Bidirectional LSTM to a hidden size of 256 (total output 512) to match a `D_MODEL` of 512.

My optimization strategy focuses on restoring and enhancing model capacity while maintaining the beneficial Strict Identity architecture:
1.  **Restore Model Width (Cite Lesson 42, 55):** I will increase `D_MODEL` to 1024 and `LSTM_HIDDEN` to 512. This ensures the Bidirectional LSTM outputs 1024 features ($512 \times 2$), matching the wider `D_MODEL`. This restores the high capacity of Idea 10 while satisfying the dimension requirements for the Strict Identity Residuals (Cite Lesson 64) without needing learnable projections on the residual path.
2.  **Optimize Training Duration (Cite Lesson 28):** I will adjust `EPOCHS` to 30. The previous best model (Idea 10) converged well in 25-30 epochs. Since the new model is larger (4x parameters), 30 epochs provides a safe margin for convergence within the 4-hour time limit.
