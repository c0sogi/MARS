1.  **Implement Explicit Context Injection**: Modify the model to extract static lung attributes (`R`, `C`) from the input features and concatenate them to the input of *every* LSTM layer in the backbone. 2.  **Restore High-Capacity Backbone**: Use a "Bottleneck Initialization" strategy where the stem produces a 512-dim output, which is then expanded to 1024-dim by the first Bidirectional LSTM layer. This balances capacity with training stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).
3.  **Integrate Pointwise FFNs**: Interleave LSTM layers with 2x expansion Feed-Forward Networks to decouple temporal and channel mixing, enhancing the model's expressivity for non-linear physical interactions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
4.  **Optimize Training Schedule**: Increase the training duration to 25 epochs to ensure full convergence of the deep architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
