To optimize the solution, I will upgrade the model architecture from `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` to `microsoft/deberta-v3-small`. DeBERTa V3 introduces a disentangled attention mechanism and a mask language modeling objective using replaced token detection, which generally yields significantly better performance on semantic similarity tasks (NLU) compared to standard BERT/RoBERTa models of similar size.

I will also adjust the hyperparameters to support this change:
1.  **Increase `max_length`**: Based on the EDA, the maximum target length is 98 characters and anchor is 38. A limit of 64 is risky. I will increase it to 133 to safely accommodate the longest pairs plus special tokens.
2.  **Increase `train_batch_size`**: To utilize the A100 GPU efficiently and stabilize gradient updates, I will increase the batch size from 32 to 128.
3.  **Update Cache Version**: Since the tokenizer for DeBERTa is different from MiniLM, I must increment the `CACHE_VERSION` in the dataset loader to force re-tokenization and prevent loading incompatible cached data.
