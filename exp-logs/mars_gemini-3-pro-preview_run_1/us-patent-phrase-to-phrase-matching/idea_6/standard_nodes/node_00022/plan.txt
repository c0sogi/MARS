To resolve this, I will reduce the `train_batch_size` to 4 and compensate by setting `gradient_accumulation_steps` to 4, maintaining an effective batch size of 16 while significantly lowering peak memory usage. I will also reduce the `valid_batch_size` to 16 to ensure validation runs safely. This approach follows the strategy of applying the least invasive fix first to address OOM issues (Cite debug_lesson_3).
