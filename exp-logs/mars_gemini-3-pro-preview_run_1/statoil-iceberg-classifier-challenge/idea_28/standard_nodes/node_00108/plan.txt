The strategy involves three targeted optimizations based on the provided lessons:

1.  **Reduce Batch Size (Cite Lesson 38):** The batch size is reduced from 64 to 32 in `library/config.py`. Lesson 38 highlights that for small datasets (like this one with ~1,600 samples), a larger batch size reduces the total number of gradient updates, leading to poorer convergence. A smaller batch size ensures more updates per epoch and better generalization.
2.  **Decouple Scheduler from Milestones (Cite Lesson 107):** The production training phase in `library/production.py` is modified to use `CosineAnnealingLR` instead of `MultiStepLR`. Lesson 107 warns that "replaying" specific learning rate decay milestones found during cross-validation is unreliable due to high variance and noise. Instead, it recommends using the *total epoch budget* found during calibration but applying a smooth, data-agnostic scheduler (Cosine Annealing) for the final training on the full dataset.
3.  **Enable Full Convergence (Cite Lesson 65):** The "fast execution" overrides in `runfile.py` (which limited training to 10 epochs) are removed. This allows the calibration phase to run for up to 50 epochs with higher patience, ensuring the model actually converges before the optimal epoch count is determined.
