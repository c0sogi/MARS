The issue is that the production models are trained on the concatenated training and validation datasets but are subsequently evaluated on the validation set. This constitutes data leakage, as the models are being tested on data they have already seen, resulting in an invalid and overly optimistic metric. The fix is to modify `library/calibration.py` to return the unbiased Cross-Validation (CV) score (average best validation loss across folds) from Phase 1. Then, `runfile.py` will be updated to use this CV score to decide whether to proceed with production training and submission, removing the flawed validation step entirely.
