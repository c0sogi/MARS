To optimize the solution, I will focus on aligning the training dynamics between the calibration phase and the final ensemble training, while adopting a more conservative learning rate suitable for fine-tuning.

**Implementation Strategy:**
1.  **Lower Learning Rate**: Reduce `LEARNING_RATE` from `1e-3` to `1e-4` in `config.py`. Lesson 9 indicates that `1e-3` is too aggressive for fine-tuning on this dataset and leads to instability, whereas `1e-4` allows the model to settle into better minima.
2.  **Align Schedulers & Trajectories**: In `runfile.py`, switch the calibration phase scheduler from `ReduceLROnPlateau` to `CosineAnnealingLR` to match the scheduler used in the final SWA training. Crucially, set `T_max` in the final training phase to `config.MAX_EPOCHS_PHASE_1` (instead of the variable `conv_epochs`). This ensures that the learning rate decay trajectory in the final training exactly mirrors the calibration phase, making the "optimal epoch" found during calibration valid for the final run (Cite Lesson 40, 38).
3.  **TTA-Aware Selection**: Enable Test-Time Augmentation (`use_tta=True`) during the calibration phase evaluation. As per Lesson 25, model selection should be based on the metric used for inference (TTA) rather than a single-crop proxy, ensuring we select the epoch that maximizes ensemble performance.
