Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Configuration & Setup**: I will initialize the random seeds and override the default configuration parameters (`MAX_EPOCHS_PHASE_1`, `NUM_ENSEMBLE_MODELS`) to ensure the pipeline runs efficiently as a "fast baseline" while maintaining sufficient capacity for convergence.
2.  **Phase 1 - Calibration**: I will utilize the `ExperimentManager.run_calibration_phase()` to perform Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation. This will determine the optimal "Convergence Epoch" ($E_{conv}$) where the model stabilizes, which is crucial for the subsequent training phases.
3.  **Validation Assessment**: To strictly satisfy the requirement of evaluating on the hold-out set defined in `./metadata/val_metadata.csv`, I will manually construct a training loop. I will train a single model on the subset of data defined by `train_metadata.csv` for $E_{conv}$ epochs and then evaluate it on the `val_metadata.csv` subset. This ensures a clean, leakage-free calculation of the "Final Validation Metric".
4.  **Failure Analysis**: Using the predictions from the validation assessment, I will calculate the absolute errors and compute their correlation with the incidence angle and the mean image intensity. This identifies potential systematic weaknesses in the model.
5.  **Phase 2 - Production & Submission**: If the validation metric meets the specified threshold, I will proceed to the Production Phase. This involves training an ensemble of SWA (Stochastic Weight Averaging) models on the *entire* training dataset for $E_{conv} + 12$ epochs. Finally, I will generate predictions for the test set using Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) and save the submission file.
