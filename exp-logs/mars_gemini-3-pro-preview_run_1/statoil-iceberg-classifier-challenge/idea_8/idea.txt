Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous attempts and the derived lessons, I propose the **Stratified ResNet-18 Ensemble with Mixup Regularization and Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**.

This solution introduces two structural innovations to the training pipeline: **Mixup** (a data-agnostic regularization technique that addresses data scarcity by constructing virtual training examples) and **Stochastic Weight Averaging** (an optimization technique that improves generalization by finding the flat center of the loss minimum). These additions directly address the issues of overfitting and optimization instability observed in previous ideas, while strictly adhering to the successful architectural choices identified in the lessons.

# Model
*   **Backbone Architecture:** We will utilize the **ResNet-18** architecture pretrained on ImageNet. We strictly adhere to the finding that shallow, dense convolutional networks are superior to deeper variants (ResNet-34) or depthwise-separable architectures (EfficientNet) for this small, noisy dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Pooling & Feature Extraction:** We will use standard **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** to extract a 512-dimensional feature vector. We explicitly reject Dual-Stream (Max+Avg) pooling, as Max Pooling amplifies speckle noise in SAR imagery (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Late Fusion:** We will retain the **Late Fusion** strategy. The scalar `inc_angle` will be normalized and concatenated with the image features. This allows the model to calibrate visual signals based on the viewing geometry (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Minimalist Classification Head:** The fused vector will be passed directly to a **Minimalist Head** consisting of a single Batch Normalization layer, Dropout (rate 0.5), and a single Linear layer (Linear Probe). We explicitly avoid complex MLP heads to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

# Data
*   **Input Construction:** We will construct a **3-channel composite image** using Band 1 (HH), Band 2 (HV), and their arithmetic mean.
*   **Global Normalization:** We will implement **Global Min-Max Scaling** using statistics from the entire training set. We explicitly reject per-sample normalization to preserve the absolute physical magnitude of the radar backscatter (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Upsampling:** Images will be upsampled to **224x224** to match the backbone's receptive field (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Geometric Augmentation:** We will apply **RandomRotate90** (discrete 90-degree rotations) alongside flips. This is critical for teaching rotational invariance in top-down imagery (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Mixup Regularization (Structural Innovation):** We will implement **Mixup**, which trains the model on convex combinations of pairs of examples and their labels (including the `inc_angle`). This effectively expands the training distribution and enforces linear behavior in-between training samples, acting as a powerful regularizer for small datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006). Note: We will **remove Label Smoothing**, as Mixup naturally provides soft targets, and stacking both can cause underfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

# Training
*   **Pipeline Strategy:** We will use **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** to ensure robustness.
*   **Optimizer & Schedule:** We will use **AdamW** with `weight_decay=0.01` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007). The training will proceed in two phases:
    1.  **Standard Phase:** Train with a **ReduceLROnPlateau** scheduler (factor 0.1) to converge to a local minimum (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
    2.  **SWA Phase (Structural Innovation):** After convergence, we will switch to **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**. We will run for additional epochs with a constant or cyclical learning rate, averaging the weights of the model at each step. This allows the model to settle into the "flat" center of the loss basin, improving generalization and robustness to shift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **TTA-Aware Checkpointing:** During the Standard Phase, we will save the "best" model state based on **TTA-Validation Loss** (using a lightweight flip-based TTA) rather than single-crop loss. This ensures the saved model is optimized for the inference strategy, not just the training proxy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

# Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image and its geometric transformations (flips, 90-degree rotations), averaging the probabilities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the predictions from the **5 SWA-enhanced models** from the cross-validation folds.
