The `TypeError` during training initialization is caused by the `verbose` argument in the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler constructor, which has been deprecated and removed in recent PyTorch versions. To fix this, I will remove the `verbose=True` argument from the scheduler instantiation in `library/train.py`. Cite debug_lesson_2.
