The Local-Anisotropy Enhanced Wide Deep Sets (LA-WDS) strategy improves upon standard point-cloud approaches by explicitly encoding local structural anisotropy (via eigenvalue decomposition of local neighborhoods) alongside standard atomic features. This allows the model to distinguish between different bonding environments (e.g., planar vs. tetrahedral) that might have similar radial distribution profiles. The architecture uses a wide MLP for atomic encoding to capture high-dimensional interactions, coupled with a global stream for thermodynamic context, merging via late fusion.
