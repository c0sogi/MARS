The issue is a mismatch between the data structure returned by the `collate_materials` function and the structure expected by the training and inference loops. Specifically, `collate_materials` returns `(atomic_x, global_x, batch_indices, batch_y, batch_ids)`, but the consumers expect `targets` (`batch_y`) to come before `batch_indices`. This causes the `targets` variable in the consumer to receive the 1D `batch_indices` tensor instead of the 2D targets tensor, leading to an assertion error on dimensionality. Additionally, the training loops currently unpack only 4 items, which will fail when the tuple contains 5 items.

To fix this:
1.  In `library/data.py`, I will reorder the return tuple in `collate_materials` to place `batch_y` (targets) before `batch_indices`. This aligns the producer with the consumer expectations (Cite debug_lesson_4).
2.  In `library/train.py`, I will update the unpacking statements in `train_one_epoch` and `validate` to accept 5 elements, ignoring the `ids` which are not needed for training.
