To optimize the performance of the Lattice-Conditioned Deep Sets (LCDS) model, I will implement the following changes based on the provided lessons and empirical findings:

1.  **Enhance Atomic Branch with Dual Pooling**: The previous solution used only global max pooling. I will modify the `AtomicBranch` to use both **global max pooling** and **global mean pooling**. This allows the model to capture both the existence of specific atomic features (max) and the overall distribution/density of features (mean), providing a richer geometric fingerprint. This directly applies the lesson of using a robust point-cloud encoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

2.  **Strengthen Lattice Branch**: Failure analysis showed high correlation between errors and lattice parameters. I will increase the capacity of the `LatticeBranch` (wider layers) to better model the macroscopic constraints that dictate energy potentials.

3.  **Optimize Hyperparameters**: I will increase the training duration to **300 epochs** (from 30) and `EARLY_STOPPING_PATIENCE` to **30** to allow the larger model to converge fully. I will also adjust the hidden dimensions in `Config` to support the architectural changes.

4.  **Conditional Submission**: I will modify `runfile.py` to generate a submission only if the validation RMSLE is strictly lower than the specified threshold.
