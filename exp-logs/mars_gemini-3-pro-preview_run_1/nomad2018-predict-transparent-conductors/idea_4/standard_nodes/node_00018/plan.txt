3.  **Use Full Training Schedule**: In `runfile.py`, I will remove the `num_epochs=50` override in the `train_model` call, allowing the training to proceed for the full duration specified in the config.
4.  **Update Threshold**: In `runfile.py`, I will update the submission generation threshold to `0.05479004207787702` as required.
