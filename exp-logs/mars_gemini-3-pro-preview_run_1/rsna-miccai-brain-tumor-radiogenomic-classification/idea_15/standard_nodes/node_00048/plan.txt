Here is the implementation strategy and the complete `runfile.py` script.

### Implementation Strategy

1.  **Configuration Patching**: To satisfy the "Fast Baseline" requirement, I will monkey-patch the `library.config` module immediately after import. I will reduce `NUM_EPOCHS` to 5 and `NUM_FOLDS` to 2. This ensures the training completes quickly while still verifying the pipeline's stability.
2.  **Training Orchestration**: I will load the training metadata and use `sklearn.model_selection.GroupKFold` to generate splits. I will then iterate through the folds (limited by the patched `NUM_FOLDS`) and call `library.train.run_fold` to train the models.
3.  **Validation on Hold-out Set**:
    *   I will load the hold-out validation metadata (`val_metadata.csv`).
    *   I will instantiate a `SlabDataset` for this set using validation transforms.
    *   I will load the best saved models from the training step and perform ensemble inference (averaging predictions across folds).
    *   I will aggregate the slab-level predictions to subject-level predictions by taking the mean.
    *   I will compute and print the `Final Validation Metric` (ROC AUC).
4.  **Failure Analysis**:
    *   I will calculate the absolute error for each subject in the validation set.
    *   I will extract metadata features (specifically file counts for each MRI modality) by inspecting the file system.
    *   I will compute the correlation between the prediction error and these features to identify potential sources of bias or failure.
5.  **Submission**:
    *   I will compare the calculated validation AUC against the threshold (0.6705454545454544).
    *   If the metric exceeds the threshold, I will invoke `library.predict.inference_fn` to generate the final `submission.csv`.
