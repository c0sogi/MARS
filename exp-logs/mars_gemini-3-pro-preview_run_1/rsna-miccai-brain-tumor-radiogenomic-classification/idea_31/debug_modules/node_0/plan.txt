The following script demonstrates the usage of the provided library modules to perform the task of glioblastoma genetic subtype prediction. It outlines a complete pipeline: loading metadata, computing Region of Interest (ROI) boundaries, creating the custom dataset (RN-WIV), initializing the EfficientNet-based model with structured dropout, and executing a training and validation step. To ensure the demonstration runs quickly within the time limit, it operates on a small subset of the training data.
