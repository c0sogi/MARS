Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons and the identification of specific failure modes—specifically the "Middle Slice Fallacy" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002), the violation of spatial congruence in channel-stacked architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00106), the subject-identity overfitting caused by pseudo-independent sampling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058), and the silent pipeline failures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00092)—I propose the following optimized strategy.

# ROI-Adaptive Spatially-Stratified Ensemble (RASSE)

This solution introduces a **Structural Innovation** by decomposing the volumetric classification problem into a **Spatially-Stratified Ensemble of Experts**, coupled with **Content-Adaptive Resolution Enhancement**.

Instead of forcing a single model to handle volumetric ambiguity (which leads to signal dilution) or stacking depth into channels (which violates spatial priors), we train three lightweight "Expert" models, each specialized for a specific anatomical plane relative to the brain's center. Crucially, we introduce **XY-Plane ROI Cropping** to mathematically increase the spatial resolution of the brain tissue, addressing the "Small Data" signal-to-noise ratio problem.

### Model
*   **Architecture:** We utilize an ensemble of **three independent EfficientNet-B0** models.
    *   **Expert A (Lower):** Specialized for the anatomical plane at **-15%** relative to the Brain Center of Mass (CoM).
    *   **Expert B (Center):** Specialized for the **Brain CoM** plane.
    *   **Expert C (Upper):** Specialized for the anatomical plane at **+15%** relative to the Brain CoM.
*   **Input Structure:** Each expert receives a standard **3-channel** 2D input $(224 \times 224 \times 3)$ comprising **[FLAIR, T1wCE, T2w]** at its designated depth.
    *   *Rationale:* This adheres to **Early Fusion** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031) while strictly respecting **Spatial Congruence** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00106). By training separate models, we ensure each network sees only one view per subject, preventing the "Subject Identity Overfitting" caused by mixing correlated views in a single training loop (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).

### Data
The pipeline employs **Independent Content-Based Anchoring**, **ROI-Adaptive Cropping**, and **Strict Integrity Verification**.

1.  **Independent 3D Anchoring & ROI Detection:**
    *   For **each modality independently**, we load the volume and threshold pixels $>0$ to identify the brain tissue.
    *   We calculate the **Z-axis Center of Mass (CoM)** and the **XY-Plane Bounding Box** of the brain tissue.
    *   *Benefit:* This anchors sampling to the biological center (solving the "Middle Slice Fallacy", Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) and handles varying Fields-of-View without misalignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
2.  **Spatially-Stratified Extraction:**
    *   We extract slices at three relative depths: $Z_{CoM} - 0.15 \times Depth$, $Z_{CoM}$, and $Z_{CoM} + 0.15 \times Depth$.
3.  **Content-Adaptive Resolution Enhancement (The Innovation):**
    *   Instead of resizing the full $512 \times 512$ scan (which is mostly black background) to $224 \times 224$, we **crop the slice to the computed XY-Bounding Box** *before* resizing.
    *   *Benefit:* This effectively "zooms in" on the brain tissue, significantly increasing the pixel resolution of anatomical features fed to the CNN, maximizing the information density of the small dataset.
4.  **High-Fidelity Preprocessing:**
    *   **Precision:** Use **float32** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
    *   **Normalization:** Apply **Independent Channel Min-Max Scaling** to $[0, 1]$ *before* stacking (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
5.  **Integrity Check:** Assert dataset length matches ~523 subjects and ROI metadata is present. Remove legacy fallbacks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00092, solution_lesson_node_00071).

### Training
*   **Procedure:** Train the three Expert models (A, B, C) independently.
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) with Logits.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a learning rate of $1e-4$.
*   **Regularization:**
    *   **Aggressive Weight Decay:** Set to **$1e-2$** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Dropout:** Enforce a rate of **0.3** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
*   **Spatially-Coupled Augmentation:**
    *   Apply **Elastic Transformations**, **Grid Distortions**, **Rotations**, and **Flips**.
    *   **Strictly Exclude:** Random Translations (Shift) and Scaling. Since we are using CoM Anchoring and ROI Cropping, the brain is spatially centered and normalized. Translation augmentation destroys this strong spatial prior (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065, solution_lesson_node_00102).
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Cross-Validation** grouped by Subject ID (Cite solution_lesson_node_00051).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For each test subject, calculate the Independent CoM/BBox per modality and deterministically generate the three inputs (Lower, Center, Upper) with ROI cropping.
*   **Prediction:** Pass each input to its corresponding Expert model.
*   **Consensus:** Average the probabilities from the three experts (and across their 5 folds). This leverages volumetric coverage to detect off-center tumors without the complexity of 3D architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
