The issue is caused by **Data Leakage** in the `get_fold_dataloaders` function. The function currently loads both the training metadata (`train_metadata.csv`) and the hold-out validation metadata (`val_metadata.csv`), concatenates them, and then performs cross-validation on the combined dataset. This results in the validation set samples being used for training in 4 out of 5 folds, rendering the final evaluation metric invalid.

To fix this, I will modify `library/dataset.py` to strictly separate the datasets. The `get_fold_dataloaders` function will be updated to load only `train_metadata.csv` and use `save_name="train"` for caching. This ensures that the hold-out validation set remains unseen during the training phase and prevents the loading of the stale, combined cache (Cite `debug_lesson_1`).
