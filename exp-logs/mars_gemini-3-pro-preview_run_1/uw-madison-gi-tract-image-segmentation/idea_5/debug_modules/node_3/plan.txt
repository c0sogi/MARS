The issue stems from a change in the default behavior of `albumentations` (version 2.0+), where geometric transformations like `ShiftScaleRotate`, `GridDistortion`, and `ElasticTransform` now default to `border_mode=cv2.BORDER_CONSTANT` (zero-padding). This introduces zero values at the image borders during augmentation. Since the 4th input channel (depth map) is constructed as a spatially constant value (e.g., 0.5 everywhere), the introduction of zeros breaks the invariant that the depth channel must be constant across the entire image, causing the `AssertionError` in `runfile.py`.

To fix this, I will explicitly set `border_mode=cv2.BORDER_REFLECT_101` for all geometric transformations in `library/dataset.py`. This ensures that border pixels are filled by reflection rather than zero-padding, preserving the constant nature of the depth channel.
