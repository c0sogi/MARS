The `TypeError` occurs because the `hausdorff_distance` function in `library/utils.py` relies on `np.argwhere`, which cannot operate on CUDA tensors. While `dice_coefficient` internally handles tensor-to-NumPy conversion, `hausdorff_distance` does not, leading to a crash when `runfile.py` passes CUDA tensors directly. To resolve this, I will modify `runfile.py` to explicitly detach the tensors, move them to the CPU, and convert them to NumPy arrays before invoking the metric functions. This ensures the consumer (`runfile.py`) correctly adheres to the input requirements of the utility functions (Cite debug_lesson_1).
