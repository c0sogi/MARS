Based on the analysis of the previous ideas and the specific lessons learned, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **3D U-Net** architecture to fundamentally resolve the Z-axis inconsistency limiting the Hausdorff score, while strictly adhering to the robust preprocessing and post-processing protocols established in the lessons.

# Model
**Architecture:** **3D U-Net** with a **ResNet-18 (3D)** backbone.
*   **Structural Innovation (Exploration):** I will transition from 2D/2.5D slice-based processing to **Volumetric (3D) Processing**. As identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**, the primary bottleneck of the current best solution (2.5D) is the lack of Z-axis consistency, which causes stagnation in the Hausdorff metric. A 3D U-Net processes volumetric patches (e.g., $D \times H \times W$), allowing the model to explicitly learn 3D spatial continuity and organ shape priors, eliminating the "ghosting" artifacts of naive channel stacking (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**) and the discontinuity of slice-independent predictions.
*   **Backbone (Exploitation):** I will utilize a **3D ResNet-18** as the encoder. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012** demonstrated that ResNet architectures provide superior convergence speed and stability compared to EfficientNet for this dataset. Given the increased computational load of 3D convolutions, using a lightweight, parameter-efficient backbone like ResNet-18 is critical to ensuring the model trains to convergence within the 24-hour limit, adhering to the warning in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025** to avoid unnecessary complexity.
*   **Decoder:** A standard 3D U-Net decoder with skip connections will be used. We will avoid complex nested topologies (like U-Net++) to maintain training stability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**).

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Volumetric Construction:** Training data will be organized into full 3D volumes $(Z, H, W)$ per (Case, Day) pair.
*   **Normalization (Exploitation):** I will strictly apply **Robust Percentile Normalization** (clipping to [1st, 99th] percentiles before scaling) to handle the high dynamic range and outliers, as mandated by **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
*   **Interpolation (Exploitation):** All image resizing will use **Bilinear Interpolation** to preserve texture gradients, preventing the aliasing artifacts caused by Nearest Neighbor interpolation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
*   **Patch Sampling:** Due to GPU memory constraints, we cannot train on full volumes. We will sample **random 3D patches** (e.g., $32 \times 256 \times 256$) from the volumes during training. This allows the model to learn local 3D context efficiently.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** A combined **BCE + Dice Loss** will be computed on the 3D volumetric outputs.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing learning rate scheduler.
*   **Strategy:** The model will be trained on the `metadata/train.csv` split and validated on `metadata/val.csv`. We will use a sliding window approach on the training volumes to ensure all informative regions (foreground) are sampled frequently.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** We will perform **Sliding Window Inference** with overlap (e.g., 50%) along the Z-axis and spatial dimensions to generate predictions for the full test volumes. The overlapping regions will be Gaussian-weighted and averaged to prevent boundary artifacts between patches.
*   **3D Reconstruction (Exploitation):** Validation will strictly follow the "reconstruct-then-score" methodology. Metrics will be calculated on the fully reconstructed 3D volume for each case, as **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006** established that slice-averaged metrics are invalid for Hausdorff distance.
*   **Post-Processing (Exploitation):** We will apply **3D Connected Component Analysis (CCA)** to the predicted volumes. For each class, only the largest connected 3D component will be retained. This is the critical defense against "shot noise" outliers that destabilize the Hausdorff metric (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
