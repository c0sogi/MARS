Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, the primary optimization strategy is to restore the **parsimonious set of 5 geometric features** used in the "Current Best Solution" (Idea 53) referenced in Lesson 00182 and Lesson 00173.

The previous solution included `Roundness` as a sixth feature. Lesson 00173 explicitly notes that adding `Roundness` (a derived ratio of Area and Perimeter) alongside `Area` in a pipeline with learnable normalization (`PowerTransformer`) degraded performance from `3.06e-14` to `3.49e-14`. This degradation is attributed to the redundancy (Cite Lesson 00150) and the introduction of noise from perimeter measurements (Cite Lesson 00162) which are sensitive to pixelation. By removing `Roundness` and relying on the robust 5-feature triad (Area, Eccentricity, Solidity, Extent, Aspect Ratio), we expect to recover the superior performance.
