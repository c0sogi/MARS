Based on the lessons learned, specifically **Lesson 00119** and **Lesson 00120**, the strategy is to refine the feature engineering pipeline to prioritize a compact set of highly discriminative, invariant geometric ratios over high-dimensional shape descriptors.

1.  **Remove Elliptical Fourier Descriptors (EFDs)**: As noted in **Lesson 00119**, adding ~60 spectral coefficients introduced noise and sparsity that degraded the performance of the Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) model in the high-precision regime. I will disable EFD calculation.
2.  **Optimize Spatial Features**: I will replace raw dimensions (Area, Perimeter) with the "complete triad" of macro-geometric descriptors (**Solidity, Aspect Ratio, Extent**) as recommended in **Lesson 00120**. Additionally, I will include **Roundness** and **Eccentricity** (from Lesson 00119) and retain **Equivalent Diameter** to capture the absolute size signal (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00118**).
3.  **Update Threshold**: The submission threshold will be updated to the new target value.
