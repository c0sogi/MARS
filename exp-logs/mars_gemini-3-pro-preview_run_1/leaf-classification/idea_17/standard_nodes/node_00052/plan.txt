I will optimize the solution by reverting the Transductive OAS implementation to a standard Supervised OAS Discriminant. The previous transductive approach degraded performance (Log Loss ~0.0043) because it used fixed training means to compute residuals for test data, introducing errors due to distribution shifts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).
