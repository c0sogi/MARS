The `ValueError` occurs because the naive `head(100)` slicing in debug mode creates disjoint sets of classes between the training and validation subsets due to the dataset's high cardinality (99 classes). The `LabelEncoder` fitted on the training subset encounters unseen labels in the validation subset.

To fix this, I will modify `library/data_pipeline.py` to filter the validation subset so it only includes species present in the training subset (Cite debug_lesson_1). Additionally, I will update `runfile.py` to relax the assertion on validation set size and use dynamic shape checks, as the filtering step may result in fewer than 100 validation samples (Cite debug_lesson_6).
