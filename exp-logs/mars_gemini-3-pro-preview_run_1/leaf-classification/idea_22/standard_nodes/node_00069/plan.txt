Based on the provided lessons and the performance analysis of the previous solution, the primary cause for the degradation in validation log loss (from ~1.21e-09 to ~6.29e-09) was the use of the **Geometric Median** instead of the **Arithmetic Mean** for class centroid estimation.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Revert to Arithmetic Mean:** As cited in **Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00066`**, when data is Gaussianized via Power Transformations (which is done in the preprocessing pipeline), the Arithmetic Mean is the Maximum Likelihood Estimator (MLE). The Geometric Median, while robust to outliers, introduces bias and variance that degrades performance on clean, Gaussian-like data.
2.  **Maintain OAS and Precision:** I will retain the **Oracle Approximating Shrinkage (OAS)** (Cite **Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00047`**) and the **`float64` precision** (Cite **Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00057`**) throughout the pipeline, as these were identified as optimal components in previous best solutions.
