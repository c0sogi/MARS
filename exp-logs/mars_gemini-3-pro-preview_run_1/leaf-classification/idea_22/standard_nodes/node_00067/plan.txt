The strategy involves replacing the **Geometric Median** centroid estimation with the **Arithmetic Mean** in the Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) pipeline. According to **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00066**, using the Geometric Median on features that have been explicitly Gaussianized (via Yeo-Johnson Power Transformation) degrades performance significantly (from ~1.21e-09 to ~6.29e-09) because the Arithmetic Mean is the statistically optimal Maximum Likelihood Estimator for Gaussian data. I will revert to the Arithmetic Mean while retaining the **Oracle Approximating Shrinkage (OAS)** for covariance estimation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00047**) and the **Linear Formulation** for inference (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00055**) to ensure high numerical precision and stability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00057**).
