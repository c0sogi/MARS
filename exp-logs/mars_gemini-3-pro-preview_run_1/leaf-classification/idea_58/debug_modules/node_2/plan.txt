The error `ValueError: y contains previously unseen labels` is caused by the naive subsampling strategy in `load_metadata` when `debug=True`. By simply taking the first 20 rows (`head(20)`), the code creates training and validation subsets with disjoint class labels (e.g., the training subset contains species A and B, while the validation subset contains species C). This causes the `LabelEncoder`, which is fitted only on the training subset, to fail when it encounters the unseen species in the validation subset.

To fix this, I will modify `library/utils.py` to apply **debug_lesson_1** ("Filter Classes, Don't Just Slice Rows"). Before slicing the dataframe to 20 rows, I will filter it to include only a small, deterministic subset of species (the first 10 sorted alphabetically). This ensures that both the training and validation debug sets are drawn from the same restricted pool of classes, guaranteeing that `y_val` only contains labels present in `y_train`.
