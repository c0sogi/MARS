My implementation strategy focuses on optimizing numerical precision to break the `10^-7` log loss floor imposed by `float32` arithmetic, as identified in Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00057`.

1.  **Preprocessing (Quantization Regularization):** I will modify `library/preprocessing.py` to cast the transformed features to `float32` immediately before saving/returning. This applies "Quantization as Implicit Regularization" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00042`), filtering high-frequency numerical noise from the feature engineering pipeline while maintaining `float64` precision during the transformation steps themselves (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00046`).

2.  **Model (High-Precision Inference):** I will update `library/model.py` to remove the explicit downcasting of model weights and bias to `float32`. Instead, the `LinearizedOASDiscriminant` will store parameters in `float64` and perform the inference (dot product and softmax) in `float64`. This ensures the model can resolve probabilities closer to 1.0 than `1 - 10^-7`, which is critical for minimizing log loss in high-separability regimes (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00057`).
