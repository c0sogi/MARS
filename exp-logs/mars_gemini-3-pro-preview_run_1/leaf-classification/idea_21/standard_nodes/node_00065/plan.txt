The optimization strategy focuses on implementing a **mixed-precision pipeline** that leverages **quantization regularization** during training while ensuring **high-precision inference**.

Based on the provided lessons:
1.  **Quantization Regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042, solution_lesson_node_00049):** Training data is explicitly quantized to `float32` after preprocessing. This filters out high-frequency numerical noise generated by the Yeo-Johnson transformation, preventing the model from overfitting to artifacts in the feature tails.
2.  **High-Precision Inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057):** While the model parameters (weights and biases) are derived from `float32` data, the final inference step (dot product and softmax) is upcasted to `float64`. This avoids the `float32` machine epsilon floor (~1e-7), allowing the model to output probabilities closer to 1.0 (e.g., `1.0 - 1e-9`) for highly confident predictions, which is critical for minimizing Log Loss in the saturation regime.

This approach resolves the conflict between the need for noise filtering (best with `float32`) and the need for numerical resolution (best with `float64`).
