The `ValueError` arises because `StratifiedKFold` in `library/data.py` requires `n_splits` to be at least 2 to generate valid train/test splits, but `Config.N_FOLDS` was set to 1 in `runfile.py`. To fix this, I will increase `Config.N_FOLDS` to 2 in `runfile.py`. This change satisfies the initialization requirements of the cross-validator without affecting the runtime duration, as the training loop in `library/engine.py` is already designed to `break` after the first fold.
