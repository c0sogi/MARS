Based on the provided lessons and the analysis of the previous solution, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Remove the LSTM Layer**: As detailed in **Lesson 19 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019)**, stacking Recurrent Neural Networks (like LSTMs) on top of pre-trained Transformers often degrades performance and increases latency for span extraction tasks. The self-attention mechanism of DeBERTa-v3 is sufficient for global context, and the LSTM introduces a bottleneck.
2.  **Maintain the CNN Head**: I will retain the 1D Convolutional layer in the head, as **Lesson 11 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011)** indicates that a CNN-enhanced head helps capture local boundary cues better than a simple linear projection.
3.  **Restore Training Epochs**: The previous solution reduced training to 3 epochs for speed, but the "Current Best" result was achieved with 5 epochs. I will revert this to 5 to ensure the model converges fully, as the removal of the LSTM will also speed up per-epoch training time.
