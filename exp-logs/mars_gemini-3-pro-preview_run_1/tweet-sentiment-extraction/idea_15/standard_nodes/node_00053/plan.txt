The issue is that the validation data loader in `runfile.py` calls `load_and_cache_data` with `mode="val"`, which sets `is_train=False`. In the original implementation, `is_train=False` disables both the **filtering of neutral tweets** (correct behavior for validation) and the **extraction of ground truth labels** (incorrect, as we need `selected_texts` for evaluation). This leads to a `KeyError: 'selected_texts'` when `runfile.py` attempts to access the missing labels.

To fix this, I will modify `library/data.py` to decouple the concept of "training mode" (which applies filtering) from "having labels" (which is needed for both training and validation). I will update `process_data` to accept a `has_labels` flag and update `load_and_cache_data` to set this flag for both "train" and "val" modes, ensuring `selected_texts` are loaded for the validation set without applying training-specific filters. This aligns with **Cite debug_lesson_2** (Decouple Training Data Optimization from Validation Integrity).
