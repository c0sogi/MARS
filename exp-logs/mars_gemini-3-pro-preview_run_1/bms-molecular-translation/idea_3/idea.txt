Based on the provided dataset and model architectures, I propose the **"Visual Transformer" (CNN-Transformer Hybrid)** approach. This method synthesizes the visual feature extraction power of Model 1 (CNN) with the sequence modeling capabilities of Model 2 (Transformer), offering a distinct, highly parallelizable, and modern baseline compared to the RNN-based approaches in Idea 1 and Idea 2.

### Model
The architecture is a hybrid Encoder-Decoder design that leverages the strengths of Convolutional Neural Networks for visual pattern recognition and Transformers for sequence generation:
*   **Encoder**: The backbone is a lightweight **ResNet-18** CNN. We discard the final classification and global pooling layers, extracting the raw spatial feature maps from the last convolutional block. These 2D feature maps are flattened into a 1D sequence of feature vectors. To retain the critical spatial arrangement of atoms and bonds, **learnable 2D positional embeddings** are added to these vectors before they are passed to the decoder.
*   **Decoder**: A lightweight **Transformer Decoder** (consisting of 3 layers and 4 attention heads). Unlike the LSTM/GRU in previous ideas, this decoder uses **Masked Self-Attention** to understand the grammar of the InChI strings and **Cross-Attention** to dynamically attend to the relevant parts of the image encoding. This design avoids the sequential bottleneck of RNNs during training and better captures the long-range dependencies inherent in complex chemical identifiers.

### Data
*   **Image Preprocessing**: Training and test images will be resized to a standard resolution of **$224 \times 224$ pixels** to balance detail preservation with computational speed. Pixel values will be normalized using standard mean and standard deviation (e.g., ImageNet stats).
*   **Text Preprocessing**: The InChI strings will be tokenized at the character level using the vocabulary derived from the training set. Special tokens (`<SOS>`, `<EOS>`, `<PAD>`) will be added. Target sequences will be padded to a fixed maximum length to facilitate efficient batch processing.

### Training
*   **Loss Function**: **Cross-Entropy Loss** will be used to measure the discrepancy between the predicted token probabilities and the ground truth characters.
*   **Optimizer**: The **AdamW** optimizer will be employed, as it is the standard for training Transformer-based models, offering decoupled weight decay for better regularization.
*   **Strategy**: The model will be trained using **implicit Teacher Forcing** via causal masking. Unlike RNNs which process token-by-token, the Transformer Decoder processes the entire ground-truth sequence in parallel during training, predicting the next token for every position simultaneously. This results in significantly faster training epochs. A **Linear Warmup** learning rate schedule will be used to stabilize the Transformer gradients in the early stages.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference Process**: For the test set, the model operates in autoregressive mode. The image is encoded once. The decoder starts with the `<SOS>` token and predicts the next character based on the image features and previously generated characters.
*   **Decoding Strategy**: **Greedy Search** will be used for inference. At each step, the character with the highest probability is selected and fed back as input for the next step. Generation halts when the `<EOS>` token is produced or the maximum sequence length is reached.
