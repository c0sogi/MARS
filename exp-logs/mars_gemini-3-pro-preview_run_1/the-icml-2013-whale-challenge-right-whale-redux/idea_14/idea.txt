Based on the comprehensive history of experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Ensemble of High-Resolution Spectrally-Adaptive Hierarchical CA-ResNet-18 CRNNs**.

This solution synthesizes the structural innovations of the "Spectrally-Adaptive" architecture (Idea 13) with the critical data resolution constraints identified in Lesson 00064, while addressing the dominant run-to-run variance issue (Lesson 00066) via a system-level evolution to Deep Ensembling.

### Model
The solution employs a **Deep Ensemble** strategy where the base learner is a **Spectrally-Adaptive Hierarchical CA-ResNet-18 CRNN**.

1.  **Backbone: Time-Preserving ResNet-18 with Coordinate Attention**
    *   **Base Architecture**: Use **ResNet-18** initialized with **ImageNet weights** (averaging the first layer for 1-channel input) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020). This backbone is chosen for its proven robustness over EfficientNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Coordinate Attention**: Replace standard Residual Blocks with **Coordinate Attention Blocks** to capture long-range spectral and temporal dependencies (Cite solution_lesson_node_00047).
    *   **Strides**: Maintain **Standard Strides** in Layers 1 and 2 to preserve the receptive field hierarchy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059). Apply **Asymmetric Strides `(2, 1)`** (Frequency, Time) in Layers 3 and 4 to preserve temporal resolution for the RNN (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

2.  **Feature Aggregation: Adaptive Spectral Pooling & Bottleneck Fusion**
    *   **Hierarchical Extraction**: Extract feature maps from **Layer 2**, **Layer 3**, and **Layer 4** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).
    *   **Adaptive Pooling**: Instead of Global Pooling (which destroys frequency info) or fixed Multi-Band splitting (which creates high dimensionality), apply **Adaptive Average Pooling** along the frequency axis to varying resolutions:
        *   **Layer 2 (Texture)**: Pool to **4 Frequency Bins**.
        *   **Layer 3 (Shape)**: Pool to **2 Frequency Bins**.
        *   **Layer 4 (Semantic)**: Pool to **1 Frequency Bin**.
    *   **Fusion**: Flatten and concatenate these features along the channel dimension. This preserves coarse frequency localization (e.g., distinguishing low-frequency calls from high-frequency noise) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060). Pass the result through a **1x1 Conv Bottleneck + SE Block** to compress dimensionality and recalibrate feature importance.

3.  **Head: Bi-GRU with Attention Pooling**
    *   Process the fused sequence with a **Bi-Directional GRU** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) and aggregate via **Attention Pooling** (Cite Idea 3).

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Input Window**: Load the full **2.0-second** clip. Do not truncate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Resolution (Critical Fix)**: Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms using a **Large FFT Window (e.g., 1024 samples)** to ensure high frequency resolution (~2Hz/bin). This corrects the failure of Lesson 00064, where reducing `n_fft` to match the pooling bins destroyed the tonal signal. The Adaptive Pooling layer in the model will handle the downsampling, not the STFT.
    *   **Channels**: Use **1-Channel** input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).

2.  **Augmentation**:
    *   **Mixup**: Apply Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$) to training batches (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **SpecAugment**: Apply Frequency and Time Masking.
    *   **Constraint**: Strictly limit **Time Mask width to < 200ms** to prevent signal occlusion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training
1.  **System Strategy (Ensembling)**: Train **$N$ (e.g., 5) independent models** with different random seeds. This addresses the "Run-to-Run Variance" bottleneck identified in Lesson 00066, where stochastic noise dominated marginal architectural gains in the high-precision regime.
2.  **Loss Function**: Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Positive Class Weight** (e.g., 9.0) to handle the 1:9 imbalance. This is preferred over Focal Loss for this specific ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).
3.  **Target Handling**: Compute loss against **mixed soft targets**.
4.  **Metric Validity**: Monitor **Validation AUC** on unmixed data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
5.  **Optimizer**: Use **Adam** with **ReduceLROnPlateau** scheduler.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: Process test clips into High-Resolution Log-Mel Spectrograms (2.0s).
2.  **Ensemble Prediction**: Pass inputs through all $N$ trained models. **Average the probability outputs** to produce the final prediction. This stabilizes the decision boundary and ensures the submission exceeds the high-performance threshold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
