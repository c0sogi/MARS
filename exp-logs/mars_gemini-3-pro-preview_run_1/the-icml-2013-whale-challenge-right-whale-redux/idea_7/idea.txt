Based on the comprehensive history of experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Multi-Resolution Time-Preserving ResNet-34 CRNN**.

This solution introduces a **Multi-Resolution Input Strategy** to leverage the 3-channel architecture of ImageNet-pretrained models naturally, avoiding the weight-averaging compromise of previous iterations. It upgrades the backbone to a **ResNet-34** to increase feature extraction capacity while strictly adhering to the "Time-Preserving" structural constraints that proved superior to EfficientNet.

### Model
The architecture is a **Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network (CRNN)** designed to capture multi-scale time-frequency features.

1.  **Input: Multi-Resolution Spectrograms (Structural Innovation)**
    *   Instead of a single-channel spectrogram, we generate a **3-Channel Image** where each channel corresponds to a Log-Mel Spectrogram computed with a different FFT window size but a fixed hop length.
        *   **Channel R (Red):** Short Window (~10ms) $\rightarrow$ High Temporal Resolution (captures transient clicks).
        *   **Channel G (Green):** Medium Window (~25ms) $\rightarrow$ Balanced Resolution (standard representation).
        *   **Channel B (Blue):** Long Window (~50ms) $\rightarrow$ High Frequency Resolution (captures harmonic stacks and pitch contours).
    *   This allows the model to simultaneously view the signal with high temporal precision and high frequency precision, effectively bypassing the Heisenberg uncertainty limit of a single STFT view. This also allows direct use of **ImageNet Pretrained Weights** without modifying the first convolutional layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).

2.  **Backbone: Time-Preserving ResNet-34**
    *   We upgrade the backbone from ResNet-18 to **ResNet-34**. This deeper network provides higher capacity for feature abstraction while maintaining the uniform Residual Block structure that is robust to stride modifications, unlike the brittle EfficientNet architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Time-Preservation**: We modify the strides of the final two residual stages (Layer 3 and 4) to be **Asymmetric `(2, 1)`** (Frequency, Time). This downsamples the frequency dimension to extract abstract features but preserves the temporal sequence length (~50-60 steps), ensuring the downstream RNN receives sufficient resolution to detect the "up-sweep" motion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

3.  **Head: Bi-GRU with Attention Pooling**
    *   The feature maps are pooled along the frequency dimension and passed to a **Bi-Directional GRU**, retaining the component proven most effective for modeling the whale call's temporal shape (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    *   The sequence is aggregated via **Attention Pooling** to dynamically focus on the call segment and suppress noise (Cite Idea 3).

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Duration**: Load the full **2.0-second** audio clip. Do not truncate, ensuring the signal is never discarded (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Spectrogram Generation**: Generate three Log-Mel Spectrograms (128 Mel bins) using window sizes of `[200, 500, 1000]` samples (assuming 20kHz, approx 10/25/50ms) and a fixed hop length (e.g., 10ms) to ensure all channels align on the time axis. Stack them to form a `(3, 128, T)` tensor.

2.  **Augmentation**:
    *   **Mixup**: Apply Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$) to training batches to accelerate convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **SpecAugment**: Apply Frequency and Time Masking to the 3-channel image.
    *   **Constraint**: Strictly limit the **Time Mask width to a maximum of 200ms** (approx. 20 frames). This ensures the augmentation does not occlude the entire short-duration whale call (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training
1.  **Loss Function**: Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss**.
    *   **Class Weighting**: Apply a **Positive Class Weight** (e.g., 9.0) to explicitly handle the 1:9 class imbalance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Target Handling**: Compute loss against the **mixed soft targets** generated by Mixup.
2.  **Optimizer**: Use **Adam** with a standard learning rate.
3.  **Scheduler**: Implement **ReduceLROnPlateau** to decay the learning rate when Validation AUC stagnates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
4.  **Metric Validity**: Monitor **Validation AUC** on unmixed data. Do not rely on training metrics computed against hard labels during Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: Process test clips into the 3-channel Multi-Resolution format (2.0s duration).
2.  **Prediction**: Pass inputs through the model. Apply Sigmoid activation to the output.
3.  **Submission**: Save clip IDs and probabilities to the submission file.
