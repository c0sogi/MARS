Based on the comprehensive history of experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Ensemble of SpecFPN-Enhanced Time-Preserving ResNet-18 CRNNs**.

This solution introduces a **Structural Innovation** in the feature aggregation stage: the **Spectrogram Feature Pyramid Network (SpecFPN)**. This architecture supersedes the previous "Hierarchical Concatenation" approach (Idea 14) by replacing simple concatenation with a top-down pathway that explicitly restores the frequency resolution of deep semantic features. This allows the model to leverage the high spectral fidelity of early layers enriched with the semantic context of deep layers, strictly adhering to the resolution and stability constraints identified in the lessons.

### Model

The architecture is a **Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network (CRNN)** equipped with a Feature Pyramid Neck.

1.  **Backbone: Time-Preserving ResNet-18 with Coordinate Attention**
    *   **Base Architecture**: Use **ResNet-18** initialized with **ImageNet weights** (averaging the first layer for 1-channel input) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020). This backbone is chosen for its proven robustness over EfficientNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Coordinate Attention**: Replace standard Residual Blocks with **Coordinate Attention Blocks** to capture long-range spectral and temporal dependencies (Cite solution_lesson_node_00047).
    *   **Strides**: Maintain **Standard Strides** in Layers 1 and 2 to preserve the receptive field hierarchy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00059). Apply **Asymmetric Strides `(2, 1)`** (Frequency, Time) in Layers 3 and 4 to preserve temporal resolution for the RNN (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

2.  **Neck (Innovation): Spectrogram Feature Pyramid Network (SpecFPN)**
    *   **Motivation**: Previous hierarchical approaches (Idea 14) simply concatenated features from different layers. This ignores the spatial (spectral) misalignment between layers and fails to fully recover the fine-grained frequency details lost in deep layers.
    *   **Mechanism**: Implement a **Top-Down Feature Pyramid**:
        1.  **Lateral Connections**: Project the feature maps from **Layer 2**, **Layer 3**, and **Layer 4** to a common channel dimension (e.g., 256) using 1x1 convolutions.
        2.  **Top-Down Fusion**:
            *   Upsample the **Layer 4** projection (Frequency axis only) by 2x and add it to the **Layer 3** projection.
            *   Upsample this fused map (Frequency axis only) by 2x and add it to the **Layer 2** projection.
        3.  **Result**: A final feature map with the **High Frequency Resolution** of Layer 2 (e.g., 32 bins) but enriched with the **High Semantic Information** of Layer 4.
    *   **Bottleneck**: Flatten the frequency bins of this fused map into the channel dimension and pass through a **1x1 Conv Bottleneck** to compress dimensionality before the RNN (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).

3.  **Head: Bi-GRU with Attention Pooling**
    *   Process the SpecFPN output sequence with a **Bi-Directional GRU** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    *   Aggregate the sequence via **Attention Pooling** to focus on the call segment and suppress noise (Cite Idea 3).

### Data

1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Input Window**: Load the full **2.0-second** clip. Do not truncate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Resolution**: Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms using a **Large FFT Window (e.g., 1024 samples)** to ensure high frequency resolution (~2Hz/bin). This provides the raw signal fidelity required for the SpecFPN to operate effectively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00064).
    *   **Channels**: Use **1-Channel** input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).

2.  **Augmentation**:
    *   **Mixup**: Apply Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$) to training batches (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **SpecAugment**: Apply Frequency and Time Masking.
    *   **Constraint**: Strictly limit **Time Mask width to < 200ms** to prevent signal occlusion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training

1.  **System Strategy (Ensembling)**: Train **5 independent models** with different random seeds. This addresses the "Run-to-Run Variance" bottleneck identified in Lesson 00066, ensuring the final submission is robust and exceeds the high-performance threshold.
2.  **Loss Function**: Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Positive Class Weight** (e.g., 9.0) to handle the 1:9 imbalance. This is preferred over Focal Loss for this specific ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).
3.  **Target Handling**: Compute loss against **mixed soft targets**.
4.  **Metric Validity**: Monitor **Validation AUC** on unmixed data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
5.  **Optimizer**: Use **Adam** with **ReduceLROnPlateau** scheduler.

### Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test clips into High-Resolution Log-Mel Spectrograms (2.0s).
2.  **Ensemble Prediction**: Pass inputs through all 5 trained SpecFPN-CRNN models. **Average the probability outputs** to produce the final prediction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
