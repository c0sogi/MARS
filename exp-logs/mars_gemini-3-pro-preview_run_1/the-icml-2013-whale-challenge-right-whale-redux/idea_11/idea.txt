Based on the comprehensive history of experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Hierarchical Coordinate-Attention ResNet-18 CRNN**.

This solution introduces a **Structural Innovation** via **Hierarchical Feature Aggregation**. Instead of relying solely on the final convolutional layer—which contains the most abstract but spatially compressed features—this architecture explicitly aggregates multi-scale features from intermediate layers of the backbone. This allows the downstream recurrent network to simultaneously process fine-grained spectral textures (from shallow layers) and abstract semantic shapes (from deep layers), strictly adhering to the "Time-Preserving" and "Lightweight" constraints established in previous lessons.

### Model

The architecture is a **Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network (CRNN)** that creates a dense information flow between the feature extractor and the temporal model.

1.  **Backbone: Time-Preserving ResNet-18 with Coordinate Attention**
    *   **Base & Attention**: We retain the **ResNet-18** backbone equipped with **Coordinate Attention (CA)** blocks. This combination has proven to be the most robust and efficient feature extractor (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031, solution_lesson_node_00047).
    *   **Time-Preservation**: We strictly maintain **Asymmetric Strides `(2, 1)`** (Frequency, Time) in the final two stages (Layers 3 and 4). This ensures that the temporal resolution is preserved at `T/4` relative to the input, preventing signal collapse (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   **Initialization**: Initialize with **ImageNet pre-trained weights**, averaging the first layer to accept 1-channel input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).

2.  **Structural Innovation: Hierarchical Feature Aggregation**
    *   **Mechanism**: Instead of only passing the output of Layer 4 to the RNN, we extract feature maps from **Layer 2**, **Layer 3**, and **Layer 4**.
    *   **Alignment**: Due to the "Time-Preserving" strides in Layers 3 and 4 (stride 1 in time), the temporal dimension of these three layers is identical (`T/4`).
    *   **Aggregation**:
        *   Apply **Global Average Pooling** along the **Frequency axis** for each layer's output, collapsing $(C, F, T) \to (C, T)$.
        *   **Concatenate** the resulting sequence vectors along the channel dimension. This creates a rich representation containing low-level texture details (Layer 2) and high-level semantic abstractions (Layer 4) at every time step.
        *   Apply a **1x1 Convolution** (Projection Layer) to reduce the channel dimensionality (e.g., to 512) before feeding into the RNN, keeping the model lightweight (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).

3.  **Temporal Modeling: Bi-Directional GRU**
    *   The hierarchical sequence is fed into a **Bi-directional GRU**, retaining the component proven most effective for modeling the temporal contour of the call (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

4.  **Aggregation: Attention Pooling**
    *   Utilize **Attention Pooling** to compute a weighted sum of the GRU outputs, focusing the prediction on the signal segment and suppressing noise (Cite Idea 3).

### Data

1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Input Window**: Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms covering the **full 2.0-second duration** of the clip. Do not truncate to 1.0s (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Channel Format**: Use a **1-channel** input representation. Do not repeat the spectrogram to 3 channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
    *   **Spectrogram Parameters**: Use **128 Mel bins** and a hop length tuned to produce ~200 time frames.

2.  **Augmentation**:
    *   **Mixup**: Apply Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$) to training batches to regularize the model and accelerate convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **SpecAugment**: Apply Frequency and Time Masking.
    *   **Constraint**: Strictly limit the **Time Mask width to a maximum of 200ms** (approx. 20 frames). This ensures the augmentation does not occlude the entire short-duration whale call (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training

1.  **Loss Function**: Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss**.
    *   **Class Weighting**: Apply a **Positive Class Weight** (e.g., 9.0) to explicitly handle the 1:9 class imbalance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Target Handling**: Compute loss against the **mixed soft targets** generated by Mixup.

2.  **Optimizer**: Use **Adam** with a standard learning rate.

3.  **Scheduler**: Implement **ReduceLROnPlateau** to decay the learning rate when Validation AUC stagnates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

4.  **Metric Validity**: Monitor **Validation AUC** on unmixed data. Do not rely on training metrics computed against hard labels during Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

### Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test clips into 2.0s Log-Mel Spectrograms (128 bins).
2.  **Prediction**: Pass inputs through the Hierarchical Coordinate-Attention ResNet-18 model.
3.  **Output**: Apply a Sigmoid activation to the final output of the Attention Pooling layer to generate the probability for the submission file.
