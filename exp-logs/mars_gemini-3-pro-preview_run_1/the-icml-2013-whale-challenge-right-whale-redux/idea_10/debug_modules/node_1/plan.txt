The `TypeError` encountered during execution is caused by the inclusion of the `verbose` argument in the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler constructor. In recent versions of PyTorch (2.2+), the `verbose` parameter was deprecated and removed from scheduler initializers, as logging logic has been decoupled from the scheduler itself. To resolve this, I will remove the `verbose=True` argument from the `ReduceLROnPlateau` instantiation in both `library/trainer.py` and `library/model.py`. Cite debug_lesson_2.
