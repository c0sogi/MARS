The previous implementation caused a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` because it indiscriminately modified all downsampling layers in the EfficientNet backbone to preserve the temporal dimension (stride `(2, 1)`). This maintained a very large temporal resolution (250 frames) throughout the entire network, leading to excessive memory consumption.

To fix this, I will apply **Lesson ID: debug_lesson_7 (Target Structural Modifications to Specific Model Stages)** by refining the stride modification logic in `library/model.py`. The new logic will allow the first two downsampling operations to proceed normally (reducing temporal resolution by 4x), and only preserve time in the deeper layers. Additionally, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 128 to 32 to further reduce memory pressure.
