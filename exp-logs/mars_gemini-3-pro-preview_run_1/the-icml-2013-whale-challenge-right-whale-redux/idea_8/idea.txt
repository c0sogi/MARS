Based on the comprehensive history of experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Coordinate Attention Time-Preserving ResNet-18 CRNN**.

This solution introduces a **Structural Innovation** by integrating **Coordinate Attention (CA)** mechanisms directly into the ResNet backbone. This design synthesizes the robustness of the ResNet architecture (Lesson 00031) with the feature-selection capabilities sought in the failed EfficientNet experiments (Idea 5), while explicitly preserving the temporal and spectral structure of the audio data.

### Model

The architecture is a **Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network (CRNN)** enhanced with axis-aware attention mechanisms.

1.  **Backbone: Time-Preserving ResNet-18 with Coordinate Attention**
    *   **Base Architecture**: We retain the **ResNet-18** backbone, as it has proven structurally robust for this task compared to brittle NAS-optimized architectures like EfficientNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Structural Innovation (Coordinate Attention)**: We replace the standard Residual Blocks with **Coordinate Attention Blocks**. Unlike standard Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) blocks—which pool the entire feature map into a single vector, losing spatial context—Coordinate Attention factorizes the attention operation into two parallel 1D feature encoding processes:
        *   **Vertical Pooling (Frequency Attention)**: Aggregates features along the frequency axis to generate a time-aware attention vector. This allows the model to highlight specific time segments *within the backbone*.
        *   **Horizontal Pooling (Time Attention)**: Aggregates features along the time axis to generate a frequency-aware attention vector. This allows the model to selectively emphasize specific frequency bands (e.g., the 50-250Hz whale call range) while suppressing noise bands.
    *   **Time-Preservation**: We strictly maintain the **Asymmetric Strides `(2, 1)`** (Frequency, Time) in the final two residual stages (Layers 3 and 4). This ensures the temporal sequence length remains sufficient (~50-60 steps) for the downstream RNN to detect the "up-sweep" motion, preventing the signal collapse observed in standard backbones (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   **Initialization**: Initialize with **ImageNet pre-trained weights**. Programmatically average the weights of the first convolutional layer (3 channels) to 1 channel to adapt to the spectrogram input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).

2.  **Temporal Modeling: Bi-Directional GRU**
    *   The output feature maps are pooled along the frequency dimension and permuted to a sequence format $(T \times C)$. This sequence is fed into a **Bi-directional GRU**, retaining the component proven most effective for modeling the specific temporal contour of the right whale call (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

3.  **Aggregation: Attention Pooling**
    *   We utilize an **Attention Pooling** layer (instead of Global Max Pooling) to compute a weighted sum of the GRU outputs. This focuses the final classification decision on the specific timeframe containing the call.

### Data

1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Input Window**: Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms covering the **full 2.0-second duration** of the clip. Do not truncate to 1.0s, ensuring the signal is never discarded (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Spectrogram Parameters**: Use **128 Mel bins** to provide sufficient frequency resolution for the Coordinate Attention to discriminate between signal and noise bands.
    *   **Channel Format**: Use a **1-channel** input representation. Do not repeat the spectrogram to 3 channels, as this increases computational cost without adding information (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).

2.  **Augmentation**:
    *   **Mixup**: Apply Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$) to training batches to regularize the model and accelerate convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **SpecAugment**: Apply Frequency and Time Masking.
    *   **Constraint**: Strictly limit the **Time Mask width to a maximum of 200ms** (approx. 20 frames). This ensures the augmentation does not occlude the entire short-duration whale call, preventing the introduction of label noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training

1.  **Loss Function**: Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss**.
    *   **Class Weighting**: Apply a **Positive Class Weight** (e.g., 9.0) to explicitly handle the 1:9 class imbalance. This corrects the "Loss Misalignment" failure observed in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Target Handling**: Compute loss against the **mixed soft targets** generated by Mixup.

2.  **Optimizer**: Use **Adam** with a standard learning rate (e.g., 1e-3).

3.  **Scheduler**: Implement **ReduceLROnPlateau** to decay the learning rate when Validation AUC stagnates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

4.  **Metric Validity**: Monitor **Validation AUC** on unmixed data. Do not rely on training metrics computed against hard labels during Mixup, as they are mathematically invalid (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

### Evaluation

1.  **Inference**: Process test clips into 2.0s Log-Mel Spectrograms (128 bins).
2.  **Prediction**: Pass inputs through the Coordinate Attention CRNN model.
3.  **Output**: Apply a Sigmoid activation to the final output of the Attention Pooling layer to generate the probability `p` for the submission file.
