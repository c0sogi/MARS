The `AttributeError: 'SelectiveKernelBasic' object has no attribute 'bn1'` occurs because the `CABasicBlock` wrapper unconditionally attempts to access `bn1` and `act1`, assuming a standard ResNet `BasicBlock` structure. However, the `skresnet18` backbone uses `SelectiveKernelBasic` blocks where the first convolution (`conv1`) encapsulates the normalization and activation logic, meaning `bn1` and `act1` are not exposed as separate attributes. To resolve this, I will modify the `forward` method in `library/model.py` to conditionally apply `bn1` and `act1` only if they exist on the underlying block. This change targets the specific structural differences of the backbone (Cite debug_lesson_7) and resolves the mismatch signaled by the missing attribute (Cite debug_lesson_6).
