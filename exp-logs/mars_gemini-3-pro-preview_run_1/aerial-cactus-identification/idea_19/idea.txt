Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous iterations and the extracted lessons, I propose a solution that structurally evolves the **Heterogeneous Stacking** approach by implementing a **Chromatic-Quality Decomposition Strategy**. This approach addresses the "Data Quality" bottleneck (compression artifacts) by explicitly decoupling the input signal into "Texture/Structure" (susceptible to noise) and "Color" (robust to noise) streams, while retaining the proven quality-aware modulation mechanisms.

# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Chromatic-Quality Stacking Ensemble**.

### 1. Domain-Specialized Base Learners
Instead of training multiple models on the same RGB inputs, the ensemble will consist of three "Specialists" that consume distinct views of the data. This enforces feature diversity and allows specific architectures to focus on signal components (texture vs. color) that are differentially affected by the identified compression artifacts **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.

1.  **The Structural Specialist (CactusRepVGG-L):**
    *   **Input:** A 2-channel tensor consisting of the **Luminance (L)** channel (from CIELAB space) and the **Laplacian Edge Map**.
    *   **Rationale:** This model focuses purely on high-frequency texture (spines) and structural edges. Since JPEG compression artifacts primarily manifest as high-frequency noise in the luminance channel, this specialist is the most sensitive to quality issues and will benefit most from the quality-aware mechanisms.
    *   **Backbone:** **RepVGG** (re-parameterizable), chosen for its efficiency in processing texture-heavy inputs **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
2.  **The Chromatic Specialist (CactusResNet-AB):**
    *   **Input:** A 2-channel tensor consisting of the **A and B** channels (from CIELAB space).
    *   **Rationale:** This model focuses purely on color information (vegetation green vs. ground brown). Chrominance channels are typically subsampled during compression but contain less high-frequency "blocking" noise. This specialist provides a robust, low-noise signal that complements the noisy structural view.
    *   **Backbone:** **ResNet**, chosen for its robust residual learning of abstract color features.
3.  **The Holistic Specialist (CactusNeXt-RGB):**
    *   **Input:** Standard **RGB** images.
    *   **Rationale:** Provides a baseline holistic view.
    *   **Backbone:** **CactusNeXt-3x3**. This is a modernized architecture (Depthwise Conv, LayerNorm, GELU) that strictly enforces **3x3 kernels** to avoid the signal blurring observed with standard 7x7 ConvNeXt models on 32x32 images **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.

### 2. Universal Structural Components
All three specialists will incorporate the following proven components:
*   **Conservative Downsampling:** All stems will use a 3x3 convolution with stride 1 and no pooling to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Deep Quality Awareness (FiLM + MTL):**
    *   **FiLM:** Every block will be equipped with **Feature-wise Linear Modulation** layers. A shared MLP will project the normalized **Image File Size** into scale and shift parameters to modulate feature maps, allowing the model to dynamically suppress noisy texture filters in highly compressed images **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00035)**.
    *   **MTL:** An **Auxiliary Regression Head** will be trained to predict the log-transformed file size, forcing the backbone to explicitly encode quality-related features **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00045)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** The entire dataset will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors to eliminate I/O latency **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Color Space Conversion:** Images will be converted to CIELAB space on-the-fly or pre-calculated.
    *   **Edge Extraction:** Laplacian maps will be computed for the Structural Specialist.
    *   **Metadata:** Image file sizes will be extracted, Z-score normalized (for FiLM), and log-transformed/scaled (for MTL targets).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip** to exploit rotational invariance **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** **Mild Mixup** ($\alpha=0.2$) applied to inputs and targets to close the generalization gap **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004, solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

# Training
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** will be performed for each of the 3 specialists (15 models total). This generates unbiased Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions for the meta-learner **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** will be used for effective weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing** for **30 epochs** per fold. This duration ensures sufficient convergence for Mixup **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** Activated in the final 5 epochs of training to locate flat minima and improve robustness without the computational cost of SAM **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.
*   **Loss Function:** A weighted Multi-Task Loss: $L_{total} = L_{BCE}(class) + \lambda L_{MSE}(quality)$.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, the RepVGG-L models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing the convolutional branches into single layers while retaining the FiLM modulation **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:**
    1.  **TTA:** Each of the 15 trained models will generate predictions using **4-view Test Time Augmentation** (original, horizontal flip, vertical flip, 180-degree rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Stacking:** The aggregated OOF predictions from the Structural, Chromatic, and Holistic specialists, concatenated with the file size metadata, will be fed into a **Logistic Regression Meta-Learner** to produce the final probability. This hierarchical approach leverages the decomposed signal streams to maximize robustness against data quality variations **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
