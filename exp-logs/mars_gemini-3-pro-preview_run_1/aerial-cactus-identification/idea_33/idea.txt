Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, specifically the validation of **Heterogeneous Stacking** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033), the power of **Test Time Augmentation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002), and the critical invalidation of "Metadata/Auxiliary Trust Scores" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00072), I propose a solution that constructs a **Heterogeneous Geometric-Consistency Stacking Ensemble**.

This approach replaces the failed "File Size Trust Score" with an **Intrinsic Stability Score** derived from the variance of TTA predictions. It leverages the insight that if a model is robust, its predictions should be consistent across geometric transformations, allowing the meta-learner to discount "unstable" predictions without relying on uncorrelated auxiliary tasks.

# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Stacking Ensemble** composed of two distinct backbone architectures to maximize feature diversity while adhering to strict resolution constraints.

### 1. Diverse Base Learners
The ensemble will consist of two architectures that learn different feature hierarchies (VGG-style vs. Residual-style).
1.  **CactusRepVGG:** A re-parameterizable architecture using multi-branch blocks (3x3 + 1x1 + Identity) during training for stability, fused into single-path 3x3 convolutions for inference (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
2.  **CactusResNet:** A standard residual network backbone providing a stable baseline.

**Universal Structural Constraints:**
Both architectures will strictly adhere to the following design principles:
*   **Conservative Downsampling:** All stems will use a single 3x3 convolution with stride 1 and **no pooling** to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Small Kernel Constraint:** All convolutions will strictly use **3x3 kernels** to avoid the signal blurring observed with larger kernels (e.g., 7x7) on small thumbnail images (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Deep Supervision:** Both architectures will feature an **Auxiliary Classification Head** attached after the intermediate stage. This enforces discriminative feature learning early in the network, aiding convergence without the distraction of uncorrelated auxiliary tasks like file size regression (Cite solution_lesson_node_00072).

### 2. Structural Innovation: Geometric-Consistency Meta-Features
Instead of injecting metadata (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054) or using uncorrelated auxiliary errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00072), the Meta-Learner will be fed **Geometric Consistency Features**.
*   **Mechanism:** During inference (and OOF generation), each base model generates predictions for 4 TTA views (Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, 180 Rotation) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Feature Extraction:** For each image and each model, we calculate two values:
    1.  **Prediction Mean ($\mu$):** The standard averaged probability.
    2.  **Prediction Standard Deviation ($\sigma$):** A proxy for the model's uncertainty regarding that specific image's geometry.
*   **Rationale:** A high $\sigma$ indicates the model is unstable under rotation (likely reacting to noise/artifacts). The Meta-Learner can learn to down-weight models with high $\sigma$ for specific samples, effectively implementing a "Geometric Trust Score."

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** The entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors to eliminate I/O latency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Stateless Execution:** To avoid the catastrophic failure observed in previous runs, the pipeline will explicitly **disable caching of checkpoints or intermediate features** between runs, ensuring a clean, end-to-end execution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip** will be applied during training to exploit rotational invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Regularization:** **Mild Mixup** ($\alpha=0.2$) will be applied to the classification labels to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

# Training
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** will be performed for each of the 2 architectures (10 models total). This generates the unbiased Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions required to train the Meta-Learner (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** will be used for effective weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be employed for **30 epochs** per fold. This duration ensures sufficient convergence for Mixup (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** SWA will be activated during the final 10 epochs (using a cyclic learning rate) to locate flat minima in the loss landscape. This improves generalization robustness without the 2x computational cost of SAM (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Loss Function:** A weighted sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from the Main Head and the Auxiliary Classification Head ($L_{total} = L_{main} + 0.4 \times L_{aux}$).

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, the RepVGG models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their multi-branch blocks into efficient single-path networks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Inference Strategy:**
    1.  **TTA Generation:** Each of the 10 trained models generates predictions for 4 views per test image (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    2.  **Feature Construction:** For each model, compute the Mean ($\mu$) and Standard Deviation ($\sigma$) across the 4 views.
    3.  **Stacking:** Concatenate these features (20 total: 10 Means + 10 Stds) and feed them into a **Logistic Regression Meta-Learner** (trained on OOF data) to produce the final calibrated probability.
