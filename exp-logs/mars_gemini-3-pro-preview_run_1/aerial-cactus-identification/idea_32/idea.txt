Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, specifically the validation of **Heterogeneous Stacking** (Lesson 00033), the power of **Test Time Augmentation** (Lesson 00002), and the critical invalidation of "Metadata/Auxiliary Trust Scores" (Lesson 00072), I propose a solution that constructs a **Heterogeneous TTA-Stability Stacking Ensemble**.

This approach replaces the failed "File Size Trust Score" with an **Intrinsic Stability Score** derived from the variance of TTA predictions. It leverages the insight that if a model is robust, its predictions should be consistent across geometric transformations.

# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Stacking Ensemble** composed of three distinct backbone architectures to maximize feature diversity.

### 1. Diverse Base Learners
To ensure the ensemble captures different "views" of the data (e.g., spatial vs. depthwise features), I will employ three architectures. Crucially, all three will strictly adhere to the **Conservative Downsampling** and **Small Kernel** constraints to fit the 32x32 resolution.

1.  **CactusRepVGG:** A re-parameterizable architecture using multi-branch blocks (3x3 + 1x1 + Identity) during training for stability, fused into single-path 3x3 convolutions for inference **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
2.  **CactusResNet:** A standard residual network backbone providing a stable baseline.
3.  **CactusMicroNeXt:** A modernized backbone inspired by ConvNeXt (Depthwise Conv, LayerNorm, GELU) but strictly adapting the design to use **3x3 kernels** instead of 7x7. This avoids the signal blurring observed with large kernels on small images while providing the inductive bias of inverted bottlenecks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.

**Universal Structural Constraints:**
*   **Conservative Downsampling:** All stems will use a single 3x3 convolution with stride 1 and **no pooling** to strictly preserve the 32x32 spatial resolution **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Deep Supervision:** All architectures will feature an **Auxiliary Classification Head** attached after the intermediate stage. This enforces discriminative feature learning early in the network, aiding convergence without the distraction of uncorrelated auxiliary tasks like file size regression **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020, solution_lesson_node_00072)**.

### 2. Structural Innovation: TTA-Stability Meta-Features
Instead of injecting metadata (Lesson 00054) or using uncorrelated auxiliary errors (Lesson 00072), the Meta-Learner will be fed **Intrinsic Stability Features**.
*   **Mechanism:** During inference, each base model generates predictions for 4 TTA views (Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, 180 Rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
*   **Feature Extraction:** For each image and each model, we calculate two values:
    1.  **Prediction Mean ($\mu$):** The standard averaged probability.
    2.  **Prediction Standard Deviation ($\sigma$):** A proxy for the model's uncertainty regarding that specific image's geometry.
*   **Rationale:** A high $\sigma$ indicates the model is unstable under rotation (likely reacting to noise/artifacts). The Meta-Learner can learn to down-weight models with high $\sigma$ for specific samples, effectively implementing a "Geometric Trust Score."

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** The entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors to eliminate I/O latency **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip** will be applied during training to exploit rotational invariance **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** **Mild Mixup** ($\alpha=0.2$) will be applied to the classification labels to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004)**.

# Training
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** will be performed for each of the 3 architectures (15 models total). This generates the unbiased Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions required to train the Meta-Learner **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** will be used for effective weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be employed for **30 epochs** per fold. This duration ensures sufficient convergence for Mixup **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** SWA will be activated during the final 10 epochs (using a cyclic learning rate) to locate flat minima in the loss landscape. This improves generalization robustness without the 2x computational cost of SAM **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.
*   **Loss Function:** A weighted sum of **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits** from the Main Head and the Auxiliary Head ($L_{total} = L_{main} + 0.4 \times L_{aux}$).

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, the RepVGG models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their multi-branch blocks into efficient single-path networks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:**
    1.  **TTA Generation:** Each of the 15 trained models generates predictions for 4 views per test image **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Feature Construction:** For each model, compute the Mean ($\mu$) and Standard Deviation ($\sigma$) across the 4 views.
    3.  **Stacking:** Concatenate these features (30 total: 15 Means + 15 Stds) and feed them into a **Logistic Regression Meta-Learner** (trained on OOF data) to produce the final calibrated probability.
