Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose a solution that synthesizes the robustness of **Heterogeneous Stacking**, the efficiency of **Structural Re-parameterization**, and the novel stability metric of **Geometric Consistency**.

This strategy explicitly rejects "Metadata Injection" and "Auxiliary Quality Regression" (which were proven redundant or uncorrelated in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00054** and **Cite solution_lesson_node_00072**). Instead, it focuses on constructing a diverse ensemble of pure vision models and weighing them based on their intrinsic stability under geometric transformation.

# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Geometric-Consistency Stacking Ensemble**.

### 1. Diverse Micro-Scale Base Learners
The ensemble will consist of three distinct backbone architectures to ensure diversity in feature representation (e.g., spatial vs. depthwise). Crucially, all three will be structurally adapted to the 32x32 resolution constraints.

1.  **CactusRepVGG:** A re-parameterizable architecture using multi-branch blocks (3x3 + 1x1 + Identity) during training for stability, fused into single-path 3x3 convolutions for inference **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
2.  **CactusResNet:** A standard residual network backbone providing a stable baseline.
3.  **CactusMicroNeXt:** A structural innovation adapting the modern ConvNeXt design. It retains inverted bottlenecks, depthwise convolutions, Layer Normalization, and GELU activations, but strictly enforces **3x3 kernels** instead of the standard 7x7. This avoids the signal blurring observed with large kernels on small images while providing the inductive bias of modern vision transformers **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.

**Universal Structural Constraints:**
All architectures will strictly adhere to **Conservative Downsampling**: stems will use a single 3x3 convolution with stride 1 and **no pooling**. This strictly preserves the 32x32 spatial resolution entering the first stage, preventing the signal destruction caused by aggressive downsampling **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.

### 2. Meta-Learner: Geometric Consistency Features
Instead of raw probabilities, the Meta-Learner (Logistic Regression) will be trained on **Geometric Consistency Features** derived from Test-Time Augmentation (TTA).
*   **Feature Extraction:** For each image and each base model, we calculate the **Mean ($\mu$)** and **Standard Deviation ($\sigma$)** of the predictions across 4 geometric views.
*   **Rationale:** The Standard Deviation acts as an intrinsic "Trust Score." A high $\sigma$ indicates the model is unstable under rotation (reacting to noise/artifacts), allowing the meta-learner to discount it without relying on uncorrelated auxiliary tasks like file size regression **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00079)**.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:** The entire dataset (train and test) will be decoded and **cached in RAM** as floating-point tensors to eliminate I/O latency **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)**.
*   **Stateless Execution:** The pipeline will explicitly **disable caching of checkpoints or intermediate features** between runs. This ensures a clean, end-to-end execution and prevents the corruption observed when loading stale states **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00077)**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** **RandomHorizontalFlip** and **RandomVerticalFlip** will be applied during training to exploit rotational invariance **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    *   **Regularization:** **Mild Mixup** ($\alpha=0.2$) will be applied to the classification labels to close the generalization gap without drowning out the signal **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00004)**.

# Training
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** will be performed for each of the 3 architectures (15 models total). This generates the unbiased Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions required to train the Meta-Learner **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00033)**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** will be used for effective weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be employed for **30 epochs** per fold. This duration ensures sufficient convergence for Mixup **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00003)**.
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** SWA will be activated during the final 10 epochs (using a cyclic learning rate) to locate flat minima in the loss landscape. This improves generalization robustness without the 2x computational cost of SAM **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00029)**.
*   **Loss Function:** **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits**.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Fusion:** Before inference, the RepVGG models will undergo **Structural Re-parameterization**, fusing their multi-branch blocks into efficient single-path networks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
*   **Inference Strategy:**
    1.  **TTA Generation:** Each of the 15 trained models generates predictions for 4 views per test image (Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, 180 Rotation) **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.
    2.  **Feature Construction:** For each model, compute the Mean ($\mu$) and Standard Deviation ($\sigma$) across the 4 views.
    3.  **Stacking:** Concatenate these features (30 total: 15 Means + 15 Stds) and feed them into a **Logistic Regression Meta-Learner** (trained on OOF data) to produce the final calibrated probability **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00079)**.
