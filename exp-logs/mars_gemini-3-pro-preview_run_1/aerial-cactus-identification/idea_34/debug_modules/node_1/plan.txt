When loading these checkpoints into a standard, unwrapped model instance in `library/stacking.py`, PyTorch fails to match the keys. To fix this, I will modify `load_checkpoint` in `library/utils.py` to sanitize the state dictionary by stripping the `module.` prefix and removing the `n_averaged` key before loading, as recommended in Cite debug_lesson_11.
