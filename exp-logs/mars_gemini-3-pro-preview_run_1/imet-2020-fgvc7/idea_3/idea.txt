Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, I propose an optimized strategy that evolves the architecture to a higher-capacity backbone while integrating robust training techniques to handle label noise and sparsity.

### Model
I propose upgrading the backbone architecture to **ConvNeXt-Small**.
*   **Architecture:** The transition from ConvNeXt-Tiny to **ConvNeXt-Small** increases the model's depth and width, providing the necessary capacity to disentangle the complex correlations among 3,474 attributes. This addresses the need for higher capacity when scaling performance, as observed with ResNet50 (Cite Lesson 7).
*   **Pooling:** I will retain the **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** layer at the head. This component is critical for handling the mixed dataset of "centered objects" and "scenic rooms" by dynamically focusing on salient feature regions rather than averaging background noise (Cite Lesson 9).
*   **Robustness:** I will implement **Model EMA (Exponential Moving Average)**. By maintaining a "shadow" model whose weights are a slowly decaying average of the training weights, we create a temporal ensemble. This is structurally more robust to the "noisy" and "unverified" annotations than the raw weights from the final epoch.

### Data
*   **Resolution:** I will maintain the input resolution of **320x320**. This resolution was proven to provide a significant performance boost over 224x224 by preserving fine artistic details (Cite Lesson 9).
*   **Pipeline:** I will strictly enforce the optimized data loading configuration (`num_workers=4`, `pin_memory=True`) to ensure the GPU remains saturated, which is vital for training the larger ConvNeXt-Small backbone efficiently (Cite Lesson 4).
*   **Augmentation:** I will apply the aggressive augmentation strategy (including `ShiftScaleRotate` and `ColorJitter`) established previously. This is essential to regularize the increased capacity of the ConvNeXt-Small model and prevent overfitting (Cite Lesson 7).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** I will use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** with a **moderate positive weight (e.g., 10-12)** to handle the extreme class sparsity without causing precision collapse (Cite Lesson 1, 3). Additionally, I will retain **Label Smoothing (0.05)** to prevent the model from becoming overconfident in the noisy ground truth labels (Cite Lesson 9).
*   **Optimization:** I will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Duration:** Leveraging the 24-hour runtime limit, I will extend the training duration to **15-20 epochs**. This ensures the heavier ConvNeXt-Small model has sufficient time to converge, avoiding the "undertraining" pitfall observed when complexity was increased without adjusting the training budget (Cite Lesson 8).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the **EMA weights**, which typically yield better generalization than the final training weights.
*   **Threshold Tuning:** I will perform a grid search for the optimal probability threshold on the validation set, strictly searching the **full range (0.01 to 0.99)**. This ensures the selected threshold is mathematically optimal for the weighted loss configuration and is not artificially limited by a narrow search window (Cite Lesson 6).
