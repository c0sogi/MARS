# Model
**Architecture:** **EfficientNet-B2 with Attention Pooling**
- **Backbone:** Replace the ResNet34 backbone with **EfficientNet-B2**. EfficientNet uses inverted residual blocks and compound scaling, offering a more parameter-efficient hierarchy for feature extraction compared to standard ResNets. We will initialize this with **ImageNet pretrained weights** to leverage transfer learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
- **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer will be modified to accept 1-channel input (spectrograms). Crucially, a **learnable Batch Normalization layer** will be inserted immediately before the backbone to adapt the statistics of the audio spectrograms to the range expected by the pretrained weights, preventing the training instability observed previously (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
- **Aggregation Head (Structural Innovation):** Instead of the Global Max Pooling used in the best previous solution, we will implement **Attention Pooling**.
    - *Rationale:* While Max Pooling outperformed Average Pooling by focusing on sparse events (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005), it is a "hard" selection that discards context. Attention Pooling treats the output feature map as a sequence of time steps (a "bag" of instances). It learns a weighting mask to emphasize time steps containing relevant sound events and suppress background noise. This acts as a soft **Multiple Instance Learning (MIL)** mechanism, directly mitigating the label noise introduced by short crops (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) by allowing the model to dynamically focus on the signal.

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
- **Spectrograms:** Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms with **128 Mel bins** (increased from 64 to capture more frequency detail) and a standard hop length (e.g., 320 samples).
- **Training Input:** Continue to use **random 5-second crops** for training. This maximizes signal density and acts as implicit augmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

**Augmentation:**
- **Mixup:** Apply Mixup augmentation (interpolating inputs and labels) with a high probability (e.g., 0.5 - 1.0), as this has been proven to significantly boost performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
- **SpecAugment (New):** Introduce SpecAugment as a distinct feature engineering paradigm. This involves randomly masking vertical strips (time masking) and horizontal strips (frequency masking) of the spectrogram. This forces the model to learn robust features and not rely on specific time-frequency artifacts.

# Training
- **Loss Function:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` will be used to handle the multi-label nature of the task.
- **Optimizer:** Use **AdamW** with a weight decay (e.g., 1e-2) to prevent overfitting, coupled with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler to converge efficiently.
- **Strategy:** Train on the combined dataset (Curated + Noisy). The Attention Pooling mechanism allows the model to better handle the "Noisy" dataset by learning to down-weight sections of audio that do not match the label features.

# Evaluation
- **Inference Strategy:** Unlike the training phase which uses crops, inference will be performed on the **full-length audio clips**.
    - *Mechanism:* Since the EfficientNet backbone is fully convolutional and the Attention Pooling head aggregates over the time dimension regardless of its length, the model can process the entire ~13s clip in a single forward pass. This ensures no sound events are missed (addressing the crop limitation from Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) while maintaining the efficiency benefits of crop-based training.
