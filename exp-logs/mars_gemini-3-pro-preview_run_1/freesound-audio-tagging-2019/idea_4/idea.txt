Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the distilled lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution synthesizes the architectural strengths of the "Pure CNN" approach (Idea 2) with the robust data adaptation and regularization strategies of the "CRNN" approach (Idea 3), while discarding the detrimental recurrent components and scaling up the backbone capacity.

# Model
**Architecture:** **EfficientNet-B3 with Input Repetition and Attention Pooling**

*   **Backbone:** Upgrade the backbone to **EfficientNet-B3** initialized with **ImageNet pretrained weights** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004). This provides a "Structural Innovation" by increasing the model's capacity and receptive field compared to the B2 variant used previously, allowing for the capture of more complex spectral features without the computational cost of the largest models.
*   **Input Adaptation Strategy:** Unlike Idea 2 which modified the first layer, we will use **Input Repetition**. The single-channel Log-Mel spectrogram will be repeated three times along the channel dimension to match the 3-channel RGB input expected by the pretrained backbone. This preserves the original filter initialization and structural integrity of the pretrained network, which has been proven to yield higher final performance than weight modification (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Normalization:** Prepend a **learnable Batch Normalization layer** (2D) before the backbone. This ensures the statistics of the audio spectrograms are dynamically adapted to the distribution expected by the ImageNet weights, preventing training instability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Aggregation Head:** Use **Attention Pooling** directly after the convolutional backbone.
    *   *Crucial Design Choice:* We explicitly **exclude** any Recurrent Neural Network (RNN/GRU) layers. Previous experiments demonstrated that adding a BiGRU (Idea 3) degraded performance and slowed convergence compared to a "Pure CNN" (Idea 2). Attention Pooling alone is sufficient to aggregate temporal features and acts as a soft Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) mechanism to handle weak labels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010, Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Spectrograms:** Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms with **128 Mel bins** to capture high-fidelity frequency details.
*   **Training Input:** Perform **random 5-second crops** on the audio data. This serves as implicit data augmentation and increases signal density within the training batches (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

**Augmentation:**
*   **SpecAugment:** Apply time and frequency masking to the spectrograms to force the model to learn robust features rather than relying on specific artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Continuous Mixup:** Apply Mixup augmentation to **100% of training batches** (probability 1.0) with a Beta distribution ($\alpha=0.4$). This continuous regularization prevents memorization and improves generalization, provided the training schedule is sufficiently long (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

# Training
*   **Strategy:** Train on the combined dataset (Curated + Noisy).
*   **Loss Function:** Use `BCEWithLogitsLoss` for multi-label classification.
*   **Optimizer:** Use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing learning rate scheduler.
*   **Duration:** significantly extend the training schedule (e.g., to **25-30 epochs**). The use of continuous Mixup (100%) makes the optimization task harder and delays convergence; therefore, a longer runtime is required to overcome the underfitting phase and reach the model's higher performance ceiling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference Process:** Perform inference on the **full-length audio clips** (approx. 13s) rather than crops.
    *   Since the model is fully convolutional with a global Attention Pooling head, it can process variable-length inputs in a single forward pass. This ensures no sound events are missed due to cropping, while the Attention mechanism automatically focuses on the relevant segments of the longer clip (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001, Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
