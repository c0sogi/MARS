The strategy involves optimizing the data loading and training schedule to address the "false-positive label noise" issue identified in `solution_lesson_node_00001`. By increasing the `TRAIN_DURATION` from 5 seconds to 10 seconds, the model will see a larger portion of the audio file (mean duration ~13.4s) during training, increasing the likelihood that the global labels are actually present in the input crop. To accommodate the larger input tensors within GPU memory constraints, the `BATCH_SIZE` is reduced to 32. The `EPOCHS` are increased to 35 to allow sufficient convergence time for the larger inputs and continuous Mixup regularization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`). `SPEC_AUG_TIME_MASK` is scaled up to maintain efficacy on longer clips.
