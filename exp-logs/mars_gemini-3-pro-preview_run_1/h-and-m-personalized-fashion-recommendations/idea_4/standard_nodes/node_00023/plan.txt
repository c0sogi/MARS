I will optimize the solution by removing the Visual Similarity component, as empirical evidence indicates it introduces noise and degrades performance compared to a pure Behavioral/Repurchase cascade (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}). I will also increase the number of Collaborative Filtering neighbors from 50 to 60 to better capture long-tail signals (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}). The solution will rely on a strict cascade of Repurchase -> Behavioral Co-occurrence -> Global Trends (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}).
