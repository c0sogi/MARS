The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurred during the MLM phase because the large vocabulary of `deberta-v3-base` combined with a batch size of 16 requires a logits tensor that exceeds the available VRAM (approx. 16GB). To resolve this, I will reduce `mlm_batch_size` from 16 to 4 in `library/config.py`. I will also reduce the supervised `batch_size` from 16 to 8 to ensure sufficient memory is available for the additional overhead of Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP) in later epochs (Cite debug_lesson_3). Furthermore, I will update the masking constants in `library/model.py` from `-1e9` to `-1e4` to prevent potential numerical overflows in `float16` when mixed precision is used (Cite debug_lesson_1).
