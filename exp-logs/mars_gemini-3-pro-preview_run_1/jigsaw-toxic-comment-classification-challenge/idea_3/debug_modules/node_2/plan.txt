The `RuntimeError` is caused by the use of `-1e9` as a masking value, which exceeds the representable range of half-precision (FP16) floating-point numbers used during mixed-precision training. To fix this, I will replace `-1e9` with `-1e4`, which fits within the FP16 range while remaining sufficiently small to effectively act as negative infinity for softmax and max-pooling operations (Cite debug_lesson_2). I will apply this correction to both the `LinearAttentionPooling` module and the global max pooling logic in `ToxicityModel`.
