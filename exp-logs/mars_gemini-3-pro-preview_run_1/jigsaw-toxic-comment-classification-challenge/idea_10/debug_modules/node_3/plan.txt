The solution addresses the `torch.OutOfMemoryError` and potential FP16 overflow issues by implementing three key changes:

1.  **Mixed Precision Training (AMP):** I will modify `library/engine.py` and `runfile.py` to incorporate `torch.cuda.amp.autocast` and `GradScaler`. This significantly reduces memory usage by performing operations in half-precision where safe. Cite {debug_lesson_10}.
2.  **FP16-Safe Masking:** I will update `library/model.py` to replace the hardcoded `-1e9` masking constants with `-1e4`. This prevents numerical overflow errors when the model runs in mixed precision. Cite {debug_lesson_8}.
3.  **Resource Management:** In `runfile.py`, I will reduce the batch size to 4 and temporarily offload the `ToxicModel` to the CPU during the `run_mlm` execution. This prevents the GPU from holding two large model instances simultaneously (Double Model Allocation). Cite {debug_lesson_10}.
