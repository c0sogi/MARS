    - Save the backbone weights (the encoder) to transfer to the supervised stages.

    - Train on the labeled training subset using `train_fn` (includes Deep Supervision and AWP).

5.  **Stage 3: Student Self-Distillation**:
    - Create a combined dataset of Hard-Labeled Train data and Soft-Labeled Test data.
    - Initialize a fresh `DeepSupervisedModel` (Student) with the DAPT backbone.
    - Train the Student on this combined dataset.
6.  **Validation & Analysis**:
    - Evaluate the Student model on the full hold-out validation set.
    - Print the `Final Validation Metric`.
