The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the batch size of 32 is too large for the DeBERTa-v3-base model on the available GPU, specifically due to the memory-intensive disentangled attention mechanism. To fix this, I will decouple the VRAM constraints from the batch size using Gradient Accumulation (Cite debug_lesson_5). I will reduce the physical `mlm_batch_size` to 4 and introduce `mlm_grad_accum = 8` to maintain the effective batch size of 32. I will also reduce the supervised `batch_size` to 4 to prevent OOM in the second stage, although gradient accumulation will not be applied there to avoid conflicts with AWP. The `train_mlm` function in `library/engine.py` will be updated to accumulate gradients over multiple steps before performing an optimizer update.
