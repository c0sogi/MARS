The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU VRAM (approximately 16GB) was exhausted by the large activation maps generated from a `BATCH_SIZE` of 32 combined with high-resolution inputs (`256x1280`). To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 32 to 16, which will significantly decrease the memory footprint per batch and allow the model to fit within the available hardware limits.
