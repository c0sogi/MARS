Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the **Scale-Invariant High-Fidelity Ensemble Distillation with Multi-Scale TTA**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation—**Dynamic Temporal Scale Jittering**—integrated into a high-fidelity distillation pipeline. It addresses the limitations of fixed-resolution training (Idea 11) and the failures of multi-resolution ensembles (Idea 9) by enforcing scale invariance through continuous dynamic resizing within the optimal "high-fidelity" band. This is coupled with **Multi-Scale Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** for pseudo-label generation to maximize target quality.

### Model
*   **Backbone:** Utilize the **Vanilla ResNet-34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* This backbone is retained as the optimal choice for this micro-dataset. It significantly outperforms MLPs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006** and avoids the suppression of co-occurring classes observed with SE-ResNet architectures in multi-label tasks **Cite solution_lesson_node_00056**.
    *   *Homogeneity:* Both Teachers and Student will use this architecture to ensure capacity alignment **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.
*   **Classification Head:** Use a standard **Linear Layer** projecting to the 19 species classes. Strictly avoid complex aggregation heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007** and structural noise like Head Dropout **Cite solution_lesson_node_00063**.
*   **Input Adaptation:** Use **Channel Replication** (copying the single-channel spectrogram to R, G, and B inputs) to preserve natural image statistics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.

### Data
*   **Source:** `supplemental_data/spectrograms`.
*   **Preprocessing (Structural Innovation):**
    *   **Vertical Dimension:** Fix height at **256 pixels** to strictly preserve native frequency resolution **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
    *   **Horizontal Dimension (Dynamic Jittering):** Instead of a fixed width, implement **Dynamic Temporal Jittering**. During training, randomly resize each batch to a width sampled from the range **[576, 704]**.
        *   *Rationale:* This centers on the optimal 640px high-fidelity width **Cite solution_lesson_node_00041** but forces the model to learn features robust to temporal scaling (speed variations in bird calls). It acts as a powerful dynamic augmentation, effectively expanding the dataset size **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**, while avoiding the low-resolution dilution that caused Idea 9 to fail **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.
    *   **Normalization:** Explicitly apply **ImageNet Mean and Standard Deviation normalization** to maintain the transfer learning contract **Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**.
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   **Dynamic Loading:** Implement purely dynamic in-memory loading without disk caching **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
    *   **Deterministic Seeding:** Explicitly pass a fixed seed to the NumPy `default_rng()` generator within augmentation classes to prevent hidden variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Unstructured Cutout:** Apply random rectangular masks **Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**.
    *   **Horizontal Flip:** Enable random time inversion **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
    *   **Conservative Mixup:** Apply Mixup with **alpha = 0.2** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**.

### Training
I will implement a **Single-Stage Distillation Pipeline** with extended schedules, avoiding the iterative regression observed in Idea 15 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00065**.

1.  **Stage 1: Scale-Invariant Teacher Ensemble**
    *   Train **3 independent ResNet-34 models** on the labeled training set (Fold 0).
    *   **Training Mode:** Apply **Dynamic Temporal Jittering [576, 704]** + Mixup + Cutout + Flips.
    *   **Schedule:** Train for **50 epochs** to ensure convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**.
    *   **SWA Protocol:** Activate **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** for the final 25% of epochs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**.
    *   **Safety:** Explicitly disable Early Stopping during the SWA phase **Cite solution_lesson_node_00050**.
    *   **Loss:** BCEWithLogitsLoss **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

2.  **Stage 2: Multi-Scale Pseudo-Labeling**
    *   Generate predictions for the Test Set (Fold 1) using the 3 Teachers.
    *   **Innovation (Multi-Scale TTA):** For each test image, generate predictions at three distinct widths **[608, 640, 672]** combined with **Horizontal Flips**. This results in 6 views per teacher.
    *   **Consensus:** Average the 6 views per teacher, then average across the 3 teachers. This produces extremely robust, scale-invariant soft targets.
    *   **Sanitize:** Assert no `NaN` values **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.

3.  **Stage 3: Scale-Invariant Student Training**
    *   Train a single **ResNet-34 Student** on the **Combined Dataset** (Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test).
    *   **Training Mode:** Apply **Dynamic Temporal Jittering [576, 704]** + Mixup + Cutout + Flips.
    *   **Schedule:** 50 epochs with SWA active in the final 30% **Cite solution_lesson_node_00029**.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess test images to the fixed center resolution of **256 x 640**, apply **ImageNet Normalization**, and perform a single forward pass using the final **SWA Student Model**.
*   **Output:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the logits to generate probabilities. This leverages the finding that a single model trained on maximal data outperforms cross-validation ensembles **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
