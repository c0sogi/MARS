Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the **Augmentation-Stratified ResNet-34 Ensemble Distillation with Temperature Scaling**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation in the **Ensemble Construction** and **Distillation Signal** phases. It addresses the limitations of Homogeneous Ensembles (identical errors) and Heterogeneous Ensembles (capacity mismatch) by inducing diversity through **Augmentation Stratification**—training identical architectures with distinct regularization policies. Furthermore, it incorporates **Temperature Scaling** to calibrate the pseudo-labels, ensuring the student learns robust class correlations ("dark knowledge") rather than overfitting to the teachers' overconfidence. This is integrated into the proven High-Fidelity SWA pipeline, strictly adhering to the stability constraints identified in the lessons.

# Model
*   **Backbone:** Utilize the **Standard ResNet-34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights.
    *   *Strategic Retention:* This backbone is retained as the optimal choice. It outperforms MLPs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**, avoids the suppression of co-occurring classes seen in SE-ResNet **Cite solution_lesson_node_00056**, and avoids the shift-invariance/weight-incompatibility issues of ResNet-34d/Anti-aliased variants **Cite solution_lesson_node_00079, solution_lesson_node_00081**.
    *   *Homogeneity:* Both Teachers and Student will use this identical architecture to ensure inductive bias alignment **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.
*   **Classification Head:** Use a standard **Linear Layer** projecting to the 19 species classes, strictly avoiding complex aggregation heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.
*   **Input Adaptation:** Use **Channel Replication** (copying the single-channel spectrogram to R, G, and B inputs) to preserve natural image statistics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.

# Data
*   **Source:** `supplemental_data/spectrograms`.
*   **Preprocessing (High-Fidelity Alignment):**
    *   **Resolution:** Resize images to **256 pixels (Height) x 640 pixels (Width)**. This resolution strictly preserves the native frequency resolution **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013** and captures the fine-grained temporal morphology required for high performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00041**.
    *   **Normalization:** Explicitly apply **ImageNet Mean and Standard Deviation normalization**. This is mandatory to uphold the transfer learning contract **Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**.
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   **Dynamic Loading:** Implement purely dynamic in-memory loading without disk caching to prevent stale data issues **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
    *   **Deterministic Seeding:** Explicitly pass a fixed seed to the NumPy `default_rng()` generator within augmentation classes to prevent hidden variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Unstructured Cutout:** Apply random rectangular masks **Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**.
    *   **Horizontal Flip:** Enable random time inversion **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
    *   **Input Mixup:** Applied with varying intensities (see Training) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**.

# Training
I will implement a **Single-Stage Homogeneous Ensemble Distillation Pipeline** with extended schedules.

**Stage 1: Augmentation-Stratified Teacher Ensemble (Structural Innovation)**
*   Train **3 independent ResNet-34 models** on the labeled training set (Fold 0).
*   **Stratification Strategy:** Instead of identical training configs, vary the regularization intensity to decorrelate errors while maintaining architectural homogeneity:
    *   **Teacher 1 (Texture-Focused):** High Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$), Low Cutout. Forces linearity and texture bias.
    *   **Teacher 2 (Feature-Focused):** Low Mixup ($\alpha=0.1$), High Unstructured Cutout. Forces robust feature detection under occlusion.
    *   **Teacher 3 (Balanced):** Standard Mixup ($\alpha=0.2$) and Cutout.
*   **Optimization:** Train for **50 epochs** with **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** active in the final 25% **Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**.
*   **Safety:** Disable Early Stopping during SWA **Cite solution_lesson_node_00050**.
*   **Loss:** BCEWithLogitsLoss **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

**Stage 2: Calibrated Pseudo-Label Generation**
*   Generate predictions for the Test Set (Fold 1) using the 3 Teachers.
*   **TTA:** Apply **Test-Time Augmentation** (Horizontal Flips).
*   **Temperature Scaling:** Divide logits by **$T=1.5$** before Sigmoid. This softens the distribution, preserving information about inter-class relationships and reducing the impact of teacher overconfidence.
*   **Ensemble Averaging:** Average the calibrated probabilities.
*   **Sanitize:** Assert no `NaN` values **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.

**Stage 3: Student Training**
*   Train a single **ResNet-34 Student** on the **Combined Dataset** (Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test).
*   **Config:** Use the "Balanced" augmentation policy ($\alpha=0.2$).
*   **Schedule:** 50 epochs with SWA active in the final 30% **Cite solution_lesson_node_00029**.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess test images to **256 x 640**, apply **ImageNet Normalization**, and perform a single forward pass using the final **SWA Student Model**.
*   **Output:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the logits to generate probabilities. This leverages the finding that a single model trained on maximal data outperforms cross-validation ensembles **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
