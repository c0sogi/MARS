Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the specific lessons learned, I propose the following **Spectral-Dynamic ResNet-34 with Self-Distillation** strategy. This approach introduces a structural innovation in feature engineering (Delta-Spectrograms) to capture temporal dynamics and employs a rigorous semi-supervised pipeline to address the critical data scarcity, while strictly adhering to the stability constraints identified in the lessons.

# Model
*   **Backbone:** Utilize a **ResNet-34** architecture initialized with ImageNet weights. This backbone has proven to be the optimal balance of capacity and regularizability for this dataset, outperforming both shallower networks and complex architectures like EfficientNet/GeM **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006, solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Input Adaptation (Structural Innovation):** Instead of the standard approach of replicating the single-channel grayscale spectrogram to 3 channels (R=G=B), I will construct a **semantically meaningful 3-channel input** to explicitly encode signal dynamics:
    *   **Channel 1:** Original Spectrogram (Static Intensity).
    *   **Channel 2:** Spectral Delta (First derivative along the time axis).
    *   **Channel 3:** Spectral Delta-Delta (Second derivative along the time axis).
    *   *Rationale:* Bird calls are defined by their temporal modulation (chirps, trills) distinct from stationary noise (wind, rain). By explicitly providing the gradients of the spectrogram, we give the model direct access to "motion" features, reducing the burden of learning these from scratch in a micro-data regime.
*   **Head:** A standard **Linear Layer** projecting to the 19 species classes. I will strictly avoid complex aggregation heads (like ConcatPooling) to minimize overfitting risks **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.

# Data
*   **Source:** `supplemental_data/spectrograms`.
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Frequency Preservation:** Resize images to a fixed height of **256 pixels**. This matches the native frequency resolution of the source files, preventing the loss of spectral lines caused by downsampling to 224px **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
    *   **Temporal Densification:** Resize images to a fixed width of **512 pixels**. This "Global Resize" strategy ensures the model sees the entire 10-second context **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**, while simultaneously compressing the sparse signals into a denser representation that is easier to learn than high-fidelity 1280px inputs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
    *   **Delta Computation:** Compute the Delta and Delta-Delta channels using simple finite differences on the resized spectrograms before normalization.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Conservative Mixup:** Apply Mixup with **alpha = 0.2**. This ensures synthetic samples remain predominantly one class, preserving distinct features **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.
    *   **SpecAugment:** Apply random time and frequency masking.

# Training
*   **Strategy:** Implement a **Two-Stage Self-Distillation Pipeline** to maximize the utility of the unlabeled test data, addressing the micro-dataset limitation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
    *   **Stage 1 (Teacher):** Train a ResNet-34 on the labeled training set (Fold 0). Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.
    *   **Stage 2 (Student):** Use the best Teacher model to generate **soft pseudo-labels** (probabilities) for the unlabeled Test Set (Fold 1). Train a new ResNet-34 (Student) on the **combined dataset** (Labeled Train + Pseudo-Labeled Test). The loss for test samples will be the BCE between the Student's logits and the Teacher's soft probabilities.
*   **Data Integrity:** Implement **strict cache validation** (checking file counts and metadata) or use purely dynamic loading to prevent the "stale cache" data starvation issue that caused previous semi-supervised attempts to fail **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
*   **State Preservation:** Use `copy.deepcopy(model.state_dict())` when saving the best model to ensure the checkpoint is an immutable snapshot **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with Cosine Annealing scheduler.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess test images (Resize to 256x512, Compute Deltas, Normalize) and perform a single forward pass using the **Stage 2 (Student)** model.
*   **Output:** Apply Sigmoid activation to the logits to produce final probabilities.
