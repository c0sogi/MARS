Based on **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00029**, I will extend the training duration for both the Teacher and Student stages of the pipeline. The lesson demonstrates that prolonging the "standard" training phase before engaging Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) allows the model to converge to a more optimal region of the loss landscape, significantly improving validation AUC from ~0.9277 to ~0.9434.

My implementation strategy involves:
1.  **Teacher Stage:** Increasing `TEACHER_EPOCHS` from 25 to 35 to ensure the ensemble provides higher-quality pseudo-labels.
2.  **Student Stage:** Increasing `STUDENT_EPOCHS` from 35 to 50 and delaying `SWA_START_EPOCH` from 26 to 35. This extends the standard convergence phase from 26 epochs to 35 epochs, followed by 15 epochs of SWA refinement.
3.  **Submission Threshold:** Updating the validation threshold in `runfile.py` to `0.9433543480067271` as required.
