1.  **Enable Mixup Augmentation (Cite Lesson 7):** I will modify `library/engine.py` to implement Mixup logic within the training loop. Mixup creates convex combinations of image-target pairs, encouraging the model to behave linearly in-between training examples. This is particularly effective for stabilizing training on small datasets and improving robustness.
    *   **Constraint:** Mixup will be applied during the standard training phases (Teachers and Student pre-SWA) but **disabled** during the Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) phase. This ensures that SWA averages weights from a stable, non-augmented distribution, allowing it to settle effectively into the flat minimum (Cite Lesson 28).

2.  **Extend Training Schedule:** Since Mixup increases the difficulty of the training task (regularization), the model requires more iterations to converge. I will modify `library/config.py` to increase `TEACHER_EPOCHS` from 25 to 35 and `STUDENT_EPOCHS` from 35 to 50 (adjusting `SWA_START_EPOCH` accordingly).
