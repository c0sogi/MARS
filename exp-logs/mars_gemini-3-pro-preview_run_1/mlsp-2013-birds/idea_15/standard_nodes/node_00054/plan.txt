To address this, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Lower Learning Rate**: Reduce the learning rate from `1e-3` to `3e-4`. This aligns with Lesson {solution_lesson_node_00025}, which identified `1e-3` as a cause of instability in small-data regimes.
2.  **Increase Mixup Regularization**: Increase `mixup_alpha` from `0.2` to `0.4`. Lesson {solution_lesson_node_00007} suggests that stronger manifold regularization is beneficial for this dataset.
3.  **Optimize SWA Schedule**: Adjust the SWA learning rate to `3e-4` to match the new base LR and ensure smooth transition into the averaging phase.
4.  **Simplify Distillation Pipeline**: Remove the "Generation 2" Student (Student 2) and focus on maximizing the performance of the first Student. 5.  **Ensure SWA Execution**: Disable early stopping (by setting patience equal to epochs) to guarantee that the Stochastic Weight Averaging phase always completes, as premature termination prevents the benefits of SWA (Lesson {solution_lesson_node_00050}).
6.  **Extend Training Duration**: Increase epochs from 50 to 60 to allow for convergence with the lower learning rate (Lesson {solution_lesson_node_00029}).
