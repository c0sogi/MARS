I will update `library/data.py` to replace the deprecated `height` and `width` parameters with `size=(CFG.image_size, CFG.image_size)` in both the training and validation/test transformation pipelines. This fix directly addresses the `ValueError` and applies the knowledge from **debug_lesson_1** and **debug_lesson_3**.
