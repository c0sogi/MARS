Based on the lessons learned (specifically `solution_lesson_node_00007`), I will implement the following optimizations to improve the model's performance and generalization capability:

1.  **Upgrade Backbone & Resolution**: I will switch from `efficientnet_b1` (256x256) to `efficientnet_b3` (300x300). The larger backbone and higher resolution will help capture fine-grained dermatological features.
2.  **Enhanced Augmentation**: I will add `RandomErasing` to the training transforms. This regularization technique forces the model to learn robust global features rather than relying on local artifacts, which is crucial for the larger B3 model. (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`)
3.  **OneCycleLR Scheduler**: I will replace `CosineAnnealingLR` with `OneCycleLR` in the training loop. This schedule (warm-up + annealing) is empirically shown to stabilize the fine-tuning of larger backbones on this dataset. (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`)
4.  **Training Duration**: I will increase the epoch count in `runfile.py` from 3 to 10 to allow the OneCycle schedule sufficient time to converge.
