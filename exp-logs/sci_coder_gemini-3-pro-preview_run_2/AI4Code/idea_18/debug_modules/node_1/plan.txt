The issue is that the `Stage2LGBM.fit` method unconditionally adds an `early_stopping` callback to the LightGBM training process, even when no validation dataset (`eval_set`) is provided. LightGBM requires at least one evaluation dataset to perform early stopping, raising a `ValueError` when `eval_set` is empty. To fix this, I will modify the `fit` method to only append the `lgb.early_stopping` callback if `eval_set` is populated.
