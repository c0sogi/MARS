The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU ran out of VRAM while attempting to train the `resnext101_32x8d` backbone with `1024x1024` images at a batch size of 8. This configuration exceeds the capacity of the A100 40GB GPU because high-resolution inputs generate massive intermediate feature maps during the forward pass. To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 8 to 4, ensuring the memory footprint fits within the available hardware limits. This approach of adjusting hyperparameters in the central configuration file follows the structural best practices highlighted in **Cite debug_lesson_3**.
