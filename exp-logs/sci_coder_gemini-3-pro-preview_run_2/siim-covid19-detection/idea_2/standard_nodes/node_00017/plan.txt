To optimize the model performance for the COVID-19 detection task, I will implement a strategy focused on increasing input resolution and extending the training duration, supported by robust augmentations.

1.  **Increase Image Resolution**: I will increase `IMG_SIZE` from 800 to 1024. The failure analysis (Cite Lesson 00004) indicated a correlation between loss and object density. Higher resolution allows the model to better resolve dense, small opacities that are characteristic of severe cases.
2.  **Extend Training Schedule**: I will increase `NUM_EPOCHS` from 6 to 12 and adjust the `LR_STEP_SIZE` to 7. Previous experiments (Cite Lesson 00014) showed that decaying the learning rate too late relative to the stopping point harms performance. A 12-epoch schedule with decay at epoch 7 allows for a substantial fine-tuning phase.
3.  **Enhance Augmentations**: To prevent overfitting with the longer training schedule, I will add `GaussNoise` and `MotionBlur` to the training pipeline.
4.  **Update Threshold**: I will update the submission threshold to the target value of `0.4329`.
