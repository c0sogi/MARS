The strategy involves three main optimizations based on the provided lessons and failure analysis:

1.  **Scale to Full Data**: The previous Multi-Task experiment showed promise (decoupling error from object density) but was limited by the small subset (1500 images). I will remove the subsetting constraints in `runfile.py` to train on the complete dataset (`debug=False`), as the runtime (estimated ~75 mins for 10 epochs) fits well within the 4-hour limit.
2.  **Optimize RPN & Detection Capacity**: Addressing the bottleneck identified in `solution_lesson_node_00005`, I will increase the RPN capacity (`RPN_POST_NMS_TOP_N_TRAIN`) to 3000 and the maximum detections per image (`BOX_DETECTIONS_PER_IMG`) to 200. This ensures the model can handle the high density of opacities found in severe COVID-19 cases.
3.  **Tune Multi-Task Loss**: I will reduce the auxiliary global classification loss weight (`LAMBDA_GLOBAL_CLS`) from 1.0 to 0.5. While `solution_lesson_node_00008` validates the regularizing effect of this head, the previous logs showed it dominating the total loss. Reducing the weight allows the optimizer to focus more on the primary bounding box regression and classification tasks.
