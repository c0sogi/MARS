I will optimize the solution by upgrading the feature extraction backbone from `ConvNeXt-Large` (ImageNet-1k trained) to `ConvNeXt-Large` pre-trained on **ImageNet-22k** and fine-tuned on ImageNet-1k at a higher resolution (**384x384**). This leverages the "Better Backbone" and "Resolution" strategies. The ImageNet-22k dataset contains significantly more dog breed classes than ImageNet-1k, resulting in a much richer feature space for this specific task. I will use the `timm` library to load this specific model (`convnext_large.fb_in22k_ft_in1k_384`) and its corresponding preprocessing transforms, ensuring optimal alignment between data and model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

Here are the specific changes:
1.  **`library/config.py`**: Update `IMG_SIZE` to 384, `EMBEDDING_DIM` to 1536, and `MODEL_NAME` to the `timm` identifier.
2.  **`library/data_loader.py`**: Integrate `timm` to dynamically generate the exact preprocessing transforms (Resize, Crop, Normalization) required by the specific model configuration.
3.  **`library/feature_extractor.py`**: Switch from `torchvision` to `timm` for model instantiation, using `num_classes=0` to obtain the pooled feature embeddings directly.
