The `TimeoutError` during model training indicates that the serial execution (`n_jobs=1`) of the `LogisticRegressionCV` solver is too slow given the high dimensionality of the embeddings (over 4,000 features). To fix this, I will enable process-level parallelism by setting `n_jobs=-1` in the configuration, allowing the training to utilize all available vCPUs. Additionally, to prevent the deadlocks and resource contention that typically occur when `joblib` spawns multiple workers that each attempt to use multi-threaded BLAS operations (Cite debug_lesson_5), I will set the `OMP_NUM_THREADS` environment variable to `1`. This isolates the threading layers, ensuring that the worker processes run efficiently without fighting for CPU threads.
