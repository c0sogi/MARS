# Optimized Strategy: ResNet-34 with Resolution Matching and Full-Scale Training

### Model
**Architecture:** I propose replacing the ResNet-18 backbone with **ResNet-34**.
*   **Structural Innovation:** This choice represents a strategic architectural pivot to the "Goldilocks" zone of deep learning efficiency. While ResNet-50 and ConvNeXt-Tiny (tried in previous ideas) offer high theoretical capacity, their computational weight restricts the number of training iterations possible within the 24-hour limit, leading to under-convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015). ResNet-34 utilizes the same efficient "BasicBlock" structure as ResNet-18 (avoiding the computationally expensive "Bottleneck" blocks of ResNet-50) but doubles the depth. This provides a necessary increase in parameter capacity to handle the 5270 classes without sacrificing the high throughput required to process the massive dataset.
*   **Head:** A standard fully connected layer mapping the 512-dimensional features to the 5270 category logits.

### Data
**Preprocessing (Training):**
*   **Full Dataset Utilization:** We will train on the **entire labeled dataset** (approx. 12 million images). As established, maximizing the volume of unique samples is the primary driver of performance, far outweighing the benefits of repeating a smaller subset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Resolution Matching:** Crucially, we will resize all input images to **224x224 pixels**. Previous attempts using the native 180x180 resolution failed because pre-trained weights (from ImageNet) are optimized for specific spatial scales. Upscaling ensures the receptive fields align with the pre-trained feature extractors, accelerating convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Metadata Indexing:** We will retain the CSV-based metadata index to enable efficient random-access reads from the monolithic BSON file (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Flattening:** Images will be treated as independent samples to maximize training throughput.

**Preprocessing (Evaluation):**
*   Validation and Test loaders will group images by `product_id` to support multi-view aggregation.

### Training
**Procedure:**
*   **Scheduler:** We will employ the **`OneCycleLR`** scheduler. This is essential for achieving super-convergence on the massive 12-million-image dataset within a limited number of epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Duration:** We will target **2 epochs** on the full dataset. The efficiency of ResNet-34 ensures this fits within the 24-hour window, unlike heavier architectures.
*   **Regularization:** We will apply **Label Smoothing (0.1)**. With the full dataset, the model will encounter sufficient diversity to benefit from this regularization (preventing overconfidence in the 5270 classes) without the convergence penalty associated with Mixup on limited training schedules (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Precision:** Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) will be used to maximize throughput.

### Evaluation
**Inference:**
*   **Late Fusion:** We will strictly maintain the Late Fusion strategy. The model will generate probability distributions for every image associated with a product, and these vectors will be averaged element-wise to produce the final consensus prediction. This leverages the multi-view nature of the data to improve accuracy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
