# Optimized Strategy: Full-Scale ResNet-50 with Mixup and Metadata Indexing

### Model
I propose upgrading the backbone architecture to **ResNet-50**.
*   **Structural Innovation:** Replacing the shallower ResNet-18 with ResNet-50 significantly increases the model's parameter count and depth. This is essential for distinguishing between the high cardinality of classes (5270 categories) and capturing fine-grained visual details that the smaller model might miss.
*   **Head:** The final classification layer will be adapted to output logits for the 5270 categories.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing (Training):**
    *   **Full Dataset Utilization:** We will train on the **entire labeled dataset** (approximately 12 million images). Previous experiments demonstrated that maximizing data diversity is the primary driver of performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Metadata Indexing:** We will retain the efficient CSV-based metadata indexing system to perform random access reads directly from the monolithic BSON file. This ensures the pipeline remains compute-bound rather than I/O-bound (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    *   **Flattening:** Images will be treated as independent samples during training to maximize throughput.
*   **Preprocessing (Evaluation):**
    *   The validation and test loaders will group images by `product_id` to enable the aggregation of predictions.

### Training
*   **Strategy:**
    *   **One-Cycle Policy:** We will utilize the **`OneCycleLR`** scheduler. This has proven effective for converging rapidly on large datasets within limited epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Regularization at Scale:** We will re-introduce **Mixup** and **Label Smoothing**, techniques that failed in previous low-data experiments. As established in the lessons, these techniques require sufficient data volume to be effective (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008). With the full 12-million-image dataset, the model will see enough unique examples to benefit from the improved generalization and robustness provided by these methods without underfitting.
    *   **Duration:** We will target **2 epochs** on the full dataset. Based on previous throughput metrics, this is estimated to take approximately 20 hours on the A100 GPU, fitting within the 24-hour limit.
    *   **Precision:** Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) will be used to maintain the necessary throughput for the larger ResNet-50 model.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will employ the **Late Fusion** strategy. The model will generate probability distributions for every image belonging to a product, and these vectors will be averaged to produce the final prediction. This method effectively leverages the multi-view nature of the data to reduce error rates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
