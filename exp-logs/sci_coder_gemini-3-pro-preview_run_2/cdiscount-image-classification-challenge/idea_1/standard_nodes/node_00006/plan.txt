The following changes address the runtime error and optimize the training configuration:

1.  **Disable Pinned Memory:** The `torch.AcceleratorError: CUDA error: invalid argument` originating in the `pin_memory` thread indicates a failure to allocate page-locked memory for specific batches, likely due to fragmentation or driver limitations with the large dataset/batch size. I will set `pin_memory=False` in all `DataLoader` instances to bypass this optimization and resolve the crash.
2.  **Use Full Dataset:** To maximize categorization accuracy as requested, I will change `TRAIN_LIMIT` from `2000000` to `None`, enabling training on the entire dataset.
