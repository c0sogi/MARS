3.  **Enhanced Augmentation**: I will add `ShiftScaleRotate` and `CoarseDropout` to the training transformations. This adds necessary regularization to prevent the model from memorizing the training data, addressing the overfitting issue.
4.  **Training Duration**: I will increase `EPOCHS` to 10 to allow the model sufficient time to converge with the new regularization and larger batch size.

    """
    Trains the model for one epoch.
    """

    losses = AverageMeter()
