The issue identified is **data leakage** caused by training the models on the validation set. In the previous implementation, `train_df` and `val_df` were concatenated into `full_train_df` before cross-validation, meaning the models saw the validation samples during training. When `runfile.py` later evaluated these models on `val_df`, the performance metric was invalid because it was an in-sample evaluation.

To fix this, I will modify `library/trainer.py` to remove the concatenation step. The `Trainer` will now strictly use `train_df` for feature extraction and cross-validation, ensuring the validation set (`val_df`) remains completely held-out and unseen by the models.
