To optimize the solution, I will implement a **CV-Bagging (Cross-Validation Bagging)** strategy to replace the single-split retraining approach. As identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**, averaging predictions from models trained on different folds of the data consistently outperforms a single model trained on the full dataset, especially for small datasets where variance is high.

I will also remove the `alpha` parameter (Differential Scaling) from the hyperparameter search space. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00035** demonstrated that manually up-scaling metadata features provides no benefit and complicates the optimization landscape; we will revert to standard feature concatenation (`alpha=1.0`).

Finally, I will ensure the hyperparameter grid remains constrained (capping `C` at 10.0) to prevent overfitting in the high-dimensional text embedding space (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
