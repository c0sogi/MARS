During training, the pipeline applies Partial Least Squares (PLS) dimensionality reduction to the text embeddings before feeding them into the classifier (reducing 768 features to ~20-50). However, the validation loop in `runfile.py` loads the raw text embeddings and feeds them directly into the classifier without loading or applying the PLS transformation, causing a feature count mismatch (778 vs 30).

To fix this, I will update `runfile.py` to load the saved `pls` and `pls_scaler` artifacts for each fold and apply the transformation to the text features before concatenation, ensuring the input dimensions match what the classifier expects.
