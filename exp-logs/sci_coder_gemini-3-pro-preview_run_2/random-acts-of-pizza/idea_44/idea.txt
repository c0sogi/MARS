Based on the synthesis of lessons learned—specifically the breakthrough success of **Multi-Field Embeddings** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077), the necessity of **Asymmetric Dimensionality Reduction** for auxiliary backbones (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046), and the proven stability of **CV-Bagging** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027)—I propose the **Fully-Segregated Asymmetric Dual-Backbone Ensemble (FS-ADBE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Multi-Field" approach (Idea 39) by extending the **Field-Specific** logic to the Auxiliary Backbone. Idea 39 successfully split the Anchor (MiniLM) into Title and Body views but relied on a "Global" concatenated view for the Auxiliary (MPNet). This "Global" view risks diluting the critical "Hook" signal (Title) within the longer "Narrative" (Body). By fully segregating the Auxiliary backbone into distinct **Title** and **Body** projections, we ensure that the "Deep World Knowledge" of MPNet is applied specifically to each rhetorical component, maximizing signal resolution while maintaining a strict dimensionality budget.

# Model
The architecture is a **Bagged Ensemble of Logistic Regression Classifiers** operating on a fused, fully-segregated multi-view feature space.

1.  **Field-Specific Anchors (High-Resolution):**
    *   **Title Anchor:** `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` applied to `request_title` (384d).
    *   **Body Anchor:** `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` applied to `request_text_edit_aware` (384d).
    *   **Rationale:** Retains the stable, high-sample-efficiency baseline established in Idea 39 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077, Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

2.  **Field-Specific Auxiliaries (Low-Resolution):**
    *   **Deep Hook:** `sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2` applied to `request_title` (768d).
        *   **Transformation:** **PCA (20 components)**.
        *   **Rationale:** Explicitly captures the deep semantics of the Title (e.g., urgency, specific needs) without interference from the Body. Compressed to 20d to provide a dense summary (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046).
    *   **Deep Narrative:** `sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2` applied to `request_text_edit_aware` (768d).
        *   **Transformation:** **PCA (30 components)**.
        *   **Rationale:** Explicitly captures the deep semantics of the Body (e.g., context, backstory). Compressed to 30d.
    *   **Total Aux Budget:** 50 dimensions (20+30), matching the previous "Global" budget to prevent dimensionality explosion.

3.  **Classifier:** **BaggingClassifier** wrapping **Logistic Regression** (Ridge).
    *   **Rationale:** We preserve the high-bias linear core (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005) and use Bagging to reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

# Data
We construct five distinct feature views and fuse them via **Early Fusion** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020):

1.  **View 1: Title Anchor (384 dims)**
    *   **Processing:** Encode `request_title` with `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (Frozen) -> **L2 Normalization** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
2.  **View 2: Body Anchor (384 dims)**
    *   **Processing:** Encode `request_text_edit_aware` with `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (Frozen) -> **L2 Normalization**.
3.  **View 3: Deep Hook (20 dims)**
    *   **Processing:** Encode `request_title` with `all-mpnet-base-v2` (Frozen) -> **PCA (20)** -> **L2 Normalization**.
    *   **Rationale:** Normalization after PCA ensures alignment with anchors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
4.  **View 4: Deep Narrative (30 dims)**
    *   **Processing:** Encode `request_text_edit_aware` with `all-mpnet-base-v2` (Frozen) -> **PCA (30)** -> **L2 Normalization**.
5.  **View 5: Robust Metadata (~10 dims)**
    *   **Features:** All numerical metadata, **explicitly including** `unix_timestamp_of_request` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Scaling:** Apply **QuantileTransformer (output_distribution='normal')** (RankGauss) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

# Training
1.  **Pipeline Efficiency:**
    *   **Pre-computation:** Compute raw embeddings for all 4 text streams (MiniLM-Title, MiniLM-Body, MPNet-Title, MPNet-Body) **outside** the cross-validation loop (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Cache Validation:** Implement strict row-count checks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

2.  **Stratified Cross-Validation:**
    *   Use 5-Fold Stratified CV.
    *   **Encapsulation:** The PCA models (Views 3 & 4) and QuantileTransformer (View 5) must be fitted **inside** the cross-validation loop (on training folds only) to prevent statistical leakage (Cite solution_lesson_node_00047).

3.  **Context-Aware Hyperparameter Tuning:**
    *   Perform Grid Search within each fold.
    *   **Crucial:** Tune the hyperparameters (`C`, `class_weight`) of the **Full Bagged Ensemble**, not just the base learner (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Search Space:**
        *   `C`: High-Regularization Regime (`1e-4` to `10.0`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
        *   `class_weight`: `['balanced', None]` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
        *   `Penalty`: **L2 (Ridge)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).

4.  **Ensemble Strategy:**
    *   Train a Bagged Ensemble (`n_estimators=20`) on each fold using the best parameters.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Generate raw embeddings for the test set.
    *   For each of the 5 trained fold-pipelines:
        *   **Anchors:** L2 Normalize MiniLM Title/Body.
        *   **Auxiliaries:** Project MPNet Title/Body using fold's PCA models -> L2 Normalize.
        *   **Metadata:** Transform using fold's RankGauss.
        *   **Fusion:** Concatenate all views (~828 dims) and predict.
2.  **Prediction:**
    *   **Average the probability outputs** from all 5 fold-ensembles. This **CV-Bagging** strategy maximizes stability and generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
