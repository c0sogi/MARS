The proposed strategy transitions from a **Late Fusion (Stacking)** architecture to an **Early Fusion** architecture. Based on Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00020`, Early Fusion (concatenating embeddings and metadata) outperforms Late Fusion on small multi-modal datasets because it allows the linear model to learn a joint decision boundary, weighting semantic and metadata signals simultaneously without the information bottleneck of intermediate probabilities.

The solution will:
1.  **Concatenate Features**: Combine the 384-dimensional text embeddings (L2 normalized) with the RankGauss-scaled metadata into a single feature matrix.
2.  **Unified Bagged Model**: Train a single Bagged Logistic Regression ensemble on this combined feature set.
3.  **Optimize Hyperparameters**: Update the grid search to include very strong regularization values (`C=0.0001`, `C=0.001`) to handle the high dimensionality (384 text + 10 meta) relative to the sample size (~2300), as advised in Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00012`.
4.  **Reduce Variance**: Increase the number of bagging estimators to 20 and average predictions across 5 stratified folds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).

This approach simplifies the pipeline while directly addressing the lessons regarding dimensionality and signal integration on small datasets.
