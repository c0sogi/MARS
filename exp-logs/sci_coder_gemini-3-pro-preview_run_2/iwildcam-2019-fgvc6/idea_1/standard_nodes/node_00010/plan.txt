The `torch.optim.LBFGS` optimizer does not support the `weight_decay` parameter in its constructor, which caused the `TypeError` during initialization. To fix this, I will remove the `weight_decay` argument from the `LBFGS` instantiation and instead manually implement L2 regularization within the optimization `closure` function. This involves calculating the sum of squared parameters, scaling it by `0.5 * weight_decay`, and adding it to the loss function before computing gradients (Cite debug_lesson_3).
