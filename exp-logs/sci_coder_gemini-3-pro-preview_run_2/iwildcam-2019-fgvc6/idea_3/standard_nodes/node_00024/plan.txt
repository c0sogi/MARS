I will implement an optimization strategy focused on **Native Resolution Scaling** by upgrading the model architecture from EfficientNet-B3 to **EfficientNet-B4**. This change increases the input resolution from 300x300 to **380x380**, which is critical for preserving fine-grained features of small animals in camera trap images (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022). To accommodate the larger model within the memory constraints, I will reduce the batch size to 32. Additionally, I will extend the fine-tuning stage to 5 epochs to ensure the larger model has sufficient time to converge on the target domain features.
