To optimize the solution, I will switch the backbone model from `microsoft/deberta-v3-base` to `microsoft/deberta-v3-xsmall` and train on the full dataset instead of a subset. This aligns with the finding that data volume is more critical than model size under compute constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015). I will also increase the training duration to 2 epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) and increase the batch size to 12 to leverage the smaller model's efficiency. Finally, I will remove the subsampling logic in the execution script to ensure the model sees the complete data distribution.
