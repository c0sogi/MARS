# Proposed Strategy: Siamese DeBERTa-Base with Meta-Feature Fusion

### Model
I propose a **Siamese Dual-Encoder with Meta-Feature Fusion**, upgrading the backbone and integrating structural signals directly into the classifier.

*   **Backbone Architecture:** The solution will transition from the lightweight `xsmall` model to **`microsoft/deberta-v3-base`**. This significantly increases the model's capacity to understand complex linguistic nuances and reasoning, addressing the limitations of smaller models in judging quality.
*   **Siamese Processing:** To prevent the input truncation observed with Cross-Encoders, the model will process `(Prompt, Response A)` and `(Prompt, Response B)` as independent passes through the shared backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Pooling Mechanism:** The model will utilize **Mean Pooling** (averaging token embeddings weighted by the attention mask) rather than `[CLS]` token extraction to generate robust sentence representations ($u$ and $v$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Hybrid Classification Head:** The final classification layer will be a "Wide and Deep" structure. It will concatenate:
    1.  The dense semantic embeddings: $u, v$.
    2.  The explicit interaction terms: $|u - v|$ and $u \cdot v$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
    3.  **New Structural Innovation:** A vector of **explicit meta-features**, specifically the normalized character lengths of the prompt, Response A, and Response B. This explicitly provides the "verbosity" signal identified in the EDA, allowing the model to balance semantic quality with response length biases.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Text will be cleaned, and the character lengths for the prompt and both responses will be calculated. These lengths will be normalized (e.g., using Standard Scaler) to match the scale of the neural embeddings.
*   **Tokenization:** The text inputs will be tokenized into pairs `(Prompt, Response A)` and `(Prompt, Response B)` with a maximum sequence length of **512 tokens**. This ensures the model views the full context of the prompt and the specific response being evaluated without truncation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

### Training
*   **Optimization:** The model will be trained end-to-end using the AdamW optimizer.
*   **Learning Rate:** A conservative learning rate of **2e-5** will be applied to the backbone to preserve pre-trained knowledge and prevent divergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Schedule:** A linear warmup followed by cosine decay will be used.
*   **Duration:** Training will be strictly limited to **2 epochs** to maximize generalization and avoid the overfitting observed in longer runs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Loss Function:** Cross-Entropy Loss will be minimized.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** The test set will be processed by generating embeddings for the A and B branches using the fine-tuned backbone and computing the corresponding meta-features.
*   **Prediction:** These combined features will be passed through the hybrid classification head to output the probabilities for Winner A, Winner B, and Tie.
