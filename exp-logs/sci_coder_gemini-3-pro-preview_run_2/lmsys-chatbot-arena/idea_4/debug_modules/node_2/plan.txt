The `AssertionError: Probabilities do not sum to 1` during the inference step is caused by numerical overflow when using Mixed Precision (FP16). As explained in **debug_lesson_2**, the limited dynamic range of `float16` can cause logits to overflow to infinity (`inf`) during the forward pass. When `torch.softmax` processes these `inf` values, it produces `NaN`s, which do not sum to 1. To resolve this, I will disable `autocast` in the `infer` function within `library/engine.py`, forcing the model to run in full `float32` precision during inference to ensure numerical stability (Cite debug_lesson_2).
