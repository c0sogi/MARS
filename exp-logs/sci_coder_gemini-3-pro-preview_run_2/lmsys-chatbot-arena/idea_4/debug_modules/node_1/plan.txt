The `AssertionError: Probabilities do not sum to 1` occurs because the model inference runs within a mixed precision context (`torch.cuda.amp.autocast`), producing `float16` logits. When `torch.softmax` is applied to these logits, it preserves the `float16` data type. The limited precision of `float16` causes the sum of the resulting probabilities to deviate from 1.0 by more than the strict tolerance (`1e-5`) expected by the assertion in `runfile.py`.

To fix this, I will modify `library/engine.py` to cast the logits to `float32` (single precision) before applying the softmax function in both the `validate` and `infer` functions. This ensures the probabilities are computed with sufficient precision to sum to 1.0.
