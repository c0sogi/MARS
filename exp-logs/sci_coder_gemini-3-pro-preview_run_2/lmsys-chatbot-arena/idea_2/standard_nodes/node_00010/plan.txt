To optimize the solution, I will upgrade the model backbone from `microsoft/deberta-v3-xsmall` to `microsoft/deberta-v3-small`. This increases the model's capacity (hidden size 768 vs 384) to better capture semantic nuances and quality differences in the chatbot responses, addressing the need for better representation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008). I will also increase the training duration to 3 epochs to allow the larger model to converge, as the previous run showed continuing improvement at the end of epoch 2. Finally, I will increase the batch size to 16 to utilize the available A100 40GB GPU memory more effectively and stabilize training, and update the submission threshold to the required value.
