# Proposed Strategy: K-Fold Ensemble of Siamese DeBERTa-Base with LLRD

### Model
I propose a **Siamese DeBERTa-v3-Base** architecture enhanced with **Multi-Sample Dropout** and integrated into a **Cross-Validation Ensemble**.

*   **Backbone Upgrade:** The solution will utilize **`microsoft/deberta-v3-base`**.
    *   *Rationale:* Previous attempts with this backbone failed because they were undertrained (1 epoch) or subsampled due to time constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015, solution_lesson_node_00019). With the confirmed 24-hour budget and A100 GPU, we can afford the computational cost to train this larger, more capable model to full convergence (approx. 2-3 epochs), unlocking semantic reasoning capabilities superior to the `xsmall` version.
*   **Siamese Architecture:** The model will process inputs as two independent passes: `(Prompt, Response A)` and `(Prompt, Response B)`. This preserves the full 512-token context for each response, avoiding the truncation issues of Cross-Encoders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Pooling:** We will retain **Mean Pooling** to generate robust sentence representations ($u$ and $v$), as it outperformed `[CLS]` token usage (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008) and proved more reliable than complex weighted pooling for this specific task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Augmented Classification Head:** The head will concatenate the embeddings ($u, v$) with explicit interaction terms ($|u - v|, u \cdot v$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) and the normalized meta-features (character lengths) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Structural Innovation (Head):** We will implement **Multi-Sample Dropout** in the final classification layers. Instead of a single dropout mask, the network will compute the loss using multiple different dropout masks and average the results. This technique accelerates convergence and improves generalization, acting as a mini-ensemble within the model itself.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Text will be cleaned, and character lengths for the prompt and responses will be calculated and normalized using a standard scaler fitted on the training data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Tokenization:** Inputs will be tokenized into pairs with a maximum sequence length of **512 tokens**.
*   **Splitting Strategy:** Instead of a single Train/Validation split, we will generate **4 Stratified Folds** from the full training dataset. This ensures we utilize 100% of the available data for training across the ensemble and provides a more robust estimate of model performance.

### Training
*   **Pipeline:** We will train 4 separate instances of the model (one for each fold).
*   **Optimization Strategy:**
    *   **Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD):** To prevent the "catastrophic divergence" observed with aggressive fine-tuning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004), we will apply LLRD. The classification head will receive a learning rate of **2e-5**, with the learning rate decaying multiplicatively (e.g., by 0.9) for each lower transformer layer. This stabilizes the training of the `base` model by preserving lower-level linguistic features.
    *   **Scheduler:** A linear scheduler with **warmup** will be used to further stabilize the early training phase (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Batch Size:** We will maintain a small effective batch size (e.g., 8-16) to leverage the regularization benefits of gradient noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Loss Function:** Cross-Entropy Loss.
*   **Duration:** Each fold will be trained for **2-3 epochs**, utilizing the full dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For the test set, we will generate predictions using all 4 trained fold models.
*   **Ensembling:** The final submission will be the **arithmetic mean** of the probabilities predicted by the 4 models. This structural change from a single-model prediction to a K-Fold ensemble will significantly reduce variance and mitigate the overfitting risks observed in single-run experiments (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
