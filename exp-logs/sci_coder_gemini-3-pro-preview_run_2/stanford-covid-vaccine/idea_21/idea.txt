Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the progression of previous ideas, I propose the **Scale-Decoupled Compact Dense Network**.

This strategy is a structural evolution of the state-of-the-art "Dense-Context" architecture (Idea 8). It addresses the "information smashing" limitation of single-stream compression (Idea 8) and the "optimization failure" of multi-scale attempts (Idea 20, Idea 16) by implementing a **Dual-Stream Compression** mechanism. This ensures that local chemical motifs and global structural contexts are processed with semantic distinctness while maintaining the compact dimensionality required for effective RNN aggregation.

# Model

1.  **Input Representation (Partner-Aware):**
    *   **Standard Features:** We utilize **One-Hot Encoding** for `sequence`, `structure`, and `predicted_loop_type` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Explicit Partner Identity:** We **retain** the explicit injection of the **Partner Base Identity** (one-hot vector of the paired base) at the input level. As established in **Lesson 00052**, removing this input cripples the encoder's ability to form basic chemical representations.

2.  **Backbone (Dense Dilated TCN):**
    *   **Architecture:** A stack of **Single-Layer Dilated Residual Blocks** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) utilizing **Dense Connections** (concatenating outputs of all prior blocks). This preserves the full multi-scale history required for complex interaction modeling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00047).
    *   **Configuration:** We employ exponentially increasing dilation rates ($d=1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32$).
    *   **Capacity & Regularization:** We strictly set the **Growth Rate / Channel Width** to **64** to avoid capacity bottlenecks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037) and apply **Dropout (0.1)** within every block to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Convolution Type:** We use **Standard Convolutions** throughout (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

3.  **Innovation: Scale-Decoupled Latent Compression:**
    *   **Concept:** Previous ideas failed because they either compressed the entire history into one vector (mixing local/global signals, Idea 8) or fed the uncompressed history to the RNN (exploding dimensionality, Idea 20/16).
    *   **Partitioning:** We split the concatenated backbone output (Total ~384 channels) into two semantic groups:
        *   **Local Scale:** Outputs from layers with dilation $d=1, 2, 4$.
        *   **Global Scale:** Outputs from layers with dilation $d=8, 16, 32$.
    *   **Independent Compression:** We apply separate **1x1 Convolutions** to project each group into a compact latent space (e.g., 32 channels each). This produces $Z_{local}$ and $Z_{global}$. This adheres to **Lesson 00062** (Compress before gather) and **Lesson 00068** (Compact bottlenecks).

4.  **Dual-Scale Structural Interaction:**
    *   **Gather:** For every position $i$ paired with $j$, we gather the compressed partner features:
        *   $P_{local} = Z_{local}[j]$ (The partner's local motif).
        *   $P_{global} = Z_{global}[j]$ (The partner's global fold).
    *   **Fusion:** We concatenate the self-features and partner-features: $[Z_{local}[i], Z_{global}[i], P_{local}, P_{global}]$.
    *   **Result:** A compact 128-dimensional vector that explicitly disentangles local chemistry from global structure (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).

5.  **Global Aggregation (BiGRU):**
    *   The fused features are processed by a **Bidirectional GRU** to capture global constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Constraint:** Hidden size is set to `input_dim // 2` (64) to ensure the output dimension matches the input, preventing parameter bloat (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

6.  **Output Head:**
    *   Linear projection to the 5 target variables.

# Training

1.  **Loss Function (Masked Optimization):**
    *   Use **MCRMSE** (Mean Columnwise Root Mean Squared Error).
    *   **Strategy:** Calculate loss **only on the three scored columns** (`reactivity`, `deg_Mg_pH10`, `deg_Mg_50C`).
    *   **Rationale:** We strictly adhere to **Lesson 00016**, masking out auxiliary targets (`deg_pH10`, `deg_50C`) to prevent negative transfer and align the optimization objective directly with the competition metric.

2.  **Optimizer:**
    *   Use **AdamW** with a learning rate of 1e-3 and a `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler.
