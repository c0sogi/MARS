The error is caused by passing the deprecated `verbose` argument to the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler constructor, which has been removed in recent PyTorch versions. To fix this, I will remove the `verbose` parameter from the scheduler initialization in both `runfile.py` and `library/train.py`. Cite debug_lesson_5
