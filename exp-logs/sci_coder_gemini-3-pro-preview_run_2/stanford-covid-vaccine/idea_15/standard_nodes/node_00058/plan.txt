Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Simplify Structural Interaction (Cite Lesson 00023)**: Reduce `STACKING_NEIGHBORS` from 3 to 1 in `Config`. This removes the "Partner Triplets" (neighbors of the paired base) which were found to introduce noise and degrade performance, focusing the model on the high-signal "Partner Identity" gathering.
2.  **Optimize Model Capacity (Cite Lesson 00037)**: Increase `HIDDEN_DIM` from 64 to 80. This compensates for the reduced dimensionality in the gathering head and provides slightly more capacity to the dilated backbone for feature extraction, without reaching the over-parameterization levels (e.g., 256) that caused overfitting in Lesson 00017.
3.  **Update Data Processing**: Modify `library/data.py` to generate `neighbor_indices` with shape `(N, L, 1)` (containing only the partner index), aligning with the configuration change.
4.  **Cache Invalidation (Cite Lesson 00032)**: Update cache keys in `Config` to `_v2` to ensure the new data processing logic is executed.
5.  **Extended Training**: Increase `EPOCHS` in `runfile.py` from 15 to 25 to ensure the slightly larger model converges fully.
