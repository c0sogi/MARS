Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the progression of previous ideas, I propose the **Decoupled Multi-Scale Dense Network**.

This strategy optimizes the current state-of-the-art "Dense-Context" architecture (Idea 8) by resolving the "information smashing" limitation identified in Lesson 00062 and Lesson 00068. By decoupling the latent representation into "Local" and "Global" views and compressing them independently before interaction, we preserve semantic distinctness without exploding the parameter space, strictly adhering to the parsimony and regularization constraints established in the lessons.

# Model

1.  **Input Representation (Partner-Aware):**
    *   **Standard Features:** We utilize **One-Hot Encoding** for `sequence`, `structure`, and `predicted_loop_type` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Explicit Partner Identity:** We **retain** the explicit injection of the **Partner Base Identity** (one-hot vector of the paired base) at the input level. As established in **Lesson 00052**, removing this input cripples the encoder's ability to form basic chemical representations.

2.  **Backbone (Dense Dilated TCN):**
    *   **Architecture:** A stack of **Single-Layer Dilated Residual Blocks** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) utilizing **Dense Connections** (concatenating outputs of all prior blocks). This preserves the full multi-scale history required for complex interaction modeling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00047).
    *   **Configuration:** We employ exponentially increasing dilation rates ($d=1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32$).
    *   **Capacity & Regularization:** We strictly set the **Growth Rate / Channel Width** to **64** to avoid capacity bottlenecks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037) and apply **Dropout (0.1)** within every block to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Convolution Type:** We use **Standard Convolutions** throughout (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

3.  **Innovation: Decoupled Latent Compression & Interaction:**
    *   **Concept:** Previous successful models (Idea 8) compressed the entire dense history into a single vector, mixing local motifs with global folds. Previous failures (Idea 16, 20) fed uncompressed features to the RNN, causing optimization issues. We propose a middle ground: **Semantic Partitioning**.
    *   **Partitioning:** We split the concatenated backbone output into two semantic groups:
        *   **Local View:** Outputs from layers with low dilation ($d=1, 2, 4$).
        *   **Global View:** Outputs from layers with high dilation ($d=8, 16, 32$).
    *   **Independent Compression:** We apply separate **1x1 Convolutions** to project each view into a compact latent space (e.g., 32 channels each), yielding $Z_{local}$ and $Z_{global}$. This adheres to **Lesson 00062** (Compress before gather) and **Lesson 00068** (Compact bottlenecks).
    *   **Decoupled Gather:** For every position $i$ paired with $j$, we gather the partner's features independently:
        *   $P_{local} = Z_{local}[j]$ (The partner's local motif).
        *   $P_{global} = Z_{global}[j]$ (The partner's global fold).
    *   **Fusion:** We concatenate the self-features and partner-features: $[Z_{local}[i], Z_{global}[i], P_{local}, P_{global}]$. We use simple concatenation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066) and avoid redundant neighbor gathering (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).

4.  **Global Aggregation (BiGRU):**
    *   The fused features (Total 128 channels) are processed by a **Bidirectional GRU** to capture global constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Constraint:** Hidden size is set to `input_dim // 2` (64) to ensure the output dimension matches the input, preventing parameter bloat (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

5.  **Output Head:**
    *   Linear projection to the 5 target variables.

1.  **Loss Function (Masked Optimization):**
    *   Use **MCRMSE** (Mean Columnwise Root Mean Squared Error).
    *   **Strategy:** Calculate loss **only on the three scored columns** (`reactivity`, `deg_Mg_pH10`, `deg_Mg_50C`).
    *   **Rationale:** We strictly adhere to **Lesson 00016**, masking out auxiliary targets (`deg_pH10`, `deg_50C`) to prevent negative transfer and align the optimization objective directly with the competition metric.

2.  **Optimizer:**
    *   Use **AdamW** with a learning rate of 1e-3 and a `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler.
