My implementation strategy focuses on optimizing the **Topology-Informed Stabilized Recurrent Network (TI-SRN)** by addressing three key areas derived from the provided lessons:

1.  **Feedback Signal Normalization (Cite Lesson 00085, 00086):** The current solution naively concatenates raw prediction logits (high variance) with one-hot topology features (0-1 range) in the recycling loop. I will introduce a **Feedback Projection Layer** (`Conv1d` + `BatchNorm` + `ReLU`) to project the 5-channel predictions into a normalized 16-dimensional embedding space before concatenation. This prevents scale mismatch and saturation issues.
2.  **Model Capacity scaling (Cite Lesson 00037):** I will increase the `backbone_channels` from 64 to 96 and the `rnn_hidden_dim` from 128 to 256. The previous configuration was likely underfitting given the complexity of the task and the availability of compute.
3.  **Training Optimization:** I will increase the `batch_size` to 64 (Cite Lesson 00104) for better gradient estimation and extend the training duration to 30 epochs in `runfile.py` to ensure convergence with the larger model.
