Based on the provided lessons and analysis, I will optimize the solution by simplifying the architecture to a **Dense Dilated TCN with Latent Partner Gathering**, effectively reverting the "Cascaded" complexity introduced in the previous iteration which likely caused overfitting or signal dilution.

**Optimization Strategy:**
1.  **Architecture Simplification (Cite Lesson 00017):** Remove the second stage of dilated convolutions ("Stage 2"). The previous solution (Idea 11) added a secondary stack of convolutions after feature gathering, which increased parameter count and depth without clear benefit, leading to a validation score of ~0.68. I will revert to the simpler, high-performing design (implied from Lesson 00031/00038) where the backbone features are compressed, gathered, and immediately fed into the BiGRU.
2.  **Dynamic Feature Gathering (Cite Lesson 00031):** Retain the mechanism that gathers latent features from paired bases using structural indices. This allows the model to incorporate the *state* of the interacting partner rather than just its identity.
3.  **Backbone Capacity (Cite Lesson 00037):** Maintain the `GROWTH_RATE` at 64 and `DROPOUT` at 0.1. Lesson 00037 showed that reducing channel width hurts performance, and Lesson 00038 showed that removing dropout leads to overfitting.
4.  **Training Configuration:** Increase `BATCH_SIZE` to 64 to better utilize the A100 GPU and potentially stabilize batch statistics. Update the `WORKING_DIR` to `idea_12` to ensure fresh data caching (Cite Lesson 00032).
