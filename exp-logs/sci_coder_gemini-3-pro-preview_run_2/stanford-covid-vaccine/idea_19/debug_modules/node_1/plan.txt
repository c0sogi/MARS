The issue lies in the numerical stability epsilon (`1e-8`) used in `library/loss.py` within the `MaskedMCRMSELoss` class. When the Mean Squared Error for a column is exactly zero (as in the unit test case), the term `sqrt(0 + 1e-8)` results in `1e-4`. This introduces a bias of approximately `3.33e-5` to the final loss average, which exceeds the strict tolerance (`1e-5`) of the verification script. To fix this, I will reduce the epsilon to `1e-12`, which results in a bias of `1e-6`, satisfying the assertion requirements while maintaining numerical stability.
