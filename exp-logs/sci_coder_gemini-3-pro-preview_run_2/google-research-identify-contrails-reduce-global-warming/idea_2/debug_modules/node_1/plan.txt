The issue identified is that the `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2` transform, used in `get_transforms`, defaults to `transpose_mask=False`. This means that while images are converted from HWC to CHW format, the segmentation masks remain in HWC format `(256, 256, 1)`. PyTorch models and loss functions expect the channel dimension to be first (CHW), leading to the `AssertionError` when the DataLoader batches the masks.

To fix this, I will update the `get_transforms` function in `library/dataset.py` to initialize `ToTensorV2` with `transpose_mask=True` for both the training and validation/test splits. This ensures that masks are correctly transposed to `(1, 256, 256)` before being yielded by the dataset.
