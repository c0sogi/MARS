To establish a highly efficient baseline for contrail detection, I propose the **Temporal-Difference MobileNet U-Net**. This approach synthesizes the segmentation capability of the U-Net (Model 1) with a lightweight, motion-aware input strategy that avoids the computational cost of processing full temporal sequences (Model 3/4) or heavy 3D stacks.

### Model
The architecture is a **2D U-Net** utilizing a **MobileNetV3-Small** encoder. 
- **Backbone**: MobileNetV3-Small is selected for its extreme efficiency and low parameter count, prioritizing speed over the heavier EfficientNet-B0 used in Model 1. It serves as the encoder to extract multi-scale spatial features.
- **Decoder**: A lightweight decoder composed of bilinear upsampling layers concatenated with skip connections from the encoder, followed by standard convolutional blocks.
- **Input Layer**: The model accepts a **6-channel input tensor** (256x256x6). This is distinct from Model 1 (which stacks all 8 frames) by explicitly engineering temporal features to reduce depth:
    1.  **Channels 1-3 (Static Context)**: The "Ash" false-color composite of the labeled frame ($t=4$). This captures the spectral signature of ice crystals and temperature.
    2.  **Channels 4-6 (Dynamic Context)**: The pixel-wise difference between the Ash composite of the labeled frame ($t=4$) and the previous frame ($t=3$). This explicitly highlights moving boundaries and evolving structures, providing the network with a strong "motion" signal without requiring it to learn temporal dynamics from scratch.

### Data
Data preprocessing focuses on generating the specific 6-channel input efficiently:
1.  **Band Selection**: Load only Bands 11, 14, and 15 for the labeled frame ($t=4$) and the preceding frame ($t=3$).
2.  **Ash Composite Generation**: For both time steps, normalize the raw brightness temperatures and construct the Ash composite:
    -   Red: Band 15 - Band 14 (Optical depth/Ice signature)
    -   Green: Band 14 - Band 11 (Particle phase)
    -   Blue: Band 14 (Temperature)
    -   *Note: Values are linearly scaled to the [0, 1] range based on standard physical bounds.*
3.  **Temporal Differencing**: Compute the element-wise difference: $Input_{diff} = Ash_{t=4} - Ash_{t=3}$.
4.  **Stacking**: Concatenate $Ash_{t=4}$ and $Input_{diff}$ to form the 6-channel input.
5.  **Ground Truth**: Load the binary consensus mask (`human_pixel_masks.npy`).

### Training
The training procedure is designed for stability and convergence speed:
-   **Loss Function**: A combination of **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE)** and **Dice Loss** (e.g., $L = L_{BCE} + L_{Dice}$). BCE stabilizes pixel-level classification, while Dice directly optimizes the competition metric.
-   **Optimizer**: **AdamW** with a standard learning rate (e.g., 1e-3) and weight decay.
-   **Scheduler**: A Cosine Annealing learning rate scheduler to smoothly reduce the learning rate, aiding convergence.
-   **Strategy**: Train for a fixed number of epochs (e.g., 10-20) with Early Stopping based on validation Dice score to prevent overfitting. No heavy augmentations (like MixUp or CutMix) are used, strictly adhering to the baseline philosophy.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: Process test samples to generate the 6-channel input and pass them through the trained model.
2.  **Post-Processing**: Apply a sigmoid activation to the output logits. Threshold the probabilities at **0.5** to generate a binary mask.
3.  **Formatting**: Flatten the binary mask and convert it to Run-Length Encoding (RLE) strings. Empty predictions are marked with `-` as required.
