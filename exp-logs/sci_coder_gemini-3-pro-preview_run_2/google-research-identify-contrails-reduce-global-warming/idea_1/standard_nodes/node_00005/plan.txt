To optimize the performance of the contrail segmentation model, I will implement the following changes based on the provided lessons and analysis:

1.  **Upgrade Model Backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002):** I will replace the `ResNet18` encoder with `ResNet34`. The previous solution showed that increasing capacity (MobileNet -> ResNet18) significantly improved performance. Given the complexity of the task and the available compute time, `ResNet34` provides a better trade-off between capacity and speed than `ResNet18` without the heavy computational cost of `ResNet50`.

2.  **Enhance Augmentation Strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004):** I will add `RandomRotate90` and `Transpose` to the training augmentations. Satellite imagery is rotationally invariant (top-down view), so these affine transformations increase the effective dataset size and diversity without introducing non-linear distortions that could harm the learning of linear contrail features.

3.  **Adjust Training Schedule:** I will increase the training epochs from 20 to 25 in `runfile.py`. The larger model may require slightly more iterations to converge optimally, and the estimated runtime (~60-70 mins) fits comfortably within the 4-hour limit.

4.  **Conditional Submission:** I will update `runfile.py` to check if the final validation Dice score exceeds the previous best (`0.54546...`) before generating the submission file.
