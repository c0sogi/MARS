The issue identified in the traceback is caused by the default behavior of `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2`, which does not transpose masks from HWC to CHW format by default, resulting in a tensor shape of `(256, 256, 1)` instead of the expected `(1, 256, 256)`. To fix this, I will explicitly set `transpose_mask=True` in `ToTensorV2` within `library/dataset.py` (Cite debug_lesson_1). Additionally, to prevent a potential subsequent failure where the model checkpoint is not saved if the validation score remains 0.0, I will initialize the `best_dice` tracker to `-1.0` in `library/train.py` (Cite debug_lesson_2).
