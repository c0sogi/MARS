Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Extended-Kernel ConvNeXt U-Net with Focal-Batch Optimization**.

This strategy synthesizes the "Linearity Prior" success of the Large-Kernel architecture (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`) with a structural evolution in the decoder to further enhance connectivity. It couples this with a refined "Physics-Decoupled" input strategy (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`) and a "Hard-Example" loss formulation to directly attack the False Positive bottleneck identified in **Lesson 2**.

### Model
The architecture is a **U-Net** variant designed to maximize the isotropic receptive field at every stage, specifically targeting the continuity of long, thin contrails.

*   **Backbone (Encoder):** We utilize **ConvNeXt-Tiny** (initialized with ImageNet weights).
    *   **Retention:** We retain the **7x7 Depthwise Convolutions**. As established, this large isotropic receptive field enforces a strong linearity prior, which is superior for contrail segmentation compared to standard 3x3 CNNs (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`) or dilated backbones (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00033`).
*   **Context Bridge:** We retain the **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** module at the bottleneck. This ensures the model captures multi-scale global context, which is critical for distinguishing contrails from background cirrus (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`) and performs better than axis-aligned pooling (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00025`).
*   **Decoder (Structural Innovation: Extended Kernels):**
    *   We employ a standard **Sequential U-Net Decoder** (Stride 32 $\to$ 16 $\to$ ... $\to$ 1) to ensure pixel-level refinement (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00046`).
    *   **Innovation:** We replace the standard convolutional blocks in the decoder with **Extended ConvNeXt Blocks** utilizing **11x11 Depthwise Convolutions**.
    *   **Rationale:** While 7x7 kernels in the encoder are effective, the decoder must reconstruct continuous linear features from low-resolution semantics. By increasing the kernel size to 11x11 in the upsampling path, we significantly expand the physical receptive field, allowing the model to bridge gaps between disjoint contrail segments more effectively than the standard 7x7 or 3x3 counterparts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00036`).
    *   **Upsampling:** We use **Bilinear Upsampling** followed by convolutions, strictly avoiding Transposed Convolutions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00008`).
*   **Attention:** We retain **SCSE (Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation)** blocks after each concatenation to adaptively suppress noise in the skip connections (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`). We explicitly reject Attention Gates (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00055`).

### Data
*   **Input Engineering (Decoupled Physics):** We generate a **6-channel input tensor** that explicitly decouples spectral and temporal signals.
    *   **Channels 1-3 (Spectral):** "Ash" False Color Composite of the labeled frame ($t=4$). This encodes static physical properties (optical depth, temperature) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00001`).
    *   **Channels 4-6 (Temporal):** **Raw Band Differences** ($t=4$ minus $t=3$) for Bands 11, 14, and 15. We explicitly use raw values to preserve the dynamic range of the cooling signal, avoiding the information loss caused by differencing processed composites (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00045`).
*   **Augmentation:** We employ a strict **Affine Transformation** pipeline (Rotation, Scale, Shift, Flip). We explicitly **exclude** elastic, grid, or optical distortions, as these warp the linear morphology of contrails into curve-like shapes that resemble natural clouds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).
*   **Dataset:** We train on the **full dataset** (18,673 images) to ensure the model learns to suppress the "long tail" of background clutter (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`).

### Training
*   **Loss Function (Focal-Batch Hybrid):** We employ a **Focal + Batch-Dice Loss**: $L_{total} = L_{Focal} + L_{BatchDice}$.
    *   **Focal Loss:** We replace the standard BCE with Focal Loss ($\gamma=2.0$). This addresses the "False Positive" bottleneck (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`) by down-weighting easy background examples and forcing the model to focus on hard negatives (cirrus clouds) and sparse positives (contrails).
    *   **Batch-Level Dice:** We compute the Dice score over the **entire flattened batch** (treating the batch as a single volume). This stabilizes gradients and aligns with the global metric, avoiding the instability of sample-level averaging (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`) and the degradation caused by pure Dice loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00018`).
*   **Optimization:** We use **AdamW** with a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
*   **Batch Size:** We utilize a **Batch Size of 32**. This is critical to ensure the statistical stability of the Batch Dice loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00043`).
*   **Duration:** We train for **30 epochs**. We explicitly avoid shortening the training horizon to prevent underfitting on fine-grained details (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference Strategy:** We implement **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**.
    *   For each test image, we generate predictions for the original, horizontally flipped, vertically flipped, and 180-degree rotated versions.
    *   The four probability maps are averaged before thresholding. This ensemble-like approach smooths out prediction noise and boosts the Global Dice score (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00024`).
*   **Post-Processing:** We apply a standard pixel-level threshold of **0.5**. We explicitly reject "Hard Gating" mechanisms (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`).
