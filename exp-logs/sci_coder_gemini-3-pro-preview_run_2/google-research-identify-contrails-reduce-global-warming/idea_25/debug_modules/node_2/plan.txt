This occurs because the `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2` transform defaults to `transpose_mask=False`, leaving the masks in their original HWC format `(256, 256, 1)`, whereas PyTorch expects CHW `(1, 256, 256)`.

To fix this, I will:
1.  Update `get_transforms` in `library/dataset.py` to explicitly use `ToTensorV2(transpose_mask=True)` (Cite `debug_lesson_1`).
2.  Update `_apply_transform` in `library/dataset.py` to explicitly permute the mask dimensions if they remain in HWC format, ensuring robust compatibility (Cite `debug_lesson_6`).
