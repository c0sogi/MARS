The `ValueError: too many values to unpack` is caused by a discrepancy between the `ContrailDataset` output and the iteration loops in `library/train.py` and `runfile.py`. The dataset returns a tuple of 4 elements (`image`, `mask`, `label`, `record_id`), but the loops are configured to unpack only 3. This issue corresponds to **debug_lesson_3**, which emphasizes synchronizing dataset outputs with training loop unpacking when refactoring pipelines. I will update the unpacking logic in `train_one_epoch`, `validate`, and `inference` within `library/train.py`, as well as the validation loop in `runfile.py`, to correctly accept the 4 returned values.
