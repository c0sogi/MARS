The issue is that the metric tracker `best_dice` is initialized to `0.0` in the training loop. When the model achieves a validation Dice score of exactly `0.0` (which happened in the demo run), the condition `if val_dice > best_dice` evaluates to `False`, causing the checkpoint saving logic to be skipped. Consequently, the `best_model.pth` file is never created, leading to an assertion error in the runner script. To fix this, I will initialize `best_dice` to `-1.0` to ensure that the first epoch's model is always saved, even if the score is zero. Cite debug_lesson_2.
