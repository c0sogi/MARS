The `torchvision.models.resnet18` implementation raises a `NotImplementedError` when `replace_stride_with_dilation` is provided because its building block (`BasicBlock`) does not support this argument, unlike the `Bottleneck` block used in ResNet50+. To resolve this while maintaining the desired Output Stride of 8 for the `DilatedResNetUNet`, I will initialize a standard ResNet18 and manually modify the convolutional layers in `layer3` and `layer4` to apply the required stride reduction (from 2 to 1) and dilation (2 and 4, respectively).
