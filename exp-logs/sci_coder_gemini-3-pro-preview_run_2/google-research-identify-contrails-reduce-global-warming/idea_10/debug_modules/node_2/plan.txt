The issue is that the training loop initializes the best score tracker (`best_dice`) to `0.0` and uses a strict inequality (`>`) to determine if the model should be saved. In the debug run, the validation Dice score was exactly `0.0`, causing the condition `0.0 > 0.0` to fail, which prevented the model checkpoint from being created and triggered the `AssertionError`. To fix this, I will initialize `best_dice` to `-1.0` in `library/train.py`, ensuring that even a score of `0.0` is treated as an improvement over the initial state and triggers a save.
