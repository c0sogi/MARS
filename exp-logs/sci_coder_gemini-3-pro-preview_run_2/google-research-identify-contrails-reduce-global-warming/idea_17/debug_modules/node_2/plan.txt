The issue is that the `best_dice` metric tracker is initialized to `0.0`, and the validation score in the first epoch is also `0.0`. The checkpoint saving condition `val_dice > best_dice` evaluates to `False` (`0.0 > 0.0`), resulting in the model never being saved and triggering a `FileNotFoundError` later in the pipeline. Following `debug_lesson_2`, the fix is to initialize `best_dice` to `-1.0` to ensure the first validation score is always captured, even if it is zero.
