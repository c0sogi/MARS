The optimization strategy involves transitioning from a frozen Linear Probe to full **Fine-Tuning** with **Differential Learning Rates**. By unfreezing the ResNet-18 backbone, we allow the model to adapt its feature extraction to the specific domain of pet "Pawpularity," addressing the limitation where the frozen model struggled with high-score outliers. We will use a low learning rate (`1e-5`) for the backbone to preserve ImageNet features and a standard rate (`1e-3`) for the regression head. To mitigate overfitting caused by the increased trainable parameters, we will introduce data augmentation (Horizontal Flip and Affine transforms).
