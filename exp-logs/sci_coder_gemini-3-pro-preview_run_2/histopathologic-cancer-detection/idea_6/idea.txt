Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous iterations and the specific constraints of the task, I propose an optimized solution that structurally evolves the "Deep Ensemble" concept by correcting the pooling mechanism and integrating temporal weight averaging to maximize stability and performance.

### Model
I will implement a **5-Fold Ensemble of ConvNeXt-Tiny** models, enhanced with **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** and **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**.
*   **Backbone**: I will utilize the **ConvNeXt-Tiny** architecture. This backbone has proven to be highly data-efficient and capable of rapid convergence compared to ResNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009), while avoiding the "training inertia" and underfitting issues observed with the larger "Small" variant (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Pooling Mechanism (Correction)**: I will revert to **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** instead of the previously proposed Generalized Mean (GeM) pooling. Empirical evidence demonstrated that for this specific pathology task—where the tumor signal is defined by dense texture rather than sparse focal points—GAP provides superior generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Head Architecture**: I will ensure the **LayerNorm** preceding the classifier is strictly retained to maintain feature scaling, addressing the instability observed when customizing heads (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Model EMA**: I will implement **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** of the model weights for each fold. By maintaining a shadow copy of the model that updates via a moving average of the training weights, we create a "temporal ensemble" that smoothens the decision boundary and improves robustness against the noise introduced by Mixup regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

### Data
The data pipeline will strictly adhere to the "Augment-then-Crop" paradigm to maximize context usage and minimize artifacts.
1.  **Normalization**: I will use **dataset-specific mean and standard deviation** to align with the pathology scans' dynamic range (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
2.  **Augmentation Pipeline**:
    *   **Global Augmentation**: Apply geometric (flips, rotations) and intensity (ColorJitter) augmentations to the full **96x96px** source patch. This ensures intensity statistics are derived from the global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005) and prevents rotation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    *   **Contextual Crop**: Perform a **Center Crop to 64x64px**. This preserves the 32x32px ROI and a 16px context buffer while removing the irrelevant outer rim, preventing spatial label noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

### Training
The training strategy is designed to prioritize full convergence for every model in the ensemble, leveraging the 24-hour runtime budget which was previously underutilized.
*   **Ensemble Structure**: I will train **5 independent models** using 5-Fold Cross-Validation. Unlike previous attempts that compromised training duration to fit the ensemble (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014), I will train each fold to full convergence.
*   **Regularization**: I will apply **Mixup** (alpha=0.2) combined with high **Weight Decay** (e.g., 0.05). This combination allows the ConvNeXt architecture to generalize better than Label Smoothing alone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
    *   **Learning Rate**: Use a **conservative learning rate** (e.g., 2e-4) to preserve pre-trained feature extractors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Duration**: Each fold will be trained for **20-25 epochs**. This ensures the model has sufficient iterations to digest the dataset complexity, avoiding the performance regression caused by schedule compression (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### Evaluation
The evaluation process maximizes robustness by integrating spatial and temporal ensembling with geometric invariance.
*   **Inference**: Predictions will be generated using the **EMA weights** of the models, which offer superior generalization to raw weights.
*   **TTA**: I will implement **8-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**, averaging predictions across the original image and its 7 Dihedral variants (rotations and flips) to exploit rotational invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Aggregation**: The final submission will be the average of **40 probabilities** per image (5 folds $\times$ 8 TTA views). This extensive averaging marginalizes out model variance and orientation noise, resulting in a highly calibrated prediction.
