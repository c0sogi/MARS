Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **Memory-Resident Heterogeneous Ensemble** pipeline. This structural innovation leverages the available RAM to eliminate I/O bottlenecks, enabling the training of a computationally intensive, fully-converged heterogeneous ensemble that was previously infeasible within the runtime limit.

### Model
I will implement a **Heterogeneous Deep Ensemble** composed of **ConvNeXt-Tiny** and **EfficientNetV2-Small** backbones.
*   **Structural Heterogeneity**: By combining ConvNeXt (which uses isotropic, Transformer-style blocks) and EfficientNetV2 (which uses hierarchical Fused-MBConv blocks), the ensemble will exploit diverse inductive biases to decorrelate errors.
*   **Pooling & Head Design**: Both architectures will utilize **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**. I will explicitly retain the backbone-specific normalization layers (e.g., `LayerNorm` for ConvNeXt, `BatchNorm` for EfficientNet) immediately preceding the classifier. This corrects the instability observed when stripping default heads (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) and adheres to the finding that GAP outperforms focal pooling for this texture-based task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Corrected EMA**: I will implement **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** with a decay rate strictly tuned to the training duration (e.g., $\beta=0.99$ for 25 epochs). This addresses the failure of previous attempts where a high decay rate ($\beta=0.9999$) caused the EMA model to retain initialization noise due to the short schedule (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Data
The data pipeline will be fundamentally restructured into a **Memory-Resident Isotropic Pipeline** to maximize throughput and geometric invariance.
1.  **In-Memory Caching**: I will load the entire training dataset (~140k images) into system RAM at the start of execution. This eliminates disk I/O latency, reducing epoch time by ~40% (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026) and buying the computational budget required for the ensemble.
2.  **Isotropic Augmentation Pipeline**:
    *   **Step 1 (Global Context)**: Load the full **96x96px** source patch.
    *   **Step 2 (Geometric Completeness)**: Apply **Random Flip** (Horizontal and Vertical) followed by **Continuous Random Rotation** ($[-180^{\circ}, 180^{\circ}]$). This corrects the critical flaw in previous solutions that omitted flips (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026) or used discrete rotations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021), ensuring the model learns full Dihedral invariance.
    *   **Step 3 (Intensity)**: Apply **ColorJitter** to the transformed 96x96px patch to derive statistics from the global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
    *   **Step 4 (Contextual Crop)**: Perform a **Center Crop to 64x64px** as the final step. This preserves the 32x32px ROI and a 16px context buffer while removing rotation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
3.  **Normalization**: Use **dataset-specific mean and standard deviation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

### Training
The training strategy prioritizes **Full Convergence** for every model in the ensemble, utilizing the time saved by in-memory caching.
*   **Ensemble Structure**: I will train **10 independent models** (5-Fold Cross-Validation for ConvNeXt + 5-Fold Cross-Validation for EfficientNet).
*   **Regularization**: Apply **Mixup** (alpha=0.2) combined with high **Weight Decay** (e.g., 0.05). This recipe is essential for modern architectures to generalize well (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization**:
    *   **Optimizer**: AdamW with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
    *   **Duration**: Train each model for **25 epochs**. This extended duration ensures the models overcome "training inertia" and fully converge, correcting the underfitting caused by schedule compression in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00014).
    *   **Learning Rate**: Use a conservative learning rate (e.g., 2e-4) to preserve pre-trained features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

### Evaluation
The evaluation process maximizes robustness by integrating architectural, spatial, and geometric ensembling.
*   **Inference**: Predictions will be generated using the **Corrected EMA weights** of all 10 models.
*   **TTA**: I will implement **8-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**, averaging predictions across the original image and its 7 Dihedral variants (rotations and flips). This marginalizes out any remaining orientation-specific noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Aggregation**: The final submission will be the average of **80 probabilities** per image (10 models $\times$ 8 TTA views).
