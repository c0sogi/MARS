Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous iterations and the specific constraints of the task, I propose an optimized solution that leverages the high data efficiency of modern architectures while utilizing the available compute budget to minimize prediction variance through ensembling.

### Model
I will implement a **ConvNeXt-Tiny** architecture with a **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** head.
*   **Backbone**: I will retain the **ConvNeXt-Tiny** backbone. Previous experiments demonstrated that this architecture offers superior data efficiency and convergence speed compared to ResNet (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009), while scaling up to "Small" introduced detrimental training inertia under limited epoch constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Structural Innovation (GeM Pooling)**: Instead of the standard Global Average Pooling (GAP), I will replace the final pooling layer with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. In binary tumor classification, the presence of tumor tissue might be localized to a small portion of the 32x32px ROI. Standard GAP dilutes this signal by averaging it with the healthy background. GeM pooling introduces a learnable parameter $p$ (where $p \to \infty$ approximates Max Pooling and $p=1$ is Average Pooling), allowing the network to dynamically tune its sensitivity to high-activation features (tumor presence) versus global texture.
*   **Ensemble Structure**: To fully exploit the 24-hour runtime limit and the A100 GPU without risking the optimization difficulties of a single massive model, I will implement a **5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensemble**. Each of the 5 models will be trained on a distinct 80% subset of the data. This structural change reduces generalization error and variance more effectively than simply increasing model depth.

### Data
The data pipeline will strictly adhere to the "Augment-then-Crop" paradigm to maximize context usage and minimize artifacts.
1.  **Normalization**: I will calculate and use **dataset-specific mean and standard deviation** for normalization to align with the pathology scans' dynamic range (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
2.  **Augmentation Pipeline**:
    *   **Global Augmentation**: Apply geometric (flips, rotations) and intensity (ColorJitter) augmentations to the full **96x96px** source patch. This ensures color statistics are derived from the global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005) and prevents rotation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    *   **Contextual Crop**: Perform a **Center Crop to 64x64px**. This preserves the 32x32px ROI and a 16px context buffer while removing the irrelevant outer rim (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
3.  **Regularization**: I will apply **Mixup** (alpha=0.2) to the training batches. This encourages the model to learn linear interpolations between classes, preventing memorization and improving robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
The training procedure is designed to ensure full convergence for each fold in the ensemble.
*   **Optimizer & Schedule**: Use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Duration**: Train each fold for **20 epochs**. This ensures the model has sufficient iterations to converge, avoiding the under-fitting observed when compressing the schedule (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Hyperparameters**:
    *   **Learning Rate**: Use a conservative learning rate (e.g., 2e-4) to preserve pre-trained feature extraction capabilities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Weight Decay**: Use a high weight decay (e.g., 0.05) to complement the Mixup regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **EMA**: Implement **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** for each fold to smooth the weights and improve validation stability.

### Evaluation
The evaluation strategy maximizes the exploitation of the model's learned invariances and the ensemble's diversity.
*   **Inference**: For each test image, predictions will be generated by all **5 models** in the ensemble.
*   **TTA**: For each model, I will implement **8-view Test Time Augmentation (TTA)** (combinations of 4 rotations and 2 flips). This marginalizes out orientation-specific noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Aggregation**: The final submission probability will be the average of **40 predictions** per image (5 models $\times$ 8 views). This extensive averaging is computationally feasible within the 24-hour limit (estimated < 4 hours total) and will yield a highly robust prediction.
