My implementation strategy focuses on two key optimizations to surpass the high validation AUC threshold of ~0.9821.

1.  **Expanded Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** Building on **Lesson 2 (Exploiting Rotational Invariance)**, I will expand the TTA pipeline from 4 views to 8 views. The previous solution used flips and 180-degree rotations. I will implement the full D4 dihedral group of symmetries (Identity, Rot90, Rot180, Rot270, and their horizontally flipped counterparts). This ensures the model's predictions are fully invariant to rotation and reflection, which is critical for pathology patches where orientation is arbitrary.

2.  **Label Smoothing & Extended Training:** To improve generalization and calibration, I will introduce **Label Smoothing (0.05)** into the training loop. This prevents the model from becoming overconfident on the training data. I will also slightly increase the training duration from 10 to 12 epochs to allow the model to converge fully with this regularization, while keeping the `patience` for early stopping proportional.

These changes are targeted to squeeze out the remaining performance gains needed without changing the core architecture or exceeding the runtime constraints.
