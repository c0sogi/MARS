The lessons indicate that TAPT on a subset is ineffective compared to fully converging the supervised model, and that a single epoch is insufficient for robust decision boundaries even with preserved step counts.

The implementation involves modifying `runfile.py` to:
1.  Increase `Config.TRAIN_PARAMS["epochs"]` to 2.
2.  Remove the TAPT execution block.
3.  Configure the supervised training calls to use standard pre-trained weights (`pretrained_path=None`) instead of looking for TAPT artifacts.

This targeted change optimizes the training schedule to maximize the ROC AUC score within the time limit.
