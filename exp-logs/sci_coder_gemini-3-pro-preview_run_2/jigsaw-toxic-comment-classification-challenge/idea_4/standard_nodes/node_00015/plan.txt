The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` occurs because the **Gradient Checkpointing** mechanism (enabled on the backbone) conflicts with the **Multi-Sample Dropout** head during the backward pass. Gradient checkpointing re-computes forward pass activations during backpropagation, but the multiple branches of the dropout head cause the autograd engine to attempt to traverse the graph in a way that requires retained tensors which have been freed or re-computed. Since the available hardware (NVIDIA A100 40GB) has sufficient VRAM to train the `base` models without this optimization, I will disable gradient checkpointing in `library/models.py` to resolve the graph traversal error.
