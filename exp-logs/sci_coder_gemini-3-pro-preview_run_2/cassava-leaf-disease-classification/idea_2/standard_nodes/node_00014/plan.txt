1.  **Enhance Data Augmentation**: In `library/dataset.py`, I will replace `transforms.Resize` with `transforms.RandomResizedCrop` for the training split. This will force the model to learn robust, scale-invariant features rather than memorizing the global composition of training images (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
2.  **Adjust Training Duration**: In `runfile.py`, I will increase the number of epochs from 5 to 10. Stronger geometric augmentation increases the difficulty of the training task, requiring more iterations for the model to converge. Given the A100 GPU availability, this increase fits comfortably within the time limits.
3.  **Update Configuration**: I will reflect the epoch change in `library/config.py` for consistency.
