My optimization strategy focuses on correcting the slice alignment method and improving data loading efficiency based on the provided lessons.

1.  **Absolute ROI Indexing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00024`):** The previous solution calculated the "anchor" slice in the FLAIR modality and converted it to a relative ratio (e.g., 50% depth) to apply to other modalities. As Lesson 00024 highlights, this fails when modalities have different Fields of View (FOV) but similar resolutions. I will modify `library/data_loader.py` to cache and use the **absolute integer index** of the anchor slice. This ensures that if the tumor is at slice 50 in FLAIR, the model looks at slice 50 in T1w/T2w, preserving anatomical alignment better than relative mapping.
2.  **Parallel Data Loading (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00030`):** I will increase `num_workers` from the default (2) to 4 in `runfile.py` to better utilize the available vCPUs and hide I/O latency, as suggested by Lesson 00030.
