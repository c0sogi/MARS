To optimize the model's performance, I will implement a strategy focused on correcting architectural inconsistencies and refining the training objective based on previous lessons.

1.  **Homogeneous Modality Grouping**: I will restructure the input tensor construction in `library/data.py` to stack slices by modality (e.g., Group 1: FLAIR slices, Group 2: T1w slices) rather than mixing modalities within groups. This ensures that the grouped convolutions in the stem process semantically consistent features, adhering to the "Rule of 3" and avoiding the degradation observed when mixing spectral and spatial features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00076).
2.  **Disable Label Smoothing**: I will set `LABEL_SMOOTHING` to 0.0 in `library/config.py`. In low-signal tasks like this, label smoothing dampens the gradient signal for the few correctly classified examples, hindering the model's ability to learn a sharp decision boundary (Cite solution_lesson_node_00077).
3.  **Normalized ROI Selection**: I will modify the `calculate_anchor` function in `library/data.py` to sum pixel intensities *after* normalization. This replicates the specific preprocessing step of the high-performing "Current Best" solution, which likely helped in selecting more robust anchor slices compared to raw intensity sums (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
4.  **Cache Invalidation**: I will update the cache path to ensure the ROI anchors are recomputed using the new normalized logic.
