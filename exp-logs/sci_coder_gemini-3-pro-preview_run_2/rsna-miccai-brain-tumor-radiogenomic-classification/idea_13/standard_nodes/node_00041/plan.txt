My implementation strategy focuses on two critical optimizations derived from the provided lessons:

1.  **Learning Rate Reduction**: The previous run used a learning rate of `1e-3`, which resulted in overfitting (Training AUC ~0.80, Validation AUC ~0.53). Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00040` explicitly states that a lower learning rate of `1e-4` is crucial for fine-tuning on this small dataset, improving Validation AUC from ~0.528 to 0.625. I will reduce the learning rate in `runfile.py` and `library/train.py`.

2.  **Absolute ROI Indexing**: The current solution calculates a relative anchor position (0.0-1.0) in the FLAIR modality and applies that ratio to other modalities. Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00024` demonstrates that this "relative alignment" degrades performance because field-of-view differences cause anatomical misalignment. I will modify `library/data_loader.py` to store and use the **absolute** slice index from the FLAIR modality across all channels, ensuring anatomical consistency. This involves updating the cache filename to `roi_cache_abs.parquet` to force regeneration with the new integer indices.
