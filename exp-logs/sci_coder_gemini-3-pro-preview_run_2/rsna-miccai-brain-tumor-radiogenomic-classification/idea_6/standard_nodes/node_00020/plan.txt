Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, the primary cause of the performance degradation in Idea 6 was the introduction of a "Center-Focused" ROI selection heuristic. **Lesson 00019** explicitly warns against aggressive spatial cropping (like center crops) for variable-location pathologies like glioblastoma, as it can inadvertently filter out the target signal if the tumor is peripheral.

To address this and restore/improve performance (targeting the ~0.625 AUC of Idea 2), I will:

1.  **Modify `library/config.py`**: Update the working directory to `./working/idea_7` to ensure a fresh cache is generated. Remove the `CENTER_CROP_RATIO` parameter.
2.  **Modify `library/data_loader.py`**:
    *   Remove the import of `CENTER_CROP_RATIO`.
    *   Update `compute_anchor_ratio` to calculate the mean intensity of the **entire slice** instead of a center crop (Cite **Lesson 00019**). This robust global heuristic ensures that the slice with the most significant signal (tumor/edema) is selected regardless of its spatial location within the brain.
    *   Retain the `EXCLUDE_TOP_BOTTOM_RATIO` (Cite **Lesson 00018**) to avoid selecting artifacts at the scan boundaries.

This strategy leverages the robust "2.5D" architecture (EfficientNet-B0 with Grouped Convolutions, Cite **Lesson 00007**) and geometric augmentations (Cite **Lesson 00015**) that were proven effective in Idea 2, while correcting the data selection flaw introduced in Idea 6.
