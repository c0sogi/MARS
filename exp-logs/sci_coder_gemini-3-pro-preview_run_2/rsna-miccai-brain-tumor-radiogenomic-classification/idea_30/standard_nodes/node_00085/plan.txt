My strategy to improve performance focuses on adhering to the provided lessons to mitigate overfitting and improve data quality:

1.  **Refine ROI Selection (Cite Lesson 00053, 00038)**: I will replace the "Hybrid-Integral" selection (FLAIR + T1wCE) with a **FLAIR-Only Integral** selection. 2.  **Optimize Augmentation (Cite Lesson 00048)**: I will change the rotation padding mode from `BORDER_CONSTANT` (zero-padding) to `BORDER_REFLECT`. 3.  **Hierarchical Data Ingestion (Cite Lesson 00039)**: I will modify `read_dicom_image` to attempt reading with `cv2` *before* falling back to the raw binary heuristic. 4.  **Regularization**: To combat the rapid overfitting observed (Training AUC rising while Validation AUC drops), I will increase the classifier `dropout_rate` from 0.2 to 0.5.
