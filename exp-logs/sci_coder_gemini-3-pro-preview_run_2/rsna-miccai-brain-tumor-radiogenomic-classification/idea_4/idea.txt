Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, I propose the **Self-Distilled 2.5D ROI Network**. This solution structurally evolves the "Content-Adaptive" approach by integrating a **Dual-View Consistency Training** paradigm. This addresses the instability observed in heuristic-based sampling (Cite 00005) while strictly adhering to input parsimony (Cite 00002) and avoiding the noise injection of global/geometric views (Cite 00009).

### Model: Grouped-Stem EfficientNet-B0
We will utilize **EfficientNet-B0** as the backbone due to its proven efficiency (Cite 00001), with two critical structural modifications:
1.  **Modality-Grouped Stem:** The first convolutional layer will be configured with **Grouped Convolutions** (`groups=4`). This forces the network to learn independent feature representations for each of the 4 MRI modalities (FLAIR, T1w, T1wCE, T2w) in the initial stage, acting as a structural regularizer (Cite 00007).
2.  **Robust Initialization:** We will explicitly **replicate the pre-trained RGB weights** four times to initialize this 12-channel stem. This ensures that edge/texture detectors are preserved for all modalities, avoiding the instability of random initialization (Cite 00006).

### Data: Stochastic ROI Consistency Generation
To solve the "Instability" of the Content-Adaptive approach (Cite 00005) without reverting to the inferior Geometric sampling (Cite 00004), we will implement a data pipeline that generates **two correlated views** for each patient during training:
1.  **Anchor Identification:** For every scan, we calculate the signal intensity profile of the **FLAIR** modality to identify the "Peak Intensity" slice index (Cite 00005).
2.  **View A (Primary):** A 12-channel stack constructed using the Peak Index as the center, with a **Stride of 5** (Cite 00010).
3.  **View B (Jittered):** A second 12-channel stack constructed by applying **Spatial Jitter** (shifting the center by $\pm 1-2$ slices) and **Stride Jitter** (randomly selecting a stride between 4 and 6).
4.  **Parsimony:** Both views strictly contain only **3 slices per modality** (12 channels total) to prevent overfitting via input inflation (Cite 00002).

### Training: Dual-Stream Consistency Learning
The training loop is structurally altered to enforce robustness against slice selection noise:
*   **Forward Pass:** Both *View A* and *View B* are passed through the model (sharing weights).
*   **Loss Function:** We employ a composite loss: $L = L_{BCE}(y, \hat{y}_A) + L_{BCE}(y, \hat{y}_B) + \lambda \cdot L_{MSE}(\hat{y}_A, \hat{y}_B)$.
    *   The **Consistency Term** ($L_{MSE}$) forces the model to produce invariant predictions despite small anatomical shifts (Jitter), directly counteracting the brittleness of the max-intensity heuristic.
*   **Regularization:** We will maintain **aggressive Weight Decay** (`1e-2`) (Cite 00011) and strictly limit augmentation to geometric transforms (flips/rotations), avoiding destructive techniques like Cutout (Cite 00003).

### Evaluation
For inference on the test set, we will use **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** to leverage the trained consistency:
1.  Generate 3 input tensors for the subject using the FLAIR Peak Intensity as the anchor, with Strides of **4, 5, and 6**.
2.  Pass all 3 tensors through the model.
3.  Average the probabilities to produce the final `MGMT_value`. This ensures the prediction is not dependent on a single, potentially noisy slice selection.
