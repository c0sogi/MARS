To address the Steganography Detection task, I propose an optimized strategy that builds upon the successful residual-extraction paradigm while introducing architectural enhancements to better capture localized signals and improve training efficiency.

### Model
The architecture is designed to prioritize the extraction of high-frequency noise residuals while maximizing the receptive field and signal retention.
*   **SRM Residual Stem:** The network will commence with the **SRM Conv layer**, consisting of 30 fixed, non-trainable high-pass filters (5x5 kernels). This component is retained to suppress semantic image content and maximize the Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) of the steganographic artifacts immediately at the input level. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}.
*   **Backbone Upgrade (EfficientNetV2-Small):** I propose upgrading the backbone from EfficientNet-B2 to **EfficientNetV2-Small**. EfficientNetV2 utilizes Fused-MBConv layers that are optimized for modern accelerators (like the A100), offering faster training throughput than the B-series. This allows us to utilize a higher-capacity model (addressing the need for capacity in low-SNR tasks) without sacrificing the ability to run extended training schedules within the 24-hour limit. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}.
*   **GeM Pooling Head:** Instead of standard Global Average Pooling (GAP), which risks "washing out" faint, localized steganographic signals by averaging them with benign background noise, I will introduce a **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** layer. GeM pooling includes a learnable parameter $p$ (where $p=1$ is Average Pooling and $p \to \infty$ is Max Pooling). This allows the network to dynamically tune its focus towards regions with high activation (suspicious noise), effectively acting as a soft attention mechanism for anomaly detection.

### Data
*   **Dynamic Pairing Strategy:** The training data loader will enforce a strict paired sampling regime. For every epoch, each unique Cover image is loaded and paired with exactly one of its three corresponding Stego variants (JMiPOD, JUNIWARD, or UERD), selected at random. This forces the model to learn the differential noise patterns rather than memorizing specific image content. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}.
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be ingested at full resolution (512x512) and normalized to [0, 1].
*   **Augmentation:** "D4" augmentations (random 90-degree rotations and horizontal/vertical flips) will be applied during training to exploit rotational invariance.

### Training
*   **Prolonged Convergence Schedule:** Recognizing that low-SNR features require significantly more iterations to converge than semantic features, the training duration will be extended to **40 epochs**. This builds on the finding that extending training from 8 to 25 epochs yielded substantial gains. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00005}.
*   **Optimization:** The model will be optimized using **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler. Crucially, the scheduler's `T_max` will be dynamically set to match the extended epoch count to ensure the learning rate decays correctly to a fine-grained minimum (e.g., 1e-6) by the end of training. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00004}.
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross Entropy with Logits Loss will be used as the objective function.

### Evaluation
*   **5-View Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, predictions will be generated for the original image, horizontal flip, vertical flip, and 90/270-degree rotations. The final score will be the average of these 5 views. This strategy has been proven to boost the Weighted AUC by leveraging the model's robustness to geometric transformations. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00005}.
*   **Metric Monitoring:** The Weighted AUC (with the 0.0-0.4 TPR region weighted 2x) will be calculated on the validation set to select the best checkpoint, ignoring minor fluctuations below the noise floor (<0.001). Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}.
