Based on the analysis of previous attempts and the specific lessons learned, I propose a **Tri-Modal Heterogeneous Stacking Strategy with Intra-Fold Model Soups**.

This solution structurally evolves the successful heterogeneous ensemble (Idea 10) by integrating a third distinct architecture (MBConv) to maximize feature diversity and incorporating "Model Soups" (Weight Averaging) to minimize the variance of the base learners. It corrects the implementation flaw of Lesson 26 (which failed due to data truncation) by applying the strategy to the full dataset.

### Model
I propose a **Tri-Modal Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of three high-capacity backbones that leverage distinct inductive biases:
1.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** A modern pure CNN that excels at hierarchical feature extraction **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.
2.  **Swin-Small:** A Hierarchical Vision Transformer that captures global dependencies via shifted-window attention **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.
3.  **EfficientNetV2-Small:** An MBConv (Mobile Inverted Bottleneck) architecture with extensive Squeeze-and-Excitation. This adds a third, distinct representation to the ensemble, further decorrelating errors compared to the bi-modal approach.

**Structural Innovation (Soup-then-Stack):**
Instead of standard ensembling, we will implement a two-stage aggregation pipeline:
1.  **Intra-Fold Model Soups:** For every fold of every architecture, we will not rely on a single "best" checkpoint. Instead, we will average the weights of the checkpoints saved from the final 3 epochs of training. This creates a "Soup" model that sits in a flatter minimum of the loss landscape, improving generalization without increasing inference cost **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.
2.  **Meta-Learner Stacking:** We will generate Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions using these robust "Soup" models. These OOF logits will be fed into a **Logistic Regression** meta-learner to optimize the final ensemble weights specifically for Log Loss **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** All images will be resized to **224x224** pixels. We strictly adhere to this resolution to ensure we can train all 15 models (3 architectures $\times$ 5 folds) to full convergence within the 24-hour budget, avoiding the under-training pitfalls of higher resolutions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Dataset:** We will utilize the **full training dataset** (18,000+ images). We explicitly reject the data truncation used in the failed Lesson 26 experiment and the pseudo-labeling used in Idea 11, as the latter proved detrimental in this label-rich regime **Cite solution_lesson_node_00024**.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **RandomResizedCrop:** We will strictly enforce a minimum scale of **0.8**. This prevents the semantic noise introduced by aggressive cropping, ensuring the subject remains the focus **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
    *   **Regularization:** We will apply **Mixup** (`alpha=0.2`) and **CutMix** (`alpha=1.0`) stochastically. This is critical for preventing overconfidence and ensuring the probability calibration required for minimizing Log Loss **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.

### Training
*   **Strategy:** We will implement **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** for each of the 3 architectures.
*   **Optimization:** We will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate** scheduler.
*   **Convergence & Souping:** Each fold will be trained for **20 epochs**. We will **disable early stopping** (or set patience to the full duration) to allow the scheduler to fully decay the learning rate **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**. We will save checkpoints at epochs 18, 19, and 20 to construct the Model Soup for that fold.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For the test set, we will generate predictions using the 15 "Soup" models (5 ConvNeXt + 5 Swin + 5 EfficientNetV2).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each Soup model, we will average the predictions of the original and a horizontally flipped version to further reduce variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
*   **Submission:** The TTA-averaged predictions will be fed into the trained Logistic Regression meta-learner to produce the final calibrated probabilities.
