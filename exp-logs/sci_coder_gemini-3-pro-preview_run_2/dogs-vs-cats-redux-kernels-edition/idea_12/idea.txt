Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose a **Tri-Modal Heterogeneous Stacking Strategy with Model Soups**.

This solution evolves the successful heterogeneous ensemble from Idea 10 (Lesson 23) by expanding the architectural diversity to a third distinct inductive bias (MBConv) and introducing "Model Soups" (Weight Averaging) to improve the generalization and calibration of the base learners. It explicitly rejects the Semi-Supervised approach (Idea 11) which failed due to the label-rich nature of the dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Model
I propose a **Tri-Modal Heterogeneous Ensemble** composed of three distinct high-capacity backbones.
1.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** Represents modern pure Convolutional architectures (Retained from **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
2.  **Swin-Small:** Represents Hierarchical Vision Transformers, capturing global dependencies (Retained from **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
3.  **EfficientNetV2-Small:** Represents **MBConv (Mobile Inverted Bottleneck)** architectures with extensive Squeeze-and-Excitation. This adds a third, distinct inductive bias to the ensemble, further decorrelating errors and maximizing the utility of stacking.
*   **Weight Averaging (Structural Innovation):** For each fold of each architecture, we will implement **Model Soups (Uniform Averaging)**. Instead of using a single "best" checkpoint, we will average the weights of the checkpoints from the final 3 epochs of training. This technique finds a flatter minimum in the loss landscape, improving robustness and calibration on unseen data without additional inference cost.
*   **Meta-Learner:** A **Logistic Regression** model will be trained on the concatenated Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions from all base models to optimize the final ensemble weights for Log Loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).

### Data
*   **Resolution:** All images will be processed at **224x224** pixels. We strictly adhere to this resolution to ensure we can afford the computational cost of training 15 high-capacity models to full convergence, avoiding the under-training pitfalls of higher resolutions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **RandomResizedCrop:** We will strictly enforce a minimum scale of **0.8**. This preserves the semantic integrity of the object, preventing the noise introduced by aggressive cropping (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).
    *   **Regularization:** We will apply **Mixup** (alpha=0.2) and **CutMix** (alpha=1.0) stochastically. This is essential for preventing overconfidence and ensuring the probability calibration required for minimizing Log Loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
    *   **HorizontalFlip:** Standard geometric invariance.

### Training
*   **Strategy:** We will implement **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** for *each* of the 3 architectures, resulting in **15 trained models**. This ensures robust OOF generation for the meta-learner (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).
*   **Optimization:** We will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate** scheduler.
*   **Convergence:** Each fold will be trained for **20 epochs**. This extended duration ensures that the "Small" capacity models fully converge. We will **disable early stopping** (or set patience to 20) to allow the scheduler to fully decay the learning rate, ensuring the models settle into the global minimum (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).
*   **Compute Feasibility:** Based on previous runtimes (~50 mins for 10 models at short duration), scaling to 15 models for 20 epochs is estimated to take ~12-14 hours on the A100, fitting comfortably within the 24-hour limit.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For the test set, we will generate predictions using all 15 models (using their averaged weights).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will apply horizontal flip TTA for each base model, averaging the prediction of the original and flipped image to reduce variance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**).
*   **Stacking:** The TTA-averaged predictions from the 15 base models will be fed into the Logistic Regression meta-learner (trained on the OOF logits) to generate the final calibrated probability for submission.
