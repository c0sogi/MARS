To optimize the technosignature detection performance, I will implement a strategy focused on better signal extraction and robust training, guided by the provided lessons.

1.  **Architecture Upgrade (EfficientNet + MaxPool)**: I will replace the ResNet18 backbone with **EfficientNet-B0**. EfficientNet is generally more effective at capturing fine-grained features in spectrograms. Crucially, I will change the global pooling layer from Average to **Max Pooling**. As the "needle" signals are sparse anomalies (thin diagonal lines) in a large field of noise, Average Pooling tends to dilute the signal, whereas Max Pooling preserves the strongest activations corresponding to the signal presence.
2.  **Data Augmentation (Horizontal Flip)**: I will introduce random **Horizontal Flipping** (frequency inversion) during training. Since Doppler drift can be positive or negative (approaching or receding relative motion), flipping the frequency axis creates physically valid synthetic samples, effectively doubling the training data diversity without breaking the temporal cadence structure (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
3.  **Training Regime Optimization**:
    *   I will **disable the DEBUG mode** to train on the full dataset (43k samples) instead of the 10k subset, as the previous model failed to converge on the subset and we have sufficient compute time (approx. 1 hour for full training).
    *   I will lower the **Learning Rate to 1e-4** to ensure stable convergence with the new architecture.
    *   I will increase **Epochs to 10** to allow the model sufficient time to learn from the full dataset.
