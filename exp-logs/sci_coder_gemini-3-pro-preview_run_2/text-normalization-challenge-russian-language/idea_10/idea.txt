Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the progression of previous ideas, I propose the **Confidence-Gated Hybrid with Class-Balanced Semiotic Training**.

This strategy synthesizes the stability of the statistical baseline (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}) with the generalization power of neural networks, specifically addressing the "Data Density" failure of previous hybrid attempts (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}) and the "Distribution Mismatch" of residual training (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00013}).

# Model

The solution employs a **Two-Tier Cascade** architecture that routes tokens based on **Granularity** and **Confidence**.

1.  **Tier 1: Confidence-Aware Memory Engine (HFBB)**
    *   **Structure:** A 4-level backoff hierarchy: **Trigram $\rightarrow$ Bigram (Prev) $\rightarrow$ Bigram (Next) $\rightarrow$ Unigram**.
    *   **Innovation:** The **Unigram** layer explicitly stores a **Confidence Score** ($P(\text{Mode}) = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{Mode})}{\text{Total Count}}$).
    *   **Role:** This tier resolves all context-dependent matches (Trigrams/Bigrams) with high precision (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00006}). It also resolves "stable" context-free tokens (e.g., "2012" $\to$ "две тысячи двенадцатый", Confidence $\approx$ 1.0) while flagging "ambiguous" tokens (e.g., "2", Confidence < 0.9) for the neural network.

2.  **Tier 2: Semiotic Transformer (Char-to-Subword)**
    *   **Architecture:** A standard Encoder-Decoder Transformer (e.g., comparable to T5-Small or BART).
    *   **Input:** **Character-level** tokenization of the target token and its surrounding words (`Prev_Word <SEP> Target_Chars <SEP> Next_Word`). Character-level input is crucial for parsing the internal structure of numbers and symbols.
    *   **Output:** **Subword (BPE)** tokenization of the normalized text. This ensures the output adheres to valid Russian morphology and vocabulary.
    *   **Role:** To generate normalizations for **OOV Semiotics** (unseen numbers) and **Ambiguous Semiotics** (where Tier 1 lacks confidence).

# Data

The critical innovation is the **Class-Balanced Semiotic Dataset** construction, which solves the conflict between "learning grammar" (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00014}) and "focusing on hard cases" (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00013}), while ensuring sufficient data density (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00026}).

1.  **HFBB Data:**
    *   Computed on the full training corpus.

2.  **Transformer Data:**
    *   **Filtering:** Extract *all* tokens from the training set that belong to **Semiotic Classes** (e.g., `DATE`, `MONEY`, `CARDINAL`, `ORDINAL`, `DECIMAL`, `TELEPHONE`, `LETTERS`) or contain digits/special characters. Exclude `PLAIN` and `PUNCT`.
    *   **Rationale:** This removes the 90% of "easy" text that causes distribution mismatch (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00013}) but retains the full spectrum of semiotic examples (from simple "1" to complex "$3.50") to preserve the "grammar" of normalization (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00014}).
    *   **Class Balancing:** Compute the frequency of each semiotic class. **Upsample** the rare, high-error classes (specifically `MONEY`, `DECIMAL`, `TELEPHONE`) to match the frequency of the dominant class (usually `CARDINAL` or `DATE`).
    *   **Effect:** This forces the model to dedicate equal capacity to learning the complex rules of rare entities (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00002}) rather than overfitting to simple years/integers.

# Training

1.  **Tier 1 (HFBB):**
    *   Single-pass Maximum Likelihood Estimation.

2.  **Tier 2 (Transformer):**
    *   **Objective:** Minimize Cross-Entropy Loss on the target BPE sequence.
    *   **Strategy:** Train from scratch on the **Class-Balanced Semiotic Dataset**.
    *   **Optimization:** Use `AdamW` with a linear warmup and cosine decay scheduler.
    *   **Regularization:** Apply Dropout (0.1) and Label Smoothing (0.1) to improve generalization on unseen numbers.

# Evaluation

The inference pipeline strictly follows the **"Exhaust Lookup"** principle (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}) with a safety valve for ambiguity.

1.  **Step 1 (Context Match):** Query HFBB Trigram $\rightarrow$ Bigram(Prev) $\rightarrow$ Bigram(Next).
    *   If Match Found: **Return Prediction**. (These are specific, high-precision context hits).
2.  **Step 2 (Unigram Check):** Query HFBB Unigram.
    *   If Match Found **AND** `Confidence > Threshold` (e.g., 0.95): **Return Prediction**. (Stable tokens like "2012" are handled instantly).
    *   If Match Found **AND** `Confidence <= Threshold` **AND** `Is_Semiotic`: **Route to Tier 2**. (Ambiguous tokens like "2" need neural context).
3.  **Step 3 (OOV/Fallback):**
    *   If `Is_Semiotic` (contains `\d` or `[a-zA-Z]`): **Route to Tier 2**. (Unseen numbers/money).
    *   Else: **Identity Fallback**. (Unknown proper nouns).

4.  **Submission:** Format predictions into the required CSV.
