The `RuntimeError` during validation inference is caused by a preprocessing mismatch between the training and inference pipelines. While the training data loader (`NeuralDataset` in `library/data_factory.py`) explicitly truncates input sequences to `Config.MAX_SEQ_LEN` (128), the inference method (`HybridInference.predict` in `library/pipeline.py`) does not. Consequently, when the inference pipeline encounters a token context longer than 128 characters (in this case, 363), it passes an oversized tensor to the model, causing the positional encoding addition to fail due to a shape mismatch. To fix this, I will add truncation logic to `library/pipeline.py` to ensure all inference inputs adhere to the maximum sequence length defined in the configuration.
