Based on the provided lessons and previous ideas, I propose a **Hybrid CRNN (Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network) with Attention** strategy. This approach evolves the successful hybrid architecture by replacing the static Global Average Pooling with a recurrent mechanism to explicitly model the temporal evolution of seismic events, which was identified as a key factor in previous lessons.

### Model
The architecture is a dual-branch network that fuses temporal-visual representations with robust statistical summaries:

1.  **Branch 1: Spectrogram CRNN (Time-Frequency & Sequential Encoder)**
    *   **Input**: 10-channel Log-Mel Spectrograms derived from the sensor data.
    *   **Backbone**: **EfficientNet-B0**. This replaces the ResNet18 used previously, offering better parameter efficiency and feature extraction capabilities via MBConv blocks.
    *   **Weight Adaptation**: The first convolutional layer will be modified to accept 10 input channels. We will initialize this layer by averaging the pre-trained ImageNet weights across the RGB dimension and replicating them for the 10 seismic channels to preserve learned spatial filters **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.
    *   **Recurrent Head (Structural Innovation)**: Instead of the Global Average Pooling (GAP) used in the previous idea (which discards temporal order), we will apply **Frequency-Global Pooling** to collapse the frequency dimension while retaining the time dimension. This sequence of feature vectors will be fed into a **Bidirectional GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit)**. This allows the model to capture the "temporal evolution" and sequence of tremors identified as critical in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
    *   **Attention Pooling**: The output of the GRU will be processed by an **Attention** layer to compute a weighted sum of time steps, allowing the model to focus on the most predictive seismic events within the 10-minute window.

2.  **Branch 2: Statistical MLP (Global Feature Encoder)**
    *   **Input**: A vector of engineered statistics (Mean, Std, Skew, Kurtosis, Quantiles, and NaN counts) for all 10 sensors.
    *   **Architecture**: A Multi-Layer Perceptron (Dense $\rightarrow$ Batch Norm $\rightarrow$ ReLU $\rightarrow$ Dropout).
    *   **Purpose**: This branch is retained to preserve explicit access to global signal magnitudes and distributions, ensuring the model does not lose this information during spectrogram normalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.

3.  **Fusion Head**:
    *   The Attention-pooled embedding from the CRNN and the output of the MLP are concatenated.
    *   A final regression block predicts the scaled target value.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Spectrograms**: Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms for the 10 sensors, filling NaNs prior to generation.
    *   **Target Scaling**: Apply **StandardScaler** (subtract mean, divide by std) to the `time_to_eruption` target variable. This is mandatory to resolve the convergence failure caused by the large magnitude ($10^7$) of the target **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
2.  **Augmentation**:
    *   Apply **SpecAugment** (Time and Frequency Masking) to the spectrograms during training to improve generalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.
    *   **Constraint**: Configure the masking parameters **conservatively** (e.g., masking <15% of frequency bands). Excessive masking was shown to destroy critical contiguous signal artifacts and degrade performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

### Training
*   **Loss Function**: Mean Absolute Error (MAE) on the scaled target.
*   **Optimizer**: AdamW with weight decay.
*   **Strategy**:
    *   **Transfer Learning**: Initialize the EfficientNet backbone with ImageNet weights (except the first layer, which is adapted) rather than training from scratch, as this yields significantly better performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.
    *   **Duration & Patience**: Train for a longer duration (e.g., 40-50 epochs) to allow the recurrent layers to converge. Set the **Early Stopping patience** to a high value (e.g., 15-20 epochs) to prevent premature termination due to the noisy nature of seismic validation curves **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: Generate spectrograms and statistical features for the test set.
2.  **Prediction**: Pass inputs through the trained Hybrid CRNN to obtain scaled predictions.
3.  **Inverse Scaling**: Apply the inverse transform (multiply by training std, add training mean) to the model outputs to restore the `time_to_eruption` to its original scale **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
4.  **Submission**: Save the `segment_id` and restored predictions to CSV.
