Based on the extensive failure analysis and lessons learned from previous iterations, I propose the **Stabilized SE-ResNet34 Hybrid with Dual-Domain Feature Injection**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Current Best" (Idea 3) and the "Failed" (Idea 5, Idea 7) experiments. It synthesizes the channel-adaptive capabilities attempted in Idea 6/7 with the robust convolutional inductive bias of Idea 3, while correcting the preprocessing and stability flaws that caused Ideas 5 and 7 to fail.

### Model
The architecture is a dual-branch network designed to maximize feature extraction capacity while ensuring training stability:

1.  **Branch 1: SE-ResNet34 Spectrogram Encoder**
    *   **Backbone Upgrade:** Upgrade the backbone from ResNet18 to **ResNet34**. This provides the deeper capacity needed to model complex seismic textures, which was attempted in Idea 5 but failed due to input complexity issues **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.
    *   **Structural Innovation (SE Blocks):** Integrate **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE)** blocks into the ResNet34 architecture.
        *   *Purpose:* SE blocks explicitly model the interdependencies between the 10 seismic sensors by recalibrating channel weights dynamically. This achieves the "channel awareness" sought in the EfficientNet experiment (Idea 6) but retains the **standard convolutions** of ResNet, which are critical for preserving inter-sensor phase relationships **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
    *   **Pooling:** Use **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**. We explicitly reject Attention Pooling, as the target variable represents a cumulative global state best captured by averaging **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
    *   **Initialization:** Initialize with **ImageNet weights**. Adapt the first convolutional layer by averaging the RGB weights and replicating them 10 times to preserve learned spatial filters **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.

2.  **Branch 2: Dual-Domain Wide MLP**
    *   **Input Expansion:** Extend the statistical feature set to include both **Time-Domain** (Mean, Std, Skew, Kurtosis, Quantiles, NaN counts) and **Frequency-Domain** (Spectral Centroid, Spectral Bandwidth, Dominant Frequency) statistics. This ensures the high-SNR tabular branch has explicit access to spectral summaries **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.
    *   **Architecture:** Use a **Wide MLP** (e.g., Hidden Dim 256 $\to$ 128). We explicitly avoid under-parameterizing this branch to prevent the information bottleneck observed in Idea 6 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.

3.  **Fusion Head:**
    *   Concatenate the GAP output from the SE-ResNet34 and the output of the MLP.
    *   Pass through a final regression block to predict the scaled target.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Imputation:** Fill `NaN` values in the raw sensor data with **zeros**. Do not use linear interpolation, as it creates artificial spectral artifacts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
    *   **Spectrogram Generation:**
        *   **Resolution:** Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms with **`n_mels=128`** to ensure sufficient frequency resolution **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
        *   **Normalization (Critical Fix):** Apply **Slaney Area Normalization** (`norm='slaney'`) to the Mel filterbanks. This corrects the flaw in Idea 7 where high-frequency noise dominated the unnormalized features **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.
        *   **Scaling:** Apply **`AmplitudeToDB(top_db=80)`** to normalize dynamic range **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.
        *   **Single View:** Use a single, optimized spectrogram view (e.g., `n_fft=1024`) rather than stacking multi-resolution views, to avoid the instability observed in Idea 5 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.
    *   **Target Scaling:** Apply **StandardScaler** (subtract mean, divide by std) to the `time_to_eruption` target **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

2.  **Augmentation:**
    *   Apply **SpecAugment** (Time and Frequency Masking).
    *   **Constraint:** Configure masking **conservatively** (masking <15% of the spectrum) to preserve contiguous signal structures **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** Mean Absolute Error (MAE) on the scaled target.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with weight decay.
*   **Strategy (Stability Fix):**
    *   **Learning Rate Warmup:** Implement a **Linear Warmup** for the first 3-5 epochs. This is critical to stabilize the training of the deeper ResNet34 backbone and prevent the early-epoch oscillations that destabilized Idea 5 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00021**.
    *   **Scheduler:** After warmup, switch to **`ReduceLROnPlateau`** to fine-tune weights and achieve high precision **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
    *   **Patience:** Set Early Stopping patience to **20 epochs** to allow the model to navigate the noisy validation landscape **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   For each test segment, generate the zero-filled, Slaney-normalized, dB-scaled spectrogram and the dual-domain statistical features.
    *   Pass inputs through the trained SE-ResNet34 Hybrid model.
2.  **Inverse Scaling:** Apply the inverse transform (multiply by training std, add training mean) to the model outputs to restore the `time_to_eruption` to its original scale.
3.  **Submission:** Format the `segment_id` and restored predictions into the required CSV file.
