The `CUDA out of memory` error is caused by an excessively large batch size (`256`) for the EfficientNet-B3 backbone on the available 16GB GPU. To fix this, I will aggressively reduce `PHASE1_BATCH_SIZE` to `32` and `PHASE2_BATCH_SIZE` to `16` in `library/config.py` (Cite debug_lesson_3).

Additionally, I will address the potential issue of non-contiguous `category_id`s causing index-out-of-bounds errors in the loss function (Cite debug_lesson_4). I will modify `library/taxonomy.py` to generate a contiguous `species_id` mapping (0 to N-1) and update `library/dataset.py` to use these mapped IDs for training. To ensure the submission file remains correct, I will update `library/engine.py` to map predictions back to the original `category_id`s. Finally, I will update the taxonomy cache path in `library/config.py` to force regeneration of the mapping file (Cite debug_lesson_5).
