The `RuntimeError` is caused by a device mismatch between the GPU-resident input tensors (`logits` and `targets`) and the CPU-resident internal buffers (`pos_weight` and `class_weights`) of the custom `WeightedMultiLabelLogLoss` module. As explained in **debug_lesson_4**, stateful loss functions that inherit from `nn.Module` and use `register_buffer` must be explicitly moved to the target device using `.to(device)`, just like neural network models.
