The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurred because the model processes a sequence of 48 high-resolution images (384x384) for each sample in the batch. With a `BATCH_SIZE` of 2, the backbone CNN attempts to process $2 \times 48 = 96$ images simultaneously, which exceeds the GPU's memory capacity. To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to 1, effectively halving the memory footprint to 48 images per forward pass (Cite debug_lesson_1). To maintain the same training dynamics (effective batch size of 16), I will proportionally increase the `GRAD_ACCUM_STEPS` from 8 to 16.
