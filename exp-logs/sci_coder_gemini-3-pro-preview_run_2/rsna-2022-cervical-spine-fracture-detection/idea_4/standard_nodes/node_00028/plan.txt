The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurs because the model architecture collapses the batch and sequence dimensions (`x.view(b * s, ...)`) to process slices in parallel. With a configured `BATCH_SIZE` of 4 and `SEQ_LEN` of 64, the effective batch size passed to the EfficientNet-B4 backbone is $4 \times 64 = 256$ high-resolution images, which exceeds the GPU memory capacity (Cite debug_lesson_1). To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to 1 and `IMAGE_SIZE` to `(256, 256)` to lower the memory footprint. I will also increase `GRAD_ACCUM_STEPS` to 16 to maintain the same effective optimization batch size and enable `grad_checkpointing` in the backbone to further reduce memory usage by recomputing activations during the backward pass.
