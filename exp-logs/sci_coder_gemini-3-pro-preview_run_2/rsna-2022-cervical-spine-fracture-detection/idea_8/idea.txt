This strategy evolves the successful **Multi-Scale 2.5D Network** (Idea 7) by introducing **Spatially-Guided Feature Aggregation**. This addresses the limitation where Global Average Pooling (GAP) dilutes the signal of small, focal fractures within the feature maps before the sequence model processes them.

# Proposed Solution: 2.5D Dual-Attention Network with Spatially-Guided Feature Aggregation

## Model
The architecture is a hierarchical attention network that applies attention mechanisms at both the **intra-slice (spatial)** and **inter-slice (temporal)** levels, anchored by dense supervision from bounding boxes.

1.  **2.5D Multi-Scale Backbone:**
    *   **Input:** The model accepts **2.5D stacks** (slices $z-1, z, z+1$) to capture local volumetric continuity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
    *   **Feature Extraction:** An **EfficientNet-B4** extracts feature maps. To preserve fine-grained details, features are extracted from both the **P4 (penultimate)** and **P5 (final)** blocks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

2.  **Structural Innovation: Spatially-Guided Attention Pooling:**
    *   Instead of standard Global Average Pooling (which averages fracture features with healthy background noise), the model employs a **Spatial Attention Module**.
    *   A lightweight convolutional layer projects the concatenated P4/P5 feature maps to a single-channel **Spatial Attention Map**.
    *   The final slice embedding is computed as the spatially weighted sum of the feature maps using this attention map. This forces the model to "focus" on the specific region of the slice containing the fracture before compressing the data into a vector.

3.  **Sequence Modeling:**
    *   **Bi-LSTM with Positional Embeddings:** The sequence of spatially-focused embeddings is processed by a Bidirectional LSTM to model Z-axis anatomical continuity. Learnable **Positional Embeddings** are injected to provide explicit anatomical references (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

4.  **Dual-Level Output Heads:**
    *   **Primary (Temporal Attention):** 8 Class-Specific Attention Heads (C1-C7, Overall) aggregate the LSTM outputs to predict study-level probabilities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   **Auxiliary 1 (Slice Supervision):** A linear head on the LSTM output predicts the presence of a fracture on each slice (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   **Auxiliary 2 (Spatial Supervision):** The **Spatial Attention Map** (from step 2) is supervised directly to align with ground-truth bounding boxes.

## Data
1.  **Preprocessing & Sampling:**
    *   **2.5D Stacking:** Inputs are constructed by stacking 3 adjacent slices.
    *   **High-Density Sampling:** The loader samples a sequence of **64 to 96 slices**. This prioritizes Z-axis coverage over model complexity, avoiding the sampling pitfalls of Transformer approaches (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
    *   **Volumetric Augmentation:** Geometric transformations (rotation, shift, scale) are applied **identically across the entire volume** within the `__getitem__` method to preserve physical alignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008, solution_lesson_node_00010).

2.  **Hierarchical Target Generation:**
    *   **Slice Targets:** Binary vectors indicating if a slice falls within a fracture bounding box (from `train_bounding_boxes.csv`).
    *   **Spatial Masks:** For slices with bounding boxes, a binary 2D mask is generated corresponding to the box coordinates. This provides the ground truth for the Spatial Attention Module.

## Training
1.  **Tri-Level Loss Function:**
    *   The total loss is $L_{total} = L_{study} + \lambda_1 L_{slice} + \lambda_2 L_{spatial}$.
    *   **Study Loss ($L_{study}$):** Weighted Multi-Label Logarithmic Loss on the Primary Heads with **Positive Class Weight > 2.0** to maintain sensitivity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   **Slice Loss ($L_{slice}$):** Masked Binary Cross Entropy on the Auxiliary Slice Head (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   **Spatial Loss ($L_{spatial}$):** **Masked Dice Loss** applied to the Spatial Attention Map. This term is only computed for slices that have ground-truth bounding boxes. It forces the backbone to spatially localize the fracture, ensuring the embeddings passed to the LSTM are rich in fracture-relevant signal.

2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Gradient Accumulation:** Used to maintain a stable effective batch size (8-16 studies) while accommodating the memory requirements of the EfficientNet-B4 backbone and spatial attention maps (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Test studies are processed through the backbone, Spatial Attention Module, and LSTM.
    *   The **Primary Heads** (Class-Specific Attention) generate the final submission probabilities.
    *   The Auxiliary heads (Slice and Spatial) are discarded; their purpose is solely to structure the latent space and focus the feature extractor during training.
