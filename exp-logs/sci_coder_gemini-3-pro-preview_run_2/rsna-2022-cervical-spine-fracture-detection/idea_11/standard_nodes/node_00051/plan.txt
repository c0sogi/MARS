The issue is caused by **numerical instability in Mixed Precision (FP16) training**, leading to corrupted Batch Normalization statistics. Specifically, the `SoftSpatialAttention` layer performs a summation over spatial dimensions in FP16; if the sum exceeds the dynamic range of FP16 (approx. 65,504), it produces `Inf` values. These `Inf`s propagate into the Batch Normalization layers, corrupting the running mean and variance, which results in `NaN` outputs during validation. This instability is exacerbated by an excessively high `MAX_GRAD_NORM` (1000), which effectively disables gradient clipping.

To fix this:
1.  **Reduce `MAX_GRAD_NORM`** in `library/config.py` from `1000` to `1.0` to enforce proper gradient clipping and prevent exploding gradients (Cite debug_lesson_1).
2.  **Stabilize `SoftSpatialAttention`** in `library/model.py` by casting the feature map to `float32` before performing the spatial summation. This ensures the accumulation happens in single precision, preventing overflow.
