The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU memory is exhausted because the model attempts to process too many high-resolution slices simultaneously. The current configuration uses a batch size of 4 with a sequence length of 48 (set in `runfile.py`), resulting in an effective batch size of $4 \times 48 = 192$ images for the CNN backbone (Cite `debug_lesson_1`). To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to 2 and increase `ACCUMULATION_STEPS` to 8 to maintain the same optimization trajectory. Additionally, I will enable gradient checkpointing on the `timm` backbone in `library/model.py` to significantly reduce activation memory usage during training (Cite `debug_lesson_10`).
