The lesson demonstrates that scaling the hidden dimension to 192 and extending training to 45 epochs allows the model to better capture the complex, non-linear geometric dependencies of quantum mechanical couplings, significantly improving the metric to -1.407.

I will also modify the `predict` function in `library/engine.py` to bypass the strict existence check for test structure files, which caused a fallback to a baseline submission in the previous run. This ensures the optimized model is used for the final submission.

1.  **`library/config.py`**: Increase `HIDDEN_DIM` to 192 and `MAX_EPOCHS` to 45 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
2.  **`library/engine.py`**: Remove the early exit block in `predict()` that triggered when test structures were not immediately found, allowing the data loader to attempt loading them via the standard pipeline.
