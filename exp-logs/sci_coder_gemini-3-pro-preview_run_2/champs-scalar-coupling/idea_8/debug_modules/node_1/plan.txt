The error `AssertionError: Scaler reconstruction error too high: nan` is caused by the `TargetScaler` generating `NaN` standard deviations when fitted on a small subset of data where some coupling types appear only once (singleton groups). This results in `NaN` propagation during transformation and inverse transformation. To fix this, I will modify `TargetScaler.fit` in `library/utils.py` to inspect the calculated standard deviations and replace any `NaN` or near-zero values with `1.0`. This ensures numerical stability even when data is sparse, directly applying the guidance from **debug_lesson_11** (Cite debug_lesson_11).
