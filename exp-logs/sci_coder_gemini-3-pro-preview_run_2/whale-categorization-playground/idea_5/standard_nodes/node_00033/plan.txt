To optimize the solution, I will switch the backbone from EfficientNet-B4 (384px) to EfficientNet-B3 (256px). This "Capacity-Resolution Swap" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) allows for faster training and better convergence without the need for gradient checkpointing. I will also increase the learning rate to `1e-3` to prevent the underfitting observed in the previous run (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032) and extend the training duration to 25 epochs by removing the hardcoded limit in the run script (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
