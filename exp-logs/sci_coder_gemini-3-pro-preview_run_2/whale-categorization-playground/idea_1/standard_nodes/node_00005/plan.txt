2.  **Data Curation (Optimization):** I will modify `SiameseWhaleDataset` in `library/dataset.py` to exclude the `new_whale` class from the training pairs. Since `new_whale` represents a collection of distinct, unknown individuals, treating them as a single class with Contrastive Loss forces the model to cluster disparate features together, confusing the embedding space. Excluding them allows the model to learn a cleaner metric space for known identities, while `new_whale` predictions during inference will rely on the distance threshold.
3.  **Hyperparameters (Optimization):** I will increase `IMG_SIZE` from 224 to 384 in `library/config.py` to capture finer details of the whale flukes. To support this and ensure convergence, I will increase `NUM_EPOCHS` to 30.
