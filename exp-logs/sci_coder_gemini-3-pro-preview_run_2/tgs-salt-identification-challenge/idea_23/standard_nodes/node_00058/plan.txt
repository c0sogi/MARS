The issue is a dimension mismatch during validation: the model's predictions are cropped to the original image size (101x101), but the ground truth masks collected from the `DataLoader` are padded to (128x128) by the validation transforms. This causes a `ValueError` when calculating IoU.

To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to stop collecting the padded masks (`val_targets`) from the validation loader. Instead, I will use the original, unpadded `val_masks` array (which is already loaded in memory) for metric calculation and failure analysis. This ensures that both the predictions and the ground truth masks are 101x101.
