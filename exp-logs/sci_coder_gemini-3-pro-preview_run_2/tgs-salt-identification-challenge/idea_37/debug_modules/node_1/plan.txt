The execution failure `AssertionError: Segmentation loss is NaN` indicates that the loss calculation resulted in Not-a-Number. This is primarily caused by the use of the deprecated `torch.autograd.Variable` wrapper in `library/losses.py`. In modern PyTorch, tensors natively support autograd, and wrapping them (especially constants like `signs` or `grad` derived from ground truth) can lead to graph detachment or numerical instability. I will remove these wrappers to ensure correct autograd behavior (Cite {debug_lesson_22}). Additionally, I will update `library/dataset.py` to use `np.nanmean` and `np.nanstd` and handle potential NaNs during depth normalization to prevent invalid inputs from propagating to the model.
