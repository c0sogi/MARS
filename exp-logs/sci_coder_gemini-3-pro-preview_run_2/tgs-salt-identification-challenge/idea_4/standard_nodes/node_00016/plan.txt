The `ZeroDivisionError` is caused by the training `DataLoader` yielding zero batches. This happens because the cached dataset contains only 20 samples (likely from a stale debug run), which is strictly less than the `BATCH_SIZE` of 32, and `drop_last=True` is enabled. Consequently, the `DataLoader` discards the incomplete batch, the training loop is never entered, and `dataset_size` remains 0.

To fix this, I will modify `library/dataset.py` to check if the dataset size is smaller than the batch size. If it is, I will disable `drop_last` to ensure the data is processed. This directly applies the guidance from `debug_lesson_1`.
