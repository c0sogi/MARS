### Model: Depth-Conditioned U-Net++ with Deep Supervision
I propose replacing the lightweight LinkNet architecture with a **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net)** architecture. While LinkNet is efficient, its simple addition-based skip connections may fail to capture the complex, fine-grained boundary information required for high-precision salt segmentation. U-Net++ introduces nested, dense skip pathways that bridge the semantic gap between the encoder and decoder feature maps more effectively.

*   **Backbone:** Retain the **ResNet34** encoder pretrained on ImageNet. This provides a robust, transferable feature extractor that converges faster and better than custom encoders Cite solution_lesson_node_00002.
*   **Bottleneck Depth Injection:** Continue to inject the scalar depth information at the network bottleneck. The depth value will be normalized, projected, and concatenated with the final encoder feature map (the bridge) before it enters the decoder pathways. This ensures the model remains conditioned on the global geological context Cite solution_lesson_node_00001.
*   **Deep Supervision:** Unlike the standard single-output LinkNet, this model will utilize Deep Supervision. We will attach auxiliary segmentation heads to the output nodes of the nested decoder branches (at the original resolution level). This forces the intermediate layers to learn semantically meaningful features and improves gradient flow.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   Convert images to single-channel grayscale.
    *   Pad images from 101x101 to **128x128** using reflection padding to align with the network's downsampling factor.
    *   Normalize pixel values to [0, 1].
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Horizontal Flip:** Retain as a baseline rigid augmentation.
    *   **Elastic Transformations:** Apply `ElasticTransform` to simulate the organic plasticity of salt bodies Cite solution_lesson_node_00003.
    *   **Optimized Parameters:** Crucially, use the tuned parameters identified in previous experiments: `alpha` $\approx$ 120, `sigma` $\approx$ 6, and `alpha_affine` = 0 Cite solution_lesson_node_00004.
    *   **Probability Regularization:** Apply ElasticTransform with a probability of **$p=0.2$**. Higher probabilities (e.g., 0.5) have been shown to degrade convergence by shifting the distribution too aggressively Cite solution_lesson_node_00007.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** Use a combined **BCE + Dice Loss**. This loss will be calculated for the final output *and* all auxiliary Deep Supervision heads. The total loss is the weighted sum of these individual losses.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with weight decay.
*   **Schedule:** Cosine Annealing LR scheduler.
*   **Training Horizon:** Train for **50 epochs** with Early Stopping. Previous experiments showed that cutting training at 20 epochs truncates the model's peak performance, which typically occurs around epoch 23-25. Extending the horizon ensures we capture the true convergence point Cite solution_lesson_node_00006.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    *   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Predict on the original image and its horizontal flip, then average the probability maps Cite solution_lesson_node_00002.
    *   **Deep Supervision Ensemble:** Average the probability maps from all Deep Supervision output heads to produce the final prediction. This acts as an internal ensemble, further smoothing predictions and reducing variance.
*   **Post-Processing:**
    *   Center crop the 128x128 prediction to 101x101.
    *   Threshold at 0.5 to generate the binary mask.
    *   Convert to RLE.
