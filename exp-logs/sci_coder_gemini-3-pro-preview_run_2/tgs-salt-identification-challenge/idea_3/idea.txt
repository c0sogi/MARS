Based on the accumulated lessons and the requirement for structural evolution, I propose the following optimized strategy.

### Model: Depth-Conditioned Attention-LinkNet with SE-ResNeXt50

I propose upgrading the architecture to an **Attention-Gated LinkNet** utilizing a **SE-ResNeXt50** backbone. This design evolves the previous LinkNet approach by integrating stronger feature extraction and attention mechanisms to refine boundary precision without incurring the computational cost of dense concatenation (as seen in U-Net++).

*   **Backbone:** Replace the ResNet34 encoder with a **SE-ResNeXt50 (32x4d)** pretrained on ImageNet. While Lesson 00002 established the superiority of pretrained backbones over custom ones Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, moving to ResNeXt50 increases the network's cardinality and capacity to capture complex textures, while the Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) blocks implicitly handle channel-wise feature recalibration.
*   **Decoder Structure:** Retain the **LinkNet** decoder architecture. Its use of **additive skip connections** is computationally efficient and was proven to outperform the complex, concatenation-heavy U-Net++ on this specific dataset Cite solution_lesson_node_00008.
*   **Structural Innovation (scSE Attention):** Integrate **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules within the decoder blocks. Immediately after the element-wise addition of the encoder and decoder features, the combined feature map will pass through an scSE block. This mechanism allows the network to spatially attend to relevant salt boundaries and suppress background noise in the restored high-resolution features, effectively "cleaning" the signal from the additive skip connection.
*   **Depth Conditioning:** Continue to inject the depth metadata at the **bottleneck**. The scalar depth will be projected to a low-dimensional embedding (e.g., 16 channels) and concatenated with the encoder's final feature map before entering the decoder. This avoids the parameter explosion of convolutional fusion while maintaining global context Cite solution_lesson_node_00001, solution_lesson_node_00009.

### Data

*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Padding:** Pad images from 101x101 to **128x128** using reflection padding to align with the encoder's downsampling factor.
    *   **Depth Normalization:** Apply **Standard Scaling** (zero mean, unit variance) to the depth values ($z$). This ensures the injected metadata aligns with the distribution of the neural network weights, preventing convergence issues associated with Min-Max scaling Cite solution_lesson_node_00010.
    *   **Channel Format:** Convert all images to single-channel grayscale.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Affine Transformations:** Incorporate **ShiftScaleRotate** to ensure the model learns geometric invariance, which is critical for seismic structures Cite solution_lesson_node_00010.
    *   **Non-Rigid Transformations:** Apply **ElasticTransform** to simulate the organic plasticity of salt bodies Cite solution_lesson_node_00003.
    *   **Tuned Parameters:** Use the optimized parameters for elastic deformation (`alpha` $\approx$ 120, `sigma` $\approx$ 6) to ensure the deformations are physically significant relative to the image size Cite solution_lesson_node_00004.
    *   **Regularization Strength:** Apply these non-rigid augmentations with a low probability (**$p=0.2$**) to avoid shifting the training distribution too far from reality, acting as a regularizer rather than a dominant noise source Cite solution_lesson_node_00007.

### Training

*   **Loss Function:** Use a combined **BCE + Dice Loss**. This standard objective functions well for optimizing the IoU-based metric and handling class imbalance.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing learning rate scheduler.
*   **Training Horizon:** Train for **50 epochs**. Previous experiments demonstrated that truncating training at 20 epochs prevents the model from reaching its peak performance, which typically occurs later in the schedule Cite solution_lesson_node_00006.

### Evaluation

*   **Inference:**
    *   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Generate predictions for the original image and a horizontally flipped version, then average the probability maps. This provides a robust boost to metric stability Cite solution_lesson_node_00002.
    *   **Cropping:** Center-crop the 128x128 predictions back to the original 101x101 resolution.
*   **Post-Processing:**
    *   **Threshold Optimization:** Instead of a fixed 0.5 threshold, determine the optimal binarization threshold based on the validation set performance to maximize the mAP score.
    *   **Formatting:** Convert the binary masks to Run-Length Encoding (RLE) for submission.
