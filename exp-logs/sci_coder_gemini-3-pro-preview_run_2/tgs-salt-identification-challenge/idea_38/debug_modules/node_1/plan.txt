Second, the dataset pipeline produces masks with the channel dimension at the end `(H, W, C)` because `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2` does not transpose masks by default. I will fix the assertion in `runfile.py` to expect 1 channel and update `library/dataset.py` to use `ToTensorV2(transpose_mask=True)`, ensuring masks are transposed to the PyTorch-standard `(C, H, W)` format Cite {debug_lesson_4}.
