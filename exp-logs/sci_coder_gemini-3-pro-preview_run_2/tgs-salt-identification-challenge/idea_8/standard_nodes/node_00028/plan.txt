The `RuntimeError` in `runfile.py` occurs because the `masks` tensor is a slice of a larger tensor (returned by `crop_to_original`), making it non-contiguous in memory. The `.view()` method requires contiguous tensors. Replacing `.view()` with `.reshape()` resolves this, as `.reshape()` handles non-contiguous inputs by copying data if necessary. Additionally, I will fix the `SyntaxWarning` in `library/losses.py` by marking the docstring as a raw string to correctly handle the backslash in the LaTeX math notation.
