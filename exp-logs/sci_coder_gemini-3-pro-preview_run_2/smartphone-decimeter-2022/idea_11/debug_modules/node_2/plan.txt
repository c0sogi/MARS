The issue is a data integrity problem caused by index misalignment during DataFrame concatenation. In `library/data_loader.py`, the `gnss_epoch_df` DataFrame is filtered using `dropna` to remove rows with missing WLS positions, which creates gaps in its index. However, the `imu_feats` DataFrame, generated subsequently based on the filtered timestamps, has a reset (sequential) index. When these two are concatenated using `pd.concat(..., axis=1)`, pandas aligns them by index, resulting in `NaN` values for mismatched rows. These `NaN`s propagate through the pipeline, causing the validation loss to be `nan`, which prevents the model from being saved and leads to the `AssertionError`.

To fix this, I will reset the index of `gnss_epoch_df` immediately after the `dropna` operation in `library/data_loader.py`. This ensures that both DataFrames have sequential indices (0 to N-1) and align correctly during concatenation (Cite debug_lesson_15).
