The execution failed with a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` because the batch size of 512 was too large for the GPU memory when computing the full vocabulary logits (and their gradients) during the Sequential Model training. Specifically, projecting the hidden states to the vocabulary size of ~100k items creates a dense tensor of approximately 4GB, which doubles during backpropagation (Cite debug_lesson_5). To fix this, I will reduce the `batch_size` in the `FAST_SEQ_CONFIG` dictionary within `runfile.py` from 512 to 128, which will reduce the memory footprint of these intermediate tensors by a factor of 4, allowing them to fit within the available 16GB VRAM.
