To optimize the solution, I will implement a strategy based on Lessons `solution_lesson_node_00019`, `solution_lesson_node_00021`, and `solution_lesson_node_00022`.

1.  **Listwise Ranking Objective**: I will switch the LightGBM objective from `binary` (pointwise) to `lambdarank` (listwise) to directly optimize the ordering of candidates rather than their independent classification probabilities.
2.  **Latent Behavioral Embeddings (Item2Vec via SVD)**: I will implement `TruncatedSVD` on the User-Item interaction matrix to generate 128-dimensional item embeddings. This allows the model to capture semantic similarities between items that may not co-occur frequently but are conceptually related.
3.  **Semantic Retrieval & Features**:
    *   I will augment the candidate generation stage with **Semantic Retrieval**, finding items whose embeddings are closest to the user's history vector.
    *   I will add two dense features to the ranker: `user_item_sim` (similarity between user history and candidate) and `last_item_sim` (similarity between the user's *most recent* purchase and the candidate), capturing both long-term preference and immediate sequential intent.
4.  **Model Capacity**: I will increase the ranker's complexity (`num_leaves=100`, `learning_rate=0.03`) to enable it to effectively learn decision boundaries in the new dense feature space.
