To optimize the performance of the Crystal Graph Network, I will implement the following changes based on the lessons learned and the analysis of the previous solution:

1.  **Graph Topology Optimization (Lesson 81, 78)**: I will modify the graph construction in `library/data.py` to enforce a **k-Nearest Neighbor (k-NN)** topology with $k=12$. The previous solution used a radius-based graph with a large cutoff (5.0 Å) and a high maximum neighbor count (50), which likely introduced noise ("over-smoothing") by including distant, weakly interacting atoms. By restricting the graph to the 12 closest neighbors and ensuring the message passing flows from neighbors to the central atom (`j -> i`), the model will focus on the primary coordination shell, which is physically most significant for formation energy and bandgap.
