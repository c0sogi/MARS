The error `ValueError: invalid literal for int()` occurs because `ase.io.read` attempts to parse the geometry files as standard XYZ format due to the `.xyz` extension, but the content is actually in FHI-aims format (indicated by `lattice_vector` and comments). I will fix this by explicitly specifying `format='aims'` in the `read` call, referencing `debug_lesson_1`. Additionally, I will remove the deprecated `verbose=True` argument from the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler in `library/train.py` to prevent a potential `TypeError` in recent PyTorch versions, as per `debug_lesson_3`.
