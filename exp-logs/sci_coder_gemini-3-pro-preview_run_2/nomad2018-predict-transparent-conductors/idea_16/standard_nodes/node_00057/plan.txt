The `TypeError` indicates that the `verbose` argument passed to `torch.optim.lr_scheduler.ReduceLROnPlateau` is no longer supported in the installed version of PyTorch (2.0+). To fix this, I will remove the `verbose=True` argument from the scheduler initialization in both `runfile.py` and `library/train.py`. This aligns with the guidance in `debug_lesson_1` regarding deprecated arguments in newer framework versions.
