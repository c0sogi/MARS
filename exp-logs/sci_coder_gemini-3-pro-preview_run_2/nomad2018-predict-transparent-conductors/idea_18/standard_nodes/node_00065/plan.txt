Implementation strategy:
1.  **Optimize Model Capacity (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00004}):** In `library/config.py`, reduce `ATOM_EMBEDDING_DIM` from 128 to 64 and `BATCH_SIZE` from 48 to 64. This reduces parameter count to prevent overfitting on the small dataset.
2.  **Implicit Geometric Encoding (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00022}):** In `library/model.py`, remove the `LatticeEncoder` and the concatenation of global lattice features in the `LI_CGCNN_ELR` model. The model will rely on the graph structure (implicit encoding) which is superior to explicit conditioning for this task.
3.  **Training Stabilization:** In `library/train.py`, introduce a `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler to adjust the learning rate dynamically, addressing the validation loss volatility observed previously.
4.  **Extended Convergence:** In `runfile.py`, increase `Config.NUM_EPOCHS` to 80 to allow the optimized model sufficient time to converge.
