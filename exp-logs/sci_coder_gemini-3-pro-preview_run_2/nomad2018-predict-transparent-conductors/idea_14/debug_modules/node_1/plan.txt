The issue is that the geometry files are in FHI-aims format but have a `.xyz` extension, causing `ase.io.read` to fail with the default parser. Additionally, the neighbor list calculation requests displacement vectors (`"d"`) but the model likely expects scalar distances, and handling vectors without computing norms would lead to shape mismatches or incorrect features.

I will fix this by explicitly setting `format='aims'` in `ase.io.read` to correctly parse the files (Cite debug_lesson_1). I will also change the `neighbor_list` request from `"ijd"` to `"ijD"` to directly obtain scalar distances, ensuring compatibility with the RBF expansion layer and preventing shape errors (Cite debug_lesson_16).
