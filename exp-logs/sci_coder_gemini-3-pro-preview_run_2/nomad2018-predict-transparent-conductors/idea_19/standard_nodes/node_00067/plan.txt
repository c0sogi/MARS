To optimize the performance of the Crystal Graph Neural Network, I will implement the following targeted changes based on the provided lessons:

1.  **Enhance Model Capacity and Resolution**: I will increase the `ATOM_EMBEDDING_DIM` from 128 to 192 and the `RBF_BINS` from 60 to 80 in `library/config.py`. This aligns with **Lesson 00005**, which suggests that scaling width and edge resolution allows the model to capture richer feature representations and distinguish subtle variations in interatomic distances.

2.  **Adopt Physical Inductive Bias in Pooling**: I will modify `library/model.py` to replace the Dual Pooling (Mean + Max) with **Global Mean Pooling** only. As per **Lesson 00033**, mean pooling is physically more appropriate for intensive properties like formation energy and bandgap, as it represents an average of local environments rather than being skewed by extreme local features (max pooling). This also reduces the dimensionality of the readout layer, potentially reducing overfitting.

3.  **Extended Training Schedule**: I will increase `NUM_EPOCHS` in `library/config.py` to 120 and update `runfile.py` to use this configuration instead of the hardcoded 30 epochs. The previous run showed the loss was still decreasing at epoch 30, indicating premature termination.

4.  **Batch Size Adjustment**: I will keep the batch size at 48 to maintain the regularization effect noted in Lesson 00005.
