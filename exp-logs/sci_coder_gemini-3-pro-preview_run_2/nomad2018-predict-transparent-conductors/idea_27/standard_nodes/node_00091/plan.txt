1.  **Correct Residual-Normalization Ordering (Cite solution_lesson_node_00090):** In `library/model.py`, I will modify the `InteractionBlock` to apply the residual connection *before* Batch Normalization. The previous implementation normalized the convolution output before adding the residual (`BN(Conv(x)) + x`), which allowed the unconstrained residual signal to dominate the normalized structural features, washing out geometric information. The new order (`BN(Conv(x) + x)`) ensures the combined signal is properly normalized.

2.  **Enforce Locality in Graph Construction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078):** In `library/config.py`, I will reduce `MAX_NEIGHBORS` from 50 to 12. This constrains the radius graph to a k-Nearest Neighbor-like topology, reducing noise from distant, weakly interacting atoms and focusing the message passing on the primary coordination shell.
