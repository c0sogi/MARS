Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Refine Graph Topology (Cite Lesson 00081):** In `library/config.py` and `library/data.py`, I will restrict the graph connectivity by capping the number of neighbors per atom to 12 (`MAX_NEIGHBORS = 12`). In `library/data.py`, I will implement a filtering step within `process_graphs` that sorts neighbors by distance and selects the top-k closest ones. This reduces noise from distant, weakly interacting atoms while preserving the most chemically relevant local environment.
2.  **Amplified Residual Initialization (Cite Lesson 00054, 00060):** In `library/model.py`, I will initialize the learnable residual scaling parameter `epsilon` to 1.0 (instead of 0.0). This sets the initial residual connection to $x' = 2x + \text{message}$, providing a strong inductive bias towards preserving stoichiometric information (node identity) early in training, which has been shown to improve generalization for formation energy prediction.
3.  **Extended Training Schedule (Cite Lesson 00083):** In `runfile.py`, I will increase `Config.MAX_EPOCHS` from 50 to 120. This allows the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler sufficient time to decay the learning rate and fine-tune the weights, ensuring the model can escape local minima and converge to a lower error floor.
