To improve the model's performance and address the identified bottleneck of information loss due to downsampling, I will implement a **Compound Scaling** strategy.

1.  **Resolution & Model Upgrade**: I will upgrade the backbone from EfficientNet-B0 to **EfficientNet-B3** and increase the input resolution from 224x224 to **300x300** (the native resolution for B3). This directly addresses the failure analysis finding that error correlates with image size, allowing the model to utilize more fine-grained details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
2.  **Scheduler & Patience**: To prevent the "lucky spike" early stopping trap observed in previous experiments, I will introduce a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate Scheduler** and increase the early stopping patience from 5 to 8. This ensures the model has sufficient time to converge to a robust minimum (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
