The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU ran out of VRAM while attempting to process a batch of 16 high-resolution images. Although the configuration was designed for an A100 (40GB), the actual environment has a GPU with ~16GB capacity (Cite debug_lesson_1). To fix this, I will reduce the `batch_size` in `library/config.py` from 16 to 8, which will halve the memory requirement for activations and allow the training to proceed within the hardware constraints.
