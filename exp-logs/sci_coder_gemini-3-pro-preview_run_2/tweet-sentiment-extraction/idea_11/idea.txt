Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous ideas and the accumulated lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the approach from a "Single Best Model" paradigm to a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Tokenizer Diversity**, combining the proven strengths of DeBERTa-v3 with the complementary architectural features of RoBERTa to maximize boundary precision.

### Model
*   **Architecture (Structural Innovation):** Implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of two distinct high-capacity backbones:
    1.  **`microsoft/deberta-v3-large`:** Retained as the primary anchor due to its **Disentangled Attention** mechanism, which is superior for modeling relative token positions `Cite solution_lesson_node_00019`.
    2.  **`roberta-large`:** Introduced as the secondary backbone. Unlike DeBERTa, RoBERTa utilizes **Absolute Position Embeddings** and a **Byte-Level BPE** tokenizer (vs. DeBERTa's SentencePiece).
    *   *Rationale:* The primary source of error in this task is boundary ambiguity. By ensembling models with fundamentally different tokenization schemes and positional encoding strategies, we mitigate architecture-specific biases and tokenizer artifacts, achieving a robustness that scaling a single architecture cannot provide `Cite solution_lesson_node_00010`.
*   **Prediction Head:** For both backbones, utilize a **Simple Linear Head** (a single linear projection on top of the final hidden state). We explicitly reject complex heads (e.g., CNNs, Weighted Pooling) as they have been proven to introduce optimization difficulties and dilute feature signals without improving performance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00031`.
*   **Input Representation:** Adapt the input format to the specific tokenizer of each backbone (e.g., `[CLS] <Sentiment> [SEP] <Tweet Text> [SEP]` for DeBERTa, `<s> <Sentiment> </s> </s> <Tweet Text> </s>` for RoBERTa). This allows both models to condition the text representation on the sentiment token via self-attention `Cite solution_lesson_node_00009`.

### Data
*   **Alignment-Preserving Tokenization:** Perform tokenization strictly on the **raw, un-normalized text**. Generate and store **offset mappings** that link every token back to its exact character start and end positions. This is critical to prevent the misalignment issues observed when normalizing text `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.
*   **Dynamic Padding (Smart Batching):** Sort the training data by sequence length and construct batches from samples of similar lengths. This maximizes computational throughput, allowing the training of multiple large models within the time limit `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Stratification:** Implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** scheme for *each* model, stratifying by the `sentiment` label to ensure consistent class distribution `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.

### Training
*   **Objective Function:** Utilize **Cross Entropy with Label Smoothing** ($\epsilon=0.1$) for both start and end targets. This stabilizes training by penalizing overconfidence in noisy boundaries `Cite solution_lesson_node_00012` and avoids the convergence latency associated with complex hybrid losses `Cite solution_lesson_node_00026`.
*   **Efficiency Protocol:** Mandate the use of **Mixed Precision (AMP)** training. This is the non-negotiable enabler that allows the memory-heavy ensemble to be trained on the full dataset `Cite solution_lesson_node_00023`.
*   **Optimization:** Use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Uniform Learning Rate** (e.g., 1e-5). We explicitly avoid complex schedules like Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD), which have proven unstable for this task `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`.

### Evaluation
*   **Heterogeneous Ensemble Inference:** For every test sample, generate start and end logit vectors from all 10 trained models (5 folds $\times$ 2 architectures). **Sum these logits** to create a single, highly robust probability distribution. This leverages the variance reduction of the ensemble to bridge the gap between validation and test performance `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.
*   **Decoding & Reconstruction:** Select the indices `(start, end)` that maximize the sum of the averaged log-probabilities (subject to `start <= end`). Use the stored **offset mappings** to extract the exact substring from the original raw tweet `Cite solution_lesson_node_00017`.
*   **Neutral Heuristic:** Apply a deterministic override: if the sentiment is "neutral", predict the **entire text** of the tweet. This exploits the high correlation (Jaccard $\approx$ 0.98) effectively `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001`.
