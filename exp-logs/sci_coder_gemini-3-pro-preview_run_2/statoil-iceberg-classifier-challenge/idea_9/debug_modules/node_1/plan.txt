The `ValueError` encountered during training is caused by the dataset size not being perfectly divisible by the batch size, resulting in a final batch containing only a single sample. Batch Normalization layers require at least two samples to calculate variance, causing the crash when the batch size is 1. To fix this, I will add `drop_last=True` to the training `DataLoader` in `library/data_loader.py` to discard the incomplete final batch (Cite debug_lesson_3). Additionally, I will remove the deprecated `verbose` argument from the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler in `library/trainer.py` to prevent a potential `TypeError` in the updated PyTorch environment (Cite debug_lesson_2).
