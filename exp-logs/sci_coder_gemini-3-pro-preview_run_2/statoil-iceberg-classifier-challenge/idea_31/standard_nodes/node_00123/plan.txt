The issue is that the training pipeline loads the entire dataset from `train.json` and performs cross-validation on it, ignoring the predefined train/validation split in `metadata/train.csv` and `metadata/val.csv`. This causes the hold-out validation samples to be included in the training folds, leading to data leakage and an invalidly low validation metric. To fix this, I will modify `library/data.py` to filter the loaded data, ensuring that only the samples listed in `metadata/train.csv` are used for the cross-validation process. This enforces the predefined data splits as per **Cite debug_lesson_10**.
