### Strategy
1.  **Remove Redundant Global Attention (Cite Lesson 00147):** The previous solution (Idea 38) introduced a Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) block at the global bottleneck. Empirical evidence showed this was redundant because the backbone already employs hierarchical attention (CBAM). I will remove the `SEBlock` from the `CRWBN` model to reduce complexity and potential optimization interference.
2.  **Alleviate Terminal Bottleneck (Cite Lesson 00055):** The current architecture compresses the spatial feature path from 256 channels to 64 channels before flattening. Given the low spatial resolution (4x4) at this stage, this compression risks discarding critical information. I will increase the `READOUT_SPATIAL_DIM` from 64 to 128 to preserve more discriminative features in the spatial readout path.
3.  **Update Configuration:** I will update the working directory to `idea_39` to ensure a clean execution environment for this iteration.
