This provides a more robust metric and leverages the power of ensembling to improve performance.
3.  **Hyperparameters**: In `library/config.py`, I will increase `EPOCHS` to 30 to ensure the K-Fold models have sufficient time to converge, and increase `NUM_WORKERS` to 4 for better data loading efficiency.
