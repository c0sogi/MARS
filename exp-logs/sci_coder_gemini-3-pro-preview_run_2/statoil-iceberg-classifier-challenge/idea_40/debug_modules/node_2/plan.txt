The issue is that the model outputs predictions with shape `(Batch_Size, 1)` to satisfy the `BCEWithLogitsLoss` requirement (Cite debug_lesson_5), and `np.concatenate` preserves this 2D structure, resulting in a final prediction array of shape `(N, 1)`. However, pandas requires 1D arrays (shape `(N,)`) when constructing a DataFrame column from a dictionary. To fix this, I will modify the `predict` and `validate` functions in `library/trainer.py` to explicitly flatten the concatenated numpy arrays to 1D before returning them.
