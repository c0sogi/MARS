1.  **Architecture (Cite Lesson 00049, 00041, 00029):** I will replace the complex WEBN with the simpler, wider `A2SHN`. This model prioritizes backbone width (64-128-128 filters) over depth, uses **CBAM** for feature refinement, and implements **Channel Contraction** (reducing filters to 64 in the final layer) to control the parameter count of the dense head.
2.  **Training Strategy (Cite Lesson 00052):** I will switch from a Fixed-Split Deep Ensemble to **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation**. The previous solution wasted 20% of the training data by holding it out permanently. By using K-Fold on the *full* dataset, the ensemble learns from every available sample, which is critical for small datasets.
3.  **Optimization (Cite Lesson 00023):** I will adopt the "Low and Slow" strategy by increasing `PATIENCE` to 20 and `NUM_EPOCHS` to 100, while maintaining a low learning rate.

    Wide-Expanded Bottleneck Network (WEBN).

    Features:
    1. Visual Branch: Expansion-Compression topology with CBAM attention.
    2. Metadata Branch: MLP for incidence angle.
    3. Fusion Head: Concatenation and classification.

        super(WEBN, self).__init__()

        in_channels = config.IMG_CHANNELS

        self.stage1_conv = nn.Conv2d(in_channels, filters[0], kernel_size=3, padding=1)
        self.stage1_bn = nn.BatchNorm2d(filters[0])
        self.stage1_pool = nn.MaxPool2d(2, 2)  # 75 -> 37

        # Stage 2 (Expansion): 128 filters, CBAM, MaxPool
        self.stage2_conv = nn.Conv2d(filters[0], filters[1], kernel_size=3, padding=1)
        self.stage2_bn = nn.BatchNorm2d(filters[1])

        self.stage3_cbam = CBAM(filters[2])
        self.stage3_pool = nn.MaxPool2d(2, 2)  # 18 -> 9
