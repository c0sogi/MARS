1.  **Architectural Regularization via Channel Contraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041):** I will modify the `WB_DSN` model in `library/model.py` to implement a "bottleneck" in the final convolutional stage. Specifically, I will reduce the number of filters in Stage 4 from 128 to 64. This reduces the size of the flattened feature vector from 4,096 to 2,048, significantly decreasing the parameter count in the first dense layer. This structural constraint forces the network to learn more compact features and mitigates the risk of overfitting on this small dataset.

2.  **"Low and Slow" Training Schedule (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023):** I will update `runfile.py` to increase the maximum number of epochs from 30 to 80 and the early stopping patience from 7 to 20. The previous run converged prematurely; extending the training duration with the existing low learning rate (2e-4) allows the optimizer to navigate the noisy loss landscape of SAR data more effectively and settle into a deeper minimum.
