The training process failed with a `ValueError: Expected more than 1 value per channel when training` because the size of the training dataset was not perfectly divisible by the batch size, leaving a final batch with a single sample. Batch Normalization layers require at least two samples to calculate batch statistics (mean and variance) during training; a single sample results in undefined variance, causing the crash (Cite debug_lesson_3). To fix this, I will modify the `DataLoader` initialization for the training set in `library/data_loader.py` to include `drop_last=True`, which ensures that any incomplete final batch is discarded.
