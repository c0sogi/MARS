The execution failed because the `verbose` parameter has been removed from the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler constructor in recent PyTorch versions (specifically 2.2+), causing a `TypeError`. To resolve this, I will remove the `verbose` argument from the scheduler initialization in both `library/train_eval.py` (where the error occurred) and `library/model.py` (to ensure consistency and prevent future errors).
