The issue is caused by the training dataset size not being perfectly divisible by the batch size, resulting in a final batch containing a single sample. When the model is in training mode, Batch Normalization layers require at least two samples to calculate the mean and variance, causing a `ValueError` when a batch of size 1 is encountered. To resolve this, I will modify the training `DataLoader` in `library/data_loader.py` to include `drop_last=True`, which discards the incomplete final batch of the epoch. Cite debug_lesson_3.
