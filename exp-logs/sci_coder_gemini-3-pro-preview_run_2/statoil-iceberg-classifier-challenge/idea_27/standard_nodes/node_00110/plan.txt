I will modify `library/data_loader.py` to explicitly apply `np.clip` after scaling.

Lesson 00023 highlights that "Low and Slow" training is superior for this dataset. I will update `runfile.py` to increase `EPOCHS` to 60 and `PATIENCE` to 15, allowing the model to converge more robustly. I will also relax the learning rate scheduler patience to prevent aggressive learning rate reduction.
