Based on the lessons learned from the successes of the Bi-LSTM baseline and the specific failure modes of the MS-TCN, Hybrid, and Transformer experiments, I propose a **Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (CRCN)** with **Intermediate Supervision**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Hybrid" concept (Idea 3) by correcting the critical data and loss configuration errors that caused its failure, effectively combining the robust feature extraction of the Bi-LSTM (Idea 1) with the temporal refinement capabilities of the TCN (Idea 2).

### Model
**Architecture: Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (CRCN)**
The model consists of two distinct stages trained end-to-end, leveraging "Deep Supervision" to ensure both stages learn effective representations.

1.  **Stage 1: Sequence Encoder (Bi-LSTM)**
    *   **Role:** Acts as the primary feature extractor and initial predictor. We retain the **Bi-LSTM backbone** because it has proven to possess the necessary inductive bias for continuous gesture dynamics, unlike the Transformer which suffered from over-segmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Input:** Frame-wise feature vector (Upper-Body Skeleton + Audio).
    *   **Structure:** A stack of Bi-Directional LSTM layers.
    *   **Output:** Frame-wise class probabilities (Softmax).

2.  **Stage 2: Temporal Refinement (Dilated TCN)**
    *   **Role:** Acts as a learnable smoothing filter. Instead of raw features, this stage takes the **probability distribution output from Stage 1** as input. It uses dilated convolutions to look at a wide temporal context and correct "jitter" (isolated errors) and inconsistent boundaries produced by the LSTM.
    *   **Structure:** A Multi-Stage Temporal Convolutional Network (MS-TCN) block with dilated residual layers ($dilation=1, 2, 4, \dots$).
    *   **Output:** Refined frame-wise class probabilities.

### Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Feature Selection (Critical Correction):** We will strictly limit the skeletal input to the **12 Upper-Body Joints** (Head, Shoulders, Elbows, Wrists, Hands, Spine, HipCenter) and **Audio MFCCs**. We explicitly **discard lower-body joints**. This addresses the failure of the previous Hybrid model (Idea 3), which failed because the inclusion of noisy, irrelevant lower-body joints overwhelmed the TCN (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Dynamics & Normalization:** Joint coordinates will be normalized relative to the `HipCenter` and concatenated with their **temporal velocity** (first derivative) to explicitly encode motion dynamics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

**Validation Strategy:**
*   **Deterministic Validation:** The validation dataset will be instantiated with **augmentation disabled** (`is_train=False`). This ensures that metric fluctuations are due to model learning rather than random noise, preventing the unstable checkpoint selection observed in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training
**Loss Function:**
*   **Multi-Stage Weighted Loss:** We will minimize the sum of losses from both stages: $L_{total} = L_{Stage1} + L_{Stage2}$. This forces the Bi-LSTM to learn strong standalone predictions (preventing the "cold start" problem of TCNs) while the TCN learns to refine them.
*   **Aggressive Class Weighting:** We will apply Weighted Cross-Entropy with a strict ratio: **0.1 for Background and 1.0 for Gestures**. This addresses the "background collapse" failure of the pure MS-TCN (Idea 2) by ensuring the model is penalized heavily for missing sparse gestures, even if it increases false positives slightly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

**Optimization:**
*   **Regularization:** We will apply **Gaussian Noise** to the skeletal inputs during training to prevent overfitting and enforce robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Convergence Monitoring:** We will monitor the **Validation Loss** for early stopping to avoid the pitfalls of discrete metric fluctuations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
1.  **Forward Pass:** Pass the test sequence through the full CRCN. Use the output of **Stage 2** (Refinement) as the final prediction stream.
2.  **Temporal Smoothing:** Apply a **Median Filter** to the Stage 2 predictions. Crucially, use **Nearest-Neighbor Padding** (replicating boundary values) instead of zero-padding. This preserves valid gestures that occur at the very beginning or end of a video, which were previously deleted by zero-padding (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
3.  **Decoding:** Apply greedy decoding (argmax), collapse consecutive repeated labels, and remove the background class to generate the submission.
