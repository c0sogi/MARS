Based on the extensive empirical analysis and the specific failure modes identified in previous iterations (particularly the implementation errors in Idea 6 and Idea 8 that prevented the successful evolution of Idea 5), I propose the **Masked Dual-Stage Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (MD-CRCN)**.

This solution structurally evolves the successful "Cascaded" architecture (Idea 5) by adding a second refinement stage to incrementally sharpen predictions. Crucially, it introduces a **structural masking mechanism** and a **probability-space smoothing objective** to rigorously address the "padding leakage" and "optimization instability" issues that caused previous multi-stage attempts to fail.

# Model
**Architecture: Masked Dual-Stage Cascaded Recurrent-Convolutional Network (MD-CRCN)**
The model is composed of three sequentially connected stages trained end-to-end with Deep Supervision. A novel **Inter-Stage Masking Layer** is injected between stages to strictly enforce sequence validity.

1.  **Stage 1: Generation (Recurrent Encoder)**
    *   **Backbone:** A **Bi-Directional LSTM** (2 layers, 256 hidden units). We retain the RNN backbone to exploit its inductive bias for continuous temporal dynamics, which proved superior to Transformers for this task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025). We maintain high capacity (256 units) to ensure robust feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Input:** Frame-wise feature vector (Upper-Body Skeleton + Velocity + Audio).
    *   **Output:** Initial frame-wise Softmax probabilities ($P_0$).

2.  **Inter-Stage Masking:**
    *   **Operation:** Before passing $P_0$ to the next stage, we explicitly multiply the probability tensor by the binary sequence mask. This ensures that padding frames are strictly zeroed out, preventing "ghost" activations from propagating into the receptive field of the subsequent TCN (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

3.  **Stage 2: Coarse Refinement (Deep TCN)**
    *   **Backbone:** A **Single-Stage Temporal Convolutional Network (SS-TCN)** with **10 dilated residual layers** ($dilation=1, 2, 4, \dots, 512$). We maximize the depth to ensure the receptive field covers the entire sequence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Input:** The masked Softmax probabilities ($P_0$) from Stage 1.
    *   **Output:** Refined probabilities ($P_1$).

4.  **Stage 3: Fine Sharpening (Deep TCN)**
    *   **Backbone:** Identical to Stage 2. This second pass focuses on sharpening boundaries and correcting residual jitter.
    *   **Input:** The masked Softmax probabilities ($P_1$) from Stage 2.
    *   **Output:** Final probabilities ($P_2$).

# Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Robust Ingestion:** We will implement a defensive data loader that explicitly checks for and corrects matrix dimension transpositions (e.g., ensuring shape is `[Frames, Features]`) to prevent the semantic corruption observed in previous runs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00048).
*   **Feature Selection:** We will strictly limit skeletal inputs to the **12 Upper-Body Joints** (Head, Shoulders, Elbows, Wrists, Hands, Spine, HipCenter) and **Audio MFCCs**. We explicitly **discard lower-body joints** to eliminate noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Dynamics & Normalization:** Joint coordinates will be normalized relative to the `HipCenter` and concatenated with their **temporal velocity** (first derivative) to explicitly encode motion dynamics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

**Validation:**
*   **Deterministic Split:** The validation dataset will be instantiated with **augmentation disabled** (`is_train=False`) to ensure that checkpoint selection is based on true generalization rather than random noise artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

# Training
**Loss Function:**
*   **Multi-Stage Deep Supervision:** We minimize the sum of losses from all three stages: $L_{total} = L_{gen} + L_{ref1} + L_{ref2}$.
*   **Aggressive Class Weighting:** We apply Weighted Cross-Entropy with a strict ratio: **0.1 for Background and 1.0 for Gestures**. This is critical to prevent background collapse (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Probability-Space Smoothing (T-MSE):** We apply the **Truncated Mean Squared Error (T-MSE)** loss to the outputs of Stage 2 and Stage 3. Crucially, we compute this on the **Softmax probabilities**, *not* the Log-Softmax, to ensure gradient stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Masked Reduction:** All loss terms will be computed using the sequence mask to exclude padding regions entirely from the gradient calculation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

**Optimization:**
*   **Learning Rate:** We will use an aggressive initial learning rate (e.g., `1e-3`) rather than a conservative one, to ensure the TCN stages can escape the local minimum of predicting "background" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).
*   **Regularization:** Gaussian noise will be applied to skeletal inputs during training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

# Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
1.  **Forward Pass:** Pass the test sequence through the full MD-CRCN. Use the output of **Stage 3** ($P_2$) as the final prediction stream.
2.  **Label-Space Smoothing:** Apply a **Median Filter** to the discrete class labels (post-argmax), *not* the probabilities. This effectively merges fragmented segments and enforces temporal contiguity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).
3.  **Boundary Protection:** Use **Nearest-Neighbor Padding** during filtering to prevent the deletion of valid gestures at sequence edges (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
4.  **Decoding:** Collapse consecutive repeated labels and remove the background class.
