Based on the lessons learned from the successful Bi-LSTM baseline and the failed MS-TCN attempt, I propose a **Hybrid Bi-LSTM-TCN Architecture**.

This approach leverages the Bi-LSTM's proven ability to extract robust motion features from skeletal data (exploiting the success of Idea 1) while utilizing a Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) as a refinement stage to enforce temporal consistency and reduce jitter (addressing the structural weakness of pure RNNs). This hybrid design aims to solve the "background collapse" issue of the pure MS-TCN by anchoring the training with the robust recurrent encoder.

### Model
**Architecture: Hybrid Recurrent-Convolutional Network**
The model consists of two cascaded stages trained end-to-end:
1.  **Stage 1: Sequence Encoder (Bi-LSTM)**
    *   **Input:** A frame-wise feature vector concatenating skeletal data and audio features.
    *   **Backbone:** A stack of Bi-directional LSTM layers. This component retains the architecture that successfully converged in previous experiments (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004), ensuring the model can effectively learn the local dynamics of the gestures.
    *   **Output:** Frame-wise class probabilities (Softmax).
2.  **Stage 2: Temporal Refinement (Dilated TCN)**
    *   **Input:** The probability distribution output from Stage 1.
    *   **Backbone:** A Single-Stage Temporal Convolutional Network (SS-TCN) composed of dilated residual layers. The dilation factors ($1, 2, 4, \dots$) allow this stage to see a wide temporal context, enabling it to correct isolated errors ("jitter") and refine segmentation boundaries based on the confidence of neighboring frames.
    *   **Output:** Refined frame-wise class probabilities.

### Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Skeleton Normalization:** Compute all joint coordinates relative to the `HipCenter` to ensure user-position invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Explicit Dynamics:** Calculate the first derivative (velocity) of the coordinate vectors and concatenate them with the position data. This inductive bias is critical for capturing motion patterns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Audio Features:** Extract MFCCs aligned to the video frame rate and concatenate them with the skeleton features.

**Augmentation & Validation:**
*   **Training:** Apply Gaussian noise to the skeleton and velocity features to prevent overfitting and improve robustness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
*   **Validation:** Enforce a strictly **deterministic validation set** by disabling all augmentation (setting `is_train=False`). This ensures that metric fluctuations are due to model learning rather than random noise, enabling reliable checkpoint selection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Training
**Loss Function:**
*   **Aggressive Class Weighting:** We will apply Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss to the outputs of *both* Stage 1 and Stage 2. Crucially, we will use the aggressive weighting scheme derived from the successful baseline (e.g., **0.1 for Background, 1.0 for Gestures**) rather than the milder ratio that caused the MS-TCN to collapse. This ensures the model prioritizes sparse gestures over the abundant background class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Smoothing Regularization:** We will apply a Truncated Mean Squared Error (T-MSE) loss to the Stage 2 output to encourage smooth transitions, but with a low weight to prevent it from dominating the gradient and causing collapse.

**Strategy:**
*   **Convergence Monitoring:** We will monitor the **Validation Loss** (continuous) rather than the Levenshtein metric (discrete) for early stopping, as the discrete metric can exhibit spurious minima during overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
1.  **Forward Pass:** Pass the test sequence through the full Hybrid model. Use the output of Stage 2 (Refinement) as the prediction.
2.  **Post-Processing:** Apply a **median filter** to the prediction stream to remove any residual high-frequency noise. Crucially, use **nearest-neighbor padding** (replicating boundary values) instead of zero-padding to prevent the deletion of valid gestures at the start or end of the sequence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
3.  **Decoding:** Perform greedy decoding (argmax), collapse consecutive repeated labels, and remove the background class to generate the final submission string.
