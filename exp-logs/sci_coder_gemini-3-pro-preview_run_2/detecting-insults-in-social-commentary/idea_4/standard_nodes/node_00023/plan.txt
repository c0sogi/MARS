Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, the performance degradation in the last iteration was caused by two factors:
1.  **Ensemble Dilution**: Mixing the superior `microsoft/deberta-v3-large` model with the weaker `roberta-large` model using simple averaging lowered the overall AUC (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
2.  **Under-convergence**: The inclusion of Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP) and the multi-model setup necessitated reducing training to just 2 epochs to fit within time constraints, preventing the large models from fully converging (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

To optimize performance and restore the score above the threshold (0.9632), I will:
1.  **Revert to a Single Strong Backbone**: Remove `roberta-large` and focus computational resources solely on `microsoft/deberta-v3-large`.
2.  **Disable AWP**: Remove the expensive adversarial training step to free up runtime for more epochs.
3.  **Increase Training Epochs**: Increase `epochs` from 2 (runtime override) and 4 (config default) to **5**. This aligns with the configuration that previously achieved the best results (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

I will modify `library/config.py` to update these settings and `runfile.py` to remove the epoch override.
