It strips away the redundant complexities of previous iterations (Deep Supervision, Pyramidal Heads, Covariance Pooling) to focus on the two structural factors proven to matter most: **Channel Capacity** and **Signal Propagation**. By combining the robust "Wide" backbone with an improved residual block topology, we create a model that is both maximally expressive and exceptionally fast to converge.

### Model
I propose a **Custom Wide ResNet with Dense Residual Projections**.

*   **Backbone (Wide ResNet):** We will utilize the **Wide Channel Configuration** (`[32, 64, 128]`) established as the performance champion in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**.
    *   **Structure:** A 3-stage residual network designed for 32x32 inputs.
    *   **Spatial Preservation:** The network will downsample 32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8. We strictly preserve the 8x8 resolution to avoid the information loss associated with aggressive downsampling **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.
    *   **Kernels:** We will exclusively use **3x3 convolutions** **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00011)**.
    *   **Attention:** We will **exclude** SE blocks, as they add latency without improving the saturated metric of wide models **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00013)**.

*   **Structural Innovation (Dense Residual Projections):** We will fundamentally alter the downsampling mechanism within the residual blocks.
    *   **Standard Approach:** Typical ResNets use a $1 \times 1$ convolution with stride 2 in the shortcut path to match dimensions. This effectively discards 75% of the pixels in the skip connection, causing aliasing and information loss.
    *   **Proposed Innovation:** We will replace the shortcut downsampling layer with a **$3 \times 3$ convolution with stride 2**. This "Dense Projection" preserves spatial coherence and ensures a richer gradient flow during backpropagation. Previous experiments demonstrated that this specific architectural tweak accelerates convergence by approximately 33% **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00060)**.

*   **Head (Streamlined Global Pooling):** We will revert to a simple **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** head followed by a single dense layer.
    *   We explicitly reject **Multi-Scale Concatenation** because the "Wide" backbone already retains sufficient information, making the head complexity redundant **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00049)**.
    *   We explicitly reject **Deep Supervision/Pyramidal Heads** because auxiliary heads were found to degrade inference performance **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00058)** and are unnecessary for shallow networks **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00059)**.
    *   We explicitly reject **Covariance Pooling** as first-order statistics (GAP) are computationally superior and sufficient for this high-signal task **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00053)**.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00002)**.

### Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy is chosen to eliminate stochastic residual ranking errors **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)** while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00008)**.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00007)**.
*   **Training Duration:** We will utilize a **strictly reduced training schedule** (e.g., 15 epochs). The combination of the "Wide" capacity **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00049)** and the "Dense Shortcut" topology **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00060)** ensures the model will saturate the metric rapidly, making longer training runs wasteful.
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

### Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00005)**.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds. This maximizes robustness and minimizes stochastic noise.
