Based on the lessons learned, specifically **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00058**, the strategy of averaging predictions from auxiliary Deep Supervision heads during inference is detrimental to performance. The auxiliary heads are useful for gradient flow during training but are less discriminative than the final head. Therefore, I will modify the inference and validation logic to rely solely on the final classification head. Additionally, I will slightly increase the number of epochs to ensure convergence, as some seeds in the previous run peaked at the final epoch.

Here are the specific changes:
1.  **`library/inference.py`**: Update `_get_batch_probs` to ignore the intermediate head and return probabilities from the final head only.
2.  **`library/train.py`**: Update the `validate` function to calculate the AUC metric using only the final head's output, while maintaining the joint loss for optimization.
3.  **`runfile.py`**: Update the `validate_ensemble` function to align with the new inference strategy (using only the final head).
4.  **`library/config.py`**: Increase `NUM_EPOCHS` from 20 to 25 to allow for fuller convergence.
