To optimize performance, I will implement the following changes based on the provided lessons:

1.  **Architecture Optimization**: I will replace the "Ultra-Wide SE-RepNeXt" (Channels: `[96, 192, 384]`, Groups: 32) with a narrower "Multi-Scale SE-RepVGG" (Channels: `[32, 64, 128]`, Groups: 1).
    *   **Rationale**: Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00019` demonstrates that for low-resolution (32x32) inputs, a narrower channel configuration (`[16, 32, 64]`) is sufficient to saturate performance and significantly faster. I will use `[32, 64, 128]` as a safe, efficient middle ground.
    *   **Rationale**: Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00103` highlights the efficiency of RepVGG-style inference (Groups=1) over complex grouped convolutions for this task.
    *   **Rationale**: I will retain the Multi-Scale Aggregation head, as Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00016` shows it allows narrower models to match the performance of wider ones by preserving mid-level features.

2.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**:
    *   Increase `BATCH_SIZE` from 128 to 256 to leverage the reduced model size and improve throughput.
    *   Increase `NUM_WORKERS` from 4 to 8 to better utilize the 12 vCPUs.

3.  **Refactoring**: I will rename the model class from `UltraWideSERepNeXt` to `CactusModel` to reflect the architectural generalization and update all references.
