Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the progression of previous experiments—specifically the metric saturation achieved by the "Wide SE-ResNeXt" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**), the rapid convergence enabled by "Structural Re-parameterization" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00124**), and the efficiency of "Multi-Scale Aggregation" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**)—I propose the **Custom Ultra-Wide DBB-SE-ResNeXt with Multi-Scale Aggregation**.

This strategy represents the optimal structural synthesis. It evolves the "RepVGG" concept into **Diverse Branch Blocks (DBB)**. While standard Re-parameterization (RepVGG) sums a $3\times3$, $1\times1$, and Identity branch, DBB enriches the training topology further by including sequential convolutions (e.g., $1\times1 \to 3\times3$) and pooling branches that all mathematically collapse into a single $3\times3$ kernel for inference. This maximizes the "Training-Inference Decoupling" principle (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00103**), allowing us to train an **Ultra-Wide** architecture with complex gradient paths that collapses into a highly efficient, high-capacity inference engine.

# Model
I propose a **Custom Ultra-Wide DBB-SE-ResNeXt Architecture**.

*   **Backbone (Ultra-Wide ResNeXt):** We will implement a 3-stage backbone using **Grouped Convolutions** to maximize width efficiency.
    *   **Channel Configuration:** We will strictly adhere to the **"Ultra-Wide" Channel Configuration** (`[96, 192, 384]`). This massive representational capacity is identified as the primary driver of performance, allowing the model to fully capture the data distribution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**).
    *   **Cardinality:** We will use high cardinality (e.g., 32 groups) to enable this width without parameter explosion.
    *   **Spatial Preservation:** The network will downsample 32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8. We strictly preserve the 8x8 resolution to avoid the loss of fine-grained spatial details (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).

*   **Structural Innovation (Diverse Branch Blocks):** Within each residual bottleneck, we will replace the standard grouped convolution with a **Diverse Branch Block (DBB)**.
    *   **Training Topology:** The block will aggregate outputs from multiple parallel paths, including:
        1.  **Grouped $3 \times 3$ Convolution**.
        2.  **Grouped $1 \times 1$ Convolution**.
        3.  **Sequential $1 \times 1$ conv $\to$ $3 \times 3$ conv**.
        4.  **Average Pooling**.
        This rich topology provides diverse gradient paths, accelerating convergence and improving feature quality beyond standard RepVGG blocks (**Evolution of Cite solution_lesson_node_00124**).
    *   **Inference Topology:** Before inference, all these branches will be mathematically transformed and fused into a single **Grouped $3 \times 3$ Convolution**, ensuring zero computational overhead compared to a standard ResNeXt (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00103**).

*   **Attention:** We will integrate **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Blocks** after the fused convolution in each block. This accelerates convergence by adaptively recalibrating the rich, multi-group features (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).

*   **Downsampling:** We will use a **Dual-Path Downsampling Block** (fused for inference) that sums a Stride-2 $3 \times 3$ convolution and a Stride-2 $1 \times 1$ convolution. This satisfies the need for $3 \times 3$ spatial projection (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00060**) while retaining the precision of $1 \times 1$ projection (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00063**).

*   **Head (Multi-Scale Aggregation):** We will implement a concatenation-based aggregation head.
    *   We will extract feature maps from the end of **Stage 2** (16x16) and **Stage 3** (8x8) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).
    *   We will apply **Global Average Pooling (GAP)** to both. We explicitly reject Covariance Pooling or GeM, as standard GAP is sufficient for this high-signal task and avoids statistical instability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**).
    *   The concatenated vector will be passed to a single dense classification layer.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling to prevent interpolation artifacts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).

# Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy eliminates stochastic residual ranking errors (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**) while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**).
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
*   **Training Duration:** We will utilize a reduced training schedule (e.g., 15-20 epochs). The combination of the "Ultra-Wide" capacity, SE blocks, and DBB-enhanced gradient flow facilitates rapid convergence (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00124**).
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

# Evaluation
*   **Structural Re-parameterization:** Prior to inference, the model will be converted from its complex training form (DBB multi-branch) to its efficient inference form (single-branch grouped convolutions) to maximize throughput (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00103**).
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds. This maximizes robustness and minimizes stochastic noise.
