To optimize the solution, I will focus on improving training efficiency and hardware utilization while maintaining the high predictive performance (AUC ~1.0) established by the previous solution.

The strategy involves three targeted modifications:
1.  **Architectural Simplification**: I will reduce the depth of the `LightweightPyramidNet` by decreasing the number of residual blocks per stage from 2 to 1. This aligns with the finding that shallow architectures are sufficient and more efficient for low-resolution 32x32 images (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
2.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**: I will reduce the number of training epochs from 20 to 12, as previous experiments showed the model converges rapidly and extended training is unnecessary (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019). Simultaneously, I will increase the batch size from 64 to 256 to better utilize the A100 GPU and scale the learning rate accordingly.
3.  **System Optimization**: I will increase the number of data loader workers to prevent data bottlenecks given the higher throughput from the increased batch size.
