To optimize the solution, I will implement a **`FastRepVGG`** architecture, replacing the previous `WideRepVGG`. This new model is designed based on **Lesson 00019** (Right-Sizing Model Capacity) and **Lesson 00001** (Shallow Architectures for Low-Resolution), reducing the channel width from `[64, 128, 256]` to `[32, 64, 128]` and limiting the depth to 5 convolutional layers (blocks `[1, 2, 2]`). This significantly reduces parameter count and computational cost while maintaining the representational power needed for 32x32 images. I will retain the **Multi-Scale Feature Aggregation** (Cite **Lesson 00016**) and **Structural Re-parameterization** (Cite **Lesson 00103**) to ensure high accuracy and inference efficiency.

    Wide RepVGG Architecture:
    - Backbone: 3 Stages with [64, 128, 256] channels.

    - Head: Multi-Scale Aggregation (Stage 2 + Stage 3) -> Dense.
    """

        super(WideRepVGG, self).__init__()
        self.deploy = deploy

        # We use 2 blocks: 1 for transition/stride, 1 for depth
        self.stage1 = self._make_stage(3, 64, stride=1, num_blocks=2, deploy=deploy)
