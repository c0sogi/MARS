The `RecursionError` is caused by the `switch_to_deploy` method in the `UltraWideECARepNeXt` class. The method iterates over `self.modules()`, which yields the module itself (`self`) as the first item. Consequently, `self.switch_to_deploy()` is called within the loop, triggering infinite recursion. To fix this, I will add a check to skip the iteration when `m is self`, ensuring the method only propagates to submodules. This solution directly applies the guidance from Cite debug_lesson_8.
