Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Custom Wide ResNet with Hybrid Texture-Semantic Aggregation**.

This strategy represents the culmination of the architectural search. It synthesizes the **Raw Capacity** of the "Wide ResNet" (which achieved the highest metrics, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**) with the **Texture Sensitivity** of "Global Covariance Pooling" (which improved efficiency, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**), while strictly adhering to the **Statistical Stability** constraints identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**.

Previous attempts at using advanced pooling (Covariance) failed to surpass the baseline because they were either applied to "Narrow" models lacking capacity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**) or applied at deep layers where spatial resolution was insufficient for robust estimation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**). This solution resolves both conflicts by applying the advanced pooling *only* where it is mathematically robust, on a backbone that is proven to be sufficient.

### Model
I propose a **Custom Wide ResNet with Hybrid Texture-Semantic Aggregation**.

*   **Backbone (Wide ResNet):** We will utilize the "Wide" channel configuration that achieved the best historical performance.
    *   **Structure:** A 3-stage residual network.
    *   **Channels:** The network will scale as `[32, 64, 128]`. This ensures the model has the raw representational power to fully capture the data distribution, avoiding the limitations of narrower models (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**).
    *   **Kernels:** We will exclusively use **3x3 convolutions** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**).
    *   **Spatial Preservation:** The network will downsample 32x32 $\to$ 16x16 $\to$ 8x8. We strictly preserve the 8x8 resolution to avoid information loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).
    *   **Attention:** We will **exclude** SE blocks to minimize latency, as the wide backbone saturates performance without them (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).

*   **Head (Hybrid Texture-Semantic Aggregation):** We will implement a bifurcated aggregation head that assigns specific pooling operations based on the statistical properties of the feature maps.
    *   **Texture Stream (Stage 2):** We will extract the feature map from **Stage 2** ($16 \times 16$ resolution, 64 channels). Here, the spatial dimension ($N=256$) is four times the channel dimension ($C=64$). We will apply **Global Covariance Pooling (GCP)** with Newton-Schulz normalization. This explicitly captures second-order texture statistics (e.g., spine correlations) in a regime where the covariance estimate is statistically stable ($N > C$) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**).
    *   **Semantic Stream (Stage 3):** We will extract the feature map from **Stage 3** ($8 \times 8$ resolution, 128 channels). Here, $N=64$ and $C=128$, making covariance estimation impossible ($N < C$). Therefore, we will apply **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**. This captures high-level semantic context and is robust to low spatial resolution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**).
    *   **Fusion:** We will flatten the upper-triangular covariance vector from Stream 1 and concatenate it with the average pooled vector from Stream 2.
    *   **Classification:** This fused vector will be passed to a single dense layer.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Images will be loaded in their native **32x32** resolution and normalized to the [0, 1] range. We will strictly avoid resizing or upscaling (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will strictly adhere to the "light augmentation" strategy, applying only **Random Horizontal Flips** and **Random Vertical Flips**. We will explicitly avoid geometric rotations, color jittering, or cutout, as these techniques act as noise on this high-signal dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).

### Training
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will employ **Homogeneous Seed Averaging**. We will train **5 independent instances** of this architecture using different random seeds. This strategy eliminates stochastic residual ranking errors (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**) while avoiding the instability risks of heterogeneous ensembles (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**).
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler. This combination ensures robust convergence and generalization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
*   **Loss Function:** Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss.

### Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the original image, a horizontally flipped version, and a vertically flipped version for each of the 5 model instances (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**).
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission probability for each image will be the arithmetic mean of all TTA predictions across all 5 seeds. This maximizes robustness and minimizes stochastic noise.
