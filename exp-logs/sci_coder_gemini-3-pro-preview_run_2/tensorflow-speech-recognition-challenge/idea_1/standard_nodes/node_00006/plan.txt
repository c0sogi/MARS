I will increase the resolution to 128 Mels x 100 Time Steps (by reducing hop length).

Since the input dimensions are increasing significantly, I can safely increase the masking parameters (`freq_mask_param` and `time_mask_param`) to maintain effective regularization without destroying the signal, as the relative size of the masks will remain conservative compared to the new feature dimensions.

Additionally, I will switch the learning rate scheduler from `StepLR` to `CosineAnnealingLR` to provide a smoother decay profile, which typically aids convergence in transfer learning tasks. I will also increase the number of data loader workers to utilize the available vCPUs better.
