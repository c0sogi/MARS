I will optimize the solution by implementing **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00043}) to improve model generalization and stability. To ensure this addition does not negatively impact training throughput (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00045}), I will refactor the `EfficientNetV2Audio` class to remove the internal `GPUNoiseInjector`. Instead, I will instantiate the noise injector in the `Trainer` and apply it to the data batches externally. This prevents the EMA mechanism from redundantly copying the large, static background noise buffer at every step. Finally, I will increase the training duration to 50 epochs to allow the EMA model to fully converge.
