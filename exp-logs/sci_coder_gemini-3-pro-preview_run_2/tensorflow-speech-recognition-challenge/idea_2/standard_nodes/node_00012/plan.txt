I will implement the following optimization strategy:
1.  **Architecture Upgrade**: Switch from EfficientNet-B0 to **EfficientNet-B1**. This increases model capacity to capture more subtle audio features while maintaining reasonable inference speed (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).
2.  **Extended Training with Mixup**: Introduce **Mixup regularization** (`alpha=0.4`) to improve model generalization and robustness. To compensate for the increased difficulty of the training objective, I will extend the training duration from 20 to **50 epochs** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).
3.  **Label Smoothing**: Replace the standard CrossEntropyLoss with **Label Smoothing** (`0.1`) to prevent the model from becoming overconfident on training data and further improve generalization.
4.  **Throughput Optimization**: Increase `NUM_WORKERS` to 8 to maximize data loading efficiency on the available vCPUs.
