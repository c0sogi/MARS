The issue is caused by the default behavior of `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2`, which converts masks to PyTorch tensors but does not permute their dimensions from `(H, W, C)` to `(C, H, W)` unless explicitly instructed. The dataset class detects that the output is already a tensor and skips its own manual transposition logic, resulting in masks with shape `(Batch, Height, Width, Channels)` reaching the training loop, where `(Batch, Channels, Height, Width)` is expected.

To fix this, I will modify `library/data_loader.py` to initialize `ToTensorV2(transpose_mask=True)` in the `get_transforms` function. This ensures that multi-channel masks are correctly transposed to channel-first format during the augmentation pipeline. Cite debug_lesson_3.
