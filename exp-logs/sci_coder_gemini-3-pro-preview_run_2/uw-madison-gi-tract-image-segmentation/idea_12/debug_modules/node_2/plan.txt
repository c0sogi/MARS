The issue is that the `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2` transform does not transpose masks (from HWC to CHW) by default, unlike images. This results in the mask tensor having dimensions `(Batch, Height, Width, Channels)` instead of the expected `(Batch, Channels, Height, Width)`, causing a shape mismatch assertion failure in the training/verification loop.

To fix this, I will modify `library/data_loader.py` to explicitly set `transpose_mask=True` in all `ToTensorV2` instantiations within the `get_transforms` function. This ensures that multi-channel masks are correctly permuted to the channel-first format required by PyTorch models. Cite debug_lesson_3.
