The `InvalidSubmissionError` is caused by the inference pipeline enforcing a single output resolution for all slices in a volume based on the first slice's dimensions. Since the dataset contains cases where slice dimensions vary (inhomogeneous shapes), this leads to RLE predictions that do not match the original image dimensions expected by the grading system.

To fix this, I will modify `library/model.py` to remove the global dimension enforcement. Instead, inside the final encoding loop, I will retrieve the specific original dimensions for each slice from `orig_dims` and resize the predicted mask to match those specific dimensions before RLE encoding. This ensures every prediction matches its corresponding original image geometry, preventing shape mismatches during evaluation. This approach directly applies **Cite debug_lesson_4** (Verify Dimension Homogeneity Before Stacking Grouped Data) by handling slice-specific shapes, and **Cite debug_lesson_2** (Restore Original Dimensions Before Encoding) by ensuring the submission format matches the original image resolution.
