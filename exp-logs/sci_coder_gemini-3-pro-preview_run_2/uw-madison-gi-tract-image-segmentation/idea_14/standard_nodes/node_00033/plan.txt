Since `UWMadisonDataset` processes and groups the data (reducing its length), the raw indices are out of bounds. The fix is to remove this incorrect, redundant code block (lines ~60-190) and rely solely on the subsequent, correct implementation that uses a `DataLoader` to iterate over the dataset. This respects the dataset's internal structure and avoids index mismatches (Cite debug_lesson_9).
