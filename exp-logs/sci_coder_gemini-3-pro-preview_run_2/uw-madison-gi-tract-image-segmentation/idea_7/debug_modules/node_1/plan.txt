The issue is that the `albumentations.pytorch.ToTensorV2` transformation, by default, only transposes images from `(H, W, C)` to `(C, H, W)` but leaves masks in `(H, W, C)` format. This causes a shape mismatch in the PyTorch DataLoader, which expects channel-first tensors. As identified in the error analysis and `debug_lesson_3`, I will fix this by explicitly setting `transpose_mask=True` in the `ToTensorV2` initialization within `library/data.py`. This ensures that segmentation masks are correctly permuted to `(C, H, W)` before being passed to the model.
