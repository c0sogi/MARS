The issue is that the `LabelEncoder` for categorical features (position and team) is currently fitted only on the training dataset. In the demo environment, where data is subsampled to a small number of rows (2000), the training subset may not contain all possible categories (e.g., 'MLB') that appear in the validation or test subsets. This causes a `ValueError` when the encoder attempts to transform unseen labels in the evaluation sets.

To fix this, I will modify `library/dataset.py` to fit the `LabelEncoder` on the union of unique values from all data splits (`train`, `validation`, and `test`). This ensures the vocabulary is comprehensive and prevents crashes due to unseen labels, aligning with the principle of respecting vocabulary limits across datasets (Cite debug_lesson_19).
