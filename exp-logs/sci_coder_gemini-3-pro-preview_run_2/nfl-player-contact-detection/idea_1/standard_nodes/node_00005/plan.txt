This is a critical discriminator for contact (high closing speed at short distance) versus just being near each other (low closing speed).
    *   **Log Distance**: Since contact occurs at very small distances, the distribution of the `distance` feature is highly skewed. Adding `log(1 + distance)` provides the MLP with a more favorable distribution for learning spatial relationships.
    *   **Ground Handling**: For ground contacts (where distance is imputed as 0), `closing_speed` will naturally resolve to 0. This creates a distinct feature signature (Distance=0, ClosingSpeed=0, IsGround=1) compared to active player collisions (Distance~0, ClosingSpeed>0), helping the model differentiate these states.
