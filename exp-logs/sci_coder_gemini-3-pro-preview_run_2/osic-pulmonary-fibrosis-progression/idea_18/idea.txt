Based on the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose the **2.5D Context-Aware Hybrid Quantile-Elastic Pipeline**.

This solution introduces **2.5D Slice Encoding** as a structural innovation to capture local volumetric texture (e.g., continuity of honeycombing) without the computational cost or artifacts of full 3D reconstruction. It fuses this with **Explicit Radiomics** under strict dimensionality constraints, reinforcing the physical inductive bias. It strictly adheres to the "Deep Feature + Regularized Linear Solver" architecture and the statistical optimizations (Quantile/Median, ElasticNet, Analytical Scaling) validated in the lessons.

### Model

The architecture consists of a **2.5D Hybrid Feature Extractor** feeding into a **Mathematically Specialized Linear System**.

1.  **2.5D Slice Encoding (Structural Innovation):**
    *   **Concept:** Instead of feeding the CNN a single grayscale slice (which lacks Z-axis context), we construct a **3-channel input** for each selected position using the target slice $z$ and its immediate neighbors: $[z-1, z, z+1]$.
    *   **Rationale:** This allows the pre-trained CNN (designed for RGB images) to interpret local volumetric coherence (e.g., distinguishing cylindrical bronchiectasis from spherical cysts) while strictly remaining in the native **Axial Plane**, avoiding the interpolation artifacts of multi-view reconstruction **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00032)**.

2.  **Content-Aware Sampling:**
    *   We select the **Top 5 Axial Locations** with the highest pixel variance. This ensures the model focuses on the most heterogeneous (fibrotic) regions **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00031)**.

3.  **Hybrid Feature Fusion:**
    *   **Deep Texture:** We pass the five 2.5D images through **EfficientNet-B0** (pre-trained on ImageNet) and aggregate them using **Mean Pooling**. We explicitly avoid higher-order statistics (Std/Var) to maintain signal stability **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00034)**.
    *   **Explicit Radiomics:** We compute a **Global Density Histogram** (4 bins: Emphysema, Healthy, Fibrosis, Consolidation) over the full lung volume. This re-introduces the **Inductive Bias** that lung capacity is physically proportional to healthy tissue volume **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00013)**.

4.  **Dimensionality Reduction (PCA):**
    *   The Deep Texture embeddings (1280 dim) are compressed using **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** to **30 components**.
    *   The Radiomic scalars (4 dim) are concatenated *after* PCA to preserve their physical interpretability.
    *   This ensures the final feature vector remains small (~34 + clinical), preventing the curse of dimensionality **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00014)**.

5.  **FVC Predictor (Linear Quantile Regressor):**
    *   Target: **Median (q=0.5)**, minimizing L1 loss to align with the Laplace metric **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00016)**.
    *   Inputs: Features ($X$), Time ($t$), and **Interaction Terms** ($X \times t$). This preserves the **Varying-Coefficient** property for patient-specific decline rates **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00008)** and the **Linear Prior** **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00006)**.

6.  **Uncertainty Predictor (Elastic Net):**
    *   Target: **Absolute Residuals** ($|y - \hat{y}|$) of the FVC predictor **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00001)**.
    *   Inputs: Features ($X$) and **Time Horizon** ($|t - t_{baseline}|$) to model **Temporal Heteroscedasticity** **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00017)**.
    *   Constraint: We use **Elastic Net** (Regularized) to prevent overfitting to noisy residuals **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00023)** and exclude complex interactions to maintain **Parsimony** **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00011)**.

### Data

1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Volume Loading:** Load DICOMs and stack to 3D. Threshold (HU -1000 to -400) to create a lung mask.
    *   **Sampling:** Calculate variance for all axial slices. Identify indices of the Top 5.
    *   **2.5D Construction:** For each index $i$, stack slices $[i-1, i, i+1]$ into a 3-channel array. Pad with the center slice if at boundaries. Resize to 224x224.
    *   **Radiomics:** Compute the 4-bin normalized histogram on the masked 3D volume.
    *   **Tabular:** Standard Scale clinical features; One-Hot encode categorical features.

2.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   Extract EfficientNet features for the five 2.5D images. Compute the Mean Vector.
    *   Fit **PCA** on the training Mean Vectors to reduce to 30 dimensions.
    *   Concatenate: $[X_{PCA}, X_{Radiomics}, X_{Clinical}]$.
    *   **FVC Set:** Generate interactions: $X_{Fused} \times \text{Weeks}$.
    *   **Uncertainty Set:** Append horizon feature: $|\text{Weeks} - \text{Baseline\_Weeks}|$.

### Training

The training utilizes **Decoupled Residual Regression**:

1.  **Stage 1 (FVC Optimization):**
    *   Train **Linear Quantile Regressor (q=0.5)** on the FVC feature set.
    *   **Objective:** Minimize Mean Absolute Error (L1).

2.  **Stage 2 (Residual Computation):**
    *   Generate median predictions for the training set.
    *   Compute absolute residuals: $r = |y_{true} - y_{pred}|$.

3.  **Stage 3 (Uncertainty Optimization):**
    *   Train **Elastic Net** on the Uncertainty feature set with target $r$.
    *   **Objective:** Minimize Mean Squared Error (L2) + Regularization.

### Evaluation

1.  **Inference:**
    *   Construct 2.5D inputs and Radiomics for the test patient. Extract and PCA-compress features.
    *   Predict Median FVC using the Quantile Regressor.
    *   Predict the expected Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD) using the Elastic Net.

2.  **Analytical Scaling:**
    *   Convert the predicted MAD into the final confidence ($\sigma$) using the formula: $\sigma = \text{MAD} \times \sqrt{2}$. This analytically maps the predicted error to the optimal scale parameter for the Laplace metric **(Cite solution_lesson_node_00009)**.

3.  **Formatting:**
    *   Clip $\sigma$ at 70 ml.
