The error `ValueError: Input X contains NaN` during PCA transformation is caused by numerical instability when `StandardScaler` processes constant or near-constant features in the small "Mini" dataset (Cite debug_lesson_12). To fix this, I will modify `library/model_pipeline.py` to include a `VarianceThreshold` step in the preprocessing pipeline. This will remove features with zero variance before they reach the scaler, preventing division-by-zero artifacts and subsequent NaNs in PCA. I will also ensure this selector is persisted and loaded alongside the other preprocessors to maintain consistency between training and inference.
