The `TypeError` encountered in `QADataset` is caused by the `offset_mapping` column being loaded from the Parquet cache as NumPy arrays of objects, which `torch.tensor` cannot handle. To resolve this, I will modify `runfile.py` to disable cache loading. This forces the data to be re-tokenized from scratch, ensuring that `offset_mapping` is passed to the dataset as a standard Python list (compatible with PyTorch) and effectively invalidates the problematic cache (Cite debug_lesson_5).
