2.  **Ensemble Integration**: I will modify `library/model_trainer.py` to transform `AuthorClassifier` into an ensemble wrapper. It will train a `MultinomialNB` model (a strong baseline for text) alongside the `LogisticRegression` model.
3.  **Joint Optimization**: The training loop will be updated to optimize the **ensemble's** validation loss. The Logistic Regression component will be trained iteratively with early stopping, but the stopping criterion will be the log loss of the weighted average prediction (0.6 LR + 0.4 MNB). 4.  **Submission Control**: I will update `runfile.py` to force feature re-extraction (to apply the `min_df` change) and strictly enforce the conditional submission logic based on the validation score threshold.
