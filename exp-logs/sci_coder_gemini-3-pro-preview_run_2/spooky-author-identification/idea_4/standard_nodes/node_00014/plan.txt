5.  **Validation & Analysis**:
    *   I will train a proxy Logistic Regression meta-learner on the meta-training set and evaluate it on the meta-validation set. This strictly simulates the performance on the hold-out validation set.
    *   I will calculate the **Final Validation Metric** (Log Loss) on this hold-out set and print it.
    *   I will perform **Failure Analysis** by calculating the correlation between the per-sample log loss and the input text length (character and word counts) to identify error patterns.
6.  **Submission**: I will compare the calculated validation metric against the specified threshold (0.23237805822413304). If the metric is lower (better), I will call `library.stacking.train_meta_learner` to generate the final submission file.
