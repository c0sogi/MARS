1.  **Increase Input Resolution (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00005`)**: The failure analysis showed a significant negative correlation between image dimensions and F1 score (`Width: -0.30`, `Height: -0.25`), indicating the model struggles with larger images due to downsampling. I will increase `MIN_SIZE` to 1024 and `MAX_SIZE` to 2048 in `library/config.py`.
2.  **Correct Model Initialization**: I will modify `library/models.py` to pass `min_size` and `max_size` directly to the `fasterrcnn_resnet50_fpn` constructor. Setting these as attributes after initialization (as done in the previous code) does not update the internal `GeneralizedRCNNTransform`, meaning the model was likely still using the default 800px size.
3.  **Optimize Training Schedule (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`)**: I will increase `DET_EPOCHS` to 12 and delay the learning rate decay milestones to `[8, 11]` in `library/train.py`. This allows the model more gradient updates at the higher learning rate, which is crucial for distinguishing between the ~4000 character classes.
4.  **Update Threshold**: I will update the submission threshold in `runfile.py` to the new best of `0.6865`.
