To improve performance, I will switch the model architecture from **EfficientNet-B4** to **EfficientNet-B1**. While B4 is more powerful, its high computational cost forced the use of a small subsample of data (50k images) and limited training (2 epochs), which degraded performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012). By using the lighter EfficientNet-B1, I can train on the **full dataset** (186k images), which is critical for learning the long-tail distribution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

I will also increase the training duration to **15 epochs** to ensure better convergence for fine-grained classification (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005) and increase the batch size to **128** to maximize throughput on the A100 GPU.
