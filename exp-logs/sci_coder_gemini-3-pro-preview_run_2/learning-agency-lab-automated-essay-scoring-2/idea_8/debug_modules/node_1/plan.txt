The issue is a `RuntimeError` in `library/neural_net.py` caused by a dimension mismatch during tensor broadcasting. The `WeightedLayerPooling` class initializes weights for all layers (e.g., 25), but the `forward` method pools only a subset of layers (e.g., the last 4). Attempting to expand the full weight tensor to match the shape of the subset tensor fails. The fix is to slice `self.layer_weights` to match the specific layers being pooled (`[-self.layer_start :]`) before performing the expansion.
