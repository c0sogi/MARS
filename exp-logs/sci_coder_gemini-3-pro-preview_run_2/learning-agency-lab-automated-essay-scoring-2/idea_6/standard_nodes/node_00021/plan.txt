The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the DeBERTa-v3-large model with a sequence length of 1024 exceeds the available GPU memory when using a batch size of 4. To resolve this, I will first reduce the `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` to 2 and `VALID_BATCH_SIZE` to 4 in `library/config.py` to lower the memory footprint. Second, I will enable gradient checkpointing in `library/model_semantic.py` to trade computation for memory. Crucially, I will configure checkpointing with `use_reentrant=False` (Cite debug_lesson_2) and call `enable_input_require_grads()` (Cite debug_lesson_1) to prevent the "double backward" and disconnected graph errors common with custom training loops.
