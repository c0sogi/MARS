The issue arises from numerical instability when applying `F.softmax` to `float16` tensors produced within the `autocast` (Automatic Mixed Precision) context. The limited range of half-precision floating-point numbers causes the exponential function in softmax to overflow, leading to `NaN` values or probabilities that fail to sum to 1. To resolve this, I will explicitly cast the `logits` to `float32` before computing the softmax in `library/engine.py` (within both the `validate` and `generate_submission` methods) and in the inference loop of `runfile.py`. This ensures the probability calculation is performed with full precision, preventing overflow and ensuring valid probability distributions.
