The `RuntimeError` occurs because initializing the `ConvNeXt` model with `global_pool=""` alters the internal structure of the classification head (converting `Linear` layers to `1x1 Conv2d`), which creates a shape mismatch against the pretrained weights that expect a standard `Linear` layer (Cite debug_lesson_3). To fix this, I will remove the `global_pool=""` argument to ensure the model architecture matches the checkpoint. I will then update the `forward` method to call `self.backbone.forward_features(x)` instead of `self.backbone(x)`. This explicitly extracts the spatial feature maps `(B, C, H, W)` needed for the custom GeM pooling layer, bypassing the now-compatible (but unused) classification head.
