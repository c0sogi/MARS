The `CUDA out of memory` error occurred during the training of the `swinv2_base` model because the intermediate activation maps generated by the Transformer architecture at a resolution of 384x384 with a batch size of 8 exceeded the available GPU memory (approx. 16GB). To resolve this Activation-Induced CUDA OOM, I will reduce the `batch_size` from 8 to 4 and proportionally increase the `gradient_accumulation_steps` from 4 to 8. This reduces the peak memory requirement for storing activations during the forward pass while maintaining the same effective batch size for training stability. Cite debug_lesson_1.
