My strategy focuses on refining the initialization schemes for both the Transformer and the Residual Backbone components, based on specific lessons learned from previous iterations.

1.  **Positional Embedding Initialization**: I will modify `library/model.py` to initialize the learnable positional embeddings with a low standard deviation (`0.02`) instead of the default standard normal distribution (`1.0`). As per **Lesson 81**, high-variance positional noise can disrupt the semantic signal of token embeddings at the start of training, delaying convergence.
2.  **GLU Backbone Initialization**: I will modify `library/utils.py` to remove the explicit Kaiming Uniform (ReLU-targeted) initialization for `nn.Linear` layers. **Lesson 82** indicates that forcing ReLU-specific statistics on Gated Linear Units (GLU) degrades performance. Reverting to PyTorch's robust defaults is empirically superior for GLU-based architectures.
3.  **Cache Update**: I will update the cache directory in `library/config.py` to ensure a clean data processing run for this new configuration.
