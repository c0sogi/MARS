I will optimize the current Hybrid Attention-ResFunnel architecture by explicitly incorporating **Learnable Positional Encodings** into the Transformer branch and increasing the Transformer's depth from 1 to 2 layers.

**Reasoning:**
1.  **Positional Awareness (Cite Lesson 29):** The failure analysis of the previous solution showed that prediction errors were strongly correlated with specific character positions in the `f_27` sequence (e.g., `f_27_char_5`). Standard Transformer encoders use permutation-invariant self-attention, meaning they cannot inherently distinguish between characters based on their position unless positional information is injected. By adding learnable positional embeddings, the model can better capture the position-dependent grammar of the manufacturing control codes.
2.  **Increased Depth:** Increasing `TRANSFORMER_LAYERS` to 2 allows the model to learn more complex, non-linear dependencies between the position-aware character embeddings before fusion.
3.  **Constraint Adherence (Cite Lesson 5 & 16):** I will maintain the embedding dimension at 32 and the dropout at 0.35. Previous lessons indicated that inflating the embedding dimension beyond the vocabulary size (26) or reducing regularization leads to overfitting in this tabular domain.

I will also update the training epochs to 35 to accommodate the slightly deeper architecture and update the submission threshold to the previous best score.
