Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the implementation of a **Hybrid Attention-ResFunnel-GLU**.

This strategy introduces a structural innovation—**Sequence-Aware Feature Extraction**—to the categorical processing pipeline, while explicitly retaining and refining the highly successful **ResFunnel-GLU** backbone (Cite Lesson 17, Lesson 6). It addresses the limitation of treating the decomposed `f_27` string as a fixed-position "bag of embeddings" by using Self-Attention to capture inter-character dependencies before the deep fusion stage.

# Model
The architecture is a dual-stream network that fuses a sequence-aware categorical branch with a continuous branch into a deep, compressive residual backbone.

1.  **Stream 1: Sequence-Aware Categorical Processing (Innovation)**
    *   **Input**: The `f_27` feature is decomposed into 10 distinct character tokens (Cite Lesson 1).
    *   **Embedding**: Tokens are passed through a shared embedding layer (dimension 32).
    *   **Transformer Encoder**: Instead of immediately flattening the embeddings, the sequence of 10 vectors is passed through a single **Transformer Encoder Layer** (Multi-Head Self-Attention $\to$ Add & Norm $\to$ FeedForward $\to$ Add & Norm).
    *   **Rationale**: This allows the model to learn context-dependent representations of the characters (e.g., the significance of "A" at position 1 may depend on the presence of "B" at position 3). This captures the internal structure of the composite feature more effectively than a simple flattened concatenation.
    *   **Output**: The contextualized embeddings are flattened.

2.  **Stream 2: Continuous Processing**
    *   **Input**: The 30 numerical features are standardized.
    *   **Normalization**: A Batch Normalization layer is applied to the raw inputs to ensure distribution stability before fusion.

3.  **Backbone: Residual Funnel Gated Network (Exploitation)**
    *   **Fusion**: The flattened categorical vectors and continuous features are concatenated and projected to an initial width of 512.
    *   **Structure**: The backbone follows the successful **ResFunnel** topology (Cite Lesson 15), consisting of 3 stages with progressively decreasing widths (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128).
    *   **Residual Gated Blocks**: Within each stage, the network employs stacked blocks of the form $x_{out} = x_{in} + \text{Dropout}(\text{BatchNorm}(\text{GLU}(\text{Linear}(x_{in}))))$.
        *   **Gating**: The GLU uses a **Sigmoid** activation for the gate branch to ensure bounded feature selection (Cite Lesson 10, Lesson 13).
        *   **Regularization**: Aggressive **Dropout** (rate 0.35) is applied within the blocks to prevent overfitting (Cite Lesson 16).
    *   **Downsampling**: Transitions between stages utilize **Projected Residual Connections** (a linear projection on the skip path) to strictly preserve the gradient flow across dimensionality changes, avoiding the signal breakage observed in previous attempts (Cite Lesson 24).

4.  **Output Head**:
    *   The final stage output (width 128) is passed through a Linear layer with Sigmoid activation.

# Data
1.  **Decomposition**: The string feature `f_27` is strictly split into 10 integer-encoded columns (Cite Lesson 1).
2.  **Normalization**: Continuous features (`f_00` - `f_30`) are Z-score normalized.
3.  **Splitting**: Use the metadata-defined stratified validation split.

# Training
1.  **Optimizer**: Use **AdamW** with a high weight decay of `1e-2`. This aggressive regularization is critical to constrain the parameters of both the new Transformer block and the deep backbone (Cite Lesson 8).
2.  **Loss & Metric**: Optimize Binary Cross-Entropy. Crucially, the training loop must **decouple the stopping criterion from the loss function**. Training should continue as long as the **Validation AUC** improves, even if the validation loss plateaus, to fully refine the ranking of difficult examples (Cite Lesson 3).
3.  **Schedule**: Use a constant learning rate or a simple step decay. Avoid complex cyclic schedulers like Cosine Annealing to prevent overfitting (Cite Lesson 5).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: Iterate through the test set using the trained Hybrid Attention-ResFunnel-GLU.
2.  **Submission**: The raw probability outputs are saved directly to the submission file.
