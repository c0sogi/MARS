When merging these DataFrames on `id` without handling the duplicate column, Pandas automatically renames them to `target_x` and `target_y` to resolve the collision, effectively removing the expected `target` column name. To fix this, I will drop the `target` column from `df_train_raw` before the merge operation, as the metadata already contains the authoritative `target` values for the stratified splits. This prevents the collision and ensures the resulting DataFrame retains the correct `target` column name, applying the solution from **debug_lesson_1**.
