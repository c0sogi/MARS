The following changes optimize the solution based on the provided lessons:

1.  **Architecture (Lesson 00124)**: In `library/model.py`, I am removing the `BatchNorm1d` (Interface Normalization) applied to the flattened Transformer output. Mixing normalization scopes (applying batch/layer stats to embeddings before concatenating with Z-scored features) has been shown to degrade performance compared to direct concatenation.
2.  **Initialization (Lesson 00082, Lesson 00130)**: In `library/utils.py`, I am changing the initialization for `nn.Linear` layers from `kaiming_uniform_` (optimized for ReLU) to `xavier_uniform_` (optimized for GLU/SwiGLU). Additionally, I am removing the forced zero-initialization of biases to allow the default Uniform initialization, which helps break symmetry in projection layers. I also remove the redundant manual stem bias initialization in `library/model.py`.
3.  **Training Schedule (Lesson 00128)**: In `runfile.py`, I am increasing `EPOCHS` from 3 to 35. High-precision tabular models require multiple stages of learning rate decay (annealing) to resolve fine-grained decision boundaries, which cannot be achieved in a short run.
