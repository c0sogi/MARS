4.  **Model Architecture Verification**: Instantiate the `HybridResFunnel` model. Perform a dummy forward pass with the fetched batch on the configured device to verify the network architecture and output dimensions.
5.  **Training & Inference Execution**: Call the high-level `train_and_predict` function. Limit the training to a small subset of data (e.g., 2000 samples) to demonstrate the full workflow—training, validation, checkpointing, and inference—without waiting for a full epoch on the large dataset.
