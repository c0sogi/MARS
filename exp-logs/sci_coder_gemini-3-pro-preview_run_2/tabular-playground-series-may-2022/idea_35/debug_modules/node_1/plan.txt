The error `IndexError: index 1 is out of range` is caused by the `forward` method in `library/model.py` relying on a hardcoded sequence of three backbone stages and two transition layers. When `runfile.py` overrides the configuration to use a smaller, two-stage architecture (`Config.BACKBONE_STAGES = [64, 32]`), the model only initializes one transition layer. The hardcoded `forward` pass, however, blindly attempts to access `self.transitions[1]`, which does not exist. To fix this, I will replace the manual unrolling of layers with a dynamic loop that iterates through `self.stages` and applies the corresponding transition only if it exists. This ensures the forward pass automatically adapts to the depth of the network defined in the configuration, effectively applying **Cite debug_lesson_7**.
