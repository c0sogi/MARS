Optimization Strategy:
1.  **Increase Backbone Depth**: I will increase the depth of the `ResFunnel` backbone by adding one additional `GLUBlock` to each of the three stages (increasing from 2 to 3 blocks per stage). This leverages **Lesson 17** ("Residual Connections Enable Depth") to capture more complex feature interactions, which is crucial for pushing the AUC from ~0.996 to >0.997.
2.  **Remove Quantile Embeddings**: I will remove the quantile embedding stream. **Lesson 38** ("Direct Feature Fusion vs Early Projection") and **Lesson 19** ("Diminishing Returns of Width Scaling") suggest that adding pre-projected embeddings (like quantiles) can be redundant or detrimental when a powerful deep backbone is present. Relying on the raw normalized continuous features and the Transformer-encoded sequence simplifies the fusion layer and reduces noise.
3.  **Optimize Training Schedule**: I will increase `EPOCHS` to 50. With the `StepLR` scheduler (step size 10), this ensures the model benefits from a full 10-epoch "tail" at the lowest learning rate ($10^{-6}$), maximizing precision as per **Lesson 45** ("Optimizing Convergence Tails").
4.  **Full Training**: I will modify `runfile.py` to train on the full dataset instead of the fast baseline subset, enabling the model to reach the target performance.
