Based on the failure analysis and the provided lessons, the regression in performance (from ~0.9970 to 0.9962) in the previous solution was caused by the introduction of a **Gated Stem** (Linear -> GLU) at the fusion point of the sequence embeddings and continuous features. 

**Optimization Strategy:**
1.  **Revert to Linear Stem:** I will replace the "Gated Stem" with a simple **Linear Projection** stem. According to Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00078`, complex non-linearities at the fusion point lead to overfitting, whereas a simple linear projection allows the network to learn robust shared representations. This also aligns with Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00073`, which emphasizes the need for a dedicated linear stem before the residual backbone.
2.  **Maintain Hyperparameters:** I will retain the batch size of 1024 and the 40-epoch training schedule with `StepLR`. This configuration has been proven effective for this architecture (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00044` and `solution_lesson_node_00045`).
3.  **Rename Model:** I will rename the model class from `GatedStemHybridNet` to `HybridResFunnel` to reflect the architectural change.
