Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the implementation of a **Wide & Deeply Supervised ResFunnel-GLU**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution that addresses the specific failure modes of previous "Input Injection" attempts (Cite Lesson 20, Lesson 22) while maximizing the depth capabilities of the successful "ResFunnel" architecture (Cite Lesson 17). It synthesizes the "Wide & Deep" paradigm to anchor predictions to raw signals and "Deep Supervision" to enforce discriminative feature learning at all hierarchical stages.

# Model
The architecture is a dual-stream, multi-output network designed to combine the benefits of deep hierarchical compression with robust linear signal preservation.

1.  **Input Processing**:
    *   **Categorical**: The `f_27` feature is decomposed into 10 distinct character tokens (Cite Lesson 1). These are passed through a shared embedding layer (dimension 32) and flattened.
    *   **Continuous**: The 30 numerical features are standardized.
    *   **Fusion**: The flattened embeddings and continuous features are concatenated to form the **Input Vector**.

2.  **Stream 1: The Deep ResFunnel Backbone** (Cite Lesson 15):
    *   This stream processes the Input Vector through a 3-stage funnel structure with progressively decreasing widths (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128).
    *   **Residual Gated Blocks**: Within each stage, the network employs a stack of Residual Gated Blocks ($x_{out} = x_{in} + \text{Dropout}(\text{BatchNorm}(\text{GLU}(\text{Linear}(x_{in}))))$) (Cite Lesson 14).
    *   **Downsampling**: Transitions between stages utilize **Projected Residual Connections** (e.g., a $1\times1$ linear projection on the skip path) to preserve the identity signal across dimensionality changes, fixing the breakage observed in Idea 7 (Cite Lesson 24).
    *   **Deep Supervision**: Auxiliary Classification Heads (Linear $\to$ Sigmoid) are attached to the output of **Stage 1** and **Stage 2**. These heads allow gradients to be injected directly into the early and middle layers, preventing signal attenuation in this deep structure.

3.  **Stream 2: The Wide Path**:
    *   A parallel linear branch projects the **Input Vector** directly to a scalar output.
    *   This explicitly models the linear relationship between the raw features (including the shallow character embeddings) and the target, ensuring the model retains the baseline performance of the "Shallow MLP" (Cite Lesson 2) and is not solely reliant on the deep non-linear path.

4.  **Final Output**:
    *   The output of the Deep Stream's final stage (Stage 3) is passed through a classifier head.
    *   The final prediction is the sum of the **Deep Head logits** and the **Wide Path logits**, passed through a Sigmoid activation.

# Data
1.  **Decomposition**: The string feature `f_27` is strictly split into 10 integer-encoded columns to preserve position-specific semantic information (Cite Lesson 1).
2.  **Normalization**: Continuous features (`f_00` - `f_30`) are Z-score normalized to ensure scale consistency with the embeddings.
3.  **Splitting**: Use the metadata-defined stratified validation split.

# Training
1.  **Loss Function**: Optimize a **Weighted Multi-Task Binary Cross-Entropy Loss**.
    *   $L_{total} = L_{final} + \lambda_1 L_{aux1} + \lambda_2 L_{aux2}$
    *   Where $L_{final}$ is the loss on the combined Wide+Deep output, and $L_{aux}$ are losses on the intermediate stage outputs.
    *   Weights $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ (e.g., 0.3) ensure that the early stages learn discriminative features immediately, facilitating the training of the deep backbone.
2.  **Optimizer**: Use **AdamW** with a high weight decay of `1e-2`. This aggressive regularization is critical to constrain the high capacity of the ResFunnel and Wide branches (Cite Lesson 8).
3.  **Regularization**: Apply aggressive **Dropout** (rate 0.3 - 0.4) within the GLU blocks (Cite Lesson 16) and **Batch Normalization** (Cite Lesson 7) to prevent overfitting.
4.  **Strategy**: Train for an extended duration (30+ epochs). Decouple the stopping criterion from the loss; continue training as long as the **Validation AUC** improves (Cite Lesson 3).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: Iterate through the test set.
2.  **Prediction**: Generate predictions using **only the Final Output head** (combining the Deep Stage 3 and Wide Path). The Auxiliary Heads are discarded at inference time.
3.  **Submission**: The raw probability outputs are saved directly to the submission file.
