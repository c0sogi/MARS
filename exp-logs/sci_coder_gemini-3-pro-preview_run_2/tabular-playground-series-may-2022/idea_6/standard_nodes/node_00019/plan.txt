The optimization strategy focuses on scaling up the successful **ResFunnelGLU** architecture and correcting a critical execution bug.

1.  **Fix Execution Bug**: The `library/model.py` file contains a `main()` call at the global scope, causing the training loop to run implicitly upon import. This will be removed to prevent double execution and wasted compute time.
2.  **Scale Model Capacity**: Based on **Lesson 2** (Deepening Capacity) and **Lesson 17** (Residuals Enable Depth), I will double the width of the funnel architecture from `[512, 256, 128]` to `[1024, 512, 256]`. This increased capacity allows the model to capture finer-grained interactions in the high-performance regime.
3.  **Enhance Regularization**: To counter the increased parameter count, I will raise the `DROPOUT_RATE` from `0.35` to `0.40`. This aligns with **Lesson 16**, which emphasizes that GLU-based networks require aggressive dropout to generalize well on tabular data.
4.  **Extend Training**: The previous explicit run was truncated at 20 epochs. I will increase `MAX_EPOCHS` to `60` and `PATIENCE` to `15` in `runfile.py`. This applies **Lesson 3**, ensuring we do not stop early based on loss plateaus while the AUC metric is still climbing.
