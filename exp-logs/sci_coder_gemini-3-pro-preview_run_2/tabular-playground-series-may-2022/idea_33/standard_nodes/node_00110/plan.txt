To optimize the solution, I will revert the "Position-Distinct Tokenization" strategy (which degraded performance in Lesson 109) to a **Shared Vocabulary with Additive Positional Embeddings** strategy. This aligns with the findings that decoupling token identity from position allows for better generalization on this dataset.

Specific changes:
1.  **`library/config.py`**: Reduce `TOTAL_VOCAB_SIZE` from 260 to 26 to use a shared vocabulary.
2.  **`library/data.py`**: Modify `process_f27` to remove the position-based offset, mapping all characters to `0-25`.
3.  **`library/utils.py`**: Update `get_optimizer_params` to explicitly exclude "pos_embed" from weight decay, preventing the regularization from destroying positional information (Cite Lesson 98).
4.  **`library/model.py`**: Introduce learnable `pos_embed` parameters initialized with low variance (`std=0.02`) to the `ManufacturingNet` (Cite Lesson 81).
5.  **`runfile.py`**: Increase training epochs from 20 to 35 to allow the model to fully converge, as early stopping was identified as detrimental (Cite Lesson 67).
