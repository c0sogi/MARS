Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the specific nature of the task (denoising scanned text), I propose the **Wavelet-Integrated Context-Aware Coordinate ResUNet (Wave-CAC-ResUNet)**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** by replacing the spatial downsampling/upsampling operators with **Discrete Wavelet Transforms (DWT)** and **Inverse Wavelet Transforms (IWT)**. This addresses the "High-Frequency Reconstruction" challenge (Lesson 00020) and the "Inductive Bias" trade-off (Lesson 00031) by explicitly separating signal (text structure) from noise in the frequency domain without the information loss of pooling or the blurring of bilinear interpolation.

### Model: Wavelet-Integrated Context-Aware Coordinate ResUNet
The architecture is a U-Net that integrates frequency-domain processing into the feature extraction pipeline to maximize the preservation of edge details.

*   **Structural Innovation: Wavelet Downsampling/Upsampling**:
    *   **Encoder (Downsampling):** Instead of strided convolutions or max pooling (which are lossy), we will use the **Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT)** (using the Haar basis). DWT decomposes features into four frequency subbands: Low-Low (LL), Low-High (LH), High-Low (HL), and High-High (HH). These are concatenated channel-wise. This preserves all spatial information while reducing resolution, allowing the network to explicitly process high-frequency text edges (LH, HL) and noise (HH) separately from the background (LL).
    *   **Decoder (Upsampling):** We will use the **Inverse Discrete Wavelet Transform (IWT)** to reconstruct the spatial resolution. This provides a mathematically lossless reconstruction path, superior to the learnable transposed convolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020) or fixed bilinear interpolation.
*   **Backbone Blocks**: We retain the **Residual Blocks** with **3x3 Convolutions**. This maintains the strong local inductive bias required for denoising, avoiding the overfitting observed with large-kernel architectures like ConvNeXt (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Coordinate Attention (CA):** We integrate **Coordinate Attention** into the residual blocks. This allows the model to attend to specific spatial locations within the frequency subbands, effectively distinguishing between structural text edges and random noise artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
*   **Capacity**: We strictly maintain a base filter count of **64**, ensuring the model has sufficient width to process the expanded channel dimensions from the DWT (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Global Residual Learning**: The network is designed to predict the **noise residual** ($Clean = Input - Prediction$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

### Data: High-Density Contextual Processing
*   **Patch Size**: We will strictly use **128x128** patches. This size is compatible with the multi-level DWT decomposition and ensures sufficient spatial context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Sampling Density**: We will employ **High-Density Sampling**, extracting **100 random patches per image per epoch**. This ensures the model receives a rich and varied gradient signal, which is critical for training the attention mechanisms (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Augmentation**: We will apply aggressive geometric augmentations (random horizontal/vertical flips and discrete 90-degree rotations) to exploit the rotational symmetry of the noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training: Regularized Residual Optimization
*   **Loss Function**: Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the predicted noise residual. We avoid Deep Supervision to prevent the optimization conflicts observed in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Optimizer**: We will use **AdamW** with an aggressive **weight decay of 1e-2**. This strong regularization is necessary to prevent the high-capacity model from overfitting the small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Scheduler**: A **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler will be used to gradually reduce the learning rate, ensuring convergence to a sharp minimum (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Duration**: We will train for **100 epochs** to ensure full convergence, acknowledging the "convergence tax" of the sophisticated architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Evaluation: Robust Ensemble Inference
*   **Tiled Inference with Overlap**: Test images will be processed using a sliding window with **50% overlap**. Predictions in overlapping regions will be averaged to eliminate boundary artifacts and ensure every pixel is predicted with centered context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**: We will generate predictions for the original image and its geometric transformations (flips/rotates) and average the results to reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
