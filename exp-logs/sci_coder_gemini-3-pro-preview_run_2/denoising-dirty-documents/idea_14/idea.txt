Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the specific nature of the task (denoising scanned text), I propose the **Two-Stage Progressive Coordinate ResUNet (TSP-CResUNet)**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** by transitioning from a single U-Net to a **Cascaded Refinement Architecture**. This addresses the diminishing returns observed in single-pass models by decomposing the problem into "coarse estimation" and "fine refinement" stages, while strictly adhering to the robust component choices (Coordinate Attention, 3x3 Convolutions, Transposed Upsampling) validated in previous lessons.

### Model: Two-Stage Progressive Coordinate ResUNet (TSP-CResUNet)
The architecture consists of two identical U-Net sub-networks stacked sequentially. This design allows the second network to correct the errors of the first, leveraging the "Residual Learning" principle at a macro level.

*   **Stage 1 (Coarse Estimation):**
    *   **Input:** The single-channel noisy image.
    *   **Backbone:** A **Coordinate ResUNet** (based on Idea 5/6). We retain the **Coordinate Attention (CA)** modules in the residual blocks to preserve spatial precision (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**) and the **ASPP** module at the bottleneck for multi-scale context (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**).
    *   **Components:** We strictly use **3x3 Convolutions** and **Batch Normalization** to maintain the strong local inductive bias and convergence stability required for this task (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**). We reject large kernels (ConvNeXt) or adaptive kernels (SK-Net) which caused overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
    *   **Upsampling:** We utilize **Transposed Convolutions** to ensure sharp edge reconstruction, rejecting Bilinear Interpolation and PixelShuffle (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
    *   **Output:** Predicts an initial noise residual map ($R_1$).
*   **Stage 2 (Fine Refinement):**
    *   **Input:** A 2-channel concatenation of the original noisy image and the Stage 1 noise prediction ($R_1$). This conditions the second network on the initial guess, allowing it to focus on the "hard" residuals (e.g., noise near text edges) that Stage 1 missed.
    *   **Architecture:** Identical to Stage 1 (Coordinate ResUNet).
    *   **Output:** Predicts the final, refined noise residual map ($R_2$).
*   **Capacity:** We strictly maintain a base filter count of **64** for both stages to ensure sufficient representational power (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00021**).

### Data: High-Density Contextual Processing
*   **Preprocessing:** Images are loaded in grayscale and normalized to [0, 1].
*   **Patch Size:** We will strictly use **128x128** patches. We reject smaller patches to ensure the Coordinate Attention and ASPP modules have sufficient spatial context (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
*   **Sampling Density:** We will employ **High-Density Sampling**, extracting **100 random patches per image per epoch**. This ensures the cascaded model receives a rich and varied gradient signal (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply aggressive geometric augmentations (random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and discrete 90-degree rotations) to exploit the rotational symmetry of the noise (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).

### Training: Multi-Stage Residual Optimization
*   **Objective:** The model will be trained using a **Sum of Losses** approach. $L_{total} = \text{MSE}(R_1, GT_{noise}) + \text{MSE}(R_2, GT_{noise})$. This ensures that Stage 1 learns a meaningful coarse estimate while Stage 2 learns the final refinement, facilitating gradient flow through both stages.
*   **Target:** Both stages predict the **noise residual** ($Clean = Input - Prediction$) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with an aggressive **weight decay of 1e-2**. This strong regularization is essential to prevent the increased parameter count of the two-stage model from overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler will be used to gradually reduce the learning rate, ensuring convergence to a sharp minimum (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Duration:** We will train for **100 epochs**. We explicitly acknowledge the "convergence tax" of this deeper architecture and leverage the full 24-hour runtime to ensure convergence (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).

### Evaluation: Robust Ensemble Inference
*   **Inference Pipeline:** Test images are passed through Stage 1, the output is concatenated with the input, and passed through Stage 2. The final prediction is $Input - R_2$.
*   **Tiled Inference with Overlap:** Test images will be processed using a sliding window with **50% overlap**. Predictions in overlapping regions will be averaged to eliminate boundary artifacts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image and its geometric transformations (flips/rotates) and average the results to reduce variance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).
