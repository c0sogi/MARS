The `RuntimeError` is caused by a spatial dimension mismatch between the upsampled feature maps in the decoder and the corresponding skip connections from the encoder. This occurs because the input image dimensions (e.g., height 258) are not perfectly divisible by the network's total downsampling factor ($2^4=16$), causing rounding differences during downsampling that `ConvTranspose2d` does not automatically recover (Cite debug_lesson_5).

To fix this, I will modify `library/model.py` to explicitly check if the dimensions of the upsampled tensor match the skip connection tensor at each stage of the decoder. If they differ, I will use `torch.nn.functional.interpolate` to resize the upsampled tensor to match the skip connection's spatial dimensions before concatenation.
