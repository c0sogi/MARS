The `RuntimeError` occurs because `output_sum` resides on the GPU (inheriting the device from the input image), while `predicted_patches` was explicitly moved to the CPU to conserve memory during the inference loop. PyTorch cannot perform in-place addition between tensors on different devices. To fix this, I will modify `library/inference.py` to dynamically move the specific patch from `predicted_patches` to the device of `output_sum` before adding it to the accumulator. This ensures the operation is valid regardless of where the accumulator resides, applying the principle of dynamically aligning devices in accumulators. Cite {debug_lesson_7}
