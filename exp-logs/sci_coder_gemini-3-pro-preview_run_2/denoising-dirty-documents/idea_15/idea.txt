Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Reparameterized Coordinate ResUNet with Skip Refinement (Rep-CResUNet-SR)**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** by applying **Structural Reparameterization** to the convolutional building blocks. This addresses the "Capacity vs. Complexity" trade-off (Lesson 00021) and the "Inductive Bias" requirement (Lesson 00031) by decoupling the training-time architecture (multi-branch, high capacity) from the inference-time architecture (single-branch, strong local bias).

### Model: Reparameterized Coordinate ResUNet (Rep-CResUNet-SR)
The architecture is a U-Net that utilizes a "multi-branch training, single-stream inference" topology.

*   **Structural Innovation 1: Reparameterized Residual Blocks**
    *   **Training Topology:** Instead of standard Residual Blocks, we will use **Reparameterized Blocks**. Each block comprises three parallel branches:
        1.  A **$3 \times 3$ Convolution** (captures spatial context).
        2.  A **$1 \times 1$ Convolution** (captures channel correlations and increases non-linearity).
        3.  An **Identity Branch** (preserves gradient flow, if dimensions match).
        *   *Rationale:* This multi-branch structure significantly expands the solution space and gradient pathways during training, effectively increasing the model's capacity to learn complex noise patterns without the optimization difficulties of deep narrow networks (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00021**).
    *   **Inference Topology:** After training, we perform a **structural reparameterization** step. The parameters of the parallel branches ($3\times3$, $1\times1$, Identity) are mathematically fused into a **single $3 \times 3$ convolution**.
        *   *Rationale:* This ensures that the final model retains the strict **local inductive bias** of standard $3 \times 3$ convolutions, which was proven superior to complex kernels (ConvNeXt) for denoising (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**), while incurring zero computational overhead compared to a standard ResUNet.
*   **Structural Innovation 2: Coordinate-Refined Skip Connections**
    *   **Mechanism:** We insert a **Coordinate Attention (CA)** module on the skip connection path *before* the concatenation with the decoder.
    *   **Rationale:** Encoder features inherently contain input noise. Direct concatenation reinjects this noise into the reconstruction path. By applying CA here, we filter the features based on their spatial statistics, suppressing random noise specks while preserving structural text information before fusion (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**).
*   **Backbone Components:**
    *   **Attention:** We integrate **Coordinate Attention** after the reparameterized convolution in each block to preserve vertical/horizontal positional details (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**).
    *   **Bottleneck:** **Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP)** is retained to capture multi-scale context (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**).
    *   **Upsampling:** We utilize **Transposed Convolutions** to ensure sharp edge reconstruction, rejecting fixed interpolation or PixelShuffle (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
    *   **Activation:** **SiLU (Swish)** is used throughout (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).
    *   **Capacity:** We strictly maintain a base filter count of **64** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00021**).
*   **Global Residual Learning:** The network predicts the **noise residual** ($Clean = Input - Prediction$) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).

### Data: High-Density Contextual Processing
*   **Preprocessing:** Images are loaded in grayscale and normalized to [0, 1].
*   **Patch Size:** We will strictly use **128x128** patches. This ensures sufficient spatial context for the Coordinate Attention and ASPP modules (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
*   **Sampling Density:** We will employ **High-Density Sampling**, extracting **100 random patches per image per epoch**. This provides the rich gradient signal necessary to train the multi-branch Reparameterized blocks effectively (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply aggressive geometric augmentations (random horizontal flips, vertical flips, and discrete 90-degree rotations) to exploit the rotational symmetry of the noise (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).

### Training: Regularized Residual Optimization
*   **Objective:** Minimize **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** on the predicted noise residual.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with an aggressive **weight decay of 1e-2**. This strong regularization is essential to prevent the increased training-time capacity from overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**).
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler will be used to gradually reduce the learning rate, ensuring convergence to a sharp minimum (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Duration:** We will train for **100 epochs**. We explicitly acknowledge the "convergence tax" and allocate sufficient time for the complex training topology to converge (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).

### Evaluation: Reparameterized Ensemble Inference
*   **Reparameterization Step:** Before inference, the model's multi-branch blocks must be **fused** into single convolutional layers. This converts the heavy training model into the efficient inference structure.
*   **Tiled Inference with Overlap:** Test images will be processed using a sliding window with **50% overlap**. Predictions in overlapping regions will be averaged to eliminate boundary artifacts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**).
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will generate predictions for the original image and its geometric transformations (flips/rotates) and average the results to reduce variance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**).
