Based on the lessons learned, specifically the critical need for **data density** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019), **extended training duration** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013), and **multi-modal handling** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011), I propose the following optimized strategy.

This solution introduces a **Dual-Backbone Ensemble** trained via **Progressive Resolution**, combining the structural strengths of CNNs and Transformers without the data subsampling that caused previous ensemble attempts to fail.

# Idea: Dual-Backbone Ensemble with Progressive Resolution and Graph-Refined Retrieval

### Model
To maximize feature diversity, I propose an ensemble of two architecturally distinct models. This ensures the system captures both local high-frequency details (CNN) and global semantic context (Transformer-style), which is crucial for distinguishing hotels that share similar decor but different layouts.
1.  **Backbone A: EfficientNet-B4**: Retained for its proven efficiency and ability to resolve fine-grained textures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
2.  **Backbone B: ConvNeXt-Small**: Selected to provide a global receptive field and modern isotropic architecture, complementing the EfficientNet features.
3.  **Heads**: Both backbones will utilize **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** followed by a **Sub-Center ArcFace Head** ($K=3$). The Sub-Center mechanism is strictly retained to handle the multi-modal nature of hotel classes (e.g., grouping "bathrooms" and "facades" under one ID) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Data
*   **Coverage**: We will utilize **100% of the Training Dataset**. We explicitly reject subsampling. As established, model complexity cannot compensate for the loss of manifold density in high-cardinality retrieval (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Augmentation**: A robust pipeline using `albumentations` (HorizontalFlip, ColorJitter, ShiftScaleRotate, CoarseDropout) will be applied to force the model to learn structural identity rather than pixel statistics.

### Training
To enable the training of two models on the full dataset within the 24-hour limit while ensuring sufficient convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013), I introduce **Progressive Resolution Training**:
1.  **Phase 1 (Epochs 1-10)**: Train both models with an input resolution of **256 $\times$ 256**. This accelerates the initial convergence of the Sub-Center clusters and allows for a larger batch size, rapidly establishing the embedding space structure.
2.  **Phase 2 (Epochs 11-18)**: Fine-tune the models with an input resolution of **384 $\times$ 384**. This adapts the features to the higher resolution required to resolve small discriminative details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) without incurring the computational cost of a full high-res training run.
3.  **Loss Function**: Use **Sub-Center ArcFace Loss**. Crucially, **Label Smoothing is disabled**. The margin-based objective requires sharp gradient signals to compact clusters; smoothing dilutes this signal and causes underfitting in high-cardinality regimes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
4.  **Optimizer**: AdamW with a Cosine Annealing scheduler.

### Evaluation
The inference pipeline employs **Feature Fusion** within the **Train-with-Proxy, Infer-with-Instances** paradigm (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
1.  **Gallery Construction**: Pass the full training set through both trained backbones (discarding heads). For each image, **concatenate** the L2-normalized embeddings from EfficientNet-B4 and ConvNeXt-Small into a single vector.
2.  **Database Augmentation (DBA)**: Refine the Gallery. For every gallery image, find its $k$ nearest neighbors within the gallery and replace its embedding with a weighted average of itself and these neighbors. This smooths the manifold and reduces the impact of noisy training samples.
3.  **Query Generation**: Generate concatenated embeddings for the test set.
4.  **Query Expansion (QE)**: Retrieve the top $k$ matches from the refined Gallery for each query. Average these retrieved embeddings with the original query vector to form a robust search vector.
5.  **Prediction**: Perform a final Cosine Similarity search using the expanded queries against the augmented gallery to generate the top 5 `hotel_id` predictions.
