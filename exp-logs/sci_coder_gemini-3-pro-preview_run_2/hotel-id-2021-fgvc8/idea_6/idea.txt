# Idea: Dual-Backbone Sub-Center ArcFace with Graph-Based Embedding Augmentation

### Model
To capture the full spectrum of visual features—from the geometric lines of building facades to the fine-grained textures of interior decor—I propose a **Dual-Backbone Ensemble**.
*   **Backbone 1: EfficientNet-B5**: Retained for its proven capability to capture high-frequency structural details at high resolutions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Backbone 2: ConvNeXt-Base**: Introduced as a structural innovation. ConvNeXt modernizes CNNs with Transformer-like design principles (isotropic stages, larger kernels), allowing it to capture global context and textures that complement the localized features of EfficientNet.
*   **Pooling**: Both backbones will utilize **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** to efficiently aggregate spatial features while focusing on salient activations.
*   **Head**: Both models will employ a **Sub-Center ArcFace Head** ($K=3$). This is critical to handling the multi-modal nature of the "hotel" class (e.g., grouping "bathrooms" and "lobbies" into distinct sub-centers while mapping them to the same ID), which single-center models fail to do (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Data
*   **Resolution**: All images will be resized to **384 $\times$ 384** pixels. This resolution was empirically validated as necessary to resolve the fine-grained details required for this task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Preprocessing**: Images will be normalized using standard ImageNet mean and standard deviation.
*   **Augmentation**: A rigorous augmentation pipeline (Albumentations) including **HorizontalFlip**, **ColorJitter**, **ShiftScaleRotate**, and **CoarseDropout** will be applied. This prevents the model from overfitting to superficial pixel statistics and forces it to learn robust, invariant identity features.

### Training
*   **Loss Function**: The primary objective is **Sub-Center ArcFace Loss**. Crucially, **Label Smoothing must be disabled**. As learned, smoothing dilutes the sparse gradient signals required for margin-based losses to converge in high-cardinality regimes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Optimizer**: Use **AdamW** with a weight decay of $1e-2$.
*   **Scheduler**: Implement a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate schedule.
*   **Duration**: The models will be trained for a minimum of **15 epochs**. Margin-based objectives require significantly longer training phases to compact intra-class clusters compared to standard classification; short runs ("fast baselines") lead to underfitting and false negative evaluations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

### Evaluation
The inference process undergoes a structural innovation, moving from simple nearest-neighbor search to a **Graph-Based Embedding Augmentation** pipeline.
1.  **Feature Extraction**: Pass the Test (Query) and Train (Gallery) images through both backbones. The final embedding for each image is the **concatenation** of the L2-normalized vectors from EfficientNet-B5 and ConvNeXt-Base.
2.  **Inference Paradigm**: Strictly follow the **Train-with-Proxy, Infer-with-Instances** approach. Discard the Sub-Center heads and use the training set embeddings as the Gallery (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
3.  **Database Augmentation (DBA)**: Before querying, refine the Gallery. For each gallery image, find its top $k$ nearest neighbors within the gallery and replace its embedding with a weighted average of itself and these neighbors. This reduces noise (e.g., occlusion, bad lighting) by pulling outliers towards the local cluster center.
4.  **Query Expansion (QE)**: Perform an initial retrieval for each test image. Take the top $k$ retrieved gallery embeddings, average them with the original query vector, and perform a second, final retrieval. This leverages the high-confidence matches to "denoise" the query.
5.  **Prediction**: Return the top 5 `hotel_id`s based on the cosine similarity scores from the final retrieval step.
