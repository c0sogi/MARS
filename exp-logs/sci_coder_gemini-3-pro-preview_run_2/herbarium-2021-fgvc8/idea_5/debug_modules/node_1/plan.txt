The condition `if val_f1 > best_f1` (i.e., `0.0 > 0.0`) evaluated to `False`, so the model checkpoint was never saved during Stage 1. Consequently, Stage 2 failed with a `FileNotFoundError` when attempting to load `best_model.pth`.

To fix this, I will initialize `best_f1` to `-1.0`. This ensures that the model is saved at least once (after the first epoch) even if the F1 score is `0.0`, guaranteeing that the checkpoint file exists for Stage 2.
