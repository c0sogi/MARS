Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the extracted lessons, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble of Physically-Aligned Legacy Architectures (EfficientNet-B0 & ResNet-34)**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution from the single-model baseline (Idea 4) to a robust ensemble. It explicitly corrects the signal processing errors (missing standardization, resizing artifacts) that caused the failure of previous ensemble attempts (Idea 6, Idea 7) and the optimization errors (missing scheduler) that caused the failure of the cross-validation attempt (Idea 10).

# Model
We will construct a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of two distinct convolutional architectures. This combines the high-performance feature extraction of EfficientNet with the architectural distinctness of ResNet to maximize variance reduction, strictly adhering to "Legacy" backbones for stability.

1.  **Component A: EfficientNet-B0**
    *   **Retention:** We retain the EfficientNet-B0 backbone initialized with standard ImageNet weights. This was the core of the current best solution (Idea 4). We explicitly reject "Noisy Student" weights as they transfer poorly to this domain **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**.
2.  **Component B: ResNet-34**
    *   **Evolution:** We re-introduce **ResNet-34**. Although it was part of a failed ensemble in Idea 6, the failure was attributed to missing normalization, not the architecture itself. ResNet-34 is a "Legacy" architecture that is significantly more robust than modern alternatives like ConvNeXt **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013** and provides complementary inductive biases to EfficientNet.
3.  **Common Structural Modifications:**
    *   **Pooling:** Both models will replace Global Average Pooling with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. This is critical for detecting transient whale calls that would otherwise be washed out by background noise **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
    *   **Input Adaptation:** Both models will be modified to accept **1-channel input**. The weights for the first convolutional layer will be initialized by **averaging the weights of the original pretrained RGB channels** to preserve learned spatial filters **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

# Data
The data pipeline is rigorously standardized to correct the "Resizing" and "Normalization" errors that undermined previous ensemble attempts.

1.  **Spectrogram Generation:**
    *   **FFT Parameters:** We will set `n_fft=1024` and `hop_length=64`. This ensures a high ratio of FFT bins to Mel bands (>4:1) to prevent aliasing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** and provides sufficient temporal resolution to resolve short call contours **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Mel Filterbank:** `n_mels=128`, `normalized=False`. We explicitly disable area normalization to preserve the environmental "Pink" noise spectral tilt **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Native Resolution:** Inputs will be fed to the models at their **native resolution** (approx. $128 \times 63$). We will **NOT** resize them to $224 \times 224$, as upsampling introduces interpolation artifacts that degrade performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**.
    *   **Instance Standardization:** We will apply **Zero-Mean, Unit-Variance standardization** to each clip individually. This is the critical missing component that caused the failure of Idea 6 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
3.  **Augmentation:**
    *   We will use **SpecAugment** (Time and Frequency Masking) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**. We will explicitly **exclude Mixup**, as it degrades the integrity of transient signals **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

# Training
We will implement **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** for both architectures, ensuring robust generalization.

1.  **Optimization Schedule:** We will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate Scheduler**. This corrects the fatal error in Idea 10 (which used a constant LR) and ensures the models can fine-tune effectively **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**.
2.  **Regularization:** We will use a **Low Weight Decay of `1e-4`** to avoid over-constraining the transfer learning process **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
3.  **Sampling & Batching:** A **WeightedRandomSampler** will be used to balance the classes, with a **Batch Size of 128** to stabilize Batch Normalization statistics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
4.  **Convergence:** We will prioritize full convergence (e.g., 20 epochs with patience of 5-7) for all folds, avoiding the under-training that handicapped the ensemble in Idea 7 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Test clips will be processed using the exact same pipeline (1024/64 STFT, Native Resolution, Instance Standardization).
2.  **Ensembling:** We will generate predictions using **Soft Voting** (averaging) of the probability outputs from all 10 trained models (5 folds $\times$ 2 architectures). This combines the strengths of two structurally distinct, fully converged, and correctly normalized legacy architectures.
