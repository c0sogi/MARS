To optimize the performance of the Right Whale Call detection model, I will implement a strategy focused on improving input representation quality and training robustness.

**Strategy:**
1.  **High-Resolution Spectrograms:** I will reduce the `HOP_LENGTH` in `Config` from 128 to 20. This increases the time resolution of the generated spectrograms (from ~32 frames to ~200 frames), significantly reducing the information loss that occurred when resizing the original low-resolution spectrograms to the 224x224 input size required by the model.
2.  **Data Augmentation:** I will update `WhaleDataset` to support **SpecAugment** (Frequency and Time Masking) during training. This helps the model generalize better by forcing it to rely on partial spectral features.
3.  **Regularization & Optimization:**
    -   I will implement **Mixup** in `trainer.py`. This technique encourages linear behavior in-between training examples, smoothing the decision boundary.
    -   I will add a **Cosine Annealing Learning Rate Scheduler** to `trainer.py` to refine the weights as training progresses.
    -   I will increase `NUM_EPOCHS` to 15 to allow sufficient time for the model to converge with these regularizers.
4.  **Conditional Submission:** I will modify `runfile.py` to only generate a submission if the validation AUC exceeds the previous best of `0.993639`.

These changes build upon the effective weight initialization strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) already present in the solution, ensuring we maintain the benefits of transfer learning while enhancing the training pipeline.
