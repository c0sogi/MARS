Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the extracted lessons, I propose a **Metric-Aligned Heterogeneous Stacked Ensemble with Noisy Student Initialization**.

This strategy represents a structural synthesis of the most successful components from previous ideas (Idea 4, Idea 12) while explicitly correcting the implementation flaws that caused the failure of the advanced ensembles in Idea 14 and Idea 15. It combines the superior feature extraction of **Noisy Student** weights with the robustness of **ResNet**, strictly enforces the "Golden Recipe" of signal processing, and resolves the conflict regarding convergence criteria by prioritizing **Rank-Based Early Stopping**.

# Model
We will construct a **Two-Stage Stacked Generalization Ensemble**.

**Level 0: Heterogeneous Base Learners**
We will employ two structurally distinct "Legacy" CNN architectures. This combination exploits the diverse inductive biases of Residual learning and MBConv blocks to maximize error decorrelation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**.

1.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Noisy Student):**
    *   **Initialization:** We will utilize weights pre-trained on JFT-300M (`ns_jft_in1k`). Lesson 00054 confirmed that these weights, when properly regularized, outperform standard ImageNet weights for this domain **Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**.
    *   **Role:** Primary feature extractor using Inverted Residuals and Squeeze-and-Excitation.
2.  **ResNet-34 (ImageNet):**
    *   **Initialization:** Standard ImageNet weights.
    *   **Role:** Provides deep residual learning biases. It serves as a robust complementary model to EfficientNet, stabilizing the ensemble **Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**.

**Common Structural Components:**
*   **Pooling:** Both models will replace Global Average Pooling with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** to maximize the detection of transient whale calls in noise **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer of both models will be adapted to **1-channel input**, initialized by averaging the original RGB weights to preserve learned spatial filters **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

**Level 1: Meta-Learner**
*   A **Logistic Regression** model will be trained on the concatenated Out-Of-Fold (OOF) probability predictions from the Level 0 models. This learns the optimal weighted combination of the base learners **Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**.

# Data
The data pipeline is rigorously standardized to the "Golden Recipe" identified in Idea 4, incorporating the critical dynamic range correction missed in Idea 15.

1.  **Spectrogram Generation:**
    *   **FFT Parameters:** `n_fft=1024` and `hop_length=64`. This ensures high frequency resolution (>4:1 bin-to-mel ratio) to prevent aliasing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** and sufficient temporal resolution to resolve short call contours **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Mel Filterbank:** `n_mels=128`, `normalized=False`. We explicitly disable area normalization to preserve the environmental "Pink" noise spectral tilt **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
    *   **Dynamic Range (Correction):** We will apply **`top_db=80`** during the Amplitude-to-dB conversion. This clamps the noise floor, preventing the "long tail" of silence from skewing the subsequent normalization, a critical error in Idea 15 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00062**.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Native Resolution:** Inputs will be fed to the models at their generated dimensions (approx. $128 \times 63$). We will **NOT** resize to $224 \times 224$ to avoid interpolation artifacts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**.
    *   **Instance Standardization:** We will apply **Zero-Mean, Unit-Variance standardization** to each clip individually. This is critical for convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
3.  **Augmentation:**
    *   **SpecAugment** (Time/Freq masking) will be used **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**. Mixup is explicitly excluded **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

# Training
We implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** workflow optimized for rank-based performance.

1.  **Sampling:** A **WeightedRandomSampler** will be used to balance the classes in every batch **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
2.  **Batch Size:** We will maximize the batch size to **128** to stabilize the gradients **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
3.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a **Low Weight Decay of `1e-4`**. This prevents over-constraining the Noisy Student weights during adaptation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
    *   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing to ensure effective fine-tuning **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**.
4.  **Convergence Strategy (Structural Correction):**
    *   We will use **Early Stopping monitoring Validation AUC** (Target Metric).
    *   *Justification:* We explicitly reject the Loss-based monitoring from Idea 15. Lesson 00066 demonstrated that for rank-based tasks, the model often continues to improve its discrimination (AUC) even after the Loss begins to degrade (calibration drift). Since our metric is AUC, we must optimize directly for it **Cite solution_lesson_node_00066**.

# Evaluation
We utilize a **Bagged-Stacking** protocol to ensure robust inference.

1.  **Base Prediction (Bagging):**
    *   For the Test set, we will generate predictions from all 5 folds of the EfficientNet model and average them. Similarly, we will average all 5 folds of the ResNet model. This "Bagging" reduces variance and provides a stable input to the meta-learner **Cite solution_lesson_node_00061**.
2.  **Stacking:**
    *   The averaged test predictions will be fed into the trained Level 1 Logistic Regression model to generate the final submission probability.
