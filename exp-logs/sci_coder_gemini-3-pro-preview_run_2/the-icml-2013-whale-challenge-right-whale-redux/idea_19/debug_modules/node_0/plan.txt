Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Configuration Setup**: I will override specific parameters in `library.config` (e.g., `NUM_EPOCHS`, `BATCH_SIZE`, `WORK_DIR`) to ensure the script runs quickly and writes to a dedicated demo directory.
2.  **Data Loading**: I will utilize `library.dataset.get_dataloaders` with a `debug_limit` to load a small subset of the data. I will verify the integrity of the loaded data batches (shapes, types).
3.  **Model Initialization**: I will instantiate a model using `library.models.get_model` and perform a dummy forward pass to verify the architecture and output dimensions.
4.  **Training Simulation**: I will execute `library.engine.train_fold` for a single epoch on the small dataset. This effectively demonstrates the training loop, validation, and checkpointing mechanisms.
5.  **Ensemble/Stacking Logic**: Since training multiple large models is time-prohibitive for this demo, I will generate synthetic "Out-Of-Fold" (OOF) predictions matching the schema expected by `library.stacking`. I will then use these to demonstrate training the meta-learner, saving coefficients, and generating stacked predictions.
6.  **Submission Generation**: I will use the stacked predictions to generate a sample submission file using `library.stacking.create_submission` and verify its format.
