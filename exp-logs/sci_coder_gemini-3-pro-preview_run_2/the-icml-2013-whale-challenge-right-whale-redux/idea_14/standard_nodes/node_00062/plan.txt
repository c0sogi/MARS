To improve the solution, I will implement a robust **Stacking Ensemble with Bagged Evaluation**, reverting to the strategy that yielded the "Current Best" score of 0.9960 while addressing the evaluation flaws of the previous attempt.

### Strategy Outline
1.  **Refined Model Selection**: I will remove `densenet121` from the ensemble. As noted in **Lesson 00037**, adding weaker models to a high-performing ensemble via simple averaging can degrade performance. I will stick to the strong combination of `tf_efficientnet_b0.ns_jft_in1k` and `resnet34` which proved effective in **Lesson 00053**.
2.  **Strict Hold-out Evaluation (Bagging)**: I will fundamentally change the data splitting strategy. Instead of merging Train and Validation sets (which led to underestimating performance via OOF metrics as per **Lesson 00061**), I will perform 5-Fold Cross-Validation strictly on the `train.csv` data. The `val.csv` data will be treated as a pure **Hold-out** set.
3.  **Bagged Predictions**: The final prediction for the Hold-out set (and Test set) will be the average of predictions from all 5 fold-models (Bagging). This reduces variance compared to single-model OOF predictions.
4.  **Stacking on OOF**: The Meta-Learner (Logistic Regression) will be trained on the OOF predictions from the `train.csv` CV loop and the ground truth `train_labels`. It will then be evaluated on the Bagged predictions of the Hold-out set.
5.  **Hyperparameters**: I will maintain the successful settings: `Batch Size 128`, `15 Epochs` with `Early Stopping` monitoring **Validation Loss** (Cite **Lesson 00055**), and `Cosine Annealing` scheduler.
