The previous implementation suffered from **data leakage** in the stacking phase. The meta-learner (Logistic Regression) was trained on the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions of the entire dataset (including the hold-out validation set) and then evaluated on that same validation set. This resulted in an invalid, overly optimistic validation metric because the model had "seen" the validation labels during training.

To fix this, I will:
1.  **Split the OOF data**: Separate the OOF predictions and labels into a training portion (corresponding to the original training set) and a validation portion (corresponding to the original hold-out set).
2.  **Isolate Meta-Training**: Train the meta-learner strictly on the training portion of the OOF predictions.
3.  **Validate Correctly**: Evaluate the meta-learner on the isolated validation portion to calculate the final metric.
4.  **Retrain for Submission**: If the validation metric passes the threshold, retrain the meta-learner on the *full* OOF dataset (Train + Val) to maximize performance on the test set, as is standard practice in stacking.
5.  **Enable Full Training**: Remove the debug override `Config.EPOCHS = 5` to allow the base models to train for the full duration specified in the configuration.
