Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the extracted lessons, I propose a **Homogeneous Ensemble of Early-Stopped EfficientNet-B0s**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution from "Architecture Search" (which failed in Ideas 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8) to **Variance Reduction** of the proven optimal architecture. It explicitly corrects the implementation flaws that caused the failure of the previous K-Fold attempt (Idea 9) by reverting to standard supervised weights and replacing the detrimental Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) with rigorous Early Stopping.

### Model
We will construct a **Homogeneous Ensemble** consisting of 5 instances of the **EfficientNet-B0** architecture.
*   **Backbone:** We will utilize **EfficientNet-B0** initialized with **Standard ImageNet-1k weights**.
    *   *Evolution:* We explicitly reject the "Noisy Student" weights used in Idea 8 and Idea 9. Lesson 00044 demonstrated that despite their theoretical superiority, Noisy Student weights transfer poorly to the audio domain compared to standard supervised weights **Cite solution_lesson_node_00044**.
*   **Pooling:** We will retain **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. This is structurally non-negotiable for detecting transient whale calls in noise, as it prevents the signal from being washed out by global averaging **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer of each model will be modified to accept **1-channel input**, initialized by averaging the original RGB weights to preserve spatial filters **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

### Data
The data pipeline is strictly defined to preserve physical signal fidelity, combining the successful elements of Idea 4 with the necessary corrections for ensemble consistency.
*   **Spectrogram Generation:**
    *   **FFT Parameters:** `n_fft=1024` (High Frequency Resolution) and `hop_length=64` (High Temporal Resolution). This ensures the FFT bins exceed Mel bands (>4:1) to prevent aliasing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** and resolves short call contours **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Mel Filterbank:** `n_mels=128`, `normalized=False`. We explicitly disable area normalization to preserve the environmental "Pink" noise spectral tilt **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Native Resolution:** Inputs will be fed to the model at their **native resolution** (approx. $128 \times 63$). We will **NOT** resize to $224 \times 224$, as this introduces interpolation artifacts that degrade performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**.
    *   **Instance Standardization:** We will apply **Zero-Mean, Unit-Variance standardization** to each clip individually. This is critical for model convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will use **SpecAugment** (Time/Freq masking) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**. We will explicitly exclude Mixup, as it damages transient signal integrity **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

### Training
We implement **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** with a corrected convergence strategy.
*   **Convergence Strategy:** We will use **Early Stopping** (monitoring Validation AUC with a patience of 5-7 epochs) for each fold.
    *   *Evolution:* We explicitly reject **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** used in Idea 9. Lesson 00042 proved that SWA degrades performance if the averaging window includes epochs where the model has begun to overfit. Early Stopping ensures we capture the exact peak of generalization for each fold **Cite solution_lesson_node_00042**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00041**.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with a **Low Weight Decay of `1e-4`**. This ensures the pretrained weights are not over-constrained during adaptation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
*   **Sampling:** A **WeightedRandomSampler** will be used with a **Batch Size of 128** to stabilize Batch Normalization statistics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.

### Evaluation
*   **Ensembling Strategy:** We will generate predictions using **Soft Voting** (simple averaging) of the probability outputs from the 5 independent fold models.
    *   *Justification:* Lesson 00037 dictates that effective ensembling requires base learners of comparable strength. By using a homogeneous ensemble of the single best architecture (EfficientNet-B0), we maximize the strength of every voter while reducing variance, avoiding the "pollution" caused by weaker models in previous heterogeneous attempts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.
*   **Inference:** Test clips will be processed at native resolution using the exact pipeline defined above.
