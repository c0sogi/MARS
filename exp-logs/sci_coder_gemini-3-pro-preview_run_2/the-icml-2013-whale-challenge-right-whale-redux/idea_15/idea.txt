Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous experiments and the extracted lessons, I propose a **Calibrated Heterogeneous Stacked Ensemble with Dynamic Range Correction**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the Stacking approach (Idea 12, Idea 14). It explicitly optimizes the **"Stackability"** of the base learners by shifting the convergence criteria from Ranking (AUC) to Calibration (Loss), corrects the signal processing flaw (`top_db`) that degraded the previous ensemble, and refines the architecture selection to maximize quality over quantity.

# Model
We will construct a **Two-Stage Stacked Generalization Ensemble**.

**Level 0: "Best-in-Class" Heterogeneous Base Learners**
We will employ two structurally distinct CNN architectures. We explicitly **exclude** weaker models (like DenseNet-121 from Idea 14) to prevent the dilution of high-quality predictions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.

1.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Noisy Student):**
    *   **Initialization:** We will utilize weights pre-trained on JFT-300M (`ns_jft_in1k`). These weights have been empirically proven to outperform standard ImageNet weights in this specific domain when regularized correctly **Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**.
    *   **Role:** Captures features using Inverted Residual blocks and Squeeze-and-Excitation.
2.  **ResNet-34 (ImageNet):**
    *   **Initialization:** Standard ImageNet weights.
    *   **Role:** Provides deep residual learning biases. It serves as a robust, complementary architecture to EfficientNet, decorrelating errors for the ensemble **Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**.

**Common Structural Components:**
*   **Pooling:** Both models will replace Global Average Pooling with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** to maximize the detection of transient whale calls in noise **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer of both models will be adapted to **1-channel input**, initialized by averaging the original RGB weights to preserve spatial filters **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

**Level 1: Meta-Learner**
*   A **Logistic Regression** model will be trained on the concatenated Out-Of-Fold (OOF) probability predictions. This learns the optimal weighted combination of the base models **Cite solution_lesson_node_00053**.

# Data
The data pipeline is rigorously standardized to the "Golden Recipe" identified in Idea 4, with a critical correction for dynamic range.

1.  **Spectrogram Generation:**
    *   **FFT Parameters:** `n_fft=1024` and `hop_length=64`. This ensures high frequency resolution (>4:1 bin-to-mel ratio) to prevent aliasing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** and sufficient temporal resolution to resolve short call contours **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Mel Filterbank:** `n_mels=128`, `normalized=False`. We explicitly disable area normalization to preserve the environmental "Pink" noise spectral tilt **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
    *   **Dynamic Range (Correction):** We will apply **`top_db=80`** during the Amplitude-to-dB conversion. This clamps the noise floor, preventing the "long tail" of silence from skewing the subsequent normalization, a critical error in Idea 14 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00062**.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Native Resolution:** Inputs will be fed to the models at their generated dimensions (approx. $128 \times 63$). We will **NOT** resize to $224 \times 224$ **Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**.
    *   **Instance Standardization:** We will apply **Zero-Mean, Unit-Variance standardization** to each clip individually **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
3.  **Augmentation:**
    *   **SpecAugment** (Time/Freq masking) will be used **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**. Mixup is explicitly excluded **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

# Training
We implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** workflow optimized for probability calibration.

1.  **Sampling:** A **WeightedRandomSampler** will be used to balance the classes in every batch **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00056**.
2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a **Low Weight Decay of `1e-4`**. This prevents over-constraining the Noisy Student weights during adaptation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
    *   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing to ensure effective fine-tuning **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**.
3.  **Convergence Strategy (Structural Change):**
    *   We will use **Early Stopping monitoring Validation Loss** (not AUC).
    *   *Justification:* Maximizing AUC often degrades probability calibration in later epochs. Minimizing Loss ensures the base learners produce reliable probabilities, which is essential for the performance of the Level 1 meta-learner **Cite solution_lesson_node_00055**.

# Evaluation
We introduce a **Bagged-Stacking** evaluation protocol to correct the bias identified in Lesson 00061.

1.  **Base Prediction (Bagging):**
    *   For the Test set, we will **NOT** use single-fold predictions. Instead, we will generate predictions from all 5 folds of the EfficientNet model and average them to create the `EffNet_Test_Prob`. Similarly, we will average all 5 folds of the ResNet model to create `ResNet_Test_Prob`.
    *   This "Bagging" step reduces the variance of the inputs fed to the meta-learner **Cite solution_lesson_node_00061**.
2.  **Stacking:**
    *   The averaged `EffNet_Test_Prob` and `ResNet_Test_Prob` will be fed into the trained Level 1 Logistic Regression model to generate the final submission probability.
