The optimization strategy focuses on three key improvements based on the lessons learned:

1.  **Refine Ensemble Composition**: I will remove `densenet121` from the ensemble. The previous execution showed that `densenet121` did not significantly outperform `resnet34` but added computational overhead. By reverting to the proven "Golden Recipe" of `tf_efficientnet_b0` and `resnet34` (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00053}), we can allocate more computational resources to training the stronger models longer.

2.  **Enhance Regularization**: I will add `TimeMasking` to the data augmentation pipeline in `WhaleDataset`. While Frequency Masking is already implemented and effective (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00071}), adding Time Masking (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}) forces the model to learn robust temporal features and prevents overfitting to specific transient artifacts, acting as a crucial regularizer for the extended training schedule.

3.  **Optimize Training Schedule**: I will increase `Config.EPOCHS` from 5 to 15 in `runfile.py`. The previous run's aggressive reduction to 5 epochs likely truncated the Cosine Annealing scheduler before it could reach the low-learning-rate fine-tuning phase (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00070}). Extending the training duration allows the models to converge more deeply into the loss basin.
