The previous setting of 5 epochs severely compressed the Cosine Annealing learning rate schedule, preventing the optimizer from reaching the low-learning-rate fine-tuning phase necessary for convergence. Extending this to 12 epochs allows for a proper decay curve while remaining well within the 4-hour time limit.
