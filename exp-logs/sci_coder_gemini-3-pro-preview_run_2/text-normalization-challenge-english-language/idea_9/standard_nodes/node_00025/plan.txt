The issue is a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` caused by the accumulation of GPU memory across sequential training steps in a persistent environment, where resources from the first model (Router) are not released before the second model (Generator) is initialized. To fix this, I will reduce the `GENERATOR_BATCH_SIZE` to 16 in `library/config.py` and explicitly enforce this setting in `runfile.py` to bypass potential module caching (Cite debug_lesson_1). I will also insert `gc.collect()` and `torch.cuda.empty_cache()` calls between training stages in `runfile.py` to force the release of "zombie" tensors (Cite debug_lesson_2) and clear `sys.last_traceback` at the start to remove references to previous crash states (Cite debug_lesson_3).
