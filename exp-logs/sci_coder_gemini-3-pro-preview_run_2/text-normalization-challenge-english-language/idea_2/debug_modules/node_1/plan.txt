The execution failure was caused by a schema mismatch between the training subset (which, due to aggressive subsampling, lacked the 'TELEPHONE' class) and the validation set (which contained it). The `LabelEncoder` was fitted only on the observed training labels, causing it to crash when encountering the unobserved 'TELEPHONE' class during validation. To fix this, I will explicitly define the full taxonomy of 16 semiotic classes in `Config` and initialize the `LabelEncoder` with this fixed vocabulary. This ensures the encoder is aware of all possible classes regardless of the specific data sample used for training. Cite {schema_mismatch_sampling}.
