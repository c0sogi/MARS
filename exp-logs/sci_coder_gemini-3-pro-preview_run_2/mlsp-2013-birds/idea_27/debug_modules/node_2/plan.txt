The issue is that the validation AUC evaluates to `nan` due to the small dataset size in the demo run, causing the comparison `val_auc > self.best_score` to fail (since `nan > 0.0` is False). Consequently, `torch.save` is never called, leading to an `AssertionError` when `runfile.py` checks for the model file. To fix this, I will modify `library/engine.py` to initialize `self.best_score` to `-np.inf` and sanitize `val_auc` by replacing `nan` with a fallback value (0.5) before the comparison, ensuring a checkpoint is saved even when metrics are undefined.
