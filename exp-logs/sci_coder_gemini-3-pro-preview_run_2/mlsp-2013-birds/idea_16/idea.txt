Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of "Previous Ideas," I propose a **Tri-Architecture Multi-Resolution Ensemble with Time-Rolling Data & Test-Time Augmentation**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by formalizing **Spectrogram Time-Rolling** (circular shifting) as a core data augmentation technique and introducing **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** to the inference pipeline. These additions directly target the "Micro-Data" variance bottleneck (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**) by exploiting the temporal translation invariance of audio, effectively multiplying the dataset size and stabilizing predictions without the noise risks associated with Semi-Supervised Learning (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00061**).

### Model
**Architecture: Heterogeneous Tri-Backbone Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity, which has proven superior to weight averaging techniques like SWA (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones trained in parallel:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** Retained for its proven robustness and stability on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** Integrated to capture fine-grained features; its previous optimization failures are resolved via the weighted loss formulation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** Retained for feature reuse, acting as a structural regularizer when constrained to lower input resolutions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).

**Head Design: Multi-Sample Dropout**
All three backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head. Instead of a single dropout layer, the Global Average Pooling output will pass through 5 parallel dropout layers (with different random masks), each followed by a shared fully connected layer. The outputs are averaged during the forward pass. This "ensemble within a model" accelerates convergence and provides internal regularization (**Cite Idea 13**).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Multi-Resolution Strategy (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**):**
    *   **ResNet18 / EfficientNet-B0:** Resize to **224 (Freq) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls better than square resizing (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
    *   **DenseNet121:** Resize to **160 (Freq) x 320 (Time)**. This lower resolution forces the deeper model to learn macro-scale patterns, preventing it from overfitting to high-frequency noise (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights, avoiding the pitfalls of engineered Delta channels (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: Augmented Optimization Regime**
*   **Structural Innovation (Augmentation):** Implement **Spectrogram Time-Rolling (Circular Shift)**.
    *   *Mechanism:* Randomly roll the spectrogram along the time axis (x-axis) with wrap-around during training.
    *   *Rationale:* Bird calls are translation-invariant. This augmentation generates "new" valid samples from the limited 206 recordings, directly mitigating data scarcity without the ambiguity introduced by aggressive Mixup (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
*   **Standard Augmentations:** Retain **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**), **SpecAugment**, and **RandomBrightnessContrast** to handle gain variations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**. Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable for this imbalanced dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with Learning Rate **`1e-3`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**) and conservative Weight Decay **`1e-4`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**).
    *   **Schedule:** Use a **Constant Learning Rate**. This is required to escape local minima in the noisy loss landscape of micro-datasets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
    *   **Reproducibility:** Use `worker_init_fn` to enforce RNG diversity across data loader workers (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**).
*   **Validation:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).

### Evaluation
**Inference: Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) & Bagging**
*   **Structural Innovation (Inference):** Implement **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**.
    *   For each test recording, generate 4 variants: [Original, Time-Roll(25%), Time-Roll(50%), Time-Roll(75%)].
    *   Pass all 4 variants through the model and average the probabilities.
    *   *Rationale:* This further reduces variance by averaging out the model's sensitivity to the specific temporal position of the bird call, acting as a final stabilizer for the predictions.
*   **Aggregation:**
    1.  Apply TTA for each model instance.
    2.  Average the TTA outputs across all 15 trained models (5 Folds × 3 Architectures).
    3.  This "Grand Ensemble" combines the stability of bagging, the diversity of heterogeneous architectures, and the robustness of TTA to maximize AUC.
