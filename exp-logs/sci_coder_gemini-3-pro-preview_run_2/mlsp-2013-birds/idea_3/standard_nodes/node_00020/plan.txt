The issue identified is **Data Leakage** caused by inconsistent random states between the training phase and the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) validation phase. The `IterativeStratification` splitter relies on the global random state to determine fold assignments. Since the training process (which runs before OOF validation) heavily consumes random numbers, the global state changes, causing the splitter in the validation section to generate different folds than those used during training. This results in the model evaluating on data it has already seen.

To fix this, I will reset the random seed using `seed_everything(Config.SEED)` immediately before initializing the `IterativeStratification` splitter in the OOF validation section of `runfile.py`. This ensures the fold splits are identical to those generated during the training phase.
