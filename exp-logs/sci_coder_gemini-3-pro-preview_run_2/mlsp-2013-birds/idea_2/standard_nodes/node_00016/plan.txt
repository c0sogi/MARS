To improve the model's performance, I will implement a strategy focused on enhancing the visual representation of the audio data and upgrading the feature extractor.

1.  **Optimize Input Resolution**: The EDA revealed that the spectrograms have a mean width of 1246 and height of 256. Resizing to `(224, 224)` severely compresses the time axis, potentially blurring distinct short-duration bird calls. I will change the input size to `(256, 512)` (Height, Width). This rectangular aspect ratio better preserves the temporal structure of the audio signals while maintaining compatibility with standard CNNs.
2.  **Upgrade Architecture**: I will replace `resnet18` with `efficientnet_b0`. EfficientNet models generally provide better parameter efficiency and feature extraction capabilities, which is beneficial when treating audio classification as an image recognition task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
3.  **Refine Training Schedule**: To ensure the new architecture converges properly with the larger input features, I will increase `EPOCHS` to 50 and `PATIENCE` to 15.
