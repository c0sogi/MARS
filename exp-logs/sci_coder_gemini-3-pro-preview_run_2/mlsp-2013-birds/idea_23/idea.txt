Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** to the optimization pipeline by replacing the standard minimization objective with **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)**. This directly addresses the critical instability observed in the DenseNet121 backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073) and the high variance inherent in micro-data training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018). By simultaneously minimizing the loss value and the loss *sharpness* (geometry), SAM forces the model to find flat minima, which generalize significantly better on small datasets than the sharp minima found by standard AdamW.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity, which has proven superior to weight averaging techniques (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones trained in parallel:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** Retained for its proven robustness and stability on this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** Retained to capture fine-grained features; optimization issues are resolved via the weighted loss formulation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** Retained for its feature reuse capabilities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). The optimization instability previously observed with this backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073) will be explicitly corrected by the SAM optimizer.

**Head Design:**
All three backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head. The Global Average Pooling output will pass through 5 parallel dropout layers (with different random masks), each followed by a shared fully connected layer. The outputs are averaged during the forward pass. This "ensemble within a model" accelerates convergence and provides internal regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013). We strictly avoid learnable pooling layers like GeM (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039) or noise-sensitive Dual Pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Rectangular Resolution:** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls better than square resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) while avoiding the overfitting risks of excessive resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights, avoiding the pitfalls of engineered Delta channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).

**Augmentation Strategy**
*   **Cyclic Time-Rolling:** Apply random circular shifts along the time axis during training. This exploits the temporal invariance of audio to multiply the effective dataset size without destructive artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).
*   **Mild Mixup:** Apply Mixup with **$\alpha=0.4$**. We strictly adhere to global mixing rather than "Selective Signal-Noise" mixing to ensure the model learns inter-class boundaries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00070), while keeping the strength moderate to avoid ambiguity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Standard Augs:** Retain **SpecAugment** and **RandomBrightnessContrast** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

### Training
**Procedure: SAM-Stabilized Optimization**
*   **Structural Innovation (Optimizer):** Implement **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)** wrapping the AdamW optimizer.
    *   *Mechanism:* For each training step, SAM performs a dual forward-backward pass to estimate the "sharpness" of the loss landscape and updates weights to move into a flatter region.
    *   *Rationale:* This explicitly counteracts the "noisy loss landscape" of micro-datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036) and stabilizes high-capacity models like DenseNet that previously diverged (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**. Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable; we reject Asymmetric Loss (ASL) as it degraded performance in this regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071).
*   **Hyperparameters:**
    *   **Base Optimizer:** AdamW with Learning Rate **`1e-3`** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040) and conservative Weight Decay **`1e-4`** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Schedule:** Use a **Constant Learning Rate**. This is required to escape local minima in the noisy loss landscape (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Duration:** Target **1000 Total Update Steps** per fold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
*   **Validation:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** with `worker_init_fn` for reproducibility (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018, solution_lesson_node_00045).

### Evaluation
**Inference: TTA & Grand Bagging**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants: [Original, Time-Roll(25%), Time-Roll(50%), Time-Roll(75%)].
    *   *Rationale:* Averaging predictions across these cyclic shifts stabilizes the output against framing bias and exploits the model's learned temporal invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00073).
*   **Aggregation:** Apply TTA to all 15 trained models (5 Folds × 3 Architectures) and average their outputs. This "Grand Ensemble" combines the stability of bagging, the diversity of heterogeneous architectures, and the geometric regularization of SAM to maximize AUC.
