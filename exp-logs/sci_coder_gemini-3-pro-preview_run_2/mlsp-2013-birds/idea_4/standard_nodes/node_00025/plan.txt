Based on the provided lessons, specifically **Lesson 00024**, the introduction of Dual Pooling (Max + Average) caused a significant performance regression compared to the simpler Global Average Pooling used in the previous best solution. Max pooling tends to amplify noise in spectrograms, which is detrimental for this dataset. Additionally, the previous best solution utilized Dropout for regularization, which is missing in the current model implementation.

Therefore, the optimization strategy is to:
1.  **Revert to Global Average Pooling** by setting `use_dual_pooling = False` in `library/config.py` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
2.  **Add Dropout** to the classification head in `library/model.py` to match the regularization strategy of the best-performing model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
3.  Update the working directory to ensure a fresh training run.
