Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Anchor-Based Self-Distillation**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** in the training pipeline: **Anchor-Based Distillation**. We identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00073** that while the Heterogeneous Ensemble is powerful, the **DenseNet121** component is unstable (failing to converge effectively) despite its theoretical advantages for small data (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**). Instead of discarding it (which reduces diversity, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00074**), we will stabilize its training by distilling knowledge from the "Stable Anchors" (ResNet18 and EfficientNet-B0). This uses the ensemble's own high-quality Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions to smooth the decision landscape, addressing the "Micro-Data" variance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**) without the noise risks of Semi-Supervised Learning (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00061**).

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**):
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** The "Stable Anchor," proven robust on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** The "Stable Anchor," optimized via weighted loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** The "Student," retained for feature reuse (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**). Its instability is addressed via the new training pipeline.

**Head Design:**
All backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head (**Cite Idea 13**). The Global Average Pooling output passes through 5 parallel dropout layers, averaged to accelerate convergence and provide internal regularization.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Rectangular Resolution:** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves temporal fidelity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**) and avoids the artifacts associated with lower resolutions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00070**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: Two-Stage Anchor-Based Distillation**

**Stage 1: Train Stable Anchors**
*   Train **ResNet18** and **EfficientNet-B0** using **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)**.
*   **Loss:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with **`pos_weight`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Augmentation:** **Cyclic Time-Rolling** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**), **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**), and **SpecAugment**.
*   **Optimization:** **AdamW** (LR `1e-3`, Decay `1e-4`) with **Constant Schedule** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
*   **Output:** Generate **Out-Of-Fold (OOF) Soft Probabilities** for the entire training set.

**Stage 2: Distilled Training (Stabilizing DenseNet)**
*   Train **DenseNet121** (and re-train the Anchors as "Born-Again" models) on the same folds.
*   **Composite Loss:**
    $$ L = \text{BCE}_{\text{weighted}}(\text{Target}, \text{Label}) + \lambda \cdot \text{KLDiv}(\text{Target}, \text{Anchor\_OOF}) $$
    *   *Rationale:* The Anchors provide stable, smoothed probability distributions (Soft Targets) that guide the DenseNet away from the overfitting/divergence observed in Lesson 73. This "Soft Supervision" prevents the model from chasing outliers in the micro-dataset.
*   **Augmentation & Optimization:** Identical to Stage 1.

### Evaluation
**Inference: TTA & Grand Bagging**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants (Original, Roll-25%, Roll-50%, Roll-75%) and average their predictions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
*   **Aggregation:** Apply TTA to all 15 trained models from Stage 2 (5 ResNet + 5 EfficientNet + 5 DenseNet) and average their outputs.
*   This combines the stability of the Anchors, the feature diversity of the stabilized DenseNet, and the temporal robustness of TTA to maximize AUC.
