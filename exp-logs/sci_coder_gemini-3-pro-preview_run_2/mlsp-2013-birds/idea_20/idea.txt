Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Siamese Temporal Consistency Regularization**.

This strategy introduces a **Structural Innovation** to the training loop: a **Siamese Consistency Loss**. While "Idea 16" and "Lesson 73" successfully identified *Cyclic Time-Shifting* as a powerful augmentation and TTA technique, they treated it purely as a data generator. This new solution structurally enforces **Temporal Invariance** directly in the optimization objective. By penalizing the divergence between the model's predictions on an image and its time-shifted counterpart, we explicitly regularize the model against the "framing noise" inherent in the micro-dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**), stabilizing convergence where previous deep architectures (like DenseNet) failed.

### Model
**Architecture: Stabilized Heterogeneous Tri-Ensemble**
We refine the ensemble composition to maximize diversity while eliminating the instability observed in previous iterations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** Retained for its proven robustness and stability on this dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** Retained to capture fine-grained features; optimization issues are resolved via the weighted loss formulation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
3.  **ResNet34 (Depth Bias):** We replace the unstable DenseNet121 (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**) with **ResNet34**. While architecturally related to ResNet18, its increased depth allows it to model more complex spectral hierarchies without the optimization volatility of DenseNet or the artifact sensitivity observed when combining DenseNet with low-resolution inputs.

**Head Design:**
All three backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head (**Cite Idea 13**). The Global Average Pooling output will pass through 5 parallel dropout layers (with different random masks), each followed by a shared fully connected layer. The outputs are averaged during the forward pass to accelerate convergence.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Unified Rectangular Resolution:** Resize **all** inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**.
    *   *Rationale:* We standardize the resolution to the successful 1:2 aspect ratio (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**). We discard the "Multi-Resolution" strategy (160x320) used for DenseNet, as it likely contributed to the instability observed in Lesson 73 by creating aliasing artifacts during Time-Rolling.
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: Siamese Temporal Consistency Loop**
*   **Structural Innovation:** Implement a **Siamese Training Step**. For each batch of images $x$:
    1.  Generate $x_{aug}$: Apply standard augmentations (Mixup, SpecAugment).
    2.  Generate $x_{roll}$: Apply a random **Cyclic Time-Shift** (Roll) to $x_{aug}$.
    3.  **Forward Pass:** Compute logits for both: $\hat{y} = Model(x_{aug})$ and $\hat{y}_{roll} = Model(x_{roll})$.
    4.  **Loss Calculation:** Minimize a composite objective:
        $$ L = L_{BCE}(\hat{y}, y) + L_{BCE}(\hat{y}_{roll}, y) + \lambda \cdot L_{MSE}(\sigma(\hat{y}), \sigma(\hat{y}_{roll})) $$
        *   *Rationale:* The first two terms ensure both views predict the correct labels. The third term (Consistency Loss, where $\sigma$ is sigmoid) explicitly forces the model's internal representation to be invariant to temporal start positions. This acts as a powerful regularizer for the small dataset, effectively "baking in" the benefits of TTA into the weights.
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** (LR `1e-3`, Decay `1e-4`) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**, **solution_lesson_node_00033**).
    *   **Schedule:** **Constant Learning Rate** to ensure escape from local minima (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
*   **Augmentation:** **Mild Mixup ($\alpha=0.4$)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**), **SpecAugment**, and **RandomBrightnessContrast**.
*   **Validation:** **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**).

### Evaluation
**Inference: Consistency-Enhanced Bagging**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants (Original, Roll-25%, Roll-50%, Roll-75%) and average their predictions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
*   **Aggregation:** Average the TTA outputs across all 15 trained models (5 Folds × 3 Architectures).
*   This strategy combines the structural stability of the new ResNet34 component, the regularization of the Siamese Consistency Loss, and the inference-time robustness of TTA to maximize AUC.
