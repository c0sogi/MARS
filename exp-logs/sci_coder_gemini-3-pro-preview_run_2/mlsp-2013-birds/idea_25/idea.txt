Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the trajectory of previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM) and Cyclic Test-Time Augmentation**.

This strategy represents the synthesis of the most successful architectural choices (Tri-Architecture Ensemble) and data strategies (Cyclic Time-Shifting) while introducing a **Structural Optimization Innovation** (SAM) to resolve the persistent instability of high-capacity backbones in micro-data regimes. It explicitly corrects the failure mode of **Cite solution_lesson_node_00080**, where advanced optimization (SAM + Distillation) failed solely due to an unweighted loss formulation.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
We retain the **Tri-Architecture** composition to maximize Inductive Bias Diversity, which is critical for generalization on small datasets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**). The ensemble consists of three distinct backbones trained in parallel:
1.  **ResNet18 (Residual Bias):** Retained for its proven robustness and stability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**).
2.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Inverted Residual Bias):** Retained to capture fine-grained features; optimization issues are resolved via the weighted loss formulation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).
3.  **DenseNet121 (Dense Bias):** Retained for its feature reuse capabilities (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**). The optimization instability previously observed with this backbone (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**) will be explicitly corrected by the SAM optimizer.

**Head Design:**
All backbones will terminate in a **Multi-Sample Dropout** head. The Global Average Pooling output will pass through 5 parallel dropout layers (with different random masks), each followed by a shared fully connected layer. The outputs are averaged during the forward pass to accelerate convergence and provide internal regularization.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Input Topology**
*   **Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signal representations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
*   **Rectangular Resolution:** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**. This 1:2 aspect ratio preserves the temporal fidelity of rapid bird calls better than square resizing (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**) while avoiding the overfitting risks of excessive resolution (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**).
*   **Adaptation:** Use **Pseudo-RGB** (channel replication) to align with ImageNet weights, avoiding the pitfalls of engineered Delta channels (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).

### Training
**Procedure: SAM-Stabilized Supervised Optimization**
*   **Structural Innovation (Optimizer):** Implement **Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM)** wrapping the AdamW optimizer.
    *   *Mechanism:* For each training step, SAM performs a dual forward-backward pass to estimate the "sharpness" of the loss landscape and updates weights to move into a flatter region.
    *   *Rationale:* This explicitly counteracts the "noisy loss landscape" of micro-datasets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**) and stabilizes high-capacity models like DenseNet that previously diverged (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
    *   *Correction:* Unlike the failure in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00080**, we apply SAM **exclusively to the Supervised Weighted Loss**, strictly avoiding unweighted distillation terms that caused performance collapse.
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**. Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable for this imbalanced dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**, **solution_lesson_node_00071**).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Cyclic Time-Rolling:** Apply random circular shifts along the time axis during training. This exploits temporal invariance to multiply the effective dataset size without destructive artifacts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
    *   **Mild Mixup:** Apply Mixup with **$\alpha=0.4$**. We strictly adhere to global mixing to ensure the model learns inter-class boundaries (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00070**), keeping strength moderate to avoid ambiguity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
    *   **SpecAugment** & **RandomBrightnessContrast**.
*   **Hyperparameters:**
    *   **Base Optimizer:** AdamW with Learning Rate **`1e-3`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**) and conservative Weight Decay **`1e-4`** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**).
    *   **Schedule:** **Constant Learning Rate** to ensure escape from local minima (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**).
    *   **Duration:** Target **1000 Total Update Steps** per fold (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**).
    *   **Reproducibility:** Use `worker_init_fn` to enforce RNG diversity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00045**).

### Evaluation
**Inference: Cyclic TTA & Grand Bagging**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each test recording, generate 4 variants: [Original, Time-Roll(25%), Time-Roll(50%), Time-Roll(75%)].
    *   *Rationale:* Averaging predictions across these cyclic shifts stabilizes the output against framing bias and exploits the model's learned temporal invariance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
*   **Aggregation:** Apply TTA to all 15 trained models (5 Folds × 3 Architectures) and average their outputs. This "Grand Ensemble" combines the stability of bagging, the diversity of heterogeneous architectures, and the geometric regularization of SAM to maximize AUC.
