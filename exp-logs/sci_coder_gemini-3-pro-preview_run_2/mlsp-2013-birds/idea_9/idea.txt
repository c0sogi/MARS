Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a **Tri-Architecture Heterogeneous Ensemble with Rectangular Resolution**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the "Heterogeneous Ensemble" (Idea 8). It expands the ensemble's diversity by re-integrating the **EfficientNet** architecture—which previously failed due to optimization errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020)—and aligning it with the successful **Rectangular Input** topology (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034). This creates a "Maximally Diverse" CNN ensemble (Residual, Dense, and Inverted Residual biases) stabilized by the specific training protocols proven to work for this micro-dataset.

### Model
**Architecture: Tri-Backbone Heterogeneous Ensemble**
The solution will utilize three distinct CNN architectures trained in parallel to maximize feature diversity:
1.  **ResNet18 (Exploitation):** Retained for its proven robustness and stability on this dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
2.  **DenseNet121 (Exploitation):** Retained for its "dense connectivity," which maximizes feature reuse in low-data regimes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
3.  **EfficientNet-B0 (Structural Innovation):** We introduce **EfficientNet-B0** as the third pillar.
    *   *Rationale:* EfficientNet utilizes **MBConv (Inverted Residuals)** blocks, providing a fundamentally different inductive bias compared to the standard Residuals of ResNet and Concatenations of DenseNet. While this architecture failed in Idea 3, the failure was attributed to the lack of class weighting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020). By integrating it with the correct loss and input shape, we leverage its parameter efficiency to capture features the other models might miss.

**Head Design:**
All three backbones will terminate in a **Global Average Pooling** layer (strictly avoiding Dual Pooling, Cite solution_lesson_node_00024) followed by a single fully connected layer with 19 outputs and **Sigmoid** activation. This maintains the **Binary Relevance** strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Input Source:** Use **Filtered Spectrograms** to ensure training on denoised signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Rectangular Resolution (Exploitation):** Resize all inputs to **224 (Frequency) x 448 (Time)**.
    *   *Rationale:* We strictly adhere to the 1:2 aspect ratio. This preserves the temporal fidelity of short bird calls better than square resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) but avoids the overfitting caused by excessive resolution (e.g., 224x896) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
*   **Pseudo-RGB:** Replicate the single-channel grayscale spectrogram 3 times. This adapts the data to the pretrained weights better than engineering Delta channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Mixup:** Apply **Mixup with $\alpha=0.4$**. This provides the necessary regularization for small data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) while avoiding the destructive ambiguity of aggressive Mixup ($\alpha=1.0$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Photometric:** Apply **RandomBrightnessContrast** to handle gain variations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **Geometric:** Apply **SpecAugment** (Time/Frequency Masking).

### Training
**Procedure: Corrected Optimization Regime**
*   **Splitting:** Implement **Iterative Stratified K-Fold (K=5)** to mitigate stochastic variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) with Logits Loss** combined with **`pos_weight`**.
    *   *Rationale:* Explicitly weighting positive classes is non-negotiable. This is the critical correction that enables EfficientNet to function effectively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Optimization Strategy:**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with conservative weight decay (`1e-4`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Scheduler:** Use a **Constant Learning Rate** (or very slow decay).
        *   *Rationale:* Given the micro-dataset size, aggressive schedules like Cosine Annealing decay the learning rate too quickly, preventing the model from navigating the noisy loss landscape. A sustained learning rate is required to escape local minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Duration:** Define training by **Total Update Steps** (target ~1000 steps), ignoring standard epoch counts to ensure convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).

### Evaluation
**Inference: Grand Ensemble**
*   **Process:** For each test recording, resize to 224x448 and generate predictions using all trained models (5 ResNet + 5 DenseNet + 5 EfficientNet = 15 models).
*   **Aggregation:** Average the probability outputs of all 15 models.
    *   *Rationale:* This "Grand Ensemble" combines the stability of bagging (averaging across folds) with the diversity of heterogeneous architectures (averaging across biases), providing the ultimate defense against the noise and variance inherent in training on ~200 samples (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
