To optimize the solution, I will implement a robust **5-Fold Cross-Validation** strategy combined with a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** of ResNet18, DenseNet121, and EfficientNet-B0. I will replace the Asymmetric Loss with **Weighted Binary Cross Entropy (BCEWithLogitsLoss)**, as previous lessons (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071) indicated that ASL degrades performance in this micro-data regime compared to explicitly weighted BCE. I will also calculate `pos_weight` dynamically for each fold to handle class imbalance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).

The training pipeline will use **Mixup** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013) and **Time-Rolling** augmentation. I will maintain the **batch size of 16** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042) and **learning rate of 1e-3** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040) to ensure effective convergence on the small dataset. The validation strategy will shift from a single hold-out split to an Out-Of-Fold (OOF) evaluation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) to maximize data utilization and provide a reliable metric.
