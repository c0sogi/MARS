Lesson 52 indicates that in small-data scenarios, the regularization benefit from architectural diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052) outweighs the gains from weight averaging alone. I will maintain the Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) and the `worker_init_fn` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045) to ensure robust optimization and data diversity. EfficientNet-B0 will be configured with an input resolution of `(224, 448)` to preserve temporal fidelity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
