# Model: Parallel-Context Gated-Injection BiLSTM (PC-GI-BiLSTM)

I propose a structural evolution that refines the "Context Generation" mechanism. While previous successful models used a single global GLU to extract context, I propose a **Parallel-Context** approach that explicitly separates the processing of static physics and dynamic controls before fusion. This addresses the risk of high-variance control signals drowning out static lung attributes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028) while avoiding the overfitting associated with deep serial front-ends (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).

*   **Parallel-Context Extractor (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Instead of feeding all features into a single Gated Linear Unit, we will split the input features into three logical groups and process them in parallel branches:
        1.  **Static Branch:** Inputs `R`, `C`. Processed by a specialized GLU to extract latent lung characteristics.
        2.  **Dynamic Branch:** Inputs `u_in`, `dt`, derivatives, and lags. Processed by a specialized GLU to extract instantaneous control dynamics.
        3.  **Global Branch:** All inputs. Processed by a GLU to capture cross-interactions.
    *   **Fusion & Mild Bottleneck:** The outputs of these three branches will be concatenated and then projected down to a moderate dimension (e.g., 128 units). This "Mild Bottleneck" retains the efficiency benefits of compression (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045) without the signal degradation caused by aggressive narrowing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00056).
*   **Dual-Path Injection Payload:**
    *   We retain the **Dual-Path** strategy. The "Parallel Context" generated above is concatenated with the **Identity Path** (raw uncompressed features).
    *   **Rationale:** This ensures the deep layers have access to both the highly refined, domain-separated context and the exact numerical magnitudes required for physics calculations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039, solution_lesson_node_00018).
    *   **Deterministic Constraint:** We strictly **exclude Dropout** from this injection payload to ensure it serves as a stable ground truth (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Deep Recurrent Backbone:**
    *   The core consists of **4 layers of Bidirectional LSTMs**.
    *   **Deep Injection:** The "Injection Payload" is concatenated to the input of **every** LSTM layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Inter-Layer Connectivity:** We use **Layer Normalization** and **Dropout** *between* LSTM layers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037) but reject complex inter-layer gating to maintain high gradient flow (Cite solution_lesson_node_00048).
    *   **Head:** A final Dense layer projects to pressure. We reject Output Skip-Connections (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

# Data: Robust Physics-Fidelity Engineering

We will implement the refined feature engineering pipeline that strictly adheres to the calculus of the physical system and robust statistical principles.

*   **Robust Scaling:**
    *   We will apply **RobustScaler** (Median/IQR) to all continuous inputs. This handles the ~14% outliers in `u_in` significantly better than Standard Scaling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
*   **Time-Weighted Integration:**
    *   We will calculate the time delta `dt` and generate the volume feature as `u_in_cumsum = cumsum(u_in * dt)`. This corrects the integration error of early baselines (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
*   **Physics Interaction Terms:**
    *   We will calculate `R * u_in` (Resistive Pressure) and `u_in_cumsum / C` (Elastic Pressure) using the time-weighted volume (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Explicit Dynamics (Refined):**
    *   We will augment the input with **Past Lag features** and **Finite Differences** (1st and 2nd derivatives) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    *   Crucially, we will **exclude Future Lags**. Lesson 00055 demonstrated that explicit future lags in a BiLSTM cause overfitting and degrade generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
*   **Signal Fidelity:**
    *   Lung Resistance (`R`) and Compliance (`C`) will be treated strictly as **continuous numerical features** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

# Training: Stretched-Horizon Convergence Protocol

*   **Loss Function:** We will use a **Weighted L1 Loss**, assigning a weight of **1.0** to the inspiratory phase and **0.1** to the expiratory phase (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Stretched Horizon:** We will set the training duration to **200 epochs** and explicitly set the scheduler's `T_max=200`.
    *   Deep recurrent models on this task exhibit "long-tail convergence" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   Stretching the scheduler horizon keeps the learning rate higher for longer, preventing premature convergence to shallow local minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
