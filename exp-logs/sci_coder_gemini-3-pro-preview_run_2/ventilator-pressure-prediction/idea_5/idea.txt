# Model: Dual-Stream Physics-Informed Network (DSPIN)

I propose a **Dual-Stream Architecture** that structurally separates the modeling of the two fundamental physical components of the respiratory system: the **Elastic Pressure** (volume-dependent/temporal) and the **Resistive Pressure** (flow-dependent/instantaneous).

*   **Concept:** The Equation of Motion states $P_{total} = P_{elastic}(Volume) + P_{resistive}(Flow)$. Standard RNNs attempt to learn both simultaneously. By splitting the network into two parallel streams that are summed at the output, we provide a strong structural inductive bias that aligns with the additive nature of the physics.
*   **Stream 1: The Recurrent "Elastic" Path:**
    *   **Backbone:** A **Deep Residual Bidirectional LSTM** (4 layers). This path is responsible for modeling the time-dependent accumulation of air (Volume) and the complex hysteresis of the lung's compliance.
    *   **Enhancements:** We will implement **Input Injection** (concatenating input features to every hidden layer) (Cite Lesson 00021) and **Layer Normalization** after every recurrent block (Cite Lesson 00023). This ensures signal fidelity is maintained across depth.
*   **Stream 2: The Instantaneous "Resistive" Path:**
    *   **Backbone:** A **Deep Residual MLP** (Feed-Forward Network, 4 layers) operating on each time step independently. This path focuses on mapping the instantaneous control signals (Flow) and lung attributes (Resistance) to the resistive pressure drop, bypassing the difficulty RNNs have with high-frequency, non-temporal mappings.
    *   **Enhancements:** Like the recurrent path, this will utilize Input Injection and Layer Normalization.
*   **Fusion:** The outputs of the two streams are summed element-wise to produce the final pressure prediction.
*   **Design Choice:** We explicitly avoid CNN stems (Cite Lesson 00017) and Transformer blocks (Cite Lesson 00012) to maximize training throughput and preserve the numerical fidelity of the signal (Cite Lesson 00018).

# Data: Physics-Based Signal Preservation

We will strictly adhere to the feature engineering protocols that preserve the continuous nature of the physical signals.

*   **Physics-Based Integration:** We will explicitly generate the `u_in_cumsum` feature to represent cumulative air volume, relieving the Recurrent Path of the burden of linear integration (Cite Lesson 00003).
*   **Equation of Motion Interactions:** We will calculate and include the interaction terms `R * u_in` and `u_in_cumsum / C` (Cite Lesson 00004).
*   **Explicit Dynamics:** We will augment the input with **Lag features** and **Finite Difference features** (1st and 2nd derivatives of `u_in`) to provide the Instantaneous Path with explicit velocity and acceleration data (Cite Lesson 00008).
*   **Continuous Attributes:** We will treat Lung Resistance (`R`) and Compliance (`C`) strictly as **continuous numerical features**. We will **not** use categorical embeddings, ensuring the model preserves the ordinal/ratio magnitude information required for the physics calculations (Cite Lesson 00018).
*   **Normalization:** All inputs will be scaled using RobustScaler.

# Training: Stabilized Deep Convergence

*   **Loss Function:** We will use a **Weighted L1 Loss**, assigning a weight of **1.0** to the inspiratory phase (the target metric) and **0.1** to the expiratory phase (Cite Lesson 00021). This focuses the model's capacity on the scored region while maintaining the temporal context required for stable hidden states.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Regularization:** We will apply **Dropout** within the residual blocks (Cite Lesson 00023) to prevent overfitting given the increased capacity of the Dual-Stream model.
*   **Duration:** We will set the training duration to **50+ epochs**. The high throughput of the LSTM/MLP architecture (avoiding CNN/Attention overhead) allows us to reach full convergence within the compute budget (Cite Lesson 00005).

# Evaluation

*   **Inference:** The model will process the test set in batches. The Dual-Stream architecture allows for efficient inference as the MLP stream is highly parallelizable and the LSTM stream is standard.
*   **Prediction:** The output sequences will be flattened and mapped to the `id` column.
*   **Validation:** We will monitor the MAE on the validation set, specifically analyzing the error distribution across high-resistance breaths to verify that the "Resistive Path" (MLP) is effectively capturing the instantaneous pressure drops.
