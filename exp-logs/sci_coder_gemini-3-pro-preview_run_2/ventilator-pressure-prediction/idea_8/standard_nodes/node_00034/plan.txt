Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Extend Training Horizon**: Increase `EPOCHS` to 180 in `Config` to leverage the "long-tail convergence" property of deep RNNs modeling physical dynamics. This addresses the underfitting observed in the baseline run (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
2.  **Enhance Input Injection**: Increase `GLU_DIM` to 512 in `Config`. This ensures the projected input signal constitutes a significant fraction (~33%) of the deep layer inputs (512 vs 1024+512), preventing the physical control signals from being "drowned out" by the recurrent state in deep layers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
3.  **Maximize Throughput**: Increase `BATCH_SIZE` to 512 in `Config` to utilize the A100 GPU efficiently, allowing for faster epoch completion.
4.  **Full Data Training**: Modify `runfile.py` to remove the 50% data subsetting and configuration overrides. Training on the full dataset is essential for achieving the high precision required by the metric, as deep sequence models require extensive data to generalize (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
