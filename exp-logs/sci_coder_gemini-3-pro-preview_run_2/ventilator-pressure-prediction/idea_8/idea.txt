# Model: Gated-Injection Deep Bidirectional LSTM (GI-DBiLSTM)

I propose a structural evolution of the "Deep Projected-Injection" architecture (Idea 7) that incorporates a **Gated Linear Unit (GLU)** mechanism to refine the signal injection process.

*   **Gated Latent Projection (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Instead of a simple Linear layer for projecting inputs, we will employ a **Gated Linear Unit (GLU)** block to map the raw features into the high-dimensional latent space (e.g., 512 dimensions).
    *   **Rationale:** The relationship between static lung attributes (Resistance, Compliance) and dynamic controls (Flow) is highly conditional (e.g., Resistance is most relevant during high-flow events). A simple linear projection mixes these signals uniformly. A GLU allows the network to learn a "Physics Gate," dynamically suppressing or amplifying specific physical signals (like Resistance or Volume) before they are injected into the deep network. This provides the specialization benefits of the "Dual-Stream" architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024) without the limitations of hard-coded branching (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Deep Recurrent Backbone with Deterministic Injection:**
    *   The core will consist of **4 to 6 layers of Bidirectional LSTMs**.
    *   **Input Injection:** The output of the GLU (the "Gated Context Signal") will be concatenated to the input of **every** LSTM layer in the stack (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021). This ensures deep layers retain access to high-fidelity physical signals.
    *   **Deterministic Constraint:** We will strictly **exclude Dropout** from the GLU projection block. The injected signal must remain a deterministic "ground truth" reference for the deep layers to rely on (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Stabilization:**
    *   **Layer Normalization** will be applied after every recurrent block to stabilize hidden state dynamics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   We explicitly reject CNN stems (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017) and Transformer blocks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) to maximize training throughput.

# Data: Time-Weighted Physics Engineering

We will upgrade the feature engineering pipeline to strictly adhere to the calculus of the physical system, correcting the integration approximation used in previous ideas.

*   **Time-Weighted Integration:**
    *   We will calculate the time delta `dt` between steps and generate the volume feature as the cumulative sum of the product of flow and time: `u_in_cumsum = cumsum(u_in * dt)`.
    *   **Rationale:** Previous ideas used a simple cumulative sum, which assumes uniform sampling. Explicitly weighting by time provides a mathematically accurate integral of flow (Volume), significantly reducing error (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
*   **Physics Interaction Terms:**
    *   We will recalculate the interaction terms using this improved volume feature: `R * u_in` (Resistive Pressure) and `u_in_cumsum / C` (Elastic Pressure) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Explicit Dynamics:**
    *   We will augment the input with **Lag features** and **Finite Differences** (1st and 2nd derivatives of `u_in`) to provide explicit velocity and acceleration data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Signal Fidelity:**
    *   Lung Resistance (`R`) and Compliance (`C`) will be treated strictly as **continuous numerical features**. We reject embeddings to preserve ordinal magnitude information (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

# Training: Long-Tail Convergence Protocol

*   **Loss Function:** We will use a **Weighted L1 Loss**, assigning a weight of **1.0** to the inspiratory phase and **0.1** to the expiratory phase. This focuses capacity on the metric while maintaining the temporal context required for stable hidden states (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Extended Duration:** We will set the training duration to **150+ epochs**.
    *   Deep recurrent models on this task exhibit "long-tail convergence," where significant error reduction occurs well after epoch 50. By utilizing the high-throughput LSTM architecture (avoiding CNN/Attention overhead), we can fit this extended schedule within the compute budget (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
