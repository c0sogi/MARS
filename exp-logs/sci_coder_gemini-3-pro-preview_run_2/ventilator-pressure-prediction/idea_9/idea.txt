# Model: Skip-Context Gated-Injection BiLSTM (SC-GI-BiLSTM)

I propose a structural evolution of the "Gated-Injection" architecture that explicitly bridges the gap between the "Unified" and "Dual-Stream" approaches via a **Skip-Context Readout** mechanism.

*   **Gated Latent Projection (Context Generation):**
    *   The raw input sequence will be projected into a high-dimensional latent space (e.g., 512 units) using a **Gated Linear Unit (GLU)**.
    *   This creates a "Physics Context" signal where specific physical attributes (like Resistance during high flow) are dynamically amplified or suppressed (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   We will strictly **exclude Dropout** from this block to ensure the context signal remains a deterministic ground truth (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030).
*   **Deep Recurrent Backbone with Input Injection:**
    *   The core will consist of **4 layers of Bidirectional LSTMs**.
    *   **Input Injection:** The "Physics Context" generated above will be concatenated to the input of **every** LSTM layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021). This ensures deep layers retain access to high-fidelity signals without dilution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Layer Normalization** will be applied after every recurrent block (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
*   **Skip-Context Readout (Structural Innovation):**
    *   In standard RNNs, the final prediction is made solely from the last hidden state. I propose concatenating the **"Physics Context"** (the output of the GLU) directly with the **Final LSTM Output** before feeding it into the Regression Head.
    *   **Rationale:** The Equation of Motion has two distinct components: $P_{resistive}$ (Instantaneous, Flow-dependent) and $P_{elastic}$ (Temporal, Volume-dependent). The LSTM excels at modeling the Temporal/Integral component. By skipping the LSTM and feeding the "Context" directly to the readout, we provide the Regression Head with a direct, unfiltered path to model the Instantaneous/Resistive component. This achieves the specialization benefits of the "Dual-Stream" architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024) while allowing the final Dense layer to learn the non-linear cross-terms that simple addition fails to capture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

# Data: Time-Weighted Physics Engineering

We will implement the refined feature engineering pipeline that strictly adheres to the calculus of the physical system.

*   **Time-Weighted Integration:**
    *   We will calculate the time delta `dt` and generate the volume feature as the cumulative sum of the product of flow and time: `u_in_cumsum = cumsum(u_in * dt)`. This corrects the integration error of previous baselines (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
*   **Physics Interaction Terms:**
    *   We will calculate the interaction terms using this improved volume: `R * u_in` (Resistive Pressure) and `u_in_cumsum / C` (Elastic Pressure) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Explicit Dynamics:**
    *   We will augment the input with **Lag features** and **Finite Differences** (1st and 2nd derivatives of `u_in`) to provide explicit velocity and acceleration data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Signal Fidelity:**
    *   Lung Resistance (`R`) and Compliance (`C`) will be treated strictly as **continuous numerical features**. We reject embeddings to preserve ordinal magnitude information (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
*   **Normalization:** All inputs will be scaled using RobustScaler.

# Training: Long-Tail Convergence Protocol

*   **Loss Function:** We will use a **Weighted L1 Loss**, assigning a weight of **1.0** to the inspiratory phase and **0.1** to the expiratory phase. This focuses capacity on the metric while maintaining the temporal context required for stable hidden states (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Extended Duration:** We will set the training duration to **150+ epochs**.
    *   Deep recurrent models on this task exhibit "long-tail convergence," where significant error reduction occurs well after epoch 50. By utilizing the high-throughput LSTM architecture (avoiding CNN/Attention overhead, Cite solution_lesson_node_00017), we can fit this extended schedule within the compute budget to minimize the final error (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
