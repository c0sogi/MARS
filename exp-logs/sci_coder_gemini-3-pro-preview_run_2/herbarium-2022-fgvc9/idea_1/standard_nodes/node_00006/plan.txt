The execution failure is caused by a mismatch between the sparse taxonomic `category_id` values (which go up to 15505) and the model's output dimension (configured for 15501 classes). When the loss function receives a label $\ge$ 15501, it triggers a CUDA device-side assertion (index out of bounds) (Cite `debug_lesson_2`). To fix this, we need to map the raw labels to a contiguous range `[0, 15500]` during training and map the predictions back to the original IDs for submission (Cite `debug_lesson_3`).

Here are the changes:

1.  **`library/dataset.py`**: Update `PlantDataset` to accept a `label_map` and transform labels in `__getitem__`.
2.  **`library/prototype_manager.py`**: Update `generate_submission` to accept an `idx_to_label` map for inverse transformation.
3.  **`runfile.py`**: Generate the mappings from `train.csv` and pass them to the dataset and submission method.
