Based on the empirical lessons learned, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution structurally evolves the approach by transitioning from a single-model point estimate to a **Cross-Validated Ensemble** to address the high variance observed in small-data training, while upgrading the backbone to the next tier of compound scaling.

# Model
I propose upgrading the backbone to the **EfficientNetV2-L (Large)** architecture within a **Multi-Label Decomposition** framework.
*   **Structural Innovation (Backbone):** We will advance the compound scaling strategy `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008` by adopting **EfficientNetV2-L**. This model offers significantly higher capacity and faster training dynamics (via Fused-MBConv layers) compared to the B5 and V2-M variants used previously. It is designed to handle the 480x480 resolution natively, allowing it to resolve the finest disease artifacts that smaller models might miss.
*   **Classification Head:** We will retain the successful **Multi-Label Decomposition** head `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`. Instead of a 4-class Softmax, the model will output 2 binary logits (Sigmoid) corresponding to **"Is Rust"** and **"Is Scab"**. This allows the model to learn shared features from the "Multiple Diseases" class (which contains both rust and scab) rather than treating it as a disjoint minority class.

# Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Resolution:** Images will be resized to **480x480 pixels**, matching the native input resolution of the EfficientNetV2-L backbone `Cite solution_lesson_node_00008`.
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **CoarseDropout:** We will apply **CoarseDropout** with scaled parameters (e.g., max hole size 100x100) to force distributed feature learning `Cite solution_lesson_node_00006`. We will strictly enforce **minimum hole dimensions** (e.g., 16x16) to prevent trivial occlusions `Cite solution_lesson_node_00005`.
    *   **Exclusions:** We will explicitly exclude CutMix, as area-based mixing introduces label noise for localized pathologies `Cite solution_lesson_node_00013`. We will also exclude global photometric distortions to preserve color semantics `Cite solution_lesson_node_00004`.

# Training
*   **Structural Innovation (Pipeline):** We will implement **5-Fold Cross-Validation**.
    *   **Rationale:** Previous iterations showed that validation performance was highly sensitive to stochastic factors like data loading seeds `Cite solution_lesson_node_00009`. Given the small dataset size (~1,300 images), a single train/validation split is unreliable. By training 5 independent models on different data folds, we reduce variance and ensure the final predictions are robust to data selection bias.
*   **Loss Function:** We will use **Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) Loss** with **Positive Class Weights**.
    *   We will calculate the frequency of "Rust" and "Scab" across the dataset and weight the positive terms in the BCE loss inversely to their occurrence. This maintains the benefit of imbalance handling `Cite solution_lesson_node_00001` within the multi-label framework.
*   **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** will be used for stable convergence.
    *   **Batch Size:** We will strictly enforce a batch size of at least **16** (using gradient accumulation if necessary) to ensure stable batch normalization statistics `Cite solution_lesson_node_00014`.
    *   **Reproducibility:** We will use a `worker_init_fn` to seed DataLoaders for deterministic training within each fold `Cite solution_lesson_node_00009`.

# Evaluation
*   **Ensemble Inference:** For each test image, we will generate predictions from all **5 fold models**.
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each fold model, we will average predictions from the original and horizontally flipped image `Cite solution_lesson_node_00002`.
*   **Aggregation:** The final "Rust" and "Scab" probabilities will be the average of the 5 models' TTA outputs.
*   **Reconstruction:** We will mathematically reconstruct the required 4-class probabilities from the aggregated binary scores ($P_r, P_s$) as established in the decomposition strategy `Cite solution_lesson_node_00020`:
    *   `Healthy`: $(1 - P_r) \times (1 - P_s)$
    *   `Rust`: $P_r \times (1 - P_s)$
    *   `Scab`: $(1 - P_r) \times P_s$
    *   `Multiple`: $P_r \times P_s$
