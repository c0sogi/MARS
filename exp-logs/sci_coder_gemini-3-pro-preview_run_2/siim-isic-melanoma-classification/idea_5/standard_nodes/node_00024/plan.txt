The optimization strategy focuses on upgrading the feature extractor while maintaining computational efficiency and enhancing the multi-task learning signal.

1.  **Backbone Upgrade & Resolution Optimization**: We upgrade the backbone from `EfficientNet-B2` to `EfficientNet-B3` (Noisy Student weights). To balance the increased model capacity and stay within the runtime limit, we reduce the image resolution from 384x384 to 320x320. This resolution is closer to B3's native resolution (300x300), reducing the computational overhead of excessive upsampling while providing richer features. (Cite Lesson 00007).
2.  **Enhanced Auxiliary Supervision**: We increase the `aux_loss_weight` from 0.1 to 0.5. Since the architecture uses Hierarchical Injection (feeding diagnosis logits into the malignancy head), improving the quality of the diagnosis predictions is critical. A higher weight forces the backbone to learn more discriminative features for the specific lesion types, which directly aids the primary task. (Cite Lesson 00020, 00023).
3.  **Regularization**: We increase the dropout rate in the fusion module from 0.3 to 0.4 and introduce **Gradient Clipping** (max_norm=10.0) in the training loop. This helps stabilize the fine-tuning of the larger, unfrozen B3 backbone and prevents gradient explosions in the multi-head architecture. (Cite Lesson 00006).
