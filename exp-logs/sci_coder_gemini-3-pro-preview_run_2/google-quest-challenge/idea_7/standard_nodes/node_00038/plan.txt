The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` arises because the `GranularSiameseDeBERTa` architecture processes two input streams (Question and Answer) per sample by invoking the heavy DeBERTa backbone twice. This necessitates storing two sets of intermediate activations simultaneously for backpropagation, which exceeds the available 16GB VRAM when using a batch size of 8 with sequence lengths of 512. To resolve this, I will reduce the `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` from 8 to 4 and the `VALID_BATCH_SIZE` from 16 to 8 in `library/config.py`. This reduction directly addresses the budget for cumulative activations in multi-pass architectures. Cite debug_lesson_1.
