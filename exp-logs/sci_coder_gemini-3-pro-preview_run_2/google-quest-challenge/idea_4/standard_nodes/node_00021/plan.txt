The `CUDA out of memory` error indicates that the current batch size of 8 is too large for the available GPU memory (approx. 16GB), especially given the Siamese architecture which requires two forward passes (one for the question and one for the answer) per sample. As noted in `debug_lesson_1`, multi-pass architectures effectively double the memory required for storing activations during training. To resolve this, I will reduce the `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` from 8 to 4 and the `VALID_BATCH_SIZE` from 16 to 8 in `library/config.py`. Cite debug_lesson_1.
