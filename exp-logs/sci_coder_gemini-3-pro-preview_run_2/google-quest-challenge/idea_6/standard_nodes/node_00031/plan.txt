The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurs because the `GranularSiameseModel` executes the heavy DeBERTa backbone twice (once for the question and once for the answer) within a single forward pass. As explained in **debug_lesson_1** ("Budget for Cumulative Activations in Multi-Pass Architectures"), this dual-stream approach requires storing two sets of intermediate activations simultaneously for backpropagation, effectively doubling the memory footprint compared to a standard model. The current batch size of 8 exceeds the 16GB GPU capacity.

To fix this, I will reduce the `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` from 8 to 4 and the `VALID_BATCH_SIZE` from 16 to 8 in `library/config.py`. I will also update `runfile.py` to explicitly reflect these configuration changes, ensuring the runtime respects the memory constraints.
