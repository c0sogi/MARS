The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurs because the Siamese architecture executes the heavy DeBERTa backbone twice per training step (once for the question, once for the answer). This doubles the memory required for storing intermediate activations compared to a standard single-pass model. With a batch size of 8 and sequence length of 512, the activation memory exceeds the GPU's 16GB capacity (Cite debug_lesson_1). To resolve this, I will reduce the `train_batch_size` from 8 to 4. I will also increase `accumulation_steps` from 1 to 2 to preserve the effective batch size of 8, ensuring the training dynamics remain consistent with the original configuration.
