The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurred because the Siamese network architecture invokes the heavy DeBERTa backbone twice per batch (once for the question, once for the answer), forcing PyTorch to store two full sets of intermediate activations in VRAM for backpropagation. As explained in **debug_lesson_1**, shared weights do not share activation memory; therefore, the memory footprint scales linearly with the number of passes. To resolve this, I will reduce the `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` in `runfile.py` from 8 to 4, ensuring the cumulative activations fit within the GPU's memory limits.
