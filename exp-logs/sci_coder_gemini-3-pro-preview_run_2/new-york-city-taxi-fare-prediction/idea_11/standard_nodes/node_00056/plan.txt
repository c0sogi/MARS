2.  **Switch to Soft Integration (Cite Lesson 55)**: Instead of forcing the model to learn a residual (`Target - Prior`) via `base_margin` (Hard Integration), I will supply the hierarchical prior as a standard input feature (`mean_fare_estimate`). This allows the Gradient Boosting model to dynamically weight the prior against other features, preventing the catastrophic failure observed when the prior is imperfect (e.g., in sparse regions).
3.  **Scale Up Training (Cite Lesson 45)**: I will increase the training subsample from 2M to 6M rows. Lesson 45 demonstrated that higher data density significantly improves the resolution and accuracy of spatial target encoding (the "prior"), which is the primary driver of performance in this architecture.
