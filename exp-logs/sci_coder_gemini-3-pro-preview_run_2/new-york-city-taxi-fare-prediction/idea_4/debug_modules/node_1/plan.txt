The error `TypeError: XGBModel.fit() got an unexpected keyword argument 'early_stopping_rounds'` occurs because newer versions of XGBoost (scikit-learn API) require `early_stopping_rounds` to be passed to the model constructor rather than the `fit()` method. To fix this, I will modify `library/model_trainer.py` to add `early_stopping_rounds` (sourced from `Config`) to the parameters dictionary passed to `XGBRegressor` during initialization, and remove the argument from the `fit()` call. This aligns with the guidance in debug_lesson_1.
