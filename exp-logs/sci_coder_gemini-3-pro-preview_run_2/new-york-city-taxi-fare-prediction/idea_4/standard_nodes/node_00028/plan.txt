The log-transform optimizes for Mean Squared Logarithmic Error (MSLE), which systematically underestimates the mean of the target distribution when reverted, leading to poor RMSE performance on heavy-tailed data. I will revert to training on the raw `fare_amount` to directly minimize the Squared Error (L2), which aligns with the RMSE metric. I will also update the working directory to ensure fresh data processing and adjust the training script to handle raw predictions.
