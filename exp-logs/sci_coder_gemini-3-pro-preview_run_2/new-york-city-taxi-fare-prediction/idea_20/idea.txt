Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the evolution of previous experiments, I propose a **Factorized Multi-Moment Hierarchical Gradient Boosting** strategy.

This approach structurally synthesizes the **Hierarchical Spatial Context** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00072}), the **Risk-Awareness of Distributional Moments** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00071}), and the **Stability of Dual-Hygiene** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00066}). It introduces a refined **Conditional Vectorized Subtraction** pipeline to correctly implement K-Fold encoding on dually-filtered data, resolving the leakage and logic errors seen in previous attempts.

# Model
I will utilize the **XGBoost Regressor** as the backbone, leveraging its proven superiority for spatial tabular tasks (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00030}).

*   **Structural Innovation (Factorized Probabilistic Priors):** Instead of providing a single "Expected Fare" feature, I will decompose the prior knowledge into two factorized probabilistic views, provided as **Input Features** ("Soft Integration" - Cite {solution_lesson_node_00055}):
    1.  **Spatial Interaction Moments:** The **Mean** (Expected Price) and **Standard Deviation** (Price Risk) for the specific A-to-B route. The Standard Deviation explicitly signals the model to look for temporal triggers (e.g., Rush Hour) on volatile routes, addressing the "Heavy Tail" prediction problem safely (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00018}).
    2.  **Temporal Rate Moments:** The **Mean** and **Standard Deviation** of the *Fare-Per-Km* for the neighborhood at the specific hour. This captures dynamic pricing pressures (e.g., traffic congestion) orthogonal to the static route distance (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00065}).
*   **Hierarchical Architecture:** These moments will be generated at three simultaneous spatial resolutions (Geohash Levels 5, 6, and 7). This creates a "Pyramid of Priors" that allows the tree to dynamically shift focus from fine-grained memorization (in dense areas) to regional smoothing (in sparse areas) (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00072}).

# Data
The data pipeline employs a **Dual-Hygiene Strategy** combined with **Conditional Vectorized Subtraction** to maximize signal quality while ensuring mathematical correctness and preventing leakage.

1.  **Input Sanitization:**
    *   Strictly clamp all pickup and dropoff coordinates in the **Training, Validation, and Test sets** to the NYC bounding box immediately. This prevents linear extrapolation failures (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00003}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00070}).

2.  **Dual-Hygiene Definitions (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00066}):**
    *   **Wisdom Criteria (Strict):** Rows where `Fare` is between $2.50 and $200, AND `Fare/Km` < $10. Used to generate clean, robust statistical priors.
    *   **Learner Criteria (Loose):** Rows where `Fare` > $2.50. Retains valid high-fare outliers (e.g., >$500) to ensure the model learns to predict the "Heavy Tail" required for the RMSE metric (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00018}).

3.  **Feature Engineering (Conditional Vectorized Subtraction):**
    *   **Global Aggregation:** Compute `Sum_Fare`, `Sum_FareSq`, and `Count` for all Geohash levels (L5, L6, L7) using the **Full 55M Dataset** filtered by **Wisdom Criteria**.
    *   **Training Encoding:** For the Learner Set (Subsample), use **K-Fold Target Encoding** (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00060}).
    *   **Correction Mechanism:** To compute out-of-fold stats efficiently without leakage:
        *   Identify rows in the current fold that *also* meet the **Wisdom Criteria**.
        *   Compute the sums/counts of *only* these rows.
        *   Subtract these "Fold Wisdom Stats" from the "Global Wisdom Stats" to get the "Rest-of-World Wisdom Stats".
        *   *Note:* This corrects the logic error of simply subtracting the Learner Fold (which contains outliers) from the Wisdom Global (which does not), ensuring the priors remain strictly clean (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00045}).

4.  **Inductive Bias:** Retain explicit geometric features (Haversine, Manhattan, Rotated Coordinates) and raw temporal features (Hour, Year) (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00046}, Cite {solution_lesson_node_00065}).

# Training
1.  **Objective Function:** Use **`reg:squarederror`** (Standard L2 Loss).
    *   **Rationale:** We are training on a stable subsample with robust, clean Factorized Moments as features. The optimization landscape is stable enough to use L2 loss, which aligns perfectly with the RMSE metric. We avoid robust losses (Huber) as they fail to prioritize the high-fare outliers explicitly retained in the Learner target (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00037}).
2.  **Target:** Train on the **raw `fare_amount`** (Loose Filtered). Do not log-transform, as this biases the RMSE (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00025}).
3.  **Dataset:** Train on a stable subsample (e.g., 5M rows) to avoid gradient instability (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00017}).

    *   Map coordinates to Geohashes (L5, L6, L7).
