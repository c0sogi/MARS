My implementation strategy focuses on optimizing the expert pool and hyperparameter tuning based on the provided lessons.

1.  **Remove Kernel LDA**: Based on **Lesson 00061**, kernel approximations in high-dimensional, low-sample settings often degrade performance and introduce noise. The previous run confirmed this with a high log loss for the Kernel expert. I will remove `get_kernel_lda` and its dependencies.
2.  **Optimize Logistic Regression (Linear LR)**:
    *   **Grid Density & Range**: I will increase the `Cs` grid from 20 to 100 points and broaden the range to `[-5, 5]`. **Lesson 00047** and **Lesson 00037** emphasize that dense, broad grids are critical for tuning linear models on small data to find the precise regularization strength.
    *   **Cross-Validation Strategy**: I will reduce `cv` from 5 to 3. **Lesson 00010** suggests that fewer folds stabilize tuning on small datasets by ensuring validation partitions are large enough.
    *   **Convergence**: I will increase `max_iter` to 5000 to prevent underfitting (**Lesson 00010**).
3.  **Refine Linear LDA**: I will increase the tolerance precision (`tol=1e-8`) in the LDA solver to ensure maximum numerical stability for the generative component, which is currently the strongest performer.

These changes aim to refine the individual experts before the Greedy Ensemble Selection step, maximizing the quality of the candidate pool.
