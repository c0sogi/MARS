Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Stratified-Discriminative Precision-Generative Ensemble (SDPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Cross-Domain Interaction" (Idea 54) and "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Idea 45, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) concepts. It addresses the limitation that Global LDA projections (Idea 49) may compress domain-specific signals too aggressively, while raw feature interactions are computationally infeasible. By introducing a **Stratified Discriminative Projection** topology—which projects each semantic feature group (Margin, Shape, Texture) into its own optimized discriminative subspace *before* modeling interactions—we create a "Bottleneck Interaction" view. This allows the ensemble to capture complex non-linear dependencies between biological traits (e.g., how Margin discriminability interacts with Texture discriminability) without the noise of raw features, integrated into a library managed by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Topologies**, allowing the ensemble to exploit Global Linear, Physical Non-Linear, and Stratified Interaction signals simultaneously.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Topology A: Global Statistical Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
    *   **Topologies:**
        1.  **Marginal:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`. (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`)
        2.  **Rotational:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`. Aligns data with principal axes without noise amplification (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).
        3.  **Robust:** `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=50)`. Strictly constrained rank-based normalization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, modeling global linear correlations.

*   **Topology B: Physical Polynomial Experts (The Domain Signal)**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
        *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
    *   **Rationale:** Captures non-linear physical constraints (e.g., $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) from raw images, treating them as an orthogonal view (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

*   **Topology C: Stratified Discriminative-Interaction Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** Split Global View into **Margin**, **Shape**, and **Texture** subsets.
    *   **Pipeline:**
        1.  **Stratified Projection:** Apply `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **LDA_Transformer** to each subset independently. This projects each 64-dim group onto its most class-discriminative axes (e.g., reducing to ~15 dims each).
        2.  **Concatenation:** Combine the three projected vectors.
        3.  **Interaction Expansion:** Apply **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2, interaction_only=True)**. This generates terms representing interactions *between* the discriminative essences of Margin, Shape, and Texture (e.g., $Margin\_Discriminant_1 \times Texture\_Discriminant_1$).
        4.  **Re-Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Unlike Global Projection (which mixes domains) or Raw Expansion (which is noisy), this topology models interactions between the **purified discriminative signals** of each biological domain. This bridges the gap between linear models and complex biological dependencies (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`, `solution_lesson_node_00064`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss. This ensures that the ensemble relies on the robust Global Anchor unless the Interaction view provides orthogonal gain (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Stratified Views:** Slice Global into Margin (0-63), Shape (64-127), Texture (128-191).
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** and **Geometric Scalars** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipelines:** Apply the specific transformation sequences (Marginal, Rotational, Robust, Stratified-Interaction, Poly-Physical) to generate the feature views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Topologies A, B, C $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
