The issue is that the `LabelEncoder` is being fitted on the training dataframe *after* it has been reduced to a small subset (50 rows) by the inner merge with the debug-mode features. This causes the encoder to miss classes that are present in the validation set but absent from the training subset, leading to a `ValueError` when transforming the validation labels.

To fix this, I will move the `LabelEncoder.fit` call to occur immediately after loading the full metadata, before any feature merging or row filtering takes place. This ensures the encoder learns the complete set of classes regardless of subsequent data subsampling, as recommended in `debug_lesson_8`.
