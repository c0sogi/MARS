Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Full-Rank Stratified-Polynomial Precision Ensemble (FR-SPPE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) and "Full-Rank" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00129`) concepts. It directly challenges the assumption made in previous "Interaction" attempts (e.g., Idea 64, 66) that dimensionality reduction (bottlenecks) is required before polynomial expansion.

Lesson 129 proved that bottlenecks degrade performance in this regime, while Lesson 90 and Lesson 64 proved that **Analytical Shrinkage** (OAS/Ledoit-Wolf) can robustly handle high-dimensional spaces ($P \gg N$) without feature selection. Therefore, instead of compressing semantic groups (Margin, Shape, Texture) into low-dimensional subspaces, we will apply **Full-Rank Polynomial Expansion** to each group independently. This generates high-dimensional "Component-Interaction" views ($D \approx 2000$) that capture complex intra-domain non-linearities (e.g., Texture complexity), relying on **High-Precision Shrinkage LDA** to manage the dimensionality. This is integrated into a library managed by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Feature Scopes** and **Complexity Levels**, strictly maintaining **Double Precision** and avoiding dimensionality reduction.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

**Group A: Global Linear Anchors (The Baseline)**
*   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
*   **Topologies:**
    1.  **Marginal:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`. Stabilizes variance feature-wise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
    2.  **Stratified-Rotational:** Split Global into Margin, Shape, Texture. Apply `Power` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `Power` to each subset independently to align manifolds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`). Recombine.
*   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
*   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, modeling global linear correlations.

**Group B: Physical Polynomial Experts (The Domain Signal)**
*   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
    *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
*   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
*   **Algorithm:** **Regularized QDA** (`reg_param` grid `[0.1, 0.5]`).
*   **Rationale:** Exploits the low dimensionality ($D \approx 11$) of the physical view to use QDA, capturing non-linear shape constraints (heteroscedasticity) that Linear models miss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`, `solution_lesson_node_00094`).

**Group C: Stratified Full-Rank Polynomial Experts (The Innovation)**
*   **Input:** Independent subsets: **Margin** (64), **Shape** (64), **Texture** (64).
*   **Pipeline:**
    1.  **Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer` to the subset.
    2.  **Full-Rank Expansion:** Apply **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)**. This expands the 64 features to $\approx 2080$ interaction terms.
    3.  **Re-Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer`.
*   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with **OAS** and **High Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.1, 0.5]`).
*   **Rationale:** Explicitly models non-linear interactions *within* each biological domain (e.g., complex texture patterns). Unlike previous attempts that compressed these groups to ~10 dims (losing signal), we keep the full expansion. We rely on **Shrinkage** to regularize the covariance estimation in this $P \gg N$ regime ($2000 \gg 700$), which Lesson 64 proved is superior to feature selection (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00129`, `solution_lesson_node_00064`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global/Stratified Views:** Load 192 features; slice into Global, Margin, Shape, Texture.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Application:** Apply the specific transformation sequences to generate the candidate feature matrices.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Power, PCA, Poly) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full library of experts (Groups A-C $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
