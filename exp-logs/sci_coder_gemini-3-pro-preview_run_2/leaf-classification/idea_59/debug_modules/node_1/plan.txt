The `AssertionError` is caused by the `RotationalBasis` transformer, which internally uses PCA. PCA implicitly limits the number of components to `min(n_samples, n_features)`. In the test setup, `n_samples` (50) is less than `n_features` (192), causing the transformer to reduce the dimensionality to 50, which violates the assertion expecting the original 192 dimensions. Additionally, the subsequent `FactorizedInteractionProjector` test expects 5 LDA components, which requires at least 6 classes (since LDA components $\le K-1$), but the synthetic data only has 5 classes.

To fix this, I will increase `n_samples` to 200 (ensuring $N > D$) to prevent PCA dimensionality reduction (Cite debug_lesson_9) and increase the number of synthetic classes to 10 to satisfy the LDA component requirements.
