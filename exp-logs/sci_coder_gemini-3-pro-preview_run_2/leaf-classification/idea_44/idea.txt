Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Polynomial-Morphometric Precision-Generative Ensemble (PMPGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Gaussianized" (Idea 30) concepts. It resolves the tension between "Geometric Features Help" (Lesson 50) and "Geometric Features are Redundant" (Lesson 52/90) by acknowledging that while linear geometric scalars may be redundant to high-dimensional histograms, **Non-Linear Physical Constraints** (e.g., interaction terms like $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) provide orthogonal signal. By combining **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Lesson 106) with **Polynomial Expansion** and **Precision-Generative Models**, we construct an ensemble that is mathematically robust, physically informed, and strictly protected against overfitting.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that vary in **Feature View** (Statistical vs. Physical-Polynomial), **Gaussianization Basis** (Parametric vs. Robust), and **Covariance Estimation**, allowing the Greedy Selector to mathematically determine the optimal density estimation surface.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Group A: The Statistical Anchors (Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
    *   **Preprocessing:** **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, utilizing the high-dimensional statistical signal with robust covariance estimation.

*   **Group B: The Polynomial-Physical Experts (Innovation)**
    *   **Input:** **Polynomial Morphometric View**.
        *   *Base:* Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics (Hu Moments + Scalars).
        *   *Expansion:* **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2, interaction_only=False)**.
    *   **Preprocessing:** **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson).
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage.
    *   **Rationale:** While simple scalars were found redundant (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052`), non-linear combinations (e.g., curvature constraints) are not captured by linear models on the provided histograms. By expanding the low-dimensional physical view ($D \approx 12 \rightarrow D \approx 90$), we allow the Linear solver to model **Quadratic Physical Boundaries** without the sample-size prohibitive cost of full QDA (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`).
    *   **Polarity Correction:** Mandatory to ensure physical validity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).

*   **Group C: The Robust Distributional Experts**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Preprocessing:** **QuantileTransformer** with `output_distribution='normal'` and **`n_quantiles=50`**.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (OAS).
    *   **Rationale:** Strictly constrained rank-based normalization. Handles skewed distributions that Yeo-Johnson misses, with `n_quantiles` constrained to ~7% of $N$ to prevent the overfitting observed in Lesson 76 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
    *   **Polynomial View:** Apply `PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)` to the Morphometric View.
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Transformations:** Apply the specific transformer (Power or Constrained-Quantile) to the respective views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Statistical Anchors, Poly-Physical Experts, Robust Experts) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set and generate Polynomial features.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
