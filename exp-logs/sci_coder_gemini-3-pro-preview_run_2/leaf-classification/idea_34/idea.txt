Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Dual-Basis Gaussianized Generative Ensemble (DBGGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Gaussianized" (Idea 30) concepts by addressing the specific failure mode of Non-Parametric Gaussianization observed in Lesson 76. By introducing a **Dual-Basis Preprocessing Architecture**—combining the proven Parametric (Yeo-Johnson) path with a strictly constrained Non-Parametric (Rank-Based) path—we create a "Stereoscopic" density estimation framework. This allows the ensemble to exploit the robustness of rank-based transforms for skewed data without the overfitting caused by high quantile counts, all managed by Greedy Selection.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that vary in **Gaussianization Basis** (Parametric vs. Rank-Based), **Covariance Estimation** (Analytical vs. Empirical), and **Feature View** (Global vs. Macro).

### 1. The Dual-Basis Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to prevent numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Basis A: Parametric Gaussian Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Preprocessing:** **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with a library of shrinkage estimators: **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`), **Ledoit-Wolf** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`), and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 features).
    *   **Role:** Models class densities assuming the data can be stabilized via power transforms. This preserves the state-of-the-art baseline (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Basis B: Constrained Non-Parametric Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Preprocessing:** **QuantileTransformer** with `output_distribution='normal'` and **`n_quantiles=30`**.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library (OAS, Fixed).
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Rationale:** Lesson 76 failed because `n_quantiles` was set to ~700 (matching $N$), causing the transformer to memorize the training distribution and destroy linear separability. By strictly constraining `n_quantiles` to a small value (e.g., 30, approx 5% of $N$), we create a **Regularized Rank-Based Normalizer**. This handles multi-modal or highly skewed features that Yeo-Johnson cannot fix, providing a mathematically distinct "Gaussian View" for the LDA models without overfitting.

*   **Basis C: Orthogonal Morphometric Experts**
    *   **Preprocessing:** **PowerTransformer**.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
    *   **Input:** **Macro-Resolution View** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
    *   **Rationale:** Captures invariant physical shape constraints orthogonal to the texture histograms. We use LDA instead of QDA because $N_{class} \approx 8$ is too small for class-specific covariance estimation even in low dimensions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`, `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss. This ensures that if the Parametric Anchor is sufficient, the ensemble converges to it; if the Non-Parametric view adds signal, it is incorporated (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Macro View:** Load raw binary images. Extract **Deterministic** Morphometrics: **Hu Moments** (7 invariant descriptors) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity). We explicitly reject Monte-Carlo augmentation (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00089`) and ImageNet features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00049`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64`. This is mandatory for minimizing Log Loss near the theoretical floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Specifics:**
        *   Basis A/C use `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
        *   Basis B uses `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=30)`.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Parametric LDA variants, Constrained Non-Parametric LDA variants, Morphological LDA) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Macro features for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts (applying the specific Gaussianization and `float64` casting).
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
