Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Stratified-Manifold Full-Rank Interaction Ensemble (SM-FRIE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Full-Rank Stratified-Manifold" (Idea 69) and "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) concepts. It addresses the critical finding in Lesson 129 that dimensionality reduction (bottlenecks) degrades performance in this High-Dimensional Small-Sample (HDSS) regime. Previous attempts to model interactions (Idea 64, 66) failed because they compressed features too aggressively before expansion.

The **SM-FRIE** strategy posits that **Analytical Shrinkage** (OAS/Ledoit-Wolf) is sufficient to handle the high dimensionality of polynomial expansion without explicit bottlenecks, provided the input manifolds are correctly aligned. We construct a library of experts that model **Linear**, **Physical**, and **Polynomial** signal structures, strictly maintaining **Full Rank** (or near-lossless 99% variance) to preserve subtle biological signals, managed by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Topologies**, **Feature Scopes**, and **Complexity Levels**.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

**Tier 1: Linear Manifold Anchors (The Baseline)**
*   **Expert A: Global Marginal Anchor**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 features).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the robust state-of-the-art baseline (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
*   **Expert B: Stratified Rotational Anchor**
    *   **Input:** Split Global View into **Margin**, **Shape**, and **Texture**.
    *   **Pipeline:** Apply `Power` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `Power` to each subset independently to align manifolds without truncation. Concatenate results.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with **OAS**.
    *   **Role:** Aligns biological manifolds independently, preventing high-variance groups (Texture) from dominating the rotation of lower-variance groups (Margin) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`).

**Tier 2: Physical Domain Experts (The Geometry)**
*   **Expert C: Physical QDA**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
        *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Regularized QDA** (`reg_param` grid `[0.1, 0.5]`).
    *   **Role:** Exploits the low dimensionality ($D \approx 11$) of the physical view to capture non-linear shape constraints (heteroscedasticity) via class-specific covariance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00094`, `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

**Tier 3: Full-Rank Interaction Experts (The Innovation)**
*   **Experts D, E, F: Intra-Component Polynomial LDAs**
    *   **Input:** Individual subsets: **Margin**, **Shape**, **Texture**.
    *   **Pipeline:**
        1.  **Gaussianization:** `PowerTransformer`.
        2.  **Full-Rank Expansion:** **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)**. Expands 64 features to $\approx 2080$ dimensions.
        3.  **Re-Gaussianization:** `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with **High Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.1, 0.5]`).
    *   **Rationale:** Explicitly models non-linear interactions *within* each biological domain. We rely on strong shrinkage to regularize the $P \gg N$ covariance estimation ($2000 \gg 700$), avoiding the information loss of bottlenecks (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00129`).
*   **Expert G: Global High-Variance Polynomial LDA**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Pipeline:** `Power` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(n_components=0.99)** $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `Power`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with **High Fixed Shrinkage**.
    *   **Rationale:** Captures global interactions. We use PCA to retain **99% variance** (lossless compression) rather than an arbitrary $n=10$ bottleneck. This keeps the dimensionality manageable for shrinkage while preserving virtually all signal.

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global/Stratified Views:** Load 192 features; slice into Global, Margin, Shape, Texture.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Application:** Apply the specific transformation sequences to generate the candidate feature matrices.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Power, PCA, Poly) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Tiers 1-3) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
