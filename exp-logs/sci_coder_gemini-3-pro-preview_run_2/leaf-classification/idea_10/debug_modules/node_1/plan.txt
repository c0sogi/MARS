The `ValueError` encountered during `LogisticRegressionCV` execution is caused by the aggressive subsampling (`debug_limit = 150`) applied to a dataset with 99 unique classes. This results in cross-validation folds where the validation set lacks classes present in the training set, leading to a mismatch between the number of classes in `y_true` and the predicted probabilities `y_prob` within the `log_loss` scorer. To resolve this, I will set `debug_limit` to `None` in `runfile.py`, allowing the model to train on the full dataset. This ensures that even rare classes have sufficient samples (minimum 5 per class vs. 3 folds) to be represented in all cross-validation splits, preventing the dimension mismatch (Cite debug_lesson_2).
