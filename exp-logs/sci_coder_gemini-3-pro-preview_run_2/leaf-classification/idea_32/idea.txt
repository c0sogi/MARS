Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Hierarchical Bagged-Generative Ensemble (HBGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Generative" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the "Metric Floor Paradox" (Lesson 90) by acknowledging that while analytical shrinkage (OAS) is asymptotically optimal, the finite sample size ($N \approx 700$) introduces variance in the covariance estimation that a single model cannot capture. By introducing **Bagging** (Bootstrap Aggregation) to the Generative Baseline and integrating **Additive Taxonomic Constraints**, we construct a solution that is statistically robust, biologically informed, and mathematically precise.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that vary in **Stability Mechanism** (Analytical vs. Bootstrap) and **Biological Granularity** (Species vs. Genus), allowing the Greedy Selector to construct the optimal predictive surface.

### 1. The Hierarchical Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`) and utilize **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson) preprocessing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Tier 1: The Analytical Anchor (Baseline)**
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **Oracle Approximating Shrinkage (OAS)**.
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 features).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art performance of the single best estimator (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`).

*   **Tier 2: The Bagged Generative Expert (Innovation)**
    *   **Algorithm:** **BaggingClassifier** wrapping **LDA** (with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage).
    *   **Configuration:** `n_estimators=50`, `bootstrap=True`, `max_samples=0.8`, `max_features=1.0`.
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Rationale:** Lesson 27 showed that *feature* subsampling hurts linear models. However, **sample** bootstrapping (Bagging) reduces the variance of the covariance estimate itself. By averaging 50 LDAs trained on perturbed sample sets, we smooth out noise in the decision boundaries that a single analytical model (even OAS) might overfit to, providing a "Robustness" layer orthogonal to shrinkage.

*   **Tier 3: The Macro-QDA Expert (Physical Signal)**
    *   **Algorithm:** **Regularized Quadratic Discriminant Analysis (QDA)**.
    *   **Input:** **Macro-Resolution View** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars, $D \approx 12$).
    *   **Rationale:** Captures non-linear, class-specific shape variance (heteroscedasticity) in the low-dimensional physical space, which the shared-covariance LDA misses (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`, `solution_lesson_node_00029`).

*   **Tier 4: The Taxonomic Prior (Biological Constraint)**
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** trained on **Genus Labels**.
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Mechanism:** The model predicts the Genus (e.g., *Acer*). This probability vector is mapped to the 99 Species by distributing the mass uniformly among species belonging to that genus (e.g., $P(S_{Acer\_X}) = P(G_{Acer}) / N_{Acer\_spp}$).
    *   **Rationale:** Lesson 30 failed because it used *multiplicative* constraints (Vetoing). This expert provides an **additive** signal. If the Species model is confused between two species of different genera, this expert injects a strong phylogenetic prior to resolve the ambiguity safely.

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Macro View:** Load raw binary images. Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariant descriptors) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
    *   **Genus Labels:** Parse the `species` string (e.g., `Acer_Capillipes` -> `Acer`) to create a target vector for the Taxonomic Expert.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')` to all feature views (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:**
        *   Train OAS-LDA and Bagged-LDA on Global View.
        *   Train Regularized QDA on Macro View.
        *   Train Genus-LDA on Global View (using Genus targets).
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set. (Map Genus predictions to Species space).
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Macro features for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
    *   For the Taxonomic Expert, predict Genus probabilities and map them to the Species format.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
