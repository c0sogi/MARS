Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Constrained-Basis Dual-Stream Generative Ensemble (CB-DSGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Gaussianized" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the "Metric Floor Paradox" (Lesson 90) and the specific failure mode of Non-Parametric Gaussianization (Lesson 76). While Lesson 76 proved that `QuantileTransformer` overfits when `n_quantiles` approaches the sample size ($N \approx 700$), it did not invalidate the utility of rank-based normalization itself. By introducing a **Dual-Stream Architecture** that runs a proven **Parametric Stream** (Yeo-Johnson) alongside a strictly **Constrained Non-Parametric Stream** (Low-Rank Quantiles), we create a stereoscopic density estimation framework. This allows the ensemble to exploit the flexibility of rank-based transforms for non-Gaussian features while using the parametric stream as a robust anchor, all managed by Greedy Selection.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture composed of probabilistic experts generated from two parallel preprocessing streams.

### 1. The Dual-Stream Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Stream A: Parametric Gaussian Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Preprocessing:** **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson).
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with a library of shrinkage estimators: **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Models class densities assuming the data can be stabilized via global power laws. This preserves the state-of-the-art baseline (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Stream B: Constrained Non-Parametric Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Preprocessing:** **QuantileTransformer** with `output_distribution='normal'` and **`n_quantiles=50`**.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library (OAS, Fixed).
    *   **Rationale:** Lesson 76 failed because `n_quantiles` was set to ~700 (matching $N$), causing the transformer to memorize the training distribution (overfitting) and destroy the linear structure required for LDA. By strictly constraining `n_quantiles` to a small value (e.g., 50, approx 7% of $N$), we create a **Regularized Rank-Based Normalizer**. This handles multi-modal or highly skewed features that Yeo-Johnson cannot fix, providing a mathematically distinct "Gaussian View" without overfitting the empirical distribution function (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00076`).

*   **Discriminative Backup:**
    *   **Algorithm:** **Logistic Regression** with L2 regularization applied to **Stream A**.
    *   **Configuration:** `LogisticRegressionCV` with a **Dense Logarithmic Grid** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00047`) and explicitly optimizing `neg_log_loss` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00031`).
    *   **Role:** Re-introduces discriminative boundary optimization to complement the generative density estimation.

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss. This ensures that if the Parametric Anchor is sufficient (as in Lesson 90), the ensemble converges to it; if the Constrained Non-Parametric stream adds orthogonal signal, it is incorporated (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Selection:** Utilize the **192 extracted features** (Margin, Shape, Texture) exclusively. We explicitly reject Morphometrics and Zernike moments, as robust covariance estimation (OAS) rendered them redundant in previous iterations (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`, `solution_lesson_node_00052`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64`. This is mandatory for minimizing Log Loss near the theoretical floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Stream Generation:**
        *   Apply `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')` for Stream A.
        *   Apply `QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', n_quantiles=50)` for Stream B.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train all experts (Parametric LDA variants, Constrained Non-Parametric LDA variants, Parametric LogReg) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts (applying the specific Gaussianization stream and `float64` casting).
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
