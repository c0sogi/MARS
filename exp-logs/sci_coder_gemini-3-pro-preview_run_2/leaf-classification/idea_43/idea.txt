Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Rotational-Gaussianized Dynamic Generative Ensemble (RGDGE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Precision-Gaussianized" (Idea 30) concepts. It addresses the limitation identified in Lesson 104, where "Spectral Gaussianization" (Whitening) failed due to noise amplification. Instead of scaling the data (Whitening), I propose a **Rotational Gaussianization** pipeline that aligns the data with its principal axes *before* the final Power Transform. This allows the ensemble to approximate **Multivariate Normality** (required by LDA) more rigorously than Marginal Gaussianization, without the instability of inverse-variance scaling. Furthermore, it integrates the critical **Polarity Correction** (Lesson 106) to ensure physical morphometric signals are valid.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that vary in **Gaussianization Topology** (Marginal vs. Rotational) and **Covariance Estimation**, allowing the Greedy Selector to mathematically determine the optimal density estimation surface.

### 1. The Gaussian Topology Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Topology A: Marginal Parametric Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with Shrinkage Library (**OAS**, **Fixed**).
    *   **Mechanism:** Gaussianizes each feature independently.
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline which is robust but ignores non-Gaussian dependencies between features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Topology B: Rotational Parametric Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ `PCA(whiten=False)` $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Mechanism:**
        1.  **Stabilize:** Apply initial Power Transform to marginals to handle skew.
        2.  **Rotate:** Apply **PCA without Whitening**. This aligns the data with its principal axes of variation *without* rescaling by the inverse singular values (which amplifies noise in small-sample regimes, causing the failure in Lesson 104).
        3.  **Re-Gaussianize:** Apply a second Power Transform to the Principal Components.
    *   **Rationale:** LDA assumes the data follows a *Multivariate* Gaussian distribution. Topology A only guarantees *Marginal* Gaussianity. By rotating the data to uncorrelated axes and then Gaussianizing, Topology B constructs a feature space where the joint distribution is the product of the marginals, providing a superior approximation of the LDA assumptions (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`).

### 2. The Expert Library
For each Topology, we train LDA experts on two Feature Views:
*   **Global View:** The 192 provided features.
*   **Combined View:** Global + Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics.
*   **Estimators:** **OAS-LDA** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed-Shrinkage LDA** (`[0.001, 0.01, 0.1]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).

### 3. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering (Polarity-Corrected):**
    *   **Global View:** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check the corner pixels of the image. If the mean corner value is high (white), invert the image so the leaf is always the foreground (value 1). This fixes the invalid shape descriptors generated in previous attempts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariant descriptors) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
    *   **Combined View:** Concatenate Global and Morphometric matrices.
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Topology Generation:** Apply the Marginal and Rotational pipelines to generate transformed versions of the Global and Combined views.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full Cartesian product of (Topologies $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators $\times$ Views) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values, PCA components) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometric features for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts (applying the specific Topology transform and `float64` casting).
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
