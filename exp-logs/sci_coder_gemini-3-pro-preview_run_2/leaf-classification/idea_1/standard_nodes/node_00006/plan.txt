I will implement an optimization strategy that builds upon the success of the linear baseline (Cite Lesson 00001) and the importance of dynamic regularization tuning (Cite Lesson 00004).

The strategy involves replacing the single `LogisticRegressionCV` model with a **Soft Voting Ensemble** consisting of:
1.  **Logistic Regression CV**: Retaining the highly effective discriminative model with dynamic C-tuning (Cite Lesson 00004).
2.  **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) with Shrinkage**: Introducing a generative linear model. LDA is theoretically more efficient than Logistic Regression for small sample sizes (N=~7 per class) when class distributions are approximately normal. The `shrinkage='auto'` parameter (Ledoit-Wolf lemma) is crucial here to handle the high dimensionality (192 features) and potential collinearity, which complements the L2 regularization of the Logistic Regression.

This ensemble approach aims to reduce the variance of the predictions and improve the log loss metric by averaging the probability estimates of two distinct but complementary linear solvers.
