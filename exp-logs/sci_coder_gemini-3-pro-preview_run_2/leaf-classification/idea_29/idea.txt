Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Hierarchical Covariance Multi-Resolution Ensemble (HCMRE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Generative Library" (Idea 23) and "Multi-Resolution" (Idea 28) concepts. It addresses the "Metric Floor" paradox observed in Lesson 90 (where OAS-LDA appeared perfect on validation) by acknowledging that while Linear boundaries are sufficient for the *provided* high-dimensional features, the underlying biological classes likely exhibit **heteroscedasticity** (varying shape variance per species). By formalizing a **Covariance Hierarchy**—ranging from Diagonal to Shared to Class-Specific covariance—and mapping these complexities to the appropriate Feature Resolution (Micro vs. Macro), we construct an ensemble that is mathematically exhaustive in its density estimation capabilities while strictly protected against overfitting by Greedy Selection.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts that span the **Covariance Complexity Spectrum**, allowing the ensemble to empirically determine the optimal balance between Bias (Linear/Diagonal) and Variance (Quadratic).

### 1. The Hierarchical Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`) and utilize **PowerTransformer** (Yeo-Johnson) preprocessing (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`) to strictly satisfy normality assumptions.

*   **Tier 1: Diagonal Covariance Experts (High Bias)**
    *   **Algorithm:** **Gaussian Naive Bayes (GNB)** with a grid of `var_smoothing` parameters.
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 features).
    *   **Role:** Assumes feature independence. Serves as a high-bias regularizer/anchor. If the complex covariance estimators become unstable due to the "Small N" problem, these experts provide a safe fallback.

*   **Tier 2: Shared Covariance Experts (The Baseline)**
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with a library of shrinkage estimators:
        *   **Oracle Approximating Shrinkage (OAS):** Proven to be optimal for Gaussianized high-dimensional data (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`).
        *   **Ledoit-Wolf:** The analytical standard (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00063`).
        *   **Fixed Shrinkage Grid:** `[0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1]` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 features) and Combined View (192 + Morphometrics).
    *   **Role:** Models correlations between features but assumes all classes share the same shape (homoscedasticity). This is the current state-of-the-art "Super-Expert."

*   **Tier 3: Class-Specific Covariance Experts (The Innovation)**
    *   **Algorithm:** **Regularized Quadratic Discriminant Analysis (QDA)** with `reg_param` grid `[0.1, 0.5, 0.9]`.
    *   **Input:** **Macro-Resolution View ONLY** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars, $D \approx 12$).
    *   **Rationale:** Standard QDA is impossible on the Global view ($D > N_{class}$). However, on the low-dimensional Macro view, we can afford to estimate **class-specific covariance matrices**. This allows the model to capture **heteroscedasticity** (e.g., "Species A has rigid shapes, Species B has highly variable shapes"), creating quadratic decision boundaries that Linear models cannot express. This exploits the physical signal (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`) without the dimensionality curse.

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts from the library to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss. This ensures that the QDA experts are included only if their non-linear signal outweighs their estimation variance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering (Multi-Resolution):**
    *   **Macro-Resolution (Physical):** Load raw binary images. Extract **Deterministic** Morphometrics: **Hu Moments** (7 invariant descriptors) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity). We explicitly avoid Monte-Carlo augmentation as it was proven redundant for stable invariants (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00089`).
    *   **Micro-Resolution (Statistical):** Load the provided 192 feature vectors.
    *   **Combined:** Concatenate Macro and Micro feature matrices.
2.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64`. This is mandatory for minimizing Log Loss near the theoretical floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Gaussianization:** Apply `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')` to all views. This forces the skewed geometric scalars and non-normal histograms to conform to the Gaussian assumption of the generative models (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:**
        *   Train GNB and LDA variants on Global/Combined views.
        *   Train Regularized QDA variants on the Macro view.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., specific shrinkage or regularization values) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Macro-Resolution features for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts (applying `PowerTransformer` and `float64` casting).
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
    *   This strategy combines the robustness of the Global OAS-LDA baseline with the specialized, non-linear capacity of the Macro-QDA expert, strictly managed by validation performance.
