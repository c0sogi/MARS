Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the distilled lessons, I propose the **Full-Rank Stratified-Rotational Precision Ensemble (FR-SRPE)**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Orthogonal Preprocessing Views" (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00109`) and "Stratified-Rotational" (Idea 63/67) concepts. It directly addresses the critical failure identified in Lesson 129: that **dimensionality reduction (bottlenecks)**—even when discriminative—degrades performance in high-dimensional, small-sample regimes compared to full-dimension regularization.

Instead of compressing semantic feature groups into low-dimensional bottlenecks to force interactions (which failed), we will construct a library of **Full-Rank Orthogonal Views**. We will apply **Stratified Rotational Alignment**—rotating each biological domain (Margin, Shape, Texture) into its own principal axes *without truncation*—to provide the Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) solver with optimally Gaussianized manifolds. This allows the model to exploit the full 192-dimensional signal with robust covariance estimation, managed by **Greedy Forward Selection**.

# Model
The solution utilizes a **Dynamic Ensemble Selection** architecture. We generate a library of probabilistic experts defined by distinct **Preprocessing Topologies** that maintain the full dimensionality of the data, avoiding information loss.

### 1. The Expert Library
All experts operate in **double precision (`float64`)** to strictly minimize numerical noise at the metric floor (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).

*   **Group A: Global Marginal Anchors (The Baseline)**
    *   **Input:** Global View (192 provided features).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer(method='yeo-johnson')`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** with **OAS** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00090`) and **Fixed Shrinkage** (`[0.001, 0.01]`, Cite `solution_lesson_node_00081`).
    *   **Role:** Preserves the state-of-the-art baseline, modeling global linear correlations with feature-wise Gaussianization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00070`).

*   **Group B: Global Rotational Experts (The Alignment)**
    *   **Input:** Global View.
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Aligns the global data with its principal axes *without* the noise-amplifying effects of Whitening (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00104`). The second PowerTransformer Gaussianizes the uncorrelated components, providing a better approximation of Multivariate Normality.

*   **Group C: Stratified Rotational Experts (The Refinement)**
    *   **Input:** Split Global View into **Margin**, **Shape**, and **Texture** subsets.
    *   **Pipeline:**
        1.  **Independent Rotation:** Apply `Power` $\rightarrow$ **PCA(whiten=False)** $\rightarrow$ `Power` to each 64-feature subset independently. Crucially, **do not truncate components** (keep all 64).
        2.  **Concatenation:** Recombine the three transformed views into a Full-Rank (192-dim) matrix.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** with Shrinkage Library.
    *   **Rationale:** Prevents high-variance feature groups (e.g., Texture) from dominating the rotation of lower-variance groups (e.g., Margin). By aligning each biological manifold independently, we ensure that the LDA solver receives a representation where *all* domains are optimally Gaussianized, without the information loss of the bottlenecks used in previous attempts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00129`, `solution_lesson_node_00109`).

*   **Group D: Physical Polynomial Experts (The Domain Signal)**
    *   **Input:** **Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics** (Hu Moments + Geometric Scalars).
        *   *Correction:* Invert images if corner mean > 0.5 to ensure leaf is foreground (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
    *   **Pipeline:** `PowerTransformer` $\rightarrow$ **PolynomialFeatures(degree=2)** $\rightarrow$ `PowerTransformer`.
    *   **Algorithm:** **LDA** (Ledoit-Wolf).
    *   **Rationale:** Captures non-linear physical constraints (e.g., $Solidity \times Eccentricity$) from raw images, treating them as an orthogonal view (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).

### 2. Selection Mechanism
*   **Greedy Forward Selection:** We employ the selection strategy from Lesson 56. We iteratively add experts to the ensemble *if and only if* they maximize the improvement in Validation Log Loss (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00056`).

# Data
1.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Global/Stratified Views:** Load 192 features; slice into Global, Margin, Shape, Texture.
    *   **Morphometric View:** Load raw binary images.
        *   **Polarity Correction:** Check corner pixels; if mean > 0.5 (white), invert image (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00106`).
        *   **Extraction:** Extract **Hu Moments** (7 invariants) and **Geometric Scalars** (Aspect Ratio, Solidity, Extent, Eccentricity) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00050`).
2.  **Preprocessing (The Rigor):**
    *   **Precision:** Cast **all** feature matrices to `float64` (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00073`).
    *   **Pipeline Application:** Apply the specific transformation sequences to generate the candidate feature matrices.
    *   **Leakage Prevention:** Transformers (Power, PCA) are fit on the training split and applied to the test split.

# Training
The training procedure rigorously separates the Selection phase from the Final Retraining phase to maximize data utilization (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).

1.  **Phase 1: Selection (Train/Val Split)**
    *   Split training data (80/20).
    *   **Train Library:** Train the full library of experts (Groups A-D $\times$ Shrinkage Estimators) on the 80% split.
    *   **Generate Predictions:** Compute `float64` probability matrices on the 20% hold-out set.
    *   **Run Selection:** Apply Greedy Forward Selection (with replacement) to determine the optimal subset of experts and their integer weights.

2.  **Phase 2: Final Retraining (Full Data)**
    *   Retrain *only* the selected experts on the **Combined Training + Validation Set**.
    *   Use the exact hyperparameters (e.g., shrinkage values) identified in Phase 1 to prevent configuration drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00028`).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:**
    *   Extract Polarity-Corrected Morphometrics for the Test set.
    *   Process the test set through the retrained pipelines of the selected experts.
2.  **Aggregation:**
    *   Compute the final submission probabilities using the **Weighted Average** determined in Phase 1.
