To optimize the model's performance, I will implement the following changes:

1.  **Model Architecture**: I will replace the Global Average Pooling (GAP) layer in `AudioMobileNet` with **Global Max Pooling (GMP)**. As noted in the lesson (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002), GAP dilutes the signal when applied to zero-padded sequences, causing the model to underperform on shorter audio clips. GMP extracts the most prominent features regardless of the padding, making it more robust for variable-length inputs.

2.  **Training Configuration**: I will increase the training duration from 5 to 30 epochs in `runfile.py` and increase the early stopping patience from 3 to 7. The previous run showed a decreasing loss trend, suggesting the model was under-trained.

3.  **Submission Logic**: I will modify `library/engine.py` to remove the automatic submission generation within the training loop. Instead, I will implement conditional submission logic in `runfile.py` to generate predictions only if the final validation LWLRAP exceeds the specified threshold of `0.6388546084534379`.
