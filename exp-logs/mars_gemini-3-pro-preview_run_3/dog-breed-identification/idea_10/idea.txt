# Proposed Solution: Fully Converged Homogeneous Stratified Ensemble

## Model
We will implement a **Homogeneous Stratified Ensemble** consisting of **5 independent instances** of the **ConvNeXt-Small** architecture.
*   **Architecture Choice:** We strictly retain `ConvNeXt-Small` as the backbone. Empirical evidence confirms this is the capacity "sweet spot" for this dataset, significantly outperforming smaller variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) while avoiding the overfitting and optimization instability observed with "Base" and "Large" variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013, Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Ensemble Structure:** Unlike previous heterogeneous attempts that diluted performance by including weaker architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022), this approach uses **Bagging**. We will train five identical high-performance models on different data splits. This maximizes the utilization of the limited dataset (~9,200 images) and reduces prediction variance, which is critical for minimizing Log Loss.

## Data
We will enforce the rigid preprocessing pipeline identified as optimal, strictly avoiding the modifications that caused regression in previous experiments.
*   **Preprocessing Pipeline:** The training data will undergo **`RandomResizedCrop`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandomHorizontalFlip`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandAugment`**.
    *   We explicitly retain `RandomResizedCrop` to prevent the overfitting caused by static resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   We ensure `RandomHorizontalFlip` is applied *before* `RandAugment` to guarantee the model learns essential spatial symmetries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Resolution:** Inputs will be fixed at **224x224**. We explicitly reject scaling to higher resolutions (e.g., 384x384) as previous experiments proved this necessitates reducing batch size, which destabilizes training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Targets:** We will use **Hard Labels** (0 or 1) and strictly avoid Label Smoothing. This ensures the training objective maximizes the Log Loss metric without artificial penalties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Training
We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** training loop.
*   **Convergence Priority:** A critical failure in previous ensemble attempts was shortening the training duration to fit a time budget, resulting in underfitted models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024). We will run the full **30-epoch** schedule for *each* of the 5 folds. Given the single-model runtime of ~42 minutes, the total training time will be approximately 3.5 hours, which is well within the 24-hour limit.
*   **Phases:** We will apply the successful **Two-Phase Transfer Learning** strategy for each fold:
    1.  **Warm-up:** Freeze the backbone and train the head for 1 epoch to align weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Unfreeze the entire network and train for 30 epochs.
*   **Optimization:** We will use a **Conservative Learning Rate** of `1e-5` for the fine-tuning phase. We explicitly reject aggressive learning rates (e.g., `1e-4`) or complex Layer-Wise Decay schemes, as these have been shown to disrupt the pre-trained weights and degrade performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Scheduler:** We will use a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler with `T_max=30` epochs to allow for deep convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss:** We will use standard **Cross-Entropy Loss** for both optimization and validation to ensure metric coupling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will generate predictions for the test set using all 5 trained models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each of the 5 models, we will predict probabilities for the original image and a **horizontally flipped** version. These two views will be averaged to produce the model's prediction.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probabilities from all 5 models. This combination of Bagging (across folds) and TTA (across views) will minimize the Log Loss.
