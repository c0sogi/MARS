Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the specific failure modes identified in the lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. The core structural innovation is the transition from a single-split training loop to a **Homogeneous Stratified K-Fold Ensemble (Bagging)**. This approach exploits the proven performance of the `ConvNeXt-Small` architecture while mitigating the variance and data scarcity issues inherent to the dataset, avoiding the pitfalls of heterogeneous ensembling observed in previous attempts.

# Proposed Solution: Homogeneous Stratified K-Fold Ensemble with ConvNeXt-Small

## Model
We will construct a **Homogeneous Ensemble** consisting of **5 independent instances** of the **ConvNeXt-Small** architecture.
*   **Architecture Choice:** We strictly retain `ConvNeXt-Small` as the sole backbone. It has been empirically proven to be the "sweet spot" for this dataset, significantly outperforming smaller variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) while avoiding the overfitting and optimization difficulties observed with "Base" and "Large" variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013, Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Ensemble Strategy:** Unlike the failed heterogeneous ensemble which diluted performance by including a weaker Swin Transformer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022), this "Bagging" approach combines five strong models trained on different subsets of data. This reduces prediction variance—critical for minimizing Log Loss—without introducing weak learners.

## Data
We will enforce the rigid preprocessing pipeline that was identified as optimal, strictly avoiding the modifications that caused regression in previous ideas.
*   **Preprocessing Pipeline:** The training data will undergo **`RandomResizedCrop`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandomHorizontalFlip`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandAugment`**.
    *   We explicitly retain `RandomResizedCrop` to prevent the overfitting caused by static resizing, ensuring the model learns robust features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   We ensure `RandomHorizontalFlip` is applied *before* `RandAugment` to guarantee the model learns essential spatial symmetries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Resolution:** Inputs will be fixed at **224x224**. We explicitly reject scaling to higher resolutions (e.g., 384x384) as this necessitates reducing batch size, which destabilizes training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Targets:** We will use **Hard Labels** (0 or 1) and strictly avoid Label Smoothing. This ensures the training objective maximizes the Log Loss metric without artificial penalties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Training
We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** training loop.
*   **Procedure:** The dataset will be split into 5 stratified folds. We will train 5 separate models, where each model trains on 4 folds (80%) and validates on the remaining fold (20%). This ensures that every image in the dataset contributes to the feature learning of the ensemble, addressing the data scarcity limitation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Strategy:** We will apply the successful **Two-Phase Transfer Learning** strategy for each fold:
    1.  **Warm-up:** Freeze the backbone and train the head for 1 epoch to align weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Unfreeze the entire network and train for 30 epochs.
*   **Optimization:** We will use a **Conservative Learning Rate** of `1e-5` for the fine-tuning phase. We explicitly reject aggressive learning rates (e.g., `1e-4`) or complex Layer-Wise Decay schemes, as these have been shown to disrupt the pre-trained weights and degrade performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Scheduler:** We will use a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler with `T_max=30` epochs to allow for deep convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will generate predictions for the test set using all 5 trained models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each of the 5 models, we will predict probabilities for the original image and a **horizontally flipped** version. These two views will be averaged to produce the model's prediction.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probabilities from all 5 models. This averaging of high-performing, low-variance models will penalize overconfidence and smooth out individual errors, directly optimizing the Multi-Class Log Loss.
