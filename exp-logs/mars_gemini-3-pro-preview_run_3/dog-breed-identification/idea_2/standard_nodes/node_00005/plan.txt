The optimization strategy focuses on upgrading the model architecture and refining the training pipeline to improve the Multi-Class Log Loss, leveraging the available computational resources (A100 GPU).

1.  **Upgrade Architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004)**: Transition from `convnext_tiny` to `convnext_small`. The "Small" variant offers higher capacity (more blocks), which is beneficial for separating fine-grained classes like dog breeds.
2.  **Increase Resolution**: Increase input image size from `224x224` to `384x384`. Fine-grained classification benefits significantly from higher resolution to capture subtle breed-specific details.
3.  **Enhance Augmentation**: Incorporate `RandAugment` into the training transforms. This improves model generalization by introducing a wider variety of geometric and photometric distortions, preventing overfitting on the larger model.
4.  **Extend Training**: Increase the fine-tuning phase from 5 to 20 epochs. The larger model and higher resolution require more iterations to converge optimally. The batch size is reduced to 32 to accommodate the larger resolution within GPU memory limits.
