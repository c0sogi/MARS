# Proposed Solution: Hierarchical Stratified Ensemble with Corrected Transfer Dynamics and Manual Soups

## Model
We will utilize the **ConvNeXt-Small** architecture, optimized for the specific capacity and initialization requirements of this task.
*   **Architecture Choice:** We strictly retain **ConvNeXt-Small** as the backbone. It has been empirically proven to be the capacity "sweet spot," significantly outperforming "Tiny" variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) while avoiding the optimization instability and overfitting observed with "Base" and "Large" variants on this limited dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013, Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Initialization:** We will initialize the model with weights pre-trained on **ImageNet-21k** (or 22k). This leverages a prior learned from ~14 million images, providing far richer feature separation for the 120 dog breeds than standard ImageNet-1k weights. While previous attempts with this initialization failed due to premature stopping (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024), we will unlock its potential by enforcing full convergence.
*   **Head:** A simple linear classification head initialized randomly.

## Data
We will enforce the rigid preprocessing pipeline identified as optimal, strictly avoiding the modifications that caused regressions in previous experiments.
*   **Preprocessing Pipeline:** The training data will undergo **`RandomResizedCrop`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandomHorizontalFlip`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandAugment`**.
    *   We explicitly retain `RandomResizedCrop` to prevent the overfitting caused by static resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   We ensure `RandomHorizontalFlip` is applied *before* `RandAugment` to guarantee the model learns essential spatial symmetries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Resolution:** Inputs will be fixed at **224x224**. We explicitly reject scaling to higher resolutions (e.g., 384x384) as this necessitates reducing batch size, which destabilizes training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Targets:** We will use **Hard Labels** (0 or 1) and strictly avoid Label Smoothing. This ensures the training objective maximizes the Log Loss metric without artificial penalties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Training
We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** loop. Within each fold, we will implement a **Corrected Two-Phase Transfer Learning Strategy** followed by **Manual Weight Averaging**.

*   **Phase 1: High-LR Head Warmup (The Fix):** We will freeze the backbone and train *only* the classification head for 1 epoch using a **high learning rate** (e.g., `1e-3`). This is critical to align the random head with the pre-trained features. Previous attempts failed because they applied a low fine-tuning rate to a random head, leading to "optimization starvation" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001, Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Phase 2: Conservative Fine-Tuning:** We will unfreeze the entire network and train for **30 epochs** using a **conservative learning rate** (e.g., `1e-5`) and a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007). We explicitly reject aggressive learning rates or complex Layer-Wise Decay, which disrupt pre-trained features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022). We also reject shortening the epoch count, as ensembling cannot compensate for underfitted models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Intra-Fold Aggregation (Manual Model Soup):** To capture the generalization benefits of weight averaging without the implementation risks of SWA wrappers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021), we will implement **Manual Weight Averaging**. For each fold, we will save checkpoints of the top 3 epochs (based on validation loss) and mathematically average their state dictionaries to create a single "Soup" model for that fold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).

## Evaluation
The inference process will use a hierarchical aggregation strategy to minimize variance at multiple levels:
1.  **Level 1 (Weight Space):** Load the **Manual Soup** model for each of the 5 folds.
2.  **Level 2 (View Space):** For each of the 5 models, perform **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** by predicting on the original image and a horizontally flipped version, averaging the results.
3.  **Level 3 (Data Space):** The final submission is the arithmetic mean of the probabilities from all 5 folds (Bagging). This ensures that predictions are robust to model initialization, view variance, and data split bias.
