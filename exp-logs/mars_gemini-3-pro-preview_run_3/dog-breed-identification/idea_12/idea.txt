This strategy structurally evolves the approach by implementing a **Hierarchical Ensemble with Greedy Model Soups**, leveraging superior pre-training priors and weight-space optimization to maximize performance within the computational constraints.

# Proposed Solution: Stratified Ensemble with ImageNet-22k Priors and Greedy Model Soups

## Model
We will utilize the **ConvNeXt-Small** architecture, but with a critical initialization upgrade.
*   **Architecture & Initialization:** We will initialize the model with weights pre-trained on **ImageNet-22k** (fine-tuned on 1k), rather than the standard ImageNet-1k used in successful baselines or the under-trained attempts in previous failures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024). This leverages a prior learned from 14 million images, providing far richer feature representations for the 120 dog breeds than the standard 1 million image subset.
*   **Capacity:** We strictly retain the "Small" variant, as it has been empirically proven to be the capacity "sweet spot," outperforming "Tiny" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) while avoiding the optimization instability of "Base/Large" on this dataset size (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013, Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

## Data
We will enforce the rigid preprocessing pipeline identified as optimal, strictly avoiding the modifications that caused regressions.
*   **Preprocessing Pipeline:** The training data will undergo **`RandomResizedCrop`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandomHorizontalFlip`** $\rightarrow$ **`RandAugment`**.
    *   We explicitly retain `RandomResizedCrop` to prevent the overfitting caused by static resizing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
    *   We ensure `RandomHorizontalFlip` is applied *before* `RandAugment` to guarantee the model learns essential spatial symmetries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Resolution:** Inputs will be fixed at **224x224**. We explicitly reject scaling to higher resolutions (e.g., 384x384) as this necessitates reducing batch size, which destabilizes training (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Targets:** We will use **Hard Labels** (0 or 1) and strictly avoid Label Smoothing. This ensures the training objective maximizes the Log Loss metric without artificial penalties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

## Training
We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** loop with a **Greedy Model Soup** construction phase.
*   **Convergence Priority:** We will run the full **30-epoch** schedule for *each* of the 5 folds. We explicitly reject shortening the training duration, as previous attempts showed that ensembling cannot compensate for underfitted models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Phases:** We will apply the **Two-Phase Transfer Learning** strategy:
    1.  **Warm-up:** Freeze the backbone and train the head for 1 epoch to align weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Unfreeze the entire network and train for 30 epochs.
*   **Optimization:** We will use a **Conservative Learning Rate** of `1e-5` for fine-tuning. We reject aggressive rates or complex Layer-Wise Decay, which disrupt pre-trained features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022). The scheduler will be **Cosine Annealing** with `T_max=30` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Structural Innovation (Greedy Model Soup):** Instead of using a single final checkpoint or a uniform average (SWA), we will implement **Greedy Weight Averaging** at the end of each fold.
    1.  We will save checkpoints for the final 10 epochs.
    2.  We will sort them by validation Log Loss.
    3.  We will iteratively add checkpoints to a "soup" (average) *only if* the addition improves the validation Log Loss.
    This constructs a single optimal model per fold that is robust and calibrated in weight space, avoiding the implementation risks of SWA wrappers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** We will generate predictions using the **Greedy Soup Models** from all 5 folds.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** For each model, we will predict probabilities for the original image and a **horizontally flipped** version. These two views will be averaged.
*   **Aggregation:** The final submission will be the arithmetic mean of the probabilities from all 5 folds. This hierarchical strategy combines **Weight-Space Ensembling** (Intra-fold Soup) and **Prediction-Space Ensembling** (Inter-fold Bagging) to minimize variance and Log Loss.
