Lesson 00012 explicitly identified the removal of `RandomResizedCrop` as the root cause for the performance degradation in the high-capacity model experiments. Geometric diversity is critical for preventing overfitting in fine-grained classification.
3.  **Training Schedule**: I will configure the training for 30 epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) to ensure full convergence, removing the 5-epoch override used in the previous rapid test.
