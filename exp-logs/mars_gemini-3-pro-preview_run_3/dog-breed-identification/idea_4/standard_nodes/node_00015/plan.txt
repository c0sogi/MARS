The solution implements a progressive resolution fine-tuning strategy using the ConvNeXt-Base architecture. The training process is divided into three phases:
1.  **Warmup (Phase 1)**: The backbone is frozen, and only the classification head is trained for 1 epoch at 224x224 resolution to align the weights.
2.  **Standard Training (Phase 2)**: The entire model is unfrozen and trained for 15 epochs at 224x224 resolution using a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
3.  **High-Resolution Fine-Tuning (Phase 3)**: The input resolution is increased to 384x384, and the model is fine-tuned for another 15 epochs with a lower learning rate to capture fine-grained details.

After training, the script evaluates the best model on the validation set, performs a failure analysis by correlating error magnitudes with image metadata (file size, dimensions), and generates a submission file with Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) if the validation metric meets the specified threshold.
