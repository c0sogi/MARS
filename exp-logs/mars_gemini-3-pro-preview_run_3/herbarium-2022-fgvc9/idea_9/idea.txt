Based on the comprehensive failure analysis of previous experiments, I propose the **Cascading Hierarchical EfficientNetV2-B0**. This strategy structurally evolves the "Parallel Multi-Task" approach into a "Cascading Conditional" architecture, while strictly adhering to the "throughput-first" principle derived from the complexity tax lessons.

# Model
We will construct a **Cascading Hierarchical EfficientNetV2-B0** to explicitly model the conditional probability of species given their genus and family.

*   **Backbone:** We will utilize **EfficientNetV2-B0**. We explicitly select this lightweight variant (~7M parameters) over the "Small" variant (~22M) used in previous failed attempts. This strategic downsizing directly addresses the "Complexity Tax" **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**, ensuring the model is computationally light enough to be trained for the extended duration required to master 15,501 classes.
*   **Pooling:** We will replace the standard Global Average Pooling with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. This learnable layer focuses on high-activation spatial features (salient regions like flowers) rather than averaging the background, preserving critical fine-grained details.
*   **Cascading Heads (Structural Innovation):** Unlike previous ideas that used independent parallel heads, we will implement **Cascading Classification Heads** to enforce taxonomic consistency and guide the model:
    1.  **Family Head:** Receives the GeM features $\rightarrow$ Predicts Family.
    2.  **Genus Head:** Receives the GeM features **concatenated with the projected Family logits** $\rightarrow$ Predicts Genus.
    3.  **Species Head:** Receives the GeM features **concatenated with the projected Genus logits** $\rightarrow$ Predicts Species.
    *   *Note:* We will use a linear bottleneck projection (e.g., reducing logits to 512 dimensions) before concatenation to prevent parameter explosion in the final head. This structure allows the Species classifier to be explicitly conditioned on the broader taxonomic context.

# Data
*   **Dataset Integrity:** We will train on the **full dataset (532,576 images)**. We strictly reject random subsampling, as reducing sample density prevents the model from learning tail classes **Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**.
*   **Progressive Resolution:** We will implement a two-stage resolution strategy to balance throughput and detail:
    *   **Phase 1 (Epochs 1-15):** 224x224 resolution. High batch size, maximum throughput for feature learning.
    *   **Phase 2 (Epochs 16-25):** 288x288 resolution. Lower batch size, focusing on resolving fine-grained textures (e.g., venation) that are lost at lower resolutions.
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply **Strong Data Augmentation** (`RandomResizedCrop`, `ShiftScaleRotate`, `ColorJitter`). This is critical for preventing overfitting during the long training regime **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
*   **Preprocessing:** Metadata will be processed to generate Family and Genus labels for the cascading heads.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** We will use a **Weighted Multi-Task Cross-Entropy Loss** with **Label Smoothing (0.1)** applied to all heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**. The total loss will be $L_{total} = L_{species} + 0.2 L_{genus} + 0.1 L_{family}$.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will use **AdamW** with the **OneCycleLR** scheduler. This combination has proven to yield the fastest convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Regularization Exclusion:** We will **exclude Mixup and CutMix**. These techniques obscure the training signal and have been shown to hinder convergence when the model capacity or training time is constrained **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** We will enable SWA during the final 5 epochs of training. This technique averages the weights of the model at different points in the trajectory, improving generalization and robustness for the final submission without adding any inference cost.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the **Species Head** at **288x288** resolution.
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will employ **Horizontal Flip TTA**, averaging the probabilities of the original and horizontally flipped images. This reduces prediction variance and improves the Macro F1 score on hard-to-classify examples.
