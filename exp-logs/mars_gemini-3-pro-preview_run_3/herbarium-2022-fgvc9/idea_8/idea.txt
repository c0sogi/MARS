Based on the lessons learned from previous experiments, I propose the **Lightweight Hierarchical GeM Network**. This strategy synthesizes the structural benefits of taxonomic modeling and fine-grained pooling with a strict adherence to the "throughput-first" principle derived from the failure analysis of heavier models.

# Model
We propose a **Hierarchical Multi-Task EfficientNetV2-B0** with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**.
*   **Backbone:** We will utilize **EfficientNetV2-B0**. This is a structural evolution from the MobileNetV3 used in the baseline, offering modern inverted residual blocks and faster training on A100 GPUs. Crucially, we select the **B0** variant (approx. 7M parameters) rather than the **Small** variant (22M parameters) used in **Idea 2** and **Idea 7**. This strategic downsizing directly addresses the "Complexity Tax" observed in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**, ensuring the model is lightweight enough to be trained for many epochs on the full dataset.
*   **Pooling:** We replace the standard Global Average Pooling with **GeM Pooling**. This learnable pooling layer focuses on high-activation spatial features (salient regions like flowers or unique leaf textures) rather than averaging the entire background, which is critical for fine-grained species distinction.
*   **Hierarchical Heads:** The model will feature **three parallel linear classification heads** branching from the GeM pooling layer, corresponding to the biological taxonomy:
    1.  **Family Head** (~270 classes, Auxiliary)
    2.  **Genus Head** (~2,564 classes, Auxiliary)
    3.  **Species Head** (15,501 classes, Primary)
    This structure enforces taxonomic consistency, using the coarser tasks to regularize the feature extractor and improve representation for tail classes without the need for complex sampling strategies.

# Data
*   **Dataset Integrity:** We will train on the **full dataset (532,576 images)**. We explicitly reject the random subsampling strategy that caused the failure in **Idea 5**, as reducing sample density destroys the model's ability to learn high-cardinality targets **Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**.
*   **Resolution:** We will standardize inputs to **256x256** pixels. This resolution offers a balanced trade-off, providing more detail than the 224px baseline while avoiding the excessive computational cost of the 384px inputs that led to undertraining in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply **Strong Data Augmentation** using `RandomResizedCrop`, `ShiftScaleRotate`, and `ColorJitter`. As demonstrated in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**, these invariances are essential for preventing overfitting during the extended training regime required for this task.
*   **Preprocessing:** We will join the training metadata with the taxonomic structure to generate the ground truth Family and Genus labels for every image.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** We will use a **Weighted Multi-Task Cross-Entropy Loss** with **Label Smoothing (0.1)** applied to all heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**. The total loss will be formulated as $L_{total} = L_{species} + 0.1 L_{genus} + 0.1 L_{family}$.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** We will utilize **AdamW** combined with the **OneCycleLR** scheduler. This configuration provided the most rapid and stable convergence in previous successful runs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Regularization Exclusion:** We will **exclude Mixup and CutMix**. Given the high number of classes and the lightweight backbone, these heavy regularizers risk obscuring the training signal and hindering convergence, as observed in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
*   **Strategy:** We will train for **15-20 epochs**. By using the lightweight B0 backbone and moderate 256px resolution, we can achieve this high epoch count within the 24-hour limit. This maximizes "Total Data Exposure," which was identified as the primary driver of performance in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004** and **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated solely from the **Species Head**.
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will employ **Horizontal Flip TTA** (averaging the probabilities of the original and horizontally flipped image). This low-cost technique reduces variance and boosts the Macro F1 score on hard-to-classify examples.
