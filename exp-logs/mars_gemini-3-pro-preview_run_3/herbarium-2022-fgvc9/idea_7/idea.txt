# Proposed Strategy: Hierarchical EfficientNetV2 with GeM Pooling and Progressive Resolution

We propose a structural evolution of the classification pipeline that synthesizes the robustness of the MobileNet baseline with the architectural advantages of hierarchical multi-task learning, while correcting the sampling and regularization errors identified in previous lessons.

## Model
We will utilize the **EfficientNetV2-Small** architecture, chosen for its superior parameter efficiency and convergence speed compared to ConvNeXt. We introduce two key structural innovations:
1.  **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling:** We will replace the standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) layer with a learnable **GeM Pooling** layer. Unlike GAP, which dilutes features by averaging the entire spatial map, GeM focuses on salient regions (high activations), which is critical for fine-grained plant classification where distinguishing features (like flower texture) are often localized.
2.  **Hierarchical Multi-Task Heads:** The GeM pooling output will feed into **three parallel linear classification heads** corresponding to the taxonomic hierarchy:
    *   **Family Head** (Auxiliary, ~270 classes)
    *   **Genus Head** (Auxiliary, ~2,500 classes)
    *   **Species Head** (Primary, 15,501 classes)
    This structure forces the backbone to learn taxonomically consistent features, using the coarser labels to regularize the learning of the fine-grained species.

## Data
*   **Dataset Integrity:** We will train on the **full dataset (532,576 images)**. We explicitly reject random subsampling, as reducing sample density destroys the model's ability to learn tail classes **Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**.
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply **Strong Data Augmentation** using `RandomResizedCrop`, `ShiftScaleRotate`, and `ColorJitter`. This strategy is essential to prevent overfitting during extended training runs **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
*   **Regularization Exclusion:** We will **exclude Mixup and CutMix**. These techniques obscure the training signal and have been proven to hinder convergence when training time is constrained **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
*   **Preprocessing:** Metadata will be processed to assign Family and Genus labels to every training image based on the provided taxonomic structure.

## Training
We will implement a **Progressive Resolution** training strategy to balance convergence speed with fine-grained precision:
*   **Stage 1 (Feature Learning):** Train for **12 epochs** at **224x224** resolution. This phase leverages high throughput to learn robust general features and converge quickly **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
*   **Stage 2 (Fine-Grained Refinement):** Fine-tune for **8 epochs** at **384x384** resolution. This higher resolution allows the model (and the GeM layer) to resolve minute details (e.g., leaf venation) necessary for species distinction.
*   **Loss Function:** We will use **Cross-Entropy Loss with Label Smoothing (0.1)** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**. The total loss will be a weighted sum: $L_{total} = L_{species} + 0.1 L_{genus} + 0.1 L_{family}$.
*   **Optimizer:** We will use **AdamW** with the **OneCycleLR** scheduler to ensure rapid super-convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.

## Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the **Species Head** at **384x384** resolution.
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will employ **Horizontal Flip TTA**, averaging the probabilities of the original and horizontally flipped images to reduce prediction variance.
