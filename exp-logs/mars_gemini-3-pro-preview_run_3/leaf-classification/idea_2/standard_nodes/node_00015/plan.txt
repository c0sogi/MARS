Based on the lessons learned (specifically `solution_lesson_node_00013` and `solution_lesson_node_00014`), the previous solution failed to meet the strict validation threshold because the continuous optimization method (`minimize_scalar`) used for ensemble weighting did not converge to exactly `0.0` or `1.0`. Even a microscopic weight assigned to the significantly weaker Logistic Regression model "contaminated" the near-perfect predictions of the Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) model, raising the log loss by orders of magnitude (from ~1e-12 to ~1e-8).

To fix this, I will replace the continuous `minimize_scalar` optimization with a discrete grid search over `alpha` values (e.g., `np.linspace(0, 1, 101)`). This ensures that the boundary conditions (pure LDA or pure LR) are explicitly evaluated, allowing the ensemble to select the pure LDA model if it is indeed superior, thereby recovering the optimal performance.
