# Proposed Solution: Hyper-Densified Independent-Component Linear Discriminant Analysis

## Model
The architecture utilizes a **Dual-Stream Early Fusion** design driven by a single **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** classifier. This design is chosen to leverage the proven linear separability of the feature space (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019) while strictly enforcing the statistical assumptions required for stable covariance estimation.

1.  **Dual-Stream Feature Extraction**:
    *   **Global Geometry Stream**: We will utilize **DINOv2 (ViT-Large)**. Its self-supervised training provides superior geometric inductive biases for binary shapes compared to supervised baselines (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Local Texture Stream**: We will utilize **ConvNeXt Large**. This modern CNN architecture captures high-frequency margin details and generates feature manifolds that are more linearly separable than ResNets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
2.  **Independent Subspace Reduction**: We will apply **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** to each visual stream *independently*, retaining **99% of the variance** for each. This ensures that the distinct manifold structures of the ViT and CNN embeddings are preserved without one dominating the other, correcting the fusion flaw identified in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052).
3.  **Global Gaussianization**: We will apply a **Quantile Transformer (Output distribution = Normal)** to **ALL** feature streams: the PCA-reduced DINOv2 embeddings, the PCA-reduced ConvNeXt embeddings, and the 192 tabular features.
    *   *Rationale*: LDA theoretically assumes class-conditional Gaussian densities. While PCA decorrelates features, it does not guarantee normality. Explicitly warping the deep feature space to a Gaussian distribution strictly satisfies the classifier's assumptions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
4.  **Early Fusion**: The Gaussianized vectors from all streams are concatenated into a single feature vector. We strictly prefer Early Fusion to leverage cross-modal correlations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
5.  **Classifier**: A single **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** model with **Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage**. We rely on this stable classifier to dominate the few-shot regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009), avoiding the data starvation caused by bagging (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).

## Data
We introduce **Hyper-Densification via Orthogonal View-Set Averaging**, a structural innovation that maximizes the training sample size ($N$) to stabilize the High-Dimension ($P$) covariance estimation ($N \gg P$).

1.  **Multi-View Extraction**: For every image in the dataset (Train and Test), we extract features for **36 equidistant rotations** (0°, 10°, 20°, ..., 350°).
2.  **Training Topology (Hyper-Densification)**: For the training set, we generate **9 distinct "Orthogonal Centroids"** per image by averaging mutually exclusive sets of 4 orthogonal views:
    *   Centroid 1: Average of {0°, 90°, 180°, 270°}
    *   Centroid 2: Average of {10°, 100°, 190°, 280°}
    *   ...
    *   Centroid 9: Average of {80°, 170°, 260°, 350°}
    *   *Impact*: This increases the effective training sample size from $N$ to **$9N$** (approx. 6,300 samples). Crucially, each centroid has the exact same variance profile ($\sigma^2/4$) as the inference samples, satisfying the "Train on the Aggregation" principle (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049, solution_lesson_node_00053).
3.  **Inference Topology**: For validation and test sets, we generate a **single canonical centroid** per image (Centroid 1: Average of {0°, 90°, 180°, 270°}).

## Training
We utilize **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation (K=10)** to construct an ensemble, ensuring robustness against "lucky split" variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).

1.  **Feature Caching**: Execute the 36-view extraction pipeline once for the entire dataset using the GPU (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
2.  **Ensemble Construction**:
    *   Split the unique image IDs into 10 stratified folds.
    *   For each fold $k$:
        *   **Expand**: Construct the **Hyper-Densified Training Set** ($9 \times$ samples) from the training IDs.
        *   **Fit Transformers**: Fit the Independent PCAs and the Global Quantile Transformer on this densified training set.
        *   **Train Classifier**: Train the LDA model (Ledoit-Wolf) on the transformed densified set.
        *   **Store**: Save the pipeline as member $k$ of the ensemble.

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: For each test image:
    *   Generate the canonical centroid (Average of 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°) for both visual streams.
    *   Pass these centroids and the raw tabular features through all 10 saved ensemble pipelines.
    *   Each pipeline applies its specific Independent PCA, Global Quantile transformation, and LDA prediction.
2.  **Aggregation**: Compute the **arithmetic mean** of the probability vectors from all 10 models. This averaging smooths out residual decision boundary noise while utilizing 100% of the available training data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
