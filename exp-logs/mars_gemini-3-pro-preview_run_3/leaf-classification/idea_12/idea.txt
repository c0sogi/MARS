# Proposed Solution: Cross-Validated Manifold-Expanded Linear Discriminant Ensemble

## Model
The proposed architecture is a **Cross-Validated Manifold-Expanded Linear Discriminant Ensemble**. This design synthesizes the "View-Expanded" training strategy (to stabilize covariance estimation) with a "Cross-Validation Ensemble" (to resolve split instability), strictly adhering to the "Early Fusion" and "LDA Dominance" principles established in the lessons.

1.  **Dual-Stream Feature Extraction**:
    *   **Global Geometry Stream**: We will utilize **DINOv2 (ViT-Large)** to capture self-supervised geometric priors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Local Texture Stream**: We will utilize **ConvNeXt Large** to capture high-frequency margin details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
2.  **Early Fusion**: The embeddings from both backbones will be concatenated with the **Quantile-Transformed** tabular features. This **Early Fusion** strategy is preferred over late fusion to leverage cross-modal correlations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
3.  **Dimensionality Reduction**: **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** will be applied to the fused vector, retaining **99% of the variance** to preserve discriminative tail signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
4.  **Classifier**: The core classifier is an **Ensemble of $K$ Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) models** (with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage). This replaces the single-model approach (which is prone to "lucky split" bias, Cite solution_lesson_node_00033) and the bagging approach (which causes data starvation, Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).

## Data
The core innovation is the **Asymmetric Data Topology** used to decouple training stability from inference precision:
1.  **Multi-View Extraction**: For every image in the dataset (Train and Test), we generate 4 canonical rotated views (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°) and extract embeddings using both backbones.
2.  **Training Topology (Manifold Expansion)**: For the training data, we **keep the 4 views as distinct samples** and replicate the corresponding tabular features 4 times. This artificially quadruples the sample size ($N \to 4N$), providing the LDA classifier with a dense "cloud" of points per class. This directly stabilizes the covariance matrix estimation in the HDLSS regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
3.  **Inference Topology (Centroid Consolidation)**: For the test data (and validation folds), we compute the **element-wise average** of the 4 view embeddings to produce a single **centroid** vector. This enforces strict rotation invariance during prediction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

## Training
We will employ **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation (K=10)** to construct the ensemble:
1.  **Fold Splitting**: The dataset is split into 10 folds based on unique image IDs, ensuring no leakage of views between folds.
2.  **Pipeline Fitting (Per Fold)**: For each fold $k$:
    *   **Expansion**: Construct the **Expanded Training Set** using the 4 views of the training IDs.
    *   **Transformation**: Fit the **Quantile Transformer** (on tabular data) and **PCA** (on fused data) solely on this expanded training set.
    *   **Classification**: Train an **LDA model with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage** on the transformed, expanded training data.
    *   **Storage**: Save the fitted pipeline (Transformers + LDA) as member $k$ of the ensemble.
This strategy ensures that each LDA model learns from the full rotational manifold of the training classes while being validated on the clean centroids of the hold-out set.

## Evaluation
1.  **Centroid Inference**: For each test image, generate the **centroid feature vector** (average of 4 views + transformed tabular).
2.  **Ensemble Prediction**: Pass the centroid vector through all $K=10$ saved pipelines. Each pipeline will apply its specific PCA projection and LDA classifier.
3.  **Aggregation**: Compute the **arithmetic mean** of the probability vectors from all $K$ models. This averaging smooths out the bimodal stability issues observed in single-split experiments (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033) while utilizing 100% of the training data.
4.  **Formatting**: Apply probability clipping ($[10^{-15}, 1-10^{-15}]$) and format the submission CSV.
