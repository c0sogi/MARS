# Proposed Solution: Moment-Aligned Selective-Topology Linear Discriminant Analysis

## Model
The architecture utilizes a **Dual-Stream Early Fusion** design driven by a single **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** classifier. The design is optimized to preserve the natural linear separability of deep features while enforcing strict statistical compliance for tabular data.

1.  **Dual-Stream Feature Extraction**:
    *   **Global Geometry Stream**: We will utilize **DINOv2 (ViT-Large)** to capture self-supervised geometric priors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Local Texture Stream**: We will utilize **ConvNeXt Large** to capture high-frequency margin details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
2.  **Selective Feature Topology**: We apply distinct geometric transformations to visual and tabular streams to strictly preserve their optimal discriminative properties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071):
    *   **Visual Streams (Linear Preservation)**: We apply **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** to DINOv2 and ConvNeXt streams *independently*, retaining **99% of the variance** for each (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052). Crucially, we do **NOT** apply Quantile Transformation to these embeddings, preserving their natural linear separability.
    *   **Tabular Stream (Gaussian Alignment)**: We apply a **Quantile Transformer (Output distribution = Normal)** *exclusively* to the 192 engineered tabular features. This aligns the arbitrary distributions of the handcrafted histograms with the Gaussian assumption of the LDA classifier.
3.  **Early Fusion**: The Linear PCA-reduced visual embeddings and the Non-Linear Gaussianized tabular features are concatenated into a single feature vector (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
4.  **Classifier**: A single **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** model with **Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage**. We rely on this stable classifier to dominate the few-shot regime (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).

## Data
We introduce **Moment-Aligned Canonical Anchoring**, a preprocessing strategy that structurally reduces intra-class variance before feature extraction, eliminating the need for excessive "densification" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).

1.  **Moment-Based Alignment (Innovation)**: Before feature extraction, we compute the second-order image moments of each binary leaf mask to determine its principal axis (orientation). We rotate the image such that its principal axis aligns vertically.
    *   *Rationale*: Lesson 00065 established that consistent alignment yields compact, linearly separable clusters. By explicitly aligning all samples, we remove the random rotational noise present in the raw dataset, ensuring that the "canonical" view is semantically consistent across all samples (e.g., "tip-up").
2.  **Sparse Orthogonal Anchoring**: From the aligned image, we extract features for **4 orthogonal views** (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°) and compute the **element-wise average** (centroid).
    *   *Impact*: This creates a single, highly robust representation per image. Because the image is pre-aligned, the average of {0, 90, 180, 270} is invariant to 90° steps and robust to 180° ambiguity (since 0° and 180° are averaged), capturing the leaf's "Platonic ideal" without the computational cost of 12+ views (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).
3.  **Training/Test Topology**: We apply this alignment and averaging process to **both** Training and Test data. This ensures we "Train on the Aggregation" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049) using the most compact manifold representation possible.

## Training
We utilize **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation (K=10)** to construct an ensemble, ensuring robustness against "lucky split" variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).

1.  **Feature Caching**: Execute the alignment and 4-view extraction pipeline once for the entire dataset using the GPU.
2.  **Ensemble Construction**:
    *   Split the unique image IDs into 10 stratified folds.
    *   For each fold $k$:
        *   **Fit Transformers**: Fit the Independent PCAs (on visual cols) and Quantile Transformer (on tabular cols) on the training fold.
        *   **Train Classifier**: Train the LDA model (Ledoit-Wolf) on the transformed training fold.
        *   **Store**: Save the pipeline as member $k$ of the ensemble.

## Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: For each test image:
    *   Perform Moment-Based Alignment.
    *   Generate the Canonical Centroid (Average of 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°).
    *   Pass the centroid through all 10 saved ensemble pipelines.
2.  **Aggregation**: Compute the **arithmetic mean** of the probability vectors from all 10 K-Fold models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
