The issue is caused by **Data Leakage** where the hold-out validation set is merged with the training set during the Cross-Validation process. This allows the models to train on samples that are later used for the final performance assessment, resulting in an invalidly optimistic metric. To fix this, I will modify `library/workflow.py` and `library/model_builder.py` to strictly isolate the validation set, ensuring that the Cross-Validation loop operates solely on the training data. This adheres to the principle of keeping hold-out sets completely unseen during training (Cite debug_lesson_14).
