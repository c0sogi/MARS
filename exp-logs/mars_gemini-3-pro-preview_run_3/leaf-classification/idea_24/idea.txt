# Proposed Solution: Stratified Orthogonal-Expert Ensemble with Selective Feature Topology

## Model
The architecture utilizes a **Dual-Stream Early Fusion** design driven by a bank of **Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)** experts. This approach replaces the single "pooled" LDA classifier with a specialized multi-expert system to maintain linear separability across the full rotational manifold.

1.  **Dual-Stream Feature Extraction**:
    *   **Global Geometry Stream**: We will utilize **DINOv2 (ViT-Large)** to capture self-supervised geometric priors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Local Texture Stream**: We will utilize **ConvNeXt Large** to capture high-frequency margin details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
2.  **Selective Feature Topology**: We apply distinct transformations to visual and tabular streams to strictly preserve their optimal geometric properties (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071):
    *   **Visual Streams**: Apply **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** to DINOv2 and ConvNeXt streams *independently*, retaining **99% of the variance** for each (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052). Crucially, we do **NOT** apply Quantile Transformation to these embeddings, preserving their natural linear separability.
    *   **Tabular Stream**: Apply a **Quantile Transformer (Output distribution = Normal)** *exclusively* to the 192 engineered tabular features to align them with the Gaussian assumption of the LDA classifiers.
3.  **Early Fusion**: The Linear PCA-reduced visual embeddings and the Non-Linear Gaussianized tabular features are concatenated into a single feature vector (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039).
4.  **Classifier Bank (Structural Innovation)**: Instead of training a single LDA on a pooled dataset of all rotations (which destroys separability at high view counts, Cite solution_lesson_node_00065), we deploy a bank of **9 Independent LDA Experts** (with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage).
    *   Each expert is specialized for a specific orthogonal subspace (e.g., Expert 1 learns from $0^\circ$-aligned centroids, Expert 2 from $10^\circ$-aligned, etc.).
    *   This ensures that each LDA model sees a **unimodal, linearly separable** distribution, avoiding the complex "ring" manifolds created by pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).

## Data
We employ **Orthogonal View-Set Partitioning** to generate specialized training data for each expert, maximizing the utilization of the rotational manifold without introducing noise.

1.  **Multi-View Extraction**: For every image (Train and Test), we extract features for **36 equidistant rotations** (0°, 10°, 20°, ..., 350°).
2.  **Orthogonal Centroid Generation**: We partition these views into 9 mutually exclusive sets per image (Set 1: {0°, 90°, 180°, 270°}; Set 2: {10°, 100°, ...}; etc.) and compute the **element-wise average** (centroid) for each set (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).
3.  **Expert Allocation**:
    *   **Expert 1** trains *only* on Centroid 1 (and replicated tabular features).
    *   **Expert 2** trains *only* on Centroid 2.
    *   ...
    *   **Expert 9** trains *only* on Centroid 9.
    *   This maintains the effective sample size $N$ for each expert (avoiding data starvation, Cite solution_lesson_node_00028) while ensuring strict variance alignment (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).

## Training
We utilize **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation (K=10)** to construct the final ensemble, ensuring robustness against "lucky split" variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).

1.  **Feature Caching**: Execute the 36-view extraction pipeline once for the entire dataset using the GPU (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
2.  **Ensemble Construction**:
    *   Split unique image IDs into 10 stratified folds.
    *   For each fold, train the **9-Expert Bank**.
    *   Fit the Selective Transformers (Independent PCA, Tabular QT) and the LDA classifier for Expert $k$ using exclusively the data from Centroid $k$ of the training IDs.

## Evaluation
1.  **Multi-Expert Inference**: For each test image:
    *   Generate all 9 Orthogonal Centroids.
    *   Pass Centroid $k$ through the pipeline of Expert $k$ (Specific PCA projection $\to$ LDA prediction).
    *   This generates 9 probability vectors per fold, each derived from a model specialized for that specific alignment.
2.  **Aggregation**:
    *   Compute the **arithmetic mean** of the 9 probability vectors to produce the fold prediction.
    *   Compute the **arithmetic mean** across the 10 folds to produce the final submission (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   This strategy leverages the robustness of 36 views without suffering from the manifold complexity penalties observed in previous attempts.
