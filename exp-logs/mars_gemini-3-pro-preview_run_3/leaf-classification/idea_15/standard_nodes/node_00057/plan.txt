The error arises because the `StratifiedKFold` cross-validator is configured with 10 splits (`N_FOLDS = 10`), but the dataset contains classes with as few as 5 samples. Stratified splitting requires that the number of folds be less than or equal to the minimum number of samples per class to ensure every fold receives at least one instance of each class. To resolve this, I will reduce `N_FOLDS` to 5 in `library/config.py`, satisfying the constraint $K \le N_{min}$ (Cite debug_lesson_9).
