Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and the evolution of previous ideas, I propose the **Adaptive High-Fidelity SWA Ensemble** strategy.

This solution introduces a structural evolution in the training paradigm: **Adaptive Curriculum Learning** combined with **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**. While previous iterations relied on fixed-epoch schedules (which risk premature switching or wasted computation, Cite solution_lesson_node_00054), this strategy dynamically triggers the transition from structural learning to metric fine-tuning based on convergence detection. Furthermore, it integrates SWA to explicitly target the "broad" minima required for generalization in the complex Lovasz loss landscape.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with SE-ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **SE-ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. This explicitly retains the Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks, which were proven critical for capturing seismic textures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049), correcting the backbone downgrade in Idea 15.
*   **Decoder:**
    *   **Structure:** **U-Net++** to bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
    *   **Capacity:** **Lightweight channel widths** (starting at 16 filters) to act as a structural regularizer against overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Output:** Terminate at **Stride 2** (1/2 resolution) and use bilinear upsampling for the final output to avoid artifacts at the noisy full resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node to suppress background noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth (`[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`), preserving the pre-trained ImageNet weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Consolidated Stratification:** I will merge the provided `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Splitting** based on salt coverage. This ensures the model learns from 100% of the available data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Uniform Normalization:** Input channels will be scaled uniformly to `[0, 1]`. I will explicitly reject ImageNet normalization to preserve signal consistency across the multiplexed channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This provides regularization without destructive interpolation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Target Normalization:** Ground truth masks will be cast to **float [0, 1]** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Precision:** **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to enable a batch size of **64** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Total Duration:** Budget for **80+ epochs** per fold to ensure deep convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

**Adaptive Loss Curriculum (Structural Innovation):**
Instead of fixed epoch transitions, I will implement a **Metric-Triggered Phase Switch**:
1.  **Phase 1 (Structural Warmup):**
    *   **Loss:** **BCE + Dice**.
    *   **Scope:** **Deep Supervision Active** with **Equal Weights (1.0)** for all heads (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    *   **Trigger:** Train until the validation BCE+Dice loss plateaus (e.g., patience of 5 epochs). This ensures the structural representation is fully converged before fine-tuning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).
2.  **Phase 2 (Metric Fine-Tuning):**
    *   **Transition:** Upon Phase 1 plateau, load the best checkpoint, switch loss, and reset the learning rate to a **Conservative Value ($\le 1e-4$)** to prevent gradient shock (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Loss:** **Lovasz-Hinge Loss** calculated **per-image** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Scope:** **Deep Supervision Disabled** (Loss applied only to the final head) to prevent auxiliary gradients from conflicting with the global metric optimization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046).

**Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):**
*   In the final 20% of the training epochs (during Phase 2), I will implement **SWA**. This averages the weights of the model at different points in the Lovasz loss trajectory, finding a broader, more robust minimum that generalizes better than any single point estimate.

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** Validation predictions will be cropped to **101x101** *before* metric calculation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Threshold Optimization:** I will aggregate **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from all 5 folds and perform a single threshold sweep to identify the global probability threshold that maximizes the mAP (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Ensemble Prediction:** The final submission will be the average of the probability maps from all 5 SWA-averaged models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Each ensemble member will apply TTA (averaging original and horizontally flipped inputs) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
