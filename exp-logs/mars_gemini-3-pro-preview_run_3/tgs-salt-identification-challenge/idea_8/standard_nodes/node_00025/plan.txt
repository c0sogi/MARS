2.  **Dynamic Threshold Optimization (Cite Lesson 00011)**: I will modify the validation routine to perform a threshold sweep (0.3 to 0.75) at the end of each epoch. 3.  **Configuration Updates**: I will update `EPOCHS` to 80 in `config.py` and remove the override in `runfile.py`.

The estimated runtime for 80 epochs on a single fold is approximately 11-15 minutes, well within the 4-hour limit.
