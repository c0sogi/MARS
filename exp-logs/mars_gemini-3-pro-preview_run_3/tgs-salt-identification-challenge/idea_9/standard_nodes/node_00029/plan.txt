2.  **Extended Convergence for Lovasz Loss:** I will increase the total epochs to 80 and the warmup phase to 20 epochs. This provides the necessary runway for the Lovasz-Hinge loss to effectively optimize the IoU metric after the initial BCE+Dice warmup (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00020`).
3.  **Dynamic Threshold Validation:** I will modify the validation loop to perform a threshold sweep (0.3 to 0.75) at the end of each epoch. This ensures that model checkpointing and learning rate scheduling are driven by the model's maximum potential discriminative power, rather than its calibration at a fixed 0.5 threshold (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`).
