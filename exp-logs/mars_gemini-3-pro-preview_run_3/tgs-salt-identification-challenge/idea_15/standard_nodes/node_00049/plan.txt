The strategy focuses on maximizing the performance of a single model by extending its training duration and refining the optimization process, rather than training an ensemble of under-converged models.

1.  **Single-Fold Deep Training (Cite Lesson 00024)**: The training loop is modified to run only a single fold (Fold 0) for 80 epochs, instead of 5 folds for 15 epochs. This ensures the model fully converges, particularly for the Lovasz-Hinge loss which requires a long fine-tuning phase (Cite Lesson 00020).
2.  **Deep Supervision Averaging (Cite Lesson 00038)**: The loss calculation in `engine.py` is updated to average the auxiliary losses from Deep Supervision heads rather than summing them. This normalizes the gradient scale and prevents optimization instability.
3.  **Dynamic Threshold Validation (Cite Lesson 00011)**: The `validate_one_epoch` method is rewritten to perform a sweep of probability thresholds (0.3 to 0.75) and report the maximum mAP. This ensures that the learning rate scheduler and early stopping react to the model's optimal performance capability.
