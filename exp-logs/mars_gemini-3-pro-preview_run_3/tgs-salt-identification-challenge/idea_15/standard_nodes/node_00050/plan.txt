Based on the analysis of the previous solution and the provided lessons, I will implement the following optimizations:

1.  **Backbone Upgrade**: I will replace the `resnext50_32x4d` encoder with `seresnext50_32x4d` (Squeeze-and-Excitation ResNeXt). Lesson 00049 indicates that SE blocks are critical for distinguishing salt textures, and removing them caused a performance regression.
2.  **Depth Over Width (Single Fold Convergence)**: Instead of training 5 folds for 15 epochs each (which led to under-convergence in the Lovasz phase), I will train a **single fold (Fold 0) for 60 epochs**. Lesson 00024 and Lesson 00020 emphasize that surrogate losses like Lovasz-Hinge require a long fine-tuning runway to maximize IoU, and a single converged model often outperforms an ensemble of under-trained models under time constraints.
3.  **Deep Supervision Normalization**: I will modify the loss calculation to **average** the Deep Supervision losses instead of summing them. Lesson 00038 highlights that summing auxiliary losses can destabilize the optimization scale, whereas averaging acts as a proper regularizer.
