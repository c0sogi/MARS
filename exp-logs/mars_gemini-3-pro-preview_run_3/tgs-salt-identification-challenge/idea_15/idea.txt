Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons" and "Previous Ideas," I propose the **Dynamic Deep Supervision Stratified Ensemble** strategy.

This solution introduces a structural evolution in the training paradigm by implementing a **Dynamic Deep Supervision (DDS)** strategy. While previous iterations successfully utilized U-Net++ and Deep Supervision, they failed to recognize that auxiliary losses become detrimental when optimizing global metric surrogates (Lesson 46). This strategy corrects that flaw while leveraging the full computational budget to train a high-fidelity ensemble on the consolidated dataset, explicitly rejecting the unstable Semi-Supervised approaches (Lesson 45).

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. This architecture provides superior texture extraction compared to ResNet34 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) and avoids the redundancy of encoder-side attention (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Decoder Structure:**
    *   **Nested Architecture:** I will use **U-Net++** to bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
    *   **Lightweight Capacity:** The decoder will use **lightweight channel widths** (starting at 16 filters) to act as a structural regularizer against overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Stride 2 Output:** The decoder will terminate at **Stride 2** (1/2 resolution) and use bilinear upsampling for the final output. This prevents artifacts caused by learnable operations at the noisy full resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node to suppress background noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth. The input tensor will be constructed as `[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`, preserving the pre-trained ImageNet weights of the 3-channel encoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Dataset Consolidation:** I will merge the provided `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` into a single master dataset and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Splitting** based on salt coverage. This ensures the model learns from 100% of the available data, correcting the "recursive splitting" error (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Uniform Normalization:** Input channels will be scaled uniformly to `[0, 1]`. I will explicitly reject ImageNet normalization to preserve signal consistency across the multiplexed channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This provides necessary regularization without introducing destructive interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Target Normalization:** Ground truth masks will be explicitly cast to **float [0, 1]** to prevent numerical divergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Full Convergence Ensemble:** I will train 5 separate models (one per fold) for **80 epochs** each. This prioritizes the "depth" of convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024) while mitigating variance via ensembling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Precision:** I will use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to enable a batch size of **64** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).

**Dynamic Deep Supervision (DDS) Curriculum (Structural Innovation):**
1.  **Phase 1: Structural Warm-up (Epochs 1-20):**
    *   **Loss:** **BCE + Dice Loss**.
    *   **Scope:** **Deep Supervision Active.** I will calculate the loss at all nested decoder nodes (L1, L2, L3, L4) with **Equal Weights (1.0)**. This enforces multi-scale feature consistency during the initial feature learning phase (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
2.  **Phase 2: Metric Fine-tuning (Epochs 21-80):**
    *   **Loss:** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (calculated per-image, Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Scope:** **Deep Supervision Disabled.** I will calculate the loss **only on the final output head**. This prevents intermediate layers (which lack full context) from introducing conflicting gradients when optimizing the complex global IoU metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046).
    *   **Stability:** I will enforce a **Conservative Learning Rate ($\le 1e-4$)** during this switch to prevent gradient shock (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

**Scheduling:**
*   I will use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP** (derived from a dynamic threshold sweep), ensuring the scheduler reacts to discriminative power rather than calibration drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** During validation, predictions will be cropped back to **101x101** *before* metric calculation to eliminate padding noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Threshold Optimization:** I will aggregate the **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from all 5 folds and perform a single threshold sweep to identify the global probability threshold that maximizes the mAP (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Ensemble Prediction:** The final submission will be the average of the probability maps from all 5 trained models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Each ensemble member will apply TTA (averaging original and horizontally flipped inputs) to further reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
