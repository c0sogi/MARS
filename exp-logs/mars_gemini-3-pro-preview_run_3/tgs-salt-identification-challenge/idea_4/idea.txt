Based on the empirical findings from the provided lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a structural evolution to the architecture (U-Net++) to better capture fine-grained seismic features while rigorously adhering to the training protocols proven to maximize the specific IoU metric.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Structural Innovation (U-Net++):** I will replace the standard U-Net decoder with a **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net)** structure. While previous comparisons showed little difference between LinkNet and U-Net (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004), U-Net++ introduces nested, dense skip connections. These connections bridge the semantic gap between the encoder and decoder feature maps more effectively than simple concatenation, facilitating better gradient flow and more precise boundary delineation for small salt bodies.
*   **Backbone Retention:** I will retain the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. This architecture provides the necessary capacity to capture complex seismic textures, significantly outperforming shallower ResNet18/34 variants (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Attention Mechanism:** I will integrate **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules within the decoder nodes. This attention mechanism is critical for recalibrating features to suppress background noise, specifically addressing the "empty mask" failure mode (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Modification:** The first convolutional layer will be modified to accept 4 channels (3 RGB + 1 Depth) to fully utilize the provided depth context.

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Padding Strategy:** Input images (101x101) will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding**. This preserves texture statistics at the boundaries better than zero padding, which is crucial for the convolution operations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Depth Fusion:** The scalar depth variable (`z`) will be normalized and fused as a 4th spatial channel, ensuring the model has local texture and global depth context simultaneously.
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will strictly limit augmentations to **Horizontal Flips** and **Random Brightness/Contrast**. I will explicitly **exclude** aggressive geometric transformations (e.g., GridDistortion, ShiftScaleRotate), as these have been proven to introduce interpolation artifacts that degrade performance on this low-resolution, physics-constrained data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

### Training
**Loss Curriculum & Scheduling:**
*   **Two-Stage Loss:** I will implement the proven loss curriculum:
    1.  **Warm-up:** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** to establish stable convergence and robust feature representations.
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** to directly optimize the Jaccard index and handle class imbalance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Synchronization Fix:** Crucially, I will schedule the switch to Lovász-Hinge to occur **early** (e.g., at epoch 15 of a 50+ epoch run) and ensure that early stopping is either disabled during the first stage or has a patience counter that resets upon the loss switch. This prevents the training from terminating prematurely before the fine-tuning phase—a critical failure mode identified in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** I will generate predictions for both the original image and a horizontally flipped version, averaging the outputs. This effectively reduces prediction variance and boosts the metric without retraining (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Threshold Optimization:** I will not use a default 0.5 probability threshold. Instead, I will perform a post-inference sweep on the validation set to identify the optimal binarization threshold that maximizes the mAP. This step is essential for filtering out low-confidence false positives in empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
