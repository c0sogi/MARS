The `RuntimeError` in `library/model.py` occurs because the U-Net++ decoder nodes in the first column (`convX_1`) are initialized expecting inputs from other decoder nodes (using `self.filters`), but they actually receive inputs directly from the encoder (which has `enc_channels`). Specifically, `conv3_1` expects `enc_channels[3] + self.filters[4]` channels, but receives `enc_channels[3] + enc_channels[4]` channels. This mismatch applies to `conv2_1`, `conv1_1`, and `conv0_1` as well. I will fix this by updating the initialization to use `enc_channels` for the upsampled inputs in these specific layers. Cite debug_lesson_5.
