Based on the comprehensive failure analysis of previous ensemble attempts and the specific empirical findings regarding optimization stability, I propose the **Stabilized High-Fidelity Stratified Ensemble** strategy.

This solution introduces a structural evolution from the single-model paradigm to a **Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation Pipeline**. While previous ideas (8, 9, 10) attempted this, they failed due to specific architectural and optimization flaws (data starvation, normalization errors, gradient shock, and supervision weighting). This strategy synthesizes the lessons to correct these structural defects, leveraging the full 24-hour compute budget to maximize the "depth" of convergence for every ensemble member.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. It provides superior texture extraction compared to ResNet34 and avoids the redundancy of encoder-side attention (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Decoder:** The decoder will be a **U-Net++** structure. The nested dense skip pathways bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder more effectively than standard U-Net (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Deep Supervision Strategy (Critical Correction):** I will enable Deep Supervision but explicitly assign **Equal Weights (1.0)** to all auxiliary output heads (L1, L2, L3, L4). Previous attempts failed by assigning low weights (e.g., 0.1) to auxiliary heads, which diminished the regularization benefit of the nested architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node to suppress background noise in empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Structure:** I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth, constructing the input as `[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`. This preserves the pre-trained ImageNet weights of the 3-channel encoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Dataset Consolidation:** I will merge `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` into a single master dataset and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Splitting** based on salt coverage. This ensures the model learns from 100% of the available data, correcting the "recursive splitting" error of previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Uniform Input Normalization (Critical Correction):** I will apply **Uniform Scaling** (dividing by 255.0 to range `[0, 1]`) to all three input channels. I will explicitly reject ImageNet normalization, which was proven to break signal consistency when applied to multiplexed grayscale/depth channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This provides regularization without introducing destructive interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Training
**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Full Convergence Schedule:** Each of the 5 folds will be trained for **80 epochs**. I will prioritize the "depth" of convergence, rejecting the truncated schedules (e.g., 40 epochs) that caused previous ensembles to underperform single models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP):** I will use AMP to enable a batch size of **64**, stabilizing Batch Normalization statistics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Gradient-Stabilized Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up (Epochs 1-15):** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** to establish convergence.
    2.  **Fine-tuning (Epochs 16-80):** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (calculated per-image, Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Shock Mitigation (Critical Correction):** I will strictly enforce a **Conservative Learning Rate ($\le 1e-4$)** during the loss switch. Previous attempts failed due to "Gradient Shock" caused by high learning rates (5e-4) when transitioning to the non-convex Lovász landscape (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
*   **Scheduling:** I will use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP** (derived from a dynamic threshold sweep), ensuring the scheduler reacts to discriminative power rather than calibration drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011, solution_lesson_node_00013).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** During validation, predictions will be cropped back to **101x101** *before* metric calculation to eliminate padding noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Threshold Optimization:** I will aggregate the **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from all 5 folds and perform a single threshold sweep to identify the global probability threshold that maximizes the mAP (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Ensemble Prediction:** The final submission will be the average of the probability maps from all 5 models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Each ensemble member will apply TTA (averaging original and horizontally flipped inputs) to further reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
