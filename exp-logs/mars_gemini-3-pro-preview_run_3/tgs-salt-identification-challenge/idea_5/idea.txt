This solution structurally evolves the architecture to **U-Net++ with Deep Supervision**, correcting the implementation flaws identified in prior attempts, while rigorously adhering to the data and training protocols proven to maximize the specific IoU metric.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder, scSE Attention, and Deep Supervision.**
*   **Structural Innovation (U-Net++ & Deep Supervision):** I will replace the standard U-Net decoder with a **U-Net++** architecture. Unlike the standard U-Net used in the "Current Best" solution, U-Net++ employs nested, dense skip connections that bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder feature maps. Crucially, I will enable **Deep Supervision**, calculating the loss at multiple decoder outputs (nodes L1, L2, L3, L4). This forces intermediate layers to learn semantically discriminative features early in training, improving gradient flow and convergence speed.
*   **Backbone Retention:** I will retain the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder, as it has been proven to provide the necessary capacity for complex seismic textures, significantly outperforming ResNet34 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Attention Mechanism:** I will integrate **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules within the decoder nodes. This mechanism is essential for recalibrating features to suppress background noise in "empty" masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Modification:** The first convolutional layer will be modified to accept **4 channels** (3 RGB + 1 Depth) to fully utilize the depth context without breaking pretrained weight initialization (weights for the new channel can be initialized as the mean of RGB weights).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Padding Strategy:** Input images (101x101) will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding**. This preserves texture statistics at the boundaries better than zero padding (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Target Normalization (Critical Fix):** I will explicitly ensure that ground truth masks are normalized to the **[0, 1]** range (float) before being passed to the loss function. This corrects the catastrophic "negative infinity loss" failure observed when using raw [0, 255] values with U-Net++ in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will strictly limit augmentations to **Horizontal Flips** and **Random Brightness/Contrast**. I will explicitly **exclude** aggressive geometric transformations (e.g., GridDistortion, ShiftScaleRotate), as these introduce interpolation artifacts that degrade performance on low-resolution seismic data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).

### Training
**Loss Curriculum & Scheduling:**
*   **Two-Stage Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up:** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** (applied to all Deep Supervision outputs) to establish stable convergence.
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (applied only to the final output) to directly optimize the Jaccard index and handle class imbalance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Synchronization:** The switch to Lovász-Hinge will be scheduled **early** (e.g., epoch 15 of 50) to ensure the fine-tuning phase begins well before any early stopping mechanism can terminate the run (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Adaptive Scheduling:** I will use the **ReduceLROnPlateau** scheduler rather than CosineAnnealing. Adaptive scheduling is more effective for fine-tuning with surrogate losses like Lovász, as it reacts to metric stagnation by forcing the model into sharper minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Metric-Driven Optimization:** The scheduler and model checkpointing will be driven by the **Optimized mAP** (calculated via a threshold sweep at the end of each epoch), not the mAP at a fixed 0.5 threshold. This ensures we select models with the best discriminative power, even if their calibration drifts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** I will generate predictions for both the original image and a horizontally flipped version, averaging the outputs. This reduces variance and boosts the metric without retraining (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Threshold Optimization:** I will not use a default 0.5 probability threshold. Instead, I will perform a post-inference sweep on the validation set to identify the single optimal binarization threshold that maximizes the mAP. This is critical for filtering out low-confidence false positives in empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
