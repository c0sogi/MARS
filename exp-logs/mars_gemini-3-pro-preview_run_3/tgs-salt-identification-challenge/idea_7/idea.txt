Based on the empirical insights from the provided lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution retains the structural benefits of the U-Net++ architecture identified in previous iterations but introduces a **High-Fidelity Training Regime** characterized by Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP), scaled batch sizes, and conservative geometric regularization. This approach directly addresses the optimization stability and generalization issues found in earlier attempts.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder, scSE Attention, and Deep Supervision.**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. Previous experiments demonstrated that this backbone outperforms ResNet34, and adding encoder-side attention (SE-ResNeXt) is redundant when the decoder already possesses attention mechanisms (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Decoder Structure:** I will employ a **U-Net++** decoder with **Deep Supervision**. The nested skip pathways bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder more effectively than standard U-Net, and Deep Supervision (calculating loss at nodes L1, L2, L3, L4) enforces feature consistency at multiple scales (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into the decoder nodes to suppress background noise in empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will strictly use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth. The input tensor will be constructed as `[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`. This preserves the pre-trained ImageNet weights of the 3-channel encoder, avoiding the performance degradation caused by modifying the first convolutional layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Conservative Geometric Regularization:** Unlike previous ideas that excluded geometric transforms, I will introduce **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale/Shift < 5%) alongside Horizontal Flips and Random Brightness/Contrast. This specific configuration provides necessary regularization for the structured seismic data without introducing the destructive artifacts seen with aggressive distortions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** to maintain texture continuity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Target Normalization:** Ground truth masks will be explicitly cast to **float [0, 1]** to prevent the "negative infinity loss" divergence observed with integer targets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).

### Training
**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Mixed Precision & Scaled Batch Size:** I will implement **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** training. This reduces memory usage, allowing the batch size to be doubled (e.g., from 32 to **64**). The larger batch size stabilizes Batch Normalization statistics and gradient estimation, leading to superior generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Extended Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up:** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** (on all Deep Supervision outputs) to establish convergence.
    2.  **Fine-tuning:** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (on the final output) to optimize the IoU metric directly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Schedule:** The training duration will be extended to **80 epochs**, with the loss switch occurring early (e.g., epoch 15). This provides the Lovasz loss with an extended "runway" to navigate its complex gradient landscape, preventing the premature plateauing observed in shorter runs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
*   **Adaptive Scheduling:** I will use the **ReduceLROnPlateau** scheduler. It will monitor the **Optimized mAP** (derived from a dynamic threshold sweep), ensuring learning rate decays are triggered by stagnation in discriminative power rather than calibration drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011, solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Synchronization:** The loss switch will be scheduled well before any early stopping trigger to ensure the fine-tuning phase is fully executed (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Predictions will be generated for the original image and a horizontally flipped version, then averaged. This reduces variance and improves the metric without retraining (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Threshold Optimization:** I will perform a post-inference sweep on the validation set to identify the single optimal binarization threshold that maximizes the mAP. This is critical for filtering out low-confidence false positives in empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
