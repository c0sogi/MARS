Based on the extensive failure analysis and empirical successes documented in the lessons, I propose the **Corrected High-Fidelity Stratified Ensemble** strategy.

This solution represents a structural evolution from the single-model paradigm to a **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation Pipeline**. While previous attempts at ensembling failed due to specific implementation defects (truncated training, incorrect normalization, and weighted supervision), this strategy synthesizes the lessons to correct these flaws. It leverages the full compute budget to train every ensemble member to deep convergence, utilizing 100% of the available data to address the high variance and data scarcity inherent to the task.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. This architecture provides superior texture extraction capabilities compared to ResNet34 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) and avoids the redundancy of encoder-side attention since the decoder handles feature recalibration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Decoder Structure:** I will employ a **U-Net++** decoder with **Deep Supervision**. The nested dense skip pathways effectively bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
    *   **Lightweight Capacity:** I will explicitly configure the decoder with **lightweight channel widths** (starting at 16 or 32 filters). This acts as a structural regularizer, preventing overfitting on this small dataset, which was a failure mode of heavier decoders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Equal Supervision Weighting:** I will assign **equal weights (mean)** to all Deep Supervision auxiliary heads. This corrects the failure of previous ensembles that down-weighted auxiliary outputs, ensuring the nested architecture provides maximum regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node to suppress background noise in "empty" masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Input Strategy:** I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth. The input tensor will be constructed as `[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`. This preserves the pre-trained weights of the 3-channel encoder without modifying the first convolutional layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Dataset Consolidation:** I will merge the provided `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` into a single master dataset and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Splitting** based on salt coverage. This ensures the model learns from 100% of the available data, correcting the "recursive splitting" error of previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Uniform Input Normalization:** I will apply **Uniform Scaling** (dividing by 255.0 to range `[0, 1]`) to all three input channels. I will explicitly reject ImageNet normalization, which was proven to break signal consistency when applied to multiplexed grayscale/depth channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** to maintain texture statistics at boundaries (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This provides necessary regularization without introducing destructive interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Training
**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Full Convergence Schedule:** Each of the 5 folds will be trained for **80 epochs**. I will explicitly prioritize the "depth" of convergence, rejecting the truncated schedules (e.g., 40 epochs) that caused previous ensembles to underperform (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP):** I will use AMP to enable a batch size of **64**, stabilizing Batch Normalization statistics and improving generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Gradient-Stabilized Loss Curriculum:**
    1.  **Warm-up (Epochs 1-15):** Train with **BCE + Dice Loss** (on all Deep Supervision outputs) to establish convergence.
    2.  **Fine-tuning (Epochs 16-80):** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (calculated per-image, Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Shock Mitigation:** I will strictly enforce a **Conservative Learning Rate ($\le 1e-4$)** during the loss switch and for the unfrozen backbone. This prevents "Gradient Shock" and catastrophic forgetting of pre-trained features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034, solution_lesson_node_00037).
*   **Scheduling:** I will use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP** (derived from a dynamic threshold sweep). This ensures the scheduler reacts to discriminative power rather than calibration drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011, solution_lesson_node_00013).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** During validation, predictions will be cropped back to **101x101** *before* metric calculation to eliminate padding noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Threshold Optimization:** I will aggregate the **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from all 5 folds and perform a single threshold sweep to identify the global probability threshold that maximizes the mAP. This provides a statistically robust estimate for the binarization cutoff (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Ensemble Prediction:** The final submission will be the average of the probability maps from all 5 trained models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Each ensemble member will also apply TTA (averaging original and horizontally flipped inputs) to further reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
