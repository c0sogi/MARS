This ensures that model checkpointing and learning rate scheduling are driven by the model's maximum discriminative capacity, preventing premature stagnation due to calibration shifts.
3.  **Extended Convergence Horizon (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020):** I will increase the `EARLY_STOPPING_PATIENCE` from 15 to 25 epochs. This provides the model with a longer runway to recover and fine-tune after the switch to the Lovasz-Hinge loss, ensuring the optimization phase is not truncated prematurely.
