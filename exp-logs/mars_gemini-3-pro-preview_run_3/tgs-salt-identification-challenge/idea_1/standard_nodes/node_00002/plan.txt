ResNet34 provides a deeper feature extraction capability which is beneficial for distinguishing subtle salt textures in seismic images, without significantly increasing computational cost.
2.  **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**: I will implement horizontal flip TTA during validation, threshold optimization, and inference. By averaging predictions from the original and flipped images, we reduce model variance and suppress noise, which is particularly important for the "empty mask" cases identified in the failure analysis. This directly supports the threshold optimization strategy by providing more stable probability estimates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
3.  **Training Schedule**: I will increase the training epochs from 15 to **50**. The previous run showed the loss was still decreasing, and the stronger ResNet34 model will benefit from a longer training schedule to converge fully.
