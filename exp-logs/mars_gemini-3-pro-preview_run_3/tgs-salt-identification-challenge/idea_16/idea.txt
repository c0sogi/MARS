Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, specifically the failure analysis of Idea 15 (Lesson 49) and the optimization insights from Lessons 46 and 24, I propose the **High-Fidelity Dynamic Ensemble with SE-ResNeXt50**.

This solution corrects the critical architectural flaw in Idea 15 (which downgraded the backbone) while retaining the **Dynamic Deep Supervision** strategy that was proven to optimize the fine-tuning phase. It leverages the 24-hour compute budget to execute a fully converged **Stratified 5-Fold Ensemble**, strictly adhering to the "Depth over Width" principle by training every fold to saturation.

### Model
**Architecture:** **U-Net++ (Nested U-Net) with SE-ResNeXt-50 (32x4d) Encoder and scSE Attention.**
*   **Backbone:** I will explicitly utilize the **SE-ResNeXt-50 (32x4d)** encoder. While Lesson 17 suggested encoder attention might be redundant, Lesson 49 confirmed that removing Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) blocks from the backbone caused a ~1% mAP drop in high-fidelity regimes. The SE blocks are critical for capturing the subtle texture variations in seismic data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00049).
*   **Decoder:** I will employ a **U-Net++** architecture to bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015).
    *   **Lightweight Capacity:** The decoder will use **lightweight channel widths** (starting at 16 filters) to act as a structural regularizer against overfitting on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **Stride 2 Output:** The decoder will terminate at **Stride 2** (1/2 resolution) and use bilinear upsampling for the final output, avoiding artifacts associated with learnable operations at the noisy full resolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).
*   **Attention:** **Concurrent Spatial and Channel Squeeze & Excitation (scSE)** modules will be integrated into every decoder node to suppress background noise in empty masks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation:**
*   **Input Strategy:** I will use **Input Channel Multiplexing** to incorporate depth. The input tensor will be constructed as `[Seismic, Seismic, Depth]`. This preserves the pre-trained ImageNet weights of the 3-channel encoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
*   **Uniform Normalization:** I will apply **Uniform Scaling** (dividing by 255.0 to range `[0, 1]`) to all three input channels. I will explicitly reject ImageNet normalization to preserve signal consistency across the multiplexed channels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Consolidated Stratification:** I will merge the provided `train_metadata.csv` and `val_metadata.csv` into a single master dataset and perform **Stratified 5-Fold Splitting** based on salt coverage. This ensures the model learns from 100% of the available data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
*   **Padding:** Images will be padded to **128x128** using **reflection padding** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Conservative Augmentation:** I will apply **Horizontal Flips**, **Random Brightness/Contrast**, and **Conservative ShiftScaleRotate** (Rotation < 5°, Scale < 5%). This provides necessary regularization without introducing destructive interpolation artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Training
**Optimization Paradigm:**
*   **Full Convergence Ensemble:** I will train 5 separate models (one per fold) for **80 epochs** each. I will explicitly reject truncated schedules, prioritizing the "depth" of convergence for each ensemble member (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Precision:** I will use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to enable a batch size of **64** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).

**Dynamic Deep Supervision (DDS) Curriculum:**
I will implement the **Dynamic Deep Supervision** strategy to resolve the conflict between auxiliary losses and global metric optimization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046):
1.  **Phase 1 (Epochs 1-20): Structural Warm-up.**
    *   **Loss:** **BCE + Dice Loss**.
    *   **Scope:** **Deep Supervision Active.** I will calculate the loss at all nested decoder nodes with **Equal Weights (1.0)** to enforce multi-scale feature consistency (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
2.  **Phase 2 (Epochs 21-80): Metric Fine-tuning.**
    *   **Loss:** Switch to **Lovász-Hinge Loss** (calculated per-image, Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Scope:** **Deep Supervision Disabled.** I will calculate the loss **only on the final output head**. This prevents intermediate layers from introducing conflicting gradients when optimizing the global IoU metric (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046).
    *   **Stability:** I will enforce a **Conservative Learning Rate ($\le 1e-4$)** during this switch to prevent gradient shock (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

**Scheduling:**
*   I will use **ReduceLROnPlateau** monitoring the **Optimized mAP** (derived from a dynamic threshold sweep), ensuring the scheduler reacts to discriminative power rather than calibration drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### Evaluation
**Inference Pipeline:**
*   **Metric Alignment:** During validation, predictions will be cropped back to **101x101** *before* metric calculation to eliminate padding noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
*   **Global Threshold Optimization:** I will aggregate the **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions from all 5 folds and perform a single threshold sweep to identify the global probability threshold that maximizes the mAP (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Ensemble Prediction:** The final submission will be the average of the probability maps from all 5 trained models.
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** Each ensemble member will apply TTA (averaging original and horizontally flipped inputs) to further reduce variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
