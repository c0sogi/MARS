To optimize the performance of the Hindi/Tamil Question Answering model, I will implement a strategy based on the provided lessons, focusing on mitigating model collapse and reducing variance.

**Strategy:**

1.  **Optimize Batch Size and Epochs**:
    *   I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to **4**. As noted in **Lesson 00015**, large batch sizes on small datasets (<1000 samples) reduce the frequency of gradient updates, leading to majority class collapse (predicting "Outside" everywhere). A smaller batch size ensures sufficient updates to learn sparse answer spans.
    *   I will restore `EPOCHS` to **10** (previously overridden to 3). **Lesson 00005** highlights that validation performance often has a "delayed onset," and extended training is required for convergence.

2.  **Implement Seed Ensembling**:
    *   To combat the high variance inherent in small datasets (**Lesson 00008**), I will switch from K-Fold Cross Validation to **Seed Ensembling**.
    *   I will modify `get_fold_dataloaders` to yield the fixed Train/Val split multiple times instead of splitting the data into folds.
    *   I will modify the training loop in `runfile.py` to set a distinct random seed for each "fold" (run), and update `train_qa_fold` to not reset the seed internally.

3.  **Ensemble Validation**:
    *   I will update the validation assessment in `runfile.py` to generate predictions using all trained models and aggregate them using **Majority Voting** (**Lesson 00009**), providing a more accurate estimate of the final submission performance.
