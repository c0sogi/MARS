The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU VRAM is insufficient to handle the training process with the current `IMG_SIZE` of 640 and `BATCH_SIZE` of 16, particularly due to the memory-intensive U-Net decoder and EfficientNet backbone. To resolve this, I will reduce the `IMG_SIZE` to 512 and the `BATCH_SIZE` to 8 in `./library/config.py`. This reduction in both resolution and batch size will significantly decrease the memory footprint required for intermediate activations, ensuring the model fits within the available 16GB VRAM (Cite debug_lesson_1).
