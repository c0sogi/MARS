To improve the model's performance, I will implement the following targeted optimizations:

1.  **Increase Input Resolution**: I will increase `IMAGE_SIZE` from 512 to 640 in `library/config.py`. The failure analysis showed a correlation between prediction error and aspect ratio. Increasing the resolution helps preserve the fine details of thin linear structures (catheters) that are otherwise degraded when high-aspect-ratio images are resized, even with letterboxing. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
2.  **Enhance Regularization**: The previous training run showed signs of overfitting (validation loss increased after epoch 3). I will increase `DROPOUT_RATE` to 0.25 in `library/config.py` and add a `nn.Dropout` layer before the final classification head in `library/model.py`.
3.  **Improve Data Augmentation**: I will add `CLAHE` (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) to the training transforms in `library/dataset.py` to enhance the local contrast of catheters against the chest background.
4.  **Adjust Training Duration**: I will slightly increase `EPOCHS` to 12 to allow the model to converge with the added regularization.
