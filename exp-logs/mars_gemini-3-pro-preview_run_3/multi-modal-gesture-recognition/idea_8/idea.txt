Based on the progression of experiments and the specific insights from the "Lessons Learned," I propose the **Boundary-Aware Kinematically-Consistent Iterative Refinement Network (BA-KC-IRN)**.

This solution structurally evolves the successful "Iterative Refinement" architecture (Idea 7) by introducing a **Multi-Task Learning** framework. It addresses the inherent trade-off between "smoothing noise" and "preserving transitions" by explicitly modeling gesture boundaries and using them to gate the information flow and the loss functions.

### Model
The architecture is a **Three-Stage Multi-Task Network** that processes multi-modal data to predict both gesture classes and transition boundaries.

1.  **Stage 1: Dual-Head Sequence Encoder**
    *   **Input:** An **Early Fusion** vector consisting of Kinematically Consistent Skeleton Features (Position, Velocity, Acceleration) and Audio MFCCs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Bi-directional GRU (Bi-GRU)**. This is retained for its superior sample efficiency on this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Structural Innovation (Dual Heads):** Instead of a single output layer, the encoder branches into two parallel projection heads:
        1.  **Classification Head:** Outputs probability distribution over 21 classes ($P_{cls}$).
        2.  **Boundary Head:** Outputs a binary probability ($P_{bnd}$) indicating whether the current frame represents a gesture transition (start/end).
    *   **Output:** A concatenated probability vector of dimension 22 (21 Class + 1 Boundary).

2.  **Stage 2 & 3: Boundary-Informed Gated Refinement**
    *   **Input:** The **concatenated probability vector** (22 dims) from the previous stage. This adheres to the strict **Information Bottleneck** principle (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020) by passing only semantic signals (soft labels and boundary confidence) rather than dense latent features.
    *   **Architecture:** Stacked **Gated Dilated Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCNs)**. The use of **Gated Activations** ($\tanh \times \sigma$) is retained to effectively control information flow (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
    *   **Mechanism:** By receiving the explicit $P_{bnd}$ channel, the TCNs can learn to apply aggressive smoothing within gesture segments (where $P_{bnd} \approx 0$) and suspend smoothing at transitions (where $P_{bnd} \approx 1$), solving the over-smoothing problem of standard MS-TCNs.

### Data
The data pipeline combines rigorous physical consistency with new target generation.

1.  **Kinematically Consistent Augmentation:**
    *   Apply random 3D rotations and scaling to the **raw skeleton positions** first.
    *   Mathematically derive **Velocity** and **Acceleration** from these *augmented* positions. This prevents the "Kinematic Inconsistency" failure mode (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
2.  **Boundary Label Generation:**
    *   Generate a binary target sequence for the Boundary Head. Frames within a small radius (e.g., $\pm 2$ frames) of a ground-truth segment change are labeled as 1, others as 0.
3.  **Windowing:**
    *   Use **Sliding Windows of 64 frames** to match the atomic gesture duration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

### Training
The training strategy employs a novel **Boundary-Adaptive Loss** to enforce intelligent smoothing.

1.  **Multi-Task Cascaded Loss:**
    *   The total loss is calculated at the output of **all three stages** (Deep Supervision) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   $L_{total} = \sum_{s=1}^{3} (L_{cls}^{(s)} + \lambda_{bnd} L_{bnd}^{(s)} + \lambda_{smooth} L_{adaptive}^{(s)})$.
2.  **Loss Components:**
    *   **Weighted Cross-Entropy ($L_{cls}$):** For gesture classification, with a weight of 0.2 for the background class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010). We avoid Focal Loss due to the noisy nature of the data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Binary Cross-Entropy ($L_{bnd}$):** For the boundary prediction head.
    *   **Boundary-Adaptive Smoothing ($L_{adaptive}$):** A structural innovation over the standard smoothing loss.
        *   $L_{adaptive} = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t} (1 - y_{bnd}^{(t)}) \cdot || \log P_{cls}^{(t)} - \log P_{cls}^{(t-1)} ||^2$
        *   This term forces the model to be smooth **only** when the ground truth ($y_{bnd}$) indicates there is no boundary. At transition points, the smoothing penalty is effectively disabled, allowing sharp class changes.
3.  **Optimizer:** **Adam** optimizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Process test sequences using sliding windows with overlap.
2.  **Prediction:** Extract the final **Classification Probabilities** ($P_{cls}$) from **Stage 3**.
3.  **Decoding:**
    *   Apply `argmax` to obtain frame labels.
    *   Perform **Run-Length Encoding** to collapse consecutive identical labels.
    *   **No Heuristic Filtering:** We rely entirely on the model's learned smoothness, avoiding the destructive interference of post-hoc median filters (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
