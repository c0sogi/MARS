Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the progression of "Previous Ideas," I propose the **Boundary-Aware Attentive Kinematic Network (BA-AKN)**.

This solution represents a structural evolution of the "Kinematically-Consistent Iterative Refinement Network" (Idea 7). It synthesizes the successful backbone and data pipeline of Idea 7 with the **Boundary-Awareness** concept (Idea 8) and **Channel Attention** mechanisms (Idea 9), while strictly avoiding the failed geometric normalization and post-processing heuristics identified in the lessons.

# Model
The architecture is a **Three-Stage Multi-Task Network** designed to balance aggressive temporal smoothing with precise boundary detection.

1.  **Stage 1: Multi-Task Sequence Encoder**
    *   **Input:** An **Early Fusion** vector. This is critical to leverage the coupled statistics of the multi-modal data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Bi-directional GRU (Bi-GRU)**. This is retained for its superior sample efficiency on this small dataset compared to Transformers or CNN-only backbones (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Dual Heads:** The encoder projects to two distinct semantic outputs:
        1.  **Classification Head:** Outputs probabilities for the 21 classes ($P_{cls}$).
        2.  **Boundary Head:** Outputs a binary probability ($P_{bnd}$) indicating the presence of a gesture transition (start/end).

2.  **Stage 2 & 3: Attentive Boundary-Informed Refinement**
    *   **Input:** A concatenated vector of **Class Probabilities** and **Boundary Probabilities** (22 channels). This strictly enforces the **Information Bottleneck**, preventing the overfitting observed when passing dense latent features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Architecture:** Stacked **Gated Dilated Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCNs)**.
        *   **Gating:** We retain **Gated Activations** ($\tanh \times \sigma$) to control information flow (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
        *   **Structural Innovation (Attentive Blocks):** We integrate **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Blocks** within the residual layers. This allows the model to dynamically recalibrate channel weights based on the global context of the window, addressing the inter-class dependency limitations of standard TCNs.
    *   **Function:** These stages iteratively refine the predictions. The inclusion of the explicit $P_{bnd}$ channel allows the network to learn to suspend smoothing operations specifically at transition points.

# Data
The data pipeline is designed to ensure physical validity and provide auxiliary supervision.

1.  **Kinematically Consistent Augmentation:**
    *   We strictly adhere to the protocol of augmenting **Raw Positions** (Rotation, Scaling) *before* mathematically deriving **Velocity** and **Acceleration**. This prevents the "Kinematic Inconsistency" that degraded performance in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
2.  **Boundary Label Generation:**
    *   We generate binary target labels for the Boundary Head, marking frames within a small radius of a ground-truth segment change as "1".
3.  **Windowing:**
    *   Training samples are generated using **Sliding Windows of 64 frames**. This duration is calibrated to the average atomic gesture length, optimizing the signal-to-noise ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

# Training
The training strategy employs a **Boundary-Adaptive Loss** to resolve the conflict between smoothness and precision.

1.  **Multi-Task Cascaded Loss:**
    *   Loss is calculated at the output of **all three stages** (Deep Supervision) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   $L_{total} = \sum_{s=1}^{3} (L_{cls}^{(s)} + \lambda_{bnd} L_{bnd}^{(s)} + \lambda_{smooth} L_{adaptive}^{(s)})$.
2.  **Adaptive Smoothing:**
    *   We implement a **Gated Smoothing Loss**: $L_{adaptive} = (1 - y_{bnd}) \cdot || \log P_{t} - \log P_{t-1} ||^2$.
    *   This term enforces the **Log-Space Smoothing** proven effective in Lesson 00015, but multiplies it by the inverse of the ground-truth boundary label. This effectively **disables the smoothing penalty** at true transition points, allowing the model to make sharp predictions where necessary while smoothing aggressive noise elsewhere.
3.  **Optimizer:** **Adam** optimizer, avoiding AdamW to ensure stable convergence of the recurrent backbone (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
4.  **Loss Function:** **Weighted Cross-Entropy** for classification (0.2 weight for background) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010), avoiding Focal Loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** We employ **Sliding Window Inference** with 50% overlap. The probability outputs from overlapping windows are averaged to perform temporal ensembling, which mitigates distribution shifts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
2.  **Prediction:** Final predictions are derived from the Classification Output of **Stage 3**.
3.  **Decoding:**
    *   Apply `argmax` to the averaged probabilities.
    *   Perform **Run-Length Encoding (RLE)** to collapse consecutive identical labels.
    *   **No Heuristic Filtering:** We explicitly discard the Median Filter post-processing, relying entirely on the model's learned, boundary-aware smoothness to avoid the destructive interference observed in Lesson 00036.
