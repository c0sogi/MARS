Based on the lessons learned from previous iterations, particularly the failure of Idea 41 and the success of the baseline Idea 7, I will implement the following optimizations to the **Physically-Aligned Kinematic Refinement Network (PAK-RN)**:

1.  **Revert Feature Scaling (Cite Lesson 00082):** Idea 41 scaled skeleton coordinates from millimeters to meters (`0.001`), which reduced the dominant kinematic signal magnitude (~1.0) below that of the auxiliary audio features (~10-100), causing the model to lose focus on the primary modality. I will revert `SKELETON_SCALE` to `1.0` (raw millimeters) to preserve the implicit weighting that favors the skeleton data.

2.  **Restore Encoder Depth (Cite Lesson 00069):** Idea 41 reduced the Bi-GRU encoder to a single layer, which likely caused the "under-segmentation" and lack of robustness seen in the failure analysis. I will restore `GRU_LAYERS` to `2` to ensure sufficient capacity for modeling complex temporal transitions.

3.  **Optimize Hidden Dimensions:** I will adjust `GRU_HIDDEN_SIZE` to `64` (per direction, total 128) and `TCN_CHANNELS` to `128`. This matches the capacity of the successful baseline model, avoiding the instability observed with larger models (Lesson 00110) while providing more robustness than the lightweight Idea 41.

4.  **Training Duration:** I will slightly increase the epoch limit in `runfile.py` to `50` to guarantee convergence, as the deeper encoder may require a few more epochs to stabilize than the shallow one.
