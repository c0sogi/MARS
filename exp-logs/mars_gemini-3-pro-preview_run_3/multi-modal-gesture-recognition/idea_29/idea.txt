Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the progression of "Previous Ideas," I propose the **Normalized Gated-Kinematic Refinement Network (NG-KRN)**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Robust Gated High-Capacity Monotonic Network" (Idea 28) by explicitly addressing the **Feature Magnitude Alignment Failure** (Lesson 00082). It integrates the **High-Capacity Encoder** (Lesson 00066), **Input Gating** (Lesson 00058), and **Monotonic Non-Causal MS-TCN** (Lesson 00080) into a unified framework, underpinned by a **Polymorphic Data Pipeline** (Lesson 00045) and strict **Modality-Specific Normalization**.

# Model
The architecture is a **Three-Stage Cascaded Network** designed to ensure balanced multi-modal learning, noise suppression, and stable temporal refinement.

1.  **Stage 1: Normalized Gated Kinematic Encoder**
    *   **Input:** An **Early Fusion** vector consisting of Audio MFCCs and Kinematically Consistent Skeleton features (Position, Velocity, Acceleration) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
    *   **Structural Innovation (Modality-Specific Normalization):** Before fusion or gating, we apply **Independent Z-Score Normalization** to the Skeleton and Audio substreams. This scales the skeletal data (originally ~1000 or ~1 depending on units) and audio data (MFCCs ~100) to a common zero-mean, unit-variance distribution. This prevents the magnitude dominance observed in Idea 28, ensuring the encoder learns from motion dynamics rather than just raw scale (Cite solution_lesson_node_00082).
    *   **Feature-Wise Input Gating:** The normalized, fused vector $\hat{X}_t$ is passed through a learnable gating layer: $\tilde{X}_t = \hat{X}_t \odot \sigma(W_{gate} \hat{X}_t + b)$. This allows the model to dynamically suppress noisy sensor channels at a frame-wise level (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Bi-directional GRU (Bi-GRU)** with a hidden dimension of **128 units per direction** (256 total). We explicitly maintain high capacity to prevent underfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Output:** Initial frame-wise class probabilities ($P_1$) over 21 classes (Softmax).

2.  **Stage 2: Monotonic Non-Causal Gated Refinement**
    *   **Input:** Strictly the **class probabilities** ($P_1$) from Stage 1 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Architecture:** A stack of **Gated Dilated Temporal Convolutional Layers** ($\tanh \times \sigma$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032). We use **Standard Convolutions** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Dilation Schedule:** We employ a **Monotonically Increasing Schedule**: $[1, 2, 4, 8, 16]$. This maximizes the receptive field (63 frames) within the 64-frame window without reading invalid padding, correcting the Sawtooth/Causal failures of previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080, solution_lesson_node_00057).
    *   **Causality:** We use **Centered Padding** (Non-Causal) to leverage future context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078).
    *   **Output:** Refined class probabilities ($P_2$).

3.  **Stage 3: Independent Iterative Refinement**
    *   **Structure:** Identical to Stage 2 but with **independent weights** to allow for specialized boundary polishing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).
    *   **Output:** Final class probabilities ($P_3$).

# Data
The data pipeline incorporates critical engineering fixes for robustness and strict physical consistency rules.

1.  **Robust Polymorphic Parsing:**
    *   The data loader implements a **Polymorphic Parser** for the `.mat` files, dynamically handling struct vs. cell array vs. primitive types to prevent silent failures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).

2.  **Kinematically Consistent Augmentation:**
    *   **Load:** Raw 3D Joint Positions ($P_{raw}$).
    *   **Augment:** Apply random 3D rotations (Y-axis) and scaling to $P_{raw}$.
    *   **Derive:** Calculate **Velocity** ($V_t$) and **Acceleration** ($A_t$) from the *augmented* positions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
    *   **Fusion:** Concatenate $P_{aug}$, $V_t$, $A_t$, and Audio MFCCs.

3.  **Sampling Strategy:**
    *   **Windowing:** Generate training samples using **Sliding Windows of 64 frames** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Stride:** Use a **Moderate Stride** (e.g., 32 frames) to avoid overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).

# Training
The training strategy prioritizes stability and smoothness using Deep Supervision.

1.  **Cascaded Loss Function:**
    *   $L_{total} = L(P_1) + L(P_2) + L(P_3)$ (Deep Supervision) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Weighted Cross-Entropy:** Applied with a weight of **0.2** for the "background" class (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010). We strictly avoid Focal Loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
    *   **Log-Space Smoothing Loss:** A Truncated MSE loss applied to the log-probabilities of adjacent frames.
    *   **Threshold:** We use a **Low Truncation Threshold** (e.g., 1.0) to strictly enforce smoothness (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).

2.  **Optimizer:**
    *   Use the **Adam** optimizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
    *   **Regularization:** Apply standard **Weight Decay** (L2) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).

3.  **Validation:**
    *   Perform model selection based on the **Levenshtein Distance** computed on **Full Sequences**, ensuring alignment with the final objective (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052, solution_lesson_node_00075).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** Process test sequences using **Sliding Window Inference** with 50% overlap. The probability outputs from overlapping windows are averaged to perform temporal ensembling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
2.  **Prediction:** Extract the final predictions from **Stage 3**.
3.  **Decoding:**
    *   Apply `argmax` to the averaged probabilities.
    *   Perform **Run-Length Encoding (RLE)**.
    *   **Min Duration Filter:** Explicitly remove segments shorter than **5 frames** to align with physical constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071). We explicitly discard Median Filtering (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
