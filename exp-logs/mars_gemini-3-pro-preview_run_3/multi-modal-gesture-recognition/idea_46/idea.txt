Based on the rigorous analysis of the "Lessons Learned" and the failures of recent proposals (specifically Idea 45's receptive field mismatch and Idea 28's scaling issues), I propose the **Central-Kinematic Aligned-Receptive-Field Network (CK-ARF-Net)**.

This solution represents a structural synthesis that explicitly resolves the **Receptive Field Mismatch** (Lesson 00127, Lesson 00057), the **Kinematic Noise Amplification** (Lesson 00127), and the **Input Engineering Trilemma** (Lesson 00082, Lesson 00085). It reverts to **Raw Millimeter Features** to preserve signal dominance, adopts **Central Difference Derivation** for phase-aligned kinematics, and enforces strict **RF-Window Alignment** in the refinement stage.

# Model
The architecture is a **Three-Stage Cascaded Network** designed for noise robustness, optimal temporal context, and geometric consistency.

1.  **Stage 1: Root-Centric Moderate-Capacity Encoder**
    *   **Input:** An **Early Fusion** vector consisting of two streams:
        *   **Skeleton Stream:** Kinematically Consistent features (Position, Velocity, Acceleration).
            *   **Preprocessing:** We apply **Root-Relative Centering** (subtracting Hip Center coordinates) to ensure translation invariance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00102).
            *   **Raw Magnitude Retention:** We explicitly **retain Raw Millimeters** (~1000). We reject the conversion to meters (Idea 45) because reducing the magnitude allows the auxiliary Audio signal (~100) to drown out the primary skeletal signal during gradient updates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00082). Raw millimeters ensure the Skeleton remains the dominant modality.
        *   **Audio Stream:** MFCC features.
        *   **Fusion:** Concatenation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00097).
    *   **Backbone:** A **2-Layer Bi-directional GRU (Bi-GRU)** with a hidden dimension of **96 units per direction** (192 total).
        *   *Rationale:* We restore the depth to **2 layers** to correct the underfitting observed in 1-layer encoders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00069). The 192-unit width provides a "Goldilocks" capacity, avoiding the bottlenecks of 64-unit models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066) and the instability of 256-unit models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00110).
    *   **Regularization:** **Dropout (p=0.4)** is applied between GRU layers to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091).
    *   **Output:** Initial frame-wise class probabilities ($P_1$) over 21 classes.

2.  **Stage 2: RF-Aligned Monotonic Refinement**
    *   **Input:** Strictly the **class probabilities** ($P_1$) from Stage 1 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Architecture:** A stack of **Gated Dilated Temporal Convolutional Layers** ($\tanh \times \sigma$) using **Standard Convolutions** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Dilation Schedule (Structural Innovation):** We employ a **Monotonically Increasing Schedule**: $[1, 2, 4, 8, 16]$.
        *   *Rationale:* This yields a receptive field of **63 frames**. This matches the training window of 64 frames almost perfectly ($63 < 64$), maximizing temporal context without reading invalid zero-padding. This explicitly corrects the failure of Idea 45 (RF=31) which lacked sufficient context, and Idea 17 (RF>64) which learned edge artifacts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00127, solution_lesson_node_00057).
    *   **Causality:** **Centered Padding** (Non-Causal) to leverage future context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078).
    *   **Output:** Refined class probabilities ($P_2$).

3.  **Stage 3: Independent RF-Aligned Refinement**
    *   **Structure:** Identical to Stage 2 but with **independent weights** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).
    *   **Output:** Final class probabilities ($P_3$).

# Data
The data pipeline incorporates a critical structural change in feature derivation to reduce noise and ensures robust ingestion.

1.  **Robust Polymorphic Parsing (Structural Requirement):**
    *   The data loader implements a **Polymorphic Parser** for the `.mat` files, dynamically handling struct vs. cell array vs. primitive types to prevent "silent failure" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045).

2.  **Central-Difference Kinematics (Structural Innovation):**
    *   **Load:** Raw 3D Joint Positions ($P_{raw}$).
    *   **Center:** Apply **Root-Relative Centering**.
    *   **Augment:** Apply random 3D rotations (Y-axis) and scaling.
    *   **Derive:** Instead of the forward difference (`np.diff`) used in Idea 45, we use **Central Differences** (`np.gradient`).
        *   *Rationale:* Forward difference introduces a half-frame phase shift and amplifies high-frequency noise. Central difference provides a phase-aligned, smoother estimate of Velocity and Acceleration, improving the signal-to-noise ratio of the input features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00127).

3.  **Sampling Strategy:**
    *   **Windowing:** Generate training samples using **Sliding Windows of 64 frames** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
    *   **Stride:** Use a **Moderate Stride** (e.g., 32 frames) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053).

# Training
The training strategy prioritizes stability and smoothness using Deep Supervision.

1.  **Cascaded Loss Function:**
    *   $L_{total} = L(P_1) + L(P_2) + L(P_3)$ (Deep Supervision) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Weighted Cross-Entropy:** Weight of **0.2** for "background" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    *   **Log-Space Smoothing Loss:** Truncated MSE on log-probabilities.
    *   **Calibration:** **Low Weight** ($\lambda=0.15$) and **Low Threshold** (1.0) to prevent under-segmentation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00111, solution_lesson_node_00055).

2.  **Optimizer:**
    *   **Adam** optimizer with **Weight Decay** (L2) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** **Full Sequence Inference** (Batch Size=1) to preserve global temporal context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00079).
2.  **Model Selection:** Select best checkpoint based on **Levenshtein Distance** on full sequences (Cite solution_lesson_node_00052).
3.  **Decoding:**
    *   Apply `argmax` to $P_3$.
    *   Perform **Run-Length Encoding**.
    *   **Min Duration Filter:** Remove segments shorter than **5 frames** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00071).
