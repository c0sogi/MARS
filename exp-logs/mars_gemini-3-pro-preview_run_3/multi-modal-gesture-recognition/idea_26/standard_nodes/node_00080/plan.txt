1.  **Encoder Capacity:** I will increase the `GRU_LAYERS` from 1 to 2. The previous reduction to a single layer created an information bottleneck that degraded performance (Cite Lesson 00069).
2.  **Temporal Receptive Field:** I will replace the sawtooth dilation schedule with a standard exponentially increasing schedule `[1, 2, 4, 8, 16]`. The previous max dilation of 8 was insufficient for capturing long-range dependencies, and the sawtooth pattern introduced segmentation artifacts. Increasing the max dilation to 16 maximizes the receptive field while remaining safely within the training window constraints (Cite Lesson 00048, Lesson 00057).
3.  **Model Architecture:** I will rename the model class from `HCNCSN` to `KC_IRN` and update the `Trainer` to use this restored architecture.
