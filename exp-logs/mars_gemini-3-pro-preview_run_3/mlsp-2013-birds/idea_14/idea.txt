# Optimized Strategy: Heterogeneous Ensemble with Concat Pooling and Corrected EMA

### Model
The solution will evolve the successful **Heterogeneous Ensemble** (Idea 12) by introducing a structural modification to the pooling layer and a corrected optimization wrapper to address stability issues.
- **Backbones:** Utilization of three distinct architectures to maximize feature diversity and leverage the "Diversity of Errors" principle (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**):
    1.  **ResNet-18:** The proven baseline for capacity/generalization balance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**).
    2.  **EfficientNet-B0:** For parameter efficiency and MBConv feature extraction.
    3.  **DenseNet-121:** For feature reuse and robustness in small-data regimes.
- **Structural Innovation (Concat Pooling):** Replace the standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) layer with **Concatenated Pooling (GAP + GMP)**.
    - *Rationale:* Standard GAP dilutes sparse signals (short bird calls) by averaging them with long periods of background silence (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**). While learnable pooling (Attention) failed due to overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**), **Global Max Pooling (GMP)** is parameter-free and specifically captures the *peak* activation (the strongest presence of a bird call) regardless of its duration. Concatenating GAP (background context) and GMP (signal peak) provides a richer feature representation without adding learnable parameters.
- **Optimization Wrapper (Corrected EMA):** Re-implement **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** but with a **Low Decay Rate** (e.g., 0.90 - 0.95).
    - *Rationale:* Idea 13 failed because the standard decay (0.999) was too slow for the low number of training steps in this micro-dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**). A lower decay ensures the shadow model updates sufficiently fast to track the active model while still smoothing out the stochastic noise from Mixup and small batch sizes.
- **Input Adaptation:** Strictly adhere to the **3-Channel Rule** by replicating the single-channel spectrograms 3 times to form pseudo-RGB images (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).

### Data
The pipeline will leverage a "Diversity of Data" approach to complement the model diversity.
- **Dual-Source Pipeline:** Train separate instances of the ensemble on two distinct data sources:
    1.  **Standard Spectrograms** (`supplemental_data/spectrograms`): To capture the full acoustic scene.
    2.  **Filtered Spectrograms** (`supplemental_data/filtered_spectrograms`): To capture the noise-suppressed representations provided by the organizers.
    This prioritizes provided domain features (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**) while mitigating reliance on a single preprocessing view.
- **Preprocessing:** Resize all spectrograms to **224x224**. This rigid adherence to the pre-trained model's spatial expectations is superior to tiling or custom aspect ratios for this micro-dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).
- **Augmentation Pipeline:**
    1.  **Horizontal Translation:** Apply time-shifting using **Zero-Padding** (not wrapping) to preserve temporal causality (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**).
    2.  **Photometric Augmentation:** Apply **Brightness and Contrast Jitter** to mimic Gain and SNR variations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
    3.  **Mixup:** Apply **Mixup Regularization** to prevent overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
    4.  **Constraint:** Strictly **disable Horizontal Flipping** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).

### Training
- **Splitting Strategy:** Implement **Iterative Stratification** (5 Folds) to ensure representative distribution of rare classes (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**).
- **Loss Function:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss**. Avoid specialized imbalance losses like ASL which suppress necessary gradients in small datasets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
- **Optimizer & Schedule:** Use **AdamW** with a **Monotonic Cosine Annealing** scheduler (no restarts) to ensure stable convergence (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
- **Validation:** Use a **Robust Metric Implementation** that explicitly handles missing classes in validation batches (checking for both positive and negative samples per class) to prevent premature stopping (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**).
- **Stability:** Relax **Early Stopping patience** (e.g., to 20 epochs) to accommodate the slower convergence induced by Mixup (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**).

### Evaluation
- **Ensemble Inference:** The final prediction will be an average of **30 models**:
    - 3 Architectures $\times$ 2 Data Sources (Standard/Filtered) $\times$ 5 Folds.
- **Process:** Pass the test set spectrograms (resized to 224x224) through all 30 models (using their EMA weights) and average the resulting probabilities. This massive ensemble maximizes variance reduction (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**).
