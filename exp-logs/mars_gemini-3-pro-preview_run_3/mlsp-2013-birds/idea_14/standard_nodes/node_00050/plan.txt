To improve the model's performance, I will implement the following optimization strategy based on the provided lessons:

1.  **Remove "Filtered" Data Source**: I will restrict the ensemble to use only the "standard" spectrograms. Empirical evidence indicates that the "filtered" spectrograms (with stationary noise reduction) are destructive for this dataset, causing signal loss that degrades the overall ensemble performance when averaged with the baseline. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00048}.
2.  **Disable Model EMA**: I will disable Exponential Moving Average (EMA). On this micro-dataset (~200 samples), the total number of optimization steps is very low (< 1000). Even with a tuned decay, EMA often causes the shadow model to lag behind the active model's convergence, leading to suboptimal validation scores. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00046}.
3.  **Extend Training Schedule**: I will increase `EPOCHS` from 50 to 75 and `PATIENCE` from 20 to 25. Since we are employing Mixup regularization, which smooths the decision boundaries and increases training difficulty, the model requires more iterations to converge fully. Relaxing these constraints ensures we don't stop prematurely. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00040}.
