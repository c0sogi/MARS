# Optimized Strategy: Multi-Instance ResNet-18 with Tiled Inference

### Model
The solution will transition from a standard "Single-Image" classification framework to a **Multi-Instance Learning (MIL)** architecture to resolve the conflict between input resolution and parameter efficiency.
- **Backbone:** Use **ResNet-18** (pretrained on ImageNet). This remains the backbone of choice due to its balance of capacity and generalization on this micro-dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**).
- **Structural Innovation (MIL Aggregation):** Instead of processing the entire 10-second recording as a single compressed image, the model will treat each recording as a "bag" of overlapping temporal tiles.
    - The network will process $N$ tiles (e.g., 3 overlapping windows) independently through the shared ResNet-18 backbone to generate $N$ sets of logits.
    - A **Max-Pooling** operation will be applied across the tile dimension to aggregate predictions. This ensures that if a bird species is detected with high confidence in *any* specific time window, the recording is labeled positive.
    - *Rationale:* This approach allows us to feed **224x224** inputs to the network, strictly adhering to the pretrained model's expected spatial structure (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**), while avoiding the severe temporal distortion caused by squashing 10 seconds of audio into 224 pixels. Unlike the failed Attention Pooling (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**), Max-Pooling introduces **zero additional learnable parameters**, preventing overfitting.

### Data
The pipeline prioritizes preserving signal fidelity while simulating acoustic variations.
- **Spectrogram Generation:** Generate Log-Mel Spectrograms from raw audio.
- **Tiling:** Slice the spectrogram into **3 overlapping temporal tiles** (e.g., covering 0-5s, 2.5-7.5s, 5-10s).
- **Input Adaptation:** Resize each tile to **224x224** and **replicate the single channel 3 times** to form a pseudo-RGB image. This ensures the pretrained filters are utilized effectively without modifying the first convolutional layer (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
- **Augmentation Pipeline:**
    1.  **Photometric Augmentation:** Apply **Brightness and Contrast Jitter** to the tiles. This mimics variations in recording gain and Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
    2.  **Time Shifting:** Apply random **Horizontal Translation** within the tiles to simulate temporal variability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
    3.  **Mixup:** Apply **Mixup Regularization** at the bag level (mixing tiles and labels) to smooth decision boundaries (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
    4.  **Constraint:** Strictly **disable Horizontal Flipping**, as it violates temporal causality (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).

### Training
- **Splitting Strategy:** Implement **Iterative Stratification** for Cross-Validation. This is crucial to ensure that rare species (appearing <5 times) are distributed representatively across all folds, preventing training collapse on those classes (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**).
- **Loss Function:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** applied to the max-pooled bag logits.
- **Optimizer & Schedule:** Use **AdamW** with a **Monotonic Cosine Annealing** scheduler (decaying over the full epoch count without restarts). We avoid cyclic restarts and low learning rates to ensure the model escapes local minima on this micro-dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).

### Evaluation
- **Ensemble Inference:** Train 5 independent models (one for each fold of the Iterative Stratification).
- **Inference Process:** For each test recording, generate the 3 tiles, pass them through each of the 5 models, apply max-pooling to get bag probabilities, and then **average the probabilities across the 5 models**. This ensemble approach mitigates the high variance inherent in learning from such a small dataset.
