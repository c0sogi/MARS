2.  **Switch Loss Function**: I will modify `library/trainer.py` to use `nn.BCEWithLogitsLoss` instead of `AsymmetricLoss`. Specialized imbalance losses can suppress gradients from "easy" negatives, which, in data-scarce regimes, reduces the learning signal too much. Standard BCE provides stronger gradients for feature learning (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}).
3.  **Optimize Training Duration**: I will modify `runfile.py` to increase the number of epochs from 30 to 50. The use of Mixup regularization requires more iterations to converge effectively (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00040}).
