# Optimized Strategy: Dual-Stream Heterogeneous Ensemble with Model EMA

### Model
The solution will evolve the **Heterogeneous Ensemble** approach by introducing a **Dual-Stream** input strategy and a **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** mechanism to address the stability issues identified in previous runs.
- **Backbones:** Utilization of three distinct architectures to maximize feature diversity:
    1.  **ResNet-18:** The proven baseline for capacity/generalization balance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**).
    2.  **EfficientNet-B0:** For parameter efficiency and MBConv feature extraction.
    3.  **DenseNet-121:** For feature reuse and robustness in small-data regimes (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**).
- **Optimization Wrapper (Structural Innovation):** Implement **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**. During training, a shadow copy of the model weights will be maintained and updated via a moving average (e.g., decay=0.999). Inference will be performed using these EMA weights rather than the raw weights. This explicitly addresses the "volatility" and "collapse" of individual fold models observed in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00043** by smoothing out the stochastic noise from Mixup and small batch sizes, leading to flatter and more generalizable minima.
- **Input Adaptation:** Strictly adhere to the **3-Channel Rule** by replicating the single-channel spectrograms 3 times to form pseudo-RGB images (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).

### Data
The pipeline will expand to leverage *all* provided domain-curated data, creating a "Diversity of Data" ensemble to complement the "Diversity of Architectures".
- **Dual-Source Pipeline:** Train separate instances of the ensemble on two distinct data sources:
    1.  **Standard Spectrograms** (`supplemental_data/spectrograms`): To capture the full acoustic scene including background context.
    2.  **Filtered Spectrograms** (`supplemental_data/filtered_spectrograms`): To capture the noise-suppressed, signal-enhanced representations provided by the organizers.
    This strategy adheres to **Cite solution_lesson_node_00028** (prioritizing provided features) while mitigating the risk of relying on a single preprocessing view.
- **Preprocessing:** Resize all spectrograms to **224x224**. This rigid adherence to the pre-trained model's spatial expectations is superior to tiling or custom aspect ratios for this micro-dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**).
- **Augmentation Pipeline:**
    1.  **Horizontal Translation:** Apply time-shifting using **Zero-Padding** (not wrapping) to preserve temporal causality (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**).
    2.  **Photometric Augmentation:** Apply **Brightness and Contrast Jitter** to mimic Gain and SNR variations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
    3.  **Mixup:** Apply **Mixup Regularization** to prevent overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
    4.  **Constraint:** Strictly **disable Horizontal Flipping** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).

### Training
- **Splitting Strategy:** Implement **Iterative Stratification** (5 Folds) to ensure representative distribution of rare classes (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**).
- **Loss Function:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss**. Avoid specialized imbalance losses like ASL which suppress necessary gradients in small datasets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
- **Optimizer & Schedule:** Use **AdamW** with a **Monotonic Cosine Annealing** scheduler (no restarts) to ensure stable convergence (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
- **Stability Measures:**
    - Relax **Early Stopping patience** (e.g., to 20 epochs) to accommodate the slower convergence induced by Mixup (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00040**).
    - Use a **Robust Metric Implementation** that explicitly handles missing classes in validation batches to prevent premature stopping (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**).

### Evaluation
- **Ensemble Inference:** The final prediction will be an average of **30 models**:
    - 3 Architectures $\times$ 2 Data Sources (Standard/Filtered) $\times$ 5 Folds.
- **Process:** Pass the test set spectrograms (resized to 224x224) through all 30 models (using their EMA weights) and average the resulting probabilities. This massive ensemble maximizes the "Variance Reduction" benefit identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**.
