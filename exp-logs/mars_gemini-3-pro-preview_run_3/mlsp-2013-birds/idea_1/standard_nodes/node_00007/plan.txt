I will explicitly avoid horizontal flipping to prevent time-reversal artifacts.
2.  **Mixup Regularization**: In `library/trainer.py`, I will implement Mixup in the training loop. This technique creates synthetic training examples by linearly interpolating between pairs of inputs and their labels, which is highly effective for stabilizing training on small datasets.
3.  **Optimization Schedule**: In `library/runfile.py`, I will introduce `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler to adjust the learning rate during training, aiding convergence.
4.  **Configuration**: I will update `library/config.py` to define Mixup parameters and slightly increase the number of epochs to accommodate the scheduler and regularization.
