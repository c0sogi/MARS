# Optimized Strategy: Hybrid Neuro-Symbolic Ensemble (CNNs + Bag-of-Words MLP)

### Model
The solution will implement a **Hybrid Ensemble** that fuses two distinct modeling paradigms to maximize feature diversity and robustness.
1.  **Deep Learning Stream (CNNs):** A **Heterogeneous Ensemble** of three distinct CNN architectures: **ResNet-18**, **EfficientNet-B0**, and **DenseNet-121**. This leverages the "Diversity of Errors" principle to stabilize predictions (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**) and strictly adheres to the **3-Channel Rule** (replicating single-channel inputs) to preserve pre-trained weights (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
2.  **Shallow Learning Stream (MLP):** A **Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)**. This stream explicitly exploits the **Bag-of-Audio-Words** features (`histogram_of_segments.txt`), which were proven to yield high performance (AUC ~0.907) and robustness in this data-scarce regime (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**). The MLP will consist of a simple architecture (e.g., Input $\to$ Dense(128) $\to$ ReLU $\to$ Dropout $\to$ Dense(19)) to prevent overfitting.
3.  **Fusion:** The final prediction will be an ensemble average of the Deep Learning Stream and the Shallow Learning Stream. This combines the global time-frequency texture analysis of CNNs with the local cluster-frequency analysis of the Bag-of-Words model.

### Data
The pipeline will utilize two distinct data sources, prioritizing domain-curated features.
-   **Stream A (CNNs):**
    -   **Source:** Strictly use the **Provided Standard Spectrograms** (`supplemental_data/spectrograms`). Explicitly exclude "Filtered Spectrograms" (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00048**) and raw audio (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
    -   **Preprocessing:** Resize all spectrograms to **224x224**. This rigid adherence to the pre-trained model's spatial expectations is superior to tiling or custom aspect ratios (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).
    -   **Augmentation:**
        1.  **Restricted Horizontal Translation:** Apply time-shifting using **Zero-Padding** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**) limited to **<10%** of the image width to prevent moving signals out of frame (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00071**).
        2.  **Photometric Augmentation:** Apply **Brightness and Contrast Jitter** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
        3.  **Calibrated Mixup:** Apply **Mixup (alpha=0.4)** to bias synthetic samples closer to ground truth (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**).
        4.  **Constraint:** Disable Horizontal Flipping (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).
-   **Stream B (MLP):**
    -   **Source:** `supplemental_data/histogram_of_segments.txt`.
    -   **Augmentation:** Apply **Mixup (alpha=0.4)** directly to the feature vectors during training to improve decision boundary smoothness (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).

### Training
-   **Splitting Strategy:** Implement **Iterative Stratification** (5 Folds) to ensure representative distribution of rare classes across all models (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**). Ensure dynamic splitting to prevent model cloning (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00063**).
-   **Optimization (CNNs):**
    -   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **High Weight Decay** (`1e-2`) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**).
    -   **Scheduler:** **Monotonic Cosine Annealing** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**). Avoid Layer-Wise Decay (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00073**).
    -   **Batch Size:** Maintain physical batch size of **32** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**).
    -   **Early Stopping:** Aggressive patience (15 epochs) (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00060**).
-   **Optimization (MLP):** Standard Adam optimizer with Dropout regularization.
-   **Loss Function:** **BCEWithLogitsLoss** for all models (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
-   **Validation:** Use a **Robust Metric Implementation** that handles missing classes in validation batches (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**).

### Evaluation
-   **Snapshot Averaging:** For the CNN stream, save the **Top-3 Best Checkpoints** per fold and average their predictions to mitigate single-epoch volatility (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**).
-   **No TTA:** Strictly avoid Test-Time Augmentation with padding/shifting, as it introduces artifacts (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00074**).
-   **Final Inference:** Average the probability outputs of the CNN Ensemble (15 models $\times$ 3 snapshots) and the MLP Ensemble (5 models).
