# Optimized Strategy: Heterogeneous Ensemble with Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) and Large-Batch Optimization

### Model
The solution will implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** of three distinct CNN architectures to leverage the "Diversity of Errors" principle (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00043**), integrated with a **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)** mechanism to explicitly target generalization in this micro-data regime.
- **Backbones:**
    1.  **ResNet-18:** The proven baseline for capacity/generalization balance (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**).
    2.  **EfficientNet-B0:** For parameter efficiency and MBConv feature extraction.
    3.  **DenseNet-121:** For feature reuse and robustness in small-data regimes.
- **Structural Innovation (SWA):** Instead of relying on the final model weights or Exponential Moving Average (EMA) (which failed due to decay rate sensitivity in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**), the solution will implement **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**.
    - *Mechanism:* After the initial convergence phase, the optimizer will switch to a constant, low learning rate. The model weights will be averaged at the end of each epoch in this final phase.
    - *Rationale:* SWA finds a solution centered in the flat region of the loss landscape. On small datasets where standard training often converges to sharp, non-generalizable minima (overfitting), SWA provides a robust, flatter solution without the lag issues of EMA or the complexity of custom heads.
- **Input Adaptation:** Strictly adhere to the **3-Channel Rule** by replicating the single-channel spectrograms 3 times to form pseudo-RGB images. This preserves the pre-trained weights' integrity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).

### Data
The pipeline will prioritize the "Golden Recipe" of domain-curated features and rigid spatial adherence.
- **Source Selection:** Strictly use the **Provided Standard Spectrograms** (`supplemental_data/spectrograms`). Explicitly exclude "Filtered Spectrograms" to avoid signal loss (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00048**) and avoid raw audio generation (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00028**).
- **Preprocessing:** Resize all spectrograms to **224x224**. This rigid adherence to the pre-trained model's spatial expectations is superior to tiling or custom aspect ratios for this micro-dataset (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00030**).
- **Augmentation Pipeline:**
    1.  **Horizontal Translation:** Apply time-shifting using **Zero-Padding** (not wrapping) to preserve temporal causality (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00033**).
    2.  **Photometric Augmentation:** Apply **Brightness and Contrast Jitter** to mimic Gain and SNR variations (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
    3.  **Mixup:** Apply **Mixup Regularization** to prevent overfitting (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
    4.  **Constraint:** Strictly **disable Horizontal Flipping** (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).

### Training
- **Splitting Strategy:** Implement **Iterative Stratification** (5 Folds) to ensure representative distribution of rare classes (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**).
- **Optimization Strategy (Large Effective Batch Size):** Implement **Gradient Accumulation** to achieve an effective batch size of **64** (e.g., 2 accumulation steps if physical BS is 32).
    - *Rationale:* Previous experiments failed when batch size was reduced (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**). Large batches are critical for stabilizing Batch Normalization statistics and ensuring a diverse pool of sample pairs for Mixup regularization.
- **Regularization:** Maintain **High Weight Decay** (e.g., `1e-2`) to strictly control model complexity (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**).
- **Loss Function:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss**. Avoid specialized imbalance losses like ASL which suppress necessary gradients in small datasets (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**).
- **Scheduler:** Use a **Monotonic Cosine Annealing** scheduler for the initial training phase (75% of epochs), followed by a **Constant Learning Rate** for the SWA phase (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**).
- **Validation:** Use a **Robust Metric Implementation** that explicitly handles missing classes in validation batches to prevent premature stopping (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00038**).

### Evaluation
- **Ensemble Inference:** The final prediction will be an average of **15 models** (3 Architectures $\times$ 5 Folds).
- **Process:** Pass the test set spectrograms (resized to 224x224) through all 15 SWA-averaged models and average the resulting probabilities. This combines the variance reduction of the heterogeneous ensemble with the generalization benefits of SWA.
