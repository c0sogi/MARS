Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the **Band-Adaptive Multi-Resolution Network with Temporal Attention**.

This solution introduces two key **Structural Innovations**:
1.  **Band-Adaptive Feature Engineering:** It resolves the conflict between Idea 8 (successful bandwidth filtering) and Idea 10 (failed multi-resolution) by strictly partitioning the input into biologically relevant frequency bands with matched temporal resolutions.
2.  **Temporal Attention Pooling:** It replaces the standard Global Average Pooling of CNNs with an attention mechanism, addressing the specific challenge of the "central 10 seconds" label by allowing the model to dynamically focus on relevant time segments within the 50-second window.

# Model

The architecture is a dual-stream Convolutional Neural Network that fuses fine-grained, multi-scale morphology with long-term context.

1.  **Stream A: Band-Adaptive Morphological Encoder (50s Window)**
    *   **Input:** A unified tensor containing **3 distinct Mel-Spectrograms** for each of the 19 EEG channels, stacked depth-wise. Unlike Idea 10, these are not broadband; they are strictly band-limited to maximize Signal-to-Noise Ratio **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00022}**.
        *   **Band 1 (Delta/Theta):** 0.5–8 Hz. Generated with a **Long STFT Window** (e.g., 2.0s) to maximize frequency resolution for detecting rhythmic patterns like GRDA/LRDA.
        *   **Band 2 (Alpha/Beta):** 8–25 Hz. Generated with a **Short STFT Window** (e.g., 0.2s) to maximize time resolution for detecting transient spikes and seizure onsets.
        *   **Band 3 (Broadband Morph):** 0.5–25 Hz. Generated with a balanced window (e.g., 1.0s) to capture general morphology.
    *   **Tensor Shape:** `(Frequency, Time, Depth = 19 channels × 3 bands = 57)`.
    *   **Adapter:**
        *   **SE-Block & Projection:** A Squeeze-and-Excitation block followed by a learnable `1x1 Convolution` projects the 57 channels to 3. This preserves the "Unified Input Tensor" strategy **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00021}** and allows the model to dynamically attend to specific bands/channels **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}**.
    *   **Backbone:** **EfficientNet-B2** (Pretrained on ImageNet) to extract robust texture features **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00018}**.
    *   **Pooling Innovation (Temporal Attention):** Instead of Global Average Pooling (which destroys temporal information), we collapse the spatial frequency dimension and apply **Temporal Attention Pooling** to the time dimension of the feature map. This computes a weighted sum of time steps, allowing the model to focus on the labeled central 10 seconds or specific paroxysmal events, re-integrating the temporal precision concept from **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}** into the stable CNN architecture.

2.  **Stream B: Long-Term Context Encoder (10m Window)**
    *   **Input:** 10-minute Spectrograms (4 regions stacked depth-wise) **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00006}**.
    *   **Backbone:** **EfficientNet-B0** (Pretrained).
    *   **Role:** Provides orthogonal long-term context (e.g., background slowing) **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}**.

3.  **Fusion Head: Gated Context Mechanism**
    *   The context vector from Stream B modulates the detail vector from Stream A via element-wise multiplication with a sigmoid gate **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}**.

# Data

1.  **Stream A Preprocessing (Band-Adaptive Generation):**
    *   Load 50s raw EEG.
    *   Apply 3 parallel STFT operations with the specific window sizes and frequency cutoffs defined above (0.5-8Hz, 8-25Hz, 0.5-25Hz).
    *   Convert to Mel-Scale, Log-transform, and Resize to align spatial dimensions.
    *   Stack along the depth axis to create the 57-channel input. This maintains channel independence until the learned projection **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00006}**.
    *   Normalize to ImageNet statistics.

2.  **Stream B Preprocessing:**
    *   Load 10m spectrograms, Log-transform, resize to `(256, 256)`, and stack the 4 regions depth-wise.

3.  **Augmentation:**
    *   **SpecAugment:** Apply Frequency and Time masking to both streams **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00001}**.
    *   **MixUp:** Apply MixUp regularization to training batches.

# Training

*   **Loss Function:** **Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
*   **Strategy:** Use a single, consistent learning rate (e.g., `3e-4`) for the entire network. Since both backbones are **Pretrained**, we avoid the optimization instability of mixing scratch/pretrained models **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00016}**.
*   **Epochs:** Train for ~10-12 epochs with Early Stopping.

# Evaluation

    *   Load 10m Spectrogram $\to$ Stream B.
