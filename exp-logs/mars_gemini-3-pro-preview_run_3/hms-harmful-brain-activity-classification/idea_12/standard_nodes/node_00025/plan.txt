First, I will decrease the `BATCH_SIZE` in `runfile.py` from 64 to 32, which directly halves the memory required for activations and gradients. Second, I will modify `library/train.py` to explicitly delete the original input tensors (`x_a` and `x_b`) immediately after MixUp augmentation is applied, freeing up VRAM before the memory-intensive forward pass.
