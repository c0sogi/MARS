The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` is caused by using a `BATCH_SIZE` of 512, which is suitable for the previous NBOW model but too large for the memory-intensive `distilbert-base-uncased` Transformer model on the available GPU. To fix this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to 64 (Cite debug_lesson_2). Additionally, I will lower the `LEARNING_RATE` to `2e-5`, which is the standard range for fine-tuning Transformers, as the previous value of `2e-3` (intended for NBOW) would likely lead to divergence or instability even if the memory issue were resolved.
