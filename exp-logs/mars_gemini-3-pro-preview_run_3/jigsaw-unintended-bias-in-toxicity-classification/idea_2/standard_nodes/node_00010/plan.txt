To optimize the model's performance on the bias metrics, I will implement **Sample Weighting** in the loss function. While the current solution uses Multi-Task Learning (MTL) and Stochastic Identity Masking (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) to learn robust features, the standard Binary Cross-Entropy loss treats all examples equally. However, the competition metric disproportionately penalizes errors on specific subsets:
1.  **Non-Toxic Identity Mentions (Hard Negatives)**: These are critical for the BPSN metric.
2.  **Toxic Identity Mentions (Hard Positives)**: These are critical for the BNSP and Subgroup metrics.

By assigning higher weights to these "hard" examples during training, we force the model to prioritize distinguishing the *context* of toxicity from the mere *presence* of identity terms. I will modify the `ToxicityDataset` to calculate these weights and the `Trainer` to apply them to the main task loss. I will also optimize the `Collator` to convert numpy arrays to tensors more efficiently, addressing the performance warning observed in the logs.
