I will optimize the solution by implementing Multi-Task Learning (MTL) and training on the full dataset with Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP).

1.  **Multi-Task Learning (MTL):** I will modify the model to predict 6 auxiliary toxicity attributes (e.g., `identity_attack`, `severe_toxicity`) in addition to the main target. By explicitly supervising the model on "Identity Attack", we force the shared representation to distinguish between the mere mention of an identity (which shouldn't be toxic) and an attack on that identity. This directly addresses the BPSN/BNSP bias metrics, applying the principle that contextual embeddings can disentangle correlated signals (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).
2.  **Full Dataset & Extended Training:** I will remove the data subsampling to train on the full 1.44M example dataset and increase training to 2 epochs. Bias mitigation requires high data density to correctly learn decision boundaries for rare identity subgroups.
3.  **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP):** To ensure this increased workload fits within the 4-hour limit on the A100 GPU, I will implement PyTorch's `GradScaler` and `autocast`.
