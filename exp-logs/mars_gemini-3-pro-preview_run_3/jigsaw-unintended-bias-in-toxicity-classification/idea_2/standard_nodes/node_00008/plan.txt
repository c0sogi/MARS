The issue is located in `runfile.py` where the validation loop collects predictions and targets for all output classes (main toxicity + 6 auxiliary tasks), resulting in arrays of shape `(N, 7)`. The `JigsawEvaluator` expects 1D arrays of shape `(N,)` (representing only the main toxicity score) to perform broadcasting operations against 1D identity columns. Passing the multi-column arrays causes a `ValueError` due to shape mismatch during the bitwise operations in the evaluator.

To fix this, I will modify the validation loop in `runfile.py` to extract only the first column (index 0) of the probabilities and targets, which corresponds to the main toxicity score required for the competition metric.
