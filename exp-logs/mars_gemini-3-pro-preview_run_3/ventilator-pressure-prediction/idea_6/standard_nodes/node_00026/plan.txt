Based on the lessons learned, specifically **Lesson 00025**, the previous "Physics Skip Connection" (concatenating raw inputs to the final fusion layer) was detrimental because it allowed the model to bypass the temporal integrators (LSTM/TCN) required to model the pressure-volume relationship. I will remove this skip connection to revert the architecture to the more robust "CAP-Net" style. Additionally, referencing **Lesson 00018**, I will maintain the small batch size (128) for better generalization but significantly increase the epoch count (from 6 to 35) and remove the batch limit per epoch to train on the full dataset, ensuring the model has sufficient capacity and data to converge to the target metric.
