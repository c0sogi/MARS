### Strategy: Pyramidal Context-Aware Hybrid Network (PCA-Net)

Based on the progression of insights from the provided lessons, I propose the **Pyramidal Context-Aware Hybrid Network (PCA-Net)**. This strategy resolves the conflict between the "Simple LSTM" (Lesson 14) and the "Hybrid" approaches by addressing the specific structural and training deficiencies—namely, channel bottlenecks and batch size sensitivity—that previously held back the Hybrid architecture. It combines the physical decoupling of the TCN-LSTM backbone with rigorous context injection and a generalization-focused training regime.

#### 1. Model: Pyramidal Context-Aware Backbone
The architecture retains the parallel branch design to structurally separate high-frequency (resistive) and low-frequency (elastic) dynamics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**, but introduces two critical structural innovations to maximize expressivity and physical fidelity.

*   **Input Layer: Contextual Parameter Injection**
    *   Instead of feeding generic control signals to the deep networks, the static lung attributes ($R, C$) are concatenated with the dynamic control features (Flow, Acceleration, Volume) **at every time step**. This ensures that the deep layers condition their learned dynamics (e.g., dampening rates) on the specific physical constants of the lung from the very first layer, correcting the "Late Fusion" flaw. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.

*   **Branch 1: Pyramidal TCN (The Resistive Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models the fast, derivative-dependent resistive pressure ($R \cdot \dot{V}$).
    *   **Structure:** A Temporal Convolutional Network with dilated convolutions. Crucially, this branch implements **Pyramidal Channel Scaling**, where the number of feature channels doubles as the dilation factor increases (e.g., 64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 256). This ensures that as the receptive field grows to capture longer-range dependencies, the network capacity increases proportionally to prevent information bottlenecks. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00024**.

*   **Branch 2: Deep Bidirectional LSTM (The Elastic Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models the slow, integral-dependent elastic pressure ($\frac{V}{C}$).
    *   **Structure:** A deep Bidirectional LSTM (2-3 layers). This component is retained for its superior ability to model state accumulation (integration) over time, which aligns perfectly with the volume-pressure relationship. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.

*   **Fusion & Physics Skip Connection**
    *   The outputs of the Pyramidal TCN and the LSTM are concatenated.
    *   Additionally, the raw engineered physics terms ($R, C, R \cdot u_{in}, V/C$) are concatenated again at this fusion point (Skip Connection) to ensure the final regression head has direct, uncompressed access to the governing terms of the Equation of Motion. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.

#### 2. Data: Robust PID Pipeline with Integrity Controls
We will employ a comprehensive feature engineering pipeline that represents the full PID state, protected by strict integrity controls to prevent silent failures.

*   **Feature Engineering (PID + Physics):**
    *   **Proportional (P):** Raw `u_in`.
    *   **Integral (I):** Cumulative sum of `u_in` (Volume proxy). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
    *   **Derivative (D):** First and second finite differences of `u_in` (Velocity and Acceleration proxies). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    *   **Interactions:** Pre-computed physics terms $R \cdot u_{in}$ and $Volume / C$.

*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   **Unified Configuration:** A single configuration object will define both the dataframe column generation and the model input dimension to guarantee synchronization. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   **Automated Cache Invalidation:** The script will automatically detect and delete stale `.npy` or `.pt` files at the start of execution to ensure the model trains on the most up-to-date feature set. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.

#### 3. Training: Noise-Injected Optimization
The training regime is fundamentally altered to prioritize generalization over hardware throughput, addressing the specific failure mode of previous Hybrid attempts.

*   **Loss Function:** **Masked L1 Loss (MAE)**. The loss is calculated strictly where `u_out == 0` (inspiratory phase) to focus capacity on the scored metric. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Batch Size:** We will strictly use a **Small Batch Size (128)**. Previous experiments showed that large batches (512+) trap the Hybrid model in sharp local minima. Smaller batches introduce gradient noise that acts as implicit regularization, allowing the complex hybrid surface to converge to a robust solution (MAE ~0.36 vs 0.60). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with `ReduceLROnPlateau` to fine-tune the parallel branches.

#### 4. Evaluation
*   The test set will be processed using the identical `FEATURE_CONFIG` and cache-cleared pipeline.
*   Inference will be performed in batches, and predictions will be flattened and joined with `id` for submission.
