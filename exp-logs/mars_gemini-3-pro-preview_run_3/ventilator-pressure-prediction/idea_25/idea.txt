### 1. Model: Momentum-Corrected Residual-Hybrid (MCRH-Net)

The architecture retains the **Parallel Hybrid** design to structurally decouple resistive and elastic dynamics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**, utilizing the deep residual backbone that has proven best for local fidelity, but with specific configuration constraints.

*   **Branch 1: Deep Residual Dense TCN (The Resistive Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models high-frequency, derivative-dependent dynamics ($P \propto R \cdot \dot{V}$).
    *   **Structure:** A deep stack of **Residual Dense Blocks** (Input $\to$ Conv $\to$ BN $\to$ GELU $\to$ Dropout $\to$ Conv $\to$ BN $\to$ GELU $\to$ Dropout $\to$ Add). This allows for significant depth without optimization degradation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00075**.
    *   **Geometry:**
        *   **Dilation = 1 (Dense):** We strictly use dense convolutions to preserve high-fidelity local information for derivative modeling, avoiding sparsity artifacts **Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**.
        *   **Kernel Size = 9:** We use large kernels to smooth physical signals and approximate derivatives better than small kernels **Cite solution_lesson_node_00062**.
        *   **Centered Padding:** We use non-causal padding to exploit the offline nature of the task **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.
    *   **Scaling:** Channel capacity increases progressively (e.g., 64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 512) to match receptive field growth **Cite solution_lesson_node_00024**.

*   **Branch 2: High-Capacity Bidirectional LSTM (The Elastic Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models low-frequency, integral-dependent dynamics ($P \propto V/C$).
    *   **Capacity:** A **3-layer Bidirectional LSTM** with **512 hidden units**. We prioritize capacity here to serve as the system's numerical integrator **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**.

*   **Fusion Head: Wide-Latent Integration**
    *   **Structure:** The outputs of both branches are **concatenated** and projected to a **Wide Hidden Layer (e.g., 1024 units)** before the final output. This prevents information bottlenecks **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.
    *   **Constraint:** We explicitly **exclude input-output skip connections**, forcing gradients through the LSTM integrator **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.

### 2. Data: Complete Kinematic State Engineering

We employ a rigorous feature engineering pipeline that resolves the conflict between "Momentum" and "Intent" by explicitly including both, while correcting previous exclusions.

*   **Kinematic Features:**
    *   **Backward Velocity (Momentum):** Explicitly compute $u_{in}(t) - u_{in}(t-1)$. This captures the instantaneous physical momentum acting on the system, which was missing in Idea 23 **Cite solution_lesson_node_00090**.
    *   **Forward Lookahead (Intent):** Explicitly include raw shifted columns for $u_{in}$ at $t+1 \dots t+4$ to provide zero-lag future context to the dense layers **Cite solution_lesson_node_00051**.
*   **Physical State:**
    *   **Numerical Integration:** Calculate Volume ($Area$) using physically accurate integration $\sum u_{in} \cdot dt$ **Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**.
    *   **Time-Delta (`dt`):** Explicitly include $dt$ to allow learning of variable-rate physics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00067**.
    *   **Input Completeness:** Explicitly **retain `u_out`** to allow the Bi-LSTM to explain expiratory drops during backpropagation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00072**.
    *   **Physics Interactions:** Pre-compute $R \cdot u_{in}$ and $Area / C$ and concatenate to input **Cite solution_lesson_node_00046**.

### 3. Training: Corrected Regularization Regime

We enforce a training regime that specifically corrects the "Regularization Sensitivity" failure of Idea 24.

*   **Regularization Correction:** We strictly use **Low Weight Decay (e.g., 1e-4)**. Previous attempts failed because high weight decay (`1e-2`) on unscaled regression targets (0-64 cmH2O) penalized the large weights necessary to scale the output, causing underfitting **Cite solution_lesson_node_00093**.
*   **Batch Size:** Strictly use a **Batch Size of 128**. This balances gradient noise for generalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018** with stability for Batch Normalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00076**.
*   **Gradient Clipping:** Apply `clip_grad_norm_(max_norm=1.0)` to prevent exploding gradients in the hybrid architecture **Cite solution_lesson_node_00065**.
*   **Budget:** Train for **80+ epochs** to synchronize distinct gradient dynamics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039**.
*   **Dataset:** Train on the **Full Training Set (100%)** to avoid phase transition failures **Cite solution_lesson_node_00054**.
*   **Loss Function:** **Masked L1 Loss (MAE)**, calculated strictly on the inspiratory phase (`u_out == 0`) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
