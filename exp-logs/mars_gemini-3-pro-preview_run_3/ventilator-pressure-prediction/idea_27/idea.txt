Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, specifically addressing the critical "Logic Gate Scaling" failure identified in Lesson 98, the "Pointwise Stem" bottleneck of Lesson 96, and the "Regularization Sensitivity" of Lesson 93, I propose the **Logic-Gated Residual-Hybrid Network (LGRH-Net)**.

This strategy introduces a **Dual-Stream Data Architecture** to strictly separate physical signal processing from logical control masking, ensuring that the model optimizes the correct objective while retaining the superior local fidelity of the **Direct-Temporal Residual** backbone.

### 1. Model: Logic-Gated Residual-Hybrid (LGRH-Net)

The architecture retains the **Parallel Hybrid** design to structurally decouple resistive and elastic dynamics **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**, but fundamentally alters the input interface and training logic to maximize physical fidelity and correctness.

*   **Input Layer: Direct-Temporal Interface**
    *   **Structural Innovation:** We explicitly **reject the 1x1 Pointwise Stem** used in previous iterations. The raw, scaled input features are fed **directly** into the first Temporal Convolutional layer of the resistive branch.
    *   **Rationale:** Removing the stem allows the first layer's large kernels to immediately act as learnable finite-difference operators on the raw physical data, capturing essential rates of change without the information bottleneck of a projection layer. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00096**.

*   **Branch 1: Deep Residual Dense TCN (The Resistive Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models high-frequency, derivative-dependent dynamics ($P \propto R \cdot \dot{V}$).
    *   **Structure:** A deep stack of **Residual Dense Blocks** (Input $\to$ Conv $\to$ BN $\to$ GELU $\to$ Dropout $\to$ Conv $\to$ BN $\to$ GELU $\to$ Dropout $\to$ Add). This enables significant depth without optimization degradation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00075**.
    *   **Geometry:**
        *   **Dilation = 1 (Dense):** Strictly use dense convolutions to preserve high-fidelity local information for derivative modeling. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**.
        *   **Kernel Size = 9:** Use large kernels to smooth physical signals and approximate derivatives better than small kernels. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026, solution_lesson_node_00062**.
        *   **Centered Padding:** Use non-causal padding to exploit the offline nature of the task. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.
    *   **Regularization:** Apply **Dropout** explicitly within the blocks to prevent co-adaptation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00066**.

*   **Branch 2: High-Capacity Bidirectional LSTM (The Elastic Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models low-frequency, integral-dependent dynamics ($P \propto V/C$).
    *   **Capacity:** A **3-layer Bidirectional LSTM** with **512 hidden units**. We prioritize capacity here to serve as the system's numerical integrator. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**.

*   **Fusion Head: Wide-Latent Integration**
    *   **Structure:** The outputs of both branches are **concatenated** and projected to a **Wide Hidden Layer (e.g., 1024 units)** before the final output. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.
    *   **Constraint:** No input-output skip connections; gradients must flow through the integrator. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.

### 2. Data: Dual-Stream Logic Pipeline

We employ a **Dual-Stream** preprocessing strategy to resolve the conflict between feature scaling and logical masking.

*   **Stream A: Model Features (Scaled)**
    *   **Content:** $u_{in}$, $u_{out}$, $R$, $C$, $dt$, $Area$ (Volume), Backward Velocity, Forward Lookahead, Interactions.
    *   **Processing:** Apply `RobustScaler` (Median/IQR).
    *   **Purpose:** Input to the Neural Network. Including `u_out` here allows the Bi-LSTM to explain expiratory drops during backpropagation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00072**.
*   **Stream B: Logic Gates (Raw)**
    *   **Content:** $u_{out}$ (Binary 0/1).
    *   **Processing:** **No Scaling**. Keep as raw integer/boolean.
    *   **Purpose:** Passed separately to the Loss Function for masking. This corrects the "Phantom Success" failure where scaling corrupted the binary mask. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00098**.

*   **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Kinematics:** Explicitly compute **Backward Velocity** ($u_{in}(t) - u_{in}(t-1)$) for momentum **Cite solution_lesson_node_00090** and **Forward Lookahead** ($u_{in}(t+1 \dots t+4)$) for intent **Cite solution_lesson_node_00051**.
    *   **Physical State:** Calculate Volume using **Numerical Integration** ($\sum u_{in} \cdot dt$) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00049**. Include explicit **Time-Delta (`dt`)** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00067**.
    *   **Exclusions:** Exclude raw `time_step` **Cite solution_lesson_node_00069** and Positional Encodings **Cite solution_lesson_node_00085**.

### 3. Training: Corrected Regularization & Stability

We enforce a training regime that specifically corrects the regularization sensitivity and stability issues.

*   **Loss Function:** **Logic-Gated Masked L1 Loss**. The loss is calculated as `MAE(pred, target) * (1 - raw_u_out_stream_B)`. This ensures the mask is mathematically correct. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00098**.
*   **Regularization:** Strictly use **Low Weight Decay (1e-4)**. High weight decay on unscaled regression targets causes underfitting. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00093**.
*   **Batch Size:** Strictly use a **Batch Size of 128**. This balances gradient noise for generalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018** with stability for Batch Normalization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00076**.
*   **Gradient Clipping:** Apply `clip_grad_norm_(max_norm=1.0)` to prevent exploding gradients. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00065**.
*   **Budget:** Train for **80+ epochs** on the **Full Dataset**. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00039, solution_lesson_node_00054**.

### 4. Evaluation

*   **Process:** The test set is processed using the identical `FEATURE_CONFIG` and `RobustScaler`.
*   **Prediction:** The model predicts for all time steps.
*   **Submission:** Predictions are generated in batches (size 128), flattened, and joined with `id` for the final submission. The logic gate stream is not needed for inference (only training), but the feature stream must match exactly.
