I will optimize the previous solution by reverting the detrimental addition of Positional Encodings and scaling up the architecture's capacity and training duration, based on specific lessons learned.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Remove Positional Encodings**: As identified in `solution_lesson_node_00085` and confirmed by the previous failure analysis (where PE features correlated highly with error), explicit sinusoidal encodings act as noise in architectures with inherent sequential bias (LSTMs/TCNs). I will remove them from `config.py` and `data.py`.
2.  **Increase CNN Branch Capacity**: `solution_lesson_node_00057` highlights that the CNN branch (modeling resistive dynamics) must have capacity commensurate with the LSTM branch. The previous `CNN_FILTERS=64` was likely a bottleneck. I will increase this to `256` in `config.py`.
3.  **Increase Training Epochs**: Hybrid architectures require longer to converge (`solution_lesson_node_00039`). The previous 12 epochs were insufficient (Val MAE 0.74). I will increase this to 50 epochs in `runfile.py` to allow the model to reach the target metric (~0.16).
4.  **Architecture Renaming**: I will rename the model class from `PERDHNet` to `CWDHNet` (Corrected Wide-Context Dense-Hybrid Network) in `model.py` and `runfile.py` to reflect the removal of PEs and alignment with the successful CWDH-Net architecture described in `solution_lesson_node_00075`.
