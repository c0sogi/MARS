The `AttributeError` occurs because `RDHNet` in `library/model.py` references `Config.CNN_FILTERS` and `Config.CNN_BLOCKS`, which are undefined in `library/config.py`. The configuration instead defines `CNN_CHANNELS`, a list of channel sizes intended for a pyramidal architecture. Additionally, the `ResidualDenseBlock` instantiation incorrectly uses a `channels` argument instead of `in_channels` and `out_channels`.

To fix this, I will update `RDHNet` to utilize `Config.CNN_CHANNELS`. I will rewrite the CNN branch initialization to iterate through the channel list, correctly chaining `in_channels` and `out_channels` for each block to ensure proper tensor dimensionality flow (Cite debug_lesson_8). I will also update the fusion head to determine its input dimension from the final layer of the CNN branch.
