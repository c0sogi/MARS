### Strategy: Robust Pyramidal Context-Aware Network (RPC-Net)

Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous successes and failures, I propose the **Robust Pyramidal Context-Aware Network (RPC-Net)**. This strategy synthesizes the structural decoupling of the Hybrid architecture with the rigorous capacity and data handling requirements identified in the lessons. It specifically corrects the "capacity mismatch" of Idea 7, the "additive flaw" of Idea 8, and the "feature scaling" issues of previous iterations.

#### 1. Model: Deeply Contextualized High-Capacity Hybrid
The architecture employs a parallel branch design to structurally separate the modeling of resistive (fast) and elastic (slow) dynamics, while strictly enforcing channel capacity and input conditioning rules.

*   **Input Layer: Deep Contextual Injection**
    *   The model input at *every time step* consists of the dynamic PID features (Flow, Volume, Acceleration) concatenated with the static lung attributes ($R, C$) and interaction terms ($R \cdot u_{in}$, $V/C$).
    *   **Rationale:** This ensures that both the convolutional and recurrent layers condition their internal state transitions on the physical constants from the very first layer, allowing the network to learn parameter-specific dynamics (e.g., dampening) rather than generic responses. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**.

*   **Branch 1: Pyramidal Wide-Kernel TCN (The Resistive Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models high-frequency, derivative-dependent dynamics ($R \cdot \dot{V}$).
    *   **Structure:** A Dilated Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN).
    *   **Innovation 1 (Scaling):** Implements **Pyramidal Channel Scaling** (e.g., 64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 512). As dilation increases the receptive field, channel depth increases to prevent information bottlenecks. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00024**.
    *   **Innovation 2 (Geometry):** Uses a **Kernel Size of 7**. Larger kernels are strictly preferred over small kernels (size 3) to smooth the physical signal and accurately approximate local derivatives. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**.
    *   **Activation:** Uses **GELU** activations to improve gradient flow and convergence speed. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.

*   **Branch 2: High-Capacity Bidirectional LSTM (The Elastic Stream)**
    *   **Function:** Models low-frequency, integral-dependent dynamics ($V/C$).
    *   **Structure:** A **3-layer Bidirectional LSTM** with a large hidden dimension (256 or 512).
    *   **Rationale:** We explicitly reject the "lightweight" RNN approach. The RNN acts as the numerical integrator for the Volume state; reducing its capacity leads to underfitting. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**.

*   **Fusion Head: Coupled Latent Integration**
    *   **Structure:** The outputs of the TCN and LSTM branches are **concatenated** and passed to a deep Dense MLP.
    *   **Constraint 1:** We explicitly **reject Additive Decomposition**. The head must be allowed to learn non-linear coupling terms between the branches. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**.
    *   **Constraint 2:** We explicitly **exclude the Input-Output Skip Connection**. The head must rely entirely on the deep latent states to prevent the gradient from bypassing the LSTM integrator. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.

#### 2. Data: Robust PID Pipeline with Integrity Controls
We upgrade the data pipeline to handle outliers and ensure synchronization.

*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Robust Scaling:** Replace `StandardScaler` with `RobustScaler` (using Median and IQR). This prevents extreme valve setting values from dominating the gradients and accelerates convergence. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.
*   **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **PID State:** Compute `u_in` (Proportional), `u_in_cumsum` (Integral), and first/second finite differences (Derivative/Acceleration). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    *   **Physics Interactions:** Pre-compute $R \cdot u_{in}$ and $Volume / C$. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   **Unified Configuration:** Define a single `FEATURE_CONFIG` to synchronize generation and model input. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   **Automated Cache Invalidation:** Automatically delete stale `.npy` files at the start of execution to ensure the model trains on the new Robust-Scaled features. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.

#### 3. Training: Noise-Injected Optimization
The training regime prioritizes generalization and convergence in the complex hybrid landscape.

*   **Loss Function:** **Masked L1 Loss (MAE)**. Calculated only where `u_out == 0` (inspiratory phase). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Batch Size:** Strictly use a **Small Batch Size (128)**. This introduces gradient noise to act as implicit regularization, preventing the hybrid model from trapping in sharp local minima. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with `ReduceLROnPlateau`.
*   **Budget:** Train for **80 epochs**. The hybrid architecture with Robust Scaling converges efficiently but requires the full duration to fine-tune the coupled fusion layer. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.

#### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference:** The test set is processed using the identical `FEATURE_CONFIG` and `RobustScaler` pipeline.
*   **Submission:** Predictions are generated in batches (size 128), flattened, and joined with `id` for the final submission.
