My optimization strategy focuses on refining the physics-based feature engineering and extending the training duration to ensure convergence, leveraging the available time budget.

1.  **Physics-Based Feature Refinement (Cite Lesson 1, Lesson 47)**: The previous solution used `u_in_cumsum` as a proxy for Volume. I will replace this with `area`, calculated as the cumulative sum of `u_in * dt` (flow $\times$ time delta). This provides a more physically accurate estimation of Volume ($V = \int \dot{V} dt$), accounting for any variations in sampling rate. I will also explicitly add `dt` (time delta) as a feature, which allows the model to understand temporal dynamics without overfitting to absolute timestamps (which were removed in the previous iteration).
2.  **Cache Invalidation (Cite Lesson 6)**: Since I am modifying the feature set, I will update the cache filenames (appending `_v2`) to force the data pipeline to re-process the raw data with the new features.
3.  **Architectural & Training Adjustments (Cite Lesson 26, Lesson 39)**:
    *   I will increase the TCN Kernel Size from 7 to 9 to provide a slightly broader receptive field for the resistive branch.
    *   I will increase the training budget from 35 to 80 epochs. The previous run completed in 10 minutes, so 80 epochs is well within the 4-hour limit and will allow the `ReduceLROnPlateau` scheduler to fine-tune the model for lower error.
