To improve the model's performance and reach the validation threshold, I will implement the following optimizations based on the provided lessons and failure analysis:

1.  **Architecture Balancing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010, solution_lesson_node_00014)**: The failure analysis showed a correlation between error and `time_step`, suggesting the model's ability to track the integral state (Volume) over the breath duration was insufficient compared to its ability to model instantaneous resistance. I will decouple the LSTM capacity from the TCN base dimension and increase the LSTM hidden size to 256. This ensures the LSTM branch (responsible for "slow" integral dynamics) has equal feature capacity (512 output features) to the TCN branch (512 output features), preventing the fusion head from being dominated by the "fast" TCN features.
2.  **Receptive Field Expansion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026)**: I will increase the TCN `KERNEL_SIZE` from 5 to 7. Larger kernels in the convolutional branch improve the approximation of local derivatives and smooth out noise in the continuous physical signal.
3.  **Training Convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018)**: I will increase `EPOCHS` to 100 and remove the debug override in `runfile.py`. Previous runs showed the loss was still decreasing at epoch 35, and successful solutions required ~60+ epochs.
4.  **Batch Size Preservation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018)**: I will maintain the `BATCH_SIZE` at 128 to leverage gradient noise for better generalization.
