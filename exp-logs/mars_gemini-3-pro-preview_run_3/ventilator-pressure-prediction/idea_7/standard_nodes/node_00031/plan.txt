The error `TypeError: ReduceLROnPlateau.__init__() got an unexpected keyword argument 'verbose'` is caused by the deprecation and removal of the `verbose` parameter in PyTorch 2.0+ schedulers. To fix this, I will remove the `verbose=True` argument from the `ReduceLROnPlateau` initialization in both `runfile.py` and `library/train.py`. Cite debug_lesson_1
