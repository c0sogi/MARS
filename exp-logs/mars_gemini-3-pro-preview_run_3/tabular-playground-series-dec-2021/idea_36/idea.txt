Based on the comprehensive analysis of the experimental history and the accumulated lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This approach introduces a **Deeply-Supervised Asymmetric Parallel Vector-DCN-ResNet**.

This structural evolution addresses the "Capacity vs. Optimization" paradox identified in previous experiments. While **Lesson 00054** established that depth is the primary lever for performance, **Lesson 00073** demonstrated that naive depth scaling (to 6 blocks) leads to optimization failure, even with specialized initialization. This strategy employs **Auxiliary Supervision** (Deep Supervision) to inject gradient signals directly into the intermediate layers, enabling the training of a deeper backbone (5 Blocks) without the stagnation observed previously, while strictly adhering to the **Asymmetric** (Lesson 00071), **Warm-Start** (Lesson 00066), and **Full Pre-Activation** (Lesson 00064) protocols.

# Model: Deeply-Supervised Asymmetric Parallel Vector-DCN-ResNet

I propose a hybrid architecture that processes inputs through two parallel branches, augmented with an auxiliary training head to stabilize the optimization of the deep backbone.

1.  **Input Layer:** The model will accept a dense concatenated vector of standardized continuous features and **raw one-hot encoded** binary features. We explicitly reject entity embeddings, adhering to **Cite Lesson 00011**.
2.  **Branch 1: Asymmetric Vector-Based Deep & Cross Network (Warm-Start):**
    *   **Asymmetric Depth:** We will strictly limit this branch to **3 Cross Layers**, decoupling it from the deeper backbone to avoid high-order noise **Cite Lesson 00071**.
    *   **Mechanism:** We will use **Vector-based (Rank-1) Cross Layers** with **Dot-Product Mixing** ($x_{l+1} = x_0 \odot (x_l^T w) + b + x_l$) **Cite Lesson 00032**.
    *   **Initialization:** We will initialize the learnable weight vectors $w$ with a **Near-Zero Standard Deviation** (e.g., $\mathcal{N}(0, 10^{-4})$) to ensures the branch starts as an approximate identity mapping **Cite Lesson 00066**.
3.  **Branch 2: Deeply-Supervised Full Pre-Activation ResNet:**
    *   **Structural Scaling:** We will scale the backbone to **5 Residual Blocks** to increase representational capacity beyond the current 4-block limit, leveraging the insight that capacity drives performance **Cite Lesson 00054**.
    *   **Topology:** We will use **Full Pre-Activation Blocks** (`Input -> BN -> ReLU -> Dropout -> Linear -> BN -> ReLU -> Dropout -> Linear -> Add(Input)`) **Cite Lesson 00064**. We reject Swish **Cite Lesson 00076** and Zero-Gamma initialization **Cite Lesson 00073**.
    *   **Structural Innovation (Deep Supervision):** We will attach an **Auxiliary Classification Head** (Linear Layer) to the output of **Block 3**. This head will predict the target class during training, injecting strong gradient signals into the middle of the network. This directly counteracts the vanishing gradient/optimization stagnation issues that caused the 6-block model to fail **Cite Lesson 00073**, allowing us to train the deeper 5-block stack effectively.
4.  **Combination Head:** The output of the final ResNet block and the DCN branch will be concatenated and passed through the primary linear classification layer.

# Data: Augmented Physics-Informed Engineering

We will employ a feature engineering paradigm that prioritizes physical consistency and **Augmentation over Replacement**.

1.  **Cyclical Augmentation:** We will calculate `Aspect_Sin` and `Aspect_Cos` but **retain the raw `Aspect` column**. **Cite Lesson 00034** demonstrated that replacing the raw value destroys the direct magnitude signal utilized by ReLU networks.
2.  **Geometric Magnitude:** We will explicitly calculate the **Euclidean Distance to Hydrology** ($\sqrt{\text{Horizontal}^2 + \text{Vertical}^2}$) **Cite Lesson 00016**.
3.  **Directional Preservation:** We will calculate **Absolute Hydrology Elevation** (`Elevation - Vertical_Distance`) to preserve water flow directionality **Cite Lesson 00019**.
4.  **Global Context:** We will include **Mean Distance to Amenities** (Hydrology, Roadways, Fire Points) **Cite Lesson 00009**.
5.  **Preprocessing:** Continuous features will be standardized. Binary features will remain as 0/1 integers.

# Training: Multi-Loss Optimization & Aggressive Scheduling

The training pipeline is designed to enforce convergence in the deeper architecture.

1.  **Loss Function:** We will minimize a weighted sum of the primary and auxiliary losses: $L_{total} = L_{primary} + 0.3 \times L_{aux}$. Both terms will use Cross Entropy. The auxiliary loss is active only during training.
2.  **Optimizer:** We will strictly use **AdamW** (Decoupled Weight Decay) **Cite Lesson 00058**.
3.  **Scheduler:** We will employ a **ReduceLROnPlateau** scheduler with an **aggressive decay factor of 0.1**. **Cite Lesson 00068** demonstrated that aggressive decay is required to freeze the model into a sharp minimum.
4.  **Regularization:** We will maintain a **Dropout rate of 0.2** within the blocks **Cite Lesson 00056**.
5.  **Budgeting:** We will use a batch size of **4096** and strictly set the training duration to **60 Epochs** **Cite Lesson 00022**.
6.  **System:** We will **disable strict CuDNN determinism** to maximize kernel performance **Cite Lesson 00070**.
7.  **Checkpointing:** We will implement Early Stopping with a callback that uses `copy.deepcopy(model.state_dict())` **Cite Lesson 00003**.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:** The test set will be processed in batches using the best-checkpointed model weights (restored via the deepcopy mechanism). The auxiliary head will be discarded during inference; predictions will be generated solely from the primary head.
2.  **Prediction:** The class with the highest softmax probability will be selected for each `Id`, and the results will be formatted into the required CSV submission file.
