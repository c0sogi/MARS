This solution synthesizes the architectural "sweet spot" of **Idea 3**, the optimization rigor of **Lesson 00019**, and the resolution insights of **Lesson 00030**, while introducing a novel **Multi-Stage Inference Pipeline** to address the open-set ranking problem.

### 1. Model: EfficientNet-B2 with Linear ArcFace
**Architecture:**
*   **Backbone:** I will utilize the **EfficientNet-B2** architecture.
    *   *Reasoning:* This backbone provides the optimal balance between feature extraction capacity and convergence speed (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**). It avoids the underfitting issues observed with larger backbones like B4 in this sparse-data regime (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**).
*   **Pooling:** I will employ **Global Average Pooling (GAP)**.
    *   *Reasoning:* I will strictly avoid learnable pooling layers (like GeM), which were shown to introduce instability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).
*   **Neck (Crucial Correction):** The projection head will consist of `BatchNorm -> Dropout -> Linear -> BatchNorm`.
    *   *Reasoning:* I will explicitly **exclude** any non-linear activation functions (like PReLU) before the final embedding layer. **Lesson 00029** demonstrated that non-linearities distort the hypersphere manifold, causing model collapse.
*   **Head:** I will use the **ArcFace (Additive Angular Margin)** head.
    *   *Reasoning:* ArcFace is proven to be superior to Triplet Loss for high-cardinality tasks (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**) and more robust than adaptive margins (AdaFace) when batch sizes are small (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**).

### 2. Data: High-Resolution Geometric Consistency
*   **Preprocessing (Structural Innovation):** I will increase the input resolution to **512x512**.
    *   *Reasoning:* **Lesson 00030** proved that input resolution is a primary bottleneck, with 384px significantly outperforming 256px. Given the available A100 GPU, pushing to 512px will allow the model to resolve fine-grained fluke details (scars, notches) that are lost at lower resolutions.
*   **Augmentation:** I will strictly apply **Geometric Augmentations** (rotation, scaling, shear, flip).
    *   *Reasoning:* I will explicitly exclude occlusion-based augmentations (Cutout). As shown in **Lesson 00014**, occluding unique features in singleton classes is detrimental.
*   **Class Handling:** Training will be restricted to known `Id`s. `new_whale` images will be excluded from the training loop (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**).

### 3. Training: Stable Optimization Strategy
*   **Loss Function:** ArcFace Loss with a margin $m=0.5$ and scale $s=30$.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a learning rate of **3e-4** and a batch size of **16**.
    *   *Reasoning:* This specific configuration was the key to the success of the "Current Best Solution" in **Lesson 00019**. It avoids the underfitting caused by conservative learning rates (1e-4) and the instability caused by aggressive ones (1e-3) observed in **Lesson 00028**.
*   **Schedule:** 30 Epochs with Cosine Annealing.

### 4. Evaluation: Multi-Stage Retrieval Pipeline
**Structural Innovation (Inference):**
Instead of relying on a single cosine similarity lookup, I will implement a **Query Expansion (QE) + k-Reciprocal Re-ranking** pipeline.
*   **Validation Protocol:** I will use a **Retrieval-based Validation Loop** (Gallery=Train, Query=Val) on known identities only, ensuring alignment with the inference goal (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**).
*   **Inference Steps:**
    1.  **Initial Search:** Compute Cosine Similarity between Test images and the Training Gallery.
    2.  **Query Expansion (QE):** For each test image, retrieve the top $k$ neighbors. Compute a weighted average of the test embedding and these neighbor embeddings to form a "denoised" query vector, then re-query the gallery. This leverages the cleaner features of likely matches to overcome noise in the test image.
    3.  **k-Reciprocal Re-ranking:** Calculate the Jaccard distance between the neighbor sets of the query and the gallery images. This penalizes false positives that do not share a common context with the query.
    4.  **Open-Set Thresholding:** Apply a distance threshold to the final re-ranked scores. If the top match is too distant, predict `new_whale`. This robust metric allows for better separation of known vs. unknown whales compared to raw cosine distance.
