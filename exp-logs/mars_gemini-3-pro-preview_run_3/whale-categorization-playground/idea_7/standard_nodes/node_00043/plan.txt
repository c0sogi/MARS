To optimize the solution, I will switch from the dual-backbone ensemble back to a single, stronger **EfficientNet-B3** backbone. The previous ensemble attempt demonstrated that concatenating embeddings from a weaker model (B2) degraded the performance of the stronger model (B3) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042). Furthermore, I will increase the training duration from 12 to **24 epochs** to ensure the margin-based loss converges effectively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019). Finally, I will integrate **Query Expansion (QE)** into the inference pipeline before Re-ranking to refine the query embeddings using the manifold structure of the gallery (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
