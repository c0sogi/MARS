The `CUDA out of memory` error occurred in the `k_reciprocal_rerank` function because the Jaccard distance calculation involves broadcasting tensors to a shape of `(Batch, Gallery_Size, Total_Features)`. With the original `batch_size` of 100, this operation attempted to allocate approximately 15GB of VRAM, which exceeded the GPU's available capacity. To resolve this, I will reduce the `batch_size` for this specific calculation loop from 100 to 20. This reduction will lower the peak memory usage to approximately 3GB, allowing the operation to complete successfully within the hardware constraints. This applies the lesson of scaling batch sizes inversely to resource demands to prevent OOM errors (Cite debug_lesson_9).
