Based on the comprehensive analysis of the "Lessons Learned," particularly the critical need for **Magnitude Alignment** in composite features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00105), the superiority of **Finite-Difference Dynamics** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068), and the established success of the **Hybrid-Context Dual-Stream** architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091, solution_lesson_node_00025), I propose the **Scale-Aligned Dual-Stream GBDT with Rotational Energy Profiles**.

This solution structurally evolves the previous best approach (Idea 30) by correcting the feature engineering flaw identified in Lesson 105. Instead of combining raw physical and visual rates (which have incompatible scales), we introduce a **Scale-Aligned Residual Layer** to Stream A. Additionally, we augment Stream B with **Rotational Energy Profiles** to better distinguish controlled athletic cuts from uncontrolled tumbles, leveraging the "Hybrid Context" paradigm.

# Model

The architecture consists of two specialized **XGBoost** classifiers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016) operating on physically disentangled feature spaces with asymmetric complexity constraints.

1.  **Stream A: The Interaction Model (Consistency & Energy)**
    *   **Scope:** Predicts contact between two players (`player2 != 'G'`).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Conditional Early-Fusion GBDT** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
    *   **Structural Innovation:** **Scale-Aligned Cross-Modal Residuals**.
        *   Previous attempts to detect perspective artifacts by subtracting `Visual_Looming_Rate` from `Physical_Closure_Rate` failed because the magnitudes were mismatched (yards/s vs. IoU/s) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00105).
        *   This model applies **Dynamic Scaling** (e.g., Z-score normalization over the temporal window) to both signals *before* calculating the difference. This ensures the feature captures the *divergence in signal pattern* (the "Phantom Approach" artifact) rather than just the magnitude of the speed.
    *   **Base Features:** Fuses **Relational Scalars** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00062), **System Energy** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00075), and **Visual Consensus Pyramids** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).

2.  **Stream B: The Impact Model (Hybrid Context & Rotational Energy)**
    *   **Scope:** Predicts contact between a player and the ground (`player2 == 'G'`).
    *   **Backbone:** A **Uni-Modal Hybrid GBDT**.
    *   **Structural Innovation:** **Rotational Energy Profiles**.
        *   Augments the Finite-Difference Ego-Dynamics with **Lateral Kinetic Energy** terms ($0.5 \cdot v_{sway}^2$).
        *   **Rationale:** While Ego-Jerk captures the *shock* of impact, Rotational Energy captures the *state* of the body. A "controlled cut" has high Sway Acceleration but stable Sway Energy. A "tumble" exhibits chaotic energy dissipation. Flattening this profile helps the tree distinguish the two (Extension of Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).
    *   **Constraint:** Retains **Hybrid Context** (Raw + Invariant) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091) but explicitly blocks visual features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

# Data

**Preprocessing:**
*   **Pipeline Integrity:**
    *   Implement **Configuration-Hashed Cache Invalidation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00093).
    *   Enforce **Strict Schema Validation**; raise a `RuntimeError` if features are missing/zero-filled (Cite solution_lesson_node_00088).
    *   Explicitly map `validation` mode to `train_player_tracking.csv` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00054).

**Feature Engineering - Stream A (Interaction):**
1.  **Relational Primitives:** Calculate **Euclidean Distance** and **Closure Rate** (Finite Difference: $-\Delta d / \Delta t$) at sparse exponential lags ($t, t\pm1... t\pm15$). Reject vector projections (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080).
2.  **Scale-Aligned Consistency (The Innovation):**
    *   Calculate raw `Closure_Rate` and `Visual_Looming_Rate` ($\Delta \text{Max\_IoU} / \Delta t$).
    *   Compute rolling mean/std for both over the macro window ($t\pm15$).
    *   Generate **Standardized Residual**: $\frac{\text{Closure} - \mu_C}{\sigma_C} - \frac{\text{Looming} - \mu_L}{\sigma_L}$. Flatten this sequence.
3.  **Visual Consensus:** Flatten `Max_IoU`, `Min_IoU`, and `IoU_Diff` at sparse lags ($t, t\pm4, t\pm8, t\pm15$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00074).
4.  **System Energy:** Flattened pyramids of Absolute Speed and Scalar Acceleration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00075).

**Feature Engineering - Stream B (Impact):**
1.  **Hybrid Context:**
    *   **Field-Centric:** Flattened pyramids of `x`, `y`, `speed`, `acceleration`, `direction`, `orientation` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091).
    *   **Ego-Centric Dynamics:**
        *   Project Velocity $v$ onto Orientation $\hat{o}$ $\rightarrow$ $v_{surge}, v_{sway}$.
        *   Differentiate $v \rightarrow a \rightarrow j$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00068).
        *   **Rotational Energy:** Calculate $E_{sway} = v_{sway}^2$.
    *   **Flattening:** Flatten all derived sequences to capture the trajectory shape (Cite solution_lesson_node_00086).
2.  **Exclusion:** Block all visual features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

# Training

*   **Sampling:** Apply **Targeted Majority Undersampling** (10:1 ratio) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00060).
*   **Algorithm:** Train both streams using **XGBoost** (`gpu_hist`) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Asymmetric Regularization:**
    *   **Stream A:** **Standard Depth** (`max_depth=6`) for complex interaction logic.
    *   **Stream B:** **Shallow Depth** (`max_depth=7`) and **Conservative Learning Rate** (0.02). This prevents the overfitting observed when training fast on noisy sensor derivatives (Cite solution_lesson_node_00105, solution_lesson_node_00070).
*   **Objective:** Minimize **Binary Logistic Loss**.

# Evaluation

1.  **Inference:**
    *   Route test samples to Stream A or Stream B based on the `contact_id`.
    *   Stream A receives the "Scale-Aligned Consistency" vector. Stream B receives the "Rotational Energy + Hybrid Context" vector.
2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Thresholding:** Decouple the decision boundary from the probability. Perform a linear search on the Validation set to find the optimal probability threshold **independently for each stream** that maximizes the Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002, solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Submission:** Apply these specific thresholds to the Test set predictions.
