The `TypeError: unhashable type: 'Series'` is caused by an index misalignment between the DataFrame `df_main` and the boolean mask `missing_mask`. `df_main` retains a non-contiguous index from `df_stream_a` (a subset of the original data), while `missing_mask` is derived from `df_merged`, which has a default `RangeIndex` generated by `pd.merge`. When `df_main.loc[missing_mask]` is called, pandas attempts to align the indices, fails, and incorrectly treats the Series as a key. The fix involves two steps: 1) Using `.values` on the mask to extract the underlying boolean array, bypassing index alignment (Cite debug_lesson_19), and 2) Resetting the index of the working DataFrame in `generate_stream_a_features` to ensure a consistent `RangeIndex` throughout the pipeline, guaranteeing alignment with downstream merge operations.
