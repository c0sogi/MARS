The previous solution exhibited clear signs of overfitting, with validation log-loss increasing significantly in the later epochs for both streams (Stream A: 0.026 $\to$ 0.031, Stream B: 0.086 $\to$ 0.091). Despite `EARLY_STOPPING_ROUNDS` being defined in the configuration, it was not passed to the model's `fit` method, causing the training to continue unnecessarily and degrade generalization.

My optimization strategy focuses on:
1.  **Enabling Early Stopping**: I will modify `library/training.py` to pass `early_stopping_rounds` to the XGBoost `fit` method. This ensures the model state corresponds to the best validation performance, not the last epoch.
2.  **Regularization (Cite Lesson 00045)**: I will increase `min_child_weight` for Stream A from 1 to 3 in `library/config.py`. Stream A showed the most severe overfitting, and slightly increasing the required weight for leaf nodes will prune overly specific branches that memorize noise in the interaction features.
