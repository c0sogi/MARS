    *   Increase `UNDERSAMPLE_RATIO` from 1.0 to 5.0. A 1:1 ratio discards too much negative class diversity; a 1:5 ratio retains enough "hard negatives" to define the decision boundary while keeping the dataset size manageable.
    *   Increase `n_estimators` from 100 to 450 and adjust `learning_rate` (0.05-0.08). 100 trees resulted in underfitting; 450 trees with a slightly higher learning rate allows the model to converge to a better solution within the 8-minute limit.
