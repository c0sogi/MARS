The issue stems from a "many-to-many" merge explosion in the `generate_stream_a` method, caused by the `_load_helmets` function mapping multiple video frames (59.94 Hz) to a single tracking step (10 Hz) without aggregation. This results in duplicate rows for each player-step combination in the helmet data, causing the dataset size to balloon from ~4 million to ~426 million rows during the join, triggering a `numpy._core._exceptions._ArrayMemoryError`.

To fix this, I will modify `library/features.py` to aggregate the helmet data within `_load_helmets`. Specifically, I will group by `game_play`, `step`, `nfl_player_id`, and `view`, and calculate the mean of the bounding box coordinates. This ensures a 1-to-1 relationship between tracking steps and helmet boxes, preventing the memory explosion (Cite debug_lesson_3).
