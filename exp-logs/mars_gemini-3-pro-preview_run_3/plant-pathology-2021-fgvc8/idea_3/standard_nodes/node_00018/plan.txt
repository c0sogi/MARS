To counteract the increased risk of overfitting associated with a larger model, I will significantly increase the Stochastic Depth (`drop_path_rate`) to 0.4. Consequently, I will extend the training duration to 20 epochs to ensure convergence with this stronger regularization. Finally, I will update the submission threshold to the previous best score.
