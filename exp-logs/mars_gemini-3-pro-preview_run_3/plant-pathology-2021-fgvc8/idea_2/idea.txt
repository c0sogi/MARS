Here is a proposal for an optimized strategy based on the lessons learned.

### Model
We will transition the backbone architecture from ResNet-18 to **EfficientNet-B0**.
*   **Rationale**: Lesson 1 identified that the ResNet-18 baseline exhibited "excess capacity," leading to rapid overfitting (Train F1 0.97 vs Val F1 0.88). EfficientNet-B0 is designed with a compound scaling method that optimizes depth, width, and resolution, typically achieving better parameter efficiency and generalization than ResNet architectures on image classification tasks.
*   **Head Design**: The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling layer followed by a fully connected layer mapping to the 6 disease classes.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing**: Images will be resized to **224x224** (or the native resolution of the chosen EfficientNet variant) to maintain computational efficiency.
*   **Augmentation Strategy**:
    *   **RandomResizedCrop**: We will explicitly retain this augmentation. As established in **Cite Lesson 4**, this is a superior regularizer compared to deterministic resizing with affine transformations, as it forces the model to learn from partial views and prevents memorization of global spatial structures.
    *   **Color Jitter**: We will retain brightness and contrast perturbations. **Cite Lesson 1** identified a correlation between prediction errors and image intensity statistics, making this invariance crucial for generalization.
    *   **MixUp and CutMix**: We will introduce these advanced mixing augmentations. This is a structural innovation to directly address the "generalization gap" noted in **Cite Lesson 1**. By training on linear combinations of images and labels (MixUp) or pasting patches of one image onto another (CutMix), we enforce smoother decision boundaries and further prevent the model from overfitting to the training set.

### Training
*   **Loss Function**: We will replace Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) with **Asymmetric Loss (ASL)**.
    *   **Rationale**: In multi-label classification, most labels for a given image are negative. Standard BCE can be overwhelmed by these easy negatives. ASL dynamically down-weights the contribution of easy negative samples, allowing the gradient to focus on positive samples and hard negatives. This is particularly effective for imbalanced datasets.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler**: We will continue using **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler, as this setup provided stable convergence in previous experiments.
*   **Target Handling**: Due to the introduction of MixUp/CutMix, the training loop will be modified to accept "soft" targets (continuous values between 0 and 1) rather than hard binary labels.

### Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**: To further close the generalization gap identified in **Cite Lesson 1**, we will implement TTA during inference.
    *   **Process**: Instead of a single forward pass, the model will predict on the original image and a horizontally flipped version (and potentially a slightly zoomed version). The logits will be averaged before applying the sigmoid activation. This helps smooth out predictions and reduces the variance caused by the aggressive `RandomResizedCrop` used during training.
*   **Thresholding**: We will maintain the fixed threshold of 0.5, applied to the averaged probabilities.
