I will optimize the solution to fit within the 26-minute time limit while maintaining predictive performance.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Model Distillation**: I will switch the backbone from `roberta-base` to `distilroberta-base`. This model is approximately 2x faster for both training and inference while retaining the same architecture (RoBERTa), ensuring compatibility with the existing `PointerLocator` and `Filler` classes.
2.  **Sequence Length Optimization**: Based on the EDA (Mean word count ~25), I will reduce `MAX_LEN` from 256 to 128. This significantly reduces the computational cost of the attention mechanism ($O(N^2)$) without truncating the vast majority of sentences.
3.  **Hyperparameter Tuning**: I will increase the training batch size to 128 and inference batch size to 256 to fully utilize the A100 GPU. I will set the training sample size to 45,000, which is the maximum feasible amount to process (1 epoch) within the remaining time window, prioritizing unique data exposure (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
4.  **Operational Efficiency**: I will remove `tqdm` progress bars and reduce logging frequency to comply with the "no progress bars" requirement and minimize I/O overhead.
