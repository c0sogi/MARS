Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **Cross-Validated Heterogeneous Stacking Pipeline**, a structural evolution that shifts from a simple "Train-Predict" paradigm to a multi-stage "Train-MetaLearn-Predict" system. This approach maximizes data utilization and explicitly resolves the "Ensemble Dilution" risk (Cite Lesson 00033) by learning optimal fusion weights rather than relying on arbitrary averaging.

# Model
I propose a two-level **Stacked Generalization** architecture:

**Level 0: Heterogeneous Base Learners**
We will employ three distinct, high-performance backbones to ensure maximum error diversity (Cite Lesson 00038):
1.  **ViT-Base (patch16_384):** The Supervised Attention expert (Cite Lesson 00015).
2.  **BEiT-Base (patch16_384):** The Masked Image Modeling expert (Cite Lesson 00038).
3.  **ConvNeXt-Base (384):** The Convolutional expert (Cite Lesson 00047).
*Constraint:* All models will be "Base" variants to avoid the instability of "Large" architectures (Cite Lesson 00018) and will operate at **384x384** resolution to align with pre-training (Cite Lesson 00022).

**Level 1: Meta-Learner**
Instead of simple averaging, we will introduce a **Logistic Regression** (or shallow MLP) meta-learner. This model takes the concatenated softmax probabilities from the Level 0 models as input and outputs the final class prediction. This allows the system to learn which backbone is most trustworthy for specific classes or confidence levels, effectively solving the "Ensemble Dilution" problem where weaker models degrade the ensemble (Cite Lesson 00033).

# Data
*   **Splitting Strategy (Innovation):** We will move from a single train/val split to **5-Fold Cross-Validation**. This ensures that:
    1.  Every sample in the dataset is used for training (across folds), maximizing the utility of the limited data.
    2.  We generate unbiased Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions for the entire training set, which are required to train the Level 1 Meta-Learner.
*   **Preprocessing:** All images will be resized to **384x384 pixels** (Cite Lesson 00022).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Geometric:** We will apply `HorizontalFlip`, `VerticalFlip`, **`Transpose`**, and `ShiftScaleRotate`. This full suite is strictly required to provide D4 symmetry and spatial invariance for the Transformers (Cite Lesson 00027, Cite Lesson 00032).
    *   **Mixing:** We will apply **MixUp and CutMix** to all training batches to enforce linear decision boundaries (Cite Lesson 00012).
    *   **Photometric:** We will strictly avoid aggressive color augmentations to preserve disease-specific color signals (Cite Lesson 00004).
    *   **Determinism:** Loaders will be seeded deterministically (Cite Lesson 00014).

# Training
The training will proceed in two distinct stages:

**Stage 1: Base Learner Training**
We will train 15 independent models (3 Architectures × 5 Folds) using a unified, precision-optimized protocol:
*   **Precision:** **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** enabled to minimize runtime (Cite Lesson 00038).
*   **Duration:** **12 Epochs** per fold. This extended duration is necessary to allow convergence under the heavy regularization of MixUp/CutMix (Cite Lesson 00047).
*   **Regularization:** **Stochastic Depth** (drop path rate ~0.1) will be applied to prevent memorization (Cite Lesson 00040).
*   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing without early stopping, ensuring the models benefit from the final learning rate decay (Cite Lesson 00037).
*   **Loss:** Cross Entropy with Label Smoothing (0.1) (Cite Lesson 00007).
*   **Optimization:** Independent training of each model to avoid joint-training conflicts (Cite Lesson 00025).

**Stage 2: Meta-Learner Training**
*   We will collect the OOF predictions (probability vectors) from all 15 models for the training data.
*   The Logistic Regression meta-learner will be trained on these OOF features to predict the true labels, learning the optimal weighted combination of the base learners.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For the test set, we will generate predictions using all 15 base models. We will apply **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** (flips/transpose) for each model (Cite Lesson 00010).
*   **Fusion:** The TTA-averaged predictions from the 15 models will be averaged by architecture (resulting in 3 probability vectors: ViT-Avg, BEiT-Avg, ConvNeXt-Avg) or concatenated (depending on the meta-learner design) and passed to the trained Meta-Learner to generate the final submission. This ensures the final output is a calibrated, optimally weighted consensus.
