Based on the lessons learned, specifically **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00018** ("Diminishing Returns of Scaling Architectures"), I will revert the model from the computationally expensive `swin_large_patch4_window12_384` to the more efficient `vit_base_patch16_384`. The larger model showed signs of rapid saturation and forced a suboptimal batch size.

By switching to the lighter ViT-Base model, I can significantly increase the batch size from 8 to 32 (Cite **solution_lesson_node_00013**: "Maximize the batch size"). Consequently, I will apply the **Linear Scaling Rule** to adjust the learning rate from `2e-5` to `1e-4` to maintain training stability with the larger batch size. Finally, I will set the training epochs to 8, as **Lesson ID: solution_lesson_node_00015** indicates that ViT models on this task tend to peak around epoch 5.
