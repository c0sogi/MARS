To address the overfitting observed in the previous solution (97.6% Train vs 81.3% Val), I will implement aggressive data augmentation as suggested by the lesson "Limits of Loss Weighting for Imbalanced Fine-Grained Classification". The lesson states that "loss weighting must be paired with aggressive data augmentation" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) to effectively improve generalization on minority classes and prevent the model from memorizing specific samples.

My strategy involves:
1.  **Enhancing Data Augmentation (`library/dataset.py`)**: I will replace the simple resize and flip augmentation with a robust pipeline including `RandomResizedCrop`, `Transpose`, `VerticalFlip`, `ShiftScaleRotate`, `HueSaturationValue`, `RandomBrightnessContrast`, and `CoarseDropout`. This increases data diversity and forces the model to learn invariant features.
2.  **Adjusting Training Configuration (`library/config.py`)**: I will increase `NUM_EPOCHS` from 15 (effectively 8 in the runfile) to 12 to allow the model more time to converge on the harder, augmented dataset. I will also increase `NUM_WORKERS` to 8 to handle the increased CPU load from augmentations.
3.  **Updating Execution Logic (`runfile.py`)**: I will update the training call to use the new epoch count and adjust the submission generation threshold to the previous best validation accuracy (`0.8130841121495327`).
