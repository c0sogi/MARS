The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` occurred because the combination of a batch size of 32 and high-resolution images ($384 \times 384$) generated intermediate activations that exceeded the 40GB VRAM capacity of the A100 GPU. As identified in the error analysis, the memory usage for activations scales linearly with batch size, causing the crash during the backward pass. To resolve this, I will reduce the `train_batch_size` to 16 and the `valid_batch_size` to 32 in `library/config.py`. This reduction will halve the memory required for activations, allowing the model to train within the available hardware limits (Cite debug_lesson_2).
