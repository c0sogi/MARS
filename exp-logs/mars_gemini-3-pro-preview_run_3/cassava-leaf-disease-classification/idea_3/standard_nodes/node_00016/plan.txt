To improve the model's performance and stability, I will implement **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA)**. The previous solution showed that the Vision Transformer model converges rapidly (peaking at Epoch 5) but then oscillates around the optimal accuracy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00015). SWA helps find a broader, flatter minimum in the loss landscape by averaging the weights of the model across multiple epochs towards the end of training, which typically leads to better generalization on the test set.

**Optimization Strategy:**
1.  **Extended Training with SWA**: I will increase the total epochs from 10 to 12 and enable SWA starting from epoch 8. This allows the model to settle into the basin of attraction before averaging begins.
2.  **SWA Configuration**: I will use `torch.optim.swa_utils` to manage the averaged model and a specific SWA learning rate (`1e-5`) to ensure small, stable updates during the averaging phase.
3.  **Model Selection**: The final solution will automatically select the better performing model between the SWA-averaged model and the best single-checkpoint model found during the standard training phase.

This approach leverages the existing high-capacity ViT backbone while addressing the variance and plateauing observed in the previous run.
