The validation failure is caused by a caching collision where the validation step reuses cached features (`features_test.parquet`) that were generated for the leaderboard test set. Since the validation users differ from the test users, this leads to a mismatch in `customer_id`s, resulting in empty predictions and a `NaN` metric during evaluation. To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to explicitly disable cache loading (`load_cached_data=False`) during the validation feature generation (Cite debug_lesson_2). I will also disable cache loading for the training and final submission steps to prevent data leakage and ensure that the feature cache is not corrupted or stale between the validation and inference phases.
