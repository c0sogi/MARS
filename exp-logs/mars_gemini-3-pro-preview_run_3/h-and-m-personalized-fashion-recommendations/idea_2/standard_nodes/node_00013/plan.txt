I will implement a power-law time decay for transition weights ($1/(1+d)^{2.0}$) to better capture recent trends (Cite Lesson 00002) and introduce "Importance-Based History Truncation" to construct more robust user profiles by selecting the top 30 weighted items (Cite Lesson 00012). Finally, I will adjust the hyperparameters `DECAY_RATE` to 2.0 and `HISTORY_WEIGHT` to 1.5 to balance transition signals with repurchase bias (Cite Lesson 00011).
