Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Group-Normalized Deep Multi-Task MIL Network**.

This solution structurally evolves the architecture by addressing the "Micro-Batch Instability" bottleneck (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022), enabling the use of a deeper backbone (ResNet-34) within the computational constraints. It strictly enforces the "Multi-Task Learning" paradigm (Cite solution_lesson_node_00045) and the "Instance-Level Aggregation" strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024) proven to be superior.

### 1. Model
**Architecture:** 2.5D ResNet-34 with Group Normalization and Multi-Task Heads
*   **Backbone:** Upgrade to **ResNet-34**, but structurally replace all **Batch Normalization (BN)** layers with **Group Normalization (GN)** (e.g., 32 groups).
    *   *Justification:* Lesson 00022 identified that ResNet-34 failed due to noisy batch statistics when training with small batch sizes ($N=8$). Group Normalization is independent of batch size, stabilizing the training dynamics and allowing us to leverage the deeper feature extraction capacity of ResNet-34 for detecting subtle fractures.
*   **Input:** 2.5D Stacks (3 channels: slices $z-1, z, z+1$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Context Module:** **Non-Linear 1D Convolution with GroupNorm**.
    *   Structure: `Conv1d(k=3, padding=1) -> GroupNorm -> ReLU`.
    *   *Justification:* We retain the 1D Convolution to leverage spatial continuity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014). We include Non-Linearity/Normalization for stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027) but switch to GroupNorm to match the backbone's stability strategy. We explicitly reject Residual connections (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026) and complex Multi-Scale blocks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028) to prevent overfitting.
*   **Multi-Task Heads:**
    *   The model outputs **8 distinct logits** for each slice: [C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6, C7, Patient_Overall].
    *   *Justification:* We reject "Logical Aggregation" (deriving patient from vertebrae) in favor of **Multi-Task Learning**. Lesson 00045 proved that learning a dedicated patient head yields significantly better performance by allowing the model to aggregate global evidence independently.
*   **Aggregation:** **Global Max Pooling** applied per channel.
    *   *Justification:* Max Pooling is superior to Attention for sparse anomaly detection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).

### 2. Data
*   **Ingestion Pipeline:**
    *   **Sorting:** Sort slices strictly by `ImagePositionPatient` z-coordinate (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Calibration:** Apply `RescaleSlope` and `RescaleIntercept` to convert to Hounsfield Units (HU) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
    *   **Windowing:** Apply **Standard Bone Window** (Level: 400, Width: 1800). Do not use High-Density windows (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
*   **Caching:** Preprocess and cache 2.5D stacks as **`uint8`** to maximize I/O throughput (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Sampling:** Uniformly sample **64 slices** per exam (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Volumetric-Consistent:** Apply the exact same geometric transform to every slice in the bag (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **Single-Step Affine:** Fuse rotation, scaling, and shifting into a single affine operation to minimize resampling blur (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).
    *   **Non-Destructive:** Avoid `Cutout` or `Dropout` on inputs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Implicitly Weighted Multi-Task Loss**.
    *   $L = \text{mean}(\text{BCE}_{C1..C7}) + \text{BCE}_{\text{Patient}}$.
    *   *Justification:* This formulation naturally creates the **1:7 weighting ratio** required by the competition metric (where the patient label is weighted 7x more than an individual vertebra) without using explicit scalar multipliers that can distort gradients (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Batch Size 8**.
    *   *Justification:* Small batch size acts as a necessary regularizer for this small dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00032).
*   **Scheduler:** **Decoupled Cosine Annealing**. Set `T_max` to **1.5x the number of epochs**.
    *   *Justification:* Prevents premature learning rate decay (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Regularization:** Do **not** use Dropout in the classification heads (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    *   Load test scans using the sorted/calibrated pipeline.
    *   Pass through the GroupNorm-ResNet34 -> Context -> Multi-Task Heads.
    *   Aggregate via Global Max Pooling to obtain 8 probabilities.
    *   Submit the 7 vertebral probabilities and the distinct `patient_overall` probability directly.
