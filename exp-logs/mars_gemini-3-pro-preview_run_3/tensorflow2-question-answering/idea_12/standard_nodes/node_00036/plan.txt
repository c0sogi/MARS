This happened because the `prepare_ranker_data` function automatically applied the `Config.VAL_SAMPLE_SIZE` limit when `is_train=False`. To fix this, I will modify `prepare_ranker_data` to accept a `perform_sampling` flag (defaulting to `True`), and then explicitly pass `perform_sampling=False` during the validation and test inference steps to ensure the full datasets are processed.
