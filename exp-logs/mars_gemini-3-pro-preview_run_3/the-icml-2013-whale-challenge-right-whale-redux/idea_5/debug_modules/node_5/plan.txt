The `RuntimeError: Unknown model (convnext_small.fb_in1k)` indicates that the model identifier `convnext_small.fb_in1k` is not present in the registry of the installed `timm` library. As highlighted in **Cite debug_lesson_1**, using unverified string identifiers often leads to such errors. To fix this, I will update the `BACKBONE` setting in `library/config.py` to `convnext_small.in12k_ft_in1k`, which is the correct and standard identifier for the ConvNeXt Small model (pretrained on ImageNet-12k and fine-tuned on ImageNet-1k) within the `timm` ecosystem.
