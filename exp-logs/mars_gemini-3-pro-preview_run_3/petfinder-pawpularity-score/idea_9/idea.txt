Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **Multi-View Quad-Stream Ensemble with Manifold-Regularized Stacking**.

This strategy introduces two key structural innovations:
1.  **Multi-View Feature Extraction**: To address the "Curse of Spatial Granularity" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031) while still capturing high-frequency subject details (which Global Average Pooling often blurs), we will extract features from both a **Global View** (full image) and a **Zoomed View** (center crop). This acts as a "soft" attention mechanism, allowing the model to analyze the subject's texture (fur, eyes) independently of the background composition.
2.  **Manifold-Regularized Stacking**: We integrate a **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) Regressor** into the Level 1 ensemble. This complements the model-based learners (SVR, Trees) with an instance-based learner, explicitly leveraging the local manifold structure of the embeddings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

### Model

The architecture consists of a **Dual-View Quad-Backbone Extractor** feeding into a **Diverse Stacking Ensemble**:

1.  **Multi-View Quad-Stream Backbones (Frozen)**:
    We employ the four high-performing backbones identified in Lesson 00025 (Swin-L, EfficientNetV2-L, DINOv2-L, CLIP-L). However, each image is processed in two distinct views:
    *   **Global View**: The image is resized to 224x224. This captures the overall composition, layout, and background context (strengths of Swin/CLIP).
    *   **Zoomed View**: A central crop covering 60% of the original image area is taken and resized to 224x224. This effectively "zooms in" on the pet (assuming rough centering), allowing the backbones (especially EfficientNet and DINO) to capture fine-grained details like eye sharpness and fur texture without the noise of the background.
    *   This results in **8 parallel feature streams** (4 Backbones $\times$ 2 Views).

2.  **Independent Component Compression**:
    We apply **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** independently to each of the 8 streams to retain 95% of the variance per stream (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022). This is critical to prevent the dimensionality explosion from the multi-view expansion and to filter view-specific noise.

3.  **Manifold-Regularized Stacking Ensemble**:
    We expand the heterogeneous stacking architecture (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) to include an instance-based learner:
    *   **Level 1 Base Learners**:
        *   **Support Vector Regression (SVR)**: RBF Kernel, $C=20$ (Cite solution_lesson_node_00026).
        *   **ExtraTrees Regressor**: Regularized with `min_samples_leaf=5` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039) and `max_features=None` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
        *   **LightGBM**: Gradient Boosting with Early Stopping (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
        *   **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) Regressor**: Configured with $K=50$ and distance weighting. This learner explicitly predicts based on the similarity to the closest training examples in the dense embedding space, adding a robust "retrieval-based" signal that complements the hyperplane (SVR) and decision boundary (Trees) approaches.
    *   **Level 2 Meta-Learner**: Ridge Regression to aggregate the four base predictions.

### Data

1.  **Preprocessing**:
    *   **Global View**: Resize to 224x224, Normalize.
    *   **Zoomed View**: Center Crop (60% area), Resize to 224x224, Normalize.
    *   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**: Apply Horizontal Flipping to *both* views. Average the embeddings of the original and flipped versions for each stream (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
    *   **Metadata**: Scale the 12 binary metadata features by a factor of 10.0 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025) and concatenate them with the compressed image features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00037).

2.  **Caching**:
    *   Implement configuration-hashed caching that includes the "MultiView" flag in the filename to ensure fresh extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### Training

1.  **Feature Extraction**:
    *   Pass the dataset through the frozen backbones for both Global and Zoomed views using TTA.
    *   Fit 8 separate PCA transformers (one for each Backbone-View pair) on the training set and transform all data.
    *   Concatenate the 8 compressed vectors and the scaled metadata.

2.  **Ensemble Training (Cross-Validation)**:
    *   Perform **5-Fold Cross-Validation**.
    *   **LightGBM**: Train with **In-Fold Early Stopping** using the validation fold to determine optimal trees (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).
    *   **KNN/SVR/ExtraTrees**: Train on training folds and generate Out-of-Fold (OOF) predictions.
    *   **Meta-Learner**: Train the Level 2 Ridge Regressor on the OOF predictions to minimize RMSE.

3.  **Final Retraining**:
    *   Retrain all PCA transformers, Level 1 models (using the average optimal iterations for LightGBM), and the Level 2 learner on the full training set.

### Evaluation

1.  **Inference**:
    *   Generate Global and Zoomed views for test images.
    *   Extract features using frozen backbones with TTA.
    *   Project using learned PCA transformers.
    *   Concatenate with scaled metadata.
2.  **Prediction**:
    *   Feed the feature vector into the retrained Level 1 models (SVR, ET, LGBM, KNN).
    *   Aggregate predictions using the Level 2 Meta-Learner.
    *   Clip results to [1, 100].
