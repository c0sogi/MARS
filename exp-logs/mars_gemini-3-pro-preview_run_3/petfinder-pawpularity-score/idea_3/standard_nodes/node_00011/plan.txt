The issue lies in `runfile.py` where the Level 2 Meta-Learner is trained on the full set of Out-of-Fold (OOF) predictions, including those corresponding to the validation set. This constitutes data leakage because the model sees the validation targets during training, rendering the subsequent validation metric invalid as it reflects training error rather than generalization performance. To fix this, I will modify the script to split the OOF predictions back into training and validation subsets. The Meta-Learner will be trained strictly on the training subset's OOF predictions and targets, and then evaluated on the unseen validation subset's OOF predictions.
