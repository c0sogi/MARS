Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Quad-Stream Semantic-Geometric Ensemble with CLIP and Independent Component Compression**.

This strategy evolves the successful "Triple-Backbone" approach (Idea 5) by integrating a fourth modality—**CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training)**—to capture the semantic and affective qualities of the images ("vibe" or "cuteness") which are often orthogonal to the object classification signals (ImageNet) and geometric signals (DINO). Furthermore, it introduces a specific metadata scaling strategy to ensure these critical binary signals are not drowned out in the high-dimensional embedding space used by the Support Vector Regressor.

### Model
The architecture employs a **Quad-Backbone Feature Extractor** followed by a **Compressed Stacking Ensemble**:

1.  **Multi-Paradigm Feature Backbones (Frozen)**:
    *   **Swin Transformer (Supervised)**: Retained for its superior ability to capture global composition and layout (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
    *   **EfficientNetV2 (Supervised)**: Retained to capture high-frequency local details, texture, and image quality (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
    *   **DINOv2 (Self-Supervised)**: Retained for its class-agnostic understanding of object geometry and part-whole correspondence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   **CLIP Vision Model (Image-Text Contrastive)**: We introduce the visual encoder of a pre-trained CLIP model (e.g., `ViT-B/32` or `ViT-L/14`). Unlike the other backbones trained on labels or self-reconstruction, CLIP is aligned with natural language descriptions. This allows it to capture high-level semantic concepts (e.g., "funny", "sad", "fluffy") that directly drive human engagement ("Pawpularity"), addressing the efficacy gap of pure classification features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

2.  **Independent Component Compression**:
    *   We apply **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** independently to the output of *each* of the four backbones to retain 95% of the variance per stream (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
    *   This is critical to prevent the high-dimensional embeddings (especially from larger CLIP or Swin models) from dominating the concatenated vector and to filter out backbone-specific noise.

3.  **Stacking Ensemble Head**:
    *   **Level 1 Base Learners**: We utilize the robust heterogeneous mix of **Support Vector Regression (SVR)** with RBF kernel, **LightGBM**, and **Ridge Regression** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
    *   **Level 2 Meta-Learner**: A Linear Regression model aggregates the predictions.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing & Augmentation**:
    *   Images are resized to 224x224 and normalized.
    *   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)**: We apply Horizontal Flipping during feature extraction. The final embedding for an image is the average of the original and flipped views (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Metadata Amplification**:
    *   The 12 binary metadata features (Focus, Eyes, etc.) are concatenated with the compressed image features.
    *   **Crucial Step**: Since the SVR kernel relies on Euclidean distance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012) and the image feature vector has ~1500 dimensions, unscaled binary features (0/1) contribute negligibly to the distance matrix. We will **scale the metadata features** by a factor (e.g., 5.0 or 10.0) to ensure they exert a meaningful influence on the kernel computation.
3.  **Robust Caching**:
    *   We enforce **configuration-hashed caching**. The cache filename will include a hash of the active backbones (now including CLIP) and TTA settings to ensure that stale features from previous experiments are not loaded (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### Training
1.  **Feature Extraction**:
    *   Pass the entire dataset through the four frozen backbones (Swin, EffNet, DINOv2, CLIP) using TTA.
    *   Fit four separate PCA transformers on the training set embeddings and transform both train and test sets.
    *   Concatenate the four compressed vectors and the amplified metadata.
    *   This avoids the overfitting and high cost of fine-tuning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
2.  **Stack Training (Cross-Validation)**:
    *   Perform 5-Fold Cross-Validation on the final feature matrix.
    *   Train Level 1 models (SVR, LGBM, Ridge) on training folds and generate Out-of-Fold (OOF) predictions.
    *   Train the Level 2 Meta-Learner on OOF predictions to minimize RMSE.
3.  **Final Retraining**:
    *   Retrain all PCA transformers, Level 1 models, and the Level 2 learner on the full training dataset.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference**: Process test images through the four frozen backbones with TTA.
2.  **Projection**: Transform raw embeddings using the learned PCA matrices.
3.  **Prediction**: Generate base predictions using the retrained Level 1 models, aggregate with the Meta-Learner, and clip outputs to the valid range [1, 100].
