The execution failure is caused by a dimensionality mismatch in the `PCA` initialization within the `StackingRegressor`. Specifically, `n_components` is hardcoded to 64, but during the demonstration run, the training fold size is only 32 (due to `DEMO_TRAIN_SIZE=64` and `N_FOLDS=2`). The `PCA` algorithm requires `n_components` to be less than or equal to the number of samples. To fix this, I will modify `_create_base_models` in `library/ensemble.py` to accept the sample count and dynamically clamp `n_components` to `min(64, n_samples)`. This ensures the model remains robust on small datasets or debug runs, addressing the failure foreshadowed in the debug notes (Cite debug_lesson_5).
