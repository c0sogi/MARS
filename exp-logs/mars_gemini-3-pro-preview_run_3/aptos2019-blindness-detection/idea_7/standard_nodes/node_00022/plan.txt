The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the **Physical Batch Size** of 32 (for 512x512 images with a ConvNeXt Base model) exceeds the available 16GB GPU memory. To resolve this while maintaining the training dynamics, I will decouple the physical batch size from the effective batch size (Cite debug_lesson_2).

I will:
1.  Modify `library/config.py` to reduce `STAGE1_BATCH_SIZE` from 32 to 8 and introduce `STAGE1_GRAD_ACCUM = 4` to maintain the effective batch size of 32.
2.  Update `runfile.py` to pass the `accumulation_steps` parameter to the `train_one_epoch` function during Stage 1 training.
