Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the lessons learned, I propose a **Chemically-Aggregated Structural Hologram (CASH)** strategy.

This approach evolves the successful **Radial Distribution Function (RDF)** strategy (Idea 10, Lesson 29) by integrating **Angular Distribution Functions (ADF)** to capture 3-body topological constraints (bond angles). Crucially, to address the **feature dilution** (Lesson 16) and **over-resolution** (Lesson 30) risks associated with 3-body terms in multi-component alloys, this solution introduces a **Chemical Aggregation** layer. Instead of enumerating sparse histograms for every possible element triplet (e.g., Al-O-Ga, In-O-Al), we aggregate angles into dense, chemically meaningful categories (e.g., "Metal-Oxygen-Metal" bridging angles).

# Model
-   **Architecture**: **XGBoost Regressor** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003). Tree ensembles have proven to be the most robust predictor for this dataset when provided with high-signal physical features.
-   **Input Features**: A concatenated vector of:
    1.  **Explicit Physical Descriptors**: Unit Cell Volume and Mass Density (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
    2.  **2-Body Fingerprints (RDF)**: Element-resolved pairwise distance histograms.
    3.  **3-Body Fingerprints (Aggregated ADF)**: Chemically grouped bond angle histograms (The Structural Innovation).
    4.  **Tabular Metadata**: Atomic composition and Spacegroup.

# Data
1.  **Geometric Feature Extraction (Raw Geometry)**:
    -   Load crystal structures from raw `.xyz` files using `ase`.
    -   **Physical Descriptors**: Calculate **Unit Cell Volume** and **Mass Density** directly from the atomic objects to ensure fidelity to the simulation constraints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
    -   **2-Body (RDF)**: Compute Radial Distribution Functions for specific high-relevance pairs (**Al-O**, **Ga-O**, **In-O**, **Metal-Metal**). Use moderate binning (e.g., 40 bins, 0-6 Å) and normalize by **atom count** (not volume) to decouple topology from density (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
    -   **3-Body (Aggregated ADF)**:
        -   Compute nearest neighbor lists with a bond cutoff (e.g., 3.0 Å).
        -   Calculate bond angles $\theta_{ijk}$ for all valid triplets.
        -   **Aggregation**: Instead of sparse specific triplets, pool the angles into two dense distributions:
            1.  **Polyhedral Angles ($\theta_{O-Metal-O}$)**: Angles centered on any Metal atom (Al/Ga/In) with Oxygen neighbors. This captures the internal distortion of the coordination polyhedra.
            2.  **Bridging Angles ($\theta_{Metal-O-Metal}$)**: Angles centered on Oxygen with Metal neighbors. This captures the tilting and connectivity between polyhedra.
        -   Bin these angles (e.g., 0-180°, 30 bins) to form the ADF features. This avoids the "feature dilution" of rare triplets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

2.  **Preprocessing**:
    -   **Target Transformation**: Apply $z = \log(1 + y)$ to the targets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    -   **Feature Cleaning**: Drop constant columns.

# Training
-   **Algorithm**: Train separate XGBoost regressors for formation energy and bandgap energy.
-   **Optimization Strategy**:
    -   **Shrinkage**: Use a low learning rate ($\eta \approx 0.01$) and high number of estimators ($n \approx 3000$) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
    -   **Stochastic Subsampling**: Set `subsample` and `colsample_bytree` to $\approx 0.6 - 0.7$. This forces the model to utilize the diverse structural information (RDF/ADF) rather than relying solely on global density (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
-   **Loss Function**: Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the log-transformed targets.

# Evaluation
-   **Inference**:
    1.  Process test set `.xyz` files to generate Physical, RDF, and Aggregated ADF features.
    2.  Predict log-scale values using the trained XGBoost models.
    3.  Apply the inverse transformation $\hat{y} = \exp(z) - 1$ to obtain final energy predictions.
