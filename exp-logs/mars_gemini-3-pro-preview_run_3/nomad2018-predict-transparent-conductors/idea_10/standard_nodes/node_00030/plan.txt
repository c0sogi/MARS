To optimize the performance of the formation and bandgap energy prediction model, I will implement the following changes based on the identified lessons and empirical findings:

1.  **Enhance Structural Fingerprinting (RDF):**
    *   **Strategy:** Increase the resolution and range of the Element-Resolved Radial Distribution Function (RDF) features. The previous solution used a cutoff of 6.0 Å with 30 bins (0.2 Å resolution). I will increase the **cutoff to 8.0 Å** and **number of bins to 80** (0.1 Å resolution).
    *   **Rationale:** Higher resolution (0.1 Å) allows the model to distinguish between subtle bond length variations, while a larger cutoff (8.0 Å) captures medium-range order and periodicity effects which are critical in crystal structures. This aligns with **Lesson 29**, which highlights RDFs as effective structural fingerprints when GNNs are not viable.

2.  **Hyperparameter Tuning for High-Dimensionality (Shrinkage):**
    *   **Strategy:** Adjust XGBoost hyperparameters to handle the increased feature space (from ~300 to ~800 features). I will lower the **learning rate to 0.005**, increase **n_estimators to 5000**, and reduce **colsample_bytree to 0.5**. I will also increase **max_depth to 7**.
    *   **Rationale:** With more features, the model needs more capacity (`max_depth=7`) but also stronger regularization to prevent overfitting. Lowering the learning rate and column subsampling (Shrinkage, **Lesson 4**) encourages the model to learn more robust, distributed representations rather than relying on a few dominant features.

3.  **Cache Management:**
    *   **Strategy:** Update the cache directory to `./working/idea_11` to ensure the new features are computed and stored fresh, avoiding conflicts with previous experiments.
