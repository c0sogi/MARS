The solution enhances the previous XGBoost-based approach by introducing three key optimizations:
1.  **Feature Engineering**: A new feature, `geo_mean_mass`, is added to the geometry extraction pipeline. This captures the average atomic mass of the unit cell, providing the model with explicit information about the material's composition weight, which is relevant for bandgap prediction.
2.  **Model Ensembling**: The solution now employs an ensemble of **XGBoost** and **LightGBM** regressors. Averaging predictions from these two distinct gradient boosting implementations typically reduces variance and improves generalization.
3.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**: The hyperparameters for both models are tuned for **shrinkage** and regularization to combat the observed overfitting (Training RMSLE 0.028 vs Validation 0.067). Specifically, the learning rate is lowered to 0.01, the number of estimators is increased to 4000, and subsampling/colsampling rates are reduced to 0.6, along with increased L1/L2 regularization.

These changes are applied across `library/config.py`, `library/geometry_loader.py`, and `library/model_trainer.py`.
