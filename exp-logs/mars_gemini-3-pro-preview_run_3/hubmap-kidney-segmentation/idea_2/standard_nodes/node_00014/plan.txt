1.  **Increase Input Resolution**: Scale up `img_size` from 512 to 640. Higher resolution allows the model to better resolve the boundaries of glomeruli, which are small structures. Cite solution_lesson_node_00003 (ROI sampling makes this efficient by avoiding background).
2.  **Extend Training with Single-Cycle Scheduler**: Increase `epochs` to 12 and set `T_0` to 12. This ensures a smooth learning rate decay over the entire training duration, avoiding premature restarts and allowing the model to settle into a better minimum.
3.  **Enhanced Regularization**: Add `CoarseDropout` to the training augmentations. This forces the model to rely on distributed features rather than local textures, improving generalization.
