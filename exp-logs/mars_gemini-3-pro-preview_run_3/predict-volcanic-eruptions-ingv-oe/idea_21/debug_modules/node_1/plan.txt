The issue is that the current imputation strategy `df.fillna(df.mean())` fails when a sensor column contains entirely `NaN` values. In such cases, the mean is undefined (`NaN`), causing `fillna` to have no effect, which allows `NaN`s to propagate into the feature extraction process and trigger the assertion error in `runfile.py`.

To fix this, I will modify `library/feature_engineering.py` to chain a fallback `.fillna(0)` after the mean imputation. This ensures that any remaining `NaN`s (from all-empty columns) are replaced with zeros, preventing downstream numerical instability. This solution directly applies the guidance from **Cite debug_lesson_1**.
