To optimize the solution, I will enhance the feature engineering pipeline by incorporating granular distributional statistics (quantiles) into the kinematic features (Trend, Velocity, Acceleration), as suggested by Lesson 00059. While the previous solution relied on higher-order moments (Skewness, Kurtosis), failure analysis showed these were correlated with errors, likely due to sensitivity to outliers. By adding explicit quantiles (`1%, 5%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 95%, 99%`) alongside moments, we provide the model with a stable "shape" of the signal's movement profile (Cite Lesson 00026).

Simultaneously, I will adjust the LightGBM hyperparameters to accommodate this richer feature space. I will lower the `learning_rate` to `0.02` and increase `n_estimators` to `10000` to ensure convergence, while increasing L1/L2 regularization to `1.0` to prevent overfitting to the new features (Cite Lesson 00064).
