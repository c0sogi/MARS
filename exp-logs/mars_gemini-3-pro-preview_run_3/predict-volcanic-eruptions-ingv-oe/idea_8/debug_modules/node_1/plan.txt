Calculating the Pearson correlation involves dividing by the standard deviation; for constant signals, this division by zero produces `NaN`s (Cite debug_lesson_4). The fix involves replacing these undefined correlation values with `0` in the correlation matrix, effectively treating constant signals as having no correlation with others.
