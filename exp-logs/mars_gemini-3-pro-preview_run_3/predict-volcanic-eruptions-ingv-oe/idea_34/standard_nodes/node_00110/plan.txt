My optimization strategy focuses on reducing overfitting and improving the signal-to-noise ratio of the features, informed by the failure analysis and lessons learned.

1.  **Feature Engineering Refinement**:
    *   **Remove Acceleration Features**: The failure analysis showed a high correlation between prediction error and acceleration kurtosis. As per **Lesson 00054**, higher-order derivatives (acceleration) amplify noise in high-frequency sensor data. I will remove acceleration features entirely.
    *   **Robust Velocity Features**: I will replace the unstable Skewness and Kurtosis moments for Velocity with **Quantiles** (Cite **Lesson 00026**). Quantiles provide a more robust description of the kinematic distribution without being disproportionately affected by outliers.
    *   **Remove Entropy**: I will remove Spectral/Texture Entropy (Cite **Lesson 00012**) as it can memorize noise textures and was negatively correlated with error in the analysis.
    *   **Retain Texture Moments**: I will keep Skewness and Kurtosis for the Texture (Residuals) component, as **Lesson 00049** suggests these effectively capture signal roughness/tremors.

2.  **Model Regularization**:
    *   **Reduce Complexity**: I will reduce `NUM_LEAVES` from 128 to 63 to limit the model's capacity to memorize noise.
    *   **Add Regularization**: I will introduce L1 (`REG_ALPHA`) and L2 (`REG_LAMBDA`) regularization to penalize complex feature interactions.
    *   **Increase Randomness**: I will slightly lower `FEATURE_FRACTION` and `BAGGING_FRACTION` to 0.7 to improve generalization.

3.  **Configuration**:
    *   I will update the working directory and increase early stopping rounds to accommodate the more regularized training process.
