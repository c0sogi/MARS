1.  **Feature Engineering Optimization**: In `library/feature_extraction.py`, I will replace the calculation of Skewness and Kurtosis for raw, velocity, and acceleration signals with explicit Quantiles (1st, 5th, 95th, 99th percentiles) and a robust range (q99 - q01). This change is motivated by **Lesson 26**, which highlights that higher-order moments are unstable and correlate with error in this dataset, while quantiles provide robust distributional boundaries. It also aligns with **Lesson 21** by preserving kinematic derivatives but measuring them more robustly.

This ensures that the pipeline generates fresh features and does not load the previous suboptimal feature set (Cite **Lesson 13** regarding cache invalidation).
3.  **Execution**: The `runfile.py` will remain largely unchanged as the model training parameters (4000 estimators, early stopping) are already well-tuned for this scale, but the improved feature quality is expected to lower the MAE below the threshold.
