The strategy implements a Peak-Aware Stacked Kinematic Ensemble. It orchestrates a two-level stacking pipeline where Level 0 consists of diverse Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost) and Level 1 is a Ridge Regression meta-learner. The pipeline first validates the model on a hold-out set by training strictly on the training partition (avoiding data leakage), performing failure analysis, and then—conditional on meeting the performance threshold—retrains the ensemble on the combined dataset to generate the final submission.
