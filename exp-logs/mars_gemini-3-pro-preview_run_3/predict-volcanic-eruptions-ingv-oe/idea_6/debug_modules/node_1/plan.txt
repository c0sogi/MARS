The issue stems from `NaN` values persisting in the feature set due to two reasons: (1) `df.fillna(df.mean())` fails to fill columns that are entirely `NaN` (as the mean is also `NaN`), leaving them empty (Cite debug_lesson_1); and (2) constant signals (resulting from filling with 0 or dead sensors) have zero variance, causing division-by-zero errors (yielding `NaN`) when calculating skewness and kurtosis (Cite debug_lesson_4). Additionally, constant signals result in undefined (`NaN`) correlations in the spatial features.

To fix this, I will:
1.  Update the imputation logic in `extract_features_for_segment` to chain `.fillna(0)` as a fallback for all-NaN columns.
2.  Modify `compute_basic_stats` to set skewness and kurtosis to 0.0 if the standard deviation is near zero.
3.  Update `compute_spatial` to fill `NaN` correlations with 0.
