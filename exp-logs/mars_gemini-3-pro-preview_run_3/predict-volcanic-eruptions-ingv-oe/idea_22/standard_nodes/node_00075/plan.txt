Since `pywt` is unavailable, I will compute "Texture" as the residual of the signal (`Raw - Trend`) and extract RMS, Skewness, and Kurtosis. 3.  **Shift-Invariant Temporal Features (Cite Lesson 00050):** I will modify `_get_temporal` to aggregate window statistics (e.g., mean of window means, std of window RMS) instead of flattening them into distinct features. This ensures the model learns the temporal evolution/volatility regardless of the specific start time of the window.
4.  **Kinematic Shape Statistics (Cite Lesson 00021):** I will add Skewness and Kurtosis to the `_get_kinematics` method to capture impulsive movements in the velocity and acceleration profiles.
5.  **Refine Spectral Features:** I will add relative power features to the spectral extraction, as the failure analysis indicated the model was sensitive to spectral distribution.
