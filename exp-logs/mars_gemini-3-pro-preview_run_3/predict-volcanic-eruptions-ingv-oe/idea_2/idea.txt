# Overview
To address the limitations of the previous baseline and leverage the insights gained, I propose a **Time-Frequency Spectral Fingerprinting** strategy. This approach evolves the feature engineering paradigm from simple global time-domain statistics to a comprehensive representation that captures both the spectral characteristics and the temporal evolution of the seismic signals. This structural innovation aims to expose the "hidden" frequency patterns (e.g., harmonic tremors) that are critical in volcano seismology but were invisible to the previous model.

# Model
The core predictive model remains a **LightGBM Regressor**, chosen for its superior efficiency and performance on tabular data derived from signal processing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
- **Architecture**: A Gradient Boosting Decision Tree (GBDT) ensemble.
- **Input Layer**: A significantly expanded feature vector that concatenates global time-domain statistics, frequency-domain spectral features, and windowed temporal trend features.

# Data
The data processing pipeline will undergo a fundamental expansion to extract a richer set of descriptors from the raw sensor logs.

1.  **Preprocessing**:
    - **Imputation**: Handle missing values in sensors (e.g., Sensor 2, 5, 8) by filling with the segment-wise column mean to preserve signal offset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    - **Detrending**: Remove linear trends from the signal before frequency analysis to avoid spectral leakage.

2.  **Feature Engineering Paradigm Shift**:
    - **Global Time-Domain (Exploitation)**: Retain the successful extraction of central tendency (Mean, Median), dispersion (Std, MAD, IQR), and shape (Skewness, Kurtosis) metrics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    - **Frequency-Domain (Innovation)**: Apply the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to each sensor channel. Extract spectral fingerprints such as:
        - **Spectral Centroid & Bandwidth**: To characterize the "center of mass" and spread of the frequency distribution.
        - **Peak Frequency**: To identify dominant harmonic tremors.
        - **Spectral Entropy**: To measure the randomness/complexity of the signal.
        - **Band Power**: Aggregate energy in specific frequency bands (e.g., low-frequency vs. high-frequency) relevant to seismic precursors.
    - **Temporal Evolution (Innovation)**: Divide the 60,000-point sequence into $N$ (e.g., 4 or 10) non-overlapping windows. Compute basic statistics (Mean, Std) for each window to capture the *rate of change* or acceleration of seismic activity, which global stats obscure.

# Training
The training strategy is updated to address the underfitting observed in the previous iteration.
- **Algorithm**: LightGBM Regressor with `objective='mae'`.
- **Hyperparameters**: Drastically increase `n_estimators` (e.g., to 10,000) to allow the model to fully converge. The previous limit of 2,000 was identified as a bottleneck preventing the model from minimizing the residual error (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
- **Regularization**: Enable Early Stopping (e.g., patience of 100 rounds) based on the validation MAE to prevent overfitting given the expanded feature space.
- **Validation**: Use the stratified splits provided in `metadata/train.csv` and `metadata/val.csv`.

# Evaluation
1.  **Feature Extraction**: Process the test files listed in `metadata/test.csv` through the exact same Time-Frequency and Windowing pipeline.
2.  **Inference**: Generate predictions using the trained LightGBM model.
3.  **Submission**: Output the results in the required `segment_id,time_to_eruption` CSV format.
