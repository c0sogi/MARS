The optimization strategy involves three key changes based on the provided lessons:
1.  **Feature Engineering**: Re-introducing `min`, `max`, `skew`, and `kurtosis` features. Lesson 31 indicates that for peak-driven signals, raw extrema (`min`/`max`) are critical signal components, not just noise. Lesson 21 highlights that shape statistics (`skew`/`kurtosis`) are essential for physical signal regression.
2.  **Ensemble Pruning**: Removing CatBoost from the stacked ensemble. Lesson 8 warns against ensembling models with vastly different performance characteristics. CatBoost's MAE (~11.6M) was significantly worse than LightGBM (~3.17M), likely introducing noise into the meta-learner.
3.  **Hyperparameter Tuning**: Increasing `n_estimators` and `early_stopping_rounds` to allow the remaining strong models (LightGBM and XGBoost) to fully leverage the expanded feature set.
