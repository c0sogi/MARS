The rationale is that text normalization often depends on both preceding and succeeding context (e.g., distinguishing "1998" as a year in "in 1998" vs. a quantity in "1998 items"). By exhausting explicit memorization with larger and more specific context windows before falling back to general rules, we can better handle the "long tail" of ambiguous tokens.
