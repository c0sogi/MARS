The `AssertionError: Probabilities do not sum to 1.0` occurs because the model inference runs in mixed precision (`float16`), and applying softmax directly to these logits produces `float16` probabilities. The limited precision of `float16` causes the sum of probabilities to deviate from 1.0 beyond the strict tolerance (`1e-5`) checked in the verification script. To fix this, I will explicitly cast the logits to `float32` before applying softmax in both the evaluation and prediction loops. This ensures the probability distribution is calculated with sufficient precision to sum to 1.0, satisfying the validation check (Cite debug_lesson_4).
