The execution failed with an `AssertionError: Probabilities do not sum to 1.0` during the inference phase. This is a common issue when using Mixed Precision (`float16`), as the `softmax` operation can suffer from numerical instability and precision loss, resulting in probability distributions that do not sum exactly to 1.0. As detailed in the error analysis and **debug_lesson_6**, the solution is to explicitly cast the logits to `float32` before applying the softmax activation. This ensures sufficient precision for the probability calculation while maintaining the performance benefits of Mixed Precision for the model backbone.

I will modify `library/engine.py` to cast the logits to `float32` in the `inference_fn` before both softmax operations (standard and TTA).
