Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose a structural evolution that transitions from scratch-trained Recurrent Neural Networks to a **Pre-trained Long-Context Transformer** architecture. This approach directly addresses the limitations of LSTM bottlenecks, independent encoding, and sequence truncation.

# Model
I propose using the **Longformer (allenai/longformer-base-4096)** as the backbone architecture.
*   **Cross-Encoder Architecture:** Instead of processing the prompt and responses independently (Siamese), the model will process the concatenated sequence: `<s> Prompt </s> Response A </s> Response B </s>`. This allows the self-attention mechanism to model the direct interaction between Response A and Response B within the context of the Prompt, addressing the performance ceiling observed with independent encoders (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006) and leveraging the Transformer's ability to handle global dependencies better than RNNs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Long-Context Attention:** The Longformer supports sequences up to 4096 tokens. This effectively eliminates the information loss caused by aggressive truncation in previous experiments, ensuring the model has access to the full context required for reasoning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Hybrid Classification Head:** I will retain the successful feature engineering strategy by extracting the global `<s>` (CLS) token embedding and concatenating it with **explicit scalar features** (the log-transformed token counts of the Prompt, Response A, and Response B). This preserves the high-signal length heuristics that improved convergence in previous iterations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Global Attention Mechanism:** Global attention will be explicitly configured on the `<s>` classification token to ensure it aggregates information from the entire sequence.

# Data
*   **Tokenization:** Use the pre-trained `LongformerTokenizer`.
*   **Feature Consistency:** Calculate the scalar length features based on the *tokenized* sequences (not raw text) to ensure the explicit features align mathematically with the inputs seen by the encoder (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Formatting:** Construct the input strings by concatenating the prompt and both responses with the appropriate separator tokens.
*   **Sequence Management:** While the model supports 4096 tokens, I will use a dynamic padding strategy (collating batches to the longest sequence in the batch) with a reasonable maximum (e.g., 2048) to optimize training speed on the A100, only extending to 4096 for outliers.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** Cross-Entropy Loss.
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a standard learning rate for fine-tuning transformers (e.g., 2e-5) and weight decay.
*   **Precision:** Utilize Mixed Precision (FP16) to maximize throughput on the NVIDIA A100 GPU.
*   **Strategy:** Fine-tune for 2-3 epochs with a linear learning rate warmup (10% of steps) followed by decay. This is more efficient and stable than training embeddings from scratch.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Preprocess the test set using the same concatenation and tokenization logic.
*   **Prediction:** Pass the inputs through the fine-tuned Longformer. The final MLP head (fed by the CLS token and scalar features) will output logits for the three classes.
*   **Output:** Apply Softmax to generate the final probabilities for submission.
