Based on the comprehensive analysis of the experiment history and the derived lessons, I propose the **Energy-Attentive Isomorphic CNN (EA-IDPH-CNN)**.

This solution represents a structural synthesis of the most effective components identified to date, while introducing a physics-informed innovation to the attention mechanism. It addresses the limitation of **Global Average Pooling** in Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks (Lesson 104), which can cause signal cancellation between positive peaks (icebergs) and negative shadows (voids) when features are centered by Batch Normalization. By replacing it with **Global Energy (L2) Pooling**, we capture the total "activity" of a channel—both bright backscatter and deep shadows—robustly, without the noise sensitivity of Max Pooling (Lesson 104) or Standard Deviation (Lesson 145). This is integrated into the robust **Plain CNN** backbone with the **Corrected Isomorphic Readout**.

# Model
The architecture is a **Custom 4-Stage Attentive Convolutional Network** optimized for energy-based feature recalibration and dual-polarity extraction.

*   **Backbone:** The network employs a **Plain CNN** structure (no residual connections) with 4 sequential blocks. We explicitly retain this over ResNets to enforce aggressive sequential downsampling (75x75 $\to$ ~4x4), which filters high-frequency speckle noise via convolution (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046).
    *   **Block Structure:** `Conv2d` (bias=True) $\to$ `BatchNorm2d` $\to$ `LeakyReLU` $\to$ **Energy-SE Module** $\to$ `MaxPool2d`.
    *   **Bias Retention:** We explicitly retain bias terms to preserve initialization dynamics (Cite solution_lesson_node_00076).
    *   **Activation:** **`LeakyReLU`** (negative slope 0.1) preserves negative values (shadows) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00091).
    *   **Width Strategy:** **64 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 128 $\to$ 128** (Early Expansion) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00050).

*   **Structural Innovation 1 (Energy Squeeze-and-Excitation):**
    *   We replace the standard SE module with an **Energy-SE Module**.
    *   **Energy Squeeze:** Instead of Global Average Pooling ($\frac{1}{N}\sum x$), we use **Global L2 Pooling** ($\sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum x^2}$).
    *   **Rationale:** After Batch Normalization, background clutter tends to be near zero, while Icebergs (Peaks) are large positive and Shadows (Voids) are large negative. Average pooling risks canceling these out ($+x$ and $-x$ sum to 0). L2 Pooling measures the total **Energy** (magnitude) of the channel, ensuring that channels rich in *contrast* (both peaks and shadows) are upweighted, aligning with the physics of SAR detection. It is more robust to single-pixel noise than Max Pooling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00104) and more stable than Variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00145).
    *   **Leaky Excitation:** The MLP uses `LeakyReLU` in the hidden layer to handle potentially negative gradients/inputs, maintaining consistency with the backbone.

*   **Structural Innovation 2 (Corrected Isomorphic Readout):**
    *   We extract features from **Stage 3** and **Stage 4** (Selective Hierarchical) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   **Decoupled Projections:** We use **separate** 1x1 Convs for Stage 3 (`Proj3`) and Stage 4 (`Proj4`) to learn depth-specific transformations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00144).
    *   **Isomorphic Dual-Polarity:**
        *   **Stage 3:** `Proj3(128 -> 64)` $\to$ **Global Max Pooling** + **Global Min Pooling**.
        *   **Stage 4:** `Proj4(128 -> 64)` $\to$ **Global Max Pooling** + **Global Min Pooling**.
    *   **Constraint:** Max and Min pooling share the *same* projection within a stage (Cite solution_lesson_node_00142).
    *   **Result:** A 256-dimensional vector that captures **Signal-to-Shadow Contrast** without parameter explosion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00124, solution_lesson_node_00134).

*   **Feature Fusion (Raw Scale):** The feature vector is concatenated with the scalar **incidence angle**. We explicitly **do not normalize** the angle (Cite solution_lesson_node_00057).

*   **Classification Head:** `Linear(257 -> 256)` $\to$ `LeakyReLU` $\to$ `Dropout(0.5)` $\to$ `Output`. Dropout is applied *only* after activation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).

# Data
*   **Input Composition:** 3-channel tensor (HH, HV, Synthetic Avg) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Resolution:** Native **75x75** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
*   **Augmentation:** `torchvision` RandomHorizontalFlip and RandomVerticalFlip (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
*   **Leak-Free Preprocessing:** Imputation of missing incidence angles is performed using the median calculated **only on the training fold indices** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00147).

# Training
*   **Strategy:** **5-Fold Cross-Validation Ensembling** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a **constant learning rate** (e.g., 1e-3). This decouples weight decay from adaptive updates, stabilizing the fusion of the raw scalar input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00019).
*   **Loss Function:** **`BCEWithLogitsLoss`** combined with **L2 Regularization (Weight Decay)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).
*   **Duration:** Generous budget (e.g., 75 epochs) with moderate patience (e.g., 12 epochs) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00094).
*   **Initialization:** **PyTorch Default Initialization** (Kaiming Uniform/Fan-In) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00078).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Explicitly **disable Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
*   **Ensembling:** Simple average of predictions from the 5 CV models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
