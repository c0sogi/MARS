Based on the lessons learned from the previous experiments, I propose a structural evolution of the solution that transitions from a pure Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to a **Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network (CRNN) with Attention**.

This strategy addresses the "Simple CNN" limitation of treating audio solely as a static image (ignoring sequential phoneme dependencies) and corrects the "Over-regularization" and "Resolution" issues identified in the EfficientNet attempt.

### Model: ResNet34-BiGRU with Attention Pooling
I propose a hybrid architecture that combines the robust feature extraction of ResNet with the sequential modeling capabilities of Recurrent Neural Networks.

*   **Backbone (Feature Extractor):** I will use a **ResNet34** pretrained on ImageNet.
    *   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer will be modified to accept 1-channel input by summing the weights of the original RGB channels to preserve pretrained edge/texture detectors **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   **Resolution Preservation:** Standard ResNets downsample the input by a factor of 32, which would reduce the temporal dimension of a 1-second clip to a negligible size (e.g., 3-4 frames). I will modify the strides of the final two residual blocks (layer3 and layer4) to `(1, 1)` instead of `(2, 2)`. This ensures the output feature map retains sufficient temporal resolution (e.g., ~25-50 time steps) to distinguish short commands **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
*   **Neck (Sequence Modeling):** The output features $(B, C, F, T)$ will be averaged over the frequency dimension $F$ to produce a sequence of feature vectors $(B, T, C)$. This sequence will be passed through a **Bidirectional GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit)**. This layer explicitly models the time-series nature of speech (e.g., the sequence of phonemes in "s-t-o-p"), which pure CNNs often miss.
*   **Head (Aggregation):** Instead of Global Average Pooling (which treats all time steps equally), I will implement **Attention Pooling** (Attentive Statistics Pooling). This mechanism learns a weight for each time step, allowing the model to focus on the spoken command and ignore the surrounding silence or padding before producing the final classification vector.

### Data: High-Fidelity Preprocessing & Context-Aware Augmentation
*   **Class Balancing:** I will strictly adhere to the dual-sampling strategy: **undersampling** the `unknown` class to the median count of target labels and **oversampling** `silence` by stochastically cropping background noise files **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Spectrogram Generation:**
    *   **Resolution:** I will use **64 Mel bands** to match the capacity of the ResNet34 backbone **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
    *   **Temporal Fidelity:** I will use a Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) window size of approximately **30ms** (e.g., 480 samples at 16kHz) rather than larger windows. This prioritizes the temporal resolution necessary to resolve rapid phoneme transitions in short commands **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
*   **Augmentation (SpecAugment Only):**
    *   I will apply Frequency and Time Masking.
    *   **Mask Value:** Masked regions will be filled with the **minimum value** of the spectrogram, not zero, to correctly represent silence in log-space **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**.
    *   **Mask Width:** Time masks will be strictly limited to **<20%** of the total duration to preserve the context of monosyllabic words **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.
    *   **Implementation:** I will use **inclusive bounds** for random mask generation to ensure maximum regularization coverage **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.
*   **Exclusion:** I will **NOT** use MixUp. The previous lesson demonstrated that stacking MixUp on top of SpecAugment with a standard training schedule leads to underfitting and over-regularization **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** Standard **Cross-Entropy Loss**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a standard learning rate (e.g., 1e-3) and weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** A **Cosine Annealing** scheduler will be used to smoothly decay the learning rate, facilitating convergence into a robust minima.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Test audio clips will be preprocessed into Log-Mel Spectrograms using the same parameters (64 mels, 30ms window) without any augmentation.
*   **Prediction:** The model will output the class with the highest probability. The architecture's Attention Pooling layer will naturally handle varying amounts of silence in the uncropped test files.
