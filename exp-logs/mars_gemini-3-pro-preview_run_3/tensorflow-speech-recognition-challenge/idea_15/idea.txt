Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Frequency-Attentive Multi-Resolution ResNeSt-CRNN with GPU-Native Physics Augmentation**.

This strategy introduces two key structural innovations. First, it implements a **Frequency-Attentive Pooling** mechanism at the CNN-RNN interface, addressing the information loss inherent in standard Global Average Pooling (Lesson 00041) without incurring the parameter explosion of flattening. Second, it fully exploits the **GPU-Native Pipeline** (Lesson 00052) to introduce **Physics-Based Augmentations** (Pitch Shifting and Time Stretching), which were previously computationally infeasible with cached features but are critical for speaker invariance.

# Model
I propose the **Frequency-Attentive Multi-Resolution ResNeSt-CRNN**.

*   **Input:** **3-Channel Multi-Resolution Log-Mel Spectrogram**.
    *   We retain the stacking of Short (20ms), Medium (40ms), and Long (60ms) window spectrograms to map the time-frequency trade-off to the channel dimension **Cite solution_lesson_node_00020**.
    *   **Resolution Scaling:** We will increase `n_mels` to **224**. This aligns the spectral dimension with the native spatial resolution ($224 \times 224$) of the ImageNet-pretrained backbone, minimizing the domain gap and ensuring feature fidelity scales with model capacity **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
*   **Backbone:** **ResNeSt50 (Split-Attention Network)**.
    *   We use ResNeSt50, whose "Split-Attention" mechanism dynamically reweights channel groups, making it ideal for the multi-resolution input **Cite solution_lesson_node_00029**.
    *   **Temporal Preservation:** The strides of the final two residual stages will be set to `(1, 1)` to maintain temporal resolution **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.
*   **Neck (Structural Innovation):** **Frequency-Attentive Pooling**.
    *   Instead of the standard Global Average Pooling over the frequency dimension (which treats all frequency bands as equally important) or Flattening (which explodes parameters **Cite solution_lesson_node_00041**), we introduce a learnable **Attention Mechanism**.
    *   *Mechanism:* A lightweight $1 \times 1$ convolution followed by a Softmax computes an "importance map" along the frequency dimension for each time step. The feature map is then aggregated via a weighted sum.
    *   *Benefit:* This allows the model to dynamically focus on specific frequency bands (e.g., high frequencies for sibilants like "s", low frequencies for vowels) at each time step before passing the sequence to the RNN.
*   **Sequence Modeling:** **Bidirectional GRU (BiGRU)**.
    *   The frequency-aggregated sequence is passed to a BiGRU to model phoneme dependencies **Cite solution_lesson_node_00034**.
*   **Head:** **Multi-Head Attention Pooling**.
    *   Aggregates the recurrent states using multiple attention heads to capture distinct temporal events (onset/offset) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00031**.

# Data
*   **GPU-Native Pipeline (Structural Innovation):**
    *   We utilize a fully GPU-resident data pipeline where raw waveforms are loaded and all signal processing occurs on the device **Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**.
*   **Augmentation 1: Physics-Based Transformations (New):**
    *   Leveraging the GPU pipeline, we introduce **Random Pitch Shifting** and **Time Stretching** (Resampling) on the raw waveform. These augmentations simulate vocal variations (different speakers, speaking rates) that are impossible to accurately replicate with SpecAugment on cached spectrograms.
*   **Augmentation 2: Environmental Robustness:**
    *   **Background Noise Injection:** Dynamically mix background noise into the waveform **Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**.
    *   **SpecAugment:** Apply Frequency and Time Masking to the generated spectrograms (inclusive bounds **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**, min-fill **Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**, <20% time mask **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**).
*   **Sampling:**
    *   Use the **Full Unknown Dataset** (~30k samples) with a **Weighted Random Sampler** to ensure balanced batches **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.

# Training
*   **Loss Function:** Standard **Cross-Entropy Loss**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler.
*   **Strategy:** Train end-to-end. The Frequency-Attentive Pooling layer is differentiable and will learn to highlight relevant spectral features automatically.

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    1.  Load Raw Waveform.
    2.  Generate 3-Channel Multi-Resolution Spectrogram ($224$ mels) on GPU.
    3.  Pass through the Frequency-Attentive ResNeSt-CRNN.
    4.  Output the class with the highest probability.
