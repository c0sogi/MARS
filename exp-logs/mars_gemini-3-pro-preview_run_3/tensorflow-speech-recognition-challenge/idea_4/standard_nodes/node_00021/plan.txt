I will implement a strategy focused on improving data utilization and feature fidelity to boost the model's generalization capabilities.

1.  **Dynamic Data Balancing (Cite Lesson 1)**: The previous solution statically undersampled the massive "Unknown" class (30,000+ files) to a fixed subset of ~1,700 files. This discarded ~95% of the negative examples. I will replace this with a `WeightedRandomSampler` that samples from the **full** dataset during training. This ensures the model sees a diverse, constantly changing stream of "Unknown" samples while maintaining class balance within each batch, preventing overfitting to a specific subset of background noises.
2.  **Increased Feature Resolution (Cite Lesson 6)**: I will increase the Mel-spectrogram resolution (`N_MELS`) from 64 to 80. The ResNet34 backbone has sufficient capacity to process higher-fidelity inputs, and this finer spectral detail will help distinguish phonetically similar commands.
3.  **Extended Training**: I will increase the training duration from 20 to 25 epochs to allow the model to converge on the significantly larger and more diverse training distribution.
