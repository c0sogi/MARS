Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the specific lessons learned, I propose the **Hybrid 1D-2D Dual-Stream CRNN**.

This strategy addresses the fundamental limitation of converting audio to spectrograms: the inevitable loss of fine-grained temporal details (phase and transient micro-structures) due to the STFT windowing process (Lesson 00012). By introducing a **Dual-Stream Architecture**, we fuse the robust spectral feature extraction of the **Multi-Resolution SK-ResNet** (Lesson 00029) with the high temporal precision of a **1D Convolutional Network** operating on raw waveforms. This hybrid approach leverages the strengths of both domains, processed by the proven sequence modeling backbone (BiGRU) and attention head (Multi-Head Attention).

### Model
I propose a **Hybrid 1D-2D Dual-Stream CRNN** that processes raw audio and spectrograms in parallel branches before fusing them for sequence modeling.

*   **Stream 1: Spectral Context (2D Branch)**
    *   **Input:** **3-Channel Multi-Resolution Log-Mel Spectrogram** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
        *   Channels correspond to Short (20ms), Medium (40ms), and Long (60ms) STFT windows to capture both transient and tonal features.
    *   **Backbone:** **Selective Kernel ResNet34 (SK-ResNet34)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).
        *   The SK-Net's dynamic receptive field is ideal for the multi-resolution input.
        *   **Temporal Preservation:** Strides in the final two residual stages will be set to `(1, 1)` to maintain sequence length (Cite solution_lesson_node_00017).
*   **Stream 2: Temporal Precision (1D Branch)**
    *   **Input:** **Raw Waveform** (16,000 samples).
        *   Processing raw audio preserves phase information and rapid transients often smeared by spectrogram generation.
    *   **Backbone:** **Deep 1D CNN**.
        *   A stack of 1D Convolutional blocks (Conv1D + BatchNorm + ReLU + Pooling).
        *   The network will be designed to downsample the 16,000 samples to match the temporal dimension of the Stream 1 output (e.g., ~100 frames), ensuring alignment.
*   **Fusion & Neck:**
    *   **Concatenation:** Feature vectors from the 2D Stream (flattened along frequency) and the 1D Stream are concatenated along the channel dimension at each time step.
    *   **Sequence Modeling:** **Bidirectional GRU (BiGRU)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).
        *   The fused sequence is passed to a BiGRU to model phoneme dependencies. We explicitly prioritize RNNs over Conformers for this short-sequence task.
*   **Head:**
    *   **Multi-Head Attention Pooling** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00031).
        *   Aggregates the recurrent states using multiple attention heads to capture distinct temporal events (e.g., onset vs. offset) simultaneously.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing & Caching:**
    *   **Stream 1 (2D):** Pre-compute the 3-Channel Multi-Resolution Spectrograms and **cache them to disk** as `.npy` files. This avoids the CPU bottleneck of on-the-fly STFT calculation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
    *   **Stream 2 (1D):** Load raw audio waveforms on-the-fly. Since raw loading is I/O bound and fast (unlike STFT which is CPU bound), this does not violate the efficiency constraints.
*   **Sampling Strategy:**
    *   Use the **Full Unknown Dataset** (~30k samples) and employ a **Weighted Random Sampler** to ensure balanced batches. This maximizes negative class diversity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).
*   **Augmentation:**
    *   **Stream 1 (2D):** Apply **SpecAugment** (Frequency and Time Masking) to the cached features.
        *   Use **inclusive bounds** for mask generation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
        *   Fill masks with the **minimum value** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
        *   Limit time masks to **<20%** of duration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    *   **Stream 2 (1D):** Apply simple Gain or Gaussian Noise.
    *   **Exclusion:** Do **not** use MixUp or Label Smoothing to avoid underfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016, solution_lesson_node_00019).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** Standard **Cross-Entropy Loss**.
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with weight decay.
*   **Scheduler:** **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler.
*   **Procedure:** Train the end-to-end hybrid model. The gradients will flow through both the 1D and 2D backbones, allowing them to learn complementary features.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    1.  Generate the 3-Channel Multi-Resolution Spectrogram (Stream 1 input).
    2.  Load the Raw Waveform (Stream 2 input).
    3.  Pass both through the Hybrid CRNN.
    4.  The Multi-Head Attention Pooling will aggregate the features, and the model will output the class with the highest probability.
