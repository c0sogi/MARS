The Conformer model in the previous run achieved only ~91.27% accuracy, whereas the BiGRU variant is expected to reach >98%.

I will also optimize hyperparameters by increasing the **batch size to 128** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00031`) to better utilize the A100 GPU and increasing **epochs to 20** to ensure convergence.
