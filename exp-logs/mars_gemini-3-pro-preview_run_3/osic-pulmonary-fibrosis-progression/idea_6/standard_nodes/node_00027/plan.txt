To optimize the performance of the CAP-Net solution, I will implement a **fine-tuning strategy for the CNN backbone** combined with **increased model capacity**. The failure analysis of the previous run revealed a significant correlation between prediction error and the `Percent` feature (0.31), suggesting that the model's frozen ImageNet features were insufficient to capture the specific texture patterns of lung fibrosis severity that correlate with a patient's functional capacity.

My changes include:
1.  **Fine-Tuning the Backbone**: I will unfreeze the last two blocks of the EfficientNet-B0 backbone. This allows the model to learn domain-specific representations (e.g., honeycombing, ground-glass opacity) rather than relying on generic features.
2.  **Differential Learning Rates**: I will apply a lower learning rate (`1e-4`) to the backbone to preserve pre-trained knowledge and a higher rate (`1e-3`) to the head.
3.  **Increased Capacity**: I will double the `PROJECTION_DIM` (128 $\to$ 256) and `HIDDEN_DIM` (256 $\to$ 512) to allow the fusion layer to model more complex interactions between the enriched image features and clinical metadata.
4.  **Extended Training**: I will increase the training duration to **50 epochs**. As noted in `solution_lesson_node_00009`, models with projection heads and fine-tuning require sufficient gradient updates to converge effectively.
