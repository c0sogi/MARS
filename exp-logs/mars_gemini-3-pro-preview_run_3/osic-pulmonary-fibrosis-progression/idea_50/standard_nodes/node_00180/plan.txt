My strategy focuses on implementing the **Latent Fusion** architecture described in Lesson 00060 and Lesson 00052, which was identified as the key differentiator between the "Current Best" (DSPRNet) and the underperforming "New Solution" (EADSNet/CRDSNet).

**Key Changes:**
1.  **Architecture Upgrade (Cite Lesson 00060, 00052)**: I will modify `CRDSNet` in `library/model.py` to project the linear autoregressive features (`Baseline_FVC`, `Time`) into a high-dimensional latent space (64-dim) before fusing them with the deep image features. This contrasts with the previous implementation which fused them in the low-dimensional output space. This "over-parameterization" of the linear stream facilitates optimization of the strong linear signal.
2.  **Shared Head for Uncertainty (Cite Lesson 00055)**: By fusing in the latent space, the final projection head (predicting both $\mu$ and $\sigma$) will have direct access to the strong linear priors from the autoregressive stream, ensuring robust uncertainty estimation.
3.  **Training Duration**: I will increase `NUM_EPOCHS` in `runfile.py` from 20 to 25 to allow the slightly deeper architecture sufficient time to converge, matching the convergence profile (Epoch 14-24) noted in the lessons.
