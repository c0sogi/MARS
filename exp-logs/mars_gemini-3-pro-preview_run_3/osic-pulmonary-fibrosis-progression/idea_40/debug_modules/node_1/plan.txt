The issue arises because the `LabelEncoder` for `SmokingStatus` is fitted only on the small training subset (20 samples in the demo), which happened to exclude the "Currently smokes" category. When the pipeline processed the test set containing this category, the encoder failed with an "unseen label" error. To fix this, I will explicitly initialize the encoders with the complete list of known domain categories (`['Male', 'Female']` for Sex and `['Ex-smoker', 'Never smoked', 'Currently smokes']` for SmokingStatus) within the `LungDataProcessor.fit` method. This ensures the encoders are robust to data scarcity in small splits or debugging runs.
