Based on the systematic analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the **Spatially-Attentive Parametric Network (SAP-Net)**.

This solution represents a structural evolution of the **CAP-Net** (Idea 6) and the **Fine-Tuned Network** (Idea 7). It integrates the superior trajectory modeling of the former with the domain-adaptive capabilities of the latter, while introducing a novel **Spatial Attention Mechanism** to explicitly address the "Deep Semantic" requirement **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025** without the pitfalls of multi-scale dilution.

### 1. Model
The architecture is a hybrid CNN-MLP that predicts patient-specific trajectory coefficients.

*   **Image Branch (Attentive Fine-Tuning):**
    *   **Input:** Three content-adaptive slices (Apical, Middle, Basal) per patient **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
    *   **Backbone:** **EfficientNet-B0**. Unlike previous iterations, we **unfreeze the top convolutional blocks** (blocks 6 and 7) and the final head to allow domain adaptation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.
    *   **Spatial Attention Module (Innovation):** Before the final Global Average Pooling layer, we insert a lightweight **Spatial Attention Block** (a $1\times1$ convolution followed by a Sigmoid activation). This generates a spatial mask that weights the feature map, allowing the network to focus on high-signal regions (e.g., fibrotic lesions, honeycombing) and suppress background noise. This enhances the quality of the "Deep Semantic Features" **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
    *   **Projection:** The weighted feature maps are pooled and projected to a lower dimension (e.g., 128) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

*   **Tabular Branch (Signal Preservation):**
    *   **Input:** `Baseline FVC`, `Age`, `Sex`, `SmokingStatus`.
    *   **Architecture:** A simple Linear layer.
    *   **Constraints:** We explicitly **exclude Batch Normalization and Layer Normalization** from this branch. This preserves the absolute magnitude of the `Baseline FVC`, which is the strongest linear predictor of the target **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.

*   **Fusion & Parametric Head:**
    *   **Strategy:** **Late Fusion**. The image and tabular embeddings are concatenated only at the final stage to prevent the high-dimensional image noise from drowning out the strong tabular signal **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.
    *   **Output:** The network predicts 4 scalars: $\alpha$ (Baseline Coefficient), $\beta$ (Intercept), $\gamma$ (Slope), and $\delta$ (Uncertainty).
    *   **Trajectory Logic:** $\mu_{pred} = \alpha \cdot \text{Baseline}_{scaled} + \beta + \gamma \cdot \text{Weeks}_{scaled}$. This multiplicative interaction ($\gamma \cdot Weeks$) correctly models the variable rate of decline **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00022**.
    *   **Uncertainty:** $\sigma_{pred} = \text{softplus}(\delta) + \sigma_{floor}$. We use a small numerical floor (e.g., 0.05 scaled) to prevent the NLL singularity **Cite solution_lesson_node_00013**, but we **do not** enforce the metric's 70ml clip in the architecture to allow gradient flow **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.

### 2. Data
*   **Content-Adaptive Slice Selection:**
    *   We employ the robust heuristic: Select the "Anchor" slice (Max Lung Area) and two boundary slices (50% area threshold) to ensure anatomical consistency across patients **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Target Standardization:**
    *   The target `FVC` is **Z-score standardized** during training to resolve the magnitude gap **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
    *   Inputs (`Baseline FVC`, `Weeks`) are similarly standardized.

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** Modified Laplace Log Likelihood ($L = \frac{|y - \mu|}{\sigma} + \ln(\sigma)$).
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **Differential Learning Rates**.
    *   Backbone (Unfrozen layers): Low LR (e.g., 1e-4) to preserve pre-trained filters.
    *   Head (Attention + MLP): High LR (e.g., 1e-3) for rapid convergence **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.
*   **Schedule:** **Cosine Annealing** over **50 Epochs**. The extended duration is mandatory for the fine-tuning process and uncertainty calibration **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    *   Calculate $\mu_{scaled}$ and $\sigma_{scaled}$ using the predicted trajectory parameters.
*   **Inverse Transformation:**
    *   $\mu_{final} = \mu_{scaled} \times \sigma_{target} + \mu_{target}$.
    *   $\sigma_{final} = \sigma_{scaled} \times \sigma_{target}$ (Scale only) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
*   **Post-Processing:**
    *   Apply the hard clip $\sigma_{final} = \max(\sigma_{final}, 70)$ strictly for submission **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.
