To optimize the solution, I will implement a three-pronged strategy focusing on training dynamics, feature fusion stability, and data augmentation.

1.  **Training Dynamics (Scheduler & Epochs)**: I will introduce a `CosineAnnealingLR` scheduler and extend the training duration to 50 epochs. This addresses **Lesson 11** (Convergence Lag in Uncertainty), allowing the model to settle into a broader optimum and better calibrate the uncertainty estimation (`sigma`) which converges slower than the point prediction.
2.  **Feature Fusion Stability (LayerNorm)**: I will upgrade the projection heads in `MultiViewNet` by adding `LayerNorm` before the activation functions. This balances the feature distributions from the frozen EfficientNet backbone and the learnable tabular MLP, ensuring effective multimodal fusion as per **Lesson 8** and **Lesson 10** (preferring LayerNorm over BatchNorm for regression).
3.  **Data Augmentation**: I will integrate `albumentations` into the `LungDataset` to apply mild geometric transformations (Shift, Scale, Rotate). Since the backbone is frozen, this is essential to prevent the projection head from overfitting to the static cached features of the small dataset.
