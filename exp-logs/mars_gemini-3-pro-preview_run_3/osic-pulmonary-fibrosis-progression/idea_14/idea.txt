Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Decoupled Dual-Stream Residual Network (DDSR-Net)**.

This solution represents a structural evolution of the successful **Dual-Stream Point-Wise Residual** approach (Idea 13). It integrates the superior backbone efficiency of **EfficientNet-V2**, the physical robustness of **Relative Time**, and introduces a **Decoupled Head Architecture** to explicitly address the convergence lag between mean and uncertainty estimation identified in Lesson 011.

### 1. Model
The architecture is a hybrid CNN-MLP designed to predict lung function ($\mu$) and confidence ($\sigma$) for a specific time point $t$. It employs a "Wide-and-Deep" residual design with specialized prediction heads.

*   **Image Branch (Modernized Backbone):**
    *   **Backbone:** **EfficientNet-V2-S**. We upgrade from EfficientNet-B2 to the V2-S variant. This modern architecture offers faster training speeds and better parameter efficiency, allowing for a "moderately heavier" model that remains computationally feasible.
    *   **Fine-Tuning:** We **unfreeze the top two convolutional stages** to allow domain adaptation to fibrotic textures while keeping the lower layers frozen to preserve robust edge detection **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.
    *   **Feature Extraction:** Features are extracted via **Global Average Pooling** from the final layer to prioritize deep semantic signals (lung volume/shape) over multi-scale textures **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
    *   **Projection:** The concatenated vector (3 slices) is projected to 128 dimensions via a learnable Linear layer to balance modalities **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

*   **Dual-Stream Fusion:**
    *   **Stream A (Linear Residual):**
        *   **Input:** Concatenation of [`Baseline FVC`, $t_{rel}$].
        *   **Structure:** A single Learnable Linear Layer (No Activation, No Bias).
        *   **Purpose:** This stream learns the dominant autoregressive trend (Baseline + Average Decay) directly. This prevents the strong linear signal from being obscured by high-dimensional image noise **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00052**.
    *   **Stream B (Deep Interaction):**
        *   **Input:** Concatenation of [Image Projection, Raw Tabular Features, $t_{rel}$].
        *   **Structure:** A shared MLP (`Linear -> ReLU -> Linear`).
        *   **Purpose:** This stream captures complex non-linear corrections and cross-modal interactions (e.g., "Fibrosis $\times$ Time") **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**.

*   **Decoupled Prediction Heads (Structural Innovation):**
    *   Instead of projecting the Deep Stream output directly to $[\mu, \sigma]$, we split it into two independent MLPs:
        1.  **Mean Head:** Projects to a scalar correction term $\mu_{resid}$.
        2.  **Uncertainty Head:** Projects to the confidence value $\sigma$.
    *   **Final Output:**
        *   $\mu_{final} = \text{StreamA}_{output} + \mu_{resid}$. (Summing the Linear Trend and Deep Correction).
        *   $\sigma_{final} = \text{Uncertainty Head Output}$.
    *   **Rationale:** Uncertainty calibration converges significantly slower than point prediction **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**. Decoupling the heads ensures that the gradients for variance estimation do not interfere with the convergence of the mean correction, allowing the uncertainty head to specialize in modeling the time-dependent confidence degradation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.
    *   **Constraints:** No Dropout **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007** and No Batch Norm on the tabular/linear branches **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.

### 2. Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Content-Adaptive Slice Selection:** For each patient, select the "Anchor" slice (Max Lung Area) and two boundary slices (50% area threshold) to ensure anatomical consistency **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
    *   **Relative Time ($t_{rel}$):** Calculate $t_{rel} = \text{Weeks} - \text{Baseline\_Week}$. Scale by 0.01. We **do not** Z-score this variable, strictly preserving the zero at the baseline to ensure the model correctly interprets "distance from origin" for uncertainty estimation **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.
    *   **Target Standardization:** The target `FVC` is **Z-score standardized** during training to resolve the magnitude gap **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
    *   **Input Normalization:** `Baseline FVC` and `Age` are standardized but passed as raw scalars **Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**.

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** Modified Laplace Log Likelihood ($L = \frac{|y - \mu|}{\sigma} + \ln(\sigma)$).
*   **Uncertainty Constraints:**
    *   Use `sigma = softplus(raw_output) + epsilon`. Do **not** enforce the metric's 70ml clip in the architecture to allow natural gradient flow **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.
*   **Optimizer & Schedule:**
    *   **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing**.
    *   **Differential Learning Rates:** `1e-4` for the Unfrozen Backbone, `1e-3` for the Heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.
    *   **Duration:** **50 Epochs**. This extended duration is mandatory to allow the decoupled uncertainty head to fully calibrate **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    *   For each test `Patient_Week`, calculate $t_{rel}$.
    *   Predict $\mu_{scaled}$ and $\sigma_{scaled}$.
*   **Inverse Transformation:**
    *   $\mu_{final} = \mu_{scaled} \times \sigma_{target} + \mu_{target}$.
    *   $\sigma_{final} = \sigma_{scaled} \times \sigma_{target}$ (Scale only) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
*   **Post-Processing:**
    *   Apply the hard clip $\sigma_{final} = \max(\sigma_{final}, 70)$ strictly for the submission file.
