Implementation Strategy:
1.  **Downgrade Backbone**: Switch from `tf_efficientnetv2_s` to `efficientnet_b0` in `Config`. The smaller backbone matches the limited dataset size (126 patients) better, reducing the risk of overfitting (Cite Lesson 00030).
2.  **Optimize Training**: Reduce `LR_HEADS` to `5e-4` and set `EPOCHS` to 30 in `Config` to improve training stability and convergence.
3.  **Regularize Deep Stream**: Add `Dropout(0.2)` to the `stream_b_mlp` in `DDSRNet` to prevent the deep branch from memorizing noise.
4.  **Enforce Sigma Floor**: Update the sigma calculation to `F.softplus(sigma_raw) + 0.1` in `DDSRNet`. This enforces a minimum uncertainty of ~80ml (in Z-score space), which aligns the training loss with the evaluation metric's clipping at 70ml and prevents the NLL singularity where the model predicts near-zero uncertainty to minimize training loss at the expense of validation performance (Cite Lesson 00013).
5.  **Clean Runfile**: Remove the redundant epoch override in `runfile.py`.
