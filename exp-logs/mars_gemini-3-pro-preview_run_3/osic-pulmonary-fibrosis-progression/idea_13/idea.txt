Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the **Dual-Stream Point-Wise Residual Network (DSPR-Net)**.

This solution synthesizes the superior temporal modeling of **Point-Wise Estimation** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037** with a **Dual-Stream Architecture** designed to resolve the conflict between capturing complex cross-modal interactions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032** and preserving the strong linear signal of the baseline measurements **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023**.

### 1. Model
The architecture is a hybrid CNN-MLP that predicts lung function ($\mu$) and confidence ($\sigma$) for a specific time point $t$, utilizing a "Wide-and-Deep" residual design.

*   **Image Branch (Fine-Tuned Content-Adaptive 2.5D):**
    *   **Input:** Three slices per patient selected via the **Content-Adaptive Heuristic** (Apical, Middle, Basal) to ensure anatomical consistency **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
    *   **Backbone:** **EfficientNet-B2**. We upgrade the backbone to B2 to better capture fine-grained fibrotic textures. We **unfreeze the top two convolutional blocks** to allow domain adaptation while keeping lower layers frozen to preserve robust edge detection, utilizing **Differential Learning Rates** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.
    *   **Feature Extraction:** Features are extracted via **Global Average Pooling** from the final layer only. We explicitly reject multi-scale pyramids to prioritize deep semantic signals (lung volume/shape) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00025**.
    *   **Projection:** The concatenated vector (3 slices) is projected to 128 dimensions via a learnable Linear layer to balance modalities **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.

*   **Tabular & Temporal Inputs:**
    *   **Relative Time ($t_{rel}$):** We calculate $t_{rel} = \text{Weeks} - \text{Baseline\_Week}$. We **do not** Z-score this variable, strictly preserving the zero at the baseline to maintain the physical origin **Cite solution_lesson_node_00036**.
    *   **Static Features:** `Baseline FVC`, `Age`, `Sex`, `SmokingStatus`. These are passed as **Raw Scalars** (normalized but not encoded) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00035**. We explicitly **exclude Batch/Layer Normalization** to preserve the absolute magnitude of the `Baseline FVC` **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.

*   **Dual-Stream Fusion (Structural Innovation):**
    *   **Stream A (Deep Interaction):**
        *   **Input:** Concatenation of [Image Projection, Tabular Features, $t_{rel}$].
        *   **Structure:** MLP (`Linear -> ReLU -> Linear`).
        *   **Purpose:** Captures non-linear conditional effects (e.g., "Fibrosis $\times$ Time = Accelerated Decline") **Cite solution_lesson_node_00032**.
    *   **Stream B (Linear Residual):**
        *   **Input:** Concatenation of [`Baseline FVC`, $t_{rel}$].
        *   **Structure:** Single Learnable Linear Layer (No Activation).
        *   **Purpose:** Learns the optimal autoregressive coefficient $\alpha$ and linear decay rate $\gamma$ directly. This prevents the strong baseline signal from being drowned out by high-dimensional image noise **Cite solution_lesson_node_00023** and avoids the pitfalls of hard-coded residuals **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.

*   **Final Head:**
    *   **Operation:** The outputs of Stream A and Stream B are **Summed**.
    *   **Output:** The summed vector projects to $\mu$ (FVC) and $\sigma$ (Confidence).
    *   **Constraints:** No Dropout **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.

### 2. Data
*   **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Slice Selection:** Identify the "Anchor" slice (Max Lung Area) and two boundary slices (50% area threshold) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
    *   **Target Standardization:** The target `FVC` is **Z-score standardized** during training to resolve the magnitude gap **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
    *   **Input Normalization:** `Baseline FVC` and `Age` are standardized. `Weeks` is converted to $t_{rel}$ and scaled (e.g., by 0.01).

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** Modified Laplace Log Likelihood ($L = \frac{|y - \mu|}{\sigma} + \ln(\sigma)$).
*   **Uncertainty Constraints:**
    *   We use `sigma = softplus(raw_output) + epsilon`. We **do not** enforce the metric's 70ml clip in the architecture, allowing natural gradient flow **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.
*   **Optimizer & Schedule:**
    *   **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing**.
    *   **Differential Learning Rates:** `1e-4` for the Unfrozen Backbone, `1e-3` for the Fusion Heads **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.
    *   **Duration:** **50 Epochs** to ensure convergence of the fine-tuning and uncertainty calibration **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inference:**
    *   For each test `Patient_Week`, calculate $t_{rel}$.
    *   Predict $\mu_{scaled}$ and $\sigma_{scaled}$.
*   **Inverse Transformation:**
    *   $\mu_{final} = \mu_{scaled} \times \sigma_{target} + \mu_{target}$.
    *   $\sigma_{final} = \sigma_{scaled} \times \sigma_{target}$ (Scale only) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
*   **Post-Processing:**
    *   Apply the hard clip $\sigma_{final} = \max(\sigma_{final}, 70)$ strictly for the submission file **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.
