To improve the model's performance, I will implement a **Dual-Stream Point-Wise Residual** architecture as described in **Lesson 00052**, incorporating the "Over-Parameterized Linear Baseline" strategy from **Lesson 00060**.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Architectural Refinement (`library/model.py`):**
    -   Replace the Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) in `Stream A` (Clinical Anchor) with a simple **Linear Projection**. This stream will strictly process the dominant autoregressive features (`Baseline FVC` and `Relative Week`) without non-linearities. This allows the model to easily learn the identity mapping and linear decay trends (Cite **Lesson 00052**).
    -   Ensure this linear stream projects to the full latent dimension (64) rather than a minimal output, preventing optimization bottlenecks (Cite **Lesson 00060**).
    -   `Stream B` (Visual Interaction) will remain a deeper MLP processing the image embeddings and full tabular context to capture complex residuals.

2.  **Training Optimization (`library/config.py`):**
    -   Reduce `EPOCHS` from 50 to 30. The previous execution logs showed the validation metric peaking around Epoch 14 and then degrading due to overfitting. Residual architectures typically converge faster (Cite **Lesson 00052**), so a shorter schedule acts as a form of early stopping.

3.  **Input Handling:**
    -   In the model's `forward` pass, explicitly slice the tabular input to feed only `Base_FVC` (index 0) and `Rel_Week` (index 2) into the linear stream.
