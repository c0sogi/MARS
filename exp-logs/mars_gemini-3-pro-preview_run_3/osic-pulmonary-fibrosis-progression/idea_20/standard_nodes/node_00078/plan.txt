My implementation strategy focuses on restoring and refining the **Dual-Stream Point-Wise Residual (DSPRNet)** architecture which previously yielded the best results (Lesson 52). The current "Cascaded" approach (Stream B depending on Stream A) appears to introduce optimization difficulties, likely bottlenecking the strong linear signal from the baseline FVC.

1.  **Refactor Model Architecture (Cite Lesson 52, 60)**: I will modify `TSCRNet` in `library/model.py` to use two parallel, independent streams that are summed at the end:
    *   **Stream A (Linear Trend)**: Takes only `Baseline_FVC` and `Relative_Time` as inputs. It uses an MLP (Linear->ReLU->Linear) to over-parameterize the linear trend, ensuring the model can robustly learn the identity mapping and decay rate (Cite Lesson 60).
    *   **Stream B (Deep Interaction)**: Takes the concatenated `Image Projection` and *all* `Tabular Features`. This stream learns the complex, non-linear residuals (Cite Lesson 52).
    *   **Fusion**: The outputs of these parallel streams are summed (`h_trend + h_deep`) before passing to the heads. This removes the cascaded dependency.

2.  **Optimize Training Duration (Cite Lesson 9, 11)**: I will increase `Config.N_EPOCHS` from 20 to 30 in `runfile.py`. Previous lessons demonstrated that uncertainty calibration ($\sigma$) is a second-order effect that requires more iterations to converge than the point prediction mean, and the parallel residual architecture is robust enough to handle this without early overfitting.
