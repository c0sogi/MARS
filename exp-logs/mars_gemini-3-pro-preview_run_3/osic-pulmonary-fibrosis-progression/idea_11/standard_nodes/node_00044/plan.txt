The following changes implement a targeted optimization strategy based on the provided lessons:

1.  **Increase Fusion Capacity (Cite Lesson 00027)**: In `library/config.py`, I will increase the projection dimension `N_FEATURES` from 128 to 256. The lesson indicates that a larger projection dimension is necessary to preserve the rich semantic information from the EfficientNet backbone when fusing it with low-dimensional tabular data, preventing the image signal from being compressed too aggressively.

2.  **Fix Validation Metric Logging**: In `library/train.py`, I will flatten the `raw_target` tensor before passing it to the `metric_score` function. The previous implementation suffered from a broadcasting bug (subtracting a `(B,)` tensor from a `(B, 1)` tensor resulted in a `(B, B)` matrix), which caused the validation score logged during training to be incorrect. Fixing this ensures that the Early Stopping mechanism relies on the actual competition metric.

3.  **Extend Training Schedule (Cite Lesson 00009, Lesson 00027)**: In `runfile.py`, I will increase the training epochs from 15 to 50. The lessons highlight that multimodal architectures with frozen or fine-tuned backbones require significantly more gradient updates for the projection head to converge compared to tabular-only models. The previous 15-epoch limit likely caused the model to underfit.
