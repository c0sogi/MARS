My strategy focuses on addressing the "modality collapse" and early overfitting observed in the previous solution.

1.  **Balanced Multi-Modal Fusion**: The previous model concatenated 1408-dimensional image features directly with 5-dimensional clinical features. This dimensionality mismatch likely caused the optimizer to ignore the clinical signals or overfit the image noise. I will implement a projection layer to reduce the image features to 128 dimensions before concatenation, ensuring balanced contribution from both modalities. This applies **Cite solution_lesson_node_00146**.
2.  **Regularization**: To address the rapid overfitting (peaking at Epoch 3), I will increase the weight decay from 0.01 to 0.02.
3.  **Training Duration**: I will increase the epoch count to 25 to ensure the model has sufficient time to converge with the added regularization and bottleneck constraints.
