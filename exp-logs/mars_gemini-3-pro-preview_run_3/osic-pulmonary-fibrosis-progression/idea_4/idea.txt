Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the **Dual-Path Transformer-Fused Network**.

This solution introduces a structural innovation by decoupling the **non-linear contextual modeling** (analyzing lung texture and patient demographics) from the **linear progression modeling** (analyzing the baseline lung function and time). This addresses the critical conflict identified in **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**, where normalization layers in deep networks destroyed the strong linear signal of the baseline FVC.

### 1. Model
The architecture consists of two distinct pathways that merge only at the final regression head.

*   **Path 1: Contextual Interaction (Transformer-Based)**
    *   **Image Input:** The model processes **three content-adaptive slices** (Apical, Middle, Basal) per patient **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
    *   **Backbone:** A frozen **EfficientNet-B0** extracts a high-dimensional feature vector (1280-dim) from each slice.
    *   **Tokenization & Projection:**
        *   The three image vectors are projected to a lower dimension $D$ (e.g., 128) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**. Learnable **Positional Embeddings** (representing "Top", "Middle", "Bottom") are added to these tokens to preserve vertical spatial awareness.
        *   Categorical demographics (`Sex`, `SmokingStatus`) and `Age` are projected into a fourth "Metadata Token" of dimension $D$.
    *   **Transformer Encoder:** These 4 tokens (3 Image + 1 Meta) are passed through a lightweight **Transformer Encoder** (2 layers). This allows the model to learn complex "All-to-All" relationships (e.g., conditioning the visual analysis of the basal slice on the patient's smoking status) via self-attention mechanisms.
    *   **Output:** The output tokens are flattened to form the `Context_Vector`.

*   **Path 2: Linear Signal Preservation (Direct Skip)**
    *   This path takes the **Standardized Baseline FVC** and the relative time variable **Weeks**.
    *   Crucially, these features **bypass** the Transformer and all Normalization layers. They are fed directly to the final fusion layer. This prevents the "magnitude destruction" observed when LayerNorm is applied to strong linear predictors **Cite solution_lesson_node_00017**.

*   **Fusion & Head:**
    *   The `Context_Vector` (from Path 1) and the `Linear_Features` (from Path 2) are concatenated.
    *   **Regression Head:** A simple MLP (`Linear -> ReLU -> Linear`) maps this combined vector to the outputs $\mu$ (FVC) and $\sigma$ (Confidence).
    *   **No Dropout:** Dropout is explicitly excluded from this head to maintain the precision of the linear signal and uncertainty calibration **Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**.

### 2. Data
*   **Content-Adaptive Slice Selection:**
    *   We reject geometric slicing (e.g., "slice 10, 20, 30") due to registration failures **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
    *   Instead, we scan the CT volume to find the slice with the maximum lung area (Anchor). We then select two additional slices at the upper and lower boundaries where the lung area drops to 50% of the maximum. This ensures the model consistently views the Apical, Middle, and Basal regions regardless of patient positioning **Cite solution_lesson_node_00015**.
*   **Target Standardization:**
    *   The target variable `FVC` is **Z-score standardized** (mean 0, std 1) during training to resolve the magnitude gap between initialization and targets **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
    *   Input features (`Baseline FVC`, `Age`, `Weeks`) are similarly standardized.

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** Modified Laplace Log Likelihood.
    *   We optimize the competition metric directly: $L = \frac{|y_{true} - y_{pred}|}{\sigma} + \ln(\sigma)$.
*   **Uncertainty Constraints:**
    *   We do **not** enforce the metric's 70ml clip within the differentiable architecture. The model predicts raw uncertainty using `softplus(x) + 1e-3` to allow natural gradient flow **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.
*   **Optimizer & Schedule:**
    *   Optimizer: **AdamW**.
    *   Scheduler: **Cosine Annealing**.
    *   Duration: **35 Epochs**. This extended duration is mandatory to allow the Transformer projection layers to converge and the uncertainty head to calibrate itself after the mean prediction stabilizes **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**, **Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**.

### 4. Evaluation
*   **Inverse Transformation:**
    *   **FVC ($\mu$):** Unscale using the full transform: $\mu_{final} = \mu_{pred} \times \sigma_{target} + \mu_{target}$.
    *   **Confidence ($\sigma$):** Unscale using scale only: $\sigma_{final} = \sigma_{pred} \times \sigma_{target}$ **Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**.
*   **Post-Processing:**
    *   Apply the hard clip $\sigma_{final} = \max(\sigma_{final}, 70)$ only at the final submission generation step.
