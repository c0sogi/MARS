The implementation strategy involves refining the PCDS-Net architecture to align with the successful "Dual-Stream Point-Wise Residual" (DSPR-Net) design principles derived from the lessons. Specifically, I will modify `Stream A` in `library/model.py` to be a **Linear Autoregressive Residual** stream that projects only `Baseline FVC` and `Time` into the latent space using a single linear layer (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00052` and `solution_lesson_node_00060`). This enforces a strong inductive bias for the dominant linear trend, leaving `Stream B` to learn non-linear corrections from the image and full clinical context. Additionally, I will increase the training epochs in `runfile.py` to 25 to allow sufficient convergence for this residual structure.
