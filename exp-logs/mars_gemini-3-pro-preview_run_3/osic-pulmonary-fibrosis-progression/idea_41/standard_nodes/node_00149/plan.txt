To improve the model's performance, I will implement a "Dual-Stream Point-Wise Residual" (DSPR) architecture as recommended in Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00052`. The current PCDS-Net uses non-linear MLPs for both the clinical and visual streams. I will modify "Stream A" to be a strictly linear projection of the dominant autoregressive features (Baseline FVC and Time), removing the ReLU activation. This allows the model to easily learn the strong linear identity/decay relationship ($FVC_{t} \approx FVC_{base} + \text{rate} \times t$) without optimization bottlenecks, while "Stream B" (Deep Interaction) handles non-linear corrections from the image and full clinical context.

Additionally, I will increase the training duration to 25 epochs in `runfile.py` to ensure the Cosine Annealing scheduler completes its full decay cycle, as advised in Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00100`.
