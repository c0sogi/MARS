Based on the analysis of the previous attempts and the "Lessons" derived from them, I propose the following optimized strategy. This solution introduces a **Modernized CNN Backbone** and a **Multi-Sample Dropout Head** to address the limitations of receptive fields and convergence speed, while strictly adhering to the successful data and pooling strategies identified previously.

### Model
I propose a **ConvNeXt-Nano Audio Tagger** with **Dual-Stream Pooling** and **Multi-Sample Dropout**.

*   **Backbone (Structural Innovation):** I will replace the EfficientNet-B0 backbone with **ConvNeXt-Nano**.
    *   **Rationale:** While EfficientNet-B0 is efficient, its small 3x3 kernels limit its effective receptive field. ConvNeXt incorporates Transformer design principles—specifically **large 7x7 kernels** and Layer Normalization—into a pure CNN architecture. This allows the model to capture longer-range temporal dependencies and richer spectral textures (similar to a Transformer) without the optimization instability and data hunger associated with actual Transformer layers (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
    *   **Input Adaptation:** I will modify the first convolutional layer to accept **Single-Channel Input** by summing the pretrained RGB weights. This avoids the detrimental effects of engineering synthetic 3-channel inputs (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).
*   **Pooling Head (Strategic Retention):** I will retain the **Dual-Stream Pooling** mechanism, processing the feature map $X$ through two parallel branches:
    1.  **Non-Linear Attention Pooling:** Flattens the $F \times T$ dimensions and applies a `Linear -> Tanh -> Linear -> Softmax` scoring network. This preserves the spectral resolution required to disentangle polyphonic sounds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00016`) and uses non-linearity to effectively distinguish signal from noise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`).
    2.  **Global Max Pooling:** Captures the highest intensity activations to ensure sparse, transient events are not diluted (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00003`).
    The outputs of these branches will be concatenated (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).
*   **Classification Head (Structural Innovation):** I will implement **Multi-Sample Dropout** in the final classification layer. Instead of a single Dropout -> Linear path, the model will employ multiple parallel Dropout masks (e.g., 5 branches) followed by shared Linear weights. The outputs will be averaged during training. This technique acts as a strong regularizer and significantly accelerates convergence, directly supporting the goal of maximizing performance within limited training iterations (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00013`).

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Audio will be converted to **Log-Mel Spectrograms** (128 Mel bins).
*   **Sampling Rate:** I will strictly use a **32 kHz sampling rate** to minimize input dimensionality and maximize iteration velocity (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00013`).
*   **Duration Strategy:** Inputs will be fixed to **30 seconds**. Shorter clips will be padded with silence, and longer clips will be truncated. This ensures the model views the full context, avoiding the label noise from naive cropping (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`).
*   **Augmentation:** I will apply **Mixup** (alpha=0.4) and **SpecAugment** (Time/Frequency Masking). Mixup is critical for regularizing the single-channel input (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss (BCEWithLogitsLoss)**.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** **AdamW** optimizer with a **OneCycleLR** scheduler to facilitate super-convergence.
*   **Metric-Driven Early Stopping:** The training loop will monitor **Validation LWLRAP** to trigger early stopping. I will strictly decouple this from Validation Loss to prevent premature stopping when the loss rises due to calibration shifts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`).
*   **State Preservation:** I will explicitly use `copy.deepcopy()` when saving the best model checkpoint to ensure the weights corresponding to the peak metric are preserved (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the full 30-second spectrograms of the test files. No windowing or test-time cropping will be applied, ensuring the inference pipeline matches the training signal integrity.
