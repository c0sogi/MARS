Based on the provided lessons and previous ideas, I propose a structural evolution of the solution that transitions from a pure Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to a **CNN-Transformer Hybrid** architecture. This approach addresses the limitation of CNNs in modeling long-range temporal dependencies while leveraging the robust feature extraction of stronger backbones.

### Model
I propose a **Context-Aware Hybrid Architecture** that integrates three distinct stages:

1.  **Backbone (Scaled):** I will upgrade the backbone from EfficientNet-B0 to **EfficientNet-B2** initialized with ImageNet weights. This leverages the dynamic feature extraction logic established previously (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) to increase the model's capacity for discriminating complex spectral patterns without requiring manual hardcoding of feature dimensions.
2.  **Temporal Context Module (Innovation):** Unlike previous ideas that pooled CNN features directly, I will insert a **Transformer Encoder** block (with a projection layer to reduce dimensionality, e.g., to 768) between the CNN backbone and the pooling head. While the CNN captures local time-frequency patterns, the Transformer will model the *sequence* of these patterns over the 30-second clip, allowing the model to better distinguish between sound events based on their temporal context and rhythm.
3.  **Head:** I will employ **Multi-Head Attention Pooling**. This preserves the success of the Learnable Attention Pooling mechanism (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) but extends it to multiple heads. This allows the model to simultaneously attend to distinct, non-overlapping sound events (e.g., a "Dog Bark" at t=5s and a "Siren" at t=20s) within the same clip, which is critical for the multi-label nature of this task.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** Audio will be converted to **Log-Mel Spectrograms** with 128 Mel bins.
*   **Duration Strategy:** I will strictly adhere to the **30-second fixed duration** strategy. Audio shorter than 30 seconds will be padded with silence, and audio longer than 30 seconds will be truncated. This ensures the model processes the full context of the clip and avoids the label noise introduced by naive cropping of weakly labeled data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
*   **Augmentation:** I will retain the **Mixup** and **SpecAugment** (Time and Frequency Masking) strategies, as these have been proven to significantly improve generalization in multi-label settings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** The model will be trained using **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss (BCEWithLogitsLoss)**.
*   **Optimizer & Scheduler:** I will use the **AdamW** optimizer paired with a **OneCycleLR** scheduler for efficient super-convergence.
*   **State Preservation:** To ensure the final submission utilizes the optimal weights, the training loop will explicitly use `copy.deepcopy()` when saving the best model state, preventing the degradation of the checkpoint during subsequent training steps (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
*   **Dataset:** The model will be trained on the combined `train_curated` and `train_noisy` datasets to maximize data availability.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the full 30-second spectrograms of the test files. No windowing or test-time cropping will be applied, ensuring the inference pipeline matches the training signal integrity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
