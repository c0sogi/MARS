This is a schema mismatch between the raw data loader and the downstream optimization logic. To fix this, I will rename the `utcTimeMillis` column to `UnixTimeMillis` in the `df_wls` DataFrame immediately after loading it, ensuring consistency with the expected schema. Cite debug_lesson_4.
