To fix this, I will modify `library/features.py` to:
1.  Construct the `tripId` column in `process_drive` using `drive_id` and `phone_name`.
2.  Explicitly whitelist `tripId` in the `keep_cols` list for both the training/validation and inference branches, ensuring it is retained in the final dataset.
