Based on the accumulated lessons, I propose the **Sector-Aware Physics-Boosted Residual Ensemble** strategy.

This approach synthesizes the robustness of residual learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007) and Gradient Boosting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010) with a new feature engineering paradigm designed to solve the "Magnitude without Direction" problem identified in Lesson 00033. It explicitly rejects temporal smoothing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014) in favor of robust ensemble aggregation.

### 1. Model
The architecture consists of an **Ensemble of LightGBM Regressors**.
*   **Backbone:** Independent LightGBM models trained on $K$ folds.
*   **Targets:** The model predicts the **ENU (East-North-Up) Residuals** in meters:
    $$ \Delta E = East_{GT} - East_{WLS}, \quad \Delta N = North_{GT} - North_{WLS} $$
    This leverages the WLS baseline as a strong physical prior (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

### 2. Data
The preprocessing pipeline introduces **Spatially Binned Physics Features** to provide the directional context required to correct vector errors.

*   **Coordinate Transformation:** Convert Ground Truth and WLS positions to a local ENU frame.
*   **Physics Consistency (Doppler Residuals):**
    *   For each satellite, calculate the **Doppler Residual**: The difference between the measured `PseudorangeRateMetersPerSecond` and the theoretical range rate derived from satellite velocity and user-satellite geometry.
    *   This residual is a high-fidelity proxy for non-line-of-sight (multipath) errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00033).
*   **Sector-Based Feature Engineering (Innovation):**
    *   Divide the sky view into **4 Azimuthal Sectors** (North-East, South-East, South-West, North-West).
    *   For *each* sector, compute aggregated statistics:
        *   **Signal Quality:** Mean `Cn0DbHz`.
        *   **Geometry:** Count of satellites, Mean `SvElevationDegrees`.
        *   **Error Indicators:** Mean and Max **Doppler Residual**.
    *   **Rationale:** This explicitly couples error magnitude signals (Doppler residuals) with directional information (Sectors), allowing the tree model to learn rules like "High Doppler error in the North sector implies a Southward position correction" (Addressing Lesson 00033).
*   **Global Features:** Standard aggregates (Mean Cn0, IMU acceleration magnitude) to capture global signal state and device dynamics.
*   **Constraint:** Strictly **Point-Wise** features only. No temporal windowing to prevent trajectory memorization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. This L1 loss ensures the model learns the median correction and remains robust to the heavy-tailed outliers inherent in urban GNSS data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Strategy:** Train $K$ models using **GroupKFold** cross-validation grouped by `drive_id`. This guarantees evaluation on unseen drives.

### 4. Evaluation
The inference pipeline replaces the problematic Kalman Smoother with a robust ensemble method.
1.  **Feature Generation:** Compute the Sector-Based Physics features for the test set.
2.  **Ensemble Prediction:** Generate predictions from all $K$ fold models.
3.  **Robust Aggregation:** Compute the **Pixel-wise Median** of the predictions.
    *   This filters out stochastic outliers generated by individual models without "smearing" errors across time, avoiding the failure mode of temporal smoothing on heavy-tailed data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
4.  **Reconstruction:** Apply the median residuals to the WLS baseline to generate the final trajectory.
