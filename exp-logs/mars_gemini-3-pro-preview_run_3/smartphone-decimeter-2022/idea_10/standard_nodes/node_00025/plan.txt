This results in `gt_x` having more elements than the filtered WLS position array, causing the subtraction to fail.

To fix this, I will reorder the operations to perform the `dropna` on the dataset *before* calculating the Ground Truth ECEF coordinates. This ensures that all arrays involved in the residual calculation have the same length (Cite debug_lesson_8).
