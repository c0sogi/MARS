2.  The feature engineering function `project_forces` aggregates the data but only includes specific columns in the aggregation dictionary (`agg_funcs`). Since `drive_id` was not in the dataframe (and not in the aggregation list), it is dropped during the `groupby().agg()` operation.

1.  Modify `library/data_loader.py` to add `drive_id` and `phone_name` columns to the `aligned_gnss_df` immediately after alignment and before caching/returning.
2.  Modify `library/feature_eng.py` to include `drive_id` (and `phone_name`, `tripId`) in the aggregation dictionary within `project_forces` if they exist in the input dataframe, ensuring they propagate to the final feature set.
