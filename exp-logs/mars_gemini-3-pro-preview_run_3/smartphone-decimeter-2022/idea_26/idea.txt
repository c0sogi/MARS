Based on the accumulated lessons, specifically the critical importance of **Receiver State Metadata** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058), the precision of **Carrier Phase (TDCP)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00046), and the benefits of **Geometric Projection** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043), I propose the **State-Uncertainty Weighted Graph Fusion with Altitude-Corrected Geometric Boosting** strategy.

This strategy synthesizes the best components of previous ideas while introducing a novel **Dynamic Anchor Weighting** mechanism in the graph optimizer. It combines the clean "Altitude-Corrected" targets of Idea 24 with the rich feature set of Idea 16, and upgrades the trajectory reconstruction to explicitly use receiver health flags to modulate the trade-off between absolute position (ML) and relative motion (Physics).

### 1. Model: Context-Aware Geometric Ensemble
The predictive core remains an ensemble of **LightGBM Regressors**, optimized to map instantaneous signal snapshots to position errors.

*   **Architecture:** Independent models for **East** and **North** error components.
*   **Target:** The model predicts the **Altitude-Corrected ENU Residuals** in meters.
    *   **Method:** Project the Ground Truth Latitude/Longitude to the **WLS estimated altitude** before converting to ENU.
    *   **Rationale:** This removes artificial horizontal errors caused by projecting the ground truth to the wrong elevation, providing a cleaner training signal for the horizontal bias (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).
*   **Input Features (Unified & Contextual):**
    *   **Unified Geometric Force:** $\vec{F}_{PR} = \sum_{all} w_i \cdot r_{pr,i} \cdot \vec{u}_i$. The net error gradient derived from pseudorange residuals of *all* visible satellites (L1+L5) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00055).
    *   **Geometry Stiffness:** Elements of the weighted Geometry Matrix (DOP).
    *   **Receiver State:** `BiasNanos`, `BiasUncertaintyNanos`, `DriftNanosPerSecond`, `DriftUncertaintyNanosPerSecond`. These are critical proxies for hardware stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00058).
    *   **Exclusion:** Explicitly **exclude Doppler-derived features** from the ML input (Cite solution_lesson_node_00056).

### 2. Data: Point-Wise Features & Hybrid Kinematics
The preprocessing pipeline generates parallel streams for ML features and kinematic constraints.

*   **Stream A: Point-Wise ML Features**
    *   Compute the Unified Geometric Projection features and Receiver State statistics for every timestamp. Strictly **no temporal windowing** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Stream B: Hybrid Kinematic Shape**
    *   For every pair of timestamps $(t-1, t)$:
        *   **Primary (TDCP):** Solve Time-Differenced Carrier Phase using **RANSAC**. If successful ($>4$ inliers), use this vector and assign **High Confidence**.
        *   **Secondary (Doppler):** If TDCP fails, solve for velocity using RANSAC on Doppler measurements. Use this vector and assign **Medium Confidence**.

### 3. Training
*   **Loss Function:** **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**. This L1 loss minimizes the median error, ensuring the predictions are robust to outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Validation:** **GroupKFold** cross-validation grouped by `drive_id`.

### 4. Evaluation: State-Uncertainty Weighted Graph
The inference pipeline uses a global optimization that dynamically adjusts its trust in the ML model based on the receiver's reported health.

1.  **Anchor Prediction:** Generate predicted residuals using LightGBM and add them to the WLS baseline to get $\hat{P}_{ML}$.
2.  **Global Optimization:** Solve for the trajectory $X$ minimizing:
    $$ J(X) = \sum_{t} \frac{1}{\sigma_{clock, t}} \rho(x_t - \hat{P}_{ML, t}) + \sum_{t} \omega_{kin, t} ||(x_t - x_{t-1}) - \Delta \vec{x}_{kin, t}||^2 $$
    *   **Dynamic Anchor Weight ($\sigma_{clock, t}$):** The weight of the anchor term is inversely proportional to the **Bias Uncertainty** reported by the receiver.
        *   *Innovation:* When the receiver reports high uncertainty (unstable clock/signal), the graph loosens the anchor constraint, allowing the trajectory to follow the precise shape of the Carrier Phase kinematics without being dragged by potentially erroneous WLS/ML positions.
    *   **Hybrid Shape Term:** Enforces the relative motion derived from TDCP (or Doppler fallback), weighted by the RANSAC inlier count ($\omega_{kin, t}$).
