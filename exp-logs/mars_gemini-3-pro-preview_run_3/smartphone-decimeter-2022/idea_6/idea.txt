Based on the lessons learned, particularly the effectiveness of residual learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007), the superiority of Gradient Boosting for sensor data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010), and the catastrophic failure of standard smoothing on heavy-tailed errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014), I propose the following strategy.

### **Strategy: Quantile-Boosted Residuals with Uncertainty-Gated Smoothing**

This approach introduces a **probabilistic regression framework**. Instead of treating all model predictions as equally valid, it explicitly estimates the *uncertainty* of each prediction. This uncertainty is then used to dynamically gate a Kalman Smoother, allowing it to filter out the massive outliers that previously ruined performance while smoothing the reliable sections of the trajectory.

#### **1. Model**
The architecture utilizes **LightGBM (Gradient Boosting Decision Trees)** but shifts from standard regression to **Quantile Regression**.
*   **Backbone:** LightGBM is retained for its efficiency and ability to handle aggregated sensor statistics without overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Objectives:** For each target dimension (East and North residuals), the model trains three separate estimators corresponding to the quantiles $\tau \in \{0.1, 0.5, 0.9\}$.
    *   $\hat{y}_{0.5}$: The median prediction, serving as the robust correction vector.
    *   $\hat{y}_{0.1}$ and $\hat{y}_{0.9}$: The lower and upper bounds, used to estimate the aleatoric uncertainty (confidence) of the prediction.

#### **2. Data**
The preprocessing pipeline strictly adheres to **Point-Wise Feature Engineering** to ensure generalization across disjoint domains (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
*   **Coordinate Transformation:** Convert Ground Truth and WLS Baseline coordinates from Geodetic (Lat/Lon) to **ENU (East-North-Up)** meters relative to the WLS position. This provides an isotropic, physics-based target space.
*   **Feature Engineering:** Generate a single row of features per timestamp using aggregated statistics:
    *   **GNSS:** Mean, Max, and Std of `Cn0DbHz`; Count of visible satellites; Mean `SvElevationDegrees`.
    *   **IMU:** Mean and Std of total accelerometer magnitude (proxy for motion dynamics).
    *   **Constraint:** Do **not** use temporal windowing (lag/lead features) to prevent the trajectory memorization observed in previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).

#### **3. Training**
*   **Loss Function:** **Quantile Loss** (Check or Pinball Loss). This optimizes the model to output the specific percentiles required for the uncertainty estimation.
*   **Targets:**
    *   $\Delta East = East_{GT} - East_{WLS}$
    *   $\Delta North = North_{GT} - North_{WLS}$
    *   Predicting these residuals allows the model to focus on correcting biases in the heuristic baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Validation:** **GroupKFold** cross-validation grouped by `drive_id` to simulate the unseen test domain.

#### **4. Evaluation**
The inference pipeline implements the **Uncertainty-Gated Smoothing** mechanism to address the "smearing" problem (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014):
1.  **Prediction:** For each test timestamp, generate the median correction ($\hat{r} = \hat{y}_{0.5}$) and the uncertainty proxy ($\sigma_t = \hat{y}_{0.9} - \hat{y}_{0.1}$).
2.  **Base Correction:** Apply the median residual to the WLS baseline: $\hat{P}_{raw} = P_{WLS} + \hat{r}$.
3.  **Adaptive Kalman Smoothing:** Run a Kalman Smoother on the sequence $\hat{P}_{raw}$ with a **Dynamic Measurement Noise Matrix ($R_t$)**.
    *   Set the diagonal of $R_t$ proportional to the predicted uncertainty $\sigma_t$.
    *   **Logic:** When the model detects high uncertainty (e.g., urban canyon, multipath), $\sigma_t$ spikes $\rightarrow$ $R_t$ becomes large $\rightarrow$ The Kalman Filter ignores the potentially massive outlier prediction and relies on its internal motion model (constant velocity). When $\sigma_t$ is low, the filter trusts the ML correction.
    *   This effectively implements the "strict outlier rejection" required to make smoothing viable (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).
