# Deep Residual U-Net with EfficientNet Backbone and Robust Ensembling

### Model
I propose replacing the custom, shallow "LightUNet" with a **U-Net architecture utilizing an EfficientNet-B0 encoder**.
*   **Structural Innovation:** This transitions the solution from a simple, low-capacity custom CNN to a deep, residual network. The EfficientNet-B0 backbone introduces inverted residual blocks and Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) modules, providing significantly stronger inductive biases for texture and shape recognition than the previous simple convolutional blocks.
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first convolutional layer of the EfficientNet encoder will be structurally modified to accept **6 input channels** instead of the standard 3. This allows the model to directly ingest the physics-informed composite data.
*   **Decoder:** A symmetric U-Net decoder will be used, utilizing skip connections from the EfficientNet encoder stages to preserve high-resolution spatial details essential for segmenting thin contrail structures.

### Data
I will strictly retain the successful feature engineering pipeline identified in previous experiments.
*   **Physics-Informed Input:** The input will remain the **6-channel tensor** consisting of the "Ash" false-color composite (Bands 11, 13, 14, 15) and the pixel-wise temporal difference between the current and previous frame ($Ash_t - Ash_{t-1}$). **Cite Lesson solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Preprocessing:** Brightness temperature conversion and normalization will remain consistent with the baseline to ensure the pre-trained weights (initially learned on RGB natural images) can adapt effectively to the spectral data.

### Training
The training strategy combines transfer learning with the robust stabilization techniques developed in the lessons.
*   **Initialization:** The encoder will be initialized with **ImageNet pre-trained weights**. This leverages learned spatial hierarchies (edges, textures) that are transferrable to satellite imagery, addressing the "capacity" need identified when scaling up the model.
*   **Augmentation:** I will apply strong geometric augmentations (horizontal/vertical flips, random 90-degree rotations, and shift-scale-rotate) to prevent overfitting the deeper model to the limited spatial orientations of the training set. **Cite Lesson solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Stabilization Strategy:**
    *   **Online Top-K Checkpointing:** I will implement an online priority queue to track and save the **Top-5** best checkpoints during training, deleting inferior ones on the fly to conserve disk space. **Cite Lesson solution_lesson_node_00006**.
    *   **Intra-Run Averaging:** The final model will be constructed by averaging the weights of these Top-5 checkpoints.
    *   **Type-Safe Implementation:** The averaging function will explicitly filter for floating-point parameters to avoid corrupting integer buffers (like Batch Normalization statistics). **Cite Lesson solution_lesson_node_00005**.
*   **Loss Function:** A combination of Dice Loss and Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) will be used.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Test images will be processed into the 6-channel format. The **averaged model** (derived from the Top-5 checkpoints) will be used for prediction.
*   **Post-Processing:** A sigmoid activation followed by a fixed threshold (0.5) will generate the binary masks, which are then flattened into Run-Length Encoding (RLE) for submission.
