Based on the lessons learned from the previous solution, specifically **Lesson 00002** (Capacity + Augmentation requires sufficient training budget) and **Lesson 00007** (Weight Averaging requires convergence), the primary optimization is to extend the training duration. The previous run demonstrated that the EfficientNet-B0 U-Net with strong augmentations was still improving at Epoch 5, and averaging weights from the early, under-trained epochs degraded performance.

I will implement the following changes:
1.  **Increase Training Epochs**: In `library/config.py`, I will increase `EPOCHS` to 30. This ensures the model has enough time to converge on the augmented dataset, and that the "Top-5" checkpoints selected for averaging will come from a stable convergence plateau rather than the initial learning phase.
2.  **Remove Override**: In `runfile.py`, I will remove the code block that forcibly reduced epochs to 5, allowing the configuration file's setting to take effect.
3.  **Refine Weight Averaging**: In `library/utils.py`, I will modify the `average_weights` function to select integer parameters (like `num_batches_tracked`) from the *best* checkpoint (index 0) rather than the worst of the top-k (index -1), ensuring the most representative state statistics are preserved (Cite **Lesson 00005** regarding safe averaging).

These changes are computationally feasible within the 4-hour limit (estimated ~48 minutes for 30 epochs) and directly address the root cause of the previous performance regression.
