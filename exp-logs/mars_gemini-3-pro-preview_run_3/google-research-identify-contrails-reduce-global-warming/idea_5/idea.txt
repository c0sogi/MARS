# Optimized Strategy: U-Net with ConvNeXt Backbone and Extended Optimization

### Model
I propose replacing the EfficientNet-based U-Net with a **U-Net architecture utilizing a ConvNeXt-Tiny backbone**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Backbone Evolution):** While EfficientNet (used in Idea 2) is a strong baseline, **ConvNeXt** represents a "modernized" CNN architecture that incorporates design principles from Vision Transformers (e.g., larger $7 \times 7$ kernels, fewer activation functions, and Layer Normalization). This structural change provides a significantly larger effective receptive field and better global context modeling than standard CNNs, which is critical for distinguishing linear contrail features from complex, textured cloud backgrounds.
*   **Decoder:** A symmetric U-Net decoder will be constructed using feature maps extracted from the four stages of the ConvNeXt encoder (downsampling ratios of 4, 8, 16, and 32).
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first projection layer of the ConvNeXt encoder will be instantiated with **6 input channels** (via `timm`'s `in_chans` argument) to directly ingest the physics-informed tensor, preserving the successful feature engineering pipeline.

### Data
I will strictly retain the high-performing data pipeline validated in previous lessons:
*   **Physics-Informed Input:** The model will be trained on the **6-channel tensor** comprising the "Ash" false-color composite (Bands 11, 13, 14, 15) and the temporal difference ($Ash_t - Ash_{t-1}$). This explicitly encodes the physical properties (optical depth, temperature) and temporal dynamics required for the task (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**).
*   **Full Dataset Usage:** I will strictly use the **full training dataset**. Previous attempts to scale up the model (Idea 4) failed because the dataset was subsampled to save time, leading to poor generalization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00011**).
*   **Augmentation:** Strong geometric augmentations (ShiftScaleRotate, Flips) will be applied to prevent overfitting the higher-capacity ConvNeXt backbone (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**).

### Training
The training protocol is designed to correct the optimization deficits observed in failed attempts (Idea 5) and maximize the potential of the new backbone:
*   **Optimization Budget (Volume):** I will train for **40 epochs** using a batch size of 32. Previous experiments with heavier architectures (Idea 5) failed because increasing the batch size (to 64) without proportionally increasing epochs halved the number of gradient updates, leading to under-optimization (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**). Extending to 40 epochs ensures the ConvNeXt model receives sufficient gradient updates to converge.
*   **Loss Function:** A **Hybrid Dice Loss + Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) Loss** will be used. Pure BCE was shown to fail catastrophically on this imbalanced task (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00010**).
*   **Stabilization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Cosine Annealing** scheduler (T_max=40) to allow thorough exploration of the loss landscape (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**).
    *   **Online Top-K Checkpointing:** I will maintain a priority queue of the **Top-5** checkpoints based on validation Dice (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00006**).
    *   **Convergence-Aware Averaging:** The final model will be an average of the Top-5 checkpoints, but strictly restricted to those saved in the latter half of training (e.g., Epoch > 20) to ensure stability (**Cite solution_lesson_node_00007**).

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Test images will be processed into the 6-channel Ash+Difference format.
*   **Prediction:** The **Convergence-Aware Averaged Model** will generate probability maps.
*   **Post-Processing:** A sigmoid activation followed by a threshold of 0.5 will produce binary masks, which will be converted to Run-Length Encoding (RLE) for submission.
