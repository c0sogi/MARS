# Optimized Strategy: U-Net with ConvNeXt-Base on Multi-Order Temporal Composites

### Model
I propose upgrading the model architecture to a **U-Net with a ConvNeXt-Base backbone**.
*   **Structural Innovation (Backbone Scaling):** Previous experiments established that the encoder's representation power is the primary driver of performance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00018`) and that modern, isotropic architectures like ConvNeXt outperform traditional ones (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`). To push the performance ceiling further, I will scale from ConvNeXt-Small (used in Idea 7) to **ConvNeXt-Base**. This significantly increases the parameter count and feature extraction capacity, allowing the model to resolve even subtler contrail features that the "Small" variant might miss.
*   **Input Adaptation:** The first projection layer of the backbone will be structurally modified to accept **9 input channels** (an increase from 6), enabling the ingestion of the expanded temporal context described below.
*   **Decoder:** A standard symmetric U-Net decoder will be used. As proven in previous lessons, complex decoders (like U-Net++) do not compensate for encoder deficits, so resources are best invested in the stronger "Base" encoder (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00018`).

### Data
I will evolve the feature engineering paradigm from simple motion detection to **Multi-Order Temporal Encoding**:
*   **Multi-Order Temporal Input:** Instead of the previous 6-channel input (Current Frame + 1st Order Difference), I will construct a **9-channel tensor** to capture the *trajectory* and *acceleration* of contrail formation. The input will consist of:
    1.  **Channels 1-3:** "Ash" False-Color Composite at time $t$ (Current Frame).
    2.  **Channels 4-6:** Temporal Difference ($Ash_t - Ash_{t-1}$). Captures instantaneous motion/formation (Velocity).
    3.  **Channels 7-9:** Temporal Difference ($Ash_{t-1} - Ash_{t-2}$). Captures the history of motion (Acceleration).
    This expanded context helps the model distinguish between transient, growing contrails and consistent, moving cirrus clouds (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00001`).
*   **Statistical Alignment:** I will apply **ImageNet-style normalization** (subtracting mean, dividing by standard deviation) to the entire 9-channel tensor. This ensures the input distribution aligns with the pretrained backbone's internal weights, which was critical for the success of the previous best model (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00020`).
*   **Full Dataset & Augmentation:** I will use the **full training dataset** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`) and apply strong geometric augmentations (ShiftScaleRotate, Flips) to prevent the heavier ConvNeXt-Base model from overfitting (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`).

### Training
The training protocol is designed to support the heavy "Base" backbone within the 24-hour limit:
*   **Optimization Budget:** I will train for **40 epochs**. I will explicitly manage the batch size (utilizing Gradient Accumulation) to ensure the model receives a sufficient number of gradient updates, preventing the under-optimization observed in previous scaling attempts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00012`).
*   **Loss Function:** A **Hybrid Dice Loss + Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) Loss** will be used to ensure the model optimizes for the intersection metric rather than background accuracy (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010`).
*   **Precision:** I will strictly use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)**. This is essential to reduce the memory footprint of the ConvNeXt-Base model and accelerate training (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00020`).
*   **Stabilization:**
    *   **Scheduler:** Cosine Annealing with `T_max=40` to maximize exploration (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`).
    *   **Online Top-K Checkpointing:** I will maintain a priority queue of the **Top-5** checkpoints to conserve disk space (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00006`).
    *   **Convergence-Aware Averaging:** The final model will be an average of the Top-5 checkpoints, strictly restricted to the latter half of training (e.g., Epoch > 20) to ensure stability (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007`).

### Evaluation
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** The inference pipeline will include **Test Time Augmentation**. The model will predict on the original image and geometric transformations (flips), and the results will be averaged to marginalize over prediction variance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00016`).
