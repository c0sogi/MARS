The `RuntimeError` is caused by the use of `-1e9` as a masking value in the `AttentionPooling` layer. When training with Mixed Precision (AMP), operations are cast to `float16` (Half Precision), which has a minimum representable value of approximately -65,504. The value `-1e9` is far outside this range, causing an overflow during the `masked_fill` operation. To fix this, I will change `min_value` to `-1e4` (-10,000). This value fits safely within the `float16` dynamic range while remaining small enough to ensure that the Softmax output for padded tokens is effectively zero ($e^{-10000} \approx 0$). This directly applies the solution from debug_lesson_3.
