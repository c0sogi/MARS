The `RuntimeError` is caused by invoking `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` twice within a single training iteration: first inside the Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP) block and again during the standard gradient accumulation step. PyTorch's `GradScaler` forbids multiple unscaling calls per step. As explained in `debug_lesson_7`, the unscaling inside the AWP block is redundant because the perturbation calculation relies on the gradient direction (`grad / norm(grad)`), which is scale-invariant. The fix is to remove the `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` call from the AWP block, allowing the `GradScaler` to handle unscaling safely at the end of the step.
