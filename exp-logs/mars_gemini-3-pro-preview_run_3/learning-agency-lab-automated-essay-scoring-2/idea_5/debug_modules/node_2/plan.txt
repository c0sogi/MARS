The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` is caused by the default use of reentrant gradient checkpointing in the Hugging Face Transformers library, which conflicts with the PyTorch autograd engine during the backward pass, particularly when combined with `GradScaler` for mixed precision. To resolve this, I will explicitly disable reentrant checkpointing by passing `gradient_checkpointing_kwargs={"use_reentrant": False}` to the `gradient_checkpointing_enable` method in `library/model.py`.
