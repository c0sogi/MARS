1.  **Reducing Batch Size:** Lowering `TRAIN_BATCH_SIZE` to 8 and `VALID_BATCH_SIZE` to 16 in `library/config.py`.
2.  **Gradient Accumulation:** Introducing `GRADIENT_ACCUMULATION_STEPS = 2` in `library/config.py` to maintain the effective batch size of 16 (8 * 2).
3.  **Mixed Precision (AMP):** Updating `library/stage1_trainer.py` to use `torch.amp.autocast` and `GradScaler`. This reduces memory usage significantly by performing operations in half-precision where possible.
