The `RuntimeError` is caused by the use of `-1e9` as a masking value in the `AttentionPooling` module. When mixed precision training (AMP) is enabled, the model weights and intermediate tensors are often cast to `float16` (half precision). The range of `float16` is approximately $\pm 65,504$, making `-1e9` (negative one billion) unrepresentable, leading to an overflow error. To fix this, I will change the masking value to `-1e4` (negative ten thousand), which fits comfortably within the `float16` range while still being sufficiently negative to ensure the subsequent softmax operation results in zero probability for padded tokens.
