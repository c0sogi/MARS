The training failure was caused by numerical instability (NaN loss) when fine-tuning the DeBERTa-v3-large model using `float16` mixed precision. This model architecture is prone to gradient overflows in `float16`. To resolve this, I will switch the mixed precision dtype to `bfloat16` (supported by the A100 GPU), which maintains the dynamic range of `float32`. I will also reduce the `max_grad_norm` to `1.0` to strictly clip gradients and update the masking value in `AttentionPooling` to `-1e4` to prevent underflow/overflow during softmax (Cite debug_lesson_6). Finally, I will disable the `GradScaler` in the training loop, as gradient scaling is unnecessary for `bfloat16` and `float32`.
