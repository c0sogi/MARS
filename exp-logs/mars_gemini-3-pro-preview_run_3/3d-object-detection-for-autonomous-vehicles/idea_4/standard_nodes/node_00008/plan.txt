The `RuntimeError` during training is caused by the `Voxelizer` producing tensors of variable sizes (based on the number of non-empty pillars), which `torch.stack` in the `collate_fn` cannot handle. To fix this, I will modify `library/dataset.py` to ensure the `Voxelizer` always outputs tensors of a fixed size (`max_pillars`), using `-1` to pad invalid coordinates. Correspondingly, I will update `library/model.py` to mask out these padded pillars during the scatter operation to prevent them from corrupting the feature map.
