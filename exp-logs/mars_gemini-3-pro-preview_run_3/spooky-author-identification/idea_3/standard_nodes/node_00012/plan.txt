I will optimize the solution by reverting from the complex Stacking architecture (Idea 3) to a simplified weighted ensemble (Idea 2), but with significant refinements to ensure convergence and performance.

**Strategy:**
1.  **Simplify Architecture (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}):** I will remove the Cross-Validation loop and the Meta-Learner. Instead, I will train a single instance of the Statistical Expert and a single instance of the Neural Expert on the 80% training split and validate on the 20% hold-out split. This aligns with the lesson that "Simpler Ensembles Outperform Complex Stacking Under Constraints."
2.  **Enhance Neural Branch:** I will stick with `microsoft/deberta-v3-large` (which showed promise with ~0.36 loss in 1 epoch) but increase the training duration to 3 epochs to ensure convergence. The time saved by removing CV and the RoBERTa model allows for this deeper training.
3.  **Orthogonal Blending (Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}):** I will combine the "Bag-of-Patterns" (Statistical) and "Sequence-Context" (Neural) models using a scalar weight optimized via `scipy.optimize.minimize_scalar` on the validation set. This directly leverages the orthogonality of their error modes.

**Modifications:**
-   **`library/config.py`**: Increase `EPOCHS` to 3, remove `MODEL_ROBERTA` and Stacking configs.
-   **`runfile.py`**: Rewrite `main()` to execute the linear blending pipeline: Train Stat -> Train Neural -> Optimize Weights -> Predict.
