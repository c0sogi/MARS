The implementation strategy orchestrates the "Domain-Adapted Hybrid Ensemble" by sequentially executing the statistical and neural pipelines. First, it loads the data and runs the statistical branch (TF-IDF with LR/NB) to generate baseline predictions. Then, it iterates through the two neural backbones (DeBERTa and RoBERTa), performing Domain-Adaptive Pre-training (MLM) on the combined corpus followed by supervised fine-tuning on the training set. To ensure efficiency and fit within the runtime limits, it utilizes the provided configuration settings (optimized for A100) and aggressive garbage collection between neural models. Finally, it optimizes the ensemble weights on the validation set, computes the required metrics and failure analysis correlations, and generates a submission if the performance threshold is met.
