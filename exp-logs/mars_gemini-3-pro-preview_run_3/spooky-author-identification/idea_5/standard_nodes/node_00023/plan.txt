The execution failed due to a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` during the Masked Language Modeling (MLM) phase. The error analysis identified that the `MLM_BATCH_SIZE` of 16 caused a massive memory allocation (~3.91 GiB) for the logit tensor `(Batch, Seq, Vocab)` because DeBERTa-v3 has a very large vocabulary (~128k tokens) compared to other models (Cite debug_lesson_8). Additionally, the assumed hardware capacity (A100) did not match the actual available VRAM (16GB) (Cite debug_lesson_6).

To fix this, I will reduce both the supervised `BATCH_SIZE` and the `MLM_BATCH_SIZE` from their current values (8 and 16, respectively) to 4 in `library/config.py`. This reduction will lower the memory footprint of the logit tensors and activations, allowing the model to fit within the 16GB VRAM limit.
