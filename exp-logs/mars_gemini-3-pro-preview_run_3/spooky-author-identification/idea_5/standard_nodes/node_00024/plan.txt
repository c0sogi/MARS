The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` during Masked Language Modeling (MLM) is caused by the large vocabulary size of `microsoft/deberta-v3-base` (~128k tokens). With `MLM_BATCH_SIZE=16` and sequence length 512, the logit tensor `(16, 512, 128000)` consumes ~4.2GB of VRAM, which, combined with model weights and activations, exceeds the 16GB GPU capacity.

To fix this, I will:
1.  **Reduce Batch Size:** In `library/config.py`, lower `MLM_BATCH_SIZE` to 4 to reduce peak memory usage (Cite debug_lesson_8).
2.  **Enable Gradient Accumulation:** In `library/config.py`, add `MLM_GRAD_ACCUMULATION_STEPS = 4` to maintain the effective batch size of 16 (Cite debug_lesson_7).
3.  **Implement Accumulation Logic:** In `library/engine.py`, update `train_mlm` to scale the loss, accumulate gradients over steps, and only update weights every `accum_steps`.
