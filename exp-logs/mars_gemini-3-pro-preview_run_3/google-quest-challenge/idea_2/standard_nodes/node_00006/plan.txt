The `torch.OutOfMemoryError` indicates that the GPU ran out of memory during training. This is caused by the `BATCH_SIZE` of 16 being too large for the Siamese network architecture (which processes two inputs per sample) combined with the `mpnet-base` model and a sequence length of 512. To resolve this without affecting the training dynamics, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` to 8 and increase `GRADIENT_ACCUMULATION_STEPS` to 2. This reduces the immediate memory footprint while maintaining the same effective batch size for gradient updates.
