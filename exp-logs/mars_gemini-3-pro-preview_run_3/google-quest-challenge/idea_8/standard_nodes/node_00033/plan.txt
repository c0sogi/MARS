The `RuntimeError: Trying to backward through the graph a second time` encountered during fine-tuning is caused by enabling gradient checkpointing on the Transformer backbone without ensuring the input embeddings require gradients. As detailed in **debug_lesson_8**, PyTorch's gradient checkpointing mechanism relies on the inputs having `requires_grad=True` to correctly anchor the computational graph for the re-computation step during backpropagation. Since `input_ids` are integers and do not require gradients by default, the autograd engine fails to track dependencies correctly.

To fix this, I will modify `library/model_core.py` to call `self.backbone.enable_input_require_grads()` immediately after enabling gradient checkpointing in the `BackboneWrapper` initialization. This ensures the embeddings are marked as requiring gradients, allowing the checkpointing mechanism to function correctly (Cite debug_lesson_8).
