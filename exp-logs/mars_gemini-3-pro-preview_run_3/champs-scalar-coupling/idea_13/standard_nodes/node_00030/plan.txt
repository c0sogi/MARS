1.  **Mitigate Task Dominance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00009`):** The auxiliary losses (Magnetic Shielding, Charges) likely have much larger magnitudes than the standardized coupling loss, causing the optimizer to ignore the primary task. I will reduce `LAMBDA_AUX` in `library/config.py` from `0.1` to `0.001` to balance the gradients.
2.  **Extend Training Duration (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00020`):** The previous run (3 epochs) was far too short for the complex Directional Message Passing Neural Network (DMPNN) to converge, despite the high throughput (~30s/epoch). I will increase `MAX_EPOCHS` to `50` in `runfile.py`, which fits comfortably within the time limit (~25 mins total).
3.  **Optimize Data Throughput (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00016`):** The dataset (flattened arrays) is small enough (<5GB) to fit entirely in the 220GB RAM. I will remove `mmap_mode='r'` in `library/dataset.py` to eliminate disk I/O latency. I will also increase `BATCH_SIZE` to `512` and enable `DROPOUT` (`0.1`) in `library/config.py` to stabilize gradients and regularize the longer training.
