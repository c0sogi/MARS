The issue stems from unintended broadcasting in the `TargetScaler` methods within `library/utils.py`. When `transform` or `inverse_transform` operates on targets of shape `(Batch, 1)` and retrieved statistics of shape `(Batch,)`, PyTorch broadcasts the operation to produce a `(Batch, Batch)` matrix instead of the intended `(Batch, 1)` vector. This causes the validation concatenation to fail when batch sizes differ (e.g., the final partial batch), as `torch.cat` expects consistent dimensions. To fix this, I will explicitly reshape the gathered means and standard deviations to `(-1, 1)` to match the target shape, ensuring element-wise operations. Cite debug_lesson_1.
