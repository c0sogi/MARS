3.  **Optimize Training Schedule (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016):** Given the high throughput of the Structure-of-Arrays data pipeline (approx. 5 seconds per epoch in the previous run), I will increase `MAX_EPOCHS` in `runfile.py` from 5 to 20.
