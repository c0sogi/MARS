The `RuntimeError` during batch collation is caused by PyTorch Geometric's default behavior of concatenating custom attributes along dimension 0. The attributes `triplets` (shape `[2, T]`) and `coupling_atom_index` (shape `[2, C]`) are adjacency-like structures that must be concatenated along dimension 1 (columns) to preserve their structure across the batch. Additionally, the indices in `triplets` refer to edges, so they must be incremented by the number of edges in previous graphs (not nodes) during batching, as highlighted in Cite debug_lesson_13.

To fix this, I will introduce a custom `MolecularData` class inheriting from `torch_geometric.data.Data` in `library/dataset.py`. This class will override `__cat_dim__` to enforce column-wise concatenation for these attributes and `__inc__` to define the correct increment logic (incrementing `triplets` by `edge_index.size(1)`). I will then update the `MolecularGraphDataset` to instantiate this custom class.
