This script performs a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the apple disease dataset. It begins by analyzing the distribution of the target classes to identify imbalances. It then processes the image data to extract meta-features such as dimensions, channel counts, and pixel intensity statistics. Finally, it investigates relationships between these image meta-features and the disease labels to uncover potential signals or biases in the data (e.g., whether certain diseases are associated with specific lighting conditions or image orientations).
