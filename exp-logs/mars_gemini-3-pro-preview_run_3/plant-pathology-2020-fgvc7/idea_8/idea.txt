### Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble of Multi-Axis and Convolutional Experts**. This strategy structurally evolves the previous "Dual-Backbone" approach by replacing the Window-based Transformer (Swin) with a **Multi-Axis Vision Transformer (MaxViT)**, while retaining the successful EfficientNet component and the Multi-Level GeM architecture.

1.  **Backbone 1: EfficientNet-B4 (Texture Expert)**
    *   **Retention:** We retain the EfficientNet-B4 backbone initialized with ImageNet weights. Its MBConv blocks are highly effective at capturing the high-frequency textural details required to distinguish between rust and scab **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   **Head Structure:** We employ **Multi-Level Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. We extract feature maps from the last three reduction stages (strides 8, 16, and 32), apply a learnable GeM pooling layer ($p=3$) to each, and concatenate the outputs. This ensures the model retains sensitivity to small, localized lesions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

2.  **Backbone 2: MaxViT-Tiny (Global-Local Hybrid Expert)**
    *   **Structural Innovation:** We replace the Swin Transformer with **MaxViT-Tiny**. While Swin relies on Shifted Window attention (local context), MaxViT introduces **Multi-Axis Attention**, combining Block Attention (local) and Grid Attention (sparse global).
    *   **Rationale:** The "Grid Attention" mechanism allows the model to attend to spatially distant features (e.g., multiple scattered rust spots) in a single layer, providing a more robust global context than Swin's window shifting. Furthermore, MaxViT's convolutional stem and MBConv blocks provide a stronger inductive bias, addressing the training instability and variance observed with Swin on this small dataset **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Head Structure:** Similar to the CNN, we extract outputs from the hierarchical stages of MaxViT, apply **GeM Pooling** to each, and concatenate them.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **EfficientNet Pipeline:** Images are resized to **380x380** to maximize spatial resolution for texture analysis.
    *   **MaxViT Pipeline:** Images are resized to **224x224** to align with the native block configuration of the architecture.
2.  **Augmentation:**
    *   We employ a **Strong Geometric Augmentation** strategy, including `ShiftScaleRotate` (with wide scaling limits), `RandomFlip`, and `Transpose`. We maintain high intensity to force the learning of invariant features **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    *   **Exclusions:** We strictly exclude spatial occlusion (Cutout) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004** and photometric augmentations (Brightness/Contrast) **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**, as these have been proven to degrade performance by removing or corrupting the critical color/texture signals of disease lesions.

### Training
1.  **Loss Function:** We use **Cross-Entropy Loss with Inverse Frequency Class Weights** to explicitly optimize for the macro-averaged metric by penalizing misclassifications of the minority "multiple_diseases" class **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a Cosine Annealing scheduler.
    *   **Mixed Precision:** We implement **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to accelerate training and increase batch size **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.
    *   **Schedule:** We train for a maximum of **25 epochs** with a **Relaxed Early Stopping patience of 10 epochs**. This extended patience is critical to allow the Hybrid MaxViT architecture to overcome initial volatility and settle into a robust minimum, preventing the premature termination observed with stricter schedules **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
3.  **Cross-Validation:** The strategy is implemented using **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**, resulting in 10 trained models (5 EfficientNet + 5 MaxViT).

### Evaluation
1.  **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We generate predictions for the **Original** image and a **Horizontal Flip**. We average these two probability vectors for each model.
2.  **Constraint:** We explicitly **exclude Vertical Flips and Transpose** from the TTA pipeline. While useful for training, these orientations violate the natural gravity priors of the leaf images during inference and have been shown to degrade test performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.
3.  **Ensemble:** The final submission is the unweighted average of the predictions from all 10 models.
