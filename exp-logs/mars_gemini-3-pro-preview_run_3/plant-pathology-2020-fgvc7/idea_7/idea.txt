### Model
I propose a **Hybrid Ensemble with Multi-Level Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. This architecture structurally evolves the previous "Dual-Backbone Hierarchical Ensemble" by upgrading the feature aggregation mechanism to specifically target the "small, localized" nature of disease lesions identified in the lessons.

1.  **Backbone 1: EfficientNet-B4 with Multi-Level GeM Pooling**
    *   **Architecture:** We will utilize the **EfficientNet-B4** backbone.
    *   **Structural Innovation:** Instead of using Global Average Pooling (GAP), which dilutes the signal of small lesions by averaging them with the healthy background, we will implement **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. GeM is a learnable pooling layer where $p \to \infty$ approximates Max Pooling. This allows the model to focus on high-activation regions (lesions) while suppressing noise.
    *   **Multi-Level Aggregation:** We will extract feature maps from the last three reduction stages (strides 8, 16, and 32). We will apply a **GeM Pooling** layer to *each* stage independently and concatenate the resulting vectors. This ensures that fine-grained textural details from earlier layers are preserved and emphasized **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.

2.  **Backbone 2: Swin Transformer Small with Multi-Stage GeM Pooling**
    *   **Architecture:** We will upgrade the Transformer component from Swin-Tiny to **Swin-Small**. This increases model capacity to capture more complex dependencies, which has been shown to improve performance when managed correctly **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Aggregation:** We will extract outputs from **Stage 2, Stage 3, and Stage 4** of the Swin hierarchy. Similar to the CNN, we will apply **GeM Pooling** to each stage's output before concatenation. This aligns the Transformer's self-attention mechanism with the lesion-focused pooling strategy.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **EfficientNet-B4 Pipeline:** Images will be resized to **380x380** to maximize spatial resolution.
    *   **Swin-Small Pipeline:** Images will be resized to **224x224** to align with standard window configurations.
2.  **Augmentation:**
    *   We will employ a **Strong Geometric Augmentation** strategy during training, including `ShiftScaleRotate` (with wide scaling limits), `RandomFlip`, and `Transpose`. We will strictly maintain high intensity to force the learning of invariant features **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    *   **Exclusions:**
        *   We will **strictly exclude** any spatial occlusion augmentations (Cutout, CoarseDropout) to prevent the removal of small disease indicators **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
        *   We will **strictly exclude** photometric augmentations (Brightness, Contrast) as the disease classes are color-critical and uncorrelated with lighting conditions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.

### Training
1.  **Loss Function:** We will use **Cross-Entropy Loss with Inverse Frequency Class Weights**. This aligns the optimization objective with the macro-averaged evaluation metric by penalizing misclassifications of the minority "multiple_diseases" class more heavily **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
2.  **Optimization:** We will use the **AdamW** optimizer with a **Cosine Annealing** learning rate scheduler.
3.  **Strategy:**
    *   We will train for a maximum of **20-25 epochs** with an **Early Stopping patience of 10 epochs**.
    *   **Rationale:** The upgrade to Swin-Small introduces higher variance. Relaxing the patience allows the Cosine Annealing scheduler to fully decay the learning rate and lets the larger model settle into a robust minimum, preventing the premature termination observed with stricter schedules **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008**.
    *   Training will be performed using **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** During inference, we will generate predictions for the **Original** image, a **Horizontal Flip**, and a **Vertical Flip**. We will average these predictions to reduce variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
2.  **TTA Restriction:** We will explicitly **exclude Transpose** from the TTA pipeline. While useful for training, it violates the natural orientation priors of the leaf images during inference and degrades performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
3.  **Ensemble:** The final submission will be the unweighted average of the probability vectors from all 10 trained models (2 architectures $\times$ 5 folds).
