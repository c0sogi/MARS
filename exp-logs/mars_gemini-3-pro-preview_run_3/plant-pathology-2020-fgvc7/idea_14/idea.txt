Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the **Distillation-Anchored Heterogeneous Pyramid Ensemble**. This strategy structurally evolves the solution by integrating **Feature Pyramid Networks (FPN)** with **Decoupled Multi-Task Learning**, and introduces a **Knowledge Distillation** training regime to explicitly stabilize the high-capacity Transformer backbone.

### Model
I propose a dual-backbone ensemble that synthesizes the texture-extraction strengths of CNNs with the global context of Transformers, enhanced by a novel hierarchical feature aggregation and distillation mechanism.

1.  **Backbone 1: EfficientNetV2-M (Texture Teacher)**
    *   **Architecture:** We utilize the **EfficientNetV2-M** backbone. Its **Fused-MBConv** blocks are retained for their superior ability to capture high-frequency textural details (e.g., rust edges) compared to standard depthwise convolutions.
    *   **Input Resolution:** **512x512**. This high resolution is critical to prevent the vanishing of minute disease lesions during downsampling **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.

2.  **Backbone 2: MaxViT-Small (Global Student)**
    *   **Architecture:** We utilize the **MaxViT-Small** backbone. Its **Multi-Axis Attention** (Block + Grid) captures long-range dependencies across the leaf surface.
    *   **Input Resolution:** **384x384**. This balances spatial detail with the computational cost of the attention mechanism.

3.  **Structural Innovation: FPN-GeM Aggregation**
    *   **Problem:** Previous ideas utilized either simple concatenation of multi-level features (Idea 13) or FPNs without task decoupling (Idea 12).
    *   **Solution:** We implement a **Feature Pyramid Network (FPN)** neck for *both* backbones. We extract features from the last three stages and fuse them top-down. This propagates strong semantic class information to the high-resolution layers that contain fine spatial details **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005**.
    *   **Pooling:** We apply **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** ($p=3$) to *each* FPN level independently and concatenate the outputs. This ensures the model retains sensitivity to high-activation anomalies (lesions) while leveraging the multi-scale pyramid **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

4.  **Head: Decoupled Multi-Task Classification**
    *   The fused FPN-GeM vector feeds into a **Decoupled Multi-Task Head**:
        *   **Main Head:** 4-unit Softmax (Healthy, Multiple, Rust, Scab).
        *   **Auxiliary Head A:** Binary Sigmoid ("Has Rust").
        *   **Auxiliary Head B:** Binary Sigmoid ("Has Scab").
    *   This forces the model to learn robust representations for the constituent diseases (Rust/Scab) that comprise the minority "Multiple Diseases" class.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **EfficientNetV2 Pipeline:** Resize images to **512x512**.
    *   **MaxViT Pipeline:** Resize images to **384x384**.
2.  **Augmentation:**
    *   We employ a **Strong Geometric Augmentation** strategy (`ShiftScaleRotate`, `RandomFlip`) with high intensity to act as the primary regularizer **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    *   **Strict Exclusions:**
        *   **No Spatial Occlusion:** We strictly exclude Cutout/CoarseDropout. Removing image patches risks deleting the sparse disease lesions that define the class **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
        *   **No Photometric Augmentation:** We strictly exclude Brightness/Contrast shifts to preserve the specific color signals (e.g., rust red vs. scab brown) critical for diagnosis **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.

### Training
1.  **Structural Innovation: Two-Stage Distillation**
    *   **Rationale:** High-capacity Transformers (like MaxViT) exhibit high variance and instability on small datasets **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Stage 1 (Anchor):** We train the **EfficientNetV2-M** independently using the Multi-Task Loss. This model converges reliably due to its strong inductive bias.
    *   **Stage 2 (Distillation):** We train the **MaxViT-Small** using a composite loss function:
        *   $L_{Total} = L_{MultiTask} + \alpha \cdot L_{KL}(Teacher_{Logits} || Student_{Logits})$.
        *   The frozen EfficientNetV2-M acts as a teacher, providing soft labels that smooth the optimization landscape for the MaxViT, preventing it from overfitting to noise.

2.  **Loss Function:**
    *   **Main Loss:** Cross-Entropy with **Inverse Frequency Class Weights** to prioritize the minority "Multiple" class **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
    *   **Aux Loss:** Binary Cross-Entropy.

3.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Cosine Annealing**.
    *   **Schedule:** We train for **25 epochs** with a **Relaxed Patience of 10 epochs**. This extended patience is crucial to allow the **Model EMA** (Exponential Moving Average) weights to converge and prevents premature termination during the high-variance phase **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Precision:** **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** is used to maximize batch size **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference Weights:** We generate predictions using the **EMA weights** of both models.
2.  **Domain-Aware Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):**
    *   We generate predictions for the **Original** image and a **Horizontal Flip**.
    *   **Constraint:** We explicitly **exclude Vertical Flips and Transpose**. These transformations violate the natural gravity priors of leaf images, creating "unnatural" inputs that degrade inference accuracy **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
3.  **Ensemble:** The final submission is the unweighted average of the probability vectors from the Teacher (EfficientNetV2-M) and the Distilled Student (MaxViT-Small).
