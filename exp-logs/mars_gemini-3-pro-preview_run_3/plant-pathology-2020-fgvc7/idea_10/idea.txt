Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the following optimized strategy. This approach structurally evolves the ensemble by integrating **EfficientNetV2** to leverage Fused-MBConv layers for superior texture extraction and scaling the Transformer component to **MaxViT-Small**, managed by a specific training regime to handle increased capacity.

### Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble of Fused-Convolution and Multi-Axis Experts**. This design upgrades the backbone architectures to specifically target the "fine-grained texture" vs. "global context" trade-off, utilizing the successful Multi-Level GeM aggregation.

1.  **Backbone 1: EfficientNetV2-M (Texture Expert)**
    *   **Structural Innovation:** We replace the EfficientNet-B4 with **EfficientNetV2-M**. Unlike the standard EfficientNet (which uses Depthwise Separable Convolutions everywhere), EfficientNetV2 utilizes **Fused-MBConv** blocks in the early stages. These blocks replace depthwise-separable convolutions with standard dense convolutions.
    *   **Rationale:** Fused-MBConv layers are empirically better at capturing low-level, high-frequency textural details (such as the specific edges of rust spots or scab lesions) compared to the aggressive factorization of standard MBConv, while also being computationally faster on modern accelerators.
    *   **Head Structure:** We retain the **Multi-Level Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** head. We extract feature maps from the last three stages, apply GeM pooling ($p=3$), and concatenate them to preserve localized lesion signals **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

2.  **Backbone 2: MaxViT-Small (High-Capacity Global Expert)**
    *   **Structural Innovation:** We upgrade the MaxViT-Tiny to **MaxViT-Small**.
    *   **Rationale:** While previous experiments with Swin-Small showed instability **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**, the MaxViT architecture's use of a convolutional stem and MBConv blocks provides better inductive bias. Scaling to "Small" increases the capacity of the **Grid Attention** mechanism to model complex, long-range dependencies (e.g., scattered lesions across the leaf surface) that the Tiny variant might miss.
    *   **Head Structure:** We apply the same **Multi-Level GeM Pooling** strategy to the hierarchical outputs of the MaxViT-Small backbone.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **EfficientNetV2 Pipeline:** Images will be resized to **384x384**. This resolution balances the high spatial detail required for texture analysis with the memory constraints of the larger V2-M backbone.
    *   **MaxViT Pipeline:** Images will be resized to **224x224** to align with the native block configuration.
2.  **Augmentation:**
    *   We will employ a **Strong Geometric Augmentation** strategy (ShiftScaleRotate, RandomFlip) with high intensity to force invariant feature learning **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    *   **Exclusions:**
        *   **No Spatial Occlusion:** We strictly exclude Cutout/CoarseDropout to prevent the removal of small disease lesions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
        *   **No Photometric Augmentation:** We strictly exclude Brightness/Contrast shifts to preserve critical color signals **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.

### Training
1.  **Loss Function:** We use **Cross-Entropy Loss with Inverse Frequency Class Weights** to align the optimization with the macro-averaged metric, prioritizing the minority "multiple_diseases" class **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
2.  **Optimization Strategy:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Cosine Annealing**.
    *   **Relaxed Early Stopping:** We will set the Early Stopping patience to **10-12 epochs** (with a max of 25-30 epochs). This is critical to accommodate the **MaxViT-Small** backbone. Larger, high-variance models require a longer "cooldown" period at the end of the Cosine schedule to settle into a robust minimum; strict patience would terminate training prematurely during the high-variance phase **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Mixed Precision:** We use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to handle the increased memory footprint of the V2-M/Small backbones and improve throughput **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.
3.  **Cross-Validation:** 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation.

### Evaluation
1.  **Domain-Aware Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):**
    *   We will generate predictions for the **Original** image and a **Horizontal Flip** only.
    *   **Constraint:** We explicitly **exclude Vertical Flips and Transpose**. Previous lessons demonstrated that these views violate the natural gravity priors of the leaf images (stems usually point up/down), creating "unnatural" inputs that degrade inference performance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.
2.  **Ensemble:** The final submission will be the unweighted average of the probability vectors from all 10 trained models (2 architectures $\times$ 5 folds).
