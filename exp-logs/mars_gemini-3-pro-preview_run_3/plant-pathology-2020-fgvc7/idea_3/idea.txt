### Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Multi-Resolution Ensemble** that structurally evolves the previous ensemble approach by explicitly targeting the "small, localized" nature of disease lesions identified in the lessons.
1.  **High-Resolution Expert (EfficientNet-B3):** Instead of the EfficientNet-B0 used previously, we will employ an **EfficientNet-B3** backbone. Crucially, this model will be trained on **300x300** pixel inputs (up from 224x224). This structural change increases the spatial resolution of the feature maps, allowing the model to better resolve the fine-grained, sparse lesions (like rust spots) that were difficult to detect with occlusion augmentations **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
2.  **Contextual Expert (ConvNeXt-Tiny):** We will retain the **ConvNeXt-Tiny** model from the previous idea, operating at **224x224** resolution. This component provides architectural diversity (Transformer-style large kernels) to capture global leaf structure and context, complementing the texture-biased CNN.

### Data
*   **Preprocessing:** We will create two parallel data pipelines. Pipeline A resizes images to **300x300** for the EfficientNet-B3, and Pipeline B resizes images to **224x224** for the ConvNeXt-Tiny.
*   **Augmentation:** We will apply a **Strong Geometric Augmentation** strategy (specifically `ShiftScaleRotate`, `RandomFlip`, and `Rotate`) to both pipelines. We will strictly maintain high augmentation intensity (e.g., wide scaling limits) to force the learning of invariant features **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**. However, we will explicitly **exclude** any spatial occlusion techniques (such as Cutout, CoarseDropout, or GridMask) to ensure that small disease indicators are never artificially removed from the training data **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** Both models will be trained using **Cross-Entropy Loss with Inverse Frequency Class Weights**. This ensures the optimization objective is aligned with the macro-averaged metric by penalizing misclassifications of the minority "multiple_diseases" class more heavily **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
*   **Progressive Resizing (EfficientNet-B3 only):** To stabilize the training of the larger B3 model, we will employ a progressive resizing strategy: initially training on 224x224 images for the first few epochs, then switching to 300x300 images for the remainder of the training.
*   **Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA):** We will apply SWA to *both* models (not just ConvNeXt) during the final epochs to find a flatter, more robust minimum in the loss landscape.

### Evaluation
*   **Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):** We will perform inference on the original test image plus horizontally and vertically flipped versions for both models, averaging the predictions to reduce variance **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
*   **Ensemble Strategy:** The final prediction will be an unweighted average of the probability vectors from the High-Resolution Expert (EfficientNet-B3) and the Contextual Expert (ConvNeXt-Tiny).
