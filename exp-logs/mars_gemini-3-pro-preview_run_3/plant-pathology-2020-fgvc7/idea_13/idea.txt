Based on the analysis of the previous experiments and the derived lessons, I propose the **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Decoupled Multi-Task Learning**. This strategy structurally evolves the previous best approach (Idea 12) by shifting the focus from "feature scale" (FPN) to "semantic composition." It explicitly addresses the difficulty of the "Multiple Diseases" class by decoupling the learning of constituent disease features (Rust and Scab) via auxiliary tasks.

### Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble of Multi-Task Experts**. The architecture integrates two diverse backbones with a novel classification head designed to decompose the complex "Multiple Diseases" class into its fundamental components.

1.  **Backbone 1: EfficientNetV2-M (Texture Expert)**
    *   **Architecture:** We utilize the **EfficientNetV2-M** backbone. Its **Fused-MBConv** blocks are retained for their superior ability to capture high-frequency textural details (e.g., rust edges) compared to standard depthwise convolutions.
    *   **Input Resolution:** **512x512** to prevent the vanishing of minute lesions **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.

2.  **Backbone 2: MaxViT-Small (Global Expert)**
    *   **Architecture:** We utilize the **MaxViT-Small** backbone. Its **Multi-Axis Attention** (Block + Grid) captures long-range dependencies across the leaf surface, providing a robust global context that complements the CNN.
    *   **Input Resolution:** **384x384** to balance spatial detail with the computational cost of the attention mechanism.

3.  **Feature Aggregation (Multi-Level GeM):**
    *   For both backbones, we extract feature maps from the last three reduction stages (strides 8, 16, and 32).
    *   We apply **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** ($p=3$) to *each* stage independently and concatenate the resulting vectors. This ensures the model retains sensitivity to small, localized lesions by approximating max-pooling behavior while maintaining differentiability **Cite solution_lesson_node_00005** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00018**.

4.  **Structural Innovation: Decoupled Multi-Task Heads:**
    *   Instead of a single classification head, the fused feature vector feeds into a **Decoupled Multi-Task Framework**:
        *   **Main Head:** A 4-unit Softmax layer predicting the mutually exclusive classes (Healthy, Multiple, Rust, Scab).
        *   **Auxiliary Head A (Rust):** A 1-unit Sigmoid layer predicting "Has Rust" (True for Rust *and* Multiple classes).
        *   **Auxiliary Head B (Scab):** A 1-unit Sigmoid layer predicting "Has Scab" (True for Scab *and* Multiple classes).
        *   **Auxiliary Head C (Healthy):** A 1-unit Sigmoid layer predicting "Is Healthy".
    *   **Rationale:** The "Multiple Diseases" class is a composition of Rust and Scab features. By forcing the shared backbone to solve the binary "Rust" and "Scab" tasks (which have abundant data), the model learns robust feature representations for these diseases that directly transfer to the minority "Multiple" class.

5.  **Stability:** We wrap the models in **Model Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** to smooth the optimization trajectory and handle the variance of the high-capacity backbones **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **EfficientNetV2 Pipeline:** Resize images to **512x512**.
    *   **MaxViT Pipeline:** Resize images to **384x384**.
    *   **Target Generation:** We generate the binary ground truth for the auxiliary heads dynamically from the main one-hot labels (e.g., `Aux_Rust = max(Rust, Multiple)`).

2.  **Augmentation:**
    *   We employ a **Strong Geometric Augmentation** strategy (`ShiftScaleRotate`, `RandomFlip`, `Transpose`) with high intensity to act as the primary regularizer **Cite solution_lesson_node_00003**.
    *   **Strict Exclusions:**
        *   **No Spatial Occlusion:** We strictly exclude Cutout/CoarseDropout. Removing image patches risks deleting the sparse disease lesions that define the class **Cite solution_lesson_node_00004**.
        *   **No Photometric Augmentation:** We strictly exclude Brightness/Contrast shifts to preserve the specific color signals (e.g., rust red vs. scab brown) critical for diagnosis **Cite solution_lesson_node_00014**.

### Training
1.  **Loss Function:** We use a **Multi-Task Loss**:
    *   $L_{total} = L_{Main} + \lambda (L_{Aux\_Rust} + L_{Aux\_Scab} + L_{Aux\_Healthy})$ (where $\lambda=0.5$).
    *   **Main Loss:** Cross-Entropy with **Inverse Frequency Class Weights** to prioritize the minority "Multiple" class **Cite solution_lesson_node_00001**.
    *   **Aux Loss:** Binary Cross-Entropy.

2.  **Optimization:**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
    *   **Schedule:** We train for **25 epochs** with a **Relaxed Patience of 10 epochs**. This extended patience is crucial to allow the EMA weights to converge and prevents premature termination during the high-variance phase of the Cosine schedule, particularly for the MaxViT backbone **Cite solution_lesson_node_00008** **Cite solution_lesson_node_00016**.
    *   **Precision:** We use **Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP)** to enable the training of these larger backbones at higher resolutions on the A100 GPU **Cite solution_lesson_node_00019**.

3.  **Cross-Validation:** The strategy is implemented using **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**, resulting in 10 trained models (2 architectures $\times$ 5 folds).

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference Weights:** We generate predictions using the **EMA weights**, which provide a more robust estimate of the model parameters than the final training step.
2.  **Domain-Aware Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):**
    *   We generate predictions for the **Original** image and a **Horizontal Flip**.
    *   **Constraint:** We explicitly **exclude Vertical Flips and Transpose**. These transformations violate the natural gravity priors of leaf images (stems typically point up/down), creating "unnatural" inputs that degrade inference accuracy **Cite solution_lesson_node_00012**.
3.  **Prediction:** We use the probabilities from the **Main Head** only. The auxiliary heads are used solely for representation learning.
4.  **Ensemble:** The final submission is the unweighted average of the probability vectors from all 10 models.
