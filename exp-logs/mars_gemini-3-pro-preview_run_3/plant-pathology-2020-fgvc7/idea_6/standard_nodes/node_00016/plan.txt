The `CUDA out of memory` error indicates that the GPU's 16GB VRAM is insufficient to handle the memory requirements of the EfficientNet-B4 model with a batch size of 32 and image resolution of 380x380. To resolve this, I will reduce the `BATCH_SIZE` in `library/config.py` from 32 to 16. This reduction aligns the memory footprint with the available hardware capacity (Cite debug_lesson_1).
