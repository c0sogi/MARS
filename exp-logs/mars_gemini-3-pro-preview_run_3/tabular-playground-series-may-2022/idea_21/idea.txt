# Proposed Strategy: Regularization-Spectrum Parallel Funnel Ensemble (RSPFE)

## Model
We will implement a **Regularization-Spectrum Parallel Funnel Ensemble (RSPFE)**. This architecture evolves the previous ensemble designs by strictly enforcing **Regularization Heterogeneity** and **Capacity Variation** across five independent streams, while rejecting structural complexity (e.g., GLUs, Swish) that was proven ineffective (Cite solution_lesson_node_00069).

1.  **Parallel Independent Inputs:**
    *   The model will consist of **5 independent streams** trained simultaneously within a single computational graph.
    *   **Independent Embeddings:** Each stream will possess its own distinct **Entity Embedding** layers. We explicitly avoid sharing embeddings to ensure that each stream learns a unique representation of the categorical manifold (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Configuration:**
        *   **Categorical Inputs:** 10 decomposed `f_27` characters (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) and discrete features `f_29`, `f_30` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
        *   **Dimension:** Fixed at **16** for all streams. This is the optimal "sweet spot" between bottlenecking (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018) and noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
        *   **Fusion:** Early Fusion (concatenation) with normalized continuous features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

2.  **The Regularization Spectrum (Backbones):**
    *   All streams will utilize the **Funnel MLP** topology (decreasing layer widths) as it provides the superior compressive inductive bias (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004). We avoid ResNets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004) and Transformers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).
    *   **Stream 1 & 2 (Anchors):** Standard Funnel (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.20**. These provide the robust baseline performance.
    *   **Stream 3 (High Capacity):** Wide Funnel (1024 $\to$ 512 $\to$ 256) with **Dropout 0.25**. This stream captures subtler patterns via increased width (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Stream 4 (Conservative):** Standard Funnel (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.30**. This high-regularization stream prevents overfitting and improves ensemble stability (Cite solution_lesson_node_00069).
    *   **Stream 5 (Aggressive):** Standard Funnel (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128) with **Dropout 0.10**. This low-regularization stream aggressively fits the training data, capturing high-frequency signals that might be suppressed in other streams.

3.  **Architectural Constraints:**
    *   **Activation:** Standard **ReLU** exclusively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
    *   **Output:** Each stream projects its final layer (width $\ge$ 128) to a single output neuron. We avoid tapering to 64 units to prevent information bottlenecks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Exclusions:** No LayerNorm (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039), No Input Noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053), No Token Dropout (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).

## Data
The preprocessing pipeline is designed to ensure maximum signal fidelity and correct previous vocabulary alignment errors:

1.  **Transductive Vocabulary Alignment:** We will fit the categorical `OrdinalEncoder` on the **concatenation of the Train, Validation, and Test sets**. This ensures every unique token in the test set has a distinct embedding index (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
2.  **String Decomposition:** `f_27` will be split into 10 fixed-position character columns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). We avoid N-gram expansion (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
3.  **Feature Engineering:** Compute and append `unique_character_count` of `f_27` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
4.  **Normalization:** StandardScaler for all continuous features.
5.  **Data Volume:** Utilize the **full training dataset**, strictly avoiding subsampling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

## Training
The training configuration is calibrated to facilitate super-convergence while respecting the specific sensitivity of tabular optimizers:

1.  **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a calibrated weight decay of **1e-5**. We explicitly avoid the default `1e-2`, which is too aggressive for this task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
2.  **Loss Function:** The training objective is the **sum of Binary Cross-Entropy losses** from all 5 streams.
3.  **Scheduler:** **One-Cycle Learning Rate Policy**. We retain this schedule to facilitate super-convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
4.  **Batch Size:** **1024**. We leverage the implicit regularization of moderate batch sizes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
5.  **Strategy:** Direct Supervised Learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

## Evaluation
1.  **Preprocessing:** Apply global vocabulary mappings and training set scalers to the test set.
2.  **Inference:** The RSPFE model will output 5 probability predictions per sample.
3.  **Submission:** The final prediction will be the **arithmetic mean** of the 5 probabilities.
