# Proposed Strategy: Multi-Resolution Parallel Funnel Ensemble (MRPFE)

## Model
We will implement a **Multi-Resolution Parallel Funnel Ensemble (MRPFE)**. This architecture evolves the "Heterogeneous Parallel Funnel Ensemble" by introducing **Resolution Diversity** in the embedding space. While previous ensembles varied network width or dropout, they used a fixed embedding dimension (typically 16). Given that the current best models still struggle with categorical feature interactions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00096), we will explicitly vary the embedding dimensionality across streams to force them to learn representations at different levels of granularity/compression.

1.  **Parallel Independent Architecture:**
    *   The model will consist of **5 independent streams** instantiated within a single computational graph (Cite solution_lesson_node_00064).
    *   **Independent Representation:** Each stream will possess its own **independent Entity Embedding layers** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00065).
    *   **Input Fusion:** Early Fusion (concatenation) of flattened embeddings and normalized continuous features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029).

2.  **Multi-Resolution Configuration:**
    *   **Streams 1 & 2 (Anchors):** **Embedding Dim 16**. Backbone: **Standard Funnel MLP** (512 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 128). Dropout **0.20**. These provide the proven optimal baseline (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
    *   **Stream 3 (High-Resolution Variant):** **Embedding Dim 32**. Backbone: **Wide Funnel MLP** (1024 $\to$ 512 $\to$ 256). Dropout **0.25**. This stream is designed to capture fine-grained categorical distinctions and subtler patterns via increased capacity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00066).
    *   **Stream 4 (Low-Resolution Variant):** **Embedding Dim 8**. Backbone: **Standard Funnel MLP**. Dropout **0.15**. This stream forces a tight informational bottleneck, encouraging the learning of highly compressed, dominant factors.
    *   **Stream 5 (Conservative Variant):** **Embedding Dim 16**. Backbone: **Standard Funnel MLP**. Dropout **0.30**. This high-regularization stream acts as a stabilizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00069).

3.  **Architectural Constraints:**
    *   **Activation:** Standard **ReLU** exclusively (Cite solution_lesson_node_00069).
    *   **Bottleneck Avoidance:** All streams terminate at a width $\ge$ 128 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
    *   **Exclusions:** No Layer Normalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00039), No Input Noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00053), No Linear Feature Projection (Cite solution_lesson_node_00093).

## Data
The preprocessing pipeline is designed to ensure maximum signal fidelity and correct previous vocabulary alignment errors:

1.  **Transductive Vocabulary Alignment:** We will fit the categorical `OrdinalEncoder` on the **concatenation of the Train, Validation, and Test sets** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
2.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **String Decomposition:** Decompose `f_27` into 10 fixed-position character columns (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001). Avoid N-grams (Cite solution_lesson_node_00035).
    *   **Discrete Features:** Treat `f_29` and `f_30` as categorical variables (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    *   **Aggregate Signal:** Compute and append the `unique_character_count` of `f_27` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
3.  **Normalization:** Scale all continuous features using **StandardScaler**.
4.  **Data Volume:** Utilize the **full training dataset**, strictly avoiding subsampling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00022).

## Training
The training configuration is precisely calibrated to correct the optimization pitfalls identified in previous experiments (specifically regarding AdamW and Weight Decay):

1.  **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with a calibrated weight decay of **1e-5**. We explicitly revert to the lower weight decay value (`1e-5`) used in the successful Adam baselines. Previous experiments showed that `1e-4` with AdamW over-regularized the continuous features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00096), while `1e-2` was catastrophic (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
2.  **Scheduler:** **One-Cycle Learning Rate Policy** with `max_lr=1e-2`. We maintain the high learning rate to ensure super-convergence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00072).
3.  **Loss Function:** The training objective is the **sum of Binary Cross-Entropy losses** calculated independently for each of the 5 streams.
4.  **Batch Size:** **1024** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
5.  **Duration:** Train for **50 Epochs** to allow the high-capacity and conservative streams to fully converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00080).

## Evaluation
1.  **Preprocessing:** Apply the global (transductive) vocabulary mappings and training set scalers to the test set.
2.  **Inference:** The MRPFE model will generate 5 independent probability predictions per sample.
3.  **Submission:** The final prediction will be the **arithmetic mean** of these 5 probabilities.
