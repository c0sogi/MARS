Lessons indicate that high-performance tabular ensembles with heavy regularization (Weight Decay `1e-4`) require extended training schedules (30-50 epochs) to converge fully. I will increase `FAST_EPOCHS` to 50 in `runfile.py`.
2.  **Optimize Dropout Spectrum (Cite Lesson 00077):** I will slightly widen the range of dropout rates in the ensemble to `[0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.28, 0.30]`. This re-introduces the lower bound (`0.15`) used in previous successful iterations to allow one stream to be more aggressive, while maintaining higher rates for the wider streams to ensure diversity and safety.
3.  **Relax Early Stopping:** To accommodate the longer OneCycleLR schedule and potential plateaus, I will increase the `patience` parameter in the `Trainer` initialization from 5 to 10.
