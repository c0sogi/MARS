Based on the provided lessons, I will implement a **Heterogeneous Parallel Funnel Ensemble (HPFE)** with uniform optimization constraints, replacing the previous Multi-Objective Regularized approach (MORPE).

**Strategy:**
1.  **Uniform Optimization:** I will revert the optimizer from `AdamW` to `Adam` with a global weight decay of `1e-5`. Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00082` demonstrated that heterogeneous weight decay degrades performance compared to a uniform baseline, and Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00063` highlights the difficulty of calibrating decoupled weight decay in `AdamW` for this dataset.
2.  **Structural Diversity:** I will configure the ensemble streams to vary in capacity (layer width) and regularization (dropout), specifically keeping dropout within the "safe" range of 0.15 to 0.25 (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00077`). This aligns with the successful HPFE architecture (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00066`).
3.  **Extended Training Schedule:** I will increase the training duration from 15 to 50 epochs. Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00080` emphasizes that well-regularized ensembles require the "long tail" of the OneCycle schedule to fully converge.
4.  **Learning Rate:** I will maintain `MAX_LR = 1e-2`, as reducing it has been shown to cause convergence failure (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00072`).
