Based on the accumulated lessons, specifically the critical need for **Full Data Utilization** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036), the feasibility of **K-Fold Cross-Validation** when optimized (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042), and the specific sensitivity of Log Loss to **Probability Calibration** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012), I propose a **Stratified K-Fold Heterogeneous Ensemble with Progressive Resizing and Post-Hoc Calibration**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by:
1.  **Training Pipeline Innovation:** Implementing **Progressive Resizing** to accelerate the training of the 5-Fold ensemble, ensuring it fits within the 24-hour compute budget without sacrificing convergence (addressing the failure of Idea 17).
2.  **Inference Pipeline Innovation:** Introducing a **Post-Hoc Calibration** stage (Isotonic Regression) trained on Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions. This explicitly targets the Log Loss metric by correcting the inherent overconfidence of modern neural networks, replacing the fragile "weight optimization" or "stacking" approaches of previous attempts.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of three distinct backbones, replicated across **5 Stratified Folds** (Total 15 models). We retain the "Golden Trio" identified in Lesson 00040 for their maximized inductive bias diversity:
1.  **ResNet-50:** The "Standard CNN" anchor (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** The "Modern CNN" with large kernels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **MaxViT-Tiny:** The "Multi-Axis Transformer" with global grid attention (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).

**Initialization:** Initialize all models with the highest quality pre-trained weights available (e.g., `IMAGENET1K_V2` for ResNet) to adhere to the lesson that modern recipes outperform legacy weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing (Decoupled & Progressive):**
Implement a **Progressive Resizing** strategy within the **Decoupled Input Pipelines** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Pipeline A (ResNet-50):** Target 256x256. Train initial epochs at 192x192.
*   **Pipeline B (ConvNeXt-Small):** Target 288x288 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040). Train initial epochs at 224x224.
*   **Pipeline C (MaxViT-Tiny):** Target 224x224. Train initial epochs at 192x192.
*   **Interpolation:** Strictly use **Bicubic Interpolation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

**Augmentation:** Apply **Context-Preserving Augmentation**.
*   Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to prevent subject loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   Apply `ColorJitter` with intensity $\ge 0.2$ to handle lighting variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Training
**Strategy:** **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**.
*   Train an independent instance of each architecture on each fold. This ensures 100% data utilization and generates unbiased OOF predictions for calibration (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

**Optimization (Progressive & Asynchronous):**
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing** scheduler (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Function:** **BCEWithLogitsLoss** *without* Label Smoothing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Progressive Schedule:**
    *   **Phase 1 (Speed):** Train for 50% of the epochs at the reduced resolution. This significantly speeds up the early convergence phase.
    *   **Phase 2 (Accuracy):** Fine-tune for the remaining 50% of epochs at the target resolution.
*   **Durations:**
    *   **ResNet/ConvNeXt:** 8 Epochs Total (4 Low Res + 4 Target Res).
    *   **MaxViT:** 15 Epochs Total (7 Low Res + 8 Target Res) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

### Evaluation
**Inference & Calibration Pipeline:**
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** Generate raw probabilities using original and horizontally flipped images for all 15 models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Quality Gating:** Discard any model whose OOF Log Loss > 0.05 to prevent "Poison Pills" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00043).
3.  **Post-Hoc Calibration (Structural Innovation):**
    *   For each accepted model, train a univariate **Isotonic Regression** calibrator using its OOF predictions and the ground truth labels.
    *   Apply this calibrator to the model's Test Set predictions.
    *   **Rationale:** Deep models are often overconfident. Isotonic Regression non-parametrically maps the predicted distribution to the true probability curve, directly minimizing Log Loss without the complexity of a multi-variate stacker (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
4.  **Aggregation:** Calculate the final probability by taking the **arithmetic mean** of the *calibrated* predictions from all passing models.
