Based on the comprehensive analysis of the accumulated lessons, specifically the critical importance of **Data Volume** (Lesson 00036), **Convergence** (Lesson 00042), and **Diversity** (Lesson 00040), I propose a **Full-Coverage Stratified Cross-Validation Ensemble with Asynchronous Convergence Schedules**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by transitioning from a single train/validation split (which discards 20% of the data) to a **5-Fold Cross-Validation** pipeline. Unlike the failed Idea 17, which compromised model convergence to save time, this approach leverages the 24-hour compute budget to fully train every fold to convergence. This ensures the ensemble learns from 100% of the available training data while maintaining the rigorous optimization standards required for low Log Loss.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** comprising three distinct backbones, replicated across 5 folds (Total 15 models). We retain the "Golden Trio" identified in Lesson 00040 for their maximized inductive bias diversity:
1.  **ResNet-50:** The "Standard CNN" anchor. Provides robust, texture-based feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** The "Modern CNN". Captures semantic context via large kernels and inverted bottlenecks (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **MaxViT-Tiny:** The "Multi-Axis Transformer". Utilizes Block (Local) and Grid (Global) attention to capture long-range dependencies, providing a distinct error profile from the CNNs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00040).

**Initialization:** Initialize all models with the highest quality pre-trained weights available (e.g., `IMAGENET1K_V2` for ResNet, `in1k` for others) to strictly adhere to the lesson that modern recipes outperform legacy weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing (Decoupled Pipelines):** Maintain the **Resolution Diversity** strategy to maximize feature extraction at different scales without forcing a "lowest common denominator" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028, solution_lesson_node_00040):
1.  **Pipeline A (ResNet-50):** Resize to **256x256**. Balances detail with the standard receptive field.
2.  **Pipeline B (ConvNeXt-Small):** Resize to **288x288**. Exploits the modern CNN's capacity for fine-grained details.
3.  **Pipeline C (MaxViT-Tiny):** Resize to **224x224**. Matches the native grid structure of the Transformer to avoid interpolation artifacts.

**Augmentation:** Apply **Context-Preserving Augmentation** across all pipelines.
*   Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a restricted scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to ensure the subject is never cropped out (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   Apply `ColorJitter` with intensity $\ge 0.2$ to handle lighting variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   Strictly use **Bicubic Interpolation** for all resizing operations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Training
**Strategy:** Implement **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**.
*   Split the full 25,000-image training set into 5 stratified folds.
*   Train an independent instance of *each* of the three architectures on each fold (3 architectures $\times$ 5 folds = 15 total training runs).
*   **Rationale:** This allows the ensemble to learn from every training example, reducing the variance associated with a fixed validation split and improving generalization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

**Optimization (Asynchronous Schedules):**
*   **Optimizer:** **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing** scheduler (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Function:** **BCEWithLogitsLoss** *without* Label Smoothing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Schedules:** Apply the architecture-specific durations proven in Lesson 00040, explicitly correcting the underfitting error of Idea 17:
    *   **ResNet-50 & ConvNeXt-Small:** Train for **8 epochs** per fold.
    *   **MaxViT-Tiny:** Train for **15 epochs** per fold. This extended horizon is critical for the Multi-Axis attention mechanism to converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034).

### Evaluation
**Inference & Aggregation:**
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, generate probabilities using both the original and a horizontally flipped version for each of the 15 models. Average these pairs to stabilize predictions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Global Ensemble Averaging:** Calculate the final probability by taking the **arithmetic mean** of the outputs from all 15 models.
    *   **Rationale:** We explicitly choose simple averaging over complex stacking. As proven in Lesson 00023, the error reduction from averaging diverse inductive biases (CNN/Transformer) and data views (Folds/Resolutions) exceeds the gains from meta-learners, which risk overfitting.
