Based on the accumulated lessons, specifically the success of architectural diversity (Lesson 00016), the necessity of decoupled resolutions (Lesson 00028), and the failure of data subsampling when testing new architectures (Lesson 00036), I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble with Learnable Pooling and Multi-Sample Dropout**.

This strategy structurally evolves the "Current Best" (Idea 8) by integrating **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** to refine feature selection in CNNs and replacing the Swin Transformer with a **MaxViT** backbone equipped with **Multi-Sample Dropout**. This combination targets the specific Log Loss metric by sharpening feature focus and regularizing internal confidence.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Triple Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of three backbones with distinct inductive biases.
1.  **ResNet-50 (Standard CNN):** The robust anchor (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
    *   **Structural Innovation:** Replace the final Global Average Pooling (GAP) layer with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** (learnable parameter $p$). This allows the model to dynamically focus on salient features (the pet) rather than averaging background noise, improving discrimination without increasing backbone size.
2.  **ConvNeXt-Small (Modern CNN):** Captures semantic context via large kernels (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
    *   **Structural Innovation:** Also replace the final GAP with **GeM Pooling** to align feature extraction granularity with the ResNet stream.
3.  **MaxViT-Tiny (Multi-Axis Transformer):** This replaces the Swin-Tiny used in previous ideas. MaxViT utilizes **Multi-Axis Attention** (Block + Grid), enabling global interaction in a single stage, which provides a stronger global context signal than Swin's shifted windows (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Structural Innovation:** Replace the classification head with a **Multi-Sample Dropout (MSD)** head. This consists of multiple dropout layers with different rates feeding into the final linear layer, creating an internal ensemble that smooths probability estimates and explicitly minimizes Log Loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

**Initialization:** Initialize ResNet-50 with **`IMAGENET1K_V2`** weights and ConvNeXt/MaxViT with their best available pre-trained weights to ensure optimal feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing (Decoupled Pipelines):** Maintain the decoupled resolution strategy to maximize fidelity without forcing a "lowest common denominator" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
*   **Pipeline A (ResNet-50, ConvNeXt-Small):** Resize images to **256x256**. This resolution allows the CNNs to utilize fine spatial details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Pipeline B (MaxViT-Tiny):** Resize images to **224x224**. This aligns with the native grid structure of the Transformer, avoiding interpolation artifacts.
*   **Interpolation:** Strictly use **Bicubic Interpolation** for all pipelines (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

**Augmentation:** Apply **Context-Preserving Augmentation**.
*   Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to ensure the subject is never cropped out (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   Apply `ColorJitter` with intensity $\ge 0.2$ to handle lighting variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Training
**Strategy:** Train each model independently on the **full dataset**. We explicitly correct the methodology error from Idea 11 where subsampling invalidated the results (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

**Optimization:**
*   **Optimizer:** Use **AdamW** with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss** (`BCEWithLogitsLoss`) **without Label Smoothing**. We strictly avoid Label Smoothing to prevent the "loss floor," allowing the MSD head to optimize for the extreme probabilities required by Log Loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Duration:** Train for **8-10 epochs**. This ensures sufficient gradient updates for the learnable GeM parameters and the complex MaxViT attention maps to converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

### Evaluation
**Inference & Aggregation:**
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, generate probabilities using both the original and a horizontally flipped version for each model. Average these pairs to stabilize predictions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Ensemble Averaging:** Calculate the final probability by taking the **arithmetic mean** of the outputs from the ResNet-50 (GeM), ConvNeXt-Small (GeM), and MaxViT-Tiny (MSD). This leverages the uncorrelated errors of the diverse architectures and pooling mechanisms (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
