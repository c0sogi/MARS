Based on the lessons learned, particularly the failure of complex stacking (Lesson 00023) and the underperformance of EfficientNetV2 in the triple ensemble (Lesson 00024), I propose a **Hybrid CNN-Transformer Ensemble**.

This strategy structurally evolves the previous "Triple Heterogeneous Ensemble" by replacing the "Depthwise Separable" paradigm (EfficientNet) with a **Hierarchical Vision Transformer (Swin)** paradigm. This ensures the ensemble covers the three fundamental ways of processing images: Standard Convolution (ResNet), Modernized Convolution (ConvNeXt), and Self-Attention (Swin), maximizing error diversity.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Hybrid Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of three distinct backbones:
1.  **ResNet-50:** Retained as the "Standard CNN" anchor. It provides robust, texture-biased feature extraction and stabilizes the ensemble (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** Retained as the "Modern CNN". Its large-kernel, inverted bottleneck design captures semantic context better than standard ResNets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
3.  **Swin-Tiny (or Small):** **New Addition.** This replaces the underperforming EfficientNetV2. As a hierarchical Vision Transformer, Swin utilizes shifted-window self-attention, introducing a fundamentally different inductive bias (global dependency modeling) compared to the local sliding windows of CNNs. This maximizes the architectural diversity required for effective ensembling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).

**Initialization:** All models must be initialized with the best available pre-trained weights (e.g., **`IMAGENET1K_V2`** for ResNet, `DEFAULT` for Swin/ConvNeXt). This is critical to avoid the performance degradation seen with legacy V1 weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing:**
*   **Resolution:** Resize all images to **256x256**. This resolution offers a proven improvement over 224x224 by preserving spatial details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Interpolation:** Strictly use **Bicubic Interpolation**. We must align with the pre-training recipes of modern backbones (Swin/ConvNeXt) to prevent the aliasing and texture degradation observed when defaulting to Bilinear interpolation (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

**Augmentation:**
*   **Context-Preservation:** Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to ensure the subject is never cropped out (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
*   **Photometric Noise:** Apply `ColorJitter` with an intensity of at least **0.2** (brightness/contrast/saturation). This is necessary to maintain generalization across lighting conditions, as reducing this intensity previously hurt performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Training
**Strategy:** Train each of the three models independently. We avoid complex stacking or cross-validation schemes in favor of simple, robust training of diverse architectures (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).

**Optimization:**
*   **Optimizer:** Use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler. This setup has consistently outperformed standard Adam by preventing stagnation in local minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss** (`BCEWithLogitsLoss`) **without Label Smoothing**. We strictly avoid Label Smoothing to prevent the "loss floor" (approx 0.05) that makes it impossible to minimize Log Loss for high-confidence predictions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Duration:** Train for a short duration (e.g., 5-8 epochs) to respect the rapid convergence of transfer learning on this high-overlap domain (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

### Evaluation
**Inference & Aggregation:**
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, generate probabilities using both the original and a horizontally flipped version for *each* of the three models (ResNet, ConvNeXt, Swin). Average these pairs to stabilize predictions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Ensemble Averaging:** Calculate the final probability by taking the **arithmetic mean** of the outputs from the three models. This leverages the uncorrelated errors of the CNN and Transformer architectures to minimize Log Loss more effectively than single-model tuning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
