Based on the accumulated lessons and the available computational resources (A100 GPU), I propose a **Stratified K-Fold Heterogeneous Ensemble with Scaled Backbones**. This approach structurally evolves the previous "simple ensemble" idea into a robust Cross-Validation pipeline that utilizes 100% of the training data and explicitly targets the Log Loss metric through better probability calibration.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** composed of two upgraded backbones to leverage the A100's capacity:
1.  **ResNet-101:** An evolution of the ResNet-50 used previously. The deeper architecture captures more complex feature hierarchies while retaining the optimization robustness proven in earlier trials (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **ConvNeXt-Small:** An evolution of the ConvNeXt-Tiny. This model provides a "modern" architectural counterpart (transformer-inspired inverted bottlenecks) to the standard ResNet, ensuring diversity in errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).

**Initialization:** Both models will be initialized with the highest quality pre-trained weights available (e.g., `IMAGENET1K_V2` for ResNet) to ensure superior feature extraction from the start (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing:** Resize all images to **256x256**. This resolution allows the models to utilize finer spatial details than the standard 224x224, which has been proven to reduce error (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

**Augmentation:** Employ the **Context-Preserving Augmentation** strategy established in previous lessons. Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a strict scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to prevents cropping out the subject, combined with `ColorJitter` and `RandomHorizontalFlip`. This balances regularization with input fidelity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

### Training
**Pipeline Structure:** Replace the single train/val split with **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation**.
**Rationale:** A single split leaves 20% of the data unseen during training. By training 5 models per architecture (one for each fold), we allow the ensemble to learn from the entire dataset. Furthermore, this generates **Out-of-Fold (OOF)** predictions for every training image, which are crucial for assessing calibration without data leakage.

**Optimization:**
*   **Optimizer:** Use **AdamW** with **Cosine Annealing** scheduler for all folds (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **BCEWithLogitsLoss** *without* Label Smoothing. We strictly avoid Label Smoothing because it creates a "loss floor" that prevents the model from achieving the extremely low Log Loss values possible on clean datasets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Scaling:** Use a batch size of 64 or 128 (fitting the larger backbones) and train for a short duration (e.g., 5-8 epochs per fold) to respect the rapid convergence of transfer learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).

### Evaluation
**Inference & Aggregation:**
1.  **TTA:** Apply Test Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) for every fold model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Ensemble Averaging:** For the final submission, average the probabilities from all 10 trained models (5 ResNet-101 folds + 5 ConvNeXt-Small folds).
**Rationale:** Averaging across folds reduces the variance associated with any specific data split, while averaging across architectures exploits complementary inductive biases (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016). This extensive averaging acts as a strong regularizer, smoothing the probability distributions and minimizing the Log Loss penalty for confident errors.
