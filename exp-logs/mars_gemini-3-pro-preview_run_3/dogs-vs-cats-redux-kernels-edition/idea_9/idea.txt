Based on the accumulated lessons, particularly the success of architectural diversity (Lesson 00016, 00023) and the critical importance of resolution-specific preprocessing (Lesson 00028), I propose a **Scaled Heterogeneous Ensemble with Decoupled Resolutions and Learnable Pooling**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by:
1.  **Scaling Backbone Capacity:** Leveraging the A100 GPU to upgrade from "Tiny/Small" variants to "Base/Large" variants, addressing the need for higher capacity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
2.  **Integrating Learnable Pooling:** Replacing the standard Global Average Pooling (GAP) in CNNs with **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling**. This structural change allows the model to focus on salient features (the animal) rather than averaging background noise, complementing the context-preserving augmentation strategy.
3.  **Decoupling Input Pipelines:** Formalizing the resolution split to maximize the specific strengths of each architecture without the "lowest common denominator" penalty (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Triple Heterogeneous Ensemble** comprising three distinct backbones, each modified for optimal feature extraction:
1.  **ConvNeXt-Base:** Represents the "Modern CNN" paradigm. We scale up from the "Small" variant to "Base" to capture more complex semantic features.
    *   **Modification:** Replace the final Global Average Pooling layer with a **Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling** layer (initialized with $p=3$, learnable). This allows the network to transition between Max and Average pooling, focusing on high-activation regions (the pet) while ignoring background.
2.  **ResNet-101:** Represents the "Standard CNN" paradigm. We scale up from ResNet-50 to capture deeper hierarchical features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   **Modification:** Replace the final GAP with **GeM Pooling**.
3.  **Swin-Small:** Represents the "Hierarchical Transformer" paradigm. We scale up from "Tiny" to "Small". We retain the native attention-based pooling mechanism of the Transformer as it inherently handles global context (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).

**Initialization:** Initialize ResNet-101 with **`IMAGENET1K_V2`** weights and ConvNeXt/Swin with their best available pre-trained weights (e.g., `DEFAULT` or `IMAGENET1K_V1` if V2 is unavailable) to ensure robust feature extraction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing (Decoupled Pipelines):** Implement three distinct input pipelines to match the architectural requirements (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028):
1.  **Pipeline A (ConvNeXt-Base):** Resize images to **320x320**. This higher resolution allows the modernized CNN to exploit its larger receptive field for fine-grained details (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
2.  **Pipeline B (ResNet-101):** Resize images to **256x256**. This balances detail preservation with the architectural design of standard ResNets.
3.  **Pipeline C (Swin-Small):** Resize images to **224x224**. This respects the fixed window-attention mechanism of the Swin Transformer, avoiding the interpolation artifacts seen when forcing Transformers to non-native resolutions.

**Interpolation & Augmentation:**
*   **Interpolation:** Strictly use **Bicubic Interpolation** for all resizing operations to prevent aliasing and align with modern pre-training recipes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
*   **Augmentation:** Apply **Context-Preserving Augmentation** across all pipelines. Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to ensure the subject is never cropped out. Combine with `ColorJitter` (intensity $\ge 0.2$) to handle lighting variance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005, solution_lesson_node_00024).

### Training
**Strategy:** Train each of the three models independently.
*   **Optimizer:** Use **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler. This setup consistently outperforms standard Adam by preventing stagnation in local minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
*   **Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss** (`BCEWithLogitsLoss`) **without Label Smoothing**. We strictly avoid Label Smoothing to prevent the "loss floor," allowing the models to minimize Log Loss for high-confidence predictions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Scaling:** Train for **8-10 epochs**. This extended duration (compared to the baseline 3) compensates for the larger batch sizes required by the A100 and ensures sufficient gradient updates for the larger "Base" and "101" backbones (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

### Evaluation
**Inference & Aggregation:**
1.  **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** For every test image, generate probabilities using both the original and a horizontally flipped version for each of the three models. Average these pairs to stabilize predictions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  **Ensemble Averaging:** Calculate the final probability by taking the **arithmetic mean** of the outputs from the ConvNeXt-Base, ResNet-101, and Swin-Small. This leverages the uncorrelated errors of the diverse architectures and resolutions to minimize Log Loss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016, solution_lesson_node_00023).
