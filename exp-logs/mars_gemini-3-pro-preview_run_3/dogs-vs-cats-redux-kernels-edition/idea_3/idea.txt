Based on the accumulated lessons and the requirement for structural innovation, I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble Strategy** that combines the robustness of standard CNNs with the architectural advancements of modern networks, while strictly adhering to the optimization protocols identified in previous trials.

### Model
**Architecture:** Implement a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** consisting of two distinct backbones:
1.  **ResNet-50:** Chosen for its proven robustness and rapid convergence in this specific transfer learning domain (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).
2.  **ConvNeXt-Tiny:** Chosen to introduce structural diversity (modern inverted bottlenecks, larger kernels) which captures different feature hierarchies compared to standard ResNets.

**Initialization:** Both models must be initialized with the highest-quality pre-trained weights available—specifically **`IMAGENET1K_V2`** (or newer) for the ResNet-50 and the best available ImageNet-1k weights for ConvNeXt. This ensures the starting feature representations are superior to legacy V1 weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

### Data
**Preprocessing:** Resize all images to **256x256**.
**Rationale:** This resolution allows the models to utilize finer spatial details than the standard 224x224, which has been proven to improve accuracy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

**Augmentation:** Apply a **Context-Preserving Augmentation** pipeline.
**Rationale:** Use `RandomResizedCrop` with a scale of `(0.8, 1.0)` to ensure the subject is not cropped out, combined with `ColorJitter` and `RandomHorizontalFlip`. This balances regularization with input fidelity, preventing the "missing subject" noise introduced by aggressive geometric augmentations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).

### Training
**Loss Function:** Use **Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss** (`BCEWithLogitsLoss`) **without Label Smoothing**.
**Rationale:** Previous experiments demonstrated that Label Smoothing creates a theoretical "loss floor" (approx 0.05) that prevents the model from achieving extremely low Log Loss scores on clean datasets. We must allow the model to predict with high confidence (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

**Optimizer & Scheduler:** Train both models independently using **AdamW** combined with a **Cosine Annealing** scheduler.
**Rationale:** This optimization strategy has consistently outperformed standard Adam by preventing stagnation in local minima (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).

**Epoch & Batch Scaling:** Use a batch size of 128 but **increase the number of training epochs** (e.g., to 8-10).
**Rationale:** Since increasing the batch size reduces the number of gradient updates per epoch, we must proportionally increase the epoch count to ensure the model receives sufficient optimization steps to converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).

### Evaluation
**Inference Strategy:** Implement **Ensembled Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**.
**Rationale:**
1.  For each image, generate predictions using both the original and a horizontally flipped version (TTA) for *each* model (Cite solution_lesson_node_00007).
2.  Compute the average probability for the ResNet-50 stream and the ConvNeXt-Tiny stream separately.
3.  **Final Prediction:** Average the outputs of the two models. This structural combination reduces the variance of individual models and leverages their diverse architectural biases to minimize Log Loss.
