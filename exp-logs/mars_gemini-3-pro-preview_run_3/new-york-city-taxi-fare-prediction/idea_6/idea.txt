# Model
I propose a **Dual-Branch Gradient Boosting Ensemble** composed of two "strong" learners to minimize variance without introducing the bias of weak models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011).
1.  **XGBoost Regressor:** Configured to utilize the **NVIDIA A100 GPU** (`tree_method='gpu_hist'`). This model will use deep trees (e.g., `max_depth=12`) to capture complex non-linear interactions in the massive dataset.
2.  **LightGBM Regressor:** Configured to utilize the **12 vCPUs** (`boosting_type='gbdt'`). This model will use a high leaf count (e.g., `num_leaves=512`) to leverage its efficient histogram-based split finding.
3.  **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final prediction will be a **Weighted Average** of the two models. This combines the distinct algorithmic strengths (Exact/Approximate Greedy vs. GOSS) of the two frameworks.

# Data
**Preprocessing (Correction & Exploitation):**
1.  **Universal Coordinate Sanitation:** I will apply a strict bounding box filter (Latitude 40–42, Longitude -73 to -75) to the **Training, Validation, AND Test sets**. This is a critical correction to prevent the "distance explosion" caused by outliers (e.g., 0,0 coordinates) that invalidated previous ensemble attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Target Filtering:** The training data will be filtered to keep `fare_amount` between 0 and 500 to protect the MSE loss function from extreme outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
3.  **Full Dataset Utilization:** The models will be trained on the complete 55 million row dataset. I will explicitly verify data loading to ensure no debug subsampling occurs, as data volume is the primary driver of performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

# Feature Engineering
**Strategic Retention & Refinement:**
1.  **Raw & Rotated Coordinates:** I will retain both the raw latitude/longitude and the **29°-rotated coordinates**. This allows the trees to make efficient axis-aligned splits that match the NYC street grid (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
2.  **Landmark Distances:** I will include Haversine distances to a curated list of major hubs (JFK, LGA, EWR, WTC, Times Square). This provides global spatial context that pure coordinates lack (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
3.  **Exclusion of Discretization:** I will **not** use K-Means clustering or Target Encoding. Previous experiments demonstrated that discretizing coordinates into clusters is a lossy compression that degrades performance compared to raw coordinates in GBDTs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).

**Structural Innovation (Physics-Informed):**
1.  **Rotated Manhattan Distance:** I will calculate the L1 norm (Manhattan distance) within the **Rotated Coordinate System**. This feature explicitly models the "city block" driving distance (which follows the grid) rather than the "crow-flies" Haversine distance, providing a stronger physical signal for fare estimation.
2.  **Cyclical Temporal Encoding:** To capture smooth traffic transitions, I will transform `Hour`, `Day_of_Week`, and `Month` into continuous **Sine and Cosine** components, resolving the ordinal discontinuity of standard time features.

# Training
*   **Procedure:** Both models will be trained independently on the full processed dataset.
*   **Hyperparameters:** I will use **High Capacity** settings (e.g., `n_estimators=5000`, `learning_rate=0.02`) to ensure the models can learn the fine-grained spatial patterns present in the 55M rows (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:** I will employ **Early Stopping** based on the validation set RMSE to determine the optimal number of boosting rounds for each model, ensuring convergence without overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).
