Based on the comprehensive analysis of previous attempts and the lessons learned, I propose a **Corrected Heterogeneous Ensemble with Physics-Informed Feature Engineering**. This strategy focuses on combining "strong" learners (XGBoost and LightGBM) with a rigorously sanitized data pipeline and a feature set that explicitly models the physical constraints of NYC taxi travel (Manhattan grid geometry).

# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Gradient Boosting Ensemble** consisting of two high-capacity regressors:
1.  **XGBoost Regressor:** Configured to use the **NVIDIA A100 GPU** (`tree_method='gpu_hist'`) to efficiently process the massive dataset with deep trees.
2.  **LightGBM Regressor:** Configured to use the **12 vCPUs** (`boosting_type='gbdt'`), leveraging its optimized histogram-based algorithm and Gradient-based One-Side Sampling (GOSS).

**Structural Innovation:**
Unlike previous ensemble attempts which failed due to the inclusion of weak learners (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) or data pipeline errors (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016), this architecture combines two state-of-the-art "strong" learners. By averaging their predictions, we leverage the diversity in their split-finding algorithms (Exact/Approximate Greedy vs. GOSS) to reduce variance and generalization error, ensuring the ensemble outperforms the single-model baseline.

# Data
**Preprocessing (Correction & Exploitation):**
1.  **Universal Coordinate Sanitation:** I will apply a strict bounding box filter (Latitude 40–42, Longitude -73 to -75) to **both the training set AND the test/validation sets**. This is the critical correction to prevent the "distance explosion" caused by outliers (e.g., 0,0 coordinates) that invalidated previous attempts (Cite solution_lesson_node_00016).
2.  **Target Filtering:** The training data will be filtered to keep `fare_amount` between 0 and 500. This protects the MSE loss function from extreme outliers (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
3.  **Full Dataset Utilization:** The models will be trained on the complete 55 million row dataset. We will not subsample, as data volume is the primary driver of performance (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

# Feature Engineering
**Strategic Retention:**
*   **Rotated Coordinates:** We will retain the 29° coordinate rotation to align the feature space with the NYC street grid, enabling efficient axis-aligned splits (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
*   **Landmark Distances:** We will retain Haversine distances to key hubs (JFK, LGA, WTC, The Met, Times Square). These provide critical global context that purely local splits miss (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003, solution_lesson_node_00018).

**Structural Innovation (Physics-Informed & Temporal):**
*   **Rotated Manhattan Distance:** Instead of relying solely on Haversine (crow-flies) distance, I will calculate the **Manhattan Distance (L1 Norm)** within the **Rotated Coordinate System** ($|\Delta Lat_{rot}| + |\Delta Lon_{rot}|$). This feature explicitly models the "city block" driving distance, which correlates more strongly with fare amount than geodesic distance in a grid city.
*   **Cyclical Temporal Encoding:** To better capture traffic patterns, I will transform `Hour`, `Day_of_Week`, and `Month` into continuous **Sine and Cosine** components. This resolves the ordinal discontinuity (e.g., 23:00 vs 00:00) and allows the trees to learn smooth temporal transitions across midnight and weekends.

# Training
*   **Procedure:** The XGBoost and LightGBM models will be trained independently on the full processed dataset.
*   **Hyperparameters:** Both models will use high-capacity settings (e.g., `max_depth=12`, `num_leaves=512+`, `n_estimators=5000`) to ensure they can capture fine-grained spatial price variations (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
*   **Optimization:** We will use **Early Stopping** monitoring the validation RMSE to determine the optimal number of boosting rounds for each model, preventing overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00004).

# Evaluation
*   **Inference:** The test set will undergo the exact same cleaning (bounding box) and feature engineering pipeline.
*   **Ensembling:** The final prediction will be a **Weighted Average** of the XGBoost and LightGBM predictions (e.g., 0.5/0.5), combining their strengths to produce a robust final submission.
