The optimization strategy focuses on eliminating the performance regression caused by the heterogeneous ensemble and maximizing the potential of the Gradient Boosting Decision Tree (GBDT).

1.  **Remove Neural Network Stream (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011):** The previous solution demonstrated that the Neural Network failed to converge (RMSE ~15.0) and actively degraded the ensemble's performance (RMSE ~7.40) compared to the GBDT baseline (~4.38). I will completely remove the Neural Network components (model, data loaders, and training logic) to prevent "ensemble contamination."
2.  **Scale GBDT to Full Dataset (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010):** I will remove the subsampling logic (`FAST_TRAIN_SIZE`) in `runfile.py` to train on the full ~44 million row dataset. `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` is efficient enough to handle this volume within the time limit.
3.  **Enhance Model Capacity (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010, solution_lesson_node_00004):** I will update `library/config.py` to increase `max_iter` to 1200 and reduce `learning_rate` to 0.05. This allows the model to learn more fine-grained patterns from the massive dataset without overfitting, leveraging the "natural regularization" of large data.
