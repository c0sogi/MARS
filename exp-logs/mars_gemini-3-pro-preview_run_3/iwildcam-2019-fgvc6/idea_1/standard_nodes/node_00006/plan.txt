To optimize the solution, I will upgrade the model architecture from **ResNet18** to **ResNet34**. The dataset contains ~196k images, which is sufficient to train a deeper network without immediate overfitting, allowing the model to capture more complex feature hierarchies. To further improve generalization and calibration on this imbalanced dataset, I will introduce **Label Smoothing (0.1)** to the CrossEntropyLoss. Finally, I will slightly extend the training duration to **10 epochs** to ensure the larger model has sufficient time to converge under the OneCycleLR schedule.
