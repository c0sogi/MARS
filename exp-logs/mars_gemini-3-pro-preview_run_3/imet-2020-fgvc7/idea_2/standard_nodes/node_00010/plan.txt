The strategy involves reverting to a high-capacity backbone (ResNet50d) trained on the full dataset, as previous experiments demonstrated that data volume is critical (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008) and larger backbones outperform lighter ones in high-cardinality settings (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005). We will reduce the image resolution to 224x224 to facilitate training on the full 96k images within the time limit. We will also disable label smoothing (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009) to preserve hard targets for sparse labels and extend training to 10 epochs (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003) to ensure convergence.
