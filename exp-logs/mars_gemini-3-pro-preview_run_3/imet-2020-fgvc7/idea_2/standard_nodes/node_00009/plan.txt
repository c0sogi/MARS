I will implement a strategy that prioritizes data coverage and model robustness over raw architectural complexity, addressing the failure of the subsampled ConvNeXt experiment.

This model incorporates "Bag of Tricks" improvements (like anti-aliasing) over the standard ResNet50 used in the previous best solution, while preserving the 2048-dimensional feature bottleneck required for the 3,474 classes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
2.  **Full Dataset Training**: I will remove the data subsampling logic in `runfile.py`. Training on the full 96k images is critical for learning the rare classes in this high-cardinality task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
3.  **Resolution Increase**: I will increase `IMAGE_SIZE` to 256 (up from the standard 224 used in the previous ResNet50 run) to capture finer artwork details, while managing memory by setting `BATCH_SIZE` to 96.
4.  **Training Duration**: I will restore `EPOCHS` to 10 to ensure the `OneCycleLR` scheduler has sufficient time to anneal the learning rate and converge (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
