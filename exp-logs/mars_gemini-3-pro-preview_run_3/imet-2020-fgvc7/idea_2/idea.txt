Based on the lessons learned from the previous experiments, particularly the critical importance of model capacity (Cite Lesson 2) and feature dimensionality (Cite Lesson 5) for high-cardinality classification, I propose the following optimized strategy.

# Model
I propose a structural evolution from the ResNet family to the **ConvNeXt-Base** architecture.
- **Architecture:** ConvNeXt modernizes the standard Convolutional Neural Network by incorporating design choices from Transformers (e.g., larger 7x7 kernels, Layer Normalization, and inverted bottlenecks). This architecture provides a stronger inductive bias for capturing both local textures (crucial for artwork) and global context compared to the ResNet-50 used previously.
- **Feature Capacity:** ConvNeXt-Base typically outputs a 1024-dimensional feature vector (depending on the specific variant), which maintains the high-dimensional embedding space found effective in alleviating the feature bottleneck (Cite Lesson 5).
- **Head:** The classification head will remain a simple Global Average Pooling followed by a Linear layer projecting to the 3,474 classes, ensuring the model focuses its capacity on feature extraction.

# Data
- **Resolution Increase:** I will increase the input image resolution from 224x224 to **384x384** pixels. Artwork classification relies heavily on fine details (brushstrokes, textures) that are compressed or lost at lower resolutions. The increased capacity of the ConvNeXt backbone allows it to effectively utilize this higher-fidelity input.
- **Preprocessing:** Images will be resized to 384x384 and normalized.
- **Augmentation:** I will retain the lightweight augmentation strategy (Random Horizontal Flip) to maintain training speed, as complex augmentations are not the primary bottleneck.

# Training
- **Loss Function:** To address the "noisy labels" mentioned in the task description (single annotator without verification), I will implement **Binary Cross-Entropy with Label Smoothing** (e.g., smoothing factor of 0.05). This prevents the model from becoming overconfident in potentially incorrect annotations, acting as a regularizer.
- **Optimizer & Schedule:** I will retain the **AdamW** optimizer and the **OneCycleLR** scheduler.
- **Duration:** I will set the training duration to **10 epochs** (or slightly more if time permits), ensuring the learning rate schedule is sufficiently long to allow convergence in this high-dimensional space (Cite Lesson 3).
- **Precision:** Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) will be used to manage the increased memory load of the larger model and resolution.

# Evaluation
- **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** I will apply Horizontal Flip TTA during inference, averaging predictions from the original and flipped images to improve robustness (Cite Lesson 4).
- **Threshold Calibration:** I will strictly maintain the post-hoc threshold tuning step. After training, I will calculate the optimal decision threshold that maximizes the Micro-F1 score on the validation set and apply this specific threshold to the test predictions (Cite Lesson 1).
