Based on the analysis of the "Lessons" and "Previous Ideas," I propose the **Siamese Feature-Difference Network with Hybrid Pooling**.

This solution evolves the successful Siamese architecture (Idea 4) by addressing a critical limitation: the reliance on Global Average Pooling (GAP) *before* the difference operation. While Idea 4 proved that explicit differencing is superior to implicit learning, averaging the feature maps into vectors before subtraction risks washing out small, high-intensity "needle" signals in the noise. My proposal shifts the difference operation to the **spatial feature domain** and introduces **Global Max Pooling (GMP)** to specifically target localized anomalies.

### Model
**Architecture: Siamese EfficientNet-B0 with Spatial Difference and Hybrid Pooling**

1.  **Backbone:** We will use **EfficientNet-B0** initialized with ImageNet weights (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`).
    *   **Dual Inputs:** The model accepts two inputs:
        *   **Stream A (Signal):** A 3-channel stack of "On-Target" frames (Indices 0, 2, 4).
        *   **Stream B (Reference):** A 3-channel stack of "Off-Target" frames (Indices 1, 3, 5).
        *   This 3-channel structure leverages **Channel Stacking** to align temporal features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00003`) and matches the pretrained input requirements.
    *   **Shared Weights:** Both streams pass through the same backbone to produce spatial feature maps $F_{on}$ and $F_{off}$ of shape $(C, H', W')$.

2.  **Explicit Spatial Subtraction:**
    *   Instead of pooling immediately, we compute the difference in the spatial feature domain: $F_{diff} = F_{on} - F_{off}$.
    *   **Justification:** This preserves the "Explicit Difference" inductive bias (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`) but applies it to robust, high-level features. Unlike pixel-wise subtraction which fails due to drift (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00002`), feature-map subtraction is robust to small spatial shifts (Doppler drift) due to the large receptive field of the backbone.

3.  **Hybrid Pooling Head:**
    *   We extract four distinct vectors to form the final representation:
        1.  **Context Vectors:** $v_{on} = \text{GAP}(F_{on})$ and $v_{off} = \text{GAP}(F_{off})$. This preserves the absolute signal context required to distinguish clean subtraction from noisy artifacts (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00020`).
        2.  **Average Difference:** $v_{diff\_avg} = \text{GAP}(F_{diff})$. Captures the general global contrast.
        3.  **Max Difference (Innovation):** $v_{diff\_max} = \text{GMP}(F_{diff})$. Global Max Pooling specifically targets the "needle" nature of technosignatures. If a signal exists in $F_{on}$ but not $F_{off}$, it will create a high-intensity peak in $F_{diff}$. GAP might dilute this peak with the surrounding zeros; GMP captures it directly.

4.  **Classifier:**
    *   Concatenate the vectors: $[v_{on}, v_{off}, v_{diff\_avg}, v_{diff\_max}]$.
    *   Pass through a **Single Linear Layer** to predict the logit. We avoid MLPs to maintain the linear inductive bias of the difference feature (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
1.  **Input Construction:** Load the 6 cadence observations. Split into "On" (0, 2, 4) and "Off" (1, 3, 5) groups and stack to form two `(3, 273, 256)` arrays.
2.  **Resolution Preservation:** Do not resize to 224x224. Instead, **pad** the arrays to `(288, 256)` (nearest multiple of 32). This preserves the native resolution and fine-grained spectral details that resizing destroys (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`), while satisfying the stride constraints of the backbone.
3.  **Synchronized Augmentation:**
    *   Apply **Horizontal Flip** (Time Reversal) and **Vertical Flip** (Frequency Inversion).
    *   Crucially, apply these identical transformations to both the "On" and "Off" streams simultaneously to maintain pixel-wise correspondence (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`, `solution_lesson_node_00015`).
    *   Apply **Mixup** ($\alpha=0.2$) to the inputs to prevent overfitting to noise.

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss`.
*   **Optimizer:** `AdamW` with learning rate `1e-3` and weight decay `1e-2`.
*   **Scheduler:** `CosineAnnealingLR` with `T_max=12` epochs.
*   **Strategy:** Train for 12-15 epochs. The pretrained backbone combined with the explicit difference architecture should converge rapidly.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Process the test set using the metadata paths. Generate "On" and "Off" stacks, pad, and pass through the model.
*   **TTA:** Perform Test Time Augmentation by averaging predictions from the original input and a version with both Horizontal and Vertical flips applied. This maximizes geometric invariance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`).
