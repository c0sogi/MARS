Based on the accumulated lessons, specifically the limitations of small-kernel architectures in capturing global signal geometry and the proven success of explicit feature differencing, I propose the **Siamese Large-Kernel ConvNeXt Network**.

### Model
**Architecture: Siamese ConvNeXt-Tiny with Hybrid Pooling**
I propose replacing the EfficientNet-B0 backbone with **ConvNeXt-Tiny** (initialized with ImageNet-1k weights). This represents a structural shift from standard 3x3 convolutional architectures to a "Large-Kernel" paradigm.
*   **Structural Innovation (Large Kernels):** Unlike EfficientNet's small receptive fields, ConvNeXt employs **7x7 depthwise convolutions**. Technosignatures often manifest as faint, continuous diagonal lines (Doppler tracks) spanning significant spatial extents. The larger effective receptive field of ConvNeXt is structurally better suited to preserve the geometric coherence of these tracks against local noise compared to the iterative aggregation of small kernels.
*   **Siamese Streams:** The model will process two inputs via shared weights:
    *   **Stream A (Signal):** "On-Target" frames (Indices 0, 2, 4) stacked depth-wise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00003`).
    *   **Stream B (Reference):** "Off-Target" frames (Indices 1, 3, 5) stacked depth-wise.
*   **Deepest-Layer Explicit Difference:** We will extract the final feature maps ($F_{on}, F_{off}$) from the last stage of the ConvNeXt backbone (Stride 32). We will explicitly compute the spatial difference: $F_{diff} = F_{on} - F_{off}$. This adheres to the lesson that difference operations must occur at the highest abstraction level to avoid subtracting low-level noise (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00026`) and leverages the explicit difference inductive bias (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00019`).
*   **Source-Stream Hybrid Pooling:** We will apply **both Global Average Pooling (GAP) and Global Max Pooling (GMP)** to **all three** feature maps: $F_{on}$, $F_{off}$, and $F_{diff}$. This results in a concatenated feature vector of size $6 \times C$ (where $C=768$ for ConvNeXt-Tiny). This retains the critical "Source-Stream Max Pooling" strategy to detect high-intensity anomalies that might be washed out by averaging or lost in a noisy difference map (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00027`).
*   **Classifier:** A **Single Linear Layer** will map the concatenated vector to the output logit, preserving the linear transparency of the difference features (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00021`).

### Data
**Preprocessing & Augmentation**
*   **Full Dataset:** We will train on the **entire training set** (~43,000 samples) as performance scales linearly with data volume (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00024`).
*   **Native Resolution Padding:** We will **pad** the input arrays from `(273, 256)` to `(288, 256)` (nearest multiple of 32). We will **not resize**, preserving the fine-grained spectral resolution essential for low-SNR detection (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00004`).
*   **Synchronized Augmentation:** We will apply **Horizontal Flip** (Time Reversal) and **Vertical Flip** (Frequency Inversion) (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`). Crucially, these transforms will be applied **identically** to both Stream A and Stream B for every sample to maintain the pixel-wise correspondence required for the Siamese difference (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00015`).
*   **Normalization:** Inputs will be normalized to standard ImageNet mean/std to align with the pretrained backbone (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** `BCEWithLogitsLoss`.
*   **Optimizer:** `AdamW` with learning rate `1e-3` and weight decay `1e-2`.
*   **Regularization:** **Mixup** ($\alpha=0.2$) will be applied to the inputs to prevent overfitting to RFI patterns (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`).
*   **Scheduler:** `CosineAnnealingLR` with `T_max=15` epochs.
*   **Strategy:** Train for 15-18 epochs. The ConvNeXt architecture may require slightly more iterations to converge than EfficientNet, but the pretrained initialization ensures stability.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Process the test set using the padding and splitting logic.
*   **Test Time Augmentation (TTA):** Perform inference on 4 variations: Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, and Horizontal+Vertical Flip. Average the predictions to maximize geometric invariance (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00014`).
