3.  **Adjust Training Duration**: I will increase the training duration from 5 to 12 epochs. The previous run showed signs of underfitting (or rather, failure to start learning), and 5 epochs is likely insufficient for a complex backbone like EfficientNet-B4 to converge, even with deep supervision (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
