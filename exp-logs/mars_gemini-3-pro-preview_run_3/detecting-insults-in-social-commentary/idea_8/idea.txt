Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons and previous experiments, I propose a **Heterogeneous Transformer Ensemble with Full-Data Training**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by moving from single-architecture optimization to a **multi-architecture ensemble** trained on the **entire available dataset**. It synthesizes the robustness of RoBERTa-Large with the semantic superiority of DeBERTa-v3-Large, while strictly enforcing the stability constraints (Batch Size, Regularization) that caused previous advanced pipelines (Pseudo-Labeling, AWP) to fail.

### Model
The solution utilizes a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** comprising two distinct backbone architectures to capture diverse linguistic signals and minimize variance:
1.  **Backbone A: RoBERTa-Large**
    *   Retained for its proven stability and high performance on this specific dataset **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007, solution_lesson_node_00020}**.
2.  **Backbone B: DeBERTa-v3-Large**
    *   Included as the state-of-the-art backbone for NLU, providing complementary "disentangled attention" capabilities **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}**.
3.  **Aggregation Head: Mean Pooling**
    *   Both backbones will utilize **Mean Pooling** of the final hidden states. We explicitly reject complex heads like Attention Pooling, which were shown to overfit **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00013}**.
4.  **Regularization Configuration**
    *   **Dropout:** Set to **0.2** for both models to prevent overfitting on the small dataset **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}**.
    *   **Layer Freezing:** We will **freeze the embeddings and the bottom 6 encoder layers** of both models. This improves efficiency and acts as regularization by preserving general linguistic features **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}**.

### Data
1.  **Preprocessing:** Standard unicode decoding to ensure text integrity.
2.  **Tokenization:** We will use the respective tokenizers for each backbone with a **Maximum Sequence Length of 160**. This is critical to capture the context of longer comments, which are often truncated by the standard 128 length **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}**.
3.  **Dataset Construction:**
    *   **Full Data Utilization:** We will **explicitly concatenate** the Train and Validation datasets into a single training corpus **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00021}**.
    *   **No Cross-Validation:** We reject K-Fold splitting, as fragmenting the small dataset (~3,000 samples) degrades individual model quality **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00018}**.

### Training
We will train multiple independent instances (seeds) of each backbone on the full dataset.
1.  **Stability Constraint: Effective Batch Size**
    *   We will strictly maintain an **Effective Batch Size of 32** using Gradient Accumulation (e.g., Physical Batch 8 + Accumulation Steps 4). This corrects the critical failure of previous iterations where reduced batch sizes caused instability **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027, solution_lesson_node_00015}**.
2.  **Optimization Strategy**
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW with a linear learning rate scheduler and warmup.
    *   **Scheduler Decoupling:** We will calculate scheduler steps based on the fixed epoch count to avoid the "compressed schedule" issue **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}**.
3.  **Fixed Training Duration (No Validation)**
    *   Since we are training on the full dataset (no validation set), we will rely on the convergence patterns observed in previous lessons to set fixed epoch counts:
        *   **RoBERTa-Large:** Train for **3 Epochs** (Peaked at Epoch 3 in **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00011}**).
        *   **DeBERTa-v3-Large:** Train for **2 Epochs** (Peaked at Epoch 2 and overfits rapidly **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}**).
4.  **Ensemble Composition:**
    *   Train **3 independent seeds** of RoBERTa-Large.
    *   Train **3 independent seeds** of DeBERTa-v3-Large.
    *   This **Seed Averaging** mitigates the hardware stochasticity observed in small-data fine-tuning **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00012}**.

### Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The test set will be processed by all 6 trained models (3 RoBERTa + 3 DeBERTa).
2.  **Prediction:** The final submission will be the **arithmetic mean** of the probability outputs from all 6 models. This combines the benefits of architectural diversity (Heterogeneous Ensemble) and variance reduction (Seed Averaging).
