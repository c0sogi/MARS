Based on the comprehensive analysis of the provided lessons, particularly the success of Pseudo-Labeling (Lesson 35) and the catastrophic failure of the previous Distillation attempt due to poor optimization hygiene (Lesson 44), I propose a **Corrected Adversarial Knowledge Distillation Ensemble**.

This strategy structurally evolves the solution by combining the **Heterogeneous Ensemble** (Idea 8) with **Soft-Target Distillation**, while rigorously correcting the implementation flaws (Scheduler, Batch Size, Epoch count) that caused Idea 10 to fail. It aims to leverage the "dark knowledge" in the unlabeled test set while using Adversarial Training to robustify the decision boundary.

# Model
The solution employs a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** to maximize linguistic coverage and error diversity **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}**.
1.  **Backbones:** We will utilize two distinct architectures:
    *   **RoBERTa-Large:** Retained for its proven stability and high performance **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}**.
    *   **DeBERTa-v3-Large:** Retained for its superior disentangled attention mechanisms **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00020}**.
2.  **Aggregation:** Both backbones will utilize **Mean Pooling** of the final hidden states. We explicitly retain this parameter-free approach over complex heads like Attention Pooling to prevent overfitting **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00013}**.
3.  **Regularization:**
    *   **Layer Freezing:** We will freeze the embeddings and the bottom 6 encoder layers to improve efficiency and preserve general linguistic features **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00010}**.
    *   **Dropout:** Fixed at **0.2** for all models to combat overfitting on small data **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}**.

# Data
1.  **Preprocessing:** Decode unicode-escaped text to ensure character integrity.
2.  **Tokenization:** Use a **Maximum Sequence Length of 160** tokens. This is critical to capture the context of longer comments, which are otherwise truncated **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00008}**.
3.  **Dataset Construction:**
    *   **Labeled Corpus:** Explicitly concatenate the Train and Validation sets into a single corpus **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00021}**.
    *   **Distillation Corpus:** This consists of the unlabeled Test set paired with **Soft Targets** (probability distributions) generated by the Teacher Ensemble in Stage 1. Unlike Hard Pseudo-Labeling which discards uncertainty **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00035}**, this retains the full probability landscape to guide the Student models.

# Training
The training pipeline is a **Two-Stage Adversarial Distillation Workflow**.

**Stage 1: Teacher Ensemble Training**
*   **Objective:** Train high-quality "Teacher" models to generate soft targets.
*   **Procedure:** Train **3 independent seeds** of RoBERTa-Large and **3 independent seeds** of DeBERTa-v3-Large on the *Labeled Corpus*.
*   **Optimization Hygiene (Correction):** Strictly use a **Linear Learning Rate Decay** (Warmup + Decay to 0). We explicitly reject the "ramp-up only" schedule that caused the previous failure **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00044}**.
*   **Stability:** Maintain an **Effective Batch Size of 32** via Gradient Accumulation **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027}**.
*   **Target Generation:** Generate probability predictions (Soft Targets) for the entire unlabeled Test Set using the arithmetic mean of the 6 Teacher models.

**Stage 2: Adversarial Student Distillation**
*   **Objective:** Train "Student" models using **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP)** on the combined Labeled and Distillation data.
*   **Procedure:** Train a new set of **3 RoBERTa** and **3 DeBERTa** models (Students).
*   **Loss Function:** A weighted sum of Binary Cross-Entropy (for labeled data) and KL-Divergence (for distillation data).
*   **Adversarial Training (AWP):** We will enable AWP to minimize the worst-case loss under weight perturbations.
    *   *Correction from Lesson 30:* We will run these models for **5 epochs**. Previous attempts failed because AWP was restricted to 2 epochs, which was insufficient for convergence **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00030}**.
*   **Optimization Hygiene (Correction):**
    *   **Scheduler:** Use **Linear Decay** (Warmup + Decay). This corrects the critical failure in Idea 10 where a non-decaying schedule prevented convergence **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00044}**.
    *   **Batch Size:** Strictly maintain **Effective Batch Size of 32** to ensure stable gradient estimation for AWP **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00027}**.

# Evaluation
1.  **Inference:** The test set will be processed by the **6 Student Models** trained in Stage 2 (3 RoBERTa + 3 DeBERTa).
2.  **Prediction:** The final submission will be the **arithmetic mean** of the probability outputs from these 6 models. This combines the benefits of architectural diversity **Cite {solution_lesson_node_00034}**, soft-target knowledge transfer, and adversarial robustness.
