2.  **Maximize Training Data**: I will modify the training pipeline to train on the entire `train.csv` (approx. 80% of total data) and validate on `val.csv` (approx. 20% of total data), instead of splitting `train.csv` into 5 folds. Lesson 00018 highlights that in small-data regimes (<5,000 samples), fragmenting data for CV degrades individual model quality more than ensembling helps.
3.  **Hyperparameters**: I will maintain the effective settings from the lessons: Dropout=0.2, Freeze Layers=6, Max Length=160, and Epochs=4.
