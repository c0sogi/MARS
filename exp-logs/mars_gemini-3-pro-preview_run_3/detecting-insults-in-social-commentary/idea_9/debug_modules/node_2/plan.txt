The `RuntimeError` is caused by reusing the `logits` tensor from Step 4, which was computed inside a `torch.no_grad()` block. Tensors created in this context are detached from the computation graph (`requires_grad=False`), so calling `.backward()` on a loss derived from them fails. To fix this, I will perform a new forward pass in Step 5 (outside of `no_grad`) to generate logits that track gradients before computing the loss and backpropagating.
