The optimization strategy focuses on upgrading the model backbone and simplifying the training pipeline based on the provided lessons.

1.  **Backbone Upgrade**: I will replace `roberta-large` with `microsoft/deberta-v3-large`. Lesson 00022 demonstrates that using a stronger backbone architecture (DeBERTa-v3) yields better performance than applying Domain-Adaptive Pre-training (DAPT) to a weaker one (RoBERTa). DeBERTa's disentangled attention mechanism is generally superior for NLU tasks. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00022}.
2.  **Remove DAPT**: I will remove the DAPT stage entirely. It adds significant computational overhead (~18 minutes) without improving the AUC on this dataset, as the domain (online comments) is well-covered by general pre-training. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00022}.
3.  **Hyperparameter Optimization**:
    *   **Learning Rate**: Reduced to `1e-5` to accommodate DeBERTa's sensitivity to higher learning rates.
    *   **Epochs**: Increased `sft_epochs` from 4 to 5 to allow sufficient convergence time given the lower learning rate and high dropout (`0.2`).
    *   **Batch Size**: Maintained at `16` to ensure training stability, avoiding the pitfalls of small batch sizes observed in Lesson 00015. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00015}.
4.  **Efficiency**: Retained **Mean Pooling** and **Layer Freezing** (bottom 6 layers) to maintain computational efficiency and regularization benefits. Cite {solution_lesson_node_00007}, {solution_lesson_node_00010}.
