The optimization strategy involves three key changes based on the provided lessons:
1.  **Backbone Upgrade**: Switching from `roberta-large` to `microsoft/deberta-v3-large`. This aligns with Lesson 00022 and 00020, which indicate that DeBERTa-v3 provides a stronger semantic baseline than RoBERTa, even with Domain-Adaptive Pre-training (DAPT).
2.  **Removal of DAPT**: As per Lesson 00022, DAPT on this dataset yielded inferior results compared to simply using a better backbone. Removing this stage saves significant compute time.
3.  **Mixed Precision (AMP) & Gradient Accumulation**: To accommodate the large DeBERTa model on the GPU while maintaining a stable effective batch size of 32, I will enable Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) and use gradient accumulation (Batch Size 8 * 4 steps = 32). This replicates the high-performance configuration from Lesson 00020.

I will modify `library/config.py` to update the model and training parameters, `library/train_supervised.py` to implement AMP, and `runfile.py` to remove the DAPT execution block.
