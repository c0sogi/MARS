I will optimize the solution by reverting to the robust **RoBERTa-Large** backbone (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00020`) and removing the computationally expensive **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP)** (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00030`). This allows us to increase the training duration from 2 to 3 epochs per seed within the time limit, ensuring better convergence (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00011`). I will also employ a 3-seed ensemble to minimize variance and maximize the likelihood of exceeding the high validation threshold (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00018`).
