3.  **Regularization**: I will increase the Dropout rate in the classification head from 0.2 to 0.5. The previous run showed significant overfitting (97% Train vs 75% Val accuracy), and a stronger dropout is necessary for the larger ResNet18 model.
4.  **Hyperparameters**: I will increase `BATCH_SIZE` to 128 to utilize the GPU better and `EPOCHS` to 15 to allow the larger model sufficient time to converge.
