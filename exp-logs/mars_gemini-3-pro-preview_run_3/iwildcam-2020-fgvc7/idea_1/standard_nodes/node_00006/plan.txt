To improve the classification performance, I will implement a strategy focused on increasing the model's capacity and input resolution.

1.  **Upgrade Model Architecture**: I will switch the backbone from `mobilenet_v3_small` to `resnet18`. The ResNet18 architecture is deeper and has a larger capacity, allowing it to learn more complex feature representations for distinguishing between similar species, while still being efficient enough to train within the time limit on an A100 GPU. This builds upon the "Decoupling Detection and Classification" lesson (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002) by strengthening the classification stage of the cascade.
2.  **Increase Input Resolution**: I will increase the input image size from `224x224` to `256x256`. Camera trap images often contain small animals or fine details (fur patterns) that are critical for identification. A higher resolution crop preserves more of this information.
3.  **Optimize Hyperparameters**: I will increase the `BATCH_SIZE` to `128` to better utilize the A100's memory and stabilize gradient updates. I will also extend training to `12` epochs to ensure the larger model has sufficient time to converge.
