To improve the model's performance, I will implement a strategy focused on **optimization stability** and **regularization** for the DeBERTa-v3-Large backbone.

1.  **Multi-Sample Dropout (MSD)**: I will modify the `TweetModel` to use Multi-Sample Dropout (5 samples with p=0.1) instead of a single dropout layer. This technique creates an implicit ensemble during training by averaging the logits from multiple dropout masks, which helps smooth the loss landscape and improves generalization on noisy labels (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00010` regarding mitigating noise/overconfidence).
2.  **Differential Learning Rates**: I will update the training loop in `runfile.py` to apply a lower learning rate to the pretrained backbone (`1.5e-5`) and a higher learning rate to the randomly initialized classifier head (`3.0e-5`). This allows the head to converge quickly while preserving the delicate pretrained features of the large model (Cite `solution_lesson_node_00007` regarding constraining model capacity/updates).
3.  **Hyperparameter Tuning**: I will lower the base `LEARNING_RATE` in `Config` to `1.5e-5` to align with the differential strategy and ensure stable fine-tuning.
