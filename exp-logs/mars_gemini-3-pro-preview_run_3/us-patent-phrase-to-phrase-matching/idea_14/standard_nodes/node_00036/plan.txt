2.  **Gradient Checkpointing:** I will update `library/model.py` to enable gradient checkpointing on the backbone. Crucially, I will set `use_reentrant=False` and disable `use_cache` in the model configuration to prevent graph conflicts (Cite debug_lesson_7, debug_lesson_6).
3.  **FP16 Safety:** I will update the masking constant in `AttentionPooling` within `library/model.py` from `-1e9` to `-1e4` to prevent overflow in half-precision (Cite debug_lesson_9).
4.  **Batch Size Reduction:** I will lower the training batch size to 8 in `runfile.py` to fit the model within the available VRAM (Cite debug_lesson_8).
