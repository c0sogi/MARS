The `RuntimeError: value cannot be converted to type at::Half without overflow` occurs because the masking value `-1e9` is outside the representable range of `float16` (approximately $\pm 65,504$). Since the model is training with Mixed Precision (AMP) enabled (Cite debug_lesson_8), the attention scores `w` are cast to half-precision, causing the `masked_fill` operation to crash. I will update `library/model.py` to use `-1e4` instead, which is safe for FP16 and sufficiently small to ensure the Softmax output for masked tokens is zero.
