The error occurs because `Config.MODEL_NAME` is referenced in the data loader and model initialization but is not defined in `library/config.py`, causing an `AttributeError`. Additionally, `library/model.py` invokes `run_task()` at the module level, which causes the training loop to execute immediately when the module is imported by `runfile.py`, rather than waiting for `runfile.py` to control the execution.

To fix this:
1.  I will add `MODEL_NAME = "anferico/bert-for-patents"` to `library/config.py`. I will also lower the `LEARNING_RATE` to `2e-5`, which is standard for fine-tuning BERT-based models.
2.  I will comment out or remove the `run_task()` call at the end of `library/model.py` to prevent side effects on import.
