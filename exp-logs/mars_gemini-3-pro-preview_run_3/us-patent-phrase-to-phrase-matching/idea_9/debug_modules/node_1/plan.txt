The `RuntimeError: unscale_() has already been called on this optimizer` occurs because `scaler.unscale_(optimizer)` is invoked twice within a single optimization step: first inside the Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP) block and again before the optimizer update. PyTorch's `GradScaler` enforces that `unscale_` is called exactly once per step to maintain correct state. Since the AWP perturbation logic (`grad / grad.norm()`) is invariant to scalar multiplication (the scaling factor cancels out), the explicit unscaling of gradients inside the AWP block is unnecessary. Removing this redundant call resolves the error while ensuring the final gradients used for the optimizer update are correctly unscaled and validated at the appropriate time.
