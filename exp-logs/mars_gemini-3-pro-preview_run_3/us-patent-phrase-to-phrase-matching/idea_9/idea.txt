Based on the comprehensive analysis of the previous experiments and lessons learned, I propose the **"Robust Stratified Ensemble with Dynamic Layer Aggregation"**.

This strategy represents a structural evolution of the high-performing **Idea 6** (Hierarchical Context + Pearson Loss) and the proposed **Idea 8**. It integrates the advanced architectural features of **Idea 7** (Multi-Layer features, EMA) while explicitly excising the **Domain-Adaptive Pre-training (DAPT)** phase that caused catastrophic failure (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023). Furthermore, it shifts from a single-point estimate to a **Stratified Ensemble** to maximize generalization and stability.

### Model
*   **Backbone:** We will utilize **`microsoft/deberta-v3-large`** as the core encoder. Its disentangled attention mechanism provides the strongest foundation for semantic matching (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
*   **Architecture:** We will maintain the **Cross-Encoder** design, concatenating the hierarchical context and phrase pairs into a single sequence to enable deep token-level interaction (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
*   **Dynamic Layer Aggregation (Structural Innovation):** Instead of utilizing only the final hidden state or a simple concatenation, we will implement **Scalar Mixing (Weighted Layer Pooling)**. We will learn a scalar weight $w_i$ for every layer $i$ of the transformer backbone. The output representation will be the weighted sum of all layer outputs: $H_{mix} = \sum_{i=0}^{L} \frac{\exp(w_i)}{\sum_j \exp(w_j)} H_i$.
    *   *Rationale:* Different layers of a Transformer capture different linguistic properties (e.g., lower layers capture syntax/lexical matches, higher layers capture abstract semantics). By dynamically weighting these contributions, the model can better distinguish between "exact matches" (lexical) and "synonyms" (semantic).
*   **Pooling & Heads:** The aggregated sequence $H_{mix}$ will be passed through an **Attention Pooling** layer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) to generate a robust sentence embedding. This will feed into a **Dual-Head** structure (Regression + Classification) with **Multi-Sample Dropout** to leverage multi-task learning (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014).

### Data
*   **Hierarchical Context Expansion:** We will map every `context` code to its full lineage of descriptions (Section > Class > Subclass) and inject this into the input sequence (e.g., `[CLS] Section Desc [SEP] Class Desc [SEP] Subclass Desc [SEP] Anchor [SEP] Target [SEP]`). This aligns the model with the specific technical domain (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Stratified K-Fold Split:** We will implement a **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** scheme, ensuring each fold preserves the distribution of the `score` variable.
*   **Exclusion of DAPT:** We will **strictly avoid Domain-Adaptive Pre-training**. As evidenced by the failure of Idea 7, DAPT on this specific corpus leads to catastrophic forgetting of the pre-trained weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).

### Training
*   **Loss Function:** We will optimize the **Hybrid Pearson Loss**: $L_{Total} = L_{MSE} + \lambda_1 L_{CE} + \lambda_2 (1 - \rho)$. This combines geometric precision, decision-boundary sharpness, and direct metric optimization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00021).
*   **Regularization:** We will apply **Adversarial Weight Perturbation (AWP)** to flatten the loss landscape (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).
*   **Stabilization (EMA):** We will utilize **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** of the model weights during training. While Idea 7 failed, the failure was due to DAPT; EMA remains a powerful tool for improving the generalization of ensembles by smoothing out high-frequency noise from AWP.
*   **Optimization:** We will use **AdamW** with **Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD)** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00011) to preserve the pre-trained backbone features while aggressively training the new aggregation and head layers.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** For each sample in the test set, we will generate predictions using the **EMA weights** from all 5 fold models.
*   **Ensemble Aggregation:** The final similarity score will be the **arithmetic mean** of the 5 predictions.
*   **Post-Processing:** The aggregated scores will be clipped to the [0, 1] range.
