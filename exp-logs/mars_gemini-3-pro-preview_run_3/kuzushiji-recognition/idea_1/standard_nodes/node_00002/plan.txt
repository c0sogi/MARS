I will implement an optimized Single-Stage CenterNet strategy to address the classification bottleneck identified in Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00001`. The previous model failed to classify 3,848 characters effectively because it compressed features into a narrow 64-channel bottleneck. I will significantly increase the capacity of the classification branch by:
1.  **Upgrading the Backbone**: Switching from ResNet18 to **ResNet34** to extract richer, more discriminative features.
2.  **Widening the Neck**: Increasing the neck output channels from 64 to **256** to prevent information loss before the prediction heads.
3.  **Expanding the Classification Head**: Increasing the embedding dimension to **512** and deepening the MLP classifier to match the high cardinality of the label space.
4.  **Optimizing Training**: Adjusting the batch size to 8 and epochs to 15 to accommodate the larger model and ensure convergence.

    Upsampling Neck using Deconvolution layers.

    Widens the feature maps to preserve information for high-cardinality classification.
