The following modifications address the high-cardinality classification bottleneck identified in the lesson.

1.  **`library/config.py`**: I am increasing `NECK_CHANNELS` from 64 to 256 and `HEAD_CHANNELS` from 64 to 512. This significantly increases the capacity of the feature maps passed to the prediction heads, ensuring that the model retains enough information to distinguish between 3,848 classes (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
2.  **`library/model.py`**: I am widening the `ClassifierMLP` hidden dimension to 2048 (previously `in_dim * 2`). This provides the classification branch with the necessary width to learn the complex decision boundaries required for the large label space.
3.  **`runfile.py`**: I am adding the conditional logic to check if the global F1 score exceeds the threshold (0.233) before generating the submission file.
