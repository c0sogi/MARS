Based on the lessons learned from the baseline implementation and the specific challenges of the iNaturalist 2019 dataset, I propose a **Multi-Stage Progressive Resolution Pipeline with Decoupled Classifier Alignment**.

### Model
I will upgrade the architecture from EfficientNetV2-S to **EfficientNetV2-M**.
*   **Rationale:** The iNaturalist dataset contains 1,010 fine-grained categories. The "Medium" variant offers a higher parameter count and capacity, which is essential for capturing the subtle discriminative features required to distinguish between similar species.
*   **Head:** The classification head will remain a single linear layer, but its training will be decoupled from the backbone in the final stage to address class imbalance.

### Data
*   **Progressive Resolution:** To balance training speed with fine-grained accuracy, I will employ progressive resizing.
    *   **Stage 1:** Images are resized to **224x224**.
    *   **Stage 2 & 3:** Images are resized to **384x384**. Higher resolution is critical for FGVC (Fine-Grained Visual Classification) to resolve small details.
*   **Augmentation:** I will incorporate **RandAugment** (magnitude 9, std dev 0.5) to improve generalization, as the previous simple augmentations may be insufficient for the larger model capacity.

### Training
I propose a **Three-Stage Training Pipeline** to explicitly address the conflict between feature learning and tail-class performance identified in the lessons.

*   **Stage 1: Representation Learning (Low Res)**
    *   **Configuration:** Train the full EfficientNetV2-M model at **224x224** resolution.
    *   **Loss:** Standard **Cross-Entropy Loss** with Label Smoothing (0.1).
    *   **Sampling:** **Instance-Balanced Sampling** (standard shuffling).
    *   **Objective:** This stage focuses on learning robust feature representations driven by the abundant "head" classes. We explicitly avoid class-weighting here to prevent the degradation of feature quality and head-class accuracy observed previously (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

*   **Stage 2: High-Resolution Adaptation**
    *   **Configuration:** Fine-tune the full model at **384x384** resolution with a reduced learning rate.
    *   **Loss & Sampling:** Continue with Standard Cross-Entropy and Instance-Balanced Sampling.
    *   **Objective:** This adapts the learned features to higher resolution, which is crucial for distinguishing fine-grained species, without yet altering the class distribution priors.

*   **Stage 3: Decoupled Classifier Alignment**
    *   **Configuration:** **Freeze the backbone** and fine-tune *only* the classifier head (linear layer).
    *   **Sampling:** Switch to **Class-Balanced Sampling** (sampling each class with equal probability).
    *   **Objective:** This stage directly addresses the "tail" performance bottleneck (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001) by calibrating the decision boundaries for rare classes. By freezing the backbone, we ensure that the robust features learned in Stages 1 & 2 are preserved, effectively decoupling feature learning from long-tail distribution correction.

### Evaluation
*   **Inference:** Predictions will be generated using the final model at **384x384** resolution.
*   **TTA:** I will apply Test Time Augmentation (Horizontal Flip) to further boost stability.
*   **Submission:** The top-5 predictions will be generated based on the softmax probabilities from the calibrated classifier.
