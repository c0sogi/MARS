I will optimize the solution by focusing on a single, high-performing architecture (`convnext_tiny`) and extending its training duration, rather than splitting the compute budget across a heterogeneous ensemble. This decision is based on Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00008` (Prioritize Model Convergence) and Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00015` (Naive Ensembling of Disparate Performers). I will increase the training epochs from 2 to 5 per fold (Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00014`) and increase the batch size to 512 (Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00010`) to maximize convergence within the 37-minute limit. The complex stacking meta-learner will be replaced with a robust 5-fold averaging ensemble (Lesson `solution_lesson_node_00013`).
