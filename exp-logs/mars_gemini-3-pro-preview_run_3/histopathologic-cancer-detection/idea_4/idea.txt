Based on the progression of insights derived from the previous experiments, I propose a **Converged Heterogeneous Ensemble Strategy**. This approach addresses the limitations of the previous homogeneous ensemble (Idea 3) and the under-trained heterogeneous attempt (Idea 2) by combining architectural diversity with a rigorous, extended training regime.

# Model
I propose a **Heterogeneous Ensemble** composed of two distinct state-of-the-art architectures, chosen for their complementary inductive biases:
1.  **ConvNeXt-Tiny:** Retained from the previous best solution. As a "modernized CNN," it utilizes large kernels (7x7) and hierarchical transformer-like design patterns, which have proven highly effective for this task (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
2.  **EfficientNetV2-Small:** Introduced to provide a different feature extraction pathway. Its use of Fused-MBConv blocks and squeeze-and-excitation attention mechanisms contrasts with the isotropic nature of ResNets and the hierarchical design of ConvNeXt, potentially capturing fine-grained textural details that ConvNeXt might miss.

To maximize data utility and generalization, we will implement **5-Fold Cross-Validation** for *both* architectures. This results in a total of 10 independent models (2 architectures × 5 folds), creating a robust voting committee that mitigates the variance of any single data split or architectural choice.

# Data
- **Preprocessing (Cropping):** We will strictly maintain the **Center Crop of 64x64 pixels** for all inputs. This focuses the model on the labeled 32x32px ROI while preserving the optimal 16px context buffer, which has been empirically shown to maximize the signal-to-noise ratio (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
- **Augmentation:** We will apply a comprehensive geometric augmentation suite including **RandomRotate90**, HorizontalFlip, and VerticalFlip. This ensures the models learn the rotational invariance inherent in histopathology images, which was shown to improve AUC (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Training
- **Convergence Strategy:** We will train every fold of every model for **30 epochs**. This extension beyond the previous 20-epoch limit is designed to fully exhaust the learning capacity of the models, as previous experiments indicated that training loss was still decreasing at epoch 20 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014). This directly addresses the failure of the previous ensemble attempt, ensuring we aggregate fully converged models (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
- **Optimization:**
    - **Batch Size:** We will utilize the maximum possible batch size that fits in the A100 VRAM (e.g., 512+) to ensure stable Batch Normalization statistics and gradient updates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    - **Regularization:** We will maintain moderate regularization (Weight Decay `1e-4`) to prioritize ranking performance (AUC) over probability calibration, avoiding the pitfalls of over-regularization (Cite solution_lesson_node_00009).
- **Checkpointing:** We will employ **TTA-Integrated Checkpointing**. For each fold, the best model weights will be saved based on the validation AUC calculated using Test Time Augmentation, ensuring the saved state is explicitly optimized for the inference strategy (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

# Evaluation
- **Inference Strategy:** We will apply **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)** with 4 views (Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, Combined Flip) for every test image (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
- **Aggregation:** The final submission will be the **soft voting (arithmetic mean)** of the probabilities from all 10 converged models (2 architectures × 5 folds). Each model's contribution will itself be the average of its 4 TTA views. This multi-layered aggregation leverages diversity in architecture, data splits, and geometric orientation to minimize variance and maximize AUC.
