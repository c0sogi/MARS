Based on the progression of experiments and the lessons learned, I propose a **Converged Heterogeneous Stacking Strategy with TTA-Enhanced Meta-Features**. This approach structurally evolves the solution from a single-layer homogeneous ensemble (Idea 3) to a two-stage learning pipeline. It specifically addresses the failures of previous heterogeneous attempts (Idea 2, Idea 5) by enforcing strict convergence guarantees and correcting the "naive averaging" flaw through learned aggregation.

# Model
I propose a **Two-Stage Stacking Ensemble** that leverages complementary inductive biases and optimizes the aggregation logic:

1.  **Level-0 Base Learners (Heterogeneous):**
    *   **ConvNeXt-Tiny:** Retained as the primary backbone. Its hierarchical, Transformer-inspired design (7x7 kernels, LayerNorm) has proven to be the strongest individual performer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).
    *   **EfficientNetV2-Small:** Re-introduced as the secondary backbone. Its architecture (Fused-MBConv, Squeeze-and-Excitation) captures different textural features than ConvNeXt. Crucially, unlike the failed Idea 2, this model will be treated as a first-class citizen with a full training budget to ensure parity in signal quality (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
    *   **Structure:** We will implement **5-Fold Cross-Validation** for *both* architectures, resulting in 10 independent base models. This ensures 100% data utilization and robust Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013).

2.  **Level-1 Meta-Learner:**
    *   **Logistic Regression:** Instead of the arithmetic mean used in previous ideas (which dilutes strong signals with weak ones, Cite solution_lesson_node_00015), we will train a linear classifier on the OOF probability outputs of the base learners. This allows the system to learn optimal weights for each architecture.

# Data
-   **Preprocessing (Cropping):** We will strictly apply a **Center Crop of 64x64 pixels**. This isolates the labeled 32x32px Region of Interest (ROI) while preserving the optimal 16px context buffer, minimizing label noise from the periphery (Cite solution_lesson_node_00001).
-   **Augmentation:** We will employ the proven geometric suite: **RandomRotate90**, HorizontalFlip, and VerticalFlip. This forces the model to learn the rotational invariance inherent in histopathology (Cite solution_lesson_node_00002).

# Training
-   **Convergence Strategy:** We will train every fold of every model for **30 Epochs**. Previous analysis (Cite solution_lesson_node_00014) showed that loss was still decreasing at epoch 20. Extending the schedule ensures we extract the maximum discriminative power from the backbones, directly preventing the under-training failure of Idea 2 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00008).
-   **Optimization:**
    -   **Batch Size:** We will maximize the batch size (saturating the A100 VRAM, e.g., 512+) to ensure stable Batch Normalization statistics and gradient updates (Cite solution_lesson_node_00010).
    -   **Safety:** We will explicitly disable `drop_last` or ensure `len(dataset) >= batch_size` to prevent the silent data-subsetting failures observed in Idea 5 (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018).
-   **TTA-Enhanced OOF Generation:** Unlike standard stacking which uses raw validation predictions, we will generate OOF predictions using **Test Time Augmentation (TTA)**. Since TTA was shown to select better model checkpoints (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006), using it to generate the training data for the meta-learner will provide a cleaner, lower-variance signal for the Level-1 model.

# Evaluation
1.  **Base Inference:** For each test image, we will generate predictions from all 10 base models (2 architectures × 5 folds) using **4-view TTA** (Original, Horizontal Flip, Vertical Flip, Combined) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00005).
2.  **Stacked Aggregation:** These TTA-averaged probabilities will be fed into the trained Logistic Regression meta-learner to produce the final calibrated probability. This method prioritizes the "OOF Strategy" (training on all data) over the "Holdout Strategy" to maximize the final leaderboard score (Cite solution_lesson_node_00020).
