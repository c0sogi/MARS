The issue is that the Level 2 Meta-Learner is currently trained on the Out-Of-Fold (OOF) predictions for the entire dataset, which includes the validation set. This allows the meta-learner to learn from the validation set's labels, causing data leakage and an invalid evaluation metric. To fix this, I will modify `runfile.py` to calculate the index separating the training and validation partitions and pass it to the `HybridEnsembleTrainer`. Then, I will update `library/stacking_engine.py` to use this index to strictly isolate the training data when fitting the meta-learner, ensuring the validation set remains unseen during training (Cite debug_lesson_12).
