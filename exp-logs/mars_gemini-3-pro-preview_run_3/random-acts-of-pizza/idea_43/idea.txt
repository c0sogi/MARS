Based on the comprehensive empirical analysis of the "Lessons Learned," particularly the breakthrough success of **Textual Field Concatenation** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044), the superiority of **Sparse Representations** for behavioral history (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029), and the potential for **Algorithmic Diversity** in dense modeling (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025), I propose a **Hex-View Stacking Ensemble with NMF-Based Latent Context Injection**.

This solution structurally evolves the "Pent-View" architecture by introducing a **LightGBM** branch for algorithmic diversity and a **Latent Community Injection** layer using **Non-Negative Matrix Factorization (NMF)**. This injection strategy bridges the "silo" between text and history by providing dense semantic learners with interpretable "Community Topic" probabilities, without suffering from the vocabulary contention of early fusion or the information loss of scalar encoding.

# Model

The architecture is a **Stacking Ensemble** composed of **six** "Level 1" base learners, organized into three topology-matched branches. These learners feed into a "Level 2" Logistic Regression meta-learner.

**Level 1: Base Learners**

1.  **Sparse Lexical Branch (Text Modality)**
    *   **Lexical Bagger:** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **TF-IDF vectors of the Concatenated Title and Body** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044) combined with the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Random Forests on sparse TF-IDF consistently outperform dense methods for capturing specific high-impact keywords (Cite solution_lesson_node_00013). We apply regularization (`min_samples_leaf=2`) to prevent overfitting (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025).

2.  **Sparse Behavioral Branch (History Modality)**
    *   **Community Bagger:** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **TF-IDF vectors of the Subreddit History** combined with the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* We treat user history as a "Bag-of-Concepts." We strictly limit the vocabulary to the **Top 1,000** subreddits. This sparse representation is superior to dense embeddings for unordered categorical sets (Cite solution_lesson_node_00029) and prevents overfitting to rare communities (Cite solution_lesson_node_00042).

3.  **Dense Semantic Branch (Text Modality - The Diversity Trio)**
    *   **Semantic Booster (XGB):** An **XGBoost Classifier** trained on **Frozen Compact Embeddings (Title + Body)** combined with **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Captures non-linear signals in the continuous semantic space (Cite solution_lesson_node_00018). We use `scale_pos_weight` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00034) and maintain high capacity (2,000 estimators).
    *   **Semantic Gradient (LGBM):** A **LightGBM Classifier** trained on **Frozen Compact Embeddings (Title + Body)** combined with **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Innovation:* We introduce LightGBM to provide **Algorithmic Diversity** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00025), leveraging its leaf-wise growth strategy to capture different structural patterns than XGBoost.
    *   **Semantic Bagger (RF):** A **Random Forest Classifier** trained on **Frozen Compact Embeddings (Title + Body)** combined with **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Provides structural diversity. We apply **Modality-Specific Regularization** (`max_depth=12`, `min_samples_leaf=4`) to prevent memorization of dense noise (Cite solution_lesson_node_00038).

4.  **Contextual Branch (Metadata Modality)**
    *   **Metadata Anchor:** A **Logistic Regression** model trained on the **Augmented Global Metadata**.
        *   *Retention:* Acts as a high-bias regularizer (Cite solution_lesson_node_00006).

**Level 2: Meta-Learner**
*   **Logistic Regression:** Calibrates the ensemble weights (Cite solution_lesson_node_00012).

# Data

The data pipeline enforces strict leakage prevention, robust typing, and **Positive Feature Selection**.

1.  **Leakage Prevention:**
    *   Source Text: Use `request_text_edit_aware` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00003).
    *   Exclusion: Drop all columns suffixed with `_at_retrieval`.
2.  **Feature Engineering:**
    *   **Latent Community Injection (Structural Innovation):**
        *   Step 1: Compute TF-IDF on `requester_subreddits_at_request`.
        *   Step 2: Apply **Non-Negative Matrix Factorization (NMF)** (e.g., 15 components) to this sparse matrix. NMF produces additive, interpretable "soft cluster" memberships suitable for metadata injection.
        *   Step 3: Add these 15 dense features to the Global Metadata Vector. This allows Text models to condition predictions on the user's latent persona (Cite solution_lesson_node_00023).
    *   **Augmented Global Metadata:** Explicitly **Allow-List** (Cite solution_lesson_node_00030):
        *   **Raw Timestamp:** `unix_timestamp_of_request_utc` to capture temporal drift (Cite solution_lesson_node_00041).
        *   **User Stats:** Account Age, Karma, Post Counts.
        *   **Latent Community Topics:** The NMF components.
        *   **Exclusion:** Explicitly exclude derived text length features (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027).
    *   **Text Concatenation:** Join `request_title` and `request_text_edit_aware` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
    *   **Sparse Features:** TF-IDF (Unigrams/Bigrams) with `sublinear_tf=True` and `min_df=5` (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).
    *   **Dense Features:** Frozen `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` Embeddings (384-dim) (Cite solution_lesson_node_00028).
3.  **Preprocessing:**
    *   **Imputation:** Median imputation.
    *   **Scaling:** `StandardScaler` applied to dense inputs.
    *   **No Caching:** Recompute features on-the-fly (Cite solution_lesson_node_00024).

# Training

The training procedure implements the **Validation-Guided Retraining Protocol**.

1.  **Fold Generation:** 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation for the Stacking loop.
2.  **Level 1 Training (OOF Generation):**
    *   **NMF Fitting:** Fit NMF on the training folds and transform the OOF fold to prevent unsupervised leakage.
    *   **RF / Linear:** Train on 4 folds, predict on 5th. Use `class_weight='balanced'`.
    *   **XGBoost / LightGBM:** Train on 4 folds, predict on 5th. Use `scale_pos_weight`.
3.  **Level 2 Training:** Train Meta-Learner on the OOF matrix.
4.  **Final Retraining:**
    *   **NMF Fitting:** Fit NMF on the **Full Training Set**.
    *   **RF / Linear:** Retrain on the **Full Training Set** (Train + Validation) to maximize data usage (Cite solution_lesson_node_00044).
    *   **XGBoost / LightGBM:** Retrain on the **Training Set only**, explicitly passing the **Global Validation Set** (from the metadata split) as the `eval_set` to the `fit()` method to trigger **Early Stopping**. This prevents "blind overfitting" (Cite solution_lesson_node_00027) and avoids the instability of nested validation on small data (Cite solution_lesson_node_00036).

# Evaluation

1.  Process the test set to generate all feature views.
2.  Apply the global NMF model (fitted on full train) to generate Latent Community features.
3.  Pass specific views to the six base learners to obtain probability scores.
4.  Feed aggregated probabilities to the Meta-Learner for the final prediction.
