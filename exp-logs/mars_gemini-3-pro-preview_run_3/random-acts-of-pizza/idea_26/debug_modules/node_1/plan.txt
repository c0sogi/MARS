The `ValueError: empty vocabulary` is caused by three interacting issues: 1) `pd.read_parquet` deserializing list columns as NumPy arrays, which fails the `isinstance(x, list)` check and results in empty strings (Cite debug_lesson_7); 2) the high `min_df=5` threshold pruning all tokens in the small 100-row debug sample (Cite debug_lesson_12); and 3) the incorrect application of English stop-word removal to subreddit identifiers (Cite debug_lesson_2).

To fix this, I will:
1.  Update `library/feature_engineering.py` to accept both `list` and `np.ndarray` when processing the subreddit column.
2.  Update `runfile.py` to override `min_df` to 1 and disable `stop_words` during the debug run configuration.
