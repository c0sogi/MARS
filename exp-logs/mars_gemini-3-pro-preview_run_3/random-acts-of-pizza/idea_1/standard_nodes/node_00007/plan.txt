**Strategy:**
1.  **Refine Temporal Features**: The failure analysis indicated a high correlation between `unix_timestamp_of_request` and prediction errors, suggesting that the raw timestamp is acting as a noisy or drifting signal. I will replace this raw feature with cyclical temporal features: `request_hour` and `request_day` (day of week). This captures seasonality (e.g., users might be more generous on weekends or evenings) without overfitting to specific dates.
2.  **Add Text Meta-Features**: I will introduce `request_text_len` (character count) and `request_word_count` as explicit numerical features. These serve as proxies for the "effort" put into a request, which is often a strong signal in altruistic contexts, complementing the TF-IDF vectors.
3.  **Enhance Regularization**: I will increase the `min_samples_leaf` in the Random Forest component from 8 to 10 and increase `n_estimators` to 500. This is intended to further reduce the overfitting observed (Train AUC ~0.89 vs Val AUC ~0.65) and improve the stability of the ensemble.
4.  **Cite Lesson**: This approach builds on **Lesson 00006**, maintaining the Hybrid Ensemble (RF + LR) architecture while refining the dense feature set to be more robust and less prone to leakage or drift.
