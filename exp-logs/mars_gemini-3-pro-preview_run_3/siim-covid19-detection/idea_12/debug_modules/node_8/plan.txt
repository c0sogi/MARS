The issue is that the `SwinBackboneWrapper` incorrectly assumes the features returned by the underlying backbone are objects with `.pixel_values` and `.pixel_mask` attributes, whereas the runtime error confirms they are simple tuples containing `(tensor, mask)`. This mismatch causes an `AttributeError` when iterating over the feature list. I will fix this by updating the loop to unpack the tuple directly into `p_values` and `p_mask` instead of accessing non-existent attributes (Cite debug_lesson_32).
