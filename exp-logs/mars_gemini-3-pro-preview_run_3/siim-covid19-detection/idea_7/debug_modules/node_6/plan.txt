The `IndexError` occurs because the `select_training_samples` method in `library/model.py` reuses the variable name `proposals` when augmenting the region proposals with ground truth boxes. This variable shadowing causes a mismatch: the `ProposalMatcher` likely receives the original list of proposals (size N) due to scope or execution order nuances, generating a boolean mask of size N. However, the subsequent indexing operation `proposals[img_id][img_sampled_inds]` operates on the augmented list (size N+M), leading to a shape mismatch between the mask (2000) and the tensor (2001).

To fix this, I will introduce a new variable `proposals_with_gt` to store the augmented proposals. This explicitly ensures that the `ProposalMatcher` and the subsequent indexing operations both operate on the same augmented data structure, resolving the dimension mismatch.
