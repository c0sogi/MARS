# Proposed Strategy: Deep Affine-Decoupled BiGRU (DAD-BiGRU)

This strategy introduces a **4-Layer Bidirectional GRU** interleaved with **Affine Decoupled Structural Injection** modules. Unlike previous iterations that injected structural information as a simple additive bias ($h + m$), this module allows the paired neighbor to apply an affine transformation ($h \cdot s + t$) to the current state. This mimics a "gating" or "modulation" effect where the structure can amplify or dampen specific sequence features, offering superior expressivity while maintaining the stability of the decoupled design.

### Model

1.  **Input Representation:**
    *   **Tensor Shape:** $(N, 107, 14)$. Sequence dimension is preserved. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00002**.
    *   **Feature Channels:** Strictly **One-Hot Encoding** for nucleotide (4), secondary structure (3), and predicted loop type (7). No learned embeddings. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00009**.
    *   **Purity:** No concatenation of paired features at the input. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00037**.

2.  **Convolutional Stem:**
    *   **1D Convolutional Layer** (kernel size 3, filters 256, activation GELU).
    *   **Purpose:** Projects sparse inputs into a dense embedding space and aggregates local k-mers. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00027**.

3.  **High-Capacity 4-Layer Backbone:**
    *   The backbone consists of **4 Blocks** (maximizing capacity, addressing **Cite solution_lesson_node_00026**). Each block contains a **Bidirectional GRU** followed by the **Affine Structural Module** (except the final block).
    *   **BiGRU Layer:** Hidden dimension of **384** (Total 768). We maintain this high capacity to capture complex dependencies. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00103** and **Cite solution_lesson_node_00063**.
    *   **Affine Decoupled Structural Module (Structural Innovation):**
        *   **Context:** For every position $i$ paired with index $j$.
        *   **Gather:** Retrieve $h_j$ directly (Point-to-Point). **Cite solution_lesson_node_00058**.
        *   **Input Zero-Masking:** If $i$ is unpaired, explicitly force $h_j = 0$. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00064**.
        *   **Affine Decoupled Messages:** Instead of a single message, we compute two vectors derived *solely* from the neighbor $h_j$:
            *   **Scale Factor:** $s_{ij} = \text{Tanh}(W_s \cdot h_j + b_s)$.
            *   **Shift Factor:** $t_{ij} = \text{GELU}(W_t \cdot h_j + b_t)$.
        *   **Bias-Driven Refinement:** For unpaired bases ($h_j=0$), these terms become learnable biases ($s_{ij} \to \text{Tanh}(b_s)$, $t_{ij} \to \text{GELU}(b_t)$), allowing loops to self-modulate their features. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00085**.
        *   **Context-Aware Gating:** $g_{ij} = \sigma(W_g \cdot [h_i; h_j])$. Controls the injection strength.
        *   **Stabilization:** Apply **LayerNorm** internally to the inputs of the $s$, $t$, and $g$ projections to prevent saturation. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00075**.
        *   **Affine Injection:** The residual update applies the affine transformation: $u_{ij} = (h_i \odot s_{ij}) + t_{ij}$.
        *   **Update:** $h_{res} = h_i + g_{ij} \odot u_{ij}$.
        *   **Post-Normalization:** $h_{out} = \text{LayerNorm}(h_{res})$. Ensures stability for the deep 4-layer stack. **Cite solution_lesson_node_00068**.

4.  **Output Head:**
    *   Linear projection to 5 target values.
